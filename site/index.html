From the description of the Studio Construction forum, where members answered these questions for nineteen years.

John Sayers designed recording studios, and from 2003 to 2022 his forum was where people came to plan and build their own. Members posted floor plans, wall sections and build diaries, and John and a group of experienced builders answered them. This archive keeps all 15,437 threads and 140,875 posts readable now that the original site is offline.
