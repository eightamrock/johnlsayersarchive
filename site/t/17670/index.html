Soundman20202 July 2012#2
The rear end of your control room get narrower: that should not happen. The control room should get wider towards the back, or worst case, the walls should stay parallel, but it should not get narrower. Also, the rear wall of the control room is concave shaped: it is "V" shaped, with two angle walls. That should either be inverted to convex, or better still just left flat Another thing; you have the control room directly coupled to the live room: you won't get much isolation between them like that. The two walls that bypass the pillar between the rooms are attached to each other in two places: you need to break those connections, such that the control room wall does not touch the live room wall at all. - Stuart -
