SB20 January 2005#11
The trouble with removing the doors from the control room to the booth is that you'll have a very long walk through several doors to move between them. A large pain for you when plugging stuff in or changing mic placement. Also an equally large pain for talent between takes. The area behind the couch is acoustic treatment, the monitors are soffit mounted for better performance, the console is further back to put your ears in the optimal listening position (follow the dotted lines to your head.) The answers to these and probably many other questions you have (or will have soon) can be found at the link John offered: http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html Read through it all, and remember to click on all of the tabs along the top of the page to get to all of the material.
