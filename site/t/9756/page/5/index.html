jwl7 March 2008#112
BradJacob wrote:
The bass at the board is "normal" (if that makes any sense). Then, when I move to the backof the room (a whole 5 feet or so from mix position) the bass is a lot more noticable. I'm guessing that's because it just takes some physical distance for a bass wave to develop?
Bass often builds up in the rear of a room, but not because bass waves need room to develop. I hear this often, though.... The real reason has to do with how the reflections from the rear wall interact with the other sound waves in the room. This is another reason the rear wall is a common place for bass trapping.
