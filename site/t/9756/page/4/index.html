Marc - You're AWESOME man!! That's exactly "why" I used plywood. I had a 2x4 in the front and two 2x6 in the back. The wood didn't so much "sink" - but what happened was the wood shrunk - and wound up "bowing" upwards. The whole board was wobbling from side to side. Mind you, the humidity level down there is an avg of 30%. That's like Western-dryness. Not good for an acoustic guitar that you'd like to see survive!! So yeah, I cut some plywood into strips, and glued and screwed them. Marc is 100% correct... those home-made "joists" will NEVER move. Birch plywood - at Lowes, can't beat it! And yes, I picked up that cock-roach looking screw :wink:
