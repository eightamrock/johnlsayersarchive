It won't do diddley........... You need insulation with depth - the problem with this approach is going to be that the air cavity inside the wall will acheive the outside temps, as will the drywall surface - and the foil pack will not be able to keep up with the heat load. Sorry - but that's just the way it works. I made it a point to check out this material - the reccomended installation is within the wall cavity - and they stress the importance of providing a sealed air-space on either side of the insulation - thus creating a 2 cavity air system. Again - it won't work the way you want. Sincerely, Rod
