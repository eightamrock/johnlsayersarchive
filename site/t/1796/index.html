dymaxian14 June 2004#2
Greetings! Put the mixing desk on the "left" wall instead of the "top" - try to set it up so that the speakers fire down the long axis of the room. The room will be more symmetrical around the speakers this way, which will help the stereo imaging. Otherwise it's a good start! Are you going to use gobos for isolation while recording?
