Ro28 March 2009#8
Okay, I understand now. I hope :) I'd go for a 2-piece laminated glass pane. A thick sheet has lower resonance than a thinner piece. Multiple layers of DIFFERENT thickness will overlap in resonance, sure, but you want to have those as LOW as possible. Altho the TOTAL thickness is actually what your resonance will be since the 2 pieces are laminated together. (sigh) Since a laminated pane made out of 2 pieces of glass (with a layer of PVB in between) will be better, visually, and prolly a whole lotta cheaper too, I'd go with just "standard" laminated glas. Meaning; 2 pieces of floatglass with a dampening layer in between. The glazer will know exactly what the end result in thickness will be. A PVB layer can be different in size and stifness. I think you'll end up with a 1-1,5mm layer. It doesn't really matter on which side you put the glass. 3/4 on CR or tracking and 1" the otherway, doens't really matter. AS long as the total mass equals, or better exceeds, the wall mass.
