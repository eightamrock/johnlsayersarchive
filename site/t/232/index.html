do you mount them to the room walls?
you can mount them either way. If you want them portable then make them free standing otherwise build them directly onto the wall.
are you supposed to have alternating gaps per panel? or multiple panels with the same gap spacing just different spacing per panel
The idea is to vary the slot width, the slat width and the depth from the wall - these are the variables. cheers john
