gullfo13 September 2007#4
behind the slats will be an open weave cloth and then 703 insulation (or if its out of budget) pink insulation. the insulation will sit away from the slats - perhaps half-way and also on the wall side. the compartments are divided up to improve efficiency at the design frequencies. in the front bass traps you'd probably put in some hanging broadband absorbers - below the speaker (shorter ones) and behind it (taller ones).
