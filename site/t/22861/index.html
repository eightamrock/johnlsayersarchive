gullfo3 July 2021#2
the foam in and of itself will not necessarily get you 53db STC, but the construction around it along with the foam could, bearing in mind STC is only a measure of isolation down to 125Hz so bass and drum low end will not likely be as isolated as the fibrous insulation would get you. however, the injection of foam usually has better thermal properties. you want open cell foam for this and i'd recommend thoroughly checking references since the renovation by injection without removing drywall etc can lead to damage from the expansion as well as longer times for gas-off.
