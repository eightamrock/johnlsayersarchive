Actually, after looking again, the angled walls in the back of the room are soffits for the rear speakers of a 5.1 surround environment. (Looks like the right rear monitor was inadvertently left out of the drawing.) Proper placement of those rear units determines distance from the mix position, and you have a general choice of somewhere between 110 and 150 degrees... That's why all that stuff is where it is. :)
