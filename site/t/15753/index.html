Soundman202020 March 2011#10
what do I do to the point where the walls meet the floor? Caulk?
Exactly. The best method is three beads of caulk under the sole plate (one down the middle, one close to each edge) then another bead under each layer of drywall as it goes up. Why so many seals? You need both leaves to be totally airtight. If you just caulk once, and there is a gap or break in that bead, then you have no seal. Two beads is better insurance, but you still might have a couple of gaps close to each other. Three is better still. Five or more might seem like overkill, but caulk is cheap. Regarding SketchUp: there are libraries of "things" available on-line, like furniture, musical instruments, etc. You can download them and add them to your design. But beware! many of the are BIG, in terms of very detailed geometry that clutter up your design and slow down the response. There are also some pretty good tutorials for SketchUp available on-line. It really isn't hard to use, once you get your hear around a few strange ways of doing things, and it turns out to be pretty powerful once you get the hang of it! - Stuart -
