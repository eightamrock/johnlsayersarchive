Gregwor31 January 2020#2
Johnny, Sorry about the late reply. 1. the room is virtually square which is not good. I encourage you to go to Bob Golds Room Mode Calculator and punch in your dimensions. You will see that it probably fails 1 or more of the 3 tests. 2. I would probably prefer the crafts room over the current music room due to the more rectangular shape. 3. A decently treated control room typically works alright for tracking vocals and acoustic guitar so I would just have it as one big mixing/recording room. The proposed closet is tiny and probably wouldn't sound great for recording anyway. I'd probably use the closet as a storage space. 4. Get the builder to use the thickest rimboard they can for all of the walls pertaining to your room(s). 5. Get the builder to put two layers of 3/4" subfloor down. Do whatever you can to ensure they seal the seams of each layer. I would suggest having them use Green Glue as well but that will add a lot of extra cost. Having them put two layers of subfloor is extra money but I assure you, no matter the cost, it's worth it because to address it afterwards is a nightmare.. real life nightmare. It's probably the main reason I haulted my personal build. I needed a break from the nightmare. 6. Do your best to adjust the design so that the mechanical (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, central vac) are not above your room. If it has to be placed in the joists above your room, make sure they are all together so that you can box in just one cavity, not a bunch. 7. Make sure you don't have doors in the corners of your room (right now, your crafts room that I suggested should be your music room does in fact have the door in the corner). 8. Make sure there are no windows in the corners as well. This is so that you can bass trap the hell out of the room. Greg
