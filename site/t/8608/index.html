drfrankencopter16 April 2007#7
The point is that it is the mass + airgap + internal damping material that results in improving STC (and LF TL). Slanting the panes has a determental effect on STC due to a reduction in airgap size. But, I think it can affect the coincidence dip...but that can be countered by using different pane thicknesses (which is usually done anyways). The main reasons for slanting the glass have nothing to do with STC...it's done to reduce both visual reflctions, and high frequency audio reflections. Cheers Kris
