This past October I visisted the Audio Engineering Society's tradeshow in NYC and came across a lecture about the workings of acoustics in small rooms. The thought behind the lecture was that the theory applied in concert halls and larger spaces for control rooms or live studio applications can not be used when trying to tune the kind of spaces that 99% of home studio owners have at their disposal. Since it was a lecture one had to pay top dollar for and I am not an AES member I refrained from going, but wrote to the lecturer last week and asked him for a copy. he was kind enough to provide me with one in PDF file format. If anyone would like a copy, please IM me. Not that I do not check my messages every day. Also, if some of the enigineers on the site can chime in on this lecture and start a dialogue I think many of us would be well served.
