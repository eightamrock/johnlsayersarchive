donal_finn5 December 2009#2
Hi Jason. The link below to a sticky thread on this site should help a lot viewtopic.php?p=51632 If i was you i would try to be a bit more specific with questions, accoustic building is a huge subject with a million answers to every question, folks here like to help but cant answer loose random questions in any kind of relevant way, they need specifics!! and pics and plans too. get a hold of google sketchup program and start drawing a plan of what you want to build then watch the experts around the site queue up to criticise and condem !!! :D (and also point in the right direction!) I am just a beginner here and halfway through my build, so i probably will not be of so much help to you, but in the board index for the site you will find sticky threads for just about every kinda concievable building question you might have and then some!! My advice is to slow down, in order to build well acoustically one needs to understand the concepts involved, rushing in will be an expensive error that you will regret. My build is already well past my initial timeframe set out but its only 50% complete, !!!!! but i know pretty much for sure when its finished it will improve all the areas i set out to improve and work as i had planned it to work. :D Enjoy the process and Jah bless the work. peace df
