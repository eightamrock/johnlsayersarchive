I sort of want to be closer to my rack gear for ease of reach?
Soundman202029 October 2009#2
Is it ok to have the total setup somewhat to the left or right of the center of the room?
Nope. The room needs to be symmetrical, and you need to be in the middle of that symmetry. If not, then you wont get an accurate stereo image, and your mixes most likely wont translate well. So if you need access to your equipment, then design the room so that the equipment is close to you. - Stuart -
