jwl27 February 2007#4
A quick response. If you are looking for max bang-for-buck isolation in a basement on a concrete slab, you'll want to do this: * build a room-in-a-room, with the wall frames connected directly to the concrete floor. Also, use ceiling joists so you can create a ceiling in your room. * use double-drywall construction, with Green Glue in between the layers of drywall if you have the budget. As Rod shows, all the drywall should be on ONE SIDE ONLY of the frame. * make everything airtight, including the door. That should get you pretty far in terms of isolation. If you have Rod's book, it's all in there. Just realize that isolation is a lot of work, needing a lot of materials put in exactly the right place to work correctly.
