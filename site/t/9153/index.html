doublehelix5 August 2007#4
OK, I said "screw it" and went ahead and rerouted the electrical wiring in the vicinity of the audio cables. It wasn't worth taking a chance. If I ended up hearing a slight hum from those cables after things are already sewn up, I would have been really ticked, and it would have been a major task to fix later. In the end, I decided to bite the bullet and I pulled out those 2 electrical runs, and re-ran them up and around to get them out of the way. Of course, the wires were not going to be long enough, so I pulled them out, and replaced them completely. I thought that was a better solution than putting in a junction box as a splice point. Besides, I can use the old wire when I start wiring all the boxes. An hour's worth of work and $15.00 worth of wire was worth the peace of mind. Here is a new pic after the re-route:
