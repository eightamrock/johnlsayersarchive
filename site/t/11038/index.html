Still looking for advice here, but I have a little update. I borrowed a small 5k btu window ac from a friend. I just sat the ac on cinder blocks outside in free air and did a quick and dirty attachment of the hrv ducts to it with tape. It was not air tight of course, but I was hoping to at least get some valuable info from the experiment. Well, the air inside was a little cooler then before, but not nearly enough to cool the place properly. My main concern is if the fans on the AC unit and HRV will work in harmony or be bad to run with each other. bassman2 August 2008#3
I'll chime in.... I think that setup would most likely cause strain on one or both fan motors unless they are moving exactly the same amount of air. I would just have the AC unit open in that chiller room and also have the HRV open at both ends into the chiller room. The air in the chiller room will blend and mix and you should get the results you are looking for. Make sure your window unit is big enough. How much space are you trying to cool? A 5K is good for only a very small space, perhaps a few hundred square feet. Depends on how much HOT gear is in there as well. You gotta do your BTU calculations as in Rod's book to get it close. -ashley
