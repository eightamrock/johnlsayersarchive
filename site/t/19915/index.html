Hi there "Julesroad", and Welcome! As hub said: you aren't giving enough information to go on! You have over 900 square feet of space, which is great for what you want: I have built three and four room studios in half that space, so getting your three rooms in that area can be done, but you'll need to provide all of the information about the space, including doors, windows, stairs, materials, as well as support columns, HVAC ducts, plumbing, gas lines, electrical wiring and other things that could interfere with the studio.
