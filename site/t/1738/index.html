"Are there any books or places I can look so that I can find the best possible way to build this studio" - you've found it - click on the John Sayers logo at upper right, check out all the links there - you'll find pix of studios being built, a complete recording manual, designs for DIY absorbers (wall units link) - then, go to the Construction forum here and read the "sticky" section at the first of the page - more drawings of construction details, as well as do's/dont's, etc - finally, after those resources have answered several of your questions and prompted a few dozen more, you'll be ready to "make sawdust"...
