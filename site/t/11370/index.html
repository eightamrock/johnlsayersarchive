Soundman202010 November 2008#11
Question is if leaving the inner leaf off compromises the stc too much, and how much? ... but i'm still puzzled about the 1 leaf wall, anyone any STC data on that? i know the rating will be better with 2 leafs, but how much better will it be?
I've been looking around the Internet, and what I've found is that a single leaf of 1/2" drywall has an STC of 28. I haven't found data for two layers of 1/2" drywall, and I'm no expert, but I'd guess that the STC would maybe be around 30 to 34 STC. (A two-leaf single-stud wall with a layer on each side is STC 36, so I doubt that single-leaf two-layer could be better than that.) So your single-leaf wall at best is going to give you something around STC 30, give or take a couple of points Compare that with a two-leaf double stud wall with one layer of drywall on each side, which gives you an STC of 56, or a two-leaf double stud wall with two layers of drywall on each side, which gives you an STC of 63 at least, and more if you use GG in between. So I'd say that yes, the second leaf makes a big difference, probably in the region of a couple of orders of magnitude, at the very least! I'd definitely go with a two-leaf double-stud wall if isolation is important to you!!! No doubt about it.
also would adding a 3rd layer of GB to the leaf thats already been done up the STC rating? i cant get the the outside anymore since thats finished, but i can add a layer of GB from the inside , between the studs..
Adding mass will always help, according to everything I've read. Double the mass and you get another 6 dB of isolation, but that still isn't much in your case. But unless you have a full Mass-Air-Mass sealed system, then you you'll NEVER get really good isolation. I'd really think hard about leaving off that inner leaf. You'll be loosing a LOT of isolation. - Stuart -
