HI there, Apologies for late reply. IMHO these Truth monitors are absolutely fantastic. They are fine for soffit mount (set compensation on rear to -2db) See John's drawings for soffit mount - allow for air flow via convection. Some tips - the Truth boxes are shielded but ensure you use trs/xlr plugs/cables - always. NOT guitar cables. Buy the Behringer Studio Sub as well and use it for crossover. Set if flat gain and comp at -2. Make sure you leave space under front of desk! It is big. Have the electrician install power points (star grounded) to switch all speakers on from one switch at the desk. (make sure speakers switch on last and off first! - commonsense signal chain stuff) When running speaker cables and power - keep them at least 500mm apart where you can. The results in my studio are EPIC. :D For the price and my ears they are perfect - even better soffit mounted. It is an amazing difference from near field use. Hope this helps.
