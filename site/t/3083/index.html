Currently I have a fellow forumite Brady from washington state who is visiting for awhile. Last night we were playing with his mac laptop and using the inbuilt mike to measure the resonate frequency of a wine bottle - it was 118hz. a 2 litre plastic coke bottle was 94hz. So if you have finished your wine, fill it with some insulation and you'll have a 118hz absorber. Just trying to help the aussie wine industry :) cheers john
