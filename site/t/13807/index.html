biggator24 November 2009#1
I'm building two doors based on Rod's 'super-door' design.. solid core door->sheet lead->mdf Here's the question. The 'top' layer, the MDF, is set back from the edge by 1" to create the 'stepped' door seal.. do I run the sheet lead out to the full edge of the door, or trim it to the size of the MDF? I was going to run it full width, but was a little concerned about having exposed lead around the edge of the door (with nothing but primer/paint covering it). thanks!
Ro26 November 2009#2
In order to have "full" isolation, I'd extend the sheet to the edges of the door. You can add another, thin, piece of wood sandwiched between the lead and MDF to cover the whole lead sheet. (hell, you can even make that sandwiched piece a thick piece of MDF, more mass, more isolation!)
biggator26 November 2009#3
That's actually a good thought, Ro.. maybe a thin piece of 1/8" masonite or something? Just enough to cover the lead.
rod gervais26 November 2009#4
FWIW - in my design the lead stops at the edge of the plywood sheet. I do not know how much luck you will have trying to install a piece of laminate on the face of sheet lead. Rod
