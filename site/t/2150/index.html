I don't know how 'pro' it is, and barefoot certainly has more experience on this than I do, but I made a very decent sounding enclosure for a 15" speaker using 15"Hx15"Wx12"D interior measurements... So, for you 10"ers... 10"Hx10"Hx8"D... I used 3/4" particle board (and a *lot* of caulk)... need to redo it with 3/4" 7-ply, but it's 14 years old and holding strong as a bass cab. Very clean sounding.
