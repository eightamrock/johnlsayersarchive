Ro22 January 2007#9
hey hey hey! Somebody's quick on building walls. WOW. It looks great. However, (I haven't really read your previous threads, so I dunno you exact goals) What's with the steel, hand welded!, frame? Would u be better of with standard metalstuds? (looks cheaper to me). Metalstuds also are a bit more "flexible" than your frame (mass and flexibility make a better iso wall) The double wall is really just one wall. Or at least the frame. With two really separated frames you'd gain much more TL. There's no kit used for sealing up ? One layer of MDF? How thick? Again, dunno your plans and goals but I see many flaws in those walls. Maybe you don't care, than it's alright and a very nifty job indeed :) cheerio
