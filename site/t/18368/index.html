Hi again Jeroen! :)
Now my question is do i have to make a caulk with elastic sealant or is my idea
Yes. Use any good-quality acoustic caulk, or even some types of high quality kitchen/bathroom caulk: the type that never hardens and always stays soft and "rubbery".
only with foam allowed?
Depends on the type of foam, but I would still caulk, even if there is foam.
and do i need sealant between wooden floor and gypsum wall? Or is the isolator foam enough?
Seal everything! Sealing your room is probably the most important part of isolation. If you don't seal well, then you won't get good isolation. So use lots of sealant, in every possible place where there could be any leak, even if it doesn't look like there is. - Stuart -
