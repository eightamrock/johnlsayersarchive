John Sayers28 May 2006#5
Nelson - I assume that the roof line continues out to the full size of the building and the 4' wall is a false wall. You could remove the wall surface (drywall or timber I assume) and open up the triangular space behind, fill with insul or hangers and put a cloth front on it. This would give you some trapping space without affecting the room size. cheers john
