Hi John, Steve et al, I’m glad the website is back up and running – really missed it the last couple of weeks! I posted a couple of earlier designs which I’ve now revamped, hopefully not making the same mistakes as in the past! The outer skin will be constructed of concrete blocks, the 4 main rooms floated and isolated with double walls throughout in accordance with some of John’s examples in the SAE site. The type of work I want to do includes recording acoustic / electric drums / guitars, as well as vocals, keyboards etc. I know John – you dislike having to walk through the studio to access the control room – but this not critical to me. I think I’ve made the best use of the available space in the revised layout below. There is no possibility of access to the building from either the sides or the back of the building. External height will be 10’ or thereabouts – the site is currently surrounded by trees. Yes I have a tree surgeon friend who’s looking at what can be kept, lopped or felled. There should be more than enough space at the rear of the control room for adequate bass trapping, sides will be slat resonators. One question – is it necessary for there to be 2 double walls where the Control Room abuts the Vocal Booth / DJ Booth & Live room? Or will one suffice in those areas. I’ve read Steve’s guidance as in Mass-Spring-Mass but admit to still being slightly confused. Any comments or improvements you guys can make will be more than welcome. This is not a rush job. I intend to work on this project mostly myself as time and funds become available, but I do have access to a professional chippy – part of his wages will definitely be of the liquid variety! Other friends in the building trade have also kindly offered to donate their services. Sorry this is quite lengthy but hopefully all/most relevant information is included above. Cheers lads! Colin (Moleymole)
