welcome! please update your profile to include your location. you are saying the wardrobe to the left of the desk cannot be moved or you don't want to? you might consider clearing out everything in the room you don't need for your mixing so you can better apportion the space for acoustic treatments. on the ceiling - a set of clouds along the ceiling, hung at different levels and angles should help mitigate the focus issues with the concave surface but you're best advised to make some room measurements to see exactly what you're dealing with.
