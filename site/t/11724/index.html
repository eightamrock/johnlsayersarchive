Ethan Winer24 February 2009#2
Those photos are too dark to see much, and your description is also lacking. What's wrong with the sound? Muffled? Boomy? Echoes? All of the above? Generally, thin foam is not a good treatment for a small room. You also need to absorb bass frequencies so the bass is "dead" too. That requires bass traps. Not small foam corners, but real bass traps. Large bass traps. A lot of them. :mrgreen: --Ethan
