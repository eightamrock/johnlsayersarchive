matt1walsh10 July 2008#1
Hi everyone-- I've got a pretty cool 1400 sqft space with 13' ceilings.. Not bad. This place is morphing from a rehearsal space to a full-on studio as time goes by.... But I have a big problem. Cell phone interference. It comes in randomly to all the solid state gear in the place. It sounds exactly like what you hear if you put your phone on the dashboard of your car and crank the stereo up. It's intermittent, as well. I was wondering why this interference was so strong when my landlord pointed out there is a cellphone tower maybe 200 ft from building. For the time being we arent doing mission critical recordings so it's annoyign but not a big deal. I have heard tell of copper paint to build a kind of Faraday cage out of my live and control rooms, and it looks pretty expensive, but I don't know what people use. Most of these products seem geared towards doing this sort of thing to plastic boxes, not brick and sheetrock. Any ideas? Help would be greatly appreciated. Best, Matt Walsh
