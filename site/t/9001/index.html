Hello! I am building a house and have made certain to leave a useable space in which to build a studio. After reading these forums quite a bit, Ive some up with some great ideas, which I'll be happy to post, but I'd really like to see what those with more experience might do with the space. The room dimensions are laid out in the drawing. There is a load bearing wall down the middle of the open space and I can have headers added wherever I'd like (up to 48" each). The ceiling will be about 8'9" aside from a ventilation trunk line down the middle of the space (right below the wall). I'm planning a control room, live room, vocal booth, and possibly a machine room. The goal is to be able to mix without disturbing anyone in the house, and to track loud instruments without disturbing the neighbors. I would love any input and ideas. Please let me know if I've left out any important info. Thanks!

gullfo24 June 2007#5
you could leave the machine room door where it is, just make sure its seals well so you keep the booth as quiet as possible... the single slider should suffice in most cases where you're doing vox or acoustic work. probably less effective with a guitar amp or bass amp. with a single wall structure you just as likely to get sound through the wall anyways...
leadvox24 June 2007#6
Good point about the STC of the single wall. Thanks! How about the ratios of the CR and iso? The design can be tweaked from here on out, but I need to commit to where I place my headers in that load bearing wall to allow for a window from the CR to the future live room.
