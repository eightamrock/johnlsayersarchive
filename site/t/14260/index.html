rod gervais8 April 2010#2
Aaron, this has nothing to do with density - and everything to do with keeping the joints airtight....... acoustic caulks are some of the messiest caulks to work with - they stick to everything and are really much more liquid that the silicone caulks - in fact the are often referred to as floppy caulks - because they are just that. I often recommend that people turn to butyl caulks when they can't swing acoustic caulks. Don't get me wrong - silicone caulks will work fine except that I do not believe that will adhere well to the raw edge of a piece of cut drywall - and both of the other caulks will. Please remember to use backer rod before you caulk - otherwise not matter what you use - you will have caulk failures....... Rod
