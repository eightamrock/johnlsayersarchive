Bryant - what do you intend to record in this studio? The large studio looks more like an orchestral studio in which case the walls would be treated to apply to an orchestral layout - they can be made variable acoustically using specially designed wall treatments. Angling the walls 3 degrees in the vertical creates a nightmare structurally and the expense would far outweigh any acoustic advantage. At the moment we are just playing with the layout, and as I said, I haven't given much thought to the booths or the studio acoustic - just trying to figure the best way to keep the line of sight between the studios and to utilise the two floors whilst still keeping the access passages between the front and rear of the building.
