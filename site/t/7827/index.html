Do you mean a garage door, the big sectional type of door that rolls on tracks? All those types of doors are single leaf, so you're not going to get a significant improvement. If you can be much much much more specific regarding how much reduction you need and how loud you are now, what the garage is made of now, etc. -- like, as much detail as would be necessary for us to literally build another one just like it -- as well as how much you're willing to spend, we may be able to help. --Keith :mrgreen:

sharward31 December 2006#4
Doors literally made for acoustic isolation (e.g., Overly) cost thousands of dollars. Do some searches here for examples of how people have done enhancements to standard solid core doors. Just remember: if, for example, your walls suck and your door really sucks, and you replace your really sucky door with a really great door, your isolation is still going to suck. --Keith :mrgreen:
jwl31 December 2006#5
As I recall, there was some debate between the idea of using reinforced solid-core doors, and Knightfly's idea of using a hollow-core door, reinforced with a layer or three of MDF on each side. Did that debate ever get resolved?
knightfly31 December 2006#6
Andy (wise minds) was really happy with the beefed up hollow core, calculation-wise it looks close to a toss-up between that and a same-thickness solid core - but if you have room for double walls and an air lock it's a no-brainer - TWO solid slab doors with good seals is the bomb for sure... Steve
