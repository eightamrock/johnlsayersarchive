Hello, Typically for the application of computers such as USB, Firewire, etc, it's usually easier to go ahead and just buy longer cables. However, depending on your budget and your knowledge of terminating the several types of connectors; Fiber, DVI, VGA, etc, it can be expensive and a real pain in the butt.
