lex12520 July 2004#3
I decided on a lightweight burlap that cost me $3.99 sq.yd. One thing to think about that caught me off guard was the strength of the material if you plan on hanging the panels. My original goal was to simply wrap the insulation in burlap, without any wood framing. I would leave a few inches of loose material at the top of the panel and install a pair of grommets in the material. Then the panel could be hung from the grommets. Once I had both the burlap and the insulation on hand, I quickly realized that the material was not strong enough to support the total weight of the panel. A single 2ft x 4ft x 4inch panel must weigh close to 10 pounds, and I determined that the grommets would eventually tear out of the material. Hopefully someone can learn from my mistake. (the good news is that I decided to support the panels on small shelves instead of hanging them. No great loss.)

I too can get burlap at 3.99 per yard. Burlap is not very dimensionally stable, as you noted. I went with my wife on one of her trips to the fabric store (was she ever surprized when I volunteered for that) and the thing that stood out in my mind was polyester felt, $4.99 in 72 inch widths and a huge variety of nice colors. It may not be transparent, but if anything it would add to the absorbency of the panel I would think. The only problem is that it is most likely not fire retardant at all, so I am looking into fire retardant treatments that I could apply myself.
