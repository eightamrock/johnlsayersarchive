Let's slow down a bit. First: im in alberta canada and the electrical code is 12 items per circuit Not having your code book, is "items" outlets, receptacles or devices? As each word has a different meaning. In the US in a residence you can have as many receptacles on one circuit as you like. Second: The connection between your Safety Grounds (EGC,PE) and Mother Earth has almost nothing to do with audio system noise. Third: The code book has rules for minimum size wire. For 15 Amps it's 14AWG and 20 Amps it's 12AWG, but bigger is always better. Forth: Isolated Grounds are only needed if your wires are in metal conduit's and then only is the conduit's might come in contact with other grounded items like water pipes, the building's metal frame or other (non audio) electrical circuits. First thing is read the following white papers, they total about 150 pages but it's easy reading, then re-thing your plans. The Bill Whitlock of Jensen Transformers Seminar paper http://www.jensen-transformers.com/an/g ... eminar.pdf The Jim Brown of Audio Systems Group white paper "Power and Grounding for Audio and Audio/Video Systems" http://www.audiosystemsgroup.com/SurgeXPowerGround.pdf "Power White Paper" from Middle Atlantic.com http://www.middleatlantic.com/power.htm or a different version of the same paper "The TRUTH" from ExactPower of Middle Atlantic Products http://www.exactpower.com/elite/wpapers.aspx
