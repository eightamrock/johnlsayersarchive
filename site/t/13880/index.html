cadesignr21 December 2009#4
Hello again. I'd like to offer an opinion, but your information is skant. For instance, are you planning on re-surfacing all 4 walls in ALL the rooms? Are any walls exterior walls? Are you trying to improve the isolation of one room from another? What about penetrations in the walls, such as doors, HVAC registers, or pipes etc? To my way of thinking, these type of things have a bearing on choices. How bout budget? One may be more expensive than the other because of labor? And what type finishes are you applying..ie paint, wood etc? Are you attatching any acoustical treatment fixtures? How about electrical...ie..once you add thickness to the walls, the outlet boxes no longer reach the face of the new material, which may be against code. Same with lighting j-boxes. Not to mention...are these walls a ONE LEAF SYSTEM? Usually, concrete block assemblys have interior voids that are used to insert re-bar and then filled with grout or mortor, which ends up being a one leaf system. You can improve the Transmission loss factor in these assemblys by applying FURRING strips, and then fastening gypsum drywall to the furring, thereby creating an airgap, which now makes a TWO LEAF system which typically performs much better than simply adding mass to a one leaf system. However, this creates other conditions, which may or may not be easily addressed. Such as HVAC ducting extentions, and the electrical, not to mention door jambs etc. But it's hard to say in your situation as you don't know what the existing assembly Transmission loss is, nor do you state what type sound nor the level in db you will be producing in a given room. Nor do you state the purpose of any rooms, nor do you tell us WHO you are trying to keep from offending...ie..are you trying to keep EXTERNAL noise from transmitting into your rooms, or are you trying to keep INTERNAL sound from transmitting to the outside world, or another room, or to a neighbors space? These are the type of information people need to make qualified opinions. You might get more feedback if you provide more info.
