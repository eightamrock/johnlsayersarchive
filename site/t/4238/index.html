sonus22 August 2005#3
You can read the explanation on this page: http://www.mhsoft.nl/Helmholtzabsorber.asp The helmholtz resonators are the slot absorber like the john’s side wall absorbers in this page: http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm And they explain the Helmholtz panel absorber in the following way:
"By fixing a perforated wooden panel over a frame and putting an absorbent material inside the space created, a resonant bass trap is formed with each perforation acting as a single 'bottle' in our virtual bottle array. By varying the percentage of perforation, the design can be applied to both the bass and mid range. However, predicting the performance of these traps is difficult because the Q or bandwidth varies depending on the amount of internal damping."
Trying with various posibilities in the calculator I could find some options that absorb even 20Hz, I don't know if this compute is fine or not, another person with more knowledge should answer it. CAMILO
