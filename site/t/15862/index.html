gullfo22 April 2011#6
there is a lot of info on the site about soffit mounting however, just moving them off the desk onto stands may solve some of the issues. as a general rule, making your mix position asymmetrical acoustically is not typically going to help you make good mix decisions - whether that is turning your head to look at monitors on the side, or having reflections off them, or having various audio monitors not faithfully reporting the panning etc will skew your assessment of the mix. so, if you want the monitors out of the way because of visibility, and that is more important, you can do that but maybe it might be better to put them on a swing arm to re-position them as needed or if you don't need them for tracking, raise and lower them. on the audio monitors, having them all placed relative to your main sitting position is a good idea but be cognizant of the impact of them in terms of the position of the main monitors.
