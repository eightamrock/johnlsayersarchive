Soundman202019 January 2013#7
any suggestions for the 20 foot glass window on the left of the mix position? its 1 inch thick glass.
You seem to have only half of your isolation shell. From what I see on your model, there is only an outer-leaf: to isolate the room, you also need an inner-leaf, a true "room-in-a-room". That's the only way to get good isolation at a reasonable cost. Simply adding more mass to what you have already won't accomplish much, especially considering that the glass is 1 inch thick! One thing you could do, is to seal everything air-tight. Lack of good sealing is a major source of poor isolation. So check that your entire outer-leaf is sealed, very carefully. If that doesn't help enough, then you'll have to build room-in-a-room inner-leaf. - Stuart -
