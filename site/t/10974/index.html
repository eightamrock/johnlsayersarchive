Hi, first of all a big THANKS to the community for this great resource. Here in Germany there are are many suppliers who sell precast garages (precast reinforced concrete construction), that are delivered complete (or more modules depending on size) and are simply put up on a strip foundation. They are relatively cheap, about 280 EUR/m². The walls are 80mm thick. Standard height is 250cm. One can buy them second hand too. Garages are the standard application for this kind of buildings, but they are used for a wide range of purposes. So I'd like to use them as...surprise...a studio (tracking & mixing, all instruments, loudest one drums, no full band!) :D Of course this is somewhat similar to the container studios, which are known in the forum. But here in Germany these objects are cheaper than "container buildings", and I think 8 cm reinforced concrete must provide better sound insulation or at least be a better foundation for a high insulation construction. At a premium one can design the objects freely. All in all, I'd see many advantages:
    - fast, easy, and relatively cheap - readymade room with already good amount of sound insulation (?), ideal room dimensions and geometry, windows, doors, etc. in place - for more rooms one can use more modules, put them up very close, which results in a good room-to-room-insulation - mobility (transport is not that cheap, but worthwhile if I should relocate) - flexibility, simply add new modules - secure investment, better than investing in a rented space, one could even sell the whole thing.
But still my main questions: - How much sound insultaion will such an object provide? Will I need an additional drywall construction for "drum level"-insulation? (I WILL need heat insulation anyway) Should I go for thicker walls? Maybe anyone has some experience with such objects... Any comments and ideas are very much appreciated! Thanks & Regards!

AVare20 July 2008#5
As Delusion wrote, a second wall will be required for good isolation. 80mm is good for low frequency isolation, but not higher frequencies. It will provide around 45 STC, or Rw isolation. Adding a second wall of gypsum board with insulation in between will bring the isolation up around 4 to 6 points. Andre

delusion20 July 2008#7
For the internal room, you could build John's "inside out" walls in a modular fashion and then take them apart when you move. With a little planning, you could probably even do the ceiling in modular segments and lift them into place with enough help. Seal it up really well, and I think 2 layers of 5/8" drywall should get you there. 3 layers would make everything harder to put up and disassemble considering the modular fashion you're after...but it could be done...lifting ceiling sections into place with 3 layers would be more then I would want to try. The green roof option would only add to the isolation and would be a cool addition.

Thank you very much for your input guys! Update (I posted the same in another thread): Prices here in Germany start from about 200 EUR/m². There are various systems. Some come as a whole and are also poured as a whole. Some come in large parts and need to be assembled by the suppliers team. Some come as building blocks, which one can assemble by themselfs. I have insulation data for the latter, the manufacturer says 36dB. I called my acoustics consultant, he thinks the "building blocks"-system probably have better insulation data, than the other ones.
