gullfo5 July 2011#2
welcome! please update your profile to include your location information. definitely tear down anything which is going to interfere with adding mass and creating a proper double leaf construction. consider your HVAC (esp fresh air) because even musicians need to breath... the low ceiling height will be tricky but you could go with interleaving the isolation ceiling joists with your existing floor joists. you will want to beef up the upper floor using drywall then decouple the isolation walls and ceiling from the floor. sonopan can be used or Homasote 440, along with the 3x 5/8" drywall layers. definitely you will be adding a massive door and you should plug the window. on the floor - 1" rigid insulation with 2x 1/2" plywood and a 3/8-1/2" layer of finished floor. you'll likely end up with a room height of around 6'8" or so.

gullfo7 July 2011#5
building an inner frame and putting your drywall mass (double leaf assembly) is the tried and true method for increasing isolation but you lose inner room space, and if you can nest the inner isolation ceiling joists with your floor joists, that would be preferred as well. barring that, isolation clips and hat channel are a second choice for decoupling the inner mass from the existing structure.
