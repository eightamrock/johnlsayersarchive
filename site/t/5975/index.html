Also, should I keep things like the ac unit (built into the cement block wall) on the existing power? What about the computer, as it has a switching PSU?
AC, you can keep on the existing panel. First off, is the power coming into the panel single phase or three phase? This will make a difference as to your wiring. Star ground w/ new rod is good. Probably all you need for your subpanel is a 2/0 wire (the wire I ran is rated for 95 amps. It was 2 AWG I believe). What kind of dimmers do the art galleries have? This could be an issue, as many of them can create noise in the AC.
