gullfo10 February 2008#2
since the concave shape of the ceiling would tend to focus sound, hanging some porous absorbers along the ceiling will help control it and preserve ceiling height. space them a bit apart to maximize their effect. otherwise its a nice design.

gullfo23 February 2008#4
i wouldn't put up 40cm worth of absorption... i think 10cm of rigid insulation (48kg/m3) should make a good difference... the rooms will feel tight enough with only 2.3m height... for isolation - i would use double walls instead of staggered stud.
