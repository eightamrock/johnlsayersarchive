Can anyone tell me how usefull an RT60 measurment would be for a small home studio?
I agree with Steve that ETF is a better purchase. Small rooms don't really have RT60 anyway, at least not in the classic sense of reverb that swells over time and then decays. ETF does all you need, and it does it better than any handheld device costing less than a used car. :) --Ethan
