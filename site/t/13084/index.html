Ro8 June 2009#7
jbassino wrote:
Anyway, I prefer silicone than acrylic sealants... acrylics tends to crackle (at least the ones that i've tried here)
Silicon is twice as expensive as acrylic and it can't be painted over (the paint will destroy the sealant) Here are the requirements for a good sealant; - Should have mass. A coulered sealant has more mass than a transparent one (about 1000 vs 1400 kg/m3) - should stay flexible - should not crack(!) - should not shrink much (5%-10% max) applying sealant is not the most difficult job, but you've gotta learn to get it perfect.
