you could draw the plans up yourself but you should definitely have your plans reviewed by licensed architect and/or a structural engineer, and your local building officials. this will ensure compliance with building ordinances - which can prevent problems later with insurance companies, home resale, etc. as well as give you peace of mind from a safety perspective, when you start to build it.

TomVan3 March 2009#17
I also think now that he reason that Gulfo added double doors to the interior of the soundlock is so that when people are entering the soundlock any external noise does not react on the single doors to the CR and VB....is that correct?
You should have a door for each leaf. Reason one would be for isolation purposes and two if you dont have two doors you would most likely want a super door and make sure your two leaves are not coupled. Much more work in my mind. Using frabic and insulation between the two will be the proper way to install. Same as a window install. Couldn't find a door pic !!
Another quick question. The rear wall of the CR is made up of two leafs - the existing brick wall and the CR rear wall. Will I need to build a hard up against that brick wall and use insulation in it thereby adding to the mass of THAT leaf?
Your brick wall should have insulation of some sort attached to it. A simple way without having to build a false wall against the brick ( if I understand you correctly) would be to use bat hooks. Hope this helped Peace Tom
