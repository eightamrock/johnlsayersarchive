" least that has nothing else under it as i wont have to worry about isolating the floor, right? is it better nothing under or nothing above it?" /quote Both. Ideally you want to be in a position that the >building or the inhabitants< do not create sound issues. Flanking noise can be generated from many things as simple as the walking in an above apartment to loud music that brat below you in apartment 2A is making :) You want to eliminate all unwanted noise before it begins. But "ideally" is a lofty notion and leaves much to be desired. Still when you eliminate these undesirable elements you also increase what you can do with your money. The video shows a typical staggered-studd wall with wool in between and 2 layers of GB on each side. That's one way of building a wall. If that's your choise then, yeah, the video's a good example.
