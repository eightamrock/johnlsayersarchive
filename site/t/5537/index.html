OK a little off topic of studio design
how do you guys find the money to build your own studios? Whether it be a brand new building from the ground up, or converting rooms in your homes. I'm only 20 years old, I have about a year's worth of credit history, good credit history at that, and I really would LOVE to have my own building.
My answer... blood, sweat & tears resulting in a big ringing in my ears. I've been a gigging blue collar musician my entire working life. I'm 33 and have had my share of financial ups and downs. I started with good credit at age 18-20, but in my early twenties I spent a lot of money various pleasures.... long story but it screwed up my credit. Age 25 I smartened up, been clean for 8 years now. In this 8 years I've become a husband, a dad, a homeowner and cleaned up my credit (partially from moving from Canada to Florida) AND my career has taken off and stabalized. So how can I afford what I got and want? I keep good credit that I take advantage of when neccasry and pay it off. I make sure my income sources are stable and always try to make more money. As far as the big purchase goes keep good credit!!! You need two years of good credit and a minimum of a 680 credit score to get a mortgage. 720 or more is ideal. My brother in law at age 22 bought his 1st house. He did a lot of research, took advantage of many goverment grants like HUD, spent time waiting and did it. Good luck, Marc
