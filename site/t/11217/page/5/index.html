About my lights... we've done it that way, because I wanted so... because of time, money and because it's easyer to mount cloud or other acoustic elements later. Mike Vale19 February 2010#120
Hi there, I made some updates at my place in the last 2 months. I build my own desk, change my Apple cinema 23" monitors for LG 47" SL8000, bought active Avantone Mix Cubes and Crane Song Avocet. The whole update bring me a better stereo image, the bass is even more detailed. I can EQ, compress, color the sound, etc. in much more detail now, so I can't complain about anything actualy. I do have a little dip at 90-120hz (everything else looks great for now), but there is still missing 6 of bass traps in my upper corners. I will mount 4 of them this weekend. I do have measurement graphs, but I will upload all of them, when I finished with the acoustics. I will also upload one short clip of one of my tracks, so you can hear what is going on here.
