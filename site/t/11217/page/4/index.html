drthump7028 January 2009#96
Hi Mike, First off, gotta say your room looks very nice. But as far as the lights go, in most cases the lighting is installed in the clouds or false ceiling. By cutting any kind of hole in your inner leaf you are compromising your isolation. think of sound waves like water.There only needs to be a small crack for you to have a leak. What did you do to the small holes that you cut for the wiring to insure that your room is still airtight? Bryon
