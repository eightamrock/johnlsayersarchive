AndreasB6 November 2008#20
I wish I had the $$$ to put particle board on my walls... jeeez that had to be expensive and awkward putting those in position. MASSIVE BUILD! Anyways I agree with Ro that you might have spend too much time and effort on the mass part of this build since it's not floated or decoupled. You can always put another floated floor on top of the one you built I guess, making the proper calculations of the weight and making sure it's not structurally touching anything. When I built my studio a couple of years ago I had an acoustic engineer help me out with calculating the weight of my whole structure (walls built on top of floated floor). I later found out that the calculations where correct when I could measure the compression of the neoprene under the structure, so that would be something I recommend having a pro do for you. Since then I learned a lot and found out that the engineer who calculated and sold the product to me also sell a lot of things that would be treated as "bogus" or "fake" acoustic treatments on this forum.
