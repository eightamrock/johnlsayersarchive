xSpace24 April 2011#2
"4) The floor on the studio will be made of a very hard wood (yet enough flexible for supporting roofs) and with this storage below it makes it a floating floor? " ;) No it isn't...it is a framed floor coupled to the entire structure. Your room dimensions should be changed so that you do not have the potential for a square...as you currently have. If we use the 13' 2" height, based on the ratios of Louden (1: 1.40: 1.90) you would have a room dimensions of 13'2" X 18' 5" X 25' Whatever you do, do not have measurements that are the same or even divisible by one another. You create parallel surfaces and multiple the room modes which is never a good thing.
gullfo24 April 2011#3
with that size room, you could put your mix position on "C" and your work desk down on wall A near your window. this way you can build the symmetry you need for the mixing and have the rest of the space open for musicians, etc as well as having the view. for mix down you could put a couple of gobos ~3m behind you if needed. +1 on Brien's comment regarding a square value between your height and width.
