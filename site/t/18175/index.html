Soundman202023 February 2013#5
I would put the desk facing the left wall (marked "outside wall"). Speakers on heavy, massive stands, up against that wall, with 10cm of OC-703 between them and the wall. Speakers 70 cm from side walls, angled inwards at about 30°. Chair set up so your head (ears) is about 1.1 m from front wall. Speakers should be aimed so that the axes intersect about 20cm behind your head. Speakers must be set up so that the axes are 1.2m above the floor. Put large "superchunk" bass traps in both front corners, and the right rear corner, all of them floor to ceiling. Half-panel (cut vertically) of 10cm OC-703 on the first reflection points on the side walls, floor to ceiling. Full panel of OC-703 on the rear wall, centered, leaning forwards: against the wall at the bottom, about 20cm away at the top. Cloud over the mix position (first reflection point on the ceiling): full panel of 10cm OC-703 across the room, angled so that is lower over the speakers and higher over the chair. That should get you started! :) - Stuart -
