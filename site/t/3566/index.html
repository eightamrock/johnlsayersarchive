hi, just a quick thought. I suppose that you want an environment to do an accurate mix. what you don't want at the mix position is flutter echoes and early reflections. so some absorbtion at the right places is a must. also its better to fire your speakers along the "longside" direction of the room. read the SAE-stuff, there's also a bedroom studio. also check out the stickys around. After a few hours (or days :wink: ) you'll know the basics. then, if you want to "tune" your room, the guys here will help. :D
