knightfly14 January 2005#2
I like your idea of a continuous block wall for where the doors go; would you also put a second door in each cube, fastened to the inner steel frame? If so, and you float each floor separately and build the inner walls on top of each floor, that would give the best isolation between cubes. Your blocks would need to be filled with sand, and each room would have its own steel framed inner walls, all four walls for each cube would be resting on it's own floated floor, tops of walls spaced with sway brackets, all walls filled with rockwool, floors filled too (very boomy floors otherwise) What about ceilings, any thoughts on that yet?
