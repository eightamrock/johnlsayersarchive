basically create a single large room with good ratios and consider reversible treatments so you can readily alter the acoustics for tracking or mixing. also consider a bathroom, plus storage. depending on your equipment, maybe a machine closet. you also need to consider your HVAC - in the drawing i think i put in a separate pad for the external compressor equipment.

gullfo6 October 2010#6
the quickest way to decide is to determine how much your per m2 cost of quality home construction is, and divide that into your budget. so if you're cost is $2000/m2 and your budget is $30,000, then you could afford roughly 15m2. that said, most people think that a minimum of 1800ft3 or 50m3 is as low as you can go.
