Don T31 January 2007#6
Hello. I like the "foyer" idea. I would add that the distance from the rear wall to the rear absorption should equal a quarter wave to the total room length so you have 180* of diffusion at the room mode at 60% surface area. Should almost eliminate that fundamental mode and the slat resonators can handle the harmonics.
