Soundman202014 November 2010#80
and it'd make use of the insulation that would have otherwise been on the outside of the wall.
You still need insulation on the "outside" of the wall! (In the cavity between the two leaves). That is a necessary part of the MSM system. Building a wall inside out does not obviate the need for damping in the air gap. - Stuart -

gullfo17 November 2010#82
the inside-out wall would be: cloth (and/or slats) -> stud (filled with rigid insulation) -> 2-3x drywall -> [air gap] -> other wall. the other wall could be any other single leaf - block/concrete, stud frame with single face etc.
