bonzo30005 February 2011#1
Hello everybody. I've been reading articles here for some time but have never registered until today. I am trying to use a room in my basement for recording/mixing purposes. So far it's only on paper but I intend to build it all by myself even if this will take some time. Your help is of course appreciated a lot I hope my post is providing you with some good starting points for discussion. Since I am from Germany, I will use the metric system. Here are the basics: - The room: It's about 7.40m wide and 4.45m long (red lines on the plan). The height is 2.50m. Two outer walls (right and top on the plan) are concrete, two inner walls are brick walls (left and bottom on the plan). Alle four walls are rendered (hope I'm using the right vocab). There is one door about 0,80x2,00m (on the plan the outward opening one), and two windows (small basement windows about 1,00x0,60m in the concrete walls). You can see all this in my plan. - The concept: Part of this big room has to remain "musically untouched" because I (and my family) need storage. This part of the room is already fixed in its position because that's the place where a lot of network/TV/etc. cables unite and there are already shelves for storage installed along the wall. The rest of the room can be used to build in a "studio", if you want to call it so. I am not a professional musician but an ambitioned amateur and I also take fun in planning, constructing etc. What do I want to do: As a hobby musician I would like to record my own singing, guitar playing or recording other small instruments (no drums). Furthermore I would like to mix material from my bands/projects etc. Watching at other designs I thought it would be helpful to avoid parallel walls, while at the same time caring for geometrically similar left and right walls. Apart from the plan I want to treat the ceiling as well. An angle of about 12° opening towards the doors is recommended as I heard/read. After the construction there will be some fine-tuning with absorbers etc. But I am not that far. At this stage I would apreciate feedback on the general design as for example: - Is the control room big enough? - Is it possible to use the remaining room on the right for some recording (vocals, guitar)? - Should any angles be changed (of course this is limited)? - Should the sound monitors be fitted into the bass traps at the top? - Are there any practical short-comings in my plan? - Anything else? Thank you very much for reading and answering.
