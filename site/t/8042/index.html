knightfly29 January 2007#2
Didn't see any specs on DENSITY - and I'm not sure what they're using for "water repellent" - even closed cell polyurethane insulation is waterproof, but isn't worth a crap for acoustic absorption or in-wall insulation for sound blocking purposes. If the stuff is available in about 2-3 PCF (32-48 kG/m^3) and IF, IF their "waterproofing" treatment doesn't seal up all the little fibers/interstices in the material, it should be a fair substitute for the things we usually recommend. However, if they also have NON-waterproofed versions I'd play it safe and order that instead... Steve
