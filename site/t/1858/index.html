sui-city28 June 2004#1
Hello all. We have got a recording space: 32' long, 18' wide and 12' high. It is purely room 1. Our control room is seperate. It is also partly treated. The walls are slightly shaped so as not to be parallel. My biggest concern is the frequency issues we will have to deal with. Any suggestions?
dymaxian28 June 2004#2
Well, if that's the size of the space you have, IMO it's big enough where any modal problems will be low enough in freq that they'd even out by the time they got to the problem ranges; they'd also be pretty easy to deal with using bass trapping. That's a pretty nice size for a tracking room- plenty big enough to use more diffusion than absorption. Hope this helps!

dymaxian6 July 2004#7
With a ceiling that high, putting up a structurally isolatied ceiling for your space should be easy. That's about the only sure way you'll keep foot-falls from coming down from the above space. Depending on how much noise you're making there, I'd think you'd want to do this anyway- for both your sake and for the people above. You never know- they may be willing to help you out with the soundproofing; it goes both ways. If they seem doubtful, fire up a big bass guitar amp when you know they're up there, and shake them up a little bit. ;) But yeah- you have easily enough room to get a good false ceiling built in there.
