gullfo12 July 2007#2
one thought for the floor under the kit: http://www.dtxpressions.com/platform/ a third leaf is constituted by having a sealed air space acting as a spring - so if you build a 2-leaf isobooth in the middle of a 1-leaf room, you still don't really have a third leaf situation (or much of one - depending on the spacing between the walls of the isobooth and the exterior walls). the problem with building an isolation booth in the CR is that you'll loose precious volume in that space and change the room geometry. - I think you're better off building a proper isolation booth outside the CR. you should do some tests to determine what kind of isolation you need - check for flanking with a metal and rubber hammer on the floors and possibly walls and ceiling. also put on some low frequency sine waves and with a sound level meter determine the levels around the house and next door. this will give you a place to start.
