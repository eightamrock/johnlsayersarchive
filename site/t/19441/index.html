Soundman20201 July 2014#2
Hi Brendan, and welcome! :) It would be good if you could do a more complete diagram in SketchUp, and also post some photos of the empty space you have to work with. But basically the "mixing part" of the room must be symmetrical (left-right). That is critical. So you need a solid, flat front wall with two 90° side walls perpendicular to it, and your desk centered between them. That's the most basic necessity for a control room. - Stuart -
