you should turn off the caps lock when you type as it is often interpreted as "yelling" or "shouting". that said, what are you currently having built for the dividing wall? if you're not actually building it yet, what are your requirements for this wall? If you search on this site, there are quite a number of posts related to wall construction which can assist you in determining which configuration meets your needs. depending on costs and availability of materials, it might change how you construct it. what materials were you considering for building the wall? also, your control room is almost square which is actually bad for acoustics. is it possible to change the dimensions slightly?
