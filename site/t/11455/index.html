billshoup22 April 2009#9
Hi All, I am finally getting around to documenting what I did for treatment in my room. As I mentioned above, I purchased 20 pieces of 4” thick 2’ X 4’ mineral wool to build bass traps out of. I basically used the design described by studio_drums HERE which I highly recommend. The most significant change I made to this design was to put the mineral wool into garbage bags (2 garbage bags per 2’ X 4’ sheet of mineral wool) before assembling, for two reasons: 1) To make sure mineral wool particles would stay put and 2) reduce high frequency absorption. Before I started treatment, I used REW to take some initial readings, some of which I will share in the next post. Next I built bass traps out of the mineral wool, put them in place and did more testing. From my limited knowledge in looking at the post treatment REW tests, and by listening to the room, I believe this is enough treatment. I was quite surprised to see how much the graphs improved. Anyway, first I will provide diagrams similar to the treatment plans I provided above (in previous posts) to show you how I actually treated the room, which is somewhat less than I had planned. In other words, I had assumed I would need to buy more mineral wool, but to me it appears I already have about the correct amount of trapping.
