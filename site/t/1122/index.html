knightfly8 January 2004#2
Daniel, I moved this to the Design forum as John's the man on this - probably the first two things he'll tell you is that your rear side walls are backward - they should be angled to direct sound AWAY from the mix position, not toward it - and that your speaker soffits need to have more space between the speakers and the inner corners if possible. You need probably 3-4 woofer diameters of extension minimum. Third, don't use diffusion in that small a room, you'll want to absorb the rear wall. That's if John doesn't tell you to turn the room 90 degrees as well... Steve
