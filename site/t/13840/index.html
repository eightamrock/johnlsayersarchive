xSpace9 December 2009#7
jbassino wrote:
I believe a broadband panel placed in a corner then becomes a bass trap. Most bass builds up at corners so you trap a lot of them in there :)
As a "trap" is isn't, as I am coming to understand them, what it is. What it is, the word "trap" is becoming is a generic term. When people designed things, like five foot deep walls or hanging baffles in the ceiling or behind acoustical wall panels in walls, these are the bass "traps" that folks refer to. Everything else is just a marketing term :) Granted vibration moves to the edges of the panel so installing a broad band panel, or what is commonly called a "bass trap", is the obvious solution. But it does not go down [broad band] to the absorption of the 20-30Hz range that a five foot thick wall will. That's my thinking on it as of today.
