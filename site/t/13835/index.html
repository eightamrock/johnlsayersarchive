rod gervais5 December 2009#6
Niva, where are you in Quebec? My dad was born and raised in Granby. For normal corners - we caulk and then do a standard tape job for the entire corner. But for areas where we have no corner, just a flat joint (picture an exposed concrete ceiling as one example) we would install the backer rod and caulk - then install a tear away bead (which is a flat plastic flange that is held tight to the ceiling (in this case) nailed in place - with a plastic strip in a slot that sits at a right angle to the wall and is about 3/16" higher than the wall surface - this to be flat taped to the ceiling and once sanded the strip is torn away (hence tear away bead" leaving a perfect tape joint sitting about 1/16" from the ceiling. Rod
