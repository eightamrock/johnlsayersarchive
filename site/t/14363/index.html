bwilkinson17 May 2010#13
Something to keep in mind - and I am not one of the experts on this board by any means - is that you need to start thinking three dimensionally for the room. There are more than four corners in that room, and while it may only be easy to place superchunks in two corners, you can still treat the wall-to-ceiling corners with bass traps. For the flutter, there are multiple topics on this across the board. A cursory search will give you multiple ideas.
