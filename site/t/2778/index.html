Hello all, My studio construction just finished and I am in the process of putting acoustic treatment on the walls. The question I had was about ceiling treatment. My ceiling height is 7'9", I have Pergo flooring in the room. Currently, I have some custom made OC703 (3") panels in all four corners and front and back walls. You can see te pictures here: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewt ... 2&start=30 With these panels, when I clap in the room I hear a slight ringing sound that seems to be coming from the ceiling. I am hoping that when I put all my equipment, a couch and a rug in the room this ringing sound will go away. Do you think I should go ahead and put some treatment on the ceiling? If so what would be better considering the ceiling height and the flooring? Do I need absorbtion or diffusion? I was thinking about putting a mix of auralex diffusers and absorbers right above the mix area, since they are not so thick. I would really prefer to treat the ceiling before I move all my equipment in there. Thanks in advance for your help.
