Hello everyone! My first post but a long time voyeur from this forum... since I'm always interested in consuming reliable info about this kind of subjects, studio construction, design etc. So, the thing is this (I need the help of all smart and expert brains): I've been thinking to build my own studio for two years now! I've been working on a commercial studio in Portugal for about three years, but now I have decided to build one of my own! I've just bought an apartment with two bedrooms, one living room and the usual on all apartments... kitchen, wc, storage room... And my primary intention is to build a control room on one bedroom and a live tracking (recording) room on the second bedroom - this is my project... a nice looking good place where musicians could record their projects, with all the necessary means to attain a final product... tracking acoustic drums, guitars with miked amps, synths, vocals and so on... Now the details about what I Have with present conditions, Want... and questions of what I need: Below on PDF there is the self-made blueprint of the apartment and some 3D images also on PDF of the same. I've also attached some pictures taken to the two rooms for a real feeling of the rooms! So, the apartment is on a quite street with almost no traffic outside noise since its a basement, its the last floor of the building. I have neighbours on my right, left and above! Control Room: I want that this room should be suitable to be used also as a tracking room, mixing room and also mastering room. The floor is Parket, the 4 walls is 20cm brick-wall (with no further info about it). Measurements: W: 3.57m L: 2.86m H: 2.77m Tiny I know... but this is what I got! It has a window (small) on one of the walls (as you can see on the 3D PDF with 2.19m of width and 40cm of height... the elevation is about 2 meters. I would like to have a window to communicate with the tracking room. (small one) like 1.50m of width and about 1 meter height. Finally you can also see on the PDF blueprints one ceiling wall almost on the middle of the room with 40cm of height supporting the building I thing (a master span wall). Now I know how to treat acoustically the room, but Isolating it from the rest of the world that part... well not good enough for it... And what I would like from you all, which I know that you all are very nice professionals, hobbiests and ultimately persons... is: A design for isolation and acoustic treatment... I would like to know what you would do if the space was yours... and how you do it??? Now the Tracking Room: This room should be used to track acoustic drums (general mid-sized ones), guitars (electric and acoustic) with miked amps, vocals and so on... The floor is Parket, the 4 walls is 20cm brick-wall (with no further info about it). Measurements: W: 3.42m L: 2.76m H: 2.77m It has the same window connected with the control room (the one with a 2 meters elevation) and has a master window to the quite street on the left corner with 1.59m width , 1.20m height and a 1.20 m elevation What I ask for this one is almost the same on the previous one: A design for isolation and acoustic treatment... I would like to know what you would do if the space was yours... and how you do it??? I think that I didn't forget anything... the rooms have doors of course... with 80cm of width and 2.24m of height each. On the pdf you can see where they are... have not taken any exact measures about their position but you can actually see about their disposition! I know that the space is limited and tiny but unfortunately is what I have available to make what I love more... If any more info is needed please don't hesitate on asking! Advanced thanks if you read this... and hope you could help me about this long and hard task I have on my head! Cheers All!
