horizonsound11 October 2008#1
So, the forum goes down on the day construction began on my studio. I hope it's not a sign! After 10 months of design, planning, permits, laying about and the birth of my first child, the studio build has begun. It's a basic two room deal, with the option of an iso room later... The outside of the building will be brick veneer with a steel roof, to match our existing house, as per the covenant on our land. There are two large windows at the front of the building, which will be double glazed, and give a great view of the countryside. Currently waiting on the framing to arrive in the next few days. horizonsound4 May 2009#19
My soffits are at 30°. They're designed to fit passive Event 20/20 monitors in, which are about 400 x 250 in size each. ...yeah, those sound screen batts are the business....thanks to unkle K. Rudd for the $900 contribution towards them. plasterboard is on the way. i decided to go with 2 layers x 13mm fyrecheck rather than the soundchek, as the transmission properties are almost the same but they are 40% cheaper. i have been told that they use four layers of fyrechek when building cinemas!!
Ro4 May 2009#20
Are you sure those door and window frames are strong enough hold such mass? Especially the doors, which are heavy and will swing in and out the frame.
horizonsound4 May 2009#21
Absolutely sure they are strong enough. The doors are double studded on the hinged side, and the windows are fitted to non-supporting walls (90x35 studs all-round). I've used this design with larger windows before (1800 x 900 with 10mm and 6+3mm lamiate glass). ...that's ok...I get paid all day to be interrogated by structural engineers :wink:
