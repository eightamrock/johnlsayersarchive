amac2673 wrote:
Hi, Did you decouple the speaker from the baffle? It is hard to tell from the pictures exactly what you did.... And more importantly what are you powering them with and how do they sound? Allen
Hi Allen, The speakers are not decoupled from the baffle. You're right, it was effectively a size-to-size bookshelf. The speakers are a little 'tight' in the walls. I'm not sure if i wanted to get them out i could. They are being powered by an Alesis RA-100. Not that thrilled with it (the amp), but have a Yamaha P-2200 i'd like to recap and use instead. I find there is a bit of a hole in their response around 80Hz, which I always under-compensate for. It might also be just the room.
