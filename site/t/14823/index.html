hey Zach! read the "READ ME BEFORE POSTING" sticky so you can collect all the details we need plus update your location. also, you might consider spending lot's of time studying on this site (and other forums as well) to acquire the knowledge you need to understand how this equipment all works since there are a number of options of wiring it up and maintaining it... another consideration, if time is of the essence, consider hiring a local professional to come over and wire it up and pick their brains while they do it... meanwhile, it looks like you're missing the analog to digital converters you need to get the output of the mixer into the computer and vice versa. you might look into the Behringer ADA8000 or higher end products such as Apogee, Lynx, RMI, Crane Song, etc. you need to have IEEE-1394 (firewire) ports on the Mac to handle the connection. who uses cassettes any more? :)
