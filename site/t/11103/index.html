Soundman20201 November 2008#21
Your plane looks basically reasonably to me, considering the space that you have. Regarding your bass traps: I think I'd go with design number 2: In a room that small, you are going to need as much bass trapping as you can get! If your room isn't symmetrical, is there anything you can do to make it symmetrical? Remove walls? Add walls? Use gobo's? Symmetry is very important to get a good stereo image in your room. If you could make a detailed SketchUp model of your room, that would help.
