mpedrummer7 March 2007#1
So, this is very much in the "dreaming" stage, though I'd like to build an iso booth soonish. I'd like to have a general idea of where I'm going with things, because I'd prefer not to have to undo things later and do things twice. I attached two files - the Sketchup file I'm working with and a screenshot thereof. I'm not too good with Sketchup yet, so I apologize for the generally shoddy work there. There's a few "immobile" objects - the stairs really can't be relocated, the 6 green poles keep the house from collapsing on me, and the big rectangle to the right of the mix position is a chimney. I want to keep the toilet as a bathroom, and the corner between the two staircases is most likely going to remain a laundry room. The two existing rooms are actually walled in with tongue and groove flooring from the early 1900's, so I'm going to disassemble those and re-use the flooring in the studio. There isn't enough to do the whole thing, but maybe the control room and an iso booth. The two rooms will be rebuilt as a single long room the width of the stairs to be my workshop. I'm planning on putting the first iso booth where the speakers are now. I want to basically finish the hexagon, though it won't be symmetrical at all. I haven't decided if I want to continue as a one-room operation like I've been doing or put a wall up between the control room and the live room. I appreciate any input! Thanks MPEDrummer [edit]File was too big to upload, here's a link instead. http://tim.komta.com/studio/Basement.skp [/edit]
