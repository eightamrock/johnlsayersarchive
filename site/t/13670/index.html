rod gervais7 October 2009#2
Rhino, to begin - sliding doors do NOT give you a double open door size -as a matter of fact - the door in the open position is LESS than the open width of a standard door (one standard 30 door versus a 60 slider). building a pair of double doors is not that difficult - in fact the disappearing doors shown in my book (Page 5 - figure 1.3) are just that - a pair of 30 doors insie of an airlock - meant to facilitate easy access for bands gear into the space..... You just have to think through the gasketing of the door's center the same way you work though the edges...... no magic - just a little attention to detail. Rod
