giles11722 July 2004#8
Heavy Absorption could be a number of things depending on your budget and which materials you want to use. I.E. 4" Mineral wool 4pcf insulation, etc. 4" 703 Insulation (which is a tad more expensive than the mineral wool) :) These are just 2 examples of heavy absorption. Also If I could add. I would (behind the couch) build a false wall frame maybe 6" from the wall on the rt side and to the rear of the couch (if space permits) and use that false frame to "lift" the insulation off of the wall a bit wiithout losing a ton of space in the room. This Will increase bass trapping a bit. :) Realize I am only speaking of in the Control room, as I see no need for it in the live room. The space behind your resonators is adequate for hanging bass hangers, etc....
