Hi I bought several bundles of the 1" SelectSound from CWCI Insulation in City of Industry, [removed]. Ask for Brian, it was 78¢/ sq ft for the 1", and $1.14/ sq ft (or so) for the 2". The black facing is a bit fragile, but installation is a lot quicker than making fabric-covered frames, and since my place is an old movie theater is was perfect for the projection wall. Hope this helps, good luck!
