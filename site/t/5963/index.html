sharward8 April 2006#2
I would say that 2" vs. 4" is a big difference. The width of the air gap between leaves is a significant part of the mass-air-mass equation. Besides, I would not want to hang anything on a 2" stud! :shock: I would think that the gas line, if it is firmly attached to both leaves, will be a significant detriment to your isolation outcome. If your building codes allow for it, you may have to use specially designed neoprene grommets or something. Don't underestimate the importance of this design obstacle -- you are right to ask about it.

knightfly8 April 2006#6
Based on double 1/2" gyp both sides, a 2" air/insulation space will cost you about 2 dB at the low end and about 1 dB in the mid/high frequency range; again, don't pay for rockwool inside a wall unless every last dB counts; it'll ALMOST be noticeable in most cases, but NOT worth 3x the cost... Steve
