Hi everyone, I'm a newbie around here so please don't shred me into small pieces even if my questions are dumb. I'm in the process of installing a project studio in my basement. I have a problem with the 220V halogen beamers (all tied to a single light dimmer) mounted in the ceiling tiles of the recording room. They seem to radiate buzz directly into electric pickups of guitars/bass etc. only. No other audio device is affected other than the hiZ instruments. The ceiling is not so high (around 2.8 meters high). Is it a normal problem ? How can I avoid that ? Thanks in advance for any advice !!
Aaronw7 July 2004#2
What kind of dimmer are you using? Is the dimmer humming or the lamps/filaments themselves? I would suggest a variac type of dimmer. This should isolate the noise feeding back into the lines.
