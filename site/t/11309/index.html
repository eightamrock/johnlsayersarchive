"Can tradition exterior solid core doors be ultizied? " A solid core door is good. An exterior door can be good, if it is in fact, solid core. A metal exterior door is not a solid core door, not that you said that, I'm just using magic here ;) A solid wooden door or cored wooden door is often recommended. Glass needs to meet your isolation requirements. Andre (avare) is a very knowledgeable man in this field, he is around here somewhere :).
