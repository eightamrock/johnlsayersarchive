As I get more into my build space, I've realized Something, the Garage Floor is Graded! it slopes 7 inches over a distance of 18". This leaves a 4' x 20' section in the back of the garage that i was unintentionally designing around, but hadn't quite realized it. So rather than give up over 20% of the space in my room, I have come to the decision to make some decks to level the floor out. This has some benefits such as being able to run wiring through conduit in the space beneath the floor, not to mention having the control room and one of the smaller rooms being level. Budget constraints dictate building one room @ a time, So the Control room will be finished first, then the others come later. I see building each room on it's own deck, separated from each other by at least an inch. Though I'm still designing I am becoming fond of the Corner Control room concept. I have decided to Specify the Ceiling as RISC-1 / Hat Channel holding 2x 5/8" Drywall/ Green Glue from the wood 2"x6" ceiling joists. The walls are specified as 25GA Steel Framing, 2x 5/8" Drywall with green Glue and Roxul here is how I'm thinking of building the decks - check the .skp How much wieght does it take to "Short-out" a RISC-1 Ceiling when done to manufacturer spec? I'm talking about Roxul and some lights.. I should be way in the tolerance, right? Ed

sorry, missed that. if you're trying to raise the deck to match, then you're either going with concrete slab or the deck as described. definitely adding as much mass to the deck as possible will help reduce its natural resonance frequency and then ensuring its dampened (not damp!) should eliminate any booming... the RISC and hat channel should be fine. as long as the clips are spec'd to support the weight of the framing, drywall, and insulation. which i believe the RISC-1 are ok for your purpose.
