Hello. Interesting idea. trying to replicate an environment that sounds like the original old school studio's that label's like stax and motown used etc. . The 1 mic technique you mentioned is called ambient miking. And is as common today, as it was in the 60's etc. . As for room design, i guess you will have to do some extensive research into how the original studio's were built and what devices were employed to create the effects heard on tape. . You may find articles in magazines and books insightful, although I have not researched this area so I can not tell how much information is available on the subject. . Try soundonsound and the mix as a good starting point. As for drum sounds, look at the type of drum used. You may be able to research this by looking for articles in mag's, there are also a few dvds on the market, including one by the two best funk drummers in the world who were james browns main drummers. . Clyde Stubblefield and John Jab'O Starks http://www.cduniverse.com/productinfo.asp?pid=6759346
