Hi everyone, Just finished planning the acoustics for my bedroom studio. I'm hoping to get into constructing these panels when my university semester is finally over in a month. For now i just wanted to check if anyone thought there were changes i should make.? Currently i think the room sounds pretty bad. To my (untrained) ears there are problems with bass/low-mids. Definate peaks and nulls showed up in the bass range when playing sine waves through my speakers (again by ear, without measuring equipment). No doubt reflections are also going to be an issue. Basically as you can see from the sketchup pics below, i've got: 6 panels on the side walls to tame reflections. 50mm thick with 25mm air gap behind. 2 front bass traps (wall-wall). 100mm thick 2 side bass traps (wall-ceiling). 100mm thick 1 ceiling cloud 100mm thick (attempting to fit it between the speakers and the listening position as per Ethan's documentation, but that a/c vent makes it tricky) Now, the back wall has been giving me plenty of troubles, because it's not a solid wall, the majority of it is the two sliding doors of my built-in wardrobe and the other part needs to be clear so the door can still open. With that in mind i thought of this hanging bass trap idea. Essentially it's just a curtain rod fixed to the ceiling and from that hangs a bass trap measuring 1000mm(H) x900mm(L) x100mm(W). Question: Is this going to be an effective bass trap? I'm going DIY using rigid glasswool. I want to get this right so i'm willing to stretch the budget if the benefit is there. For the side panels i'm copying the mounting suggestions gullfo gave in thisthread Almost forget my room dimensions: 3.6m(L) x 2.8m(W) x 2.4m(H) Rather than overload everyone with any more information, feel free to ask me questions.
