xSpace19 March 2011#70
The difference between 3.937 inches and 4 inches is so small you cannot readily see it and it will not effect a layout until after about 31 inches...at 48 inches you will lose almost 3/4's of an inch. Round up, since you are trying to mix two different measuring standards. The one you are attempting to use is more exacting, but if you don't get it...then it's just another learning curve :) And it would have to be implemented from the start and used religiously...not a mixing of the two.
Soundman202019 March 2011#71
Like Brien said! :) Also, John's slot wall design is for a broadband device: not tuned to exact specific frequencies, and just covering the low end in general, so precision dimensions aren't really that big of an issue, I reckon. Especially if you are mounting the slats over a varying depth cavity (deeper at one end of the slat than at the other end). I'd just round to the closest Imperial equivalent, and not worry about it to much. That 1/16th of an inch difference would only be important if you were aiming at a specific problematic frequency, and needed to hit it precisely with a high Q device, for whatever reason. But for a broadband low Q device, it doesn't really make much difference. - Stuart -
