Hello everyone, I finally have the opportunity to build my own studio from the ground up :yahoo:. I'll try to give as much info as I can. This will be built in the Houston, Texas area and will be a pro studio. Luckily I am able to do the labor myself, including electrical and HVAC (father is a licensed electrician and HVAC contractor). I will have a slab poured, but other that that we will be doing all the work. The rooms I'm looking to include are a control room, live room, vocal booth, iso booth, mic locker, bathroom, kitchen/lounge, and storage. I have played around with many layouts and have come up with one I feel happy with. The whole place will be around 1,200sq ft. The building will be 30ft wide, 40ft deep, and I'm thinking 12ft walls with a peak height of 16ft. I need the building to be pretty quiet and am planning on doing double walls surrounding any of the audio rooms. My idea is 5/8 sheetrock, stud bay with insulation, 3in air gap, stud bay with insulation then 5/8 sheet rock. So this brings me to a question, on the outer walls can I swap the outer layer of sheet rock for 5/8 plywood and use this as the exterior wall of the building. For any walls not dealing with audio I'm planing on a normal single wall. As for the ceiling, non audio rooms will have a standard 8ft, but the others is where I am at a loss. I'd like to go as high as possible, but want to make sure that it is acoustically sound. Should I build them flat across? Vaulted? A combination? Also, if possible I'd like one side of the live room to be a little more "live" then other. If I am hoping for too much here let me know. For the floors, I want to do wood floors in the control room and am curious about doing bare concrete and staining it for everything else. If this a crazy let me know. As for the wood for the control room, are their benefits of solid vs engineered? I'm thinking of solid Bamboo as it can be refinished and is priced well. Also, would I be better off not doing the iso booth and doing a larger vocal booth? I'm debating just hiring a designer to optimize the room, at the very lease I will have them consult on treatment, but figured I'd post here to see what info I can get and try to save a little cash :D This is the layout I've come up with
