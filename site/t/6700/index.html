Woodstock street studios - new studio build, Reading UK. <p> Hi there - we are building a studio in small warehouse in an urban residential (i.e. not quiet). We're looking to create a live room and recording room. <p> Dimensions - rectangle of 12metres by 7 Metres. <p> Building currently has uneven concrete floor, no foundations, stone walls to a height of 2m, then timber to height of 3m, then a corrugated alloy roof supported by thin wooden truss beams. <p> Have read many good suggestions on these pages but would like some advise on the sound proofing results based on using a floating concrete slab floor / a concrete floor using rubber or neoprene sheet. <p> Would also welcome ideas regarding roof / loft insulation for the roof. <p> Main goal is have sound proof live room of about 5m by 8m ( give or take some space depending on design / sound proofing). <p> All the buildings advice we have found locally differs wildly in price and design solution..... We have a budget of 55,000 USD. <p> All advice / suggestions welcomed. <p> Some pics of the warehouse should be included here.
