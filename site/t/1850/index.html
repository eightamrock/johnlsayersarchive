Hi sourrox, if your plywood "ceiling" is sealed and no air can pass in from outside the roof then perhaps you can call it a mass leaf already..but if it is not sealed then you would need to put two real leaves of mass with insulation between them perhaps, to achieve sound isolation. good luck
