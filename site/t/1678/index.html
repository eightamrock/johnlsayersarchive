Nice space! Where aer upi putting the speakers? If in the corners, it looks like they will be more than 60 degrees apart. the preferred norm.

Look at the studios and plans under the "studios under construction" at the beginning of this site. Bass traps: back wall Resonators: side walls diffusors: side walls, combine with resonators, John's slatted absobers would be good. Ceiling construction: absorption for first reflections, or splay (angle) the ceiling to create a RFZ zone. Try that for starts.
