if you build inside-out walls you could add the treatment you want to get rid of the "cave" sound, or just some slat resonators around the room to create some angles and balance the low-mid modes. because the room is square though, you might extend one side of the booth to create a slat resonator that will go deep and also change the room shape a bit.
