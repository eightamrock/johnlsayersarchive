gullfo24 June 2007#2
here's one idea. put in a vocal booth. inside-out walls on the 2 new walls. slat resonator along existing walls. overhead put in a cloud with some plastic under the cloth to keep some highs. small carpet on the floor. you want the vox booth to have evenness but not be too live or dead. i'd use a regular solid storm door with double paned window in it. you want some air flow in and out of the booth. a slat resonator wall behind the mix desk. creates the angles along one wall. you'll want some absorption on the back wall - use heavy drapes or moveable absorbers over the windows. maybe some absorption on the vox booth wall opposite the desk. or conceptually, angle the vox booth wall instead. how-to on all this stuff can be found on this site - just search.

I love the idea of the monitors on stands. I actually thought of mounting them on the walls with brackets up reasonably high above the corner window and the same on the other side.
That's great. I'm glad it's a viable option. Be sure avoid a stand that would place the woofer in the exact center of the floor/ceiling measurement to avoid a null position. Also, try to position your monitors angled 30 degrees in from a flat wall which will result in the speaker baffles being perpendicular to the two legs of equilateral triangle comprised of two speaker centers and a point about 10" behind your head.
What is a ball park figure for making my own acoustic treatments like the 703 absorbers etc, as Auralex room kits can be had for under AUD $1000-00 from what I have been told.
Hard to say, as I don't know the cost of materials in your area. Perhaps a PM to John or a search for your fellow country-members and a PM would prove more helpful. You may also want to look into other insulation products like Roxul AFB or Johns Manville to compare prices. (Optimum density for in-wall[unit] rigid insulation is around 2.5 to 3 PCF.)
Can 703 be brought in Australia?
Here is a link to the guys at FM Insulation who have an extensive range of Rockwool & Fibreglass products for those on the east coast of Oz. http://fminsulation.com.au/cms/ Don't forget your gloves and mask, Itchy! Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
