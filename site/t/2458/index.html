knightfly10 October 2004#4
Steve, I agree you should keep looking - that ceiling arrangement would be hard to neutralize as near as I can tell. That would put your speakers really close to boundaries which would take quite a bit of absorption behind them to minimise early reflections - you would also likely need to put at least a 2-3" fiberglass cloud over the mix area, which by the time it was wide enough would drop your ceiling height in center by at least another foot. A ceiling peak running lengthwise can also horn load your sound too much, making the sweet spot pretty twitchy and small. If that's all you have to choose from, is there a possibility you could widen that tall area in the middle by building a pair of pretty large shed dormers at one end of the room? Just an idea, sometimes quirky solutions work... Steve
