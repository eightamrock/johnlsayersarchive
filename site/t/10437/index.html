gullfo8 April 2008#2
i suggest taking some time to read over the stickies on the site. cheap foam is not only not effective, it could be a safety hazard as well. bottom line - you need decent quality materials to create decent quality treatments. if you have access to Rock Wool, Rigid Fiberglass, Recycled Cotton Insulation batts, or even heavy pink/yellow insulation rolls, and some cloth (lookup flame retardant as well) you can create some effective treatments which will serve you better for little additional cost (over foam) and some manual labor. stacking insulation rolls in the corners can add bass trapping very quickly for the room - keep the plastic cover on. if you need to, put a curtain or cloth in front of them. build some rigid insulation absorbers and place them around the room - over the mic on the ceiling, on each wall near it, and a small throw rug under it. on the walls by you computer/monitors. there are lots of examples of how to place absorbers around the room.
