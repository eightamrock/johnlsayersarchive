AVare15 September 2010#5
Regarding the same thickness for both panes, it is not a factor with laminated glass. The dip in TL with the same thickness glass is at the coincidence frequency, the speed of the sound wave in the glass is the same as the speed of the sound wave in air. The two panes are vibrating at the same frequency, reducing isolation. The lamination layer in laminated glass dampens the coincidence frequency dip completely, for practical purposes. 1" laminated glass at $50? I am envious and you have my sympathies moving it. :P Andre

A quick comment on glass causing too much reflection. Glass reflects about the same as any other material. The size of the glass will not be an obstacle acoustically. The limitation (obviously) is the weight, affecting the handling. Andre
