jbassino5 September 2009#7
If you are going to leave the windows as they are now, then you are wasting your money on building everything. Lets put it this way, your windows have a very low isolation value, so see them like holes on your walls, so there is not sense about doing a room in a room build when you have holes in the OUTER leaf. You need to ideally seal them with the same mass of your walls, so if you have concrete or brickwalls, then brick wall them.
