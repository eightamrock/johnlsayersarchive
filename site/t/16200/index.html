The only option that you did not show would be your desk facing the angled wall, which would be the best place t start. Then you develop a basic reflection free zone, get some broad band damping in the corner that is now at your back...and get on with the Honeymoon!!
JEqualsMe8 June 2011#3
Thanks xSpace, Typical, the best location is the one furtherest from the only power point in the room. Just for my own education, what's the theory behind the angled wall being the best placement? Is it to try and create some kind of symmetry with the corner behind the mix position rather than a straight wall in one ear and an angled wall in the other? James.
xSpace8 June 2011#4
There is no simple answer for this room. It would actually require structurally modifications to be a better room. That said, the best symmetry that this room can produce will be along the angled wall. The problem, OVER ALL, is the potential for low frequency build up in the many corners that you have. So we move the desk away from the corners, which are notorious for 3-6 dB build up and attempt to eliminate this. We also look for a flat area in front of the "listening" position, that place where a person will be seated. Fact is, with the proper construction adjustments/treatments, the desk could be placed anywhere in this room.
