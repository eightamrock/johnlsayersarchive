dymaxian25 October 2004#3
From what I've seen and read, having the whole floor reflective (wood) and the whole ceiling absorptive would probably suit you better. If you wanted to lay down a big carpet to deaden the sound, that's easy enough... the hard floor gives you more options, and the carpet won't do much but absorb the very high freq's anyway. I don't think the curtain between the two sides would do you much good. In my opinion, you'd be best served making the whole room relatively balanced (live vs dead).
