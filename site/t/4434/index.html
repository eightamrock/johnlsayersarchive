Nate, > let me know what you think. < I think some dimensions would be useful! :P > any and all advice would be greatly appreciated. < There's an awful lot of general advice right here in this forum if you snoop around a bit. Another source is my Acoustics FAQ: www.ethanwiner.com/acoustics.html In the mean time, here's the short version: * Bass traps in as many corners as you can manage, including the wall-ceiling corners. * Mid/high frequency absorption at the first reflection points on the side walls and ceiling. * Some additional amount of mid/high absorption on any large areas of bare parallel surfaces, such as opposing walls or the ceiling if the floor is reflective. --Ethan
