looks like a nice plan. i'd add some side wall and a ceiling absorber on first reflection points and also some small absorption on the side walls and ceiling toward the back to break up parallel surface reflections.

gullfo10 February 2008#11
i think the absorber products you listed are too light. you're better off buying some 48kg/m3 rigid insulation and stacking the corners with it (covered with flame retardant treated cloth). make some 60cmx120cm porous absorbers for the ceiling and sides. make a wall of it on the space leftover on the back wall (put it in a frame and covered with cloth). nice construction!
