AVare27 May 2004#2
what do you think about making a rom 9 feet high, is this enough?
Not bad. If I was designing a control room from the beginning, with no restrictions, I would design a 10 high INSIDE HEIGHT ceiling.
And what would your studio look like without any limits ?
That is a question of taste. Have you looked at the studios under the "Studios Under Construction" section? My tastes are such that I like most of the designs of our John, Francis Manzella, Wes Lachot, Russ Berger, and Tom Hidley. I tend to not like the appearance of most studios designed by John Storyk, Pilchner-Schoustal. Regardless of what I just wrote, a good designer will design the rooms to meet your decor likes. You didn't ask, but the room dimension ratios are not that important. The one you mentioned is fine. With a 9 foot ceiling, you end up with a room of about 3000 ft^3. The EBU/ITU/tonmeister SSF (and probably several others)nominal reference volume is 100 m^3, or about 3500 ft^3. This, and many other technical recommendations are summarized in AES TD1001. Here is a link to it. http://www.aes.org/technical/documents/AESTD1001.pdf Good luck! Andre[/url]

AVare28 May 2004#4
are there any other considerations i should make when building and constructing a studio from scratch ?
Your question is too vague. Please be more specific in your data. A double 200 mm concrete wall with 1 m spacing and floating floors will give excellent isolation. Is that how much isolation you want? IS it in your budget? What do you want the isolation for? Do you want clean tracks? Do you want to not bother neighbors? Do you not want outside noise on your tracks? Do you want a nice ambiance on your tracks? These are just a few questions whose answers will help us help your needs.
