knightfly7 September 2006#9
If multiple layers of panel have ZERO air space between them, they act as one LEAF but each LAYER maintains its own coincidence frequency unless you glue them together with ACTUAL GLUE - this is NOT recommended, separate coincidence frequencies makes the leaf stronger. Your drawings show some 3-leaf and some 1-leaf constructs, neither of which are good. The simple way to figure this is to pretend you are a SOUND - try to get through from one space (outside, for example) to another - optimum is if you must pass thru mass, then air (preferably with insulation in it) and then a second mass. More than that, or less than that, and you aren't at optimum isolation for the amount of material you used. If you want the best isolation you can get, don't put any sort of resonant trap on your outside walls - it will weaken isolation at its resonance (in addition to the already existing weakness at the wall's OWN resonance). Now, pretend you're a sound; try to pass thru each of your drawings, then re-draw what you think will work, and "Arnold" (as in, "I'll Be Baaack") :wink: Steve
