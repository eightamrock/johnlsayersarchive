Jbaptista, welcome - I'm not sure if it's a language thing, but you need to check out that link to forum rules again - each BOLD statement in the rules needs to be answered, step by step, for as many of those things you have (or can get) answers to. By doing this, it will force you to think about your project in ways you haven't yet done, and you'll start to understand what is necessary for you to be successful. Sound isolation is NO ACCIDENT, there is no "shortcut" - it must be thorougly PLANNED and constructed correctly, or it's NO GOOD. This requires a lot of planning and the understanding you'll get by reading CAREFULLY through the "stickies" and Reference sections found in each of the forums. If you are an Electrical Engineer, you know that you can't design a circuit by just grabbing a few capacitors and IC's and randomly hooking wires to them - it's no different in acoustics or the physics of sound/vibration control. Also, you appear to have NOT attached whatever drawing you intended in your last post - it's easy to do when first confronted with an entirely new "language", which acoustics definitely is. Still, we can't comment on what we can't see... So, as Kendale so eloquently put it - read, study, and familiarize yourself with the reference material we've assembled here, and it'll get easier every day - but it WILL take a while before you believe that :wink: Hang in there...
