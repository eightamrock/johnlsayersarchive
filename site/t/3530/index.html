Merlin4 April 2005#1
Hello all, I'm a keyboard player living in Thailand and the boss in the club where I play is putting in a room for practice/recording. He's doing the soundproofing atm, and has asked me how I would suggest it be set out so he can have power cables etc run to suit... the room is 7.7M x 3.7M: how would you guys lay it out? Currently there is a door in the middle of one end; and at the other end in the corner is the door to a rest room/shower. In particular, how would you lay out the keys/mixer/computer/monitors? And then, where would you have drums, gtrs etc? Currently I have an old Soundcraft analog desk [about 1.3M x .8M], a couple of keyboards, and a rack of modules etc. Then there's the computer, and [hopefully] 2 x 17"LCD monitors, and a pair of HR824s for me, and a pair of JBL Control24's with subs for general rehearsal spkrs. My idea is to have everyone on headphones and be able to mix themselves ["more me"]; all keys direct, bass DI'd, and gtr to DIs straight off the fx board. We have a Roland Vdrum kit too. So it should be easy to get a starting point with everyone direct, then if we really decide we need amps, or acoustic drums etc they can be overdubbed later. One thing I've been wondering about: with a space this size, is it worth trying to put a separate control room in? Or a vocal booth? What would you guys do? Thanks a lot in advance for any advice...
