Soundman20206 June 2009#2
Whats the best insulation to use for me walls? 2 leaf system using air mass spring...
Probably the best one is the cheapest one! depending on what you mean by "best". As long as it has a density of roughly 48 kg/m3, then it will work acoustically. But there are also issues of installation: Personally, I hate the rolls of pink fluffy fiberglass because it is itchy! It makes me itch just to THINK about installing that stuff. But it works. Semi-rigid stuff works just as well, and is easy to install. Mineral wool works too, and doesn't itch like fiberglass does. Rolls, batts or slabs: it's more of a personal choice about what you like to work with, and how much you want to pay, than it is a choice about acoustic properties. - Stuart -
