gullfo21 April 2008#10
maybe some more questions are in order? what type of studio are you planning? residential (project) studio or commercial? a commercial studio typically will have a lot of different concerns than a home project studio. what type of music do you think you'll mostly record? do you need a large live space or more isolation? what type of noise do you need to keep out or in? neighbors? 30x40 is about 1200 sq ft. presumably you'll be pouring a concrete pad (or pads) for this. do you need bathroom, lounge, reception? as far as rooms, control rooms typically need to be symmetrical and reasonably low reverberation times. live rooms typically want to be large and variable in terms of reverberation time and acoustic qualities. isolation booths normally will be live or dead or variable depending on your needs. room shapes - make everything rectangular if you can. easier to build, design, and treat. make everything with at least 12' ceilings.

Rooms can be variable by using screens, hanging curtains, having different treatments on some internal walls etc. - all these things allow more flexibility when you are recording as you can move things around and tailor the room sound to suit the project.
