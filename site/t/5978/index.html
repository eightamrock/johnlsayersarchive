knightfly17 April 2006#6
Hendrik, if that's all you have available it will work better for bass absorption, especially in broadband corner traps; however, if you can't find any lighter stuff (3 PCF, or 48 kG/m^3, is ideal) then you may want to face the dense stuff with something lighter; you can cover it with a good grade of acoustic foam, for example - also, normal weight spun fiberglass insulation (no facing) in front of the denser stuff makes for a thorough absorption range. The main place you'll want less dense stuff in front of your heavy stuff is for ceiling clouds and sidewall first reflection points; you may not need it for corner traps at all... Steve
