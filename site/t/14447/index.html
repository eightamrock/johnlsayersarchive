donal_finn8 June 2010#2
Hi, i had pretty much the same dilema in a retro fit build lately. My problem was my control room was upstairs over my tracking rrom. We also had a poor quality hollow chipboard floor in pretty bad nick. What we done was removed the old chipboard floor, (bit by bit to allow standing space to put in new one!!!!) put in extra supports between the Joists of the floor and filled the voids up with Fibreglass completely. We then put in a new chipboard floor ( 1inch thick tounge and grooved) taped up all the sides and gaps etc, then put in another Marine plywood floor (1/2 inch) directly over the new chipboard one. We then put in high quality underlay and thick carpets. In my opinion it has done quite a good job. If there are drums playing downstairs i can hear them upstairs a little. But not nearly as much as i thought i would. Long story short its not 100% (more like 75 to 80 % but much better than i thought. We also built superdoors which i guess helped a lot. We were advised to remove the original ceiling also and to put a new one up using two sheets of sheetrock and a special hanging system designed for isolation ( look up hat tracks) but couldnt justify the costs and messy demolition for a home studio build. Looking back i think it might have been worth the extra work which i think would have given me about 90% / 95% but with pretty big costs and huge mess!! A full detailed and photographed blog of my build is here on this site, viewtopic.php?f=2&t=13207 If you follow closely the advice of the experts here you will end up with a very good studio, Have faith and be patient! Hope this is of some help Regards Donal
