orangenumerik18 September 2006#2
Room inside a room is the way to go as far as soundproofing goes, just remember that you can't cheat. For sound proofing you need mass an air space and mass again SEALED, so any product that doesn't have mass and goes next to your Mass or worst replace it in a wall would be a waste of money IMHO. Fiberglass, rockwool, rigid isulation 703, name it when used in a wall cavity are only used as damper, it do not keep sound from getting in or out, Mass does it. So an heavy wall, wool, an air space at least 1" more if you can, wool, another heavy wall. Is what you are after. As we speak gypsum board is the cheapeast way to add mass...as far as i know. Regards
