Soundman202023 December 2009#3
My first thought is that your control room will not be symmetrical. That's a biggie. It must be symmetrical if you hope to do decent mixes in there that translate well to the real world. No symmetry = lousy mix / lousy translation. Second thought: How on earth are you going to get an air-tight seal and enough mass on your "hinged folding walls", and how can you hinge a two-leaf wall? If it is not sealed air-tight, or has insufficient mass, or is not a two-leaf wall: = it will not isolate. - Stuart -
