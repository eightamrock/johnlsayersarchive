xSpace1 March 2010#5
This space, in general, is small so a one room environment might suit you better then trying to break it up with an isolation booth. It would be easy enough to build some movable isolation panels that could be used to work as both control room absorption/reflective surfaces and when placed edge to edge would help you to isolate the vocals. But the skylight thing seems like it may be more trouble than it would be worth just for natural light and specifically for an air in conduit. I would look deeper into the room and find the best way to get much needed air exchange/fresh air. edit: "room is 2.44 M (W) by 2.50 M (H)" Due to these room dimensions that reinforce the modes, essentially almost square in form, you will want to build an overhead soft acoustical cloud to take the energy out. Hope this is helpful.
