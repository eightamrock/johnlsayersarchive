Soundman202015 March 2010#27
Recommended height for the acoustic center of your monitors is 1.2m, which should be roughly the height of your ears when seated at the mix position (unless you are really tall, really short, or like to have your chair at an unusual height). Not that this is the height of the ACOUSTIC CENTER of the speaker, which is usually somewhere between the woofer and tweeter, probably closer to the tweeter. The manufacturer might be able to tell you where the acoustic center is for your model of speaker.
nor lower that 1.5 meter.
If you have them at 1.5m above the floor, then you will have to tilt them downwards to get the correct geometry. That complicates things, but it can be done. But it would be better to use 1.2m, or best of all measure the height of YOUR OWN ears when YOU are comfortably seated in YOUR normal chair that YOU plan to use when mixing, and use that for the height of the acoustic axis of your speakers. - Stuart -
