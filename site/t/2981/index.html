jatougas14 January 2005#10
peegee wrote:
I had heard that sounds react differently to different colors, and im assuming from your advice this is true.
No, it's purely a psychosomatic perception of space that a dark surface appears further away than it is. John's suggesting putting 703 on the ceiling, then covering it in a dark fabric so that the fibers from the 703 don't float free and get in your lungs and other vital equipment. :-)
peegee wrote:
Also - Would i need to paint the walls a different color?
That'd be up to you. All black walls and ceiling would end up making the space feel claustrophobic. Light colored walls would enhance the illusion of the dark ceiling being further away than it actually is.
peegee wrote:
And i am planning on putting laminate flooring down but wonder if a lighter maple/poplar finish would be the way to go, or something with a darker color like a mahogany finish?
Once again, that'd be up to you. While you *can* hear the difference between a maple acoustic guitar, a mahogany acoustic guitar, and a rosewood acoustic guitar, there's little research to substantiate that the reflections coming from laminate floorings differentiate much from each other, much less from concrete slab.
