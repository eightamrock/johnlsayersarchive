gullfo4 October 2007#9
the part Rod mentioned - flanking - this can be due to the floor or other structure conducting the sound around your nice walls. to get a high level (>50db) of isolation means you're going to need to consider how you avoid flanking. some options include floating floors and room-in-a-room, isolated HVAC, etc... to keep it contained within your space. Rod's book on studio construction has a lot of information related to proper isolation, including floating floors. also, is that the actual layout proposed for your studio?
