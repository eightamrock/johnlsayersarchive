gullfo7 October 2007#2
nice work on the drawings. i think if you do a search on bedroom studio or treat bedroom you'll find a lot of information on what to do (i know i've posted some in the last couple of weeks). there are options such as constructing furniture style treatments as found in John's wall unit page, or using rigid fiberglass/mineral wool ala Real Traps or GIK absorbers. generally speaking 50% on any axis is going to have a null for a given frequency based on that axis dimension. dimensions that are close or are multiples of each other will reinforce room modes.

well, there's a couple of approaches to take - build treatments based on measurements made on the room, or use broadband treatments to accomodate the small space. i'd opt for the latter. since your bedroom is pretty much a stock 11x13 bedroom, there are numerous examples of types, positions, and construction techniques for creating a balanced room on this site... as far as the vox booth, i think the clothes closet by itself will be too small, i'd build it out as a larger booth or use gobo panels to create vox booth.

gullfo8 October 2007#6
basically the monitoring position is 38% of the room length from the front wall. with the monitors about 70% of the room width apart forming an equilateral triangle on 60 degree legs. the vox booth can be in the corner as shown or in the middle - just watch rear reflections from it. I'd check the Auralex site for recommendations although someone hanging out in the acoustics forum will know something about it... http://www.acoustics101.com/ http://emusician.com/aux_hardware/emusi ... treatment/
