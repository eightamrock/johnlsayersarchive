rod gervais1 August 2008#10
At the top of the wall you ar dealing with fire plus hot gasses - and just stuffing with mineral wool will not stop the passage of hot gasses - that's why the need for the caulk. And you have to do that (by code) at each and every penetration made in a vertical manner - so if a single wire passes up the wall and through the top plate - it is supposed to be fire caulked. The code requirement for every 10 feeet of wal l(plus corners and intersections) has to d with draft stopping - not fire stopping - and mineral woll stuffed into the cavity is a perfectly acceptable manner in which to handle that - Sincerely, Rod
