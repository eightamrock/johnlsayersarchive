qiktune15 March 2008#33
If anyone wants to get some REAL cheap DIY racks, you might want to check out IKEA. Couple of weeks ago I picked up 2x "RAST" bedside tables from there for under AU$20 each. They fit a 19" unit PERFECTLY!! I bought two of them, left the bottom off one and the top off of the other then stacked them on top of each other. Screwed in 2x 12U rails at a sloped angle, some bracing at the rear and in under half an hour it was done. Now why didn't I stain it before I loaded my gear in there? :(
