I found it impossible to find any detailed plans on the net for making decent studio desks, furniture and outboard 19" racks. There are heaps of places to buy pre-made stuff but nothing for the DIY person that includes measurements for things like rack rail spacing, average desk height etc. I didn't know these things so I presume other beginner builders may not know as well. I thought maybe Steve could sticky this thread (done - Steve) as all these studios we are building eventually need desks and racks to put our gear on. Others may have links to plans or sites with plans they can add. Here's the only one I found after two days of surfing - some plans here are OK but they cost. http://plans.thefrankes.com/racks/ JohnG
