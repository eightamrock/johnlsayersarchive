knightfly14 June 2006#2
Two questions - first, what coating (if any) is on the OUTER surface of your roof/ceiling, and second - have you noticed any condensation in that area already? If so, under what conditions - (changing weather, rain, high humidity, etc) Heat drives moisture from the warmer side to the cooler side, where it will condense if the humidity is very high - so sun heating the roof will drive moisture inward. If you have some sort of waterproof membrane over the outside of the concrete ceiling, it shouldn't allow this to happen - this is why we need more info if we're to help... Steve
