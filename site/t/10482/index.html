gullfo17 April 2008#3
the problem with sound isolation is that you need more acoustic treatment because all that sound that could escape is now trapped... so to avoid getting angry neighbors and police involved, it sounds like the bulk of your work should be sound isolation - then treat as your budget allows. check out John's wall unit page to get some ideas on some very nice treatments you could adapt for your space.

I also have to block of the inside of the garage door..i'm thinking of building a brick wall on the inside with maybe foam between the gap of the garage door and the wall? thanks for any help
gullfo21 April 2008#5
the wall units are acoustic treatments not isolation. that said, depending on how things are already constructed and so on, it might be a useful exercise to test and see how much isolation you need. get a sound level meter ad set up some monitoring equipment you can then use sine sweeps and drums and bass to see whats getting out and from where. then you might find a good seal job could be all you need... or maybe you find that the location is almost completely unusable for this purpose...
