studio9118 December 2006#4
Dont know if I can be much help, but as far as framing, I am using 2X6's for the exterior walls. This will give me the room/depth I need for the R-19 code required insulation. As far as interior walls, I will be using 2x4 framing except for on the ceilings (the ceilings within the ceiling) where I will have 2 layers of Drywall attached. Due to the weight, I am going with 2X6's. Also as was pointed out in I think one of my posts by one of the masters on here,,,,I am spacing my interior walls 24" OC instead of 16 OC, so the "slop" between the 24" studs helps with sound isolation. Exterior 2X6 walls will still be 16 OC though as well as all ceilings. And I totally understand the room issue, and the want to have square rooms. I started the same way and was determined to keep things square (again for the space and ease of conversion later if need be) but since I got started on here, I have made the needed changes so the rooms arent square. And believe me, I tried every angle (no pun intended) as far as splaying walls inward, outward and even looked into making them slant in or out vertically. I wanted every last inch of space. Bottom line, you might want to conceed as well, and just splay wherever you can as you build. It appears thats the way to go and it will pay off in the end. :wink: Or just angle 2 somehow so there are no parallel walls. There,,,thats just my 2 cents,,,, and actually my first post as a response to a question. :D I am usually the one asking them, not the one trying to answer them. :lol: Good Luck AJ

gullfo7 January 2007#7
i think you should make sure your rooms are not square - extend or shrink them. see if you can get them close to one of a number of optimum room ratios and then do a good job treatign the rooms to get an even a response as you can without making the rooms too dead. put up treatments in all corners including wall-ceiling corners. clouds and carpets staggered to reduce floor ceiling reflections. a copy of Rod's book on studio construction will go a long way to assisting in getting the isolation working as expected. why are you floating the live room?
