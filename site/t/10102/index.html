What's the difference between hanging clouds from the ceiling and having a roof that inclines at 15 degree in the CR? I've always felt that the clouds with lighting gives the CR a more professional look. Are there any benefits to a sloping roof?
Would I be correct in my understanding that when you state "roof" you are referring to the "ceiling"? If so, one of the benefits of an angled ceiling (12 degrees or more) is that it redirects floor/ceiling reflections from interfering with the mix position imaging (to be absorbed and/or treated elsewhere.) One of the downsides to an angled ceiling is the expense involved. As most "home studios" are often built with considerably smaller budgets (and lower ceilings) than full blown professional facilities, clouds are an effective and budget-friendly way to treat the floor/ceiling reflections. Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
