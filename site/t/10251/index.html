jwl7 March 2008#6
Hey Jem, The first reflection points are the key to creating a Reflection-Free Zone at the mix position. Without treatment, sound reflecting off the side walls and the ceiling will arrive at your ears slightly later in time than the direct sound from the speakers; these reflections will obscure the stereo image you hear, making it more difficult to adjust reverb, pan, etc. An way to find the first-reflection points is to use the "mirror trick." Have a friend come help you with a mirror he/she can hold up against the wall, at your ear level. You will be sitting at the mix position. Have your friend move the mirror around the walls. Anywhere you can see your monitors in the reflection of the mirror, install absorption. Do the same on the ceiling (hopefully your friend is tall.... heh).[/quote]
