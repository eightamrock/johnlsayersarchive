tmix21 February 2007#2
There are probably some room ratios (height / width / depth) to consider, but speaking from a practical standpoint you really need at least a inside dimension of 5 foot by 6 foot with a 7 foot or so height to work for vocals to not sound boxy. I have a booth this size and it sounds fine. I have actually fit my small Jazz drum kit in there and recorded, but most folks would feel penned in. A minimum size for drums (typical 5 piece kit) would be closer to 7 foot by 9 foot for some room with the tallest height you caan get away with for hanging a bunch of absorbers. Smaller rooms typically have to be fully absorbed on the walls and ceiling because it is next to impossible to have the room sound "good " by itself. My opinion (and experiance) obviously. Tom
