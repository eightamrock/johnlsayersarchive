Soundman202020 August 2013#4
I have not decided the decay time. Its honestly too subjective for me to make a logical choice. What's the average?
Depends on what you want! :) There are recommendations for different types of room. Take a look at EBU-32767 or ITU BS-775 for the recommended specs for studios.
the concrete block will be my first leaf then a probably 3 inch air gap and 2x4 studs
I think you meant "3 inch gap" not "3 inch air gap"... :) With 2x4 studs, it is impossible to have only a 3" air gap, since the studs themselves are 3-1/2" deep... With a 3 inch gap plus 2x4 studs, you have a 6-1/2" air gap.
with 4lb dense 4 inch thick rock wool
4 pcf is a bit too dense for that: ideally it should be 3 pcf (about 50 kg/m3).
You just cut it a little big and wedge it in right? (The mineral wool) you dont glue it or anything?
Right. But with a 6-1/2", you would be better of with 6" insulation. That would give you better isolation.
And I was just asking if its properly "decoupled" both leaves are screwed into the same beam
If they are screwed into the same beam, then they are not decoupled at all!!! The inner-leaf is an entirely separate, independent, self-supporting structure that is not attached to the outer-leaf anywhere, at any point. Not even a single nail or screw. No mechanical connection at all. So if you screwed both leaves to the same joist or beam, then you need to fix that urgently, before you carry on. That's a major error. - Stuart -
