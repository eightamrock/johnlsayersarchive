John suggested positioning my speakers at a 90 degrees angle (see the attachement to this post). I've setup my speakers that way (on a table, the soffits aren't built yet) just to get an idea. I must say I feel a bit uncomfortable with this setup and I'm not sure why. Maybe it's because I'm reading mixed opinions on this kind of setup... Or maybe it's because the speakers are further away then I was used to. I must confess I did not play any music on this setup because the room is untreated at the moment. I guess the stereo field will be very wide, though I'm worrying it's TOO wide for me. So I made a 60 degrees version. The speaker centers are spaced about 1.2 metres from each other. I can't move back into the room (to make the triangle bigger) because that would put me in a huge null. What do you think is better: - 90 degrees: Bigger triangle, speakers further away from my ears, wider stereo image - 60 degrees: Smaller triangle, speakers closer to my ears, narrower stereo image EDIT: I'm not sure this topic should be in the speaker forum or the studio design forum... 60 degrees is the industry norm for DECADES
well yes and no - Music farm and Hello Testing and EMI's Studio 301 and Fast Forward in Melbourne were all 90 degrees here in australia - I worked at Music farm for years with 90 degrees and produced hit records. It was fine. In a small room such as this 90 degrees is a good option IMO. cheers john
