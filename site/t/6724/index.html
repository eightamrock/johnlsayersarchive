Ro29 July 2006#2
* Apply mud/plaster, as in the second picture, between the boards. * Apply sealant (kit), as in the first picture, at the outer rims where the boards meets the walls (to fill that 1/4" (about 6mm) gap) The stopgap, from Auralex, is expensive but good for the job. You might wanna shop around (if money's an issue) for a sealant kit with following properties: - Permanent flexible (will not dry and harden) - More mass than average - Will not shrink (- Will attach to gypsym, wood, etc.) Those are the things to look for concerning sealant (picture one)
