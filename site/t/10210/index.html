Hello all. Been a member for a few years now and am finally starting an actual 'build'. Any help or suggestions are welcome. OVERVIEW: I have a control room in my den set up for years. I run snakes to the basement where I record bands. I communicate with talkback/headphones combo. Have been using gobo's, spacing, and mic placement for separation so far. Lately, I've been getting 'real' musicians in with nice instruments (vibes, double bass, horns, etc...) and the concrete walls and 'one big room' have been the weak link. GOALS: Control leakage to 'up stairs' as much as possible. Control room stays up stairs, so I'm thinking three rooms with unique character (unless someone highly recommends otherwise). Possible video feeds to each room and control room. SOME 'DECIDED ON' FEATURES: Already adding mass between joists (two drywall layers/green glue between) and stuffing UltraTouch insulation below that. Ceiling to be hung below joists with RSIC or similar clips and hat channels (2 layers 5/8" drywall with green glue between. Similar isolation of soffits around HVAC and beams. Floor will remain concrete to be stained. BUDGET: I'm doing the work myself. I don't mind spending money when necessary, but would like the 'bang for the buck' options. I've spent around $2000 so far on drywall/green glue/insulation/misc..., and know I'll be spending at least a $5000 more when it comes to buying more lumber/doors/lighting/and wiring. If that's off by another $5000, so be it. It's not a 'money's no object' thing, nor 'what's the cheapest way out'. Somewhere in the middle. Here's my first Sketchup drawing idea...

gullfo22 February 2008#4
any of the angled walls would be good candidates for inside out walls. the utilities - i'd leave enough space and a door to access them - not much else you can do without moving them. good idea to beef up around the stairs as that is a big hole in the ceiling... that said, with all the plumbing overhead in the dead room, you probably want some access panels in there. sounds like you'll probably need to cut down some doors if you cannot raise the duct - maybe shallow and wider ducting in that section? for the medium room, i'd minimize the porous absorption on the walls and ceilings until you know how the room is actually behaving. maybe some reversible treatments.
