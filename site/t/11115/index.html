xSpace30 August 2008#18
Only thing I could add would be this. Balance. Symmetry, as it is used in these forums, often is nothing more then balance. Another way of saying this which is more to the point would be "mirroring". Whatever it is on the left it should be on the right, specifically in a rfz. Such is not always possible in pre-existing area but is what is looked for in a control room environment. What you are trying to achieve is to get any sound that moves out of the speakers in this room, to get to the left AND right ear at the same time, with nothing added and nothing subtracted in the process of the sound moving. You have the best helping you right now, my opinion is that a better understanding of this term symmetry is all that you need to better understand why it is these suggestions are being made. Although I do like the clean wall lines that "0 15 symmetr CR 2.jpg" produces if I had to deal with the column I would consider the way you have it in "0 16 OK big question 2.jpg". At the back of the room like it is, with bass traps, it works for me :) That's my 2
