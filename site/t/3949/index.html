Hi, and welcome; first, can you work on following this http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=3231 a bit more? The link will explain why it's necessary, thanks... I would be careful of using particle board at all in a studio; sealed up, all those formaldehyde fumes can make you sick - and the stuff can outgas for months, possibly years. There are non-formaldehyde versions, but not really common in most areas. Specialty panels are almost ALWAYS 'way more expensive for sound control than just basic MASS, used in the right places - the link above will also guide you to a few discussions on that. After you read through some of the material on the above link (and edit your profile to include a location) I'll be glad to try and answer any questions still not covered... Steve
