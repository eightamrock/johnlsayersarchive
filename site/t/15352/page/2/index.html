Cold10 January 2011#29
Spraying foam, contrary to the adds for diy kits, is not something you want to do yourself. I don't know the folks working in your neck of the woods, but if you google it, I'd bet there are several local guys who could give you a quote. I personally prefer 2lb closed cell, but 1/2 lb is better acoustically. Also depends on your local inspector as to what is acceptable. Steve

definitely better. i think you have the number of hangers probably maxed out. one thought to keep in mind is lining the cavity with rigid insulation so take that into account when building the hangers, in some cases once you line it there might not be enough room for hanger in which case just make it like a super chunk. fill the open gap between the lining insulation with the pink stuff.
