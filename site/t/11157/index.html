John Hile2 September 2008#11
Allyn, Since there are so many variations in building any wall there is no specific answer to the STC of a Steel Stud wall. Here is a simple answer for you to use as a reference. 52 STC Wall: 2 x 5/8" layers of Drywall 25 Gauge Steel Studs 24" OC r-11 Insulation in the cavity 2 x 5/8" layers of Drywall The benefit in a Steel Stud wall is that it has a bit of "resilience" in it and will give a little better STC # than wood studs. When thinking about a room's use such as yours then you want to concentrate on low end frequency noise problem. Depending solely on STC values of a product can be misleading.
