Soundman20205 October 2009#7
INSIDE gypsum | gypsum | isolation | AIR | isolation | osb | plywood | OUTISDE And also take the same treatment for the ceiling? I know it's all about mass, mass, mass & being airtight...but I really could use the help in this matter!
You have the right basic idea there. That's a proper 2-leaf MSM wall, as you describe it (provided that there are no mechanical links at all between the two leaves). However, it's hard to say if it will be "enough". It might be, or it might not be, depending on what you need and what the dimensions are. In terms of: "how loud are you" and "how loud do you need to be" (decibels). And also: how thick is the gypsum, OSB, plywood, how deep is the air gap, what kind if insulation and how much of it, etc. You need to look at all of those to determine if it is "enough". - Stuart -

Hello Geert and welcome! Since you're planning from the ground up, I would suggest separate slabs for the exterior walls and the interior building. It might not be necessary depending on your isolation needs, but it won't hurt either. About the costs/budget: you might wanna re-think building the exterior walls with OSB/plywood. Laying bricks is a lot cheaper than building with wood in our region (I'm from Groningen) You could also try it yourself :wink: I've done it; it's slow but cheap, and a lot of mass, which helps with the isolation. Just a thought... Good luck with the planning! Phybeau

Phybeau7 October 2009#14
I feel a Dutch T(h)reat coming.... :mrgreen: No Geert, the brick laying for the outside walls was done by a contractor, since it's part of an addition to our house. The inside walls were done by me. With an average of one hour brick laying per day, it took me about 3 months to just do the walls. I'm not experienced at that either, in fact it was a first time ever, but.....rewarding somehow and didn't turn out that bad, if I dare to say so myself 8) If you do decide to go with brick walls, don't forget that you might have some decent isolation with those walls but you still need to attend the roof. That's going to be the weaker link. In my build this is also the case: My outside roof consist of the following: (outside>in) : Tiles? (dakpannen), which are no help at all, isolationwise. OSB - Wood (dakbeschot) - Gypsumboard - Gypsumboard. If you want to attach mass (GB) to your roof, the construction has to be sturdy/strong enough of course. More than a normal roof. And that's going to cost :cry: My inside roof is going to be concrete in order to match the inner (concrete block) walls. At the s p e e d I'm building, it's going to take at least another six months to give any comments on the results as far as isolation goes I'm afraid. I could upload some pics if you want, but I doubt if the're going to be of any help to you at all. And,... if you decide to go with a separate foundation for the outside walls and and an isolated slab for the inside...devide the slab into 2 separate pieces; one for the CR and one for the live room. Well,...just another thought... Loads of wisdom to you! Phybeau
