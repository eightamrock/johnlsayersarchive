brianrcdd31 May 2008#4
Looks like you may have taken this path: Build > Test > Unhappy > Ask You needed to take this path for better results: Study > Plan > Ask > Revise > (Repeat last four as necessary) > Build > Test > Tweak > Enjoy It's quite possible if you start on the above-recommended path now you may come to find there is no cure for what has already been constructed. You will have to make that decision, not because of our opinions but because of your own newfound understanding of what's actually wrong with it. Best place to start is soaking up the great info offered on this site. It will require a time commitment on your part to dig through all that is recorded here. Trust me, it is time well spent. This is a complex subject, so don't expect to grasp the concepts overnite. But if you show a willingness to learn instead of asking for a quick fix, folks here will bend over backwards to help you.
