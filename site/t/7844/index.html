Ro3 January 2007#2
Sounds like a plan. Remember the wool is to absorb energy within the air cavity (the total air space is INCL the wool within the frame). So placing wool with high absorption numbers is good. And yes, the cheap stuff will do. More airspace is better isolation, so wider wool will extend the air space indeed. Also compress the wool a little against the inner leaf (gypsum board) to prevent it from resonating too much. IF you can afford to go with 100mm, I'd do so. Transmission loss for low end is a much more difficult to achieve than mid/high (cymbals 'n stuff for example). By adding basstraps etc you'll lower the bass energy in a room. That means less low-end energy to isolate.
