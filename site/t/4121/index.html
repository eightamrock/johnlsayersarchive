Deluks30 July 2005#2
I think trying to squeeze 3 rooms into such a small space is near impossible. Stick with just one room, and make a few portable screens for recording purposes, a vocal booth is not an essential. Also consider that if you intend to soundproof this in any way you'll lose about 6 inches from each side, means your floorspace will only be 9x13, tight but still doable. Do lots (and I means hours & hours) of reading on this site, and in the construction part of the forum before buying/building anything! Also I'd recommending updating your profile to include your location, otherwise you'll get a good b*llocking from site admin! Good luck, keep us posted! Deluks
