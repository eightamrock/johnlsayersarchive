Overview Greetings to all! My name is Adolfo and finished my Sound engineering career almost two years ago, after spending this entire time recording in different studios and in my humble “home studio” I finally decided, after a friend of mine offered me to start a business with him, to build my own “Real” Studio. This would grand me the chance to improve my recording skills much faster and earn more money in the process. Some of you would say, well you just finished your career and already want your own studio, and I did think like that too, but this is going to be a mayor business including a café a music store a gig stage and more. I’m at the designing stage of the Recording/rehearsal Studio Part of the complex. And would really like to have an almost ideal environment to mix and record, and isolated enough so that two bands could rehearsal at the same time and no one could listen to the other. How loud are you I’m assuming there will be playing many different styles of music from Super Fast Blast beats and screaming vocals to some quieter jazz band. So the isolation will need to be pretty good. But there are several limitations that I’m going to specify later on. Existing construction detail The existing construction actually doesn’t exists, what I mean is that I need to make a kind of ideal but down to earth design which is going to be approved by several other persons ( my friend, constructors etc) and with this we are going to search the ideal place for this. It’s not much of a problem because my partner has a construction company and has many contacts to get almost any place he wants. But as I mentioned there are some true facts that are going to be in the “existing” construction: First floor on concrete slap, Concrete Walls 20 cm thick and 2.45 meters high (I’m assuming the worst case for the height), Concrete ceiling. As I don’t know the building there will be for sure pillars and more that are going to change my design. But it’s all going to be as close as possible to the current or resulting design. Budget My budget is of more or less 15000 U.S. Dollars in construction (Audio Gear is not included, but electric and Air conditioning system are). This budged is JUST for the control room and life room, the other room is an optional if the budged is enough. And obviously if it’s possible to make it cheaper we’ll be all happy :D . My design

Well my design is the following, as you can see I have the control room in between both recording rooms so I can check both out at the same time, and or record them simultaneously. I was planning to go for an Inside out wall and ceiling for everything to not blow of my budged in treating the room afterwards and because of my room height (2.45m). And eventually would treat some of the walls (still not know which ones) with a slot finish to liven the room up. On the back wall of my control room and below my soffit mounted speakers I want to add hanger bass traps. The flooring is going to be wood for all rooms and a couch on the back of the control room will be on a riser. I didn’t used Sliding Glass doors instead of windows because I don’t want the musicians to have easy access to the control room, and for my access to the recording rooms there are two doors in the back of the room (with me the only key). I’ve tried to get the “sweet spot” in the 38 % of the room and have the soffits angled at 60 degrees. Questions What should I do with the ceiling is my height too small to angle it? If yes is the inside out ceiling enough because of its absorbent properties. Are the listeners that are sitting in the back of the room going to hear good enough? I think maybe the sound “spread” is to wide in the back. Are my Recording rooms in a way to odd shape? Are there any rules or guides to follow when angling the walls in the recording rooms? Is there any problem in having the windows that look into the recording rooms on the "slot resonator" walls? Or shouldn't I make that "walls" with slot finish? Any other suggestions are more than welcome. Thanks in advance

AVare7 May 2010#16
What should I do with the ceiling is my height too small to angle it? If yes is the inside out ceiling enough because of its absorbent properties.
With the ceilings being concrete, they give little low frequency absorption. Cover them with 100mm dep at least absorbent material.
Are the listeners that are sitting in the back of the room going to hear good enough? I think maybe the sound “spread” is to wide in the back.
Meaningless question. The people in the back are not in the "sweet spot", but are in a treated room. Other thatn bass buildup form being near a wall, they will have good listenng.
Are my Recording rooms in a way to odd shape?
No.
Are there any rules or guides to follow when angling the walls in the recording rooms?
What are you angling them for? When doing it for flutter echo control, 1 in 10 angle is usaully used.
Is there any problem in having the windows that look into the recording rooms on the "slot resonator" walls? Or shouldn't I make that "walls" with slot finish?
If they are not in the first reflection points, there is no problem. Andre
