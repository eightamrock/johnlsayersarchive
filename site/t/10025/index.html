gullfo14 January 2008#2
if you could post some information about how you plan to use the studio, music type, etc prefer more mix time, or more recording time, etc. it helps to understand the balance of rooms. for instance, I'd definitely shrink the non-studio space for the bathroom, kitchen, lounge as much as possible so you can get a larger control room and live room. plus you might want the layout to put the CR near the common space so access in and out of the CR doesn't have to interfere with the live room or booths. you also should consider load in/out as well as the lines of sight between rooms.
