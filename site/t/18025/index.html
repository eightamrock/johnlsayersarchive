What you require is a larger room. Anything you do to this space will increase the ability of that small room to hurt your hearing. You do not need isolation and you sure as heck do not need a floating floor. What you want is to treat the IMMEDIATE transfer of sound from the walls to your ears.
Soundman202010 December 2012#3
Hi "Yaybo", and welcome to the forum! :) Unfortunately, Brien is right. That space is just too small to be able to do anything except add some treatment. There is no way you can add isolation walls there: You would lose at least 8 inches on each side. And the same for the ceiling: Your final room height would be way under six feet. It really isn't enough. With only 4'10" width, you won't be able to get the speakers far enough apart and far enough away from you to get a good stereo image and sound stage, and you won't be able to angle them correctly either. I would really suggest that you find another room, one that is much bigger and has a higher ceiling. - Stuart -
