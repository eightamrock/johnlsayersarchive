Outstanding! :D Oh, and by 2-3" air gap, I'm assuming you're referring to the gap between the frames. If you're using nominal 2x4s which measure 3.5", that's 3.5" + 2-3" + 3.5" = 9-10". Since drums are involved, if you can bump that to a 5" gap between frames, you'll have 12" of air space and that's going to give you better isolation at the low end. If you can afford to lose those extra few inches (and, really, I doubt you'd miss 'em), you should.
