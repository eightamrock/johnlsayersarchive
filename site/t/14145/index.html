Htown, a lot of things will determine costs. For example, right now with the natural disasters, prices are sky high. When I built an addition, quite a few years ago. 7/16 osb was 5.88 per sheet. Then came the hurricanes, I think Charlie and more. Florida was getting nailed every other week and 7/16 osb climbed to over $15 per sheet. Then came Katrina, Rita ect. and it climbed to 15 per sheet again (names could be off a little,that was more than a few beers ago) Last May I purchased 1st phase of my materials. osb like $6.45.cool. Went through the bid room at Home depot and got another $900 discount. Fast forward to last week, osb $9.75 per sheet and bid room discount from $3700, down to $3100. If I had bought my materials 3 months ago, I would have saved an additional $500 to $600 and my room is only 19x14x8. use 7/16 osb at HD as a barometer, when it gets 7 bucks and under consider buying if budget is a concern and when isn't it? hoped this sheds some light. hopefully we have a slow hurricane season this year esp. down in your area. TomVan2 March 2010#7
Htown, Seeing how Brien is quoting what a new house would cost to build ( around$150 per square foot in "my area") You have to remember this is not a house. Regardless if you already have the shell to build in. Its a studio... with twice and sometimes three times the amount of sheet rock. At least twice the amount of wood/timbers/steel and typically the finish work is a whole lot more detailed than a house. If you want it to look like those pics. The insulation is probably 4-5 times the amount of a house. The electrical is twice if not more detailed than a house The low voltage is a major factor where a house has little to none. So $150 if you "contract it out" is a good deal in my eyes. DIY is a whole different ball park. But I don't lknow how Texas is in the market so I could be wrong on all accounts Peace Tom
