knightfly4 June 2007#4
Sabines are not coefficients, they are absolutes - coefficients assume a RATIO. A one square meter hole would still absorb everything that hits it - a one square meter concrete wall 10 meters thick would still REFLECT everything that hits it. Having nine square meters of concrete and one square meter of "black hole" would = an absorption coefficient of 0.10, as would having a 1 square FOOT hole surrounded by 9 square FEET of concrete. Eric's comments are correct as usual - but be careful not to confuse coefficients of absorption with absolute units... Steve

We use the notion A for equivalent absorption. Now the fun part is that in the ASTM standards our ISO notions are integrated, since the official acoustic US language is SI and metric.
