gullfo16 December 2010#28
as a starting point - speakers set 70% of the room width apart and forming a 60° sided triangle with the seating position located 38% of the room length from the front wall. there are a few ideas about speaker placement RE the front wall - either put as close the front wall as possible (and getting a bass boost many times), or far enough away (> 6') to avoid interference. the sides are typically handed via your side wall / first reflection point absorption. finally, the cloud helps eliminate the overhead reflections and in small rooms, helps to solve the ceiling-floor modal bits.

Soundman2020 wrote:
Mineral wool is fine. So is ordinary pink fluffy fiberglass. But density is important: Look for something in the range of 48 kg/m3, roughly (that's for mineral wool: a bit lower lower density is better for fiberglass). Styrofoam and similar closed-cell insulation products are useless for this: it has to be open cell. - Stuart -
