I've posted in the Studio Construction a summary of what I plan to do. Here it is... http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=10475 I'm also planning on building a control room as well. Attached is my first sketchup attempt of the floor plan. If you look at the area where the oil tank is I am avoiding that area because on only seldom occasion some water gets in there when it rains really hard. I've lived in the house for 8 years and this is the only damp spot. All other areas stay nice and dry. The reason for the small water problem is when the gutter overflows from clogging water spills close to the foundation and creeps in around my sewer and water lines. I am currently alleviating the problem by regrading and getting a bigger drain. Also the span of the ceiling is much longer between the Steel beam and foundation on that side of my basement. I chose the tracking/live room location since it is furthest from my neighbors and has no windows, very little wiring and piping. The exchange chamber listed in Rod's book on page 133 would be located near the windows. If you followed the link above you will see I started beefing up the ceiling with bb|sheetrock|gg|sheetrock. The section above the possible tracking/live room is done. I'll be posting different variations of my floor plan soon. If anyone has any suggestions while I'm in the process feel free to comment.
