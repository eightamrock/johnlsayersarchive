Welcome! Your measurements are either not calibrated properly, or else you did in fact capture them with your speakers way too loud. As you can see, you're peaking around 106dB! You should be around 86dB.
Image not preserved: SPL.png
Also, it appears that something went wrong because your left and right measurements are identical. That's an impossibility. Before you start worrying about your frequency response, you need to get your time domain under control. You are averaging about 150ms which seems way too short for the size of your control room. Regarding the dip your post is about, it might be due to poor modal distribution which is ultimately caused by the dimensions of your room. When you built the room did you check it against the R. Walker BBC 1996 guidelines? What are your room dimensions? Also, did you take REW measurements before and after each acoustic treatment device was swapped out/installed? This is critical to determine whether or not the devices helped or not. Personally I don't think having that diffuser overhead is a good idea. Also, there seems to be some things in your room that could be causing reflections such the screen to your left or possibly your console. I can see in your impulse response that you have a strong reflection happening at 5.28ms. I hope that helps! Greg
