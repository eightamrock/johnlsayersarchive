camistan7 May 2007#5
Nice work/pics lbc! On the pic with the Audio cables...what kind of conduit is that you're using? I've never seen conduit connectors like that. Thanx!
Lbc7 May 2007#6
Camistan, Thanks the conduit is standard pvc electrical conduit. I used ,I think 2" although it may be 1.5", an LB (a 90 degree elbow with a removable back for cable pulling) with some short peices of conduit attached to allow it to protrude through the wall. I plan on sealing the end of the conduitand stuffing it with insulation. I am thinking of using the electrical clay they use in outdoor conduit where the service cable comes out and in to your electrical meter, for example. Or using the expanding spray foam. then, after the drywall is up i am going to place a piece of wood trim around the condiuts with acoustic caulk. If any one has other ideas please let me know.

britune21 April 2010#16
Lbc- what are you doing for (HVAC) ductwork? Did you do anything to keep sound from escaping through your electrical outlets? Or is sound isolation not a concern?
Lbc21 April 2010#17
The Hvac Ducting is all the soft round duct type over sized then snaked so there is no direct "line of site(sound)" between the registers or the main duct. the electrical outlets are all wrapped with quietrock putty and the edges where they meep the wallboard will be caulked with acoustical caulk. then for added measure I may add the foam inserts used for thermal sealing however I don't know how much difference they will make. My main focus is on acoustic music/ singer songwriter stuff.
