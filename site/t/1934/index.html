brookster11 July 2004#1
After months prepping a design for the building I was supposed to be moving in to, and after all the helpful replies and tips I got from you all, I've been offered a deal to god to be true at another location. The building is near completion, but they want to know how 'I' would like the control room finished. Pic 3 is the existing layout with the window facing into the live room. They wish to put a diagonal wall in the bottom right hand corner to create symmetry. Their initial idea was to start the diagonal from the centre of the window, however I didn't think this was the best solution as it creates an off centred CR. My first suggestion was that if they wished to do this then the area from the front wall door to the left wall should be deadened entirely. However would it be better to use the space in its entirety i.e. offsetting the diagonal to the true centre of the room (pic 2), or to keep their initial scheme and build a soild wall at 90 degrees with the front wall where my initial 'dead' area would have been (pic 1) Additionally would it be a good idea for these to be false walls (RW3). Last question is if I want to put a false ceiling in, with hangers above, is there a preferred angle of slope and would a wood floor be better? As always your help and input is greatly appreciated! The room measurements are 7.15m x 5.04m. The height decreases to 2.81m on the left hand side. Any alterations to the right hand side wall are not possible as the owner has collected a number of autographs on the wall.
