knightfly14 March 2005#5
Can you do a more accurate drawing of what's really there, showing locations of speakers, your head, and all acoustic treatments so far? Remember, what you would LIKE it to be isn't what affects your sound, it's what it REALLY is... Steve BTW, if you have flutter echo then you need to kill it. ANY pair of parallel surfaces will cause flutter, so this needs to be "broken up", either through adding non-parallel surfaces on ONE of the two parallel surfaces, or by absorption, or a combination.
