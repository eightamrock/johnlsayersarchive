John Sayers27 June 2004#2
Well - your current plans are just speculative - I'd wait till you've got a real area to work with. As far as choosing features I'd look at the following 1) 9-10 ft ceilings in the basement. 2) as few support beams as possible. This would involve additional cost in steel I-Beams instead of the standard timber bearers. They would allow for greater spans without upright supports. Much easier to design around. 3) If it's an underground basement make sure there is adequate provision made for water seepage and walls are properly sealed - you haven't said where you are but it's worth considering. 4) Keep your utilities in one area and any ducting to upstairs should run along the walls instead of crossing the ceiling spaces. an additional consideration could be whether to build in a basement or an additional building out back. hope this helps cheers john
