jbassino1 June 2009#2
Hi there, First, seal your windows with brickwall, the same one you have on your walls. If you don't need them in the first place or they will be covered with the framing anyway, then just seal them with brickwall, so you equal the mass of your walls. Second, it seems that you are missunderstanding concepts and practice here. If your existing walls are leaf 1 , then you dont need to build a framing for these walls !!! They are your leaf 1 ! You just need to build a second framing, decoupled (means, not touching ANYTHING but the floor) that will become your second leaf. I dont want to be harsh, but you need a lot of reading before starting your build. Asking how to attach frames to your floor shouldnt be a question for a guy that plans to build a recording studio by himself . If you don't know what you are doing, then just hire someone experience to do it for yourself. Its like asking, I bought a guitar, now, where do I hit it to make it sound?
