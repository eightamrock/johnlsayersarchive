adrianolsen8514 March 2011#3
Okay ~ CEILING So far I have planned (and started the construction of) acoustic hanger bass traps for both the rear of the ceiling and flanking the blue colored panels already in place of the mix position. The panels will be wrapped in burlap with lights above to maintain a bigness to the room that people seem to like. The goal here is to balance out the bass response of the room. The planned dimensions of the hangers would be half sheets (triangular) of 4x8s with full 4x8s in the back of the room. The hangers would start above the first (eastern) AC vent and continue until the top of the book case. FRONTWALL In addition to the hangers, I want to readdress our front wall. Right now it's basically just a wall of 2" glass with huge rolls of light john mansville insulation back behind it in the corners. I'm thinking we need to redo the speaker stands and actually soffit mount them and then add much more dense amounts of 703 or rockwool (we have a good bit unused from our old live room) in the vertical and horizontal corners as well as the backwall? Maybe hangers here too? Any ideas would be greatly appreciated! SIDEWALLS On the front side walls (where the 'abfusers' are) I was thinking of angling the walls from around where the outside corners of the mains are and having them die into the sidewalls for a more traditional control room shape. this 'false wall' would be two triangular slot resonators that would be symetrical until where the window starts. The rest of the side walls kind of have me miffed and any design ideas here would be great. I suppose I could move the abfusers to the back of the room? BACKWALL The plan here is to build 3 QRDs in replace of the rectangular book shelfs on top of the window, door, and flat piece of wood subbing for a window on the North side of the backwall using 3/4" plywood OTHER I have some material to build 4 17" tube traps (2" glass) for under the nearfield monitor stand (directly behind the console). OK! I think that is more or less where I am right now. I hope this makes sense and PLEASE bring up any holes you see in my logic or things I might be totally missing. Best and many thanks in advance - Adrian
