Eric_Desart24 June 2006#3
I wonder about this. 1) It's still unclear how corner absorption, in this case SSC (Studiotips Super Chunks, not just solid fill) works. 2) It's unclear how the hanger principle really works. Hence it's more a question of feeling. At a first glance I see little room to improve on the Super Chunks than playing with density. One can make it partly membrane traps, but the question then is: is the enhanced resonant peak positive or negative? This idea assumes that sound waves incidence only/mainly happens in the horizontal direction (for vertical trap). A lot of modes will show velocity in the vertical direction roughly parrallel to the walls. Hence you disrupt this partly. Is that good? Bad? I don't know. Does this create overdamped 1/4 wave silencers? If so, does that work? Does this show some additional resonance absorption behavior? At the expense of, or complementary to the velocity resistance principle? I've compared and tested the hanger principle versus thick wool at a cavity, and the hanger principle did not show any advantage (I don't want to start a discussion about this, nor will I share this data, hence one can ignore this if wanted. I only mention this in function of this corner absorber design). The only way to know is making comparative measurements in very controlled circumstances. For corner absorption in itself are still a lot of question marks to be solved. My gut feeling, which can be wrong: I've question marks in the possibility to improve on the basic chunk principle, where I find linearity a defining important parameter. Also the Super Chunks, while more masked, seem to exhibit some resonance like behavior. The ones completely lacking this seem the Foam by Mail Copycat LENRDs (and maybe comparable stuff), because of their extreme low gasflow resistance. And the LENRD measurements in Rod's book I can't explain, but nothing is mentioned there in function of how and where measured. It gives me the feel that they either are measured in a smaller room, or that some kind of small cavity was still behind them, or ........, but I admit this is gambling.
