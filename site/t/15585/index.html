gullfo7 February 2011#2
you might consider swapping the cabinet and the bed and move the glass cabinet further away. stack the corners of the room and treat first reflection points, consider a cloud over the mix desk. consider some free standing absorbers behind your mix position to reduce some of the reflections from the back of the room but also treat the corners in the back, plus consider ceiling-wall "corners" as a place for additional absorption.
