Has anyone experimented with systems using pneumatic isolation as opposed to the use of neoprene. knightfly1 February 2006#2
I wouldn't think air would be that great - no damping to speak of, so probably similar problems as with neoprene/EPDM (needing extra damping in other forms in order to not cause ringing) - Air itself is pretty stiff; pressurising it would increase that stiffness, decreasing the decoupling. Think I'd stick with sylomer, or an isolated slab... Steve
AVare1 February 2006#3
Air works as the spring in an MSM ssytem. The advantage of air is when very low system resonant frequencies are required, it is an economically viable method. Andre

I thought it was a fun idea though.
