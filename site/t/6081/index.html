knightfly30 April 2006#2
You may need carpet of some kind under the drums themselves (more for avoiding movement and rattles) but I'd be really careful doing the entire floor; absorption of carpet only affects high mids/highs, so will unbalance the sound in the booth. Better to use MW batts across corners and hang a cloud, so mics don't see reflections from the ceiling. Also, the higher you mount your cymbals the more likelihood of getting comb filtering from overhead mics, although the cloud will make a big diff. for this... Steve
