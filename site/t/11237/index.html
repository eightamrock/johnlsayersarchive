Building a vocal booth -step by step

Started by g-slam on 21 September 2008. 17 replies, 2008–2011. In the Library under Walls, floors and ceilings.

g-slam21 September 2008#1
After lots of questions here and in other forums, I want to give my ideas for a way to build a good recording booth. We build one in our studio, but in rectangular dimensions, I guess this one here would be better, cause it has no parallel walls. With the double gypsum on each side (2,5cm) and a glaswool-thickness of 40mm you reach an absorption of arund 50dB. I want to start with the build of the room as itself, the acoustical treatment will follow later. Feel free to ask for the skp-file, its not perfect, but I think a good start.... P.S. please excuse my bad english.... First thing is to build the floor. We did it from a wooden frame, which lies on a 1cm Sylomer.

g-slam21 September 2008#2
On this Sylomer-Basic we build the floor construction, a frame from wood. Between the spaces we put Rockwool or similar absorption material.
Image not preserved: 2_Vocal Booth 1 - Bodengerüst.jpg
g-slam21 September 2008#3
Next thing is a wooden floor (dont know the word). Its just a 3cm thick panel of wood, maybe MDF or something else. When finished, we have a stable floor, which is "swinging" on the Sylomer. On this floor we will build all following walls, door and ceiling. So we will get a room in a room.
Image not preserved: 3_Vocal Booth 1 - Bodengerüst+Platte.jpg
g-slam21 September 2008#4
4th step ist to build the walls. We used an iron/gypsum construction, double layer gypsum each side with insulation between. We found out, that its the best way to build the backsid of one wall (the one side which faces the wall) on the floor, and then raise it up into vertical, otherwise you cant mount it. (only if you are very skinny.....) If you mout the first layer you should fill the spaces between each panel with gypsum before you mount the second layer to prevent sound leaks. Finish one layer of the whole cabin, before you mount the second layer of gypsum. Fulfill all gaps with gypsum. Also a band from fiberglass prevents fissures (right translation?).
g-slam21 September 2008#5
NExt thing is to build a stable frame for the door. We used a glass door for balkony, so you can watch through it. Watch the grade of absorption of the door, dont need to be more than 50dB, because oh the walls. You can fill the gaps with a special door mountig foam, I dont know the name.
