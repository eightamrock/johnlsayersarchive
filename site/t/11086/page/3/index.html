Alex, I don't get it that 1/2 meter either. Your frame could be 10cm. The airspace can be 2,5cm. 2 layers of 12,5=2,5 cm of drywall. Total: 2,5+10+5,2=15cm. You have 2 walls facing each other so another add that and you'll end up with 2x15=30cm. If you feel that's too much, you can also make your frame less thick. Say 7cm which will save 6cm in total which gives you 24cm of space for framing. The total airspace will be 2,5+7=10,5cm. Stag.studs are for a single frame wall. Look at the pictures in Rod's book. Are you only gonna use frames to beef up existing walls or are you also gonna make an extra wall (for separating Conrol and Live rooms for example) ? In that case, that extra (new build) wall should either be single or double frame. In case of a single frame you could stagger the studs. But, as Rod wrote, moneywise it don't make a big difference. A single wall can be a bit smaller in the end, but also weaker than a double wall. Keep on asking!
