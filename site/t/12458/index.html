Soundman20204 May 2009#4
Gluing two pieces together will increase the total mass, but it will change the stiffness. the way I understand it, MLV works in this application because it is limp mass. If you glue two pieces together, it won't be limp any more. Or rather, it will be much less limp. It will be more rigid, stiffer, and will therefore have different acoustic properties. I'm not saying that those properties will be bad: they will just be different. It probably won't work the way you expect it to work, and you'll be entirely on your own as far as getting any technical specs on those acoustic properties. I'm not aware of much independently published acoustic data on MLV anyway, and certainly nothing at all on the properties of "two MLV sheets glued together"! Why do you need to increase the mass? What effect do you predict that will have? - Stuart -
