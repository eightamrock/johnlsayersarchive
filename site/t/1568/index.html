dymaxian26 April 2004#3
If you have 1/2" brick veneer, then chances are it's some kind of prefabricated material that is meant to look like brick. Any STC ratings you find for brick aren't going to apply to that- when I think of brick veneer, it means a 4" thick course of brick that is non-structural. The veneer part doesn't mean that it's thinner... But anyway, the manufacturer might have STC ratings. If you can figure out who made it, ask them about it. Kase www.minemusic.net
