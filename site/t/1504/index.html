jazzmania10 August 2004#5
Steve, I believe there would be a distinct advantage in modifying the design of this speaker stand in such a way that the decoupling layer (in this case a mouse pad) gets moved closer to the bottom of the stand, for a very important reason: whatever you can do to keep the speaker from moving is good, so tying it down to the mass of the stand is quite desirable (as long as the wood you're constructing this from doesn't resonate. Concrete blocks filled with sand could do the trick, or even a column made from four 2x6 boards, again, filled with sand.) If your wooden stand as shown here doesn't resonate, then I would add another 2x12 to this assembly in place of the existing base. Then comes the decoupling layer. And finally, your existing base with spiked feet. Lee
