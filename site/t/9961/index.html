Hi. This is my first post here, but I've been reading a bunch of posts over the past week or two, and must say I really love this forum. I'm starting to design a studio in the garage of a place I bought recently, and will move into in a couple of weeks. I'm afraid I don't have the ceiling height yet (will measure and post as soon as I move in), but the floor measurements are as follows: L: 6,46 meters / 21,2 feet W: 4,78 meters / 15,7 feet The ceiling is probably a little bit higher than some garages, but not terribly high. Venturing a guess I'd say approximately 10 feet / 3 meters. But I can't say for sure until I measure it for myself. What I want to do is build a really solid, soundproof control room, and simply treat the rest of the space, but leave it a little raw. I am designing this primarily for recording my own music, and I do acoustic music with a lot of edge, so I don't need a lot of isolation for the live room, and I'd like to preserve as much of the room sound as I can without it compromising the recording quality. That being said I'll probably want to build a tiny vocal booth, should I need them. For the rest of my acoustic needs I'll improvise with treatment until I'm satisfied. I'll also construct some dividers to arrange around drum kits etc. when I need those. Anyway, this is all speculation, and I really don't have any experience with this, so I was wondering if any of you fine people had any tips for me on how to make the most of my little studio space, how to make my control room the best it can be and any other little advice you might be able to offer. I'm afraid my skills with Sketchup and all those drawing programs is very limited, so I can't post any pics. Sorry about that. Anyway, thank you so much. I look forward to visiting this forum a lot in the future.
