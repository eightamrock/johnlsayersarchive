gullfo27 January 2011#8
the design looks good. make the speaker boxes using 1" MDF if possible - mass. leave enough space in the box to add something to decouple the speaker from the box. if powered, leave venting on the back of the box - top and bottom. leave the box behind the front panel and allow the speaker to protrude to be flush - then seal around the speaker.

gullfo21 February 2011#16
the important part is that the speaker is kept decoupled from the box. the balance is to keep the speaker immobile and yet floating enough that vibrations are not passed directly to the front panel. if you couple the box to the front frame and panel, then you need to pay particular attention to decoupling the speaker from the box. (the first drawing) or if you have the option or want to enlarge the trapping space, then supporting the box/speaker separately can help (ala Barefoot's soffit design) or my second drawing. if you check out Genelec, they recommend a concrete soffit mounting with rubber isolators and sandbags for damping and isolations. you design as shown is fine but you will want to use something soft but firm (sylomer, sorbathane, agglomerated foam, etc) to decouple the speaker.
