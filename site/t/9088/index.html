kendale9 July 2007#2
Aloha and welcome to the forum, Congrats on your studio build. :D Here are a few thoughts to consider, perhaps. You may want to configure your control room geometry more like this - left/right side walls @ 12 degrees, soffit/speakers @ 30 degrees and rear corner bass traps @ 45 degrees. In addition, an overhead cloud or two will help treat the floor/ceiling reflections as well as some rear wall wall absorption. Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
