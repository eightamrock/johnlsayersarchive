gullfo28 December 2010#6
ok, so there is another business directly below your space which would be adverse to having significant (or any?) drum sounds coming through? is the (your) floor concrete or framed? if it's concrete then it should be feasible, if its framed, it will probably be significantly harder. from a heating/cooling perspective, you're probably going with a separate unit anyways so it would probably just make sense to find out if you can cut out and cap the baseboard plumbing (most times no) or turn it off (but then it may consume some space).

i'd get rid of the half wall as shown in my drawing and use some slider doors to separate the rooms and provide visibility. the floor is going to be tough because a drum kit is massively loud and it's likely your existing structure wouldn't handle that. of course if could be very useful to make some measurements with a sound level meter to find out how much isolation you actually need.
