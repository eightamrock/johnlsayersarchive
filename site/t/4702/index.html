New practice studio up stairs! 8) I want to put 10k US$ into recording and production for a small home computer based set up. At this point I'm trying to find the wall plate, that will connect the studio to the control desk. ProCo might be my best bet, waiting on bids. Please any other suggestions? The pic below show's me at the far end of the new 60' room. The pic was taken near where the first glass wall of the studio will be. Unfortunately, the studio only gets 12’ of 60’ making it 12 x 25’, with the peak at the center around 7’6” when the floating ceiling is in. The floor will also be floated. I will stay with cellulose blown in insulation, at a R48. 5/8’s firerock on walls and ceiling. Walnut hardwood floor in the studio. The glu-laminated beams on each side of pic are 4 x 12 x 25', the re-engineering of the roof has been the most challenging so far. I built a hoist rail system into the roof above my head to move the 13 beams about. This site ROCKS! Fred’e
