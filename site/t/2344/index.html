David, I too was a bit miffed by Jack's tone, although in private emails it appeared to me that it was NOT intentional - the "ball dropping" in this case is all my fault, been too busy to eat half the time since then. I still have his private email and hope to resume this talk sometime soon, but it probably won't be immediate; I'm still vainly hoping to accomplish a few things on outdoor projects before we run out of good weather, and starting a joint room treatment project with probably 2 or 3 different companies' products, in addition to all the other stuff which occasionally lets me get more than 4 hours sleep... So far, I've yet to have a member come back and say my suggestions didn't work; so, until/if I can get Jack to 'splain hisself, I'm sticking to my guns on what works. In my opinion, you CAN'T ignore the container, especially in limited space. I've helped people build economical free-standing rooms who've reported that they couldn't hear drums from 10 feet away; the rooms have always needed treatment inside to actually sound good, but that's a given. Point is, I tend not to argue with success, and mine's been NOT to ignore ANYTHING I'm aware of. Each 23 dB of isolation is harder than the previous one - but by total sealing, and beefing up leaves and widening air/insulation gaps, you can get a long ways toward silence - which is definitely golden, when dealing with neighbors who may be looking for a reason to complain anyway - after all, you're living YOUR dream, and they may be jealous... Steve
