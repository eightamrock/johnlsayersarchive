sharward23 July 2006#9
While waiting for help, perhaps you can study the many, many, many examples of projects here on the site and learn from them as much as you can. You'll then feel more confident to post an updated diagram with ideas of how you think you'd like to proceed. Expect that it will be criticized -- that's part of the process and you'll learn from it. Ultimately your plan will continue to be refined and you'll eventually have something you can consider "final." Basically, you have to be willing to get your hands dirty and do some heavy lifting -- not physically, but mentally. Well, physically too. ;-) Most of the members and moderators here are busy with their own projects but are happy to help others help themselves. If you aren't helping yourself, then you can't expect much in the way of help from others. --Keith :mrgreen:

John Sayers6 August 2006#24
here you go. If you load the pic into sketchup you can resize the pic to scale and get all the measurements. That's why I made it a .jpg as sketchup won't load .gif. what you do is you set your units to metric, like the drawing in model info. Then get the measuring tool and click on the end of the 9.500m dimension and drag to the other end and click on it. In the VCB window on the lower right you will see a length, could be any number, type 9.5m into the box and click enter. It will ask you if you want to resize the image, you say yup and it will resize the image to scale so all the other points can be measured in scale. cheers john
