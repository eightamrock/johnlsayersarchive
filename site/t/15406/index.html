xSpace25 December 2010#5
"if I build a room-in-a-room with a staggered stud wall 10 feet high, will the 8 foot walls count as an additional two leaves? " No sir, it will count as an additional coupled leaf. Working with as little information as you have provided, and based on my own experience, let me press on. I would build the existing stud wall all the way to the 12 foot height/roof. I would also remove the sheetrock on the interior of the room side of this existing wall. Then add additional mass to the outer/hall way side of this existing framed wall that we just built up to the 12 foot height ensuring that typical wall insulation was installed in the stud bays.. Then I would use a single framed (not staggered) stud wall with mass installed on the interior side to develop a proper m/S/m assembly.
