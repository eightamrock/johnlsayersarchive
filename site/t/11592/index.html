Hi I am new to the forum but have spent the last few days reading through a lot of the material here, it’s fantastic. I am in the planning stage of a project in which I am attempting to acquire funding for a mobile studio, accessible to rural communities, schools, and deprived areas of larger cities. The plan so far is to buy and the convert a 7.5 tonne van of which the internal dimensions would be 5.80meters (20feet) by 2.40 meters (8 feet) by 2.40 meters (8feet). I currently work in two small rooms both 2.3meters by 2.4meters. One is used as my control room and the second as a small live room and this is the set up I am hoping to be able to reproduce but in the back of the van. I have seen both the studio in a container and the click track studio threads and feel rather encouraged by similarities. I am here to ask for a little input in to my further development of my idea. Any comments are very appreciated.
