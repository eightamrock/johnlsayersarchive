John Sayers15 May 2005#6
Chupy - if you are stuck using the left room for control room and the right room for drums etc what about the main room?? I'd treat the left room for mixing - i.e. lower the reverbtime and tighten it up. then I'd build a series of moveable panels, on wheels, that have heavy absorption surfaces on one side and reflective, diffusive treatment on the other. Then you could wheel them into the right room if you want to record in there, or you could set them up in the big room and record in there. OR you could set up the mixing in the main room, using moveable panels, and set up the other two rooms for recording. cheers john
