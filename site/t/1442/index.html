The one on the right will give you plenty of early reflection problems and flutter echo, unless that flat spot is absorbed fully - the one on the left, only slightly and possibly none, depending on accuracy of the drawings. However, the left one may cause more reflections off the desk depending on the angle your mixer surface presents - I'd still go with the left one, and put a cloud under it anyway... Steve
