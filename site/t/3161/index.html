Hello All, Well I ended up constructing 18 panel absorbers (14 two by four footers and 4 two by eight footers). I placed them on all of the walls, corners, and on the ceiling. The sound of the room is SOOO much greater. In fact, I am getting the best drum kit sound by simply using one mic about 5 feet away and 5 feet up. Thanks for all of your helpful advice, Caz www.pascalgoespop.com
