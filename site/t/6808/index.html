gullfo1 September 2006#3
you might want to add a second window in on the inside of each window to allow natural light in, and maybe a load-in (double width?) door where the main garage door was/is - i.e. make the "outside" (top) room the studio and the "inside" (bottom) room the control room. this would also provide a second escape route in case of fire. I'm guessing it all depends on the amount of isolation you need... also consider how you will ventilate heat/cool/fresh air.
