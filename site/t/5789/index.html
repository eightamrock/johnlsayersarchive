Hey guys....my wife liked the work I did in my studio so much, she has "hired" me to do our Kitchen! :? Not a bad job really, just putting in a ceiling. Now I know this isn't a studio build (please don't shoot me here), but maybe someone would have to do this in a studio? Anyway, just a quick question. One part of my kitchen, a 12'x12' area needs to have the ceiling lowered. I want to go with a simple 1/2" gypsum skin here. No sound issues, etc. The beams from the floor above are 2 x 10's that span 12' long and are 16" on center. Here is my dilemma, I am wondering if any of you know the best way to drop the framing down and do the 1/2" gypsum. I was contemplating attaching 2x8 beams to the existing 2x10's. Not sure if this would be needed and I AM sure it would be heavy. Although these beams do look like they are strong enough to hold up a car. The other option is to do 2x4 framing. The ceiling only needs to drop 8" from the bottom of the existing beams. Anyone have any links or ideas on methods to do this? I will post a pic or two and a sketchup if further explanation is needed. Thanks for any ideas you have.
sharward8 March 2006#2
I would think you'd want to do 5/8" on the ceiling so it wouldn't sag or be wavy. How about using hat channel, spaced from the existing joists with two to four blocks of 2x4? You could run the hat channel perpendicular to the joists. That would be strong and relatively lightweight.
