Sound-Weavers.com24 February 2009#8
FYI, we've been using mini-split ductless systems by Hitachi 1.5 horse power. We have measured the air handler (indoor) unit's noise as going from 36 to 40 dB, with the sweep/oscillator on. We use a Radio Shacke sound level meter. Our control room's total noise is 42 to 45 dB (with the computers and other equipment turned on).
