knightfly12 October 2006#4
First thing you want is at least 150-200mm loose insulation batts laid on top of your inner gypsum to damp any panel resonances - as to the rest of it, depends on the overall size of the room, what OTHER treatments you have (or intend), etc - also, is this inner ceiling to be part of your mass-air-mass isolation or is it going to be more of a false ceiling for internal acoustics only?
