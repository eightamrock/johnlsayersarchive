AVare15 November 2005#6
Welcome back so soon.
What is the recommended control room size?
Aargh! What an incredibly vague question! Think about that. Very generally speaking, 1500 ft^3 is the minumum for a control room that can be made to "sound good". The biggest drawback is the very small listening area.
I could always make a small isolation booth for my Mesa Boogie Dual Rectifier 4x12 and Vocals. What would be a good Vocal/Amp booth size be?
You have to define "good" better. I'm certain that is grammatically wrong somehow. :) For an amp boot figure roughly: the size of the biggest amp/speaker combo you want; plus room for a mic stand and mic; plus about 6" per surface for sound absorbent material. A vocal booth is more nebulous as they can be much smaller in acoustic terms than vocalists would be comfortable in.
I think it would be hard to track drums in the same room but maybe not.
Great that you posted your thoughts on drums. People can post and give you the benefit of their experiences both ways. Don't forget that a larger room you can use more/larger goboes to isolate instruments. Keep the thoughts coming! Andre
