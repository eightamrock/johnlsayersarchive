This second pic is looking in the other direction along that same wall. Same procedure as the other side. I am glad that this part is done, I have been dreading it for a while now! You can also see a piece of white PVC pipe that is sticking through the wall on this side. I am sure that this is not the most acoustically correct (soundproof?) device, but I wanted it anyway! Basically it is a straight pipe through the wall from the live room into the iso room. This is to accommodate any weird cables that might creep up from time-to-time. For example... Leslie cables. bassman22 November 2007#98
My understanding is the best TL for a double window system is when the panes are parallel. The sympathetic resonance that can occur with parallel panes is far less of an issue than the basic distance between panes. If the panes are like you have them, then the point at which they are closest is the best TL rating you will get from the window. The best setup is to have the panes as far apart from each other as possible at all points. If you want to angle them, put them both at the same angle (not opposing) to maintain the same distance between. Reflection control on both sides of the glass can still be achieved with a parallel setup. bassman
