Honestly James, looking at those traps, the two front ones - the patchbay and outlet boxes should be concealed a lot because of the desk/console. The two back ones look like the couch will hide the electric boxes. But the tops being basically missing, will look bad. At this point, I would bite the bullet, put down a drop cloth and cut out a new box location for those lights. Then use the old box as a junction box with a flat plate that the bass trap will cover. Extend the wires to the new box, install the new sconce, and button it all back up. Then hey, spackle, sand, paint - and be done with it. Yes, it's more work, it sucks... BUT, when you sit back and look at it, you'll be plenty-glad you did. Plus it will make the studio that much more pro-looking. Just carve out a weekend, get some help and give it hell. It should only be about 2-hour fix. Thoughts?
