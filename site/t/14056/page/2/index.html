xSpace17 February 2010#32
In these times, you can blow your entire budget on the truss replacement. Even modifications with LVL's or beams to open up specific areas could cost large chunks of money. The added benefits are pretty much known of how a larger room is just easier to work with. But it can take a lot to get an existing structure to have an increased room volume. But...it is worth entertaining as an idea if you really want to get the best acoustical climate you can out of this space. Rather than fish for numbers here, and get something that is never going to be concrete...talk to some folks in your area and let them help you with that. I would welcome the opportunity to see this happen for you, but I do not have anything to add that will be positive with this type of rehab.
