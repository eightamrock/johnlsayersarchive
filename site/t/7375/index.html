DreaminDrumBeats28 October 2006#2
being a builder of homes 120 sq ft is only a 10' x 10' room so in my own personal opinion thats a bit on the crampish side to house a full band even if its just a drummer one guitarist and one bassist plus equipment. a 15' x 15' room is 225 sq ft which is more workable to house a full band. so if i was you i would make 4 15x15 rooms for rehearsal rooms. and 425 sq ft for and entire studio IS JUST NOT BIG ENOUGH for 3 live rooms and a control room. a small controll room thats 15 x 17 before splaying your walls is 255 sq ft. i would go for one live room (atleast 625 sq ft), a drum room (atleast 250 to 275 sq ft, gotta think about all the mics youll being using to record drums), a vox/ iso booth (atleast 50 sq ft). that brings your total sq footage (using 275 for drum room) 1205 sq ft for the studio alone now add your 4 rehearsal rooms and theres another 1000 sq ft bringing the total to 2205 sq ft and thats not including your lounge area. i hope this helps give you a better scope of what your wanting to do thats realistic
