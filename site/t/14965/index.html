Good Evening everybody, I did a lot of reading in this forum over the last few month, also read several books about how to plan and build your own recording studio... Let me present myself first. I am a Blues Drummer from Luxembourg Europe am 29 years old and owe a small recording studio. The studio is in a small house wich has 2 floors of ca 35 sqm per floor. On the ground floor I have a nice 20 sqm completely insulated recording booth wich I use since 2004 already, I'm happy with it since it sounds neutral, pretty dry, but not to dry and it is completely sound proof. On the first floor I have my control room, this room isnt insulated at all, so I hear cars passing the airconditioning (wich is also the heater) blowing etc. I dont need a 100% soundproofed room up here but I should be able to hear and mix low instruments in classical music. Most recordings I do are classical music and are recorded live, later I mix them in my control room. Over the last weeks I tried to learn how to use sketchup and came up with a floor plan. The sizes are exact also the wooden structure of the roof is as detailed and precise as I could get it. I tried to fit one of John's designs in but if u enable the roof layer you'll see that I have a problem with the hight. I would be thankfull for any input you guys can give me. About how to optimize this sketchup... Some more details about the control room floor. In the sketchup plan u'll see a hole, these are the stairs coming up. next to the stairs is a podium, I actualy use the space underneath for storage, the way I planed the control room now, I wouldnt be able to acces this space anymore. :-( On the front wall there is a window wich I dont necesarly have to be able to open, its nice though if u have some sun comming in or some fresh air during the breaks. I got a problem with space in my studio at the moment so a storage / machine room would be great. A room where I could put my 24 track 2" Tascam, my small run CD printing machine the drums PA etc... I also dont have any space right now for a small Kitchen ( Fridge, Coffe machine microwave oven and some diches.) I hope I didnt aske to many questions. Soundman202020 October 2010#6
Rotate it 90°! Turn the entire layout so that the speakers are firing down the long axis of the room. That will solve a large number of problem with that current layout. Also, you are talking about the need for isolation upstairs, but what you show is only treatment: you haven't shown any isolation at all. I know you are on an extremely limited budget, but isolation implies mass, so you do need to add at least some mass around you, if you want isolation. After the isolation design is in place, only then can you start thinking about treatment. - Stuart -
