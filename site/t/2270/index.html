knightfly7 September 2004#2
If you're renting, can't do construction, and are making lots of noise, you're pretty much in trouble - I don't know any way of soundproofing anything like that, short of pulling the power plug; sorry, I'd like to help but short of headphones for everyone, it's just not possible to my knowledge. For more detail on what people CAN do for soundproofing, check out the REFERENCE section, mainly the "complete section" one and the "floating floor" one - for serious soundproofing, this is what's needed. No blankets or less than airtight construction will do much at all; as in, hardly noticeable difference... Steve
