xSpace5 November 2009#2
Well, to clarify, a stringer is a framing member for the construction of a staircase or a simple set of stairs. So no you do not need this. What you are wanting to discuss is blocking, the Aussies call it noggins. An 8 feet high wall does not need horizontal blocking as it stands in the US of A and that depends on where you live because it is a faction of the Government...and the infiltration is among us.
