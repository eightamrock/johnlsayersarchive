Hi. Please read the forum rules for posting (click here). You seem to be missing a couple of things! :) Sorry, but none of those is going to isolate a drum kit. Setup #2 would probably actually make things worse for low frequencies, since it is a 3 leaf system, and the GG under the furring is pointless. All three of those are fully coupled, so you are limited by mass law, plus the slight visco-elastic damping of the GG, which frankly is not going to make a huge difference, unless you also decouple.
I don't really want to go down the resilient channel path
Why not? It is your only real option, unless you prefer to go with a full "room-in-a-room" system. Unless you decouple the two sides of your wall, it will not and cannot give you high levels of isolation. There are no ways around the laws of physics through exotic combinations of materials. They all still have to follow the laws of physics, and without decoupling, the laws of physics are just not on your side. At a rough guess, you are getting maybe 30 dB of isolation right now. None of those options is going to give you anything even approaching 40. Take off the OSB on one side, use it to beef up the other side, add resilient channel two layers of 5/8" drywall with GG, and you'd be approaching 50 dB. - Stuart -
