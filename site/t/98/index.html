barefoot20 March 2003#4
They'll probably still be bass heavy, but it's worth a try. I mean, who knows exactly how they voiced that speaker? You can experiment by just making a rudimentary front panel of the soffit out of a sheet of plywood. Roughly position and mount the panel and speaker like you would a soffit and see how it works. The front baffle is the only part of a soffit that affects how the speaker radiates anyway. The rest is just framework and support. If the experimental setup works well then construct the rest of the soffit. If it doesn't, then you haven't wasted too much time or cost. Thomas
