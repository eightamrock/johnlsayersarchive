Yes , it's ok if the walls are not parallel...have you searched around here for studio plans? There's a few in the "studios under construction" section and on the SAE site. have you got this place built and now you want to put brick walls as your internal walls, or are you bricking it up from scratch?? If you have outer shell already, it's probably cheaper to do stud framed walls inside, unless you're a brick layer or can get it done for really cheap . Also you wouldnt get much low end absorption by just sticking the wool on the walls... try and post some plans/drawings cheers
