I remember reading that less dense, fluffier material is better in the wall cavity - Is this correct?
For mineral wool, you want something in the region of 50 kg/m3. But if you are using fiberglass (not mineral wool), then it should be in the range of about 30 kg/m3. Heavier is better for absorbing higher frequencies, lighter is better for lower frequencies. The above are optimal for cavity insulation, not necessarily for other things. - Stuart -
