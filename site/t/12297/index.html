Soundman202016 April 2009#2
My first question would be: why do you need to float your floor? Do you have something extremely noisy around that is going to create problems, or do you think that you will be too noisy for your neighbors to accept? Anyway, I don't think you are going about this the right way, to be honest. I think you should first define the resonant frequency that you are aiming for ( I guess you will be shooting for 10 Hz or something like that?), then figure out the mass of your entire studio (not just the floor), THEN figure out what resilient material / air gap / insulation / other things you will need to use in order to get all of the above to work out. I may be wrong, but that's the way I would approach it. I don't think it makes much sense to first decide on the resilient material that you want to use, then try to design the studio construction to match that. That would be kind of like only looking at the tires in order to decide what car you want to buy... - Stuart -
