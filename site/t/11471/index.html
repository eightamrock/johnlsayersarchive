Help me obtain some isolation!

Started by timpoison on 6 December 2008. 2 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 11471.

timpoison6 December 2008#1
I'm trying to do some recordings in the practice space my band uses. The problem is that other bands practice in the building, and I am afraid I will get some roaring electric guitar bleed in my overhead drum mics. The sound from the other bands isn't too bad normally, but I am still worried. I am using SM81's for the overheads. One of the problems is I don't think I can physically attach anything to the walls of the room. Is there some sort of baffle I can construct to help this? Will hanging blankets over mic stands around the kit do any good? Any ideas? I was thinking of possibly making a sort of collapsable room, 6 Feet wide, 7 feet long and eight feet high, using 2x4's and plywood to make the frame, then filling it with ultratouch insulation, and covering it with another sheet of plywood. I would then hinge the 4 walls together so that when not in use, It could "unfold" and rest against the walls of my practice space. I would hinge the "ceiling" to the rear wall of the booth, so that when I set up the booth, I can flip it up and rest it on top of the opposite wall. I would then treat the inside with Auralex or something. But I would also have to throw a window in the front wall, and I am very unsure about how to soundproof that. I figure this could be done for under 500$, but does anyone think this would actually work/help? Thanks, Tim
