Darm17 November 2007#1
Finally got a chance to get out of my bedroom. I got a two rooms connected to each other. One room is 4.05*4.2 meters, and a tiny room that I am planning on using as a vocal booth is 1.3*2.54 meters. The ceiling is 2.60 meters. All the walls are made of brick. The door between two rooms is 0.80*2 meters, and I am thinking about installing a glass door there. I don't really know what would be the best way to make the booth completely dead (since its so small). The other room is going to be used as a mixing/production room, and I am planning on putting two tables there- one with a computer, controller, preams and other recording gear, and another one behind it with my synths, mpc and turntable. I am renting these rooms, so I can't really build anything. I was thinking about putting an office ceiling, maybe slanted towards the booth, placing broadband absorbers (framed rockwool covered with fabric) and corner bass traps around the room (don't know how many and how big they should be), and placing lots of same framed broadband absobers in the booth, maybe from the floor to the ceiling, maybe cutting a corner with them...
