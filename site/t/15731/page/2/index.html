maybe use the window AC as a cold closet next to the booth so you can use it to cool and exchange air with both. also, the video, it looks like a lot of unsafe construction - heavy walls unsupported at the top plus gaps between the ceiling and the frames which will be lossy since there won't be enough mass there. window and wall AC units are noisy. consider the closet idea... use two small quiet fans to move air into and out of the rooms. leave an access panel to service the AC unit. take off the cover etc and put in a switch to turn it on and off. put a small divider to allow the best flow for the unit. take out the existing window and frame it for proper support and isolation of the AC unit and improve the isolation of the window as well. save the window to put back later. if you skip the divider panel approach, reverse the role of the vents - warm at the top, cool at the bottom.
