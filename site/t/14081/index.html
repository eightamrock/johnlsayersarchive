UPDATE! So here is some pictures and specs of the framing and insulation process. We did have a professional come in and do the framing but we did all the insulation our selves. Because it is in a basement we needed this as sound proof as possible. All the walls have two layers of insulation, the standard pink R-19 and the green roxul acoustic insulation. The ceiling has these two layers and a layer of sound board (a dampening wood). Sheet rock is next!!

milesemilo21 February 2010#4
The sheet rock stage finally began this week. We decided to use QuietRock sheet rock around the entire studio, ceiling and walls. It is the same thickness as normal 1/2" sheets but with a dead space in between. We also used spacers on the wall separating the live room and control room for an added 1/4" of dead space. We had rounded corners put on all the edges for a classier look as well as helping with acoustics a little bit. The walls are up and the cracks are filled, we are just waiting for everything to dry so we can begin spraying the texture and prepare for paint!
