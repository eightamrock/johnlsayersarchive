First, welcome to the board - we too are a non-profit organization :cry: - One thing I would strongly suggest - do whatever you can to get away from building an 8' cube - I doubt if there's a way to control all the peakiness you'll run into with all three dimensions the same - there will be serious peaks at 70, 140, 210, and 280 hZ, also 350 hZ - the 280 (and possibly 350 hZ) modes will make your drums sound like crap, since the modal fundamental of a typical kick drum is around 300 hZ - If you can possibly change the interior dimensions of your drum booth to 8 x 9 x 11 feet, it would help your sound tremendously. Now, the downside - in order to successfully contain drums you will need a fully floated room-in-room construction as you said, and there can be no mistakes in construction - if you're using union carpenters, they are likely familiar with steel stud construction; if so, you can build double steel frame walls, putting two layers of 5/8 wallboard on the outside of the outer frame, and the inside of the inner one - then, using the proper weight insulation between the wall surfaces will keep your isolation optimised - this has been tested and insulation between 2 and 3 PCF density is best. NOT foam insulation; because it's closed cell it doesn't work for sound. The floor will need to be floated, check out the REFERENCE materials in the Construction forum - there's a long thread there on floating floors, and a few hundred other posts around as well.
