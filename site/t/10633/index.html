rod gervais23 May 2008#12
AVare wrote:
There is an air gap between the brick and the sheathing. Together with the inner leaf you are making a triple leaf system. As I wrote in my previous post, if you want extra board, out it in the inside leaf. On the middle leaf it does nothing useful acoustically.
Andre, I respectfully have to disagree with you here. A brick veneer tied solidly to the wall - even with an air space - is going to end up as a single leaf. There is always a certain amount of mortar that gets in between the 2 surfaces - which hardens and stops the masonry from being able to move towards the building - and the building ties stop it from being able to move away. So without an independant action - the 2 truly become one leaf. As far as the location of the dens-glas - I would leave it where it is now - and add green glue to the interior. Much more bang for the buck...... Sincerely, Rod
