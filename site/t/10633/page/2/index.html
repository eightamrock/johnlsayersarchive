Gustov Aftermath.... I am blessed!! Compared to a host of other people in this region! It amazes me that once the levee's DIDN"T fail, the media was not interested in Louisiana! Gustov was the worst storm in recorded history in a huge section of the state. We have folks that will be without power for several more weeks, with temps in the mid 90's, and about 80% humidity, not to mention the bugs and pissed off snakes! We had a guy in my unit bit by a water snake 2 nights ago!! The snake had crawled up into the unit, and when he walked by, it bit him! Anyway, I'll post a few pics of my damage. Lots of cleanup work to do, along with roof work, but overall, I was spared the worst. A lady was sleeping during the storm about 2 miles from my house when a tree came through the roof and killed her in her bed....I am blessed!
