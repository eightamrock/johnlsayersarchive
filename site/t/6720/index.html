knightfly29 July 2006#2
Couple of things I need you to do for us first - one is to edit your profile to include a general location; the other (also mentioned in this thread, which is apparently invisible to a lot of people) http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=3231 is to please post your image HERE on this site so a couple of years from now when someone else needs similar help and I want to link this thread, the pix are still available. With that room length, your ears should either be 3'3" from the front wall (behind your speakers) or 5' from it; otherwise you'll be in a 70 hZ (second harmonic of length, axial mode) NULL, not good. I've redone your drawing, maybe something more like this? I think part of your stereo image problem is lack of centering. Of course, you'd need some acoustic treatments as you mentioned; a cloud over the desk, and more absorbents behind the desk and alongside. These could be movable, and stored alongside the desk when you're not mixing (so you can still have the window, assuming you don't need more isolation/window plug)... Steve
