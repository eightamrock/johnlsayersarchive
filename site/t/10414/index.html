I see a lot of people posting pictures of their gobos, traps, etc. So I decided to post a few more pretty pictures for you guys to look at. If you have any ideas for the stands, let me know. My idea for the stands is a trapezoid with a cutout that is as wide as the gobo is deep. They would not be fastened, so that one could take them off and move them to either the broad or the narrow sides for various purposes ( stand them upright for drums, move the stands onto the other side for guitar amps ) This is probably easier to see than read, so make fun of my MS Paint drawing too :)
