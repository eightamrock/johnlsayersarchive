sharward10 August 2007#2
Welcome to the forum. First of all, there's no such thing as "soundproof insulation." Yes, there are insulation products that are marketed for their ability to reduce sound transmission a little, but that's mostly in the speech range, not the pesky low end that plagues studios. I like Thermafiber SAFB and Roxul AFB, which are in this category, but I'm more impressed with their fire resistance as mineral wool than their "soundproofing" abilities. Next, search on the forum for "quietrock" (one word) to see how well (or - hint, hint - not) it is received here. Green Glue is very good but not cheap. You may be better off spending your money more carefully, since you indicate you are on a budget. Which leads me to this: what kind of budget? This is one of a dozen or so questions to which answers have not been yet provided. Please (re)read the "before you post" announcement thread ane follow all of the guidelines in it. There's a ton to learn. :-) --Keith :mrgreen:
