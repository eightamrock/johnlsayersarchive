why would you want the glass sitting 3/4" off the window frame - typical in the industry would only be around 1/4" clearance all around the glass - and it should not be a soft rubber (which is what I picture in my minds eye when people just say rubber) it should be glazing shims (setting blocks) made of PVC, high impact polystyrene, or other hard plastic type material suitable for the work.
