It seemed better to avoid the reflections shooting the short way which is only 6'8" wide and I drew up to the height of the windows I am having a hard time using sketchup. I don't think I'd put the CPU over the heater :idea: also that seat is going to get pretty uncomfortable ;-) otherwise it looks like a workable layout. my main concern would be if you used the monitors while trying to record some acoustic guitars since the wrap around of the bass from the monitors might be a problem. headphones for tracking... have you considered a portable console rig which would enable you to move the equipment in and out of the van? this way if you found some gigs where you wanted to move inside you'd have that option. here's something i was toying with last year - a fold up box for the mixer and some key effects and patching (which connect to the rack units using snakes w/ bulk connectors). and 2 19" racks with DAW, ADATs, etc...

Hi Glenn,
gullfo wrote:
I don't think I'd put the CPU over the heater :idea: also that seat is going to get pretty uncomfortable ;-) otherwise it looks like a workable layout.
I couldn't find a place anywhere else for it. It's so deep compaired to the rest of my rack gear. The heater is forced hot air and I think it's only 1500btu. The heat coming out isn't really hot. I was going to isolate the computer rack from the heat exchange (insulation and air cavity). The heater is also pretty loud. I wouldn't use it while tracking or mixing. Only during ear breaks.
gullfo wrote:
my main concern would be if you used the monitors while trying to record some acoustic guitars since the wrap around of the bass from the monitors might be a problem. headphones for tracking...
Right on. I never use nearfields tracking in the same room with them unless I'm reamping. Always use headphones.

The ceiling has about 2" compressed opencell insulation covered by a thin carpret. I was going to pergo the floor for easy clean up and have some runners to lay down.
