Hi all, I am currently in the planing stage of building my first studio. I am hopeing to have a control room, live room and maybe a small vocal iso booth. The studio will be built in an existing room and I have approx. 6500mm x 3300mm with a 2320mm ceiling (I might be able to sqeeze a bit more out of the ceiling). I would like to stop as much sound getting in and out as possible, the studio is on the second level of a large building so I would have to treat the floor to stop sound traveling up and down. I would like the live room to have alot of character, I don't want a dead room. Suggestions on this would be great. Budget is currently hard to pin down but let's say $5000 - $10000 for fun. This is my first attempt at design and using sketchup so any help would be great. Thanks
