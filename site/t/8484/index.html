I just read a post here recently that got me to thinking about the services and help we recieve on this site. Based on the "tone" of that post and it's eventual outcome & END, it moved me to write THIS post. There are many of us that come on here not having a clue as to how to REALLY construct a studio. We come on here with questions ranging from "what's dywall" to "is the density of the insulation critical to the overall capacity of soundwaves to travel between interior and exterior walls of extreme mass producing a reduction in certain frequencies?"...(huh????) :shock: A few of us just need a *little* help in constructing a studio, but MOST OF US NEED ALOTTA HELP! :wink: When I try to research on here for hours it gets tiresome. Heck, I've even drifted off to sleep in my chair reading. As I think about the time I spend on here at my leisure just trying to do what "I'm doing" I think about the moderators who spend HOURS on here not trying to do for THEM, but for others. Most of us answer questions here and there, but we do it at our leisure and stop possibly after a few responses. The moderators do MANY more on average than most of us will ever do. From my understanding, they do it for FREE. How many of us would actually do this on a consistant basis? Even as far as being cordial and answering the same questions over and over?(Most that are even answered in the reference section) How many of us would have the patience? There are other experts that contribute to this web site (I won't name them for fear of leaving someone out) but in this post I am mainly referring to John, Steve, the moderators & the contributing expert in the Constrction Forum Rod Gervais. Rod, John & Steve...this is especially for you... I could NEVER afford ANY of you I'm sure. I'm just a common guy like most of the rest on here trying to fulfill a dream I have...to build my own studio. Your years of expertise and wealth of knowledge is WAY beyond the ability and knowledge of 99% of the people on here. Without your help a lot of us would still be using "egg cartons". As I said, after I read the post I'm referring to it moved me to write this post (Rod...you know which post it was) I just felt inside, "hey somebody's gotta say it"... So here it is... I hope I speak for most people on here to tell you your help is NOT taken for granted. So to ALL of you moderators, John, Rod & Steve, Thank You...thank you for your time, patience and understanding...thank you for "giving back" to those of us in need...thank you for caring about "our dreams". To all reading this I would ask you to show how you feel if by nothing else than to post your name to say thank-you. I think these guys should know we appreciate them and I believe a long line of names would be just the thing to do...
