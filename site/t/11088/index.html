Hi, I really feel that if your budget is what you say it is; then you should really spend it on other things that you will need for your build. After having finished my first floating wooden deck I had to tare it all up and restart because of the complex nature of the construction. With a limited budget I would focus more on building better walls and then "splurge" on the room treatment. Also worth noting: I am not a professional at this, it is simply my opinion. All the best. /Gash
xSpace13 August 2008#3
I don't think it takes an expert to figure out the complex nature is mass, and you can not achieve that with a wooden deck. Well, some mass, but certainly not enough as you found out :) I have never built a wooden floating floor but I have built many wooden floors that were supported above ground. And they each one made noise, which is typical in a building, that the floor will make impact noise via footfall or bass drums, etc. alexfigueira: If you can move your floor enough to place anything under it, consider yourself lucky. Maybe you can stand it up and use it as another wall frame because it does not have enough mass for you to use as an isolated floated floor.
