Hello! First post on this most awesome website. Just wanted to chime in quick regarding your intentions on acid staining your concrete. If you get some cracks in your floor don't worry about it detracting from the overall look of your acid stained floor. Sometimes cracks can actually enhance the aged marbled look staining provides. Also, if possible, wet cure your new concrete instead of using sprayed on curing compounds. Hopefully they didn't already! Any curing compound applied to your floor will have to be removed before you can stain it.
