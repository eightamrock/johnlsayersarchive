sharward1 July 2007#2
First of all, your friend's proposed woraround is, as you rightfully suspected, bogus... My understanding of the benefits of laminated glass (I hope I get this right, so if I don't, please correct me, folks! :-)) is that it is essentially two sheets of glass stuck together with a sticky sheet of plastic, and it is the sticky sheet that works like magic in that it helps to dampen resonance under the same principles under which QuietRock engineered drywall panels or Green Glue between two sheets of ordinary drywall perform: it's a constrained dampening layer. Perhaps you should shop around more, and also see if maybe using multiple smaller pieces would help reduce the cost significantly. For example, if you plan to have a 24" by 48" window, perhaps you could live with multiple windows next to each other, like roughly a pair of 24" by 24", or trio of 24" by 18". Another advantage of the smaller pieces is that they will be much much much easier to handle. Those suckers are heaaaaaaaavy! :shock: --Keith :mrgreen:

rod gervais2 July 2007#5
It's really quite simple folks. The reason I lay out options - and explain the differences - are for situations just exactly like this..... given the right thicknesses you can acheive essentially the same job with plate as you can with laminated.......... trust me- if you built a wall with 2 layers of 5/8" drywall each side - (even with green glue) and then installed 3/4" and 1" thick plate glass at each face - your wall woud be the weak link........ The idea is for you to figure out your best cost - and then know what you should purchase based on the product. So if your best buy is a piece of 3/4" and 1" annealed - then go for it......... just make sure you take care of all the rest of the window details. BTW - by definition - double strength glass is glass manufactured to toleances of 0.115 and 0.133 inches thick (3 to 3.38mm). 1/8” or B grade glass. So your friend cannot be getting you DS glass 1" thick - becuase they don't make that animal...... Sincerely, Rod
