Hi Folks Hi There!! A bit about me My name is Bruce; I’m 36 years of age and live near Birmingham in the UK. I write using a combination of Pro tool LE/Mbox 2 and Ableton on a PC laptop. I want to produce Cinematic a style of music for TV/Film Games. and also: My band which is Acoustic guitar/ Vocals/ Cajon Drum Box/ piano and Bass Synth I like Synthesis so will most likely be adding some more outboard Synths...
