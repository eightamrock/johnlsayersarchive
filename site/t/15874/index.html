gullfo23 April 2011#2
welcome! what type of roof is there already? concrete? whatever roof is finally used, it's likely you need to have some HVAC to regulate the temperature and humidity. one option to consider is an additional "shed" roof over the isolation roof to shade to and provide some natural convection to keep the temperature down - check with green builders in your area.
Reginiano23 April 2011#3
Hi Gullfo, I think the roof is concrete covered with an azbest shed. I need to remove the azbest anyway (cause it's not healthy). Is the concrete roof an "isolation roof"? or i need the HVAC to get the isolation?
gullfo24 April 2011#4
the concrete roof would be your isolation. the HVAC is the thing that will help prevent excessive temperature changes and humidity plus keep it comfortable and exchange the air frequently.

ideally you want as much ceiling height as you can get for the drums. for a vox booth with a dead ceiling, 2.1m might be OK but probably not too good for drums. then again, if it is all you have to work with, then maximizing the treatments in the space to get it to sound as good as possible is your next step.
