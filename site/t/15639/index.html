gullfo19 February 2011#2
Welcome! If you can put a room within a room sitting on a decoupled membrane floor, that is probably your best option for high isolation. Otherwise, just attaching new framing and drywall to existing walls is not likely to give you much for roughly the same cost as a properly decoupled room. What you want to avoid is any flanking which is why you make the floor decoupled and build your booth on that. Make sure you plan for ventilation, wiring, lights, window, and door sealing.
