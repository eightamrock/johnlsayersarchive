kendale22 January 2009#13
Aloha, Perhaps something like this for starters? In Rod Gervais’ book “Build it Like the Pros,” viewtopic.php?t=5670 he points out both L.W.Sepmeyer’s & M.M. Louden’s formulas most widely used in professional studios worldwide. L.W. Sepmeyer Height Width Length 1.00 1.14 1.39 8’ x 9’1” x 11’1” M.M. Louden Height Width Length 1.00 1.40 1.90 8’ x 11’2” x 15’2” Eric Desart on Louden’s Ratios viewtopic.php?p=64490&highlight=#64490 1.00, 1.35, 1.85 8’ x 10’8” x 14’8” This skp is based on Eric's study of Louden's ratios, which gives a starting point of 8' x 10'8" x 14'8" (H x W x L) Also, by re-orienting your control room this way, you'll have plenty of room for a vocal booth & lounge, as well as what appears to be a "front door" access. Hope this helps, Aloha 8)

drthump7022 January 2009#14
Kendale, your ratios by my calculation are off just a bit 8' x 1.35 = 10.8' which is actually ~10' 9 5/8" and 8' x 1.85 = 14.8' or ~14' 9 5/8". Not sure how much of a difference that 1 5/8" each direction would make if any. Just an observation. Also there is only 14'4" from the wall next to the stairs (front of your control room) to the outer foundation wall (back wall of CR). Once you put in your stud walls, this is going to throw your ratios off even further. Also by the looks of it in order to get from the control room to the tracking rooms you actually have to leave the building and come in through the other door. There doesn't seem to be enough space between the wall of the CR, the post and the laundry room/bathroom. You said you wanted to put a shower in the corner there correct?
