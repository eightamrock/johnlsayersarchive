Lee, there is another way of doing this - it's usually intended for retro-fits - where you don't want to rip down the ceiling below - but you can take the pipe and run it on top of your decking instead of below. This is set into a series of particle board slabs - with chamfered edges -- and then the final assembly is caulked into place. In this manner it becomes a part of the leaf assembly ands doesn't cause the problem with the 3rd leaf. here's a link with the idea shown: http://www.wirsbo.ca/default.php?id=31 Here's another manufacturer with a slightly different approach - http://www.warmzone.com/hydronic-radiant-floor-heat.asp In either case - it's more heat - faster - with less energy loss than the under floor installation. BTW - I've used this in the construction of world class studios (although then it was slab-sets). Sincerely, Rod
