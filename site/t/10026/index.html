CidyZoo15 January 2008#5
The US product I found is this: SC-175 Draft & Acoustical Sound Sealant from OSI Sealants. Description: SC-175™ Acoustical Sound Sealant is a water based sealant designed to reduce sound transmissions in walls and partition systems. Primary function is to achieve and maintain the specific STC (Sound Transmission Class) value of the system design. VOC compliant. Features: UL tested and classified ,Non-flammable formulation ,Easy application and clean up ,Will not harden crack or separate ,Non-staining and non-migrating ,High degree of adhesive and cohesive strength ,Best Uses ,Pro-Series® SC-175™ was developed primarily for commercial construction utilizing light weight cavity walls and floor systems. The sealant is used for exposed and unexposed applications at perimeter joints floor and ceiling runners (either wood or metal) cut-outs in gypsum board veneer plaster systems and other areas where a sound rated assembly is required. Comes in 29oz Tubes. I think the most important fact here is that it doesn't EVER harden (which almost all other types of caulk will eventually do), so it maintains an airtight seal when surfaces eventually shift slightly over time. The use of "backer rod" (thin flexible foam "rope") is important in giving the caulk a rear surface to hold on to, in addition to the two surfaces you are joining. You don't want to build air/sound tight walls and them have leaks appear in the form of tiny through cracks after a year. This is only from what I understand. Some one please correct me if I am wrong. ~ Bill
