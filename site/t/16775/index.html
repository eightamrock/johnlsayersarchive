Soundman202023 August 2011#6
thinking if I can do some things like build one wall at a time
Well, you CAN build one wall at a time, but it will be really hard to keep some of the walls from falling over until the others are in place! They support each other. Also, you will have zero isolation until all six sides of the room are in place, so it will be frustrating for a long time to have a lot of completed work around you with no noticeable benefit. It might just be better to save your money then do it all at once, when you have enough saved.
