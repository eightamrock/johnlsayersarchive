I've been building my studio for 6 years now this month. I've recently kicked construction into high gear because I want to get done and get on with my life, I'm a musician not a construction worker! :) I took last week off work to build and I had hoped I would finish the major construction by the time I went back to work, but it didn't happen. I did get a ton done though, and in my standard fashion I have lots of pictures of the process. I posted over at homerecording.com http://homerecording.com/bbs/showthread.php?t=192327 some pictures of the latest in the construction. Check it out. Post any replies there or here, whatever works. Once I'm done with the studio I'll be organizing all the pictures we've taken over the years and organizing them. I'll post all the high points at that time. I feel my experience can be helpful to people who are considering building a studio, or someone who's in the process of building one. My story is also a cautionary tale. :)
