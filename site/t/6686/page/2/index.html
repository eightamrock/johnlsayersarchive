sharward18 February 2007#37
Not sure what you mean by "plastic," but you definitely don't want to wrap your insulation in plastic sheting or film... The insulation as to be able to breathe (i.e. air has to be able to pass through it) for it to be considered "air" in the Mass-Air-Mass equation. Plastic "netting" or plastic/vinyl "straps" is fine to hold it in place. Twine is probably OK too. --Keith :mrgreen:
