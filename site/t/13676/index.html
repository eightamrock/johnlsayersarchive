xSpace9 October 2009#4
I agree that a booth, especially in a basement with a low ceiling, is not very helpful. You can, however, design/build an overhead acoustic cloud that will reduce/take away the early reflections that are getting jammed into your mic as a result of this low ceiling. When you see a booth in a professional studio, pay attention to the ceiling, it is going to be a lot taller than 6 feet, as a rule ;)
