Nikodemos, Not sure, What size is the piping? There are special pvc joints you can buy that decouple the pipe. You can pack the ends with ductseal to prevent sound from chasing through the pipe. And http://www.carlonsales.com/techinfo/ins ... SEXPJT.pdf You're using electrical PVC correct?

Hi there...thanks for the replies. Actually the electrical tubes here in Greece are somrthing completelly different than the unes you mean (and i've seen a lot in this forum). To be more acurate this kind of pvc tubes simply don't exist here :cry: So i decided to use the closest thing i found...PVC tubes with some kind of fire resistance...You see electrical plastic wiring conduits over here are maximum 40mm (and only the spiral kind) which is a bit small for 16 - 24ch multi cable...Anyway i searched for some kind of decouplers like the ones you mentioned but have no succes till now. So i think i'll just use a pvc tube between the CR and the main live room in order to have a way to pass cables if needed in the future and audio-wire the rest of the rooms using the space between the studs.
