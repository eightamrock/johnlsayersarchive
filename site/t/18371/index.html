Hi guys! I haven't been in for a while, hope you are all well! I hope i have posted this in the correct section. I am shortly going to be moving into a new room with my studio. It is in a building that used to be the central police station in Bristol (UK) and it used to be a cell. I have some knowledge in acoustics as it was a part of my BA Honours degree in Music Tech, but i am no expert. I treated the room that i am in at the moment and it sounds really good in there, but the fact that it was really close to the golden ratio before i even started probably helped a lot :) So in the new room i am not too concerned about soundproofing, just want to get the acoustics right and sort out any modal issues. At the moment the floor and ceiling are solid concrete and the walls are shiny white glossy bricks, or thats what it looks like. The best way to describe them is they are like bathroom tiles. I know that this will be a nightmare for reverb issues and i will probably have to cover most of that with something less reflective?? The dimensions are as follows: L: 2.96 m W: 2.68 m H: 3.03 m I realise that the length and height are very close which is going to be a nightmare. Luckily i have a good friend who is a carpenter and also a music producer in his spare time! So he has offered to help me for some free studio time after the jobs finished. Bargain!! So i thought that lowering the ceiling and maybe making it have a bit of a slope could help. Will need some guidance on this if anyone can help me i'd appreciate it :) The room will be used as a control room to produce electronic music. On rare occasions i may record vocalists or musicians but like i said, this is rare! My speakers are Adam A7's Any suggestions for layout, speaker placement, or anything else would be greatly appreciated. Thank You in advance John
