GuitarGodgt wrote:
I have $550 to spend at the moment on treatment
That will buy enough rigid fiberglass to treat your room fully. Cover as much corner surface as you can, including wall-wall, wall-ceiling, and wall-floor corners where possible. Where you can't straddle a corner, put the panels flat on the wall or door etc near the corner. Then put more panels at the side-wall reflection points, and more on the wall behind you, and you'll be good to go. For panels on walls and doors, spacing them 2 to 4 inches is better still. Yes, it really is that simple. --Ethan

jwl4 May 2008#4
Guitargodgt, In addition to 703 I'd check prices on mineral wool. Usually 703 is much more expensive, and mineral wool of equivalent density usually performs about the same (though it's not quite as "rigid" and doesn't hold its shape as well over time). But if you're on a budget, it will likely make sense for you, since you'll be able to get more material for your budget.
