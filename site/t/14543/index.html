Soundman20208 July 2010#5
Probably a drum riser for the drum kit, plus individual isolation pads for amps and things. That should take care of the impact noise. But you should do some tests first, to see how bad it is for the neighbors downstairs: set up a a drum kit in the room as it is right now, and get the heaviest, loudest drummer you can find to bash away as hard as he can, while you check the levels in your room, the room next door, and in the room underneath, using a sound level meter. That will tell you how bad the problem is, and based on that you can design the right treatment. You could even simulate a drum riser with some thick 703 and a thick sheet of plywood, to see what difference it makes. But you do need those real numbers to give you real answers. So beg, borrow or by a sound level meter, and take the readings. - Stuart -
