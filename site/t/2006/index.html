Hmmm Are you really saving that much buy going with the pink stuff over rockwool/AFB? Personally for the wall between the control room and the live room I think it would be a waste of money to spend (seemingly) less to save a couple of bucks. i did it and then ended up pulling out the pink stuff and putting in the Roackwool. much better results ;) The AFB offers improved LF damping that the pink stuff only dreams about :) Now for everywhere else, grab some R-19 so you can compress it a bit :) R-13 is not dense enuff ( but 1st compare the R-19 cost to the AFb cost. it isnt that far off :) like a couple of bucks or so.)
