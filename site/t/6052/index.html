knightfly30 April 2006#2
Check out "Are my speakers in a null" in the acoustics forum to see where to put speakers and your head, then click on the John Sayers Productions logo at the top of this page and go to "wall units" for ideas on some portable treatments; finally, what speakers will you use and what's their published low frequency cutoff? This will determine best soffit baffle size (I'll help you with this after I know the cutoff frequency) You'll also want to use a fairly deep rear trap - a stud frame and mineral wool batts aren't all that "permanent", and would be simplest - at least 2 feet deep if possible, and at least 4" of MW lining the trap and across the face (cloth covered) Side wall early reflection absorbers (search here on the "mirror trick" (no quotes tho) - also search on "cloud" - a recent member (Dave French) has an easy design over on RO http://www.recording.org/ftopict-26889.html Your dimensions don't look too bad, just a slight "hole" down around 50 hZ but otherwise fairly smooth modal distribution so you should be able to get a pretty tight sound with just normal treatments as above... Steve
