fretless - US lumber is actually smaller than the stated dimensions. 2 x 4 studs are in reality 1.5 x 3.5. Your 5 x 8 cm studs are approx 2 x 3.5 - so pretty much the same - just a bit thicker. You'll have no problem using those, or whatever is close.
