Aloha Ace, Here are a couple of revisions with their pros/cons Revision #1 Pros • retains separate musician/equipment access to live room • retains daylight window in place • front door remains in place Cons • loss of direct line of sight between control room/live room Possible suggestions/solutions • Video monitor of live/iso rooms Revision #2 Pros • retains daylight window in place • front door remains in place • retains line of sight between control room/live room Cons • loss of musician access entry/hallway Possible suggestions/solutions • Install double doors in live room for access Either way allows you to establish control room symmetry and maximizes your control room dimensions for your owner/clients/console. Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
