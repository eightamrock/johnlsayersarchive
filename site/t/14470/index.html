xSpace13 June 2010#2
Hey Wayne. So basically you are throwing up some walls and sticking in insulation at the moment, cool. What I know about drums and basements is that drums are loud and the overhead ceiling/floor is going to be a weak place in your isolation. Have you monitored that yet, like someone upstairs is hearing you play while you are in the basement? Often folks recommend beefing up the ceiling with at least, minimum, one layer of 5/8 inch fire rated sheetrock. Did you do that? You should probably do that. I know it is a pain in the behind, but it can be the difference between a happy life and divorce ;) You have now, half of a room in a room, which is not much good to you since the least amount of isolation will be the best that this room will produce. The drop ceiling....maybe, but the beefing up part is important to you as is getting the mass/spring/mass of this room contiguous. It can still be corrected but I wouldn't move to much farther ahead without getting the attention to these details that will improve your isolation and be part of getting the acoustically correct room that you are aiming for. Otherwise, it looks great ;)
