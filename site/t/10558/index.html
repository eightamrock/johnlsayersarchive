gullfo3 May 2008#2
i'd probably make the small 13x16 room your control room and then think about blocking off the windows. I'm not sure $1K will be enough to do much more than adding a door between the small and large rooms and blocking the windows, and a few treatments around the space to temper the room. if you have access to materials and skills, then maybe a couple of decent sized isolation booths could be created with decoupled floors and ceilings to reduce some of the sound leakage.

gullfo6 June 2008#5
i'd definitely do some more testing with a bass drum and bass amp as well as tapping metal/rubber to determine how much that floor is really conducting to the floor below or to the neighbors. plan on replacing the windows and doors to ensure security and sound isolation (as well as energy efficiency) and i'd make sure you cover any egress requirements when planning the replacement windows.
