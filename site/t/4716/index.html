giles11723 November 2005#2
to isolate the spaces, just follow the room in a room consturction techniques for each studio/rehearsal space. then pad those walls down. No need to go all studio acoustics you just dont want a highly reflective room. Unless you plan to have some cutting rooms.....
piva23 November 2005#3
What would u recommend to pad the rooms with?
giles11723 November 2005#4
A 2x4 frame on the interior of the room and some AFB(rockwool) in that frame, cover with cloth and cool. I know wear and tear is a concern....another alterntaive is...Absorption on the ceiling (3" rockwool cloth covered) and angleingone side wall of each room example 2 rooms = 1 rectangle, you segment them with an angled wall. That will reduce the reflection anguish considerably with the absorbed ceiling..... Rockwool will work so much better than plain ole ceiling tiles...
