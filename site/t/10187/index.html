Hi Steve - Buddy, you certaianly seem to be doing your research here! Nice one fella' always goes down well with the experts! :wink: :wink: Not that I'm in anyway an expert btw!!!! :shock: I just want to clarify my windows for you. Glass in place - right? 1. Measure glass - left to right, top to bottom. (Sorry for the potential insult, I assure you it's not intended!) :oops: 2. Cut ply the same size. 3. Paint ply NOT GLASS - Let it dry :wink: :wink: 4. Whatever distance you need to 'make up' to 'come back' to your vertical wall aperture surroundig your window do with, plasterboard, MDF etc. You'll probably have to increase the size of your materials with each layer to match the profile of your window frame. Think of it as a kind of 'lego' roof (without lego roof tiles, just regular blocks :shock: :shock: ) turned 90 degrees! :lol: 5. Each layer of material is fixed with gripfill and acoustic caulk, apart of course, from the ply which is only attached with acoustic caulk so that you don't see any crap around the glass from the outside! Brace/Fix each layer edge to your frame as you go and finally, cover the whole lot up with a bloody great sheet of plasterboard - Job done! :lol: Hope all this makes sense Dude - Great luck with the build my friend! Warm regards, Lou. 8)
