Each corner of the control room is 45 degree corners with built bass traps. The front traps are 12" shorter than the rear and 6" narrower than the rear. Being they are built in I decided to try a method for covering that is designed for building a screened in porch. The covering ends up nicely stretched and the trim turns out very nice.
