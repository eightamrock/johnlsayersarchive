shanesix wrote:
Do hollow clay blocks count as 2 leaves? I have a possible 3/4 leaf situation if that is the case...yikes.Sketchup file to follow as soon as I get the exact measurements of the room.Thanks so far. Shane
No sir. A block or even a brick will not develop an unbroken air cavity and by design of the brick/bock the material is bonded together front to rear by it's own layers which introduces the coupling that determines this material as one leaf. So how hollow the blocks are is irrelevant.
