Soundproofing: I guess I must build a door with no air or gaps underneath, correct?
Not really: You need to build a door that has a gap underneath big enough to allow it to open easily, but you also need to add at least two sets of rubber seals around the entire door (not just hte bottom) so that it seals air-tight when closed. Also, use solid-core doors, not the common hollow-core or foam-core doors. And possibly add extra mass if you need high isolation. But you didn't say how much you need, to it's impossible to say what mass you need.
What about the window, does anyone of you have any experience about special glasses or something that can contain as much SPL as possible?
Yes. :) Use two panes of laminate glass, spaced as far apart as you need to get the level of isolation you need, and use glass that is the thickness you need to get the level of isolation you need. But since you didn't actually say how much isolation you need, we can't say how thick the glass needs to be, or what air gap you need between the two panes.
Acoustic treatment: I've read lots of articles about traps and absorbers and planning on purchasing some stuff (from GIK Acoustics most probably), but in your opinion would I need diffusers? If yes, where would you place them? What to put on the ceiling, too?
There are standard ways of treating rooms. Look around the forum and you'll find that explained, in may places. And you don't need to buy commercial treatment: save money, and make it yourself. On your vocal booth question: It's impossible to say, since you didn't give enough information on what you are trying to accomplish, how much isolation you need, or many other factors. Some people do record vocals in the control room, others need isolation booths. It depends...
Am I missing something else, something really important? Too much to think about, there must be something missing...
Planning! That's what you seem to be missing. Put together a detailed plan of your room, using SketchUp, showing all of the isolation, equipment, furniture and treatment. Only then is it possible to figure out if the solution will work or not. - Stuart -
