Hi Serb. First, please read and comply with the forum rules, which you can find at the top of the forum (click here). Actually, several people, who are experts on this forum, will most likely not reply if you don't do what is written in that post. Many others who are very helpful, will most likely not reply out of respect for the moderators' wishes. In any event, for such a small room you most certainly should not have diffusers on the rear wall, and you should have much more bass trapping. I'd cover the rear wall with deep absorption, put huge superchunks in all the corners, and put deep absorption on all first reflection points (perhaps with gobos on wheels), but even then it isn't going to make a large difference to that room: It is very small, and practically square! - Stuart -
