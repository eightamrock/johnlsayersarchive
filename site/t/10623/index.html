So I am building a new control room onto my existing studio, which comprises of a live room, 2 iso booths, and a lounge. The existing studio is in a 550 sq foot detatched garage. I'm usin L.W. Sepmeyer's room ratio of 1 1.14 1.39 control room dimensions height 9.5 feet ( average) length width I'm only making the walls with a 2 by 6 staggered studd frame with drywall dona cona, drywall. The trusses will be made from 2 by 10's, as its a single pitched roof (not gabled). I'll only be monitoring in there and the wall between the live room and the new control room is a very thick triple wall.(currently its an outside wall of the live room) I'm including some very rough sketches of the new room. I'm building this from scratch, so its up to me how great... or not so great the room will be. I'd love to hear your thoughts.
