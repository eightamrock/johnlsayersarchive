As in, I've seen control room that are 20'x20', and then I've seen other ones that are 13'x13'. I'm trying to find out how big rooms should really be for me to design my studio. How big should a drum room be, nothing gigantic, but big enough, yanno? The size of my bedroom seems to be big enough, it's like 12'x13', is that big enough? Or should it be larger? What about a live room? Big enough for a drum set, two guitarists and a bassist standing in, to all perform together. How big should it be? Garage size, like 16'x16'? I'm thinking something like 16'x16' would be large enough for that, my band practices in a smaller garage than that and we have enough space...or 18'x18'. Should that cover it? And then one room divided into two iso booths for guitar amplifiers. What size should the rooms be?
