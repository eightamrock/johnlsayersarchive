I hit play, set the volume at 100db on the sound meter, closed all doors to the studio, then walked outside to see what the differences would sound like. and woo.......-6d makes a big difference with what's getting to the outside of the studio. knightfly30 May 2005#4
It's not the same; for one thing, speaker efficiency differs from speaker to speaker - you know the spec that says something like "92 dB, 1 watt, 1 meter"? SPL meters meaures ACOUSTIC watts, while proggies like Sound Forge (IIRC) measure dBV or dBU - these are both VOLTAGE levels, not POWER levels, and even POWER levels are still electrical, not ACOUSTIC. Without spending a lot of time I don't have right now, the simplest way to check what's going on is to measure INSIDE the room at 100 dB, then see what the SPL drops to INSIDE the room when your -6 dB passages play; that's the REAL acoustic difference, regardless of what your Sound Forge meters say...
