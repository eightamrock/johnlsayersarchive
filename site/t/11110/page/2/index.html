jeroenmuller28 September 2008#31
I'm planning to record mainly bands, but will do some film and concert 5.1 productions as well. Thats why I planned the 5.1 setup. Each room will have it's inner roof, the roof will rest on the inner walls and hang underneath the existing ceiling. Soffit mounting is a point of discussion, i'm not really planning on doing soffit mounting. Because you haven't got any possibilities to move the speakers afterwards. Ofcourse you got some advantages and disadvantages of soffit mounting, but mainly because I haven't got a clue which speakers i'm going to use (probably Adam S2.5a), I'm not planning to soffit mount the speakers at first build.
