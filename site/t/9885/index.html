Ro14 December 2007#2
As you figured out already it's a multi leaf construction. 4 to be precise and just like walls; it ain't optimal. Why not use thicker panes of glass, laminated for example (like Rod mentions in his book). Use 2 panes of laminated glass and yer a lot saver. If the previous (current) window is constructed out of 2 panes, rip it out and replace it with heavier (thicker) glass if you like.
