Is it better to have an 4 inch air gap totally filled with mineral wool Or 6 inch air gap with only 4inch of rockwool slabs jammed into the stud work & a 2'" (sometimes more as the stone work is not plumb) air gap with no insulation.
Definitely go with the larger air gap, even if you can't fill it entirely with insulation. As long as you have at least 4" of insulation all around, that's fine. The extra depth of the cavity will drive down your MSM resonance greatly, which improves isolation. So go with the biggest air gap you can afford, and put in as much insulation as you an afford as well. But as long as you have 4" of insulation all around, you are good. The insulation does several things, but most importantly it damps internal resonance inside the cavity, and 4" is good enough to do that quite well. - Stuart -
