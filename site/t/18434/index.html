Soundman202027 July 2013#2
Hi "Pj". welcome. Please read the forum rules for posting (click here). You seem to be missing a couple of things! :) It would help if you could provide the dimensions of your room, since many acoustic issues are related to the length, width and height of the room. Overall, the best place to start is with symmetry: the front half of your room should be symmetrical, such that the left side is a mirror-image of the right side, with you sitting on the center-line, about 38% of the way back, and the speakers spread equally to either side of the center-line, angles inwards at about 30° so they point at your ears. After that comes treatment on your first reflection points, and the front and back walls, followed by analysis of the room using REW software, then additional treatment, as required. - Stuart -
