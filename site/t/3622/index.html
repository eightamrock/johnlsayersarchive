sharward8 May 2005#6
As far as the wnidow goes, consider using video camera(s) and monitor(s) instead of a window. This would allow you to see into the room (and, if you want, vice versa) without compromising isolation. It can might be cheaper than the thick tempered glass, and it's easily reversed (i.e., no need to "unbuild" a window later).

knightfly22 May 2005#8
I've added to your drawing to show where you'll need absorbers in that room; if you're going to have frequent listeners at the couch position, you should re-aim the speakers at a point further behind the mix position (wider angle) - but not TOO wide, or it will create too narrow a sweet spot at the mix position (too little overlap) Also, if you can post the HEIGHT of your room, your head height when seated at the mix position, and center height of the woofers/woofer distance from each wall, I'll try to get a handle on which frequencies may be problem areas. HTH... Steve
