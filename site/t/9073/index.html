kendale6 July 2007#9
Aloha,
Wow that looks great !!! How much would it cost to do something like that in my room?
That depends on how much the materials are going for in your area. Perhaps a spreadsheet of the materials needed and cost will help estimate your cash outlay. (3/4" MDF sheets, 3" Roxul Mineral Wool/OC 703 @ 2.5-3 pcf, 2" x 4" stud framing, screws, acoustic caulk, etc) There are several threads that cover the building process. Perhaps a read through the "Stickies" in the construction forum would be helpful. It may also depend on whether or not you will be doing the work or hiring someone.
And would i need anything on my ceiling?
I would recommend hanging a cloud up there - do a "search" for "clouds" and read up on them and their construction.
And for the size of my space should i consider setting my monitors in wall? We have a pair of Event TRN 8's . What are your thoughts?
My opinion would be to soffit mount your monitors. Having worked in both situations in the same room has convinced me of the vast improvement to be gained by soffiting. (Mackie HR824's)
