Hannes, I'd suggest reading the stickies and generally doing more research. Stopping sound is not a simple (or cheap) task. For $300-400 you could probably make the room sound better inside, but I don't think you'll be able to achieve much isolation. You could insulate the room and that's about it for that budget. It'll help some to a point (maybe 3dB of reduction). Look at Sharward's thread and you'll see the lengths one must go in order to achieve good isolation.
