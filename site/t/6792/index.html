Question, How big of a difference does the vapor barrier make? Is it part of the "air-tight" concept, or more of an environmental thing? Looks great! Ben
orangenumerik11 September 2006#12
The vapor barrier is not part of the "air tight" concept. But rather part of the thermal insulation concept. Only used on external walls and depending on your location it goes to different place in the wall and it is needed to protect internal wall structure form moisture and mold developement. Here in Montreal the vapor barrier is always placed on the hot side of insulation. It is NOT to be overlooked. In a studio construction concept, care must be taken to make sure the barrier does not compromise the double partition wall concept by placing it, say between the two walls, thus creating a third partition and compromising the "Mass air Mass" system. Air must move freely between the two walls. Hope this clear things up a bit. Regards
