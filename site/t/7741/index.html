I would like moderate sound isolation from upstairs, but its not all that much of an issue. I also want to room to sound good. V, my first question to you is this - why are you throwing money away on RC channel when building an isolated wall assembly - you gain nothing from it - and probably loose a little bit in the low end....... Next - you have to explain a little more about your ceiling assembly in order for anyone to answer the question - I would also have to know exactly how you are dealing with the top of wall conditions at the outside where wall meets the ceiling. Next - I would point out to you that you probably aren't ready for permit if you haven't figured out the answers to these questions yet - this because they are important to the permitting process.
