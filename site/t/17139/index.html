Hi, I think you are going to have to do some research and reading regarding what you actually want before people can offer you advise. Do you need isolation from the rest of the house? Are you willing to tear out all the existing walls and ceiling to make a better room? Do you want a separate control room? Or do you not care about isolation and only want to acoustically treat the room you have? The standard flooring used is something hard. Bare cement, laminate, hardwood. But before deciding on flooring, figure out what you want. Steve
