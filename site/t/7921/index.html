I too am wondering about sound leakage, i think most of the leakage would occur the length of the metal tube used to channel the light downward into the room. Possibly the transmission loss could be reduced by insulating the tube by surrounding it in a box or just wrapping it in alot of fiberglass insulation? Leakage also would travel up the tube out through the dome, but i don't know how much sound would escape. Anyone else?
