jazzmania15 March 2005#3
In other words what will be the good way to block sound from coming or going into the space between the two walls.
Philippe, I puzzled over this very same question originally. But then it occurred to me: once you close the doors it doesn't matter. Just think of each closed door as an extension of the wall it's in. So there's sound between the two walls. So what? Each wall-door assembly considered as one integrated whole blocks the sound from going further, and that's all you want, right? For the sake of making the transition (the narrow space where the edges of the two doors meet their respective walls) look nice, first stuff some insulation into the gap between the two walls, and cover the gap with black acoustic cloth. Consider adding a cloth-covered rigid fiberglass panel on the inside face of one or both doors (facing each other) to reduce the likelihood of resonances. Lee
