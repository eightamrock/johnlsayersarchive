jwl12 October 2007#3
Agreed, there is no way to say what is "best" without context, not to mention measurement. How is your room set up? Where are your monitors and your listening position? How is sound travelling through your room? With an 11x11 room you will definitely need some good bass absorption, your nodes on 2 of the 3 dimensions are the same and will be that much stronger. I'd say superchunks in all corners would be a very good start...
BradJacob12 October 2007#4
