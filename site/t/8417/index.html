gullfo18 March 2007#6
in that case I'd extend the control room to encompass the entire back space and leave the drum and vox booth on either side. or turn things on the side... if your ceiling space is only 7'6" a good room ratio for the CR is approx 12'x17' 5" - Sepmeyer 1 7.5 1.6 12 2.33 17.475 but there are others - and with angled walls and such becomes an approximation that also has to include how you want to use the space. in the attached sketch I've turned your space on its side. you'd still have enough space in the CR for people plus a big live room if that was more useful - we'd have to play with room ratios/angles and doors/windows a bit but this is the idea.

Interesting alternative. I'm uploading my original design with a few more measurements and details. Here's some explainations of wants / requirements, etc. 1. I want a dedicated drum room. Always had one, is that wrong? (Got about 8x10 now, it serves me well.) 2. My present vocal is a closet, 2 1/2'x 5', it works. Kind of shooting for about 5x6 in the new one. 3. Hoping to have enough room in the main/control for 2 - 3 guitarist to move around in. The bands that record here are usually offered local booking asssistance, photo packages and such. Having her involved makes it much easier to justify those $1,000 microphone purchases. (The office is hers.) I'm obviously trying to pack a lot into 600 S. F. I've only seen a couple of big studios. These ideas are an evolution trial and error. The studio does make a little money though. BUT, if I seem to be going in the wrong direction with anything now's the time to re-think it. Thanks for getting involved.
