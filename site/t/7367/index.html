Creature8228 October 2006#4
Hey kal... It's not clear if you have people either below you or above you. If you have people below, you'll want floor isolation. If you have people above you'll want ceiling isolation. If you want a ceiling that spans your 17 feet, I would suggest 9-1/2" TJI210 I-joists at 24"o.c. They'll carry at least 35 psf... enough for your ceiling. (Assume 10 psf for access live load, pretty conservative.) Before you attach the gypsum board underneath, however, you must attach some 1x4 or 2x4 to the tops of the joists so they don't buckle when loaded. (laid perpendicular across the joists) You can call Weyerhaeuser iLevel for exact spacing but two rows near midspan should do it, seperate the two rows by about 5 feet. For a second floor, it's not too promising... see Rod's book pp. 52-53. However, I imagine some type of cushioning would help a little. Perhaps a layer of special vinyl or Enkasonic. Good luck. Creatch
