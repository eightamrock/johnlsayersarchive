Soundman20201 May 2013#2
Would you construct it exactly the same way but just place the plaster on the inside of the walls or are there other rules to be followed?
That's pretty much it, yes. You only need to make sure that there is continuity in the mass between the wall and the ceiling, and that you have good air-tight seals, of course. Apart from that, the details of how you accomplish that is up to you, and your building code.

Or you could build inside-out anyway, then if you do ever decide to move, just put up then drywall on the studs and paint it. That would make the room look perfectly normal again, at low cost, while still giving you full benefit of the inside-out construction for as long as you live there... :) - Stuart -
