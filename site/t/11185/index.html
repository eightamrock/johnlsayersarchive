paulrmartin wrote:
Does anyone have any experience with RASTRA (http://www.rastra.com/Library.html) for production/recording studio benefits (STC and so on)>
The product has large spaces between the cavities which is basically just foam. So much that that the 8.5" wall has less surface density than a 4" pored concrete wall. Not to mention the spaces are not effective for sound isolation. Solid concrete core ICF systems like Quad lock are good. The principles in using it are the same as regular partitions; mass. Andre
