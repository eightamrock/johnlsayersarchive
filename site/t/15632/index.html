gullfo6 March 2011#17
Download the free Room EQ Wizard and follow the detailed setup and test instructions to obtain a measurement of your room. Also, take your biggest speaker system and run some sine sweeps and bass/drum music and measure the sound levels - an inexpensive radio shack meter works quite well plus is compatible with REW. Measure in the room and all around and outside and make notes about the levels. Combined this will tell you how much isolation you have and what types of treatment you will need for that room.
