knightfly10 September 2003#2
Alain, from what I've seen most REAL mastering engineers tend to go more for a "properly ratioed living room" than something like an RFZ or LEDE control room - they then outfit it with obscenely expensive speakers, high end digital and repeatable, "click stop" controls on analog gear. They still treat their rooms, but very few seem to use non-rectangular rooms. It's supposedly an attempt to re-create, yet NOT create, an environment that duplicates, but DOESN'T duplicate, the environment where the music will ultimately be heard. If you want a more thorough idea of what others are doing, I recommend getting a copy of Bobby Owsinski's book, The Mastering Engineer's Handbook - it's available from Amazon.com, along with an equally useful one, The Mixing Engineer's Handbook. The Mastering one has several interviews with major mastering guys in addition to tech information... Steve
