gullfo22 February 2011#11
the mirror soffit doesn't extend like the other since it's sitting within the boundary of the CR and doesn't need to go beyond that. both sides of the front of the CR are symmetrical - soffits, slats on each side, even though the one side has a window in it, and on the back side where the door is located, the opposite wall is treated. the treatments on the back walls could be John's components or built in absorbers/hangers/slats/etc as needed. the front wall could be used for a large video monitor plus it's go absorption/slats/ etc as needed. the right-hand pillar would be embedded into the wall so you'd frame to accommodate that. strip out the pillar facade and rebuild for isolation.
