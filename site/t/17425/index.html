gullfo3 April 2012#5
so if you have the limitations as described, then i would say your layout if good enough. the live room and CR/booth are separated by a hallway so you should be ok from an isolation perspective assuming no significant structural transfer (flanking) and all other normal steps: enough mass, stiffness, decoupling, and sealing. the treatments themselves can then be working into the space - speaker soffits, ceiling and first reflection treatments, bass trapping etc. as well as each of the other rooms - diffusion, control of extraneous reflections in the booth and live room, etc.
