Measuring Results Garage 1 was measured by some engineers I paid. The engineers brougt an absorber to check what works in the room. It was this one...
The FLAXORBER absorbs disturbing reflections from walls. It´s compositon of materials and it´s special frame-design smoothly covers a wide frequency range from high treble down to mid-bass. The ramp-construction with a thickness from 16 to 9 centimeters enures linear broad-band response also provides a great optical apparence to your facility. It´s dimensions are 150 x 80 x 16 (9) cm Wrapped into a special acoustic garment it looks as it sounds: simply beautyful. http://www.digitalaudioservice.de/cms/m ... france.pdf
The best result came when they put the absorber on the ceeling... I think you call this cloud....

Some more informations: My neighbours only heare the basses when i turn up the max volume i work with. I think i have to live with it and don't turn up at night. I have rented the garages for 5 years (minimum this is fixed) and if i leave the place i have to take all out. This is one reason why i would not like to put a window between the garages. i thnk about using a camara to be in visual contact with the recording room. The second reason is...when you look at the rear view of the garages you see that the walls are of concrete blocks with metalstuds in between. I want to integrate aircondition because it is gettin to fast stuffy. I think about to soffit the speakers. The weakest point of all is the height of the garages. It's about 220,5 cm and scales down to 205,5 cm at the end. When I put 10 cm insulation+ sheetrock on the ceeling and make some 5 cm floor treatment and a "cloud".... there is not much hight left. It is gonna be something like a cockpit.
