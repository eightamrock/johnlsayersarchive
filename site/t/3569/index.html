AVare12 April 2005#2
Without getting into too much applied theory, the best bang for the money on the wall for you would be 4" (100 mm) thick (not critical) 3 - 4 lb/ft^3 insulation. You can cover it with fabic etc. Bob Golds absorption page http://www.bobgolds.com/AbsorptionCoefficients.htm Has suitable materials, links to companies, and from there you can get to suoplies in you area. Have fun! Andre
