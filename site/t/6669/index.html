I have a TV in my home studio. The wife was watching it while I was doing something on the computer and she brought up a really good point. You can't really "hear" the TV. I say "hear" because it's loud...there's definately sound...but it's hard to make out. You can turn it up all you want but all you get is jumbled sound. Don't get me wrong, I can understand what they're saying and all that. It's just that it's not as clear as it should be. Is it a room acoustics issue? I have some treatment over by my mixing position. Bass traps in the corners, treatment at the first reflection points...I also have panels for clouds and the back wall but I haven't hung those up yet. So...can anyone shed some light on this strange TV issue?

tmix22 July 2006#5
I think that if when you playback your monitors it sounds decent in the room but when you play the the TV it doesnt, then it is the TV. It may be that the TV sound system is optimised to have reflective surfaces around it for reinforcing the projection of the sound. By having it in a room with absorber panels , you may be robbing it of that feature and having to turn it up louder than the flimsy poweramp and speakers can handle clearly. My thoughts anyway. Tom
