Typically you would want to have your monitors firing down the length of the room. In a room as big as this, it may not be as much of an issue....still, it helps to balance the listening position. Look for "reflection free zone" using the search function or Google it...you will still get John's site...I think that is always cool :) The treatment issues you are going to deal with is the 10 feet tall ceiling and the 20 feet wide room. So treatments according to a reflection free zone are going to help, but you will most likely be in need of a hard backed overhead cloud at the listener position to break up the room modes that the divisibles produce and support.
