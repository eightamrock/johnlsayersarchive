sharward7 May 2007#2
I'm petty sure you can't do what you've drawn. I think you need to do it in pieces, vertically. Start with rectangular frames with single top plates, like you're not building an angled ceiling at all. (We call them "vaulted ceilings" in our part of the world.) Then, build essentially a triangular mini wall that will sit atop the top plates on the front and back walls. Typically, the joists would run the opposite direction and would connect with joist hangers in the middle to a beam. I'm having trouble finding an illustration that shows what I'm trying to describe. Bottom line, ceiling joists can be tipped up or down, with angled notches cut into them so that they bear solidly on top plates, but I'm almost certain they cannot be rotated a few degrees as you've drawn. --Keith :mrgreen:
