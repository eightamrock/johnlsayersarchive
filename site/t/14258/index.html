1. How does one go about building Johns inside-out ceiling? I can understand that its easy (albeit heavy) to construct the walls, having them lying down and then raising them when finished, but how is this possible when you're building the ceiling? I can't intuitively figure this out, and so far I can't find any specific instruction on this.
One way is to build it in manageable sections on the floor, then lift each section into place and bolt it there, then seal it. Of course, you need to plan to have extra room to maneuver the section around a bit, to get it over the wall edges. EG, you need to be able to push it well over the edge of one wall so that you can lift it up past the top of the other wall, then slide it back into the middle. - Stuart -
