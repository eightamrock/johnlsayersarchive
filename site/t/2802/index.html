Seems some will say almost anything will do, while others stress the importance of open weave for covering bass traps... I have some material that Id like to use. It is not unlike bed sheet material except much thinner, you can see the shadow of your hand through it. You CAN blow through it if you have your mouth right on it and blow very hard. Now, I am pretty sure that it would be ok to cover the bass traps in this stuff, yes??? I am worried about my first RFZ panels though, I want to make sure this material will not affect the absorbtion of mids and highs! What do you think? serge instrumental17 December 2004#5
I did a job recently with OC703 2" with frames covered with thin weaves fabrics. This was called Spandex, you know fabrics that are used for wearing skaters, gymnasts in artistic comprtitions. This fabric breathes, is highly strechable, witch may help to cover the module. The result was quite good, maybe not the most efficient fabric(absorbing mids and highs) but it did quite good for absorbing lows/mids/highs. Experimentation is maybe the way to do it and less costly if you do it by yourself. The worst that may happen is you will have to change the fabrics. You could try one sample and "hear" the results. Happy tweakenings :wink:

You still can put fewer staples, just enough to hold the fabric/insulation in place then hang it. Test it in place and clap you hands, talk in front of it to hear how much absorption, direct at som angle one monitor with pink noise, then test with/witout the module. You should at least hear differences in the range you want to absorb. :)
