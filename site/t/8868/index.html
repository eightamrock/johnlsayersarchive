gullfo2 June 2007#2
how is your isolation at this point? is the room actually as quiet as you wanted it to be? assuming it is, you now want to treat the space. I recommend measuring the actual room rather than calculate it. if you don't measure, then the basic advice would be to use broadband treatments. since your "control room" is an open space, you may find adding free standing slat resonators to even the symmetry around the desk will help. I'd keep the space as live as possible and use drapes or reverible absorber/reflectors on the walls to alter the space as needed - live to dead. for the back wall, you could build slat resonators and absorbers to control the reflections back to the desk. see image. you'll want to segragate the sections to improve the efficiency of the slats. you can build a couch riser to get the folks seated there better aligned as well.
