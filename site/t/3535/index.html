sharward13 June 2005#24
Dan, I have a possible solution... Although you will lose some headroom. Instead of affixing your joists to the top of your top plates, maybe you could mount them on the side of your top plates, using hangers that attach to the top and side of the top plates: This way, the joists won't have to exceed the distance between the top plates... They can be the same length, or just a tad bit shorter. Headroomwise, you'll lose the height of the joists though. :roll:
