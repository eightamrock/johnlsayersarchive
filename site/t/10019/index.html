gullfo12 January 2008#4
it looks like a nice design. since it looks like you're designing it with angled walls and losing space anyways, why not build the isolation walls as rectangles and then add treatment walls within each room with angles, absorption, slats, polys, etc. to treat the space. also, make the entry into the space in the live room rather than the control room. makes load in/out easier and avoid people dragging stuff through the CR. its not ideal since its often handy to have a separate CR entry but if it what you have...

gullfo14 January 2008#6
most of the angled treatment walls can the slat resonators - except in the back of the CR, i'd make those mostly absorptive with some slats to keep the life in the space. for polys - i'd put one on the back wall of the CR and one on each of the top and bottom walls (in the diagram) in the middle where the 2 angles join. maybe make the short treatments in the live room porous absorption. remember clouds in the CR and live room to control first and floor-ceiling reflections.
