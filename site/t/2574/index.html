Within your incredible vagueness, what are considering as a "baseline?" Commercially built studios in the northeast States run around $150 to $200 per square foot built professionally. That does not include LV (mic, cue, monitor, midi, etc) wiring. Without knowing what you expect to be of your studio, nor the exact labour and material costs in your area, yes $75,000 for the studio construction, in a satisfactory building.
