Hello, I live in a reinforced concrete slabs on the 3rd floor. My apartment is located between the four others: Two sides (left and right) and the top and a bottom. It just so happens that each of the four neighbors behaves very loud: knock, loud conversation, music, etc. I would like if possible to create a small room where I could at least sleep peacefully. It is very important that noise is dominated by the STRUCTURE-BORNE SOUNDS propagating through floor and walls. It is terribly distressing, even more than the sounds of the talks. My first thought, therefore, concerned the floating floor and room within a room. Therefore, over the past few weeks I've read a lot of theories about sound insulation. Despite this, I have very important questions: 1. if building double walls will not make of triple leaf effect? On one side there is a big air space (what about insulation eg, mineral wool on this side ?), on the others small air cavities. How much space between the walls is required before it becomes a triple-leaf system ? 2. does floating floor attenuate structure-borde sound ? I can use rubber, felt, isoplast etc. on many layers. Maybe dense mineral wool could be better ? 3. I'm not a musician who will create music in a room within a room. In view of this, the concrete slab resonance is important in the context of a floating (raised) floor ? 4. Can I treat the original walls, floor and ceiling as the structure-borne sound SOURCE and forget about it, that they are wall ? Then double-leaf wall of my RWAR would create no negative effect (triple leaf). What is more, then I could be working in a large room and my wife sleeping in RWAR would hear less of my keyboard and other sounds (eg, refrigerator) - according to decoupled wall rules in contrast to one leaf wall. 5. External air cavities are not sealed. Is this correct: when sound strikes neighbour wall, this wall is vibrating and produce sound in my large room. Then air compress and strikes outer wall of RWAR. But, as I said, air cavity is not sealed so air (soundwave) should circulate around the RWAR ? I am providing a picture where the red mark it with the noisy neighbors. Of course, above me and below me live loud people, too. Thank you for your help and best regards Plokaji
