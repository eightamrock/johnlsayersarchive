On ratios: you don't need to go overboard on that: Just stay away from the bad ones, get in the general region of some good ones, and that's fine. Soundman202014 April 2012#10
What's in that room behind the CR, where the entrance is? There seems to be plenty of space there to extend the rear of the control room, avoiding that strange concave shape at the back. Whay can't you extend the rear and put the door into the side of the CR? Also, I'd re-think the window in to the LR and the shape of the iso booth: Right now, you have no line of sight into the booth (even though you have glass there, the angles prevent you from actually seeing anything at all, except a thin slice of the far wall). So why not reduce the size of the main CR-LR window, angle the wall of the iso booth the other way, and put a small vertical window, or even a glass door, between CR and booth? The booth would be bigger that way, and you'd be making much better use of space: that triangular patch is useless right now, and the booth is rather small. Just some ideas... - Stuart -
