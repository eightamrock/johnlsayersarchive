knightfly26 July 2005#5
Andre's correct; however, for the really low frequencies (say, below about 300 hZ) the only thing that will make much difference is overall room mode treatments to minimise modal peaks and nulls; other than that, before you even START to treat a room you need to find out where the peaks and nulls below 300 hZ are; you do this either with swept sine waves (while moving around the room with a sound level meter) or (better) with a program like ETF and an OMNI mic - check out this for more - http://www.etfacoustic.com/ Steve
