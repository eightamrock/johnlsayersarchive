barefoot23 September 2003#6
I would line the inner cavity with something like 703 - then stuff the remaining volume with flexible fiberglass insulation. Owens Corning has recently come out with an excellent product called Miraflex. It's made of randomly twisted fibers and has superior acoustic absorption properties to regular fiberglass. Also it's much less itchy and irritating! Thomas
