gullfo28 June 2007#28
the key is to use broadband treatments. and by using angled slat resonators, you get a decent range of frequencies covered and you get angles which break up the flutter echo. staggering the treatments on opposite walls introduces some diffusion to the space. and this should be done in 3D if possible and paying close attention to the corners of the rooms since all room modes terminate there and therefore treatments are more effective in those locations.
