Soundman202016 September 2015#4
Pictures would help! It's hard to understand what you have if you don't show us! :) Also, if you don't want to actually get any isolation, and just want to appear to have done something to impress the inspectors, then just putting a piece of thick plexiglass in a wooden frame and sticking that over the window would do the job. It won't have any real acoustic effect, it will be cheap to build, and as long as the inspector doesn't know much about sound, it would look nice. However, inspectors often do know something about sound, and trying to fool them is not a good idea! I would not go down that path at all, to be honest. Rather, I'd do something that actually will work, such as what Brien suggested, and also isolate the rest of the room properly. - Stuart -
