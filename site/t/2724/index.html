AVare27 November 2004#2
Welcome to RSD! Good studios are 90% planning and 10% construction. You're going about it the right way. It is difficult to answer clearly with the disjointed structure of your post. In future try and keep all related points together, and include all relevant data. For example what is the height or depth of the water pipes? I have seen basements with pipes out of the floor and sticking down form the ceiling! Several times you wrote about money. What is your budget? Without knowing that ideas are almost useless to suggest. Symmetry is not needed for a tracking room. The audiomute might be good, I don't know it. In future post a link to things on the web. How concerned are you about sound leakage? How much are you willing to do with the ceiling to address isolation? That height of ceiling is very low to be of much for ambiance. My recommendation would be to make it completely absorptive. 4" of 703 type material covered with fabric would do the job. Keep coming with the questions. That is how we get the design done. Andre

AVare27 November 2004#4
Great second post. Sofffit (box in) the water pipes and ducts. Money is a concern. $1,000 will barely do the ceiling. If you are happy with the isolation, then okay. You haven't read much memorable about live room symmetry because, well, it is a non-issue and little has been written about it. The audiomutes loks like great moving tarps. Markertek has simialr tested product at probably much lower price. I wouldn't go with it all. 5 kHz is the upper limit for C423 testing. Above that isn't important for the general use of test data. Hope this helps; Andre

I strongly suggest that you get a filterless ionic air filter. If there is dust in your basement, you don't want it in your gear , and you will always battle it. I have a filter one in mine, but the filters to most of them are so costly that it is worth spending the dough up front for ones that you just wipe the element down every week or so. I intend on replacing mine with a non filter ionizer this year.
