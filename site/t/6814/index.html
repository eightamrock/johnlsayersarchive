len-morgan12 August 2006#2
I suggest as a first step you purchase Rod Gervais' excellent book on studio construction. It will answer a lot of your questions. In my studio, I used duct board for the plenums (sp?), and flex duct to get from that to the registers in the rooms. I have since been advised that flex duct is NOT the way to go if you can use duct board all the way (in my case, I couldn't). I also have "balanced" sends and returns. In other words, I have one return vent for every supply vent in the same room. Most houses I've seen have supply vents all over the house and then one big return vent near the air handler. If your rooms are sealed from each other, you obviously can't do this because the air will never circulate. len
