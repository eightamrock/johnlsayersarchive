Braxx27 April 2007#4
Hard to be fire rated when the joist ,which is wood, is exposed..are the joists fire treated? if not I would say no.. you would have to cover the ceiling completely . and I believe one layer of 5/8 board will only give you a 1/2 hr rating then with air gap to next 5/8 board that will give you an hour rating. Now Im not sure of the size of the Air gap but on a standard 3 1/2 inch stud wall you will get an hour from one side to the other..
