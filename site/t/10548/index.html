kmetal14 May 2008#6
i don't think you'll need caulking on the header...i would strongly suggest you decouple your walls from the joinst above using rc channel, or mason industries clips into your foundation. unless your want to hear foot traffic, and annoy the people upstairs with your music. this is coming from experience, you will not be sorry about the added expense wich shouldn't be more than a couple hundered dollars. this is what i built, and i recomned you eliminate huge flanking paths.

Here, here!!! Definately think about this connection point. Since my room has been sealed up and semi functional from an isolation perspective, I've brought in a parade of 5 friends who are contractors of various disciplines who ALL doubted the level of isolation I would achieve. One specifically worked in a hotel and had done double and even triple layers between rooms without iso clips or RC of any sort. He recalled that experience in detail to me - saying that you could still hear foot traffic and "loud noises seemed to have shape from the next room", as he put it... If you can afford it in terms of support performed by the walls, you may look into spots where you can independantly frame the ceiling.
