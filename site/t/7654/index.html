gullfo22 December 2006#5
ouch. you could try something like this: adding additional space onto the loft (about 3') with steps down on the end. then put up a wall (or remove/add the wall) over about 2'. then add a false ceiling with hangers in both the CR and the live room. add some additional absoption and diffusion on the walls as needed. I'd add some angled slat resonators on the walls in the live room to break up flutter echos and smooth out the response some more. in the CR I'd add some 4" absorbers at reflection points to keep the room alive but to reduce reflections. I strongly recommend reading the SAE materials (link on the top page of this site) to better understand all this. the addition to the loft floor space makes your CR rectagular. this way your control room isn't a square (like now - not good) and you have some overhead traps in the large overhead space which mean some more floor space. the traps won't necessarily deaden things just help with mid and bass trapping. having an acoustically transparent cloth over them will work nicely to absorb highs overhead. if you find its absorbing too much, you can add some plastic sheeting under the cloth to reflect them back. chances are the rest of the room will be live enough. here's an idea I had sketched a couple of weeks ago but just never got to post due to working 14 hours days 6 days a week plus trying to assist people on a variety of forums in my spare time away from my family. I'm guessing other people have been even busier.
