Ro2 June 2009#3
in "real" live, a door has a stop-trim on the left, right and top (inside of the doorframe). A threshold is only to, well, hold trash :) haha. In this design, however, it also functions as a door stop (trim all around). The SECOND inner trim is just an extra SEAL. You could go without the bottom trim as JB mentioned. It would be wise to add a drop-threshold (check JB's and LOU's threat to see what I mean). Add a smaller sheet of wood on the inside of the door to create the extra seal (in combo with the trim). Doesn't need to be THAT superheavy. 12mm MDF will do fine, seal it properly. In order to "protect" the bottom trim (threshold if you like) you can wrap it in rubber all around. Or make the wood edge 'round', or add a metal bar. There's pleny o' choices.
