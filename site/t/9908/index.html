Hi all... I am looking at hanging a 35# LCD monitor, using an adjustable monitor hanging bracket, on a 5/8 sheetrock RC1 wall. The sheetrock is held by RC1 on 24inch centers. My question is what would be the best way to hang this fairly heavy monitor.... 1) Use molly bolts to hold the bracket, taking care not to hit a stud or the RC1 2) Use the RC sheetrock, self-tapping screws to hold a piece of plywood large enough to then hold the Monitor bracket. Screw the wood into the RC1. 3) another option? thanks... jam

rod gervais22 December 2007#6
I would not consider anything other than at least option 2 - and then would want the plywood panel to be around 20" square - and glued to the drywall - as well as screwed to 2 pieces of the Resilient Channel - and I would also opt for the attachment of the hanger base to be using through bolts rather than just screwed into the plywood. The way your post reads - you have a single layer of drywall? Is that true? If hung from the ceiling I would still go for carryingf the load on 2 seperate pieces of channel - and get the plywood base large enough to really distribute the load well between the 2. Rod
