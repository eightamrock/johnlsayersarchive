So it's better to do it as depicted or could I just add angled walls inside a squared isolated room? You can go either way; for compactness' sake, making your inner mass as angled walls saves money and space. The other way would be to make a squared mass-air-mass room, make that part BETTER than it needs to be, and THEN do angled walls inside THAT. Problems here are than now it will act as a 3-leaf, so your outer two masses need to be more massive by about half in order to keep the same amount of isolation. The UP side of that idea is that you can, with some calculation, use the innermost angled walls partially as panel-type bass traps which would lessen the need for interior trapping somewhat. However, doing it that way is a LOT more expensive, and the calculations aren't real easy. Probably your best bet would be to use the sketch you just posted, only move that single layer of wallboard over and add it to the OTHER two on the INSIDE of your framework. This will increase the mass of the inner leaf AND widen the air gap, two GOOD reasons for doing so. Well the first sketch was wider than 700pixels and larger than 150kb so I had to make it smaller as you told me so, hence the bad and small and almost unreadable image on the first page. But I could try to put a new version of it here... let's see..ok here, this is the first one WITH the third leaf.. Also it has all the floor insulation touching the floor..which it shouldn't..correct? ACtually, your floor insulation SHOULD touch the floor; just not a LOT. You want a slight crushing of the insulation when you add the flooring, so that the insulation damps any ringing of the floor. Too much though, and you'll lose isolation. This is kind of a hard thing to describe, because different insulations take different amounts of crush before they get too stiff. Your floor, with two layers of 22mm "massive flooring", is one place you DEFINITELY want some sort of CLD material, such as Green Glue or maybe your "noisekiller yellow glue" - do you have any descriptive info on this stuff at all?
