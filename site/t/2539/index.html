Ethan Winer23 October 2004#3
Remo, Bryan is correct that wood panel membrane traps need to be flat against a wall (or ceiling or floor) to work best. But an extra 1/2" to 3/4" for a backing will not hurt anything. Whatever you use for a back, it should be as massive and rigid as possible. If it flexes or bulges as sound strikes the front panel, the trap will not work as well as it could. --Ethan
