Here are some specific answers.... Floor should not be carpeted. Seeing that you dont want to mess with the textured ceiling can you locate the studs in the ceiling and hang wires to place ceiling clouds for absorption???? Unless you plan on putting your recording room in another space you need to come up with an effective way to either isolate your laptop, or build a gobo to section off a portion of the room (semi-isolate it) from the sound of your hard drive... One thing to note. IF you are using close mic techniques the noise from your hard drives will be minimized when in the context of a mix...Unless it is strictly acoustic Guitar style or acapella vocals.I didnt get what your major style of music is.... I'd reorient your gear so that it is up against your window wall. And what Kendale showed you as portable treatments is your best bet for easy affordable one by one treatments. I'd start first with an absorber behind your monitors, then move to a rear wall absorber, celing clouds and lastly , the side wall resonators....
