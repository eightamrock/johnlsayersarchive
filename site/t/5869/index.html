len-morgan28 March 2006#8
Would it be unworkable to re-do the ducting in the room? Most office HVAC stuff I've seen just uses bare galvanized ducting. If you add a pickup point in the outside office area and then run new ducting (made with fiberglass ductboard) to the studio, plus add a silencer on the inside and outside of the room (4 total), you might be able to reduce the noise level to something more acceptable. len
