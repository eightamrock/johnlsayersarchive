Soundman20203 February 2009#4
Glad to help! Regarding orientation: It's not easy to decide which way around to arrange your CR! You show that "locked wooden door..." thing with two steps in one corner of your CR. Can that be sealed off? Will it ever need to be opened again? Can the steps be eliminated? If you make that wall will into the rear of your control room, with the front will face the "normal medium window", then you have plenty of space for bass trapping back there, and the window between your speakers, but then the entrance door is right where your front left speaker needs to go, and you have no chance of soffit mounting, which would be a shame. On the other, hand, if turn it around so that the front is the "step" wall and the rear is the "window" wall, then you have no place to put bass trapping in your right rear corner, where the entrance door is, and you have the window (which is very reflective, acoustically) right behind your head, which is not good. Tough call! Maybe you could think about moving the door into your CR, so that it is in the middle of the side wall? That would make it much easier to decide: If so, then I'd go with the "step" as the rear wall, and the window in the front wall, between your soffits. That would be my suggestion. - Stuart -
