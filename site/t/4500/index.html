knightfly10 October 2005#2
Ben, I moved this to the Acoustics forum; If you built your booth, you may also want to "build" your treatments - better results, lower cost. Small booth, does your voiceover talent sit or stand? Depending, you'd want at least 2" thick mineral wool at least 2 feet wide, centered at mouth height, to start - place on at least one of each pair of parallel walls. More of the same spaced a couple inches off the ceiling, full coverage. Listen. Record. Listen again. Add more surface absorption if it's still too "echoey". Too much, and you'll get really wierd vibes in the booth and too DRY a recordings... Steve
