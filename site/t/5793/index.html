Our budget allowed us to hire a studio designer to design the main control room, and give basic guidelines on the live room. I am mostly looking for ideas on programming room 1, since that is the room I will use the most. (For programming and also recording from the live room/the booths.) All outer construction is made of concrete (walls, ceiling, floor) since this used to be a storage area. Please see attached image for details. Any ideas and suggestions appreciated. We haven't really decided on what to put on floors either yet, so any suggestions as to suitable material is also most welcome! Thanks!
