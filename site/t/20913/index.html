Hi. Please read the forum rules for posting (click here). You seem to be missing a couple of things! :) So basically you want to know the difference between a Superchunk bass trap and an ordinary corner trap? THe superchunk will perform better, down to lower frequencies, as long as you do NOT " fill the gap with high density rockwool triangular sections.". Just build a normal superchunk with low density fiberglass (+/- 30 kg/m3) or low density mineral wool (+/- 60 kg/3). This is a bass trap, so if you use high density insulation the performance will suffer. Contrary to popular belief, high density insulation is better at absorbing high frequencies, not low frequencies. Lower density is better for bass traps. - Stuart -
