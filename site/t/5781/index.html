kendale7 March 2006#7
Aloha Kalli, If you can swing it, I would go with John's plan. There are a multitude of reasons why you would NOT want to have either of your rooms built as squares or rectangles. Also, you may want to either print out or point the builders to both this site (ESPECIALLY the reference sections) and the SAE site BEFORE they begin. It wouldn't hurt for them to brush up on recording studio construction techniques (which is radically different from home construction) and they might find a lot of new and helpful information and techniques for any future projects they may have. It might also be a good idea to post photos of the project in process as well as post questions, comments, asking advice all throughout the studio build, which might help you avoid any wrong turns that may be costly in the end. Here's a couple of links: Reference Area - http://johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=2125 More Useful links: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=1168 SAE Website - http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html (click on acoustics, absorbers, construction in left hand column) Wall Units - http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm Looking forward to your studio build! :D Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
