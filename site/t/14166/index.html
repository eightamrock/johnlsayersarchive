Hi there, I've built a load of 50x50cm panels with the 'red/orange' wickes 1200x600x30mm heavy duty insulation, and i'm using it at reflection points (either side of and above mix position), and above the vocal tracking area (just one slab thick for now). They are covered in ikea frames and wrapped in a very breathable cotton fabric. They are flat against the wall/ceiling, and are 'open backed' (ie the back isn't covered). It's certainly very nice to work with however, i've always thought that it could possibly be TOO dense? Or is it OK? I've searched around but never found a definitive answer. I think i've read rumours that the density is really high, which is why i'm wondering if it's TOO high. Cheers for any feedback on this Eddie
