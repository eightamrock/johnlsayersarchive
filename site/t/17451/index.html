You could angle it, yes, but the angle will depend on the geometry between the speakers, the walls, and the position of your head in the room. Probably at least 10° to 15°, I would think. Another option is to put acoustic absorption across the front of it. For example, 6" of fiberglass or mineral wool.
Just a bit and get parallel simmetry off.
Symmetry is GOOD in a control room! The front of the room should be perfectly symmetrical, so whatever you do to the left side of your room, you should also do to the right side of the room. - Stuart -
