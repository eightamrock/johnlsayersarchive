knightfly8 April 2006#4
You don't need rockwool for inside ceilings or walls; use standard fiberglass insulation and do a complete fill with slight (about 20%) compression - it'll cost closer to the same amount and do just as well. Save your rockwool for aocustic treatments. If there is heated living space ABOVE you, get UN-faced insulation batts; if it's your outer roof, get kraft paper faced, and put the facing toward the room (unless the UK has changed climates since I was there :wink: ) Steve
