knightfly17 August 2006#2
For interior walls, you do NOT want faced insulation - yes, standard fiberglass insulation works nearly as well inside a wall as the more expensive, higher density types - arguably, 2.5 PCF SAFB types are optimum for music but it's reportedly less than maybe 2 dB difference at any frequency. I'd need to see your exact intended wall construction to answer about proper thicknesses, etc... Steve
