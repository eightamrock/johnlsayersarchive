Hola, Has anyone used or got any feedback about Se Electronics Ghost Acoustics? www.ghostacoustics.com Im about to kit out 2 control rooms and live room with treatment just looking at alternatives to the Auralex gear since I hate the colours and the fact that you have to frame their panels yourself, this stuff looks very sturdy Cheers J
jwl10 February 2007#2
The specs on the ghost site don't knock me out. They don't do too well in the low end, even their corner absorbers are under 0.5 below 400Hz or so. That's after a quick glance. I think there are better options for you. If you are opposed to DIY (which I regard as by far the most cost-effective route), I'd say that RealTraps have better specs than this stuff, not to mention GIK, ReadyTraps, etc etc.
