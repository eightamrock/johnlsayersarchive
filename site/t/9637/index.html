NEW MILFORD, CT--November 24, 2007--Disappointed by lackluster sales of the MEGA and UltraMEGA bass trap line, RealTraps has announced their newest model, the PicoTrap. This diminutive bass trap measures 5 by 2.5 inches, and is slightly thicker than a standard business card. Made from a top-secret proprietary absorbing material, the PicoTrap boasts absorption coefficients greater than 1.4 to well below 100 Hz. According to RealTraps co-founder Ethan Winer, "Apparently some professional acousticians fail to understand that the size of an acoustic panel has a direct bearing on its suitability as a commercial product. Our new PicoTrap has only 0.02 sabins of absorption at bass frequencies, but by using absorption coefficients we found it compares very favorably with other, much larger bass trap products. Since coefficients are the standard measure of absorber performance, we are able to offer the PicoTrap to the popular home studio market with a straight face." Rather than sell this bass trap, RealTraps has arranged with General Mills to give it away for free inside cereal boxes. Winer continued, "If you're willing to eat a lot of Cheerios and Corn Chex, eventually you'll accumulate enough PicoTraps to treat half of one wall in a very small vocal booth."
