John Sayers15 August 2005#12
Sorry - I didn't realise the neighbours were above :) I assumed they'd be beyond the massive walls :) there is a possibility that the sound will flank through those walls but as you have minimum ceiling height I'd tend to avoid it and wait to see what gets through - if it does create a problem you can always add the floor later. Just make sure all your wall frames meet the floor with rubber isolation to start with so your new structure is floating and isolated from the main structure. A bass trap in this case is where you allow the sound to get into an area - like the cavity between the arches, and absorb it so it doesn't get back out. The port is the access to the ported area and the ceiling becomes a false ceiling between the main room and the trap area. cheers john
