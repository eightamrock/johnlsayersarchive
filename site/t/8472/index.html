gullfo30 March 2007#11
if you expand on Kendale's ideas - I'd move the storage out of the main space and into the stair and laundry area as it opens up another 4-5' and simplifies the construction (which you'll need given the tight budget). presumably the center wall is load bearing so you'd only want to open a single door between the isobooth on the end. I think with the stairs and such, the second isobooth by the stairs may be better served by making that your storage area and if you need another booth - take advantage of the (now) larger tracking room to put one in there later. the wide doors - cover them with a wall and with a smaller door each (or not even put one in the isobooth). this way you're only constructing about 4 new 2x4 walls (3 w/ drywall - 1 w/ slats) and adding 1 or 2 exterior and 2 glass/windowed doors. use plywood routed with slots for the resonator treatments unless 2x lumber is less expensive.
