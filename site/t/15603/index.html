Hello! I work as a sound engineer in a theatre in Finland. I have a small homestudio in which I do most of the theatre studio work plus a lot of other recordings. As my family grew bigger, I started to look for a place I could rent and build into a studio and I found one quite near our house. Its a rectangular room, 36m2. I'm going to start fixing it up in the summer and I would like to hear some advice from you experts out there. My goal for this room is to make it as quiet as possible and as good sounding as possible (of course within a rather small budget). I would like to be able to record both loud drums and really quiet whispers in the same room. Luckily the room is located in a very calm and quiet neighborhood. It is in one end of a one story building so there is just one next door neighbor (who seem to be nice and understanding :D ). My budget for construction is around 1000€ (about 1300$). This is a drawing of the room.
Image not preserved: studioroom1.jpg
Here is a plan that I made of a room in the room:
Image not preserved: studioroom2.jpg
I am going to remove the drywall from the existing walls and leave the insulation open. On the new walls I'll only put gypsum board inside the studio so that it'll become a 2-leaf wall. The floor stays as it is (concrete), I'll just put in new laminate flooring. The ceiling also stays as it is. My questions are: -Is this a good idea? :D -Is it enough with 5 cm free space between the existing and the new walls? -I have no idea about acoustical treatment so any suggestions on what to put on the walls are very welcome! My plan is to place the speakers in the upper part of the room facing the door. The rest of the room will be filled with various instruments and stuff... Thank you for reading.. S
