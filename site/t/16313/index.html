welcome! please update your profile to include your location. also, please take time to read the "Read-me before posting" sticky and provide the details. yes, room ratios have a place in the overall process of designing your studio but you also need to consider the volume - once you get very small, room ratios are much less helpful. so if you think about it, dividing a 4m x 5m room into 2, you end up with 2,5m x 4m x 2,7m which is not good because you're nearly square on ceiling and width. cut another way, 2m wide would not be fun... but maybe a decent control room and small vox booth are reasonable and possible.
