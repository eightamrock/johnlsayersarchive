knightfly17 October 2004#6
Tommy, rigid fiberglass or rockwool will always beat foam of the same thickness by quite a bit, including wider range of absorption; if you want portability, here's another choice - http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm Unless you have enough room to put several of each range of panel traps, you'd likely get smoother results using broadband traps instead - otherwise, it's too easy to get peaky results with frequency-specific traps... Steve
