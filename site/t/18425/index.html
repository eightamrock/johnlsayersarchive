Hi everyone, This is my first post in this forum and I would really appreciate any feedback to my wonderings. I live in Sweden and work as a music teacher, mainly drums. Beside that I also do some recordings in our studio. Now, the thing is that we (our music school) are about to move to a new place, well actually to a very charming 100 year old school building. I'm now responsible for designing the new studio which also will work as teaching room for drums, bass etc. It all must be fitted in one class room; 9,4m x 6,7m x 3,7m. As I'm sitting on two chairs, both as a teacher who want as big studio as possible and as a recording man who really would like a big control room, I have to compromise with myself. Another thing that limit the design is the two outer walls which contain huge windows. After hard thinking, reading a lot on this forum etc I would like to show you my design. I've been using Genelec 1031A for the last 15 years and I'm really curious to how the will sound flush mounted. I will not use a sub woofer. The 2 baffel walls will be build with 2 or 3 layers of gypsum bord and filled with heavy damping. Speaker distance is 1,9 meter. The wall angles are 30 degrees. The grey area in the pictures are absorbent material. Now to my questions: Is there gonna be a problem due to the narrow room ratios 1:1,22:1,35? Since the parallell sidewalls have both a huge window and a door I guess it's okey to have them parallell. Or??? Any other thoughts? Grateful for all the help I can get! //joigle
