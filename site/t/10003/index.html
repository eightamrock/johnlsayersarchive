StudioZero09 January 2008#1
Does anyone have an illustration or explanation of how to raise a floor up to run cables under the floor? I can't quite figure out the right way to do this. The floor now is just concrete. Good modern design. I want to put in a wood floor over this in the control room. I have a lot of gear and cables though. There is basically no other option than to run the cables underneath. I don't want to do a typical floating floor for obvious reasons. How the heck do I raise the damn floor? Thanks in advance
StudioZero09 January 2008#2
it seems this is what I should do: Concrete Floor->2x6 joists on uboats->4" insulation->3/4" plwood->wood floor Note: I am not doing this for isolation purposes. My walls and soffits will be on the concrete. This way seems pretty simple and not too expensive
Ratty_79 January 2008#3
Hi there, You could do what you have suggested, however, you could just put down 2" battens, run cables in between, fill with insulation, lay your flooring on top. ie 5/8 or 3/4 plywood, then underlay and finally your favourite flooring material on top. The whole thickness would probably be only about 4" in total. Hope this helps Mike
StudioZero010 January 2008#4
sounds like a plan... I am though worried about continuity between the control room and live room. I'd sure hate for there to be a 4" gap between the floors in the rooms. I guess that is the trade-off though
