If it were in fact such a slight protrusion the OP could almost just install the rock directly to the underside and that would work. But as you and I know, nails come from everywhere for many a purpose...walls, multiple layers of sub flooring, etc, so I would have a difficult time (based on what I have seen) that this would be a uniform pattern from one bay to the next. And some folks have argued over if they can or cannot just cut the nails off...and the answer (if it comes up) is no, can't or rather shouldn't do that. But, sure, if a person wanted to place caulk in-between the two materials, it wouldn't hurt a thing as long as the protrusion of the nails is really, really minimal.
