xSpace9 July 2009#10
"[Q2] The ceiling is currently at 8'. However, I have the option of raising it within the limits of structural safety. The onwer tells me we could raise the level approximately 16"." As Liliths pictures show, volume in a room is a requirement for Mastering. So raising the overhead in your issue is a good idea. Problem is, it is not as simple a thing as getting the say so from a landlord and in fact could be cost prohibitive. In order to modify the existing roof trusses the proposed rehab has to be enough to meet or exceed the existing deadload of the roof while still being able to keep the building walls in an erect position, not an easy task. A typical procedure might involve a beam installed from gable end to the other gable end with new supports installed to maintain the beam. A new truss design would have to be submitted for approval when the beam and supports were submitted. Is that what you were thinking?

John Sayers16 July 2009#25
I didn't intend them to be superchunks - I drew them as slot resonators which are absorbers aimed at the low midrange - 125hz - 500hz which is the area where the other absorbers start to roll off. They also stop the parallel walls in front of the listening spot where standing waves would have developed. the clouds are to stop the floor to ceiling standing waves which will occur in your room because the floor and ceiling are parallel. I've made the front panel at 12 degrees as it's iover your listening area/console so it won't get in the way - the rear one I reduced to 6 degrees because otherwise you'll bump into it.
