gullfo29 March 2011#2
best bet - take the cost per unit area for a high end home, add 20% and multiple by your total unit area. for example, if your high end home per unit cost is $250/ft2, plus 20% = $300/ft2 x 700/ft2 = $210,000.00. if you are doing all the work yourself, and so you only have materials to cover, then consider cutting 30%. if this is a commercial facility where you need to consider a number of additional aspects, consider adding another 15% or so. as a ballpark estimate, this number could be off +/-50% easily and doesn't include any outfitting etc.
