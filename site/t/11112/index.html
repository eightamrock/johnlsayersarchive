The experts told me: If you have a vaulted ceiling have the higher part at the back of the room. Speakers should be approx at a position 38% of the total distance from the front wall to the rear wall. The 38% is too keep you and your ears from being at 50%, so that is your starting position..not a hard and fast placement. Speakers should also be at a position to NOT be in the center of the distance from floor to ceiling height. Speakers should also be in an equilateral triangle with your head inside the third angle. Bass traps do wonders for acute angles so the one at your back would gain aid by having them. Panels should be placed in a RFZ configuration(look it up). It is all in this website..the 150 lurkers are astounded that you have no drive to get this free and wonderful stuff at no charge to you
