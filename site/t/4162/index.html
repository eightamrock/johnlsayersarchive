I'm not sure if I should double this wall. I have not had any problems in the past at other studios with sound leakage from single wall divisions and booths Depends on things like type of music, how loud, whether you'll be recording separate musicians in both rooms simultaneously, etc - if only one room will be used at a time, then I would definitely NOT worry about double walls. OTOH, framing lumber isn't real expensive compared to time/labor, etc - if the extra floor space required isn't a problem, I think I'd go for double frames in case you wanna record a fretless 6-string bass live in one room and a flute in the other at the same time :roll: Steve

sharward17 August 2005#13
Why not fill the cavities with Thermafiber Sound Attenuation Fire Blankets (SAFB) - Nominal Density (2.5 pcf) or Roxul Acoustical Fire Batt (AFB) - ≥2" (2.5 pcf)? This stuff will probably satisfy your local building department's requirements for a firebreak in the void between the walls without having to permanently couple them together with lumber. That's what I plan to do on my project. 8)
