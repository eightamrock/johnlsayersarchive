motown596 December 2004#3
First, just a comment on your monitors. The 20/20 bas are very decent monitors. Without a proper setup, bass trapping and reflection control you may not hear a huge jump in articulation...and portable mixes with more expensive speakers. I did about 12 full CDs and over 150 other projects on the Events and they didn't hold me back one bit, rather my room and bass perception were bigger fish to fry..Just me though. I like new gear too! Any way to set up with the long part of your room behind you? Looks like you'd have an over 25' round trip from your head to the back wall. Good for keeping the refections clouding up your ability to hear better. I see a window. That's ok. A drawable curtain could work there. Light and air are good. Seems like a perscription of room treatment might fix you up quite well. You need some bass traps across the wall to ceiling junctions and behind your monitors hung across the corners.Front and back walls. Then some absortion at the side walls where the first reflections off your speakers hits your head. This means the ceiling and walls around where you sit. Something is also needed to send the early reflections away from your mix position. These two things can be handled by the same kind of unit. Resist the temptation of putting up a bunch of foam all over your room. Additionally, If you have carpet you'll end up with a room with all the high-end sucked out and the bass side will drive you nuts. You may put up the foam and the room sound "nice" when you speak or clap your hands. There is much more to proper balance. I love the Realtraps. Realtraps.com has a bunch of info there. You can make your own traps and difusion panels from information here...do a search. have fun! read before spending...
