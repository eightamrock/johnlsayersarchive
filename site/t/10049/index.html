You really need to do some more research here on the forum by yourself. Lot's of stuff has been discussed in detail. Take some extra time to spend on the archives and/or recent threads. There's really some good stuff in there. Start with the design forum, this one, and draw a plan. Then think about construction, what you need, how to do it and what not to do. Gather all info about the existing building you can get like how's it constructed, what materials used, widths, lengths, heights etc etc. Every detail counts. Make/draw a floorplan, and post it here. We can discuss it. When your plan is ready, see what's need to get it working. Last but not least you'll need some room acoustic work. The while shabang will have you occupied for at least a couple 'o months (if not years, unless yer name is Lou ;) ) good luck and don't forget to post your thoughts here ;)
