I have a home studio in my basement. The total room is approx. 18' x 21'. I would like to build a guitar booth for recording and sound isolation purposes. The attached drawing shows dimensions. I will probably put one layer of 5/8" gypsum board on the inside and outside of my 2 x 4 studs with 2" rockwool inside. My inside measurements are roughly 8' high x 7'10" wide x 10'4" long. I cannot go any wider or higher. Please let me know if there are better ways to do this. Thanks My specific question: Should I drop the ceiling height some to be have a better ratio with the width or will it matter for this size room? I will be recording mostly loud guitars and some vocals. I already have 20-30 panel bass traps to install where needed.
