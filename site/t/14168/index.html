rod gervais10 March 2010#2
Have you really checked out the new LED lighting systems? I have recently began using them (main lighting source) on my projects and have fallen in love with them. One of their great features is that you can couple them with specialized systems and provide not only lighting adjusted for mood - but one light many colors....... not to mention that they are RF free, low voltage (thus tremendous savings on the electric bill). Personally i don't really care for halogen - I find it to be a harsh lighting the heat notwithstanding. If you really want to use halogen - why not use track lighting instead of cans - then the enclosure is no longer an issue. Rod

rod gervais2 April 2010#9
As I mentioned above - I worked with Progress Lighting - the products I used were standard LED down-lighting - 4 to 6" - which could retrofit into an existing recessed can - (you could also purchase a recessed frame specifically for the unit if you desired) The lights are very similar to the Y-Lights LED recessed lights. Understand - when you look at these they are not the small 1" leds you see at Home Despot - a 4" light looks light a solid 4" bulb from the bottom - these units are solid and well built - but 4" of solid LED with a built in transformer to go from line voltage to low voltage built right in to the fixture itself - that is the reason they are so expensive......... Although - when you picture not only the fact that they have a life expectancy of 50,000 hours - add in the cost savings in electrical usage - they really aren't that expensive in the long run - just the upfront investment. Cree Lighting is another decent source - You should be able to find these companies lighting products through any decent sized lighting store in your area. Rod
