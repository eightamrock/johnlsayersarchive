Ouch! Square is not good. that probably isn't the cause your problem, but it must be doing nasty things to your room response...
My new place has what appears to be concrete floors (I'm guessing rather thin ones).
Probably the easiest is like you suggested: Get the sub woofer of the floor somehow. It is probably coupling directly to the thin slab, and my good friend Mr. Murphy has a law that says it is probably exciting the exact right resonance downstairs that most annoys your neighbor! Some companies sell pads specifically for decoupling amps and speakers from the floor, but do careful research and check the specs: there's an awful lot of acoustic snake oil out there! If that doesn't work, then in the worst case you could offer to treat your neighbors ceiling for him: install RSIC clips and hat channel, attach a couple of layers of drywall to that, fill the cavity with insulation, and seal around the edge with acoustic caulk. Of course, that assumes that it is just the ceiling that is causing problems... if your sub-woofer is flanking though his walls too, then it would probably be cheaper to move to a new apartment, on the ground floor! :) - Stuart -
