Hi everyone, This is my first post. I've read a ton of great info from these forums but still have some questions about building a studio for rehearsing/recording my loud rock band (drums, bass, 2 guitars, vocals). I'm renting a loft in the basement of an old church converted to an apartment building (Downtown San Diego, CA) that has a separate room with dimensions as shown in the picture. Floors are concrete, walls are 12" thick concrete (painted but with cracks, holes, etc), and the drywalled ceiling is 18' high (not sure if there is insulation behind it). The 3' by 3' "notch" is the corner of the entire building. The wall on the right side has earth behind it, up to about 9', then an alley for the remaining height. The angled and bottom walls have my living area and kitchen behind them respectivly, and the top wall has an outdoor stairwell behind it leading up to a parking lot on the ground level. The stairwell wall and the earth/alley wall both have 1 window each about 9' above the floor. There is about a foot between the top of the windows and the lowest pipes protruding from the ceiling. There is also a 6" x 6" post as shown in the picture. Currently the doorway has no door. I've already received a complaint from our upstairs neighbor when we tested the room (bad decision). However, there is a rap group next door to him that rehearses regularly and another loft with a skate ramp next door to us. Granted, these are not nearly as loud as a full-on band, but there seems to be some tolerance within the building. Maybe the sound going straight up is my major concern? Anyway, I have about $2000 to $3000 to spend on materials (labor will be me and my bandmates). At first I thought a floating ceiling just below the windows (along with capping the windows and installing solid double doors) would be enough but after reading more about it, it seems a room in a room might be the way to go. The space is a lot bigger than what we need so I don't have to go wall to wall with the constuction. Also, I don't want to add anything to the existing concrete walls because that's a lot of square footage to fix. If I build a room with double leaf walls do I need to be a certain distance from the existing walls to avoid a triple leaf effect? If I build the room to a height just under the windows, will the extra space between the room and the ceiling cause any resonance problems? Should I isolate the room from the floor, even though it's concrete and in the basement? There seems to be a lot of options and I need some help deciding which way to go. I hope I've given enough detail. This forum is amazing. I know there's a lot more for me to learn. Thanks, Chacho
