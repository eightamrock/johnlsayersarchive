From the SAE website - http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html
because you don't want rear reflections to interfere it is better to start with a longer front to back dimension than the side to side dimension. i.e. 6m x 5m. - John Sayers

Sorry, this is not going to work. You need thickness to control the bass frequencies. I would go for John's design, increasing the thickness of the front and rear absorbers from 8' (20 cm) to 12' (30cm). It means you are going to loose 60cm in treatment.
