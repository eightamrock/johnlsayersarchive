Assuming that you are constructiing above a slab on grade - fill it with sand when you finish....... BTW - understand - the big issue is not noise in the room you're in (as far as problems go) - it's what happens to isolation if using this as part of an isolating assembly. You stated "I would think" , etc. etc........ and you are correct - it can be done - I state that in my book - HOWEVER - I also point out that you are not going to do it with a simple frame and a couple of layers of plywood, etc. Picture the mass of concrete it takes to get down around a 10Hz center frequency - and translate that (roughly) into framed mass. Can you do it - SURE,,,,,, can you do it cheaply and more effectively than concrete - doubful........ could you do it on an elevated deck that it was structurally impossible to put the concrete on due to loading - NEVER...... That's just the way it is...... Sincerely, Rod
