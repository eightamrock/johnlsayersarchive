knightfly17 April 2006#11
I have a couple of this type mat, had them for about 15-20 YEARS, and they STILL have a slight odor. Just smells like tires (believe that's because they ARE just re-gurgitated tires) - I have no idea if they'd make you sick being indoors, because I've not used them totally indoors; one is in front of a workbench in a small shop that's always open to the outside, and the other one's been laying behind an old car behind my other shop forever. These seem too stiff compared to "normal" floor floating neoprene (should be around 60 Duro) but I suppose if you loaded them heavy enough... But before you get too carried away, please read up on the dangers of improperly floated floors, (keith's thread has some good comments IIRC) and (if you're on anything BUT a heavy, ground-level concrete slab) you need to involve an ENGINEER in the calculations... Steve
