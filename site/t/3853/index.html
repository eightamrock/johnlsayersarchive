When a drawing is to scale it is proportionally smaller or larger, by some multiple, than the real object being modeled. To scale the drawing, measure a dimension, and reduce that ratio to simplify it. ...if the 31'-0" equals 3" on your measuring tape, ruler, or scale, then 1" equals 10'-4". scales....... 1/4"=1'-0" , 1"=50', 1:20 ....32'-12" = 33'-0" ?
