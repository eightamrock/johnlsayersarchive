Hi. Please read the forum rules for posting (click here). You seem to be missing a couple of things! :)
Q1, is the ceiling acting as a giant diffusor? Could we keep it like this and just have some fabric to cover it just for the looks?
It might act a bit like a diffuser, but since all the wells are the same depth and width, it will be tuned to one frequency. Plus, the ceiling will need absorption in any case.
Should we keep the concrete floor?
Concrete is about the best floor you can get. If you don't like the way it looks, then just put down laminate flooring on top of it, but do not put down any type of flooring that raises the surface and leaves an air gap below. That would be bad. - Stuart -
