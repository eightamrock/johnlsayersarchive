This is my first post, and this forum seems to vehemently encourage the following of posted rules so I will try my best. I have a 22'x20'x8' room in the back of the house with an entrance to outside and a bathroom. My goal is to get the best possible sound out of the limited space. I have read through the forum and still cannot decide on some major design aspects. Honestly, I feel that if the room were square this may be a lot easier, however the room has a bathroom in the corner and thus complicates some design aspects. I plan on using the studio for tracking local artists (i.e drums, guitars, keys, vocals) and have NO issues regarding neighbors seeing as how the property is isolated. I will summarize my questions as best I can below. 1) DESIGN - Are there any major problems, or better ideas, regarding the layout of the room? (i.e should I place my desk somewhere else, perhaps drums, or the vocal booth in another areas?) I've thought about this from a few different angles and this seems to work, at least in my mind. However I do not know if the layout utilizes the dimensions of the room optimally or not. 2) ACOUSTICS - The room has windows which take up a lot of wall space. I am planning on boarding the windows in order to utilize this space for panel absorbers? Again I am so new to this and come to you guys quite humbled. (I have tried) I plan on putting bass traps in the corners, and other than that I really am looking for ANY advice on where I could place any more acoustical treatment. However, I'm sure that's just my naivety, either way I would really appreciate any ideas on how to make it sound better, if possible. 3)"PERTINENT INFO" - If you look at the pictures you'll quickly notice the glass closet which takes up a substantial portion of one of the walls. I have a total of 16 simultaneous inputs into pro tools. I am looking to record local bands FREE as well as my own music. (myspace.com/thatswhatshesaidathens) This is not about the money for me, however I obviously care about making it the best it can be. Finally, this is my first time using sketchup (cool program) so the measurements are a little off, but not by much(also the vocal booth will be extended to ceiling) I hope you get the idea.
