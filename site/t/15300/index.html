gullfo24 November 2010#4
Even with perfect dimensions, the room is going to get clutter with equipment, bodies, furniture, groupies, etc and then all bets are off. So to compensate for the invariants, studios also need a balance of treatments to ensure the room response remains as stable as possible. So ratios are only part of the equation. To explore this ratio topic in depth, check out Eric Desart's most excellent treatises on the studio tips forum.

The problem is that too many people think there is a simple "perfect ratio", which does not exist. Unfortunately too many people who should know better think the same way. The goal is a smooth transition from the diffuse field region down to the pressure zone. IOW Bonnello criteria. A good summary is on these pages. For a bunch of "best" room ratios look at Cox's best ratios excel file here. Smoothly, Andre
