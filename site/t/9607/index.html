wolffie wrote:
what insulation should be used r13 or r19, paper or not? for that matter in the discribed wall assembly, is there anything that really is not adding much more STC.
I always go by the "more is better" philosophy, but I do know this: Do not stuff the insulation into a space where it does not fit. By compressing it too much, you will lose a bit of efficiency. If you walls are made from 2x6 studs, the R-19 fits better, if they are 2x4, the R-13 fits better. Use what fits is probably the best recommendation I can give. If you try to stuff R-19 into a 2x4 space, you might be stuffing a bit much. Regarding paper, I went with paper, but in retrospect, I have heard that I might have been better (and cheaper) not to use the paper-faced type. If you do use paper-faced insulation, make sure that the paper side is against a surface, such as drywall.
wolffie wrote:
do you need the green glue with a sound iso clip or would the green glue function better on a budget. i guess i am looking for the best assembly on a budget. i don't think i can afford the green glue and the clips.
Ouch! This is a tough one! :) Obviously, each product has a function, and both would be preferable. I would think that it partly depends on the function you intend and the design of your room. Sound proofing would probably be helped most by the clips, especially if this is a one-room place where you are trying to disturb the outside world. I am not sure if I am qualified to answer this question (actually, I am *sure* that I am not!), so I will let the experts chime in here. Can you give us a bit more detail on your build? How many rooms, what you are trying to accomplish? Will you be recording bands with drums, or just vocals, etc. Best of luck!
