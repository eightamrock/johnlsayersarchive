sharward15 September 2005#11
Having gone through a minor flood situation myself just over a year ago which ruined the laminate flooring throughout half our home, I can understand the aversion to the product... Although that didn't stop us from putting new laminate down in the same places (except the kitchen and laundry room, where we opted to do ceramic tile). Here are a few photos of the night of our water intrusion and the team of construction workers our builder dispatched to us that night. (The vinyl flooring was underneath the laminate planks.) Consider ceramic tile. The cost isn't that far off that of laminate flooring, and it tolerates water much better. Carpet surely will not. Remember that your baseboards and your walls probably won't survive a flood either.
