John Sayers7 September 2006#21
1.)I just have to check something... Are the "blue thingies" on my pictures "broadband absorbers?" there is just 703 covered in some fabric, am I correct? They're not membrane absorbers, are they?
Yes - just 703 - not a membrane.
2.)Also, as you will see in the picture, the treatment takes up alot of space... How much could I reduce the deapth of the treatment? Or is this the best effectiveness/space ratio?
yes - you could shorten them by a couple of inches to allow more room.
3.)In my room the soffit mounts will have to be quite deep (1,2m). Do you think it would be a good idea to have some hangers there, or would it be better to just fill it with 705?
I thought you had already decided to put hangers in there - I just was too lazy to draw them in ;)
4.)And about the cloud in the back... The floor will be carpeted, wouldn't it be too much to have another cloud? The surfaces are not paralell... (just my noob thinking...)
I added two clouds - one is reflective, i.e. the one over your mixer, the other is absorptive to stop reflections from the ceiling bouncing back at the mix position. The front cloud also adds reflections back to the drum area.
5.) and the last question... Since the ceiling is hm... (don't know the correct word.. concave?) would it be a good idea to put a bass trap in the center of the ceiling... it seems that there would be alot of bass there...
see my previous answer. cheers john
