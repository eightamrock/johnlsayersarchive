Hi! I'm planning to convert a double garage into a one-room recording and mixing studio. It's currently a box of something like 6m x 6,5m x 2,5m. I've been reading up a little on studio-design, and learned from my previous dead-end studio-build (>>) (Thanks for all help!), but now my question is: Should I design things myself, with as much help as I can get here and elsewhere? Soundman202030 November 2012#2
Hi G-Sun! There are pros and cons both ways: A designer can save you a lot of time, and maybe even money by helping you to avoid expensive mistakes. But if you learn all you need to know yourself, then that will REALLY help when you are actually building it, so you truly understand what you are doing and why you are doing, as well as the consequence of making any changes. The forum is here to help the DIY guy who wants to learn how to do it himself: He can post questions and proposed designs, and get help on improving them, but that can take a long time, since sometimes nobody replies to your thread for many days. So either way will work. If you have plenty of time (maybe a year or so) before you need your studio, and you like learning, researching, studying, then then DIY is a great way to do it. But if you are in a hurry and need your studio finished in a couple of months, then hiring someone would probably be better. Either way, good luck with your build! - Stuart -
