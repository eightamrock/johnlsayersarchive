gullfo24 March 2007#37
didn't mean to give you the wrong impression, I know you have more work to do before construction thread begins but it doesn't mean you can't start posting photos and get everyone here all worked up... 8) plus construction questions are more likely to get viewed in the construction thread... meanwhile, get the isolation to the level you need then work the treatments: the 3m ceiling height in the rehearsal space should be fine but you probably need to add something to the existing interior ceilings to get the isolation you need between the rooms. once that's done consider hanging some clouds to kill off floor-ceiling echoes. if your ceilings are the least level of isolation, theres not a lot of sense overdoing the windows - so if the MDF approach behind inside-out walls will be the rough equivilent of the ceiling, with some additional drywall, then thats likely to be the right choice. the existing windows that are boarded should be sealed. and assuming they're the equiv of the other sealed windows you should be good. on the inside windows between rooms and any parts of doors you need to resize, I'd use block. for trapping, in the sketches some of it is designed to reduce flutter echoes, add diffusion, as well as provide broadband treatment. what the ranges need to be on them will depend on room modes and what the acoustics measurements tell you once you have the rooms constructed.
