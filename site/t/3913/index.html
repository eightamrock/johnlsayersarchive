lovecow24 June 2005#10
Glenn, Not to add fuel to the fire, but I would like point out one more thing: Here's a good, objective way to compare Ethan's results to ours: • What we tested in 1996 amounted to 32 LENRDs.* The raw number of sabins (abbreviated "Sab" = units of absorption - like feet are units of length) measured at 125 Hz was 75.97. Divide this by the number of units tested and you get 75.97/32 = 2.37 Sab/LENRD. • Ethan's published number for LENRDs at 125 Hz (from the second table down on your link) is 9.47 sabins per 4' unit. Since LENRDs are 2' units, 9.47/2 = 4.74 Sab/LENRD. So what Ethan is showing is actually a higher value than what we measured. This is not "playing with the numbers." This is simply straightforward absorption. The purest way to compare the results. *The report says 48, but they were 16" units. Forty-eight 16" units = thirty-two 24" units. The final and current LENRD is a 24" unit.
