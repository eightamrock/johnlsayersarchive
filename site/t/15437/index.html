gullfo1 January 2011#16
you could just go with a nice large window and enhanced solid core doors instead of the sliders although a pair of exterior grade sliders can give you a pretty decent level of isolation. the glass on angles shouldn't be any more of problem than solid walls on angles :wink: the idea is to create a room within a room but if you're mostly happy with the isolation, the build the majority as treatment walls and use the angled walls to isolate the rooms. extend the isolation around the windows to the treatment framing to improve TL. the options depend on your total isolation needs versus some de-construction on the existing walls to avoid any triple leaf effects and the treatments you can afford to build.
