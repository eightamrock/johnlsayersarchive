sharward10 August 2007#2
Welcome to the forum, hildy. In general, drywall is cheaper and denser (and therefore better for isolation) than plywood. If you have lots of it on site, which is of no use to you otherwise, then yes, you can "dispose of it" in your build, as long as you are aware of its limitations and keep your expectations in check. Also, generally, one would not mount drywall plywood - the screws need to penetrate studs. You'd need to use longer screws so that they penetrate the studs at least 5/8". --Keith :mrgreen:
