gullfo20 April 2011#9
the dark spot is a trap which in reality would be part of the overall back wall treatments. on the 3" gap - if the ceiling mass and wall mass will join and seal, then it shouldn't be an issue. if it would be an opening in the isolation - then you need to cover it. so if you're using the concrete wall directly you'd extend out to it and seal. if you're looking to add another wall inside the concrete then the usual nesting of layers and sealing would apply.

Soundman202013 January 2012#11
do you put insulation between the concrete and the 2 layer's of drywall or leave it empty
Put insulation in, for sure. If not, it will cost you a lot of isolation (between 5 and 15 dB, roughly). So how do you hang the insulation in the cavity if there are no studs to attach it to? Insulation hangers: http://www.midwestfasteners.com/product ... teners.php
stud/drywall/drywall/air gap/stud/drywall/drywall?
Correct.
how much of an air gap do you leave between walls usually?
How much isolation do you need) (in decibels). And what is the lowest frequency you need to isolate? Those two help determine the size of the air gap, and also the amount of mass to put on each leaf. But don't ever go with less than 4" of air gap: it compromises isolation and pushes up the MSM resonance to frequencies where you do not it to be! - Stuart -
