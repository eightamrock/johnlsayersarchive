It is difficult to answer with such requirements, but as a response as detaield as the information given: Control room 100 m^3 with a 3.3 m ceiling height minimum. Live room 280 m^3. The control room dimensions are based on current control design recommendaions, and the live room based on the volume where individual low end modes are low enough that they are not a significant problem.
