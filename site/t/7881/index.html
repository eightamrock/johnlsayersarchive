Ro9 January 2007#2
Hai CaptainTaco and welcome to this mighty forum. What you're asking is a portable isolator. Very hard to maintain good isolation. Especially since MASS is the key factor in stopping waves from going through walls (ceilings/floors). So a see-through wall would be an option, and not very uncommon. Most Control Rooms have glass anyway. Not Plexiglas, it holds not enough mass. Glass is very heavy so good iso. But the construction takes some detail. There's much on that on this forum, so I'd suggest spending some more hours reading :)
