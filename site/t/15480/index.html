stevelaw11 January 2011#1
Hi, I need to treat/trap some bass energy around 125Hz and down. Ive already got some acoustic foam traps in the corners, but i feel they're a little inadequate. They did a good job of 125-500Hz though. So I think ill build some bass hangers to try and tame this problem area. Is there a size i should be aiming for.?? I was thinking of making a open box, wider than it is high, have a bunch of hangers in there with rockwool on all the sides and hangers, and cover the opening with some cloth. Thoughts..?? Cheers. s.
gullfo11 January 2011#2
some 4" absorbers - 2'x4' - would help. per the other thread asking the same question - you could then introduce some diffuser panels (small 2'x2' say) and tweak to bring some HF back up.
stevelaw11 January 2011#3
hey thanks.. are the 4x2 absorbers for first reflection points or bass trapping..? what'd you think of the bass hanger idea..?? s.
gullfo19 January 2011#4
the broad band trapping using the hangers is workable if you have the space.
