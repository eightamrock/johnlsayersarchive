John Sayers4 September 2008#2
Ok - if you line it totally with a layer of 703 you will drop the reverbtime significantly but because it's a small container the frequencies around the low mids will predominate as the 703 get less efficient the lower the frequency. Plus you will have sucked all the life out of the space - i.e all the high end frequencies. Adding angled slot resonators tuned to a broadband across the low mids with reduce the low-mid problem, break up wall to wall reflections plus add highs back into the space. The other space you have for treatment is the foot of depth above you as a 7' ceiling is fine. To that area you can either add 703 off the wall or install hangers. cheers john
