I'm not sure how much sound those steel doors would block though. Perhaps building something out of a layer or two of thick wood would be better, with some roofing shingles to help it withstand the weather?
There are two key issues you should take into account: mass, and seals. "Mass" as in making them heavy, and "seals" as in making them absolutely air-tight. I would aim for a mass of at least 4 pounds per square foot (20 kg/m2), and more if possible, across the doors AND the supporting structure. Plus good-quality seals all around, at least two complete sets. - Stuart -
