How about something like on of these: http://www.partsexpress.com/webpage.cfm ... oup_ID=372 If you really want to reduce the vibration transfer to the wall I'd suggest a massive support anchored to the wall studs. Go to your local scrap yard and find two thick (1/2" to 1") steel plates - wide enough to span two wall studs. Drill the appropriate holes in the center to attach the speaker mounting bracket and 4 holes towards the outside corners so you can lag screw the whole assembly into the wall studs. Thomas
