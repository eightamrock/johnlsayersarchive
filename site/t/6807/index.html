sharward12 August 2006#4
Foam, assuming it is fire resistant (unlike the crap they used in the infamous deadly fire at The Station in Warwick, Rhode Island), is for acoustical treatment, absorbing certain frequencies of sound so that they don't bounce around the room so much. It is not "soundproofing foam." There is no such thing. Any purveyor of the product who dares to call it "soundproofing foam" is a thief in my opinion. See this thread for a discussion on this fact. One of the reasons I talk so passionately about this is that I nearly wasted thousands of dollars on the lies that these criminals tell before I discovered this site and learned the truth about what it takes to increase noise isolation.
