Ted White5 August 2009#9
From a macro perspective, all of these clips work well to decouple. However as John said, you can decouple for less (and more effectively) with a double stud (room within a room) system. Ceilings become more of an issue since it's harder to insert a new ceiling joist system under the old one. So ceilings are often clipped to save height. Our website has various manufacturers data plotted as a comparison.
