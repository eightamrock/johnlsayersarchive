The idea of the economy of the 12 packs of Roxul is based on fact that I need to render that booth completely dead and that requires a lot of Roxul, but since the "inside-out-wall" has almost 99% of exposed rockwool (that I can cover with fabric) the room is already dead :D Basically the same rockwool serves as insulation and acoustic "killer".
To me, it sounds like you are planning to NOT put insulation in your air gap (the space between the inner leaf and outer leaf. If you do not put insulation in there, you will lose big time on isolation. Perhaps as much as 10 dB, or even 16 dB, will be sacrificed. Plus, the MSM resonant frequency of the wall will be much higher than it would have been if you used insulation correctly, which will compromise your isolation in the low frequency end of the spectrum. Building a wall inside-out does not mean that you can forget about putting insulation in the air gap. You still need to do that if you want good isolation. The only advantage from building inside out is that you can use the stud space (which is now inside your room) for acoustic treatment. But you still have to construct the MSM system correctly.
