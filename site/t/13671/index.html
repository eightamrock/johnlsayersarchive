Hi Folks - I posted initial plans a couple months ago and now am in construction. I am working on education my builder about sound transmission between a sound studio addition to an existing house in VT (USA). We are trying to devise a way to satisfy the need to stabilize the new construction for wind sheer, using the existing house without creating a hard connection, and therefore sound transmission, to the building. The builder discussed wall clips (RSIC-1 Resilient Sound Isolation Clip) with a sales engineer during his research and this system should achieve good results for stopping sound waves transmitting from the wall surface to the framing, but... A concern is, since the wall is built on the new floor system transmission of sound and vibration from the floor into the wall framing – and subsequently to the house (and visa versa from the house to the studio) - transmission will occur if we connect the new framing to the existing house in any way. The builder has devised some clips to use to tie the new wall to the house but we are looking for some verification that twelve of these connections won’t compromise the separation we have worked so hard to maintain. I have attached some photos to show the construction and clips proposed to aid our discussions.

rod gervais7 October 2009#4
Jim, Is he suggesting to you that due to the building height, if your (existing) building did not exist - he could not construct this building - and figure out a way to brace it? That seems absurd to me on the face of it........ I'm looking at the picture of your building - and it's a no brainer from my point of view. If the gable end opposite the existing building is properly braced from a structural point of view - all he has to do is build it's duplicate on the (existing) building side - insulate it and then structurally sheath the inside face of the wall instead of the outside (which is what he should also do with the existing building wall abutting it - this due to the 3 leaf system that the existing sheathing will create once everything is done). Rod

rod gervais7 October 2009#6
Jim, with isolated framing you are always throwing money away if you use clips of any kind - they gain you nothing. As far as drywall layers go - although the wall sheathing will add some mass to the inside - it is not really equal to the drywall mass - my recommendation would be the structural sheathing - plus 2 layers of 5/8" drywall and (if you can afford it) green glue between layers...... Rod
