Those two 'G' terminals may not be a common 'ground' connection (think about a bridged amp output). They may not be happy connected together.
Eric Best6 December 2017#3
Unfortunately it isn't that simple. Probably the best thing to do is to find the signal before it goes into the amplifier for the speaker. By taking the output out after the power amplifier, you will have level and impedance matching problems.
