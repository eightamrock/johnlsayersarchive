gullfo28 June 2007#5
you might want to read Sharward's thread in the construction forum. isolation for drums with close neighbors is very difficult, and expensive. A first step would be to do some testing to see how much isolation you need. get a sound level meter and some cooperation from the neighbors and have some one do some hammer (metal and rubber) tapping around while listening for open or flanking tranmission paths, then play some loud bass/drum music in your room and measure in the room and all around to get a sense for how much (or little) isolation you have at lower frequencies (which are typically the hardest to solve).
