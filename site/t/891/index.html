knightfly28 October 2003#2
The reason it doesn't look so good is that it's closed cell. For decent absorption, you need open cell foam. Even then, it's still foam. Depending on what project you're referring to, I'd most likely stay away. Free is only a bargain if it does the job - your labor is still involved, so I'd stay with proven materials... Steve
VSpaceBoy29 October 2003#3
I agree. Don't want to kick myself later for saving money. The only things, besides this guys "sales pitch" was in the reading when I saw: "The researchers discovered that foam with large, closed cells absorbs sound more efficiently than do open cells. The large cells absorb sound through a membrane mechanism, where a sound wave causes vibration of the surface cell walls that, in turn, induces vibration of the internal cell walls. The sound dissipates into heat by the motion of air molecules in the cavities. While the main insulation occurs due to the sound absorption, QUASH foam is revolutionary in the dual absorption that takes place in the cell walls. " SOUNDS good I thought.
