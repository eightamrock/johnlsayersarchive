knightfly12 June 2004#2
First thing - acoustics are not 2-dimensional; what's your available ceiling height, and what state of finish is the room in at the moment? (Open ceiling joists, insulated or not, what kind of insulation, wall framing (or concrete, brick, etc) - The more we know about what's already there, the better. Each type of building material has its own characteristics of absorption, and different thicknesses also change things. Fill in the details, I'm sure John will have some great ideas for dividing the space (your wanting an ISO will help get modes under control as well, by changing the dimensions of the CR space) If you're serious about acoustics, I hope you're willing to get rid of the carpet - and a rough budget would be good to know as well... Steve
