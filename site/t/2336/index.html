bassman16 September 2004#1
Hi all, I am looking at this building that has a cinderblock garage built on the back with a frame and shingle roof. The roofline goes from 9' internal upto 20' or so. I would like to have a studio that allows me to work in a surround capable control room and also have a secondary control room that doubles as a large booth during big recording dates. This second control room would be used to record things in the live room while mixing and other things are going on in the main control room. Also, just having a second system in an isolated space can speed up workflow by prepping sessions, editing, post-pro work etc... If I am going to drop 20-30K into building this (not to mention buying the building itself), the secondary income stream will be welcome. Here's a rough sketch of the layout I've got in my head. I haven't got the windows and sliders in yet, but they would go in the obvious places to maximize sight lines etc... The second control room is VERY narrow, I know. Nothing I can do about the odd dimensions of this place. Any and all ideas, bring 'em on. Have at it boys... (and girls...) -bassman
