Soundman202030 August 2009#6
If you are not getting answers to your questions, then maybe you aren't providing enough information! You ask if the roof needs soundproofing: The answer is: For what? What are you numbers? Real decibel numbers, measured with a real sound level meter. How loud are you inside? How loud do you need to be outside? How loud are you outside at present? Do you plan to record? Is there anything noisy close by, like a freeway, airport, railway track, factory, etc.? There is LOTS of info like that you need to provide in order to get answers. Right now, your question is along the lines of Is my string long enough?". The only possible answer is another question: Long enough for what? You might well know all these things, but we don't. Nobody can tell how loud you are or how much isolation you need, just by looking at your photos. - Stuart -
