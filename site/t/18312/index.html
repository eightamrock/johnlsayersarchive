Hi Brad, and welcome! First, please read the forum rules for posting (click here). You seem to be missing a couple of things! :) About your window: It sounds like you only got one half of the window: Studios are normally built with two-leaf walls between rooms, and each leaf is done to the same level, including windows and doors. It is the MSM resonance between the two leaves that provides the isolation, so it looks like you got only one leaf of that window. If you can't get the other one, then no problem: just use that as one leaf in your studio, and buy the glass to do the other leaf. Or you could see if it is possible to cut that glass, making two pieces 5 feet wide, and make your window like that. However, not all glass can be cut: laminate, for example, cannot be cut after it is made, so don't try to cut it yourself! Get a professional glazier to take a look at it, and tell you if he can cut it or not. A photo or two might help to better understand what you have! But if you listed that at US$ 100, I'm sure it will go really fast: That's a major bargain! - Stuart -
