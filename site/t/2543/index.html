I went to the SAE site and traced the corner CR design like this one above onto some wax paper (after taping the wax paper to the monitor, ha, telling you I have NO tools for this) After that I retraced over the waxpaper with a perm marker, and laid out the dimensions on the graph paper. I am considering each square on the graph paper to be 1 foot. The SAE drawing, when converted, would have worked out to be 22 by 17 feet. I then drew that out on the paper and added up the dimensions if my room were that size. The CR was kind of small for my liking. Then I went and drew my 21x21 space (21X22 technically) and added about 2 feet to all the CR dimensions. Then added up the dimensions for the other spaces. I can not beleive how this works out.
