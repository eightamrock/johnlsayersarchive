gullfo5 January 2007#8
if you open the sketchup you can see the walls. yes i'd recommend a false ceiling to add additional trapping because its such a very small space. the sliding door (esp if you make them pocket or french doors) would open up that wall for when you don't need the isolation. i think the single door and window will cause reflections as well. will the slider introduce more resonances or not? hard to say, the iso booth is very small and the angle wall will make things tight as it is. if you could do a full dormer on that back roof you'd get a lot more space and flexibility... the roof angle on the front would require you to push back your desk 5-6 feet to get enough height on any soffits. you probably should just use stands.
