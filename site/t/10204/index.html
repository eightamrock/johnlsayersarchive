Hi Here's the first idea, maybe you can mess about with it. If you post a drawing with the position of the posts and what can and can't be moved it would also help. Plus try and answer these questions in as much detail as you can as this will help with the layout decisions. What kind of music do you record? How many people do you want to record in the live room? Finally have the cash and am ready to start my project. I have taken the ideas of many and was satisfied with this layout. It satisfies my visual needs as well as the buildings existing structural needs. This drawing is just put together. My intent at the moment is getting isolated. after spend alot of time in the room , I realize it needs serious iso treatment from outside noise. mostly trucks and sub bass from car stereos, and birds and just about everything lol. I will be using roxul insulation for all of my insulation needs.
