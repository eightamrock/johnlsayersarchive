It's unique in that one side wall is sliding glass. Looking at Dynamike's design, some questions come to mind: 1. Does this mess up the "38% distance off the wall" rule? 2. It looks at if the bass trapping is handled on the bottom, but what about the top? Any suggestion for my room? The dimensions are: 11' wide by 10.5 feet deep. The left-wall has a 6-foot wide sliding door that begins at 18" from the front wall. The rear wall has a 30" door. Any suggestions or ideas would be appreciated.
gullfo18 December 2007#2
actually its 38% from the front (wall). and centered side-to-side. it can be problematic if the walls are parallel and untreated. one option is to put an absorber on a sliding hanger over the door so you can put it into place when needed. then treat the corners, side, ceiling, and back wall as usual with porous absorbers to cut reflections.

gullfo21 December 2007#6
its tight but i think your design is workable. my main concern is the nearly square room so you should plan on trapping in all the upper corners (soffits if you will) to get as much in the space as possible. the soffit angles may be too shallow for the listening position - they may need to be closer to 45 degrees instead of 30 (use 43 degrees). http://img180.imageshack.us/img180/4651/bradjacobs2kh9.th.jpg http://img299.imageshack.us/img299/2637/bradjacobs1rw6.th.jpg
