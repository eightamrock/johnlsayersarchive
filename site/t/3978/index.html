Xalky3 July 2005#5
Depending on the weight of the door, its a good idea to put an extra stud there. The hinge side of the door takes the brunt of the doors weight. The extra stud also helps to keep things rigid and straight in that area. The other side of the door frame is not as critical but should also have an extra stud. Framing lumber has a way of twisting and bowing when it drys, nailing 2 studs together keeps things straight and true over time. Marcel

Xalky3 July 2005#7
You toenail it. Drive a nail on an angle up or down into the plate. 2 nails on each end. Then at 12"- 16" intervals you'll nail 2 nails ,side by side, up the face of the stud so that the 2 studs essentially become one solid member. Marcel
