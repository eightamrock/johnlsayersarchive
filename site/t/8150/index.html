Hmmmmm... You stated...
I know I need one air in take. Can I get away with one blower or should I use two? I'm in MN and it does get cold, but the studio will have so much auralex in the walls, hear won't be a problem. I do have central air and think it would be nice to have the two blowers to bring in the a/c.
Now, I'm assuming you ALSO mean that along with the CENTRAL AIR you can also pump in HEAT in colder weather. Surely you are not depending on "body heat" to keep the area warm in the colder weather (ie: the Auralex walls...It's kind of hard to know your plans for heating as you could also be talking about some other type of heating system-device) Even if you do bring in air from a "blower" where is the stale air inside the room going to go? You need to be able to exhaust (some type of air return) the air in the room some kind of way. There are a lot of posts on here dealing with HVAC. Take the time to do some searching on this and other topics as needed.
