knightfly20 December 2005#3
1. How can I calculate the modes in these rooms. They are not Rectangle and all online calculator need Rectangle dimensions to calculate the modes. You will find it nearly impossible to calculate that shape room; measurement is about the only practical way. I have a program, CARA, made in Germany - it's very complex, and I think it might be able to do what you want - but I've yet to find enough time to dig into it deep enough to be sure. It's only about $70 euro for the whole package including tutorials, and there's an optional RS232 controller that their tech people told me will work with a USB adapter cable (the controller adds about $250 to the price) A faster, more practical way is to use a program like ETF and MEASURE your room - that way you can measure, make a change, and measure again to see the exact result. 2. When i listen to the controlroom I hear some resonances at 65 Hz, 108 Hz, 472 Hz etc. Is this correct, or do I hear it wrong. Probably not; but is your ear precise enough to measure frequencies like that, or are you generating those frequencies and listening for changes in volume? My plan is to build some Panel bass absorber for the rsonances in all edges, some "mid/High Absorber" with (18cm) mineral wool for the rest of the walls and the celling over the listening position. A better approach for nearly all rooms is first to do broadband treatments wherever possible (corners, including wall-to-ceiling corners, rear of room, "clouds" over the mix position - this will "even out" the response and may eliminate the need for "tuned" treatments, which are much more difficult to place correctly. For the Controlroom I will also put a little Diffusor on the rear to make the room not to dry. Your room isn't deep enough for diffusion in most cases; you need typically 10-12 feet behind your head before it's a good idea. In addition, if you're actually planning on 5.1 sound you'll want the room less live anyway; see this for more - http://www.grammy.com/pe_wing/5_1_Rec.pdf HTH... Steve (Your English beats the crap out of my German - no need for apology... :wink: )
