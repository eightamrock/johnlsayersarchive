Soundman202014 April 2011#56
Thanx Glenn...Do You think I have to fill the air space between concrete and framed? I've read that the air gap is suggested in some case.
the air gap is ALWAYS suggested if you want good isolation. And it is still an air gap regardless of whether or not you fill it with insulation. Like Glenn said: put in insulation to dampen the air gap. It acts sort of like a shock absorber on the springiness of the air. In fact, if you don't put it in, you will lose a considerable amount of isolation. - Stuart -
