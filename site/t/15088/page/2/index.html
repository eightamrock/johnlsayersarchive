gullfo9 February 2011#31
you could ventilate and cool off the stair area and do an air exchange from there with each room via baffle boxes. this would prevent the sound from leaking across rooms and give you a consistent air exchange (which if you're going through all this work) rather than risk issues with trying to open and close windows on a regular basis - wear on the seals, the isolation will probably be low to accommodate the movable windows, and CO2 can rise very quickly.

gullfo23 February 2011#35
in general, the wall studs will be placed on 24" (600mm) on-center. blocking between studs is common to increase rigidity and transfer load between studs and support insulation. using 1 or 2 top plates will be determined by number of layers of drywall and/or support for an isolation ceiling. the joists in the isolation ceiling will also run 24" (600mm) on-center if it supports the load intended. determining the stud and joist size are based on code, load, and space considerations.
