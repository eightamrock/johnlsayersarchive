a mini-split sytem cools /or heats the air in a room - It does NOT add fresh air to sthe system so considering that a soundproof studio is an airtight studio if fresh air isn't added you will die. of course people use them all the time as they open and close doors and windows in their houses to add the fresh air. For a sealed system you need a heat pump. here is a typical Heat Pump.
Image not preserved: sanyo_heat_pump.jpg
It consists of a fan and coil unit and an outdoor compressor unit. It's just like a split system except it is mounted in the ceiling and the air is fed via ducts into the room. It also allows for the addition of fresh air (15 - 25%) plus it is quiet as the unit is away from the room. Here's a typical ceiling installation of a heat pump unit. The flexible ducts are insulated (thus quiet).
External image, not preserved — original: http://johnlsayers.com/Guruland/Images/up4/aircon2.JPG
The outside unit and the inner fan unit are connected with pipes that carry coolent just like a split system. They are the black pipes in the above picture - the white pipes are for getting rid of excess condensation water.
