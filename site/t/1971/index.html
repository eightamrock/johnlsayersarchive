My first impression was similar. Needing to go through the drum room to get to the big practice room will annoy the drummers no end. edit.... Noticed that the title of the drawing file was churchmusic, so I am guessing that you are being asked to design a studio for a church campus. That caused me some more concerns. My experience is that once folks realize that there is such a nice sounding room on the church grounds there will be lots of requests to use it to practice...from folks who you do not want anywhere near the expensive gear in the control room. and mic closet. So I monkeyed around with it (attached diagram is very rough) so that every room has an independent entrance through the foyer. anybody can use any of the rooms for practicing while you keep the studio, and the expensive gear in the closet, secure.
