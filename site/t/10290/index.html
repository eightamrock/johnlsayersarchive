Hi all, a few question concerning diffusers and their design. Since the only book I could find elaborating on the subject is over 140 euros, I figured I 'd ask here first: 1) In the case of a QRD diffuser, do I have the design principles right? _Wavelength of lowest freq affected = 2x depth of deepest well _wavelength of highest freq affected = 2x total width of a single well _smoothness of diffusion increases with number of well depth variations 2) Does a diffuser need to be solid? two sketchup drawings as a reference to what I'm thinking about. Less material, lower construction cost. So I figured I'd pop the question. 3) Is there a minimum width to a diffuser well? I can imagine that, at some point, making a well too narrow would make it into a resilient obstacle for sound waves wanting to enter the diffuser. 4) I understand there's an upper limit to the width a diffuser should have. Exceeding this maximum would reduce effectiveness of the diffuser design. The details on this are very unclear to me. Could anyone elaborate on this? That's all I have for now. :) Sigert
