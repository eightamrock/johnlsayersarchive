JohnGardner11 June 2006#4
My room is about STC 55-60 and: We have a major road about 100feet away and even with double walls, suspended ceiling etc you can still hear the odd low engine rumble, especially when trafic stops/starts at the lights on a still night or some dude on a motor bike or car burns it up. I also found that when the neighbour starts the lawn mower you can still hear it clearly in my live room (greatly reduced, but it's still there) - luckly it's a small lawn and he only mows it once a month or so and I usually track at night when he can't mow!! I was surprised at what STC50 actually is: - it's not that much really, especially at the low end. JohnG
