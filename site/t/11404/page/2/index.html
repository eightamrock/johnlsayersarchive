xSpace wrote:
Joe, In order to get the best performance out of a mass/spring/mass wall assembly the spring has to be fully enclosed to do it's job. That enclosure is provided by the >unbroken< wall partitions that actually create the air cavity that the spring will exist inside of.
xSpace Thanks for the clarification. I believe I understand the point you are making now. Some additional information I would like to add is that the Control room and ISO room have their own ceilings completeley isolated from the floor above. The live room will use RC. Based on that, I believe that I have good isolation between A and C, A and F, F and C. Where things tend to have poorer isolation because of the M/S/M breakdown are between B (which represents the stairwell to the rest of the house) and A,F and C. Does that sound correct? Obviously C and D and E and F have the same type of issues. My only concern about the additional walls by the stairwell is the loss of square footage to an already small ISO booth. I know your are right, but it doesn't make the pill any easier to swallow. Thanks for taking the time to explain this. Dogonnit ! More Pondering...
