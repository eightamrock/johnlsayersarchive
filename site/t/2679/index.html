dymaxian19 November 2004#3
Good soundproofing isn't going to be cheap, and cheap soundproofing isn't going to be good. Carpet padding and laying mattresses up against the walls will make the room sound a little better inside, but won't keep sound from getting out. You'll most likely end up framing up around that smaller room you mentioned. If you've got neighbors who are that edgy, build a room-within-a-room and be done with it, and you won't have to worry about it anymore. The construction forum will tell you all you need to about it. The other thing you can do is get them involved just a little bit... when you get done with the room, let them know you're going to be playing down there and ask them to help you test it out by listening for it and letting you know how much they can still hear. That way they know you're trying.
