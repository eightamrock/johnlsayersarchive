cporro20 May 2009#2
i think those clips get you about 6-8db more isolation on the stc rating system. i couldn't find exact numbers for the difference between a standard wall framing (2x4, with a sheet of 5/8th on each side and the cavity filled with fibergalass) and the staggered wall. would you get more then 6-8 db? i'm not sure. but for a few extra inches why not do independent walls? with that you get like a 20db increase from the standard wall mentioned above. a different league from the clips and you can always ad more mass to the inner and possibly outer wall later for even more iso. imo (and i'm far from a pro) if you have the space independant walls are the way to go.
John Sayers20 May 2009#3
He's right - the genie clips are a quality RC clip if you use tham as suggested i.e. when you have a single stud wall frame.
