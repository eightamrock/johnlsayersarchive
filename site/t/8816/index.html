For any small room, there are two main goals: first, get the low end under control. This is done most easily by putting lots of thick absorption in the corners. In your case, the sharp corner above your head is prime, you could build a massive superchunk there, as well as in the other room corners. Second, create a RFZ at your mix position. This involves absorbing early reflections (have a friend move a mirror along all the walls while you sit at your mix position.... anywhere you see your speakers in the mirrors, put absorption there). Those are 2 very good rules of thumb to get you started.
