JCBigler17 October 2005#2
If you want the most bang for the buck, a PC laptop is the only way to go. A $1500 mac is a child's toy nowadays. If you're looking for enything with a 15" screen, then you're going to spend AT least $2000 on a mac, and probably a lot more. The AMD Athlon 64 based PC laptops are beating the pants off just about every other latptop. Check out Gateway, or Acer, or Compaq. I have a Compaq Presario R4000, with an Athlon 64 4000+ that runs at 2.4GHz, has 1 GB of RAM, an 80GB hard drive, and dual layer DVD burner, and more connectivity port than you can shake a stick at. I paid just over $1600 for it. However, I work mainly with Protools on it. As far as room analysis software, the most comprehensive system that I know about is Smaart Live by SIA Software: http://www.siasoft.com/products/smaart_live.html Though the price may be a bit high for you.
