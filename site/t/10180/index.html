Cram10 February 2008#4
What's the eternit made from? is it metalic? or of a fiber composite? Are you sure about durability with leaks? On the third example you're providing the largest amount of space between the first leaf and the second. Although you would have a bit of space created in the ribbon pattern of the roof, you have more material (insulation & space) to soften the frequencies before they get knocked down by the second layer. Plus - think of maintanence: Let's say you went through the effort to fill the gaps between the mass and the eternit. Something that formed to the bottom of the eternit or was pressed up against it - It would likely be problematic if you had to replace a section of it.
