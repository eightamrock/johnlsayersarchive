Hi Durk, and welcome! :) That's a rather complicated space you have there. I can't see how you would be able to set up a studio decently in there. You can't set up lengthways, due to the serious lack of symmetry, and if you set up crossways, then your head is way too close to the wall behind, and you have those angled windows/walls/ceiling to deal with. Is there any other room in the place that would be more appropriate to use as your studio? If you are not going to get that place, and instead plan to build something in your yard, then I would go with a more conventional rectangular shaped room.
the idea I have behind this would be that the weird shapes of the structure is really not such a bad thing for a garden office/room for a potential buyer for when we want to move.
Well, it LOOKS nice, yes, but it is just too impractical for a studio.
Where as a tuned room within a room would most likely drive the value of the house down instead of up.
Unless you build it so that it can also be re-used as a home-theater... :)
Budget ... 15k in euros for the whole thing.
I'm not so sure that is realistic for building an entire studio. If you had a good room that already exists inside your house, and you just needed to rebuild that to turn into a studio, then that would be reasonable, but not for a "ground up" studio. I would suggest that you re-think your budget, and plan on a more suitable shape. - Stuart -
