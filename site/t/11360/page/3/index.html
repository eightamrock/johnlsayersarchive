Soundman202028 August 2011#55
One question. If I build a superchunk corner bass trap with high density and thick foam instead of rockwhool or fiberglass would it be ok? could I expect similar results?
Depends on the foam! You'd need to look at things like the gas flow resistivity at the frequencies you need to isolate. But in general, heavy dense absorption is not good for low frequencies: better for highs. Low frequencies generally need lighter insulation. That said, it all depends on the specific characteristics of the specific foam you have in mind. (of course, it MUST be open-cell foam: closed cell is no use). - Stuart -
