That would be OK as a VERY temporary fix, but it's not a good idea to put wood over carpet longterm - mites and other little crawly friends seem to love carpet and carpet pad, and once the wood is down permanently you can't get rid of the little buggers - they also help carpet rot when it can't get air... Steve
