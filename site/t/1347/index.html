Ethan Winer17 February 2004#2
TB, STC specifies sound transmission, which has nothing to do with foam. Acoustic foam and fiberglass are spec'd by how well they absorb sound at various frequencies. That's completely different from how much a material blocks sound. For a more complete explanation see the Acoustics FAQ, second in the list on my Articles page: www.ethanwiner.com/articles.html --Ethan
