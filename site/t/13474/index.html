xSpace14 August 2009#10
krishna, The largest issue with your personal design is that you are making square rooms. A square floor plan has all walls with the same length dimension, E.G. 20 feet in length and 20 feet in width. Control room, check, it's square. Vocal booth, check, it is also square. I can only assume that this is news to you, but a square set of boundary measurements is not considered a good starting place for any acoustic room. Which is a real good reason for accepting what John has drawn for you, gratis, and running with it!
