One simple way to find out if you have the right density, is just to get some and weight it! For example, get a cubic meter of it, put it on your bathroom scales, and see how much it weighs... :) Or if a cubic meter costs too much, get a quarter of a cubic meter, weigh that and multiply by 4. Also, many manufacturers do provide those figures on their web sites, even if it isn't marked on the product packaging itself. And some building supplier web sites also list that in their product details on their web sites. - Stuart -
