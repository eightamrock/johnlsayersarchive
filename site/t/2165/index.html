John Sayers22 August 2004#4
Brian - the slot resonator across the front wall will help break up the parallels - yes the corner traps are as you described. For you studio I'd use a combination of slots and HF treatment. The slots will absorb the low-mids -(a typical problem in a room that size) whilst still keeping the highs active thus avoiding that "DEAD" room sound you get when it's all high end absorption. What's the ceiling height in the room?? Take a look at the booth at Left Bank - it has ceiling hangers as well as the slots - there is lottsa glass too. I've recorded drums in there and they sound great. (not live but very tight and even) cheers john
