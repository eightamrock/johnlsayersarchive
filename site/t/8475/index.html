jwl29 April 2007#7
I don't think you are going to want to add another set of joists, as that will create another leaf. What I would start with is adding mass to the floor that is there, probably by using MDF or chipboard or something, then adding a hardwood floor or whatever on top of that. I could be wrong, but my gut tells me that the added leaf is a bad idea....
