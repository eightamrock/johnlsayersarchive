Soundman20204 March 2011#4
The reason I asked the above question, is because there seem to be some conceptual problems with that drawing. The basic idea is fine (rough dimensions, layout, etc.) but the isolation and treatment concepts seem to be a bit confusing. For example, there is a 3-leaf window between the CR and the LR, but no isolation walls! 3-leaf is always a bad idea, but why go to all that trouble when there is only a single leaf wall? Also, the angled side walls on your CR cannot be gypsum with that design: you seem to be confusing isolation with treatment. Those should probably be slot walls. To answer your questions: The front angles depend on your speaker geometry, and what you are trying to accomplish. The diagonal "walls" at the rear are not walls either! They are your bass traps. The angle there is not important at all: just make them as wide and as deep as you can. Most people try to make them 36" (90cm) wide (the diagonal) and angle them at 45°, but neither of those is critical. As long as you have them at least 60 cm wide and somewhere around 30° to 60°, you'll be fine. But it is important to know how much of that has been built already, and what can be changed. - Stuart
