lex12522 July 2004#6
I'd suggest going with one decent sized room instead of splitting the space up into two smaller spaces. If you do a good job of treating the room, you should not have any problem recording vocals or acoustic guitar in there. For electric guitar, you have a few options: 1. turn down (not likely) 2. get a smaller amp 3. build or purchase an enclosure for your amp I like the 3rd option - it allows you to use your current amp and crank it as loud as you like, without disturbing anyone. And building (or buying) one of these is a lot cheaper than building out a separate booth & control room. Your existing room isn't large, but its big enough that it would be a pleasant place to sit around and play guitar or sing. Building a tiny booth creates a closet like atmosphere that few folks find comfortable. If you can get away with a one room layout, I think you might prefer it in the long run. Here's a link to several commercial products and DIY designs for amp isolation chambers: http://www.amptone.com/index.html#iboxprods good luck
