bassman12 March 2009#2
neoprene, 1/4" thick, the squishy stuff.... You might need the harder neporene to go underneath the window pane itself, but the other stuff goes all the way around on both sides of the pane. i got my stuff at a dedicated rubber supply place. maybe ask an HVAC or glass guy. -ashley
