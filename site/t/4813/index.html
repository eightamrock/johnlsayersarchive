The original drawing has better left to right symmetry for the mix position. For the wooden traps, you can cover with cloth IF you space the cloth 1/2" or so away from the front panel. If you just wrap with cloth, it will damp the front panel somewhat and make it less effective as it will be less free to vibrate at it's target frequency.

derekdun101 wrote:
1.On Ethans do it yourself low and high bass traps he actually uses ACX plywood to be painted. The cloth he uses on the mid/high traps. Thats why I was asking if using cloth on the bass traps was ok.!
Ethan's Low bass traps (the ones with 1/4" plywood fronts are a sealed system. They work because the plywood spanning the frame vibrates in sympathy with the sound waves and this vibration is absorbed by the system of the box and dissipated as heat (albeit a very small amount of heat!!) They absorb the most sound at their reasonant frequency therefore the size and shape of the box will affect how they work. My guess is that covering them with cloth will probably have only very small, if any effect in their ability to absorb low frequencies..... is it just for aesthetics that you want to cover them as opposed to painting them?
derekdun101 wrote:

There is no right or wrong here on the height issue. Only varying degrees of effectiveness based on practicality. I have probably the worst room you can have (11'x11'x9'). For practical purposes I built my absorbers 8' tall (the length of precut 1x4 lumber) and hang them with a half of foot free at the top and bottom.
