knightfly14 August 2006#36
OK, now I see why your isolator supplier doesn't think you could hang more gypsum - Here's what I would do for your ceiling - Step #1 is to talk to your supplier of hanging hardware, and find out their recommendation for spacing of hangers and track that will support at least 3 layers of 15mm gypsum - this will weigh approximately 38 kG/M^2. If your supplier is helpful, you will know how many more supports will be necessary for the added weight - remove the layer of gypsum CAREFULLY so you don't ruin the hangers and steel channels - then, add enough hangers and channel so they will support the weight of more gypsum. I would offset the hangers so they are not all in a straight line; this will give more even support for the ceiling. If this is confusing, say so and I'll do a basic sketch. In the meantime, if you've not already done so you should go here http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=503 and download the spanish version of the USG manual. Again, do NOT tear out anything until you have solid answers from your hardware source and ME... Steve
