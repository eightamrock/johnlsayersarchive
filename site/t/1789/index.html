knightfly13 June 2004#2
I'm not seeing any modal reasons for your peaks - can you post a drawing showing EXACT dimensions between walls, floor, ceiling and your head and speakers? Speakers, - how far from each wall, measured from center of woofer - ditto floor and ceiling. Head - same measurements. You have one axial mode at 113 (2nd harmonic of the 10' dimension), slightly supported by a tangential at 112 - not enough by itself to cause your problem. Other than that, a slight hole in modal density at around 55 hZ is all the dimensions show. However, positioning in the room can cause you or your speakers to be located in an antinode of one of your modes, and that WILL cause particular frequencies to be exaggerated. If you can post a plan showing all the above distances, I can probably point you toward a fix... Steve

knightfly16 June 2004#4
Your drawing was a bit rough - I resized the important part and scaled locations of your head and speakers (the circles) - I also superimposed the axial mode plots for length and width, and drew red lines for no-no's and green ones for good placement - so wherever horizontal and vertical green lines meet (and it's still symmetrical) is a good location - in some cases, just a few inches will make a noticeable difference. Your two problem frequencies are the second harmonics of height and width - for symmetry, you need to be centered left to right so there's not much that can be done about that one - if you could get your head/speaker height a bit further from centered vertically it might help the higher of the two frequencies. Your speakers and head are shown where they are now(the yellow circles) - you could try moving the speakers away from you and outward to the next green intersection (the green circles); this would put them at 44" from sidewalls and 32-33" from the front wall. Your head would move back about 2" mainly to keep close to an equilateral triangle. Does the irritating radiator go floor to ceiling, or could you possibly put some rigid fiberglass insulation across the corner ABOVE the radiator for a broadband trap effect (which would be duplicated in the other front corner)? I don't think this is the problem, it would just help if you could get symmetry side to side. Can't think of anything to add right now, maybe something else will come to me later... Steve
