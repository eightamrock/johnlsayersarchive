Just thought I would pass this link along. It shows a detailed construction schedule (among other things) for a house. While a great deal of it may not apply to your studio construction, it does give some insight into how long things normally take ('normally' is well caveated on the page). It also may help you to remember things to include in your project that you haven't thought of. http://www.b4ubuild.com/resources/schedule/6kproj.shtml Of course, the schedule is one company's or person's opinion, so take it for just that. But I don't see anything with which I radically disagree. BTW, if you try to cut-n-paste into an MS Project file, you will get some errors because of a couple of missing lines (23,24). But it can be done. :) p.s. I searched before I posted to see if this had already been shown. Forgive me if I missed it.
