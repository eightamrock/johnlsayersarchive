Nice, but how thick are your walls? And how many walls you have? You want one super door in a double wall infrastructure? Let's, for argument sake, say you have a SINGLE wall which is 3x12mm(1/2") GB. Total 36mm (1,5"). If you make a door with a MDF core (for mass) then you'll need the same thickness. Since glass has 1/3 of a mass of GB (or MDF) you'll need 12mm glass. single sheet. there you go.

Let the construction begin... I started building the door saturday morning, it was a beautiful day here in Ireland, 19 degrees, a great day to build a super door. I bought 10 second hand firedoors a few months back for €100 and I will be using one of these as the core for this super door...
