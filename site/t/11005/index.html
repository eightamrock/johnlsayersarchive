rod gervais23 July 2008#8
Ah, yes I do know that room well - as well as the rest of the rooms at Avatar........ OK - I know so much about those rooms and walls that I cannot tell you how they are constructed. BUT - I can tell you that the board are not directly attached to the drywall........ And I can give you a suggestion of how to proceed......... You could use your board instead of drywall seeing as yuo are not interested in isolation. If you haven't framed your walls yet - frame them with 2x6 at 2' centers - then insulate them with fluffy insulation - cover with your material and add strapping out of 2x2's installed parallel to the floor with 24" clearance between the 2x2's. Now install 1 1/2" 703 tight between the 2x2's - covering with a flame retardant cloth - and then the boards. I wuold space the boards 1 1/4 apart on the side walls and 1" apart on the rear wall. I would stop them shy of the corners and install corner broadband treatment there floor to ceiling. I hope that helps. Rod
