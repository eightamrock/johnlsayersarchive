Clunk Lou's studio build (located in Other Studio's) has a couple of diagrams which his pro builder allowed him to publish, that shows a multi room air con setup. In our geography, there is not much DIY knowledge on subject and to be honest, even when i approached a local guy who did a site survey of my 1 room garden studio, it was obvious to me that the best advice was on this forum. Ok, having spent some more time researching and discussing the set-up we have a basic set-up plan, though I should say there are still some issues unresolved. Our plan is to use an 8" in-line duct fan to push air into the control room and vocal booth via insulated ducting. The fan will be placed in a box measuring 2' , 3' , 3' , built into the upper-right hand doorway, an 8" duct will be run from the fan to a splitter box from which 3* 6" ducts will travel to baffle boxes ( 12" x 10" x 32" ) with 12" x 12" openings into the rooms ( 2 boxes into the control room, one to the vocal booth). The exhaust side of the system will travel via baffle boxes and ducting to the wall vents as per diagram below. A similar set-up will be used in the tracking area, with only 2 baffle boxes for the intake and 2 for the exhaust. The baffle boxes have already been made, the ducting and fans have also been ordered. The fan we have ordered can be used for either exhaust or intake so all we have to decide is which way the system will run ie: Do we use the fan to pull air through the system or do we use it to push air through Any suggestions on this would be appreciated.
