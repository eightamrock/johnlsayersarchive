delusion2 August 2009#27
Hey Ross, My Iso Room ceiling actually has a somewhat similar shape to yours. Here's two more pics. One with just the staples and the next one with the wood trim started. I don't think you have worry about height restrictions with the wood trim though. I just used 1/4" wood for the trim, and you could even get away with 1/8" if you really needed to. I don't think something that thin would really encroach on the room too much. To save money, I just bought a 4'x8' sheet of plywood with a nice finish on the face and used a table saw to cut out a bunch of strips probably about 1.5" wide or so. A sheet was only about $12 US and you'll get a lot of trim out of a single sheet.

Ross Peters9 August 2009#32
Thanks for the tip delusion, if the trim is really thin like you suggest, I'm sure it will be fine. If I can find some 3mm strip wood, that would be perfect. Thanks Steve, I really can't wait now! The interior framework does have plasterboard behind the fibreglass, but because I was trying to maximize the internal dimensions, I decided to put it on the inside of the framework rather than the back- the plasterboard panels have the same dimensions as the fibreglass panels. I cut the plasterboard panels as tight as possible, and they're held in place with caulk and nails, and obviously friction. It's surprisingly strong! Good luck with your framework, I really enjoyed putting mine up.
