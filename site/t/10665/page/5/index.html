Eran18 October 2008#108
Hey thanks Ro and jinglesmountjoy! I spent two weeks exclusively around mother and baby, lots of work! But now i'm back to studio and had a very productive day today. After installing the electricity at the beginning of the week and mounting the A/C unit i painted the room and started working on the wood panels. I finished the first batch today: One wall in booth and two 4' sections one on each side of the CR. I'm not so happy with the paint but what to do... Installed one of th two windows between CR and booth and mounted the fans on the concrete wall in the basement. I first attached them to the floor joist and that was way too loud. Important point: If you buy solid core doors from the home depot you should know they are not really solid... the inside is made of some sort of particle material not suitable for fastening. So if you cut beyond the few inches of pine at the perimeter you need to reinforce the door. I left the hinge side untouched so i only had in issue with mounting the handles. Solution was a sleeve they sell as a security protection for handles. Pics...
