John Sayers27 October 2004#5
Ok - I see what you mean. I'd get them to install the ducts running along your outer walls and up into the house from there. They usually run them down the middle which creates problems when you want to build room within rooms around them. I'd also get them to supply a feed to the entrance area so you can feeed off that later on to supply to studio. There are 4 kinds of ducts as I see it. 1. standard galvanized steel duct with no insulation. 2. the above but with semi-rigid fibreglass internal lining 3. Semi-rigid fibreglass duct with no outer gal iron sheath. 4. flexible duct made from light aluminum with a fibreglass outer lining wrapped in plastic. Version 2 is the best IMO as it is the best for sound isolation - and I'd request that version to feed the house and for the duct running to the entrance area if you want isolation from the studio to the house. From that central point in the entrance area you can then run version 4 to the respective rooms. hope this helps cheers john[/list]
