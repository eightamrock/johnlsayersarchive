Ethan Winer20 February 2007#4
Ponch wrote:
how does one calculate this with diagonal walls?
Is this a room that's already built, or a proposed design? If the room is already built, it doesn't matter what a mode calculation says because 1) mode predictions are never 100 percent accurate, and 2) there's nothing you can do about it anyway so just treat the room with as many bass traps as you can manage. Also, as Marc said, a common approach is to average the dimensions. Marc is also correct about measuring the room if it's already built. That's the only way to know what you really have. --Ethan
