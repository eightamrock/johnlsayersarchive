Greetings Redar, Welcome to the forum! I'm pretty sure someone else will chime-in on your question but just be patient for a response. Many of the moderators are tied up and can't get to some questions right away. I would recommend you give more details on the tower. I'm not the one to tell you what you need to know in this area but if you provide more info the odds are better for a response from someone "in the know".
