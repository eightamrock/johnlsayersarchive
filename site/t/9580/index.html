Aloha,
I wanted to see if this simple design would work for my small control room.
because you don't want rear reflections to interfere it is better to start with a longer front to back dimension than the side to side dimension. John Sayers SAE website http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html
Please excuse the scale accuracy and the drawing itself. I don't have anything too fancy in terms of design software.
Try here - it's free! http://sketchup.google.com/
I also have the option on using inside out walls for this space but am wondering if the angled slot resonators would be better.
You could build the walls left/right of mix position as inside-out walls/slat resonators.
Also I plan to put a cloud directly over the monitoring space. Ceiling height = 8' and the Entry door=3' wide.
Yes, that would be a good idea, if not two (or more.)
How much treatment does a small room like this need?
While every room regardless of size needs treatment, its been stated here on the forum that the smaller the room, the more treatment it requires. Typically in rooms this size you would need - corner bass traps, angled slat walls left/right of mix position, front wall absorber, rear wall absorber, ceiling absorber (cloud/s). Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
