the dreamer16 January 2005#2
hi, read this to get a better overview http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html and also visit the sticky section in the construction forum for soundproofing ideas. i attached a way to layout your studio you can play with. i'm sure the regulars here will add or correct on it. :) the sides are slat resonators. the portable gobos are for mixing and you can easily move them away from the doors. somewhere on the forum is a thread and a gobo design from john. the wall/ceiling corners are also a good place to add broadband absorbtion with rigid fiberglass. the ceiling will need heavy absorbtion also. at least above the mix position. cheers dreamer
