cvanas17 July 2008#1
'...til my puzzler was sore... Hi All. I recently purchased a house which has an empty 22'x 25' outbuilding I would like some help in designing a small commercial studio. Currently in the research stage, I am hoping to build into this existing structure a control room, studio and a vocal booth. My experience lies mainly recording pop/rock groups and would like to be able to record live drums in the studio. The surrounding neighbours' houses are 30-40 feet away. Existing construction: The floor is concrete slab. The walls are cinderblock insulated with 4" yellow fiberglass, plastic vapour barrier and covered with particle board. The roof is corrugated tin. There is no insulation in the ceiling and it is also covered with particle board. There is one entrance door and one wall contains a set of 4 windows. It's the building's ceiling design that has me puzzled. Floor to ceiling height for three of the four corners is 7' 5". The fourth and final corner has a floor to ceiling measurement is 10' 6". I have attached some crude sketchup jpegs and photos to try and show the design of the sloped ceiling. I can't seem to get my head around where to put what. Because of the ceiling's design, two of the 7'5" high corners would have the control room ceiling sloping left to right (or right to left). The remaining 7'5" corner would have my back to the rest of the room and the 10'6" corner leaves me with the least ceiling height for the studio. I have a budget of 10k, but I have yet to research material costs here in Switzerland (judging by the cost of everything else here, 10,000 should just about buy me a bag of insulation). I am hoping to do most of the work myself. Any recommendations would be greatly appreciated. Thanks, Cam.
