"I,ve read that to angle the side walls won't be the best solution, because of that i was planing to put superchunks traps in each corner." This is not an "either or" type of situation. Angling the early reflection walls is highly regarded around here and so are the use of broad band absorption (super chunks) in the front corners of the room. Often the two go hand in hand, when the walls are canted you will have hollowed out space that did not exist before you moved (angled) the walls. So you add absorption behind this wall.
