Question about ceiling

Started by tgs on 31 March 2008. 2 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 10405.

tgs31 March 2008#1
Ok, our studio construction is almost done and it seems to turn out very well. We did a sound test the other day though where we went to the neighbor upstairs to check for sound leakage, and there is a slight leakage through a ventilation duct. The duct goes right behind/above the recording room, and from this duct air is distributed individually to the rooms above. Due to a HUGE oversight, the duct wasn't properly sealed before construction began. It has one layer of gypsum board so it's boxed in and the box is filled with insulation, but it's not sealed at all and even have some gaps between the gypsum boards here and there. I've been up there, crawling on top of the recording room ceiling trying to seal the box a bit more, but it's not much more than a foot of distance so it was almost impossible to work up there and I couldn't reach properly. So the only way to reach would be to tear everything down and that's not going to happen of course. The only thing that I can do as I see it is to fill up with more insulation on top of the recording room ceiling construction. Like I said, there's about a foot available, although it's less at place where the actual duct is. I have the opinion that it will be almost no difference at all, but my dad suggested that we could at least try. So I want to check with you guys what you think. Would it make any difference to roll out more insulation up there? I can add that the sound you hear through the duct is kind of mid/treble sounding so it doesn't seem to be a problem with bass frequencies.
