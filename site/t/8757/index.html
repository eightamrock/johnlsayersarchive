Most of the designs I've seen use a false wall in front of the bass hangers, built out with studs with insulation (703, rockwool, cotton) installed, covered with cloth. They do NOT use drywall, though there are ways to build a wall like this with bass hangers behind it that do use drywall.

Standard broadband using rockwool, 705 is your best bet - it's the least expensive - good looking treatment you can build.

Typically you would do a fabric wrap over the rigid insulation and then hang it off the wall by a few incles (for general broadband) or straddle the corners (to maximize LF benifits.

Now you are building a membrane device - which would be depending on the density of the drywall what frequency it was - and even with insulation behind it (after an air space - you can't touch the panel) it is only good to about a 1/2 octave abover and below it's center frequency.

I could be dense - but i do not see how a broadband absorber is going to act as a diffusor as well.

DOn't waste your time with panel resonators - you will never have just 2 problem frequencies to deal with - you are MUCH better off sticking with broadband -

Like I said - stick to broadband
