I'm guessing John saw a way to get a "free" isolation room in the design and thought the amp room would be a good use. 8) worst case you put the amp into one of the bigger rooms if you need room input in the recording... otherwise the small amp room is primarily for isolation purposes and using a close mic. that said, you could treat the room with reversible treatments to switch it from dead to live or something in between for different effects.
