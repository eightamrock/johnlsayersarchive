you could block off the interior of the window space and leave the exterior window. leave enough opening for a small vertical window on the front of the CR so you can get some daylight into the front of the room and you'll add a heavy window on the isolation wall. plus you have the large window door on the back of the room. on the back slider you might be able to add something like the RPG clear treatments for diffusion or absorption.
