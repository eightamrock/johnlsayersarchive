Soundman202024 January 2014#2
Hi there "prairiechicken", and Welcome! :)
Let me know if you like this layout or if you see any major problems with it
The live room looks fine, but the control room is not going to work. It is way too small, it isn't symmetrical, the shape is not right, and the listening position is way too close to the rear wall. You seem to be wasting some space with the entrance lobby, and also with the vocal booth, so you could drop those out of the plans entirely and use the extra space to fix the control room. For example, to me it looks like you could do a proper corner control room with a good size, shape and layout, and still keep the overall size of the live room about the same as it is now. There really isn't enough room in there to add a vocal booth and a lobby: the space is just too small for that. Record your vocals in the Control Room instead! :) - Stuart -
