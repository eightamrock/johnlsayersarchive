John Sayers25 April 2003#16
Just a point to remember and note. When you work out the reverb time of a room using say the calculator at SAE it uses the wall areas and works on the absorption in that area. Now if you look at the area you have treated compared to the total area you will find you haven't added much treatment at all, whereas if you look at my plans you will will see I use whole walls of treatment, not just small sections. cheers john
