OK - In the book - my calcs are as follows: First - the wall is indicated as pinned to the concrete floor - so a single top brace on 8' centers will actually pick up 4' of an 8' wall if mounted at the top of wall. I do show a mount of 6" below top of wall. 8 x 4 = 32 sf of wall per brace. 2x4 framing for an 8' wall (double top brace) is 33# PCF for Doug Fir - and the wall section contains 2 cf per 8x4 top wall section. Thus 66# of framing load. Drywall - 32sf x 2 layers of 5/8" (at their 2.7# per sf per layer) = 172.8# of drywall - For a total load of 238.8 pounds per clip - max allowable is 250 - loading is OK Center frequency is 15Hz x 238.8/250 = 14.328 Hz. Andy, Well that all depends on what it is you construct. Take my example - it works- so why bother throwing money away? On the other hand - if you use 3 sheets of drywall - or step the wall height up to 9' - then it over loads the clips - so you would have to change the installation. In that case though - I would opt for the same top of wall - but layout at 4' enters. The only way I would start installing at the center line of wall horizontally would be if I was constructing some very VERY tall walls - and then would use the clips to aide in lateral bracing to stop the wall from wanting to hinge in the center under bending stresses. I hope that helped. Rod
