Ro2 November 2009#10
Recording.org has a forum which handles these subjects as well. My advise; - heavy, sturdy case (more mass is better isolation) [have a look at the antec p180 for example] - passive cooled devices. Don't you hate that little fan on your gfx card ? Look into fanless ones - same goes for power supply, there are fanless designs. or look for big fans which can rotate slowly.. calculate how much power you need and choose your power supply. 75% load is effecient. - speaking of which: use a minimum of fans and keep'm as large as possible (12cm), turn them down to rotate on low speed - use rubber hd suspension kits (or have'm build in the case) - watercooling is an option. - use a big cooler ribbon for ye cpu, might use a fan if it's getting too hot. - keep good airflow in your case to optimize cooling. good flow means less fans needed to push air around - if you don't need *that* device (fill in the blanks), don't put it in. Less heat is better. good luck for who ever reads this.
