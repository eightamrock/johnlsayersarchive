Hi Eddie, I think you need to give a little more information for people to help you, it doesnt matter if you are paying our not thats not going to be an issue, people will help you but only if they see its going to come to some use. I would add this info then see what happens: Whats your budget for this project? What do you want out of the space ie is rehearsal and a little mixing your priority or will it be more of a mixing studio with small live room....basically what do you want to be able to record? Full band or solo instruments etc. You definately dont want your entrance to be straight into the live room as you will be trapped in the control room if a band is in there. What are the ceilings and floor made from? Personally looking at the plans I would flip it and make the current live room the control room and take it down to 14.9ft and use that odd shape of the room for a double door system into the new live room. 8)
