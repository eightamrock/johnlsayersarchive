Hi there "Hovak". Welcome. Please read the forum rules for posting (click here). You seem to be missing a couple of things! :) That said, the type of fabric you use on a bass trap isn't important at all. You can use pretty much anything you want. What's important for bass traps is dimensions, location in the room, and the type of insulation material. The fabric is only there for visual reasons: to hide the ugly stuff inside the bass trap. Fabric serves no acoustic purpose at all. - Stuart -
