Soundman202027 August 2013#3
Both brick and concrete are about the same density (around 2000 - 2400 kg/m3), so there won't be a lot if difference, no matter which way you go on choice of materials, for the same thickness wall.
what are your thoughts on the bricks being laid in the other direction like this:-
That would make the wall much thicker, and therefore increase the isolation, as compared to a simple stretcher bond wall. You might also want to consider doing English bond or Flemish bond, if you want to make it that thick, for better structural integrity. - Stuart -
