FYI, floating floors are generally no longer recommended around here except in very specific circumstances, and if and only if they are engineered properly. Most floating floors that are believed to be engineered properly are most likely not. It seems you have an active thread in the design forum. You may want to start a project thread here in the Construction forum as well where you can transition the focus of your project from design it to building it. Once you have that, you can keep all your questions together in your project thread. This is important because the answers you receive will be more targeted to you and your specific situation... What's right for your case may not be right for another, and vice-versa. 8) --Keith :mrgreen:

Background Information: I am a student and I'm lucky enough to have convinced my parents to add an extention to our house for a small studio room. I want to be able to achieve as much isolation as possible from the rest of the house, so that I can have my audio levels within the extention reasonably loud. One thing that has been drilled into me is the need for 2 leaf walls, which are not attatched to one another to achieve maximum isolation. I won't go into too much detail here, as this is just a post to sketch out some ideas of the more detailed construction elements. Basically I *think* I need to keep the leftmost walls isolated from the ones to the right, as the right ones fall within the "studio" and the left are part of the existing house construction. The wall can not be left "open" though, as it would look a bit odd with a gap in the wall! Sorry if this is a bit vauge. Feel free to ask for any details.
