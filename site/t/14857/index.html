gullfo11 October 2010#8
using REW (or FuzzMeasure on Mac) and a Behringer ECM8000 is pretty common choice for this. if not any omni mic with a flat frequency response should do it. put the speakers in their approx position on stands (cement block can suffice) and use a boom mic stand to position the mic vertically at your approx listening position for data on that spot, and do another trace - put the mic in a corner and a speaker in the opposite far corner to get the overall room response.
