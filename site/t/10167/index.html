guitardad727 February 2008#2
Guilford of Main Fabric or Burlap. The same material used to cover fiberglass exposed into room for acoustic purposes. Material has to be fire rated/safe for application. Material is attached to both inner and outer assembly. There will be a little flanking but the point is to keep the fiberglass and other material particles in the wall. Being safe in this circumstance over ways the small amount of flanking caused. I believe Rod has a very good diagram of double door assembly in his book. This has been asked and answered on the forum. How ever, like many other questions with out the right search terms and the endurance to skim many pages looking for the answer it can be challenging to find. I find using using my Google Tool bar and hitting "search site" is the best method of searching for answers. Marc
