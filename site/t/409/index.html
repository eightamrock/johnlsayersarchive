Hi, I'm still calculating the cost of my soon-to-be studio, which is going to be double wall construction, which the existing walls are brick walls. First, I'm going to use the usual recommended gypsum board on wood frames, with insulation inside the frame. But when I compared the construction cost here in Indonesia, I found that cement-plastered brick wall construction is far more cheaper than the gypsum board on frames with rockwool insulation.
