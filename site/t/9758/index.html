EK Sound23 November 2007#3
I agree with Glen on this... for your listed equipment, 1 - 15A circuit will be lots. If you do find the need to add another circuit, be sure to tell the electrician to pull it from the same panel *AND* the same power "leg" (the electrician will understand). This will assure that both circuits are "in phase" Dave.
