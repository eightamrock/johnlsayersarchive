These days I'm producing mostly rock and pop stuff, but I used to (and still sometimes do) hip hop records, which is what I got my start in as a DJ and hip hop producer. Because of that, there's a lot of history in my old record collection, and I was wondering if there would be a way to utilize the collection as diffusion somehow. I think building a custom section on the rear wall of the control room and using all the old records would be super cool, but I wouldn't want to do it unless it would actually be effective. Any thoughts on this? Thanks guys!
gullfo1 November 2010#2
one option would be to make a cube shelf and stack in cube with a different number of records to create what appears to be a large 2D QRD diffuser. a small edge in the front of each to make sure the records don't slip out and also are slightly angled. not sure how this would work practically, with enough distance it may be useful. a set of heavy drapes to pull over it might ultimately make it work.

gullfo7 November 2010#6
In the event they reflect too much, having some drapes to pull over them may tone it down a bit. Plus keeps the dust off them... The reason for not stacking with the edge out was to cause small angles in each box. Depending on your taxonomical ordering system, you could group by style, artist, etc. So long as you don't try to organize in pairs of three and forming circles, or in height order alphabetically, you should be ok... :D
