Carpet vs plywood on 705

Started by tenkas on 27 July 2010. 5 replies.

tenkas27 July 2010#1
I was wondering what is the best way of reducing impact sound. Floating 2 layers of green glued plywood on 705 or carpet (method recommended by Rod and others when extreme isolation is not needed). Considering I do not mind having carpet or plywood for the acoustics of the room...
jhbrandt27 July 2010#2
Do not use carpet for your isolation spring. Carpeting varies too much to be dependable as a cushion. Besides, there is no data for this. Use OC 705 or 100kg/m3 rock wool. It is not necessary to use GG. I will have to go dig up the data on IIC ratings of the GG when used in this manner... Use 2 layers of 20mm or 3/4" plywood. Stagger the seams and screw the layers together with 1 5/8" or 42mm screws. Be sure to isolate the edges from contacting the surrounding walls - a cushion of 1/2" or 12mm thick 705 or rock wool around the perimeter will do the trick. Cheers, John
