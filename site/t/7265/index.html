knightfly23 October 2006#5
If that wall will be supporting ANYTHING, I would recommend using what we call "2x4" framing - these are, metrically speaking, 37mm x 90mm vertical wood pieces placed every 600mm along the wall, and with at least one horizontal piece the same size at the bottom and top of the frame; then, you attach your wall panels to this frame with nails (if wood) or screws (if using gypsum wallboard) and put fiberglass insulation in between the vertical studs, held in place by either more paneling on the other side, or by fiberglass strapping if this is part of a double-framed wall (for sound isolation). If you spend any time at all looking at others' construction in the 100's of pictures posted on this forum, you'll see what I mean - Another possible TEMPORARY option might be to simply MOVE the existing partition to where you want the new wall; this would be nearly FREE if it's possible, and since you've not asked anything about isolation (upper floors are NOT easy to accomplish this) the weight of the wall shouldn't make much difference in what the room sounds like inside... Steve
