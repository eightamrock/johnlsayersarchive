There are numerous of those speakers, you'll have to listen to 'm yourself. Some are powered, others are passive, need an amplifier. You can always hang 'm to a computer, be it directly or using an amp. Actives are just yielding build in amps and often EQ-possibilities. You can use stands allright, but I consider a soffited speaker as better than a free standing one. But this depends on the speaker, vent position etc. Not everybody agrees. Soffited speakers don't suffer from diffraction, have stronger mid lows and better imaging, IMHO.
