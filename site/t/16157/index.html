simo30 May 2011#2
Hi Those panels seems to be fibreboard laminate flooring underlay and that seems to be the same question I had on my thread, so I just repeat what Glenn kindly replied to me hoping that it will help you too. He suggested cellulose board or alternatively to use 3mm lightweight plywood sheets for the core of your hangers. I suspect that 10mm MDF would be too heavy for that purpose as - if I understand it correctly - they need to be light enough to move in sympathy with the sound waves hitting them in order to attenuate them (I believe by converting sound energy into heat (?)) (hope one of the expert will correct my post if I'm wrong!) Hope that helps Good luck Ciao Simo
