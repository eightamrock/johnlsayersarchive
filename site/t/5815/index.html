knightfly13 March 2006#2
John's traps are slat absorbers, a type of tuned trap that works on the Helmholtz principle. They do need to be made airtight, so the slats and cloth and insulation behind the slats can work predictably. Without the slats, absorbent across a corner or next to a wall or ceiling does NOT need to be airtight, or even framed for that matter. See this http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=4581 And this http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=5786 for more... Steve
