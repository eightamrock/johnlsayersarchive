knightfly28 June 2004#2
With the info available here, you can build a booth that's pretty soundproof for around $1000 to $2000 - for around $6000 you can buy one that's portable, and almost as soundproof. Both would be large enough for two vocalists and a guitar (only two people, so one would be playing the guitar) Anything you do with PVC pipe, moving blankets, old mattresses, etc, will NOT be as soundproof by far. Any claims to the contrary are made by people who think loss of treble means no noise - they're wrong. The ONLY thing that works for sound isolation is mass, with air/insulation and more mass. Nothing more, nothing less... Steve
