Hi David. If you could post an image of the design you're working with (including dimensions), you will probably get more useful help. How large is the garage? Are you building rooms withing rooms, or will the garage walls be the only isolation barrier? How concerned are you with isolation from neighbors?
DavidY wrote:
I'm mostly concerned about getting good acoustics for capturing a great recording sound. I would like a good listening environment to make sure I'm getting good recordings
From what I understand, plan on surface treating 33% of your live room, and 50% of your control room; that includes walls & ceiling. Allocate a bunch of corners for adequate bass trapping, and plan them well, so they don't interfere with light switch/outlet placement later. Avoid parallel walls, ceiling included if possible.
