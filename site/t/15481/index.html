it sounds like you've been reading up so you have an idea about the first reflection points, trapping your corners, etc depending on what you have going on above the drop ceiling, you might just need a small rigid insulation cloud hung overhead. you might want to add a layer of rigid insulation above the ceiling tiles to provide more bass trapping. behind you, you might want a couple of absorbers on stands to block off some reflections from the live area.

the 38% position is the starting point (might be the end point) so feel free to move it back and forth to identify the best location for listening. depending on the frequencies, the 2" Auralex could suffice. you could try it and see if it works and if not, then build some deeper (4") rigid insulation absorbers.

gullfo13 January 2011#8
positioning the speakers will also usually require some experimenting as there are differences in the response due to front and side wall proximity plus floor or ceiling proximity. most monopole speakers should begin with being placed just a bit off the front wall (say 2") , about 70% of the room width apart or about 6'-8' max and angled to form a 60° triangle with the listening position which starts around 38% of the front-to-back length. basically a triangle with 6'-8' per leg. adjust these to accommodate your room, stands, desk, and the initial seating position.
