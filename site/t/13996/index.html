Heya everyone! I'm going to start my thread as Stuart; Noob alert! I probably have no business being here, much less trying to build my own studio! I've been making music from home for quite a while now and I'm getting more and more serious about it and starting to get my foot in the business, not money-wise though. :P I've always been interested in building my own little "studio" here at home and thought that now when I have some spare money I might at least start. Okay, 'building a studio' might not be the right words for it but basically what I want is a good acoustic treated room for mixing and a small vocal booth. So what do I know about acoustics? I will be honest, very little. I know some terms and I've read some threads both here and on some Swedish forums. I'm very interested in the subject, it's just that it is a lot to learn! Don't get me wrong, I'm all about learning new things that interests me, it's just that I prefer learning during the process. That sounds a bit weird, I know. :P I will build everything by myself with help from family and friends. I know a couple of carpenters (free manpower - yay!) that won't have a problem to build and get some of the materials that I will be needing for a better price. In this case, in my mind, a fairly easy project. But what do I know, right? :) Where am I? I'm located in a flat in Stockholm, Sweden, where I'm also born and raised. The flat is on the second floor out of three, so I have neighbors both under and above me. Since I'm in a flat I'm not allowed to do huge changes to it. Although I can make minor changes that is easy to restore before I move out. According to materials here in Sweden I don't think I will have any bigger problems finding what I need since we have a couple of big warehouses, like every other country, specialized for just "building-stuff". What am I attempting to do? I'm striving for a better acoustic environment for my control room to be (now my bedroom) and vocal booth. Where am I right now in the project? First steps of planning and trying to figure out what will give the best results in my two rooms. In the mixing/control room I've been thinking about to put up either some new walls inside the room or to just put various acoustic panels on carefully chosen spots, in corners and so on. In the vocal booth I've been thinking of building a booth in the booth, to get it tight, with a window out to the control room. How loud am I? During the days I can get pretty loud but none of my neighbors have a problem with that. The one under me is a songwriter which I'm working with from time to time and the one above me is also a music lover who's pretty loud himself. During the nights I'm quiet as a mouse since other people live in this flat. And that's why we've got headphones, right? :) What do I record? I mainly record vocals, guitars and some percussions. I don't have the space to record drums and other large instruments. When I'm mixing and doing my own music it's mostly reggae, hiphop, R&B and house. What is my budget? I would say around $500-600. $1000 TOPS! I figured that it should not cost more than that since it's not a very big project/room and I'm building everything by myself. But since I have no idea of what I will be using I have no idea of how much it will cost. But like everyone else I hope for as little as possible. What do I have to work with? What I got is two rooms. The biggest one is 3.58 meters long and 3.15 meters wide. The smaller room, the vocal booth, has a length of 1.77 meters and is 1.2 meters wide. Its 2.4 meters from the floor to the roof in both rooms. I've got 2 doors where I can choose which one to use to get in to the room - either the top one, which leads to the kitchen, or the one far to the left, which leads out to the living room. What’s it made of? Two of the walls (bottom one and right one on the sketch), there the "right wall" has a window in it, is made of some type of concrete. The other walls I would say is made with some type of heavier wood. I don't know what they're "filled" with - sorry. The floor and roof is solid, probably concrete. Sorry that I don't have exact information about this. If it's needed I will try to get it. What questions do I have? • Is it worth making a room in the room in the control room? My feeling and thoughts tells me that it aint. Both for my economic situation and becuase I can't build what ever I want, because it's a flat. • Which is the best solution to furnish? This will also be my room in the flat, where I have to sleep! • If I build a booth in the smaller room, which way is the best to go with the window to the control room and how do I handle the ventilation? I'm sure I've got a lot more questions, it's just that I can't come up with anything at the moment! They will come up during all the reading I will have to do! Thanks a lot for taking the time to read this and for helping me - if you now choose to do so. :P Kind regards, Stefan. EDIT: Some typos and miss-spellings.

Soundman202011 February 2010#10
What I would measure first is how loud you are in there, and how quiet you need to be outside. That will determine the isolation that you need, which will determine, to a certain extent, the final dimensions of your inner leaf, which in turn will dictate the modal issues and most of the other acoustic facets of your design. Based on that you can determine what kind of treatment you need to put where. But even then, there are a number of simple rules of thumb for treating small rooms, so most likely your design won't be too much different from many of the others that you see here. - Stuart -
