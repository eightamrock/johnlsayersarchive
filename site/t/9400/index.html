Ok, so I'm finally done with my shed/studio. Everything is inspected and working great. However, I can't seem to get a homey feeling when there are no windows. I can't even spend more than an hour in there without taking a break, as I feel like I'm locked in a box all the time. Right now I have track lights in, and they just don't provide enough light. So I'm considering new lights. Also, I think I may have went with the wrong paint color. It's a medium dark red, about the color of blood. I'm thinking this is too dark of a color for such a small room (14x20). Any ideas on how to get it feeling like home, even without windows?

Aloha, Hey, long time no see/read/hear, Andy! Glad to read that you're up and running, and everything (except that homey feeling) is online. Here are a couple of thoughts for you: - do consider a paint job - especially a color that feels "homey" to you. n8nod9it14 September 2007#4
You can install a solar tube light in the room. THis is what i am doing. My solution to keep the isolation is to put a temporary cover over the light when i am playing my drums. Anytime else, i take off the cover and get tons of natural light. Also i put the tube portion of the light assembly that runs through the attic wrapped in thick acoustic fiberglass batting and surrounded by a box of mdf. No sound escapes from here. Just and idea! Otherwise look into a wall mural lifesize graphic poster of any type of natural daylight scenery...lots of cool ideas for that.
