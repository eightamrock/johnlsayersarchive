I've got a design for soffits for my studio monitors (ATC SCM-50A's), but due to the location of windows in my basment (which I cannot cover up...without losing my wifes approval over) I need to leave the top open. Does this remove all the benefit of soffits, or will I still get good coupling into the room by having extended the baffle at least to the sidewalls and floor. gullfo24 March 2007#2
you could build up the soffits to right under them and seal them off on the top. put some plants on it? :-) or build it out a bit and add another set of windows over the soffits to keep the soffit face even to the ceiling. you could consider turning the CR sideways - its reasonable close to a good ratio - and then only one of your side angle walls needs to have some creative window framing on it.
