gullfo13 June 2011#2
welcome! please update your profile to include your location. you should also check out the read-me before posting sticky to ensure you can provide enough details on what you are trying to achieve including isolation requirements etc. in this case i would go with an angled wall design on the CR to allow the isolation booth to be bigger and the foyer roughly the same as your proposed size. angle the front wall to enable the side walls to be symmetrical relative to your listening position. the big challenge is your ceiling and ceiling height - also the ability to beef up all the existing walls with additional mass. unless the quiet rock is less expensive than 5/8" ire code drywall (not likely) go with the drywall. depending on your structure you may also need to so something with the floor to reduce flanking.
