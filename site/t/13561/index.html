A coupla' things.... First, you're free to do what you want, but realize that canvass is usually not really all that breathable of a fabric.... think about it... it stops liquids from soaking through. The other thing is that I really wish you'd consider is the abundance of legal ramifications of not using an FR or IFR material. Either treat the material, or purchase one that will minimize your liabilities. I would call Guilford of Maine directly and ask to speak your local representative. They WILL give you better pricing on that much material. You can also safe money by selecting either a regular stocked material, or by accepting any of their materials that they have an excess of, or a factory second. When I spoke to my GOM rep, he was able to deep discount one fabric that he called a factory second which only had one or two stitches that were mistied. The problem was not noticeable to the naked eye.... go figure.
