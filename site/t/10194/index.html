gullfo4 March 2008#8
if you have floor to ceiling corners you can work with, then by all means. the bass traps could have some of their front covered but ultimately you want the bass to go in and not come out. covering completely would effectively create a panel trap, which may not be what you need until all the broadband treatment options are exhausted. i think you could put the diffusers on the front of the traps, and depending on their size, maybe be the front of the traps. that said, you probably want to be a minimum of 5-6 ft between you and the diffusers to ensure you're experiencing the full effect of the diffusers, and to avoid potential response anomalies at the listening position (and this all depends on the size of the diffusers).

depending on the speakers directionality will partly determine RFZ angles as well as generally trying to make all the angles a minimum of ~20° per leg - maybe this means shortening up the wall length to get the angles (and then maybe soffit mounting becomes an option) or your monitors are just more straight on to your listening position (which is what i did in my original drawing).
