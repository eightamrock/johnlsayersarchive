I should go for the 24 inches but 4inches thick rather than the 16 inches and 6 inches thick
i would love to know what the pros and cons are?
The 6" will absorb better, but become closer to the wall (I haven't drafted this. The higher width of the 4" will bring them further out of the corner and the width also relates to the wavelength and lower frequent edge effect.
