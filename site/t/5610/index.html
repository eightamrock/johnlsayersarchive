Slatwall is another word for Helmholtz trap - the formula is based on 48 kG/m^3 rigid fiberglass insulation by Owens Corning, and the results will change unpredictably if you change the basics. If you're just putting wide-spaced slats over a corner absorber, then sure; if you're actually tuning for a specific frequency (like your 2:1:1 room ratios) then I'd stick to ONE type of trap...
