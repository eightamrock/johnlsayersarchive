gullfo9 February 2008#11
the key to 5.1 will be placement of absorption materials around the room to kill of unwanted reflections and still keep some life in the room and not interfere with the baffle extension created by the soffits. so you can use proper panels on the soffits around your monitors (assuming they can be soffit mounted - check with the manufacturer) and add absorption at the normal key reflection points and just above and below the panels. this will be your start point, you also want to calculate the position for reflection points from the rear speakers.
