roscoenyc24 December 2007#5
take a look at Ethan's How To set up a room page. very good info there. http://www.realtraps.com/art_room-setup.htm you wont be able to put bass traps in the right rear corner but you could put 4" 703 type panels on both of those doors (the entrance and the storage room) If the window on your left ends up perfectly in the reflection area from your monitors you could put a movable absorption panel there or even some heavy curtains to set up your RFZ sweet spot.
