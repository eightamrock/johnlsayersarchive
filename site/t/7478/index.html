1/16" should be fine, even at 8x3 (24 sq foot, 8 cubic foot of 3 PCF = 24 pounds plus your 1x4 frame) it's still not too heavy. knightfly11 November 2006#6
Charlie, for those ferrules you need a special crimp tool - this http://stageriggingonline.com/1scmini.html is probably about the least expensive one you'll find at $45. IF you enlarge the pic by clicking on the tool itself, you'll see that it almost completely surrounds the aluminum ferrule and gives full circumference swaging of the aluminum. 703 is 3 pounds per cubic foot, 4" is 1/3 of a foot, so 1 square foot 4" thick = 1 pound - 24 sq ft = 24 pounds. If your 1x4 is dry, it'll weigh just under 0.7 pounds per lineal foot, so a perimeter frame would weigh about 16 pounds, adding a couple of cross-pieces would raise this to around 20-22 pounds, adding insulation weight, the total would be around 44-46 pounds. Divide by 6 hangers, and you have under 10 pounds per hanger. For something like that, you could just use #10-12 electrical ring terminals and a $15 crimper from Home Depot, being sure to tie a knot in the wire so it can't slip thru if the crimp doesn't hold - if you're not sure what I mean, I'll try to post a pic (but please try; I'm home sick and trying to stay close to the heater for the next couple days :cry: ) Steve
