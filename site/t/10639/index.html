According to your drawing you have the right idea....plenty of bass trapping spread out over as wide an area as possible and broad band panels at the first reflection points. It's a good start, for sure. That water heater is a bummer from a reflections standpoint...not sure what you can do about it though. I'm not sure how well this will function as a dual-purpose space though...it's a very challenging room because it's so small and there are so many irregular surfaces. You have to make the best out of what you have though, so it looks like you're heading in the right direction. Frank
