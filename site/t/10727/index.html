Ro18 June 2008#9
That 47,5 kg/m3 is what you need. for walls and basstraps it's good stuff. Density for absorbing pannels ain't that important it's the volume of the device that makes the difference (tho stiffer stuff will absorb more bass and reflect more high. But then we're talking great density (kg/m3) differences.). Same goes for the "thickness" (read volume) of wool in between your walls. And that 47,5kg/m3 is best in all frq ranges, for what it's worth.
