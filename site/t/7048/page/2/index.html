dennis cham1 October 2006#27
Here is a revision of the 3rd design. Changes is that we split the guitar amp room into two smaller rooms so two guitars can be tracked at the same time and be isolated. Currently we record guitars direct it with VG8s and Line 6 racks, but the guitar players in our company want to be able to record though speakers at the same time. One of the main requests of everyone I spoke to was to be able to record a live rhythm section in the control room . . . including a scratch vocal simultaneously. Thus the thinking in this 3rd try. Hey everyone . . . please vote and let me know which you think you like better. Thanks again Ozzy and Keith!
