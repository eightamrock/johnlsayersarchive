Started by packrat on 4 August 2006. 7 replies.

packrat4 August 2006#1
2" 703 is $1.87 per sq foot 2" 4lb rockwool is .60 per square foot is there a good reason to use 703 instead of rockwool?
Aaronw4 August 2006#2
Different applications, typically. The Rockwool is a little more flimsy than the 703 (which a semi-rigid board). The Rockwool is great for inside your walls and cavities. The 703 is great for making acoustic panels that you hang on ceiling or walls, etc. The 703 can also be cut into different shapes or sizes, and there's a chemical as well that you can use to harden the edges for making those panels (the name escapes me at the moment). Each one has their +/- for their application. This topic has been answered in great detail, but it's been awhile since I've poked around and found it. use the search function, and you'll get all sorts of info, specs, etc. :D
