You are correct in that the the ceiling is NOT going to support any load other than mentioned above. While I had originally planned 16" centers, I have been talking to an engineer buddy in Nashville and along with your post came up 24" centers. He also turned me on the QuietRock, but that stuff is expensive. The one thing I did come up with was that 2 pieces of 5/8" drywall on 24" centers with 2 tubes of GreenGlue have almost the STC of the QuietRock. My last studio I constructed was a double wall with 1 layer 1/2" plywood and 2 layers of 1/2 drywall on 16" centers, no corresponding seams among the 3, worked very well. I am in a rural area now with the closest noise source a interstate highway roughly 1800 ft away.
