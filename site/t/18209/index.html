Soundman20209 March 2013#2
Hi "hellviz", and welcome to the forum! :) One thing I would be concerned about with that orientation of the control room, is the steeply angled ceiling both in front of you and behind you. I get the impression that that would create serious problems with reflections, and cause many complex artifacts. I'm thinking it would be better to rotate the control room 90° anti-clockwise, so that it is facing the live room. That would be put the ceiling angles on each side of you, symmetrically, but leaving the front-to-back ceiling flat and level, with no major artifacts coming off it. That would also allow you to make the control room longer, if you wanted to, or wider, or both, and it would solve the problem with your speaker height too. I think it just makes a lot more sense to do that. The acoustics with the way you have it oriented now, would be very complex to predict, very hard to understand, and even harder to treat.
Since I live in the countryside I don't have to worry so much about disturbing anyone, except my wife.
OK, but what about outside sounds disturbing YOU? Wind, rain, hail, airplanes and helicopters flying over, cars arriving / leaving, roads / railways nearby, animals, neighbors, phones ringing, TV, radio, vacuum cleaner, washing machine, water running, toilet flushing, doors opening / closing, and many many other things. All of those could be a problem.
1. Does the woodpanel count as a leaf? It's not an airtight layer.
Yes, it still counts as a leaf. It does not need to be sealed, in order to act as a leaf.
2. Is it ok to put insulation in the back of an resonator? I don't have to but I'm curious. I'm thinking about the slat resonators on the sides in the front of the controlroom.
You HAVE to put insulation in them! Right behind the slats you put some black cloth, and right behind that you put insulation. If you don't have insulation in there, then there is nothing to damp the resonance, and that's what the slot wall is there for: to remove energy at certain frequencies, by damping the resonance.
3. I've planned to put hangers in the space left before the front wall of the controlroom. Will these do any good or are they unnessecary? There will also be hangers in the space left in the top of the ceiling. Check out the section plan.
The way you show them there, they would not be much use, as they are inside the wall cavity. But there might be a way to arrange things so that you could use that "lost" space for bass trapping. It seems a shame to have it there and not be able to use it! On the other hand, if you rotate your room as I outlined above, it would be much easier to use those spaces.
