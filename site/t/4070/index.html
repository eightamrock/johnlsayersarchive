Soundman202020 September 2011#6
Remove all unnecessary objects from your file, then PURGE it. SketchUp does not actually remove things that you delete until you purge the file. They are all still inside it, even though you can't see them, and you need to purge it to get rid of that. Also be aware that 3D text takes up a LOT of space, and so do many models and components that you can download. - Stuart -
