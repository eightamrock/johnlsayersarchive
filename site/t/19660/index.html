Unfortunately we can't make too many changes to our plan, but we can construct additional walls. I have two rooms that I can basically do whatever I want with (Rumpus and Bed 4) I have attached our floorpan from our builder. Goals/Things to consider: 1. Create a Control Room and Live Room. 2. All of the recording of live/loud instruments will be in the hours of 9am -5pm. There is no one home during these hours so noise isn't an issue during the day. However, I will need some isolation between the Control Room and Live Room when recording drums. 3. I will be mixing/editing/vocals at night, so I want to make sure the Control Room is soundproofed well enough to not annoy the rest of the family. 4. I don't often track bands 'live'. I would be recording one instrument at a time. 5. My console is a 4.5m W x 1.4 D. I will be shortening the board to 3.7m however. I also have a fair bit of rack gear that would need to be considered in the control room as well as a dozen amp heads. I have attached a rough idea I brainstormed of the studio layout.
