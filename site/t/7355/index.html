knightfly11 November 2006#16
2x12 is only part of the equation - means not much til you know how far apart they are (center to center) how far they reach before having support under them (load bearing wall) and what materials are on top/bottom of them (total weight of supported materials per square foot) - What this boils down to for adding the weight of a floated room is that, depending on spacing between these 2x12's and what kind/grade of wood they are, you might need full support under them as close as every 8 feet or as long as every 14 feet; but doubtful any further than that. Even if you only add a booth (please do NOT make it square!!?!) you need to either go downstairs and look/measure (if the joists are visible, or even nail marks, you can find out the center to center distance and SPAN between supports) or maybe get a stud finder (about $30 at hardware stores) to locate each joist and measure between them - As to a booth, when/if you decide on one - make it 5 x 7 with an 8 foot ceiling, you'll like the sound a LOT better once it's finished. You're getting closer, but still need to dig a bit more for the info you need prior to building ANYTHING in that space - maybe the downstairs nightclub is a bargain at twice the price... Steve
