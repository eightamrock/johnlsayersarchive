Ro5 October 2009#2
sorry I read this AFTER the weekend... Did you manage to get the panes in already? If not, I'd really add your glass supplier to do it for you if you don't have a clue. But here are some hints; - add PE tape (rubber) all around the frame - pre cut window-stop trim and add PE to that to on the inside (which will be touching the glass) - add 2 setting blocks, each 1/4 of the length of the frame spaced away from the left/right side. - Add the pane onto the setting blocks and add window-stop trim all around. - Caulk the trim where it touches the window. It needs to be air-tight. might clean the glass prior to installing and make sure they are DRY.
