Now, that having been said, your concern is all about noise somehow resonating back into the room - and your assumption is that the sound within the space would be louder than the resonance of the band - and the band would add little or no nise to the sound in the room. Me? I look at it in the opposite direction. I assume the possibility that it could be excited from a source coming from outside of the room - it would be a fairly low frequency - possibly excited by traffic noise (I can experiment if you'd like to find out exactly what it is- but picture the size of the member (in relation to the total thickness of a bass guitar string) and the length of the string to just get an idea of the frequency) and if excited from outside - it would add to the total amplitude of sound entering the room - and seeing as it is directly coupled to the room side of the framing - it could be a concern. So - with all of that in mind - I decide to never take the chance to see if my fears can come true......... Sincerely, Rod

sharward22 October 2006#1141
I pulled some Romex wire yesterday for a few hours. Kind of fun. 8) Except I had a strange feeling that I was doing something wrong on one of my three-way circuits -- a "two lights between the switches" run. After consulting some wiring diagrams on the 'Net, my concern was valid: I can't use a single 3-conductor wire to go from Switch A (where power source is) to Light A to Light B to Switch B. I need to go from Switch A to Light A to Switch B, and then run a simple 2-conductor wire from Light A to Light B. :roll: It's a good thing I figured this out this morning, because fixing this will be pretty easy. If I had made this mistake on my 12-guage switched outlet run, I'd have a much bigger problem to solve... And I shudder to think what I'd be dealing with if I had not followed my instincts to double-check this stuff and had already drywalled! :roll: Moral of the story: 3-way circuits can be quite tricky! Always make sure you can do what you need to do with the wiring that you run. By the way... In case you're wondering why I even care about 3-way circuits in such a small room -- I'd really like to be able to control all the lighting from the doorway and the drum kit. 8) Oh, and before anyone asks... No, I'm not doing star grounding or any of that. --Keith :mrgreen:
