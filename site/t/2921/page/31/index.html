sharward16 April 2006#773
Thanks for the sympathies, Dan (et al). I'm pleased to report that our lil' Sera is doing really well. The second antibiotic that she's on seems to be helping a lot, and yours truly is doing a great job keeping things clean. 8) The cat issue has gotten in the way of my making progress, and it's obvioius to me that I won't have my L-shaped footing poured before we go to Maui on Wednesday. :roll: But, that's OK -- as I've said numerous times, it'll get done when it gets done. 8) This attitude has been essential in retaining a proper level of sanity!! :D Anyway, I have a bit of progress to report. I spent a few hours today pulling stakes and (ta-da!!) finally pulling off one of the forms! Ugh, pulling those stakes is a pain in the rear!! :shock: The steel ones come out much easier and they aren't subject to breakage like the wooden ones are... :roll: I broke three wooden ones so far and I managed to get two of those broken stakes out of the ground. One however is buried for good, probably about 18" worth. :evil: Broken stakes in the ground = termite food. I can't recommend the steel stakes enough. They're a lot more expensive, but they're so much easier to install and remove, plus they're stronger and reusable. As I've progressed I've figured out clever ways to make removal of the stakes easier. In nearly all cases I am fashioning levers with a steel stake so that I can more easily yank the stake out. The one visible edge of the slab looks pretty good, though there is a bit of an air pocket in one area. :roll: Overall, not bad. I'm still very pleased with the work my guy did. --Keith :mrgreen:
