sharward2 January 2007#1175
Ahhhhh, progress. :-) First of all, happy new year everyone... And happy two year anniversary to me: this thread began on January 2, 2005. :-) I'm pretty close to being done with rough electrical now. The room itself is wired (pictures to ome, soon, I promise). I'm now at the point of bridging the room to the service panel. All along, I had planned to run those lines in the attic. However, I failed to recognize something I discovered today. I decided to remove the cover on the service panel, and that's when it came to me: there's only one "auxiliary" raceway (conduit) leading from the panel to the attic. It's 3/4" and nearly full, thanks to the 50 amp 240 volt (~6 guage x 4) spa circuit the previous owner had installed. I plan to use that overkill wire for my mini split. So, with no room in that conduit for four new circuits, I had to come up with a Plan B. Conduit on the exterior of the building? No way. Ripping the drywall off the garage wall to run a new raceway into the attic? I thought about it briefly... But the knockout in the service panel was not easy to get to at all. That left me with a somewhat undesirable option: conduit on the interior of the garage. I can live with that. I went to Home Depot to buy the pieces-parts, just in time to roll out the door as they closed for the day. :-) I started to piece them together already. I'll finish that this week and run the wire in it. Once I'm done with that, I think I may be ready for a combo rough electrical and framing inspection. Mechanical will have to wait. I just need something inspected to keep my permit from lapsing in February. Hopefully I can do the conduit work during the week. It's kind of fun, so I'm motivated. :-) --Keith :mrgreen:
