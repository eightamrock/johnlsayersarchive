My first ceiling drywall application is probably about the same time frame out, but I'm flexible. :) I have handled inline images in various ways over the past 18 months. At first I was using the free Web space associated with my broadband Internet service. However, the disk space allotment was very limited and I soon ran out of space. So I decided to become a "big spender" and I purchased Go Daddy's least expensive virtual Web Hosting service, where I was able to significantly increase my disk space. It only costs me about $3 per month (paid in advance). Then I decided after a while to install "Coppermine Photo Gallery" on my Go Daddy Linux hosting account. It's awesome! Here is my photo gallery. What's great is that it creates thumbnails and intermediate images automatically. I configured it to set the maximum intermediate image width to 700 pixels, and then I simply use the URLs for the intermediate images in my posts on this forum. Manually resizing images is now a thing of the past for me. 8) --Keith :mrgreen:
