knightfly13 September 2006#1050
"Stickers" UNDER the pile promote drying (or NOT wetting) - stickers between every layer will help WET wood dry, in conjunction with a large fan and lots of time. Stickers every half dozen or so layers of KD wood will keep the pile from falling over (because it's really several vertical piles of SINGLE boards without these, unless you stack it in a pyramid) Wet lumber left stacked properly, and with extra weight on top, will warp LESS than if it's allowed free movement but it will STILL WARP if it's going to; this partially depends on how it was cut from the log, and where - it also depends somewhat on EQUAL surface drying; which brings us back to the stickers, and AIR circulation... If you use lumber wet before it can warp, then you can't SEE it warping - it just causes your house to explode when it reaches its maximum tension due to warpage :twisted: (just kidding...) So much for "sticker shock"... Steve
