You are wise to be thinking of this now! Yes, retreating the soil every 5 years is typically a requirement when you've had an infestation. In my case, I want to have the perimeter of my new inner slab retreated every 5 years as a precaution against getting infested -- or, if by longshot I should become infested and not know it, retreating would kill the buggers. Definitely consult with your local termite experts on ideas of 5-year retreatments without damage to your structure. There may be a way to do some kind of plumbing solution as my pest control company and I came up with. In researching termites in your area, I discovered that there is an alternative to Termidor (by BASF), which is the product my pest control company was going to use. The alternative is called Premise (by Bayer Environmental Science). It's cheaper but supposedly very effective. I have had great experience using a professionally-applied Bayer product to eliminate aphids from some crape myrtle trees at our previous house.
