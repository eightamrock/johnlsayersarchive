sharward27 August 2006#1019
The wall I'm working on right now is actually the middle leaf in a three leaf wall. :roll: There is OSB sheathing on that wall on the garage side for seismic stability. The other side is my office, which has one layer of 1/2" drywall on it. Since beefing up the office wall was not really practical, my strategy is to disproportionately increase the mass on the middle leaf and have a generous air gap between inner leaf and middle leaf. So, I plan to use three layers of 5/8" drywall over the 3/8" OSB that is there, plus cover the whole thing with one layer of leftover 1/2" drywall with Green Glue on that the back of that last layer. That will give me about 2 3/4" of mass, with Green Glue that will in theory make it behave like about 4" of mass. My inner leaf walls and ceiling will eventually consist of OSB sheathing plus three layers of 5/8" drywall, most likely with Green Glue on the final layer. Iin the event that my three leaf wall performs poorly on the office side, I'll add a layer of 5/8" drywall to the office side and use Green Glue when doing so. Thanks for your interest! 8) --Keith :mrgreen:
