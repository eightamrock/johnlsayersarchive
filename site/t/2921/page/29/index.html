sharward30 March 2006#725
I received a PM from whatalob with a question... I received his blessing to post the question and my answer here.
whatalob wrote:
. . . why on the first page do your inner and outer leaves have an actual air gap between insulation whereas your revised drawings in on the next page have no gap and the two leaves seem to be in contact?
The building department official I consulted told me that I would have to fill the cavity with insulation in order to satisfy code requirements for a fire break between the area between the walls and the area between the ceilings. A conventional fire break would have fused the inner and outer leaves together, which would obviously ruin the decoupling aspect of having separate frames. There are also some soundproofing benefits by having the extra insulation in there, so while it will be more expensive, it's not a total waste of money. Note that I changed my wall designs numerous times throughout the protracted design phase of my project. I realize my thread is very long (actually, the longest in the forum's history), but you'll want to skim through all of its pages, maybe starting at the end, working your way backwards, to see the latest versions. Eventually I hope to index the whole thing and/or to inject warnings on posts that contain information superceded by subsequent posts... But I probably won't do that until I'm finished with the project -- whenever that might be! Thanks for your interest. BTW, your question isn't at all "off-topic" -- if you have no objection, I'd like to post your question and my answer to it in my thread. --Keith :mrgreen:
