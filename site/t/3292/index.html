knightfly25 February 2005#6
Both layers you labeled "question layer" should NOT be there - the far left and far right leaves, each shown with three layers, combined with the space and insulation between them, make up a proper mass-air-mass sound barrier. Adding ANY other non-porous layers, even a layer of paper, will reduce the effectiveness of the wall at low frequencies. One way to build this type construction is to build one wall first, insulate between studs, then nail fairly lightweight lath across the studs at approx. 2 foot intervals to hold the insulation in place. Then, you build the second wall laid down on its floor, adding the lath to the upside of that frame; then tip the frame into place, secure it with resilient mounts (sway braces) so it isn't hard coupled to your ceiling or the other wall frame, fill with insulation, and sheath with your three layers. Keep in mind that 3 layers each side is only as effective as the REST of your construction - ceilings and floors, doors, glass, HVAC, all are more difficult to build to high isolation than walls... Steve
