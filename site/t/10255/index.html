What would you do if you had a single leaf masonry garage that you want to convert to a studio space? 1) Build a blockwork inner leaf (disadvantage: wall ties fixed back to existing - required for stability) 2) Build a timber inner leaf (advantage: no fixing to existing - fixed top and bottom) 3) Thicken up existing leaf using timber strapping and RC There are a couple of things that are definite ie. a new door slapping, a window in place of the garage door. Also, the floor will be insulated and boarded and the ceiling will be strapped with RC. Cheers.
