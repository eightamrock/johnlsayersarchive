Hey Glenn! Thanks for responding. So I've built my panels with exposed pine frames and was committed to using some form of wood for the desktop mdf for base and racks (they'll be below as support, not on the bridge). I was initially planning to have a solid piece of ash or black walnut milled to spec, but I got worried about reflections. But from what you're saying, I can use the MDF for the top, cover in veneer and that should help with reflections?
