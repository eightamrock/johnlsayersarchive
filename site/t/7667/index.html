Without having any additional information, I would look some reflectiion path analysis to see where the offending reflections are from. At 1k the wavelenght is 1.1FT so the problem is a .55FT differential. I wouild suspect something like a strong reflection of the mixer, or keyboard, etc. Good luck! Andre
