Wow. The potential for nearly 20' ceilings is not an opportunity to miss. These are fully-enclosed courts? The raquetball courts at the athletic club I go to have a balcony running along the back end of the court, and the top half of the court is basically open. I suppose this would be too tough for you to close up, tho. As far as treatment goes, it wouldn't be all that different from treating any other space.
