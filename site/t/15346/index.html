the room is based on a folded primitive root sequence (no two sections are the same size but it looks symmetrical) based on a prime number of 13 and is 18'w x 23'd x 11'h. some bass trapping but the shell acts as an absorber by using low isolation.

400ms RT and flat across the audible spectrum and very much alive without distracting reflections. quiet.
