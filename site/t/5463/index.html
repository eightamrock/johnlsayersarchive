knightfly17 January 2006#3
Andreas, I'd keep looking if you really feel you need to float your room; that stuff gives no durometer or hardness values, and is pretty thin - you'd need to use several thicknesses of it to make a decent "puck" and you'd need to glue the layers together. The ribbed part makes it even harder to anticipate what it will do under compression, so about the only way I can think of to make it work would be totally "cut and try' - not the best plan if you actually want your finished room to WORK... YOu're pretty close to where Sylomer is sold; I know it's not cheap, but how cheap is it to build something that won't work? :cry: Steve
