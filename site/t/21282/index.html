I'm building some framed superchunks for front corners of my control room out of pink fluffy with a wood frame, and plywood tops and bottoms. To avoid the problem of the insulation settling/smushing down, I was going to build 2 units for each corner (a total of four), each half the height of the room, to be stacked one on top of the other. (A bonus is that they would be portable if ever we change our configuration). But now I'm wondering whether the plywood interrupting the superchunk at the midpoint between the ceiling and the floor would interfere with the functionality of the bass absorption. Any thoughts?
