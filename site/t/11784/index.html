Soundman202011 March 2009#4
I guess I've seen it in a few pictures, such as the one near the bottom of this page: http://johnlsayers.com/Studio/Pages/Sonar2.htm where the side wall has slats..is there absorbtion underneath those slats?
Actually, in those photos I see slot resonators on the front and back walls, not in the side walls. I see only glass doors on the side walls. And it looks to me like John carefully angled those side walls such that the glass is not at the first reflection points. But yes, slot resonators usually do have absorption inside. AFAIK it isn't strictly necessary, since a slot resonator is basically just a Helmholtz resonator, and they work fine without absorption, but putting absorption inside widens the Q, so you cover a broader range of frequencies. At least, that's the way I understand it. Maybe the acoustic experts here can correct that, if I'm wrong. - Stuart -
