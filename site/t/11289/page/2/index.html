xSpace7 March 2010#35
Ale, True that framing for an acoustic environment changes the approach and methods and mindset typical to construction in general. But it does not change the nature of the materials you build with. Your contractor is not experienced enough to get you on the right path. I know he is not experienced because of the waste of both time material and money to wrap a light gauge piece of metal around wood in an attempt to make the whole greater than the sum of the parts. Structural steel or wood...not both :) I think you have to settle on your design first. Once you settle on how this wall and ceiling configuration will be, then you have something that can dictate how it will be constructed and from what materials would be the best for that job. Trying to mix and match light gauge metal and wood is a waste of time and potentially dangerous to you guys. If you have wood studs...then build the walls from that, right? And if you have wood material for joists, then build it out of the proper dimensioned wooden material and be done with it. Otherwise, you require structural steel studs and structural steel joists.
