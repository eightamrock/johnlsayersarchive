well jeff it sounds like your in better shape than I gave you credit for. I still think youll need more cash. Heres why. Ultimately, if you have gear to record, cool. I am an experienced engineer, but Im no gear snob. I was just makin sure that 5k wasnt expected to buy gear. Your only real problem is that you want it quiet and sounding good. Depending on your distance to neighbors, and their attitude towards your rockin, the part you cant cheat on wether your makin abby road jr or a piece o shit studio is soundproofing. Its all about mass and air space. That means more materials and usually more real estate( as in double walls) and then theres AC. Your biggest challenge as I see it is too figure out how far you are from neighbors on all sides and where any weak spot are in general(like the guy to the left is a dick)etc. then you need to build your structure to perform for those requirements. The more soundproof you need it, the more materieal you must use. Acoustic treatment can be done later, but at least consider it now for planning. The only other catch is how isolated do you want your Control Room to be from your live room. The more , the more, as you know. I hope you dont think I was tryin to scare you. Its just since Ive started planning mine, ive not only found I underestimated the difficulty, I have found things I could not have even possibly imagined as factors to underestimate in the first place.
