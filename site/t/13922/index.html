Hi guys, I've been reading tons of posts for the last 3 months here to be able to build an almost-pro and evolutive recording & mastering facility here in France. For many reasons the projects turned into something far away from what I decided to build last November. The plan now is to have a 5000 sq.ft building in which there will be a Film Producer, a photographer, a designer, a stylist, a web designer and a recording studio. The good thing is that for the same budget I'll have a space 3 or 4 times bigger! The bad thing is that I have the same amount of money for construction work :? Fortunately I just found investors to help me but I have now to design it properly and I'm in need of advices to choose the best implantation. I (badly) drawn a few sketches with usefull enough information (I hope...). You'll also find pictures of the surroundings, the buidling of course... Basicaly what I do need is a comfortable Control Room, with daylit, and a large recording room to put metal band and quiet string quartets or speech actors for commercials in it (with a big door of course and windows if possible). I want a window and a door (sliding door?) between these 2 spaces. I do need really good sound proofing because of the other guys that will be working in the offices really close to the studio and the neighboors living in the apartment right above the office area in which I think building the control room will be cheaper (because of using the existing walls and upper floor slab). Existing floor is a concrete slab, I'm thinking about floating the floors. For costs reasons I'm wondering if a 5mm resilient layer + 8mm OSB + hardfloor would be great enough? The same for the walls?... every walls except the one between the Control Room space and the 4 other offices is a single plaster wall with windows. Again for money reason and considering the STC chart I'm planning to put 15mm of rockwool on every existing concrete walls and ceillings, then to build a 3 layers (13mm+13mm+18mm) plaster leaf with 10mm of rockwool on the outside. I'll leave a 4 inches air gap between the two walls. Am I definitely wrong by saying that this might work? I mean enough not to bother neighboors upstairs and people working in the same building in the office? I measured everything and put it on the plans attached. I've been working in Montreal 4 years ago and I know that here in France construction materials are way much expensive that in north america. Our budget for the construction is between 70 to 90k$... 100k$ MAXIMUM! and we plan to do most of the job by ourselves (5 guys ready to fight again plaster and rockwool)! Any input is more than welcome, I know I still have a lot of work but the studio has to be ready between April and May. We'll have access to the building at the end of January so this has to move forward quickly... Long nights to come... In advance, thanks!

xSpace11 January 2010#3
The machine room in between the control and tracking rooms is not a good idea. Not only will you spend more on glass with this dual type wall but you move the machine noise directly into the audio path via flanking or possibly the air space depending on how you guys build it. With a budget as large as yours, you need a qualified designer.
