Splayed walls: what angle is best?

Started by Bret Bluntskull on 7 February 2005. 8 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 3150.

Bret Bluntskull7 February 2005#1
This seems like a straightforward question, yet I'm surprised that the experts don't agree on the answer...at least from what I've read so far, and I've been reading and searching for several days. I'm in the design stage of a studio. My plan is to splay the left and right walls of the mixdown area. But the question is: To what degree? I've read that 12 degrees seems to be the right number, but can someone comment on that? Is more better? Does it matter, as long as they are splayed at least 12 degrees? The studio will be more or less a "live-end-dead-end" (LE/DE) space 18.5 ft. long and 14 ft wide at the back. The ceiling will be just under 9 ft. The DE will be the mixing end, the LE will be the rehearsal/recording end. A second question. If the answer to the above question is indeed 12 degrees, then don't I splay each wall 6 degrees? Or 12? [That's rather important to know, don't you think?]

AVare7 February 2005#4
12 degrees is enough to stop flutter echo, which is why walls are splaywed for contriol rooms. If you do 2 walls spalyed, then 6 degrees each will do. If you are considering controling early reflections, then do ray tracing, although practical experience suggests 12 degrees per wall is enough. Andre
