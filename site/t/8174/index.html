How many and what configurations, etc...
Depends on your needs, and what is your target audience. eg: typical band or a symphony.
Lounge: 12 input mic panel (parallel to 20-32) Studio: 2x16 input mic panel (1-16 on one panel and 17-32 on another panel) - 4 TRS 1/4" sends for cue mix Control Room: 4 input mic panel with 2 TRS 1/4" sends for cue mix (parallel to 16-19) Bath room (next to control but not in picture): 4 input mic panel with 2 TRS 1/4" sends for cue mix (parallel to 12-15)
Here's a couple questions... (1) What is your input availability for your console? (2) What is your cue system going to be? Amplified signal, or something like the Furman or other system? What type of headphone boxes, etc. (3) I wouldn't parallel. Bring them all up to a patch bay.
