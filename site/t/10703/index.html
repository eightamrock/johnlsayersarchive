AVare6 June 2008#4
Welcome to RSD!
How small can I go regarding the volume a control room, and still expect decent results?
There is no hard and fast rule. Industry standards (as in recommendations, not must be followed to the letter) are based on 3500 ft^3 rooms. People have had success with small sweet spots in rooms as small as 1500 ft^3. The NARAS recommendation for surround includes a minimum height of 11 ft.
What is a recommended volume for the live room?
How many musicians at once? Do you want ambience from the room for say classical recordings? The budget seems tight, especially if you are expecting significant isolation from the trains and trucks. Possibly even unfeasible. Andre
