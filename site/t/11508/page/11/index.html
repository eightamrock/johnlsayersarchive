Outrageous... what was quoted?. 3/4 stops will hold the window in place, there's hardly any weight on it. Unless you have them panes skewed too much. Just use enough height to support it.
2) On that note, I am aware to use the 1/8" thick glazing tape for the stops to glass contact, but what about the height? same as the stops or lower and fill the small remaining gap with butyl caulk? Double sided adhesive I'm assuming?
. The height of the tape should be 6mm lower, at least, than your stops. you will need that 6mm for your sealant.
3) Are 16 gauge finish nails recommended for holding the stops in place to the frame? Gun or hand - I don't want the glass to break
As said, there's not much weight (usually) on the stops. Secure them with a nail or screw which is about 3 times the length of the stop. Breaking a piece of safety-glass isn't easy. Gun'm if ye can :) I did...
