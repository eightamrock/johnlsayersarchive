Hey guys and girls, framing is almost done, then its taking down that ceiling, and removing windows. Now I don't want to get ahead of myself here, but in the very near future I will start to figure out my runs for conduits, and with that in mind, I started to think about the spectator area in the rear of the control room. I haven't really found much info, about what works and what doesn't work, but I was hoping someone could give me some input on my idea. Basically, what I plan to do is build a 4 1/2" (completed) riser in the rear of the room, with a 2" step up. This will cover probably less than 1/4 of the control room itself. I plan on bolting 2x4's on end directly into the concrete, and spacing the gaps most likely 16" oc. I would then fill in the riser cavities completely with sand, lay down MDF over the top, then my quietwalk (padding for the cork tile), and tile. This riser will install around an embedded gear rack. Do to the shape of the back of the room, I think I will have a tough time finding a couch or love seat to fit, which is why I am going this direction. The conduit will then be laid under this riser which will connect the gear rack to the patch bay near the sill of an outside wall. Now although this is just a minor extension of the floor, I still want to make it decoupled from the walls, which brings me to a couple questions. 1) Would it be better to build this prior to installing sheet rock, and build it about 1/4" away from the wall, that way I can just backer and caulk the gap between the riser and sheet rock? 2) This will be bordering with my rear bass trap, would it be better to install the riser the full distance to the back wall, and build my bass trap on top, or stop at the bass traps location and build the trap from the full concrete to the ceiling? 3) Could there be any possible issues with rear reflections off the gear rack behind the listening position? 4) Is this even a good idea???? :lol: Here's a few pictures I did so you can visualize what I'm going for, and like I said I don't want to get ahead of myself at all, but if I will be thinking about conduit shortly, than this might have to be built. If it indeed will work. Thank You kindly as always for everyone's, and anyone's opinion!! - Ian PS - anyone know how to change the damn text color in sktchup????? :?

xSpace21 March 2009#89
"1) Would it be better to build this prior to installing sheet rock, and build it about 1/4" away from the wall, that way I can just backer and caulk the gap between the riser and sheet rock? 2) This will be bordering with my rear bass trap, would it be better to install the riser the full distance to the back wall, and build my bass trap on top, or stop at the bass traps location and build the trap from the full concrete to the ceiling?" I think, for me, I would have to complete my rooms walls before building this deck. It would make the sheetrock install easier, and not have any negative effect on isolation/leakage. Seems that building it as you are will make it part of your floor area, proper. So , in sequence, build the walls and install sheetrock, build the riser, install the basstraps. I would also try to keep a small distance between the walls when building this deck/riser. You could fill it [the small distance] with backer-rod and acoustic caulk at some later point if it needs it. Hope that helps ya, Brien
