carsten_d23 December 2009#175
maybe i don't get it - maybe it's a language barrier but you cannot simply take the cross section area's square root to determine the width/length. it is simple geometry. the area of a rectangle is a*b (width*length) the area of a circle is pi*r² (radius NOT diameter) so if you have a duct with a diameter of 6" your cross sectional area will be: pi*3² = 28,3 inch² that said your register should be at least double that size: 56,6 inch² now: 56,6 inch² is your minimum goal. if you have a ROUND register it must have a diameter of: 56,6 inch²/pi = radius² square root of radius² * 2 = diameter = 8,5" should be the diameter of the register. if your register is a RECTANGLE it COULD BE: 56,6 inch"² = 10" * 5,66" or 56,6 inch² = 7" * 8" or 56,6 inch² = 9,4" * 6" and so on... (area = width * length ... or area/width = length ... or area/length = width...rule of three...rule of proportion...) and now you can see that the area of a duct and twice the area of a duct is DIFFERENT than simply double a diameter... or take a square root of sth without knowing the relation between numbers... EXAMPLE: 6" duct = area is 28,3 inch² now double the diameter: 12" duct = area is 113,1 inch² !!!!!! whereas: 28,3 inch² area = duct diameter is 6" now double the area: 56,6 inch² area = duct diameter is 8,5" got it? got it! i hope i haven't misunderstood the question... cheers. carsten.
