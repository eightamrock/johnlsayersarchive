This ain't no party...this ain't no disco...this is your nightmare!!! "The home is currently cutting about 35db (test material 105db inside, and 70 outside)." The existing basement, based on a true an accurate test is isolating or stopping 35 dB. You have already addressed the windows...and they are a major criminal in this area. I would think that with most of the basement underground you would have better isolation than this so I assume your test package may be faulty, I assume with a great degree of certainty OK? Earth is one of the best dampers and isolation is also part of why it is sought after. So we turn again to the windows. Search for how to make a "window plug", you need this information. Also there is opportunity for sound to escape up anyone of those pipe paths, may not bother your neighbor but little mama might not be a big fan of it.
