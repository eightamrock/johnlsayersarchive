Hello everyone I've been looking to build a studio in the basement of my home for quite a while. Hi knicotera, and welcome! Your vocal booth is probably fine. Maybe a bit small, but if that's the space you have for it, then that's what it is!
2) What ways can I treat the control room and live room to get the best sounds without losing a lot of space?
Can you move the dividing wall between them? I'd try to make the CR a bit bigger, and sacrifice some space in the LR. Is drums the only ting that you plan to record? How much of that exists already, and how much is going to be built? In other words, do those walls already exist, or are you going to build those? - Stuart -
xSpace24 July 2011#3
You did not mention which room is which, but judging from the coach, I would think this is the control room. So if this correct, I would move the isolation booth into the live room, get it out of the control room, it isn't helping tat room at all. To have this room(s) perform as best as possible, we need a budget. We also need to explain that treatments do not isolate sound but merely subdue the higher frequency. The low end of the spectrum, the one that you will be dealing with will require isolation walls. Are you prepared to go this far?
