The question to you is if it is possible, at all, to make this into one recording room and one control room. If it's not possible to split the room in two, how would you set up a mixing position in it?
I think your room may be a little small to do two rooms. :( Normally, one would try to shoot their monitors down the longest length of the room, however, due to the ceiling slope; you may want to first start with your monitors on the 4799mm wall with your mix position (ears) about 38% from the front wall. Try placing your monitor woofer on stands behind your mixing/area and as close to your seated ear level but NOT @ 1116.50mm height. As far as acoustic treatments, I would go with corner/bass traps, side wall absorbers, rear wall absorbers and a ceiling cloud/absorber panel centered over your mix position. Here’s a great link for info on acoustic, absorbers, construction. http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html And wall units: http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm Hope this helps. Looking forward to your build! Aloha 8)
