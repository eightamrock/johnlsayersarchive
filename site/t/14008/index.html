Soundman20201 February 2010#5
the inner and outer framed walls are going to be fairly close (1" air gap).
1" air gap!!! :shock: If you only have a one inch gap in your MSM system, then it hardly seems worthwhile building decent doors. You won't get good isolation if you only have 1" of air between your leaves, so it doesn't seem worth the trouble to build doors that might be better than your wall. Minimum air gap should be FOUR inches. Anything less and your MSM resonant frequency goes up, reducing isolation. Unless you are planning to put some really serious mass on those walls! - Stuart -
