Sweetwater sells Auralex wedges online in just about every variety you could need. http://www.sweetwater.com/store/detail/SF4in22BUR/ Generally, 4" foam is going to be the best since it'll continue to help out at lower frequencies than others do. It money is tight though, you could go with 3" or 2" foam and space it out from the wall to get some added low end absorption.
