Help Needed For Small Room In Austin With It All!!

Started by dexternyc on 1 March 2006. 0 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 5748.

dexternyc1 March 2006#1
After living in Manhattan for the past few years we are now building a house in the very musical hamlet of Austin Texas. I will still be commuting to and from new York every month but need a decent mixing/ overdub room to work in while I'm in Austin. As time progresses I can see myself spending less and less time in NYC so I want to make sure the room is basically a comfortable and workable space. However cost is certainly an issue and I am taking the approach to have the builders do it as best they can then upgrade the acoustics as needed. I wont be working loud late at night, and any drum tracking is usually done at a friends studio. There are neighbours on either side of the house. I don't need total isolation but think it might be a good idea if none of them knew there was a studio in there. So I've spent the last month reading EVERYTHING i can get my hands on about studio design. I think i'm on the right track but would really appreciate some help here. The architect just sent these plans and is waiting on me to give him any changes. i've attached a .pdf of the house plans because my efforts at sketchup were laughable!
External image, not preserved — original: As you can see the studio is attached to the front of the house, and shares a common wall. The good thing is that while the house will be pier and beam the studio will be on a slab. Wall between the studio and house will be on 6"plates with offset studs and RC on one side and insulation. All the other walls will be double drywall on RC with insulation, as will the ceiling. The control room will basically be a little less than 12'X20' with an adjoining 5'4"X12'4" area that I want to split between a bathroom and a booth. I've worked with and without booths before but if its possible I do like the option of having one. And the toilet is necessary so people don't have to use the house. Based on John's other designs I believe I should put the speakers on the short wall oppsite the door. Is 12X5 enough room to get in a toilet/sink and a booth? Do you forsee any probs with the bathroom door being so close to the mix position? I'm looking into an exterior glass screen door for the booth and just a regular solid core for the dunny. In such a small space should both those doors open into the room? The architect didn't seem to think it was a good idea. And if thats not too tall an order, I'm hoping to get my Hammond B3 and Leslie in there somewhere too!! Thanks, Dexter
