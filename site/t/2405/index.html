knightfly3 October 2004#2
Greg, probably the reason you've not gotten an answer on this is that you are asking more about a home theater system and not a recording studio - in a studio, we tend to set up things very precisely and concentrate on a single seat being accurately configured - in a home theater, you are more interested in SEVERAL people having "good seats" and so there are different ways of setting things up for that. Your comment about the 5.1 system not being important is about as far from reality as you can get - if you read some of the posts in the acoustics forum on speaker/listener placement for studios, you'll see what I mean. Here is a recent paper written by several professionals in the music industry on surround to illustrate what I'm talking about - note that even the "pioneers" can't agree on much... http://www.grammy.com/pe_wing/5_1_Rec.pdf I hope you find some of that helpful... Steve
