barefoot12 March 2003#2
Those software packages should provide manuals that describe the various measurement techniques and outline what the data means. Knowing specifically how to interpret a particular set of data, however, will probably depend quite a bit on your education and experience. Every measurement situation is different, so various factors need to be considered when trying to draw realistic conclusions. It's the art of science and engineering we develop through practice. And it's difficult to imagine that anyone would get very far without at least a rudimentary understanding of the underlying physics of the system in question. I don't think these aspects can be wrapped up in a tutorial. Thomas
