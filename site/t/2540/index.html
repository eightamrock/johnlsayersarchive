Well Seeing as this is the Design we worked on for My Studio and the One I Built I could prolly give you some info on it. :) The rear side walls are actually false walls with Rockwool insulation (Semi-Rigid) Covered in fabric. The rear walls are the same. If you choose a double door scheme, make sure the inner door traces the angle of the rear wall. this will aid in not having sound baouncing back into your mix position. if you can do a glassless door, i'd cover the door with 1.5" 703 If you build the rearward walls (side and back) these are your Bass traps :) Of course Johns soffit design is up front. visit my website to see my equipment list. The ROOM is fantastic. The Samples (Last 3 on my samples page) are samples mixed in this room.
