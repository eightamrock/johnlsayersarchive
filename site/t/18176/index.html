John Sayers20 February 2013#2
Build a 45 x 20mm frame and stretch the cloth over it and staple it to the back of the frame.
Image not preserved: Cloth Panels-PIC.jpg
Image not preserved: Clouds-cloth panels_lights..gif
It's best to make the frames on the wall then take them down, brace and add the cloth. Attach to the wall with thin brad nails that disappear behind the cloth.
