AndreasB8 February 2008#4
errr... well reading a lot will enlighten you on the many things regarding isolation and building the room properly. I know this will take a lot of time and energy and this will not progress at all building-wise but in the end things will go a lot faster and the result will be a lot better. re: (your walls): the outer wall depicted is not facing the world outside or another room? if yes, that would be your outer leaf. Your inner leaf (2nd) would be the one facing the inside of your room. But it seems you're trying to build the room with gysum boards on the inside of your studs. In my undertsanding of the 3 leaf concept that would be the third leaf. I know a lot of this doesn't make sence when thinking of how to prevent sound from entering the structure. But to get the best result you add more mass to the inner leaf and the outer leaf and get rid of the third (inside) leaf hence the term Mass-Air-Mass, where the two masses are your outer and inner leaf and the air the insulation and air between joists. So, maybe you should reconsider the staggered studs since the don't make any sence when not attached to anything. otherwise just wait until somebody with more credability chimes in on this subject (Rod, Steve anyone??) a :arrow:
