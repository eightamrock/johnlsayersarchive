knightfly10 March 2005#2
IF its the rubber that is used for door seals, it can be (probably) used for STUDIO door seals; possibly also for seals in windows; but NOT for floating floors, insulation, or acoustic treatments - floating floors need higher density, and insulation/acoustic treatments need to be OPEN celled (you can blow through them) Steve
