Roguejackal wrote:
One question Brein, do you feel it's a better job having a plaster skim over all the board, or no real benefit over just filling and taping all the joints?
It isn't going to gain you anything except for the visual appeal. You could do this type of application but there are some things you have to do first. One is that you should prime the entire wall area first to allow the mud to stick directly to the rock, other wise it will flake and peal/fall off over a short period of time. I once did something like this in a small room with really really bad and multiple joints just to get a plastered look. It was a lot of labor but it looks great and you could say it adds some diffusion. I doubt it does, but it looks good and she is happy:) You especially want to prime the places where the paper its ripped off the rock. What happens is this. Sheet rock mud is a water based material so if you skim the un papered areas the water bubbles the blistered paper and starts to deteriorate the rock. So you have to use an oil based (not Latex) primer in order to not have the primer do the same type thing.
