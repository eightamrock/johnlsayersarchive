Eric Best11 October 2012#5
With this choice of drivers you could go 3 way just fine and cover all the territory that you need to. Cross at about 2.5 k and 350. Use the scanspeak mid and the peerless sub. They look like they should match fairly well. That way you could go active all the way and you don't have to worry about the complexity of designing a passive network where you would have to worry about correct phase, padding of the tweeter and baffle step compensation. There really isn't any need to go with a 4 way. For most situations, a 2 way and an sub will work just fine. Do you know how to use any of the free software for box design?
