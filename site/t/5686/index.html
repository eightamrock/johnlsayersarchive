Hi Martin - welcome to the forum. Whereabouts in Poland are you from? My girlfriend is originally from Kielce. First things first you will find this site to be a great resource, but I would recommend spending a few weeks reading through some of the threads on here. You'd be amazed how much you will learn about your project from reading about others. A good start for you would be "DON'T EVEN think ABOUT POSTING before you READ THIS..." and then "REFERENCE AREA - Useful threads/links" This second one will give you the basics about walls, celings, doors and sound proofing myths theories. Good luck and look forward to hearing more about your project. Andy
