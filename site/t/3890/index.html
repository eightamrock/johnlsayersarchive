timwheat17 June 2005#4
OK, I've think I've asked enough questions on here, I think I"ll take my first stab at answering one. I'm actually in the process of building an entire studio in my basement and yes, I think you can probably build a 15' by 15' room that will be relatively quiet for under $5000. First of all, are your concrete blocks hollow or filled? If they're hollow, like mine, I believe they have an stc of around 40-45 which is probably good enough to leave alone. So it looks like after that you're going to have to build two walls, one door, and a ceiling. Check out all of this stuff if you haven't already. http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=2125 THis link will also give you a good idea on how to build your walls, ceilings are relatively the same and yes I did sheetrock attached to the floorboards and it seems to work quite nicely. Maybe, for a little more $ look into limp mass vinyl. http://irc.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/fulltext/ir761/ So, here's a quick pricelist based on what I had to buy here in IL. I'm going to include a drum riser to help to eliminate structure born kick drums from getting out. Lumber for walls and riser = $300 Epdm for riser = $150 Double layer drywall on ceiling= 16, 4x8 sheets @ 7$ = 112$ Double layer drywall on walls (I guessed your ceiling height @ 8')= 35 sheets @ 7$ = $245 Resilient channel (on both sides of wall and ceiling)= 35 @ 3$ for 12 footers =$105 3 inch rockwool insulation in appx 450 sq ft of cavities = appx 60, 4' x 2' batts @ 8$ = 480$ ( I was able to locally get my hands on Fibrex 3 inch rockwool at 8# pcf for 32$ per packeage of four) Usg acoustic sealant= appx $100 Door = $150 Door seal kit = $200 - $400 Screws, joint compound, etc. = maybe $300 Electrical components = $200 Total cost of materials= $2542 And then you should have one hell of a quiet room with no air. I haven't done the hvac in mine yet so I'm not sure how much that is going to cost and its going to be the only thing that I get hired out. All of these prices are based on Peoria, IL prices too and I guessed high on everything so I'd say you're definitely below your $5000 point. A floating room within a room is the way you would really isolate yourself, but is generally not possible in a basement, in the U.S., unless you're a band of little people. I hope this is helpful and if I've forgotten something just tell me. Good luck. Tim Wheat
