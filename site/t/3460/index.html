Auralex and any other type of foam material is for ACOUSTIC properties only, it does not "isolate" your room. Are you looking to make your room sound good, if so, auralex is a good product to use, although there are a lot of do it yourself options. Let us know if you are trying to isolate ("sound proof") your room or just make it sound good. Dave
