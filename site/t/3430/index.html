A four foor square room to record bass? I hope it's an upright bass, and a small one at that. It doesn't appear to be to scale, is the lounge area pic below, the same as the the control room in the top pic? It would probably be better to stick with a control room and live room, You'll be lucky to squeeze a vocal booth in there as well, and a drum room is out of the question, your 10ft X 8ft is a drum room in itself, barely. If you've got a digicam, take some pics and post them up, so peeps can get a better idea of what you've got to work with. Oh, and you'd better update your profile to include your location, otherwise you'll get nagged. Cheers Del
