Cram3 December 2007#20
This part is very important to our family budget here so I keep track. I'll update this placeholder as my project progresses, but I'm making a single room in our basement for band rehearsal. The cost here would be a lot less than for a multiple room studio... drywall around here is ~7-8 usd per 4x8 sheet 5/8. and `6-7 usd for 1/2. I had a lot laying around to use and suppliment this project so that cost has been significantly reduced. Caulking GE S II is 8.75 per 2pk. The R30 insulation I am using here for the ceiling/deck layer is ~25usd per 25 foot roll. WIC Clips - 9 * $15 = 135 Lumber to date - $236 for bottom plate PT and mostly #2 8' 2x4. Insulation so far - 10 * 25 = $250 Drywall - 7 * 12 = $94 Coarse thread screws - $20 Family Help with the following - Backer rod was given to me by my brother who works in the glass install business. Boxes - 8 outlet, 3 ceiling octagon, 2 doublegang wall switch outlets from other brother. Hilti, compressor for framing & some other equipment and advice from yet another brother... Caulking - I have to gather my total consumption on this one, but I bet I've used two cases worth. Expenses to hit are for framing and the real costs of the RC, lumber and drywall costs for the exterior and interior wall sections. Along with insulation.

Been a good long while since I was up here posting. Lemme see... 1200 Sq Ft for the studio proper... estimated was $40k. Then $15k for the lounge/server room/bathroom. OOOOPS! I'm having to trim back on external labor some to keep the budget around $65k just for the studio. So far, here's my incurred expenses: Exterior finish materials; $2500 Sand for floor (32 Cu Yd); $750 - 3 delivery charges totaling $150 hurt! R19 Insulation; $300 (Got a LOT more to get!) Studio audio wiring; $7200 (Tracking room plates to console area-Input, Cat6, midi and headphone mixes) Total=$10,750 The contractor's haven't billed yet, but these were the estimates I got: Concrete; about $16k (should be less because I supplied the sand and some labor) Foundation block work; about $750 Electrical is prolly gonna be in the $15-17k range Plumbing (including fixtures); $5000 Total=$36,750 Work I will be doing myself (I hope): Interior wall construction; $5000 Lounge framing and finish; $5000 Bathroom tile; (FREE) Leftovers from renovating house HVAC (Mitsubishi Mini-Splits); $15-18k Interior studio finish (Oak, Cherry, Cedar and Walnut); $3000 Concrete pad stain and sealing; $1500 Total=$32,500 There's probably another $10k just in overlooked stuff, with another $10k in underestimating, inspection fees and misc stuff like fire extinguishers, etc. I'll try to remember to get back to this thread as I go along and update the figures.
