Cram29 January 2008#28
I was trolling through the construction forum and making calls this morning for delivery costs for the drywall I am putting in my room. I am growing tired of muling 4-6 sheets per visit to home depot for 5/8 drywall and wondered if I could just order the remaining 30 or so for delivery. I assumed it would be around $50 for the delivery based upon other deliveries we have to the house (loam, mulch & fire wood all have a $50 fee until you exceed a yard or cord delivery amount...) Home Depot 59 for the driveway delivery. @6.77 per sheet -------------------- Total Cost - 261.10 Local Store A - .75 per sheet delivery cost. @ 10.24 per sheet. -------------------- Total Cost - 329.70 Local Store B - $50 delivery cost. @ $9 and change(I forget the amount) per sheet. -------------------- Total Cost - More than both (forgetting the .00 designation is too much of a variance at the time of writing this...) The Rub: Although that 6.77 price per sheet looks cool when I walk through home depot, the local building supply store with a per- sheet delivery cost of .75 brings the cost to a marginal advantage. PAY ATTENTION TO DELIVERY COST IN RELATION TO PRODUCT PRICE. I see this all over the place - the tires I got for my jeep were advertised at less of a cost in a store, so I went there and was hit with installation costs that brought the price up and beyond their competitor. Really though - it's only $30. I could rationalize for either way over the other, but when we're building these projects every bit can count and if there's not a dip in quality from one place to the other (as we'd expect in drywall) I would go with the lesser price. How this all would change - If I were ordering twice the amount of product, home depot would win in the price comparison as that 6.77 cost would knock the overall charge down.
