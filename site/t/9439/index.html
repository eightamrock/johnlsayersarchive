Andy25 wrote:
1.) Is ist possible to say where the best mixing position is? So that I could stem the hole for the window on the right place? 2.) Which size would you recommend? To make a compromise between sound isolation and big view. 3.) What kind of floor would you choose? - laminated wood or plastic floor?
1. about 38% of the room length from the front wall. 1a. with the speakers about 70% of the room width and an equilateral triangle formed between the speakers and listening position - with #1 in mind as well. 1b. the monitors (point between high and low frequency drivers) should be at about 42% of the height of the front wall. 1c. the triangle should be centered in an acoustically symmetric position. 2. I'd say a 1.5m to 2m wide window and about 1m high. 3. I'd clean, polish and stain the concrete floor. very durable, easy to clean, sounds good. barring that, wood finish (i'm not plastics type of guy...).

gullfo7 October 2007#17
depends on the speakers... generally speaking you do want them at least 30cm away from the wall and ideally 1m or more, depending on the speaker... if you soffit mount them, you will get a bass boost and would want to compensate for that (baffle step compensation). but most speakers are designed to be free standing. for room treatment - i'd recommend measuring everything first, once you have it built, to determine what adjustments need to be made. that said, for planning purposes, assume you want to install broadband treatments consisting of bass traps in your corners (consider wall-ceiling and wall-floor), first reflection point absorbers, and rear wall absorbers. if you want to go further, then consider slat walls to deflect the sound and rear wall diffusers.

gullfo9 October 2007#19
the window behind you may be a problem - reflecting right back at you - if you want to keep it i'd hang a heavy drape over it when mixing. as far as measuring: download the free Room EQ Wizard, grab your laptop, and your powered monitors (or amp too) and set up your monitors in your room on simple blocks/stands/stools/ladders/etc. if you don't already have a behringer ecm8000 omni mic, get one and a small phantom power capable preamp. put down tape on the floor for the approx positions of monitors and seating. then measure. you might find yourself moving things around as your do this - finding better locations for the monitors or seating. pretty soon you'll have a good picture of whats going on in your room and how you should lay it out and what treatments you'll need. after a couple of thousand measurements, you'll know that room really well. :shock:
