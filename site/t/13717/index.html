lilith_envy27 October 2009#2
Hi Cameron, With regard to the ceiling. If you face the control room so it is lowerest in the front angling to the highest point behind your head. That will force the sound waves to your absorbent rear wall. Or if you run the room the other way it will be fine as well. 2 solid doors- Unless your planning on doing a 2 leaf system, 2 doors will do nothing. It's normally one door per frame. 2 door= 2 seperate frames. You've already said you don't have neighbours, so I would just worry about sealing up the one leaf and doing some nice acoustic treatment. Have you been reading audio technology mag lately?

lilith_envy28 October 2009#4
One of the writers is slowly building a barn studio with hay bails. Interesting one large room design. With the ratios- John made a comment once that he's never worked out a ratio! He just uses as much of the space he can. Bigger the better. Higher the better. Why don't you work the other way- choose the LWH and then punch it into the calculator(you've downloaded the calculator, right) If any nasty Freq stick out change the dimension that leads up to the "foyer type" room. But go the highest you can. The easiest way to do an angled dimension is the mid point. the harder way is choose 3 points and do 3 calculations. Hope that helps
