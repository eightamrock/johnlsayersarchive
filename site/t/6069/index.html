No worries! :D Any reason/s for the floating floor? There's been some discussion over how an improperly floated floor is worse than one not floated at all.
Carpet or wooden floor? And why?
Personally, I would go with a hardwood flooring, and treat the ceiling/walls/corners for various reasons. Acoustically, I prefer a more controlled, live sounding room, and it's easier and neater to put down a temporary area rug when needed, than it is to lay down sheets of say, plywood to liven things up. I also prefer the sound of hardwood floor when recording drums/guitars than the sound of carpet. Practicality-wise, it's easier to: - vacuum a hardwood floor than carpet - roll around equipment/racks/chairs than carpet - wears better over the long term than carpet - maintain on a long term basis than carpet Just my personal preferences and thoughts.
