gullfo13 March 2011#26
the side walls won't have hangers (necessarily) because the slat resonators are sealed units. use the space in the front and rear corners, and overhead in space above the clouds to add additional trapping. on the side door, put a thick absorber so you don't get reflections off that. as far as computing the rays, using the protractor tool to draw lines off your speakers at (say) 60 degree angles to the walls and accounting for the wall angle, the combined new angle (a 60 degree line hitting a 15 degree is 75 degrees...) but overall, in your plan so far, it looks like that won't be an issue. the main reflections will be ceiling and floor and off your desk and other equipment near by. on the back wall plan on absorption with some hard surfaces to preserve the HF energy so your room isn't dead.
