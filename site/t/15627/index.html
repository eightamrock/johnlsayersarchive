gullfo22 February 2011#12
you might have to adjust the front wall a bit to get the soffits sized - its all about balancing the console you have, the monitors you have, the room size to achieve a space where you can fit the console into the right location to have the monitors focus on you correctly plus have the soffits size appropriately for the monitors - as a quick rule, 3 x LF driver radius on each side of the speaker center line - so a 12" woofer needs minimally 3' wide soffits as reasonable minimum size. so if you have a 6' wide console and you need to be @ 42% back from your isolation wall where your initial design said you only have 5', then something has to give - soffit width? console choice? monitors? sidewall treatments? for least evil, i'd choose the side wall treatments as my first compromise, then perhaps soffit angles, go from 30° to say 22.5° etc etc this is why it's critical to understand the equipment and options you might still not have made - so maybe you decide that the 48 channel board will suffice instead of the 96 channel, etc or instead of the Genelec 1037c you get the JBL 63xx monitors etc
