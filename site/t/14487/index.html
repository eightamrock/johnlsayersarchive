bwilkinson22 June 2010#2
Remember that there are more than four corners in a room. There are also floor-to-wall corners, and wall-to-ceiling corners. Those will also build up bass. If there is space against those corners, adding traps there could improve bass absorption. One other thing is that the width of the room is 3 meters, which would be one full wavelength at 114 Hz - very near that peak you are seeing. That's probably not helping much. As an aside, it would be a very good idea to add your location to your profile - rather than simply stating it in the message. A great deal of people will not respond without it.
