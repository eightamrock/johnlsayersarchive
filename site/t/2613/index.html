dymaxian9 November 2004#4
Even with this, we can give some ideas... Any windows in this bedroom? General guidelines- try to set your desk and speakers up along the center-line of the room (in your case, perhaps toward the "bottom" wall and reverse the position of the dresser and bed, but I can't tell without dimensions on the stuff). The idea is to make the wall reflections the same on each side of your head. You're in a small room and your bed is taking up a lot of it. It can be done, but it'll take a lot of getting-used-to-your-speakers and double-checking your work on other sound systems.
