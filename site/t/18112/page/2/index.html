Soundman2020 wrote:
Any idea what the size of those joists is? 2x8? 2x10? And which way do the span? And what distance do they span? And are the part of the roof trusses, or separate? Just trying to get a handle on what the load carrying capability might be...
I haven't seen any measurements in this thread but I can verify it is a Fink style truss which most likely has a 2 inch x 4 inch bottom cord/ceiling joist. The span would better dictate what it is, but this span would have to be long and carrying a large dead load to move up to a 2 inch X 6 inch. In the quest for more clarity, the measurements of the room(s) (usually a requirement to move forward) are needed, to determine the spans and the loads the joists might carry. Have a good weekend :)
