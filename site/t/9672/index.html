gullfo27 October 2007#2
i'd face the desk to the north wall and center it. on the angled ceiling and on the east wall-ceiling i'd put up absorbers across the corners or angled ceiling. on the north vertical corners - put up absorbers across the corners for bass trapping. since you don't have rear corners because of the door and window, just put up a corner trap on the ceiling-wall corner across the room. then treat the first reflection points - front, side, ceiling, and back walls with smaller absorbers. that should be a good start and you can adjust from there if needed.
