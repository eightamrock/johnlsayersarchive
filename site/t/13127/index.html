Soundman20204 July 2009#6
Its recommended 1.2m for speaker position, but that its speaker height right? So i should make my stands 1.2m MINUS the height of the speaker right?
My answer is probably too late to be useful, but the 1.2m height is neither the top nor the bottom of the speaker: It is the recommended height for the acoustic axis of the speaker, ie, the theoretical axis along which the speaker propagates sound. It's hard to find where that is for most speaker manufacturers, since not many of them bother publishing that. It will be somewhere between the woofer and the tweeter, usually closer to the tweeter. If you can't find the data for yours, it probably isn't that important anyway, for small speakers: Even if you just guess that the axis is half way between woofer center and tweeter center, then you won't be off by more than an inch or so from reality (unless you have large speakers). Worst case: you can always move your chair up or down a couple of cm. to find the right spot, after you have finished, if you didn't guess right. So you should build your stands such that the acoustic axis of the speaker is at ear height, which is generally 1.2 m above the floor. If you can't do that for whatever reason (such as computer monitors in the way, or room geometry, or whatever), then you can raise the speakers up higher and tilt them down by up to ten degrees, such that the acoustic axis still intersects your ears at about 1.2 m above the floor. If you tilt them, you need to take care that you don't create reflections off the desk or console surface. - Stuart -
