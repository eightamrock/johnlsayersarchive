The space will be used mostly for band rehersals to start with, but i'm hoping i can start to record for local bands and ourselves really soon. The space is located away from any homes, so noise leakage outside the room is not a problem. There might me some leakage in trough the front door. (There are hobby car mechanics with a space near ours) but i don't think that will be a problem Here are some plans i've made in sketchup, photos of the space and the sketchup file. This is fairly low budget, so we want to try to make the most out of as little as possible, but we will spend what we need to (altough we might then take it in steps), we don't want to spend to little and get bad results. We pay about 27000 icelandi kronur per month for the space, (wich is in direct convertion $416, for example my monthly sailary is about 180000kr ($2776). The budget might then be about 100000-200000kr ($1500-3000), but as i said we try to pay what we need. The Green lines in the top drawing indicate the direction of the first photo, the blue lines is the 2nd photo and the yellow are the last ones. The control room has to have room for the engineer, and preferably a guitarist playing a guitar (yes, inside the control room, with the Amp head in the room and the amp speaker miced up in the rehersal room) we have those small mobile walls seen in the picture that would be used in the rehersal room to isolate the sound source a bit while recording. We were planning to put carpet on walls and floor inside the control and rehersal room (not sure what we do about the cealing) The Sketchup file has about 95% accurate measurements, the cealing seen on photo is 260x280cm and is about 250cm from the floor. gullfo3 March 2008#4
symmetry is most important in the control room and since the door is a hard surface it should be reasonably comparable to your walls anyways (for sound purposes). that said, do a search here for porous or broadband absorption. do not use carpeting for anything other than floors - and you're probably better served with hard floors with small carpets as needed instead of full carpets. you could put in a window between the CR and live room so its centered on the CR wall. plan on absorption overhead as well as sides etc.
