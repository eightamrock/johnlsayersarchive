So there'd be 2 colums coming down to the floor there, and that beam would be down @ 7'? Is the ceiling of the room just above the beam? How high is that ceiling? I don't know how much sound the colums would reflect, but putting trapping on the front of the beam (the side that faces the speakers) wouldn't be too tough, I wouldn't think... Even if you only reclaim a little bit, and put the machine room back there, I'd go with the long control room facing the tracking room. Big CR with a few obstacles > small CR. Good luck!
