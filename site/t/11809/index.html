Soundman202018 March 2009#2
this is no rectangular room at all, what is good i guess).
Not really. Rectangular is good for a control room. And symmetry is essential. But your room is not symmetrical, so it will be really hard to get any kind of stereo image, and your mixes will not translate well. I would suggest that your first priority, way before you think of treatment, os to make your room symmetrical. Build a wall on the left of the mix position that mirrors the wall on the right, and put a door in it for access to the other area. Only when you have made your room symmetrical should you start thinking about treatment. Treating what you have now does not make much sense, in my opinion, since a non-symmetrical control room does not make sense. Regarding the closet: If you fill it with rockwool, then that will deaden your entire front wall, basically, making your room more or less into a LEDE studio. But not too many people build LEDE these days. RFZ seems to be the fad today, as it provides better clarity and stereo image. Maybe you could use that closet space for soffits for your speakers. - Stuart -
