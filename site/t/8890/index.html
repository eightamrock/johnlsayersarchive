The room is pretty quiet already, but I'd like to build a basstrap to control the 'boom'. Is a trap 70cm high, 84cm wide and 50cm deep a sufficient basstrap size for this small room? How big should the port be?
Placing absorbtion in your room is always good, especially in (*all*) the corners (* there are more than 4 corners in your room ;) ) The deeper the trap, the better bass absorption. Again, check the "accoustics" forum here. The matter has been addressed in detail. I also notice you gotta lot of those black/grey foam tiles on the wall and door. Too much of those will disrupt frq balance and you will end up with more bass than mids and highs. Hence your room may "sound" boomy. I'd suggest removing (some) tiles and adding corner traps. Check your room and add more absorbing materials if needed (like extra wool blankets, in frames for example. Or pressure panels. There's lots' a nice DIY stuff out there on the acoustics forum to check out)
