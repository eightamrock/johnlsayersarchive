knightfly21 March 2005#2
Acoustically, there would be little difference whether those treatments were built in or "added on" - that and the rest of your questions would depend on whether you intend this room to be "permanent" or short-term. If the room is to be "permanent" (as in, "don't know when I'm gonna move, but not in the forseeable future") then it's better to approach things with a different attitude. If you let us know which is your situation, and what you intend to use the room for exactly, it will be easier to recommend things... Steve

As to acoustic treatments, Bruce's were built in; the acoustic effect won't change much, unless you try to use 1/4" ply for the backs instead of heavy MDF (all the formulas assume INFINITE mass for the backs) - if you won't be moving soon, I'd go permanent; less chance of things rattling... Steve
