With limited space, I'm not sure if I should go for a large one room studio (e.g. real traps maximum studio) or try and do a live room/control room. Also, isolation is pretty important, things can sometimes be pretty loud. The basement foundation is entirely underground. The bottom of the above flooring is 8'5" from the foundation floor. The bottom of the joists are at about 7'8" from the foundation floor. Problem is I don't know how to ideally pick a room shape. Or if you guys think I should really try and get 2 rooms out of my space... I would like to use the side of the house with no windows for the tracking section. I can pretty much build the 'maximum studio' by scaling down the recommended dimension from a hard ceiling height of 8", but would you guys recommend that? Here's the link to that page if you haven't seen it. http://www.realtraps.com/art_studio.htm My issue with this design is that it is not necessarily maximizing my available space. There would be a lot of wasted space because to fit that room shape in my basement and still have other somewhat normal looking rooms would require squaring out the other rooms. On a side note I'll be following construction methods from Rob Gervais' Home Recording Studio:Build It Like the Pros book. I am an AV integrator by profession. My father who will be helping me build is an extremely skilled master carpenter. Unfortunately, neither of us are acoustic engineers, and neither of us have built a recording studio before.
