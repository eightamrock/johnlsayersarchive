So basically - if you want to go this route you're taking your chances...... without any data to back this up - neither I nor anyone else can guide you on how to proceed. The mineral fiber has been tested and used sucessfully on elevated concrete slabs - so the answer is - so it's better in at least that respect.... other than that - who knows? Rod
