John Sayers2 November 2005#12
Paul - the mistake a lot of people make is that they add fibreglass to their room, and sure, it sounds deader but it's only working from the low mids up - 300 - 500 hz up. It tapers off below that. The room is still live in the low end and totally dead in the high end.. The slots absorb the area from 120hz - 500hz (low mids) yet they reflect/diffuse the mid and high frequencies and stop the room from sounding lifeless, plus being angled they break up the parallel walls. cheers john
