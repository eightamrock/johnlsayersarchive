rod gervais18 January 2008#5
I would not do this...... First off - a 2" jopint is just too big........ 1" is perfect. My usual spec is the 1" iso board from Mason Industries - but I recently found out that they have discontinued that product. So I figured out a way to make your own. Buy the vinyl covered rigid fiberglass insulation from Home Depot - it is about 5/8" thick. Slice it into widths to match slab height at the joint Place 2 pieces back to back and join together with duct tape top and bottom. This places the vinyl to the outside face so the material doesn't soak up a lot of water. Fasten this to a piece of formwork using finish nails Set formwork so the face of iso is at the face of concrete (1/2" from centerline of the 2 walls. Pour your 2 outer slabs - and float - within 2 hour you should be able to remove the forms - pulling the finish nails through the iso-board. Set formwork with remaining boards and pour control room - float same - within 2 hours you should be able to remove form - Pour ramaining slab. Finish concrete slabs in order of pours - Your concrete contractor should not have any problem finish all of this within a long day. Keep concrete wet with poly over it for at least 6 days...... Remove poly - cut edge of iso board from edge of concrete to 1/2" below top of concrete to create a V-shaped recess in the caulk joint - apply caulk. Rod
