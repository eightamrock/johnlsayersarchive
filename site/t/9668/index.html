Lasso, assuming that what I am seeing is moisture (if it is it should be very obvious to you) and not dust from within the wall cavity - then you really need the silica - either way -- the only way to fix this is to take one side apart to get into the window cavity.......... I assume that you very carefully cleaned the inside of glass to a lint free condition before placing the 2nd piece of glazing. Sorry - I know it isn't what you want to hear - but even if you opened a wall to sneak the dissicant in - the windows are streaked now and need to be recleaned. As far as source goes - just google "silica gel desiccant" and you'll get over 250,000 references - a lot of which are suppliers. Rod
