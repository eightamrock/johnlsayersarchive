You didn't mention what brand of caulk you're using... (I sure hope it's not LePage! :roll:) In short, I will say that the fact that it's not drying right away means you probably got the right stuff. That's part of what makes it good -- it never fully dries/hardens like regular stuff, which means it also won't shrink and crack like regular stuff (and that's what you need to not happen). Hope that helps... :mrgreen:
