All About Diffusion is the latest release in our "See and Hear" video lecture series. Most people have no way to hear what diffusors do or how they sound in a room. So this video explains diffusion in plain terms with no math, then lets you hear an acoustic guitar recorded in close proximity to five different surface types - a QRD diffusor, a poly diffusor, absorption, a bare wall, and a typical bookcase. The video is 16x9 widescreen, and the audio is at 256 kbps for highly accurate sound. It's listed about halfway down on the RealTraps Videos page. Enjoy! --Ethan
