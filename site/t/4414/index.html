Aaronw23 September 2005#2
As far as the ceiling...you can use 703 or mineral wool (3PCF) and fill the cavity between the joists. And since you have a low ceiling and probably limited budget, hang RC1 on the joists and then 2 layers of 5/8" drywall on the RC1, being careful to not short out the scres into the joists. The RC will act similar to a spring to absorb some of the vibrations.

sharward24 September 2005#9
DeepGroove wrote:
What does "shorting out" the screws mean? How could I screw the RC up(with me that is much to possible).
If you drive a screw through the RC and into the stud or joist to which it is attached, you've fused a connection between the leaf and the structural member, and since the structural member is attached to the opposing leaf, you have fused the leaves together via the screw. Don't underestimate the severity of that mistake. Two cans and a string pulled tightly will demonstrate the power of a flanking path.
ALSO, I just remembered that I'll have a water pipe to deal with, just a copper 1" pipe . It goes directly across the ceiling. It has been noisy in the past, but I just make sure no water is running while we're tracking. But, how could I box it in, and isolate the sound?
