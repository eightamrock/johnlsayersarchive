Drum, I have Tannoys and love them. They are the Protojs however. Great sounding and have no issues with them free standing or flush mounted. Most people will tell you what ever speakers you flush mount with Johns flush mount design you will have an added bass response. But I also have a bass sub woofer. I had the same issue but have compensated with a low pass filter on my outputs. Once tuned to the room I love them like the day I purchased them. Hope this helps Peace Tom
