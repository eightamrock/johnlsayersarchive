I've recently been given the opportunity convert a disused space in a friends building though into a studio space, and the room is definitely going to need acoustic treatment to be useful for my needs, at the moment it's terribly effective as an echo chamber but not much else. Anyway, I thought I'd jump on here and see if you guys wouldn't mind giving me some advice on things I can do to improve the sound of the room. With that said though, I've read through a lot of posts on the forum and I've been making music in bedrooms for a long time now so I have a reasonable commonsense understanding of how the dynamics of a space can affect room acoustics. The purpose of the space: I plan to use the space primarily for two purposes: making electronic music in stereo and making surround (5.1) sound art/installation projects. I don't plan to record vocals or acoustic instruments. What I hope to achieve: I want a space that creates a playback and monitoring environment that is as accurate as achievable on my budget.
