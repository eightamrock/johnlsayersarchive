Goals
    1. A great control room. The control room will be the most used one, as I mostly do my own productions, mixes and remixes. It's the "work room". 2. If I have a live room, it will not be used often, so I'm considering ditching it, but if I find a nice layout that I think will work I might build it after all, and maybe do some band recording there as well. 3. To have a small, but decent vocal booth for recording one vocalist. 4. To not disturb the people living in the apartment behind the rooms.
Facts
    -I've realised that my previous "budget" plan was a bit of a joke.. It'll probably be (a lot) more expensive than that. So, maybe around $3700 - $7400 then. - The walls are cinderblock, I would think the outside walls are atleast 20cm, since the separating wall is 20cm. - The ceiling is what I so far call "cinder plank", as they are long slabs of cinder. - on 2-flip_room1.jpg you see something that looks like yellow brick on the wall, but that's not the case. For some reason the previous owners have put up some sort of paper-maché, that LOOKS like a brickwall. It's just nailed to whatever is behind. I'm not quite sure if the actual wall between this room and the apartment living room is cinderblock or just drywall/etc. I will check this out. - The apartment is now rented out to someone, and I hope I can have as little sound as possible reaching them. - I usually monitor at low levels, with the occasional high, to check things.
Problems Now, there is an issue with the ceiling height as you can see.. it's just 212cm.. There are two options I guess. Either
    1. live with it, having thinner floor and ceiling. Or, 2. using (I don't know what it's called) but, carving out the concrete floor to get more height, and make a new floor. The problem is I don't know what is underneath.. Could be hard rock for all I know..
Questions:
    1. Would it be an idea to move the entire entrance door more to the right, to have it parallel with the opening between room1 and 2? (This would allow the creation of a straight hall). 2. What kind of treatment would the cinder walls need? Should I attach some drywall or similiar? 3. Do the cinder walls act as one leaf, so I should then have air, and one more wall? If I count as describet.. 1 - 2.. 4. Or is the outside wall not related to the M-A-M principle, and I need a new set of double walls within the cinderblock. 5. Is it OK to leave the ceiling height as it is, and have less mass and only 1 leaf in the floor and ceiling, and have thicker walls, or is the room only as soundproof as it's weakest point (being its walls, floor or ceiling)?
I hope that covered it all.. If not, just ask :) I'm still absorbing information from the different threads, so I guess some of these questions will answer themselves when I find the right information for me..
