jwl7 March 2007#12
In general, you do not want your monitors firing diagonally into the room, in a rectangular room. A good guideline is to measure 38% of the way back on the longest dimension of the room, centered between the 2 side walls.... this will be your "sweet spot". Your mixing chair should go here. Then put the speakers in an equilateral triangle in convenient spaces from that point. Fire your monitors lengthwise into the room, in other words. Then with acoustic treatments you will want to create a RFZ, and put absorption on the back wall. Plus your bass trapping. That's a small space, but you could make it sorta cozy for a production-only room....
