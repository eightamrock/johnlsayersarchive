gullfo26 October 2010#52
if it was actually a hard shell, you might end up with a concave situation where the peak could result in undo concentration. in this case, the ceiling will be a false ceiling below the isolation ceiling. this leaves you a lot of options for ensuring the sound travels correctly and you don't take up valuable floor space with large traps.

gullfo26 October 2010#54
what i would do would be to build the inner leaf wall parallel to the outer truss up to the dividing wall to create as much volume in that space as possible, then add a lower false ceiling with the appropriate angles. even so, if you went with an inside out approach and given the fairly low angle it shouldn't be an issue as the inner portion of that new ceiling is all about absorption anyways. in that case, you'd build it out so the ceiling is as high as possible and use some clouds to do the final shaping.
