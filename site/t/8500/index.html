Aloha, Q - According to your initial layout, were you planning for a 5.1 setup? Here are a couple of views of an idea for your review - In Rod Gervais’ book “Build it Like the Pros,” http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=5670 he points out both L.W.Sepmeyer’s & M.M. Louden’s formulas most widely used in professional studios worldwide. He also notes that the orders listed are (top to bottom) the first to third best ratios in both cases. M.M. Louden Height Width Length 1.00 1.40 1.90 15’ x 21’ x 28’5” 1.00 1.30 1.90 1.00 1.50 2.10 L.W. Sepmeyer Height Width Length 1.00 1.14 1.39 1.00 1.28 1.54 1.00 1.60 2.33 - Side slat walls are @ 12 degrees - Front soffit walls are @ 30 degrees - Doors, windows to be determined by your work flow Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
