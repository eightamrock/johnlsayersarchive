Ro4 August 2008#5
It's "just" sealant kit. With that Greenchoise stuff you caulk the perimeters of the walls, as the label says, to close leaks. GG is used in between layers, GB for example. It's used for dampening a sandwiched layer. 2 different materials, 2 different purposes. About you Q on which caulk to use: Choose for a kit that stays flexible, has a good mass, doesn't shrink too much (pref <3%). Transparant kit has a typical mass of about 1000 kg/m3 while coloured kit is a bit heavier, more like 1400 kg/m3. Getting the later will match you GB walls mass better (since you'll only caulk about half the with of the GB and not with "full" volume) Acrylic will work just fine, silicon will do better but is much more expensive.
