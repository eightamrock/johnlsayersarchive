Well... hard for any forum member to suggest anything with no drawings or pics and limited details. Bottom line is if you are going to build rooms of a studio that have an amount of sound isolation (no room is completely sound proof) then it will aslo be air tight THUS requiring ventilation. Forgive me if I'm wrong but this sure sounds like you've not thought this. Rod's book covers (in the stickies) also covers these topics and many others. Good luck Marc
