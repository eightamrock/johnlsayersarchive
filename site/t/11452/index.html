Hi all. My question is regarding insulation type for wall construction. I have read many posts regarding Owens Corning 703 and (here in Europe) Rockwool and Steinwool being used for room treatment and bass traps. Could someone please clarify something for me? Do the same criterion apply for in-wall construction? Is a higher density insulation better inside the wall or will using a less dense (and dare I say it...cheaper....there, I said it!) type of insulation achieve the same results? I can't seem to find any posts where somebody really says it. Cam.
Soundman20201 December 2008#2
The general rule that I've seen quoted is to use mineral wool with a density of something like 48 kg/m^3. More important than the density is the depth: You want to use as much as you possibly can, deep and wide and thick, filling the entire wall cavity if you have the money for that. - Stuart -
