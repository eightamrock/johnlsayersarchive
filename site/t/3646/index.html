John Sayers29 April 2005#5
How much isolation do you need? I assume your ceiling is made up of floor joists (10"??) so your actual ceiling height to the upper floor is 6'4" + 10". If you fill the space between the joists with 703 and add a cloth finish you'll wipe out the ceiling completely acoustically. Otherwise you are going to have to add drywall on RC which is goping to make your ceiling even lower. cheers john
lazlominimart30 April 2005#6
Don't need much if any isolation. Only doing mixing and mastering. The ceiling is exposed joists, so I can certainly try the 703+cloth approach. What cloth would you recommend? Do you have pictures of such a ceiling? Thanks for these tips!
John Sayers30 April 2005#7
Any cloth really - I'd cover it with thin garden plastic after the 703 is in which will stop the constant shower of fiberglass dust you get otherwise. If you want to increase your isolation to upstairs you can always put in a couple of layers of OSB attached to the upper floor between the joists before you fill with 703. as per drawing cheers john
