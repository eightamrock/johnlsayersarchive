Hi! First of all I want to congratulate you all for creating/keeping this wonderful forum! This is my first post but I have been reading it a bunch in the last few weeks and am very impressed by the community here. right on. Major Goals - to create a fairly flat (within small-room limitations) control room, and a fairly bright and lively, yet very disperse live room (will consider dual-mode devices to deaden it, but this is not essential). I hope to create an "Illusive" live room that sounds much bigger than it is, as much as possible. So far I am just in the design process - I have not touched the rooms with a hammer or screwdriver yet. I have been doing a fair bit of research and I have a paper sketch almost finished of my own ideas of roughly what I want to do. I was hoping to have it Sketchup'd but learning sketchup is taking a little longer. Usage: My clients include everyone from singer/songwriters to punk rock bands to rappers, but I have access to a few other rooms here in Edmonton, and lately I've been recording drums at a couple of studios. Powersound is one of them (www.powersoundstudios.com, www.myspace.com/powersoundstudios) and they built a beautiful live room a year ago. Basically, I don't need to rely on my room for recording drums, but if there was a possibility I COULD get a good drum sound in it, I might be tempted to try:) Sources to be recorded: Vocals, Guitars (electric and acoustic), percussion, other acoustic instruments, and vocals!! oh and possibly also some vocals! I'm not very loud very often - So I'd rather spend money on sounding GOOD rather than being absolutely quiet. The house IS a duplex though, so I'll probably just work out with the neighbors when the best hours to record loud guitar amps are, and do it then. My wife and daughter are wonderfully tolerable. By Loud, I mean, 4x12 guitar cabs, etc. most of the time it's vocals/acoustic guitar, etc. Budget - I'd like to complete this around end of summer, or early fall, and funding it as I go, I'm looking to spend between $1000-2,000, but I do have access to some things that should hopefully help me keep the costs down (a friend with a mill, etc). I'll include some photos, my sketchup 2d, so the rooms can be visualized. feel free to let me know if I've left anything out! Explanations: the larger room is the control room, there is an electrical panel on the biggest exterior wall. Hopefully the photos will be clear! ...hmm it won't accept any of my live room pics, perhaps i've exceeded the max attach count? will be back again soon! Thankyou in advance for any feedback... Jeff

gullfo17 July 2007#8
the door would be a window door so you can see into the live room.... the drywall "beef" would be to add a layer of drywall between the ceiling joists so it contacts the upper floor. this adds mass. then the new ceiling would be hung using RC-1/RC-2 to isolate the new ceiling from the existing framing. you'll definitely need some bass trapping probably in all the corners of the CR and live room. a cloud over the mix position, maybe something in the live room as well.
