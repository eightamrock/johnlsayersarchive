gullfo24 March 2007#2
RT60 and diffusion will only be significant if the CR is large enough to actually create true reverberation, and also allows the diffusion treatments to do their job and not impact the listening position with extraneous reflections. the specs and comments you've read are not necessarily at odds. in a small CR where you're only 6-7'/2-2.5m from the speakers, you're already going to be dealing with a lot of reflections all around unless you can absorb things enough so you can accurately tell where the sound is coming from, and on top of that, you don't want it too dead or the imaging won't be right either and the mix won't necessarily translate as well... setting up a 5.1 mix room requires a lot more "little" acoustic treatments to create the right balance between treating the problem frequencies of the room, cutting unwanted reflections, keeping enough life in the room to ensure your imaging is correct, and you don't have too much reverberation... you're not going to do that with "large strokes" of treatments but you can do it with smaller ones...
