Andre is (as usual/often) correct. Yet there is some analogy with thermal isolation. Thermal isolation, based on whatever fibrous material, also needs gasflow resistance in order to prevent convection and resulting heat transport in and through the material. This property is the basis of acoustic absorption as well. But still, Andre's comments are very valid.
