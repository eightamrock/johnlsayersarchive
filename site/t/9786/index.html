sandledfoot28 November 2007#4
yeah, as you suspect, ideally you want your monitors facing down the length of the room, so as an example, in reference to your drawing, the engineer would face towards the left wall, and would be able to turn his head to the right to look into the live room when nessasary... so as drawn, yes your studio can be built and work effectively... however, JS has a few rooms that are lateral, such as music farm... so, even if your stuck with facing the live room directly, then you may want to add several more feet, you'll find that you won't be able to get the correct monitor positioning with only 7' of space between the front and rear wall. if your primarily concerned with isolation and wanting a large live room, you may just consider a one room studio... (as i'm sure you have already) k

in this case of this example control , the room is rectangular and the interior walls are treatments. nothing prevents you from using it as a single rectangular room and just applying treatments along the walls and ceiling to get the balance you want. if you needed to turn it sideways (for instance having 2 rooms) you can do that if the dimensions are good but you have to treat the walls a bit differently to get the right balance between direct and reflect sounds...
