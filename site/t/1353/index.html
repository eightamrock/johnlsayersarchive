That's looking good mate. A couple of things- firstly the main speakers should be at 30 degrees, not the 45 you've drawn and yes you can make the side splays slots. The bathroom doesn't resolve properly IMO - the door/passage into the office is way too small ;) cheers john

Thanks a lot for your input, John. Sincerely. I have modified the floorplan (as you can see, there is an updated picture now) to include a 60º angle for the big monitors... and I didn't know where to put the genelec 1031's so I put them provisionally also in a soffit position. :roll: The access to the office was not well drawn, in fact three of the walls surrounding the stairs are only 1 meter high (less than 3 feet, in fact), so it doesn't look so narrow once you are there... I built some corner traps as explained by Everest and effect was good, but not enough to tame all modal problems... this time I would like to get it right at the first time :x :?: :?: :?: In fact, I must admit I have ZERO practical info on how and where place absorption... but I'm confident that further search in the archives will bring me some light.
