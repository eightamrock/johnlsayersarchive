Hi, Thank you for being honest as to this being a project. A couple of quick pieces of advice: Spend several days going through the forum and get a handle on the standard approaches being used. Decide on a design and sketch it up then ask for feedback. Remember all of the different elements have to work together so a more holistic approach may be in order. Don't try and reinvent the wheel. If you stay within a tried and trued design the outcome will be much easier to judge. I know this doesn't answer your question directly, but it is a better method for you to be able to defend your design decisions. Steve

Cold22 March 2011#10
not too bad for a starting point, now defend this as the best design for your allotted space. Some hints: Are the access routes optimal? Are the sight lines as good as they can be? Are the window locations going to impede treating first reflection points? Have you used the space as efficiently as possible? (do you need the hallway?) Would it be better for the performers if the vocal booth was right next to the live room? Will the live room comfortably hold the required number of people? Is the control room big enough? And you may wish to sketch with proper double walls. Steve
