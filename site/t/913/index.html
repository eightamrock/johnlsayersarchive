I just made one. I had a 2'x4' piece of mineral wool leftover from my gobo so I decided to wrap it in cloth, and "float" it. I ended up putting a 1/4" plywood backing on it to make it more rigid with some eye screws to hang. It seems to work well, but I really have nothing to compare it too. One thing to add though... This 4" mineral wool that I used is ALOT heavier than I thought is was going to be. That cloud I just described probably weighs 25#, not to mention the gobo has got to be 150-200#. Ron
