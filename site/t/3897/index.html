knightfly21 June 2005#4
That type floor is notorious for crappy sound; no damping, bad ringing at resonance, good rodent habitat (in some cases) - I wouldn't recommend it. Better to do solid runners with heavy double or triple layer of wood over, and run wires between the runners. You would pick specific runs to keep clear, then damp ALL the other cavities with rockwool to prevent ringing. This is assuming you don't need super isolation/floating floor construction, otherwise it's a LOT deeper subject.
