that looks nice and spacious. i'd probably err on the side of making it more lively by adding more broadband slat resonators and but keep the ceiling somewhat/slightly more absorptive since you have a wooden floor (in the design). if its too live for a particular track you could cover the slats with some heavy drapes to cut the highs, or use some free standing absorbers.
