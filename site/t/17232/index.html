Hi everyone, I'm about to make a "heavy soundproofing" door for a rehearsal room that I recently aquired. :D I don't intend to make a double leaf construction. Just match more or less door's STC to the wall's STC. The windows are to be closed with 3 layers of gypsum board & green glue.(There will be ventilation of course with silencers :) ) Simple stuff, Low budget and no need for extreme soundproofing. The weakest link in my construction is the door, The main features of the door design are: -Mass -Heavy duty Hinges (ARRONE AR8130-SSS) -Door closer (Dorma TS83 - [size 3-6 model]) -Lorient Magnetic Seals all around for seal. Or will there be a lot of loss through the joints of the 1"x4"s? If it is not a feasable construction are plywood layers better than MDF layers for this purpose? Thank you, vakis

xSpace1 December 2011#5
Vakis, Couple things. If you have just no money left and nothing but materials...rather than the vertical/horizontal attempt, set all the 2x4's vertical but lap the joints. This eliminates the leakage areas and gives the door the structural rigidity it is going to require since it will be heavy and 4 or 5 inches think. A more possible option, if you cannot do as John suggested, would be to install 2 layers of 5/8 fire rated sheetrock and mill the frame to accept the sheetrock on the interior in place of all the lumber. This would reduce your door thickness which will reduce your door jamb thickness resulting in either breaking even or at least having a thinner door with the same amount of mass, that the hinges can support and the door closure can move :)
