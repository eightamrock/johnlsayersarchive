Cram30 January 2008#2
You may want to provide more info - What currently separates the two rooms? Is there an existing floor there and what's it made of? Joists - what size, span (this will determine weight to be added on top of deck)? Column supports? Is the room currently finished with existing wallboard or do you have a clean slate? Stairway up to the second room? is it within the structure or outside with a door above? Flat roof vs angle... These are all factors I see discussed here. There are many studio build examples here with multiple levels. barns, warehouses, basements... as always a clear and informative guide is rod's book - search amazon for "rod gervais"
