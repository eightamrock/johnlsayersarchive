jlemon21 December 2008#1
Hey people, I hope this topic belongs in this forum and not in the Construction one... Ro, I know... :) Last night I had a very interesting conversation with an old friend who happens to work as a mechanical engineer and specializes in HVAC design. Although I didn't quite get everything that he threw at me (I definately got the part that includes four figure checks :roll: ), I went home and made a quick sketch of the design I had in mind (sketchup file attached below). As you can see in the picture below (and I hope I can describe it correctly with my poor english in the way :roll: ), an intersection of a typical wall (5cm (2") of fiberglass wool insulation, 5cm (2") total thickness of drywall), with the 20cmx20cm (8"x8") minimum aluminium or plastic HVAC channel hidden behind the corner on the wall that it passes by. To cover this unwanted (visually and acoustically) corner, a 1m (3f) bass trap is attached to wall and celing with fiberglass wool insulation inside and a cloth to cover it. Fixed (and sealed) to the channel (where we want the air to get in or out of the room) we have 10cmx10cmx50cm (4"x4"x20") wooden (plywood, mdf, whatever) with silencers (covered by the bass trap of course). My question is: Can I implement this design? Is it doable? Is it gonna work? These are actually three questions but... what the heck! :) Your feedback is, as always, much appreciated. Greetings from sunny Crete 8) , John
