gullfo12 December 2010#2
the idea is simple enough - create a stand massive enough to prevent the speakers from moving and from directly vibrating the surfaces it is in contact with. the mass (stand and speaker) ensures the foam (or rubber/sylomer/sorbathane etc) is properly compressed to maximize its isolation value and not shift when the speakers are firing. can you simply put this type of stand (blocks) on the carpet? possibly if the carpet will perform the decoupling job. if not, then adding the foam will be helpful. on the top, there are several options: 1) use a layer of foam/rubber/etc under the speaker, 2) a solid block on top and the supplied feet, 3) obtain a pair of the monitor pads with the steel plate and put those on top of the stand.
