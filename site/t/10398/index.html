I'm taking it that the number in their product code refers to the kg/m3 density Example - RS33 = 33 kg/m3, RS60 = 60kg/m3 all the way up to their RS200 = 200kg/m3
Yes, your assumption looks correct. Many European manufacturers use a similar scheme with the kg/m3 in their name, afaik. Most slabs of the same density and thickness are more or less comparable when it comes to absorption (the difference is usually only minor and not possible to hear).
