D.DiAnda31 May 2008#1
Hi, I'm new here.. and new to most of the concepts discussed here (I'm trying really hard to change that though). Somehow, I've gone years playing and writing music, but never paid attention to the acoustics of the rooms I've worked in. :oops: I've been obsessively reading through these forums and have picked up a ton of useful info, and am looking forward to trying out some of the DIY projects I've seen here. I have three rooms to choose from in my house for my little studio. I'm mostly doing my music on a PC, using samplers and synths. I do occasionally record acoustic guitar too. I'm mainly looking to clear up the playback sound to improve my mixes. Sound isolation is not an issue here. Before I start on any DIY improvements, I wanted to get opinions on which room would be the best for a studio. I suspect that I'm currently in the worst of the three rooms - which I chose for convenience and aesthetics. (In my defense, you should see the view from this room - it overlooks a big slough [tidal river], and I can watch egrets, ducks, & various shorebirds from my studio). I realize that it's the most cube-like room of the three, but I'm hoping that it's possible to make it work somehow - I like this room much more than the others. Maybe the fact that it's the only one with 9 ft. ceilings redeems it somewhat (?). Thanks! Dana
