mattssons wrote:
You don´t see theses designs around anymore, we´re they that bad??... or has everybody only copied design after design until the original thoughts are missing? Any one actually knows anything about it? Or are the designer´s thought gone forever?
Studio design and use has evolved. When that studio was designed, EVERYTHING was recorded in one take. the rooms were ambient enough to give the performers a sense of ensemble and give a base sound to the track. Tracks if stereo. The basic ingredients are all still there: absorption, reflection and diffusion and heavy absorption on the ceiling to avoid comb filtering from early reflections. Why studios work well has become common knowledge so it is no longer a black art. Andre
