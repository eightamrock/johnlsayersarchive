Sound-Weavers.com1 February 2009#4
If this is a new build, I would recommend a cleared ceiling height (from floor to ceiling) of at least 10' - especially since you are going to record drums in there. Better if you can go to 12' ceiling height on the tall side (sloping ceiling height). You have a good enough space to accommodate a live band BUT my experience is in the future you will realize the floor area is really kind of limited. For a new build, is there any way you can extend the Length and Width by at least 3'? Since this is a new build you gotta ask these questions now. Expansion once the studio is built and operational is going to prove more costly. I'm also finalizing plans for a new studio build inside a shell attached to my house. the dimensions of the shell L=28.7' W=16.6' H =19.5'(sloping height 21' on house side, 18' on front side). Already, I regret not having taken more of the available floor area BUT I am stuck with the shell now, no way my wife will allow me to tear down this newly built attached to our newly built house. I'm also building a loft so I can give my asst his own Edit Room (which will sit on top of the Control Room.
