This is my first post, i am putting together a new studio in the 2nd level of an old office building, my total dimensions are 18 by 34, which will be separated into a control room and a tracking room, the control room will be 12 x 18 and the tracking room 22 x 18. I record mostly loud rock, metal, hardcore, and punk with an occasional acoustic project. As of right now, i am on the far end of the 2nd floor, and there are no businesses on my floor on my side of the building. Im on a tight budget, and am running out of time, as my possession period starts on october 1st. I am trying to finalize my ideas and design and I have 2 main questions about my control room. 1. Since my control room is 18x12 should i position my setup so it is wider (18ft wide 12 feet deep) when looking at my monitors, or longer (18th feet long 12 feet wide). I believe running the long way will be more functional for my purposes, but i have heard from a few sources that running the wide way will be easier to create a decent mixing position. 2. For my wall that separates my control room and my tracking room, would you recommend doing a double window with an extra insulated regular door or putting in 2 double sliding doors that function as a window and a door.
