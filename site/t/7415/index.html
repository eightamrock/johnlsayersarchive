knightfly2 November 2006#2
From the (no longer available) US Gypsum Sound Control Manual - "A similar situation occurs when a stairway is attached to a party wall. Often the stairway is built and installed before the gypsum panels. Attached directly to the partition studs, the stairway produces a sound path directly into the adjacent apartment. The occupants complain that "people are walking through the living room." Stairs should be supported independently to allow space between them and the partition for normal drywall treatment; or better, the stairway should be installed after the gypsum panels have been hung, taped and caulked (Figure 86)." Copy of Fig.86 below - I've attempted to contact USG's webmaster to see if this resource can be reinstated - but don't hold your breath... Steve I've not tried this, but would think that a layer of Celotex or similar soft board between stringers and drywall might further deaden any flanking.
