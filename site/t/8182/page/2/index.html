Aloha,
I changed the walls to be angles so the sound goes to the back
Very good.
where the diffusers and bass traps are
Eliminate the diffusers, completely. They are not beneficial in your situation, and will be an unnecessary expense.
Keep in mind, the stone wall will do its part,
Replace stone wall with side slat wall absorbers design posted earlier. Both left and right side front halves of your room should be slat wall absorbers. While stone will indeed reflect sound, due to the size of your room, you will need to absorb as much mid-low freqencies as you can at the same time. Using a slat wall absorber is much more efficient use of your available space as it will help you accomplish both tasks (reflecting highs and absorbing mid-lows) at the same time. The back and rear side walls should be absorbers - floor to ceiling - which is basically the side slat wall absorber design minus the slats. The corner traps will help absorb some of the low freqs. You may find that more than one will be needed, thus the corner trap above the door as well as the trap in the corner. You will also want to center your monitors on the soffit walls between the intersection of the side slat wall and the center wall section.
