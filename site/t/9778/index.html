EK Sound23 November 2007#2
To do this, you would need a console or controller with a GPI connector (general purpose interface) that would provide contact closure when in record mode. If your equipment does not have such a connector, you may have to manually close a switch (normal light switch) to activate the light. Sorry I can not be of more assistance. Dave.
