John Sayers21 October 2006#2
My goal is a natural, live sound, with a nice short decay in the room. I am tired of carpet and foam.
OK - let's get a few things straight. You said "nice short decay" Put your room dimensions into This reverb calc. Average your shorter walls say 20' x 11.5' x 8' (I assume it's 8' - you didn't mention your height). Put in Gypsum Board for walls and ceiling and timber for floor. @ 4khz it's 1.1sec @ 2khz it's 1.38sec @ 1khz it's 1.98sec @ 500hz it's 1.51sec @ 250hz it's .91sec @ 125hz it's .36sec. as you can see that's not a nice short decay, nor is it an even decay as it's longer around 500 - 1Khz than at 125hz. In fact 125hz is where you want it cos that's a nice short decay. Gypsum board is already a bass absorber. Now put the heavy drapes (the closest to 703) on the front and back walls and you get a more even decay around .5 sec. NO bass traps needed. It sortta speaks for itself doesn't it. Geometry is another story as it pertains to stereo imaging. cheers john
