Hi. Please read the forum rules for posting (click here). You seem to be missing a couple of things! :)
I've done a little reading on the subject and it appears the "crawl space" may be acting as a resonant chamber. I have also noticed that there is a slight delay between the bass being heard in the PA and it shaking a load of stuff upstairs.
Very probably, that is what is happening. the delay is the giveaway: It takes time for the resonance to build and reach the point where it amplifies, since system is very massive. I'm also guessing that when the bass note stops suddenly, the resonance continues on for a short time, probably several hundred milliseconds....
Second question: Anything I can do about it, short of demolishing the building and starting again?
It's not going to solve the problem entirely, but it might help: you could fill the cavity completely with something like OC-703, or something similar. - Stuart -
