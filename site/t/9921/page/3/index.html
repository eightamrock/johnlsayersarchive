Yes - that's what they saying - but why did hidley hang them behind walls and ceilings? Hidley originally built what were called "compression ceilings" where the ceiling over the console was solid timber with hangers behind in the ceiling cavity. In his Quad rooms there were two ceilings with hangers behind. In his live room at Paradise studios in Sydney, which I watched being built, he had hangers behind the 1/2" glass mirror walls. He never appeared to take into consideration the direction of the sound as this article suggests.
orangenumerik30 June 2009#59
There is a lot of info on hangners and hidley's design in the "recording studio design" book by P.Newell. The control room design shown in the pict is a non-enviroment studio where every surface exept the front walls and floor are made as absorbent as possible to the lowest range possible. Here's a rough "resumé" of some findings made by Mr newell in a studio of that kind during construction. The first thing that he says is that those panel do not work by themselves but rather in an array. The hangers on the side have almost no effect on low freq. but a huge effect on higher freq. Array of Hagners on the rear wall yeild better low end absorptions if they are angled about 45 deg from the source. The angle toward the speaker of the ceiling hangers is not a dominant factor but the size and angle play an important role. I might also add that hangner made by M. Newell in his numerous studio are made from chip board covered with deadsheet and rockwool or felt. Also the walls behind those hangners are not just double 5/8 gypsum board on 2X6 structure but rather a deadsheet in a double 1/2 gypsum sandwich, that itself act as a low freq absorber. There is often a curtain of deadsheet suspended in front on the walls. The good low frequency response of a room like this is a result of a multitude of construction technique to get there, not just the hangners. They are a complement. There is a section where some test where made to measure their effect but to be quite honnest i'm not sure i undestand it quite well so i won't get into that. My 2 cents
