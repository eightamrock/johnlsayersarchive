knightfly26 February 2006#2
Hi Nick, and welcome; first, I've deleted your cross-posts in other forums because it's difficult enough following ONE thread, much less 3 that each end up growing separate lives. I kept the one in Construction because you'll probably get most results here. Your questions - 1 - floating floors; have you read much on this? And why do you feel the need for floated rooms? Have you done any kind of testing in your space to see just how much isolation you actually NEED? Each level of isolation gets LOTS more expensive and time-consuming, which is why I ask. Air compressor mounts - might work, check out the first page of the Aaron's floated floor thread in the REFERENCE section of the Construction forum for ways of testing this - WITHOUT testing, you're just shooting craps (and the odds are against you) 2 - Check out the Building Products forum for some sources/ideas. 3 - 6 feet wide rooms??!? Maybe for the amp/vox booth, but drums??!? Gonna sound like CRAP, no matter WHAT you treat the room with - not to mention, it'll be tough to even cram a decent kit in that size room much less move around it enough to mic it, and FORGET a decent sound. You'd actually be better off with an electronic kit than that. I checked out your site, am wondering how you finished the wall next to the roll-up door (and whether it's working isolation-wise) - if you can put up a floor plan maybe we can figure out a better way for a decent drum room, unless you're actually LOOKING for that "crap" sound. And finally, I know the crunch of needing it up and running NOW, but rushing usually causes BIIIIG mistakes - been there... Steve
