Soundman20202 July 2009#2
My first though here is that diffusion on such a small room doesn't make much sense anyway. According to André, you need several meters of space for diffusion to avoid lobing issues, and you just don't have it in a small room. Second, 8" tubes aren't very big for diffusion in the low end, which is where you need it most, and those tubes aren't very thick or dense. Those won't have much effect at all below about 450 Hz. or so. Best case: You might get some minimal effect in the highs, but I doubt it would be worthwhile, especially if you have no absorption at all. IMHO - Stuart -
