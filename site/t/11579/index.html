Ro15 January 2009#3
Hai there TJ, 4' panels are good. Remember; thicker panel is better low-frq absorbing. There's plenty of corners in a room, wall/ceiling & wall/wall & wall/floor. The first 2 are eazy to fill with basstraps. The wall panels will act more as broadband. You might wanna put a small plastic layer on the front (under the cloth) of the BASS traps so you won't absorb too many mid/high frq with that setup. Consider ceiling panels as well if you've got a hard floor. Boominess is a word, or atleast in the world of acoustics :) And yes, by adding BASS traps (the thick panels of wool) you WILL lower that phenomenon. But soundleakage to adjacent rooms will still continue. Tho, when there's less boom there should be less low frq leakage... You can't have too many BASS trap, but you CAN have too many midrange absorption. Be ware of that. You need LOW frq absorption at first. now :shot:
