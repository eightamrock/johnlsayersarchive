knightfly19 May 2006#2
There's a bit more to it than that; a separate structure will have a LOT less flanking into the house, but if that's not an issue I'd look more at exterior shell - the more mass (and the easier to plug any holes with high mass material) the better. Strong ceiling/roof is also important, ceilings are usually the weak area. Make sure the existing space is at least 2-3 feet bigger in all directions than you need, so there's room for inner, massive walls - a heavy mass-air-mass envelope is what does the trick for stopping ALL frequencies of sound...
