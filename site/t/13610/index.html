Soundman202014 September 2009#2
You seem to be talking about two types of wiring at once: electrical and signal. If you don't feel comfortable with electrical wiring, and indeed if you are not qualified / certified to do it, then don't even THINK about trying to do it yourself! Hire a qualified electrician to do it. That really is your only option. Electricity is dangerous stuff (as you already found out, as a child!). Don't play with it. But signal cabling is a very different matter. There is little danger in doing that yourself, especially if you are handy with a soldering iron. If not, then a good, simple alternative is to just go with ready-made snakes (like the one you linked to). They are already wired, with a stage box on one end (that you can frame neatly and attach to the wall), and pigtails on the other end, that you just plug directly into your equipment or patch panel. They already have good shielded conductors, so interference and cross talk should not be an issue. The toughest problem with that, is getting the damn cables through the ducts between rooms, and then sealing the ducts! But that is (arguably) much easier and faster than trying to solder dozens of connectors by yourself, if you are not familiar with soldering. My 0.02, FWIW! - Stuart -
