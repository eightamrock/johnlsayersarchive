JohnGardner28 August 2005#41
Hi Keith, There are a couple of studios (several infact) on Johns "Studio's Under Construction" link that use this method. Check out "Siliva Productions", "Leftbank" and "GuruLand" as working examples with photo's of this method. http://www.johnlsayers.com/Studio/Mainp ... ruland.htm John also recomended this method when I used his phone service. I can actually see how it will work, especially with a existing floor like mine that is slightly uneven and has some old glue lines from where the carpet was ripped up. You have to imangine the walls are cut very tight (We measured each Stud) so they will compress the rubber top and bottom when stood into place. I actually expect to have to hammer them into there final posistion with a rubber claw hammer once the walls are lifted. The rubber is also flush with the back edge of the lumber to allow a nice small gap to caulk in the front once the walls are up. Keith, all this is pretty new to me and the problem is I suppose you never really know how it's going to work untill it's finished or if you have done it before!!. All I can do is have some faith in some of the advice I have been given / paid for and as the shoe man says "Just do It" JG
