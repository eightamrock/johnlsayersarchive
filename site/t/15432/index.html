gullfo29 December 2010#2
welcome! how high is the existing ceiling over the stage? the main challenge with the stage will be the flanking from the CR to the vox booth because of the low but solid mass of the stage. plus the stage may have any number of resonant properties to consider. depending on getting the stage to a low resonance, you could float the floor of the CR / booth to decouple or perhaps consider some concrete on the stage if it could support it. possible cut the stage into separated floors and add a layer of concrete? then dampen the internal cavity underneath?

gullfo31 December 2010#14
no deep calculations as i was trying to maximize the room space for the CR as the volume is low (1100/ft3 - 32/m3) but you want to try to avoid making the ceiling height and room width too square - either going taller (10ft/3050mm) or lower (8ft/2440mm). no ideal ratios but it's the space you have on the stage. one option would be to make inside-out walls and gain an extra 7" (175mm) between the isolation drywall layers and lose some isolation over a double-sided wall (using the space inside the hall as an air gap). this could help with the bass trapping (as it will leak into your hall). on the vox booth keep the double sided walls.
