Hello everyone! My name is Ram and I come from Vipava, Slovenia. I’ve just finished SAE and got employed there as a supervisor :D. And when I came home my father told me some magical words for every 22-year-old audio enthusiast: “Son, I would like you to build a studio in old part of the house. I have some money, can get more, but don’t have nor the knowledge, nor the will to do it.” :shock: :D 8) So my eyes are shining since then and I am lurking this forum for a few months now, gathering information on everything I think I need to know. My goal is to build a quite large CR, LR big enough for drums or so, Vocal Booth and a Concert Room, which would be also used as a big LR. I also planed to put in smallish office, “lounge”(place with a couch and TV), and of course the Machine Room. I would like to record everything in this studio. Provided that it will have Concert Room, I am dreaming of bigger groups, such as choirs or small orchestras (pardon my English, but I don’t know better words to describe this) Buget is roughly around 40000$. We're planing to do a lot of things from wood, since my father is a forester and owner of forests. Thous everything that can be made from wood will be. :) We are going to slowly start now and I have and will have a lot of questions. I am not quite sure whether this topic should be posted to Studio Design or Studio Construction but I'm putting it here as I am beginning with layouts. PS: All the measurements are in meters since I’m from a metric country. But I can do also the imperial versions if needed. I made my own personal Excel inch-feet-meter calculator. :) No questions since I'm sending new posts right away :wink: [/i]

jwl15 November 2007#5
Looks good, though the angles in the control room don't look quite right. Ideally, you want the control room as symmetrical as possible, especially from the mix position forward. I'd reduce the angle of the left wall a bit so it is the same angle as the wall leading to the machine room. Apart from that, sight-lines are a bit of a problem, you won't be able to see all the performers. This may not be the end of the world, but I just thought I'd mention it.
