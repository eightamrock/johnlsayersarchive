knightfly17 April 2006#2
Newell is English, living in Spain; good luck with translation. Near as I can tell, "deadsheet" is limp mass vinyl. "cotton waste felt" sounds like some of the stuff shoe inserts are made of; kind of thick pool table felt??!? Another possibility might be "roofing felt", known here in the US as "tar paper", but that's kind of stiff for the purpose I'd think... Steve
len-morgan17 April 2006#3
The "cotton waste felt" might be a insulation product that uses "junk" cotton (like chewed up old blue jeans). What makes it nice is that it doesn't cause skin irritation. Of course, I could be wrong about this. Do we have a sticky (or could we create one) that is an acoustic thesaurus? It might help in situations like this. It could even be used two ways - one to show names different for the same product in different parts of the world, and another to show equivalent products (for example, mineral wool at the same density as fiberglass could be interchanged). Just a thought. len
