Hello all. The Problem: After building my basement studio (many thanks to this site) and recording in it for a bit, I am finding that my drum room is just too 'roomy' I get a lot of bass and low mids that just end up saturating the recording and causing a lot of phase and standing wave issues. What I record: I mostly record rock drums so I really want a clean and punchy sound that I can play with in the mix. What I have done: I used some 2'x4'x2" foam to try and diffuse it a bit around the room but I am still having a lot of issues and the overall bass tones of the room just wont seem to go away, so I tore everything out and am starting from scratch on my treatment. Known Issues: I am aware that the tracking room is not the optimal shape as it is symmetrical, but this is the shape I was stuck with... 'poor planning' early on, I have learned my lesson. Now I just have to make the best of what I got. The good news is, she is sound tight! I get very little sound bleed from the room to the rest of the house and 0 outside the house.. so I did something right :) Anyway here is a picture of the room with dimensions. Short of deconstructing the room and reshaping, what do you all suggest to really get the most out of the room? Its really low ceilings 6'9", but the overall ratio isn't too bad so I know I should be able to do something here...

AVare19 May 2010#2
From what you have sritten, you have little absorption at low frequencies. First simple to describe treatment is use at least 4" thick fiberglass/mineral wool for absorption. After that, some low end absorbers. Superchunks etc in the corners. Andre
