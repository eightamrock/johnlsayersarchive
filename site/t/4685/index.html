What is the exact process and does it matter what gaps come between which size slats? So far as the gaps and specific when and where, it is more of a cosmetic think. You have the numbers which provides the frequencies of cancellation, now make it "pretty" I.E. with my resonators, I had 3/4" 1/2" and 1/4" gaps, and I just varied them visually, I alternated the slats visually, not mathmatically. Doesnt "jack" up your calculations just adds to visual beauty. So far as the 1" differential, I think you are navel gazing.
