John Sayers2 July 2006#4
Rolando - a big control room is around 35' x 35'. I'm not entirely sure what you are after here. I assume you will have a large Shooting Stage. Associated with it would be a small audio control room for recording the live sound from the stage with boom arms etc. there would also be a Video control room for camera control and live editing via a vision switcher etc. and a lighting control room. That would be one complete self contained system. Are you also proposing to have a music recording studio and an audio post production studio associated with this? cheers jon
