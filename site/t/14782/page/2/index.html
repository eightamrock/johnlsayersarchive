Ok, been busy with the LA AMP Show and other fun stuff, but I finally got back to see what was going on with the demo room. The electrical is all in, the dual 2x4 stud walls with their dual 5/8" sheetrock on the outside, and 2" gap are all in as well. The 1st layer of 5/8" has gone up on the inside, mudded and taped, it's now drying. The next layer with the Green Glue goes in on Monday. The doors will go in on Tuesday (solid core 36" wide, dual doors per opening) and then get thresholds that are fit for them. Storage unit w/functioning garage door. This room will get one more layer of drywall all around w/Green Glue on the walls next to the demo room (back and right side in the photo).

Office/workroom. This room gets another layer of drywall w/Green Glue on every surface. I'm going to have a "hatch" made for the window, which will swing away on hinges. It's going to have a 2x4 frame inside, with dual sheetrock layers front and back. The plan is to make it a press fit with a thin layer of material to compress (likely industrial carpet), with the sheetrock overlapping the edges about 1".
External image, not preserved — original: http://www.southbayampworks.com/dr/DSCN0493.jpg
Demo room, dual 2x4 walls, 4 layers of 5/8" drywall, R13 insulation, 2" air gap between the walls, plus an 11" air gap between the outer 2x4 wall, stuffed with R30 insulation (12" thick), and then there's the steel garage door. If this proves to be insufficient, I'm just going to put plywood on the outside of the garage door. At this point that wall (the weakest link) will be about 2 feet thick. Crossing my fingers on that one, although the amps will be pointed away from that wall and towards the back of the structure.

Up in the top of the garage, I decided to do it all. In the rooms below, there's the two layers of 5/8" for the ceiling (2nd layer w/GG on Monday), plus R13 insulation, then 3/4" pine tongue in groove on top of the insulation. Above that is R30 insulation, and then I had 3/4" plywood run from end to end over the entire structure (30x24). My wife will love the storage. My dog is dying to climb the ladder to go up there and check it out. I had all the joists reinforced and extra bracing put in specifically for the plywood to attach to. It's pretty tight in there for walls, and when you thump on the drywall now, it doesn't reflect back a lot of sound.

Southbay Ampworks6 December 2010#31
Just an update. I had a client over 10 days ago to try out his new 4x12 cabinet (97db speakers) with a very loud amp that puts out close to 95w. We set the amp on 8 (pretty much cranked), and played for two minutes. His wife was outside and said it sounded like someone had a TV on. I still haven't had the time to get the final wall covering done, and most of the demo room is still drywall, with a concrete floor that was about 75% covered. Since that demo took place, the double doors that had no threshold (1.5" tall gap under the door) have been sealed/fixed, with foam rubber sealing both doors on the inside of the door jambs. The electrical is finally installed, and sometime this week I'm ordering the carpet/pad, and I'm going with something economical on the walls, and ceiling...blue moving blankets 1/4" thick! I figured by the time all the cabinets were in place, along with posters, and other stuff that there won't be much to see, so I elected not to spend a bunch on wall treatments that won't be seen. I am going to put some stained/painted 1x6" pine boards over the places the moving blankets butt up to each other to give the room a different look. Right now, though, the demo room is containing the sound quite well, so it looks like I've been successful with my project for the most part. Pics when I get the room finished. I want to thank all of the members here who took an interest in replying to me on this thread, so thank you very much!
