rod gervais31 August 2005#3
AVare wrote:
From what you described the booth does not have a return vent. Easy enough to build. A absorbent lined duct of the same size as teh supply duct.
Adding to what Andre said above, It's important that the return vent ties into the return air plenum, otherwise the system will not work properly. I would actually oversize the return air duct - that will minimize the back pressure on the supply register. Rod
