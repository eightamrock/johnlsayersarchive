knightfly24 January 2006#3
My concerns would be that unless you have a large budget, it will probably be nearly impossible to find a space that's both large and still sounds good; your experience with the room you mentioned is a case in point. Most larger rooms aren't designed for their acoustics, they're designed to play basketball in or hold National Guard meetings in, and they sound like CRAP. To get a large room to sound like a GOOD large room, it takes a LOT of treatments of different kinds; to start from scratch, it would probably cost more to treat such a room than it would to hire a concert hall for the needed time - in fact, that's what I'd start looking for unless you're in the middle of nowhere - I think you'd have a lot better luck with a room that's INTENDED for the purpose...
