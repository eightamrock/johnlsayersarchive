gullfo4 March 2011#2
Sadly, the monitor tests for frequency response are seldom the issue. It's the speaker distortion and other characteristics which are more of an issue. One choice is to find magazines which do technical testing of speakers and if they are independent enough will often provide good analysis. The speakers you list all get rave reviews from their owners so it will boil down to you going and listening to each to determine if what you hear on your reference CD is effective for you so you can make proper mix decisions.
