dymaxian12 August 2004#2
If you're floating a floor, but want to attach it to the existing structure, it won't be floating anymore. The structural connection will defeat the purpose. Are you worried about your floor marching around on you? You'd have to vibrate the floor hard enough to make it actually bounce up and down off the sub-floor, and anything that can generate that much LF vibration is going to be heavy enough to weight your room down. Let it float.
