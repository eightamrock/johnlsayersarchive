Soundman202020 October 2011#2
QRD's are normally based on regular intervals. Not sure if you could vary the intervals and still get the full effect. And that raises another question: Is your room big enough to benefit from a diffuser? QRDs create lobing patterns that need lots of space to even out again. Does your studio have enough space for that? However, there might be a far better use for your strips of wood: Why not make a slot wall with them? They sound like they would be ideal for that. :) - Stuart -

Any chance you can change that? Normally you want the speakers firing down the log axis of the room, not across the short axis. Your room is only 10 feet wide, so I'm guessing that your listening position must be pretty close to the center of the room, about 5 feet from the front wall, correct?
