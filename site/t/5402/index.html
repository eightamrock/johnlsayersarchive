sharward7 January 2006#3
You cannot add two materials' STC ratings together and come to a sum total. It's an exponential thing. A rule of thumb is that each doubling of mass increases TL by about 6db. Soundblock, a.k.a. Mass Loaded Vinyl or "MLV" for short, is a waste of money. See the "MLV or NOT" thread. Your "two friends in the music industry" wasted their money. They could have used less expensive conventional products and achieved similar or superior results. In my opinion, your friends are suffering from a combination of placebo effect and denial -- everyone wants to "feel" that what they buy and use is good, even if it isn't true. This is true of so many things in life -- dietary supplements to "detoxify the body," fuel additives to "increase mileage" and/or to "prolong engine life," magnetic jewelry to "eliminate joint pain," etc., ad nauseum. Be wary of STC claims from soundproofing product manufacturers, some of whom we refer to as "snake oil salesmen." Without documentation of expensive third party lab testing to prove it, it's a worthless claim. Also beware of implicit claims to "just add this to your wall -- it has an STC rating of X" because the purveyor is suggesting (i.e., implicitly lying) that you can add the STC rating to the STC rating of what you're attaching it to. Finally, understand that STC ratings are rather meaningless when it comes to studio design -- STC weighting only takes a limited frequency range into account (125Hz to 4KHz) which is associated with the human voice -- so, while STC may an especially useful yardstick to use when building offices or hotel rooms, the low end and high end blind spots inherent in STC makes it a trivial measurement in a studio context. I will be the first to admit that I almost fell for the MLV joke. It was a little over a year ago when I started research for my project, and it was before I discovered this site. Thankfully I did my due diligence, which saved me a lot of money and heartache when it would have inevitably failed to perform its promise. Thanks for posting your question. Many have the same question, and will have the question in the future. The truth must be known about this stuff. --Keith :mrgreen:
