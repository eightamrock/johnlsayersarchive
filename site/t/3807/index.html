Triangle solution?

Started by Ambjörn on 28 May 2005. 4 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 3807.

Ambjörn28 May 2005#1
Hello! I'm just about to build a studio in our garage. Here are the premises: 1. The studio is going to be the rehearsal room for my three-piece rock band. It is also supposed to be a hobby/semi pro/low budget recording studio. 2. The garage is a separate building about 50 metres from our house (and a few hundred metres from any neighbours). 3. The garage is 6,6x5,3 metres with 2,5 metres from floor to ceiling. 4. Right now the garage has three 25 cm concrete walls. The north wall has a window in it. The south wall is really not a wall, but two huge car doors. (See sketch named plan1.gif below.) I have been thinking of different ways of designing the studio. First I thought of dividing the garage with a wall parallell to the north and south walls, thus making two rectangular rooms. (See sketch plan2.gif below. The circle to the left of the rectangle in the control room is supposed to be me, sitting at my desk.) Then I learned about standing waves and went back to my desk. What I came up with was the same idea once again, only that the dividing wall is going from corner to corner this time, making two triangular rooms instead. (See sketch plan3.gif below.) I have never seen a studio designed like this, so I guess something is fundamentally wrong with the triangle idea. :-) I would appreciate any comments! By the way, please note that my sketches are only sketches, made to give you an idea of what I'm talking about. They are not meant to show exact positioning of doors or width of air gaps. / Ambjörn
