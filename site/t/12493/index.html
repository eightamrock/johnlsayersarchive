Soundman20204 May 2009#3
Hi Nathan, and welcome! A couple of questions and comments: 1) On your SketchUp model, you have TWO rooms: One is marked "Current studio" and has a wooden floor. The other is the strange non-rectangular shape, with green walls. Which room are you referring to with your post? 2) What are the interior dividing walls made from, and are they load-bearing (structural)? Particularly the one that is green on one side and red on the other side, and also the green one between the "possible drum riser location" and the "storage area", right next to the double door. The reason I ask is to figure out if they can be modified (such as changing the position of the door), or even removed completely and re-built in a different place. Maybe you can mark the construction details of each wall on your plan, so it is easier to figure out what can be changed, and what is fixed. 3) I think the area where the power points and air con are located is too small to be practical as a control room area. It is only 1.9 m wide, and 2.8 long, and has various pillars and posts all over the place! I don't see how you could turn that into a usable acoustic space. By the time you have your bass traps and other acoustic treatment in there, there won't be any space left for a desk equipment and you! 4) I am thinking along the lines of moving some of those walls around to give you much better use of space 5) How high is the ceiling, and what is above you? Is there another floor up there, or is that the roof? What is the ceiling made of? 6) You didn't mention anything about the purpose of this room, how loud you are, how quite you need to be outside, what type of external sounds you need to block, how loud those sounds are, how quite you need then to be inside, etc. It's hard for folks here to recommend ways to build and treat your room for what you want, when you don't give any information about what you want! :) - Stuart -
