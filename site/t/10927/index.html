clunk16 July 2008#6
Hi, have you tried to find a structural engineer to check if your existing floor can take the additional weight? Even though the building may be old it is possible that the existing floor joist may be able to take the additional weight, but it would be essential to have this checked by a professional, a booth with 2 layers of plasterboard on each wall and ceiling can weigh over a ton. Perhaps if you post a few pictures of the room and a rough plan of what you would like to build someone here might be able to offer you an alternative solution. Have you access to the room below where you intend to build? If so you may be able to reinforce the existing floor joists to allow the additional weight to be carried, again the advice of a qualified structural engineer would be essential in determining you options. Good luck, Clunk
