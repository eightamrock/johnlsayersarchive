gullfo20 January 2011#81
for the water meter - maybe push the front wall of the CR out to 1" from the basement wall, then soffit the corner vertically to accommodate the plumbing and add an access panel there. this way the corner is only mildly offset and the front trapping / soffits etc will cover for that. on the doors - have them open into the basement instead of into the rooms - that will free up that space for people and equipment... it looks like some adjustments are needed on the ducts, the vertical PVC for the sump, and the PVC by the booth. the SU file attachment has the framing required except for the ceiling. if you're going with isolation clips and hat channel on the existing joists, you should be good. if you want to add joists to sit on the framing, then you probably could nest 2x6 joists given the small room size. another detail - fresh air. i think a few baffle boxes to exchange air with the basement should work. if you need AC or heat, then a split heat pump unit should do it.

gullfo21 January 2011#91
so here's the top level plan - some slight adjustments - front of CR out to 1" of wall. an additional inset for the water meter. access to water meter would have to be through the speaker soffit. can you provide exact height of the meter and the value? and the exact distance from the wall and the front wall? one thought - depending on how the meter is read - visual or electronic - if read visually - a small port/window to allow the reader to see it. and an access panel below for the valve. if electronic then it might not be a problem at all for viewing it. i put the ventilation silencers in 2 locations - on the inside of the booth up at the ceiling level. in the CR, the baffle boxes are in an extended soffit for the vent duct to accommodate 2 additional ducts which vent by the stairway/door entrance.
