gullfo5 January 2011#66
before buying building materials - let's review the design and make sure it's all ready... when you do buy, buy straight, decent quality but low cost lumber. i spend sometimes hours picking through piles to find the best ones for the price. otherwise if you leave it to the yard or just haphazardly pick them, it will make life harder later because of warps, knots in just the wrong place, warping, etc. this is where having a friend with experience in picking the materials will be very helpful.
