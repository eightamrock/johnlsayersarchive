the vent is to feed fresh air to your furnace no? so i don't believe it would be possible to use use for space ventilation. that said, it should be simple enough to put in some proper ventilation and cooling/heating. one thought that comes to mind immediately would be to take the space (with your actor) and make that long rectangular area into the CR and make the space under the vent pipe area your isolation room and the vent can be readily encased. this way you can have a nice sized and shaped CR and your isolation room will be bigger as well (albeit with some ceiling variations which are less an issue in the live room versus the CR).

gullfo6 December 2010#37
i wouldn't start building out without much more design because you want to make sure that you have the proper mass-spring (air)-mass arrangement - meaning creating the correct double leaf walls (using the existing basement walls as well as new walls) to ensure best isolation and keeping your costs down. i'd suggest start by clearing out the space entirely so you can layout the maximum boundaries using 2" masking/painter tape and sort out the actual walking areas and entry doors, etc. you'll need to have a discussion with your building officer about this as well since you want this constructed to code and avoid any possible legal, insurance, or other issues. once you have it cleared out and taped off, and measured (plus photos so we can see all the details), we can then discuss the design more correctly so it will be right...
