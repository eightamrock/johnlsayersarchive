gullfo27 January 2011#111
here's the latest model. need to define the internals of the ducting but otherwise pretty complete. for your decoupling, you will want to find some 1/4" neoprene as wide as the 2x4 and 35-40 hardness. this lays under the bottom plate and over the top plate of the isolation walls. it also seals the bottom of the wall. the access to the water valve - leave an opening (say 8" around) through the bottom of the right-hand soffit to an small access panel on hinges. this way you can pull aside some cloth and reach in, open the small panel and turn the valve. for the "light window" in the CR - use some laminated PVC windows to keep the mass light enough to avoid strong triple leaf effects and not resonate significantly. you will need MDF and plywood for various parts of this construction as well. i'll detail the materials being used later tonight.
