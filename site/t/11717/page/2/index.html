Soundman202019 March 2009#35
I don't see any problem with your bass trap, except that you did not show the deep, thick absorption that you will need in there. You will need a lot of mineral wool or fiberglass in there, since it is the only bas trap you have at the back. I'd say fill it up completely, floor to ceiling and front to back. The entire triangle. That would be a good start on your bass trapping. But I do see a major problem with your room: It is not symmetrical. It MUST be symmetrical, or you will not have usable stereo imaging, and your mixes will not translate. Draw a line down the center of the room, and the left side MUST be an exact mirror image of the right side. - Stuart -

donal_finn24 March 2009#37
Hi there again. and so the saga continues After a bit more reserch i have found. No 1 the floor in my upstairs room is just that, it does not connect to any other rooms at all nor does it enter any part of the house so no issues with flanking. at the moment it is just a chipboard floor with no insulation in it at all. The joists are five by two wooden beams on an 18inch centre. fitted directly into one side of the concrete wall. They run 90 degrees to the wall that has the doors in it. I plan to remove the chipbord, fill with rigid rockwool and screw down a half inch sheet of boatbuilders plywood and then a one inch solid beech floor over that. I also will need to put in some cross beams in the joists to keep things from creaking and strengten it up a bit. this work will increase the mass of the floor by about four hunderd percent. (structurally this is safe we have surveyed it well.) My approximate cost is going to be about six hundred euro to do this work which is quite a bit less than i expected so :) (another mike maybe!!) cant use the resilient channels unfortunatly as taking down the celieng is causing quite a bit of worry to the other house owners. I think this should give me a decent amount of islolation from the room below. (enough to work with for the tme being anyway) A big problem (or not) i did find however are that the stud walls of the ensuite bathroom upstairs (soon to be vox booth) are built in on top of the chipboard floor. This is going to be fixed by just cutting the chipboard away from the base of the wall and leaving a portion in place under the wall as is. we will then put down the new floor around this so as not to cause flanking. If done well and correctly it should actually help the isolation of the booth instead of hindering it but it makes the new floor much more akward to put down. my thinking here is that the iso booth will then have a totally seperate floor not connected to the cr floor anymore. However it will still sit on two joists hat run into the cr. I looked into the idea of cutting these but the bro who knows a lot about these structural issues has said in the strongest terms possible NO! We also decided that to replace the entry doors to both rooms with soundproof ones would be very exspensive so we have decided to hang two more relatively cheap doors so that both of these doors will now be doubled to provide a primitive soundlock. I also plan on covering all sides of the doors with foam to deaden them and fitting very tight seals on them (hand made from car door seals) to try and bring about a little more isolation. Our local rehearsal studio has these seals on the doors and they are quite effective. I know some people will frown at these ideas but its not abbey road, and i am on a very thight budget here. i will bet once the job is done though that things will be significantly better than before. As far as bass traps go all ideas of presses behind have been abandoned in favour of some huge superchunk traps every where there is space to fit them. I am going to use the plans in the sticky for superchunks and for the cr room wall traps. for the live room i will use the cloon ensemble design slightly modified to be revesible trap/reflector. All going according to plan i will begin building in the next few weeks as soon as my injured knee allows me too. I will start a new thread in the construcion forum as i get going and keep plenty of photos posted so people can see the rooms. cheers df
