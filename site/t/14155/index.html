well I suppose you'll need an egress window similar to the existing one as it's your escape in case of fire. Standard glass doors will get you by but id you can get thicker glass it would help. Unfortunately because you are renting you can't knock down walls :) I 've just removed the door off it's hinges to use the cupboard as a vox booth. BTW only one of the doors need be glass for visual between you and the singer. Built the place inside/out construction to get plenty of absorption in the rooms.
