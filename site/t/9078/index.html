sharward6 July 2007#2
I think you'd still have a problem because although you'd be beefing up your "upper leaf" a little (don't give too much credit to "heavy duty sound insulation" -- there really is no such thing... :roll:), you still haven't decoupled the leaves. From what I just read, it seems that your upper and lower leaves would still be firmly attached to each other via the joists. You'll probably have to rip down the ceiling in the basement and then cover the ceiling again, this time using something to decouple it from the joists (such as RSIC clips, or ideally an independently framed inner leaf ceiling firmly attached to independently framed inner leaf walls). --Keith :mrgreen:
