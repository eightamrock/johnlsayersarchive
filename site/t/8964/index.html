rod gervais15 June 2007#2
Stan, The best you can do in that case - if it's a dedicated system for the basement - would be to maximize your flow - and live with a fan when it gets unbearable. If (on the other hand) it's a whole house system - then you need to be very VERY carefull that you don't steal so much from the system that remainder of the house suffers. I have seen medium size control rooms - say 450 s.f. that require 3 tons of cooling - and have been able to handle 1,000 s.f. homes wth 2 tons....... SO if I have a thousand s.f. home - and 2 tons of cooling - then I best not steal anything for my control room.......... Sincerely, Rod
