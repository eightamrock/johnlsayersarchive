Ted White6 November 2009#3
Decoupling the three inner walls from the foundation as you described is a great start. They can be 2x4 walls with R13 or R19 fiberglass. The 4th wall would be best as a double stud wall. Decoupling the ceiling with clips will do the trick. You could also consider a floating ceiling. Essentially new joists brought in to create a new ceiling framing, disconnected from the original joists. That's a better option if you can accomodate the new joists. If not then clips can be used.

"I guess I'm wondering if this would be sufficient to make watching TV on the first floor bearable. " Typical procedure around here is to add mass to the existing overhead joist area and then install your insulation. This is, or can be, a tedious process but is the main barrier between your band and the upstairs TV. So it is a priority. Using the RISC clips is good for the decoupling benefit, but the additional mass in the overhead joist cavity is still going to make or break your isolation between floors.
