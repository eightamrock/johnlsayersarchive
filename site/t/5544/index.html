andy_eade31 January 2006#6
Hi NGN, I started out down this path and was looking at a couple of companies - Soundscukers was one of them and VocalBooth was the other I think. I had not seen or talked to these Mecart guys, and their product does look far more spiffy and dashing as we say in England. I would hope however that their specs be better. From my research and multiple conversations with these companies it would seem that they would only just about soundproof a fart in the performance room. All of them told me that they would not deliver significant isolation - even if I added an extra partition wall between it and my control room. Cost was running around $20k for my sized room (see my thread for room dimensions. Cost also depended upon door size and # of windows / vents etc. Hope this helps. Would be interested to here back about your findings. It would of course be great to find a shortcut to making music without having to learn to become a builder first, but I fear this may be the only way to have a) Decent Isolation b) Enough Money (or should I say lack of debt) to buy guitar strings and drum sticks =) Good luck and look forward to hearing your findings. Andy

andy_eade2 February 2006#8
Yeah that sounds about right. I put a call in to mecart to ask some general questions and get a ballpark figure from them. They're product looks far superior to anything else I had seen, but is unable to work in anything less than 8'6" height which makes it impossible for most people using a basement as their starting point - maybe okay for you though on a commercial building. On a side note; I did think it interesting that some of their designs seem to disobey the multiple leaf rule. :twisted: I gave them rough dimensions of 12x20 and they quoted $40k for their highest rated isolation (52db), and about $35k for the 45db model. I'm not sure that either would be offering enough isolation for most peoples application here, but again; interesting information. Good Luck and keep us all posted on your project. Andy
