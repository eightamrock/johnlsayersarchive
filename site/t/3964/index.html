I don't know if you still need it, but in Austria the Isover FDP (Fassadendämmplatte) has 50 kg/m³, The KDP (Kellerdeckendämmplatte) has 35 kg/m³. (both are glasswool) Other good Company: Heralan Heralan FP (55kg/m³) Heralan FPL (35kg/m³) ....both are rockwool. and of course Rockwool Fixrock 035 (50kg/m³) ...which I use for inroom acoustics btw. Rockwool Thermarock 40 (44kg/m³) I bet you get the same stuff in Germany. Hope this helps :D
