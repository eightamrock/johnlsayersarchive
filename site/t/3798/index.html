Hi! My first post here :) I currently have a small recording setup in a two room rented annexe. Get by doing a little work that pays for my bills. I now have to vacate and relocate by the weekend. Frantically loking for a suitable place to set up a studio. I would like to do it proper this time. Still dont have enough money, shifting is taking a lot, but id like to start and then add on as and when. In the two room set that I had, with a brick wall as seperation, i did manage to easily get by doing vocals and most instruments. Even though drums were hard, i have often recorded them too, with reasonable success. Drums leaking into the err... (well) control room would often make making immediate descissions difficult, and i would record and playback to decide on micing and stuff. Still very workable. In all probability, the new premises would be one large hall, where i would need to throw a partition to devide it into two areas. All i ask as of now is to be able to do this with as little expenditure possible, and enough TL to get by as I was before. A construction to which I could add on later would be of big help. My minimum current plan is to build a frame of about a 4-6 inch thickness, stuff it with Glasswool, and use the thicker gypsum boards on both sides. A door, and maybe a double glass window. Have this wall sit tight between the floor, ceiling and the opposite walls. I would also like to be able to save a little money to get some basic acoustic treatment done, and maybe finally get to building a few bass traps and slot resonators. though i still have to locate rigid fiberglass pannels around here. I am trying to get a place in a quiet commercial area (leading to a steep escalation in cost), this would prevent me from having isolation issues. all help greatly appreciated. Thank you Sidhu
