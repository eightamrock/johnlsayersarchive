It depends on what frequency range(s) you are trying to absorb. There is a Helmholtz resonator calculation spreadsheet in this website here. When doing calculations, bear in mind that around 20% open area and greater there is no resonance effect. There is the effect of the slats acting as a low pass (reflecting highs) filter. Andre
