John Sayers6 February 2005#2
Kieran - they are two totally different products. Sonopan is a soft fibreboard like celotex whereas 703 is an insulation and is normally used in 3 or 4" thickness when used for bass trapping. The .pdf gave no absorption figures for sonopan because it's not designed for that purpose. cheers john
