billshoup26 July 2007#2
Hi Sam, Although I'm a rookie, I thought I might offer any possible help that I can... I'm looking at doing two layers of 5/8" sheetrock off my ceiling using RC and have been trying to figure out whether to use RC-1 or RC-2. It appears most folks recommend RC-2 for this application on the ceiling for safety reasons. If you're asking about the ceiling, then my guess would be you'd want to use RC-2, and may possibly want to consider spacing it slightly closer than 24" (I think I read about this somewhere on the forum) for the increased weight (and to keep the RC more toward the middle of it's spring travel). Personally I'd still consider going with RC-1 on the walls even with 3 layers. You probably know this already, but I'll mention it anyway. The normal (non-sound-world) use for backer rod is to fill a cavity to be sealed so that it doesn't take so much caulk to fill and seal the cavity. Look for backer rod in the section of the stores where weatherstripping is, as it's made of poly foam and has similar applications. The stuff that I've been using is from a company called W. J. Dennis (www.wjdennis-rcr.com) and says it's made in Canada, but unfortunately that's all it says about the manufacture of it. On the package it also references the registered trademark of "Caulksav'r" if that helps. On the package it also says "Save on caulking materials, Great for sealing foundation bases, Insulates pipe and vent openings and ductwork". I know how frustrating it is not to be able to track down the common items we're supposed to be using to build this stuff. Good luck!! One question for you on your project:

With a soffit i will be able to keep my air velocity low, make a few 90 degree turns, and put a nice thick sound attenuating lining inside the ducts. - Control Room is bigger. - Control Room door is no longer blocked by a low hanging duct and i-beam.
