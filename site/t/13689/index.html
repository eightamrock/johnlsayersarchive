Soundman202015 October 2009#2
Okay, so we had, I thought, decided on a mini-split system since I wouldn't have to run duct work and worry about the complications with it.
You'll still need ductwork: A mini-split only recirculates the exact same air within the room. It does not bring in any fresh air, nor exhaust stale air, and you do need to do that. So you'll need ducts for bringing fresh air in, more ducts for taking stale air out, and silencers in line with those ducts to deal with the issue of noise getting in / out through the ducts. - Stuart -

xSpace16 October 2009#5
"duct work in the concrete slab or doing flex duct." Unless you have, or your finisher has, every attempted this...it is not something for the faint of heart. Typical duct may be six inches tall...your slab may be 4 inches tall. In the areas that I have seen duct in slab, it was a conduit...a wide ass open pipe that went from here to everywhere else.
