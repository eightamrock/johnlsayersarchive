gullfo15 February 2008#22
Mike A wrote:
no need to squash - i can buy higher density fiberglass. the thing is that i still have too much low frequency problems, and for that i need a mass of low density material. squashing it or putting higher density material wouldn't be as effective as low density. or am i wrong?
48kg/m3 (3pcf) in the superchunk arrangement is best for most situations. going to 96kg/m3 (6pcf) may have some benefit at very low frequencies but if you have low frequency problems at specific points, panel traps may be more effective for those (start with broadband first).
