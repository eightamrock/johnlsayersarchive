I want it to look nice but I'm going to have to build it piece by piece slowly due to budget issues. I'd rather do it over a long time and have it be nicccce than rush it. Smart. Verrrry smart! I wish everyone would take that approach to building their studios. Most do, but some don't: they want it done yesterday... :)
but lets say at max I will be 120-130db
With just vocals and guitars, no drums, and typical listening levels, you should be quite a bit lower than that. I would expect more around 100 dB, give or take a few. It's drums and heavy bass that really push the numbers up high.
It is a 'pole barn' building. Right now it's just a concrete floor with 4x6 (maybe 4x8) uprights and two by fours going across. I think it's steel on the outside. I have 12 feet of height to work with in the building.
That's a pretty good basis. Concrete floor is great, and especially the height. Should be able to get good acoustics in there.
It's basically set up in an open rectangle for me to place the room where I want, in the shape that I want. I'd obviously want to put it in a corner.
Have you considered maybe doing a "corner studio"? IT might give you better use of space. That's one possibility. Another is like you said: a simple rectangle. But do make sure you use a good room ratio if you go that route.
