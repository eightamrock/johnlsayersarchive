Dan Fitzpatrick2 February 2006#27
staggered stud uses the same top and bottom plate, often 2x6. studs alternate being flush with one side or the other of the 2x6, so the leaves on each side are not connected. the leaves are connected through the top and bottom plates however, so it is not as good as double stud. to reinforce or "beef up" as we call it the garage wall, you would add gypsum to the inside of the existing garage wall between the studs. yes it is a pain, i am in the middle of doing it, you can see from my thread what may be invovled. also Keith Sharward has done a great job documenting his "beef up."
