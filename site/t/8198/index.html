hi there, the key to understand leafs -- and isolation -- is Mass - Air - Mass. So if you reinforce the stucco by adding mass directly to it (ie, layers of sheetrock/drywall), it will beef up the mass without adding a second "leaf." However, if there is an airgap between the stucco and the mass you add, that adds another leaf, which is not ideal, but sometimes unavoidable. So ideally, if you are going to stick to a 2-leaf system, you would beef up the stucco layer with more mass, and then build an inner wall frame, putting all the drywall on one side of your framing.
