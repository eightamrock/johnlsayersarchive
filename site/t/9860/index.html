gullfo9 December 2007#4
have you tried placing your seat position @ 38% of the length of the room (assuming the monitors are near the front wall)? and the monitors (and your ears) at somewhere betwen 38-42% of the room height? as a starting position, 38% of a given dimension has a low probability of being in a strong mode location. the 360hz could be either harmonically related to a lower frequency, or something could be resonating - a "chamber" formed by something, something loose, etc.
