Ro22 October 2008#4
Okay, okay already! :) As I understand your current floor is concrete/cement? (are we talking heavy concrete, light cement.. what?) Adding ANYTHING on a already heavy floor is basically a bad idea. Concrete has mass and is stiff. Getting it to resonate is a tough job to accomplice, and that's good. You want as less resonating stuff in your room as possible. Adding a (floating) floor is mostly gonna introduce resonating panels. If your floor is made out of wood for example. Poring a elevated concrete floor might work in that case. But you're already in the "save zone" with a hard 'n heavy floor, so why bother? But, ofcourse, you want some "comfort" too, right? Adding a laminate or hardwood floor is good for aesthetics and a "warm" feeling :) But there's good options to stain or paint the concrete floor. It's a matter of taste... So, I'd say 'no' to adding anything but a topfloor (laminate or alike). You might wanna attach it to the concrete, sure. I'd use some leveling soft panels instead.
