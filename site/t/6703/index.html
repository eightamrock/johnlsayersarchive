I would build all of the interior partitions out of wood frame construction. I would build the roof out of wood trusses and install drywall on the underside of the bottom chord. For the doors - I would decide which manufacturer I wanted to work with - and I would then obtain (from them) one of their frame for a pair of 3068 doors. If you do this - then you can use a door buck and mud in the frame while constructing the walls. As far as the walls go - besides grouting the cores of the blocks you have to for structural purposes - fill the remainder of the cores solid with sand before installing your running bond on the top of the walls. This helps to give excellent isolation. As far as additional suggestions go - determine up front any pentrations of this masonry wall before you build - and install sleeves for all of those penetrations - you don't want to do this after and let all the sand out. You will most probably want some outside lights - maybe an outlet or 2 (or 3 or 4) perhaps even a red light to let people know when you're recording. ALl of this is very similar to what I constructed to house Power Station New England - the "A" Room. Rod
