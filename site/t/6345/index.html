First of All a many thanks goes out to John, Steve and the rest of you guys that make this resource available. I made my donation and am ready to start. I live in the suburbs of Portland where they dont take a drawing on a napkin. I recently purchased a older house and have remodeled the entire place from top to bottom. 80 yards of demo debris removed. I am a specialty contractor that works primarily with concrete restoration and repair but was able to do it all myself in four months. I was very lucky in finding this house. It is a double lot. Total sq ft 16000. Side access and will have a couple parking spots My idea is to build the structure first and take pictures for resale down the road. I am calling it a shop with a bath and kitchen. My understanding is if you run 220 volt you can put in a kitchen with out classifing it as a livable space. The back lot is land locked without enough easment for building a house. 2 feet shy but enough to get a cement truck through Budget for materials is $25k.. labor is all DIY cousin is an electrician Building size 25x35x10 high Roof/truss will be vaulted on the interior Concrete floors will be stained I saved a lot of insulation from the old studio..BTW what a pain in the ars it was to remove that from the old house and garage. That was a 40 yard dump And thanks to my wife and Craigs list she found more insulation for free. Great resource if you have the time. If anyone has ideas or suggestions they will be gladly taken. Once I break ground I am sure the questions will start
