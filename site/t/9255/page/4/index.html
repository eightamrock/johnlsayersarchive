BF31 December 2007#84
Thank you for the kind comments. Initial testing for final placement of the B&W 602s does sound quite nice, although I'm sure things will change a bit as I begin to introduce gear and furniture to the room. A few subtle moves and re-cuts, but no major reconstruction was required for the soffits.
CidyZoo wrote:
Could you post a couple close-up details on the finish work on the side slats? Particularly the beveling and fastening stuff.
I don't know if I have any better pics than I posted (although perhaps I am misunderstanding the question). The slats were beveled so that the ends were 90 degrees from the wall in most cases (side splays are at 12 degrees) with the variation being the front ends of the slats contacting the soffit walls which were cut to meet flush on their back sides with the 30 degree soffit walls. Rather than using 1x2s beveled inside the resonators to affix the slats I beveled/ripped 2x4s at 12 degrees (to make two usable parts per 2x4) for more 'girth' as I affixed the slats with screws rather than finishing nails. I'll attach the final sketchup file. Hope that answered your questions. * Hmm, seems the attachment size limit is smaller than my sketchup file.
