John Sayers3 May 2006#9
Not really - I realise the hallway is a thoroughfare but it seemed a shame not to use it, I've just double doored it to the rest of the house and it also becomes an entrance to both the studio and the control room. the control room will feel like it's all one connected room with the visuals you will have, room to room. The control room shape is a straight 5:1, the angles at the rear are for the rear speakers and it allows you to use the window to the outside yet not hurt the acoustic like the original design would have done. Just a note to all you new sketchupers with your free Googlesketchup ;) notice how every wall, floor, doors, windows etc are all groups or components. By grouping each part you can easily copy, paste, rotate, move, resize etc the parts without affecting the other parts. It's a good idea to setup your keyboard shortcuts with CNTRL/B for group CNTRL/SHIFT/B ungroup etc. cheers john
