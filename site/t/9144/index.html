Aaronw23 July 2007#2
Hi Dave.
First what kind of wire is recommended for the normalling contacts?
Either you can use the same cabling, or use console wire. To stay consistant, I'd probably use the same wire (eg: Mogami). I don't know what your setup is, but I'd have all your inserts (Send/Return), Tie Lines (for outboard), Multi-track send/return, Mic &/or Line inputs depending on your setup, and a few others.
