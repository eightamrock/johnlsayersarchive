dahnielson29 March 2008#6
tcdyer wrote:
This sounds like a lot of work, so before i begin, do you think this is the best solution to my problem? I am very happy with the sound i get from my Genelec 8020As, but i do not feel that i get precision that i should.
Sounds like a problem with early reflections. You need to create a reflection free zone. Hence the need to cover the windows, otherwise you would have been able to create a symmetry by putting up something mirroring the windows reflection/absorption properties on the opposite parallel wall, which in this case is not an option. http://www.realtraps.com/rfz.htm Easiest is to build SuperChunks for the corners and panel traps (cloth covered rigid fiber glass or rock wool) to hang on the wall and in front of the window and a cloud above the listening area. Just my 2c.

VK29 March 2008#12
When you are building slot resonators there are couple things you need to remember. It must be air thight, so it needs something in the back of it. Plywood is good. If you build it on the wall without the plywood back, you need to make it airthight with silicon etc. Symmetry is one of the difficult thing with slot resonators. If your gap between first and second slat is 6mm, it must be 6mm on another side of the room. That's not a good explanation but I hope you realised my point.
