John Sayers30 November 2005#6
can i have the measurements ?
just import it into that neat 3D program you have and scale it :) The red walls. well around the pillar I would have a 3 x 2 stud wall (as all walls) with 703 in between the studs and covered with cloth. The wall for the booth would be double layer drywall both sides. The wall around the speakers would be soffit mount up to the height of the bottom of your Display monitor (if using widescreen) or up to the ceiling if using triple smaller screens like in your drawing. The amp room wall is single drywall and the little angled wall on the right is a slot resonator. I'd make the little corner unit in the bottom left next to the door and the other angled wall by the bathroom entrance a slot also. The rear walls are heavy 703 with cloth cover. I'd also add a single 703 layer behind the couch and maybe lean the wall foward from above the couch to the ceiling so it's about a foot out from the wall at the top. cheers john
