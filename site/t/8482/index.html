I just bought a door that was removed from the J. Paul Getty's Malibu, CA Villa during it's $275 million dollar restoration...... that's right...... $275 MILLION! Holy cow... This door is HUUUUUGE. 41 1/2" x 93 ¼" x 3 ¼" Oak door. It is super thick and it must weight about 300 pounds. I'm planning on using it to divide my control room from my tracking room. The wall is a double framed 2x4 with a 2" gap, double 2x6" headers with plywood in between and two full king studs with jack studs. I'm thinking this door will be a good "super door" replacement. Any thoughts on special framing/hanging considerations should I make?
