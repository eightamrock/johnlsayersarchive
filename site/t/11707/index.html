xSpace19 February 2009#4
Your foundation, in anything really, is always the best place to begin. This is an area of high concern but little attention is often given to it. Assuming you are going to use concrete as this floor then I would spend time getting details about wire flow conduit and how, if you have the area and the budget, to have separate slabs of concrete poured for the live room and the control room. It has been used here at johnlsayers and in several books of noted authors, possible Rod included( I have not finished his book), that sand in your block wall cavities will produce better then concrete in the core area. "Since the framing must be decoupled onto resilient, how do I fix the horizontal part of the framing on the flor, without crossing through the resilient ?" The above line suggests you are considering floating a wooden floor, is this correct? Thanks and good luck, Brien
