gullfo17 May 2011#121
The slot spacing (or slat width depending on how how you look at things) plus the varying cavity depth and slot size, all shift the response to a wider Q which is desirable over narrow targeted frequencies (unless you know what they are and need something specific but getting any treatment to target a specific frequency is tough). So broadband tends to be the better approach.
