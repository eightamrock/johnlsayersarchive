JohnH25 May 2011#127
OK, Floors done. Bought some padded underlayment, did a test, made a plywood sandwich... useless. Looked round the barn and found several new rolls or carpet padding. Remade the sandwich, fantastic damping. Put the padding down on the floor. Bought more underpayment, put tongue and grooves joints on ALL edges and glued it all together. No screws. One giant plywood board, resting on top of the soft rubber padding. Sanded the joints flat with the belt sander. Mixed up some polyurethane resin and poured it into all the seams. Sanded again. Mixed up some floor leveler and filled in a couple of knot holes etc. Sanded again. Bought some vinyl "floating" flooring, that looks like maple wood, and was real easy to install. Maybe 1/8" inch thick and very dense/heavy. The result is a super quiet, comfortable, maintenance free floor that cuts down on what little flanking noise was left. Last night, ordered the Dayton EMM-6 measurement mic. Meanwhile I tried test fitting my old desk which was in storage, but its not right for the new room. gullfo5 June 2011#134
good thing you built this on the second floor :-) i'd say that since you have no absorption in the room, you are very clearly seeing the floor-ceiling mode, a side-mode slightly weaker, and the second and third order modes. on REW you want to follow the instruction particularly the loopback carefully to ensure you system is recording things correctly but what we're seeing is not too bad - +/-10db without any room treatment. once you get in your absorption, clouds, etc you will see this change radically. if they had 703 4" that would be a better choice for your cloth covered absorbers but you can also double up sheets as needed.
