xSpace23 January 2009#2
If you have the distance, build the two framed walls and make them as far as 12 inches apart. Two framed walls and insulate as you are saying and install sheetrock. Three frames isn't helping you a bit and only adds to the confusion. As far as making plugs for windows, you want to get a plug that resembles your wall(not the three framed one). Density on the one side, air(insulation) in the middle(framed part) and density on the other. Build the plug like a correctly built wall to achieve your best results.
