Hello to all. First post. I'm in West Australia. I have the opportunity to move to a bigger premises and build within the walls of a new warehouse. Would appreciate comments, suggestions and advice on the following scenario, and some direction as far a maximising the potential space for a new recording facility. I'm new to Sketchup and staying in two dimensions for now, much like I'm still stereo mixing and not yet doing 5.1! Existing situation: I have had a 2-room brick and mortar professional facility at the rear of my home for the past 10 years (control 5m by 4.5m, Record room 4.5m by 4m, walls 3m high with gabled roof above) I now have the chance to move into a warehouse space where I could build a bigger facility, stretch out and do bigger projects. I record a lot of roots music, bluegrass, rock, acoustic, folk. Produce many singer songwriters, do mixing and mastering. I do record bands live, but usually start with drums alone with maybe DI bass and guide vx/guitar in the control room and build from there. I want to have the option of putting a whole band in a room together, or at least in separate spaces with visual contact. Want to be able to spread my instrument collection around, so that drums, bass, keys, percussion and other junk is accessable and ready to lay down on whim. I do a lot of mixing and mastering. I want a control room at least as good as I have now , and preferably with more room so I can record in that space as well, and have good sight lines into studio space. The Opportunity: I've been looking at warehouses to share with an electrical contractor friend, aiming to share the space 50/50 but happy to blur the interface a little. (he drums, will look after my cabling and maintenance etc.) We have found a great space - one of two units built on concrete slab with tilt-up concrete construction, the other unit is a caterer. Basic details of the building (plan attached) [ Space Pete G 1] Floor space available - about 100m2 of a total 160m2 warehouse space. Roof height 6-7m. Seems best to take the rear section. Can share the rolladoor, and have a separate door which can be used for everday access. Can share existing bathroom and kitchen My specs (wishes) Control room - say 7m deep by 4.5 wide?? - currently working with 5m by 4.5m. Must have sight to most of live room and booths.. Live room - say 35m2 Drum booth - with view into live room Vocal/solo booth - say 6m2 or more I have done a few sketches of possible space usage. I am not bound to use the exact floor position I have chosen, but it makes sense to me at present. Internal studio layout could be approached in various ways too. Here's my current idea. [Studio Pete G 1] Hopefully I've presented enough to gather a few comments Actual questions: 1. I would like general and specific advice on my proposed use of the space. 2 Do you think I can design in such a way to achieve all within my Specs (above) Pete G
