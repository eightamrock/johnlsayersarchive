Pierre as X-Space mention read the stickies But rapidly Non-environement control rooms exclude reflective side walls (resonator would be reflective to high freqency) all surface exept front walls and floor should be absorbent down to very low freq. Helmotz resonator by design should not have any hangners in their cavity. Splaying walls would only help controlling modes down to roughly 300hz-200hz below that they don't do much, and just distort modal distribution in an unpredictable way.
