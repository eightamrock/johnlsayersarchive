This way the control room is perhaps not in the ideal position for one reason or another, but the dimensions are more favourable, and overall the layout will probably work better.

I think it'll work out, and I don't have to deal with the brick pillar wall or the stair landing encroaching on the control room layout anymore! trobbins25 November 2012#32
Just to clarify that my Interdyn Model 60 uses 2x 25-TV-EW, 15/11-TV-2XM, and H087, and it is closed-box. Interdyn often put in their own (simpler) crossovers, rather than the SEAS standard - although the 60 has the SEAS H8014 crossover, which appears to be the SEAS D014 (http://dalmura.com.au/projects/SEAS%20D ... ssover.pdf). Interdyn/Encel incentive would most likely have been from the Dynaco A50. Ciao, Tim
