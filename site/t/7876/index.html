Not that anyone appears to care but I've decided to go with some 4" 703 panels. I'm going to get 3 of them and hinge them together, setting them on the shelf in the closet. I will then put up some thick red velvet type curtains around the hinged panels. I'll throw up some curtains over the top of the hinged panels too...so it kind of sags down. That's where I'll add some auralex that I have laying around to catch the reflections going up and coming back down. I think it will look cool and set a nice mood. I'll leave the closet door open when tracking for good airflow. I might even install and electrical outlet in the closet and throw up a lava lamp...ooooohhhhhh. Let me know if this is a good idea or just plain crap. :)
Ro10 January 2007#3
Hai Myriad, Going with the 703 is a much better idea than that cheap curtain/blanket kinda thing you posted. (which will kill some rev and reflections, but only poorly'. There's hardly any absorption going on) Absorbing is still the keyword. The thicker sheets of 703, the better. You can easily hang'm on a mic-stand for example. Also consider the top/bottom, sound is 3D. (little more difficult to place those but I bet you'll come up with something) Won't take to much space to stack 4 pieces of 4" 703 somewhere underneath your bed or anything (when not recording) :) Good luck, and let us know the results!
