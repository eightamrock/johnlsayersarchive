JWL The place looks great! It also looks pretty isolated. I am wondering how much need you actually have for beefing up the exterior, or whether simply putting a healthy dose of sheetrock to your inner walls will suffice? Your place looks a lot like mine. I did not put as much outer beef as I should have, but I live in a residential area. I have trace elements of low frequency rumble I can hear when next door neighbors are running their lawn tractors. You can't hear it in the iso booths. Anyway, my point being, unless you have close neighbors running heavy or loud outdoor equipment, maybe you can just add the mass to the inside. Tom
