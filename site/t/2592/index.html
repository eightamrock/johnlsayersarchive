Hugo, welcome; we will do what we can to make things clear for you; and if any spanish speaking members can help with terms, even better. Before you ask about floating floors, it would be better to find out just how much sound proofing you NEED - things like how loud the neighbors are, how loud and what kind of music you record, what kind of building you want to make your studio in, and things like that. If you don't know those things yet, look around at what others are doing and ask questions - the more you learn before you start to build, the more you will like the results. OK, so hormigon = concrete; I just increased my Spanish vocabulary from 6 words to 7 - sure glad you have much more English than I have Spanish, or we would be in trouble here :oops: If you have no neighbors, and don't care about the sound getting into your house, then building a floated floor will only help if you want to build separate rooms for your studio - the extra separation will let you hear instruments only from your mics instead of through the walls, so you can hear what the instruments will sound like on the recording. We will need LOTS more information on your needs, budget, number of rooms, building experience and tools you have to use - if you're not sure about what you want yet, look around this site at all the plans and find one that comes close to what you want, and we can go from there...
