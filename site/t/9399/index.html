Hello all, My name is Clifford, I am in nassau Bahamas. I have been recording music and playing guitar for a while and it has turned into a passion. I have decided to jump in deeper by learning as much as I can ,purchasing propper equipment and spending the time to learn how to use it. And to finally build a propper home studio. I am presently in the process of construction and making all sorts of decisions. I need help. I will post pictures that will bring you up to date and if there is anything that I could improve or I am doing wrong please let me know. I have been reading this site for a while and have gained alot of knowlege. Thanks, Clifford.
