Ethan Winer20 November 2007#2
Yes, and you don't even need to glue them together. But if the panels have a paper or foil facing, remove it from one of the panels. Paper or foil between two layers, or on both sides of a panel, is best avoided. --Ethan

Ethan Winer21 November 2007#6
Bobyblues wrote:
After using the room mode calculator for my listen room, the answer is 31hz, so this type of bass trap work at this frequency?
31 Hz is pretty low to expect much from this type of trap. But that's not your worst problem in a room that size. The more important bass frequencies to trap in rooms like yours are above 60 Hz. Plus, a mode calculator program is not meant to determine what frequencies need attention. You'd really need to measure the response for that. But you don't need to do that either. Just make as many traps as you can manage and it will help a lot. --Ethan
