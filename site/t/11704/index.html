First off, some sticky details: Location - Beautiful Northwest Arkansas - Fayetteville, AR I'll be moving in the next few years and I figured, hey, why not solidify my studio design so I know what I need to do going in? I figure it will take me at least this long to plan it all out, anyway. It took me a bit just to figure out what layout I wanted. We'll probably be acquiring some land from some relatives and at that point, we'll build a house with a building next to it that will be the studio. I'm thinking I'll have the construction crew build the outside and leave the inside unfinished and unwired. Good idea? I have an electrician in the family so no worry there. The outside will be your standard red house brick, probably. No real noise concerns. I don't figure the sound will travel far enough on a one acre lot for it to matter. It won't be a commercial space. I might record bands via word of mouth but it's mostly for my own work and for friends/family that want to record. It, of course, is yet to be built, so I don't have any size constraints per se...but I would like to keep it smaller than a football field. :mrgreen: One of my questions is...I thought I might be able to enter the CR from the hallway on the left right across from the window? This is so I can open the window and let air blow in to air out the CR when I need to. I figured I could frame up a door there and treat one side...the part on the CR side. Same with getting into and out of the vocal booth...just not too sure what to do here, although I'd like to avoid the whole double sliding glass door thing. That being said, I will do it if I need to. I'm thinking about building something on the ceiling of the control room that will be OC703 in wooden frames that is mounted to joists in the ceiling so it doesn't fall. Above the mix position, I figure I'll drop it down a bit...maybe 3 inches and then put OC703 there as well. Then on the wood frame that creates the drop down, I'll install track lighting. Good idea? Do I need all that on the ceiling of the CR? And what should I put between the wall frames that are straight and the ones that create the angle of the CR on the sides? Hanging treatment? And then slot resos on the sides? And what about the back wall? Use OC703 for the bass traps and then what about that triangle thing in the back that I just ripped off of another graphic? Also, I'm not entirely sure what to do about the live room's shape. I put that one of the ISO booths is a drum iso but that's a total lie as I'll for sure use the live room for drum recording. That room will be used for storage mostly. The CR storage, I thought I could just get to off of the vocal ISO. I know an alright amount about acoustic treatment. I would call it a basic understanding. It's hard to learn all about this stuff, have a day job, play in a band, go to school, be married, raise a kid, blah blah blah and still have time to learn as much as I can about acoustics. I'm trying, I really am. Thoughts? Also, thoughts on general layout would be cool. I did this all from looking at plans from the sticky section. I also looked and saw that the ideal size of a CR is 9’6” x 12’8” x 17’6”...from http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?f=1&t=11642. The desk was my failed attempt at an Argosy desk I'm looking at getting when I do the build. hehe I tried and tried to get the hang of SketchUp but apparently I SUCK at it. I can't figure the damn thing out at all. So...I resorted to PAINT! :? I hope I following all of the required information from the "BEFORE YOU POST" sticky. If not, PLEASE forgive me. :cop: Also, I attached the picture here, but here's a link to a bigger one as well for any manipulation someone might do. http://members.cox.net/myriad_rocker/images/Studio-Layout-Myriad-Rocker.jpg
