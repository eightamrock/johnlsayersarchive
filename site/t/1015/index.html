Well if you can't add treatment - you can't do much. 8) Those curtains won't do much where they are as they only absorb highs and your speaker's tweeters are aimed away from them If you could get them behind you it would be better IMO. cheers john
knightfly25 November 2003#3
John's right, you'd need a BUNCH of treatment with a corner-loaded room like that. I recently ray-traced a similar room to see if I could take advantage of the 90 degree "splay" of a corner - that part was fine as long as I absorbed heavy behind the desk (in front of the mix position), but keep in mind - any time you fire sound into a 90 degree corner, no matter what angle it goes in, it will come back PARALLEL to what went in. Bottom line, unless you can do HEAVY absorption on the two walls OPPOSITE your mix desk, or unless the room is 2-3 times as big as yours, you won't be able to get much of a stereo image. All those early reflections from the two rear walls will kill you... Steve
