Actually, you can in worst case scenario type of way. If you use "Yellow Pine" as the species, you now can select MSR, MEL or E grade options. So I would select the E grade related to the LSL I was looking to use and get a rough idea of where I'm at in regards to span. However, I always do the calculations by hand with actual collected data prior to building, but the calculator is nice to get you in the ball park.
