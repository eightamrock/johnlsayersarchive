lilith_envy11 January 2010#139
Couch rise started. It's 18mm yellow tongue partical board on left-over 170mm ceiling span. I added Neoprene Pads/strips to the bottom of the frame to a) raise the stage a little. I felt like the step up was a litttle low b) I am not going to attached it to either the ground or walls. So I can add/fix/change my mind at a later date. Access cables and cable pass through at wall. The neoprene stops the stage from sliding around and has helped deaden the foot fall. Will fill the cavity with left over insul wool and add a mini slat res along the front edge. (pic) Still tossing up on carpet or lami flooring to match lower floor.

lilith_envy11 January 2010#140
controlroom ceiling finished!! Well, this was just as hard and scary as I thought it would be. Big thanks to my dad for coming out of "retirement" to help. Hired the plaster board lifter. But with 15 degree angles on the ceiling was a little hard to keep plasterboard on the lifter without slipping. Mum was on stand by with a broom! Had to do insulation as we went with each sheet we placed up, as access is very limited. Mud and tape, sanded still waiting on final coat and painting. Note- Was amazed at the standing waves in the flat roof area compared to the 15 degree angled areawhere there was no standing waves! I knew it would do that, but hearing it really took the knowledge out of the text book and into the real world.
