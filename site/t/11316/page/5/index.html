bassman24 August 2009#108
I would add double what you think you'll ever need as a rule of thumb. 4 mics for drums seems a bit on the light side. Go with 8 and see.... (wait, just re-read your post..... 4 for a vocal/small iso seems fine. That's what I have in the same kind of room....) I would say take all your tie lines back to a central machine/patching area so you can patch anywhere, anytime. I have 6 BNC and 6-8 Cat6 to each room regardless and who knows if I'll need them. Cat6 and RG6 cable is CHEAP, so don't scrimp! Just run it and don't terminate if that's too much to deal with. You can always add the ends later. CAT6 is great for all sorts of stuff. My headphone rig runs on it! Eucon runs on it..... put it all in a patchbay and you're good to go! :mrgreen: -ashley
