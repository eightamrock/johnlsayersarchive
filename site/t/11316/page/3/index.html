John Sayers21 May 2009#75
Q1- Should I apply a thin, neat strip of silicon or black acoustic caulk to one or both sides of the glass-frame joins?
If you feel there's leak I would - a small hole the size of a nail hole can ruin the STC.
Q2- Should I bother vanishing or waxing the raw timber.
definitely - untreated timber will easily stain and you can't remove it.
Q3-As I’m building directly on the on the slab. What should I lay under the door frame. I have Wide/flat backer rod type material (expansion joint) or the laminate flooring underlay (3 in 1- moisture, temperature, cushioning ) or some thing else?
I'd put a vapour barrier and the laminate flooring underlay should serve the purpose well as well as sealing the join.
Q4- Joining the frame to the stud. I think in Rod’s book he recommends sandwiching a ½ inch layer of compressed insulation (203) between the frames? Then I assume screwing through the door frame at multi points into the stud. Any other thoughts
I would hope you have measured your space correctly so shouldn't have to fill to much. Just make sure the drywall seals right up to the door frame and seals all the way around.
Q5-the door frame is 150mm wide, my wall is 120mm wide + 16mm plasterboard. I was thinking of installing the door flush to the inner wall. Finish off the inside with some “nice” timber strips to hide the cloth and staples. This would leave 30mm over hang on the out side, which would have the plasterboard butted up to it. I could also use it as a sort of shelve to attach some sonex (14mm). That would finish off the “look” between the rooms.
