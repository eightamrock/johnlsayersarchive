Hey, I went with Firesound by Fuller. (I think there is a picture early in the piece) Bought 2 boxes (15 sausages each box) plus one single sausage to finish of the last door! Got it from a Boral store and asked for a discount, worked out to be really cheap. pics- Had a full sheet of 16mm plasterboard plus some off cuts left over so started beefing up the drum room wall that faces the lounge/car spot. Has deadend the wall amazingly. Mainly to stop sound from ppl banging on the wall. But of course the other 3 walls are single layer....... Made the decision to lay laminate timber flooring in the drum room too. So next weekend will be laying that. I have taped and plastered the drum room ceiling, painted the 2 x hard walls and insulated and clothed one of the soft walls. Installed door handles, push plates, jams / seals and magnets on the Entrance door. Also painted door in a gun metal dark grey which looks really good against the chrome handles and push plate. This weekend I'm spending soildering the 2 drum room mic plates, ready for a jazz recording i'm doing in the coming weeks. On the list still to do is: Build the gear rack for all my gear.....anyone wanna help :) I'll pay!! Build last ceiling cloud Build and Install silencers and fans (really dragging my feet on this one) And... Find an electrician to terminate all the GPO's and Light fixtures. Shall i put a final date on it...again... Xmas?! PS- Pic in next Post...sorry Iphone is Updating
