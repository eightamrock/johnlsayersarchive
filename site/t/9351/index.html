xSpace3 September 2007#2
It depends on several factors. Your local building inspector division should be able to supply a "span table" for acceptable spans vs load in your country. As a point of interest, you can span a farther distance with a closer layout. Ontario Building Code with snow load calculated in on a building of not more then 15 feet wide, 2 X 10 on a 12" layout, 17.72' (17' 9 ") is the recommended span. Same town same 2 X 10 using a 24" layout the recommended span is 12.53' (12'-6"). In Cucamonga, Calofornia 2 x 10 on a 12" layout you can span 23’-9”. This information is all given as a means of example to you, Joey, so that maybe you will be able to understand why it is important to check with your local/regional building inspection department to get a span chart to keep you and yours safe and able to comply as needed. Hope this helps, Brien
