sharward30 May 2007#2
Welcome to the forum, Jimmy. Sorry your first post didn't get any attention, but that's probably because you posted very few details and your questions are so open ended. To get the most out of this forum and your project, you need to start at the sticky/announcement threads on this forum, study carefully, buy Rod's book, and then respond with as much detail as you can. Your goals are not detailed enough and you haven't given nearly as much detail about the state of the current structure and its construction to be of much help. With the low ceiling and concrete slab, I'm guessing it's a basement, but I can't be sure, and if it is a basement, then you'd be very wise to study the vast number of active basement project threads that are on this forum. Also, saying you have a "very limited budget" is not going to cut it -- we need a range of dollar amounts. One man's "very limited budget" could be hundreds of dollars while another man's might be $50,000. The more you put into researching and learning, the more we can help you help yourself. :) We look forward to seeing your project plan come together once you've given yourself an opportunity to absorb the wealth of information available. --Keith :mrgreen:
