What would be my best bet for a location for recording vocals? In the middle of the room? Should I convert a closet into a booth?
giles11722 November 2005#10
Walk the room speaking loudly, find the spot in the room where you get a null (no reverb/reflections, etc...) and that is where you cut vocals. ** or the least amount of reverb/reflection. If you want grab an inexpensive foam gobo solution (like Auralex's "the wall") and place it around that null spot.
