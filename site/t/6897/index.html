len-morgan26 August 2006#3
I think you might be making a mistake with the vapor barrier "blue wrap." I don't know why you feel this is necessary. No one here has ever proposed this for wall construction here that I know of. You want the drywall directly and securely attached to the studs so it won't vibrate. There is no need (and it is generally NOT recommended) to glue the drywall to the studs. Drywall screws (of the correct length of course) are good enough (and better than screws+glue). If you haven't done it yet, read a little around the site about wall construction and such before you get started. It can save you a lot of time and trouble (with poor results). Get Rod Gervais' book if you don't have it, and read it cover to cover...twice. Remember: Acoustic reality does not always follow logical thinking. len
