Cheap and simple 50$US studio'.

Started by Kaj on 8 May 2005. 5 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 3698.

Kaj8 May 2005#1
Been some time since... I move down to India and brought some of my music gear with me from sweden. I conveted the dining room to a 50 $US home studio. The accoustics was worse than bad. Lucky me working in a project where we have insulation, found 10 cm Mineral wool with the density of 90 kg per m3, get it for free as it was surplus. Put it in my jeep and drove of to the camp. I put it in double layers cross three of the corners in the room. Made a 20 cm mineral wool wall sealing of the window and a 15 cm flush to the wall opposite the window. Thats the left and right of the listening point. Went out shopping fabrics, Payed 0.5 $US per meter. I needed some support to keep the insulation inplace. I took the jeep and went to a local scaffold material supplyer and got the Bamboo cut in precise lengths, filled the car and again with materials, the Indian locals wondered what I was up to. Home to my house and in one day all work completed. How it sounds, it is a bit dead, but the the bass is tight and even over the whole range. I do not expect to get top studio quality, but for sure the remixes I done of recorded material from Sweden improved a lot. here is some pics. 8) :? :P Cheers.
