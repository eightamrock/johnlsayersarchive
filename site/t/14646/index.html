Andre, and Stuart - Tom Hidley was the first to propose the theory that you should try to avoid the right speaker signals getting to your left ear. At the site you l;inked to it is described as "8) Designed and built the first 3 angle control room wall and window system." He added a lesser angle between the monitor angle (30 degrees) and the side wall @ 12 degrees @ approx 24 degrees - this allowed him to have a wide front to accommodate the increasingly long consoles that were being built then.
Image not preserved: studio2site.jpg
he also developed the compression ceiling you see in the QUAD room above but he dropped them in later years and actually reversed the angle. I was able to see a Hidley designs in construction when I was in LA in 1974, (Kendun) in Sydney at Paradise, (1979) Melbourne at Armstrong AAV (1982) and in Singapore where 3 Hidley rooms were being built.
