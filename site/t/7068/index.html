mmcfarlane30 September 2006#6
Here's a few new pictures showing a new desk I built and the final furniture in the room. I've settled on 2 different speaker positions and 1 location for the current sub woofer. I hate to use a sub but my speakers needed the boost - so I did some careful sub positioning starting with the room mode diagram, then fine tuned with a small time delay on the tops to phase align at the crossover of ~ 50Hz and used a 1/24 octave RTA to find the final sub position in the room. I had some Auralex foam left over from another room so I threw it up to help as best it can. The room actually sounds quite good, but as you'll see in the next post I have a few problems to deal with. My wife (in the corner) and a neighbour volunteered to paint! Also, note the cynder-block speaker stands in the corner. I have it currently set up so I can easily move the speakers from the desk to the corners to improve the listening experience on the couch. There are 2 2'*3' windows at head height along the back wall, that are currently 'plugged' with some foam.

Mark - playing around with a few sheets of foam is just touching the edges of your requirements. In fact if you invited 6 people to stand in the room they would have more acoustic effect as the human body is a pretty good absorber. here's a more realistic solution. There's traps in the two front corners, there's a slot resonator to absorb the low mids plus give you a splayed wall effect on the front part of the room where it matters. Other options are treating the rear wall and adding ceiling clouds. cheers john
