Brett - how far away are the neighbours?? do you really need 6" walls? go and taqke a look at Left Bank at http://johnlsayers.com/Studio/Mainpage/MP-Leftbank.htm This is a typical Barn - the closest house is 100 yards away yet they can rewcord drums at midnight without anyone else being concerned.
