Small room bass treatment SUCCESS!!!

Started by timmcallister on 26 March 2006. 3 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 5895.

timmcallister26 March 2006#1
I had been using 2' x 6' x 4" OC 703 panels for treatment of my small room. I wasn't real happy with the huge bass "suck" in my monitoring area. So... following the advice and info on this site, here is what i did: - Super chunks in front corners. Floor to ceiling, 3' 4" wide. - Super chunks in closet in back. Only 2 feet high, sitting on floor in closet tri corners. - Broadband absorber at wall/ceiling intersection in closet. - Freestanding 6' high x 2' wide x 4" deep OC panels on side walls, and one in back left corner. - I made sure that both monitor woofers (and the genelec sub) were not in nulls (horizontally or vertically) - My ears are at 48" in an 8' room. This is a vertical null, but not much I can do about it. I did all test measurements at 48" (ear height) from floor I have NOT done a cloud yet, and plan on adding a few more corner absorbers at wall/ceiling locations. First thing i did after moving all the treatment in was fire up some music and HEY! I can hear the bass! While the bass was certainly heavier at the walls, the middle of the room was OK. And most importantly, the bass at mix location was good! Below are images of room and treatment. Note the room is 8' high, so I have terrible problems at the 4' level where the ear is for monitoring (yuck!) See next message below for ETF BEFORE and AFTER results!!!
