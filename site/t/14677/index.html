lilith_envy10 August 2010#2
Hi, slat sizes are dependent on Freq. You need to download the hemholtz excel doc. find what frequency you need to affect and have your slats that size. Or just what looks good, gap size can control the freq you want. Wall depth is: Go with the thickest you can afford and fit in the room. If your utilising the wall cavity as a bass trap the bigger is better. However 35x70, 45x 90, 120x 35, 120x45, will all work fine. get what ever you can fit and afford. You will still need to add corner traps in all corner and then some..... lil
