What you have is, unfortunately, a small square room. It is almost cubed, which is even more so not desirable. Best thing I could suggest is too take everything out but the very basic items needed to work in this room. A chair, desk and that would be about it. Bass traps in all corners and go from there. Ro22 October 2008#19
well, there's your answer already. You can do without the diffusers and use as much basstrapping as you can spare. Also use a couple of side panels for your first monitor reflections positions. A backwall absorber (opposite of the room from where ye mons are situated) might also be something to consider. The problem with tiny rooms like that is reflections, they dance around the room like Bambi on acid. Absorbing them is the best way. Diffusing them, in a small room, will not do the trick I'm afraid. Check Ethan's site for more goods on absorption techniques

The only way to know for sure is to measure the room response as you try different places. There are too many variables to be able to compute this in advance reliably. However, there's a trick you can use to find where bass traps are likely to work best, described in detail here: Pink noise aids placing bass traps --Ethan
