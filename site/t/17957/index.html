Suitable materials to line my duct silencers?

Started by hally on 8 November 2012. 3 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 17957.

hally8 November 2012#1
Hi, Just a quick question regarding the various materials which can be used to line duct silencers. I have observed people using 1" 703 fiberglass insulation, 1" RW3 rockwool, and commercial ductliner. I cannot get 703 in my part of the world ( or I can't get it at a reasonable price), I am also having trouble sourcing ductliner and i am not happy using rockwool wrapped in plastic as i have visions of it disintegrating over time. My question, Can i use regular acoustic foam for this application and if so, what should the spec ( density ) of the foam be and how thick should it be.... Any other thoughts or ideas for other materials I might be able to use would be appreciated... King regards hally
Soundman20208 November 2012#2
Hi again Hally! You should not use 703, or ordinary fiberglass, or ordinary mineral wool for that. You should use proper duct liner. Ductliner has a top layer or coating that keeps the fibers in place, which is very important. I would imagine that duct liner must available in Ireland: I guess there must be HVAC systems there somewhere! :) - Stuart -

BriHar9 November 2012#4
Rigid fiberglass panels should generally not be used for this application, however, in a pinch, regular panels could be used if they are treated once in place with an application of shellac or varnish (usually applied as a spray - not thick enough to seal, just enough to ensure the fibers will stay in place - think hairspray).
