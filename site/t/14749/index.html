xSpace1 September 2010#4
"The windows to the outside (garden) will all be alumminum frames with double laminated glass (one with 8mm and one with 6mm). Doors in the studio will be build the same way." The aluminum frame is going to be an issue. Sound moves faster through metal in general and an aluminum frame has many voids, air pockets, that take away from the mass required to isolate the sound. You would be better off building a solid wooden frame and installing glass into that.

xSpace2 September 2010#6
Aluminum is never solid in respect to frames, it is a rigid metal used to make light weight product and is cast and molded to resemble what looks solid and is rigid but is not solid even though it is rigid by virtue of the properties of the aluminum. Trust me that it is something you need to pay attention to in pre-installation mode. You may be able to install insulation into the voids if you can get access to the interior of the frame.
