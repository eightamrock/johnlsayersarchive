I'd like not to double wall, first because the space is small and every inch is precious and second is that double wall is over the budget right now.
I'm sorry mate - there's no way around it if you want to isolate from the neighbours. What you can consider is the inside-out wall system. check out Luis's studio http://johnlsayers.com/Studio/Mainpage/ ... ctions.htm The walls are built with the drywall on the other side of the stud wall. The space created by the stud walls is then used for internal acoustic treatment. You can also check out Left Bank Studios at the same site which is also built using the inside/out system. cheers John
