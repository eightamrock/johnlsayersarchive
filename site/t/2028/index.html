Hi, My setup is as follows: 1 live room with 16 feeds through inc. returns to a "control room" with Mackie 1642VLZ desk into amp & monitors. If I connect everybody through mics & XLRs to the desk that is fine. However if I connect a bassist through a SansAmp DI box to the desk, a chirruping noise appears on the desk. Pressing the ground Connect switch to disconnect ground removes it in the control room, but makes it appear in the live room amp instead?! Manageable or so I thought. Until I tried to record 2 acoustic guitars using Fishman pre-amps, direct into the desk within the control room, and the chirruping sound appeared again. Regardless of using DI boxes with ground float / lift it would not disappear. Does anybody have any hints as to how I can fix this? Tearing my hair out here! Jamie

Hi Guys, No my electricity is probably on more than one earth. I have the building for 2 years after which I will purpose build. So my cures have ended up being preventative rather than curative. The use of a humbucking pickup in the guitar improves things. Aside from that I can't cure acoustic guitars on pickups. But there we go, you can record that anywhere. Any problems created when linking live room amps to the control room mixer are solved by lifting the earth on the amp concerned and letting it find its ground through the shield wire on the cables so it shares earth with the mixer. All metal casings have been voltage-tested and I made a couple special kettle leads with skulls'n'crossbones on 'em and put in power breakers. Well, it works.... Jamie
