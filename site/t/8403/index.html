gullfo18 March 2007#2
I'd add some of the absorber panels to the back of the room to cut reflections. maybe a heavy drape over the window to cut some of the high end. since you only have 1" for now, I'd say try putting a layer across the corners and see how it works. you might want to double up across the front corners, or add some of the absorbers on the wall-ceiling corners on either side of the cloud. since you have a ceiling fan right about where the cloud is, you could (make sure its not getting hit by the fan) leave it up on the ceiling, or suspend (from the sides) below the level of the ceiling fan - but still high enough over your head on the console.

gullfo23 March 2007#4
the closets - even stuffed with clothes - will act somewhat as a bass trap because the bass likely goes right through the doors and walls. opening them up and building something specific would probably be more effective but if you need the storage space maybe some thing can be done like added some insulation behind shelving. on the absorbers in the room, you could used faced insulation or put in some plastic sheeting to add back some high end after covering them with cloth. but in such a short room you'll want less live in the back of the room when mixing and maybe more life when tracking (if recording something live) so maybe having the panels flip with 1 side live and the other more absorptive.
