Are they both right? Whats the reasoning behind the disagreement? Both are right because the first will be a broadband absorber and the second a pressure/membrane trap (for specific acoustic problems frequencies) 2) Concrete tube traps have been beaten to death in these forums as being bad... but how bad? I have found mixed results from searching this online. What do you all think? Depends of the kind of trap being build, if it´s a broadband absorber it´s ok to rip off the the foil. wha´t important is the density of the material being used, be shure to check that out 4) Do I really need to do a superchunk corner trap to get a good bass trap? No, "normal" broadband absorbers (basstraps) do the same if u cover the corners with them.Some do it top to bottom some don´t, depends of the room. it´s just a differennt approach hope to clarified something :)
