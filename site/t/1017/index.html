The radius of a beer can is rather small. If the cans are lined up in rows, they will only provide diffusion in the very highest frequency range. You probably could bring the effective diffusion down into the midrange by staggering the cans like I've illustrated below. You want the pattern to be fairly random, and different on each shelf.
