xSpace22 November 2009#2
I didn't read the specific sticky because, well, I'm lazy;) So I'm gonna w.a.g this. If an outside corner exists on a sheetrock sheathed wall, and they will on occasion, the layers will overlap at the end of the panels, on mine, I caulked it just like it was any other caulkable joint. The final layer of sheetrock that made the last outside corner, I used mud and tape on it, but my inside corners were still caulked, even the last layer, and then painted. That is from my experience so it could be done several different ways and I think that this could be as correct as another version reaching towards the same goal. It wouldn't be of any use to caulk the outside corner in the final layer because acoustical caulk doesn't dry so it would always be a potential problem from brushing into the corner. Thanks
