gullfo15 January 2011#4
One reasonable means of estimating is to take your per square-unit cost of a high end home and add 10%. If you are doing all the labor then cut that number by 40%. So if you have a 2500€/m2 and will do all the work, then something like 55000€ would be a reasonable first cut.
