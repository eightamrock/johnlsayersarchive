barefoot25 September 2003#5
Eric, From a radiation pattern perspective, you ideally want the speaker mounted in the center of a large wall as far from any other wall, floor, or ceiling intersections as possible. Of course, you have to balance this with how far back in the room it places the listening position. Ideally you also want the listening position at least 10ft or more from the rear wall. There aren't any hard fast rules. In most cases it's a compromise between those conflicting parameters. I would error on the side of keeping the listening position as far as possible from the rear wall - i.e. mounting the monitors more toward the center with a shorter listening distance. Thomas
