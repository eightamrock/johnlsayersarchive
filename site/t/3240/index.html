bouche21 February 2005#2
Hi Lex, I just read some good articles on soffit mounting on the Genelec website links someone posted. What I understand is that soffit mounting is mostly used for larger speakers, not nearfields, to control the sound (low frequencies) from the speaker cabinet itself and increase output. I don't think that in your setup with nearfields being pretty much right in front of you that you would gain anything. IMHO, if there's no way that you can use a bigger area, I think you should monitor the way you're setup now and check your mixes with a good set of phones. HTH Al

lowdbrent21 February 2005#4
I would do something like fill that cavit with rockwool. What you are doing is creating a LF resonator that is messing with your frequency response and the phase as well. Speakers should never sit in an open cavity. It should be sealed.
bouche21 February 2005#5
Good suggestion from lowdbrent. That got me thinking. What you might consider is gassing the top enclosed unit altogether and just mounting shelves for the speakers (and whatever you need above them). That way there's no more cavity. Al
