Creature827 February 2007#3
Hey jamminji - I'll say this much... I tried staggered studs and was very disappointed. The top plate connects the inner and outer stud. If I had someone tap on an inner stud and went outside to listen, I could hear it coming from the common top plate. I ended up doing a second wall with a 1" air space. I'm sorry I ever bothered with staggerd studs... they are not even close to the isolation you get with a double wall. I have no experience with the RC channel. Creatch

knightfly11 February 2007#8
You should read through Keith's thread, particularly the parts about adding "beef" between studs on outer walls - if you add a couple layers of drywall between each stud cavity on your existing frames, then leave an inch (or more, if you can) and put up a second frame with a couple layers of drywall on the INSIDE of that frame, and put strapped-in fiberglass insulation in all the stud cavities in both frames, you should be in good shape. In the stickies/reference section there are numerous threads on HOW to do this type construction - caulking, layer placement, joints, etc... Steve
