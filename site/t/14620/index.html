Well, if you are going to be looking for advice from this forum, it falls back to Sketchup. That is what John and the rest of the experts have settled on because of it's availability. Having used most of the packages that you mentioned, I agree that Sketchup is a bit - uh, how do I say this without being demeaning - simplistic, and it does take a whole lot of extra time to draw things that can be done quickly in a professional package.
