knightfly16 October 2006#4
If you build your walls out of parallel by 1 foot in 10, it will take care of flutter echo - by NOT having to do this with absorbent, you'll have more options on just how absorbent (dry, reverb-wise) you want your room. For modal calculations, use the AVERAGE distance between walls - this will get you in the ballpark, there's no software I know of that will do these calculations except for parallel walls. The ceiling should be twice as splayed, since the floor can't be. You may want to check into self-leveling concrete to fix your floor, then laying a wood floor with appropriate vapor barrier - the drumhead effect is partly from using too light a panels over joists, but mostly from under-damping - Rod has used sand-fill with success, but if you lay panels directly over concrete there shouldn't be an acoustic problem. Rod and John have both done more of this than I have, you may want to PM them and ask if they can weigh in on this... Steve
