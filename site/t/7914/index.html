Live Room: L=9.573m (measurement on the right of the room) W=7.971m H=3.81m Control Room A: L=7.971m (orientated 90 degrees to the live room) W=5.376m (measurement on the right of the room) H=3.81m The wall between the 2 is a brick wall that i would like to keep in place. The doors between the Live Room and Control Room A will be between the leftmost pillar of both rooms and the middle pillar. The window between the Live Room and Control Room A will be between the middle pillar and the rightmost pillar of both rooms. My questions are for these 2 rooms first. 1. If i follow Rod Gervais's directions, i assume that: a. I can attach a layer of drywall to the Live Room side of the wall to increase its mass. Correct? b. I will have another drywall built on the Control Room A side of the wall, following Rod's suggestion of double layer drywall, and frame stuffed with isolation. Correct? How thick should the isolation be? What should be used? OC 703 (IM475)? c. The drywall construction on the side of the Control Room A will probably be attached BETWEEN the pillars. I understand that this may well reduce isolation efficiency, however given the thickness of the pillars, will this be an issue? 2. Should i be concerned about transmission of sound ALONG the right-hand brick/concrete wall of both rooms? 3. Should i be concerned about the ceiling (concrete slab) in the way? 4. Is the placement of my doors between the 2 rooms a good idea? 5. Is the placement of my windows between the 2 rooms a good idea? 6. I intend to use Kinetic Noise Control's RIM flooring to raise the floor. Should this be done on both sides? Have any of you had experience with this product? Thank you. If there is any information i forgot to add, please let me know. I will ask questions about the left wall of the Live Room in a future post. More questions about the other rooms in future posts. HVAC is being handled and designed by an engineer with studio experience Ignore the fire escape for now.

Thanks Glenn for getting back to me. OK, answers. 1. Control Room A only 2. Any and all types of music 3. No airports or trains. Ambient Noise is very low from what i can tell. The coffee shop is going to be a fair distance away from the studio. 5. I don't think they'll have to tolerate too much. CR Equipment? In what sense? PT HD2, Focal Twin6s, Lavry converters, Distressors, FATSO, etc, the standard pro studio type stuff i think. Thanks again Glenn PS. I will be adding some pics soon.
