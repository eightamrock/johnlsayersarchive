AndreasB24 March 2008#2
Looks like a workable place if you're not planning on playing drums, bass or any loud music and still keep the neighbours happy. Building on the 2nd floor of any structure means that the whole floor needs to be decoupled from the structure or else all sound will be flanking through the floor into the surrounding walls and the rest of the house. Read the stickys and tell us some more about your idea about this place. What's your budget, what type of music, etc. etc. a :arrow:
