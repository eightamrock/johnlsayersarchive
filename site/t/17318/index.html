sounds like they should work fine. Your basic plan for layout seems fine, but it would be good if you could indicate that on your SKP file, showing the positions of the desk, speakers, etc. To me it seems that you will have a symmetry problem, as you'll have that large glass sliding door on your right and empty space on your left. Room symmetry is very important for the front half of the room, so you need a solid, hard, acoustically reflective surface the same distance to your left as the window is to your right. But I'm not sure what your question is! Do you just need guidance with where to put your treatment? - Stuart -
