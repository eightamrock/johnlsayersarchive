ParaMedia8 February 2005#1
I would like to ask for your ideas and opinions on a proposed re-build design of an existing 230 sq. foot control room we hope to modify for stereo tracking and video synced 5.1 mixing. The pre-existing walls were solidly constructed to fairly high TL levels, but the room currently lacks both symmetry and any sort of acoustic treatment other than some Auralex panels that the previous occupants placed here and there. After reading many posts here and on other sites, and a consultation with a local architect, I have come up with what I think may be a reasonable retrofit requiring a minimal amount of additional wall, soffit, slatt resonator and bass trap construction. Attached are images of the existing CR, and a possible layout using for the new left side CR gyprock wall, which I intend to splay 10.5 degrees in order to match the existing right side wall. I expect to use tilted 703 hangars under the existing 10' high flat RSIC-1 hung ceilings and a combination of Ethan Winer’s Deep Bass Absorbers for the rear corners and John Sayers' varied gap Slatt Resonators at the splayed pointy area just behind the sofa.. I am still unsure if I am missing any other important elements, and what other types of acoustic treatments may be helpful to build onto the walls on both sides of the mix position. I am also not certain if I can leave a reflective surface (painted gyprock) as the face of the proposed new left side CR wall,? Any suggestions or criticism would be helpful... Jon E.

John Sayers9 February 2005#7
Jon - I was just pointing out what the correct method is being that each speaker should be the same distance from the sweetspot and that each speaker have the same baffle around it. You can add a baffle the way you suggested - the additional bass trap etc is only a way of utilising the space behind the baffle. cheers john
