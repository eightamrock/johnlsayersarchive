xSpace4 May 2008#6
Seems what ya gotta do is establish how much monies you are willing to spend, then pick one of these that fits your money. You didn't say specifically but it reads like you are on an upper apartment floor. So you would want to get one of these boxes that weighs about as much as a regular person. Anything more and you will have serious concerns with structural failure. You would want to look at the whole physical size of the box and the overall weight to see if you can even get it upstairs and into your room. Rather then all of that, I would rehab a closet and use it for my cabinet iso-booth.
