jwl20 February 2008#42
kittyboy wrote:
When I clap my hands in my room I hear a terrible ringing echo (like the high frequency part of a star wars blaster sound) but I've realized that it mostly originates from the hallway off the room.
What you are describing sounds to me like flutter echo. Generally this is caused by large, reflective, parallel surfaces. The simplest temporary solution is to hang a blanket over one of the offending walls. That will work to a point, depending on how low a frequency the blanket will absorb to. This depends on what the blanket is made of, thickness/mass, etc. In other words, that end of things should be fairly easy to get rid of. I haven't read this entire thread in detail, but it might make sense for you to have portable absorbers on stands. This way you can move them around depending on what you are doing. They'd help with the flutter echo, too, though that problem is more about coverage area than bass trapping.
