Aloha tmay4, Most studios pics I've seen usually hang a "cloud" of some sort over the console to begin with. Sometimes the cloud is made of fabric wrapped insulation - either OC703 or rockwool/mineral wool, and sometimes a bit more elaborate with lighting fixtures included.
