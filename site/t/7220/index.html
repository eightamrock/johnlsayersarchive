knightfly9 October 2006#3
I'd go the full height, then do a frame at around 9 feet (sloped up at the rear, away from the speakers) - cover with gypsum wallboard, put several vents around the perimeter, and fill with unfaced mineral wool or fiberglass. You don't have much room in the floor plan for traps, this would do a lot of bass trapping without taking up floor space. Then REALLY thick mineral wool (3 PCF preferably behind your speakers and in front corners, a few pieces of same stuff at first reflection points, and you should be in pretty good shape... Steve

knightfly19 October 2006#7
Vents - as simple as using nicer wooden heat register covers over an appropriately sized hole - say, 4x10 inch vents every few feet. Above the ceiling I'd use unfaced fiberglass batts, as much as you can afford or stuff in there. It's simpler than trying to build a bunch of different sized hangers, and then hang them in a direction that will actually work... Steve
