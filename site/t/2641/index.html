Hi guys, I am considering resizing my studio, and need some ideas. If you have some nice photos of your setup that you want to share, please post them here. I currently have a small 10ft x 7ft (internal dimension) vocal booth and a small 10 x 10 control room (yes yes yes it's square !!) . Main work is audio for picture / audio post production, voice overs and sometimes music / song recording. beside the control room, there is another "L shaped" room that is almost 10 x 10 too that i use for my paper work, occasional MIDI programming / music composition, and sometimes double up as "Studio B". But it doesn't happen often. Less than 5 times in the past 4 years when both rooms are "fully booked" for paying jobs. I am considering resizing the main control room to 10 x 14 and make the MIDI room smaller. I also have a sofa in the control room, and it gets a bit crowded with many clients, the producers plus my AE in it. I might not be willing to combine both rooms into one big 10 x 20 (thought it might be nice). When my main room is occupied (mainly in the day time by my AE and clients or producers), i need a space for occasional paper work or internet surfing. OR is the added depth not worth the money and i should live with it until i have a bigger space, since i've been living with this setup for the past 4 years. Only thing is, the room is really crowded if there are a lot of people in the room :( Any other sugesstion? If you run a similar small facilities, please show me your pictures for ideas. Thanks & Regards, [Charles]

charleslee2 August 2012#7
Hi guys! Wow, I was going through my old topics, and found this, instead of writing up a new thread, maybe I will continue with this 8-year-old thread? Well, here in Singapore, we do not have the luxury of big spaces (large size = higher rent), and because the spaces were rented, we are always at the mercy of landlords, and I have since relocated to 2 other locations since my last post!! My last studio was great! I had two control rooms and a huge recording area. Control room A was abt 14'W x 17'L x 10' H and 5-leaf walls?? Yes, it shocked some folks here.. but sound isolation and sound proofing was superb!! But I had to vacate the place due to high rent. Now? I am down to a single Control Room, 8.2' x 11' and abt 10' Height), and my recording booth is a Whisper Room (single walled - highly uneffective, and leaks like crazy!!) The time have come when I need to relocate (ONCE AGAIN). The exciting news (for me) is that this could possibly be my permanent studio. We are buying the place; I share the facilities with a video / film production house with video editing facilities. But the not-so-good news is, I might have to go even smaller than the current 8.2' x 11' (abt 2.5 x 3.5m). Note, these excludes the vocal booth. We do mainly Audio Post Production and mixes for Broadcast. So, back to the same 8-year-old question and request .. There will be occassional visits from clients, and usually the Director / Producer need to sit in with me for the mix presentation. Can I have a suggestion on how much smaller can I go? Is a 6.5' x 10' (2 x 3m) or 6.5' x 6.5'x 11' (2x3.5m) room workable? And I would really like to have a look at photos of some of your studio with such small space for inspirations. This is really sad that I have go downsize, but I guess I have to go with the flow and work with what I have. Your sharing and links would be much appreciated! Thanks & Regards, [Charles]
