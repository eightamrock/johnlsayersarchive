John Sayers7 June 2003#4
Gavin - go to the SAE site - http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html and click on acoustics then reverberation and go to the room reverberation calculator. Put in the dimensions of your room (as close as pos) and select gypsum board for your walls and whatever floor you have. You can now check the verbtime for all the frequency bands. Now add the 4' x 4' (that's 16 x 1' x 1' sheets) for one of the walls and select heavy drapes as your treatment. (as close as it gets to foam) Now check the change it makes to your room. cheers JOhn
