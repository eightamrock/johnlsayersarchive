Soundman202011 November 2010#9
When you look at the plots I made, ignoring the title of this thread, does that seem to be the biggest problem?
That's the biggest problem all right... for that specific, exact mic position, with those specific exact speaker positions, under the exact circumstances that you measured. Put the mic or speakers in a different spot and you might well find very different issues. That's why the best approach is general broadband bass absorption, which should help to smooth out the entire low end a little. Once you have done that, then you might start noticing other issues that were being masked by the monster in the room, and they may well need treatment too. Tuning a room ain't easy... :)
By the way, just a heads up, my speakers supposedly only go down to 40 hz (with the sub off).
That spec really means that response curve tails off to -3db at 40 Hz, but the speakers will still be putting our some energy for quite a distance below that, and it may be enough to excite room modes down low. How did you take those measurements, by the way? What mic, and where was it? - Stuart -
