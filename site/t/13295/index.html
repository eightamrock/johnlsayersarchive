jbassino21 July 2009#8
People here might bash me for what I'm about to say, but I always thought that for a recording room, you always want to hear it through a microphone first. You never know how the oddest and funniest room will sound through one (maybe like crap or very good indeed), so being said that, there is no rule for a correct "live room" sound. Its all about what you like and that you're aiming for. Maybe a 200hz standing wave makes your drumkit sound huge. who knows? Different too if you only want to record vocals Control rooms are a totally different story IMHO
