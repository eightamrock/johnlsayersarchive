The cheapest method is to use the playground mats you refered to. They are typically 1/2" thick and you cut them into 4" strips and run them under all your wall frames. They also seal the bottom of the frame and allow for variation in the surface of the concrete which is typically uneven. http://www.johnlsayers.com/Guruland/Pages/page2.htm
