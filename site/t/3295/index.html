knightfly27 February 2005#6
If your room is that close to square, it's not critical that the long dimension is the direction you face; here's a pic that sort of identifies what you need in ANY small room, basically you will need absorption behind your speakers to help SBIR and smooth out speaker response, absorption at first reflection points, and MORE absorption behind you. If you hang some of that foam spaced off the wall over your bed, it will help rear reflections somewhat. Other than that, the desk/speakers/your head need to be centered SIDE TO SIDE, but NOT in any OTHER dimension. Measure the height of the center of your woofer cones, it should NOT be within 5-6" of centered VERTICALLY, nor should your head. Check out these two thread for more - http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=18233 http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=18059 HTH... Steve
