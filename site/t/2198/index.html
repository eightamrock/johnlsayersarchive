giles11728 August 2004#2
I think you can come up wth a more effective use of space. Your left side wall i'd invert that wall splay and make it a slat resonator. Your south wall I;d splay both sections and make them a slat resonator as well. Your right and top walls I'd just put absorption on them. That would keep as much room as possible in this area and still give you a nice tight room. :)
