That looks like it should work well; however, the room dimensions may be not quite long enough for diffusion to work well at the rear - a rough rule of thumb for this is to have at least 3.5 meters between your head and the rear wall for diffusion to be used. Otherwise, absorption is recommended instead. You may want to consider using slot absorbers which are deeper in the center instead; that way, you could keep the room a bit brighter and still keep reflections from coming straight back to the mix position...
