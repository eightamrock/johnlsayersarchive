Hi all, I'm getting down to the details of my studio rebuild and was about to tuck some cables between the hardwood floors and the wall and then cover with molding when I realized that I should think towards the future and build easy-access floor mount cable troughs around the perimeter of the whole room so that I can easily lay and swap cable to the machine room as well as just have everything neat when I add 5.1 and need to wire the rear speakers.
