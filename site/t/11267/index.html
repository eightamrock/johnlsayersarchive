The garage is huge ( a major reason for buying this house) I work at a studio that i pay rent for......basically im sick of that, and want to put my real estate to good use. The garage is basically split in two. A large two car bay, and a workshop side with storage loft above it. each side is 20*30. the bay side is huge wide open (future tracking room, ie stage 2) and the workshop has a drop cieling height of 8'4 and up to the rafters above is 9ft. above this is a storage loft that looks out to the garage side when up there. there is a staircase that leads to this loft, which i intend to remove and replace on the other side. This way i can seal the entire workshop side off from everything else. the floor is a concrete slab which i intend to put oak down on. I want to make a nice big room that is acoustically great with a booth (either custom or pre-fab), so I can blast drums at 2 am if possible. I would love for the room to be a large track/control setup. I really dont want to get into floating floors and decoupling 2 seperate rooms. But I have neighbors, so my first planning proprity is to make sure my neighbors dont complain. I have a few questions regarding this there is a 3inch spot on the current footing to accept a new wall frame.
