Soundman20202 May 2017#2
How do I attach the base plates / sole plates of the stud wall I'm building inside my log cabin?
Assuming you have a concrete slab floor, use expansion bolts. Drill deep enough, hammer them in, then tighten them. But before you put the sole plates in place, do run beads of caulk down the full length, to ensure that you get a good seal. Run one bead down the middle of the sole plate, and another bead about an inch to each side of that.
Advice seems to be not to float floors,
Correct! :thu:
Screw direct in the timber floor of my log cabin?
OK, so it's not a concrete slab. Pity. Before you build anything on a timber floor, get in a structural engineer to confirm (in writing) how much extra load you can put on it. Assuming you need good isolation, your new inner-leaf walls are going to weigh tons. And I do mean "tons". Very literal tons.
