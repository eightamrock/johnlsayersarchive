Hi, and welcome; most polyurethane is closed cell, so doesn't do much of anything for acoustics. This is why all you see used here is either the commercial acoustic foam (it's open-celled) or dense fiber insulation, either mineral wool or fiberglass. Might wanna check out some of the DIY projects that have been added, see the sticky above... Steve
