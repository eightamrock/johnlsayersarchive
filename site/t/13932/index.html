Ro15 January 2010#5
Okay, without reading your entire post and only skipping to your questions (since they are somewhat general);
[*]Where should I place a vocal booth in the live room?(if you think it is necessary)
If YOU think it is necessary then I'd add it at the "back" (from the CR point of view). That way you can see, for example, the whole band AND the singer in the booth. (as in: put it in your line of view)
[*]where should I place the setup of my control station? i.e computer, keyboard, mixer...
In the smaller room for example. I like to have a bigger LiveRoom. But it's, again, up to you. Since there's a door in the middle you can't obviously put your stuff there. Keep the "in line of view" in mind. So put it (per given picture) at the bottom wall for example. Replace your door for a glass one to see through it :)
[*]what should I do about isolating the live room and the control room?
Read this forum, buy Rod's book etc.
[*]where should I place acoustic panels/bass traps/ clouds/ETC. in both rooms?
Same answer as above. in short: read first, ask question later. Get involved, learn the basics and start designing. Then post your design and ask specific questions. We're happy to answer any specific questions! We're not here to design a studio for you unfortunately. Cheers, Ro
