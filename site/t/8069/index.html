I need to post some pictures for some future questions, but I have a few specific questions that I could use your advice on. 1) Right now, the outer walls (by code from when the house was built) have standard "fluffy" insulation with a vapor barrier (visquene (sp?)) over the INSIDE (cold climate) side. I'm considering removing all of this because I have other uses for the insulation and replacing it with rockwool and recovering with the plastic. Is this wise or necessary? I know I will have to order more, but I'm a little confused as to where this needs to be used. Should it literally go around every drywall joint (like corners and between butted up ends of sheets) in place of where drywall compound would normally go? 5) I obviously plan on using it on the bottom of the drywall, but since the RC-1 will hold the drywall out from the stud (as it should), when I put the backer rod in there, I assume I only push that back about halfway the thickness of the drywall and then fill with the caulk? There are wires, HVAC runs, holes for showers/tubs above etc... so I'm not sure how best to seal it up. The ceiling itself will be 2 layers of 5/8 screwed into RC-1, which should take care of that leaf, but the leaf that is the sub-floor for the floor above has all kinds of holes in it from HVAC and drains etc. The fun continues!!
