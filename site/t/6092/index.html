Just a quick one here... The builders that are working on our studio have spent the last few days putting up interior stud walling for the control room inner leaf. It is the common design of (Studio side) 3x12.5mm wallboard - 2x4 with rockwool - 9" airgap - 3x12.5mm wallboard - 2x4 with rockwool - cloth (Control room side). The ceiling is essentially the same construction, with 2 layers of wallboard rather than 3. My only concern is that they havent been overly neat with the joints on the plasterboard. Around the corners, where the top of the wall joins the ceiling, they have left gaps ranging from 1/2" to 3" at some places (this is on the inner skin). These gaps go right through both layers of plasterboard and I can see outside through them. They were intending to cut some small wedges of wallboard and glue those in, and than silicone the joins, but I am not sure of this. Are these going to cause significant problems, and should I be asking them to rip out the wallboard in the ceiling and redo it? They were suggesting expanding-foam to fill the gaps, but again I wasn't sure as I can't see this having a particularly high TL. Or am I worrying over nothing? Thanks a lot for your help, much appreciated. Al
