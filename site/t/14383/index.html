Soundman202026 May 2010#7
the ceiling is 9 ft high. I would like to splay it, but I would need to speak to the facilities manager of the building to see if it is possible. Splaying by 6 degrees will take the back of the CR to almost 11'.
The ceiling should be splayed 12 degrees, not 6. You do six on the walls because you have one wall on each side: 2 x 6 = 12: So unless your floor is already angled at 6 degrees (!) then the ceiling needs to be 12, at least. But it's more complicated than that: those angels are for certain purposes, that may or may not be your case.
1. How would I need to treat the door of the outer leaf to complete the 2 leaf system?
If you don't actually have two leaves (just one very massive leaf, for example), then maybe a Rod Gervais "Superdoor" might help. - Stuart -
