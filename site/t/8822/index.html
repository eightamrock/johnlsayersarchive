sharward23 May 2007#2
Use drywall screws, period. You want your sheets of drywall to resonate more independently, rather than fused together as basically one thicker sheet. The resonance frequencies of the individual sheets are less pronounced than would be a melded mass of multiple sheets. --Keith :mrgreen:

Keith is spot on here - so pay attention to him....... Using a construction adhesive to join sheets together will DECREASE your isolation - not increase it. Also - PL400 is an adhesive - not a caulk - and it cannot be used as a caulk. I began working with htis product almost 30 years ago - so I am pretty familiar with it. Believe me when I tell you that the manufacturers kow what they make - so buy caulk when you need caulk - and adhesive when you need adhesive........ Rod
