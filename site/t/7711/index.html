guitardad7215 December 2006#3
I echo Sideshow's comments.
need help designing the main control room for the building I'm about to lease on the corner lot of a strip mall.
Well sounds like IF you have not signed a lease agreement yet, then don't keep looking. That is of course if the location or price are not a huge concern. IF the answering service is in a different building, then that's good. Cinder block = about 4 layers of drywall. Building in part of a strip mall... the other tenants in same building may be more of a concern then answering service. Drywall is much more cost effective material in sound isolation then brick. Still you'll need a higher budget, and we'll need a lot more info to help you out. Marc
