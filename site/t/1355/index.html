Mike - I like to make the area below the speakers absorption to stop all the nasty reflections off the console, monitors etc. The layer of insulation doesn't really effect the speaker baffle much at all if the baffle continues behind the insulation. I'd put slots between the speakers. The rest of your plan is fine IMO. cheers john
