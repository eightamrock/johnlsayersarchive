sharward1 February 2007#6
Hi João, You have the benefit of a lot of overhead space... Although it will present some logistical challenges since that 12 meter high ceiling is so high. We'd need a LOT more detailed information in order to provide specifics... In the meantime, here are some thoughts I have off the top of my head. You may have so much space overhead that you might be able to get away with building (1) a room within (2) a room within (3) the huge room. Yes, technically that would be three leaves (or four, or more, depending on the ceiling construction detail of that 12 meter ceiling... But the HUGE gap you'd have between second and third leaf is so big that it's probably far enough away not to be subject to the dreaded third leaf effect. That would be a relief, since you'd not have to use a cherry picker to "beef up" that high ceiling. Assuming you can afford the square footage, you could also have a generous gap between the two leaves you'd be building, which will pay off big time for you... I'm thinking about 0.75 meter would be great. You could literally walk between your leaves. If you don't know what a leaf is, you'll beed to study the sticky threads in the Construction forum. I hope that's a good start for you. Keep the information coming... --Keith :mrgreen:
