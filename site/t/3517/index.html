New Studio Layout

Started by Biffbo on 2 April 2005. 0 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 3517.

Biffbo2 April 2005#1
This is a VERY "BETA" layout possibility for our new space. We want to do as little demolition to the office side as possible while utilizing the entire upper right warehouse portion of this building. Upper right area is clear to 12' and the office area is 8' throughout. We will be wiring two control rooms to all booths in the building and want to keep the overhead door for possible loadins. Primary use is film scoring with some after hours production work as well. Looking for ideas and the "best" way to utilize this fairly large space. Control Room A will be the main live recording CR...while CR "B" will be primarily for MIDI and preproduction work with the option for overdubs and even full sessions. May have as many as 3 or 4 projects going on concurrently at different places in the life cycle...and we will need maximum usability and isolation between projects.
