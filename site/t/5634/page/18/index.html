guitardad7211 February 2007#437
OK I've learned a few lessons about Stucco or Render. 1. Finishing stucco to match an existing stucco finish is not that easy IF you've never done it before. If I could have watched someone else do it 1st or screw up someone else's house up 1st, things would be better. 2. Stucco should be a 2 person job, with a cement mixer not a wheel barrel mixing by hand. Problem is the stuff dries and becomes hard to work with, got to be able to go fast enough to not leave lines in the work between batches of stucco and for the finish layer got to have time to work the finish before it hardens to much. 3. With no experience using a 2x4, by yourself to try and level (screed) stucco is not a good idea. They make floats on poles to do this and the tool is a little pricey. Using a trowel it takes to much time to get stucco flat, especially by yourself. 4. Corners are done with a plastic corner bead to get a straight looking corner. NOWHERE in internet directions or how to books in home improvement centers do I remember reading anything about using plastic corner bead to get a straight corner. :roll: I was going to try and get it straight by hand but... I tried the finish layer on about 1/2 of the entire area, it does not look to bad but it does not look good enough. I got real close with the matching the existing finish (which is "sand" or "sponge"), but its not flat. Can't tell from 20' away, but at 5' it's obvious. I've had two stucco guys give me quotes to finish the job, both at $500. Both will add 3/4" to 1" more stucco to fix my muck ups. This will make the end thickness 2" to 2 1/4" which is well above my minimum requirement of 1 1/4". For this additional layer of stucco I'll be using concrete bonding adhesive to make sure it sticks good. Might be over kill but oh well. As both stucco guys took the 1st look at the my job I asked... "did I pass or fail" both said they've seen worse, it was really good for my 1st time realizing I did it by myself mixing in a wheel barrel BUT I still failed. :( The stucco will get done Tuesday (hopefully) and then it's time for another inspection. Well at least I tried. Marc
