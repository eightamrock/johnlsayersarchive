Some more details about today's pour. Right after I wrote the post "it passed", which was 5 Min's after inspection, I went to work for final detail stuff. I thought I had 3 hours till pour. It turned into 1/2 an hour, mix truck was available and James was done at another job site. I lucked out with applying concrete bonding stuff. Could not apply till after I passed inspection. Directions are apply to old cement, let dry and then pour new. The container gave no indication of dry time. Maybe and hour after applying it was dry and cement got poured :D I did not have enough time to empty out all of sand from removed wall footer/ the block cavities which were exposed in my floor right under my eventual sweet spot. I got each cavity at least 6" deep and filled with concrete. I'll post a pic tomorrow. That concrete pump and mix truck were the bomb. No wheel barreling cement and very little mess from cleaning out the mix truck and pump. There should have been no spillage from a wheel barrel BUT we emptied concrete out hose into my wheel barrel and this extra was to be used as fill for low spots. I decided to move wheel barrel closer to footer, hit a bump and spilled a real wet mix of concrete onto my patio stones. I cleaned them off with water and now there is no way around it I got to really clean up around those patio stones eventually. Water from clean out got sprayed onto yard and grass will recover. The concrete was 3000 PRPM (PSI) mixed with Mono Fiber 0.75 (instead of using 6" grid rebar). Actual cost was $409.16, not $375 (cash for contractor price) like James quoted so he made up the difference... then I gave him back $25 as a tip, just cause that was change from a $100 bill. I used 1.6 yards of concrete, but the above # is min charge for 3 yards. As Steve warned earlier:
Unless you're not finished with forms, looks to me like your angled braces are too steep (easier to push over with weight of concrete) and too far between.
I went with James opinion, leave as is and yup the forms moved out at least 1/2" from where they were placed. Not a big deal but I will have to address this when I finish the bottom outside of wall, got to make sure water runs away from house. It won't be an issue where I'm putting a new screen wall for patio, does not matter there. This was a small issue in placing J bolts as we could not use the formed edge as a start mark, had to make a right angle triangle measurement to find a line to set the J bolts in a straight line. Little PITA and I'm sure Sharward will say should have built hangers/holders for J-bolts like he did, plus put in more kickers/ angle bracing. I'll take some more pics tomorrow in daylight before and after forms come off.
