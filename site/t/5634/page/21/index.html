I'm thinking about placing one side of my silencer (the bottom) directly against outer ceiling drywall. The silencer box would then be fixed in between the ceiling joists (or lower chord). This would benefit triple and quad leaf effects. I'd be able to make the box 22" wide and 15" high (outside measurements) this way. guitardad7215 February 2008#524
I did not get around to posting diagram of my silencer plan and started to build, see pic. Air enters box via 6" flex duct and collar. The air path in box is great then twice the 6" duct size (8" x 10" for most of the path, but 6" x 10" around the end of baffles). I had to build the box small enough to get it into my attic so I was limited on width. I could assemble the box in the attic but that would be a real PITA. Air will exit the box via bottom through more duct board passing through the outer ceiling. Then travel 4' between 2x6 ceiling joists 16 O.C. Finally air will pass through inner leaf through a 12x8 or other size register I've yet to buy. Box is 3/4" plywood. Used 4d 1 1/"2 nails and construction adhesive on all seems. Additional I'm going to caulk all seems and heavy glue when the top goes on. I cut the duct board to fit exact. Might be over kill but I pulled all the duct board out and duct taped (real metal duct tape) all the edges together in order to keep the vapor barrier constant. Just hope the tape does not come off when I slide the duct box back in. I'm going to use construction adhesive to adhere duct board to plywood all around. I've made sure all of the inside face is the black cloth stuff. I even cut out pieces to fit at the end off the baffles peeling enough fiberglass insulation off so that I can glue the cloth over it in order to not expose fiberglass to air path. Might be over kill but I don't want fiberglass particles floating in my air. I wore long sleeve shirt and a dusk mask while cutting. My garage ended up with a layer of fine fiberglass dust I was going to borrow my neighbors duct board V cutter but ended up just using my knife to cut corners out carefully. I measured to my corner and cut at 3/4" past and before my mark. I cut at an angle to take out a V shape being careful to not cut through the backing. After a couple passes with the board slightly bent out the V chunks came out. My 1st try gave me a near perfect 90 degree corner so I kept on. Neighbor who is AC contractor took a look today and "passed" me :D Marc
