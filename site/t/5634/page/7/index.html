I got a new dilemma for my room... CUTTING THE CONCRETE BLOCK WALL. I admit my ill planning is at fault here. Plan was: - Rent a WALL SAW... a concrete saw with 20" to 24" blade that will cut through a 8" deep wall in one slice. I wanted to rent one like in the pic attached below - Make the 2 cuts at the outer edges of wall to remove, not remove entire wall yet. - Build 2x4 frames, get inspection. - Add exterior sheathing, door, etc... to get the room "inside" the house AND put up drywall on interior walls and then... - remove the concrete block wall. Mess would be contained inside new room and at no point would a part of my garage have a big hole to the outside. Other reason for making the cuts 1st is because both cuts are at a "T" in current wall junction. Once the cut is done the walls will be at an L. In order to make a clean cut I want to cut from one side of the wall only. Once the new outer/exterior walls of studio are up, making the cut from either side of the wall would be difficult, because the saw has a guard not allowing a cut right at a corner (anybody please correct me if I am wrong and a saw which can cut flush in corner exists... which I may be able to cost effectively acquire) Please see below architect site plan. NOTE it's hurricane season right now in Florida. A news story I just watched said about garage doors "if you can see light through any crack in the door then it's not good enough... garage door failure and possible roof lifting may happen during hurricane winds" GREAT... what about a 10' by 8' hole in the side of garage... WELL at least I do have a pile a 3/4" plywood I can rig up just in case. The dilemma... I thought I could rent one of these wall saws... I can not find one :!: I can only find regular demo saws with 14" blades which only cut 5" deep for $55 a day. Plus you have to buy your own diamond tip blade at $100 or so. Now, if I do find a wall saw, a 24" diamond blade costs min $350 and I'm going to presume the rental cost of a wall saw would be expensive. My options: 1. I've found a few companies in Orlando who cut concrete walls on a regular basis and use the wall saws (I even saw of pic of one with a 60" blade :shock: ). I am going to call them on Monday and try and get some ball park numbers. My guess is it'll be expencive, but how much I don't know. 2. I rent a demo saw which only cuts 5" deep. Me and keyboard player hack away at it OR me and neighbor (general kind of contractor) who has done 2 similar but not as precise jobs in the the past month hack away at it. He says he can get it real close BUT BUT BUT.... at one of these "T's" where the cut is, sits a girder truss holding up a good part of my main house roof.... so cutting to far off to the side by accident is not a welcome option. I addition, with either option (especially #2) I am doubting whether it's not better just to completely remove the wall 1st, leave a big hole in my garage and then run as quick as I can to enclose the room before an unpredicted hurricane hits. Guess I'll see on Monday what the ball park numbers might be, and try and get someone out for a real estimate and get the job done. I already have a "that's to expencive" number so hopefully they'll come below it. :roll: To end on a good note...
