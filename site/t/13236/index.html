Soundman20204 July 2009#11
good? bad? ugly?
Good questions! No so sure about "bad" and "ugly", but I doubt that those will work "good". I have a feeling that they will flank, since it looks like you didn't do any calculations of testing on the neoprene, to find out if it will over-compress or not compress sufficiently to work as a spring. The chance that the math worked out perfectly by pure chance, is pretty remote. I guess the thing to do is to suspend that contraption in the air somehow, maybe from strings, then tap one piece of wood with a hammer while gently touching the other with your fingertips. If you feel any vibration at all, then it isn't decoupling. I have to admit that I doubt it will work.
petrovinksy10 February 2010#12
Well its been a while, im very close to being done. From my design above I am only doing the control room and will use its as a one room studio for the time being. The soundproofing is finally done. I did 2 layers of 5/8 room within a room design using my home made "isolation sway braces" to secure the inner leaf to the outer leaf. A total of 103 sheets of dry wall for the room. im guessing about 50 large tubes of acoustical sealant and an estimated 30 bags of safe and sound rock wool (including acoustical treatment). I am pretty happy with the noise reduction, With a full rock band pounding, watching TV in the living room directly above is no problem, and outside at 2 am i struggle to hear any noise at all.
