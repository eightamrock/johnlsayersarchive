gullfo31 March 2007#2
because its square and it sounds like you built it pretty solid, you're very likely to have strong room modes from around 120-300hz. so adding some slat resonators will help. if you could construct another wall inside to alter the room shape - might help but may become a third leaf situation which could result in less isolation than desired. you should take some room measurements to see where you stand in regards to problem frequencies. once you know how your room is actually behaving you will likely get some better advice.
