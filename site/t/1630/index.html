tenaciousJay7 May 2004#1
We've been planning to move and looking for a place with a likely studio space. But on getting some assessments we're realizing that our current house is a great investment; it's a 1912 craftsman with all kinds of built ins - benches, bookcases, etc. and is in a great neighborhood close to a great park and a short drive to where we work. So we're thinking maybe to stay here and put money into this one instead of moving. But first I need to see what kind of setup I could have in our basement; having a quiet upstairs and no complaining neighbors while my loud rock band plays down there is essential. The ceiling is about 100" with 7 1/2" slats and one beam that's 8" high. The outside walls are concrete (?) blocks and cement. I'll post pictures of them soon but they seem like they'll be pretty good for sound isolation. The concrete floor is pretty uneven as well. The basement is about half underground. The door on the right side goes to a landing and then either straight up or down to the left. What I'm looking for is a control room and live room with one iso booth. The live room should not be too small; it will be the site of band practices and I want it to be comfortable. Ideally the band would be in the lower half of the house; that's under the pantry/kitchen instead of the living room and is also at the back of the house. It would be nice if I could look straight in at the live room when at the mix position. I was thinking something like the corner control booth turned 90 degrees counter clockwise with Studio B being a family storage room (which needs to be planned for one way or another). The post in the middle will have to be worked around, though. The thought has also crossed my mind of adding a 1/2 bathroom to keep people from needing to go upstairs for any reason. The area right above the electrical panels is an upstairs bathroom (forgot to add the pipe to the left of the electrical panels). Oh, almost forgot. There are heating ducts that run up and down from the furnace (as you are looking at the diagram) and an intake (I believe) goes across left to right between 2 ceiling slats right below the post. I think they're about the same height as the beam over the post. We do also plan to get a new furnace before this project takes place. I'll post some pictures soon but in the meantime here's the layout (not exactly drawn to scale but pretty close). Thanks for your help!
