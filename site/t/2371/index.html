G'day, another long time reader that hasn't done much posting here. A quick thanks is in order (again) to all, I've learnt so much in the last couple of months (I hope) just reading this site. Anyway, I have a small 'Grannyflat' at the front of my house that I would like to convert into my studio in the near future. Hopefully below is my design so far. Basically a pair of 6x3m (18'x 9') rooms with a ceiling that slopes from 8' to 9' with the 9' being in the centre where the two rooms meet. The black walls in my diagram are bessa-blocks (hollow concrete bricks). I hope to fill at least the external walls. I think the dividing wall will not matter as it will be more of an obstruction than a leaf (is this correct?). I will be working mainly with Rock and Metal bands so Isolation is quite important, the nearest neighbour is about 8 metres (24') away. Floor is carpeted concrete at the moment , but I will lay timber floors and will probably float them or build a floating drum riser at the very least. Budget is tight, but the only time constraint I have is keeping my patience in order, and I have several mates I can call upon to help out with the labour. I guess I'm just looking for a little input to show whether I'm on the right track or not....
