Here is the first problem I see. You stated that the neighbors haven't compained that much. Worst case scenario is that as you increase the # of shows and bookings of recording gigs there will be more customer traffic in the neighborhood. This is sure to piss of some of the oldies in the neighborhood. They can get you on illegal business in a residential area. All depends on what the code is for your zone. Also since the house does not appear up to code this will raise issues to deal with if indeed you can build in your house. What I would do first is check the legality of your business in your current location. This will save you a ton of money, guaranteed. If you do commando punk rock style, don't worry about the sound levels since it will get shut down. I've seen it happen time and time again. Sorry for the semi doom and gloom post but I just hate to see someone who loves music so much lose money. dougo
