rod gervais28 March 2011#3
Psychoacoustics is the study of the effect of the human auditory system and brain as relates to acoustics. It is the attempt to understand how (perhaps why) our brain interprets the sounds we hear in the manner it does. In the sense of the design of acoustically pleasing (more importantly - critical listening) spaces, it is the application of the principles we have developed (from those studies), to produce physically measurable results.
