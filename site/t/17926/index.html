Hi Ayok, Please read the forum rules for posting (click here). You seem to be missing a couple of things! :) Please provide a diagram of your room, showing the dimensions. The room is way too small to be able to use diffusers: Take all of those out. They are creating more problems than they are solving. Diffusers can only be used in large rooms. The peaks and nulls in your bass response are probably due to room modes and lack of bass trapping. - Stuart -
