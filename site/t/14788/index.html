There is an announcement at the top of the forum about what to do if you want to get replies, and how this forum works. The announcement leads to this post (click here). Please read that, and especially the part relative to your post here. Folks here will help you with your design, for sure, but you have to do the basic work yourself. Nobody is going to design your entire studio for you for free. If you don't know how to do it yourself, and aren't willing to learn, then hire John to do it for you. The purpose of this forum is to help people who are prepared to do most of the learning and design themselves, and just ask here when they get stuck. The purpose of this forum is not for people to post a drawing of a rectangle and expect us to do it all for you. - Stuart -
