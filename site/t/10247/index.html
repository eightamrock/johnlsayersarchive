Ethan Winer25 February 2008#2
awilki01 wrote:
I've seen tons of photos showing panels installed vertically. Is there an advantage in doing that than having them installed horizontally at ear level throughout the room?
If 2x4 panels are vertical you can sit up tall and slouch down low and not get reflections. If they're horizontal you can lean forward and back more. The best solution is two panels side by side to cover a 4x4 foot area.
Also, for mid/high freq panels, is the air gap imperative? Couldn't I just mount it flush against the wall and get the same mid/high freq absorption?
A gap extends the absorption to lower frequencies which is always useful. But a 2-inch panel with no gap works well enough. --Ethan
