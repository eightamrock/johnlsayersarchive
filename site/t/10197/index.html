I believe that if you make holes between your live room and control room and then pass your snake between them (making sure it is not tight) and then seal around the holes tightly will insulation and caulk, it should't break the isolation. This is just my view and there are a lot more people on this site who have much more experience in these matters. Hope this was of help Tom
