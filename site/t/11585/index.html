There i have a trap in every corner, (besides from where the closets are and on the main floor. They would get in the way and get damaged) Would I benefit from putting a small superchunk-type trap next to the closet, in the corner between the closet and the door. how do you think those green supertrap layout is (just now i was thinking that superchunks at the top might be a bit difficult, since they would be hangin in the cloth. I decided to design the entire absorbers out of a single 120x60x7.5cm rockwool units. The frame is a 4x2cm wood (should I rather use some other size for the frame ?) since this won't be my permanent room (not sure how long I got it) and I want to be able to take all the absorbers, and rearranging them in any room I'd get in. I've been reading alot about things like "701, 703, 501" and so on (I just guessed random numbers) and have no Idea what they mean.. Are they different densities ? And then what is the ratio between those numbers and 50kg/m3 (everything I see in iceland is density-measured in kg/m3) - How is the design of these absorbers ? - The frame is 4cm deep, but the wool is 7.5cm deep is that good/bad ? - The foot is the same 4cm wood as in the frames (meaning there would be a 0.5cm space between the back of the wool to the wall), is that good or bad ? Should it be up against the wall, or further away, should i maybe keep the foot of the absorbers different length in the left and right side (so they would aim a little bit to the front of the room/monitors) - I was going to keep the wool in the frame using light fabric, since the wool is 3.5cm thicker than the frame, i was going to staple the backside to the frame using fabric. I was planning on puttin the front cloth on first (stapling it to the inside of the frame but not on the front, for looks), then putting the frame face down on the floor, putting in the wool and then putting cloth rather across the backside and stapling it to the backf of the frame Does it have to be tight, or is it OK if the wool is visable from the backside ?
