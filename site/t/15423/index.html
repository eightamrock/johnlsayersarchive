gullfo1 January 2011#9
you can skip the ratios since the room is literally too small to bother. this design would provide a separate hall and at a 7.2' ceiling height (assuming you are building an inside out ceiling as well as walls) you end up with a reasonable workspace. unfortunately the entry door and bathroom door prevents a good solution for air locking, having the hallway (air lock) gives you an option to do air exchange with that space (assuming that's where your windows or ventilation ducts are). otherwise consider just treating the space and making it work as-is until you can make enough to buy into a better space.
