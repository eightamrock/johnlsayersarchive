yes, one option for the electrical boxes (and light boxes) is to build frames around them and use drywall & caulk to finish them before putting on the wall drywall. another option is surface mounting things to eliminate the penetrations. and another is to build soffits to handle the wiring (on the floor-wall is a common place for a cable/wiring trough. electric can be run through conduits in the trough and outlets etc attached the trough). many times, by the time you figure out where the treatments are going and the lighting, switched, wiring etc, you can mostly use surface wiring or run the conduits through the treatments walls etc.
