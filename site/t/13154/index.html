John Sayers11 June 2009#5
There are two options with those clouds - you can add a solid (plywood) backing or not. If you don't the insulation is lifted off the ceiling thus lowering the absorption frequency, if you do you can angle the cloud thus breaking the floor to ceiling parallel standing wave. The best recipe is Coq au vin - throw away the chook and drink the vin and everything will sound better. :)
