Tile floor and drywall ceiling. Anyway, I know how to fix this in theory. I need a "cloud". The problem I have though, is that I rent... so I can't really do much to the ceiling. Is there a way to mount panels on the ceiling safely without destroying it or doing anything that would piss of my landlord? If so, how? Thanks.

knightfly4 June 2007#4
You might consider building some 2' x 8' panel resonators - ballpark would be 1/4" plywood over 2.5" air gap, leave the 1/2" closest to the front plywood panel as a gap (cleats and hardware cloth can help this) Since that frequency is a second harmonic, the panels would need to be placed horizontally against the walls at either 2' or 6' centered (panel resonators are PRESSURE devices, so need to be at high pressure nodes) Also, mount the front panels using flexible rubber and SCREWS with washers (finish washers look better) so you can TUNE them - this is NOT an exact science, too many material variables...
