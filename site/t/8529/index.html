rod gervais1 April 2007#3
Cami, the bottom drawing is the right one. 1srt off - you won't be able to get all screws into the jacks..... so you'll want to be finding 5x5 heavy duty balll bearing hinges...... that will allow you to get the inner 2 screws into the jack studs - Next - that's one of the reasons I specify a full 5/4 stock material for the jambs (thus they are 1 1/4" thick). Standard jamb stock winds up dressing to 5/8" - and isn't strong enough to support the door weight..... The first drawing (the top one) would be a disaster - the first time the door was opened carelessly it would be ripped from the jamb........ Sincerely, Rod
