Travis, They look interesting, but I'd be a little Leary without being able to hear them first. Monitors are about the most subjective and personal choice you can make in a studio. You want to be able to hear everything well enough to make good decisions on all your mix decisions. You need your mixes to sound good on a as many different speaker systems as possible. I say all this because I have found many bookshelf speakers to be made to sound BIG and HYPED. They sound pleasant on their own (made to enhance the music) but if you make decisions based on the hype they may sound wimpy elsewhere. Good luck on your search, there are many choices out there... too many sometimes.
