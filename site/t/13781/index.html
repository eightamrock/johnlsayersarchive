barri27 May 2010#13
Hi javamad Finally I designed the studio and contracted a local provider to build it. I did the acoustic treatment and right now I'm happy working in several productions. I'm pretty happy with the isolation. A qualified engineer made some measurements when the studio was finished and gave me an official isolation certificate. But the most important thing for me is that my neighbor doesn't notice any sound although I'm working at 3am. I learnt a lot with the building process even having read tons and tons of information in this forum and other online and printed resources. Now that it's finished and I have seen the procces, techniques and so, I know that I can build it for myself. But I'm not sure that I have achieved the same results if I would try to do it for myself even with all the information I had. If you have never built (or have never seen how to do it) a well isolated studio, I would recommend you to contract a specialized firm (I did it with Insorock). If not, you could make an important investment in materials that could not isolate your studio as much as you though. Those things are not so important in many houses of US because they don't share walls nor buliding structure with the neighbors... In some cases to loose a big amount of isolation as for example, 10dB, is not so important there. But here is different. Imagine 'los municipales' visiting you because the isolation that you have built doesn't work in the way you expected (very common situation in Madrid as you already know). All your money and work will be lost. If you share walls and structure, it's extremely important to be shure that your neighbor will not be affected, and as you probabbly know, local Spanish laws about noise are not enough for that. It's important to have a design and building plan that assures you a specific level of reduction dB and what happends if measurements show that isolation doesn't reach those levels. You can do the acoustic treatment for yourself and get good results. About 'that' firm, they design, build and they have a really good reputation, but don't pay attention to small projects (40000€ or so). For local providers, you can check Bricodepot, CIR62... there are many building warehouses. Try to avoid places like Leroy Merlin and so, they are more expensive. Good luck!!!
