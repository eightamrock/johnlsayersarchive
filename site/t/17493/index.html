Yup! For sure! On both counts... But the good thing is that you found us, and you are now in the right place! :)
rural fire station approx 30m away downhill on one side (with a very loud siren),
Ouch! And also probably some rather loud firetrucks coming and going occasionally? Do yourself a big favor, and get hold of a sound level meter. Beg, borrow, or buy, but get one somehow, and measure just how loud that siren is, and how loud the trucks are, and how loud YOU are. Also find out how quiet you need to be: ask your local municipality for their noise regulations. The difference between the "How loud" and "how quiet" numbers, is the amount of isolation you need. That's the starting point for defining the type of construction that you'll need.

It takes a while to figure out some of their concepts (like "components" and "groups" and "layers" and "scenes"), but once you get into it, it's a great tool for designing studios. Anyway, welcome!
