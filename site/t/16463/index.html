Nevermind what you are thinking. The process is this. Install the sheathing on all framing members with the required gap between the panels. Install the backer rod and caulk. Simple enough. Then install the next interior layer of sheathing...install the backer rod and caulk. The confusion on your part is that you have to be in two places at one time, one being on the interior and the other being on the exterior of this inside out wall...that is where you are getting confused. ;) Stay inside the room....and think about it from that perspective.

Soundman202025 July 2011#22
What I meant by "other methods" is along the lines of how the whole structure fits together: there has to be some kind of framing involved with those walls/ceiling. You show metal profiles, but have you considered wood? If you build with wood framing, then the ceiling/wall junction can be taken care of by the framing itself, and you won't need to worry about complex procedures for hoe to get backer rod and caulk in place. Of course, if you really have to build only with metal, then you don't have a lot of choice... - Stuart -
