donal_finn27 February 2010#2
The mic connections are called xlr connections. You need to run "snakes" with multiple xlr cables in one. If you have lots of money buy neutrik ones otherwise buy cheap ones online. One side usually will go to a patchbay to give multiple inputs and outputs to your mixer and the other end usually gets built into a wall plate / wall box Usually in a studio setup these will carry 48 volt phantom power from the mixer to the mics so condensor mics (which require power) can be used. This means needing to be carefull about what you do. try reading up on patchbays and it will give you an idea of what needs doing. www.thomann.de is a very good trustworthy website that supplies snake cables (and anything else you might need)very cheaply but of somewhat decent quality. http://www.thomann.de/ie/studio_multicore_cables.html I would also think ahead and put in some digital cables like adat for digital gear and Cat5 or 6 (ethernet cables) for computer networking and maybe some midi cables / usb / firewire would be good too. My main recomendation is to take your time and think about what you might need in two or three years time rather than having to pull apart walls into the future. Hope this helps a bit. df
