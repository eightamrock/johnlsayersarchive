you should check with your neighbor and do a tap test to see how much sound is coming through - also a loud stereo or drum kit as well. between some tapping with a rubber and metal hammer (lightly :-)) and some loud audio, you'll get a sense of what is coming through. you may have more pathways into the next door space than just the wall...
