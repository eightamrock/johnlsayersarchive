sharward8 June 2007#2
The consensus around here is that it makes more sense to use all of the thicker material, rather than trying to mix-and-match different thicknesses. In theory there are slight benefits to using varying thicknesses, but those benefits are generally outweighed by the benefits of having the extra mass of the thicker material throughout. Plus, it's easier to keep track of a single stack of a single thickness, vs. multiple stacks of varying thicknesses. Less waste too. My walls will be so thick that I plan to do surface-mounted steel boxes. I'm not 100% sure about this code-wise, but I think you can mount your boxes as though they'd be flush with a single layer of drywall, and then you can use this type of extender to accommodate the extra depth. --Keith :mrgreen:

I found electrical boxes at home depot that adjust via a screw to varying depths. they have a metal bracket that you screw to the stud and then a screw moves the box in or out. You can adjust them pretty far out. I plan on 2 layers of 5/8 drywall and there is plenty of adjustment for them.
