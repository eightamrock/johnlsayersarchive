gullfo21 October 2007#11
i would put the vox booth into the live room so you have easy visibility with players. you can add some inside-out walls over the big window and exterior door and add interior window and door. some additional slats to break up the wall angles and add broadband traps. in the CR you probably have enough space for some nice bass traps and soffits if needed. on the back of the CR add a couple of doors to create an air lock and double up as an amp booth. leave the other space as a lounge, bathroom and some storage. in the CR use some absorbers to cover first reflection points on side walls and ceiling. use some smaller absorbers to break up an flutter echos in the live room and CR but not make it dead.
