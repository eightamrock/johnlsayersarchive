xSpace4 November 2009#8
trodden wrote:
...since a three leaf system has a higher STC than a 4 leaf.
Not entirely accurate. Either one or the other could have the better STC rating depending on the mass involved, the air cavities and several other considerations. The issue as I have come to understand it, is the loss of isolation in the lower frequencies. A three leaf can lose this as easily as a four leaf. So STC is not the figure you want to be looking at when deciding on which wall is going to have the most negative impact, or the least negative. The TL (transmission loss) and over what frequencies (the slope), how well does it do in the lower region and also how well in the upper and just plain overall really.
