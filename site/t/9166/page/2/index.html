Dan Scott6 November 2007#27
Sorry, not trying to confuse anyone :shock: Maybe I didnt' clearly communicate what I was doing. I simply used standard framing to enclose the garage door opening using 2x4 studs. On the outside I layered sound board, OSB and siding. This wall will get rock wool insulation as well Now, on the inside next to this wall I build another wall 1" from the garage enclosure wall (double wall system). This wall will get rock wool insulation and the double sheetrock application using green glue to adhere together. The exterior soundboard, OSB and siding is 1 leaf. The secondary wall on the inside with double sheetrock is my 2nd leaf. There is an 8" airspace stuffed with insulation between these walls hence: mass...air...mass. I hope this is a better explanation :D Dan
