First off be sure to read this link before posting it will answer many of your questions. http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=3231 Just shooting from the hip. If this is a space to attract local music pros I would probably guess right off the bat that your space is a bit too small for musicians and engineers/producer. Since your headline was "If money were no object." Why not design something with a control room and perhaps a couple of isolation booths to go along with your first space you mentioned. Really think about what you want, who you really want to atrract, is it a hobby or a business, etc. Basically write up a business plan for yourself and it will help guide you in your quest for building your studio. But hey, I am a guy who records upstairs in our condo and have drums stuffed in the hallway between the control room/my office and the bathroom. Makes for those bathroom breaks a real bear after a couple of beers :o So get into some studying, put your location info in your profile and drop another message after that. I am sure you will have heaps of great questions to ask. Take care, DougO
