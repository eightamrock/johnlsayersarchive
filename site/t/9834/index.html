Coffered ceiling sound panels

Zartler30 November 2007#1
I built and installed this in a restaurant this week. I figured I would post it here for those who would like to keep a look about a room that would not hurt resale value of a house. If you want more details send me an PM if for some reason I don't get a thread response email. :D This is a 8' x 8' Coffered ceiling with recessed sound panels for taming sound levels in a restaurant private party room. Basically, I made 2 x 4' x 8' sound panels of oc 703, upholstered them, butted them together, set rails on them to lag them to the ceiling then installed a coffered ceiling beams to make them appear like 4 4' boxes. It was a pain to install. Little did I know there was a steel "I" beam at the edge of one side that had to fasten to the ceiling, but I was able to get it up there! :X Here are some piccies.
