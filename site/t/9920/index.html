gullfo21 December 2007#4
i would fire away from the slant - i.e. put the console facing the wall with the nice skylights (I have a similar arrangement but the walls are 2,6m high and slopes up to 4,5m high). the JBL's should be OK for the 2,2m triangle. when you put up your clouds, I recommend using a couple of smaller ones to allow the nice skylight in but still enough to break up the reflections off the ceiling and windows. the only concern is the monitors practically sitting into the skylight cavity... its just that having the slope to your back is going to require a bunch more treatment... so i'd build mobile treatments aka John's wall units and this will allow you to switch positions in case the front-to-slope position doesn't work out well. in this example i have a 1/2 size slat resonator on each side of the desk, the 1/2 height wall unit bass traps on the low front wall, and 2 full size wall units in back. 2 clouds over the desk. a large side wall absorber w/ slats if needed... http://img184.imageshack.us/img184/1866/jazzymike1ap5.th.jpg
