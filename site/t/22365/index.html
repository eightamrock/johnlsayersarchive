There is a case to be made for facing the West Wall. I would test the LF response in both orientations. You may get a clear answer. Treatment is pretty much the same for any rectangular space. Cloud, Corner Traps, Deep Back Wall trapping, Side Reflection killers. I don't see much point to small areas of diffusion.
