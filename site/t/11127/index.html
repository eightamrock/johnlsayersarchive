vonrock29 August 2008#11
DanDan, The controller that you linked to requires a duo core processor. That's why I suggested that you check out it's system requirements. I also dislike and love the mighty mouse. I love the scroll ball. Sure it clogs about once a week, but I'm not exactly very nice to it. Some vigorous rubbing with a clean, slightly damp cloth cleans it out rather well. What I dislike about the mouse, is the the right click. As you may know, it doesn't actually have real buttons. For me, about one in one hundred right clicks are perceived as a left click. This kills work flow. Many people have this issue. Many people don't. I've experienced it with both my wired and wireless mighty mouse. I'm also the type of person that can't wear a watch. They just stop working. For that reason, I don't wear a watch, and keep a $10 microsoft corded USB mouse on hand. I'm also a new Sketchup user. At first I found getting around a little frustrating, but the more I use it, the easier it gets, and the more I realize why it works the way it works. Keep working at it, and eventually I will be able to use you as my 'go to guy' for Sketchup help. Aloha Kendale, yes I misunderstood your meaning.
