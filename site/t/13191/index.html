chrisrnps24 June 2009#12
Would it be more advantageous to keep the last layout, but rotate the console and drum placement 180°, so that the existing ceiling height slopes up as you go from the 'mix position' end to the 'drum kit' end? Then you might move the two "iso booths" over to the left-hand side of the above layout, so that you enter the main room through one of the "booths" - which is now also your "airlock" - leaving enough clearance for trapping in the corners of the main room, and thinking about placement of doors to the two 'booths' in relation to where 'first reflection' points are going to be (of course, you could always put panel traps on the doors themselves...) Just make sure that you've figured out clearances to get anything large (desks, pianos, 4' x 8' drywall sheets) around the corner if you do that. :mrgreen:
