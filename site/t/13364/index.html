jbassino2 August 2009#10
Easiest thing to do is to build broadband panels and hang them on your walls. Harder and nicer would be to build a small lumber frame on your walls to support insulation and then cover the whole area with nice fabric made of 1x2 frames. You can also build slat resonators, in the shape you want. They can be rectangular frames, angled, zig-zag patterns, etc. Creativity is all that matters. Why don't you measure your room to determine real life problems and then build resonators to fix those frecs? Then its a matter to put absorption to create the room as dry as you need Also, you could build a cloud hanging over the place you'll put the drumkit, etc
