Soundman20207 February 2014#2
Hi there "joshcls". Welcome! Please read the forum rules for posting (click here). You seem to be missing a couple of things! :) That's a nice sized space, but you'll need to give us a lot more info on what you want to accomplish, and also provide some rough ideas of how you imagine the layout. For example, 1 room or two? Or more? That shape/size lends itself well to a control room plus a tracking room, but do you also need a booth? Storage? Bathroom? etc. To answer your specific questions: Yes 100 amps is more than enough, unless you plan on running some really major amp stacks in there! :) HVAC: You'd probably be fine with a couple of mini-split systems for that space, plus a ventilation system. That would be the least expensive.
Can this be done for ~$7,500, or is that a pipe dream?
Depends on what you want to do! But overall, that is probably very much on the low side. To get an idea of how much it will really cost, call up a few local contractors and ask for their basic "per square foot" rate for finishing an unfinished garage as a home theater, or for turning an unfinished basement into a "granny flat". Take that rate, add 25%, then multiply by your 433 square feet. That's a realistic ballpark estimate of what it will cost. For example, if they quote US$ 40 per square foot, add 25% to make it 50/square foot, multiply by 433 = US$ 21,650. Of course, you can do it a LOT cheaper if you do all/most of the labor yourself, and you can save on materials in other ways.
What to do about that massive roll up door/side door?
Here too that depends on your overall plan. For example, if you have storage and a lobby area, then you could put those next to the door and start your isolated area beyond them. Or if you just want one large space, then you'd have to either remove that door and brick it up, or seal it in place permanently and build an isolation wall a few inches behind it. - Stuart - - Stuart -
