knightfly3 April 2006#5
I personally would NOT divide a room that small; you'll end up with two VERY small, claustrophobic and unfriendly rooms. I think you'd be better off setting up a 1-room facility, probably using a few movable acoustic treatments during mixdowns and placing them around the drum kit while recording. Are you concerned with sound isolation from neighbors, family, etc, or just fitting everything in and having it sound good? Steve
