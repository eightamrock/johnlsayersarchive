When he's talking about 1" thru 4" insulation, specifically, he's usually talking about Rigid Fiberglass. Owens-Corning makes this stuff called 703, so when you see 703 on the boards, that what we mean. Many insulation companies make it in all different thicknesses. Pound-for-pound and dollar-for-dollar its some of the best sound absorbing stuff there is. So we use it a lot. ;) Kase www.minemusic.net
