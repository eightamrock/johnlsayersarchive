gullfo28 November 2010#2
i'd put the monitors on their own stands and consider a different desk to avoid the reflections off that desk. i'd consider a large cloud overhead to even out the overhead response by the desk and perhaps add some matching absorbers on stands on the open side of the desk (door side) to promote symmetry. the back wall should have plenty of absorption on it. the lava lamp also needs to be closer to the mixer person than the speakers :wink:

gullfo31 December 2010#11
in general, if you have enough space so the diffuser response is not discernible as more comb filtering, then yes. typically this is at least about 6' (2m) or more depending on the diffuser type and its operating frequency range.
