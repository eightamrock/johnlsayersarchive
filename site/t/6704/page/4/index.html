gullfo1 September 2007#87
the design is pretty much to scale based on your measurements and a couple of guesses on my part... :wink: since the room is sloping, i didn't make the framing parts angled but for construction purposes you would do that, or build in a false ceiling (since you have the height) to add additional trapping and it would give you more options for lighting (recessed vs track) etc... for lighting, my recommendation is you plan on using something like track lights on the ceiling to avoid putting holes in the roof. electrical and cables can all be routed behind the treatment walls and boxes attached to the treatment walls themselves. another thought that comes to my mind is the CR proper vs the seating area - the separating resonators walls (possibly) could be made to house speakers to make the CR and listening place each have a 5.1 mix - would take a bit more planning but its possible.
