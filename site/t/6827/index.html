Big Space15 August 2006#1
Hi all, I’m new to this forum but have been in the recording business for about 8 years. My name is Declan Legge and I run Big Space Studios in Northern Ireland. This has been (until now) a project studio built on the top floor of my house with minimum acoustic treatment and equipment. I have now decided to build my own 'proper' studio and have spent the last few months trawling through books and online resources looking for information to help me. This site is a bit of a revelation and I have spent a week going through the various designs looking for inspiration. I have just acquired an old sheep dip (yes you read that correctly!!) on a farm to convert into a studio. This is in the beautiful Northern Ireland countryside, so sound proofing is not the main issue (although I will need some) with the nearest neighbours about 200 metres away on two sides. As you can see from the photos the walls need to be built up and a roof needs to go on. From my research I believe that a height of 3.5 metres is the minimum required for a decent sounding room so I am looking at building the roof at around this height. (3.5m-4.5m). Does this sound ok? Also there are no doors or windows into the building and the doors can only go where indicated. Existing walls and floor are concrete. As you can see I am very much in the planning stages but I want to get it right so am asking for as much input as possible. I also have a builder who will do the construction work. My work at the moment revolves around recording rock bands, acoustic artists and solo singers. I need a studio with 1 comfortable control room (pro tools HD, 24 channel desk, Mackie HR24’s), a large (live) room, a vocal booth, and a chill out area. Because I need to be very adaptable, I am looking at a Live End/Dead End room or a separate 'live' room for drums (stone room??) My main concern is that I use the space as best I can. I would ideally like to have a large enough space to record a whole band in a room and to be able to record a band with as much separation as possible (separate rooms/booths) with good sight lines. (Don’t want much, do I??) My budget for construction is around £15,000 but I am still learning about cost and this can be revised if necessary. Can anyone help me with a design for this space? I am slowly working on a design myself based on a number of plans from this site but my main problem seems to be the fact that my space is long and narrow…. a lot of the existing designs here are based on squarer (is that a word?) rooms or smaller spaces and also I have only just discovered sketchup and am learning how to use it at the same time. I thought I might as well post it now as it could take some time for feedback... I have included photos and a SKP file of what will be the ‘blank canvas’ and it is to scale. This is basically the maximum space I have available with the floors flattened out , obstructions removed etc… Basically, anything can be done to this building whilst remaining within the perimeter of the external walls. I’ve tried to be as detailed as possible and follow the guidelines with this but if I’ve missed anything let me know and I’ll reply ASAP. Thanks in advance, Declan

John Sayers16 August 2006#4
Declan - an angled ceiling is preferable. YOu can either have a straight angled roof at 12 degrees or more or you can add a gable roof using a scissor truss system. cheers john
