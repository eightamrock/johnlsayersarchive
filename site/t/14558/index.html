If your budget is $300, you may want to build your own bass traps and put them in the corners of the room. Most folks also put bass traps or thinner traps on the first replection points between the speakers and your ears. If you search this site you'll find threads where a lot of folks have built their own bass traps.

xSpace11 July 2010#4
Maybe the biggest issue is that this is a square room. And then you have all those hard reflective areas, e.g. cabinets, table tops, television screens, etc that will give more surface area for sound to bounce off of. One thing that could be beneficial would be to develop an overhead cloud to reduce the action, at least, directly over your head, which may help in getting a better translating mix. The parallel boundaries (the walls) could benefit by installing broad band panels at the mid point of the wall in effort to minimize flutter echo and the square rooms potential to multiply the problem.
