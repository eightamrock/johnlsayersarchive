"Lite Wall" idea - stupid or useful?

Started by kirkbross on 6 August 2008. 1 reply.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 11071.

kirkbross6 August 2008#1
I am moving into a house in Sept. and building a studio in a 14' x 11' bedroom. I already this 5x6 foot iso room which I'm going to disassemble and put in the new studio -- probably in a corner about six inches away from each wall on a rubber gym floor (to protect the hardwood):
External image, not preserved — original: http://www.kirkross.com/ftp/images/ROOM1.jpg
I've set up my drums in this little iso room and it actually does a good job of volume reduction, so... I want to do it more and starting recording drums. But, I would like to "soundproof" the walls of the actual bedroom (in a non-destructive manner) as an additional sound reducer -- as much as possible anyway, without covering windows permanently. I was thinking of building some "lite walls" out of eight foot 1x4s with fiberglass insulation (R-13?) in between the studs, and then sandwich it with 1/8" high-density fiberboard and/ or soundboard. I figure I could move them around if I need to, but I'm wondering if there isn't a cheaper, and more thorough way to stop sound from escaping the room. Do the windows need to be covered?

I have no idea where your neighbors are located... but windows like doors are the weakest link so soundproofing can help. Also you can create a small stage for the drum to reduce impact noise if needed. PS - was in LA months ago, seems a nice crazy place to live !
