Ethan Winer29 June 2009#2
The only way to know for sure is to measure your speakers in your room. This is not trivial! Especially above maybe 500 Hz. Moving the microphone even an inch or two can change the response you measured by a lot. So then what? :D These are the programs I recommend: ETF, Windows, $150 FuzzMeasure, Mac, $150 Room EQ Wizard, Windows and Linux, Freeware This article explains how I use ETF, but the principles apply to all such programs. --Ethan
