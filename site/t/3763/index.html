For mode calculation, just take the average height of the ceiling, and consider it a rectangular room....close enough, without going to the trouble of buying boundary element software to do room analysis. There are a number of calculator spreadsheets out there (search this site) for doing modes. Reverb time will be a function of what wall treatment and construction you use....there are calculators for that too. Cheers, Kris
