I never had the time or money to take care of it, but now I plan to fix it up. Location: Sweden, Jönköping. I the basement of a school. Originally built as shelter in the 70's. I have three rooms where one is used as control room which are the subject for this matter. Measurements: I don't have the prints for the structure, and i'm not sure there are any. Floor is concrete with a plastic/rubber wood imitation carpet. Ceiling, painted concrete. The plan is only to fix the acoustics of the control room, not soundproofing or anything else. The soundproofing between the live room and control room are perfectly fine though I have no idea what's inside that wall. It was already built like that when I moved in. gullfo14 February 2011#2
welcome! it looks like a good plan - just consider opening up the back of the soffit cabinets on the corner side so you can stack those corners with light insulation and they will function as bass traps. the foam units you have, consider using them on the wall-ceiling corners spaced apart, and any left over in the back corners. on the back wall (over the radiator and on the door - put some 100mm or 150mm absorbers. consider putting light insulation up around the pipes like a super chunk and cover with cloth. over the desk, definitely a heavy cloud. maybe a couple of clouds in the room.
