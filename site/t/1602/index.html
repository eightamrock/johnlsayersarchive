John Sayers4 May 2004#4
Yiannis - I mentioned Blue Bear because it has the same access in the center of the room, like you, so you could consider putting the control room in the bottom right with a relaxation area on the bottom left. The follow with a vox booth and then studio at the top of the room. Yes concrete block is a good starting point. Check with Steve in construction as he has an "Ideal" concrete wall system. If you can get 3 -4 m ceiling height it really helps. It allows for floating floor and you can build angled ceiling below it yet still keep good ceiling heights throughout. cheers john
