So your true useable are is 13' x 19' with the side room for a vocal booth. You can actually build a decnt Control room and Live room with your seperate vocal booth. Possiby a 13x10' control room and 13 x 9' live room for cutting drums or gtr I used an area of about 13 x 25 to build a nice control room and Live room. The Live room is large enuff to accomodate a full Kit, bass and gtr Player if need be. I'd do the live room on the norther part of your diagram and the CR on the sothern part with that booth off to your left side. Orient the CR facing the live room. The 8.5 x 5' section at the bottom below where I think you should orient your control room can function as a combo basetrap/elevated couch seating area off of your control room allowing you to build a large live room. Hope that helps.
