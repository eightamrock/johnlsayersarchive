Soundman202026 September 2008#5
You might want to flip the sense of the door into the iso booth (so that it opens to the right, not the left), or try to move that door further apart from the control room door. Or move the control room door. Or use sliding glass doors on the CR. As it is, these doors will be banging into each other as you open and close them, and will end up knocking the door handles right off each other one day, as somebody rushes out of the iso booth in a hurry, while the CR door is half open.... It's not an acoustic issue: more of an operational issue. But believe me, it will be very annoying if you do it the way you have planned now. Your doors will end up scuffed and damaged before long. This is the voice of experience talking! Been there, done that.... :(
