Cram30 January 2008#5
Well, For what it's worth - I've done about 5 houses worth of insulation in my time with that stuff. Fiberglass insulation that is.. I'm no expert, but I've worked enough that I feel confident in my level of exposure during construction. My method has left me with little to no itching and no scratch in the throat - Find a mask with a tight fit around the resperatory area. Preferably one with check valve(s). Long sleeve sweatshirt and pants. My choice of garb in my room were junk jeans and a hooded sweatshirt. Leather work gloves. Nice pair of safety glasses. For the environment of the room(s) - Blankets/Tarps on the doorways. Shopvac work. Don't use a broom or anything that will kick things up during cleanup. I may be ignorant to trace levels of fiber being left behind, but I cleanup every day and the room is clean as can be. I do this precisely for my wife and kid's benefit.
