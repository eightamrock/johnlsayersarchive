hi there! this is my first post. we have been getting ready to rebuild our studio (we record all manner of rock, jazz, and folk music) after our landlord decided to put a stairwell right through our old live room. yay! javascript:emoticon(':evil:') the landlord is putting up the outer shell of the entire studio (a rectangle L 34'1" X W 30'10" X H 11') and we are finishing the interior. the top, left, and bottom spaces outside the drawing are all hallways. the right wall is brick, ceiling and floors are 8" concrete. the three rooms (2 top, 1 bottom right) will be combination workroom/isobooths with small windows facing the center of the live room. to me, these all seem to make sense and negate having the concrete pillars in the middle of the live room. the main problem i am having is figuring out what to do with the control room. we obviously want the room to be symmetrical, but large enough for our new neotek series 3 (represented by the rectangle in the room), engineer, 4 to 5 band members, a couch and some racks and a tape machine. i've posted 2 rough sketchup drawings of the designs we've been tossing around. the long CR seems to give us the most room, but i can't figure out if it makes sense (or is even possible) to have people enter from the lock (left middle) into the CR behind the console. we obviously want people to be able to get in and out of the CR without going through the live room, and avoid building any extra doors if possible. our budget is between $1-4 thousand dollars, but most of that will be on our credit cards. do either of these designs make sense? how can we optimize them? thanks for any help!!!
