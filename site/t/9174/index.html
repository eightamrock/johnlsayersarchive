Question about room dimensions when calcualting modes etc

Started by mondo on 29 July 2007. 1 reply.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 9174.

mondo29 July 2007#1
My room is 18.1ft long. The rear quarter of the room I use for storage, and is sectioned off by 1 inch foam hanging from the roof, across the width of the room, about 3/4 of the way to the floor.. Would the length dimension I use for calculations be 18.1 ft, or the distance from the front of the room to the hanging foam sectioning of the rear 1/4 of the room? thanks
Eric_Desart30 July 2007#2
Mondo, That depends on the type of foam. But normally you calculate with the acoustic boundaries, which translated means the surfaces which reflect, which in itself assumes they have a certain TL. Since you're most likely interested in the lows you must most likely take the room measures into account.
