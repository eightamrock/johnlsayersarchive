gullfo15 September 2007#3
generally speaking, the panel needs some degree of hardness to effectively diffuse. it may be the rock panel has some reflectivity for high frequencies. if you have the room for curved panels, consider using 1/8 plywood with the rockwool backing it. consider perf-board poly's straddling the corners.
