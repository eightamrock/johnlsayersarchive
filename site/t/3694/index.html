Aaronw9 May 2005#5
Do you have just the one main panel, or do you have a subpanel that is for the studio? Typically, in a star ground scenario, you have a ground wire for each individual outlet. In other words, do not tie or daisy chain the ground wire from outlet to outlet then back to the panel. Each ground wire for each outlet needs to come back to a central point, then out to a grounding rod. You can however, have multiple outlets sharing the same Hot & Neutral wires, just not the ground. If this still doesn't make since, let me know, I'll try to do another diagram. Or if have a diagram of your electrical layout, that would be helpful. :D
