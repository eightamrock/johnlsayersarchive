xSpace25 February 2009#8
Ideally, you would block in the overhead joists at the point directly above where you are installing the wall. Then frame the wall to go up to the newly installed blocks in between the joists. Then install your finish ceiling to the outer side room ceiling and to the newly framed outer wall. You could use a 12 to 16 inch layout on the overhead blocks that will go inbetween the joists. The blocks will face widest side down so that the 1-1/2 inch part butts into the joist on either side. Simple enough?
