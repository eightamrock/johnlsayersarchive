hey welcome and good luck with all this. (I'm from the area as well...) The first thread that came to mind relating to yours is here: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=9584 A similar garage. On your budget - if you're north of town, you may consider buying in bulk and hauling it down from NH. No tax would save you 5% atleast, but if you're buying framing lumber, keep it tied up or clamped until you frame so you don't wind up with hockey sticks for 2x4's... On Building Inspections - Inspectors will appreciate plans that clearly state what you're doing on paper. They'll want to approve your work at stages of construction. They'll likely have a square foot evaluation fee where approving your work to wrap up/go to the next stage. For example, they'll want to see the electric and fire block work before you put your sheets up to cover things... Your Zoning question may be termed wrong here - are you saying you'd like your property "zoned" for commercial use? Say, like your neighbor openning up a residential service such as a beauty salon or tax service. This would mean you're trying to make sure you can commercially accept clients. If so, then you're inspection just got tougher with liability crap. (sharward pontificates on this one frequently... search his posts) If zoning simply means how your garage is classified - changing from a garage to a dwelling room(s), then I think it is all taken care of with your building inspection - you'll get a certificate of occupancy upon completion of their inspection when you're done. There are also distinctions for whether or not someone can "live" and "sleep" in this room vs it being a rec room - this is the case in our state atleast. january's coming soon man...
