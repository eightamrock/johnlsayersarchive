bohemian28 July 2004#7
Dave et all, Thought about the central console idea and it seems that there is not enough room. Communication might be a bit tough over all the equipment. I had a crazy thought that i could raise the floor around a central mix position and then everyone could see over the lcd's and such. Maybe in the future... what we really want is Peter Gabriel's setup: http://realworldstudios.com/ For now I came up with the solution of creating a three sided symetrical box near the closet end of the warehouse. At least with this I know I'm getting the same reflections from both sides and deal with that with absorption and diffusion. The big issue now is dealing with the drywalled ceiling and the reverb from it. Spent yesterday making mid/high absorbers/diffusers out of acoustic 2'x4' ceiling tiles and door core. Also going to experiment with the rectangular bags of recycled newspaper insulation as extra bass traps, they weigh a good 40 lbs, so mass is not a problem. Going to cover them in fabric and use them as portable traps. (also good for holding down gobos) Giles, We do care about the sound, hence the original question... anyway... As far as tracking - we have 6-8 players and not all make it to all rehearsals so we track the practices and send out CD's with specific mixes for the specific players.... i.e. if the trumpet/keys player is out we can send her a mix both with someone else covering her part and with out someone else covering her part, so she can hear the flavor we're going for. Also tracking this way allows us to grab nuggets of the warm up jams to send to the lead singer so he can edit and create new material in his studio. Just makes for more flexibility- we're running 24-32 inputs through FOH, why not split them to a DAW while we're at it, never know when something cool will happen. I'm going to shoot the room with ETF pre-setup and then after treatment, should be interesting to see what happens. I'll post the results. Thanks again everyone, bo www.ruderockfamily.com
