John Sayers16 January 2004#8
on laptoppop, did he basically build some of these kinds of things
yes basically he did. Matta -Auralex works well for high frequency absorption, they look good and are easy to install and can be spray painted to any colour scheme. Unfortunately you also need to absorb the lows and low mids as well else you'll end up with a room that sounds boxey. Ethan's new absorbers work right across the whole range so it's all covered in one unit. The slots Laptoppop used are designed to work on the low mids, which when combined with high frequency absorption give broadband absorption. His low end absorption is in the ceiling. I hope this helps. cheers john

Talk about revisiting an old thread.... Over the last year I have reworked it quite a bit from the original drawing I posted. Firstly I lost the couch, moved my desk almost 0.5 meters away from the wall, the behind the monitors/desk is is now covered with an acoustic foam wall, along with 2 large foam corner traps (they came with the kit, I know it is not as good as a proper absorber/resonator) and the 2 side walls are covered with small panels of foam. The kit is made locally here in South Africa, the closest thing to it I can find internationally is the Primacoustic London 14 kit:
External image, not preserved — original: http://www.sonic-distribution.com/2/pages/Products/Primacoustic/images/pics/london3d-14.gif
Where they have the rear scatter blocks in the pics, I have those on the roof above my monitoring position. The other thing I have done is where the 80cm high built in cupboard I have built a stackable book self to the roof height, 2.8 meters and filled it with books of various widths, depths and heights, which I believe acts as a diffuser of sorts. I’m stuck with what sounds a little ‘boxy’, the foam has tamed the high end from banging around (this room is solid concrete), but the low end still seems undefined and the low mids. Due to the room being almost square I am suffering from resonating standing waves, I’m not there is much that can be done in such a small room to deal with/ correct it. Any suggestions?
