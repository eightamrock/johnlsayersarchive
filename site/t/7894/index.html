the configuration is substantially the same as yours... i just rotated the CR... that said, flip the CR/iso booth and the live room... and add a small iso booth/air lock over the door... the radiator is under the trap area under the window - you could just add an opening to allow the radiator to function. as far as isolation goes, you need to figure out how loud you expect your internal levels to be and then what walls, ceilings, floors will work to reduce the levels outside enough to not disturb your neighbors...
