gullfo5 October 2007#2
windows are nice if you like the view. you just have to consider any loss of isolation caused by the windows not being sealed or having enough mass to duplicate the isolation provided by the walls. also, windows can introduce reflections into your room so one option is to add heavy drapes to cover them when doing critical mixes. or you could build hinged window plugs to cover them completely when you want them sealed.
Eric_Desart6 October 2007#3
Just a small addition to Glenn, 1) As Glenn said isolation is important to take into account. 2) Reflections of a window aren't necessarily a minus if they don't disrupt anything, and can even be supportive. Floors, doors, walls in a tracking and these large sliding glass doors often used are also reflective. If disturbing see Glenn's reply. Often people DO like natural light (and view) in their work environment.
