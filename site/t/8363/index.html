gullfo11 March 2007#6
here's the sketchup files. I recommend disassembly of the drawing (its in groups) and lay it out w/ dimensions on a 4x8x15/32 (12mm) plywood. if you can create cardboard templates of each repeating piece its much easier. I've built the v17 but not the v19 so you may have to adjust v19 to meet your needs. the design uses middle atlantic brand rails (found cheapest on Amazon) but you can use whatever you need just plan on drilling the rail mount holes after you get the panels cut and have the rails in-hand. I used 1/4" x 1" #20 bolts with 9/16" washers and self-locking nuts. all assembly of the wood was glue and screw.
