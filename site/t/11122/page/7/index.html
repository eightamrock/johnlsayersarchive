Soundman202015 January 2018#153
Ive got to ask though - what happened to the studio ? was it built? are there any pics????
In the end, it never was built. For three reasons: 1) I realized that I wasn't going to get the results I was originally wanting in this sized space. It would have been pretty good, yes, but I'm too much of a perfectionist to be satisfied with "pretty good".. :) 2) I suddenly needed the space for storage, after some other things happened that I'm not going to go into.... 3) My tracking / mixing / mastering business declined at the same rate that my forum participation, acoustic understanding, and studio design business grew! This is really the biggest reasons of the three. I started getting so much business designing and tuning studios for other people, that I had less and less time left for doing my own music! And I found that I really enjoy this aspect of studios (designing them) more than I enjoyed the other aspect (using them). Maybe one day I might decide to build it anyway, more as a personal challenge to see just how good I can get it, rather than as a place where I'd mix music to make a living. It would be different from what I had planned originally, too. . . . Now, if only I could figure out what to do with all the junk seems to have accumulated in there, it might go faster! :) It's hard to believe that it's been almost ten years since I started this thread... WOW! :shock: - Stuart -
