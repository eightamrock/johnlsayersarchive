The nominal speaker height is from the acoustic centre of the speaker, which is near the tweeter. With the woofer below it, it gets out of the 50% zone. The 38% guideline is for low frequencies. The speaker height affects perception of frequencies above 160 Hz. Over 6 octaves. The low frequency range for your height is ~ 70-280 Hz or 2 octaves. Assuming your speakers have 10 cm between the woofer centre and acoustic centre, this would place the woofers at ~110 cm or 46% of the height. Well off the 50% where room mode nulls would be strongest. You are going to have a drop in that range regardless because your ears are at that height! So many people, including "experts" forget the ear position when looking at frequency charts.
