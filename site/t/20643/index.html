Hi there Ale, and Welcome! :)
Now... the guy wants to use a pre made frame from home depot so that we can have the recessed hinges,
Whooaaa! Your contractor does not know hoe to mortice hinges into frame that is not pre-hung? :shock: Sorry, but you need another contractor! Any carpenter, even a mediocre one, knows how to do that: You can do it either manually with a chisel, or used a router. Both ways work. The router is faster and cleaner, but chisels have been used for this very thing for centuries before routers were invented!
1 My question is: do i just get a 1inch and a quarter piece of stock lumber and attach the hinges on that ( without being recessed? )
Why don't you want to recess them? It's easy. See above. Do not buy a standard pre-hung door or frame: do not use standard hinges. Rod's door is HEAVY! Very VERY heavy. You cannot hang it on three normal hinges in a normal frame. You need at least four extra.heavy duty hinges, and I often use five, or more. Take a look at these photos, to see how studio doors are normally done. This is not a Superdoor: it is two typical back-to-back doors that I designed for a customer in Australia:
