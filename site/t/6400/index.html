tmix8 June 2006#2
The questions should probably be how much amperage will a particular CIRCUIT need and whether you have heavy enough wires and circuit breakers to accomplish it. If you know that a single rooms outlets are the ONLY thing on a particular circuit ( or "home run") you can begin to know what you will need. First figure out what gear you will be running. If you are running a 400 watt amp and 300 watts of computer and peripherals you can do a rough estimate by dividing the the total wattage draw by the voltage used. For example your 400 watts plus 300 watts (700 watts) divided by 110 volts equals 6.34 amps of draw. so a 15 amp circuit would be fine. But if other things like lights and hairdryes and refigerators and the like are on the same circuit, you may be in trouble. See what size circuit breaker is on the circuit you want to use as a jumping off place. And remember, the figures I gave are for rough estimate only. I am not an certified electrician. Tom
