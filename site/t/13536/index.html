Soundman202026 August 2009#3
Hi Gopendra. Welcome. A control room must be symmetrical in order to work. The design in your drawing is not symmetrical. A live room does not need to be symmetrical, but a control room does. You should modify your design to get that. Try looking around the forum at some of the many control room designs here, especially John's designs, and you'll see what I mean be symmetry. You might even find a design that will fit your space perfectly, or can be easily adapted to fit it. I also suggest that you should spend some time reading the forum, to find out about the basic concepts of acoustics, and the basic concepts behind studio design, to help you get a good understanding of what you need to do to design your studio. There is a huge amount of information on this forum about how to design and build your studio right. It might take you days or weeks to read the important parts, but I can promise you that it will be well worth it! - Stuart -

Soundman202027 August 2009#5
It needs to be fully symmetrical at least from the front wall to the mix position. It isn't so critical to have total symmetry behind the mix position. As long as you can get it symmetrical in front of your ears, then you should be OK. There isn't really any treatment you can use to fix a non-symmetrical room: The only treatment that works, is to make it symmetrical! :) - Stuart -
