rod gervais19 January 2004#3
Stve is right on the money here - but in addition The finish will in fact not only modify the density - it will change the reflective value of the material at higher frequencies as well. This is a trick we have used in studios in the past to help with "tuning" them. The higher you get the more coats of finish you apply - thus deader at the lower levels and more live at the upper levels. Rod
