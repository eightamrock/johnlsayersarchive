SonicClang29 March 2006#3
It won't change the way your room responds to the frequencies coming from your speakers. No, they don't need a solid surface to sit on. You DON'T want a desk to resonate from them, that's why they invented MOpads (monitor pads). You want them decoupled from the desk. But, just the same way as you want them decoupled from the desk, you'll want to make sure you decouple them from the cables/ceiling/joists. You could hang down 4 cables from the ceiling for each monitor. Use a solid peice of wood and tie each corner to the cables. Then set the monitor on a pad on that peice of wood. Would I ever hang my $2,000 set of monitors from cables? HELL NOOOOO!!!!! :D Are you kidding me? Some idiot comes in thinking it's fun to swing them around, suddenly one falls out and cracks the cone. There goes a thousand dollars down the drain. I know my desk isn't going anywhere, and with proper isolation from the desk that's all I need.
