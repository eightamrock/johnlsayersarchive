Soundman20208 January 2010#215
Generally Gobos have two sides, one absorptive and one reflective so you can turn it arround
Exactly. According to some sources, that's even where the name comes from. It is called a "GoBo" because it "GOes BOth" ways. In the most basic form it is just a panel of wood (plywood, MDF, etc) standing up vertically, mounted on wheels, and with a sheet of 703 on one side. When the 703 side faces your sound source, you get absorption. When the wood side faces your side source, you get refection. - Stuart -
