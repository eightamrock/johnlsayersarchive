BriHar17 March 2011#249
If you just wrapped the copper wire on the foil, as the copper oxidizes you are going to lose ground - i.e. a resistance will form creating a ground potential difference. The wire should be soldered or otherwise firmly, mechanically attached to the foil. You might need a heavier grade foil, better yet, braided shield mantle.
