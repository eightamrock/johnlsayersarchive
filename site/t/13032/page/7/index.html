jbassino29 July 2009#170
Update :D I managed to seal the 2" pvc snake conduit SUCCESSFULLY !!!! First, I put a bunch of insulation inside the conduit as long with the cables. Then, I cranked my monitoring and went to the other room, guess what: No leakage!! nada! The wool itself somehow blocked all the sound going through the conduit !! Later I went and sealed the pvc end with caulk "just in case" but I was amazed how the wool did the job by itself.. Also, I plugged my guitar into my 120watt head and my 4x12 cabinet. I set the amp to "back to the future" level, and guess what. no leakage or whatsoever in my control room! I just heard a very low lowend rumble, but it was reasonable! wonderfull :yahoo: The only problem was the recording room's door. Since I just built one door, I left the old thin door on the outer leaf and it sucks! so I have to build another super door for the outer leaf as well! It was pretty noticeable the volume from outside the room, but once you opened the door, I almost flyied like Marty McFly he he
