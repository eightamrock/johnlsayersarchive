rod gervais6 December 2007#2
Leave from 1/8" min to 1/4" max clearance at the door jamb for the door. This runner should be just snuggly fit to the door - it does not need to pass by the side of it. The trim should then be fitted to the rubber to complete a nice looking assembly. Finishing the isolation through the use of magnetic weatherstripping makes it the best it can be.
