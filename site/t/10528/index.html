yellowfever25 April 2008#1
Being an avid reader of this forum for about a year or so, I just wanted to pass on a thought to anyone designing a studio, control room etc from scratch, if you are lucky enough to be in that position. If I'm right, I believe that as long as you know your intended room height, stick to 1 of the 'ideal room ratios' and make sure you use the 38% rule for listening position, with some simple maths and careful thought / research, it is relatively straight forward to design your room as everything else will then 'fall into place'. It was fairly daunting reading these posts at first, but after a while and lots of reading, it all becomes clear! Just wanted to pass my thoughts on to any other 'newbies' who may feel like I did when I first thought about my build. Hey - the architect has been round and it looks like it will finally happen. Watch this space...... Cheers all. :D

It just seems to me that so many people make silly errors. Now, I'm not perfect, far far from it, but time and again I read about things like monitors positioned incorrectly, ignoring ratios and the 38% rule, firing sound down short walls as opposed to down the length of a room etc etc. Just wanted to pass on things I've learned from reading on places like Sound on Sound, and here for example. That's all. Happy designing all :D
