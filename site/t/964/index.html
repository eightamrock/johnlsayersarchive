giles11712 November 2003#2
Here is a suggestion, sit down and plot out your desires then order snakes to fill the need. Example, I ran a 16pr and then a 12 pr snake into my control room so I could have 20 Mic jacks and 8 Headphone Jacks. Everyone's situation is different so plot it out and adda a few more for safety. If you think 1 head phone plan for 2, if you think 2 mic jacks plan for 4. The materials to in-expensive to under plan for your needs. So plan and get a bigger snake than what you plan for. I think my 16 pr snake at 30', which was way to much, was $15.00 and my 12 pr at 30 feet was $10.00 so the materials as you can see are very cheap. Bryan Giles
