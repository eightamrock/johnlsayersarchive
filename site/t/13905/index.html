Basic CR layout? Desk facing the window, with your back to the door. Monitors on massive stands just behind the desk. Plug the window. Superchunks in the rear corners (on either side of the door) and front corners (on either side of the window), a cloud over the mix position, absorption behind the speakers and at the first reflection points. That's about as far as US$ 500 will take you, I reckon. There won't be enough for a vocal booth, nor for treatment in the LR. Nor for electrical, HVAC, etc. But I doubt you'll be able to track drums too well without any isolation between CR and LR. How high are the ceilings? - Stuart -
