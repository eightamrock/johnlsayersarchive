Here are some basic plans for my new space. I want to build a new building on a block of land in the country somewhere an hour or two from Melbourne. The idea is to build a 'nice' space with excellent acoustics for live recordings. I'm not much interested in tracking everything to death - there are plenty of studios in Melbourne where people can do that. I want to make a space where musicians can come and play music - the fact that is is being recording should almost be a bit of an after through. I want to do things like have nice windows to the world, atleast in the live room. The plans I'll post here at the start, but I've already got ideas about extending from the entrace to add a veranda + lounge etc... Perhaps more studio rooms if required. Priorities: 1) Live room must sound great - The best recordings I make now are in 'nice' acoustic spaces. I find many studios a bit of a sterile place to work. 2) Workable control room. It needs to be good enough to make great mixes, but not excessive. I'm not in to loads of outboard gear etc. Issues: With as far as I've got now I don't have any storage space - or any non studio space (I'd Imagine the second smaller studio would get used like that alot) - Also as I expand the facility this could come in time. Construction - I've been thinking, concrete slab with concrete bricks for the walls - tin roof and then standard offset plaster stud walls etc + treatments. Possible I get a big shed and then build inside that ? The space isnt' fixed, the size isnt' fixed. I think I could really be quite happy with a facailty with just a CR and a largeish - truly wonderful live room. But I can see that I'll end up doing projects which require a few more rooms etc. I'm going to leave out things like toilets (country makes this harder - septics etc) and such for the first generation. This is a cost consideration as much as anything. Thoughts ? (Images too follow in next post)
