Romo, sorry this got missed; first, if your room is really that wet you're gonna have problems with your gear; connections, rust, etc - if possible, you should get a dehumidifier in there that runs constantly. Pool paint won't take away the moisture. Second, there are basic things to do to improve the acoustics of a room - a lot of them can be found here http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm A lot more can be found by reading through posts in the Acoustics forum. Your room will need more treatment than most, because it's square. If you need sound isolation (keeping the drum sound from leaving the room) this will take a LOT more effort and cost... Steve
