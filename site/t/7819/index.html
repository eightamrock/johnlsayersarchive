gullfo7 January 2007#9
only a certified architect and the building officer can give the stamp of approval... :? building a second floor structure in an earthquake zone... permits... etc... otherwise the design looks good... get Rod's book on construction techniques so you are getting the best value on your construction work. also it probably gets warm so A/C and fresh air will be a plus (even drummers like air... ;-) so putting in proper air exchange and cooling will be important. maybe a split unit for each room and some air venting. also, on the angled door i'd hang some 703 (like) absorber to cut reflections. on the other entry door - you could do the same. as far as additional bass trapping, put 703 up in the wall-ceiling corners and leverage all the space behind your angled walls. also on the second entry (the flat door not the angled one) you could put in a "slot resonator door" that could add some balance to the room, have it hinged to open into the room (and the regular door out).
