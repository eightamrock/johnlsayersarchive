rod gervais26 December 2006#6
Jordan, As long as the new structure never comes into contact with the existing - and as long as you construct (sound) wraps for those columns that attach to the existing but never touch the new columns - and it would help a lot if ytou would spray fireproof the new stel columns to a 2 hour fire protection (this helps to damp the steel quite nicely) then you are probably in pretty good shape for the downstairs..... BUT - i see a problem then with the outside world and your new "room within a room". This being that the new roof is directly coupled to the new concrete deck - and this will probably become your weakest link from the perspective of flanking noise...... How much of an issue is iso from outside the building> I don't like the symmetry that you designed into the tracking room - that should be asymmetrical - perhaps you can rework that area......... Rod
