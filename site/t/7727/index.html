The moderators and those offering suggestions are doing so without charge and as their schedules allow. Like you, most are really busy with work, family, building their own studios and life in general, so don't be discouraged if it takes a little while for someone to chime in. Almost every question has been covered here at some point, it's really just a matter of doing a little research using the search function, and ALOT of reading. After you've a few ideas down, a floor plan with measurements (l x w x h) and as much other info as possible about your project, post it and then I'm sure you'll see alot more responses.
