AVare25 April 2008#3
The sound from the fan travels through the ducts. Without doing an engineering analysis on the sound attenuation of the ducts and knowing your desired noise level in the studio, yes. you have given the right specs in terms of ventilation performance. I am guessing that you are comfortable with doing calculations. If you want to go through the exercise, the McQuay Application Guide AG31-010 provides detailed information on how to calculate acoustic performance of ventilation systems. It is directed at the designer/engineer, so it is comprehensive and assumes familiarity with math. It will take several hours to read it, absorb it, and setup your spreadsheet to do the calculations. If you do, you will have the satisfaction of knowing that you did not spend (read waste) money on more than you need. As you have found out already, quiet HVAC (north american term for Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning) is very expensive. Good luck and let us know how things go! Coolly, Andre
