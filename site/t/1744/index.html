Hi everyone; We decided to sell our old house and found a great place that we take possession of tonight. The future studio area is 46 feet long by 14 feet wide by 7 feet 2 inches tall. At one end is a fireplace; I'd like to leave room there for a small lounge area. I'd also like to maximize the live room area; it will see as much use as a rehearsal room for my band as it will for recording and we're now used to playing in a huge open warehouse. Luckily there's only 3 of us. A vocal booth would be nice but I'd be willing to give it up for more live room space. Ideally the live area is not on the far left side of the room, that's directly under the bedrooms. My first thought was to use Garage Studio 1 and slide the control room over to shrink down the booth and expand the live room. I'm not sure if that's too tight for a vocal booth to the left; if so it could be storage (maybe machine room). But lately I've been thinking about something like Garage Studio 2 with a longer studio area. I like that I'd be facing the band directly, and a door could be added (or not) for extra access not through the live room (it can go anywhere along that wall). Obviously the door between the live room and the lounge can be anywhere along the wall, and the wall itself could be angled if that would be beneficial.
