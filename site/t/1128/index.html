"And your french is not non-existent " - certainment; jem appelle "ze knightfly", and Monsieur has caught me in a lie (albeit not a big one, since I am nearly done , other than "garage", "e claire", and possibly "fait accompli" - so I'm only 99.997% illiterate in the language of love... :roll: One thing I would consider changing is the two little "jogs" in the front walls near the speaker flush mounts - having little pockets like that can cause either "acoustic lens" effects of concentrating unwanted reflections, or strange diffractions, or both. It would be better to continue the side walls to the speaker baffle extensions, even though this may cause a bit of "horn effect", where the speakers are effectively putting out a slightly narrower sound field. I've marked the areas in lavender on your drawing. Are you clear on what types of treatment you plan, and have you checked out comments on the subject very much? What specifically are you unsure of? Layout generally looks good so far... Steve
