drfrankencopter26 March 2007#5
In a control room you want balanced modal response, and a reflection free zone for the mix position. In a live room...anything goes! You probably want to have variable acoustics...diffuse areas, and dead areas. Angled walls and ceilings will help tame flutter echoes, and reduce requirement for absobtion. Maybe what you should do is have a target RT60 in mind for your room...this may impose limits on how much ceiling height you can 'waste' by sloping. Bear in mind that in the tracking space you can hang absorbers and diffusers too... If the ceiling height is low, I'd go for an absorbtive ceiling and reflective floor... Cheers, Kris
