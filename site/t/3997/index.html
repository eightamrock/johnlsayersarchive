Mark, > Does anyone have any plans for creating a good corner bass trap for a small room. < At its most basic, you get a bunch of 2x4 foot rigid fiberglass panels, 3 or 4 inches thick, and mount them straddling as many corners as possible. --Ethan
bpape7 July 2005#3
At it's most basic, you can just build a frame that will hold the 703 and hang it from the ceiling. You can get fancier if you want and put up slats behind and in front of the edges of the 703 too. If you want to make it real easy, just cut the fiberglass into triangles and stack them up in the corner, stretch cloth across the corner, attach to the wall, and then cover the edges with trim.
