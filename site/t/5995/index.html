SonicClang17 April 2006#6
Simply do what is done in the real theaters. Create a sound proof area for the projector with a glass window for the projection to get out. The nice thing about light is it can get through glass, where as sound cannot. :) can someone kick me for stating the obvious? haha Where are you looking to use the projector? Could you make a projector closet to house the thing? The cool thing about the fact that you're only using a projector is that you know exactly what frequencies you're trying to stop. Musicians have to stop a very wide range of frequencies, including very low ones, which makes things much harder to deal with. It should be real easy to build yourself a little closet for the thing and make it really sound proof. Just make sure you run some kind of HVAC to the room because it'll get hot in there otherwise, but be careful how you connect it to the rest of your system or you'll transfer the sound from room to room through the main HVAC trunk.
