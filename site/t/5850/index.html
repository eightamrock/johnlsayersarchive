Heiko, the side absorbers you drew will help flutter, maybe make them a bit longer and at least 6 degrees each side (1:10 ratio) - also, you'll need corner absorbers at the front as well, and probably more absorption behind your speakers than just a curtain. A "cloud" over your mix position and you should be pretty good. This is generally what you need - http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm The corner absorbers can be just cloth-covered 75 to 100mm mineral wool, about 1 meter wide, placed diagonally across vertical corners from floor to ceiling - best weight for this is 48 kG/m^3. Same weight/type for a "cloud" - here's a couple of ways to do this http://www.recording.org/ftopict-26889.html http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewt ... 1&start=90 Modally, your dimensions look pretty good; but you'll still need to check out the harmon mode calculator and try to optimise location of speakers and your ears before worrying about treatments - http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=18059 If you're totally lost on this, and I get a chance, I may be able to help you figure this out; but I'm pretty buried these days so it may be a while...
