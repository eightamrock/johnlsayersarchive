sharward5 May 2006#19
Wow, I was really impressed with that unit, until I read this on page 18: "The fresh air in and exhaust cannot be operated simultaneously." That to me (and I'm sure to my building department) is a dealbreaker. To be able to run the unit to supply the room with fresh air without being able to exhaust the air entering the room with a similar amount of force will cause the room to become somewhat pressurized. The opposite is true of exhausting air without fresh air coming in. This is especially true if the room is air-tight, which if you build it properly, it will be. Again, my plan for ventilation calls for two fans working together in the same unit -- one for blowing air in and one for sucking air out. That's what you need in order to provide the amount of air exchange necessary to satisfy code requirements for replacing a window. Although this Hitachi system is surely the closest I have seen to the "magic system" that I gave up on finding, it is unfortunately not the holy grail of mini split systems that I was hoping for. If I were in the market for one for a home or office, though, yeah, I would definitely put that on my list of contenders.
