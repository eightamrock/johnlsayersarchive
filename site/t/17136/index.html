Soundman202010 October 2011#6
I was under the impression that a mini split does not recycle fresh air.
It doesn't (or rather, most don't), but we aren't talking about fresh air here: I understood the issue was heating the room, not ventilating it. Just to clarify: you need BOTH ventilation AND cooling/heating. It's not an either/or situation: you need both. Ventilation won't cool the room (or heat it) very much. It just bring in fresh air and takes out stale air, but that's all it does. The mini-split wont ventilate the room: It just cools it (or heats it, as the case may be), and equally important, it dehumidifies. That's why the entire concept is called HVAC: Heating - Ventilation - Air-Conditioning. You need to do all of those, not just some. - Stuart -
