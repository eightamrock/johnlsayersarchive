Aloha and welcome to the forum, Cliff Congrats on your studio build! Any chance you could please:
Edit your profile to include your location. This is very important, because this is a worldwide resource, and as such, material costs and availability vary widely. For example, masonry is cheaper than gypsum in some parts of the globe, whereas it's the exact opposite in other regions.
You'll probably get more of a response by doing so and this will really come in handy two or three posts down the road. It's also part of the forum guidelines: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=3231 Thanks! :wink: If you could provide a little more info, it'd be helpful: - How much isolation/soundproofing do you need from the rest of the house? - What kind of music/instruments will you be working with? - What volume levels will you be working with? - Is there insulation in your current walls? - And finally, provided the area marked out below is NOT a bearing wall, is it a viable option to knock out this corner and even out your floor plan? Otherwise, you could look into trying some of the following to get started: Setting up the Mix Position - Roughly 38% from the front wall avoiding peaks/nulls (front to back, left to right, ceiling to floor) - Equilateral triangle formed by left, right mix position - Monitor angles 60 degrees - Monitor height – woofer at ear level (avoid mid point of floor to ceiling measurement – either sit higher or lower if necessary) - Side absorption panels – left/right of mix position - Corner absorbers in front left/right and rear left/right forners - floor to ceiling - Ceiling absorber panel/s over mix position Looking forward to your build! Aloha
