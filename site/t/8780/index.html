What is cellouse? Searching the net superficially, it seems like sprayed polyurethane foam or ?, but can be something else as well. It's much easier for people if you describe a material or link to it, rather than giving some brand or local known name. This is a world wide forum. (and you don't give any location of yourself) There are fibrous spray products based on mineral or synthetic fibre which do work for acoustic absorption (which is still quite different than soundproofing), ........ and there are the more common synthetic expanding foam types which hardly have any acoustic function, and even have mostly a destructive effect on soundproofing when sprayed into cavities. As far as I know on the net, the latter is used to glue a garage door to a wall build behind it, to neutralize the negative effect of the cavity. In fact it was used to make the lightweight door acting as part of the wall. We even made fun of the fact that the discussion to solve an acoustic problem resulted in a material which standard always is referred as counter productive for acoustic applications. I recently adviced it for an exact similar situation here (for local project). But such applications where it can be useful in function of sound are extremely rare. If it's the latter type of foam then Sharward's comment is 120% valid. And I wonder what kind of guarantee you could ever have given in function of sound, whether absorption or isolation. Such expanding foams are designed for thermal, not acoustic applications.
