Ro11 July 2011#11
drummer9 wrote:
I have corner bass traps that go floor to ceiling made of OC 703- 6" thickness. Is there any advantage to moving them away from the corners so there is an air gap? Will I get better results with an air gap or should I leave them tight against the corners?
to answer your question: corner filled absorbers (for low end) should not be moved away to create an airgap. More wool is better, fill it all. For wall panels; yes space them the same size as the thickness of the wool for better results. Or double the thickness of the wool ;) (again, more wool equals more absorptions. thicker wool means lower frq abs.)
