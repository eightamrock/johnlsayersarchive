Hi rabbitgeeks, i'm not an expert but maybe i can help you with my crap english (better wait for the experts anyway :D and better upload you files inside the forum and not links to other sites). I can't download your files because of some problems with sketchup. For what i know, the best (and the only) way is to build a room sealed everywhere and fully decoupled; if i understand your problem, you don't know how to seal the drywall. Look here: viewtopic.php?f=11&t=6968&start=15 you can build the walls on the ground than raise them up, this should solve your problem. Regarding HVAC you have different options, you don't want "air circulating" but "air exchange": Suck bad air from your room and flow it outside and vice versa, take clean air from the outside and bring it in your room. Search for "inverter" and do a search inside this forum and see all the different options that you have for this purpose. Bye!! :wink:
