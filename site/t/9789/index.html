doublehelix23 November 2007#5
I am pretty sure you are completely misunderstanding what Auralex is saying. The Space Couplers are NOT diffusers, but are used to *couple* two spaces together. When they are used as ceiling mount devices, they are joining the lower space in the room to another space above the dropped ceiling. They are also used between rooms. Read the site again. EDIT: BUT, they do also make diffusers in this series, called the "SPACE ARRAY".
