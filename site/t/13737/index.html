just a 2 x layer wooden floor on joists is all you need. Fill the cavity with insulation.
JoeMilner2 November 2009#5
John Sayers wrote:
just a 2 x layer wooden floor on joists is all you need. Fill the cavity with insulation.
By "2x" do you mean 2 layers of 3/4" plywood? I was thinking about that idea, and hoped that the resulting 1 1/2" deck with insulation would be stiff enough and "filled" enough to avoid the resonance and the hollow sound when you step on it. I'm encouraged by this, as doing anything in concrete is so permanent, it's a little daunting. Thanks! Joe
