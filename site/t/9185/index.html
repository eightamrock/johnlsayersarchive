GCR222 August 2007#6
Try This... I'll explain what I did & Why in a bit in more detail... Gotta Go! The short of it its, you have French Drains around the perimeter of the space, with obvious moisture problems. You don't want to build on top of that system and you'll need to ad a vapor barrier at you new construction. The layer of joist is all PT, the sheathing, then VB, then your floor construction, bring the new height of the floor up to the same level as the door.
