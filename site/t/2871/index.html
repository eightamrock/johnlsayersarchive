Spray Foam Insulation?

Started by swolstrup on 27 December 2004. 4 replies, 2004–2015.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 2871.

swolstrup27 December 2004#1
My drummer and I are building a studio out of an addition room in his house. We've got it stripped down to the studs and we're ready top start building. Before we do though, I wanted to find out the general opinion on spray foam insulation for the walls. Is it more effective than standard insulation? Is there something better in the same pricing ballpark? Any feedback would be greatly appreciated.
motown5929 December 2004#2
For heat, yes, For sound no. All of that stuff I've seen is closed cell. like styrofoam. You're still better off with rigid insulation and even the bats. Bats are cheap...and you get to itch & breathe the fibers too!
knightfly30 December 2004#3
Motown's got you covered; if ya can't BLOW through it, don't use it for sound stuff either IN or OUT of a wall... Steve
