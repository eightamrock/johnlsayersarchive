Agree with Glenn! :) To get a rough ball-park number on what it will cost: find the price for building or renovating a house in your area, per square meter. It will cost you about the same to build your studio, and probably a bit more. You say you have about 20 M^2 of space and about US$ 10,000 budget. That implies about US$ 500 per square meter. If that's roughly what it costs to build or renovate a house where you live, then that will most likely be fine for your studio. - Stuart -
