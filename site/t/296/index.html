What do I look for?
Solid and cheap. :D In my experience speaker stands don't make much difference in the sound unless they're very poorly made. I recall seeing somewhere on Homerecording.com a design that used PVC pipe filled with sand and capped off with plywood. The pipe was connected to the plywood using toilet flanges. A simple, sturdy and cheap design. Here it is. http://homerecording.com/bbs/showthread ... let+flange Thomas
