Hi All, I represent a ministry that makes great use of live bands, we have a warehouse that contains a stage, large PA and video screen along with space for around 500 people. We are in the process of adding the appropriate tools for archiving the performances for later broadcast as well as producing DVD's. We are therefore in need of a sound proof control room that would be used for A/V production during the performances (note there is a separate FOH booth outside the structure already) and post production. This structure would be built entirely inside our warehouse as a room within a room. The warehouse has a 14 foot ceiling. I have provided a first attempt at a design based on what I've been able to find whilst googling. Please note that although the design of the walls can be changed in any fashion, due to other restrictions, the size of the structure needs to stay somewhat similar. Sagely advice, suggestions are most appriciated.
