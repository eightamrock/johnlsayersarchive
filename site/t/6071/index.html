"Gobos" or "go betweens" are used to "go between" and separate microphones, instruments, persons, and various noises from other various noises (as in: microphones from noisy computers in one room studios.) :wink: They usually have at least one absorptive surface and are somewhat portable to be positioned where some level of separation is needed.
