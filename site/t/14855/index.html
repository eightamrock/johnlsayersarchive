gullfo27 September 2010#2
for now i would build a "doorway plug" to fit into the arch and close it. then i'd face my desk into that end with proper bass trapping on the vertical and wall-ceiling corners, plus several clouds across the ceiling. light coming in the window shouldn't be a problem and you could just use shear curtains to knock of the very high frequency which might bounce off it. the doorway plug could be 4x layers of 1200mmx2400mm 5/8" drywall screwed to the wall in several places and caulk to enable you to seal around it. maybe back it (inside archway) with some cloth covered pink insulation to dampen it. later you can replace it with a door when you need to make it a vocal booth. the plywood is there to hold the shape, the drywall adds the necessary mass.
