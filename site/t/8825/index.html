Eric_Desart23 May 2007#2
Nightmusic, The first question is what is that original wall? Too often the mistake is made that one adds isolation to an existing situation, where the existing isolation is interpreted as a constant. It isn't. From the moment you add something you create a new system where the existing situation makes part of. If I mount a reslient gypsumboard in front of a heavy brick wall at a small distance, I will degrade low frequent isolation of that heavy brick wall and upgrade mid and high frequent isolation. Hence I can not add the value of that gypsum board (not even partly) to this heavy brick wall. I created something new listening to its own physics. You have to avoid triple leaf systems. A search in the forum will show a lot of related topics. Analysis of the NRC/IRC documents, and data collected and measured by Brian Ravnaas shows that you get the best overall isolation for music, by using traditional low density mineral wool filling in double leaf wall systems. Hence use the traditional boards or blankets traditionally sold for that purpose. Also other studies show that. I admit there is some slight contradictary infomation available, but in general one can state that no study advices to increase density for low frequency insulation (which is the defining factor) and the previous paragraph confirms the negative effect of higher densities on low frequent isolation.
