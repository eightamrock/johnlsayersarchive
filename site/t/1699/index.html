knightfly27 May 2004#4
Dave, most RC manufacturers don't recommend more than two layers on their RC for ceilings - and acoustically, traps don't belong on resilient surfaces - if you can put RC on only one side, and the traps on the other, then the only drawback is the STC hit you will take when putting ANY holes in a sound wall. Probably the least obtrusive thing would be to mount the traps through the drywall and into the STUDS, and only from the non-RC side. Also, isolation will suffer a small amount wherever traps are located against walls - it's the TANSTAAFL principle, anything you "trap" that doesn't get back into the room, has to go somewhere... best way to handle this is an extra layer of mass in one of the leaves of your 2-leaf wall to compensate for the slight weakening caused by the trap. Speaking of ceilings, what did you decide on? can you post a pic showing what you've done with your flimsy roof framing and how you're planning on doing your ceilings now?
