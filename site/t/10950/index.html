This is my first post in this forum and I'd like to say a big thanks to the people involved in this site for all this information they provide. Also, my english are not that good, my apologies. Me and my brother (we are from Crete, an island in Greece) decided to invest our savings to raise a 4-floor building with appartments to rent, including a 2-floor garage for parking space to rent. I play semi-professionally drums and I always thought of having my own personal recording studio for me, my band and other musicians. I can't afford to buy or rent another property for that, so I decided to sacrifice parking space from the garage to build it. Images of the underground garage with that space allocated.

External image, not preserved — original: http://www.marirena.gr/songs/garaz_final2.jpg
My budget for this project will be about 20000euros or 30000$ The following attachment contains a .skp file I created according to the real plans of the garage (it's my first drawing in Sketchup 6.4, sorry about that, dimensions are pretty close though) and shows the space available to build my studio. The area painted in wood is that space. I've included dimensions (in cm) of that particular space. Keep in mind that there is an identical garage floor right above this one and after that we have the appartment floors. We are underground, so in every other direction we will meet mother Earth. The rest of the garage space will be open or closed parking spaces. Now, let's talk about designing the studio. I need the following rooms: Control Live Vocals Drums Kitchen/Lounge Toilet The area I have available for the studio is quite big (the selected area is about 95m2) but very difficult to work on due to its non-symmetrical shape. It's very long and narrow (7m the biggest width, about 5m the shortest width and 16m the length, height is the same for the whole floor, about 3m). I just need some starter-pointers for sqeezing all these rooms in that space. A rough placement, nothing fancy, just to get me started. If you require more information of this projetc, I will gladly provide you with it. Thank you for your patience for reading this long post and thank you in advance for your time helping me with this design.
