I'm currently in a makeshift control room on a stage in an old vestry hall. I have decided that I want to share the hall with the community as it's way too big for my projects.... it can still be my big live room, but also a multifunction / performance space! I'm in discussion with my architect about building a steel mezzanine gallery structure, with concrete floor, above the hall's entrance (utilizing the loft space above false ceiling) with entrance onto it from another room (through wall). This could house a decent control room and recording booths. Before we get into nitty gritty details I'd really appreciate your views on here with regard to wall systems / materials and layout. The floor dimensions for the proposed mezzanine is 10m (W) x 3.40m (L). This is based within the A frames in the hall (between windows)... I could possibly push it to 4m (L)... but this will then put the A frame within the proposed rooms (in a decorative sense). Get it? Height: If the mezzanine is at 3m from hall floor, this leaves about 4m to pitch... so proposed control room / recording booth will have irregular ceilings. Not sure on exact dimensions... need to go into loft to measure. The entrance onto mezzanine would be from next door, through a 2ft granite wall. As it stands, I would do a corridor here, seperating two rooms for sound isolation. But as I only have roughly 3.4m to play with... I'm unsure about layout of c.room. Current thinking: A. Is it a good idea? B. How can I make the most of sound isolation from hall? Need to make the space as multifuction as possible. C. How should I layout the c.room with regard to view into booths. + where would door to c.room be? D. Should I expand the 3.4m? Remember, there are windows in the hall... so need it to look ok too! Really appreciate your help. (1st pic of elevation: I imagine the control room in one room, and booth in other, seperated by corridor...)
