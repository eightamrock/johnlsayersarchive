blujemz12 April 2004#1
I live in a brownstone in Brooklyn and I would like to build a small project studio in the basement. Does anyone know where I kind find the City's laws concerning this type of venture?
AndrewMc13 April 2004#2
Typically you could go to the building code / zone enforcement office. For construction you need a building permit For a commercial situation then you need to ensure the area is zoned for a business. If it's a pure hobby then you will not need to check zoning. Now, having said that - the other option is to build and don't tell anybody. Upside - a ton less hassle, downside - if you get caught then multiply the hassle you avoided by 10x and maybe have to tear it out. If the area is not zoned for a business - then say its a hobby and then make sure you get paid in cash.
