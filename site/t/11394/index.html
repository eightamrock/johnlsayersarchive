Soundman202017 November 2008#2
If I understand you right, that would mean that you are creating a three-leaf wall, which is not good. Far better to put that sheetrock in between the joists of that floor above you, cut to fit and pressed up tightly against the sub-floor, sealed all around the edges with plenty of caulk, and held in place safely with good cleats. If you can get two or three layers of sheetrock in there, plus another couple of layers of sheetrock on your booth ceiling, and plenty of insulation in the air gap (fiberglass or mineral wool), then you should be fine. However, you should check if your joists can safely take the extra weight of a couple of layers of sheetrock: that's a lot of mass! (Which is the point: you WANT lots of mass up there, but you need to make sure that the joists can handle it.) But you should definitely avoid creating three leaves if you possibly can. You want two leaves, and only two. - Stuart -
