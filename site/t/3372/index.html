Hello guys, my name is Michael from Berlin (germany) and I´m building my home studio in the basement at the house I´m living in, see the pics please. I started whith one of johns designs and have finished the room dividing walls already, doors and window included. My room dimensions are: control room: 10.1 feet width 12.5 feet in length 7.5 feet ceiling record room/vocal booth 10.1 feet width 6.5 feet long 7.5 feet ceiling I´m also right now building my soffit´s where I want to put dynaudio bm6a´s in it. I will use metall perforated whith 4" rockwool insulation between original ceiling and acoustical ceiling. This would give me also a good absorbing effect in the upper mids and bass or? Is this enough? More is not possible cause of the ceiling height. This is enough for now. I try to post alot of pics... thx ms-scope

here are some more pics of my finished ceiling and the broadband panel absorbers I made for the control room. I have to do some mixing jobs right now and therefore I placed them in the corners and on the side walls to have a better acoustic for these jobs.
