gullfo16 February 2008#14
the control room could remain rectangular with treatments flat on the walls and ceilings, or you can build angled treatment "walls" with slats, porous absorption etc.. you could leave the vox booth as-is and add the extra booths. i would treat the vox booth for mids using slats and overhead absorption with the option to hang additional absorbers to adjust it. in the room designated for bass or drums, i'd make panel traps on the walls and absorption overhead w/ same option for additional absorbers like vox room.
