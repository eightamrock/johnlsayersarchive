billshoup23 May 2007#1
Hello All, My second post. Originally I planned to use an odd shaped room for my basement studio, but after more thought, my wife and I are considering attempting to add a room (in a different space in our basement) that could possibly be considered a bedroom when we sell the house. If you'd like you are welcome to check out my previous post here. After thinking about all the musical situations I get involved in, I thought it might be good to have a little more room. I will go over the basics again here: Budget I would like to stay around $2,000 but I realize this may not be possible and if not, I'm willing to go beyond that, hopefully $4,000 would be reasonable cap. I do plan to do most of the work myself. Recording Environmet/Loudness I plan to build a combination room, as I mainly work alone. I currently record much of my stuff direct and/or MIDI. Currently my drums are MIDI. The instruments I generally record acoustically include congas, bongos, shakers, cabasa, mandolin (rarely), acoustic guitar (generally in combination with the pickup & Fishman Aura), mic’d guitar amp for electric guitar (although many times I go directly from an effectrs processor) and of course vocals (generally close mic’d). I’m hoping that when I’m done, the room will sound good enough as to inspire me to record more things acoustically. :) Although I play a reasonably large variety of music, most of my stuff could likely be best described as James Taylor-like. In regard to loudness, I cranked my studio monitors a touch louder than I would expect I would ever run them, and measured about 92 db in the listening position. My recording set up is pretty lightweight compared with most folks on this forum. Alesis Studio 32 board feeding Sonar PC setup via an Echo Mia Midi (audio & midi) card, and a set of Behringer Truth B2031A active monitors. Goals My goal is to create a space that I can quietly record in, while not bothering the rest of the household. I have currently been recording in the basement, although not in the exact space where I'm now planning to build the room. The issues I currently have in this space that I want to resolve as a part of this project are: 1) Noise from the utilities (furnace, water heater, water softener), the fan in my PC, and floor noise from upstairs being picked up excessively by my large condenser mic. I am currently thinking I would like to locate my CPU outside the room to remove that part of the problem. 2) Stop sound (notice it’s no longer “noise” :wink: ) from my recording/mixing from disturbing the rest of the house. My family is very supportive and have not complained, but I’ve noticed many times my wife moving to the bedroom to watch tv, rather than staying in the living room where it’s obvious my music heavily bleeds into. When I had my monitors running at 92 db, the reading in the living room (directly above) was around 72 db. My perception is that much of the sound was coming through the floor through the openings for the heat vents into the room from below. I should mention that the basement is on one end of our house, we have no backyard neighbors, and the closest neighbor on the “basement end” is 75 feet away. Although I have not taken db readings outside, I’ve never had any complaints, and when I go outside and listen with music loudly playing, even now, it doesn’t appear to be a problem, so I’m not concerned about reducing the sound leakage to the outside world. 3) The space is currently almost always chilly, so it should be much easier to quickly warm with the space enclosed and insulated. 4) Of course I’m hoping to ultimately have a much better space acoustically in which to work. Space Description The space I'm looking at is in the basement of our multi-level home. The space is currently unfinished, and mostly concrete walls. I’ve provided a sketchup file trying my best to describe the unfinished area and that is currently in place. Above this area is a living room with carpet on the floor. The living room has a 13 foot ceiling (not that I believe that has any bearing on anything). There is plywood above the floor joists. The joists as you can probably tell, are 2 X 10, 16” OC. There is plenty of existing ductwork and PVC pipe for the heating & cooling system as well as to vent the water heater to get in the way. I should mention that on the other side of the angled stud wall are the stairs that go up to the family room, which sits atop the half concrete wall (behind the furnace). Construction Plans for Isolation I hope to beef up the ceiling by adding two pieces of 5/8” sheetrock between the existing floor joists. Then R-13 insulation, then RC-2, then 2 more layers of 5/8” sheetrock. I haven’t contacted a structural engineer yet (although I'm in the process) but I’m assuming I will probably need to attempt to add another new set of joists sitting on top the new walls which will be tricky with all the ductwork, etc. For the walls, I’m looking at 2 layers of 5/8” sheetrock on the outside, 2 X 4 framing, filled with R-13 insulation, RC-1 on the inside with two more sheets of 5/8” sheet rock. Closet Possibilities As I said, my wife finds the idea of building this room much more palatable if we can build in a “closet” so that when we sell the house, this room could be considered another bedroom, so I have included a couple of drawings suggesting a couple of possible layouts for this space including a closet. Feel free to tell me I’m nuts! Questions I’m basically looking for reactions to this room shape and possible layout suggestions. I feel like I have a pretty good handle on the “construction for isolation” aspect of the project, but am not nearly as comfortable with how to make this room shape work as well as possible for a combination room acoustically, so would truly appreciate any advice or suggestions. I am especially interested in where you would suggest locating the monitors, etc. Of course at this point I'm mainly interested in the basic functionality of the room, but would definitely welcome any input in regard to room treatment as well. Please let me know if there are any pictures that you would find helpful in picturing this space and it’s possibilities. Thanks so much for any help you are willing to give me! Oh-oh, I worked on this sketch all day, and now it's too big to upload to the sight, so I gues I'll just have to do a jpg of it. I hope you can see everything ok!
