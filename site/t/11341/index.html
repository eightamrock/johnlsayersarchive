xSpace25 October 2008#5
If you move forward with this idea, might want to fill the open space between the two speakers. Sound will travel at a rapid rate around the edges of the "rack" and go right into the void area behind them. Hell, it may sound good ;) But I'm thinking you are going to have a big hole in your sound at the listening position. Sides of this rack will work the same way but may not have as negative an impact on your listening. Sound is going to move into the area behind the racks and fill that place up, reflecting or getting absorbed(however you have or will treat this part) so I am thinking it has too many negatives (as it is currently)to produce a critical monitoring area.
Soundman202025 October 2008#6
I was thinking more or less the same as xSpace: that gap in the middle doesn't seem like a good idea. Also, it looks like your speakers are pretty high up in the soffits: I think you really should angle the upper portion of the soffits downwards slightly, so that the speakers are also pointing downwards, towards the mix position. Either that, or position the speakers much lower, with the tweeters at ear height. One final comment: Your angles don't look right. It's hard to be sure from the pictures you posted, but it doesn't look like you have 60 degree angles, creating an equilateral triangle between the speakers and your head. Did you check that? - Stuart -
