gullfo5 June 2011#30
it's always a struggle to find the right spot - shoot for best LF response and imaging. when in doubt imaging is likely to matter more for most people. you could trim the channel to compensate - the key is that your mixes translate so you might set up a process where you can have some friends check them periodically - plus use some tools (SPAN or equiv is good) to look at the overall levels across your final mix and consider making a "mix" of noise where you compensate on the monitors and then adjust the l-r levels in various steps and see (using the tools) to see if you are balanced in the mix even though the monitoring is skewed...
