One problem that caught my eye was the rear of the control room that features a 90 degree corner. You will probably experience lots of buildup in that corner unless you plan to put a bass trap there. The same goes for the live room. Just my two n00b cents...

gullfo8 June 2007#6
the corners are opportunities to put in some nice broadband trapping to get the room modes under control. I'd make the traps as large as necessary to get the low end nice and tight. the back corner of the CR is a good spot for a large trap. same for the live room plus some longer slat resonators on the "smaller" corners. iso room the same. overhead, if you have the space in the CR - a false ceiling with trapping to give your a reflection free zone. on top of the rear CR trap - diffusion to keep things alive without strong reflections. same for live room - and add some changeable absorber/reflector-diffusers along the walls so you can alter the room characteristics as needed.
