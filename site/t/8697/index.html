I started taking the insulation from my walls today for the ol' beef up party. I've got nails sticking out of my outdoor wall sheathing. Is anyone familiar with how siding is installed and if large nails like this are used to secure it? Has anyone else run into this problem with nails pertruding from a sheathing WALL? sharward28 April 2007#2
Looks to me from the close-up shot (and your testimony of being able to push some of them through really easily) that it could be two things, each related:
  • Nails intended to secure the OSB shear wall sheathing to the studs
  • Nails intended to secure siding to the studs
...but the framer "missed" the studs a few times. I dealt with some of those myself. The nailing pattern for shear wall sheathing is intense -- something like one nail every two inches or so. Zapping a mile-a-minute with a pneumatic nailer, it's bound to happen. Ordinarily I would not advocate grinding, cutting, or pushing through nails that are protruding like that. However, if I am right about my assumption, I think you may be able to do so safely in this case. If you have horizontal clapboard or cementboard siding, I imagine some of the sheathing nails will encounter some resistance at some point when you push on them, some moreso than others, depending on how closethe nail is to the overlap, whereas the siding nails may actually come out of the exterior of the house and would need to be pulled from the outside. Confirm my assertion, then lightly tap all of them with a nail until they are clear or you encounter resistance. Then use a grinder bit on your drill to grind off any remaining protrusions. Be sure to use eye protection! Sparks are gonna fly! 8) In the case of the nails attaching siding, you may want to drive a new nail outside into the stud. --Keith :mrgreen:

sharward29 April 2007#4
OK -- in light of the new information (I didn't know the siding was vinyl -- yes, those nails are indeed inaccessible and need not to be driven all the way), scratch all my earlier advice. Plan on using foam as per Rod's recommendation on these matters, but make one adjustment. Instead of planning to push the nails into the foam, drill holes on the foam where the nails would penetrate it. Even very large, oversized holes won't be a problem: a small pocket of air around a handful of nails in this wimpy mass layer shouldn't affect you transmission loss much or at all. You are wise to be concerned over pushing these vinyl siding nails through. --Keith :mrgreen:
