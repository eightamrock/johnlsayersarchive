gullfo5 November 2007#2
one option - place the monitors on stands right behind your desk and reduce the width and angle of their positions (say 15-20 degrees instead of 30) and make the listening triangle size based on that. another option, this may be one of the rare cases where you turn the CR sideways and deaden the "back" wall quite a bit (not fully) this way you can get symmetry to your layout and also the side reflections are longer even if the front-back is now shorter.
Ethan Winer5 November 2007#3
sevenways wrote:
I can't place my monitors in symmerically in my control room.
Actually, you probably can. In a room 15 feet long, the ideal place for you to listen is about 5-6 feet back from the front wall you face. Once you're that far back you might be able to be centered left and right and still have room for the door to open and close?
some treatments idea for control room. And for the recording room should I place foam in everywhere in the walls for recording vocals?
There's plenty of treatment and placement advice all over this forum. There's also my Acoustics FAQ which puts much of it all in one place: http://www.ethanwiner.com/acoustics.html --Ethan
