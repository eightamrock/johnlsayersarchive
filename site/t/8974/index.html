A. Foam/Glass wool about 1.56 cm, min 5cm thickness. Empty Space behind A can be filled with absorber material or just empty or put PVC, height 1.5m from floor (c)
Generally speaking, most of the acoustic treatments prescribed here call for either 2"-3" semi rigid mineral wool/rock wool or fiberglass, what ever of which is available in your area. Foam based materials are NOT usually suggested.
3. Kendal, how thick the "new" acoustic wall and how's the construction?

4. Which one is absorber and which one diffusor and which one the bass trap...?
The rear wall is the absorber, the angled sidewalls are slot resonators and the bass trap is in the rear ceiling/wall area. In a room your size, I would not recommend diffusers. Depending on the results of closing off one of the doorways you mentioned, if you can fit in more bass trapping, that would work to your benefit.
