construction has started in my basement, i ended up using the sonar studio design from this site. but anywho, one wall is single walled, and the other one is double walled with 2x3 studs, now i have read somwhere about leaves of a wall, and if you have 3 of them its bad? but 4 or 2 are good? what exactly is a leaf, and what about having 3 being bad? so would a drywall/insulation/soundboard/airspace/insulation/drywall wall be bad?
giles1177 July 2004#2
Yes that would be bad. a leaf is one composite wall of solid materials. ex. drywall/soundboard/celotex/frywall is one leaf that would then be attached to studs. Basically the studs just old the leaf up. On that double sided wall, just remove the drywall off of the inside leaf leaving the outer leaf in tact. so to modify your problematic design, just place the soundboard underneath or on top of the drywall layer. to read like this drywall/soundboard/insulation/airspace/insulation/drywall Bryan Giles
