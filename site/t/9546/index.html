Hello Valentin If the LSR6332 speaker enclosure is completely sealed and flush mounted in a baffle, then anechoic response will rise a total of 6dB with decreasing frequency. The rise may start as high as 700 Hz, and the peak may be somewhere between 150Hz and 300 Hz when measured in an anechoic chamber. However in your room, if you EQ the speaker to be flat, the speaker may sound thin because the in-room response may require adjustment to achieve a good balance of high, mid and bass frequency response. Therefore, it is best to perform an in-room measurement and adjust the EQ as needed. At the same time you can adjust for other balance and room modes issues. Regards, Peter Peter Chaikin
