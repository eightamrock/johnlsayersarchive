Ro29 October 2007#2
Hai Philippe, Acoustically you don't have to angle the 2 panes of glass. Visually it'll be an improvement on mirroring issues. Why glue? Visit your local glass dealer and ask for laminated glass (which is actually 2 panes "glued" together already). The thicker the better. Don't make it your weakspot in the wall. Glass is about 3 times as heavy as gypsum, that'll help you calculate the minimum needed width.
