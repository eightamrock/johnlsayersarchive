Aloha Kelly, Hey, how's it going?
Is 4" rockwool okay for a slot resonator wall? Is 2" rockwool better? or Does it matter?
I know there's a post covering this somewhere but anyway, 4" is fine if the price suits your budget. What you want to keep in mind is the density of your RW. Here's what was suggested when I first started out.
What you need to ask for is either rigid fiberglass, such as Owens Corning 703 (un-faced) or rockwool, mineral wool, somewhere between 2 to 4 pounds per cubic foot density; Johns Manville and Knauf also make similar products. Steve
I ended up ordering 7 bags of 3" X 24" X 48" Roxul AFB 2.5 PCF @ $53.76 /BAG 8 PC/BAG from AMS in Honolulu. Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
