Gerry, I know exactly what you man about the costs pf imports to the islands...... it's insane. I designed the new "H" room that a company called Hit Productions just opened in the Philippines, and the imports cost them a ton of bread. I have to tell you that the Hat Section (and yes that is what the picture is of) is pretty much useless for isolation by itself - great when coupled with RISC clips - but useless by itself. But then again - in comparison to double wall construction - so isn't RC-1 - so I have to ask you - why aren't you just building nice neat isolated walls and not taking the chance of shorts with things like resilient channels? You would be so much happier in the end with your results. Rod

I don't know if you saw that Rod also mentioned that even if you could get proper resilient channel where you are, it still would not be worth it: It won't help you at all, in your case, since you said you are already building double-stud walls. Putting RC on top of that would be extra time, money and effort for no reason. If you build double-stud walls properly, then you already have things about as decoupled as you can get, so adding RC would be pointless. RC is only useful where you have no choice but to use singles stud walls, or on ceilings where your only option is existing joists with a floor right on top, and cases like that. Just my two cents worth! - Stuart -
