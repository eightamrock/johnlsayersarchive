jwl31 December 2007#5
I'm a big fan of moveable acoustic panels. Absorptive gobos can be very effective in creating a RFZ. The one thing to keep in mind is that gobos generally will not absorb bass frequencies. Ultimately, gobos are designed to block sound. They absorb down to a certain point, then once the wavelength is larger than the size of the gobo, the sound will tend to just go around the gobo. Absorbers, otoh, work since sound passes through them. So definitely get some sort of bass trapping strategy happening, then use the gobos to create a RFZ when mixing, or to put in strategic places while tracking. This is an ideal setup for one-room studios, it gives you a lot of flexibility to deal with different situations.
