Chaz14 November 2003#32
Personally.... I would advise you against soffit-mounting your LSR28s. Nothing against you Giles or anyone else, but I am talking from experience of using the same LSRs in our studio here for quite a while now. Our LSR28s are free-standing and we are getting very tight and accurate mixes from them that way (demo and commercial release mixes). I am not so sure that that would be the case if they were soffit-mounted; especially since they are rear-ported monitors. I know sjoko has his LSR28s soffit- mounted, but again, I am not personally for soffit-mounting this particular monitor or any other rear-ported monitor for that matter. Just my 2 cents. 8)
