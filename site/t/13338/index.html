Hi there, I am new to the forum and after searching now for about 2 hours cant find a definite answer for a question I had. (although I found plenty of other answers for many other questions!) I am planning to do a full post with proposed layout etc etc but this is just a very quick question... I have come across a very cheap source of 16mm MDF (cheaper than GIB) would I be able to use this as a first layer for my interior walls and then put a layer of GIB over that? Would that be ok or does MDF have some sort of bad resonant quality? I did see in one build someone planned to use GIB-MDF-GIB and John said that was excellent? if so I might go for that as the MDF is only about NZ$14 per sheet 2440 x 1220 x 16mm I am building on to a concrete slab (and dont plan to float floors) and my main concern is isolation of drums from the exterior world. Thanks for any help, I have a very limited time frame to pick up this deal and do understand the correct procedures for posting here and I promise to make an official start with plans etc etc when I have time to do so! Dave
John Sayers28 July 2009#2
Yes - you can use the MDF Dave - treat it like plaster by sealing it before you add the plaster on top.

I used OSB in my room but would have to think about using MDF in any kind of wall that has one side exposured to the exterior. OSB can take the deterioration but I wouldn't want MDF as a base layer, in a situation like that.
