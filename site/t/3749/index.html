knightfly26 May 2005#4
Hey Sam, if you read that SPECIFIC link (just the first post is fine) you'll see that we need things like your general location (you need to edit your profile to include this), along with as specific details as you can give; you don't mention whether the blocks are solid or hollow, what the thickness is for the chipboard, plaster, etc - Everything, and I do mean EVERYTHING, in that first post is IMPORTANT if I'm to figure out how to help you - if I were psychic, I would have already won a huge lottery and would be in my own multi-million $tudio instead of trying to find time to work on my own projects. All through this board, I've made reference to "2-leaf walls", "mass-air-mass" construction, etc - the REFERENCE section's first link covers that pretty thoroughly. I bring this up because your construction has chipboard and cladding (one leaf) air(insulation), 200mm block (at least one leaf, more if it's hollow block) , insulation (air), 100mm block (yet another leaf), insulation, and plaster board (still ANOTHER leaf) - Each combination of mass-air-mass as sound travels through the wall will have its own resonant frequency, plus the resonant product of all those separate resonances; at each resonance, the wall will be weak. About the only range of frequencies that might be blocked better with this construction would be midrange and highs; for lows (drums) you want a simple but effective 2-leaf wall system, with as much mass and space between masses as you can get. Please, study what's here some more; and if you've not already built that room, please DON'T... Steve
