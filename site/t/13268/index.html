xSpace7 August 2009#7
"The roof is pitched, with concrete tiles.... The ceilings are being removed; this is in anticipation of installing a vaulted ceiling." These modifications you are doing to the ceiling/roof, is this with the help of a professional or are you simply pulling out boards to do what you want? A tile roof is heavy by design, the framing is more and the tiles weigh more then a standard asphalt roofing system. Buildings that are up to >code< are not necessarily overbuilt. Far from it. Meeting code is the equivalent of, if this were a school grading system, getting a "C". So anything that takes away from this effectively reduces the grade. Remove enough or add too much and you could be graded an "F". And an F is the same in all countries, failure. In this case structural failure. The "anticipated vaulted ceiling" which will require more framing/weight on the existing walls comes to mind. Even if you do not add additional framing, and for this to be correctly built you will have to, the additional sheetrock alone, with the deleted joists that at one time tied the walls together and maintained the roof against external environmental conditions will be an issue, if you have not taken this whole structure into consideration.
