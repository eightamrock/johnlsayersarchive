greetings fellow designing-studio-in-illustrator guy. :D if you put your location in your profile, that's better because it will always be there for reference. if this thread goes on to the next page, nobody will remember where you said you are. goofy picture optional. i'm in somewhat of the same spot as you in the research/plan stages, so i know that one of the first things that will be asked is exactly what is the construction of your existing walls, ceiling and floor. very importantly, how high is the existing ceiling. Vivid12 May 2005#11
I pay drums as well. I just built a complete live room for about 1500 bucks total. I'm actually just put the carpet in over the weekend. I live in a brand new subdivision, so I was worried about sound. I didnt float a floor. I sealed the floor and put cheap carpet with the pad that is already attached down. Menards or HD has it. Works great. I built the walls about a 8 inches ot a foot out from the concrete wall. I used R19 insulation all around. I used a mix of R19/R13 on the cealing behind the drywall. (R13 because of the duct work clearance) I screwed the drywall right to the floor joists. The window well is insulated as well. I just used a mix of things and sealed it up tight. I dont have any acoustic foam yet so I put som large moving blankets on the walls and cealing. I had my wife go outside and listen as I beat the heck out of the drums. She could bearly hear anything. Even right outside the window well. She then stood in the room above the live room. Not to bad. No China rattled nothing. I wanted to build a room on a small budget. I did just that. Once I get the bass traps and other things to fill up the room, the sound should deaden even more. You dont HAVE to spend thousands on a live room. Our neighbor down the street from us built a live room for his band. He spent about 6k on it. Used all those fancy clips for hanging drywall on the tracks, expensive cauk, and so on. It literally sounds the same as my room. There is no difference except he spent a ton of dough and I didnt.
