xSpace1 November 2009#6
Actually, what John showed you is exactly like your room. The difference is that there are knee walls already installed [Johns link] so that you cannot see the "wall", or rafter meeting to the floor and creating that large triangle. It is a good layout that you should not dismiss so quickly, reason being is that all those tight angled corners are going to be going away in your build, you just are not aware of it yet. When they do go away it will be by means of shorter walls [knee walls] and boundaries installed into the space you are considering building in.
