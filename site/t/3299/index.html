knightfly26 February 2005#2
You can put anything you want behind cloth, and it will look the same (if the cloth isn't too open-weave :=) - if you look at most of John's CR designs, you will see some areas with slat resonators, some with just semi-rigid insulation, etc - if you like the look of natural wood slats, the resonators can be left open. IF not, your favorite cloth (treated for fire retardance of course) will cover anything. Some rooms may have several FEET of space behind the cloth, like most Newell or Hidley-designed "non-environment" rooms. Have a look around the Design forum, you'll see what I mean; then click the John Sayers Productions logo at top right, click on Wall Units, for some DIY ideas - again, remember ANYTHING can be disguised with cloth stretched over an open frame... Steve

knightfly27 February 2005#5
Andre's got it - although, in a veeeerrry picky way you might notice a SLIGHT decrease in brightness with some cloth coverings over a slat resonator - just a bit of "sheen" off the top end. All in all, not a bad thing; if it's bothering you, and your speakers have a tweeter adjust, increase it by a dB or two... Steve BTW, if you spray your cloth with fire retardant it will brighten the sound a bit so it's pretty much a moot point...
