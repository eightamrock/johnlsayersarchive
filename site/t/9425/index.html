gullfo21 September 2007#8
carpeting the walls is bad for a couple of reasons - one acoustics, two safety. its a very tight space not matter what... you could try things sideways as a possible means of fitting things. making the back wall very dead. if nothing else, try to angle the dividing wall or plan on extra absorbers to cut echos.

Sigert21 September 2007#9
carpeting the walls is bad for a couple of reasons - one acoustics, two safety.
No knowledge bout the safety part, but acoustic-wise carpet is indeed not a very good idea, as it only takes on the highest frequencies and gives a room a "claustrofobic" sounding feel.
gullfo21 September 2007#10
generally, carpet on the walls is a fire hazard unless specially treated.
