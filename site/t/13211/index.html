John Sayers25 June 2009#4
simon - before you add the cloth finish to the cloud make sure you wrap it in plastic otherwise every morning you will have a micro fine layer of dust on all your gear and console caused by the rockwool shedding and getting through the weave in the fabric.

thanks for the reply! i read about that problem in this forum (probably one of your posts) and bought a fleece for that purpose. but probably i should use some really thin plastic foil too... should i angle the cloud than because of the (high frequency) reflections caused by the plastic? thanxs a lot simon
John Sayers26 June 2009#6
you'll find the cloth stops too much high freq reflection.
