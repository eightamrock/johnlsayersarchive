This is my attempt to better my acoustical sound in the overall room for mixing and to build a vocal booth. I what to start by saying, the shape of this room has been a real challenge but I've made the best of it with 2'x4' bass traps. The walls are standard double leaf with cheap isolation if any. Some questions of mine: -Is my desk in the best place for mixing? -Would it be a good idea to have a 4x6x7 booth in front of the desk where the mic is? -I have no ceiling traps but the ceiling has that cheesy popcorn texture, I that enough for sound control?

gullfo18 February 2011#3
i would center the desk on the window, absorber the wall (then) on the back-right hand side, space the couch out from the closets and square on to the desk. then angled absorbers on either side of the desk to give a bit of symmetry, the back of the the angled absorber could help enclose the "vox" booth.

gullfo18 February 2011#5
something like that except i wouldn't put an actual booth there, just the angled absorbers and just use that corner (with more absorbers as your "booth", if you actually do need a booth, then i would put the booth opposite the wall that juts out (basically between the windows) then turn the desk to face the wall to the left and treat first reflections etc. you might have to turn to see someone in the booth and for mixing you can stand up an absorber to control reflections off the booth.
