No, you wouldn't be able to find pricing on the internet - it comes in sheets from JM distributors, and you'd need to get a quote from them. You fabricate the ducts from the sheets, so price would depend if you're fabricating yourself, or paying someone to do it. Don't know if it'll help, but in my little corner of the US, I recently received a quote from a local distributor for $60/sheet (4' x 10' x 1"). To fabricate the sheets you might also need tape, cutting tool, collar, etc.
