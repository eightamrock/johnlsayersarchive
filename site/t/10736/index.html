Preludetojune wrote:
Hi guys, i would like to clear something up, i have built my studio using a single brick wall air gap single brick wall, hence a cavity wall (i did not put any insulation in the cavity), it is supposed to work great in aiding isolation better than a normal double brick wall, no 1: is this true?
I don't know what the question is. Your double brick wall will give much better isolation than your roof. If you made the walls with more sound isolation, you would not hear any difference because the sound would still be coming through the weakest link (the roof). The isolation you have from the walls resembles what is in the attached graph.
no 2: is it going to create a big problem with acoustics or not in the room
It will have no effect on the room acoustics.
