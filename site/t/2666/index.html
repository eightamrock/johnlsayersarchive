dymaxian16 November 2004#2
Greetings and welcome! If you're just starting out, and have a lot of time to study, take a look at the Recording Manual (get there from the John Sayers productions link at the top right). When you get thru that, register yourself over at recording.org and read everything you can- they have a "newbie" area over there that will do you justice. To answer your question, the gear and the space will kinda need to happen at the same time- the room won't do you much good without the gear, and vice versa. But if you have a bedroom and an instrument, you can at least get going on the experimenting/learning end. Good luck!
