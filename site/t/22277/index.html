Gregwor17 February 2020#2
Welcome!
Should I hang the Absorbers on the wall, so that the have equal distance from floor and ceiling?
I would put them at ear and/or microphone height.
Should I add Diffusors?
Your mix position is far enough from the rear wall to allow you to have some, yes.
On the wall facing the monitors I would double the thickness up to 16cm.
Your picture only shows 2 panels. You should build a frame across the entire rear wall and completely fill it. 2 panels won't be enough. Also, I would go as thick as possible.
My goal is to eliminate the flutter echo as well as the bass build up. Cause if I play the Kickdrum right now it just sounds like a big wall of bass echo.
Remember that you have 12 corners in your room. Bass traps in all of them :D Greg
