Flush mount plan

Started by g-slam on 19 January 2011. 6 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 15513.

g-slam19 January 2011#1
Hi Folks, here are my plans for the flush mount for my JBL 4425. Maybe some experienced user has some comment. I want to cover the front with 5cm of Fermacell to have enough mass, and a thin layer of nice wood, for optical reasons. I took the center of the speaker, put it in 1,50m height and angled the whole thing about 10 degrees. Then I will hit exactly the sweet spot in 2,50. Listeners ear position at 1,10m The box for the speaker stands on a pole filled with sand to make it solid. The space behind the speakers I want to fill with Sonorock, to have a superchunk. My questions: Superchunks or Hangers? The area below the speaker: Should I make it refelctive (drywall) with a port for bass absorption, or should I cover it with fabric for better working superchunks`?

gullfo21 January 2011#2
if you have room - hangers. if not, build up the walls of cavity with the rigid insulation and fill the remaining space with pink (light) insulation. make sure the speaker is decoupled from the box - use some sylomer etc to keep it in place but not directly contacting the frame, or soffit front plate.
g-slam21 January 2011#3
Thats exactly my plan. I have some sylomer here, should be enough. I got a recommendation not to angle the speakers, to have less phase shifting when moving my head forward and back. What do you think?
gullfo21 January 2011#4
you could angle them but not too much as your ears are designed to hear a certain way but 8 to 10° as a maximum angle is a good guideline.
