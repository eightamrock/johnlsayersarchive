Hello, I am currently building a basement studio in Atlanta for a client about the same size as your drawing and my 2x4's and sheet rock alone cost more than $1500. Im not trying to be down about your situation. i run into a lot of folks with a small budget. It's just that you might consider treating the room acoustically with that money instead of trying to build a full on studio. i know that is not what you want to hear, it's just really really a small budget. Yes a reg. contractor can build a couple sheetrock walls and put some lights and doors in for that budget but its not a studio. It wont be soundproof or really even stop much sound at all. i try to suggest to my clients that call with that small of a budget to build removable acoustic treatments for now and save money. That way you can still work in the space while trying to save the cash to build a proper studio. If you build now, not building correctly, you will have to tear it all out to rebuild a proper studio later. Or just treat the room in a way that once you build the new room you can take the treatment with you. Sorry for the downer, I know its not what you wanted to hear, but its realistic. There is some great advise here about treatment options. Good luck and remember it's just my 2 cents. Jai "Love the Music in Yourself, Not Yourself in the Music."
