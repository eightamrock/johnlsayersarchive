Thanks www.deltahdesign.com ZR Acoustics [Zero Reflection Acoustics] is a modus operandi for designing audio production spaces with crystal clear acoustics + world class acoustic signatures. Utilizing everyday construction materials, ZR Acoustics renders rooms acoustically invisible, extending the near field environment to the entire air volume of the space. i'd agree they have some nice projects listed but i think this is the case of someone taking the non-environment room model and creating a set of construction tactics which if you where to analyze John's inside-out wall as a porous absorber instead of slats, or Philip Newell's similar designs using a variety of materials to alter the absorption characteristics, you'd probably find you could make a number of similar claims. i guess one concern would be the reflections - if we assume they're correct 1/2 of the sound is reflected on each bounce, we're still seeing things calculated down to 10 reflections... what exactly are these reflections bouncing off of? it would also seem to be a fundamental altering of physics if there were no modes, standing waves, etc... maybe someone from their firm could come on the forum and openly discuss - they're protected by trademarks and patents - so it would probably be good to hear directly how they do it.
