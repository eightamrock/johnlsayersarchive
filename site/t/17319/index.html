Hi Giovanni and Kristian. Welcome. Please check the rules for posting on the forum: there are a couple of things that you are missing. :) One of those is that all photos should be posted directly on the forum.
we have some problems to recreate the symmetry behind the listening position because of the sloping wall and two doors.
Symmetry behind the listening position isn't critical. Symmetry in FRONT of the listening position is absolutely necessary, but the rear half of the room isn't so important, if it is treated well. Of course, if you can get the rear half symmetrical too, then that's great, but from that basic SKP file, it doesn't look like it will be too much of a problem. - Stuart -
