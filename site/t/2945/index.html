jatougas10 January 2005#4
The site uses frames, so it won't lead you directly to it. Hit the 'Fitting Out/Electrics' on the left, then the 'Fittings' tab at the top. They're not measured building schematics, but they give a good idea of what to do... FWIW, standard rack gear screw holes are 18" on center. So, 19" across the face of the rack, 20" or so (depending on how stout you make it) for the full width of the rack.
