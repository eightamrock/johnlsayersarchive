rod gervais15 February 2007#10
Flolks, I've worked with this material and it's what it's portrays itself to be..... Very sound effective in the frequencies that STC are concerned with - BTW - I wouldn't really view that as a 3 leaf system - with a rigid channel on the left hand face - and only a 5/8" air cavity (filled with insulation) - I doubt very much that it would exhibit the characteristics of 3 leaves when finished. Steve, Buddy - I don't agree with that assessment of the reasons for the industry to continue using STC Ratings...... STC works very well in the world of hotels / motels / apartments / offices, etc. The fact that no-one has stepped up to the plate to develope a test criteria suitable for Studios might well be that there isn't a large enough piece of the market place to justify the expense involved. After all - it isn't as simple as just coming up with something - once you establish a set of standards - they have to be submitted to - reviewed - and accepted by the vast majority of the orginazations in the field - and that in and of itself is no small endevor.......... The question becomes this I suppose............ do we really need a standard at all? We seem to be doing quite well as it sits now......... what are we really missing? Even if we did estabish a standard.......... I don't picture things like the existing studies being duplicated to veryify results - so we'd be starting over from scratch as it were........ Then again maybe it's me- what is it that I am missing? Sincerely, Rod
