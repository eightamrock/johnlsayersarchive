Aaronw1 November 2004#2
Depending on budget... Balanced power. Run the subpanel separate from any HVAC, refridgerators, or other major appliances w/ motors, etc. Realistically...when you run the studio wiring, star ground all outlets, and use isolated ground outlets. I'm outta time right now, but Steve may have a few more things to add when he pops in.
