Greetings and welcome! That space isn't too terrible for a control room, other than being pretty close to square. It shouldn't be too tough to get it sounding good. But it'd help to know what you expect out of it. Do you need more sound isolation than the room is giving you? Is this in a rental property that you can't do any major renovations to? Are you planning on recording a 30-piece orchestra in there? Even a drum kit would have a tough time squeezing in there once you get the mixing desk in there... Help us help you. ;)
