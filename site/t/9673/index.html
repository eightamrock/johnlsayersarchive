gullfo29 October 2007#3
the angled booth gives you more space and breaks up parallel wall echos. resonant modes will still exist regardless of room shape.
britune30 October 2007#4
I'm considering using double wall construction for the front, back and knee walls. I will probably have to use drywall on RC for the side walls and ceiling. If that doesn't give me enough sound isolation, I may use Green Glue on the floor and walls and add another layer of drywall. If I still need more isolation after that, I can tear out the drywall in the garage ceiling and decouple that ceiling from the floor above. I plan on doing a good amount of treatment, using 703 type panels in the corners and first reflection points. I have a few more pics here
