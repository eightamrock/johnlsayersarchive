Deluks9 December 2007#25
That sipcrete stuff looks pricey/fiddly. Would be much more economical to use solid 100mm concrete blocks, You can get them delivered by the palletload and they are relatively cheap. They will be a bugger to haul down the garden though, but you might be able to hire a mini dumper to get them down the garden, or have a word with the farmer, he might hire you the services or his tractor :wink: *You probably know this next bit already* Another thing you should think about is building regs and planning permission. You will almost certainly need planning as you are increasing the size of the current structure. And as you will be installing toilet, electrics etc, building regs will most certainly apply. Could be a costly exercise to demo the building because you didn't use correct footings etc
