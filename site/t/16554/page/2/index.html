gullfo13 August 2011#29
i realize i'm late on this but the amps generally should not go below the monitors because the heat rising off them will interfere with the HF response (remember air velocity is dependent on temperature and while all frequencies respond to the same air velocity, the shifting heat patterns rising (as presumably you have air movement) will tend to interfere more audibly on the HF range). if you can, move them to the center.
