The room is 6.5' x 5.5' (small, I know).
Very small! That's only 36 ft2. Studio specs call for a minimum of 215 ft2 of floor area for a "critical listening room, so you'd be at about 17% of the smallest recommended size. Small rooms need a LOT of treatment. The smaller they are, the more treatment they need. By the time you put treatment in that room, there'd be practically no place left for you or your gear! That said, if this is the ONLY option that you have, then it's better than nothing at all, but I would really, strongly suggest looking for another location where you'd have enough space to do it right. It would probably be better to set up your bedroom itself as a studio, rather than try to use the closet inside the bedroom. - Stuart -
