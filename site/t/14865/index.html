Soundman20201 October 2010#4
The problem with that layout, Tom, is that you have no symmetry, which is rather important if you want your mixes to translate! Without decent room symmetry you cannot get a good stereo image. You should try to ensure that, at the very least, the front half of your room is symmetrical. You could do that by turning the desk around 180° (in your second picture), and putting it up against the wall there. That would put your back to the booth, which might or might not be a problem. - Stuart -

Nope. That wouldn't help much. You'd still have a wall in front of you on the right but not on the left (behind your speakers, plus the acoustics of the absorption on the right over a solid wall would not correspond to the acoustics on your left, which is basically just empty space.
