Small rooms will always have a "boxy" sound to them. That's unavoidable. The only real choice is to make the room rather dead, acoustically, then add a bit of ambience in the mix. This thread might interest you. It's about a room for tracking drums, but the same applies in the case of vocals, to a lesser extent: viewtopic.php?t=21108 - Stuart -
