Hi SH Depends on what's behind the cloth :) .Cloth is usually fixed to a frame, which you would have filled with sound absorption material (rockwool....), for esthetical purposes (the cloth is not the rockwool) :lol: :lol: .It wouldn't be pretty to see the rockwool directly...also to stop the fibres flying all around your room.If you replace the cloth with sheetrock, then you get those unwanted reflections your friends are telling you about. cheers
