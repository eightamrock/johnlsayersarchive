The door is 4 actual doors each with two pieces of glass and argon gas in between them. They are "energy saver" rated. gullfo1 January 2011#2
if you go with a good quality (Marvin, Pella, Anderson, etc) exterior grade suitable for high wind usage, they usually have something around an STC32-36 rating so a pair of those back-to-back (separated by say 6" or so) is going to be close to STC-45 but given most of those doors are dual panes, you will have a triple leaf effect so the total TL will be lower than just combining them. they're also probably going to cost about $2500 each (versus an acoustic speciality slider which could easily start at $5000).
