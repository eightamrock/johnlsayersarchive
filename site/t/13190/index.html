Hi Jdb - yes you can do it - don't over do it, cos it will cost more. You have a typical mediteranean brick and concrete construction. Just don't connect to it apart from the floor. I see you have the modules which are fine but you need to remove yourself from the rest of the building by the room within room system. Build an inside/out wall system - walls and ceiling with 2 layers of 5/8" Pladur (drywall) no green glue and you'll be fine. Then build the modules inside that shell. Don't forget the rear absorbers in the room design - they are very important.
