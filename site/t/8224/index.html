gullfo22 February 2007#7
by utilities i mean your water heater, boiler, furnace, a/c, electric panel, etc.. things you need for the home :-) its really a quick sketch to think about the rooms in terms of position and workflow. the rooms would need as much treatment and isolation as necessary to meet your isolation objectives, but in the CR I'm assuming you are isolating the other rooms from each other so to keep the space, if you can go to the concrete walls (minus some depth for treatments - maybe an inside out wall with slats), then I'd do that. the far right lower corner booth would work for drums or the one next to the CR. or the main room. depends on the music and how you want to record it. the "booth" by the interior stairs (i'm assuming thats what that ramp inside is) is more of an air lock but could be usable.
