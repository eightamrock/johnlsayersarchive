EB120026 February 2006#1
Hi everybody! I finally found a space for my new studio. I'm gonna share it with a company that (amongst other things) produces an online radio show. They are going to have their office on the ground floor, plus a radio studio in the basement. The place is nice and big, only downside is the ceiiling height (2,8 m) and the pillars (4 in the space that the studios are going in) and beams that run across the ceiling. Pillars and Beams are 30x30cm, the beams run vertical with the pillars. (The foto shows a view from the area marked as lounge towards the emergency exit in the upper left corner) The emergency exit needs to stay accessible, too. The areas marked as "Lounge" and "Storage" hold most of the water and heating pipes for the building, so I figured that it's gonna be way easier and more cost effective to keep the studio rooms in the other parts. Maybe the radio room could go there, but that would mean running ac ducts all the way there. I'm mostly doing vocal recording and mixing, but want to have the opportunity to do the occasional band recording in the future. The Control room should be pretty big, since it has to hold a big DDA AMR24 desk (3m!) and lots of outboard, and I often got clients in for mixing sessions. The Live Room should be at least 20m2 big. Also we're planing on the Radio room (about 20m2, to hold a DJ setup, a small edit workstation and a desk for interviews) and a small preproduction room that will hold a pro tools rig and loads of synths. There is no air conditioning so far, but the emergency exit goes straight to the roof over the office space, so it would be easy to run ducts up there. Apart from the layout, my main questions are: -do we need to float the floors? I don't want to lose too much ceiling height, and as long as i can monitor recording at low levels and there is no sound leakage to the upstairs floor, I should be fine. -what's the easiest (and cheapest!!!) way to install air conditioning and heating? I have been following this forum for years now, I think I get the basic idea on how to build the rooms, but I'm very busy at the moment, and construction is supposed to be done in 3 months time (I know it sounds crazy, but otherwise I have to miss out on this great opportunity) I'm willing to donate a fair amount of money and also use the phone service for further questions during construction, but I just couldn't come up with a design that really made sense on my own. I know I'm asking for a lot here, and I will keep doing my research and post new ideas. thanks in advance! Erik ps: is there any software like smart draw for mac osx? Right now I'm working in Photoshop, and that's not really going anywhere..
