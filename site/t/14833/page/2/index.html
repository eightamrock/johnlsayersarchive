you could create the purple wall as individual 4x8 or 4x10 modular panels bolted together so you can move and re-configure as you expand, say add 2 more rooms etc. or use (re-use) as an exterior wall of a room later. same approach for the green room as well.

gullfo30 September 2010#31
build the modular walls... 3 metal 2x4 studs, 2 tracks per 4x8/4x10 section, 1x 1/2" drywall. some R11 insulation to dampen. bolt each section together... a bit more materials (studs) but shouldn't be too bad. cross-brace for support and/or some suspension cables to ensure they stay upright.
