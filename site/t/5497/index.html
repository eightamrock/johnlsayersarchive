So, here it is...after having problems about how to solve the layout I came up with this one. It's a compromise in some way but as far as I can see it should work out just fine (I hope). This is a first step design and if I decide to go with this one I will do another one more detailed where you can see the structure of the walls, floor, ceiling etc. I wanted to create a nice controlroom (not tooo small) and even get some space for the studioroom so I can put up a drumkit without problem. Take a look at it and please tell me what you think about it. If it sucks, dont be afraid to say so :D Things you cant see in this design are: * Floating floor for each "room", to get better isolation when recording drums etc * 35/40cm thick isowalls (double walls) * Ceiling height 2.40 m (15°-20° leaning in the front of the room) * Absorbation and diffusor treatment In this design I have come up with: - 1 Controlroom (13 sq meter) - 1 Studioroom (15.4 sq meter) - 1 Storageroom (that also can act as an isobooth when needed) (2.2 sq meter) Any suggestions would be nice...if things can be done in another way or so, another design etc. I'd be glad to hear what ya think about it. Best! /Mike
