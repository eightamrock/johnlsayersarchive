I am now relocating my studio into a new building adjacent to my house. The studio is in a 4000sqft cinderblock building with 17' ceilings. I have begun construction on the main studio with an architect but I have now decided to build a smaller B room at the other end of the building. I would like to build a second story where a loft would be above studio B. I basically have a 40'X20' (with 10' ceilings) open area where I would like to build a control room live room and possibly a small booth as well as stairs which would go up to the loft above the studio. I am trying to get ideas on how to design it. If I set up equal space for the both the control room/studio They both would be appx 20x20x10 which might not be ideal dimensions. I would like to keep the live room on the side with the glass block window and try to utilize it for some natural light. (Luckily I am in a rural neigborhood with virtually no nieghbors by the studio so I dont have to deal with outside loudness issues) I would like to keep the stairs above the control room on the other side. I was lloking at some of the posted SAE plans and I was intrigued by one of the designs with the set of sliding glass doors between the control room and studio. I thought it might be a efficient use of space in my situation. Anyways, If anyone has any thoughts or ideas I would be very grateful to hear them.
