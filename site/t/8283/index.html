OK i found this to answer these questions. knightfly---"Airtight isn't necessary - the main thing is that you don't have gaps in the plane of the front baffle, either the part that's the face of your speaker or the baffle EXTENSION that is the face of the soffit. These two planes need to be flush with each other, but you can have up to about 1/2" between the speaker box and the soffit construction - but, once the speakers are in place you should fill any gaps with insulation, keeping it even with the face of the speaker and the face of the soffit. Using insulation just helps you keep from coupling the baffle extension (the face of the soffit) to the speaker box, so that the soffit doesn't become a passive radiator for the speaker. The end result should be that you have only a narrow gap around the speaker box, and that the front face of the speaker box is even with the front face of the soffit... " If we are trying NOT to create a passive radiator from the soffit baffle extension then I would imagine it would be important to isolate the massed box around the speaker from the soffit baffle extension, so why use the box at all?
