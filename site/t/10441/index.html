The ideal listening position is 38% away from the wall you will be facing when looking at your monitors. I guess you will have the bass trap behind you, so 38% from the window. The listening position is measured just past your head. Also, remember the equilateral triangle rule. Looks like you will have fun with acoustics in that shape room, I think (?). Don't forget absorption in the corners facing you and 1st reflection points - side walls, ceiling, floor, rear walls etc, but hey, I'm no expert :lol: Good luck.

gullfo14 April 2008#12
keep in mind you want to create a equilateral triangle for the monitors so you need to look at the width of the monitors as the approx depth for the seating. so if you can space the monitors 280cm, then your seating is going to be 280cm on each leg as well. so the 38% rule might not be applicable unless your far enough from the front wall (which then could result in low mid cancellations at the sitting position). as far as treatment, you want the back corner to be a bass trap, and you'll need reflection treatments on each side of the back walls as well. plus there is no door shown so you may have other compromises yet to be made. lastly, if you check Rod's CR layout, there is a front wall indentation, its not a pentagon...
