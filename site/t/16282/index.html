gullfo20 June 2011#2
overall a nice size - would you be bringing equipment in and out frequently? if so, you might consider a separate air lock and doors for the live room. you could also angle the walls between the CR and live room if desired. you might consider 2x6 instead of 2x4 for the walls to support the inner isolation ceiling and exterior roof. since this is related to your construction thread - maybe keep posting there - presumably you have worked with your local building officials and contractors to properly design the concrete floor - footings, etc to support the walls and related mass. are you considering splitting the concrete floor between the live room and control room?

i would make the dividing wall on 15 degrees and a front wall in the CR @ 7.5 degrees. this will form a 7.5 degree set of angles in the CR. if you can also make the isolation ceiling heights 4m, you then create a false ceiling in the CR for treatment and have plenty of space in the live room.
