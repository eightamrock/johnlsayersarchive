John Sayers29 December 2006#2
Mika - as far as I know there is no formula for the hangers. When placing them in a corner they obviously change shape as the corner narrows so you get a variety of mass etc. When placing between two walls, as in a ceiling, I divide the distance by two and place a hanger in the middle point. I then divide the distance again and place two more. Then I divide again and and add four more - get the idea? this places the hangers on the modal positions. cheers john
