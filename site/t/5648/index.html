Aloha and welcome to the forum Ryan!
things change and somehow this became my fulltime job; the terribly uneven response of this room hurts more every day.
I hear you there. I struggled with mine for 5 years until I found this site and made the changes. A few things I would suggest: - You may be sitting in a peak. The width of your room has a 166Hz peak at about 2 1/2 ' from the wall your desk is on, and an overlap of 161Hz & 242Hz about 3 feet from the wall. It is quite possible that being set up on this wall will place your mix position at a point where all of these frequencies are building up. I would try setting up your layout lengthwise. Depending on what is in your electrical cabinet (that might cause some interference), you could try positioning your mix position (ears) approximately 38% (a little over 3') from the front wall, placing your monitors on stands behind the desk, sofa 2' or so from the rear of room. I would then place bass traps in corners where possible, side absorbers on either side of the mix position, rear absorbers behind the sofa and then see where you are from there. Here's a link for the bass trap, side absorber units that might be helpful: http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm and one for general info on acoustics, absorption and lots of other good stuff: http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html (click on tabs on left and at top of pages.) If you care to, you might check out these two links as they cover a similar space needing treatment as yours: http://johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic ... ght=#15644 and http://johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=4091 Also, is it possible for you to post photos of room, please? Thanks! :wink: Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
