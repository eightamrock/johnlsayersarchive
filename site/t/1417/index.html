barefoot6 March 2004#4
As long as it's not too thick, cloth shouldn't have much of an effect. If you plug your baffle width into my 1D Wall Bounce Calculator and set D to zero you'll see the 6 dB low end shelf. Only the frequencies that rise significantly above the 0 dB line are influenced by the soffit baffle. The higher frequencies are only influenced by the speaker baffle. So, if the cloth has little absorption in the range of the shelf, it will have little impact on the direct response. However, the cloth will certainly inflence the ambient response of the room. Thomas
