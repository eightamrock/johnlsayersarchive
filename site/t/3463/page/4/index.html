Theory: The first principle in my design is a "cathedral" or "unvented attic". To do this I must seal the outer shell completely. This allows me to use the outer structure as my outer leaf( 2 leaf MAM rule). The benefits to this are... A) simpler construction B) Higher ceilings and less lost real estate than a 2 leaf within the garage structure. Also if I create vented attic space by drywalling under rafters, and flattening under collar ties, then my airspace decreases from when the mass (outer leaf) was ontop of the rafters. I learned of this technique from steve, he gave me this link http://www.buildingscience.com/resource ... Design.pdf and more... http://www.buildingscience.com/resource ... efault.htm This technique if done properly should have adequate humidity and heat performance. Method: 1)Seal any holes in outer structure A) Im filling in an old door( on the back of garage) A window on left side of diagram, two upper vents on back wall, lower side wall vents, and upper side vents found where the rafters meet the wall top. B) The upper side vents and 2x4 filled crevices should all be refilled with new 2x4s. If the top of the 2x4 needs to be cut to match pitch of roof for tight fit then do so. Seal with cock. C) Since the wall is (out to in) Stucco, cement board, vapor seal, and then studs. I should 1)Repair any damaged vapor seal. 2) Attach 5/8" drywall in between studs to outer wall(vapor seal). Cock on out side of drywall (touching vapor seal).
