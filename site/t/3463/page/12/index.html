If you're only going to use one layer, and since the area isn't too large, perhaps you should consider using one of these products (all similar/competing, just different names):
  • Hardibacker® (by James Hardie)
  • WonderBoard (by Custom Building Products)
  • Durock (by USG Corporation)
It's extremely moisture tolerant -- it's made for bathtub surrounds, tile countertops and backsplashes, etc. It's more expensive and heavier than drywall, but it might be a good fit. Regarding the product...
