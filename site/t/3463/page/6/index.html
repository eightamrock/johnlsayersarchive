Hey Steve, I noticed your span calculator fuses "dead load" with "live load," such that it seems you can't spec your own figures there. The span calculator I've used on the Web lets you choose live load specs separate from dead load specs. In other words, you could spec especially heavy dead loads but leave the live loads really light -- ideal for figuring heavy ceilings below attics that will take on minimal live loads, or (even better) interleaved/staggered joists that will never take on a live load.
