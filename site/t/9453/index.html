xSpace23 September 2007#2
Is that a truss or post and beam construction, maybe a stick built frame? What you don't want to do is inhibit the ability of air to move in between the rafter cavities. The build up of heat in the roof system needs a direct path to exit the building such as soffit venting with gable vents, assuming it is a gable roof type.
