John Sayers12 October 2006#5
Hi guys - it all depends on treatment. Take a look at this room http://johnlsayers.com/Pages/Hello_Testing.htm It's a small bedroom around 14' x 10'. I tell you what - that room sounds better than many large rooms I've heard. Everytime I visited I was always amazed at how good the room sounded. the owner produced global award winning commercials out of that room. The point to note is the amount and type of treatment. Soffit mount front (90 degrees, not 60) with heavily treated rear wall and ceiling. It even has a compression ceiling over the console. So Yes - a small room can sound OK if treated correctly. cheers john
