xaviFarre wrote:
I think that’s horrible graph, but I don’t know why?
1) Compared to a preamp or sound card, all rooms look horrible when graphed, even rooms that are well treated. 2) Your 2-inch panels are not thick enough for bass frequencies. For panels made of plain rigid fiberglass or mineral wool, four inches thick is the minimum. --Ethan
