knightfly19 September 2004#13
While you're planning, keep in mind that concrete weighs roughly 2 TONS per cubic yard - so if you're planning on ICF walls for inner walls as well, look carefully at downstairs support. If only the perimeter walls will be ICF, depending on soil type the 8" concrete ICF's will go at least 16 feet if I remember correctly, before needing a floor to tie them together. I'm still not sold on them though - I've yet to figure out what that 2-3" of foam is going to do when I add an inner frame and second leaf; seems like it might act as a third leaf, but I've not gotten solid answers yet. Might just go with surface-bonded hollow block, sand filled, instead. At least I know how to calculate that when adding gypsum inner wall leaf. Here's a link to some ICF stuff if you're interested - http://www.icfweb.com/
