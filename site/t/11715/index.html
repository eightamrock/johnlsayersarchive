biggator wrote:
I still had to push the actual speaker soffit in closer to the middle of the room
Well, you shouldn't push the soffits around, they should be placed by themselves according to your room's and speakers' dimensions. My method to determine the position of the soffits for any room is this: 1. Draw a line that crosses the middle of your room lengthwise. 2. Multiply the room's length by 0,38 3. Mark the spot from the front wall across the line up to the number you just calculated. That's your listening position. 4. Mark the spot about 20cm (8") from the listening position across the line to the front wall. That's where you gonna sit. 5. From the listening position, draw a line 30-45 degrees in respect to the original line, that's where the speakers axis should meet. 6. Measure your speakers length and divide it by 2. 7. Draw two parallel lines left and right of and in respect to the "speaker lines", further apart by the number you calculated. That's your speaker cabinets walls. 7. Measure your speakers width. 8. Draw a line 90 degrees away from the point of the speaker wall lines that meet your front wall. 9. Draw a parallel line to that line you just drew, 10cm (4") away from the front wall to compensate for the soffit frame space. That's your speaker cabinet's back wall. 10. Draw a line parallel to the last one, further apart by your speakers width measurement. That's your speaker cabinet's front wall. All you have to do now is draw a symmetrical outer frame of your soffits to include the speaker boxes you just made. See? Placed by themselves alright! :wink: John
