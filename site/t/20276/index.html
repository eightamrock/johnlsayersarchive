Not very good at all for low frequency absorption, I'm afraid! Compare that with the specs for plain old OC-703:
Image not preserved: 703-specs.jpg
The highlighted line is ordinary unfaced 703, 4" thick. As you can see, it is many times better than this cellulose product you mentioned. It would have some uses, certainly: For example, of you needed something to specifically absorb the high mids without the lows or highs too much, then this would be a good product for that. But pretty useless for bass traps or wall cavity damping. - Stuart -
