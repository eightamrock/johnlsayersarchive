MErlewine2 October 2005#1
Here is a problem. Building a small studio for acoustic instruments recording. The actual recording area is about 20'x13' (8' ceiling), as you can see from the diagram. On three sides of that room are wood shelves, nailed and glued, that did hold music CDs. The shelves are about 6-1/2" deep. Rather than tear these shelves out and expose the concrete walls, it occurs to me that if these shelves are filled with some substance, that this would probably give much better sound. Also, it could be one or many substances, and they could be moved around, changed, etc. - more or less. Almost a way to fine tune the room. I would think these shelves would be good to help deal with bass - acoustic bass being used. But what do I know. That is why I am here. My questions are: (1) Is this a good idea to put something in these shelves? (2) What would be a good substance? and (3) Should I put fiberglass ceiling tiles on the ceiling? Any help is very much appreciated. Thanks. Michael Erlewine.

WiseMindsRecordingStudios2 October 2005#2
Michael, welcome. Your best bet, right now is to read, read, read. Do nothing on the building end. It is a story told over and over here. There is a sense of urgency to build, get it done, and get back to your music. Do not fall into this trap. If you want a good or even great studio, one word......read. Read then plan, then read some more. Repeat. I spent about 3 months just reading here before I started my studio. I have a lot less questions now, and a real sense that I actually know what I am talking about now. I also have a better feeling about what I am doing in my studio. Building with confidence if you will. Start off by reading the board rules. Yes, it's a pain in the ass but the site owner and moderators put a lot of time in here for free. They have designed these rules so that your questions will be fashioned to get the maximum effectiveness in their answers. It is well worth it to play by the rules. Then move on to the reference section: CLICK HERE Read, Plan, Repeat. After you have done your reading and basic planning, then ask your questions. After that, when you are confident, build.
