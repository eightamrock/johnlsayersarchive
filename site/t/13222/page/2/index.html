arcane wrote:
Yep, I'm talking to DH. I want to hear ZR before deciding on an approach.
Hi I thought I should post my findings regarding Delta H Designs' ZR Acoustics. I've worked in a fair number of studios over the past 25 years and I have often been disappointed with the sound of the control room. As part of my studio design, I wanted to maximise room transparency/accuracy and minimise fatigue and guess work. I also wanted to maximise the use of floor space. I had read about ZR and I was very curious. So in a trip to the States, I had several listens to a bunch of Delta H Designs ZR Acoustics rooms, including Universal Music's new mastering facility in LA. I took a bunch of my mixes. I was very impressed with the transparency and detail, and the size of the "sweet spot". I stood in corners, sat on the floor in corners, lay on the floor, sat in windowsills (yes, windows!), by the door, etc - the stereo image was always there and there was virtually no bass buildup. I spent hours trying to critique the room "sound". I wanted to find problems. All I ended up doing was critiquing the mixes and the monitoring in each room. That was unexpected. I expect that these rooms would not contribute to listening fatigue. So: ZR Acoustics is real and it's in use in top studios. And the owners/studio staff seemed pretty chuffed when I asked them about it. I'm not prone to hyperbole (and just to be clear I'm not being paid to say this! :-)), but to my ears the result of the ZR Acoustics approach is incredibly close to the neutral room sound that I have always wanted. So close in fact that I have engaged them to help with the design of my Sydney studio: control room+live room. I anticipate that a bunch of people won't believe me, and I don't blame you - a healthy dose of skepticism is a good thing. I also appreciate that this is not entirely "on topic" - many of the people on this site are building DIY home studios, and this may not be for you. But for folks who are interested, if you get the opportunity, I recommend you have a listen to a ZR room and decide for yourself. That's the only way - trust your ears. I'll post again when the studio is built and give some more feedback. Forgive me for a little bit more hype, but the Delta H Design folks are a pleasure to work with too - across the Pond! Thanks Richard
