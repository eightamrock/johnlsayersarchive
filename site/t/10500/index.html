Ethan Winer21 April 2008#4
reelinr wrote:
what are the advantages of having a separate control room?
If the studio is in a house and you have only so much space, a one-room setup is preferred. That is, it's better to have one large room that sounds good versus two rooms each too small to have a decent bass response. And for a one-person project studio one room makes much more sense. I have a large home studio, and I greatly prefer one room. --Ethan
