Soundman20206 December 2008#11
What does explode do?
It "unmakes" a group or component. Very useful when you forgot to include something in a group, or included it at the wrong level. Explode, shift-select the missing item, make group again. Also, did you know that different levels of a group or component can belong to different layers? That one REALLY confused me for a while! I'd have a wall that would disappear when I clicked off my "floor" layer, and it took me ages to figure out that somewhere down inside the levels of the group I had that wall also assigned to the floor layer! It would be neat to have some kind of hierarchical diagram so you can see easily what belongs to which layers, and vice-versa. I've figured out workarounds, though. Cumbersome, but they work. Are you using the animation tabs too? They are a BIG help when you realize that you can turn off changes in camera angle for different tabs! So you can have one tab which shows only your sheetrock, another which shows only your framing, etc, and without changing your viewpoint! It's like being able to group layers easily, but unrelated... LOVE! HATE! LOVE! HATE!! :) - Stuart -

Hi lilith_envy, A sump hole, is basically a hole that is lower than the grade beneath the house. Since my area has a dense layer of clay about 12 inches below grade, it is called "Marl Clay" hence the name of my town, water will not drain through it. So, after any heavy rain, the water that accumulates drains down and flows to the low point where the sump hole is. It is then pumped out toward the street where it makes its way to the sewers. Sorry about the basic explanation but I figured I'd give you some insight into our geology here too. :) As for your corner idea, I thought about that.
