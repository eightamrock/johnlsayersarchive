Well, that sort of answers the basic question here, but not really. The basic question is what Brien already asked: How much isolation do you need? Not in subjective terms of "Mostly vocals and sometimes drums", but rather in terms of actual, objective, real-world decibels. Get a sound level meter, and measure how loud you are. Then also measure how quiet you have to be, or check your local municipal noise regulations to find that answer. The difference between the two ("How loud" vs. "how quiet"), is the answer to how much isolation you need, in terms of decibels. Once you know THAT number, then the answer to "What do I do with the door" will also become apparent, and so will the basic plan for the entire studio. You can't do anything at all until you know that number. It defines the type of isolation that you will need, which in turn defines the building materials and techniques that are available to you to attain that level. So the real answer to your questions so far, depends on getting a sound level meter (they are not expensive) and doing some tests. But based on what I know of drums and municipal regulations, plus "I would like to record drums at times too... ", all of the above suggests that the garage door is history, and unless you have a very decent sized budget for lots of thick glass and complex construction, then the idea of French doors is also sliding downhill, real fast.... - Stuart -
