gullfo8 November 2007#8
not really, its a pretty concise design. you should just remember to add a cloud absorber over the mix position and make the make the rear wall mostly absorbant. if i was going to change anything i would make the angled side walls shorter (front-to-back) and have them join the existing wall just past the mix position so you could increase the angle of them a bit (leave the front depth the same), then treat the back-side walls with a couple of absorbers to cut any flutter echo. this would give you more space on the back end of the room and increase the angle of the side walls to help create a reflection-free zone.
