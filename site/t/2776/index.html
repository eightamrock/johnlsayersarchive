Paul Woodlock10 December 2004#5
Oh and btw - don't put standing wave traps flat against a surface, The bass aborption performance is GREATLY DIMINISHED. enough to make them not worth it. Place at 45 deg across a corner. Vertical ( wall-wall ) corners, or Horizontal corners ( wall-ceiling, wall-floor ) can both be used. The BEST place is tri-corners ( wall-wall-ceiling, wall-walll-floor ). As well as standing wave trapping, you also ideally want Early Reflection absorption. The place for this absorption is easily done by the mirror trick. Sit in your mix position and get a friedn to move a mirror around all surfaces of the room. Wherever you can see a speaker, this is where to palce the absoprtion panels. An air gap behind them will increase their performance considerably ( and is FREE :) ) hth Paul
