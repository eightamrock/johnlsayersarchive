I hope you post your build as I will defiantly follow it, If you have the money and can get John to design for you I'm sure from all the builds on his site that I'v viewed you will have a great job, not being an expert and looking to this forum for help in building my very small home studio for my sons to use this forum was and is a fantastic help, like you I have some construction skills as I am a ground worker in the UK and nearly all the work is done by me, how ever I am building with no planning so have to stick to all the council guide lines for out buildings and sheds, one of which is not being taller than 2.5m total high, This would have meant very low ceilings inside which is not good, but it was Glenn who suggested going down in the ground which is a lot of work but means I have 2.8m ceiling height internally and can honestly say this is the best thing ever as I am sure that one of the reasons that the sound is so low outside of my build so far is that my slab is around 0.8m below grade, so if possible that would be my bit of advice consider going down in the ground to get your ceiling heights :D Good luck mate :D
