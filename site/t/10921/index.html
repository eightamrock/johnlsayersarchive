Well, here we go! I've been in the process of building my studio for a year or so, but only now are things starting to take shape. My diary starts now, because progress has been fairly sporadic over the last year. I'm 20 years old, doing a Computer Science degree. I've wanted a studio for a while since I’d like to become an audio engineer. My parents have been very generous in letting me locate one at home, and in the after much thought, we decided to build an extension on to our existing house. The extension is divided into three areas. The Control Room/Studio A small shower room A Gym, also functioning as my “live room” As I’m a student, I’m away from home for around 30 weeks of the year, hence why the live room will also function as a gym. Currently, most of my work is location recording, since this year I’ve been the Head of Technical team in my University radio station. I’ll probably still do some of the recordings next year, but may not have as much time as I’ve been elected Station Manager for this year =) Either way – the main purpose is to have a mixing room, with the ability to record small groups when the opportunity arises. I don’t need a huge amount of isolation, so everything was built as if it was a domestic extension. Time for the pictures! Here is the area before anything happened:
