BriHar23 October 2012#2
I suggest you don't divide the room up, instead go for a typical project studio design. Desk on the right wall (re: your floorplan) with speakers firing down the length. Treatment on all 1st reflection points and especially corners. You've not mentioned any consideration to sound isolation, so I've not addressed it, but you might need to think about that! Not only from sound getting out (complaints from neighbours - drums can be pretty loud) but also from sounds getting in ruining a recording session.
