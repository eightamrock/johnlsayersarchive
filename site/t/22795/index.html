Hi folks, Some of you might recognize my name as the guy who has the monstrous thread on this board for my partial garage conversion in Sacramento, California, which I finally finished last year. While on vacation in Florida this week, my wife and I are visiting some retirement communities for consideration in the future. Among them is Lake Ashton in Polk County which includes a limited number of recreational vehicle garage homes, meaning a detached home with an attached garage large enough to contain a 45 foot long Class A motorhome.
