Guillo18 March 2011#9
Well, since I'm not an American I can't help a lot with the dimensions, since I only understand the metric system. However, going by standard one car garage dimensions, I'm guessing your space is quite limited. Which is why you should probably provide more info regarding how you will be using this space. You want a comfortable CR, but if you are going to be recording full live bands at once, then studio area will be much more important, and you'll have to deal with a small CR. Isolation booths will be pretty cool, but I'd keep them small. Not only for vocals, but they are good for keeping an amp while recording, so that you can give the musicians in-ear mixes for playback. However, if you are not gonna be dealing with full bands playing at the same time, then yes, you should divide the space a little more. In any case, for those dimensions, I'd leave maybe one tiny booth, and invest in moving panels and some plexi. That'll be the most practical setup, with a small-ish CR.
