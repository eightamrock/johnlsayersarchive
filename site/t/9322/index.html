StudioF28 August 2007#1
Hi Everyone, I just bought a house in NJ and am starting to plan the basement studio renovation. I'm in the very preliminary stages and would really just like some ideas in the best use of the space available. My goals would be to have decent sized control room with a drum booth and a vocal/guitar booth/room. I'm also used to outboard gear being to my right so I would probably have racks to the right of the mix position. I don't know if this matter but in most of the rooms I see on this site the racks are part of the mixer/control surface furniture. My closest neighbor is probably about 100' away, so outside noise is probably not to much of an issue. However, I do want to try and get some sound proofing to the first floor if possible. The first picture shows the basement as is. The second show what I want to do to get a little more room to work with. The third show some of the ducting. The two blobs next to the fireplace are support columns. The ceiling height on the right hand side is 8' to the joist. The ducting side is about 7.5'. The windows are full size, meaning double hung. I haven't decided on a budget but I keep thinking that 20K is about what i need to spend....call me delusional! Thanks a lot, Ernie
