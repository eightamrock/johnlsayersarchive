knightfly11 August 2005#2
For hangers, heavier insulation isn't needed; the "R-11" designation is apparently not a worldwide spec, it refers to standard weight fiberglass insulation that is 3.5" thick (90mm), to be placed between 2x4 (35x90mm) studs in a wall frame. This insulation has typical density of just under 1 PCF, or 16 kG/m^3. If you can find any standard household fiberglass insulation that comes close to those specs, it will work fine... Steve
