knightfly30 November 2003#2
I'd do the front wall between speakers absorptive to kill any reflections off the rear of the console/desk. The rear wall should be just absorptive, unless you have at least 10-12 feet between the back of your head and the rear of the room - otherwise, early reflections smear your stereo imaging by random phase cancellation - The slots on the front sides are fine, as are the hangars in bass traps at the rear corners. Hope that helped... Steve
