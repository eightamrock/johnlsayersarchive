len-morgan7 September 2006#7
If you use just plain 'ol microphone cable between each pair of XLR connectors, it won't be that expensive. You can get a roll of it (about 50') at a RadioShack here for about USD 8. I'm sure you can find something similar where you are. It's important to use the right kind of wire. An impedance mismatch will mess up your signal and using something like CAT6 cable will cause problems because it's almost always solid core. Just a little flexing while you're installing it and it will break. len

Hi Niccie, Make sure that you don't mount the jackplates opposite each other on the walls, back to back. This will negate the effect of the double wall, because the sound will travel straight through the assembly. Mount the jacks at least a meter apart.
