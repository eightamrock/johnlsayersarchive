jatougas28 December 2004#1
Hi folks - I'm posting a new take on my old plan. The ceilings are 8'. The gas water heater will be removed, and replaced with an electric one. The walls enclosing the water heater will still hinge, but now there's no worry about ventilation, so they'll be sealed. The door into the control room will be full ceiling height (otherwise, I was looking at removable sections of wall held in place with bolts, and decided against that). The ceiling of the control room will be vaulted, but flat from the listening position to about 4 ft back (so to where the side walls meet the back wall). I hope I've got the geometry right for soffit mounting the monitors. All of the windows will be double paned, and I'm hoping to find storm doors I can use for all four doors in the tracking areas. The vocal booth will be as dead as a layer of acoustic foam will make it, with a drawable curtain over the window. The wall construction will be 6" double-leaved, with staggered studs, and stuffed with insulation. 3 new power circuits, 1 for lights, 1 for power to the tracking area, 1 for control room power. Please let me know watch you think. I'm planning on recording mostly acoustic music, with not a lot of drums. The nieghbors are 30 yards to the east, and 60-70 yards to the north (the neighbors in the other two directions aren't worth worrying about... they're too far... :wink: ). Scale is 6" to a block, 20' by 20' square. Thanks for looking - John Tougas
