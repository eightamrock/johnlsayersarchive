jwl1 April 2008#2
Hi there, I've been using the gobos I built in your linked thread for a while now. I like them very much for tracking. There are a few things to keep in mind. First, Gobos are NOT bass traps. Gobos are designed to block sound, whereas bass traps depend on sound passing through them in order to work properly (here I'm speaking of common velocity absorbers, ie, absorptive material straddling corners or superchunks, etc). The gobos work well as first-reflection point absorbers, and I often use them as such while mixing. I also use them on the rear wall. But, I do highly recommend using them in conjunction with bass traps, rather than instead of. If your wall/wall corners are (largely) inaccessible, what about the wall/ceiling corners, or even the wall/floor corners?
