Still have to install glass in control room and install central air. But its heated and functional. Acoustic treatments are in my next phase. Its 30' x 30'. Plus I have an additional 20' x 15' which will be finished as 2 iso booths and closet space at a later date. I let each of my kids pick out the colors and one of my daughters is a art major in college and her paintings are on display (some are quite scarey). Plus she is doing a wall painting behind the drums (its in the layout stage).
