Johnnie30 October 2013#2
Hey Man! This isn't really a place to come and get a freebie professional design. There's some real experts on the site, who often give pro help to noobies like us, but they help us with OUR designs... This site is a great place to help you get started in understanding what you need to know... I know I've learned a ton from this site, and keep on learning how little I know. If I can suggest, why don't you put together a basic plan of what you think might be good, and then ask for comments on that? There's several designs on the site that you can use for inspiration. If you really don't want to put in much work to learning, you'll probably be best off just using one of those without any modifications. Here's an example that John Sayers generously posted onto the site. Just looking at the details is an education in what's important and what's not important in a small studio. viewtopic.php?f=1&t=5457 Good luck with your project! In answer to your costs question, they generally estimate it to be $100 per square foot to build a studio, so that should give you a framework in figuring out how large a studio you can construct.
