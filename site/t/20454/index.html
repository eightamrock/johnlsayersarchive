Soundman20205 April 2016#4
How you use a poly depends on what you want to do! If you explain more about your room, and the problems you are experiencing in it, then we can help you figure out the best way to treat that. It might turn out that polys are a good fit for what you need, or it might turn out that they would not be useful at all. It's generally not a good idea to start out with treatment that you like then look for a place to use it: it is much more sensible to start out by first identifying what the problems are in your room, then looking for the treatment that can fix them. - Stuart -
