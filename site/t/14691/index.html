jhbrandt15 August 2010#6
Is your ceiling open or finished? Can you see the joists? For sound proofing, use mass, decoupling, and insulation in the cavity. IE; gypsum board & resilient clips. The acoustic tiles may provide absorption in the speech range but not more and will not sound proof. Cheers, John
