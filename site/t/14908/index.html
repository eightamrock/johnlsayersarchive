gullfo15 October 2010#7
looks reasonable. i'd probably go with flat walls in the control room and add some small angled slat boxes and appropriate reflection point absorption to maximize the amount of space around the desk. if you don't need to soffit mount your speakers, you could put in slider doors so they don't have to swing and it will give you more visibility. otherwise, back to back doors with windows in them are ok. the iso-room could also have one angled wall and the rest as flat isolation walls and angled wall treatments.
