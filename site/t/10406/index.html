Genelec 8030 Monitor Speakers - Soundcard Cables?

Started by Warlord on 31 March 2008. 3 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 10406.

Warlord31 March 2008#1
Hey, I'm planning on buying the Genelec 8030A Monitor Speakers, but I need to know what cables I need for connecting them to a Focusrite Saffire LE external sound card. I believe it's a 1/4 inch jack to female XLR cable, but can someone confirm this? I presume I need two mono cables to connect each speaker to the sound card, right? So would I be right in saying 2 mono 1/4 inch jack to female XLR cables? Cheers.
Ro1 April 2008#2
Warlord, welcome. Strange place to ask that question, but it's an easy one to answer:
External image, not preserved — original: http://www.focusrite.com/media/product_images/40.jpg
As you can see the "outputs" are TRS jacks (it says so in the specs too), that means they're balanced. Since the 8030s inputs (XLR) are also balanced you have to get XLR-TRS. That's a balanced line-level cable you need. (* TRS stands for Tip, Ring, Sleeve which you might describe as "stereo" plug if you want)
tcdyer1 April 2008#3
Hi, You will need male XLR (the one with the pins) to go into the Genelecs. So i think 2 balanced TRS-Male XLR cables are what you want. Tom
Warlord1 April 2008#4
Brilliant, thanks very much guys! Kind of new to all this so needed the help :). Time to go order.
