Wherever you see them for sale the wait listed is often incaccurate, and last I heard it was 3 weeks to get a demo pair and then 2-3 months to get your actual pair. Actually it's been one employee and myself building them for the past year. We've finally moved into a new facially, our production rate has increased dramatically, and we're hiring more people. Currently the lead-time is 6 weeks and dropping. I understand some folks can't wait. And I sincerely apologize for not being able to meet everyone's schedule. I know it's frustrating, for me too. The bright side is, I've received just amazing feedback from literally everyone who we've shipped to. The MM27 is unlike anything out there and I couldn't have dreamed of a better reception!
