Hello Carsten, I’m from Holland too and I hope to build a similar rehearsal room /studio sometime soon. I also wanted to make a room within a room type of construction and was wondering if you, or someone else of course!, has any thoughts about things like condense or other moist-like substances building up between the two rooms. Normally here in Holland people tend to keep some holes in the outside wall to let the air flow in between. This way they want to prevent the in-between-space to go moist. Has anyone figured out a way to go around this, or is it simply not necessary in case of two airtight rooms and thus an airtight in-between-space? Help would be much appreciated! ’74 Loveplane
John Sayers24 March 2005#7
The only way around that problem I know is to build lined ducts around the holes so the sound has to travel through the insulated duct to get to the outside but the outside air can still get in. cheers john
