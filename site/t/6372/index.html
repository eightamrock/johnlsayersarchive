This is continuation of this post from the construction forum. Here's my layout i did with Sketchup. If its possible to have good acoustics with an open control room, i would really like to pursue that. Current progress: Vocal Booth - This took a lot of research, professional advice, testing, and guts to pursue, but I am now in the contstruction phase of the vocal booth. The ground has been broken and the beautiful sand beneath has been removed. Sump pump plumbing is happening this week and soon after, the cement walls and floor will be poured (the framing for pouring the walls has already been built). Ceiling - All of the outgoing HVAC ductwork has been acoustically sealed and the layers of rockwool/drywall pictured in the Sketchup file have been installed. New firesafe Halo light fixtures have been installed where the rockwool is (not pictured in the Sketchup file, see photos from my first post). Computer Isolation - The HVAC room in the middle of the basement is going to be where the computer is housed and there will be access to the front of the computer via neoprene-sealed cabinet doors on the outside of this room. This has not been installed yet. Monitor and keyboard/mouse wires from the computer will travel to BOTH the control room and the vocal booth. (wiring not pictured). The vocal booth will be wired up to control the main computer so one person can record their own vocals. I appreciate any suggestions on the layout of the CR. Thank you, HBomb
