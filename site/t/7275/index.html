John Sayers13 October 2006#3
Obviously i need some kind of duct connected to a fan pushing air into the studio from outside. Do i also need an "exhaust" ( i dont know the correct term ) duct aswell?
If you don't have an exhaust there is nowhere for the air to go - it will just build up pressure and cease airflow.
If there is no exhaust duct, and sealed room inside, will artist spontainiously combust over my nice new LR floor?
see above.
Whats to stop someone shouting into the fan on the outside of the building going down the duct and into the studio? Whats to stop joe blogg's guitar sound traveling down the duct and out of the building?
The duct is lined with absorption that absorbs the sound as it travels down the duct. Thus stopping the effect you are refering to. cheers john
