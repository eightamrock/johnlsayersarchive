Electrical usually comes from overhead while audio/video comes from the bottom side of a wall. Many an on line expert has said that maintaining a 12 inch clearance between parallel power/audio lines will work to reduce and or diminish hum. Frankly I wouldn't do it because it is easier to install your electrical wires from the top and you audio/video from the bottom.
