sharward17 November 2005#40
Assuming it's all fire-legal and all, I think you should aim for the shortest cooridors. That's where you're "wasting" usable space the most. Along those lines:
  • Can the entrance door be moved? Having the door in the corner guarantees at least some wasted floorspace.
  • Any chance you can have multiple doors to the adjacent space? If so, perhaps you could have a door from the live room to the adjacent space, thereby eliminating your need to have a door to a hallway inside your space. I would imagine that would be helpful for loading/unloading.
Those are some immediate thoughts I have... I'm trying to "think outside the box," literally -- right into the neighbor's box! :lol: --Keith :mrgreen:
