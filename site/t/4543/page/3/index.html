A pocket door (as we call them in these parts) is a door that slides into the wall when open. It's like a sliding glass door, but no glass, and no stationary window part. See "Install a Pocket Door" on DoItYourself.com.

The main studio area is quite a bit smaller in size than the last place, which im a bit bummed about, but the good news is the ceilings are at least 9 1/2' high, which means floating floor, and independent ceiling/ shell design! Woohoo! To one side the walls are at minimum 13" thick stone and concrete, and the other at minimum is 30"!! (Were talking VERY old buildings here!) Excellent potential isolation? The ceiling was exposed so I could have a good look. To a novice untrained eye (mine), the struts looked like 2x8", with single wooden floorboards (that are gonna be ripped up for a sealed flooring). The concrete floor is really thin, aparently about 1/2 inch thick over hardcore! (the builder described it as a buscuit!) They plan to dig it up and lay a new floor, couple of inches thicker. If you remember, the studio wil be built by contractors (bringing in sub-contractors for special areas such as damp membrane lining, etc). I met the "head" of the builders, and to my joy he had experience in studio construction. We chatted and he knew the basic principals of what i want to do; floating floors, 2 layers ofset drywall, isolated ceiling on new walls, non touching frames e.t.c. Im still gonna spec everything but everything I said, he understood/agreed with, and everything he said tallied up with what ive learned here (and rods book). I was also chatting to the project manager of the whole build, and he said, "You know most ppl think insulation helps improving isolation, but really decoupling and sheer mass are the answers." I thought "excellent!" I wanted to put plans up here for ppl to see/comment on etc, but as my trials of smartdraw and sketchup were used up, i decided to use good ol' paint until to express ideas, as ill end up doing a hand drawn layout to give to the project manager. I appologise in advance for the pics, They'll be in 1:50 scale if your using a samsung syncmaster172 :wink: , but i included dimensions (these are lifted straight from the architects floorplan, so they should be pretty acurate!). The red lines outline a support beam that drops approx 45cm below the bottom of the ceiling joists. The Circle is a pole thing (cant remember the term :roll: ) that is there perminantly. The Dark grey block is a new wall they are putting up to support the old walls (in accordance with structural engineers wishes), and the light grey boxes are these strange footings that poke out of the bottom of the wall up to about 1'/30cm. Apparently these are part of the foundations and cannot be removed, which impacts on usable floorspace. Ive included the only plan (pic 2) that i could see to be usable, but hope the experts here could see any better alternatives, or agree with this one (apparently, i dont need a straight exit from each room, as its now all done on distance vs obstructions? cool 8) ) Sorry for the long post, but im pretty excited of the studio actually happening again!! I apreciate any views comments, so lets here em people!!
