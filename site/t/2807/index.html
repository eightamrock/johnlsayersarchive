motown5914 December 2004#4
Seems like that would be 3 leaves; glass, MDF and another wall. maybe find a way to keep some insulation just behind the blinds(if you want to keep them) without making a hard panel, and fill in towards the new wall? I just got through figuring out a similar situation. I ended up painting the inside of the glass with a color that looked like a roman blind from the outside. Then I glued a fitted panel of celotex to the glass(still one leaf) for damping effect, and that gave me the dimension so I could screw on a panel of OSB to the inside wood frame, squeezing the celotex. MDF would probably work better. It took a while and some care not to botch up the painted glass but it looks great from the outside, especially from 20' or more(like my van!). anyhow, good luck
