Ro20 April 2008#11
Great! Seems like you already got yeself a nice shed. First a bit on the construction (tho we're here to design ....) It's already a two layer construction made out of bricks, must be good enough. But there's, ofcourse, the details... every chain has a weak link. In this case it might be the detailed spend on the construction. Every hole is a leak. Every seam is a leak. A door can be a the weak link. Might wanna make it a double door (with air space) to sort of complement the walls. Or build a very heavy 2 leaf door. There's good examples on this forum. Check for yourrself. The roof could use some extra gypsum layer(s). It's looks like the weakpoint at this moment. All seams must be closed with kit. A concrete floor (already sorta floating..?), don't change a thing just add laminate or whatever. Gotta do some tests to know how much your shed leaks... use a DB meter. There's good examples on this forum on the why, what and how. So let's leave all that to the construction part. mkay. =-=-=-=-=-= Now, the design. There's not lotsa space and you occasionally wanna practise with your band. So maybe something portable would be what you might need. I'm thinking about a portable mixdesk which also holds your monitors. A desk on wheels. Why? cuz you wanna have a RFZ somewhere on 38% of the room while mixing etc. Check this forum to understand why... You will need absorption, so check the acoustics forum to see what you need. There's a pretty basic set up, Ethan has got a nice article on his site about that which he promotes like a few times a week. So, no hard time finding that link ;) There's also a STICKY in the acoustics which shows how to make panels/traps. Check that, it's worth every penny/dime/euro. :P Kay, now let's have that floorplan (design), kay?
