I learned this a bit too late myself, but in general, wider stud spacing tends to increase isolation. So, if you can safely do the wider spacing, you should do so. --Keith :mrgreen:

knightfly16 October 2006#4
Generally a wall with 24" centers when properly damped (insulation with or without green glue) will flex more, absorbing more sound than it transmits (as compared to a stiffer 16" center framework. I've yet to see anyone build/test a wall with 24" centers one side, 16" the other... Steve
