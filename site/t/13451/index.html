jbassino wrote:

Ok. Got it. Then you have what I am talking about. I did the same thing. Though it was done by accident. Just build a floated floor not touching the walls. Use some acoustical sealant to "seal" the seam between the floor and wall. I understand your loss of height concern.. AT worse case I see you losing 101mm of height. Thinking 1/2" Neoprene, 1.5" Stud laying on side, .75" Plywood, .5" Drywall,. .75" Ply or Particleboard Put in 2" rockwool for insulation. You will get noise transmission down that wall, but it will be nothing like what you have now with that Kick drum directly on the floor.
