This I do know, if you have a bigger room that you are trying to make two smaller rooms out of, consider keeping the one bigger room. Usually the one room is easier to work with, but one of the issues that I have seen is people make small square rooms out of the one bigger rectangular room. It is the being square that creates acoustic issues. It is just one more thing to deal with. Good luck
