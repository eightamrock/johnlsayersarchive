Stick5 December 2006#186
So, I'm more than happy to give detail on how and why I did things, so fire away if you have questions, but probably won't take the time to chew through every detail without prompting. In general, I'm thrilled with the form and function of the studio. The soundproofing is excellent... especially the tracking room... it's SUPER quiet in there. Like, "hear your own heartbeat" quiet. If the gear is all off in the control room and the boys are stomping through the house, I can just make out the thumps, but they're pretty faint. You don't hear music from the speakers outside, at all. (Again, I'm not tracking drums...) As far as the looks and comfort, hopefully the pictures speak for themselves. The Murphy Bed works perfectly, as tested by my In-Laws over Thanksgiving, and completely disappears and functions as a bass trap when you close it up. The bathroom turned out cool... June keeps saying "I wish OUR bathroom was this cool". I still need to design and build the desk, but I have to decide on and buy a new controller keyboard first, so, that'll probably wait awhile. I'd like a new chair too... perhaps an Aeron, or maybe something like this. But again, what I have now works well, so it can wait until the next big record budget.... HA! Like that'll ever happen! So, next up is a slick website so I can start to get the word out. But, until then, if any of you guys and gals need a mix guy, producer guy, or track guy, hit me up. I'm ready to go. :D [/url]

Stick5 December 2006#188
guitardad72 wrote:
WOW!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! I am completely impressed with the final look of every room you've built. You're bath looks GREAT! That pull down bed is really cool!
Thanks. Yeah, I was really happy when we came up with the way that the bed hides completely in the wall. I had planned for it from the beginning, but hadn't really figured out exactly how the fabric panels would fit to cover it up. I'm stoked.
The pics are showing a pretty dark room... was this the intent or are the pics just dark?
Yeah, when I took the pics with the flash it looked terrible. So, they look a little dark, and the room IS a little dark, mostly because the transformers for the low voltage halogens are a little undersized. I didn't account for the cable length when I put the transformers in the machine room, so I wouldn't hear them buzz. So, I just need to order some higher power ones so the lights in the back of the room light to full power.
Are you pleased with the choice to "wrap" your clouds with rope light? Are you obtaining the effect you planned on?
Yeah, it worked out great. I thought it might be a cooler effect on it's own that it is, but with everything else on, it's very cool. It is great for watching movies... just enough indirect light.
What is the end hieght of your TR, both to ceiling and to bottom of clouds? It looks a little under 8'... does it feel small?
The hard ceiling is just under 8, and the clouds are about 3 or 4 inches down from there. It doesn't feel too small... the window to the CR is big, and there the window to the outside in there too, so it's not claustrophobic. It would've been nice to go up higher, but there was just no way with the existing structure.
Sure is impressive. Marc
Thanks! It was a lot of work. But, really worth it. Both for the end result and the process. I really learned a lot...
