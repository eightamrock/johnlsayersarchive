dymaxian8 June 2004#2
That layout will work, depending on what you'll be recording. You sure don't have to worry about parallel surfaces. The only 2 things I can see about it (without any dimensions) are that 1 the kitchen/lounge area doesn't leave much apparent room to stand or sit around. Since you have double doors on each end, and the room isn't much wider than the doors, it makes the whole room feel like a corridor, and putting cabinets/refrigerators/couches/chairs in there will make it seem like you're just obstructing it. If you can make it a foot or two wider than the doors, it'd help a little bit, but having a single 3' wide door on each end- not in the middle of the wall, but near the corners- would make it seem more like a 'room' in it's own right. 2 that iso booth looks pretty big in comparison to everything else. Is that meant to be a drum booth? I'm usually recording rock bands, so the first thing in my mind is to make enough room in the main tracking room for everyone. I'd steal some of the space back from the booth and give it to tracking. Just thinking out loud. It's tough to really analyze a floor plan without knowing how big everything is going to be...
