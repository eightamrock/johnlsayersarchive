gullfo26 March 2021#2
if you don't use the heater, then the basement. long room, better ratios from the appearance. and you won't have to get rid of the tv and other furniture to make the upstairs room work. also likely better isolation in the basement. if you do need the heater in the basement - i'd make a "chimney" of plywood so the heat is pass up and above where you're mixing desk is to avoid having the heat cause acoustic issues and of course safer for your equipment. then treat front, side reflection, and ceiling cloud as necessary, and also the back wall. on door, back wall, and sides in back, to avoid slap echoes. probably a total of 12 absorbers - 100mm thick x 600mm wide x 1200mm tall. stand off from walls and ceiling - 100mm 3 front, 4 side, 2 ceiling, 3 back
