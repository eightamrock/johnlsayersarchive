Without having a system to review, I would go with a concrete or brick wall to get the mass for LF isolation. No SPL data doesn't help. If staying with gypsum build, use another layer of drywall to replace the bloc stuff. Green glue is more effective than rc. Rockwool is mineral wool. Andre

Its impossible to answer all that with what I now of the project. Around here, ignoring the border, which is similar to your area, concrete block is actually a little bit cheaper (5-10%) than drywall for wall construction. If they are going to get decent LF isolation with dywall, it will be significantly more expensive than having concrete as one of the numerous leafs. Yes, I am taking for granted at least a triple leaf system. Working with cost, GG is much more expensive than rc. Steel studs is getting into a strange question. I am think it popped up while you were active here, but rc was created to give steel stud like acoustic performance from wood stud walls. IOW steel studs = wood studs + rc.
