jbassino20 May 2009#4
My friend, Unless you either have the knowledge, experience or even the TOOLS (or machines ! ) to build a house/room whatever from stratch, I suggest you get in touch with professionals. Thats unlikely a DIY for sure! First you must pour a solid concrete foundation, and then decide (within your local building codes / culture) how you want to build the house. What do you normally use? Wood and drywall? Concrete? Brickwalls? Also, thats going to be expensive. So first get in touch with a contractor/builder and get a quote for the dimentions you are wishing for Furthermore, if you don't have any neighbours and you just want one room, maybe you could save some money from building a MAM room, because if you create a thick enough (concrete?) structure, it will hopefully get you pretty isolated from the outside world. Do you have storms? How loud is the ambient noise there? Dogs? Cows? Lightings? You must know these factors in order to determine which kind of construction you're aiming to. If your land is pretty quiet, like i said, with a solid concrete or brickwall building you could be good. Now, about if you want two or just one room. Who can answer that question if not YOU? You are the only guy that know what YOU want. In my case, I KNOW that i want two rooms, because it works for ME. and not because it will sound BETTER or be COOLER. So, in conclution, first think what you want, what are your needs (are you going to record a drummer? are you going to work completely alone? are you going to record LOUD guitar amps? Personally, recording a loud guitar amp next to me, using headphones.. SUCKS, so you will definately need another room. On the other side, if you only want to compose songs, play with midi stuff, record some guitars here and there with guitar rig, make cool songs you know etc, so then you need just one room. Cheers -Javier

jbassino20 May 2009#12
Then build a room with suggested ratios. Now, how big it will be? Its up to you (or more likely, to your budget) Any reasonably large room by those ratios, will work. Now, you must decide if you want a 5m ceiling, o 3m. After you get a quotation for that room (rectangular), You should build an inner timber structure to splay walls to a certain angle, support insulation for slots or bass traps, space for ducts /HVAC etc. Here is thousands of ideas and already designed studios with splayed walls, so you must spend a few days eating this forum to get your mind full of creative ideas. For a starting point, splay your walls 6 degrees each, as long with your ceiling.
