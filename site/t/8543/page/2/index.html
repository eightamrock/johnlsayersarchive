Hai Ork! Nice building progress so far, I'm sure yer delighted to finally have some GB walls up eh. After all that planning. good to see it happen! 'Bout the acoustic panels, you'd better have the questions outline in the Acoustics forum. The answer you're prolly get is to get a fire proof fabric. Like jute etc. Ask your local fabric dealer, it comes in various forms, colours and thickness. I recently asked for the same stuff and my local dealer got me a list of fabrics, prices etc which are kinda fire-proof and not to dense (depends on application) So, shop around.
