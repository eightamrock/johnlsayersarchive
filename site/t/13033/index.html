John Sayers13 May 2009#3
That's a nice space you have Justin - you can adequately fit a control room, live room and drum booth in that space. The question you have to start asking yourself is how do I want to access the upstairs over the control room. The obvious is to add a staircase in the studio, solved, or is it? what if you are recording a friends arrive and want to go upstairs? do you stop the recording session and let them tromp through the studio, tripping on mike cables etc? My advice would be to move the control room to the right and put a door in the bottom left corner that allows for a staircase up. The staircase could dog-leg over a lower ceiling-ed vox booth - to rise to 12ft requires a long staircase.
