Soundman202022 July 2010#4
What would you do to make the best of this room?
Personally, I would move the booth to one of the rear corners, and move the desk up to the front wall! The way you have it, I reckon you'd have some strange things going on with those huge asymmetrical cavities behind your speakers. :shock: You also have your head way too far back in the room, and too close the the rear wall/ door. I'd also replace the "diffuser" on the door with absorption, since the room isn't really large enough to benefit from diffusers. - Stuart -
