dymaxian30 August 2004#2
You mean design a recording studio to suit this room? It'd be pretty easy, I'd think. 23'x83' is a pretty nice space, and 15' ceilings will give you plenty of headroom. Do you have more specific questions? Or do you want us to design the whole thing for you? Someone here will probably post a design, but we'll need to know more about the space- existing door and window locations, where that dock is, etc- and exactly what you need out of a studio (multi-studios with control and tracking for each? I'd think we'd be able to fit 2 decent size control room/ tracking room pairs in there comfortably, maybe 3 if we had to). If you sketched out the space as it is now, that'd be the biggest help. And take a close look at the construction of the existing walls- if we know what they're made out of, we'll have a better idea of what our design will need. The more you tell us, the more we can help.
