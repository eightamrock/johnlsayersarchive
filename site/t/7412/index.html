Not too bad, looks like your CR is sorta squished tho - no speaker shown, soffited or otherwise, and if you want a 60 degree monitoring setup the chair/desk will need to be further back in the room (or the front of the room pushed toward the live rooms) Doors - sliders make things open, which is really nice - but decent ones are expensive and getting harder to find in this country anyway - almost everything is double pane, so two of 'em make a 4-leaf wall :cry: Swinging doors - less open feel, better seal, cheaper, need room to swing. Don't make the mistake of thinking a raised panel door is what we mean by "solid core", some have. You want a full, solid, 1-3/4" slab door minimum per opening, adding sheet lead or MDF can help even more. Corner traps - that small (if they're to scale) won't begin to be noticeable. Figure on at least 3 feet across the face, 4's better. If you don't want to take up all your corners, consider wall/ceiling corner traps instead in some areas. Reverb time - http://johnlsayers.com/Pages/Reverb_Calc.htm for CR, shoot for even RT of around .2-.3 seconds, longer in live rooms; somewhere around .4 is about right. Keep at it, you've got a good start... Steve
