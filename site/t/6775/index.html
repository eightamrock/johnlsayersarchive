My basement studio design has been changed so I thought I'd start a new post. Here is an sketchup plan. There will now be a wall dividing the room for storage and isolating the A/C and water heater without getting too hot. This is not a real problem space-wise as I am mostly mobile and will not do too many full bands in this space, although it is still a decent size. The "vocal booth" gobo system will be a homemade modular affair with covered fiberglass or foam wedges on casters and have plexi-glass windows set at an angle. The floor is covered in rubber backed carpet squares (low nap). I am planning on hanging clouds over the mix position and booth. The block walls are painted and treated. Please give me advice on treatment and positioning. Construction needs to be started soon. Thanks in advance.

rnewton2 September 2006#9
Well, some things have changed and some more advice is needed. The layout will be essentially the same, but my question with that is; what would be the optimum desk placement?? The changes are the booth treatment will not have the gobo system I had thought of as I won't have the budget to make them for now. I will be using sound blankets across the opening instead (perhaps with a vinyl window installed in one). The panels and other treatment will actually be better than I thought originally as my wife and I have come up with what we think is a good design consisting of a layer of soundboard, an airspace, another layer of soundboard, and fiberglass batts for both the booth walls and probably the bass traps in the corners of the room. Other panels will probably just be single-layer soundboard with fiberglass. There will also be a bent wood reflecter/diffuser/trap in the middle of the back wall panels. Oh yeah. There will also be a loveseat against the back wall and a large chair on the sheetrock wall toward the back corner (making an L with the loveseat).
