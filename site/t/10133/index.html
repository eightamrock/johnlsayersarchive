Cram9 February 2008#4
simply put the common and advised line in the forum here from people with vast experience in this construction - ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- Frame a wall with baseplate nailed/screwed into concrete. Insulate in between. RC1 - (this is the main part of the decoupling.) Drywall layer(s) that are off of the concrete and sealed off by a bead of caulk along with backer rod. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- Compare the cost for thin strips of metail used in that example to the cost of neo pads where you'd have to calculate how much weight the layers and leafs (is the ceiling supported by your frame?). So the first method as GuitarDad72 highlights as well is less expensive in labor and time and is less error prone. That's where the rubber meets the road with these projects..
