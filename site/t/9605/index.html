xSpace22 October 2007#7
The room is located above an existing bar, so we have jukebox noise coming up at night, don’t know if it’s through the floor or the HVAC system?
What would it take to find out? Realistically these types of noise infiltrations need to be addressed. I would suspect that the hvac chase is a conduit for the sound coming from below. Only one way to identify this and that is to open up a piece of the wall around the bump out. You may find that there is nothing to stop the transfer of sound from behind the wall to your area. Personally, I do not have, like nor want flourescent lighting. And the older the ballast is the less I like it. They are noisy and I cannot really see using them:) It reads like, overall, you are OK with whatever sound is coming into this space. If it can come in it can also exit. This is when we find out how tolerant our neighbors are:) I can't help ya with design but no mention of isolation was made...so I made some:)
