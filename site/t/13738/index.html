Good Evening PK1, I don't have much to offer in the way of help, but it should be helpful ;) My thinking here is that this shed does not have the capabilities to support much more weight then it already has. Is that a fair assessment? The best you can do with this space is to add insulation and sheetrock on the inside.But I caution you that you should consider the help of someone local that has experience in building...local to you. Fact is, this type of structure cannot support much more weight then it already is supporting. BUT, it can maintain the additional items like insulation and sheetrock. But then you have a concern with getting fresh air in and stale air out, warm air in, in the cold climate, cold air in, in the warm climate...
