Greetings, What a thrill to find this site! A dream come true. Thank you for all your generous help. I just bought a tiny house in East Northport, New York on Long Island. After much discussion with the wife I have been moved to a little dormer room upstairs. Until I can build a studio from scratch in the back yard I am tired of fighting with her and will use this as my studio space. Its so small! I can barely stand up. The door opens out into the hallway, not into the room. The walls are old gypsum board with a plaster layer that is peeling because the glue that binds it to the board has failed. There is an oak floor. I am a violinist/Composer so I'm not loud. Its a quiet neighborhood and I have two little windows. I can hear the fire siren and the Long Island Railroad. I want to leave the windows alone for light and air so I don't get clausterphobic. My budget is 1000 to 2000. I had a brand new electric service put in the house and will want a clean line directly from the utility box in the basement and would like some suggestions regarding power I can tell the electrician. All the construction work I do myself. Here is a drawing and a funky photomerge I did in photoshop. Thank you! Matt Pierce www.matthewpierce.com
