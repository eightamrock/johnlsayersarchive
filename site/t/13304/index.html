Acle23 July 2009#1
Finally finished it today! Thought i'd share some pics as I learnt alot about the DIY panels on this forum. Theres still a few things to do like the main light, mood lights, a couple of more panels possibly and some finishing touches. It looks a little like a sauna haha but its sweet when you're in there. I have some left over insulation which I may use to make some more panels to go behind the monitors. I listened to some CD's today and WOW. I really recommened treating your room if it isn't when it comes to mixing...or just listening to music in general! Has a really nice immersive ambience to it which should be great for being absorbed into the music when it comes to writing. I can hear alot of detail I was missing before and can actually hear what my KRK's are pumping out. I listened to Michael Jackson (because the produciton is amazing) and dam I can hear a vertical depth which I havn't heard before. Like the vocals coming from high up, the guitars low down and drums/everything else in the middle. Spatial depth is alot clearer now also...like the room sounds/reverbs. Its not perfect by a long shot as its only a small room but its a dam big improvement on what i'm used to.
