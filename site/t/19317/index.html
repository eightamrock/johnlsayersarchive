There are no "magical" materials that can beat the laws of physics, unfortunately. It's not just the materials you use, but also the way you use them. Done properly, two sheets of drywall can far outperform a single sheet of lead of the same total weight.... Search the forum for "fully decoupled 2-leaf MSM", and you'll find many references on how to go about isolating your space. Basically, you need to build a complete isolation system around the room, not just one layer of something massive. - Stuart -
