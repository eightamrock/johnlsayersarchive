rod gervais31 May 2007#2
Nymo, 1st - you must have a home depot on the coast...... they sell rolled lead in 18" wide sheets - which you can use side by each to get the job done. 2nd - I thin the addition of green glue can only enhance the isolating value of the door. 3rd - I am not crazy about the idea of a 1/4" door facing - you would be much better off with a finished faced piece of 3/4" 4th - I m a bit confused by your termonoology - the inner face of a door is always the face that colses to you - it has nothing to do with what room you are standing in. If the door opens into the control room - then the inner face (the face not flush with the room finish) is in the space outside the control room. ytou have a drawing in the book that shows exactly what you have to do - just follow it - obviously you can't do that in the control room if that is the flush side of the door. you aren't building a diode here - the isolation is exactly the same from both sides...... it doesn't matter how the door opens......... I do not understand when you say this:
I have sorted a drop down bottom seal. But what of the actual door seal ?? Are you recommending an professional adjustable acoustic seal or are you saying just put trunk rubber on the inner jamb part ??
PLease explain better what you mean....... Rod
