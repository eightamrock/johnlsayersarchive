AVare28 September 2005#8
Your welcome. If you look at the 5.1 surround recommendations, note the locations vary from 110 to ~150 degrees of axis. For 7.1 use the two extremes for the side and rear surround. Soffit mounting is achievable, but very cumbersome in requiring additional detail in locations. As you get firmer in your ideas and post them, no doubt more responses will start to happen. The vaguer ( a word?) a query is, the more difficult it is to respond to concisely. Also many people have my habit of following a thread and unles I have something specific to add to, I let the main responders handle the discussion, knowing that in the course of the thread my thoughts will be brought out eventually. Just not int he exact same manner that I would have presented them. Personally I suggest add tot his thread, it maintains a continuity on the project from inception. Good and looking forward to more from you! Andre
