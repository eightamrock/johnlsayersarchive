I Annoy wrote:
Am I missing something or am I better off using Roxul safe n Sound for my traps and absorbers. The low end absorbtion seems to be better than OC703 and its cheaper and more readily available.
Both products work well, so buy the one that costs less or is more readily available. Rock wool is not as nice to work with as rigid fiberglass, and it tends to sag over time. If $$$ matters more than anything else, RW does a good job. Also, what you're missing is that standard acoustic data like this is not nearly as precise as you'd hope. Lab results can vary as much as 50 percent at 125 Hz, even when the exact same material is tested. --Ethan
