EK Sound7 June 2008#2
Hello, The only thing you really need to *mix* in 5.1, is the surround monitors and an audio interface capable of 6 simultaneous outputs. Each channel would be routed to an output and subsequent monitor speaker. Encoders are only used to create 5.1 mix files, but mostly, studios deliver "stems" (seperate files for each channel) to the authoring facility that is doing the "layback" to picture. As for monitors, there are lots to choose from. The size and acoustics of your mix environment will dictate what monitor would work best, but you should use 5 identical speakers for this task (plus the sub of course). Dave.
