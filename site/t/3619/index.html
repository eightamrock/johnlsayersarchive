I have read every entry in this forum and cannot locate the pix. Does this ring a bell for anyone? Thank you for any suggestions (other than for me to take my medication and go to bed... :lol: ). Also...I read a question about vapor barriers under a floated floor and wanted to mention that I sealed by cement floor with "Miracle Glaze", a water curing (single component) polyurethane coating that contractors use for commercial buildings. It is a Diamond Vogel product. My reasons for doing it this way were: 1. Minimize wood damage due to water vapor, and 2. Avoid the slippage encountered with plastic sheeting material. Hope this was not a giant mistake!! Eric
