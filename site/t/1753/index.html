John Sayers5 June 2004#2
Looks like you've got rooms with built in bass traps :) all that paneling will act as bass traps. I'd build free standing gobos (8 x 4) either vertically or horizontally made from MDF covered with 703 and cargo. Use them to break up all the parallel walls in the live room, to block off the kitchen and to tame the highs. Have one side MDF (bright) and the other side 703 (dead) I'd also put some 703 panels (hang the cargo over them) on the walls behind you in the control room as you suggested. cheers john
