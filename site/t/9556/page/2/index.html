No Problem, happy to help So if you consider that the Indoor Unit has a Fan assembly thats going to "Send" conditioned air and "Return" stale air. Normally, the indoor unit would fit above the celing and literally pump direct into the room (at least thats what the mistubishi manuals described in their diagrams). So if you put the indoor unit away from the celing, then as you say you need a plenum. What I did was had a local steel company fabricate a plenum chamber that had a 6" Send and a 6" Return and I also has them attach a 4" Fresh Air feed on the return side. So the idea is that you blend the fresh air with the retrun air which then gets conditioned. The whole thing cost about £40. I gave them the indoor unit and a week later picked it up with plenum attached. I removed and made sure there was no swarf and also sealed the plenum with acoustic sealant for good measure. The fresh air connection currently feeds direct from within the external structure i built. One of the outstanding tasks is to finish this of with external cladding and a section of 4" ducting to actual outside air. I introduced a barometric pressure relief (as per Rods book) but to be honest, since my HVAC design was not scientific and considering that I'm not a professional builder, then i suspect theres a bit of leakage here and there. However, I would love to do a pressure test, according to a builder friend of mind, this is required by building regulations now in the UK. Certainly, isolation from outside is stunning (IMHO)
