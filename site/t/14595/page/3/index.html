Riff17 May 2011#62
Here's the readings I got when I checked the isolation. The bigger number is with the PA on. The smaller number is with the PA off with ambient noise. You can see where the doors are weak spot. In the closet on the bottom of the page the readings are a lot higher than the storage room on the right side where there are no direct doors to the studio. The sound is very obvious in the garage but once I walked down the driveway towards the road, I couldn't hear it at all. There was no sound at all anywhere in the backyard. This proves that a four leaf system will work IF the air gap is large enough. From inside the studio to outside I have 2 layers of 5/8" sheetrock, ~8" air gap, 2 layers of 5/8" sheetrock, BIG air gap, 1 layer of 1/2" sheetrock, 3.5" air gap, OSB layer, outside brick wall. The closet is a little over 4 feet and the storage room is right at 7 feet. Inside the house, in the hall and room that connect to the garage, I could hear a slight bass rumble. Further into the house, I could hear no sound at all. So far, everything is working perfectly, except the spring door hinges which is easily fixable. :D Whoo!
