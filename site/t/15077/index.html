If it is any help, I often have to run a couple of dozen stage lighting cables close to my audio snake at live events. Those lights are on dimmers, and the electrical noise coming off those things must be pretty drastic when they are dimmed. I very rarely get any hum from that setup. The few times I've had hum, it has been due to grounding issues, shared power circuits, and once or twice due to some "clever" musician deciding it was easier to plug his guitar amp into the lighting circuits, "because it was much closer than the plug you told me to use...". You shouldn't have too much of a problem in your setup. Balanced audio cable is, by nature, highly resistant to interference.
