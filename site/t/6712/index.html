rod gervais27 July 2006#3
Cami, it won't even help with impact noise (foot fall) - in fact - it will probably make it even worse. Because of the fact that this is a high impact hard plastic base - all of the energy will now be transmitted through the plastic "spikes" that connect with your floor........... bad idea - I would suppose it works well (however) for it's intended purpose - which is comfort - not isolation. Rod
