Read Rod's book cover to cover and have a question about adding mass to a floor with drywall. I'm referring to the method of adding drywall between joists in a basement studio ceiling. I notice that you want to 'seal' the edges and wonder if there are the same problems inherent in walls/doors that aren't sealed properly. More specifically, my joists include a bunch of HVAC venting that feeds the house above. So there would be gaps and areas that wouldn't be reachable to apply the drywall to. Would this be a 'wasted' step in this case? Would I be better suited to just use a hat channel/double layer of drywall (or Green Glue / drywall) combo with insulation above? Mark
