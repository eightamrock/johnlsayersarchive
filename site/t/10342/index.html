gullfo21 March 2008#9
no, a single wall will not do more for isolation than a double wall. drums = 100db+ "silent" control room = 15db you'd need at least 85db of isolation. :shock: IR-761 should be your first source for identifying tested wall constructs that will meet your isolation needs. probably want to carefully consider how much isolation you really need.

AVare21 March 2008#11
Mass loaded vinyl is waste of money in this application. The money would be better spent on extra layers of drywall and/or isolation clips like the RSIC. The most difficult part of sound isolation is the bass frequencies. As you study IR 761, bear that in mind. Keep asking questions. The more you know, the less likely that you will mistakes. Good luck! Soundly, Andre
