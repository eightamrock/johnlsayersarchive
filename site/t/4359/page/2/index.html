Hi Def23 November 2005#34
Hey "len-morgan"! I mainly did the solid front wall because that is what I am used to. It may not be the correct way but...! All of my studios control rooms always had that same type of vibe with the soffit mounted speakers in a pine covered wall. To me I notice that it freaks the uppermids a little but, again, I'm used to it and doesn't bother me one bit. Because I have a fair amount of absorption on the back walls, It helps quite a bit with the reflections. As well, and I'm no expert here, because of the crazy shape of the front wall there is alot of sound difussed away from me at mix position. Its not a flat wall. Its angled and splayed! Hopefully that little bit helped.
