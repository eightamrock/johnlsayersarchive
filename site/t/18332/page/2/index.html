Soundman20209 February 2014#26
are you refering to the outer room? that is based on one of bolts' golden ratios
No, I'm referring to the final dimensions inside the final complete room, as measure from the hard surface of the inner-leaf in all directions. The ratio of the outer-leaf is not relevant. All acoustics of the room itself happen within the inner-leaf, not the outer leaf. The dimensions of the outer leaf as compared to those of the inner leaf are only useful for calculating MSM isolation.

The easiest way is to use a stethoscope. If you have a friend who is a doctor or nurse, then borrow one from them.
