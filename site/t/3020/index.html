8) anyone out there looking to buy any rack mount furniture, i highly suggest building them yourself! what would probably cost $300.00 or so cost me all of about $75.00 !!! i bought a 4x8 piece of cabinet grade oak plywood from lowes ($45.00) and stain, and polyurathane ($16.00) 2 sets of rack rails around $20.00. out of that i got 2 rack mount cabinets each able to hold up to 8 pieces of gear in the top, and 2 power amps (or 4 more pieces of gear) in the bottom! this what my son, and i came up with in about 4 hours!!!

Congratulations - it always feels good to build something of your own - One thing you may want to add to your racks while they still look square - some sort of corner bracing - otherwise, the first time someone leans on them from the side you'll have a rhombus. I know the gear itself will add some stiffening, but it's much better if the box can stay square on its own - If you don't want to use wood (a 6-inch wide piece of ply across the bottom rear, held in with screwed/glued cleats will work) you might look for http://doityourself.com/store/6792261.htm Either way, doing this will keep your racks looking good for a lot longer...
