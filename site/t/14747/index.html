Soundman20206 September 2010#3
Adding to what Leon already said: the title of Rod's book is "Home Recording Studio: Build it Like the Pros". Very much worthwhile buying! The other book I'd recommend is the "Bible" of acoustics: "Master Handbook of Acoustics", by F. Alton Everest. With those you will get a solid foundation in acoustics and in basic studio design. They sure did help me! - Stuart -
