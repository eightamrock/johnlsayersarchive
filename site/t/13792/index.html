i think i have an even better idea now. cotton batt insulation is cheap. about .70/sqft or cheaper. it's easy to work with, way less toxic, and supposed to absorb 30% more sound then fiberglass batts. the sound spec is from the manufacturer's website and probably meant from within the wall cavity. bet they do even better uncovered. seems panels and lots of other acoustic absorbers could be made out of this stuff..although no one is doing it yet to my knowledge. it's also mold and fire retardant. treated with a borate compound which is also pretty safe. in fact they even suggest it could be installed raw on the website. seems pretty awesome and it's renewable. the fiberglass shown above has a smell to it. and is a pain to install. another item i came across that may be useful for people like me is a frost blanket for crops. it's super breathable, pretty strong, very very cheap. only issues i see are limited colors (white) and possible gassing out. but i don't know about that for certain. and they are rated for 10 degrees so a thermal insulator as well. i do have 6 traditional acoustic panels and 4 corner traps. so putting up a cloud is a possibility.
