Deluks5 August 2005#2
It's the quantity and speed of the fans that all adds up to make a racket, I'd suggest getting a fan speed controller for a front bay in your case, or fitting a Zalman fanmate to each fan and lowering the speed by about 50%, be sure to keep an eye on temps though. The graphics card is usually the noisiest culprit, swap it out for a passive cooled Geforce4 or ATI equivalent, or get a low noise or passive cooler for it. Again see Zalman or Arctic Cooling for ideas. For ultimate isolation build an mdf box lined with rock or glasswool, and then another mdf box inside of that with your PC in it. Cut a hole for a 12cm case fan at the back of the inner box and connect it to your motherboard, then fit a vent to the top of the outer box so heat is driven out the back of the inner box, then just rises from the external box. oh yeh, don't be fooled into spending loads on computer case acoustic foam, it doesn't help all that much in my experience.
Tomas6 August 2005#3
There are components for "silencing" your computer. Be warned that it is not a cheap solution. Here is a list of fans or other noisy stuff that you can take care of: Mother board, look for design with passive cooling. 0dB. Vide card. There are video cards with passive cooling. If you want some power there, I believe Gigabyte Geforce 6800 is one of such cards. You could put passive cooler like Zalman ZM80D-HP on existing card. That is 0dB. Processor. Good cooler (like thermaltake silent tower) with fan control can make it quite silent. Yet better solution is water cooling, but you should read many reviews as some ready made water cooling systems have fans and/or pums that make noise themselves! I have DIY water cooling and noise level is none, because I got good water pump used for aquarium water pumping, that is really silent. But water cooling is not very convenient. Another solution is DIY PASSIVE cpu cooling, based on heatpipes. I have read description of one successful project, but it was in russian. My friend is doing it right now, if he succeeds I will make one for myself. This would mean 0dB from CPU. Power supply. Get fanless power supply, for example Yesico FL-480(T). It's 0dB. Case fans. What's that? Really, I use none and it works somehow, althought it's one hell inside. No fans - 0dB. But if it overheats, you could put one or two 12cm fans powered by 5V, it's should be impossible to hear at 1m distance. Now the hardest part where is impossible to avoid moving parts - hard drives. HUGE reduction in noise level is decoupling them from case. I use Zalman ZM-2HC2 it helps drives to dissipate heat more efficiently and has dampers to keep noise down. I will try to make a double isolated box and put heatpipes damped in silicone sealant to keep it cool inside after I finish my sound treatment for the room. If any success I will post it in these forums. When I swich my PC on I get only ~1-2dB above ambient noise at listening position (and those are 4 harddrives). Hope that will get you started.
