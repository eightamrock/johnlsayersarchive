Idealmind81 August 2011#2
its been a couple of days since I last posted but I got a lot done. Completely finished the demo 2nd Framing is done Drywall is up on the outer leaf Started caulking with acoustic seal...and wow...I wouldn't want to wish this on my worst enemy, I think I've been caulking for a total of 11 or so hours and still not done. Also a couple of things I learned for next time... 1. Put up your drywall on the outer leaf first, then start caulking and sealing before you build your second Frame. (Probably the reason its taking so long to seal the outer leaf, dancing between the staggered frames) 2. Always buy the heavy duty caulking gun... 3. Dont let your wife help you put up drywall.....
