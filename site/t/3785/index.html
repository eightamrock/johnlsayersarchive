knightfly26 May 2005#2
Plywood isn't quite as dense as hardboard, but it should still work; wrap it in insulation, duct tape every 2 feet (like a belt to hold your pants up) and hang them in a herringbone pattern inside your traps, close enough together so the insulation just touches from one panel to the next (it doesn't HAVE to touch, but it won't hurt) Steve
