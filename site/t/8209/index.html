Here is what I am including in the build: 2000 sqft live room w/ large control Adr/voice over room w/ separate control room two editing suites (no real sound proof needed) Lounge/ dinette 3 to 4 offices Conference room Reception Video viewing room if room permits The current space is 60x118 feet.
