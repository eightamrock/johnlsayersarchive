gullfo12 January 2011#6
the walls in the small studio design posted - if you look closely - the dividing wood walls are "inside-out", the drywall is on the cavity side between them - this is the isolation mass. you need to have some mass - air - mass between the rooms to get any isolation. this includes a wall to the interior side of the basement too. the exterior walls may already be suitable as part of the MAM (MSM) equation. then on the inside of the interior walls - rigid insulation (703 or equiv) and a variety of treatment options like slats, cloth coverings, etc.
