Ethan Winer13 February 2009#4
If this is the best you can get with placement only, that means you need more bass traps. The entire front and rear wall are good places, as are wall-floor and wall-ceiling corners. --Ethan

Ethan Winer16 February 2009#6
Bass traps don't have to make the room too dead at mid and high frequencies. For example, rigid fiberglass with thin cardboard attached in front absorbs bass but not highs. --Ethan
