Just wondering if anyone has made any high resolution low-end frequency response measurements in their home studios. I've been playing around with the ETF acoustic software (www.etfacoustic.com), and the results are really interesting. I want to do a complete series of before and after graphs after my insulation traps are installed. I've seen plenty of frequency curves in both treated and non-treated rooms, but never any of the same room measured before and then after bass trapping was applied. Does anyone have any direct comparisons of their rooms they would like to share? I think small-room measurement comparisons like this would go a long way in figuring out exactly what works and what doesn't, and how it works, rather than relying on subject listening tests. -casey
