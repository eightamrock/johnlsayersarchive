Good morning! I am really stuck between a rock and a hard place on this one. We are in the middle of building a commercial rehearsal space and are trying to balance solid sound isolation against city/state code. We had to go with a title 24 (mandatory energy compliance) which only allows us 1.2 watts/sq.ft. We have a 4 room complex. each room is approx. 180 sq.ft. This only allots us 170-180 watts of lighting per room. We would have liked to have gone with track lighting but even the low voltage tracks would have only allowed us a maximum of 3-4 bulbs. The title 24 is based off of potential ballast/transformer usage not the actual wattage of the bulb. We were also denied surface mount lights due to sprinkler compliance. Here is our room design

bstephens6 wrote:
We were also denied surface mount lights due to sprinkler compliance.
Althuogh I can understand that your lights cannot sit side by each with a sprinkler head - it cannot be possible that you can't use surface mounted fixtures - fit within the layout of the sprikler head grid - and still meet code. Whoever is telling you this is mistaken. everything that creates an obstruction has to do with distance of travel (from the head) AND the size of the obstruction itself - . It just isn't as cut and dry as they are telling you....
We are being forced to go with 26 watt fluorescent can lights, 6-8 per room. This completely compromises our sound isolation. It is too late in the process to go back for redesign so now we need to find a way to work with these.
I don't think it's going to be as big a deal as you're making it out to be Use 6" recessed cans andjust insulate the heck out of them with thermafiber (you'll need cans certified to allow the insulation). Rod
