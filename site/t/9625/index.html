You have to take the frame apart - it should be screwed at the 4 corners - and probably stuck together with an adhesive caulk- but once you remove all of the screws - you should be able to work it apart. If it's a wood frame - and put together with staples - then you will have to remove the staples in order to do it - But - either way - you have to take the frame apart. Everything begins there. Rod
