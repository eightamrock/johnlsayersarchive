Alex, your drawings still aren't very clear on where things are; is this garage door on the lower right side, opening to the right? Size-wise, you'll have enough room for a decent mix area if you use the low ceilinged space for your vox booth - while that low ceilings aren't ideal, you can help modal issues somewhat by making a larger footprint than normal to compensate for the lower ceiling.
