HEre is a ceiling view in the old ISO room, now to be the CO. You can see the drop ceiling in which I filled them with 5/8 gypsum and caulked. THis is the only way I could block flanking sound coming from the other rooms in the building. Above those drop ceiling frames is an open space that connects to the intire rest of the building. You can see the air ducts that were waiting for me! 8) The wall to the left I drywalled to the top so I have a compete seal.
