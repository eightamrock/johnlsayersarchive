In my opinion you are trying to use a kind of RFZ shape room that is used in a situation where the speakers are placed into the walls and in your case you have the speakers ahead of the front wall Therefore I would suggest 1) Make the front of the room absorptive to reduce the front wall reflection 2) "Bass traps" on all corners of the room 3) Diffusers or a mix of diffusers/absorbers in the rear wall, depending on the dimension of the room (which I cannot find) 4) Side walls I would use typical acoustical panels placed away from the wall 5) ceiling I would also place acoustical panels placed away from it (and these quit thick) What do you mean by sounding odd at lower mids ? Have you made any measurements in the room ?
