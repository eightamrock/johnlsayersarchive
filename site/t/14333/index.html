limsa-terry12 September 2010#8
Small update. I've been mostly working to beef up my outer leaf walls. I used 2 layers of 13mm (1/2") drywall strips between the studs. I know 16mm (5/8") would be far more better but I manage to bought those 13mm drywall sheets almost free. And I'm not looking for a perfect isolation. Only little improvement is fine. Both layers are caulked and sealed. I used backer rod and acrylic sealant. Insulation back in place. Thin metal wire holds insulation in the cavity. Second outer wall beef up is done. If I forgot to mention, I used finishing nails to hold drywall strips in the cavity. I'm gaining my ceiling insulation for extra 50mm. So overall thickness is 250mm at outer ceiling and 70mm at inner ceiling. Total of 320mm. It's a minimum recomentation for ceiling insulation thickness here in Finland... Next step is to remove old garage door and replace it with regular outdoor... -janne
