It's been over two months since my last post :? Not much studio construction over that time.. Me and my wife were two week vacation in Mexico and 12 days in Scotland :D . Last winter I promised to my wife that I build a greenhouse for her right after all the snow goes away.. ..so I had to build it... Pic 1. shows you the proof :D Well there happened something in my studio also :twisted: Pics. 2-5. I've been working on my inner isolation door. Firstly I made a sketchup model of it (I could post it here if someone likes to see it). I used old bathroom door which I manage to get free of charge from my neighbour. It was not solid core door so I had to "beef it up". I fillet it up with 2 layers of 15mm drywall and a layer of 12,5mm drywall. It got pretty heavy. My cat was ispecting the results :D I covered it up with a layer of plywood. Pic. 6. Door frame is made from 45x125mm pine. I used same lumber in my ceiling panels. I used four quality adjustable hinges. I can move the door up or down if needed. Pics. 7-10. Heavygoddamn door lifted, installed and adjusted in place :twisted: Pic. 11-12. I found that there are smaller gabs on hinge side of the jamb than the other side, so I ended up using O-profile EPDM rubber seals on both jambs. It can be used in 1-7mm gaps. I installed four cupboard style magnets (4kg pull load each) two up on the frame and two down. I bought also a door closer which is designed for 60kg doors. I'm gonna install it in place next weekend. I hope that it closes the door perfectly against the seals and the magnets do their tricks to finish it perfectly airtight.. -janne

I made a little mistake :oops: That Finnfoam closed cell foam thermal insulation which I bought was wrong choice. I cutted one package to right siced pieces and covered half the floor. Fortunately I tried to lay couple of drywalls over that and found that the result was way too hard sounding floor. Like a drum head... I returned unopeden Finnfoam packages to the store and bought 50mm rigid Paroc stone wool slabs with density 80-105kg/m3 http://192.49.230.181/CSharpSite/extran ... ublic=true Way much better now! :D I'll post floor construction pics soon.
Soundman202019 August 2011#48
That Finnfoam closed cell foam thermal insulation which I bought was wrong choice.
Yup! Not only that, but CLOSED CELL foam is not very useful in acoustics anyway! Glad you got rid of it: it would not have helped with your floor. (Fine for thermal insulation: not so fine for acoustic isolation).
50mm rigid Paroc stone wool slabs with density 80-105kg/m3
Much better, but probably a bit too dense. It will still work, but density of around 50 kg/m3 is better. - Stuart -
