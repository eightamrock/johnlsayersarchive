wow, great progress and cool space but Janne, why didn't you use a foil for the ceiling? The rockwool will trickle trough the fabric caused by bass from the ceiling :horse:
limsa-terry18 June 2012#154
Yeah that's a good point, but if I'd used plastic foil before the fabric then I'll end up with two vapor barriers on my ceiling... I though that if I use thicker molton fabric Weight: 300g per sq/mtr. that might keep most of the rockwool dust away from the air..
