lowswing wrote:
1.the speaker are alway soffit mount, is it the only way to go in this sizes of control room? (my speakers are dynaudio bm15a, what do you think on soffit them?)
soffit mount is the best way to mount a speaker - I haven't tried the BM15a - maybe someone else on this site has tried it???
2. the transparent moving door is there in all your designs, very classy indeed but ain't it super expensive? not too reflective? really isolate as good?
The sliding glass doors offer great communication/visual between rooms - provided they are placed correctly their reflections don't offer a problem - in fact they keep the highs moving in the room.
