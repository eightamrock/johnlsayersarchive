Dobz, welcome; I know you gave your location in the post itself, but as threads get longer, things get lost; so you still need to edit your profile and include your location there; that way it appears under your name for each post. Second, actual measurements would help figure out what's doable, along with details on the existing construction, surrounding neighbors/noise control needs, etc - see the very top link in this forum for a checklist (each BOLD point is something that helps everyone figure things out) Third, I think you left off a zero in your square footage; probably more like 3000? Typically the cheapest one can build acoustic space for is around $50-60 per square foot, and that's with 8 foot ceilings and completely DIY - so your size facility should cost around $120-$150,000 for materials alone, possibly a bit less if your outer shell is already heavy mass and pretty well-sealed. If you go thru that top link I mentioned, answer all you possibly can, things may start to look a bit more rosy but this stuff ain't cheap or fast generally; good luck... Steve
