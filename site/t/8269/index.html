my 1st comment would be relating to the lack of symmetry in your control room. A symmetrical control room is KEY for proper stereo imaging. So begin there.......... Next - with the space you have I think you woud be better off loosing the vocal booth and leaving the open space....... otherwise the speace between the 2 is pretty wasted - not a whole lot you can do in there. Actualy - looking at this begining with an empty room - you would probably get your best return on the investment making a combination control room / tracking room because the space is soooooo small.
