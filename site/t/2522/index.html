Huh on the price remark. Yhick absorbent material IS what many traps are! Fiberglass/ rockwool//mineral wool are all interchangeable. USe the cheapest in your area. For any any given glass density, go one density heavier for mineral wool type. Eg: 2.5>3.0, 3.0>4.0. Absorbingly: Andre
