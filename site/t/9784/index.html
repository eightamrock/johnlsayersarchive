andrebrito30 November 2007#9
One word: ceiling ! You need to place absorbers in the ceiling to treat the execess in reverberation. And you also need to add diffusion to the space since large rooms with exclusive absorption treatment usually sound bad. There are several options here. Is this room dedicated to speech only ? Or more into music ? Now it is impossible just with pictures to have any idea of the reverberation time in the room. This could be 2 or 5 s, who knows ? The addition of acoustical material depends on the current reverberation of the room and the target reverberation. If possible you should try to measure the RT60 there and we will start from those values. If it is not possible to measure then the other solution (which is approximate) is to build an acoustical model of the room and try to evalute it. I can provide with an acoustical model of the room using specific software if you want to but feel that even with having the acoustical model you should try to make measurements in the room since the more accurate is the information regarding these values the more precision you will have on the final results. You are now dealing with a large room, forget about bass traps and other small room acoustics terms. This is a different subject, you don't treat large rooms like you treat small rooms.
