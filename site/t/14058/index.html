hello Lafronde,
Lafronde wrote:
i was thinking of making new acoustic panels to suspend on the ceiling,
mr sayers sometimes recommends a cloud with a solid back. try a search on this. and indeed the 132/132Hz you mentioned is your 0,0,2 axial mode. also you should have a dip @ 67Hz right in the middle of the height (must be a few cm above your head/listening position @ 128cm) I guess sooner or later someone with more knowledge will chime in and give you proper tips. so long, carsten
