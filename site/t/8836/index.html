thanks for the kind words Jeff. This was a modular system I've been playing around with. The slots are mounted on boxes that are removeable. In fact the whole front system can be walked out the room. I would divide the box up into further boxes behind the slots to broaden the absorption as each section would be tuned to it's own center frequency. One more general question, for some reason All the Lowe's and Home Depot's and the smaller supply houses are out of 3/4 MDF and apart from that only carry 5/8 particle board. Does it all have to be built using 3/4 stock? Is 5/8 still acceptable? I can understand the thicker the material the more dense,,,, what about plywood, or the 3/4 sub floor material looks like wafer board, but it is 3/4 and not your typical 7/16 OSB
MDF is denser than plywood, so would have a lower resonant frequency - either panel will resonate, but the stiffer/denser you make them, the less (and lower frequency) the resonance. I ended up going with 3/4" particle board because that is what was available at the time. You could go with 2 layers of 5/8", but I would probably avoid using the OSB material. Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
