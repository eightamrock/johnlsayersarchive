knightfly26 May 2006#11
Alex, I think the first thing you need to do is to make whatever concrete repairs you plan to do - then, if you want sound isolation between rooms you should build a second wall, with only one area of mass, on only ONE side of the concrete wall you're trying to isolate - the same, if possible, for ceilings (I know they're low) Isolation without several FEET of concrete is only practical by building mass-air-mass construction, with insulation (not foam type) between these two masses - more than two masses or one air gap (insulation is considered "air" for this purpose) is NOT good - it will only cost you more money for less results. Whatever you do in your rooms, you will need to plan on quite a bit of space being taken up by broadband absorbers (like mineral wool) - possibly even building some resonant panel traps to help kill some of the bass that will be rampant in your concrete rooms. You will also need to plan for heavy doors, so anywhere a door is hinged will need to be examined for sturdiness, else the heavy doors needed for isolation will cause failure of the hinges and door jambs. The floor area around the doors will need to be leveled and smoothed if it's not already, so you can put good door seals there; without them, the sound will get in AND out. IF the noise level in your movie was any indication, you'll need quite good door seals to keep things quiet inside. I think that part of the reason you've not heard from anyone else on this project is that it seems pretty daunting - but I think you can get there with enough patience and will power... Steve
