I posted this previously in the studio construction area but I think it's more appropriate here. I was all set to finish my basement (with mixing room) when I discovered moisture behind the current paneling. There is staining on the walls and I also just found out my walls are bowed (towards the basement) over 1”. I’m not going to finish the basement when I have doubts as to whether it will stay dry. I have a few options available. One is to excavate, straighten, waterproof and reinforce the inside walls. Either way, the cost of this would really push out the construction of the finished basement. I need an interim solution. Half of the basement is dry with no issues so I can move things to that side and use that as a temporary mixing space. However the South and West walls are still paneled with block behind and the North and East are open to the rest of the basement. My question is: is there a good way to make some sort of false walls on the two open (North and East) sides that will still enable a good mixing area until I can complete the basement? Can I just hang sheets of plywood from the joists to keep things somewhat symmetrical? I can still apply base traps and absorbers in the enclosed area to manage bass and reflections but I'm worried that the lack of sound containment will still end up sounding bad. The area in question is about 12'x16'x7' Thoughts??
