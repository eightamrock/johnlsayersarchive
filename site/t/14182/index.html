If a tedious testing process were done on a room with the numbers you presented here, the five inches of space wind up being the weakest part of the wall (because it is the closest) in the walls isolation ability. So any thing gained from the eight inches is effectively reduced to the five inches. The largest >consistent< distance between the hard boundaries is always recommended. What might be happening is that with parallel walls people make attempt to eliminate them (the parallel walls on the inside of a room) to get rid of flutter echo and help with added diffusion. Maybe you are thinking about this, may I say, inside out?
