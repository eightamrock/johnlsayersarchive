Minimum wall angle?

Started by Ryan Lindow on 1 March 2005. 1 reply.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 3319.

Ryan Lindow1 March 2005#1
If you are going to skew your walls so they are not parallel, is there a minimum difference in angle you should achieve?
AVare1 March 2005#2
If you are splaying (a common word for angling the walls in studios) for stoping flutter echo, about 6 degrees is the minimum. If you are doing it to direct early reflections, then you have to do ray tracing etc. Reflectively: Andre
