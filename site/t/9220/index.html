I'm having trouble nailing the 2x4s to the concrete in the room. I got the fluted masonry nails but then just bust out the concrete and then I can pull the 2x4 up. Whats the work around for this, I was thinking getting plugs so I can screw the wood in?
gullfo19 November 2007#8
use a nail gun (powder or pneumatic type) to mount brackets to the wall. then attach the 2x4 to those. or drill using a masonry drill and use anchors and screws or nails to attach the bracket. use brackets top and bottom about 4' oc or minimum 1 pair per wall. (you can get all this at home depot or lowes, rent the nail gun or buy the masonry bit - a bit smaller than the anchors) re: absorber/slat q: if you leave the slats off they'll be aborbers...
