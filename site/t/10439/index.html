Colfin8 April 2008#1
Hi There, I have been going over this web site now for sometime and have decided on my final layout / construction method for my studio. Its a new build double wall timber frame based on the SAE garage studio design 2. I am taking the MAM approach to the construction. For practical / personal / cost and technical reasons i have decided to build of a timber joist floor and not concrete which probably flies in the face of all the advice on the site / books etc but this is the decision i have reached. I have noted others who have constructed in this method with successful results. I tend to record live drums and di bass / guitars etc, then over dub guitars miked in the live room. I dont use any bass amps as i am getting good results with di / Ampeg modelling software. This is not a commerical studio so i pretty much dictate how things are done, mainly working with young bands / musicians etc. I would greatly appreciate any comments on the design / construction of the studio and i also have a few questions. I am really not sure how to ventilate the studio. Air conditioning is not common where i am (East Lothian Scotland) so i will only be providing heating and ventilation. Will probably just use electric panel heaters but the ventilation solution eludes me. Any suggestions as most of the info on the web relates to full HVAC sytems which is not what i am after. Only supply air and extract. The thought of building this airtight / soundproof building then punching holes in it kind of scares me !!!! Any comments / feedback we be greatly appreciated. Colin.

Sorry not been back sooner on this post. Started work on this studio now. My reason for the roof design was that i read that control room ceilings are best sloped away from the mixing position at min 10 deg to help reflections. Decided that the same roof would give a bit of space over the live room for ducting and to give the room a bit of 'space'. I appreciate that the water would collect in the middle but given a well detailed / specified valley gutter and roofing system i dont see this as an issue. Colin.
