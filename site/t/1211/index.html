knightfly30 January 2004#4
Hey Harper - sorry no responses yet - I just saw this, and downloaded the incredibly large ??!? pdf - then dnl the other two, and found nothing in any of them about acoustic performance. From their description, it sounds like the stuff is just heavy mineral wool - till you get to the statement and pic about "waterproof" - from that, I would hazard a guess that they run the stuff through a bath that coats the fibers with a sealant of some kind. That would change the acoustic response, but with zero data I wouldn't care to guess HOW it changes. The stuff requires a water barrier behind it, and is apparently intended to give a porous layer next to concrete to promote drainage away from the concrete. All in all, it seems like a gamble to me - with no specs on acoustics, the waterproofing treatment, and the extra heavy weight, I wouldn't take the chance anywhere that's going to get covered up. For an experiment, however, a gobo or two (or a piece just thrown up in the corners of a room) might prove interesting. At that price, as long as you don't build it into your structure without knowing what it can do, it wouldn't cost much to "play with"... Steve
