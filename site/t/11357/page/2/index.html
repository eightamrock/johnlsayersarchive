Ro6 November 2008#31
Phazinhead wrote:
- double insulation flat panel (maybe more efficient than single panel) at 45° in the corner.
No, it's the VOLUME (thickness) of the wool that counts. 2x 2cm panels will perform worse than 1x5cm for example. Just make the wool as thick as possible, and yes you can just add 2 pieces together to get thicker wool traps. That's the whole idea of having more pieces of wool; just to add more volume. In John's design it shows just 2 pieces to make it easier to build it. Else you have to BEVEL the whole piece.
just to have an answer about my idea of the 2 panels in the same wood frame, with air between them so as to act as 2 spring-mass systems. Would it be really more efficient than just a single panel in the wood frame, or it would be just a waste of insulation ?
No, they don't act as 2 sprint-mass systems. There's no (significant) mass in the wool. Just add as much wool as possible; one big piece or multiple smaller pieces together. As long as there's no "barrier" on the pieces of wool (paper/plastic etc) just bare wool.
John wall unit pix show what would be the best solution I think... and I think you agree with it ( no ? lol), but I can't stay without asking stupid things :lol: ... On the John wall unit pix in the red circles, would it makes any comb filtering with all those concave volumes made by the sides of the broadband absorbers ?
