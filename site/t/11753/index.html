Soundman20204 March 2009#4
Am I better to glue 4 sheets of 1/2" drywall together to form one side of the mass or should they not be glued (i.e. rather screwed together instead).
Like Brien said, don't glue them, just screw them. Green Glue is not really glue at all, so maybe that's what is confusing you? GG is not meant to be an adhesive, and does not count as glue, for what Rod was saying. GG is designed to damp and absorb acoustic energy between the sheets of drywall, whereas normal construction adhesive does not do that. Rod is talking about construction adhesive there, I think. From what I understood of what you are saying, this is just for beefing up the underside of whatever is above you, in strips between the joists? If so, then I don't think GG will help you much there, as the drywall will be in little chunks. I thin I'd save the GG for use on the large surface area of full drywall panels that go on your inner leaf. That's what it is designed for. I think you'd be better off with just screwing or using cleats to hold your drywall "beef" up between the joists, and caulk each layer all around the edges for a good seal. At least, that's what I've seen recommended around here. - Stuart -
