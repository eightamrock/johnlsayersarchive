Hi there "LHGriffin", and welcome! :) I'd suggest that you start I'd suggest two books: "Master Handbook of Acoustics" by F. Alton Everest (that's sort of the Bible for acoustics), and "Home Recording Studio: Build it Like the Pros", by Rod Gervais. And also read John Sayers own work here on the forum, at http://johnlsayers.com/Recmanual/index.htm. Those will give a really good basis on how to proceed with your design and build. The first book ("MHoA") gives you a great intro to the world of acoustics in simple terms, without going into too much complicated math. The second book shows you how that all works out in practice, in actually building a studio. John's manual is old and could do with an update, but it provides insights that the others miss. And of course, the forum itself is a huge gold-mine of information on how to build studios of all shapes and sizes, with numerous practical tips from people who have "been there and done that". Many many hints on traps to avoid, things that will make your life easier, important points that need great attention that you probably never thought of, ways to save money, etc., etc. Reading through a few of the larger threads can be a real education on how to go about this. - Stuart -
