wow that's low - I'd just make it by 2" :) your best bet in this circumstance is to deaden the ceiling as much as you can i.e. rigid fibreglass 703 type over the lot!!. BTW if you want to ceiling to feel higher cover it in a dark material i.e. Black ;) cheers john
