John Sayers20 February 2003#3
looks more like a readout of the NS10s more than your room. :) Note the typical NS10 roll off below 100hz. Your response is +-6db all through which ain't too bad actually. When you say you are going to put slots up to 30" I hope you are going to seal off the box behind them else they won't work. I'd finish with what you are doing, especially the rear wall. You probably won't see a huge difference in the plot but it will sound heaps better. cheers JOhn
