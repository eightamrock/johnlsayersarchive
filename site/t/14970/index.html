Soundman202020 October 2010#4
The ceiling unfortunately will only give me 7'6" and there will be a HVAC main duct to be boxed in basically across the middle of the control room.
A couple of questions on that: 1) When you say 7'6", where are you measuring that? Does that mean: A) from the floor to the lowest point of the HVAC duct? B) from the floor to the underside of the joists above you? C) from the floor to the underside of the sub-floor above you? D) from the floor to the inside surface of the final inner-leaf, once the room is finished? E) something else? 2) Is that HVAC duct only for the control room? (Meaning that it does not continue on to feed any other rooms). 3) Is there any chance you can re-route that HVAC duct? It is on the worst possible place as shown. For example, could you route it along the top edge of the CR wall, or through the booth, to get to the front of the CR? Or maybe take it through the top rear of the CR, right along the rear wall? Anyway, assuming that all of those dimensions (12', 16', 7'6") are for the final inner-leaf, that's actually not a bad ratio: it's close to Louden's third best ratio, and the modal spread is fairly even. - Stuart -
