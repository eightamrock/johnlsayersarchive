gullfo6 November 2007#2
part of the problem may be your mix desk if off center. adding the corner bass trapping (or wall-ceiling corner trapping) will help as will some smaller absorbers on the front back, side walls and a cloud on the ceiling to tame reflections. don't make the room dead though so start with the corners and basic reflection point and see where you get to before adding more.
