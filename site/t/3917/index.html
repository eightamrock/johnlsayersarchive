Quick "Bang for Buck" wallboard question?

Started by JohnGardner on 20 June 2005. 2 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 3917.

JohnGardner20 June 2005#1
If you could only afford one sheet of plaster board what one of the three following would provide the best isolation verse dollars: 13mm standard 7kg per m2 - $20 per sheet 15mm standard 7kg per m2 - $25 per sheet 13mm "noiseline" 12kg per m2 - $30 per sheet thanks JG
Dan Fitzpatrick20 June 2005#2
assuming they are all the same square footage, the "noiseline" would appear cheapest. it costs more but it more than makes up for that with increased mass. and mass is the factor that matters, not thickness. 13mm standard 7kg per m2 - $20 per sheet $2.86 per kg/m2 15mm standard 7kg per m2 - $25 per sheet $3.57 per kg/m2 13mm "noiseline" 12kg per m2 - $30 per sheet $2.50 per kg/m2 it seems weird that the 13mm and 15mm "standard" have the same mass/m2 ... i'd double check those numbers. and the noiseline is nearly twice as heavy as standard ... what's in there that's so heavy? dan :D
JohnGardner20 June 2005#3
Hi Dan, you were right - the 15mm standard is actually 12.7kg/m2 so it looks like it's the winner at $1.96kg/m2. Thanks JohnG
