". I don't know the exact dimensions of the room but if I had to guess I would say ..." Well, for the most part, we don't do equipment here. Like what kind of computer, guitar, mics, drums, etc...we leave that to other forums. In this one, John Sayers has developed a forum with pretty much all the information you could need to begin understanding not only how difficult this process will be but helps to get the answers to the difficult questions, no matter how difficult people may think it to me. The best thing I can say is that you will get out of this what you put into it. If the best you can do is have "approximates" and some guesses in an area that requires some pretty exacting information, then the help may not be forth coming in the flood that you are hoping for. In your education for this build I suggest that you give it the proper attention that it is due, not to treat it like it is some magic thing or that you are some how elevated in stature just because you will be learning a few things foreign to you and especially those around you that you are want to impress. Your course in designing a recording room begins now!
