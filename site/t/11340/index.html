Ro12 February 2009#8
Those speakerstands are light and will flank easy. Making a heavy stand will help "isolate" the waves going through the material (mass law). Easy to make them "jump" on the laminated floor. Make sure they're stable for starters. Those auralex decouplers, are they any good? I mean, do they "spring" correctly? (when placing a speaker on those pads, will the material compress a bit. say 10-20%?)
