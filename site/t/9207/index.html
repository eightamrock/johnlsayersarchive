My wife and I would go to the local town and check into the local motel when the baby was due. We lived down a long steep dirt road and didn't want to get stuck if it rained and turned the road into sludge. The babies (the 3 from this marriage) all arrived on time around 10pm at night for some reason. I would then pick up my wife the next day and take her home with the new baby. The babies all slept with us in our bed when we got home from the hospital. My wife would breast feed on demand, sometimes she'd wake up in the morning not remembering having fed in the night. As they grew they were slowly moved out to an adjacent cot that sat next to the bed. Once they were happy with the cot we slowly moved the cot further and further away. It was a great system, very happy content babys. cheers john
