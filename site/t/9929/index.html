bert Stoltenborg26 December 2007#4
If you do the chunks thing (wool in the corner), you're on the right way. Reflections you can control with mineral wool behind cloth, 10 cm is perfect. Cheaper than foam. Use the mirror trick: if you see your speakers reflecting from the wall or ceiling, place wool.
