Soundman20206 April 2013#2
Hi Marc. Please read the forum rules for posting (click here). You seem to be missing a couple of things! :) Regarding your question: No, that stuff won't do anything to kill first reflections. It is way too thin, and doesn't have the open-cell structure that is needed. The best solution is to buy a panel of Owens Corning OC-703, 4" thick, cut it in half (vertically), and put one half on each of the side walls, at the first reflection points. If you can't find OC-703, then ordinary mineral wool or fiberglass insulation will do. The most important thing is that it mus be thick: 4" at least. You can make it more effective by spacing it away from the wall as well. For example, leave a gap of 2 or 3 inches behind it. - Stuart -
