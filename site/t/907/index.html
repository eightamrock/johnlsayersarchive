giles1174 November 2003#10
Chris, they are way easier to build than you think. It is just a simple stud network at an angle. I used drywall to close the inside of the stud walls and sealed it with acoustical sealent. Then I put the insulation between the studs/stapled fabric and then put up the slats I used 1x4 and 1x6 in my control room, 1x3 and 1x6 in the live room. Bryan Giles
