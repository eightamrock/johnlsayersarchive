TomVan26 September 2013#27
Almost finished. Waiting for the glass for the windows. They show up on Tuesday Desk is being stained today. It is a sit stand desk and can load up to 500 lbs of weight. Speakers were ordered today "Barefoot" Micro main 35 Gen 2s Some small amounts of trim work still left to do around doors and windows Floors are sealed and we loaded in some equipment. The Live room sounds incredible. Still need some bass trapping but WOW it sounds great. Especially the :shot: :yahoo: Isolation (without control room windows) is pretty good but will have to wait until windows are in to see exactly how we fared with isolation from the upstairs living space. Not bad so far Enjoy the pics Peace T
