No. These will work fine in absorption panels or whatever. If you got it.. use it. ;) [They also work well as ceiling treatment in a dropped ceiling grid. - It's best to remove the reflective plastic and cover with fabric but you should have them about 2" thick for decent treatment and not more than about 4 to 6" of air space - and they function will with 12" of air space. This goes for any OC 703 or similar rock wool.]

Paper side UP. Make the ceiling fully absorbent. R-19 is 6.25" thick, R-21 is 5.5", R-30 is 9.5" thick. Get the thickness that you need to fill the joist space. Cheers, John
