xSpace28 November 2008#4
Bill Shoup has a basement build and did some overhead mass add. viewtopic.php?t=9343 You can use small finish nails to support the plasterboard in place. While adding the pieces you would drive the small nails into the joists under the piece, as a means to hold the plasterboard in place. If this isn't working then you may need short temporary wooden strips that you can screw into the joists directly underneath the plasterboard while you build up your layers. Maybe 12 inch (30 cm) pieces of 1X2 that you can install to overlap at the joint area. This would allow you to continue your install and add and remove the temporary wood strips until you are completed. Then install a final full piece to support the entire thing. Certainly the extra load on the joists is to be taken into consideration. This can be more of a load then your overhead joists are expected to support. Especially when you add the impact of the human body and/or dead loads of furniture, appliances, etc. Good luck
