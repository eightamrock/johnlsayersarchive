Hi there Ryan, and welcome! :) Sometimes it can take a while to get a reply here on the forum: there's a lot of activity, and not too many people answering questions right now....
dimensions 24 ft 8 inches wide by 38 ft 10 inches long with a 13 ft ceiling that is in the shape of a triangle (so its 13 ft in the center and gradually declines in height each side) approx 920 sq ft to work with.
Nice! That's a pretty decent size place, and with nice high ceilings.
i live in fl and yes it is humid.
So HVAC will be a big part of your studio, then.
1. the floors are cement...should i get wood or stain them or?
Stained cement can look really nice, and is excellent acoustically. Or if you prefer, you could use laminate flooring. In expensive, looks good, easy to install, and also very good acoustically.
2. what to insulate the walls with.
When you say "insulate", are you referring to the damping in the MSM cavity, or are you referring to the room treatment, once the isolation is finished?
3. leave the ceiling open? (no drywall ceiling, or just leave the live room with the ceiling?)
That depends on how much isolation you need. - Stuart -

Soundman202024 June 2013#6
i was just going to put two window air condition units in the windows....
Those things are way too noisy for a studio. The recommendation for studio control rooms is around NC 20, and I totally doubt there is any window unit quite enough to even get close to that. Most people here use "mini-split" systems (which are much quieter), in combination with a ducted ventilation system. - Stuart -
