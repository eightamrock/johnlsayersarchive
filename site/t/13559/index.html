xSpace2 September 2009#4
Soffit (from French soffite, Italian soffitto, formed as a ceiling; directly from suffictus for suffixus, Latin suffigere, to fix underneath), in architecture, describes the underside of any construction element. Mr. Newell understands the importance of the right word in the right context.
John Sayers2 September 2009#5
In the original Hidley QUAD rooms the rear speakers were mounted over the soffit for the tape recorders under the speakers. Hence the term soffit started to emerge. The correct IMO name is flush mount as the speaker is mounted flush with the front wall.
