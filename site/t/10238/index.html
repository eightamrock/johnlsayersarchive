gullfo22 February 2008#2
get some 4" rigid insulation absorption over the drums and mix desk. put a 2' wide 4" rigid insulation absorber over the corners. this should cut the close reflections off the ceiling to the overhead mics and trap some bass.
DCinFrance22 February 2008#3
Not that I've earned my stripes or anything, but you want to seriously think about getting those drums out of the corner. Put them in the middle, back third of the room. After that, it looks like they are sitting on carpet. Bummer. Put some hardwood under them and treat the ceiling and corners as Glen suggests.
