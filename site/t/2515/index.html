Is my control room too big?

Started by John_Burdick on 20 October 2004. 5 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 2515.

John_Burdick20 October 2004#1
Hi, I just moved into a new space, and I have decided to put my control room in a room that is (W)160" X (L)332" X (H)124" I am in a month-to-month lease in an office builing, so I don't want to invest too much money in demolition and construction. The north wall and the west wall are exterior walls which are solid concrete. The west wall has a lot of windows to the outside. The south wall is wooden 2X4s with 1/2" drywall on either side filled with pink insulation. The east wall has 2 solid-core doors, and a large window into my tracking room. It is made of steel 2X4s and 1/2" drywall and is hollow. The ceiling is concrete which has a dropped ceiling at 12" which is drywall with acoustical tiles glued to it, then there's another dropped ceiling about 8" down from there which is a metal ceiling grid and acoustical tiles. The floor is carpet which is glued to harwood. (LAME!) My questions are: Are the ratios for this room too screwy? I realize that it's pretty close to being 2 squares side-by side. Since there are windows limiting where I can put permanent walls, can I build a free-standing wall to shorten to room? Where would I position my speakers in the room? Can you think of any problems I might encounter with the current state of the room? I've started scraping the carpet glue in my tracking room and it's a pain in the ass. Would this room benefit from the wood floors? thanks in advance for your expertice. John
