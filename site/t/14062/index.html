jbassino2 March 2010#5
The drywall is on the OUTSIDE of the framing, thats why its called "inside out". Mass is mass, it doesnt matter if its either inside or outside the stud. Inside out is cool because you gain space for your treatment since the same studs that supports the mass, are used for the acoustic treatment. The only problem with inside out regarding acoustic treatment is that you are almost limited to cloth or slat walls. If you ever want a 100% reflective surface, you'll creating a 3rd leaf so you must carefully design your acousitc treatment before so you might have a couple of walls where it would just be a "normal" wall (not inside out)
