Soundman202028 September 2016#2
No, that's not really a triple leaf system, because the insulation is polystyrene, which is closed-cell.
What would you recommend between the OSB and the parquet?
Use whatever the manufacturer of the parquet recommends. The main thing is to make sure that there's no air gaps inside that floor, in any of the layers. - Stuart -
