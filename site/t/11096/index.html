Ethan Winer16 August 2008#4
You should rotate your setup 90 degrees clockwise and sit centered left and right. See this: How to set up a room You also need bass traps. More here: Acoustics FAQ RealTraps Articles RealTraps Videos There's no real reason to measure your room because no matter what is measured, the solution is always the same - lots of bass traps. But if you want to measure anyway, you should use a small diaphragm omni condenser microphone. Here are some good ones at many different price points: Comparison of Ten Measuring Microphones Your use of widely spaced frequencies is not useful. More here: Using ETF That should keep you busy for a while! :) --Ethan
