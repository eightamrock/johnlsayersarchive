Soundman2020 wrote:
Can I just cut a large new piece, say a few feet bigger than the hole, and tape it in place over the old stuff with duct tape or some such? Glue it? Just staple it?
A good diagram of how to do it their way. http://www.smithphillips.net/pdf%20file ... nstall.pdf I install any new house wrap pieces >under< the existing house wrap rather than on top of. Then nail as usual. Some joints will require taping depending on the situation. Where any good sticky tape will do the job, it is preferable to lean on the manufacturers products unless you can match the tape with one that will maintain integrity and remain in place for the expected time period to fulfill warranty obligations. Especially around penetrations in exterior walls.
