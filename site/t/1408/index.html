jdean4 March 2004#7
if you're recording acoustic jazz, no, sound transmission shouldn't be a problem with the single wall. if you like nu metal, i think you will eventually want double walls. are you doing the construction work yourself? the material cost isn't much higher than single wall w/staggered studs. same number of verticals, and the extra base plate and top can be 2X4s instead of more expensive 2X6s. the amount of drywall should be about the same also (you don't want drywall on the sides of the walls that face each other). it will eat up more space, but you have quite a bit to work with. i do my recording in an unheated, partially insulated garge. keep that in mind before putting too much stock in anything i have to say about all this. i hope someone more qualified joins the discussion.
