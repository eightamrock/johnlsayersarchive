Peaks and nulls are caused by acoustic interference. Peaks are generally only a few dB, but nulls can be nearly infinitely deep. See the first section of this article: http://www.realtraps.com/facts.htm There's also a video that shows this. Find Non-Modal Peaks and Nulls on this page: http://www.realtraps.com/videos.htm The Comb Filtering video also discusses this phenomenon. --Ethan
