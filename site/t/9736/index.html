...soundproof plaster is really worth the extra cash if you have insulation...
The product that you suggest here is more dense and that is the reason it is recommended over standard sheetrock. But insulation as part of a wall assembly adds to the walls ability to isolate sound. So it is not whether the rock is better then the insulation because the two products together give a better result then one of them or the other alone.
