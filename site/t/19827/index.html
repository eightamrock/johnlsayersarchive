Mike80813 February 2015#1
Looking into purchasing solderless wall plates for my studio. I was wondering if anyone has had any problems with them and is there a difference between the wall plates you solder? Thank you Also does anyone have any ideas for alternatives to using wall plates.
Johnnie3 March 2015#2
well, it doesn't look as clean, but there's always the snake option. I've seen several studios that went that route rather than using a wall plate. Just ran a snake through the wall between the control room and the studio (with a 90 degree bend and insulation stuffed in and with PVC tubing as close to the size of the snake as possible) then simply plugging into the snake rather than a wall plate.
Mike8083 March 2015#3
Thank you very much i did pick up a 16x8 snake for the live room and a 8x4 snake for the iso booth. I ran 2 inch pvc pipe thru the walls at an angle and will be stuffing them with insulation.
