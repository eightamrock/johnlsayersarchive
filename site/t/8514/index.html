Is it possible to create a modular and portable practice room or drum booth like a Whisperroom? I'm sure I'm not the only one who's ever looked at these rooms and said "I could build that"... The reason I'm asking is I followed the USG link on one of the stickys and I came upon some specialty USG steel studs like the C-H and H steel studs that would make it possible to connect wall partitions... Having something that worked reasonably well and is portable is a fantastic concept...but why does it have to be so expensive? Can't I build one myself cheaper? I know for a fact that's how Vocalbooth.com got started... 6 months of trial and error and now the guy is in the booth-making business. I wonder if the code/permit requirements would be different...
