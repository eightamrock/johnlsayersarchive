The clamping of the wire into the new and out of the old wouldn't be housed wire, unless you could pull another wire. You cannot have a junction box which is hidden in most code law for electric. That outlet in the wall would be turned into a junction box with another run out to the new outlet in your suggestion I believe. But if it were worth the effort and you could pull a new line and/or have enough slack to reach the new box - and the bass trap was made to be perminent, you could cut into the wall and create a narrow newly framed box on the outlet side of the bass trap. - Note - this is all from second hand information passed on to me from a master electrician. I had to pull a new wire in my room because there was a light (junction) in the line for the basement where my ceiling would go. It was either keep the light there (didn't want that) or pull a new line up stairs.
