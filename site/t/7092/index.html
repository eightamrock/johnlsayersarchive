Howdy! First of all, this resource is amazing and your dedication to build an extremely usable knowledge base is nothing less than admirable, cheers mates! For few months already I've been immersed into planning of an upcoming studio, located in the city of Riga (Latvia, EU). Most of the time has been spent learning, raising funds and checking for available options. The time is getting tight as the winter is approaching, so I have to make some important decisions in order to proceed with the construction works. Being an all-electronic producer I'm striving just for a decent monitoring. Although I'm not pretending to the status of sound engineer, my sonic requirements are quite high. I may easily use live recorded sequences without much editing while spending many hours on tuning a tiny tick/click until it fits the mix precisely the way I feel it should. I also tend to go low with the bass, in fact handling sub-bass is what bothers me first of all. Since I'm sometimes working with singers and recording some percussions, I'm also considering building a vocal booth or using an extra dead corner for the purpose. LAYOUT The dimensions of the whole available space are 9.6x6x2.8 M (that's 31.5x19.7x9.2 FT), however I'm going to divide it into 2 rooms. The studio room would be notably bigger and the other room would serve as storage and bedroom. As you can see in the photos, the walls are all bare brick except for one largely consisting of windows (as much as I love the beauty of industrial landscape, I reckon dealing with those will be a tricky affair).

Positioning workspace is also confusing due to the windows.

 INSULATION What I'd really like to avoid is building extra walls. Idea of (re)movable panels seems very appealing to me, so I decided to design appropriate panels and basstraps using fiberglass while leaving floor and ceiling untreated. However, none of known materials of the kind can be found in Latvia.  ELECTRICITY There is no electricity yet, I have to do all the installation including setting up a meter. Here I'm going to have a temporary connection first. All I need is a stable grounded circuit but where to draw the line?
