Closed cell foam is foam (usually fairly soft) that has no breathable qualities; if you were to hold a piece up to your mouth and try to blow through it, you couldn't do it. Things like the door seals in cars are closed cell foam; a harder version (not what I meant) would be the white foam insulation board that is used to insulate under concrete slabs in houses. Celotex is basically shredded paper byproducts mixed with adhesive and compressed into flimsy, aerated boards; it usually costs about twice what gypsum wallboard does, and is soft enough not to cause hard coupling in some cases. It's stiff enough to work for a surround in windows, but light/soft enough not to cause serious flanking if installed right. Another term for this type of Celotex (since Celotex is a BRAND name as well) is Homasote... Steve
