kendale14 September 2007#13
Aloha,
Do you think having the opening for the stairs to the 2nd floor will create problems with the acoustics in the live room?
That may depend on how much bass build up there is and at what frequency. You may also want to consider treating the live room walls, ceiling and corners with various broadband absorbers (preferably NOT the "garden variety" foam products on the market), cloud absorbers and bass traps to control flutter and bass build up. Another thought to consider is "How much isolation will you have between the live room and the rest of your home?" as a single door at the top of the stairs will probably not be enough to keep from disturbing the rest of your household. Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
