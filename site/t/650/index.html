knightfly4 September 2003#4
Sen, believe the 2-leaf thing, everything I've seen since Eric first mentioned it on the Yahoo site has supported it. As to your walls, you cheated and didn't mention the REST of the construction - if you do a double thickness brick wall, then air/insulation, and a stud frame with a second leaf on one side of that frame, you should have a really good wall setup. Even though the STC rating of such a wall might not sound like it's much better than other designs, the REAL performance for Music Transmission Loss will be better because of the high mass in the bricks. Combine that with a 2 or 3 dissimilar layered, partly resilient stud wall, and you should have VERY good isolation at ALL frequencies. Have you ironed out your differences with the Bumbling BureauCrazy yet? I haven't noticed any more plans or ideas from you, just curious... Steve
