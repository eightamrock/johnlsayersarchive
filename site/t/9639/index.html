gullfo26 October 2007#14
if you follow Ethan's site on placement of the absorbers (or even the folks who sold it...) you would find that you want to treat the corners with heavy absorbers for bass trapping, use medium sized ones for clouds, and lighter ones on first reflection points of the wall. also, front and back wall - use the heavy absorbers. on the window - hang a drape you can pull over. on the closet side - block those openings with doors. obviously you can use a single size of absorbers all around if thats what you have or are inclined to build. otherwise, using different sizes lets you attack each problem area with the right amount to keep the room balanced. use stand offs on the wall and ceiling absorbers to increase their effectiveness. don't use the standoffs on the corner ones.
