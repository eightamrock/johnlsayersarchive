If there are no windows or doors from his room directly to the outside - what he's thinking (and you're proposing) is a violation of both the building and mechanical codes. The code requires each habitable room have a direct connect to the outside world in the form of either windows - doors - or through mechanical means. The code then goes on to describe how one might take air from an adjacent space into another space. One of the requirements for this to be the means is that there can be NO doors between the 2 rooms - there must be a clear opening - of the size proscribed by the code - that can never be closed off - and THAT other room or space has to then have the direct access to the outside world. For mechanical means to satisfy the code - the ducting itself must be directly connected to outside air - and the existing HVAC system (even if it had fresh air supply - which is VERY doubtfull) does not meet the requirements under the code. The poster needs to begin thinking along the lines of how he can go about getting outside air ducted into his room. Sincerely, Rod
