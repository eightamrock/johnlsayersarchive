Soundman202010 September 2008#1
A question about the best way to mount Adam A7 speakers, which have some strange angle faces at the top. Which of the following three is the best way to mount them? Mounting "A": bottom front surface of speaker mounted flush with soffit, leaving top recessed. Mounting "B": Top front surface of speaker flush with soffit, leaving bottom protruding. Mounting "C": Carving out the back of the soffit panel to get a perfect fit. Mounting "D": None of the above!

padded cell10 September 2008#3
why not A? the tweeter section and the bass are on the same plane, and that would be flush with the soffit in example A. wow to the graphics!!!! Iain
AVare10 September 2008#4
padded cell wrote:
why not A?
The indents and sharp corners would act as secondary sources at high frequencies. Hence C is definitely better. BUT, I don't know how much of the tweeter response is affected by working into a solid 2π, B might be better. Andre
Ro11 September 2008#5
as soffit mounting means "flush with the wall" I'd say go with option 3. The bevels are made for "better" low-end frq folding. By mounting them flush, you'd cancel that (by "removing" the body of the speakers)
