I would suggest that you rotate your CR 90° to the right, so that it is facing the "water heater" area. They way you have it now, your speakers are firing across the short axis of the room, not down the long axis. Rotating the room will fix that, and will also allow you to make it much more symmetrical.

John Sayers17 May 2009#13
The platform is a communication addition. If you sit at a console in a typical office style chair on wheels and turn around and converse with someone sitting on a couch the person on the couch looks up at you - not good if they are the client. When you raise the couch they then come up to the same eye level as you. It sounds pedantic but it really makes a difference - the other aspect is it's great to lay back in the raised couch at the end of a session and blast your self with the track you worked on :) Yes - an inside out ceiling is a must for such a low ceiling.
