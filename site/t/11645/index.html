Soundman202030 January 2009#2
so the question is: what about hanging rectangular shaped bass traps in the corner, above the heating element?
That would help, yes. Make them THICK. But you might need to figure out some way of building / mounting / treating them so that they are not damaged by the heat from that heater.
something like a 60cm x 100 cm bass trap placed inclined with the respect to the wall, and more or less 1 meter high with respect to the floor.
Make it as big as possible, from just above the heater, all the way up to the ceiling, and also wide and deep. You need as much trapping as you can possibly fit in.
i could even build panels that go up to the ceiling
That would be excellent.
is there a correct heigth to place bass traps to make them effective??
Yes! The correct height is: "all the way from the floor to the ceiling"!!! :) Or as much of that as you can manage.
would this be a wise solution?
Very wise!
i could place these object at each of the 4 corners of the ceiling. would it be effective?
Yes it would, but those manufactured units are expensive, and you are on tight budget. You can make something siliar yourself with a large triangular frame of wood that fits exactly across the "tri-corner", and fill it with thick rock wool or fiberglass. Put wire or string across the front to keep the insulation in place, and cover over that with acoustic cloth, or some similar type of fabric. Much cheaper, and just as effective. You seem to have the right idea! - Stuart -
