Swamp House Studios, Austin TX - Photo Journal

Started by JLynn on 24 June 2009. 16 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 13208.

JLynn24 June 2009#1
I had meant to jump in here long ago. Long time lurker of this forum. Anyway, I'd like to share the photo history of my construction journey in the event someone might find if useful. This project started off around October of 2007 and we completed it in December of 2008. My father and I did all of the construction with the exception of trusses, shingles, and final drywall mud/texture. The general purpose of this space was for my own greedy purposes (non commercial) as well as serve as a home office (write offs woohoo!). The basic idea was to build a budget conscious structure while obtaining enough sound damping properties to allow me to work back here effectively any time of the day. As you'll see we did some things that would be considered pretty bad for sound isolation (mainly the peer and beam foundation) but I'm happy to say that the building has more than exceeded my needs for sound isolation. I'm not certain this would hold up when mic'ing a cranked SVT Head and cab but I don't really have a need for that level of isolation. The weakest link is the entry door even with second door closed though it can be improved I believe with a little additional weather striping or the like. My little project pails in comparison to almost everything I've seen here. Major kudos to all the amazing photo blogs on this site. It was a major inspiration for me. Total size of space is 25'x26' Staggered stud construction through out. outer layer is siding and OSB ply Inner walls is a layer of sound choice fiber board and 5/8 rock. No green glue, etc. Insulated using blown in Rock Wool (walls and attic) - it was cheap and works great. Total cost of project (including electrical and HVAC) was roughly 15k. Having lived in the space for the last few months it was a bargain well spent. I'm more than happy to answer any questions if ya got em.

JLynn25 June 2009#13
Thanks guys. Ro, I was getting quotes of around 5k to poor a slab. Believe me I wanted one. I think it was only around $400 to do the same thing with wood at that time. OSB was really cheap and we even did 2 layers of it. I wasn't trying to be a cheap skate but that was too large of a savings to ignore and allowed me to put that money elsewhere. Looking back I'm glad I made that choice. What little bleed through from the floor that is getting out is well below the legal decibel levels by the time your at the edge of my yard. The only thing I'd do differently now is have the entry into my mix room instead of the live room. At the time I didn't think I'd want guys carting gear through my mix/office...I still don't but I think it would have been a better in the long run. A) better isolation of course and B) It makes it difficult to step in or out when you've got someone tracking in the big room.
