Welcome to the forum. From a high level, it sounds like you're on the right track. Increasing the gap between leaves from 6" to 12" will give you better results. Note that if the frames themselves are 5" apart and you're using 2x4 framing (1.5" x 3.5" nominal), you end up with 12" between inner wall surfaces not counting the frames, which is very good. SonicClang21 March 2006#5
A well-built window will be just as effective at stopping sound as a wall. And for doors, you could have two doors built very poorly without proper seals at the perimeters and they'd stop sound like junk, or have one really well-built air tight door that works wonders. I'm personally building my own doors instead of buying solid core ones from the store. Not only will I save money but I'll design them for maximum sound stopping. So my point is, make sure you seal the doors as they shut. If air can leak through, sound will too. Use some closed cell weather stripping and make sure it compresses when you shut the door. Make sure every side of the door is sealed, including the bottom.

sharward26 March 2006#10
Yeah, you can't use drywall as an exterior... But you'll have to have some kind of outer skin. What do you plan to use? Stucco is probably one of the more cost effective skins to use. Building a beefy outer layer from scratch is probably as simple as framing walls with 2x4s or 2x6s, sheath it with 1/2" OSB, then cover that with maybe two layers of Hardipanel® Vertical Siding. I'm not sure what you'd want to do about a roof, but it would have to be similarly "beefy" to keep up with the walls. Inside you can build independent frames with one layer of OSB and two or three layers of 5/8" drywall. This is just a conceptual idea and by no means a finalized plan. You'd need to follow all the guidelines in the "Before You Post" announcement with details of your goals, photos and drawings of the space where you intend to build, and all that good stuff. So far you're receiving much more information than you are providing... That will only work for so long... ;-) --Keith :mrgreen:
