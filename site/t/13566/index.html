xSpace1 September 2009#3
Magnetic seals are not uncommon in discussion about doors. But developing some type of latching that locks from the inside of the room and no one can open the door from the outside of the room is never a good idea. Used to you could get an entry door lock, what is now referred to as a suicide lock, that was keyed on both sides of the door...inside and out. On the first look it seems like a good idea, unless the house is on fire, you are inside and the key is no where to be found. Hope that helps ya in some way.

i thought that perhaps it would still be possible to make absorber door covers that are fitted with hinges on one side and spring loaded claw latches on the other side. That squish the door covers over the doors in order to make a seal. I thought that this would be much safer as well as in a real emergency, you could always do a Mr. T karate kick and break open the door cover. Thats if the person inside can not or is incapable of opening the latches. :-) This type of door cover may allow for a slightly higher level of isolation for those on a low budget.
