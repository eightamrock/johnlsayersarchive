knightfly14 April 2003#2
To do what? Multitrack digital audio? Midi sequencing? Both the above? Generate test tones? Balance the books? Surf the web in search of software? Generate track sheets? Rhyme lyrics? Print sheet music? I could go on, but surely you get the point. There is such a thing as TOO succinct when asking questions - Didn't mean to come across as bitchy, but you caught me when my laptop is doing things that make me want to cut down a 4-foot diameter tree with a dull butter knife. Anyway, more specifics get you more and better answers - Now that I've calmed down some, I use Cake Pro Audio9 for MIDI, synced to Samplitude 6 for audio, by using Hubi's Loopback device for internal sync - Samp is master, Cake is slave. Cake runs about 20 various brands/types of modules, some with keys, some without - 3 different drum modules, 2 different drum controllers, 8x8 MIDI interface, 3 different keyboard controllers, 2 by Roland, one by Yamaha - I use Sound Forge for 2-track editing, sometimes Samplitude for that too. Soundquest's Midiquest for librarian software, integrated into Cakewalk. Tascam M-1516 analog and DM-24 digital mixers, Yamaha and TC hardware reverbs, and a bunch of other stuff as the mood calls for it. For analog recording I have a Tascam 38 8-track. Mixdowns either to CD or DAT, or usually both... Steve
