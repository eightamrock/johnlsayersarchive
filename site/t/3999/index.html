Hi folks I have a shell of a building that is 6 meters by 8 meters. I have ordered roof trusses and hope to have the roof on by early August. So I am keen to settle on a layout. I know the advice is not to have rectangle shaped control rooms and I am not disputing the reasons why. I like the designs I have seen here but from looking at them it seems to me that I would have to use 2 thirds of my entire space to get a good size, correctly shaped control room. Have a look at my current layout idea. With regards the control room, I am looking for a lot of work space around me. I have two LCD displays for pro tools. I have a mixer that is only used to feed pro tools and is not needed in front of me. I want a keyboard to the side of me for composition when I am working alone. At the same time I want a chill area in the control room for guests or myself when I just want to just listen to music and have a beer. It terms of potential acoustic problems I was going to angle the ceiling above the control room sloping up away from the mix position and use acoustic products like Auralex to treat the back wall and sides. I do want to create a good mixing environment but I am not trying to create a state of the art mathematically designed mastering room. The live room will be used for acoustic instruments such as Guitars and other stringed instruments and I will have a wooden floor which has always provided me with great results in the past. The drum booth is a good size for comfort and the vocal booth can also be used for instruments and could easily take two people without feeling cramped. I have racked my brain to think of the best way to use the space I have. This is my best layout so far but I ask you guys what potential problems it might create. I am on a very limited budget. I've a roof to finance as well as external and internal doors. I have budgeted for double stud walls for the control room and double layers of plasterboard packed full of rock wool everywhere. The drum booth will be room within room idea. The floor is currently a concrete slab. I think my budget will cover the materials. I don't have any labour costs. I'd appreciate any advice on the layout. Cheers
