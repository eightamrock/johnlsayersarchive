Ro9 September 2008#4
Sound like a typical 2-leaf system already. The best thing to do is just beef-up the existing parts. That means extra MASS on either sides, or just one side if that's your only option. For example. Add extra Gypsum boards on the inner side and seal them all with sealant. The same goes, actually, for you ceiling, floor and other walls. That is IF you want good iso all around. Have you measured the transmission loss yet? gotta know WHAT you wanna isolate, eh.

Ro13 September 2008#7
sorry, I forgot to take my pills this morning :oops: a 2 leaf is MASS-AIR-MASS. The MASS are the walls and the AIR is.. well, it's the air. Oh, WOOL is also considered AIR in this case. there you go! Yes, just beef up the brick wall. You could make a frame, if the brick wall isn't straight for example, and add COMPRESSED wool in it so it will become a part of the leaf (mass) just so you won't have air pockets in between your leafs. I have a feeling you haven't spend too much time, yet, on the matter of sound isolation etc. You say you're in a hurry, but understanding soundproofing/acoustics etc you should spend some time on that. It's not magic, it's science.
