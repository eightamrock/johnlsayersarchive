i think a 6" slab with reinforceing steel is good. footings are dependant on the ground. you can use l bolts or just dill after slab is poured and use fixing. you can use ply or osb or a brick/block for exterior leaf. doubling of mass adds 6 db of isolation so the more the structure and budget can handle the better.(infomation is in the book). celing outer leaf ply with drywall cut inbetween joist is good. (in the book). just insulate up to the cross bracing. attach the lead with screws and adesive sandwiched between solid core door and ply. if you have a door or window that opens directly to out side you do not need fresh air supply. if not there are a few options search hrv, chiller room. ducted hvac. for ac on its own you can use whatever as long as you can take the noise. heating is fine. hope this helps joey.

Q: Is it highly recommended to use the "j" shaped rods implanted in the foundation to attatch the exterior walls to?
Here at Exit 4 Alabama we are required to use go bolts http://www.go-bolt.com/ in new construction and have used them in many a remodel project. You can get literature from your local building permit office that may can tell you what kind of attachment device is required and what spacing is needed in your area.
Q: Since gyp. board can't be used on the exterior wall what is recommended as far as the exterior is concerned?
Usually a layer of OSB is recommended covered with James Hardi siding.
