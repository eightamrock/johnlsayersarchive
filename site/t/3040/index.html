The ICON is a system, not a digital desk. It is a very expensive mouse. When pricing one out, consider that you not only need the ICON, but you need the monitor controller, Digi PRE's, Pro Tools HD 3 (bare minimum) Accel and a G5. This should add another $20k to $30k easily. If you want a digital desk (which I cannot understand why, as they are all just as obsolete as the computer's that drive them, then look at the Soundtracs, Euphonix (which a digitally controlled analog desk), Sony Oxford (great desk, but good luck getting support from Sony). SSL digital SUCKS and is too pricey. If it were me, I would get the API rack system and a good controller for Pro Tools. It is easier to break down and sell if you have to, plus you will not have a seperate machine room and AC for the electronics.

It depends on what your real needs are and what you plan to use it for. Budget is usually the first barrier. Then you have to think about dimensions for the control room. How many inputs do you require (tracking and/or mixing) What type of automation feature do you want/need? Are all your recordings going to be done via Hard Disk (such as Protools, Radar, or any other HD recorder)? Or will you be doing analog such as 2"? Do you want an all digital desk/controller or an analog desk w/ automation recall? Are you exclusively going w/ protools or ??
