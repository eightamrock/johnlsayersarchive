David8 February 2005#2
first, by no means am I an expert. File me under newbies. For what concerns your booth idea: I played around with one of my closets for a year as well and I found the following: 1: if there is little to no insulation on the inside closet wall cavity, no amount of padding will really help you to the extent that you might want. 2: my biggest challenge apart from creating a dead sound tight room, was keeping it cool on the inside. This is what deterred me eventually from further pursuing the idea. A possible solution if you really want to go this route maybe something similar to what one guy has done with his PC, he boxed it and used super quiet pc fans, at staggered angle and with sound proof panels in between. Look through John's studio gallery page for pictures of it.
