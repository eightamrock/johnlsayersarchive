Hi Guys, Having removed the Sheetrock from the walls and ceiling to see what I'm dealing with and finalize by design and plans, I see that I have engineered joists. Soundman202026 January 2014#2
That doesn't look load-bearing to me, so it should be OK to take it out, but do NOT take my word for it! I'm not a qualified structural engineer, so you need someone who is to take a look at the and give you his professional opinion. On paper. Signed. He'll likely charge you a few hundred dollars to visit your place and give his opinion, so make sure you have a whole long list with all your structural questions ready to hit him with when he gets there, to get the best value for your money. Get your preliminary plans ready in advance, and ask about everything you can think of that relates to loads, weights, stress, tension, sheer, etc. He is the only guy who can tell you for sure if that is or is not load-bearing, and give you the OK (or not!) to take it out. - Stuart -
