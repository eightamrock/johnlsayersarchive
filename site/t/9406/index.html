gregovertone wrote:
are these usable?
Sure. The best way to use these is as corner bass traps. You also need to double them up to be four inches thick, so then you'll have only nine. You don't have to glue them together or anything - just place them adjacent and stack them up in wall-wall corners, and hang them in wall-ceiling corners. --Ethan
