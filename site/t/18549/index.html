You can do that, yes. It's often refereed to as a "corner control room", since it is usually arranged diagonally across one corner of a larger space. A couple of things to keep in mind: 1) Door sweeps can be a problem, since you have doors opening onto other walls at strange angles 2) You can easily do a very effective RFZ design like this. 3) Soffit mounting your speaker is also easy to accomplish. 4) It's hard to fit in a sofa at the rear, since its a corner! 5) You need major bass trapping in that rear corner: it's the only one you've got!
Noise will not be an issue as I have no nearby neighbors.
What about noise coming the OTHER way? Wind, hail, rain, thunder, aircraft overhead, roads nearby. railway lines, cars arriving/leaving, other noises in the building, etc....
