First of all, nice to be back in your community. I've spent the morning catching up on all the Studio construction threads and I must say I'm very jealous :D Since I last came here I've developed my ideas further and now am planning a smaller basement studio. The only issue is the ceiling height. It looks as if it's going to be 6' 6" or 7' at a push. Right now I'm looking at ratios to use for the control room. From using RealTraps' modecalc and reading around on the forums I've seen that 1 : 1.50 : 2.10 is a decent ratio to use. This would give the room dimensions of (L)13.65' (W)9.75' (H)6.5'. Does anyone have any advice on whether or not these are good measurments for a small hobby studio? (I will also have 1 or 2 isolation booths for vocals and drums) Regards, Joe.
