knightfly18 May 2003#2
Here's a basic idea of what's necessary for door seals (scroll down a bit) http://www.saecollege.de/reference_mate ... indows.htm Somewhere I have some specific links but it's tired and I'm late, so will have to do more later. As to the paneled doors, they are, as you suspect, less than ideal. If you can find bulk quantities of Fixall plaster or something similar, you could fill the paneled areas flush with the stiles (the thick parts ) then cover with a layer of gyp board and veneer - this would give enough mass to be workable. When you say "double doors", do you mean side-by-side for load-in/out ease, or do you mean a sound lock arrangement, or both?
