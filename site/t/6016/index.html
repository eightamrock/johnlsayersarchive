Aloha,
what would i need to do for DECENT ISOLATION. I know i wont ge the best but anyone have ideas?
Depends on what you are isolating from. Is your main concern outside noise getting in (traffic, neighbors, dogs barking, aircraft, train), or inside noises (vocals, guitars) getting out and disturbing the neighbors. The reason I say "depends" is to determine how much isolation you might need. Once you determine the amount of isolation you need, you can then build for it. To accomplish isolation, the theory here is called mass-air-mass. For example, 2-3 layers of drywall - 3"-12" of airspace filled with rockwool/fiberglass, and then another 2-3 layers of drywall. If possible, you also want to avoid having a "three leaf" structure, which tends to let more LF in/out. Also in your situation, the window may need to be plugged, depending on the level of isolation you are hoping to achieve. There is a post somewhere about building a "window plug." Perhaps a search on the topic will prove useful for you. Take a look at the attached to see what you may be looking at in regard to your "vocal booth." The Axial Standing Wave graph is to help you see what areas of acoustical treatment you will need. For a room your size, "dead" is better. Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
