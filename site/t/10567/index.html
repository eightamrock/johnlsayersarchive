gullfo11 May 2008#23
since the ceiling is only 290cm high, I think you'll find appropriate ratios will keep your CR "small". given that, you may find that you have space for an isolation room, machine closet. the problem with the low ceiling is that you're trying to create a lot of volume (normally good) but the ceiling/floor interactions are going to be problematic to solve for folks in the back of the room... you might consider creating a second set of soffits for the back room so your producers can hear whats going without reliance on the front speakers. you could also consider putting the producers and clients into a separate room facing the control room - this way you could also use that space for isolation when needed. if the split between rooms is a sliding or french door, visibility and opening it up becomes possible as well...
