This is a strange kind of basstrap, on java jive studios. this is the link: http://www.javajivestudio.com this is what the engeneer says about this kind of basstrap: " What’s up will all the books? There are two answers – the first is that bookshelves, especially when the books are placed at varying depths in the shelves, make wonderful diffusers, and are therefore an excellent acoustic treatment for the tracking room and iso booth. The second reason is that Dave was tired of keeping all of these books in storage, and wanted his entire library to be immediately accessible. There are between two and three thousand volumes in the library, and yes, he has read them all."
