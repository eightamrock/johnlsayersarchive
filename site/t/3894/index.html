Hello all, first let me say I've been reading a lot at this forum and my head is spinning. I wasn't sure if I should post here or in the construction forum, if I've erred please excuse me. I am in the process of finding a home in SE Michigan to purchase with the idea of then building a studio there. Since the studio will probably entail a lot more requirements than the house, I am treating it as a priority in my house buying decision. Dan Fitzpatrick17 June 2005#2
seth floating the "studio" floor and then also floating the "rooms" on top of the the studio floor is not the right way to do it. this is three leaf floor which has been demonstrated to perform worse than two leaf in a lot of cases. you should count the garage floor as your first mass, and the floors that you float for your different rooms will be the second mass. that is two leaf. outer leaf walls will connect to the garage floor & ceiling. some people might decouple these but i'm not too sure about that. the forum is very random-access, not linear like a book, so it can get jumbled. but there is tons of info here. maybe keep a notebook? and make LOTS of bookmarks and keep them well organized. and spend LOTS of time reading. dan
