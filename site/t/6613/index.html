BG: Here are a few known effective devices you can build yourself inexpensively. http://forum.studiotips.com/viewforum.p ... 3a9a4d0ec4 I'd start by installing a pair of panels mounted across the diagonal of each corner running from floor to ceiling [or the use the SuperChunk design linked above] and then, use similar 4” panels to treat the first reflection points on the front wall, side walls and ceiling. If possible, hang all the wall /ceiling panels so as to achieve +/- 4” gap between the back of the panel and the wall / ceiling (the gap increases low frequency absorption). This will yield a treatment that has broadband absorption properties that will give pleasing results. Alternatively - buy factory made - there are lots of choices.
