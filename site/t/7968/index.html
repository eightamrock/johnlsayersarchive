Kaj18 January 2007#2
Hi, Had a music session in the new rehearsal room, and it sounded so much better than previous room we used to have. The only thing I found out as a direct problem, was that electric bass on low open E string was filling the room more than desired. We moved the Bass speaker towards the big Bass trap and it improved a lot, but still not as tight as I would like it to be. We have not moved the bass amp around to more locations yet, but we will try it in four different corners and compare where the low E firming up, I knew that there was one of the modes at 70 Hz that would give problem but did not target on those in the Helmholtz absorbers as it is too low to be practically efficient. I took a few two mic recordings with my omni dynamic Zennheiser mics placed them 2/3rds down the length of the room and could be very happy to hear the balanced and detailed sound and the smooth treble from HH and Cymbals. My guitar (Variax through POD XT live and to PA) Bass through amp all recorded only through the stereo pairs, and it sounded like a lot of presence compared to earlier tests in previous rehearsal rooms. I changed my omni to Condencer mics phantom powered small capsule cardiod and placed them closer to the PA speakers angled towards Bass amp and drum kit, second recording session gave a more crisp presence of the drum kit, still >2m from the drum kit, so I can be confident that I can use the omni for adding ambience to the drums. Finally, did I hook up a Omni Condenser small capsule in the middle of the room and a cardiod condensor 1m from a PA speaker L+R room+speaker and compared the response, and it was fairly aligned. But I noticed the hump at 70Hz area, and thats my first tweak trouble and 141 Hz as well. I can move speaker/mic 1/4 of wave length and see if it is related to standing wave or bad resonance from PA speaker. My next move are to record a new test CD with the identified standing waves and non critical frequencies as reference, our PA is not so straight in frequency responce as I would like, but I will be able (I hope) to use the PA for a A+B comparisation at close up recordings of test signals, and at different distances to create the references of how much the room colour the sound. I want to digest my first impression and hopefully, get some questions and/or comments on what I have done so far. I will try to get a curve for each mic I use in comming test, but today was more a ear test, and it passed except for 70,141 Hz. And I will take pictures of Mic positioning and curves from that take. but today was it more fun just to play. Questions? Any thing I should go fore as priority? Cheers
