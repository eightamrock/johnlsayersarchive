A couple of things that may help - try and make your dimensions clearer, they are very hard to read. And look around the forum for a plan that comes close to what you want, then post your version of it for critique; it's much less time-consuming to explain what needs changing than it is to draw plans from scratch, and neither John or myself has all that much free time anymore. The more you give, the more you'll get... Steve
