Hi "Jure V.". Please check the forum rules: you seem to have missed one!! :) Regarding your room: you have the layout backwards! The desk needs to go under the window, in the middle. Symmetry is VERY important, and right now with your desk in the corner, up against the door, you do not have symmetry. It will be impossible to do a good mix in there that translates well to other places. So first put the desk under the window, and move the drums to the other end of the room. Next, get the speakers off the desk and onto stands. The speakers are causing the desk to vibrate and resonate, which messes up the entire response curve: you are not hearing the mix correctly like that. Put your speakers on very heavy, massive stands that rest only on the floor, and do not touch the wall or desk. The, get the speakers further apart: they are much too close. Then put large bass traps (superchunks) in as many corners as you can. Then treat the first reflection points. Then do an analysis of the room with REW, and post the results here, so we can see what else needs doing. - Stuart -
