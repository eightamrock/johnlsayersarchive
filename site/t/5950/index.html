sharward7 April 2006#2
Are you sure you want to have to go through Iso 1 to get in and out of the control room? If you only have a single main entry door, what's the advantage of a double door on the live room? I would think you'd just want to do a single door to the live room that would match the width of your main entry door. With the exception of the office/kitchen area, which can act as a sound lock, you'll need opposing doors between rooms throughout. But since you don't have a building yet, you'll probably be better off waiting until you have one (or at least one in mind specifically) so that you can post a diagram of its layout. Every building is different, with its own unique challenges in terms of entrance points, utility access, pathways to restroom(s), etc. --Keith :mrgreen:
