gullfo8 July 2007#2
i think from a mixing desk perspective you have the right idea. my only thought is that the small window/wall on one side and the curtain on the other side will potentially alter the balance. if you add another set of (free standing/hanging?) absorber panels (green) on either side (angled to reflect past you - if they're slightly reflective - a good thing) then you'll have gone further to create the desired symmetry. put a couple of absorbers on the back wall and a cloud over the mix position. maybe a cloud of the "live" portion to cut echos. some small carpets to cut echos but keep it alive.
