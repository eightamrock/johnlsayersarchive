Ethan Winer27 May 2004#2
Tom, > Are there any alternative options for treating the room other than the huge bass traps < First, understand that all room acoustic problems are caused by reflections off the walls, floor and ceiling. If a room has 700 square feet of surface area, then you can't expect a tiny bass trap with only, say, four square feet of surface to do very much. You really do need to cover some reasonable percentage of the room's surfaces to convert them from reflecting to absorbing. That said, the most space-efficient design I know of is the wood panel bass trap. You can build these to work as low as 60-80 Hz with a depth of only a few inches. Another option is corner mounted rigid fiberglass or a commercial product like the MiniTraps you mentioned. Although mounting a 2x4 foot panel across a corner impinges on the room, if you put them in the corners where the walls meet the ceiling they'll be up high and out of the way. --Ethan
