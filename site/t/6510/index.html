Hey there! nice looking studio! ok so you wanna big screen?? how big do you want it?? and are you using it for recording software? if you are using it for recording software etc there is no way that s video will work...s video has a resolution of around 400 lines or so.. lines as in tv resoltion not pixels! equiv might be 200x 150 pixels!!! most plasmas have a resolution of around 800x whatever ratio (16.9, 16.10..up to around 1024...now try looking at your audio software in these resoltions they are extremely disapointing... not to mention the refresh rates on plasmas are worthless..so now we move on to lcd tvs..the highest one ive seen so far is 1336x 42 inch sony this still is to low in my opinion for audio work... there is rumour of 1600x lcd tvs coming out soon... for instance im on mac as well i have 23 inch screens at 1900x1200 ... moving to lower resoltion would disapoint me extremely! i think the only thing to do is fork out the cash for a 30 inch dell or apple with a resoltion of 2600x 1600! and they dont use fans etc most lcd tvs dont use any fans! or wait until higher resoltion lcd tvs become more cheaper which they seem to do every month! hope i have been a help Govinda

Govinda6 July 2006#5
well then the question of quality still arises! sure a plasma screen will work for what you are doing...for instance when i do sound to picture i get the movie guys to reduce down to a small avi etc cause i really dont care much of the quality ..half the time its not even fully finished edits and missing cgi...looking at green screen with actors never excited me much! hehe :shock: ntsc is cool butttttt i dont know what the go is in the usa atm but everything over here, broadcast and film digital and analogue and dvd's are getting standardised to HD format ..now hd format is a grey area in terms of resolutions..until recently where everything and i mean everything will eventually be Standard HD 1900x1200...tvs included, digital boxes films on dvd you name it...so if you want to view quality on your wall which im assuming you do because your gonna edit footage..your gonna need a hd lcd or plasma tv at a resolution of at least 1,366 x 788 !! now my experience is this plasma tvs are hot they run a bit hotter than an lcd hence the use of fans...plasma tvs used to have the best black colour vs the lcd but this is not true in modern lcd tvs... plasma that are large with low resolution look pixelated and have the famous fly screen effect... also if you dont know this heres a handy hint on big tvs...you should be any closer than 3 times the diagonal measurement of the tv...so if you want a 52 inch tv you need to be 156 inches away from it for opti viewing.. plasmas because of refresh rates are prone to smearing and streaking.. also alarmingly if you leave a still image like your studio logo up on the plasma, if left on there to long ie 10-30 minutes and up it BURNS IN...meaning there is the logos impression faintly burnt into the screen forever! now try watching your movies after thats happened!!!! :( CONCLUSION (mine of course hehe) IF you ask me and you didnt, stay clear away from plasma tvs they have more minus' than positives..go an lcd with a reso of at least 1,366 x 788. one of the best pictures i have seen lately was a sony lcd bravio, do you guys have those over there? 1,366 x 788 really good refresh rates really awesome clear picture and a 40 inch lcd at aus $3100 (us $ 2291) is a steal it was however $4800 and just got reduced so they are on the down run boys ..start rubbing your hands together! :twisted: To answer your question simply i have had plasma, rear projetion etc.. plasmas are hot..they are all easy to mount onto walls using a vesa type bracket they can however be very expensive from aus$ 250-$1000 depending on tv brand and type..you have to watch out , for instance you *can not* flush mount them *inside* walls, it covers the air vents and your tv will die.. mounting onto a wall doesnt cover the air vents so all is ok! Noise the plasma in a normal living room would not be that noisy, but in a studio thats soundproofed from external noise etc .. it would most likely drive you nuts, but then again i saw that you have a G5 sitting next you in your studio so maybe it might be ok for you.. my G5 is a few meters away from in an equipment room, cause it drives me crazy...i still dont know how close you are to the wall for best distance for screen size! hope ive helped, Govinda
