Ethan Winer16 December 2009#2
I measure low frequencies (below about 300 Hz) at high resolution because that's The Truth and you need to know the true response. At mid and high frequencies it's better to use third-octave averaging. Beware of people who claim our ears / brain can't hear finer than 1/3 octave because that's not true: Audibility of Narrow-Band EQ This next article is about something else, but I link it here because it shows how drastically the response can change over very small distances even at very low frequencies: A common-sense explanation of audiophile beliefs --Ethan
