I am ready to put the flooring in my control room and wonder what's best to use in a mixing room, laminate/hardwood or carpet or a combination of both? It's just going over a concrete slab floor. It's not floated or anything. I just ripped up the old carpet and want to put something else down. (I know, a floating floor would be best, but I'm not looking for a "pro" studio, just something for me and my friends) Thoughts? Thanks in advance!

Ro7 November 2008#3
If you read through this, mighty if I may add, forum you'll come across threads with the same question. Mostly the advise is to get a wood floor with a soft ceiling (absorbers). If you can't or won't tread the ceiling, a carpet is the least you can do in order to prevent flutter echo. If it's within the budget, get a wooden floor.
Lou7 November 2008#4
Hardened laminate wood flooring front 60%, and heavy duty carpet tiles for the back 40% would be my suggestion. :wink: That's what I have and it works great Hope that's of some help. Regards, Lou. 8)
yellowfever8 November 2008#5
I've just finished my control room. All 1st reflection points treated and back wall with 80mm thick absorption and went for laminated floor; cheap as chips and lets your chair roll over smoothly as well ;-) Tip - if you go for the laminate, make sure you put some ply onto the concrete and a purpose made laminate floor underlay as well. Is more comfortable and keeps your toes warm as well :-) Hope that helps. I'll even put a pic up as well. The room sounds great by the way :D
