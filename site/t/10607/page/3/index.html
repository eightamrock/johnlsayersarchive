Preludetojune11 June 2008#56
Hi guys, ive started my planning acoustics wise for my build, i have recieved a qoute from a respectible acoustication company but at the moment whats left of my budget after the roof delema is looking a bit slim, so my other option is doing the acoustics myself. Ive started with the control room. Below are the pics of my setup i have planned this far, any help would be greatly appreciated. The first pics are what i have planned using a combination of 20mm fibre board and rockwool for the bass traps in the corners (specs of the products are included), i have another option and have included it, where instead of fibre board i can use cloth and wooden frames for the bass traps as seen used alot on this site. I am still going to slant my celing at the front of the room aswell. Room dimensions are on the first pic. I have not calculated this layout using mode calcs ect i have planned it according to what i thought would be the best positions for the traps. The construction is basically a corner frame filled with rockwool OPTION 1 IS: the front of the trap is built using 20mm fibre board and then the cavity is filled with rockwool. OPTION 2 IS: the front of the trap is covered in cloth with wooden slits and the cavity is filled with rockwool. (probably the most pleasing looking option) If you perhaps have a better method for my application i would love to hear it.....
