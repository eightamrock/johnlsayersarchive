auroramusicproject23 April 2008#1
Hello everyone. Firstly, what a great site!! We, The Aurora Music Project, are in the process of building our new studio/office here in the North of the UK. We teach recording and live sound workshops to disadvantaged young people. We are located in the North of England, the business in Killamarsh - Derbyshire - UK and the premises in Doncaster - South Yorkshire - UK Our website is here We have acquired premises, 13.7m (l), 5.8m (w) and up to 7m high. They are not affected by sound issues too much. The case of the building is red brick and concrete block, the floor is concrete. We can change any aspect of the internal walls and ceilings. We would need to keep the external doors where they are shown. The internal wall that runs 4.49m (office internal wall) the full width cannot be moved as it is load bearing. We will be adding the internal skin once we have the wall dimesions. We have a plan, we are just unsure about the following points at this stage. 1. The control room dimensions/design given the attached dimensions 2. Live room design 3. We have a solid floor, do we need to float a floor? 4. And also the whole layout perhaps We have a build budget of approx. $14,000 There is so much information on this great site for the details of the build, but we want to make sure that the control room if the right proportion before we build walls and ceilings. And also that we have not missed any obvious parts, or maybe someone has a better idea? We would be very pleased to have any other layout ideas, bearing in mind that we need a toilet and an office. Any suggestions at all will be gratefully excepted - thank you. Brian Tucker
