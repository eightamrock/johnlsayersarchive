Danate, I've been watching this for a bit and trying to figure out how to calculate any value - but at that time I thought you were talking about a mineral fiber ceiling tile - Now that you painted a clearer picture of what exactly the tiles are - I can answer without a doubt. The answers above are correct - the tile are useless for the purpose of sound isolation. I'm sorry that you can't afford more than 1 layer of sheetrock - perhaps you shouldsave your oney until you can afford to do this right. The best thing you could do with those tiles would be to strip the facing off of them and use the rigid fiberglass for room treatments (put them together back to back to build them out to 4 inches or so) - at least there you would have a little value. Rod

danatedrummer wrote:
Each 2x4' panel weighs 6.75 lbs
So that means that each panel weighs .84 psf - roughly 1/3 the weight of a 5/8" sheet of drywall (about 2.6 psf) - so I suppose that if you could find a way to install it - seal all of the joints - for 3 layers deep - and then install a sheet of drywall over it - that it would be equivilent to a little less than 2 sheets of 5/8 drywall. Of course you would have to be very carefull to satagger all of the joints - and you would have 2 extra caulks joints on the wall's perimiter - with backer rod, plus all the additional labor........... IMHO - not worth any of it - but at least you have a rough idea of what it would take to perform as compared to standard drywall.......... Rod
