kendale30 December 2005#2
Aloha Stan, Forgive me if I'm wrong, but I think the rear wall treatment you are refering to in the picture is a diffusor rather than a bass trap. (It looks like a cloth covered RPG QRD 734 (see attached.) One of the reasons I think this is:
Low frequencies are big waves, consider that a 50Hz wave is 6.6m (21' 8'') and a 30Hz wave is 11m (36ft) long! That's 11m peak to peak -There's a lot of guys around here who would love to surf a wave like that! So to stop it requires special techniques.
The diffusor pictured does not have the depth needed to absorb such a long wave. In any case, here's a link to the SAE website which has some great info on bass traps. http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html Click on absorbers in left hand column and then click on the low frequencies tab at top of page. Hope this helps. Aloha 8)
