Well, I do have the Killatone design posted. http://johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=297 However, you'll notice that I give strong caveats in that thread about NOT using these as your primary monitors. Yes, there are tradeoffs with crossover networks and such, but full range drivers have horrible levels of distortion. Some people might enjoy this sound, but full range drivers simply cannot be compared to the accuracy of multi-way systems. Thomas
