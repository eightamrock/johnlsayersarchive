High there "DT", and welcome to the forum! :) Your numbers seem about right to me: General ball-park you can figure on about US$ 100 per square foot for a typical studio like yours these days, give or take maybe 20%, depending on where it is located, how much isolation you need, condition of the existing building (if any), and other specifics for each case. Don't forget to allow for the usual "hidden" costs, structural engineering, architect, HVAC, electrical, sewerage (if applicable), permits, insurance, rubble removal, landscaping, and all the others: each one isn't a lot, in terms of percentage of the entire project, but together they can add up. Considering the size of this project, I'd suggest that you might want to contact John directly, and talk to him about a quote for doing the actual acoustic design for the project, since he has a lot of experience in that area. What you spend on his fees you can save many times over on not wasting materials, time and mistakes along the way. When you say "video production", what exactly are you envisioning there? That term covers a broad range of possibilities, and that part of the project is right up my street: I've been involved in designing and building video facilities for many years, so if you can explain what you need there more completely, I might be able to help you there specifically, as well as in the rest of the project. It sounds like a fantastic project, by the way! - Stuart -
