gullfo26 November 2010#80
One option is to flatten the angle on the soffits a bit to accommodate. Based on your drawing, the sitting position to best view out the window is beyond the sweet spot. Another consideration is multiple angled walls instead of a single long one which may also give you the option to widen the soffits a bit so the distance from the front wall is long enough. Also, (given the large window) it might be better to turn then entire room to face the window and do 90 degree soffits. Then make the wall behind as absorptive as possible.
