Also, if using UPS AND balanced power you need a UPS that's large enough to run your balanced power transformer, assuming you want your gear that's getting balanced power to ALSO be on a UPS. Once the power is balanced, you cannot put a UPS on the balanced power and get it to work. Check the link Bryan gave, they have some white papers on this... Steve
