gullfo8 December 2010#2
for your budget - plan on getting a bunch of 1x lumber, rigid insulation, and cloth to build absorbers. using a bunch of 1x1 and rigid insulation and cloth you will build 12 4" thick absorbers for the front wall, side walls, closet doors, and several on the ceiling. you will place several absorbers on your back wall. here's a dynamic SU component absorber which you can dissect to build yours. you could use some rolls (smallest in diameter to fit your space) of pink insulation and stack them in all of the corners, cover the corner with cloth and 1x1 trim to conceal. the compression of the rolls and the plastic on the rolls increases the density of the insulation and reflects some highs. you might consider removing the carpet and polishing the concrete (?) or placing some heavy finished plywood over a portion where you record acoustical instruments. get your monitors up and off your desk to ear height or slightly above - maybe behind the desk on stands - maybe replace your desk (assuming it's like the one in the drawing) with something smaller and/or less reflective.
