knightfly6 October 2004#2
I would stick with Rockwool, that other stuff is over $4 per square foot and not thick enough. For floors and walls, you want a slight overfill of the cavities; especially on floors. Too much and you'll decrease isolation by coupling too firmly between inner and outer panels - too little, and you won't get damping that's required to keep things from "ringing"... Steve
