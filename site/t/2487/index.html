bassman15 October 2004#1
OK. I've bought a building for investment purposes but also to get my studio out of the attic. The kids need another bedroom. The building is a historic 3-story brick storefront-type place on a main street/shopping area. There are apartments and a store in the main building. There is a concrete block garage added to the back of the building where I plan to put in the studio. The space is oddly shaped but I think I can make it work. Its not big enought to build the ultimate studio for what I would like but I don't think I'll be there for 20 years either. Hence the budget won't be huge for renovating. Whatever is built here must be also usable for another tenant in the future once I've left so making a bunch of oddly shaped booths and full-floating construction is not really going to happen. BUT... I make a living mixing and producing albums, audio for film, scoring, foley, you name it... I need to build at least one room that is floated so I can have a quiet space to do stuff like foley and also to record drums at 3am without bothering the neighbors. Secondly, I want to be able to do two sessions at one time. I am currently turning down work that could be accomplished with a second engineer and another room. I don't like turning down work. :oops: So, in the following plans, there are two main rooms, the one at top would be floated and be the primary recording room. The bottom one would be the control room and not floated. I monitor pretty low and am not too concerned about noise transmission from there. The little narrow space at lower left would be basically an office/storage space that could double as a booth in a pinch. The ceiling there is 15' and I would like to make a loft-like storage area and bass trap above the office. I really want good bass response, no really freeckin' awsome bass response! Its the one area that is sooo difficult to get right without a decent sized room. I'm hoping that putting up a bass trap above the office area could give me that. We'll see. This is just a basic floorplan layout. Treatments would have to be thought through and added inside these walls (soffits, slats, hanger up in ceiling etc...). Any and all feedback is desired... Please let me know what you like and hate about it.... Thanks, Ashley
