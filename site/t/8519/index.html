rod gervais30 March 2007#2
Geoff' They're garbage - but then - so are the others......... self adhesive stick on gaskets will eventually stop sticking - that plus that fact that they are not capable of being adjusted to fit the actiul seat of the door - nor can adjustments be made on them as things move - all make for a strong - "Dont waste your money here" vote from me. You are much better off spending the money on good quality seals from Pemko, etc........... Something like these, with multiple seals whenever possible - This is just like caulking drywall - the more layers you seal - the less chance you have of weak points allowing sound passage........ Rod

Geoff, typically we use heavy duty closures on studio doors........ you could always use a throw bolt into the top jamb - and a foot bolt on the outside as the door opens to you........ BTW - just for the record - this is (IMHO) an area where people get carried away........ I have no problem with drilling a hole through a door to install a dead bolt........ in fact - if you looked at figure 1.1 in my book carefully - you can see a dead bolt installed directly above the door handle in the main room............ a dead bolt is a fairly tight package of dense mass - and as long as you use a small bore that the body just clears - there is very little room for air movement - Heck - you could even caulk around the body once installed as long as you were carefull not to get caulk in the mechanism itself...... If you do that though - just make sure to use a dead bolt that has a thumb latch on the room side of the door - you do not want someone fumbling aroung trying to open a lock with a key if ever there was an emergency............ Sinerely, Rod Rod
