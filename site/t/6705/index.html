My idea for sealing between spaced walls.

Started by tmix on 26 July 2006. 5 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 6705.

tmix26 July 2006#1
What are your thoughts on this Idea? I am considering ways to seal between the walls at my double door openings and control room windows. I have anywhere from a 2 to 3.5 inch gap between the walls. I was simply going to cover them with fabric so they would breath a bit and not make the doors so hard to open do to a vacuum forming between them. But I got to thinking about folks poking their fingers though the fabric etc. So here is my idea: What if I were to get some thin paneling (1/8 inch thick or so) , enough to cover the openings, but only attach it firm to one side letting it cantilever to the other side, then put a bead of acoustic caulk (or silicon) to lightly adhere the unattached side to allow the walls to not be tightly bridged. I could even put a spacer of 1/8 in or so on the hard fastened side to provide a small space on the free side to where it is only resting on the adhesive. If you can picture it, do you think this will compromise things too much by causing considerable flanking? I'd appreciate the thoughts. Tom
