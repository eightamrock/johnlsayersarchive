bluesbaz12 June 2009#14
The details of the project compounded by being by myself are making this Shhhh.. Take for ever but here are some updates of the control room. I reinforced an anderson door with an extra half inch of glass over a 1.5 inch space, beefed up the windows 1/2 lami over 3/8 lami glass. I build frames for bass traps and treatment in my live room and control room. I filled the traps with rockwool 4inch firesafing 45min and fronted the whole thing with faced dense fiberglass and chickenwire to hold the whole thing in. I installed some clouds with lighting. I ordered the flooring yesterday and faced one wall in my live room with wood luan.
