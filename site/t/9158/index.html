Ethan Winer26 July 2007#2
Taisoul wrote:
The idea is to build a wall of semi circles using this semi circle pipe insulation glued to MDF backing. It should wind up looking like a ASC Attack wall to some extent.
I don't see an advantage to a wall of absorbers unless you're trying to make a dead sounding mixing area within a very large space. The more usual method is to treat the corners with bass traps, and treat the first reflection points on the side walls and ceiling.
This is the material I would use.
That will work, but only if the diameter is suitably large, like 20 inches or more. You'll definitely want flat panels for first reflections.
Also do you think I should seal the ends of each semi circle cavity or let the air flow?
No, sealing is not necessary. --Ethan
