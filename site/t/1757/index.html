Hello, I’m building a new studio in a 70 by 36 pole barn. I have some rough Ideas down but I’m running into a couple of problems. There is already a concrete slab in half of the room (36 by 35) So I’ve decided that’s what I will work with for now. The bathroom is already in place! Thanks of reading this and if anyone out there has been in a similar situation, or has any suggestions I would love to hear about them. Jeff www.lafayettestudio.com
