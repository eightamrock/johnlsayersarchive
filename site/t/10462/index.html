Again, great job, thanks for your time and effort with the pictures. James.
The idea behind the polyester was two-fold - more bass trapping (albeit small) and to help keep the rock wool fibers from being released. I did not use the polyester on the front side of my mid/high panels - only the back of those. The front of the panels (the side facing me) sticks out 8 inches. The back of the panel (the side facing the wall) is 3.5" from the wall (the rock wool is 4" from the wall though). Room dimensions - 13.5ft x 12.833ft x 9ft high Each panel fully built weighs about 19 pounds With regards to your comment about a closed in frame, as was explained to me, the moving air needs to go somewhere. If you close it in, the air cannot properly move. You need movement of the air from the sound/pressure waves to really get the full benefit of bass trapping. Given the fact that my first reflection point mid/high frequency absorption panels are spaced away from the wall at the optimal air gap of 4", I am also reaping the benefits of bass trapping as well. If I were to put them flush against the wall, bass trapping would suffer. I'm no expert, but many here in this forum are. I learned it all from the people here. There are many good links posted here to a wealth of information. Let me know if you have any other questions. If I don't know it, I'm sure someone here does. :wink: I'll post more pics and comments to the website when I build my superchunk bass traps - give me a couple months though. From what I've done so far, the room sounds sooooooo much better than it did. Now, if life would quit getting in the way, maybe I could start on some serious music production one day :( Take care, Adam
