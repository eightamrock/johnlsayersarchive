I know we all spend a lot of time working on the isolation and acoustics in the design of our studios on this forum, but I want to pose a different question. What design features have you incorporated or seen done that are good ways of improving storage capacity? I find an organized studio is always easier to work in, and sometimes even makes it easier to be creative. Where do you put the mic stands? Your mic collection? Amps, cables, random junk? Some studios I know use specifically designed storage closets, but what about those single control rooms? Just wanted to start an interesting discussion. Please post any pictures, links, or sketches you might come up with.
