Aloha,
The dimensions of the corner units - 22/24" along the back sides. Is that adjustable or is there very good reason for those dimensions?
Yes, there is a good reason for it, but I don't recall the answer at the moment :mrgreen: Its been several years since I was at this junction.
The insulation material in the diagrams - rockwool or something similar I assume?
Yes, rockwool or something similar will work - try to get something rated about 2.5 pcf
Skirting board (which isn't in my sketchup, sorry) - that will keep things slightly out from the corner, a cm or so. Big problem?
I'll hazard a guess that would be something similar to what we call baseboards out here. Shouldn't be a problem. One thing I would point out is to be sure to attach these units to your walls with some sort of bracket to prevent them from tipping over on you, especially if your floors are not completely level.
I had been considering superchunks - would the slotted panels work on the front of something like that?
Yes, I believe that would work. The only point about superchunks is that they require more insulation material.
The rear corners would be a bit different to that plan due to my big alcove. I was considering loading it with 703/equivalent, at least in the corners.
This is one known area where bass frequencies are prone to build up. Perhaps as an alternative to the alcove area, you could mount a bass trap where the rear wall and ceiling come together, or perhaps place that alcove corner absorber on wheels to roll it into/out of position as needed. Again, be sure to secure it from toppling over.
Finally - probably the biggest one; the window. Having absorbers and so on around the room is fine, but blocking the window would have my missus reaching for the rolling pin No getting around that really, is there...
You could construct the front wall absorber with a cut out to fit your window dimensions. :wink: Aloha :mrgreen:

Macc, hello again, I am indeed familiar with Wickes from SOS magazine, and yes you could use this product, I could only see that it was or looked like "Rockwool" and not FibreGlass. It really depends on what you are going to do. This is what I would suggest: You have quite a few areas in your small room that need plugging w/ broadband bass-trapping (Common Name) or absorbers (Generic name). Look under Acoustics on this site or go to the Acoustics forum and you'll find 5-10 great examples from the elaborate to the simple on how to make these Traps, I made 5 small and 5 Huge just this year, and it wasn't easy though retail would have cost over $3,800.00 $AUD & I was proud that I had built them and will use them for the next 5-10 years. What you need in terms of Insulation is Rigid /Semi Rigid 3 PCF (Pounds per Cubic Feet of Density) or 48/Kg Per Sq Meter. This has enough gas-flow resistance to stop most high to mid-low (Let's say as an SAC Graph between 200-khz and 4000-6000-khz, once again all this info is in the Acoustics section) then you should be able to wrap 2 or 3 50 Mill /2 inch pieces together for a mass back wall Bass Trap, then look at the other 12 corners of the room and see where you can make the most effective difference, believe me in a room that small you will have peaks and nulls you can't control, though you have to live with what you have, then when you move, take the traps with you. As I am very allergic to both Rockwool & FibreGlass I use Polyester 90% recycled, and for the large Traps I built which are 950-mm x 2300-mm they are actually usable as beds, as well as being anti-fungal & antibacterial, the Kids have slept on them and their 100% safe. I bought a Drill, some wood, and slowly progressed, now I've just finished my Studio Furniture saving AUD $2K on racks and benches, so learning and doing is your best bet, I will be happy to help you and guide as will others, though have a good look on the acoustics forum here and customise your own design, as I did, or use a simple design on the forum, this site is a very detailed & knowledgeable site, once you find what you need and want to build, leave a message and I will walk you through it, how does that sound! Best of Luck, PM me after you have spent serious time in the Acoustics room asking Q & A then we shall com back here. Cheers ADDA of Wimbledon Church St. Now in OZ!
