Ted White11 December 2009#9
In general terms, a ceiling that is framed with new joists which are hung on the newly framed inner walls is best. This creates a complete room within a room. This is a decoupled assembly. Improvements to low frequency performance come from adding (not stuffing) insulation, mass from 5/8" drywall, and increased air cavity depth.
