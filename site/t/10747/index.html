AVare18 June 2008#2
The biggest problem is your ceiling, not the concrete block walls. I see no details on door sound isolation. That gets expensive very quickly as increased isolation is desired. You do not need clips on your concrete wall drywall. A good point! You will need to beef up the walls between the recording space and furnace room. MLV is a waste of money in that application. You need about 25 iso-clips for the ceiling. @$5.00 that is $125. Green Glue at recommended application rate is around $30 per sheet of drywall. That is around $240 just for the ceiling. I see nothing about heating/ventilation ducts and vents. Your $1,500 budget is not sufficient. Honestly, Andre

gullfo25 June 2008#18
depends on how you use them. MLV as mass is generally a waste because its cheaper (in most cases) to get 5/8 drywall. MLV as a limp mass behind the drywall can be useful - armed with the knowledge it has some impact as a 3rd leaf (if its a separate layer), but might help with some low frequency absorption (just not in-between drywall layers - use Green Glue). homasote can be useful for acoustic wave guides (hangers) when wrapped with dacron insulation and suspended in bass traps. in some cases you use it because you need a "soft" layer somewhere. sometimes an on-hand supply matters more than expanding the budget to buy other products.
