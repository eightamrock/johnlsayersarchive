Variable Acoustic Louvers

Started by aheadbehind on 18 February 2017. 4 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 20892.

aheadbehind18 February 2017#1
I'm designing a new studio and I'm looking into variable acoustic devices such as curtains that can be opened/closed to adjust reverb time, etc. The curtains don't work well in my space because the wall is curved in plan view. So, I'm wondering if anyone has ever seen a variable acoustic louver. Like, imagine vertical wooden louvers running most of the height of the wall and placed just in front of it. Each louver is 2-6" wide and the whole system could be as wide as the wall. The outside of the louvers is wood or something reflective and the inside is highly absorptive. The wall is also covered in highly absorptive material. Then you can vary how far you open the louver to adjust reverb time. Has this been done? Any thoughts on how well it would work?
Soundman202029 March 2017#2
That could work, yes. The only issue with louvers is that you'd be changing the reflections too. You'd be sending reflections to different parts of the room that didn't have any before... - Stuart -
aheadbehind30 March 2017#3
Thanks for the thoughts. You raise an interesting point on reflections. I'll research that some more, but I'm leaning toward doing this.
