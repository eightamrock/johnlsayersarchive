So I just got out of the shower from installing them and the protection caps and the rest of the gravel. I have my inspection tomorrow and the concrete is already ordered for saturday. Steve it is strong, its kind of a woven fiber poly plastic stuff. However I have two people on form watch for the pour when typical is just one person On another note... air entrainment is typically for exterior slabs or pours. And yes is helps the freeze thaw cycle by allowing the moisture in the slab to have a place to expand to when it freezes. There would be no reason for a climate controlled slab to have air entrained concrete And yes by using a stinger you can get the air bubbles out (bubbles just from pouring not plant induced) of the foundation and slab if needed for a stronger and asthetic looking foundation And Kenni yes you can put and overlay type self levler over the MRB as long as you broadcast some sand into the MRB while it is still wet to give a bond line for the overlay. I am doing a stained floor just for the cost savings alone. I do them all the time at my real job ....fairly simple as long as you clean well before sealing the stain. There is a residue left behind from the acid and lime reaction that you get when you stain. Hard to see and its called floro silicate. A white fine powder that acts as a bond breaker if you dont get it all cleaned off. Just nuetralize with soap,vinegar, or TSP
