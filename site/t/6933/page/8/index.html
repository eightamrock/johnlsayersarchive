Steve aka Knightfly had mentioned a dehumidifier early on in my build. I did purchase one 2 weeks ago because I was noticing some dampness. My build was during the winter and spring months and the timbers got pretty soaked....plus the self leveler on the floor plus the stain plus the paint plus the sheet rock mud and then sealing it all up tight. add some heat and wow it got humid. So long story short I have been running the dehumidifier for 2 weeks and taken out about 20 gallons of water. And is still running. Its still a rainy time of the year here so outside fresh air is damp in itself. Just a warning to all about how tight your build can be and drying it all out is very important.
