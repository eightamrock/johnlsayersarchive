jwl26 March 2006#4
Wow. What a challenging room. The short answer is to put as much absorption in as many places as possible. What I would probably do is put the desk on the window wall; the ceiling that angles upward should go up behind you as you sit at the mix position. But obviously the wall shapes will be a problem. If you are willing to give up the window, probably the best thing you could do would be to partition off the entire window area with a false sloped ceiling, fill the area behind the partition completely with rockwool, and cover it with cloth. In addition, you would need to treat as many corners as possible in the room, either using the superchunks method or having 4" panels straddling the corners. You will also need absorption on the side walls and the rear wall to take care of the first reflections. A room this size needs LOTS of absorption. I don't think diffusion will help you in a room that small. In short, spend nearly all of your budget on rockwool (or rigid fiberglass or maybe acoustical cotton), with enough left over to get framing lumber and cloth to cover things with. Good luck.
