the ionizer doesn't purge CO2 or create oxygen. you need to have the air exchange. a simple way to do this is to use the existing basement space (assuming it has air exchange) and a pair of baffle boxes to draw air through. there are also products like AeroPac which are nice and since you're up near Canada, you might be able to find one locally.
