Hello, and welcome; you have a start, but it will take more info before we can help much - please read through the "don't even think about posting..." thread at the top - you can use it for a "check list" of things we need to know about your space. Things like what your wall construction is EXACTLY - is it solid concrete, hollow concrete blocks, bricks, wood frame with timber on outside, plasterboard, ??? Without serious detail, we might wrongly suggest another wall, when there may already be one too many for best sound isolation. The REFERENCE section will also help you learn more about this type of construction - since we are not in your space, you need to be our "eyes and ears" and the more you understand about this special kind of construction, the better you can inform us so we can help...
