John Sayers6 November 2010#2
The advantage of Drywall is it can be made with special properties and is easier to handle. Gyprock Fyrchek for example is described thus:
Gyprock Fyrchek™ can be used in wall and ceiling systems where fire resistance is to be achieved and is useful where improved acoustic performance is required. Gyprock Fyrchek™ is machine made sheet composed of a specially processed glass fibre reinforced gypsum core encased in a heavy-duty linerboard, which is pink on the front face.

The difference is testing, gypsum (sheetrock) is what most tests are based on due to the ease of installation/availability/cost, etc, so information is readily available. Plaster is a rigid boundary, somewhat like concrete with a lessor mass/density and not much information readily available.
