gullfo3 April 2008#6
broadband porous absorption tends to work best wherever the velocity of the 1/4 wavelength to be trapped is highest or at key reflection points for higher frequencies. tricorners, vertical, horizontal corners where modes terminate are typically good locations. other mode/standing wave points also tend to be good. arbitrary placement tends to be better at dealing with higher frequencies but there are some research papers that describe good effects at lower frequencies. in the end, if you don't have a "clean" way to mount everything according to "the book" then you're restricted to doing what you can... so finding a bunch of places to add the necessary absorption will require more time and effort (and might even be better for your situation - dunno) and should result in a reasonable solution, if not, you might have to make other changes to the room, or layout of equipment and furniture, or just live with the results.
