Hi I'm Pete, I'm about to start work on my new recording / rehearsal studio It's a 33ft x 10ft 6" double length garage at the moment, I have plans to build a 'room within a room' inside the front half, with a removable partition that operates like a garage door (because it is one) :) The back half will be a control room and lounge area I'm looking to spend in the region of £2000-£3000 with a build time of around a year.
