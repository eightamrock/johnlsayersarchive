Bass Rocks9 January 2007#3
artwhore wrote:
3 questions about moisture in the basement. 1. do cement sealers prevent nature breathing and do they in fact prevent moisture. 2. can i drywall/rockwool the ceiling of a 100 y/o house and would that make any difference in sound outside 3. can i float a floor if there is any moisture in the basement thanks and good day.
1. I've used DryLock, and it works very well. I don't know about it preventing natural breathing, but if the basement's breathing is bringing in water, I say smother it. 2. As for drywall, it depends on how much weight the floor joists can support. Check this out carefully. The drywall will help prevent sound transmission to the outside; I don't think the rockwool will at all (correct me if I'm wrong, folks!). 3. I'd suggest you do. To raise the floor up on rubber and allow air to flow underneath it is far preferable to locking the moisture under a hard floor. I'm not doing the whole floated floor thing in my basement, but I am using DriCore subfloor tiles which raises the floor on polyethylene cleats, so that air can flow under it. You can check it out at http://www.dricore.com/. - Andy

artwhore wrote:
3. can i float a floor if there is any moisture in the basement
No. In fact, you shouldn't build anything in the basement if you have an unresolved moisture problem down there. Check out the numerous active basement build projects on here. I realize they're probably much more ambitious than anything you're able and willing to do right now, but there's a lot to learn from them. In particular, Andy Eade's Rotten Basement Conversion Thread is a great education in how not to finish a basement when there's an existing moisture problem... :roll: Andy's doing everything right, but the previous homeowner did not, so he ended up with a huge mess to deal with before he could move forward. And I also challenge you on your purported need to float a floor in the first place... Study the "Is a Floating Floor Right For You? Answer: Probably NOT" sticky thread. Congratulations on the house buy. 8) --Keith :mrgreen:
