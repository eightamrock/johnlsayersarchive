gullfo15 April 2008#9
not sure about that product - do you have a link to it? in EU there are also other products that may be more suitable for floating floors. couple of ideas on the room - can you test the room to see how much isolation you actually need? move in a PA and do some pink noise and sine wave sweeps at 105db to see how much and what frequency sound is actually getting out (and into your neighbors). then take some metal and rubber mallets and tap to see how much is conducted through the structure. see if you have any common plumbing, ducts, etc. that might also transfer sound into your neighbors.
