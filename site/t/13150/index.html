Cold16 June 2009#22
Hi, A couple of quick comments. If you are going to partially bury this building have you considered plumbing drainage for your sink and toilet? Or will you have to use a pump? Have you checked the water table? can you get good drainage from around the building? You may wish to rotate the control room so that the front faces the live room. Bad sight lines will drive you crazy. Try to place the vocal booth also for clear sight lines with both the control room and the live room. Have fun! Steve
