Aloha,
Well, maybe just an advice on which wall is better to place the near-fields. To me, best option is wall 3. Will I get too many reflections? Or is it not that important?
The majority of designs I've seen on this forum tend to have the monitors firing down the longest wall, which in your case would be from wall #2. Reducing the amount of relections in the mix position is very important, hence the term RFZ (reflection free zone) when designing control rooms. (see attachment) A lot of info can be found here: http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html (especially under the acoustics, absorption and construction tabs at left and again at the top of the pages) Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
