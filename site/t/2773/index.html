10x10x10, a perfect cube. Not an easy way to start but some nice mixes/recordings can be made in almost any room. Greetings Mini-Traps do NOT work better than proper acoustic foam for standing wave absorption. Mini-Traps work better than cheap rip of foam as sold by Ebay and Foam by Mail. However IMO there are beter solutions than Mni-Traps. Good foam for one, as made by Aurlex and other REPUTABLE Folks. This is a comparitive absorption data graph from test results done in acoustic labs. Bear in mind standing wave trapping is not just a bass thing. It's also need in the lo-mids. The Auralex foam came out the best, followed VERY closely by DIY rockwool/glasswool corner wedge absorbers.
External image, not preserved — original: http://www.acoustics-noise.com/AFBsite1/PicsDownloadDocs/Riverbank002x.gif
The lowest red line ( 16) is the cheap rip off foam. No wonder people think foam is crap :( ;) If your DIY skills are fair to moderate, I would recommend this, as it's one helluva lot cheaper than the foam. Personally I would NOT recommend the Mini-Traps. hth :) Paul

greetings Motown59 :) The data above is not skewed. It's source as shown on the graph is from BOTH Realtraps AND Auralex/StudioTips, so it's unlikely the data has been skewed to make something look worse. ;) The data above compares like for like, and a MegaLENRD, the rockwool slabs and a Mini-Trap take up almost the same effective space when placed in a corner. A megaLENRD protrudes only 1.5" into the room further than a Mini-Trap. See this link for a comparitive plan view... http://forum.studiotips.com/viewtopic.php?t=536 A standard LENRD is much smaller than any of the devices in those tests, and that's why they weren't included inthe tests I guess. Becuase of that I wouldn't argue that a standard LENRD performs worse than either a Mini-Trap and a Mega LENRD or the DIY rockwool designs. but you compare devices in lab tests, you have to make sure they are of the same or very similar size, otherwise the tests are meaningless. I hope that clarifies things a bit :) Paul
