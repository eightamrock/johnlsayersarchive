gullfo12 March 2011#36
In the example provided you would extend the isolation bits so the angles match up and build your slats around it. This way you have the sealed cavity for the slats and the sealed isolation mass for the windows. I would probably shrink the CR a bit.
xSpace12 March 2011#37
Another word of caution when cutting sheetrock from ceilings or walls....be prepared to hit electrical and or plumbing/vents. Use shorter blades on the sawzall and be prepared to hit electrical wires/plumbing/vents. Be safe...
gullfo12 March 2011#38
If you caulk line the ceiling joists on the drywall and use a small circular saw or dremel tool saw to cut the the line to the thickness of the drywall you can avoid too much nicking of important things like Brien points out. Have a T support you can slide up to support the drywall as it is cut or work with a buddy. Nails and screws will hurt you blade so mark the joist on either side (an ice pick is handy for finding the edges and holding the chalk line...)
