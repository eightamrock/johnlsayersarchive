Hi there, Just wanted to know whether a JBL 4412 was suitable for soffit mounting ? It is a 3 way passive speaker with a 12 inch bass and 5 inch mid and 1 1/4in tweeter. Would there be too much bass coming from this....or would i be better off flying them ?? Cheers N Y M O
gullfo16 May 2007#2
it should be fine depending on the soffit size (should be 3-4x the diameter of the woofer - knightfly posted the calc). depending on what option you have for crossover, you might need to biud a crossover to perform the bass compensation (barefoot has explanation posted on the site).
knightfly5 June 2007#3
How big's your CR? Just about any speaker can benefit from PROPER soffit mounting (solid construction, flush baffles, large enough baffle extensions, reasonably sized speakers for room volume - but it's also possible to overpower a room... Steve
