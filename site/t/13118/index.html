John Sayers4 June 2009#7
Matt - take a look at the booth at Left Bank. http://johnlsayers.com/Studio/Mainpage/MP-Leftbank.htm it's a small room (like yours) yet I was talking with Amirel a couple of weeks ago and he commented that he often puts drums in there. :shock: The vocals recorded in the room are right in your face. The room treatment is two walls being glass doors, one wall is cloth over 703 with a window, and one wall is a slot resonator and carpet floor. BUT the ceiling is chock full of hangers down to just above door height. When you talk in there it's bright and short. There's no boom from the low end, it's clean.
