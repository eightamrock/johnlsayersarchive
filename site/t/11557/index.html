Soundman202026 February 2009#17
They are ugly. so are ... some diffusors. ... Have you seen RPG's Skyline? Does it work?
That depends entirely on where you install it, and what you expect of it. Install it in the wrong place, and it will make matters much worse, not better. Expect it to be a magic bullet to cure all your CR ills, and you will be sorely disappointed. But install it correctly, along with a few identical neighbors, on the back wall of a well-designed CR for example, and it will indeed do what it was designed to do. Proven concept. No guessing. Plenty of science, math, experience and real-world reports to back it up. Unlike the Alien/Predator reject, as far as I can see. One more time: Show me the multiple independent lab analysis reports on how it ACTUALLY performs in a controlled test environment, and real disinterested independent testimony from real studio designers (not the manufacturers own hype nor "testimony" from their "customers"), and I might be inclined to think that it does something useful. It may well do so, and it may well do acoustic wonders even for small CR's, which would indeed be amazing. But you'll never know until you can find real tests of the product, by independent testers. That's my beef with the product. (That, and how ugly it is.) You can hide "ugly" behind acoustic cloth if you don't like it, but you can't make an oversize egg crate perform like a good multi-dimensional QRD, no matter how much you spruce it up with pretty curves and marketing hype. Like they say here about studio builds "no pics means it didn't happen": I'll re-phrase that a little here: "No data means it doesn't work". Show me the data and prove me wrong. - Stuart -
