Extremely helpful I am in the VERY early stages of planning a studio in my detached double garage this summer and I have a few quick questions before I post all of my info etc etc etc ... Can someone (with experience) please explain the difference between a control room and a mixing room? What is the purpose of both? Are they sometimes completely seperate rooms?? AVare7 December 2007#2
Acoustically there is no significant difference. Practically, the tracking room control requires some form of visual contact with the tracking room and space for many musicians when playing back. Spaciously; Andre
