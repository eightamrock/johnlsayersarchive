gullfo26 February 2011#5
at a fundamental level, yes, mass law applies but you also have to factor in MSM in terms of coincident resonance and so to fully appreciate the isolation levels of your design. from a purely empirical perspective, the best source is the NCR IRC documents which show comparative wall assemblies and their measured performance. here's some of the documents which help guide us in figuring out all the various things to be considered: http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/obj/irc/doc/p ... /ir761.pdf http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/obj/irc/doc/p ... /ir832.pdf and some other material which is very helpful as well: http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/obj/irc/doc/ctu-n66_eng.pdf http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/eng/ibp/irc/c ... s-n13.html http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/eng/ibp/irc/ctus/ctus-n1.html http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/eng/ibp/irc/c ... s-n27.html http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/eng/ibp/irc/c ... s-n25.html http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/eng/ibp/irc/c ... s-n35.html http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/eng/ibp/irc/c ... s-n39.html http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/eng/ibp/irc/c ... s-n50.html http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/obj/irc/doc/pubs/bpn/44_e.pdf http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/obj/irc/doc/pubs/bpn/56_e.pdf
