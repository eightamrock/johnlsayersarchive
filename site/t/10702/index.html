I cannot imagine how to create a skylight without connecting the two structures in an undesirable way, unless I have skylight / airspace / skylight, with no enclosed conduit for the light. And that sounds like a dim solution, if you'll pardon the pun. Does anyone else out there have good things to say about skylights? Could you guide me to some plans for building a skylight with sound isolation qualities?

quiet, Kinda' like I said over in another forum... The mass of an opening "should" be equal to or greater than that of the rest of the surrounding surface. A rule of thumb is just a general rule. Anything specific should be checked and verified with measurements and calculations. Just because someone says that a piece of glass should be 1/3 the thickness of the wall, doesn't literally mean just the thickness of the gypsum. It really means the density of the wall as a system... trifling again... but a potentially HUGE difference. Case in point... a wall that is of 2x4 construction with 2 layers of gypsum (with or without green glue) would require the same thickness of glass as a 2x8 stud wall with 3/4" MDF and 2 layers of gypsum with green glue? Not hardly... There is at least 50% more mass in the 2x8 wall. How much more... I honestly don't know. But before I'd go slapping any old piece of glass in there, I'd be finding out how much mass that glass should really have. Make sense? Max
