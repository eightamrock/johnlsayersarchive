DirkB19 July 2007#13
On paper splitting up a room in several smaller rooms might look nice, but in reality the acoustics suffer and it becomes claustrophic pretty quickly IMHO. I work in a relatively small room and my next, bigger room will also be a one room design, with the possibility to put a guitar amp in the storage room. Preferably a space with enough ceiling height and daylight. Nothing beats a nice big room where a band can really jam in and where the acoustics can develop. A not so small advantage is that you don't have all these widely space roommodes, so you don't need to start to basstrap the space to get some even low-end. Greetings, Dirk
