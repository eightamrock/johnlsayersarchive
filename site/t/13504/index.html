Hey Joe, your ceiling height will most likely be a determination defined by local building constables. Where I am, 7 feet to the finished ceiling height is a minimum. Which means, on a ground floor level in a residential structure that the elevation of the walls, minimum are to be 7 feet. But since that is labor intensive and creates excessive waste, the walls are built to the taller and more accepted 8 feet height. It may be 2.438 meters or it may be 3 meters for you guys. It may be something else entirely. The diminsions of standard building materials often have been modified to conform with the minimim standards. My 8 foot tall wall can be built with 1 bottom plate and 2 top plates and a 92-5/8 inch stud which will yield an 8 foot tall framed wall. Typical 16 inch on center wall layouts covered with typical building products that are 4 feet wide by 8 feet tall, waste is minimum and installation time is maximized. Good luck
