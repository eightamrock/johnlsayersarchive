Soundman20208 June 2010#2
Hi Frank. Welcome! A few quick things that I noticed: 1) You don't seem to have very much bass trapping in that room. Bass trapping is very important, and your graph also seems to show that you don't have anywhere near enough of it. 2" of absorption on all the walls isn't bass trapping. That will just make the room sound dull. 2) Your room is not symmetrical. It must be symmetrical at least from the front wall back to the mix position. 3) Dimensions and geometry are important. You don't seem to have a good layout for your room. 4) I wouldn't be too worried about your 78 Hz mode concerns: your room has such an odd shape that pretty much anything could be going on! In fact, your 0-1-1 tangential mode is at 78.4 Hz, theoretically, for the central part of the room, but that is meaningless anyway, since your room isn't rectangular so tangential and oblique modes are probably all over the place, and pretty much unpredictable. You have far bigger problems that need fixing, than a worrisome mode at 78 Hz.
"Maybe some kind of LEDE design would be suitable?"
LEDE is no longer the fad. The currently philosphy around here is that RFZ (Reflection Free Zone) design is the best way to do it. You could base your new layout on that. I'd start by re-arranging your room to get good symmetry at the front end, good dimensions, good geometry, install large bass traps in as many of the 12 corners as you can, and remove some of the absorption on the walls. Or even better, leave it in place and build slot walls over it. Then, when all that is done, measure the room again and see what still needs to be done. - Stuart -
