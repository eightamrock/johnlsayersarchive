xSpace20 February 2010#2
groundhog wrote:
would you recommend getting a prehung exterior door from home depot and cutting it on the bottom to make it shorter, or custom ordering one from a millwork shop?
Unless you have more money than you know what to do with I wouldn't recommend either one. A prehung exterior door from the big box stores, as a generic term, will always, always be a metal door. And you are not cutting one of those with any degree of success >if< you want to have a working door. And then you have to modify the frame... But even if you are talking about a wooden door, well the same thing applies. A custom millwork shop...well, I rebuilt two exterior solid raised panel doors a few weeks ago...you don't want to pay for that unless, of course, you are spending Church monies as was the case. Your inside. Get a solid core blank door :)

xSpace22 February 2010#8
groundhog wrote:
the trim would be installed over the edges of the cloth covering the insulation. hope that makes sense now.
You can use any type of trim you have experience enough to cut and install. I have used wooden quarter inch X 1-1/2 inch batten strips to handle this issue. It gives a clean neat finish and is a fairly simple solution. It might be in your favor to stain and seal or paint the trim before you install it ;) You can also locate some batten that has a fabric wrap, designed to match vinyl covered plywood wall panels. Might not be your cup of tea, just another thought.
