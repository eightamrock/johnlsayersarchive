Schroeder26 May 2005#7
Just my humble advice... ;-) Don`t ever ever ever install eggcartons, because it looks like shit! The carpet will absorb the highs so don`t absorb highs on the ceiling as well. The room would probably sound to dead. When you want diffuse and absorb some mids/low mids (that`s how eggcartons are supposed to work, don`t they?), why don`t you apply some of John`s slat-absorbers on the ceiling? You can tune them to your special problem frequencies so they absorb those and diffuse as well. Easy to build. Just search in the forum (slat/slot absorbers...) or look in the reference section - no problem. regards, Schroeder
