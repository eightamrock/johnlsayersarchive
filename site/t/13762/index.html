PrairieDog10 November 2009#1
Overview: I am currently using a fairly large room in my house (about 65m2) as a project studio. I occasionally use other parts of the house as isolation rooms. In the past, most of my recording was for myself and for friends. More recently I have been doing some commercial work and I expect this to increase. I'm in the final stages of obtaining planning permission to build a 70m2 extension which is intended to house a control room, WC and lobby area. I expect the planning application to be approved next week. The current room will become my live room. Although my initial questions relate to the layout of the extension, I am including information about the existing room as this may affect the design of the new section. Edit: Planning permission and listed building consent were granted on 19 November. How loud: I record everything from classical guitar to heavy metal. The levels depend on the clients and are not usually within my control. My nearest neighbours are about 80m away. I've never had any complaints from neighbours, even when recording rock bands late in the evening. I do occasionally have problems with noise from outside affecting my recordings. The problem is mainly low frequency noise from road traffic, trains and aircraft. Existing construction: The existing studio is based in a listed building originally built in 1662. Although much of the original fabric has been replaced with modern materials, I am quite limited in what alterations I am allowed to make. Here's an outline of the construction: External walls – cavity walls. Inner leaf brick/block, plastered. Outer leaf, some areas brick, others oak framing with plaster infill. Internal walls – brick/block, plastered. Roof construction – slate tiles, insulation, plasterboard. Floor – stone tiles on concrete slab. The extension has not been built yet. The size and shape cannot be changed. It has been arrived at after a series of discussions with the local council. Minor details such as positions of windows, etc. could probably be adjusted if necessary. The external walls will be brick and the roof will be slate tiles. Plan: Questions: My initial question relates to the layout of the extension. I've come up with 3 possible layouts, each with various advantages and disadvantages. I'm not certain which will prove to be the best compromise in the long term and I'd like to hear opinions from anyone who is willing to comment. Option 1 – This design has a large lobby which doubles as a kitchen, and could probably be used as an isolation room. The main disadvantage is that there are no sight lines from the control room to the live room so I would have to rely on a video link. Option 2 – This design has a large control room with sight lines into the live room. The main problems are that the lobby is rather small and clients have to go through the control room to get to the WC. Could possibly install a small WC between the live room and the main house although this would not meet regulations regarding disabled access. Option 3 – This design seems to work better except that the shape of the control room is not ideal. I'm concerned that it may feel cramped due to the smaller width. Budget: My budget is around £100,000 for construction and acoustic treatment. I've not done detailed cost estimates yet but it's already looking like it will run over budget. I will be managing the project myself. I may also do some of the more specialist work myself. Thanks in advance for any suggestions and advice. Demetris
