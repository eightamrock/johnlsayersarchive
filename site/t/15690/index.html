Wow. That's kind of like asking what kind of vehicle you need to get from A to B. Ask a hundred people, and you'll get a hundred different answers. Plus it all depends on a million factors, such as your own voice and the acoustics of the room where you'll be recording, for example. ("Vehicle" could mean a bicycle, or it could mean a 747... same with mics). That said, a good general-purpose mic that works reasonably well for most things is the good old Shure SM-58. Reasonable price, and sounds good for most singers in most locations. It ain't perfect, but it should do the job. Unless you have lots of money to spare, and you can afford a nice U47... :) - Stuart -
