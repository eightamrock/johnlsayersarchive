John Sayers3 May 2006#2
There are two factors here. One is splaying of the side walls which is to create the RFZ across the fron of the room.Here we are talking about reflections. the other factor is low mid absorption created by the slots. The absorption covers the whole room as far as the low mids are concerned whereas the reflections are purely related to the first reflections, not the overall room response, i.e. the decay. they are two different aspects and are not related IMO. cheers john
