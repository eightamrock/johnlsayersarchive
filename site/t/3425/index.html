John Sayers19 March 2005#3
Olivier - I've had experience with cork back in the late 60s early 70s when it was the main acoustic treatment in the studios of the time here in Australia. We used to use sheets of dark brown cork that was 1" thick and came in sheets 2' x 1' but it was also available in 2" and 3" thickness. Obviously the thicker the better but normal 1" has a NRC of around .85. So it's a reasonable absorber. For those who don't wish to have fibreglass in their rooms because of it's toxic effect (which it isn't but some think it is) it's a reasonable alternative - looks good too :) I am a fan of cork floors though - they wear extremely well, especially around heavy areas like the console and it has some give so cups, glasses and expensive gear doesn't break when dropped. cheers john
