rod gervais30 May 2007#11
OK - so what you are saying is that the system you use now works sometimes - but not when it comes down to critical mass in the room with the doors shut - right? In other words it works by sucking air from other rooms into that room when it's only warm - but that this doesn't get the job done when you need AC. If that is the case then what you have to do is add a 2nd fan - and connect it to a new return air register in the room. This register has to dump the air back into the cool air room - not the outside. Just like I show in the book. Your problem is this - if you do not have a way to get return air into your cooling chamber - you will suck air out to the point where the negative pressure in the room is equal to the fans capacity - at which point you will then have just stagnant air........... the fan will run - but it won't move air. That is the reason that ducted air conditioning system have return air ducts - basically - what goes in - has to come back - in huge commercial systems sometimes - we might do 100% fresh air to a space - but then that means we need 100% exhaust to balance it out........ But it is impossible to do 100% exhaust and thingk that you could ever draw all of that air through cracks under doors, etc......... and even if you could - that doesn't create enough negative pressure in the room to steal it from another room with NO free air in it. But if you could - those babies (around the door and the small cracks in the room) would whislte something fierce.......... So, if you add the 2nd set-up like I said to - then when you need the AC - you turn off the exhaust fan and turn on the air conditioning fan and your problem will be solved. Good luck......... oh - btr - that is not 4" flex duct - it looks more like 8"......... Sincerely, Rod
