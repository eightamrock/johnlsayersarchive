Soundman202029 July 2010#2
I'd suggest that you download the SketchUp program and use that to draw an accurate model of the space you have available, showing how the existing structure is built, and also your rough plan of what you intend to build inside it. Also, please describe in detail what it is that you want to accomplish: is this going to be a listening room? A studio? For hobby or commercial use? How loud are you? How quite do you need to be? Neighbors? Type of music (genre)? What instruments? etc. Finally, your budget is not going to take you very far. US$ 1000 is most likely not going to even get you much treatment for two rooms that size... - Stuart -
