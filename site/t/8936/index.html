rod gervais11 June 2007#2
Stan, did you ever go to my website and download the HVAC calculator? You should...... BTW - you want high return in summer and low return in winter - you have it backwards...... Cold air settles to the bottom of the room so you want to leave it there and dump ore on top of it in order to get it to cimb up to your face....... IN the winter you want to leave the thrermal layer at the ceiling and to heat up the cold air near the floow - the more hot air you dump in (with the already hot at the ceiling) the faster you develope the thermal layers you need to get the hot air downto where you live.......... your best bets are high and low returns - shut off and open up as you need them - your supply lines sound very undersized to me in general - I never expect to see less than 12" round duct feeding a control room - and usually morethan one of them to move enough air at a low enough velocity so as to be quiet.............. how did you arrive at the duct sizes you have now? What is the name and model number of your air handler unit? Rod
