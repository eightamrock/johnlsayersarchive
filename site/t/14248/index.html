rod gervais8 April 2010#5
John, this material is not a substitution for rigid anything - it is a fluffy fiberglass insulation . The stuff we install inside wall cavities except they are using a different binder - which is why they are giving it STC ratings inside walls - and why they are NOT providing us with absorption coefficients like rigid fiberglass........... Sorry - this is not the same as 703 - not even in the same ballpark......... The hard cold facts are that it is standard fiberglass with a new Green Binder to hold the fibers together....... they got rid of the formaldehyde binder. Dave, I always wonder why people go find something and then (instead of taking a moment to read the literature that is right in front of them) run here or there asking other people for their opinions. If you had spent even 45 seconds reading their available literature you would have already know what I am telling you........... Rod
