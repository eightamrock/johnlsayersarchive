barefoot8 January 2004#2
Daniel, Welcome to the forum! :D First of all, I think you're going to have a lot of problems with that short control room. Standing waves and early reflections from the rear wall will be very difficult to deal with in a room this size. You might seriously consider using the space in "studio A" for the control room as well. The acoustic treatment in "studio B" could be variable, so you could easily make the room more live or dead. Would a two room configuration work for you? Thomas
