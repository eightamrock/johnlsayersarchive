He " sagar4848". Please read the forum rules for posting (click here). You seem to be missing a couple of things! :)
Will it be okay if i make this 4th wall out of wood (single stud) cover this wooden wall with drywall on the outside (the side which won't be visible when inside the studio) stuff 6inch rock wool in the wooden frames and then cover the whole wall with breathable fabric??
Depending on which wall it is (front, back, left, right), then yes that would work, if done correctly. That is called an "inside out" wall.
Though i plan on putting a few diffusors at the back to keep it reflective.
The room is not really big enough to be able to use most types of diffusers. Also, diffusers do not make a room "reflective": They make it diffusive! If you want the room reflective, you'd have to use large, heavy, solid, rigid panels at the places where you want reflection to happen, and they would have to be larger than half the wavelength of the lowest frequency you want to reflect.
And since anyways its an extremely compact room i guess it would be fine.
"Guessing" is not a good idea with studio design! It's much better to plan it properly from the beginning, acoustically, then test the room acoustically, several times, as you install the treatment, to make sure that it is progressing as desired. - Stuart -
