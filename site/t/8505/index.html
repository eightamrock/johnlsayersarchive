Ethan Winer wrote:
I just measured the inside of my RS meter and it's 5/8 inch. Why the inside? Because microphone diameters refer to the diaphragm size, not the housing diameter! :)
You're not wrong. But we're kind of talking about different things. When discussing the size of an SLM microphone for fitting things like windscreens and calibrators, it is the outside diameter that is referenced. Hence my "standard" in quotes. I am referring to microphone sizing for SLMs, as opposed to something that would be more broadly applied to any microphone. The terminology to which you refer would be applicable to, e.g., a large diaphragm condenser mic for recording, which might have a 1" diameter mic contained within the (much larger and sometimes oddly shaped :) ) protective screen. On a more "professional" (e.g., ANSI Type 1 or 2) SLM, the actual microphone element would be sized such that the o.d. of the protective screen (not to be confused with the windscreen) is a "standard" 1/2" or 1". (Actually, the "standard" designation is more typically metric - 13 or 25 mm, respectively.) The diaphragm size itself would be a little smaller than the protective screen into which it is fit. The reason this is done is to accommodate "standardized" fittings on things like calibrators. The RS meter is unique. It has a rather bulky protective screen relative to more "professional" SLMs. (A "professional" SLM with a 1" mic would have an element that was much closer to 1" diam. than the RS' 5/8" diam.) However, the RS SLM can be calibrated with a standard 1" (25 mm) fitting on, e.g., an ACO calibrator. For the topic at hand, a windscreen for the RS SLM would be difficult to find since, not only is it a 1" diameter, but it is also rather "stubby". :D
