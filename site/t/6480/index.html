Just a note to say thanks John for the great forum. Also thanks to Ethan and Knightfly who I have gathered info from on many hours of reading. I am about 2/3 of the way through treating my bedroom studio. Just a 1 room job using large amount of broadband absorbers being used across corners and at first reflection points. Anyway, I just played a CD on my dynaudios and the difference is HUUUUUUUUGE!!!, tight bass, clear imaging. Its awesome, and I've still got 2 more 4" traps to make and a cloud to put up. To any who are wondering "is it worth the money?" This is the best $500Aus that I've ever spent on my studio. No questions. My monitors are like different speakers! This made more of a difference than anything i've bought. Cant wait to get recording. thanks again. Peter

Peterr23 June 2006#3
Hi, I dont have a link to anything on the forum - I haven't really needed to talk about it much on the forum, I read up on other peoples situations and matched the stuff told to them that would suit my studio, I ended up using doubled up 50mm (100mm total) 32kg/m3 semi rigid in pine frames: *2.4x1.2m broadband corner traps across the front corners of the room (floor to ceiling). *more corner traps across all ceiling-wall intersections. *Cloud over mix position *2.0x1.2m Rear wall trap - 100mm off wall. *small 50mm absorbers at first reflection points of speakers on side and front walls. all of these are non-reflective to high frequencies because of my small room (4.0x3.5x3.0m). There is still plenty of bare wall space so its not completely dead. I may add some small diffusers over sections of the traps if I start to feel like im in a padded cell. :P
