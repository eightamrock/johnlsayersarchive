Ethan Winer16 July 2003#2
Darryl, I think most "cheap looking" ceiling tiles are made of shredded paper. You can buy tiles made of rigid fiberglass, and the ones I've seen tend to have a thin white plastic facing. I've bought 1/2 inch thick tiles at Home Depot, and you can get them up to an inch thick elsewhere. --Ethan

Ethan Winer17 July 2003#4
Darryl, > white plastic facing is acoustically transparent? < Nothing is full transparent. Most plastic facings have a pattern of holes, and this determines how much the panel reflects at the highest frequencies. > If you double layer them ... probably wrap them inside some kind of fabric < Then why pay extra for ceiling tiles? Just get 1-inch 703, which will cost less than tiles. --Ethan
