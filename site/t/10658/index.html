Paul, thats a nice design you have going there. i think the mdf vs ply is a matter of cost and aesthetics... though, i would think ply is stronger (but i'm sure your bro can answer that). as for the speaker position... that would depend on how big your room is... your trying to keep an equilateral triangle... ideally, probably on stands which would give you better isolation from misc vibrations and reflections from your desk.... but that being said, mine are on my desk sitting on top of some insolation (temp)... and it's not terrible... Ro is correct though, theres a bunch of posts on furniture/desks... prob both materials on the site, have a look around. sandled
