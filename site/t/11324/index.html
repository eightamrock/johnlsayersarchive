Soundman20204 November 2008#10
a. I prefer a wooden floor ( floated ) not only for cosmetic reasons but also for wiring ducts
Probably not a good idea: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=8173
c. Should I angle the ceiling in the control room or live room,? And what would be the highest point.
The general recommendation that I've seen is to angle your ceiling at 12 degrees, with the lowest point at the front wall (over your speakers) and the highest point behind you, at the back wall. Your side walls should be splayed 6 to 12 degrees too, and it looks like yours are splayed about that much. Have you thought about how you are going to mount your speakers? Stands or soffit? I don't see them shown on your plan, and there doesn't seem to be a lot of space there to put them on stands, so I guess you are going with soffit mounting? - Stuart -

Not just for all the reasons mentioned in the topic but also the fact that if you build a floating floor in a basement , the only way to keep moisture from slowly eating the frame is ventilate. which means more construction problems , more money, more time.... concerning the walls, it's probably not that clear on the drawing but i've splayed them at exactly 6 degrees.
