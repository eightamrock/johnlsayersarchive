gullfo14 November 2010#2
i would center the mix desk on the long exterior wall. placing the absorbers around the room and overhead on first reflection points and the entry door. one option might be to make several book cases to be gobos between the wardrobe and entry door wall - a pseudo wall with spaces between the bookcases to allow the sound to enter the wardrobes. and be moved when needed. adding a cloud will definitely help. scattering absorption is more effective on the walls and ceiling than blanketing them. using the superchunks in the corners (and use wall-ceiling corners as well) is good idea. if things start getting too dead, consider some plywood panels over the corner traps and various places in the room in order to keep HF alive.
