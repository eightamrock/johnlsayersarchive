I'm planning on building a pair of the Killatones but the FE127E has been discontinued. I don't want to pay extra for the FX120's and was wondering if anyone has tried the FE126En? It is the same housing and cone as the FE127E except it has a slightly bigger magnet. I've read reviews that say the high end is a bit harsher and lacks the body that the FE127E has, but this might be compensated by a series resistor to raise the Q. I don't know enough about speakers to know what that means as far as what value resistor to choose, or how it would implement with the existing Killatone circuit. Was also noticing that the Killatone schematic specifies four 8 ohm resistors and was wondering if there is any reason why I can't use two 16 ohm resistors instead? Thanks in advance for any help on this.
