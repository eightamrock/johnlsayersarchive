rod gervais18 April 2006#3
Travis, No one can answer your quiestion - because it isn't done like that. A partition is given an STC rating by measuring its Transmission Loss over a range of 16 different frequencies between 125-4000 Hz. Not only that - it has to be done in a lab setting where the flanking limits are known. Seeing as there are no individual measurements to average - nor is there any way to determine (for either us or you) what contribution flanking has to do with your results (even if you measured individual frequencies) no one can answer the question. The layman's answer would be that the original wall would seem to be around an STC 41 and the best fix was an STC 46. But I don't understand why you aren't just using tested designs - why experiment with anything new? Sincerely, Rod
