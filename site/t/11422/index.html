AVare21 November 2008#8
Flat reverb time over frequency. Reverb time = .15logV(in ft^3). Evenly spread treatment in room. Dead around stage. With all you have given, that is more than enough. Obviously you know that you have no large reflecting surfaces, as you have not detailed that. Simialrly there are no coupled spaces or acoustic shadows. Unless it is acoustic music.... Andre
