Soundman202031 July 2014#5
Nope! You are not doing it wrong at all. In fact, you seem to be doing things right! :) But I'm pretty sure that nobody here will give you a quote unless John can't take on your project for whatever reason. Those of us who do offer design services here respect the fact that this is John's forum, not ours, and we are only helping out here by John's invitation. So we'll only quote for a project if John isn't able to do it himself. It's just a matter of ethics, not anything that you did or didn't do. There just aren't that many good and ethical studio designers around, and those of us who participate on this forum all respect each other, so we won't be trying to grab projects from others, especially not from John. As far as I'm aware, the situation on Gearslutz is different, since it isn't owned and operated by a studio designer, so probably anybody would be willing to quote over there. I'd suggest that if you don't get a reply from John, then PM us individually (the moderators). I'd also suggest that it would be a good idea to be wary of quotes you get after broadly publishing a request on the Internet! It would probably be a good idea to carefully check out the people who send you quotes, check out the work they have done, and ask for references from their clients. It's real easy to pretend to be someone or something on the Internet... - Stuart -
