Berre10 September 2007#1
Hello everybody. Already for a long time I am dreaming of building my own space an I believe that now the time has finally come! I am currently planning to build a studio in my backyard that also needs to serve as a rehearsal space. I already have a fairly good idea of how I want the space to look like, but would like to have some other, fresh ideas from you guys, too. it never hurts to get a second opinion, eh... Well, here's the plan. Location I live in a fairly quiet neighbourhood near Leuven, Belgium. This means that I want/need to keep the outside levels to an absolute minimum. Local regulations impose a limit of 35dBA at 4m! SO this means that the isolation requirements are fairly heavy. Construction The studio will be built in a completely new cave/cellar/basement... So, basically, completely under the ground. It will have no connection whatsoever with my or anyones house. So this means that 5 sides of the 'box' will be surrounded with soil 100%. This should help the isolation, no? The top (=weakest link) will also be covered with soil for the better part. however, to cater for the entrance with stairs, the lavatory and some other stuff, a small shed will be built on top. The only measurement that is a real hard limit is the length of 10m (outside measurement). The width I would like to keep it around 6.5m inside. My goal is to have ca. 3m height inside the innner rooms. The cave itself will be made with cast concrete to ensure that it is 100% waterproof. The 'roof' of the cave will be constructed with giant concrete blocks (I don't know the english name for it). So basically, the only whole will b ethe one for the stairs. Inside rooms will be built using concrete blocks of 150mm thickness. That is basically the standard way over here and is also very good for isolation between the rooms. Rooms I mainly want three rooms: a control room (my room), a recording/rehearsal area and a machine/storage room. The control room and recording area should have sufficient isolation to allow me to work on my own stuff in the control room while another band is rehearsing in the big room. That's why I think I will need two room-within-the-room's. My first idea was to build each of these two rooms on its own floating floor. However, because of cost reasons I would like to know whether that is really required? Can I achieve my isolation requirements if I would build only one of the rooms (e.g. the control room) on a a floating floor? If so, Do I still need to build the other room as a room-within-the-room (i.e. cave) too? Layout I have included a first shot of how I think this space can be organised. My main gripe is that I currently cannot make a window between the two rooms. I also would like to make the rehearsal area a little bit bigger, but the stairs are a bit in the way. I'm also not 100% satisfied with the way the doors are positioned. So, to sum up, I am not very happy with my own results yet. For my control room I used the ratios of 1:1.14:1.39 which gave me a nice area with a height of 3m, that doesn't take too much space for the rest of the rooms. Anyway, if anyone has some more ideas/suggestions, they are more than welcome. Many thanks in advance. Kind regards, B-)
