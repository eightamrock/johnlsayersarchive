jwl11 August 2008#2
Most standard ceiling tiles (such as Armstrong here in the states) are designed for office buildings, etc., and to be effective mostly at speech frequencies. They are usually white, with a hardened, textured surface. In my experience, these tiles are too reflective at high frequencies to be useful for studios. In general, I prefer absorptive ceilings, so you'd be better off using cloth-covered rigid fiberglass or rockwool panels dropped in your ceiling grid. For more on using ceiling tiles in a studio ceiling, see: http://www.realtraps.com/p_ceiling.htm
