Has anyone taken a look at the updated Green Glue website? Looks like they've added a tone of Test Data and i've got a question. Based of the numbers it looks like using 2 sheets of 1/2 inch drywall with green glue is better than 2 sheets of 5/8 with green glue. The difference is mostly in the Mids-HF. The 1/2 assembly has 8dB of TL at 2kHz more than the 5/8. Where as the 5/8 has only 2db of TL more at 160Hz. I was originally going to build my walls from 5/8s but I'm reconsidering now that I've gone through this data. Does anyone have any thoughts on this?

Soundman20202 August 2019#3
Which two are you comparing? There about two dozen different wall tests show there, with many different combinations of materials and dimensions. It's nt clear which two you are comparing. In any even, I would not be too concerned about how the wall performs at 2 kHz. 8 dB extra up so high is nice, but what's more important is the low end. IF the low end is good, then you are pretty much guaranteed that the mids and highs will be doing fine as well. But anyway, please identify which two walls you are looking at, so we are all on the same page. - Stuart -
