I have a stupidly small space to use as a studio for production and some basic recording. The room is 7.5x7x8 feet (LxWxH). It only has half of a back wall due to the fact that there is a garage door (insulated) on the right side. I've gone thru a few renditions of the acoustic treatment layout in Sketchup and I think I'm getting close to a workable concept. Let me know any critiques you have. Images linked below. https://imgur.com/a/R4hglGv The monitors will be angles at 34 degrees from parallel instead of the traditional 30, I'm trying to avoid a cloud unless its a must since it would have to be blocking the only light in the space.
