Deluks6 October 2009#2
No takers it seems. If you haven't yet done a runner then I'll briefly chip in with a few thoughts. Do you share the flat roof with your neighbours? If not then stripping and reboarding on top of the existing deck would increase the mass somewhat so that it becomes closer to that of the concrete blocks walls. (you won't be able to match it for performance but the current 20mm sterling board is in no way adequate for an outer leaf) The blocks you have inserted between top of existing wall and roofdeck are a smart move soundwise, but you may now be restricting ventilation to the roof timbers. These must be ventilated and kept dry, so you will have to hermetically seal the inner room by using vapour barrier (plastic sheeting) or foil backed plasterboard on one of the inner wall layers. The timbers can still be ventilated by using mechanical extraction (ie: an extractor fan or two drawing air across the roof void) This will obviously need to have baffles on the in and outtake. You may get away with not doing this if you can insulate on top of the roof (warm deck construction) or can completely fill the roofspace with rockwool, right up against the OSB, before sealing in with plastic. Thus denying moist air the chance to circulate. You say the neighbours garage is seldom used. Could you afford to make 'em an offer for it?
