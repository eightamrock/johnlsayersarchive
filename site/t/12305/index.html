Hey everyone, after about a month of research on this board and months of research on AVS(a home theatre board-helps with acoustic/soundproofing concepts), I think I am ready to to start framing. My goal is to build a room that I can play my drums in the basement and not disturb my grandparents on the first floor. I have zero desire the prevent sound from being transmitted to other rooms on my basement floor. Eventually it may become a jam room for a typical 5pc. band. P.S. I'm loud, :shot: Current State -No construction has been done. -Basement room -1 Window -1 Obstruction-drain pipe Plans - Desired Room Dimensions: approx. 19.5' L x 10.5' W x 7' H - Floor: Delta FL(vapor barrier), OSB on top, Carpet over OSB - Framing: 2x4 studs framed over the osb - Wall Construction: Not sure what to do here. I have a general idea of what to do but I can't decide on whether or not to build inside-out walls or not. I tried searching but "inside" and "out" are too common and it won't let me search them. I like the idea of inside walls because it seems easier/more effective for acoustical treatment but I'm not sure if this is the right path, any suggestions? I also planned on framing 1-2" away from the foundation walls, so if I do inside-out walls the air-gap is 3.5" less, is that a concern on walls next to the foundation? -Ceiling: 2x6 joists attached to the new room's frame/not coupled to the existing frame; approx. 1" lower that existing joists. 1- layer Gypsum board between existing joists/ insulation/ 2 layers of gypsum or plywood attached to new joists. -Budget- Next to nothing? haha, seriously my budget would probably be around $1000.
