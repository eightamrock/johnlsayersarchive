John Sayers29 June 2006#7
Hi Michel - Normally I hold it all up by wrapping it up with a plastic wrap. Yes - garden plastic - like Alex did in the following pic for his ceiling. I've found it acts as a HF reflector despite the cloth cover yet the rest goes through. It keeps the liveness and clarity in the room IMO. http://johnlsayers.com/Studio/Mainpage/MP-Funky_1.htm
External image, not preserved — original: http://johnlsayers.com/Studio/Images/Funky_2/ceiling1.jpg
The speaker front can change for any speaker, providing the depth is OK for the speaker size. It basically extends the speaker baffle to the floor except it adds an absorbent front to allow for reflections off the rear of the gear and puter screens etc. The port allows for floor/wall bass buildup to have somewhere to go where it hopefully dissipates. ;) cheers john

John Sayers28 January 2007#12
student - the rear port on many speakers is there to support the low end response. Soffit mounting ALSO supports the low end response. IMHO they balance out in the low end but the soffit mount also supports the rest of the response by making the stereo imaging much tighter and more accurate. cheers john
