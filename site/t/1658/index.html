Ooops. I guess more info is in order: I record music mostly, light rock to hard rock, some jazz, and occasional voice overs. Accomodating a full band is a requirement. Isolation from the upstairs is important - i would like to get 60STC, but i think 40 is more realistic on my budget. All mixing and most mastering is done in house, so a good sounding control room is a must. If i did my math right, i think i'm right around 1500 cubic foot. Anything bigger than that really messes up the ceiling to width to length ratios since the ceiling is so low, so i think that's all i can get. The seating position is at about 45% of the depth of the room, which is close to the 9th order node, but i think i'm safe. Someone with more experience in this please help. :-) Tanx again!

Aaronw27 May 2004#12
Veatch, Another thing you can do (and this may depend on your ceiling height), but you can do what I did, which was remove a post and installed a steel beam under the existing beam if needed, to widen your space. You will want to consult w/ a structural engineer though. Fortunately, my step father does this type of work, so he made all the calcs and told me exactly what I needed. I was surprised, only a 4" steel beam for a 18' 10" span. And the cost was only about $200 + beer & Ribs for all the help. :D Aaron
