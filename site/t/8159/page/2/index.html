gullfo wrote:
what is the room constructed of? how does it currently sound? one thought is to "incrementally" treat the room. put in absorbers in the corners to improve your low-mid broadband asorption. put up some wall and ceiling based absorption to kill off flutter echos. put in some carpeting. keep working the room until it sounds good. keep in mind if the treatments can be adjusted then you can change the room - if you need it deader, turn the treatments around to face the "soft" side out, when you need more life, turn them face out to the "hard" side.
