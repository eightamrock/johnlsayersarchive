soffit speaker angle

Started by bambino on 11 December 2004. 3 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 2800.

bambino11 December 2004#1
Hi, I am currently building a new studio and the main speakers (Genelec 1034B) will be soffit mounted. I understand the ideal angle of the speakers is 30 degrees. If i do that though the sweet spot will be too far back in the room since the speakers are quite far apart. Is it possible to go up to a 45 degree angle?
John Sayers12 December 2004#2
officially it is 30 degrees but I've worked sucessfully at 45. cheers john
bambino12 December 2004#3
Thanks for the reply. Would it have to be either 30 or 45 degrees or could it be anywhere in between? And does the speaker angle have any consequences for the acoustics in the room? The room looks as follows: the side walls are splayed at 6 degrees and the ceiling at 12. There is diffusion at rear wall, bass traps in rear corners, in ceiling and hanging bass traps in some of the cavities between floating room and external construction. Mid and high absorption in side walls.
John Sayers13 December 2004#4
It can be anywhere in between and it shouldn't affect the rest of your room. cheers john
