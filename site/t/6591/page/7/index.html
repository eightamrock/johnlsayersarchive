sharward18 July 2007#167
Nope, mechanical fresh air ventilation must port directly to/from the outdoors. I'm pretty sure exhausting into an attic is against code (I know it won't pass in the case of exhaust fans in laudry rooms and bathrooms), and I'm nearly certain sucking air out of the attic is a no-no as well. Have you studied the benefits of HRV and ERV systems? There's great information about them on the Fantech Web site. Mind you, the systems are designed for whole houses and are designed to improve indoor air quality with relatively small amounts of air exchange. In our case we're using them in lieu of operable windows, so the exchange rate is higher, but the square footage is lower to make up for that. In short, during the heating season, warmth from air being exhausted warms the incoming air, so the room is not chilled as much, which saves energy. A similar effect occurs during the cooling season, when cool air being exhausted is warmed slightly by the warm air entering the system, thus transferring some of the heat out of the incoming air, which has the effect of cooling it slightly. More energy is saved during the heating season than the cooling season to be sure, but it's still a pretty cool concept (no pun intended ;-)). Rod is arguably the most knowledgeable person on the forum on the subject of HVAC, so you might PM him with an invitation to visit your latest posts for his feedback. As always, his words on the subject trump mine! :lol: --Keith :mrgreen:
