My Opinion: You do have a little more space then some of us. Basically GG at 100% coverage makes 2 layers of drywall act like 4 and at 50% GG makes 2 layers of drywall act like 3. Why not save some money use GG only on ceiling and areas where dead load, space and the left over GG where you want to beef up iso? Adding layers of drywall is cheaper then GG although a little more work. The TR should be isolated well enough BUT personally I would have concerns about at least having some iso in CR, BUT this is not my build... non the less I'll state my opinion. -You are going to end up with a double wall between CR and TR anyways. -Recommended mixing level (without causing listeners discomfort) is 85 -dB's which does have the potential to bother neighbors, but not drastically like a live drum kit. -With out a well isolated TR you loose the option of recording in the TR. For a solo working singer songwriter/wanna be engineer (like me) not being able to recording in TR with access to controls would be PITA. -While mixing or editing a dynamic track I would not want to be disturbed by outside noise. -Why not have some amount of iso in the CR, the windows and doors will be really good sound leaks. You've probably thought all this threw, thought I would "double check you" AND that's my opinion.
