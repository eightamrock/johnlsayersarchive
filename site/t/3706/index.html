Sounds like it's Knauf's version of OC 700 series; however, according to that page "4"x48"x96" KNAUF FG 850F ET BOARD 96/SLV Fiber Glass insulation board is a thermal and accoustical insulation product made from inorganic glass fibers preformed into 24 in. x 48 in. boards bonded by a thermosetting resin. The boards are available plain, with a factory applied foil-skrim-kraft (FSK) facing or with a factory applied all service jacket (ASJ) facing. Sold in full cartons only. Fiber Glass board is available in selected densities and thickness" According to their text, these are either 48" x 96" boards or they are 24" x 48" boards - this makes them either $.30 a square foot (not a bad deal depending on thickness/density) or $.07 a foot (a great deal REGARDLESS of thickness or density) - hmmmmm...
