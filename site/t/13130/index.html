Soundman20206 June 2009#6
The problem with your second layout is that it is not symmetrical. Room symmetry is very important. Without symmetry you cannot get an accurate sound stage, and your mixes will not "translate" very well. Also, with your design, you have a hard, reflective surface right behind you: the window. You will get reflections off that coming back to your ears. There are a couple of other reasons why I would not go with your second layout. As John already said, the first one is much better. - Stuart -

Ro7 June 2009#10
depends on the wool for practical reasons. If your wool is 120x60cm for example, you can cut it into half en cut those half pieces into quarters using a diagonal cut. Then your width will be 60*60cm :) For basstraps a 50x50cm is a starting point, more wool is better low end absorption.
