cporro16 July 2009#5
i think mineral wool and fiberglass are both mold resistant. but honestly i'd make sure the waterproofing was sound first. mold or no mold excessive water/moisture is bad. a good product for "waterproofing" is xypex concentrate. i've used it twice with success. is this the drylock paint you are using? from what i know that stuff isn't nearly as effective. then again xypex is more difficult to apply. maybe you should wait for rain season before you close up the wall. serious. but i don't really understand what the concern is fiberglass or rockwool in a wall. what do you expect to gain from thermafiber?

cporro17 July 2009#7
i think the difference between 2.5pcf and 4pcf in a wall cavity is going to be negligible. if you were using them as room treatments...well, there would be a difference in absorbtion but nothing huge to my knowledge. even if you leave the insulation out of the wall you aren't going to see dramatic changes in the STC ratings. i have a standard wall (2x4 framing, 2 sides of 5/8th" drywall) at an STC of 33 and one with insulation at 36. if you fill something with like 9" of insulation you will start to see a greater (maybe 10-14) reduction. personally i would not sweat what was in the wall, just put something in there.
