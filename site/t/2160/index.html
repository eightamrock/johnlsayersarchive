If you want a 5.1 room, you can't use a LEDE design. Check out the AES library. The white paper states that the acoustics must be uniform (treatment must be the same on all four walls). There is lots of interesting reading material on the AES site. Check it out.

I don't understand your post. Why are you mentioning LEDE? Just for some more info... I don't mind having my drum set in its own isolated room. In the "band" I'm jamming with, I'm not the drummer-- and he brings his own set. Also, I don't mind having the baby grand roomed off either-- there is an upright that can be out on the stage area. The only door I have shown in the pic is the entrance to the building, mostly because I don't know where to place the other doors. I figured the control room would "face" the stage and have a window, as well as a window provided for the vocal booth, piano and drum rooms. The vocal room was a last minute thought, but it would be nice, as I record readings of newspapers for the blind. One change that the drawing doesn't reflect is the width of the "entertaining area." After some discussion with the other half, and things this space would be useful for, we figured a 24' width might be better than an 18' width. Of course, since this is the "less professional" area of the building, I guess that doesn't matter too much. This drawing is only an idea, no part of it is set in stone. I know alot will likely have to be changed, and I can accept it if the entire idea is wrong or undoable. Fire away!
