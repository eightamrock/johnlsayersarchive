lovecow16 January 2008#4
If you do hear a difference, make sure your friends don't mind holding the loudspeakers while you mix. :lol: Of course, if they start complaining of arm and/or back pain after the first or second day (ingrates!), there are a couple of commercial products I know of that can replace your friends (neither of which will demand pizza and beer for their trouble): Auralex - MoPads Primacoustic - Recoil Stabilizer There may be others as well. More low-tech, you could try using mouse pads or even mineral fiber insulation (preferably wrapped in something). For these options, you may need to experiment with different thicknesses to figure out what's going to work best. There is an "optimum" thickness for this sort of thing - i.e., not too thick; not too thin - that will depend on the material. HTH. Best regards,
