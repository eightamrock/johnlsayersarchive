John Sayers18 May 2009#2
Hi Russ - welcome. You will have to build a room within room construction to isolate the sound of the drums so you will have to devise an internal truss system to enable you to utilise the ceiling space. the window is not a problem as you can just add another window to the inner skin - pretty normal practice. It's not a very large space so it will need pretty comprehensive treatment to tame a drum kit yet still allow you to mix in a not too dead room.
Soundman202018 May 2009#3
And the second thing is the window - i would like to keep daylight in there but it would be quite close beside the mix position (which i'd like to be at the opposite end to the door) - can anyone point me to any posts on a similar situation - how best to keep the daylight without the problems of a sheet of glass next to my right ear?
It depends on your layout for your mixing room, but if it ends up with that window at a first reflection point (which probably will be the case, according to my good friend Murphy!) then maybe the easiest thing would be to build a gobo on wheels, and just wheel it into place in front of the window, with the absorbent side facing your head, whenever you need to mix. Then you can wheel it away again when you are not doing critical listening, and want to admire the view or get daylight in there.
but i'm probably going to need some way of screening off his kit so i dont get problems with it resonating away in the corner while i'm working - could it be as simple as covering it up with heavy drapes or is that just wishful thinking?
That might work, provided that the cloth thoroughly damps all of the resonant surfaces, meaning that it needs to be in contact with any part of the kit that is designed to vibrate. I'm not sure what that would do to the skins, though: I'm not a drummer, but it occurs to me that having a heavy item resting on the skins for long periods of time might not be a good idea. I'm not 100% convinced that this would work, since the resonant cavities inside the drums would still be there, but I reckon that if the skins are damped then it should be OK. But the best way to find out is probably just to try it!
