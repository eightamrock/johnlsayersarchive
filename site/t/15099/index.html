Hello gang, been a long time since I worked on my studio, but I'm finally back at it. My situation is this: I started in an old shed that I wanted to do a room-in-a-room 2 leaf configuration, but also, since I was low on ceiling height, use hat channel on clips for the ceiling. I have installed Green Glue Whisperclips and the hat channel, to 5/8" drywall, then greenglued on 1/2" drywall. At this point, I am getting ready to frame in the inner room. The plan is to have these free-standing walls glued to the cement below and nailed down, to insure they are secure. My question is this: Should the frame be attached to the suspended ceiling in any way? The only downside I can see is the reduced suspension effect, but all the same, it would still be only attached to the first leaf of the sound barrier. But I want to get your opinion on the matter before continuing my project as I want it optimized the best I can. Thanx! JW
