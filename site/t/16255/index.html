2" X 8" joists might span, if done properly a 13 foot 8 inch area from outside of wall to outside of wall. To attempt to add the additional weight you suggest is asking for trouble to begin with since this framing member will simply not support it.
gullfo17 June 2011#5
if you are thinking of nesting in the mass, it will still take away head space. even though it isn't ideal to lose the additional height, you might consider RC-2 and 2x 5/8" drywall after beefing up BUT you might not support a full 4x 5/8" drywall (2 on the floor and 2 on the RC - depending on what you have - span, existing loads, live loads, etc. you might have to nest in another set of joists (although that can also be hard because of any cross-bracing/blocking you have or need with the existing joists). definitely get a structural engineer in to examine and determine what additional load you might be able to support with the existing structure. otherwise - consider electronic drums and in-the-board DI stuff for the electric instruments. mix @ reasonable levels.
