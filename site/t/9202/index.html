Hi All, Only just registered but been reading these forums for a few months. I am very impressed by the quaility of the posters and the knowledge that is freely shared. Intro We are going to contruct a small studio in our Church. This studio will used to mix mostly live recordings and record overdubs. We would like to construct a control room and a small recording room. We have mixed a couple of albums in the current room and know we need to do more with the space to make a better space for mixing. Current Space The current space is two rooms but the rooms are not symetrical. Thinking the larger room would be the control room and the smaller room become the recording room. The current wall between the two rooms is suspected to not be load bearing but will be checked. So this possibly could be removed. Picture included of the current space with dimensions. The building is in an industial estate and therefore leaking noise out is not really an issue. The external wall faces a road and currently the road noise coming in is an issue. With the large and not properly sealed windows on the external wall leaking the noise. These will need to be sealed. First Go I've had a go at creating the control room space and have included a picture. I am after ideas whether this is a good option or whether any of you have any other ideas. Is not having a bass trap in the corner by the door an issue? Including the sketchup file for reference. Sketchup is amazing but I am still learning how to drive it. Thanks for looking. Craig.
