i'd strongly recommend you do this legally. get the proper permits and inspections. do the electric and air properly. this could be re-used later as a "home theater" etc.for resale value. if you build this without the proper permits and you have a fire or experience water damage, or someone is injured, it could void your insurance coverage. not to mention the safety aspects of no air, no egress, and improper electric. gullfo10 November 2010#7
in general, the pink stuff won't absorb as much LF as rigid insulation will. i'd hold out for the rigid insulation for the cloth cover absorption sections of the wall, and use the pink stuff in the slat and panel covered sections of the wall. this way you can mix up the absorption types used to get the room under control. yeah the room isn't ideal but if you went to 11'1" on the length it could help by putting the room into a better overall ratio 1:1.2:1.49. and then you would have the option to make two rooms instead of one with a pair of back to back slider doors. this would give you a reasonable drum and reasonable mixing space which can then be treated to be more optimal for each one.
