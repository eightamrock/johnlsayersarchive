BriHar22 June 2011#2
I would suggest looking for a somewhat higher ceiling if you can. Rectangular room should be fine, you can always add splayed slat resonators, clouds etc. to create an RFZ. I wouldn't worry too much about room ratios for the size of room you're considering. You might want to have more absorption on the rear wall. Haas kickers are not much in vogue any more as their impact on the room sound is minimal, particularly in smaller rooms where the size genrally hinders the effects of diffusion. You haven't given any regard to isolation issues. As it seems you haven't presently got a location, this might be a consideration, at which time you might want to reevaluate using angled walls.
