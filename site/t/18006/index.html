We are looking in to skim coats, self leveling underlayments and/or paint. For a 100 year old concrete floor it is in pretty good shape - but the surface has some pitting. Is this a different 'Rooster Studio' that you were posting about previously? Its a lot easier if you keep to one post and add to it that way all the information is kept together :) if its a totally different place then I apologise for asking the question. In answer to your question do you not like laminate wood or real wood floors, with the better laminates you could just hoover the dust with an industrial hoover and probably latex it to level it then lay a 500 gauge polythene and an acoustic underlay which is 6mm or 8mm thick I think with the laminate anything from 8mm to 15mm thick I think, it looks good and sounds good as far as I'v read on here and some of the flooring can have 20 or 25 year guarantee, its what I'm using for my build.
