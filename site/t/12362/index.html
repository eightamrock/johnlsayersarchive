Soundman202029 April 2009#3
Anyway we are starting work tomorrow so ...
You are starting to build tomorrow, but you don't have a plan of what you are going to build? I guess I must have misunderstood what you were saying! :) My comments on a couple of issues I see: 1) The room is not symmetrical. That should be your first priority, Make it symmetrical. A non-symmetrical control room will be a disaster. Symmetry is paramount. 2) That "triple-glazed window" is probably going to be a problem. Not what you do there, you are going to have at least four leaves, which means that your low frequency isolation will be pretty bad. Of course, you didn't say how loud you are, nor how quite you need to be outside, and neither did you mention any potential exterior noises that you need to prevent from getting in, so maybe that isn't even a problem at all. If you don't need isolation, and there is no noise at all outside, then the three-leaf window might not be an issue at all. 3) The stone walls and block walls might be OK, but it's hard to say without knowing more about them, and more about what you intend to do. What does "slatted with 2x1 and plasterboard internally" mean? 4) The plasterboard wall on the right is going to be another problem, since once again you are going to end up with three leaves there ( at least) no matter what you do. Once again, it depends what is on the other side of that wall, how loud you are, etc. You can fix it be removing the plasterboard from one side, and perhaps using what you take off to beef up the other side internally, if necessary. My $0.02 - Stuart -
