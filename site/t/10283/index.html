HOOK4 March 2008#4
I think that you are really talking about sound insulation from the other rooms (rumble from other players) and that is something you don´t solve with basstraps or other acoustic treatment. Acoustic treatment will not solve the problem with sound leaking into the room from the outside (or the other way around). A nice broadband absorber behind the singer, however, will improve the sound in the room, and the sound of your recording. Check this out: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=5930 For your other Qs I suggest you go here: http://thewombforums.com/ as the Qs about DeEsser and "settle sounds in a mix" kind of things is more suitable on a recording forum and Mixermans forum is one of the best recording forums there is. Check out the "Womb University" forum firstly. HOOK
