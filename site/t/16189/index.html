Hello, My Recording teacher, Bruce Tambling, owns a studio that has a second control room which he is currently unhappy with. Once upon a time he had someone come in and do an acoustics design which consisted of very small and ineffective absorption and trapping. He has asked me to help him bring it up to a level that he can confidently mix in it with. He is interested in consulting with John as well to make sure the design is correct and making best use of the space, and is willing to pay for his services, (preferably at an hourly rate). We have emailed John but did not receive any response, probably because he is busy. The budget is not certain but there is definitely around 1500-2000 dollars for materials (which should pay for the thermafiber (we have a pretty good deal on it of $165 for 3.5 in 24"x 48" semirigid blankets), plywood, and miscellaneous fabric, plastic, and hardware) Bruce's request for the design is for it to be modular so that when he changes spaces he can use this to begin treating a new space. Because of this I believe that broadband absorption should be used for the most part, as different spaces will have different problems and tuned panel absorbers would address the problems of this specific space (however I could be wrong about this and am open to understanding why). I showed him John's "Components" design, since the room in that design is very similar to Bruce's and the only thing he did not like in it was the soffits because he plans on upgrading from Genelec's to Focal SM9's and these speakers are definitely not similar in shape or size. Also the SM9's use a passive sub instead of a port and the passive sub is on the speaker top which I believe makes them Unsoffitable (but I may be wrong) :?: Below is a picture of them:

i think you have a pretty good design there. i would add another cloud unit sideways in the empty space behind the main cloud to break up any floor ceiling slap echoes. also, on main set of clouds - make the first pair angled around 22.5° front low, back high, then the second pair about 7.5° front low back high and have the front end of the second set be "soft". if you need the side slat SU component - it's attached.
