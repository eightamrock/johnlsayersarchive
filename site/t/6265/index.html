Started by Luftweg on 21 May 2006. 4 replies.

Luftweg21 May 2006#1
In doing searches to get rough costs of steel materials for framing (versus standard wood framing), I keep seeing 'comparable' costs... Is anyone using steel framing? What are your material costs? What attracts me to steel is consistency of material (it's always straight), and it's not affected by moisture (galvanized) as much as wood, nor will it shrink. But, I'm ordering some 4" by 1.62", 33 mil (20 gauge) studs, and these are costing $10 per 12 foot stud. Granted, these are hefty studs to be used by me as inner-leaf ceiling joists. Yet, it would seem that the material cost of steel is alot higher than wood.... Hmmm?
