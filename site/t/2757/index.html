Hi, and welcome to the forum - first, can you please edit your profile to include a location? Too hard to suggest things not knowing where people are in the world. Next, "attic" and "room in room" both raise large warning flags, not to mention "sleeping parents below" - would need a LOT more info to even hazard a guess on the feasibility. Things like - Existing floor/ceiling joist size/spacing Span between upright supports Existing construction, top to bottom, of attic floor Slope and height of existing ceiling, including floor plan showing what is where Stairwell construction and framing, as well as existing sheathing (if any) Pix would help, as well as the above info Whether or not live drums will be used Budget approximation (along with your location, this is VITAL) Anything else you can think of that may affect sound travel - About the only question I can answer without all this is - Yes, it can be done - whether it can be done in your case depends on most of the above. If your space is pretty tight already, it will get a lot tighter with the necessary changes - So please, change your profile and dig into these areas, let's get this going... Steve
