AVare18 June 2008#13
There is much involved with sound isolation for studios. The first thing is single number ratings like STC and the European Rw are not really good for music because they do not take into account the low frequencies, which are the hardest to control, and are strongest in music. For music, most designers look at how much sound is let through the partition (general word for walls, roofs, floors etc.) at different frequencies. This is referred to as the Transmission Loss (TL). Graphs are usually made up to show the TL versus frequency. A typical TL graph is attached, taken from NRC IR 761, which has test results for 151 different gypsum board walls. The graph is for a double stud wall with 16 mm drywall on one side and 2 layers on the other, with insulation in between. Andre
