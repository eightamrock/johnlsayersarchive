At a later point if i wanted to improve this i would consider another external layer as my ext cladding is the weak point. Cheers Matt
Earshot11 December 2008#34
To update progress, I have essentially completed the live room bar a few more broadband absorbers. In the last week I've moved onto the control room, laying a grooved ply lining on the ceiling and framing up the speaker soffits. After a somewhat impatient test with speakers in place, i have found a large amount of sound leakage via ducting between the rooms despite silencer boxes. Hopefully i should be able to isolate these further before the bezel is applied. I also found that mounting the monitors off a wall shelf lead to a lot of direct transfer of sound through the wall into live room. I think i was kind of hoping this somehow magically wouldn't happen but anyway... ended up going to the effort of constructing some metal speaker stands that I think will be much better. Anyhow im starting to feel excited about progress again after completing the more tedious jobs, will post pics soon so this all makes sense, Matt
