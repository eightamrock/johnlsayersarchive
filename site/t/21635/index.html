Soundman202020 June 2018#2
The best fabric for studios is made by a company called "Guilford of Maine". It's an American company, but they might have a dealer or rep in the UK. Not sure. You could e-mail them. Pretty much any good-quality fabric that is breathable will work fine. Try stores that sell fabric for upholstery, such as re-covering old sofas and suchlike. The only acoustic property that you need, is that it is "breathable": hold a piece over your mouth and nose, and if you can breath through it without too much resistance, then it should be fine. But if you really have to breath hard to force your breath through it, that's not so good. - Stuart -
