But it seems like what you are trying to do is more like a drum riser than a floating floor. There's no reason why that shouldn't work, since you are not basically just trying to stop the transmission of impact noise into the floor.
I wonder if this kind of transmission would also happen if the concrete would be directly on solid earth (ground level)?
I reckon it would, but to a lesser extent since it would be damped by the entire planet! So you'd still get some impact noise, but at a lower level, and the "ringing" would be gone.
About the floating floor. I have to agree with Mika. In order to make it work, the topfloor will have to weigh 'tons' in order to make it's own (resonance) frequency very low. Paul Woodlock had some excellent research on this in his build diary, if I'm not mistaken. Actually, GG works BEST at low frequencies! That is exactly what it is designed to do, and exactly what it has been shown to do in independent lab tests. It doesn't do much at high frequencies, but it absolutely shines in low frequencies.
