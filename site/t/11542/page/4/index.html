I started this project 2 years and one month ago by renting this facility.that was in november 2008. construction started in the middle of march 2009. main frame, electric and audio cabling and ventlation system was done a good month later. 1st layer gyprock started on 21st of april 2009. 2nd layer gyprock was done on 24th of may 2009. on 7th of july I thought I only had to build some doors and start with the ctrl room... well wood for the doors arrived on 25th of july. 1st door hung on 3rd of august 2009 (unpainted, just a test to see if I were on the right track) I finished the door and window era on 20th of september. still soooooo many things to do =( and I didn't know what...and how... exactly one year after the first wood delivery the second came: 12th of march 2010 I started with the ctrl room. 8th of june I was finished with the main work in the ctrl room but still needed to build 33 frames for the fabric...just for the ctrl room... 12th - 14th of august I built the diffusor for the ctrl rooms backwall. on 20th of september 2010 I nailed the last cloth frame on its place in the recording room. after that I built a poly, struggled with the lighting, built a seat bench for the ctrl room's backwall, built a rack for the ctrl room, set up a a small workshop in an extra room right in front of my studio and loads of small things I wasn't aware of...(a door bell for example) jesus! such a long long time. just a reminder for myself: You have done this all by yourself! two hands, two arms, two feets, one head and I was a couple of months on tour... 1st thing I want to mention is: Andre's (AVare) signature: Good studio building is 90% design and 10% construction it's true! I will never ever start such a project without knowing how it all should look in the end! just an example: if you're tight on budget (like I am) but you're willing to spend money on good quality audio cables don't think (unless you REALLY KNOW where your patchbays will be) that you might save some money by shorten these high price cables to it's minimum...this will end in PITA plus a potential degrade of quality... this happened to me. I thought that I don't want to build soffits for my speakers. and I wasn't sure in which direction I will layout the ctrl room. all my audio cables ended in a corner... unluckily in a corner where now a soffit is...I had to crawl under the soffit with a soldering iron to extend nearly all cables except for the good ones (the guy who sold me the mogami cables said to me NOT to buy 20 meters when I need 20 meters...so I bought 25. payed for 22...phew) this is dumb! now I know =) some things I want to change in case I'm doing this again: I will never ever build sth like this on the xth floor! never! underneath my feet will never ever be anything except concrete over ground/soil! I will never ever design a ctrl room without a room in room construction (except when I'm in the woods - but i guess even there I will do it. the silence in the recording room (room in room) is sooooo pleasant. when I close the doors and the silence starts to encircle me I immediately come down. this is so beautiful!) next time I will definitely consult pros when it's time to start planning the HVAC/Ventilation, electricity, audio wiring, eventually also alighting expert and a structural engineer. yes. nearly everything. It's not that I want them to do this for me (unless I have enough money) it's more like an assistance (though I'm not sure if this is common practice). I want sth I can RELY on. I think there must be a couple of other things but I'm a bit tired thinking about all this stuff... I'm done :D and yesterday I earned the first money in/with MY studio I'm happy as hell! hey mika, cool! would be nice to meet your "ears" =) I pm-ed you my email. oh :) thank you John! and thank you for this wonderful forum! as soon as I can I will contribute an adequate amount to this informative and great place!! unfortunately i'm sitting here empty-handed =( here are some screenshots. measured with REW version 5. Mic @ listeningposition. height: 122cm Mic: behringer ECM 8000 I used the left speaker only. speakers are Adam Anf 10. powered with a "modified" Alesis Ra 300 (I desoldered the crappy pots and soldered instead new and selected 1% metalfilm resistors in the gain stage of the amp. this fixes the output of the amp and also fixes the balance problem between the left and right output. i wasn't able to get the same out of the left output and the right one. the pots were incremental but not equal. I tested with a 1khz sinetone and measured the output voltage of each channel with a voltage meter. the readings were always different. for example: left was eg 900mv right was 700mv. the next step on the right pot was 1200mv...) I still haven't build barefoots DIY Filter to fix the bass boost due to soffit mounting... Oh, one thing I also tested was the TL between rec room and ctrl room. don't know if this might be a scientific way to do this... here's how: while taking measurements in the rec room (I was in the ctrl room with doors closed) I noticed that there are some frequencies way louder than others. so I took the mic into the ctrl and positioned it @ the listening position. REW played the sweep in the rec room and measured in the ctrl room. now I had a curve from the ctrl room with a source from the rec room. the last reading in the rec room was my reference curve. I simply took the reading from the ctrl room and subtracted it @ each STC frequency from the reference reading from the rec room. now it turned out that @ 125 HZ (TL:38,8db), 200 Hz (TL:36,7db) and 250 Hz (TL:32,4db) the TL was far under the expected. whereas @ all other frequencies the Tl was between 48,8db to 67,3db. there is a site where you can calculate the STC (-> http://www.diracdelta.co.uk/science/sou ... ource.html) the STC here is 47... should be 54 (http://www.sae.edu/reference_material/a ... 0Chart.htm) I haven't used 16mm plasterboard. mine are 12,5mm. I haven't used laminated glas. mine are the not recommended insulated float glass ones with two panes and a special gas inbetween. I found 'em on the yard here and thought that if they are really that worse I can change it whenever I want and have the money to do so... I noticed that the rack I built on the wall to the rec room seems to resonate at or boosts a special frequency (need to figure out that) anyway. I'm glad and still learning:) cheers, carsten here we go:
