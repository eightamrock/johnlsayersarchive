gullfo30 November 2010#11
if you have a nice slab overhead, then you probably have enough mass there. the big issue will be flanking and impacts. if you can build a new inner ceiling (perhaps with joists nested in between the existing ones) on isolated walls, you'll do much better than the RC. optionally, if you can enough space, using Kinetics, Mason, or other isolation mounts and hat/furring channel to suspend the new ceiling on, you'll get better performance than the RC. for the insulation in there, regular pink (or yellow) insulation will suffice. enough to dampen the space without packing and causing the new ceiling to become coupled...
