I've got a quick question about the "covering" between the windows in a separate stud wall partition. Almost every drawing/description I've seen uses something like Celotex covered with batting, then covered with cloth to lay between the panes of glass. My question is does this serve any purpose besides looks? Could I not just as easily glue down (carefully) black felt? I could glue it to each side (over the gap between walls) and not get any coupling between them. The reason I ask is I'm having trouble getting my Celotex to lay flat without wedging in tight between the stops that hold the glass in (which WILL couple sound from one side to the other. len
knightfly28 May 2006#2
No prob; window space needs to vent into the insulation packed between wall leaves for best isolation/damping (unless you wanna fill the space between the glasses with fiberglass too :wink: As long as your between-wall insulation can't extrude into the window area, that's all that matters IMO... Steve
