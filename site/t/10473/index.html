how to fill a gap above a double door set.

Started by MMS on 14 April 2008. 3 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 10473.

MMS14 April 2008#1
my contractor ended up leaving about a 2" gap above my double door assembly. I have a pretty solid construction (hat channell on RSIC clips, double 5/8" drywall with greenglue and rockwool insulation / two solid core doors with 5'8 insulated glass) and now I have a huge gap to fill, any suggestions for how to do that in an effective way? Jim.

gullfo15 April 2008#2
you could cut strips of MDF to fill it in on each side (don't contact them...), glue and screw, and mud over it. MDF is denser than the drywall and a bit easier to handle. and should be sufficient mass.
MMS19 April 2008#3
what do you mean by dont contact them? I'm assuming that I should just cut it so it fits nice and snug and then use acoustic caulk and mud to finish it up.
gullfo21 April 2008#4
sorry - don't contact them between walls -- that is: don't create a solid bridge between walls (if that is your case).
