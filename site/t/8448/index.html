Eric_Desart24 March 2007#5
Hi Kris, Gullfo I think you can buy fabric to cover absorbers anywhere. It's harder to buy wrong fabric than correct fabric. The first shop you encounter having nice fabric on suitable prices will do. Not taking fire properties into account, 90 % of what they sell is most likely acoustically usable. Once you can easily or normally (and the tolerance about what is normal is very large here) blow through it's very OK. It doesn't matter if this fabric in itself absorbs well or not. What you're looking for is an acoustic transparent layer to cover the wool. It hardly matters what it is. Hence the remaining questions are the fire properties. How important is it that it is self extinguishing, fireproof, whatever, or not. If that's a must that limits the choises. But one can wonder about this since one uses such non-fireproof fabrics for the curtains at home as well. There are spray products to protect flamable fabrics too.
John Sayers24 March 2007#6
another property to check is how much light gets through the cloth because if too much gets through you will be able to see the timber frame behind it. Painting the frame black will solve it but it's easier to avoid it in the first place by using a tighter weave cloth. cheers john
