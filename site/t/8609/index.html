Burn, first of all - calm down. He never said "with that kind of construction"- he said "with that construction" - go back and re-read it. what is meant is that it's hard to create good isolation with the construction you've chosen - not that the construction is bad. As a matter of fact - the construction looks pretty damned good to me - so I am pleasently surprised. One thing I would tell you though - you might not be as quiet outside as you think just because of the openness from below the deck to the outside air......... that's gonig to be your weak spot - even in comparison to your parents house. Now - that having been said - if you are truly not concerned about isolation - then it's time to answer your real question - which is "is there anything I should do before I sheetrock that will effect the sound of the room?" The answer is - "No." You already made all of the descisions that affect that - the room size being the largest...... your room ratio is actually almost perfectly on top of the Dolby recommendations - that's with averaging your vaulted ceiling..... Your ratio is ( I am assuming 2x6 studs since you said r-19 insulation) Room Ratio: 1 : 1.51 : 2.31 Dolby's optimum ratios for Film & Music Room" 1 : 1.493 : 2.313 So you're at a pretty good starting point. After drywall tape = paint and finished flooring - you're going to need around 690 sabins of treatments to get the room to meet ITU recommendations for control rooms RT60. If I were you - I would consider a decent amount of those being in the form of movable panels - that will help you with early reflections when you're playing in there - which will be different if you are doing a mix down. I hope that helped. Now - one last question - if you could find the time i would appreaciate you dropping me an email explaining what you felt the book was missing - I am curious about your statement "he's all over the place as far as actually showing or telling evrything that needs to be considered" Thanks, Rod
