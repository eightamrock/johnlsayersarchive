knightfly18 March 2005#2
If you want it all in the same room with you, one way would be the "jet cockpit" approach - If you can keep all your racks at desk height, they can be nearly anywhere in the room with not too much acoustic problem - then, the three A frames could go evenly behind you (as you face the speakers) - a good, QUIET, chair and you just spin around and play with whatever turns yer crank... I've done similar with a "writing room" approach, had mixer/nearfields in one location, guitar with floor processor at another, E-drums at another, small keystack at yet another - all within reach of the same chair location, just rotate. Some sort of CAD program can help lay this out. Of course, aesthetics and acoustics would still need to be addressed - but as I said, if you can keep most of the gear low profile that will help. The key stacks behind you shouldn't cause much problem, their angles should not return much to the mix position if the rest of the room is treated... Steve
