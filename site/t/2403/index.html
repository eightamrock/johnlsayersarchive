knightfly2 October 2004#4
Check here for possible sources http://www.roxul.com/sw34066.asp Also, look in the building supplies forum http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewforum.php?f=6 someone may have already posted sources near you. Next, check the yellow pages for commercial insulation contractors, and call every one of them to see if they will sell you either rockwool, mineral wool, slag wool, rigid fiberglass - these will all work, they're all better and cheaper than foam, you want roughly 3 pound per cubic foot density in any of the above products, 2" thick for a booth. Don't get discouraged doing this; you can't find the right stuff at Home Depot or Lowes, and not all commercial contractors are willing to deal with the public. A positive attitude and friendly demeanor will get you further than demanding, in any case. Hope that helps... Steve
