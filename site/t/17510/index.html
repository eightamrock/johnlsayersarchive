Hi, I know everyone's time is precious, so I'll try to be brief. I live in Syracuse, New York USA. I am in the planning stage of building John's vocal booth in my basement. I do voice overs and I'm looking to build a space with good acoustics. I don't care what it looks like. My budget is $1000.00. 1. The basement has cinder block walls and a flat, poured concrete floor. 2. Basement is 8ft underground. That means dirt behind the cinder blocks (so it's pretty sound proof for my needs). The basement is configured in a rectangular layout. 50feet by 20feet. 3. The ceilings are only 7feet 3 inches before you hit the joists. So, I'm going to have to shorten John's design a little to make it fit (His booth has an exterior height of 8 feet). I'm 5'11" tall. 4. I don't need a window. 5. I've read in the forums that a pre-hung, solid door will work just fine.
