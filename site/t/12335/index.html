Soundman202025 April 2009#2
But I don't know if there's a reason not to use standard patio doors
In ordinary patio doors the glass probably won't be thick enough to gie you good isolation, and it will possibly resonate at frequencies that cause trouble. Also, the seals probably wont be good enough for good isolation, although I guess you could probably fix that by adding additional seals yourself. Look at John's designs using sliding glass doors, and search the forum for the specs of the ones he uses, especially about hte glass thickness and the double seals. - Stuart -
