I was just curious if the following design would be good for sound attenuation, and at least a little bit of isolation: A movable room divider made of hollow core doors hinged together with 3 inches of Roxul Safe n Sound on each side of each door. If this arrangement went wall to wall and was modified to reach the ceiling, could it limit the amount of noise coming through from the other side of the divider? Or, could it be used as a makeshift vocal booth so that the mic inside picked up significantly less bleed? Thanks chiefs.
knightfly12 November 2006#2
The doors would physically be OK to hold up insulation if it's either glued or impaled; however, there's a good chance they would also introduce a resonance at somewhere between 350-400 hZ, not sure how much the insulation would damp that. Isolation-wise, next to nothing - probably just roll off high frequencies some (like if you have a computer in the room), that's about it... Steve BTW, a hollow core door has an STC of around 19-21, but that's assuming it's SEALED into a higher TL wall - like I said, not much.
