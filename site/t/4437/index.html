The kind of insulation injection you´ve mentioned in your post can be very expensive. I concidered that option in my basement studio to reduse the low-end leakage from the condo above... it turned out to be less expencive to tear the floor in the condo apart and add layers of sand + insulation between the two floors. Those types of injections might not be as effectfull as you may have hoped aswell. There is some kind of cheaper alternative (at least in Norway) thats mineral whool based but not rigid. It´s fragmented like cotton candy.
