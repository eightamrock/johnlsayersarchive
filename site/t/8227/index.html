barefoot25 February 2007#3
Even with a listening position as close as 4ft, that only translates to a 0.3 degrees difference in listening axis between the two monitors. So, it should make almost no difference. Not to mention our ears have far less acuity to vertical localization than they do horizontal. Thomas
