The difference between the two, green glue and construction glues, is that green glue does not "dry"(and has some chemicals in it that aid in the cld) so better creates a constrained layer damping between materials. Construction glue forms a "hard" connection between the two materials so no damping is present but more a coupling of the two materials. For what it's worth.

Speedskater wrote:
An exterior construction adhesive that delivers excellent weathering and aging, permanent flexibility, and the ultimate shear strength.[/i]
And it is exactly that fantastic shear strength that makes it unsuitable for the purpose of performing like green glue. Green glue is designed to fail under shear conditions - in fact it is designed to shear under the pressures exerted by simply the sound itself - and this material withstands shear under seismic events. GREEN GLUE IS NOT A GLUE - IT IS NOT A CONSTRUCTION ADHESIVE - IT WOULD NEVER PASS THE TESTS REQUIRED TO QUALIFY AS A CONSTRUCTION ADHESIVE. IT IS PHYSICALLY IMPOSSIBLE FOR CONSTRUCTION ADHESIVES TO PERFORM THE SAME AS GREEN GLUE. Thus - with all due respect to Dr Geddes - regardless of the fact that he has used it over a decade - if he (in fact) compares it favorable to green glue - he is very mistaken. Sincerely, Rod
