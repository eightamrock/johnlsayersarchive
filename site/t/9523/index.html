Same people here as there boss. I think that STC points are overrated in the quest for isolation. When you consider the flankingthat is inherent in multi-dwelling structures many of the best modifications Glenn has already explained to you that will improve your situation. If you spend the few minutes it may take to get the idea of mass and or density you will better appreciatte the installation of double sheetrock into the stud spaces. You may also come to understand the priority of decoupling via RC and other methods. While it is not clear that you are on a second floor, it may be so. And if it is so, you have more issues with isolation then you may be ready to digest. Even still, Glenn is of a high caliber in this area and his suggestions should be taken with some authority.
