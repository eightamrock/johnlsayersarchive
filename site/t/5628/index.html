knightfly11 February 2006#2
No; if you want to have an idea of what you'll end up with, you need to either build a slat resonator OR a bass trap. The formula for helmholtz takes into account the flow resistivity of the material (typically 3 PCF rigid fiberglass insulation), the cloth, the depth inside, width and spacing of slats, thickness of slats, etc - changing the inner fill will change both frequency (slightly) and absorption, as well as the "Q" of the trap. Mixing the two will mainly make sure you have NO IDEA what you did... Steve
