Hi folks, I've come up with a new design for a 2D quadratic residue diffusor that could be sold as a DIY kit, requiring only glue to assemble. I posting here to get a sense of how much interest there might be for something like this. The commercially available stuff is rather pricey for a lot of people's budget constraints, typically at least $30/ft assembled. I've seen a 1D kit on the market for about $15/ft. My idea is customizable, light weight, and look very cool and different from anything you've likely seen. It's mathematically correct 2D diffusion. The prototype sounds phenomenal! At a tentative price of $10/ft plus about 20min./ft to assemble, would this be attractive? Comments appreciated.
