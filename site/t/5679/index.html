Hey forum folks, I just wanted to say to all of you, Thank you. From the question askers to the answer guys (and gal :wink:), all of you helped in one way or another whether you realized it or not. Each question asked brought on new perspectives and each answer helped solve those inevitable mysteries. Without you all, this would have been just a dream without closure. While my studio is far from perfect compared to some of the projects on here, it is perfect for me, for now. And without this forum and the inspiration from an article about Tony Shepperd in Electronic Musician, I would still be in my basement hacking away. I now have a studio that I am proud to show off and because of that I am sure more business than I can handle is on the way. And as a final note, an extra special thank you goes out to Sharward! Keith, you rock bro! Thank you SO much for the great post on my thread and the awesome phone msg you left for me! The doors are ALWAYS open for you man! If you ever make it out here, I would be honored to have you stop by my humble home. Your help and encouragement (and laughs) here on the forums and now on the phone have been unsurpassed. I too wish that you "just lived across the street" as I feel we have so much in common we must have been friends in another life. Thanks bro, I look forward to continuing our friendship and acquaintance in the forums and beyond. John, you have another satisfied customer! Viva la JLS!! Thanks to all of you, Dragon Note Recording Studios is a reality!
