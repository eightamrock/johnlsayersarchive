xSpace12 March 2011#31
As a matter of physics, not only is your multiple vapor barrier in the wrong spot, it is too many as well. One barrier does one thing. If you have 2 barriers, they still do the same one thing, but now they are doing it together in a reverse fashion. Basically, the top barrier is attempting to slow the path of "warm to the cold side"(which is the direction that vapor moves) and the bottom one is attempting the same thing. But air/vapor does not exist that does not have some level of moisture. So your double barrier is working like this. The top barrier is going to trap moisture on the top side. Then the bottom barrier is going to trap moisture on the bottom side. And both will trap moisture as a unit, from the bottom side of the top barrier to the top side of the bottom barrier, respectively. Just for the record, it does not matter who told you do it like this or how much you are paying them to tell you this or where in the world you live, it does not change the form or function of a potential multiple vapor barrier as part of a monolithic slab.
