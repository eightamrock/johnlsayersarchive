gullfo14 January 2007#22
in general, 6 degrees on each side is about as low as you can go to eliminate most of the flutter echos and provide some direction to move the reflections from the console position. in addition, each angled wall can be build to house bass traps so the space is fully utilized. for soffiting, generally 30 degrees is one norm for angles. 45 also works in certain situations. the hard part is keeping track of the difference in angles and spaces that the framing and wall coverings make. so designing the base floor plan is fairly straight forward but thinking about how the soffit box for the speaker (for example) fits is hard because you have to account for that construct when laying out the floor plan... so if you account for wall thickness, etc... you should be fine with the drawings you have.
