jbassino7 September 2009#4
Don't get too itchy about it... All you have to do is to avoid a possible gap between the frames, and the pass of air. Caulking should do the job since it has a good mass and will seal every possible air gap between the woods Yes, you could first put a nice load of caulk on the side thats going to receive the other frame, then after nailing them, its gonna have a good fit. Then caulk the front gap and you should be fine
