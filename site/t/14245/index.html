As an exercise I have been reducing most any room dimensions I can get or create into the corresponding musical note(s). Having that information has been beneficial to me(and maybe only me) in my search for accuracy. http://www.phy.mtu.edu/~suits/notefreqs.html But you can take the note that corresponds with the natural frequency of a wall, multiply that by 41 and you have a number the relates directly to the one octave color spectrum we as humans can actually see with the bare eye. http://www.lunarplanner.com/Harmonics/p ... onics.html My walls dimensions, the length and width, relate to the colors red and orange/yellow respectively. I painted the room burnt orange about 2 ish years ago! Odd coincidence I find this relation so long after the room had already been painted. It's a little long hair I suppose... Brien
