Carl - The cleanest system is Variable transformers http://variac.com/staco_Variable_Transformer_Map.htm You would need one per room with individual switches after it for each area in each room. i.e. use one as an overall dimmer and switch individual lights for effect. cheers john

I have been in contact a few times with Lutron this past week about dimmers for my studio.
