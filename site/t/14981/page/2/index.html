Funkster17 January 2011#38
Reinforced 6" raft is very commonly used in the UK as a base for erecting garages made out of sectional concrete or single-thickness blockwork walls. However when used in this context the regs state that you need at least 150mm of slab extending beyond the outside of the walls, which I don't have. However the inside-out stud walls, even though they're carrying another ceiling, still turn out to be a sensible loading for this design - presumably concrete garages are somewhat heavier. I would expect that if I wanted two or more layers of plasterboard on both sides of the wall, a thicker slab with more reinforcement would be required. All the best, -- Olly
