Hello Greeny, As you live in the United Kingdom you might want to try Dunlop Foams or indeed Tontine, they come under the Company "United-Bonded" and apart from Polyester such as Acoustisorb (Like a Semi Rigid 703 Fibre Glass though better specs) or AA-29/AA30 Density-30Kg/m3 Convoluted Foam that is Fire retardent and they will sell through a Pro Forma Invoice direct; so be clever and buy enough that you need then sell the rest you don't want on Eslay! Or double the width of the foam you put up!
