Hi everyone! Thanks a lot in advance for any ideas you might share with me. I really really appreciate it! :D I just bought and industrial unit of 115 m2 (377 sq feet). It is 15 x 7,35 x 6 meters (49 x 24 x 19,5 feet). Here's some pics. I'm planning to build a (comercial) recording studio with: - Control Room - Big Live Room - Booth (for amps and maybe singing) - Bathroom - Relaxing Area I don't think I'll need a kitchen since there are several restaurants around the place. :wink: My main concern is having a nice woody big room in which to record acoustic stuff and be able to have a nice natural sound, along with a control room that's confortable enough to fit a 6-member band and me without too much clashing!! :D I do mostly pop/rock, but also classical/ancient and also some comercials/films (5.1 will be implemented) The level of noise in the Live Room will be the one produced by a whole band playing together. The Units around are empty right now since the whole place is brand new, but I have to be ready for the worst (industrial machines and annoying stuff like that). I made some SketchUp plans with my ideas. Basically what I wanna do is add a Relaxing Area on top of the Control Room and make the Live Room really tall. Does that make any sense to you guys? Any further ideas? If you need any more details, just ask! Thank you all for keeping this site alive!! :D Jordi Gil.
