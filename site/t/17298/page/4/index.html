"And my query is that the GG caulk/ they call it GG sealant, is shrinking where the sikkens and CSR did not" I guess I would have stayed with what was working and would continue to get in contact with the GG reps, if you suspect that you are having a material failure. I would think this is not common, the failure. Now there can be circumstances (freeze/thaw/extraordinary heat) that can reduce almost any type of caulk to junk. I am not saying this is your situation only that in transit(the delivery) of these materials the climates especially at this time of year can fluctuate wildly and could have affected your material, or something within the environment. You have to look into these things forensically to make a determination. The information will help to better your case in divining to whom blame should be placed, IF indeed there is blame to be placed at all. Keep us in touch with this concern if you will.
