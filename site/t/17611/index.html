gullfo18 June 2012#5
the walls are one thing, the ceilings and floors are also in need of isolating assemblies as well. sound is 3D and does not care about the budget so when having a limited budget the details become even more important as does expectation setting. assuming the floors are concrete on earth, then room within a room construction (and if the ceilings are supported by the walls, the walls are load bearing) should give reasonably good results assuming you also take care to beef up the exterior and second leaf interior walls. ensure you plan out the HVAC and the wiring as well since air exchange is critical and too many penetrations (use surface mount electrical where possible) will reduce your isolation.
