dankelly30 April 2010#3
Hi. This space is small, so you would definitely be better keeping it as one room. You can use gobo's or a reflexion filter to record vox. You need to setup facing the wall opposite the door with the speakers pointing down the long face of the room. I suppose the thing to bear in mind is not to expect miracles. But with enough acoustic treatment (bass traps), you could probably turn this pinto a usable space. Hope this helps
