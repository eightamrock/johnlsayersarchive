The large Air Conditioning units on the roof HUMmMM
It sounds like you have a problem with structure-borne sound, where the vibration is in the building structure itself. The best way of dealing with that is to stop it at the source: Instal suitable isolation mounts on the HVAC units, and the problem will go away. - Stuart -
