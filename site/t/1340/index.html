dymaxian16 February 2004#2
How heavy is your drop ceiling? How's it built- suspended from above by wires, or stood up on inside walls? The floor deck above will make a good sound stop if it's not the only one. If you have a solid, thick, air-tight ceiling above you then you're probably doing all you can there. And the 3 foot gap, with insulation, is plenty. Are the walls structurally separated from the ceiling/floor system? Could be you're getting vibes running thru the structure. Sorry to answer your questions with more questions... but the more info we have the more we can help ;) Kase www.minemusic.net
