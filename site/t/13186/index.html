Ro19 June 2009#4
Symmetry is not the word. you WANT it different concerning acoustic absorbers. Just put an aborbing panel opposite of the window on the wall. (you know: soft vs reflection, just like hardfloor, softceiling) But in RFZ we take it a bit further; If the window is a first reflection point, then your design is flawed to start with. to fix that you might wanna put a portable absorber in front of the window.

Ro20 June 2009#9
Put a low-end filter on your basstraps if possible, that'll keep the room from sounding too "dead". the other absorbers can be hung on the walls facing the bare wall on the opposite (soft vs hard). Same goes for the ceiling, esp. at RFZ position. A cloud above your mix station/chair would do. If you measure you room, before and after treatment, you'll know what to look for next. If the room's too dead (according to your ears), you can always add a filter in front of any absorbing panel afterward. Keep on posting (don't forget the pix) Ro
