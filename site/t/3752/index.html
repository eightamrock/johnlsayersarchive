The area is basically a hall/wall of windows with a really amazing view. I don't want to eat it all up. We were going to put some desks there, and I thought some vinyl shelving might be good as well as I have a lot of vinyl. Since I posted here the plan I think the max i can really bring it out is to like 5 feet i suppose. The guy drew the window in for ambience but i don't really think that's necessarily the way to go. Here's my background - I produce, record and mix urban and electronic music - a lot of hip hop and R&B. I have some decent credits including a production on 50 Cent's new 'Massacre' album and a top 40 airplay record for Nina Sky. Currently I live in a basement loft with very little natural light, below a woodshop that makes noise all day, and I share a wall with some neighbors that hate me (even though this is a commercial building.) The space I am in used to be one big, dirty space with 2 entrances and the landlord did a shitty job isolating them. The neighbors have now begun counteracting my music by blasting their own music, because banging on the wall and threatening to call the cops hasn't worked. My lease in my space is up July 1st and theres a lot of pressure for me to leave and now that I'm engaged its time to move out of the basement. My studio right now is set up open to the entire loft. The acoustics in the low end are pretty inaccurate but I am used to them. I record vocals in a 4 by 4 whisper room. I would like to graduate to something a little bigger and better than a whisper room, but my "live room" really just needs to be a vocal booth. The ceilings in the new spot are 14 or 15 feet total - concrete deck above and below. The contractor wanted to do the new studio ceilings at 8 ft 4" to allow me to use above the studio as storage or even like a 'gimp lounge' of sorts. I looked for a bigger place but this is really what I could afford right now without moving out of the area. So basically, what do you suggest? I monkeyed with the drawing a little last night and had some dimension ideas of maybe 13.5 by 11.5 (pulling out the live room 1.5 feet south and 1.5 feet west) or 14 by 12 or something like that. Again this really has to be "live/work" to a certain extent so I can't cut into the living space more than I am or my fiancee might decide to stay in Brasil permanently :). Also some advice on the inside ceiling height would be good - if we can pull off the 'gimp lounge' above, great - if not of course the studio is most important! Thanks for such a wonderful site. DD
