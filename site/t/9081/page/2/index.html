In the meantime on with the pics.... "Rondo 581" acoustic furring channel, I discovered through my next door neighbour and so I decided to get some of this wonderful but prohibitively exspensive product @ $22.00 AUD per 3.6 metres. John Sayers4 August 2009#45
Mike - there are two types of cloud - one allows the sound through so it reflects off the ceiling and goes back through the absorption again, (good low end absorption) or the cloud has a solid upper surface and when angled breaks up floor to ceiling standing waves. Lights can be installed in either, a cloud is your friend :)
