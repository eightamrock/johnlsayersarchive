giles11720 August 2009#2
Std construction is 16" OC for stability. you can go 24, but will have more "flex" in your drywall layer. Not certain you really want that.
lilith_envy20 August 2009#3
1200mm would not be legal! 600mm is great. 450 mm is OK. YOu would normally lay the dry wall horizontally anyway. Which is where the noggens come into play. the noggens would be 1200 off the ground and if your wall height is great than 2400, you add another noggen at 2400.
