out. the walls are cenderblock with cement plaster, and the floor is solid cement as is the ceiling. naturally, i will want to build out the inner rooms(control room, vocal room, instrument room, recording studio will be to conduct voice overs for my films, as well as composing my own music and have musicians come in so that i may record each instrument and put togehter the mix. i do not need projector as well as my 8mm, 16mm, and 35mm film projectors so that while in the control room, i cna view footage and mix sound ... this would be for footage i need to view large, instead of small
