We would be happy to help, but need a bit more info in order to do so, please. If you could answer the following point for point, we'll be off and running. - HOW LOUD are you? - How picky/loud are your neighbors? - How close are they? - How much room do you have to work with? Remember that acoustics is THREE DIMENSIONAL, not two - when you give dimensions, we need Length, Width, Height - Include drawings of what you're describing if at all possible. They do not need to be professional or perfectly to scale, but they do need to account for the necessary details. - Please edit your pics to no more than 700 pixels wide. - Please post ALL pix directly on this site instead of linking to them. Posting the pix HERE would ensure that others get the help YOU got; thanks... - Group your actual questions last, and put each question in its own paragraph.
