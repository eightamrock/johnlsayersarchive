rod gervais6 July 2006#2
I am very sorry to tell you this - but even if you do all of the work yourself - I do not see any way you can do anything close to what you want for 500 euros - that's roughly 640 US dollars - - you would be lucky to be able to frame a room within a room and insulate it for that amount of money - not to mention multiple layers of drywall - sound doors - sound windows - special hardware - electrical wiring - lighting - hundreds of tubes of caulk - and then you have to do the room treatments - which (even if you build them yourself) could easily cost you 500 euro. A realistic budget for a small room (if you can do it all yourself) would probably be more in line with 7or 8 thousand euros if you really need isolation. You would be better off just trying to treat the basement - and forget about isolation until you can really afford to do the job right. Sincerely, Rod
