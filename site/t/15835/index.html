I know this topic is :horse: . But I am coming from a price standpoint. I need to cut cost anywhere I can and the Quietrock seems like the best place to start. So originally I was planning on going with two sheets of 1/2" QuietRock on each surface of a dual wall to cover a total of 188.6 sq ft. At about 12 sheets I would need, that would be a total of about $451 plus tax. And QuietRock is claiming an STC of around 71. Plus add a 5/8" standard and two Green Glue tubes for the 32 sq ft ceiling and that's roughly another $53. So around $500 plus tax for the Quietrock setup. Do you guys also recommend Green Glue between QuietRock? I would think no, according to QuietRock installation documentation! But then I just did a forum search last night an saw 98% of comments about the stuff was that it's a waste of money and the same thing could be achieved with standard Gypsum and Green Glue which I read claims to be better on lows. So I priced that up and it's a decent amount less. You figure to cover a total of 220.6 sq ft (not including windows and doors) with double sheetrock, that's $147.84 for 12 sheets of 5/8" sheet rock. 220.6 sq ft of Green Glue coverage I would need roughly 12 tubes. So add on the $175 for the case and that's about $323 plus tax and any shipping if I can't find Green Glue locally for the Standard Gypsum setup. Now, you guys are saying the standard should be within a few dB of the QuietRock and should be even better with the Green Glue? What's the worst case scenario if I ditched the Green Glue altogether and just did the double standard 5/8"? What's the worst case if I just use 1 Green Glue tube per 4 x 8? The specs for the booth are as follows in case my math is off (since I calculated all of this based on square footage): Interior dimensions = 4' x 8' Glass = 5 sq ft each Doors = 17.8 sq ft each Doors + Glass = 22.8 sq ft each wall Front outer wall 63 sq ft - 22.8 sq ft = 41.8 sq ft Front inner wall 56 sq ft - 22.8 sq ft = 34.8 sq ft Rear inner wall = 56 sq ft Side inner walls = 28 sq ft x2 = 56 sq ft Ceiling = 32 sq ft Total QuietRock needed = 188.6 sq ft x2 Plus 32 sq ft standard Gypsum for ceiling and 2 Green glue tubes. Total standard drywall = 220.6 x2 Total GreenGlue needed = 12 tubes

Ted White12 April 2011#4
Keep in mind that the overall mass is going to factor heavily (no pun) in the low frequency isolation. The heaviest solution will have an advantage. That's why double standard 5/8" is such a great deal. Also keep in mind that you'll have some waste factor. Industry average is 11%. Look at the cost of the scrap piling up in the dumpster.
