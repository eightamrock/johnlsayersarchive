I agree with Ro. But.... The way you are setting up your mass is going to leave you open to sound because the exterior or neighbor side is pretty thin. How easy would it be, for you, to cut pieces of drywall to fit in between the studs on that wall? You would be able to mass up the exterior side to the same amount as the interior(studio) side plus distribute the load. Just something to look into.
