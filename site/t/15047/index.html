gullfo29 October 2010#2
there are a number of recycled cotton products, hemp fiber, etc which don't have the same irritants as mineral wool or fiberglass, but they have different absorption characteristics so some research (such as Bob Golds site) will be necessary. covering the mineral wool in resin probably is not a good idea (most resin products have VOCs). another option is "micro mesh" which is used to wrap the mineral wool before covering it with cloth.

gullfo31 October 2010#20
so now between here and the other forums you posted to, you should have several answers WRT protecting yourself: 1) micromesh - polyester mesh, or thin plastic backing to trap the fibers 2) purchase natural products like cotton, hemp, ECOSE, etc 3) spray or brush a cellulose or latex product on the surface (accepting the possible loss of performance) 4) wear a mask and protective gear when handling insulation products avoid: 1) epoxy (due to flow resistivity impact) 2) compounds with VOC emssions 3) breathing in dust and other airborne contaminants
