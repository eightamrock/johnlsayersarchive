Well, yes it would on a weighted scale - (although 80dB of LF isolation is next to impossible) given enough mass and utilizing an inner structure that was completely isolated from the exterior. But i doubt that you need a full 80dB of isolation. You will give credit to the distance - measure at 3' from the building face and then every doubling (of distance) decreases amplitude by 6dB. So for 20m (65.62') you get roughly about 26dB of reduction - and then their house has some value as well - say another 28 or 30......... so let's call it 54 db of reduction for distance and their structure. So, if your weighted average inside was 110dB - then you would really only need to isolate about 56dB.
Thinking about room-in-room designs. It is possible to remove the upper floor to get a higher room.
