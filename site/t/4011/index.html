infiniteposse10 July 2005#1
Hi all- I posted several months back as I started shopping for homes in the Portland, OR area and have no purchased and moved into a new place. Accordingly, I've had some time to think over what I'd like to do with my basement to make it a good place to write, record and mix records. I'm currently setting up my studio in the unfinished space at the moment just so that I've got someplace to work until I start the proper build-out in early '06. This exercise has shown me I don't have a ton of space to work with really... I've also decided that I would like to space to add to the overall squarefootage of the house so that the investment and work put into the studio doesn't turn the home into a place with a highly specialized and unsellable basement. I'd like to find a happy medium between well tuned/functional room and livable space. While I know this will entail compromises of some sort, I'm hopeful that there's a way this can be accomplished. From reading the site over the last few months I've gathered that the recommended approach to almost ever basement studio build out is to create 2 sonically isolated spaces, one for mixing and one for tracking and also perhaps an iso-booth if space permits. My goal for my space would be to have one larger space that is used for both mixing and tracking with an additional medium/large sized iso-booth that could be used for better isolation. Regarding my space, the basement is submerged about 5-6 feet below the ground and has a total of 3 windows in the open space that currently do open outside. From what I've read on the site, I'll need to do a mass-air-mass design to better isolate the space from the outside world. Luckily, I'm a corner lot, so I don't have any neighbors within 15-20 feet of the basement. The ceilings are between 7'-7'2", so a floating floor is not really practical. I'd hoped to somehow get some wood (or some other reflective surface)onto the tracking side of the rooms floor somehow though, so perhaps you guys could toss out some thoughts. I've created a detailed Smartdraw sketch of the room as it stands right now. The only constrution left to do in the room besides the studio is to create a utility space to box in the water heater, the gas heater and a small sink. I very much appreciate any feedback or suggestion you could make on the space and my ideas. Many thanks- Lee Shots of basement before anything had been done to it: http://www.pbase.com/infiniteposse/basement Current shots of basement: http://www.pbase.com/infiniteposse/july05update
