giles1171 August 2004#2
Looks like you have done quite a nice workaround, as your HVAC hangs out in the lounge area. Yes the green wall would act as your outer wall in the double leaf construction. As far as the duct work in the rafters, when you close up your ceiling they will be handled. I'd wrap it in some kind of insulation to further dampen/isolate noise coming from the ductwork. So far as the jack post in the CR you can purchase a jack post from your local Home Depot and place it "inside" one of your walls to replace this jack post. Do a forum search for more info. Slat Resonators for the walls of your CR are a wonderful idea. They will really help clean up the midrange in the room. The absorption on the wall in front of the console is a novel idea. Just further kills any reflections off of your front wall. :) As your ceiling height is so low, absorption for the ceiling is the correct way to go. And if you desire to angle it go forth and conquer. I did as my Flr to ceiling ht was 8.5' By angling the "drop" ceiling that was stuffed with Rockwool, it added additional bass trapping. Basically I framed a false ceiling that angled in the front and angled in the rear and covered it in cloth. You have this grasped very well. :) You could if you wanted, make ISO 1 a dry (dead) room with full on absorption if you desired, keeping your live room live. Because of the angles, you will not have an issue with standing waves clouding what your mics hear. Especially in the low end. I would deaden the celing, the east and south walls. Resonator on the north wall. that should deaden the room pretty well and not make it too dead. As far as your live room depending on how lively you want it, I'd put resonators on your west, east and south walls and deaden your north wall and of course deaden your ceiling. Will make a nice lively room that is not out of control live. :) Also verify that your north wall angles at a 12 degree angle. I cant really tell if it is. Otherwise you are looking very good and have a very good grasp of what to do. :)
