kendale19 February 2007#4
Aloha, Nice job on the skp updates. Why not put the control room into what you've just drawn? Here's another idea to give you some better room ratios. In Rod Gervais’ book “Build it Like the Pros,” he points out both L.W.Sepmeyer’s & M.M. Louden’s formulas most widely used in professional studios worldwide. L.W. Sepmeyer Height Width Length 1.00 1.14 1.39 9’ x 10’3” x 12’5” M.M. Louden Height Width Length 1.00 1.40 1.90 9’ x 12’6” x 17’1” In your case, with your ceiling height L.W. Sepmeyer's ratios are very close to the space you have to work with. Aloha 8)
