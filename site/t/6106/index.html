knightfly11 January 2007#3
Since you're talking about angled slots, this will lessen the narrow band response typical of a RESONATOR type trap - that's a GOOD thing, because I haven't seen any sign that you've actually CALCULATED what the reverb time of this room will be in each frequency band - if you actually want to know what the room will sound like, this needs to be done BEFORE you build. The very top link in this forum has a new version of the reverb calculator - go there and calculate your estimated RT at each frequency the calculator offers, chart them, and look for CONSISTENT 'verb times somewhere around 0.3 seconds. Frequencies with longer times need more absorbent that works in that range. http://www.bobgolds.com/AbsorptionCoefficients.htm will give you the absorption figures for nearly every choice you might have in materials. If you calculate your room and still want to do slats as in your sketch, I'd just make sure that each cavity is completely filled with insulation - this will widen the resonance curve and lessen any peaky response that the slats might otherwise cause - but I would still use the AVERAGE depth of each resonator to find the approximate center frequency, and try not to use as much absorbent that works only in that range (thickness can be your friend here) HTH...
