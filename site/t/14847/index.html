gullfo23 September 2010#2
here's a set of rough guidelines i tend to start out with:
  • depending on the monitors, 5ft - 7ft or maximum of 70% of the room width. large monitors could go wider if the room has the space for it.
  • 30 degrees on all sides of the listening triangle with A-B-C dimensions approximately the same.
  • listening position 38% from the front of the isolation wall. secondary position (such as a producer or client desk) at 62%.
  • vertically, 36-42% of room height unless i'm putting them over a window.
these are my general starting points but usually shift depending on analysis of the room model, layout options, equipment, workflows etc. if you like a lot of complex math: studio tips thread per Eric Desart http://forum.studiotips.com/viewtopic.php?f=1&t=3378 ideas on this from Siegfried Linkwitz http://www.linkwitzlab.com/rooms.htm - in the middle section are some room positioning ideas related to his speaker products.
