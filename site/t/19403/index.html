From what I understand automatic door closers and a handle where I don't have to drill into the door is ideal but, what about locking the door. What is the best way for me to lock the door? folly10 May 2014#2
I have just been researching sort of the same thing. I am about to settle on the Lockey M210, it's a mechanical keyless deadbolt, it looks like it requires drilling small holes through the door but not the big 2 1/8 standard hole which helps. And it is available with an extension kit for doors up to 5" thick, with which it's about 120 bucks.
