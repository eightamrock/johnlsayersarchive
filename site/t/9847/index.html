AVare6 December 2007#4
A great space and congratulations on doing design before construction. Good studio building is 90% design and 10% construction. What is your budget? Looking at your space and ideas presented so far, an idea that ran through my head is to have wall sections that fold, to make the drum space when needed for isolation. When not needed, the sections can be folded back and your live space will be much larger. This would be expensive because precision would be needed in the moveable sections to achieve isolation. An amp closet would let you have isolation while letting the musician still play with the sound they know. Keep the questions coming! Andre
