Hola "juanml", y bienvenido al foro! :) También tenemos una sección del foro en español, si prefieres. O puedes continuar en inglés también, considerando que lo dominas perfectamente! Anyway, what part of BsAs is this new district in? Just curious... I would say the choice of properties depends on what your goals are for recoding. If you expect to be recording large groups, well-know groups, or want versatility in being able to record genres that need a lot of "air" to sound good, then I'd go for the larger live room with very high ceilings. But if most of your recording is just small groups or individual musicians, or mostly electronic, then you'd probably be fine with a smaller live room. I would also suggest that you look for places that have a concrete slab on the ground that you can use as the floor for your studio: Building on raised wooden floors, or even worse, on the second or higher floors of a building, is a problem. Building on concrete at ground level makes things a lot easier. I'd also look for a neighborhood where there are no nearby sources of low frequency or loud noises, such as no railways, rivers, airport, roads, or heavy industry close by. And when I say "airport" that includes being under the approach or departure paths of Aeroparque or Ezeiza, as well as helipads. Also other occasionally noisy places, like hospitals, fire stations, police stations etc. (sirens). Good transportation access, in a safe area, with good security, and reliable, clean electrical power and telecommunications lines would also be high on my list of priorities, knowing Bs.As. as I do! :) To me, those would be just as important as the potential sizes of the rooms. - Stuart -
