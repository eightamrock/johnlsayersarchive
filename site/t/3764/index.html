MS-Scope23 May 2005#2
Hi Schroeder, I think your wall thickness of 13,4 cm is not much to isolate the sound coming from the back of your speakers. Are you building a 2 framed wall whith one frame seperated from the other or is it only one frame of studs whith 2 layers of gypsum on every side? I would go whith two seperated frames and air gap between of them this will give you the best stc . Better is to use 3 layers in the studio and 2 layers in the booth. The other option is to use some small nearfield speakers whith less bass for recordings. I myself have built an 2 framed wall whith 2 layers gypsum on every side in my studio. The wall is 22 cm thick. Whith my little auratones I hear nothing coming trough the wall and whith the adam s2a I hear the bass a little bit if I have the level at 95 - 100 db. But I only record whith the auratones so that is no problem for me. An don´t forget I have my monitors both freestanding not wall mounted, but who knows maybe I will do this in the future. :D
