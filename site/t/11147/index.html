Soundbreak looks like a version of QuietRock. They work but are expensive. For greater performance at less cost use 2 layers of regular gyprock with Green Glue in between the layers. Next step up would be to use PAC RSIC clips or Kinetics Noise Iso Max clips in addition. This would would give significantly performance than the Soundbreak. Very rough costs ignoring labor is 4'x8' gyprock $8.00 Green Glue $35 per 4'x8' section RSIC or Iso Max clips $75 per 4'x8' section Andre

And...we were aiming at probally getting the R19 insulation and with the Isomax Channels...is there a certain way to set them up on the studs etc. such as a spacing pattern?
Green Glue is also known for enhancing performance in low end frequencies in which most of life's "real world" noise problems occur. I would recommend using 2 layers of 5/8" gyprock. A comment that may come up is that you would want to use 2 different thicknesses such as 1/2" & 5/8" to have 2 different resonances. This would be the case if not using GG. Since you are using GG tests have shown that you get better performance with the extra mass of 2-5/8" layers. GG will help flatten out the coincidence dip and depending on construction, drives down or provides better TL at the low end resonance point. I found this link to help you with your Isomax Clips: http://www.kineticsnoise.com/arch/isomax/index.aspx I hope this helped.
