mrcliff30 November 2009#3
Hey Ro, Thanks for your reply and the link - I have now added some details to my profile! :oops: Due to space limitations my over-all build for this project is approx 6.4m x 3.7 internally before any stud work.. I have divided the space into a CR and LR. The CR narrows toward the front of the room which means the LR is not symmetrical, which I read is a good starting point!? My budget for stud work, doors, floor and treatment is around £5,000 My plan is to build the inner room from 4 x 2's leaving a 1 inch air gap between the stud and existing wall (Mass - Air - Mass). The stud will be filled with 100mm RW3, covered with a sheet of 18mm MDF, caulked and then plasterboard / skimmed. The property is far enough away from any neighbors that sound leakage is not a problem in that direction. My main concern is isolation between CR and LR and LR and the main house which is off the LR door opposite the piano. I will be building a "superdoor" much like Rod's - more later... I write songs and music for TV and will only ever be recording vox / strings and brass in the LR (not drums or bass) so I have not floated the floor as everything I record is effectively de-coupled from the ground by the musician. Questions: I guess at this point I am looking for some confirmation that the basic studio shape / concept is moving in the right direction and I am not going to make a costly design error before I even start?! I realise a room of this size is always going to need "fixing" when it's finished but I am keen to make the best choices I can at the design phase to limit the damage! The exact dimensions I will tweak to comply as closely a possible with one of the recognised room ratios - most likely: 1:1.50:2.10, although as my room is not square I am not 100% sure how this works? I was considering sitting the stud work on a product such as this TICO product. It's a machinery mounting product which would sit under the stud walls to reduce vibration. Any thoughts? I will be putting ducting into the roof space that will allow flow of fresh air through the room - IN at the bottom of one of the bass traps and OUT at the opposite top corner of the room. This, for the time being will run into an adjoining garage until I work out the fan / silencer details.... Airflow pic attached. All help very welcome! Thanks for your input folks... I look forward to being enlightened.
