knightfly6 December 2004#3
The first page of this link should help you understand what works and what doesn't better - http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=598 basically, you're already living in a 2-leaf world from the sound of it so building yet another wall in the form of a floated room may actually WORSEN your isolation. Also, you may have flanking problems if the space wasn't built in the last few years - if so, then it might be necessary to live with the third leaf that an inner room would create, making that leaf heavier to help compensate for the loss in low frequency isolation. Be careful though - this will take some serious examination of the existing building, to make sure structure can support the extra weight... Steve
