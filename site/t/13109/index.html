I'd add a piece of (finished) wood on the wall using special GB screws (dunno what you call'm in the US). Add the metal stageblock onto that wood. Snakes goin' through the wall means cutting and therefore compromising it. Since the wall is already up, adding a tube system in the wall is not possible anymore. Therefore I'd just put the snake through, secure it and seal it of with sealant.
