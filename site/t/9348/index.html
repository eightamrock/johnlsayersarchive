Aloha studio masters, I appreciate this forum and have learned much from this site during the last year. This is my first post as I have completed the shell of a structure that will soon be my personal recording space. I hope I don't step on any toes if I ask a very specific question without including all the details of my space and future plans. I live in an isolated area (gravel road) and rain on my metal roof will be the main concern as far as isolation goes (75 inches/year). My dilema is that I want T&G cedar or pine on the interior ceiling but I also need the isolation of 5/8" drywall with hat channel. I can't conceive of an effective means of acheiving isolation without a drop ceiling (which is not possible due to windows that are close to the finish ceiling). My gut tells me to do two layers of 5/8 and then the T&G but I'd have to nail through the drywall to secure the T&G to the rafters, thus ruining the isolation. Does anyone have any insight. I don't need total isolation; just marginal.

John Sayers3 September 2007#7
Moose - removing the iron isn't hard, just unscrew it, lay insulation over the area and put the iron back on. That will significantly improve the rain noise. If you then add an RC mounted ceiling to the underside of your joists as you suggested and you should be OK. Green glue between the 5/8" sheets is another option to consider. cheers john
