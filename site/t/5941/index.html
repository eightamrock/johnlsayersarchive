SonicClang5 April 2006#2
Where do you start? realtraps.com is a good place for that. They have a video on treating a control room, but you can apply the theory to treating a live room. Bass traps and broadband absorbtion and try evening out the frequency response of your room. You know what bass loves... corners, so stick bass traps there. Test your room before you start so you know where your peaks and nulls are.
