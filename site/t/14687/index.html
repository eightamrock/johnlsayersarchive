jhbrandt13 August 2010#4
Fawad, Your post was just fine, IMO. It just takes time for folks to review and decide to reply. :wink: Your monitoring levels may be a bit on the high side - probably averaging 85 to 90 decibels. You might get around STC63 - 67 and possibly more with that party wall. Testing should be done to determine exactly. - If you have problem neighbors, they'll really be a problem if they can hear you. :P Your choice of CR is good. You want the CR to be the best room because all your mix decisions are made here. These days the CR is used as a recording room as well and comfort is also an important factor. The 'closed cell foam for sound isolation' is a waste of time and money because it has no acoustic value. You are just fine doing all your acoustic treatment with the 65kg/m3 rock wool. Cheers, John
