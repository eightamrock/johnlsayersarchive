How much does "barrierboard" cost per unit of mass? How does it compare in density? Those are the basic questions. If it is less dense than plasterboard (kg/m3), then forget it. If it is more expensive per unit of mass, then forget it. If it is both considerably cheaper AND considerably denser, then it might be worth looking at. But I doubt it will be both. Also, what technical reference papers and independent acoustic lab tests are there on "barrierboard"? Plasterboard is a known, tried, and proven method of sound isolation, with stacks of technical literature and laboratory tests, going back dozens of years or more. Can you say the same for barrierboard? Plasterboard is a well studied, known quantity: it works. And "barrierboard"? Who knows...
