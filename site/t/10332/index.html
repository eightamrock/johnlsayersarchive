Is it possible to set up a set of insulating walls without actually attaching (=screwing) the frames to the floor/wall/ceiling? pianojam15 March 2008#2
Well, for one thing if all you have in the basement so far is concrete block walls, then a common way (judging from the build of my house) of finishing the basement is to build a stud wall about 4" in from the block, anyway. This sounds to me like a good thing to leave permanently, so you can sell your house with a 'finished' basement. Skewed walls may not appeal to most buyers, if you're planning that... but drywall in the basement in my area is a selling point. My basement walls were paneling when I moved in.
