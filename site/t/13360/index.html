Ethan Winer29 July 2009#2
lambshank wrote:
I looked into Gik and they seem to go more for corner traps than panels behind the monitors.
As someone in the business, but not affiliated with either company, I'd definitely recommend GIK. A room the size of yours needs corner bass traps much more than panels behind the speakers. Your speakers don't face that way, so that's probably the last place to worry about. More here: Front Wall Absorption Plus, the guys from GIK actually know something about acoustics. :cop: --Ethan
