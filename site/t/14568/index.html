Soundman202011 July 2010#2
Hi Lemar, and welcome! It's always nice when someone does their homework and does everything right in their first post!!! :yahoo: Nice layout, too. The only comment I can think of is that you might need absorption in the front wall behind the speakers, and it looks like the panels on the first reflection points (side walls) might need to go a bit further forward, but that might just be due to the view angle. You'll probably also need more bass trapping than what you show: maybe across the wall/ceiling corner above the door? Looks like you have space up there. And perhaps the wall/ceiling corners of the side walls too. I wouldn't bother splaying the walls, if you are concerned about space. Your room ratio isn't fantastic, but it also isn't bad: 1 : 1.17 : 1.53. It's reasonably close to Louden's 5th best ration (according to Bob Gold's calculator), and the modal distribution is reasonable. You might be able to improve on it, but it all depends on which walls you can move, how you plan to build, etc. Good start! :) - Stuart -
