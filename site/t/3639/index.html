Ethan Winer27 April 2005#7
Hugo, Good foam works, but this statement disturbs me: "We are using a wodden backpanel to fix it on the wall, that's all"< As soon as you block the rear of an absorber panel with a solid material like wood, it no longer absorbs as well to low frequencies when mounted straddling a corner or spaced off the wall. That is, the wood backing negates the benefit from an air gap. --Ethan
