Gregwor4 May 2020#2
Welcome Allan! Do you own the house? Don't waste your money on a meter because you aren't going to be addressing your isolation. Luckily, since you aren't increasing your isolation, that will help with low frequency response. This is because you won't be containing your low frequency information inside your room. It will simply blow through your walls.
getting some drapes ASAP to do a bit of sound dampening?
Drapes will attenuate some high frequencies but will not help with low frequencies.
- Get Bass traps/Acoustic Panels
Bass traps will help a ton. After that, first reflection points should be addressed. That includes your side walls, a cloud and also your rear wall. It is easy to kill too much high end so I recommend taking a lot of REW measurements and probably avoid covering your panels with fabric until your treatment stage is complete. This will allow you to add film or slats or whatever to the face of the insulation before fabric. Here are instructions for using REW: viewtopic.php?f=3&t=21122 Personally, I recommend the Dayton Audio UMM-6 microphone because it is cheap and really easy to use with REW. Greg
