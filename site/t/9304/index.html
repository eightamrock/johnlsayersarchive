gullfo27 August 2007#5
you could measure the space - try the room eq wizard. next listen to the wood wall - does it vibrate? buzz? maybe you need to line the back of it with insulation (if possible). otherwise, with the basic absorbers you've drawn, maybe a small carpet if the concrete is too live, you should be off to a decent start - re-measure after setting it up. if its still not meeting your needs, then maybe you need to consider some other work to correct it - the decoupling screen w/ absorbers, additional walls, etc...
