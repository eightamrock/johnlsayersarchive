knightfly24 January 2008#11
The extra panels aren't necessary - even straight clouds would work, but angled they should vary absorption a bit due to the varied air gap behind. Just be sure to angle the absorbers high at the rear of the room just in case you use high density material that would tend to reflect highs back to the mix position - if this happens it'll cause smearing of highs/high mids due to comb filtering... Steve
