Greetings,
I know I shouldn't run them paralell to the electical lines, but how far do they need to be away from each other.
As much as possible, but at least a couple feet would be the minimum preferred.
Is a conduit really necessary. I figured I can just run the lines between the metal studs, I am not worrying about getting them back.
In wood construction, I would say no, but if running electrical or any wires through the prepunched holes in steel, YES. Otherwise the steel will slice through the wire eventually causing a short, and either cause a fire or serious injury...
