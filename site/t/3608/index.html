drfrankencopter22 April 2005#4
Go for as much as you can get/afford... your builder will probably impose some hard limits on how deep your basement can go (since odds are there are utilities like water at some nominal depth just below the standard basement depth). So, this means that you have to build up, and re-grade the ground around the house. At a certain height, that becomes impractical (imagine regrading the ground around your house 5 feet higher). In my area it costs about $3-5K per foot you add....I went for 9 feet. Yeah, I wish I had more, but I didn't want my house to look like it was on a littel hill, and even at 9 feet my driveway ended up fairly steep. Cheers, Kris
