John Sayers12 September 2006#2
Guy - we've just put in some doors into a studio locally. They are aluminium doors in two types. They have either 10mm or 8mm glass. One is a slider and it's rated at 32db with 10mm glass. The other is opening doors that are also rated at 32db with 10mm glass. We wanted the wide opening doors as they lead into the drum booth and we wanted to be able to wheel the piano in there if we needed an iso room for the piano. The other day they installed the doors and the owner setup a 600watt bass amp in the drum room and sealed the doors. (we have guitar line links between rooms so he could stand in the main studio and play the amp in the drum room. He wound the bass amp up and closed the doors. He said he could just hear the bass amp faintly, he was very impressed. Here's the cross-section and mounting system of both sets of doors. I hope this helps. cheers john
