Do you mean: if u solo the kick? Bass trapping is ment to give u a better bass responce and flatten out problems in the bass frequencies.. It will then fix some of the canceling that your room has in these frequencies and you will actually hear the bass, and what you are working with down there better. I guess there is a lot of peoples here that can give you more info but the bottom line is: more bass traps give you a better bass responce in the sweet spot too if done correctly
