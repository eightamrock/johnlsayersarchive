jhbrandt18 August 2010#2
I would recommend keeping the room size large & not splitting it up. Using a back room with video interface is a great idea, but I believe that you will find that as much recording will be done in the CR as mixing. Just use the back room for 'loud' stuff like recording drums and guitar amps. Cheers, John
