Hi, I'm planning to build my house plus project studio in Costa Rica this year. I'm from Belgium and want to start living in CR most of the year. The studio is mostly for myself, production/composing tasks and also projects for musicians who want to record in this environment... I'm already working on plans for about six months together with my architect (from LA), so concerning the house this is decided and ready for permits. The construction will start next month. I would like to post my plan to get some advice concerning the walls and the shape of the rooms. Note that the studio is part of the house, which includes my house (2 floors) and a rental loft above the studio. There are no neighbours in the first km, only nature (I'll post a picture of the land). The total surface is 200m2. Note also that all measurements are in meter! The buget I have for acoustic treatment is around 5.000 USD. The construction budget is together with the house and the guest unit (loft). Thank you for your time, regards, Jeff
