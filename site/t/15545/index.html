welcome! could you update your profile with your location? also, do you have some photos of the space? it might be helpful to understanding more. in a large room, chances are 4 panels won't do much but you could start with some absorber panels and perhaps some polycylindrical (poly) panels to preserve the room liveness but also make it more diffuse and add some bass trapping. possibly the same in the CR or with proper absorbers, reduce the deadness by opening up the drapes (increases the folds in it increases LF absorption of it)
