John Sayers14 April 2004#2
hi Heiko - it is narrow isn't it. I really can't offer much more than what you've suggested in your drawing. Turning it sideways wouldn't even fit. I'd just add insulation down the side walls to stop the bounce and cover the window with the same. You have one direction where you could add some angular walls and that's in the ceiling. I'd build a reflective cloud over the console area and another over the rack space/couch that reflects the signals back to the rear of the room. cheers John
