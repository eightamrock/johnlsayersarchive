Ro10 November 2008#30
Great progress! Just use the "standard" stuff like you did on the walls (pref. without the paperbacking). It's to absorb, not to actually isolate. You've got iso-clips, 3 layers of GB with GG in between. That's already a great isolator. You might wanna add more mass BETWEEN the joists, DIRECTLY at the ceiling which is comparable with the 3 layers of GB you hang on the clips. That way you'll create a 2-leaf system with mass on both sides, just as you do with the walls.

putting up some of those fantastic diffusers that everyone seems to be making.
I don't think you want diffusion in such a small space. My guess would be absorption, and lots of it. The trouble is, I think you could do with some treatment on your ceiling, but clearly there just isn't any room for that. Maybe treat the ceiling/wall corners, leave the ceiling as is, and carpet the floor? I think it would sound strange, but with such a low ceiling, there really isn't much else to do, IMHO. Maybe slot resonators would be useful here? Big ones, angled, along the walls, floor to ceiling. Dunno, really. I'm certainly not the acoustic expert.
