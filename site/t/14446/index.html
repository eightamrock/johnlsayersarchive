Well, it isn't a rule and you are using it in the wrong area. For a better understanding of where it should be used and what it actually is applied to please read this linked page: The 38% Rule from Wes Lachot Ideally you want as much room as you can get but what you really should try to incorporate is the diffusion that you gain from splayed walls and overheads. I, like most people here, am challenged due to lack of floor space to do this, but you have a 14,400 cubic foot space, 12 foot ceilings and that is an awesome room size. If you haven't already looked into searching for room ratios, again Eric Desart authored this page: ROOM RATIOS Still, getting a room designed with good ratios is well and good but the splaying techniques seem to be the most effective thing that you can do, given the real estate, to develop an exciting sounding room. So don't mess it up with a fast paced design that leaves you wishing you would have read just one more thing ;) Gotta go, Brien
