knightfly25 May 2006#7
Sorry for the delay; I don't recall ever seeing pocket doors used in a studio - for ANY sound barrier, the best scenario is two heavy masses separated by as much air space as practical. Typically when sliding doors are used, they are NOT pocket types but only half of the space opens, the other half is fixed glass. Even these types, unless equipped with SINGLE panes of HEAVY glass and two of them are installed in two separate wall frames, aren't very good at isolation. A pocket door tends to weaken the wall frame it's installed in, which isn't a good idea for a sound wall - plus, when the doors are closed the pockets within the wall would make up a 2-leaf barrier for EACH half of a double framed wall, so that part of the wall would be even WORSE for isolation. I think all these reasons are why you don't see this done in studios... Steve
