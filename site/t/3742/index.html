is it possibe and/or normal to soundproof a room by creating a vacuum in between the inner and out walls of a room within a room scenario? I was thinking that if i could set up even a partial vacuum then surely would i not stop a big lot of the sound from entering the room? can somebody tell me the upsides/downsides to this line of thinking and if there are any suggestions as to how i might go about this it would be much appreciated.

an isolated vacuum. the inside room is airtight, the outside room is airtight and the vacuum is in between.
