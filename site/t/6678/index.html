sharward21 July 2006#2
You're proposing triple leaf in most areas. Is the small square room at the bottom an entrance point? If so, you should make that a sound lock and use the existing concrete walls in that area. That would eliminate your third leaf. Increase the air gap and that should work well. --Keith :mrgreen:

You got it. Basically, if you structure things so that all you see in the entrance area is concrete and doors, and all you see in the other rooms is your new wall and doors, you should be OK. (That's an extremely oversimplified way of explaining it though.) --Keith :mrgreen:
