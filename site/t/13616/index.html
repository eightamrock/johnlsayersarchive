"I've been reading PHILIP NEWELL's book and sometimes I feel enouraged , sometimes I don't at all . " Hi new guy. Question. When you were in college did you read only one book on a subject or did you read several books? Mr. Newell has a really nice and very page filled book, some about building studio recording rooms and just as much not about building studio recording rooms. It's over the top in some areas and doesn't have the depth required for other areas. My personal favorite for trying to get a handle on something completely foreign to me is to read more on the subject by different authors. Building a Recording Studio: by Jeff Cooper Acoustic Design for the Home Studio: by Gallagher Mitch Two really good books for someone beginning to get a handle on this type of building. You can expect to spend about the same per square footage cost for your build as you would for say an executive type home give or take 100.00 dollars a sq. foot.
