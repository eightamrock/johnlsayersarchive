gullfo4 November 2007#10
if you import the studio floor plan (convert to jpg) into your sketchup file, you can scale it to fit. you can then use the measuring tool to determine sizes and see if the rooms work for you. as far as modes - generally you measure to the first significantly hard boundary - this may be a wall or floor above. however, if you are planning to build a room within a room, then the modes calculations should be based on the interior space. this is true for all rooms within a given design as well as then computing the relationships of things within the rooms such as soffits, furniture, positions of speakers on walls, hard treatments, etc. as they all affect (or are affected by) to some degree the room...
