Soundman202028 December 2015#5
I would suggest that you buy two books and study them carefully: "Master Handbook of Acoustics" by F. Alton Everest, and "Home Recording Studio: Build it Like the Pros", by Rod Gervais. Those will give you the background you need in acoustics and in construction. Probably your top option is closest to something that makes sense, but you are wasting a lot of space there, and not isolation very much at the same time. For example, your sound lock is not necessary and uses up useful space. You do not need to splay the walls of the CR, unless you want a full RFZ-type design, in which case the angles are wrong. And you are also wasting space by cutting off the front and rear corners of the room, which could be used for bass trapping and speaker soffits. - Stuart -
