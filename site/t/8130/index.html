sharward4 February 2007#4
Hi Mike, Yes, I agree, steel framing should be fine... Although I prefer timber because it can absorb quite a bit of vapor moisture without rotting, whereas on steel, moisture condenses... :roll: ...So I think mold is probably more likely to occur on steel. But there's nothing inherent about steel that makes it a bad choice in double wall studio construction. I hope you'll start a new thread here with al of your plans. 2,000 is big! :-) Be prepared to answer a lot of questions about the floating floor plans... Search on the topic of floating floors/rooms here, and you're bound to find many threads where we recommend against it. It's extraordinarily difficult to get right. --Keith :mrgreen:
