Hello everyone, here's the plan and details about the studio I'm building in my basement. Location: Granby, Quebec, Canada Currently, there is no further division in the basement after a water damage (luckily I am covered by insurance). I need to maintain a tool room, a bath (laundry room) and a lounge. I would also like to make use of the live room as practice room (if possible). It is also important to have a control room as large as possible (the most important room in my opinion). Having two amps closet would also be practical for live recording session. The ceiling is 7 feet tall berore insulation. I record quite all styles of music, but many rock and metal band. The soundproofing is not a problem for me, I read a lot on the subject (thank you to this forum).So I try to maximize space, while keeping the best ratios for the best sound. After three months of research and drawings, I come with this layout, but before I start building, I want to make sure everything is optimal. Thank you giving me your advice and feedback!
