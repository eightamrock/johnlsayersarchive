Jazzooo21 July 2006#1
Hi--my first post here, though I've been lurking for a while. We're planning to build a house with a 1000sf studio down here in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico. There is the question of what kind of flooring material I should use for both my control room (10 x 20) and the live room (the rest, divided into three iso booths--drums, acoustic bass, tenor sax or vocals) and a large space for acoustic piano and additional musicians, choir etc. I want wood floors--but wood floors here in central rural Mexico are not that easy to come by without spending a ton of money. It might be cheaper to get a Pergo-type product--plywood or pressboard with a wood veneer. Is there a significant difference in the quality or utility of these kinds of products versus the real thing? Many thanks, Doug
sharward21 July 2006#2
Welcome to the family! :) I would think that a laminate floor (e.g., Pergo brand) would be easier to maintain and less prone to scuffs and scratches. However, I don't know that you'd want to put a heavy piano on it either. :roll: Acoustically, I don't think it would matter much -- but I'm not qualified to speak to that. Have you considered etched stained concrete? It's cheaper than both wood and laminate, extremely durable, and looks a lot like marble. 8) --Keith :mrgreen:
