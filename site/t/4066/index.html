OK, here is the new idea (reversed) please excuse the irregularity of speaker and desk placement (I can't seem to find an easy way to move things in Sketchup, half the time my couch is floating in mid air!) Here are my construction ideas to make this project not too expensive. Every day I spend at least an hour going through old posts, I'm just feeling overwhelmed right now. We take possession on Nov 18, then we can start the studio construction. I plan to get a few past clients that are builders to do the bulk of the work, I will do what I can. Walls: Since the basement is almost completely underground with concrete up to the ceilings, I am not going to do the room within a room approach. The contracted house builders are going to put drywall to the "frostline". I am going to ask them if they can refrain from doing any drywall, so I can put the inner 2 layers of drywall on RC, thus making my 2 leaf system with a (very minimal) air gap. Windows: Place inner windows inside (this will make a 10” air gap between the inner and outer windows). The windows are dug out with a retainer, I plan to use the windows for ventilation whenever I’m not being that loud… the neighbors are very close though! Dividing walls: Basic wall structure with steel studs and differing gyp-rock thickness on either side (also using RC on one side) I’m not too concerned about noise transfer between rooms. Floor: Hardwood over the concrete. Basic moisture resistant foam underlay as I am in a basement! Ceiling: I will be putting 2 layers of gyp-rock in between the joists (using the glue and runner method from this forum) to beef up that leaf. The main floor of the house also has an extra layer of cork underneath the laminate (as thick as I could get it). I will then put 1 to 2 layers of gyp-rock on RC attached to the joists (as long as it can support that). This is the main area that I want moderately sound-proofed to tone down my toddler daughter’s running and to minimize the amount of transfer to the rest of the house (notice I said minimize not eliminate!) Acoustic treatment: This I will tackle once the room is up, I wouldn’t mind getting started on a home –made bass trap though… I plan to put 2 bass traps in the evil corners. Also, on the wall next to the stairs there will be a duct that is closed in, will this cause any strange problems? AC: I plan to use my portable AC/dehumidifier in the entrance (as it can be easily vented outside there) With that and the windows, do I need to worry about other venting? (the venting/ HVAC stuff on this forum is way over my head!) I know that the “live room” doesn’t have any windows, but it won’t be used that much and could have a portable fan in it…. Is this lame? So, please Hammer, don’t hurt me… I’ve been searching extensively on all of my above ideas (and I will continue to do so!), I just figured it’s time to throw what I’m thinking out there and see what y’all think. Let me know if you can think of a better room placement system as well (considering my afore-mentioned needs). Thanks again for this resource, I don’t know how I would do without it!
