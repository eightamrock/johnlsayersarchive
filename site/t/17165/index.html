I was talking to the contractor that is going to be installing my second layer and he told me about the new USG lightweight gypsum. After doing to some research, it looks like some of the larger home improvement centers will be discontinuing the standard heavy gypsum/drywall soon. I checked with Home Depot and Lowe's (which is mostly who is around this area). Other lumber yards may be different, but I thought that I would post. One type is made by USG http://www.gypsumtoday.com/news/viewnews.pl/id=1873 My contractor told me that the product rep was telling him how much better this new stuff is :blah: and that though it was lighter (about 20 lbs/sheet), it was just as dense. :shock: Yeah... I told my contractor not to count on using the lighter stuff, I don't care how much lighter and easier it is to work with. Scott
