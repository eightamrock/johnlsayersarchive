TL=Transmission Loss=The accumulated decrease in acoustic intensity as an acoustic pressure wave propagates outwards from a source. So basically, the reduction in noise level resulting from passage through an obstruction. Also, the RC in your picture looks more like hat channel, then the RC that's usually suggested here on this forum.
