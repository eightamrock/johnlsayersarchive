drfrankencopter23 April 2007#3
Instead of stuffing them in your wall (where they'll reduce your sound isolation), you can stack them up on the outside of your wall (in your recording/control room) and you've got a Helmholtz resonator bass trap. Put a little bit of sand or insulation in them and you'll broaden the Q of the trap. Also, all those bottles will act as polycylindrical diffusers... Not a terribly effective bass trap...but it would be a way to employ empty plastic bottles in your studio... Cheers Kris
