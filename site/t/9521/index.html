gullfo6 October 2007#7
3/4" MDF is about 3.25lb/sqft. 5/8" drywall is about 2.2lb/sqft - depending on where you live one or the other may be cheaper... if you can double up the 5/8" you have more density... for example, by me, MDF is about $23 per panel whereas a pair of paperless 5/8" drywall is $13 and regular 5/8 is about $10 a pair so I can get way more density per $ with drywall (except labor cost...) MDF or drywall dust (like most dust) isn't great to inhale... http://www.usg.com/USG_Marketing_Conten ... 00-001.pdf http://www.flakeboard.com/msds/Flakeboa ... F_MSDS.pdf
