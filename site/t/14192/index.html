xSpace16 March 2010#3
What is an A room and a B room? A lot of folks look for a control room with a tracking room...is this what you are looking for? What shape are the walls in...block...coupled sheet rock that separates you from another bay...metal? By and large the current design you have is wasting space and is in effective for musical purposes. I do not have ability to draw, per se, but I can help you get closer to your goal, if your goal is better defined. I think when you define what an A room is and a B room is to you we might all be able to get on the same page with you. In a nutshell, you want to locate the entire build in the most isolated part of this building. That means starting at the thickest wall material area and developing from there out. Please include dimensions of this room...especially the height... Your existing HVAC and electrical will push the price out of reach if you do not get these areas closer together. And then there is the thin metal roofing. You can build block walls inside but if you do not keep your mind on this "weak link" then you build it all but still the noise from airplanes and helicopters and sirens will penetrate this corrugated roof and pierce your inner framed ceilings and destroy your isolation. Brien
