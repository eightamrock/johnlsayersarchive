That's looking Ok ChuckU - those jackposts will need to be isolated or all the slound will go straight up them and into your ceiling area so why not incorporate that post near the controil rrom into the control room wall, which will mean extending the control room forward making it slightly larger. You really need double walls to get good isolation so place the post in the cavity between the walls. also you could make a wall from Iso 1 to next to the door to create a sound lock into the studio?? cheers john
