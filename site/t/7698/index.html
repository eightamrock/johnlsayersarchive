That is not a lot of info to go on. Most standard steel "Garage to House entry doors" are steel either side, wood frame (particle board style) around the perimeter of door (around edge in between steel) and then some sort of insulation fill. Door I just purchased should/could be very similar to yours (and I very well could be wrong):

My door was not the best choice for iso, but this was my best choice to meet my building code for an exterior door. Rod has posted about someones door within the past few months on some thread on here, gave a very good response to a concern for the doors feeling light. IF the door has a manufacture stamp somewhere, find the web site... and if you can take an educated guess on the weight of door you maybe able to figure out exact construction. IMHO though FREE is a great price and bare min your door is solid filled with something (I hope) but the material (if like mine "polystyrene core") may not be the best density but will let the door act as a single leaf. Once in place you should evaluate and maybe upgrade the seals. Hope that helps :roll: Marc

Most of those fire rated doors aren't solid. I know the ones in my home aren't. THey do seal nice, but they don't have the weight of a solid door. You could make up for it by gluing MDF or Drywall to both sides of the door if your hinges are strong enough
