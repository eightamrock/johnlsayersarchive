infiniteposse13 January 2007#54
1.13.07 - Update Happy New Year! So almost too much has happened to put it under any one tidy marker... So far we're done with 99.9% of the plumbing upgrade, the HVAC and ducting is almost completed, 90% of the framing is done and the stairs started to go in late last night. ADT will be by today to get their security wiring roughed in before the softing gets knocked out for the rather large series of ducts they're putting up gets installed. The drain system got some good testing over the last month or so as the rains began around here and also a gutter got clogged and dropped gallon after gallon of water onto the side of the house. It was fateful in that it exposed some weaknesses in the system that they needed to work on some more. Rains post-fixes haven't dented it, so that's encouraging. The mud room that's being appended to the rear of the house is also completely dried in and looks great. It really achieved the goal of blending into the existing structure nicely. The roofing will hopefully gets knocked out this week if the weather allows. Interesting/exciting things to note: -The large cast iron drain pipe that was jutting into the space downstairs was taken care of and is almost flush to the wall. A small ceiling soffit is all that will be noticable in the space when finished. -The flex ducting that I was so concerned about is in, for better, for worse. Their solution for avoiding the noise caused by kinking induced by straps + gravity is to not use straps to keep the ducts in place. Instead they're going to soffit the ducts into place next to one another. Their goal is to uniformly pancake the ducting under the soffit and to keep them all straight and flat. Hopefully this will minimise any noise that might occur from air hitting irregularities in the duct's shape... I think that's it for now. I'll now post a boatload of pics to let the story tell itself... Thanks!

infiniteposse18 January 2007#62
Ah shucks, thanks Sharwood:) Question of the day: New electrical Panel install Tomorrow is the switch in power service/panel upgrade and my contractor and I got to talking about how best to deal with the fact that the panel is going to be (and has to be) accessible in the in the live room. My concern is 1) creating a hole in the lovely 2 sheets of 5/8ths sheetrock and also 2) having something that can resonate and cause noise in the space. My contractor was thinking we'd move forward with the same principals used in sealing up individual electrical outlets - no air gap on the sides, plup up any gaps in the box, rockwool against the back of the panel box, etc... Are there any special suggestions you guys might have to make this work? Visually speaking, I think we're going to mount a Real Trap "Mini Trap" over it so it won't be an eyesore. Lighting Electrical is roughed in and so we went through the specifics of lighting. I'm once again going beyond my initial budget, but I don't think I can skimp on lights. Basically we're going to have a combo of cans for general lighting in the middle of the rooms, some track lights for hitting various parts of the space to the sides (racks, amps, ambient lighting off walls, etc.) and some sconces in the space to hit spaces where a track is overkill and there are soffitts that won't allow for a can. We're going with a switched system with lots of switch options as opposed to any kind of faders. All lights will be transformerless. If anyone has any suggestions of solid manufacturers for track systems I'd love to hear them. That's about it for now. If anyone has any suggestions about the questions above I'd really appreciate the feedback. Thanks!
