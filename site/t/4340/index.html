AVare7 September 2005#2
There are numerous posts,a nd I can't rmember the threads, but basically there ae three things to look at/improve. 1. REduce/stop reflections arriving withitnt he first 15 ms. Threads on "mirror trick" will help. 2. Control reverb time to around 150 ms. 3. Low end aborption, usually done in corners. Easiest material to work with are wideband absorbers from mineral wool. Keep asking and we will help you get there! Andre
