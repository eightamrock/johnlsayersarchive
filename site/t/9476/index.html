Cat Trax27 September 2007#1
I'm new. Hi all. I'm Josh. I'm from Albany, NY. Thanks for the great forum! I currently own a professional project studio. Here is my problem. It just cost too much to keep going. I'm thinking of moving it home. I have two rooms next to each other in my house. The one that could be the recording room is 11'5 x 13'5 with 8 ft ceilings. Is there any way to get a good drum sound in such a small space? Even though there would still be the rafters, would taking out the ceiling and utilizing the attic space help any? I am spoiled by my nice room. It's twice the size and the ceilings are 10ft. I get a good drum sound there. If it's not possible to get a good drum sound with the measurments I said, I can't see moving the studio home. Any advice would be great. Thanks for the help.
doublehelix27 September 2007#2
It is definitely going to sound different in the small room, no doubt. Due to the small size, you might need to go for a dead drum sound by putting deadening treatment around the kit and on the ceiling above the OHs, and then add back in some ambiance with a convolution reverb during mixdown. Removing the ceiling might help, but with a small room like that, it is going to be hard to get a great room sound. I would close mic everything, deaden what you can in the room, and then add it back in artificially. Not ideal, but it is what a lot of us deal with in low-ceilinged rooms. You will miss your nice tracking room for sure.
