rod gervais23 March 2009#2
Ethan, where are you from?.......... My recommendation would be to look not for contractor's - but rather a GC (General Contractor) in your area to control the process with you. Any reputable GC is not only going know the Best subs in the area - they will also be able to get you the right numbers. Well worth the 4 or 5 percent OH&P you will pay them over the hard costs. The will also have the experience to fill in all of the blanks - all of the incidental things that you will never in a million years think of that become a cost of the project - and between controlling the bidding process...... assisting you through the design - handling he buyout and budget - overseeing the actual work, you will save thousands of dollars in real money - as well as hundreds of hours of you time that will otherwie be spent in frustration. They (as a GC) have a long term relationship with the subs and suppliers they do business with - those people see a future with them. On the other hand there is you - who they will view as a one shot deal......... When it comes down to a sub who is working on multiple projects - and they have you pulling their left arm to meet schedule - and me (or GC's like me) pulling their right arm to meet schedule - who do you think they will satisfy first? Think about it Sincerely, Rod
