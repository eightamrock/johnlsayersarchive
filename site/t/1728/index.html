knightfly5 June 2004#13
If your doors are all capable of good airtight seals, then that extra door would translate to a triple leaf barrier as far as the alcove area between rooms - this isn't good for anything but mid frequencies; lower ones (the hard part of sound isolation) need mass-air-mass, period. With good seals, it would be better to leave the inner alcove door off - that would also give more maneuvering room by not having any extra framing or the door itself in the way... Steve
