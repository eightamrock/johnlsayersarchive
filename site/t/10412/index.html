The space belongs to me, it's 80 square meters, but the roof is slanted at an angle of only 15 degrees so much of the space is unavailable because it's to low. In the highest point the room is 260cm high, in the lowest it's 102cm - and that's without insulation. I want to treat the roof with 15cm of rockwool, and the floor with 4cm of wool + 4cm of concrete. There are also two chimneys in the room. Please bear in mind that after the insulation comes in there will be only two meters of space on each side of the axis of the building that will be high enough to move freely. I record mostly rock/blues/metal bands so it gets really loud at times:-) I hope that you'll be willing to advise me somehow on this project. Thanks in advance for your help. Cheers

Aloha and welcome to the forum, Congrats on your studio build! A couple of thoughts for you: - Since isolation is what you are hoping to achieve, be sure to check with a structural engineer to see if your building can support the extra weight of studio construction that is involved. (Something to the effect of 2-3 layers of drywall/Sheetrock/gyp rock + framing & insulation, equipment, people, etc.) - John has worked on a few designs in which the ceilings were similar to yours. Perhaps using the search function would prove helpful. Looking forward to your build. Aloha 8)
