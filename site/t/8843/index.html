rod gervais24 May 2007#2
Nightmusic wrote:
Some people don't hesitate to keep the '2 step' attitude: they build isolation wall still parallel, then they start the room shape modifications on top of that... so i am wondering if there would be a special reason for that??
Night - generally when you see that taking place - what they are doing afterwards is treating the space - not installing walls that would (in effect) create a three leaf system. One of theproblems with designing a studio with walls out of parallel is that you cannot then use established room ratios as a part of the design. All room ratios are designed based on rectangular rooms - if you start sloping ceilings and installing walls at 6 degree (minimum) splays you change all of that. The idea that you can average out the room dimensions and have that represent a room ratio (or that you can do that and use it to determine room modes) is incorrect and false on the face of it. so you pick one and proceed - or you pick the other and proceed...... I hope that helped to clearthings up for you. sincerely, Rod
