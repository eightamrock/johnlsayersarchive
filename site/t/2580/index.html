I have read quite a bit in the basement thread ie floating floors/ wall consrtuction etc. as well as a buch of other info. Maybe too much? Questions and/or concerns so far: I would assume that it is best to avoid parallel walls in this to-be tracking room. John Sayers1 November 2004#2
That laminated beam area is a bit strange. What's the steel ladder shape doing here? There appears to be a beam above your laminated beam running the other way i.e top to bottom on your plan. I think I'd get a structural engineer to look at it - it looks like a quick fix which could probably be done better, more efficiently and not so intrusive. With your 7' 6" ceing height to float a floor and treat the ceiling will give you a very low ceiling height in the end. I assume you are trying to gain isolation from upstairs. cheers john
