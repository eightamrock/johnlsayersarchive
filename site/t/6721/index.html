knightfly30 July 2006#2
For general info, the REFERENCE section has quite a bit; until you have more detailed information on your space I'm not sure what to tell you. ONe thing is sure, 300 quid won't stretch very far so "found" materials will help. Look for anything HEAVY without holes in it - brick, concrete block, MDF, gypsum, lead, particle board, cement board, etc, all can be used to add mass to existing walls. Carpeting is only good for dogs and drums to sit on; use very much of it, and it will suck out all the highs and high mids leaving a boxy, boomy room - especially if that room is masonry walls. And no, absorption INSIDE the room will do pretty much ZIP for sound-proofing; it's still just as loud outside. Check out the first link in that REFERENCE section for more theory on mass-air-mass construction... Steve
