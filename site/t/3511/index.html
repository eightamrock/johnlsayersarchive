lunatic27 April 2005#5
Yes... progress (see pics here http://www.evergroove.com)! Rather than fight the Gestapo empty handed, we consulted a Master Electrician that really seemed to be on the ball. He had all but finished his EE degree and has spent a little time dabbling in medical laser design :shock: We told him of the plan to install isolated ground receptacles and have a seperate, insulated ground, run from each IGR's ground screw back to an isolated ground bar in the panel. He said it wasn't necessary to go to that degree of detail. So, we installed 12/3 romex in the walls, daisy chained power, daisy chained the isolated grounds and daisy chained the safety ground. The isolated grounds went to their own ground bar in the panel and the safety (or 'dirty' as we started calling them) went to their own ground bar in the panel. The receptaces were indeed IGR style (pretty orange) and these were surface mounted into metal boxes. So the dirty grounds were connected to the metal boxes (as they should be!). The isolated grounds were NOT connected to these boxes. They we're instead connected to the ground screw on the IGRs. So far so good with the local building inspectors. Thanks for asking, by the way! It's good to be remembered :wink: Do *you* have any tips for installing fabric? Keep up the good work. I'm real impressed with how this place has grown! Peace, Brad

Unfortunately I see a lot of electricians (even engineers) who are really surprised to find that there really IS a difference in noise level when running separate, TRUE star ground systems; I hope you're not disappointed because it's not nearly as easy to do this AFTER the conduit/walls are in, if it's even possible at all.
