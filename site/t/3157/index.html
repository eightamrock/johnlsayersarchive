(electricians code book) will allow 120 volt single phase 3 wire system with 60 volts on each of two ungrounded conductors to a grounded neutral conductor shall be permitted for the purpose of reducing objectioanble noise in sensitive electronic equipment locations provided that the following conditions apply: 1 The system is installed only in commercial or industrial occupancies. 2 The system's use is restricted to areas under close supervision by qualified personnel.
