Hello all, I am new to the forum and I have read the guidelines and will try to comply. I am designing a new studio with a friend who is a successful TV Commercial composer, sound designer, songwriter, music producer, mastering engineer and voiceover artist. The studio is being built out of the existing garage space in his new Malibu, CA home. As you can see from the description he is very versatile and will use the studio for all of these purposes. I have attached a JPEG of a rough Sketch Up rendering of the existing interior dimensions and geometry of the space and this rendering also includes an indication of where we are adding walls, a machine room, etc. I have tried to clearly indicate these things in the file. I have placed a chair in the realtive mix position we are considering. The monitors are Barefoot Sound MicroMain27's but are not indicated in the file rendering yet. The location of the machine room right now is being determined by the existing window opening which we plan to us for a window exchange AC unit. This will cool the machine room and provide cold air through silent ducting into the main studio. You will also notice that there are some walls where we are not adding new interior walls. This is because these walls are thick stone masonry as is also the case with the ceiling/roof. They have significant mass and we don't think we will have to add anything there. We are planning on using a double 2x4 framed wall construction with a gap between walls. No drywall in the gap. R14 insulation in both walls and double 5/8" drywall on the inside and outside walls with Green Glue between layers. One thing that we are sort of going back and forth on is whether or not we should consider moving the machine room and mix position to take advantage of the existing sloped ceiling over the back half of the space. This will add time and cost but we are open to it if it is significantly better. Also, we are currently planning on building a freestanding 6' x 4 ' IsoBooth using plans from dawbox.com. We will place this in the back of the room next to the machine room. We would like to open up this to discussion on the forum. our intial questions are; 1. Should we move the mix position to take advantage of the sloped ceiling? 2. Should we consider building a true IsoBooth instead of a freestanding/removeable one? 3. Any general suggestions you think we should consider? Cheers, LittleTokyoRodan
