I guess I have to answer your question with a question of my own: Why? I would think you could get much better results much cheaper if you used software plug-ins. The problem you'd have with building your own is that it's not controlable. All of your echos would have the same characteristics on every song. In order to vary the echo (in "hardware") you'd have to be able to vary the length of the chamber AND the dampening (so you could control how many returns you got. Are you sure you're not thinking of a reverb chamber? Again, with software, you can get much more control of the signal and for a lot less money. I'm thinking of an SIR convolution type reverb. len
