Aaronw28 January 2008#1127
Hi Steve, When I first started researching studios, building, etc. My first thought was to use sand. If my room were in another scenario, I possibly would consider it again as well, but I had to level the floor (nearly a 3" difference from one side to the other), and also about possible moisture (being in a basement) so I opted for the mineral wool solution. Sand is definately more dense than mineral fiber. It also settles more over time, which could leave you with some surfaces not being fully dampened on the floor. (probably the least of your worries though.) Sand is heavy! And depending on what the structure is under this floor, you may need to consider or consult structural engineer to support any loads, (including your frame). When I built my floor, I glued only the first layer down against the frame. You may want to put some other type of mass loaded vinyl or similar between your layers of subfloor (optional). Not sure of your isolation requirements. I have some structure borne noise, but I think most of it is because my sub is currently sitting directly on my floor. Once I isolate it some, I think it'll make a difference. But my floor in general sounds pretty solid with what I've done.
