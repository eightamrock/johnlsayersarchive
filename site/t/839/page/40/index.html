knightfly31 December 2006#986
IF you really wanna know what your tension is doing dynamically, see if you can find one of these to rent http://www.tentel.com/prod01a.htm I have one from my video tech days that I'd be glad to loan you, but it only goes up to 1" tape (didn't do enough work on 2" machines to warrant the cost) - Cool thing about these - you can watch the tension needle as the machine is actually running tape at correct speed, and any "twitches" you see can be compared to the once-around timing of each idler, capstan, take-up or supply reel, etc, so it's invaluable in finding bad bearings or dragging mechanical brake bands, etc... Steve
