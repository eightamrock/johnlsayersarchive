Nice work in the "throne room" According to the density of materials provided in the references aera of this site. Your first leaf with 2X 5/8 gypsum board is around 4.63psf the other leaf with plywood is around 6.48psf. Regular glass weight around 1.64psf for 1/8 thick. So you would need glasses of at least 1/2" thick (6.56psf) or more. So using 1/2 and 5/8 glass is OK, but i would go with 5/8 and 3/4. Rod are you there...? "Typical" window angle is at least 6 degres, less than that is useless. Regards
