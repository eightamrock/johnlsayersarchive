Aaronw18 December 2006#966
On those bays.... are you going to isolate the mic line one from all the others as far as position and grounding goes? My understanding of this suggests that it would be bad to even have that bay in the same vicinity as other line level bays for crosstalk reasons, mic level being SO much lower. Thoughts?
I've never had any issue's with tielines/mic inputs on a bay in the same vicinity as all other bays. If the engineers designing these million dollar consoles such as SSL, Neve, API, etc can have it in their consoles, I don't see any problem, nor have I experienced any problems in the past. I'm not running amplified signals through the bays. Line level yes, but not amplified.
