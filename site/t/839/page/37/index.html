Aaronw28 October 2006#906
Marc, In typical normal contruction I would. There are several reasons why I have not done this. First and foremost, I haven't done the HVAC duct work yet. In order for me to do this, I need the walls up first. Second, as Steve mentioned in that thread, to reduce the chance of shorting due to sag over any time. Third, I still need access to the ceiling for running some additional cables/wires that I haven't pulled yet. And finally...I haven't finished insulating the ceiling joists yet. Update: The first layer has been caulked in the corners & around any electrical components, and the joints taped and mudded. Used backer rod (polyfoam caulk saver) in the corners. I'm gearing up to start the second layer. As with the control room, I've opted to not use the Green Glue. It's an expense I'm not willing to justify at this time. So, I'll stick to the 2 layers drywall, plus the additional layer of ply, and whatever finish treatment I decide (T&G?). Attached are a couple pix with the first layer done...
