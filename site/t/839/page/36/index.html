Regular sheetrock suppose to cost less, is more dense (weighs more) and provide better iso then X. Steve wrote this on my thread:
BTW, "X" code drywall is about 4 pounds lighter per 4x8 foot sheet (and usually more expensive so won't perform quite as good on low end as standard stuff - Keith SHERWOOD and I went over this sometime back, it's probably on his thread on about page 5, or 8, or 11, or... - bottom line is, ANY gypsum wallboard as thick as WE would build is AUTOMAgICALLY at LEAST 1-hour fire rated when built correctly.
