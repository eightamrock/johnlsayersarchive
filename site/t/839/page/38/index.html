Ro29 November 2006#941
Ah, now my photoshop experience comes in handy to share :) There's a batch process called "Web Photo Gallery" in PS, use that. It'll save tons of manual resizing. Make sure your pix are angled right. Then it's a piece of cake. here we go: - Put all your original photo's in one folder. (example: d:\photos\original) - Make another folder for the resized ones (example d:\photos\resized) - in PS FILES / Automate / Web Photo Gallery (see screenshot) - Choose any preset ya like, I prefer "simple" (don't matter in the end) - Source: use folder (and BROWSE to original photo folder) [in the example D:\photos\original] - Select Destination [in example D:\photos\resized] - Options "large images". resize custom to 700 pix. medium Q (5) (other options don't matter, we'll use only large pix) - Press OK. Now all images will be resized to max 700x700 and put in destination folder under another folder called "images" Just upload those and yer done. voila!
