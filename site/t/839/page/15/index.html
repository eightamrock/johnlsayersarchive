knightfly23 February 2005#355
If your floor framing isn't really sturdy under that location, the block stacks would probably not be a good idea; my original idea, when filled with sand (after mortaring the outsides of the stacks) would weigh nearly 1000 pounds each. In order to use those, you would need to cut holes in your floated floor large enough to build the stands on concrete, then stuff around the stacks with insulation, and set the stacks as close to the front as possible so your speakers are supported as close to their front edge as you can get. Another option along that line would be to offset just the top row of blocks forward - Pavers are just flat, solid concrete blocks; used normally to do small patio sections bedded in sand, etc - the dim's are there, it's just solid concrete. The other blocks would be standard hollow wall blocks, buttered with anything that would stick to 'em after they're stacked. The "Pavers" would just give a solid top. Again, though, these stacks would want filled with sand, and weight would be nearly 1000 pounds each. The "pavers" could be oriented long dimension to the front/back, and then offset as far forward as possible for speaker support. All that is moot if you don't want to (or can't) cut holes in the floor at this point. Such being the case, have you looked at this lately? http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=718 HTH... Steve

knightfly24 February 2005#359
If your joists in the soffit area are 24" centers, maybe best would be to laminate the entire soffit floor with 1-1/8 ply - then tar paper and hollow 8x8x16 blocks, NOT filled, stuffed with rockwool and capped with the pavers OFFSET to the front a couple inches, floor plates in front of the speaker location cut out if necessary - you want as much mass as you can get in the stands, isolation ABOVE them and below the speakers, and enough weight on the speakers (or small enough isolators) to get 10-20% compression.
