I start physical therapy next Tuesday, so hopefully that will get me back into shape soon to move forward on the studio. I guess the next tasks are to finish up the conduit runs to the tracking rooms so I can pull electrical wiring. After that I can finish any insulation I hadn't put in yet, install the RC channel, and then drywall the tracking rooms. Yippie. Sounds simple, but I need about $1000-$1200 worth of drywall. Then on the walls, I'm attaching plywood, so I have something to attach T&G or some other type of wood finish, trim, etc. Or just be able to attach acoustic panels. I just bought 3 more variacs off ebay, so I should be able to finish out the dimmers for lighting in the tracking rooms, and the second dimmer in the control room for the back half of the room. I still need to design or figure out the configuration for the back wall of the control room. In a small design flaw, (but was actually my first intent anyway), I've got to reverse one door in the direction in which it swings, so I'll do the other one the same way. They will swing into the room, but this changes some plans I've had for the back wall. So instead of having as much of a bass trap, it'll be trimmed down some. My main objective for the back wall from the beginning, and still is, is to use/build a broadband absorber for diffusion on the back wall. Starting from about waist high to the top (except where the HVAC comes in). The lower half is where I'm putting the bass trap. I got a cost on the JM Superduct board. It's about $60 a sheet (4' x 10'). Now I just need to calculate how much I need. Man that's some expensive stuff. :shock: But, then again, what hasn't been expensive with this project? You know, the more I think about it, I'm actually pretty darn close to finishing this studio, but it's all been a matter of finances. This year has been the tightest on funds that I've been in for some time. Here's what I have left as a general thought... Finish conduits, pull electrical, run lighting electrical, insulate, rc, drywall, caulk, drywall, caulk, floor...plastic/roofing felt/plywood/hardwood/sand/finish, insulate ceiling joists, rc, drywall, caulk, drywall, caulk, trim doors, trim windows, order glass, frame windows, door jams, finish doors and install, seals for doors, rear of control room treatment, finish front wall treatment, finish all other treatments, trim out control room, pull multipair cabling for mic cables, etc, order wall plates, terminate all plates and multipin connections, buy new (used) console, 2" machine, and more new toys... Well heck, that sums up the majority of it all. Now I just need about 15k for materials, and another 50k for toys. And no more side adventures with animal bites, car wrecks, hospitals or any other potential life changing events...except maybe a good woman...who can tolerate all this.. :lol:
