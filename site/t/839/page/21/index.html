It's not IF, but when. It WILL get finished. As of this moment...REALISTICALLY...I'm going to say 2007. I am officially 2 years now in the "Reconstruction" build of the basement. I originally started demolition around July 2003. Farted around and got everything ripped out on one side of the basement, prepped it and started the first floor in the iso room around Novemeber 2003. Full scale construction started the first week of January 2004 w/ the control room floating floor, and went non stop for 4 months into mid April 2004. Then things got tight, and slowed down for the summer, then picked up again around autumn. I seem to get more accomplished during the cold months than the warm ones. Anyway, I guess I'll say my projection for the Control Room completion will be by summer 2006. Here's what I have left...HVAC duct work, rear wall absorbtion and diffusion. The front speaker baffles, the front wall reversable panels for reflective/absorbtion (depending on 2 mix or 5.1 mix). Trim for the entire control room, including door jams and thresholds, window framing, etc. Once I frame the doors, they can be installed...already have them. Have to frame the windows first to get exact measurements for the glass. Finish up the electrical for the rear of the control room lighting. Iso room, same thing w/ trim, doors, and windows. And of course acoustical treatment. Then I can do panel mounts for control and iso room as well. The new mechanical room I expect to be done probably by the end of this month as far as drywall. That shouldn't be a problem. Just gotta get the drywall. I may even have the main tracking room completely framed by the end of 2005, and possibly the one on the end. I've got a few things to figure out yet w/ existing framing that I'm leaving due to a shower on the other side. Haven't decided yet, but I may be leaning away from the sliding glass doors. Too damn expensive, to much reflection. I'd maybe rather have a 36" door and a large pane of glass for a window between rooms. Well, that's all for the moment. Back to beer drinkin' at the office...LOL. :D
