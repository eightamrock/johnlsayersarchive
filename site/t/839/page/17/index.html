Aaronw20 May 2005#415
Hi Y'all... :D Seems like I've been busy on everything but the studio lately. I've been running some tones and pink noise, etc, just to see how the room is reacting without treatment. It's very interesting to see how the room changes as you make a change. I still need to complete the rear soffits, but I've got them mostly framed. Just need to finish the conduits (low voltage and electrical). The room is farely flat at 90dB, with a boost around 80 hz, and a major roll off around 8kHz. I'm assuming this is due to the reflections, noise cancellations, comb filtering, etc. Hoping this will bounce back up once the treatment is in. Anyway, that's the latest. I'll try and get this other roll developed and post the pix of the finished floor, etc... :D
