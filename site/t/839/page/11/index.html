I'm still trying to figure this out. I've been looking at a few studios around town to try and figure this out, but no luck yet.
Are these openings for double wood doors?
In this particular picture...NO. But it could be used or reframed this way down the road if I make any changes.
Also, I've never had to deal with steel stud construction. How did you cut this thinwall stuff?
Piece of cake. A pair of tin snips. Mark your measurement, cut the sides, fold it back and forth 5 or 6 times, and it snaps off creating a straight edge. If/when I build another studio...I'll probably do the steel studs again. So much easier to work with. :D If you mess up. Back out a couple screws and move it. If you cut too short, or have scraps, you can splice them together. Can't do that well w/ wood (and pass codes).
