knightfly14 February 2005#331
If you're going to use solid wood as a face, then yes you need openings preferably near corners, like top and bottom - I would "filter" these openings with heavy rockwool, line the rear/sides of the trap, and fill the rest with alternating, herringbone pattern of hangers. Sort of like this - Keep in mind that for later 5.1 work you may get some bad reflections out of that 90 degree corner from your right rear surround speaker, so it might need some kind of gobo during 5.1 mixes... Steve

knightfly22 February 2005#350
Go buy 20 8x8x16 concrete blocks, 8 2x8x16 "pavers", a pair of MoPads, and make two stacks of blocks 16x16x40" (5 blocks high, alternately stacking the blocks like bales of hay so they're tied together; put two of the pavers across the top of the stack, set the MoPads on the pavers, set the speakers on the MoPads, wire everything up, mix some stuff, then after you've "had your fix" :wink: figure out a way to add the plywood in front so it doesn't touch either the speakers or the blocks, but is flush with the front face of the JBL's - Or, you could just do the stacks of blocks, and get $500 worth of rockwool to fill everything around and behind the speakers with, but I think you'll like soffits enough to be glad you did 'em, and you KNOW John'll say so... :lol:
