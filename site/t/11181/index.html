AndreasB6 September 2008#4
Are your family name Behringer?? wow... Anyways, there's a couple of things about your studio design and layout that needs to be corrected. The room is asymmetrical and it really should be as symmetrical as possible. ie. the distance between walls and the actual position of where you sit has to be the same overall otherwise you'll not get a proper stereo image of what your speakers are putting out. The "absorber" you put on your left side looks like a wall made of drywall and that wouldn't absorb any sound just reflect it. so, as always, the general tip for people finding this site too late; spend some days/weeks/months reading all the stuff on this site and you'll find out pretty soon what's wrong with your design and what's right. best of luck a :arrow:
