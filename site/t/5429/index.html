Aloha,
Is it a problem that I'll only have room for side wall absorbers on one side wall?
This advice was given by Knightfly during the design phase: Left/right room symmetry (at least within a foot or two of ear height) is important for good stereo imaging. Since I am building the treatments in phases, I got to experience the truth of this principle in action (fortunately only for a short time) between getting the left side absorber up after finishing the right. It really does make a difference in the left/right imaging, not to mention the sonic difference after both sides were up. If the side wall absorber design is not a viable option, how about a thinner panel treatment like this: http://johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=4581 for the side walls left & right of your mix position?
