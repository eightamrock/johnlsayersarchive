Seeing a lot of pictures on this forum that show the removal of wall boards and ceilings, it occurred to me that some of this work can be very dangerous -- especially when it comes to lead paint and asbestos. Before you deconstruct anything in your building, please take a moment to read this "Indoor Air Quality - Remodeling Practices" article on BobVilla.com.
