Hi Clarence, and welcome! For that type of project, you might want to contact John directly, and ask him to quote to do the full design for your studio. Send him an e-mail or PM, and see if he is available to do that. Or if you prefer to do the design yourself with help from the folks here on the forum, then that's fine too! It's just that your requirements and goals suggest that you might fare better by paying someone to do the design for you, rather then spending a lot of time first learning the basics of acoustics, and of design, then spending the time to actually do the design. If you want it to be a world-class facility, then you probably should consider a world-class designer to design it, and John sure meets that definition! :) So contact him directly, and see what he says. (But if you do decide to take the DIY route, then we'll all be here to help you!) - Stuart -
