barefoot11 September 2003#12
I would probably put DRSs on the flat part of the ceiling as well. Oh, and did I mention that all the auralex should be removed as well? This is a live room and a nice, smooth, lively reverberant field is desirable. First modify the room to ameliorate the modal, interference, and flutter issues. Then listen to a variety of instruments, ensembles, and vocals in the room. If the room is still too bright, then you can start... sparingly... hanging high frequency absorption on the walls to warm things up. Acoustically, I like the idea of using a dense flexible material like linoleum for the hanger panels. I also like the idea of attaching the insulation to the panel only at the very top. This way the panel and insulation can rub against one another and dissipate more energy through friction. 703 is a good choice. John or Steve might have different ideas about building hangers. Thomas
