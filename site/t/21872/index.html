Get some snips like this and snip them off.
Image not preserved: Snips.png
I recommend getting some with extra long grips so you don't have to work too hard squeezing a million times. Also, good lighting is important. Triple check that you haven't missed any! For nails that are too thick to snip off, use an angle grinder. That's what I've done and it works great. Greg
