knightfly4 August 2003#2
"What can the panel absorber do, that 6" of 703 can't?" - a - Not over-absorb your mids/highs. Depending on what the rest of your room is like, you may want to ONLY trap bass, in order to balance the reverb characteristic of the room. Only if you could find a material that was 100% absorbent to ALL frequencies of sound would you want to cover your entire room with it - then you'd be listening in a true "free field" - and even THAT would take some getting used to (can you imagine how "wet" your mixes would be at first?), since we're so used to having a certain amount of reverb... Steve
