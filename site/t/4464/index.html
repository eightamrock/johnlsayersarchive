Dan Fitzpatrick4 October 2005#6
john, you definitely need to insulate. it will increase performance SUBSTANTIALLY. however a lot of people would just use ordinary fiberglass as it has similar performance, especially at low frequencies, the performance may even be slightly better. make sure it is unfaced or if it is faced, put the facing next to the leaf surface. even the thin facing can create 3rd leaf effects. unless money is no object, then by all means use 703/5. and i think your idea of using different layers on each side is ok in principle. dan
