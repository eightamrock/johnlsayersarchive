xSpace16 March 2009#40
I think it was covered already, but maybe I can hit some of the high notes. A 2 leaf system is what you want to design for optimum isolation. You have an existing exterior wall made of concrete or filled block, as I recall, so this solid barrier is one leaf. Then you have distance/air. The farther you can get from a sound the less intinsity it will have, so this is in part why you need the air space. The other is the decoupling of framing, etc. Now you have to add one more leaf, or mass covered wall panel, to complete your mass/spring/mass. Anywhere you see that you have what can be termed as mass/air/mass/air/mass or higher, is going to cause your isolation to suffer in the low frequency part of the spectrum. This is most important for those interested in low frequency and cantaining this area. Luckily, it's just a drawing and can be corrected with a minimum of effort :)
