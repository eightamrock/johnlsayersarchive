Hey, Brian Giles - you like this stuff, where'd ya how'd ya why'd ya, etc? Can you clue our man on the stuff? Thanks... Steve BTW, Corley, I would NOT recommend anything that's petroleum based like roll roofing or building felt for limp mass layers in between wallboard - not worth dying for. You can get just as good results using three different layers of different thickness sheet rock - 1/2 and 5/8 with 3/8 between for one leaf, then 2 layers of 5/8 with 1/2 between, filled with SAFB's or fluffy fiberglas insulation, properly caulked and isolated, and you can't build a door that will keep up with it... Steve
