Thank you friends. Here in India we use a considerable quantity of wood and people like it colorful too...it the film glamor world 8) I have used a combination of plywood, MDF, laminates thick cloth at strategic places to obtain an balanced response to the room. I hate the so called dead room...its lifeless. There has to be some amount of reflections in the room and therefore I tend to play less with too much of cloth/sponge etc.
