Basement ceiling question

Started by beautyfish on 21 May 2005. 7 replies. In the Library under Walls, floors and ceilings.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 3755.

beautyfish21 May 2005#1
I'm getting ready to build my studio, and I would like to try to maximize whatever isolation I can get from the floor above. With 7 foot ceilings, I really don't have the room to hang a ceiling, and being a realist who will be working only on my own projects, I can live with less than perfect isolation. We do have hardwood floors upstairs though, and right now, with nothing but air between the joists, I can hear my dog's toenails clicking around up there no problem. My wife will not allow me to do any mods to the floor above, so I am just wondering what is the best way to maximize my situation. I was thinking about doing the following, but please tell me if this is going to do anything or just be a waste of time and materials: First, cut a sandwich of 1/2" sheetrock, 703 or that new heavy vinyl sheeting, and 5/8" sheetrock, and screw in between the joists to the subfloor above. Then, fill the rest of the space between the joists with pink fiberglass, and then screw two layers of sheetrock to the joists for the ceiling. I won't be putting any lighting in the ceiling, so I should be able to keep things pretty tight. Will this be reasonably effective? My 7' height will only be reduced by the two layers of sheetrock and whatever I do with flooring. If this won't be effective, what else can I do? Or, is the situation hopeless? Thanks for the help. Jim
