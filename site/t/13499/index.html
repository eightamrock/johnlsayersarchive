Hello everyone, It's my first post here, but I read and learn on this forum since 2006, and it's time for me to thank you all for sharing your knowledge ! I built my first studio three years ago with little knowledge and a tight budget, and the result wasn't top of the line as you could imagine. I'm moving to a new place in a couple of weeks and I'm going to build a new studio; without the mistakes of the first one I hope. So here we go : Description : The studio will be on the garage of my house at ground level. The dimensions are (L)10m – (W)4m – (H)2,3m. There's plain concrete walls (30cm wide), concrete floor; concrete ceilling. Behind two walls of the studio there's a laundry (with toilets and coffee for the bands), and the sewing workshop of my fiancee. The house is in a residential area. The nearest neighboor is at 10m, there's a little road at 6m with very little circulation, and the nearest main road is 70m away. Goal : To make a professional recording studio with a great control room and a great live room. I record loud bands with heavy drums hitters and tube amplifiers cranked on 11. Budget : Max 20000 euros. I have two plans for now : 1/ To use the space available and adapt the great design John had proposed to lumisuto.
