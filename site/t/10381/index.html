AVare11 April 2008#7
Great idea! In addition to what Glen wrote, the distance guideline is 3 times the wavelength of the lowest frequency diffused. If you want to 3/4 of width usable without lobing, go for a wavelength of 1/4 m, or about 1 kHz. Enjoy! Andre
