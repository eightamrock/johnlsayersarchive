Welcome to the forum, enjoyer of cake. I might suggest you head over to the Construction forum (on this site), and specifically check out the Reference Area thread, which will address your question quite thoroughly. Soundproofing has more to do with carefully engineered permanent construction techniques than simple and portable treatments, as you'll no doubt discover there. If you decide to post your question on the Construction forum, be sure to check out the "Read This Before You Post" post. Be prepared for some disappointment as a result of laws of physics... Soundproofing isn't easy, isn't portable, and is definitely not cheap. "A little bit" is all you'll get -- if that -- with high portability as a requirement.
