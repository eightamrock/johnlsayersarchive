What if you are recording a "once in a lifetime recording" and you pick up a sound from outside? You may not need the same levels of isolation as people in other situations but issolation is always something to consider.

First have a look at some of the designs within that manual, notice how all the rooms are what they call "two leaf". The easiest way to look at it is something with mass, like a thick sheet of plasterboard (sheet rock). These separate leafs are supported by timber frames that a decoupled from each other. these techniques help to stop sound transmission from say your control room and tracking room. Try to incorporate this into you designs. Also the control room ideally needs to be symmetrical to give you an accurate stereo image, its pretty close but have a play with all that space you have and see if you cant improve on you design. hope this helps :)
