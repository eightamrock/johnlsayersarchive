I'm in a band of basement heroes playing once a week and recording things "live" occassionally as we go. We're doing instrumental jazz & blues, with: - drums - bass - a guitarist playing a stereo guitar rig into 2 Princeton Reverbs blended with guitar synth into a small PA - a trumpet player who occasionally sings I'm using a Yamaha AW16G digital machine to record 8 mics at once with the room set up as shown here:
External image, not preserved — original: http://home.cinci.rr.com/jeffkeller/Recording/Band_layout.JPG
