knightfly1 March 2004#5
Thomas answered your question correctly. Your conversion to inches, however, is off a bit - there are 25.4 mm in one inch, so Thomas is recommending that the hole in the flush mount (soffit) be 1/4" larger TOTAL in each dimension than the dimensions of the speaker box. That small a gap dimension won't affect lower frequencies at all, because it is such a small portion of a wavelength... Steve
