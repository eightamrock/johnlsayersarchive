Soundman20205 October 2011#30
The 18 foot walls fade inward 6.5 inches to help fight off any standing waves.
That wont work! :shock: I think you are confusing "standing waves" with "flutter echo". Splaying your walls will indeed help with flutter echo, if the splay is more than 6°, but it will not have any useful effect on standing waves. The room modes will still be there, regardless of whether or not you splay the walls. The only difference is that they will be at different frequencies, and peak at different locations in the room, but splaying your walls does not make them go away.
