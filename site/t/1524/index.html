Sounds like you have great potential. I have no advice that would be better the experts here. However;
We mainly record rock bands so the live room needs to be as large as poss.
this sentence has me confused. Rock bands tend to be recorded relativley dry, so volume is not needed for room sound. What I see usually required, when bands choose to record several instruments at once, AND do overdubs later, is greater separation of the instruments.
