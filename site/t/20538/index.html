Hi there " MILESSOUND", and welcome! :)
The control room is 20'x20'
Square??? :shock: :?: Ummm.... That would be pretty bad... You'd have serious modal problems if there is a direct relationship between the dimensions of your room. You should probably look into room ratio calculations, and names like Louden, Sepmeyer, Bolt and Bonello...
may change to 20'x14' with 11' ceilings.
Hang on a sec... you already building it, but you don't yet have a PLAN? Ummm.... this does not bode well for success. Studio building is 80% design, 20% construction. If you don't design the room completely, down to every last detail, before you start building, there's a very good change it will not work out.
I have them in a pic pipe slightly larger than each cable, that is only touching outer leaf.
The conduit should be MUCH larger, not slightly larger. It needs to be large enough to get the entire cable header through, with all the connectors, with room to spare. If not, how would you ever get the cable through, or take it out again in the future if need be?
Haven't really found too much in the way of actually running a cable in bare walls pre-drywall and how to secure them for a tight cut around and not have them get messed up.

Should I run extra ethernet for headphone mixes and how many?
Definitely! Always run at least a couple of extra cables for everything you have, for two reasons: 1) to replace failed cables. 2) future expansion.
Can I use the Mogami for headphones as well with no interference?
Assuming that it is good quality cable and properly balanced, then there should be no problem with that. It's just a line-level signal gonig to the headphone amp in any case, not the actual headphone signal, and even if it were the actual headphone signal it would likely still be fine.
I am in the process of finishing up anything I need because the drywall guy is coming very soon.
Once again, I'm rather concerned that you are drywalling already, but you don't even have a plan for the the studio size or layout... - Stuart -
