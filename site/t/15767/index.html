Soundman202023 March 2011#9
If you have 12 panels of 2" 703, I would make six devices, all the same, each with 2 layers in it, for a total of 4". Each would just be a wooden frame with the 703 inside it and cloth across the front. I would put one of those in each of the vertical corners of the room, where they would work as bass traps, and one on each side of the room, at your first reflection points. Then, when you can afford more 703, I'd make a few more the same way, hang one or two from the ceiling as clouds at your first reflection points up there, put a couple more on the front wall, right behind your speakers, and a couple on the back wall. That's a pretty much standard way of treating a room. - Stuart -
