gullfo22 May 2011#2
what is going on in the "cabinet" that would drive your neighbors "insane" versus what will be coming out of your speakers? based on your drawing it would be a series of bad choices because your mix environment will suffer terribly. you may be better off isolating the entire room as much as possible and using a screen for the vox mic. this way your vox and mix do not drive anyone crazy and you will have enough space to get some form of acoustical environment set up although it will be very small when it's all done.
