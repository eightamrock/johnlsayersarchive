The other 2 walls are made of standard dry wall material and a standard wood door. What material could I use to put directly on the walls, ceiling, and door inside the room to improve the sound proofing of the room? Any recommendations?
xSpace23 December 2007#2
Anything that has been said or written on this subject of isolation has included the word "dense" or "density". Styrofoam is not dense. It will also burn at a high rate of speed and the smoke!!!! The smoke alone can choke a person to death. If I were in your shoes, I would read for a few days/weeks more until my thinking on what I am doing was based on education and not assumption or old wives tales:)
