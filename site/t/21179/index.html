Any ideas on what to do with the garage door? Is it possible to seal it in somehow?
The normal way of dealing with that is to fix it permanently in place, very solidly, remove the mechanism, then build an isolation wall immediately behind it, to complete the outer-leaf of your studio. Then you build the independent inner-leaf (which is the actual studio) in the normal manner, as a completely separate structure with no mechanical connections to the outer-leaf, with both leaves being sealed 100% airtight. - Stuart -
