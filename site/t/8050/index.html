It's actually the other way around - by building a room within a room main control room you are isolating the control room from the upstairs. the amount of flanking, associated with the upper floor, from room to room will be minimal and I wouldn't think it would be a problem as none of the upper rooms are actual recording rooms, just mixing and editing etc. cheers john
