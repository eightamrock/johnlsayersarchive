how do you calculate the ratio dimensions in order to get a more balanced mode distribution/recording environment?
No worries regarding no answer. I guess I will have to search for the answer in a book or two.
I'm afraid you won't find your answer in any books, as there is no general solution for 'strange looking' rooms. In order to study the ideal mode distribution of your (any) enclosure, you will need to be able to know the space's resonant frequencies. For rectangular enclosures, this is baby math. For non-rectangular enclosures, there is no analytic formula you can use, and you'd need to find a unique numerical solution to the wave equation. If you can refine your question and provide a sketchup drawing of that room, I can calculate for you the spatial pressure distribution for any frequency you'd like and the frequency response at mic position. Best regards, Jon.
