Bass Rocks12 January 2007#12
This thread (from Kendale's post, above) has a picture of what I meant: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=239 You'll want it to fit as tightly as possible to the windows and door -- which might be a problem with the first window shown in your pictures and the door. You might have to "scribe" it to the rock to get a tight seal. You can also use automotive door gasket to help seal it. In making a plug for the door, make sure it doesn't end up being too heavy to move conveniently. Also, make sure you don't have any fires while the plug is in place. :wink: It looks like a cool building. - Andy

Aloha, Most of the treatments prescribed here on the forum have called for either Owens Corning 703 (fiberglass) or Roxul AFB mineral wool (or equivalant) @ 2"-3" thick and a density of 2.5 pcf rather than the average "foam" treatments. Aloha 8)
