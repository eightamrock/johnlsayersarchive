gullfo4 April 2008#3
actually its applicable to design because there are significant differences between a project studio and commercial one. one of which - does your community allow businesses to be run within the residential areas or not? assuming it does, then you are probably required to adhere to a variety of laws and ordinances including business permits, parking, noise, safety, accessibility, hours of operations, and associated with those, a lot of building codes to make it all happen - all of which will tend to be much more rigorous than a simple home studio for yourself and some friends. so a commercial studio will tend to automatically require different design (thus tactics) than just converting your basement or garage for personal use.
