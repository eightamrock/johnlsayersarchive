drfrankencopter5 August 2005#4
I think the big question is how portable are you really expecting it to be? Soundpoof rooms are heavy...it's the mass law! You can probably build something like an inner frame for your RWAR, and put MDF layers on it, which can be unscrewed at a later time. However, without control over how the outter walls are built your soundproofing performance will be limited. Honestly, if you're renting, best bet is just to get to know your neighbours and work out times to be loud. Buy some rockwool, and put it up on the walls to keep the reflections down (it cuts volume a little bit too). Cheers, Kris
