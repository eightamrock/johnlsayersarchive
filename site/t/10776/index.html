jwl27 June 2008#4
In general, you will need much more bass trapping than you will get from that room kit. It includes LENRDs, which help some in the midbass but don't have much effect below 100Hz. To answer your specific questions: 1. I'd put all the diffusors on the rear wall. I wouldn't bother with foam absorption on the front wall. 2. No. Even if that is real acoustic foam (which is debatable), you will still need bass trapping in the booth, even more bass trapping proportionately because it's such a small room. 3. I have no idea how sound proof the room will be, as this will depend on the construction details. Are you building a room-within-a-room, 2-leaf system? Please understand that foam (or any acoustic treatment) does NOTHING for soundproofing, but will rather help improve the sound inside the room (if they are good products installed correctly). 4. See above #1. 5. Yes, I'd use speaker stands. The heavier and more massive the better.
