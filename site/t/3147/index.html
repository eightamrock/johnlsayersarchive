Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 3147.

BrianAltenhofel7 February 2005#1
I don't know much about room design, but I have been tinkering around trying to figure out how to get the best division out of a 24 foot by 32 foot building. I can't seem to figure out anything that feels comfortable. I'd like to have a control room live room, drum room, vocal booth, and a booth for guitar amps and other loud things like that. I'm okay with combining a drum room with the live room. I have a concrete floor/slab (not 100% level, would like to put wood over it) and a 9' ceiling to play with. It'd also be nice if I could have a consulting space at the front (24 foot) so people would also be comfortable talking business. There are two 6 foot high by 8 foot wide windows on either side of the 3 foot door situated in the center of the front 24 foot wall. The current walls (only outer) are 1 foot concrete block. My sound situation is between the county jail and a dry cleaner's, so I have to be able to keep the levels heard outside pretty darn close to nothing. I'm planning on having two 4-tracks, one 24-track, a few racks, an 8 foot couch, and an MX9000 console in the control room. I don't have much money to do this with and this building is basically a freebie from my father (he refinishes bathtubs in a metal building at the back). If anyone can be of any help, I would greatly appreciate it. I'm hoping to be able to track local bands here since the nearest studio outside of a bedroom and a boss multitrack and a couple of Nady mics is over 100 miles away.
