Hi Guys, I've been a member of this forum for a few years now and dropping in now and again to read up and what is going down and being inspired by others studios. For years I've had a tiny little bedroom studio that I've just tried to make the most of but I have the opportunity now to join forces with another studio, which has been running for a few years and build it into something better. I'm looking for any help those of you with more knowledge than I can give into the redesign and rebuild into something great. Basically the current studio is on the 1st floor of a commercial building with tenants above and below and was designed to run as a studio since the early 80's I think. The control room walls are built out of brick, with the exterior walls being brick as well, the thickness is around 18cm. I'd prefer not to knock down the walls on the control room and try and rework it to be a usable space, the speakers can be stand mounted, or soffit mounted. There is already a control room window (2.40m x 0.95m) looking into the current live room mounted into the walls with slot resonators and sloped ceiling, which at it's highest point is 2.93m and lowest 2.51m, and it is raise 0.43m off the ground with an air cap separating the floor below, with a false ceiling. Currently the walls are covered in a layer of old cork and fiber tiles, but these will come off in the redesign and rebuild. Here are a few pics: Control Room with 'Dipped Ceiling'

Yeah, Thanks guys, I didn't feel like bumping it and being rude and I know everyone is busy and this Forum is a labour of love, but any help would be most useful. This studio actually belongs to SUI and as I stated in the first post it is time to bring it all down and start a new. We are hoping to have a control room A, big live room and a second studio B with a small overdub room for vocals guitars etc, much like the one I linked above. I've been doing quite a bit of reading since my first post and have found some en lighting posts esp. on construction. From the sounds of things the best bet is to put in a floating floor with structurally isolated ceiling, to keep the outside sound out and inside sound... well in. The floor seems easy enough based on the SAE posts with us building on top of this, the ceiling seems to be more of a headache. Having read John's SAE thread, the Acoustic Myths PDF floating around and Knightfly's BASIC TERMINOLOGY thread (http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=4310) and CONSTRUCTION THREAD http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=598 I gather that the single leaf double wall with an airgap is the most cost effective way of construction, and tried and tested as one of the BEST if not THE best ways of construction. So I guess we have determined how we need to construct is, now we need to work out how best to use incorporate the existing features into a new design. Any suggestions? Thanks Matt
