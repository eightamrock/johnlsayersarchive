Clear silicon is typical to set glass. You want to make not only a seal but create a bond to keep the glass from being a loose element in the window hole...eliminate the rattle. This is in >typical< construction which is not what you are doing right? That said, you will want to review what Eric Desart has to say so you may get a better understanding of what you gain what you lose and where you may lose and or gain it from :) "If it bounces back = no good (depending on appliction: more or less important). Silicone bounces back as a perfect ball. " LINK: http://forum.studiotips.com/viewtopic.p ... 61&start=9
