xSpace11 September 2008#3
While we wait for an expert.... The 38 percent rule has several purposes, one that I can state pretty good:). It is to give you a starting point to work from. You could use a center of room dimension but this has been proven to be an area of much acoustically negative activity. I cannot recall who it was that had made this study and determined these values but for monitor speaker placement, 38 percent is the start...not the end. It will work the same way for ear height placement but you may find that ear height is good for you and is close to NOT being at 50 percent between floor and ceiling.
