the low ceiling will be the biggest challenge (in the home studio) and depending on what your isolation requirements are, may be even tougher. maybe you can post detailed measurements of the space and what your requirements are - music/work type, isolation needs, environmental and infrastructure noise, construction, etc. as long as the dimensions are accurate and include things like plumbing, ducting, structure, utilities, doors, windows, etc that shape the space, you can provide it via hand sketch (scanned or photo), sketchup (a 2d drawing works), other other tools. on the commercial project, since you'll have investors, i'd recommend you get a pro design to reduce risk.

gullfo4 March 2008#12
my first thought is that 1.9m is way too low. if this is for your own personal use and you sit pretty much all the time, you can probably build yourself a small workstation space. if you're planning on other musicians performing there, you're likely to find it a fairly uncomfortable height. depending on what you need for isolation - you just might not have enough room to be useful. you could use it for an amp room or potentially a reverb chamber but i'd suggest finding something with more height. if not, then there are plenty of examples posted here for room treatments which could tame the space.
