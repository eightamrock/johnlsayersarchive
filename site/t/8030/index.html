n8nod9it25 January 2007#7
I think this is a workable idea, and one i was planning on doing in my own non-floated floor slab concrete studio. Those gym mats are super dense, almost 90lbs per 4x6 mat and are great at noise isolation. A layer of plywood and then hardwood flooring should be enough to drop TL down quite significantly but on the second floor of any building there is going to be a more difficult job of keeping the vibrations down going through the flanking paths of the walls etc. A drum riser would be really helpful to take care of the worst offending beats.
