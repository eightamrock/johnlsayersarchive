Hi Guys, Great forum and info!! :D im now taking the big step to build my studio and would love some advice for this noob. Thank you very much! :) Location: Queretaro City, Qro. Mexico Goals: Having a professional looking and sounding recording studio (control room + studio) able to record music and media productions in a comfortable way. Overview: I’ve been following this forum for quite some time and I love it :D, really good and interesting information. I decided it was the right time to start my own studio (I’m a music student and apple technician) I have bought several books about the subject: Acoustic Design for the Home Studio, Building a Recording Studio by Cooper and Rod’s one. Have read them all :D Now I’m in the design phase of the process. I’ve been having trouble making a layout that actually convinces me 100%. Room details: Attached is the dimension plan of the room I seek to convert into my studio :). It is mainly a 3380mm by 9300mm with 2620mm height, and a smaller part with aprox. 1270mm by 3000mm with 2530mm height. There are two big windows and a smaller one, all running up into the ceiling. All the walls and ceiling are made of concrete bricks with the exterior side having cement to do the flattening and in the inside plaster. All the floor is concrete covered with tile but i think ill covered it with laminate later in the process. I’m guessing I really don’t need to float the floor, down stairs is a storage room. About the walls I don’t know if its convenient to double them in the control room for the sake of room size :S. (I recently bought a custom desk 2.2m by 1.1m for my console :D) But I would appreciate as much isolation as possible. As I said before I just need a control room and 1 studio. The loud factor: I have measured with C weighting just next to the neighbor's wall 60db while playing drums real hard. This measurement was made when the room had a third large window in the middle, all windows have float glass. I’m planning to follow strict work hours for the real loud recordings and mix at a fair 80db average. At the time I don’t have the SPL meter to do the measurement in A weighting :(. Budget: I’m about to get a loan for approximately $15,000 us. Questions: I’ve been trying to define a layout without luck so far, I need a control room able to fit my 2.2m x 1.1m desk. Any advice or suggestions? I’ll keep working on ideas for the layout, so far I had one where the control room was in the far back of the big room with a 4.94 length trying to follow M.M. Louden’s 1:1.3:1.9 ratio aprox. by having a 3380m by 2620m by 4940mm room. But you would have to enter the studio to get to the control room :S Thanks for your help again :D

"But you would have to enter the studio to get to the control room" I would rather do that then go into the control room to get to the tracking room. Have you considered converting one of the window areas into a door? One thing I have seen folks do here is to place a glass door in front of and centered on the control room monitoring position. It not only gives you a visual connection to the tracking room but gives you access to the control room after the tracking room. Just a few thoughts. Good luck, Brien

jbassino5 August 2009#19
Any kind of insulation works for damping or acoustic treatment. If you only find 2" thick, well then you'll need more panels to put to fill the cavities. As long as is dense enough (40kg/m3) you'll be safe. Denser fiberglass does actually improve its ratings but not worth the extra cash in my opinion
