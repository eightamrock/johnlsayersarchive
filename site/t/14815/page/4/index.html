gullfo17 November 2010#77
there is a slot resonator calculator on the design forum which is quite good... chances are though, if you span the corners with 3/4" thick MDF or plywood, 3' wide slat panel, using 1/4" opening for slots, 2'6" wide, and spaced between 2" and 6" apart (see some other topic areas and John's designs for patterns) you should be in good shape. optionally, 1/8" plywood 3' x 7' poly (i.e. bent about 7.5" deep) over the super chunk (leave top and bottom open) you will get diffusion and absorption. no plywood behind the absorbers.
