I have a bit over 8' of space. I may try a small diffuser out.
If you like the sound of diffusion, but you don't have enough room, I have always thought it might be interesting to try a couple of diffusors in a 'V' pointing out from the back wall (V as viewed from above), and then some additional diffusion along the side walls for the back third or half of the room. Some of that energy is going to make it back to you at some point, but it'll be a) highly diffused and b) fairly late, both of which are good things.
