If you check out the designs around here, you'll see they're generally symmetrical for the control room - also, speakers should be aimed at a point just behind your head, which point should form an equilateral triangle with the two speakers. Unless that hallway beside the CR goes somewhere, it seems like a super waste of space as well as an acoustic problem for the live room. Plane symmetry is used in CR's with good reason - you want both halves of your listening triangle to be equal, so you don't make panning/EQ decisions based on your room lying to you... Steve

knightfly12 June 2004#5
Kase caught the CR symmetry problem - the hallway, if left open, will act as a long resonant tube - without dimensions (all three axes, including height)I can't begin to guess what frequency it would resonate at. The fix, if the hallway isn't too narrow, would be to put absorbent on walls and ceiling and a door across the entrance to the hallway where the CR reaches its widest point - If you DONT put a good bottom seal on the door, the whole hallway will act as a deep bass trap to help smooth out the live room. This is why you'd need the absorbent on walls - the thicker the better, but anything is better than nothing. Also, the door across the hallway might be usable as a "tunable" trap - you would do this by "micro-adjusting" how much you leave the door ajar, then make spacers to hold the door at the best opening for trapping (these will be very small openings, increments as small as 1/8" or even smaller will change the result. Just a thought... Steve

dymaxian14 June 2004#7
I don't really have enough room in the CR to make the other wall the same shape, do you think it's gona be majour problem ?
Yeah, that's going to mess with your stereo imaging in 2 ways... The angled wall on the right of the mixing position is going to reflect sound back at you differently than the 90 degree corner on the left, but the bigger problem is this: your listening position and speakers are quite a bit closer to the left wall than they are to the right wall (once you're past that angled wall). If you can't square off that right corner (easiest fix) you really should build an identical angled wall on the left side and just pull your speakers and listening position back from the wall far enough for them to fit.
