barefoot23 September 2003#2
MDF is an excellent material for these purposes. It's dense, easy to cut, easy to finish, and dimensionally stable. Just don't use it for weight bearing applications. It tends to sag over time. My only complaint with MDF is that it contains formaldehyde, a known carcinogen. So, cut it in a well ventilated area and use a good dust mask. It also outgases small amounts of formaldehyde over time, but this is unfortunately true for many engineered building materials. I use formaldehyde-free MDF in my loudspeakers. Thomas
