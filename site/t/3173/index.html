laptoppop16 February 2005#5
Offhand, this looks like about 45'x60' -- darn big. Because they are around the edge, those hallways are taking up almost 2000 square feet, if I'm calculating correctly. I understand the desire to have paths and loadin/loadout and hall hockey -- but that's a lot of square feet/money for that kind of space. I strongly suspect you can use it better. On the other hand, a hall like this, with solid walls on both sides gives great sound isolation. People don't usually spend so much space on that, but I believe it mitigates the multiple leaf problem allowing two sets of walls. (am i wrong?) I also wonder about the two amp iso booths. If you made those a bit bigger, you could use them for overdub vocals too - a lot more flexibility. A 6'x7' room can do fine with a single vocalist or a guitar cab - flexibility is king. Having a "work area" is very cool. You'll also want to plan more storage. Microphones, mic stands, cables, headphones, instrument stands, etc. take up a ton of room. Is there another area where folks that aren't actively recording can relax? It can really help a session flow better if they don't have to be in the recording area or the control room. Its a "cheaper" area, because you don't have to do the same kind of sound treatment, but its an important addition. Also, for a facility like this, you may want to add a closet next to the control room for your computers and other noisy equipment. -lee-
