One minisplit AC with two vents?

Started by Sunshy on 19 April 2010. 3 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 14291.

Sunshy19 April 2010#1
Hello, I'm building a small garage studio build out in Los Angeles and am trying to save money on the HVAC portion of my build. Has anyone ever heard of one minisplit air conditioner with two wall vents? My thinking was to mount one wall vent on the control room side and the other on the studio side (on opposite sides of the wall so as to not have one big hole between the control room/studio) and close the studio vent when I'm only using the control room. Is this possible or even advisable? Also, the garage has a gas line. Could this be used for heating to save on the electric bill?

There is an announcement at the top of the forum about what to do to assure getting as many responses as possible. The announcement leads to this post (click here). Actually, several people, who are experts on this forum, will most likely not reply if you don't do what is written in that post. Two problems: humidity, and CO2. The studio has to be sealed air-tight in order to isolate properly. - Stuart -
