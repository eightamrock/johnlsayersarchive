AVare19 November 2004#14
Is there rhyme or reason with the odd angles?
If you d/l the image and measure the "odd angles" you will see that they are multiples of 12 degrees off right angle surfaces. This is to prevent repeated slap echoes off the otherwise parallel surfaces.
