AK-6921 May 2005#2
It is more logical to have your monitors facing the short wall. This is the case because you will want to have the back wall as far away as possible. Couldn't you permanently close one of the doors and put your desk there? That seems logical to me. Good luck with your studio! I would treat as much wall and ceiling surface as possible with broadband absorbers, with a room this small.
