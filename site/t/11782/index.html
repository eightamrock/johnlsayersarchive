Soundman202010 March 2009#2
Symmetry is most definitely something that you need in your control room. Without good symmetry, you wont' have a decent stereo image, and your mix will most likely not translate well. So in my non-expert opinion, I'd say that yes, you really do need to fix that. But the other issue is that your room gets narrower towards the rear, and starts doing so before it even gets to the first reflection point! Is there any way you can correct that? This would involve removing the angled wall at the left rear, and NOT building the angled wall at the right rear: just keeping them straight from that point back. That would make them parallel, unfortunately, but that can be treated. The way I see it, having them parallel is much less of a problem than angled back into the room. Many people successfully build their entire CR's with parallel walls, but I don't think I've seen a successful design where the room starts narrowing towards the rear, before it even gets to first reflection points! That's just my opinion, so take it for what it's worth: I'm not an expert, just a dedicated sponge, soaking up the subject of acoustics! - Stuart -
