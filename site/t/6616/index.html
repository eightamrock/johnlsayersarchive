sharward16 July 2006#5
There is no such thing as a "completely soundproof" booth... At least, not by the strict definition of the word "completely." No "soundproofing" is perfect. No matter the construction, it is always possible to generate noise in the "soundproofed" area that will leak out. You need to explain what it is that you intend to use the booth for (i.e., how loud it will be in there, and what the nature of the noise is) and what level of noise will be acceptable outside of the booth. The difference between those levels dictate the amount of soundproofing you will need. --Keith :mrgreen:
