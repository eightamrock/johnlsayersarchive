xSpace28 July 2010#5
And to add insult to injury, the span of any horizontal framing member is produced from the measurement that you get from outside of framed wall to the outside of the opposite side framed wall. Even if you move the deflection down to 240, which would be more accurate, and move the joist spacing to 12 inches on center which increases the dead load, you can see that you really need to find a way to get 2X6 framing over your head.
