I've searched for a couple of hours for a similar situation prior to posting but couldn't find anything. Also, I'm on dial-up so downloading sketchup would take forever. I want to build a one and a half story 28 x 28 detached garage / studio from scratch. Cost is a big factor so my friends and I will complete all of the labor. Downstaris: I need parking for 1 full size truck (probably 14x24 would work with vehicle entrance on the east side), a control room with double sliding glass doors to a live room for drums and acoustic instruments, and a small bathroom with a stand-up shower. 10 foot ceilings on the bottom floor. Studio entrance would be on the west side. Upstairs: 4 iso booths to record vocals and guitar amps. Stairs to this location would be from the control room. I'll pour a concrete slab to build on. Vinyl siding exterior, 2x6 walls, shingle roof. A sketch with placement ideas would be awesome. This is my first post, so please be gentle if I've screwed it up. Many thanks.
