Hi jrweeks, and Welcome! Interesting project! But a couple of comments:
Floated floor.
Are you sure you need that? That's a LOT of money you are talking about to do it right. Here is some interesting info you might want to look at, on how to do that correctly: viewtopic.php?f=2&t=8173 http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/obj/irc/doc/p ... /ir802.pdf
Staggered wall. Resilient channel.
You don't really need both. Either of those will decouple your wall to about the same extent. Once it is decoupled, then it is decoupled! Doing so again is just a waste of money, space and time. Either staggered studs or RC. You CAN do both if you want, but it really won't buy you much extra.
Roxul Safe and Sound.
That will work, but look around at your local store and see what else they have: you don't need exotic name-brand "acoustic" products: what you need is products of the right density and gas-flow characteristics, at the lowest cost. I promise you that the sound waves do not care how much you paid for the materials! All the see is the physical characteristics, and they totally ignore the price tag. So if you can find something similar for less money, then that might be a better option. Looking at your SKP file: So it's just a booth you are doing? Not the entire room? What is your design goal for isolation? (In terms of decibels). How do you plan to build that (materials, dimensions, air gap, etc).? - Stuart -
