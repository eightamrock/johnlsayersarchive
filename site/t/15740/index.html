gullfo13 March 2011#2
maybe its two things: a ducted air exchange to get air in and out and the split system. there is another product which provides some limited fresh air as part of the solution but you still need a room return for it to function within a sealed room. there are also concealed duct systems which have the compactness of the split units (the compressor is outside and the fan and evaporator are contained in a small unit). however, getting the ducting into your studio after you have built it means you will be ripping down parts of your expensive studio to retro-fit something that should have been one of the first things planned...
