Ethan Winer5 March 2009#2
The main difference is that bass traps need to be thicker to absorb low frequencies. Also, bass traps made from rigid fiberglass benefit from some sort of "membrane" material to increase LF absorption and intentionally reduce absorption at higher frequencies. There are also tuned bass traps which target specific frequencies, but those are more suited for large rooms than for small home studios. This article shows, among other tings, the improvement from having a paper membrane facing on rigid fiberglass: Density Report --Ethan
