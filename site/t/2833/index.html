Builder, sorry this managed to "slip through the cracks" - hope I'm not too late for you - Rod Gervais is the guy I ask when I'm not sure on something, and he's been good enough to chime in on this, here http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=20665 As to a light, if you have pretty good isolation and don't want to goof it up I'd stay with a surface mount light fixture - the fewer holes (caulked, of course) the better. (Needless to say, I would NOT recommend using a candle for this, especially if you have foam inside :? ) Steve
