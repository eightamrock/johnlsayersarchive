Hi! Here's the story. I have a modest home studio set-up in my basement that I use from time to time to do small projects. I will be recording a semi-acoustic Celtic band in a few weeks. Ideally I would like them to set up in one room for a more spontaneous and live performance. However, what I need is something to minimize mic leakage between the musicians, a barrier if you will, but not necessarily total sound isolation. For example, would 4'X6' panels of 1/2" sheet rock or OSB, mounted on some type of stand (picture a lollipop), accomplish what I need? Once again, I'm not looking for total isolation or absorption. I'm familiar with Tim O'Brien's DIY Soundbooth (http://www.palmcitystudios.com/timob...oundbooth.html) and was wondering whether something like this would another viable option? Another idea I had was to make simple 5'X4'x2 frames with plywood or particle board on one side and foam/carpet on the other. I am aware that there are great products (like Ethan Weiner's mini-traps, the Clearsonic acrylic panels etc) just for such purposes, but unfortunately, my budget doesn't support such an outlay of cash. Any help is greatly appreciated. Thanks! Matt
