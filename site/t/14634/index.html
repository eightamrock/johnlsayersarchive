Soundman20203 August 2010#6
So is a room 8x9x7 (feet) a total lost cause?
No, not a total lost case. If that's all you have, then that's all you have. You just have to realize that it won't be optimal, and that it will have modal issues (or rather, lack of modes) in the low end: it cannot support any modes at all below about 60 Hz, and they are very patchy and uneven up to nearly 200 Hz. As long as you are aware of those limitations, and can live with them, then you can build a room with those dimensions. Look at it this way: if its that or nothing, then it sure is a hell of a lot better than "nothing"! :) - Stuart -
