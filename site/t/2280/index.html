knightfly8 September 2004#5
If you want your panels stood off the wall for better LF performance, but still want the "frameless" look, you can glue blocks to the wall, glue the impaling clips to the blocks, wrap the panels in your cloth (hot melt glue guns work well, wrap to the rear and glue down the cloth on the back side) - Then, when you impale the absorber the blocks will stop it at a uniform distance from the wall. If you first paint the blocks to match the wall, they will almost disappear, even when looking behind the absorber... Steve
