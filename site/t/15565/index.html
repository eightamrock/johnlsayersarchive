I was thinking about Glenn's proposal but I guess having a smaller room will require more acoustical treatment and it will increase the costs
Either way, with or without that wall, it is still a small room and will still need a lot of treatment, so that isn't really a factor in deciding. I agree with Glenn: Have your desk facing the top in position "B", and maybe extend the wall if you want to. That would be good. But you could also use position "A", if you want. That will also work. If you do that, you could build an "L" shaped wall at the back of the room (where "position B" is), to give yourself a small live room or vocal booth, with some storage space in the thin section (1.185 m) at the top. gullfo1 March 2011#9
yes, just extend a wall between A and B just enough to accommodate the angled side treatments for the CR and frame things so if you decide to enclose you can make that happen fairly easily without a lot of restructuring, then the remaining space under the beam is open. later if you decide you want to enclose the CR, you can simply extend the wall the rest of the way and adjust the treatments.
