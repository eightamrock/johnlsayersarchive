billshoup25 November 2008#5
Hey Kirkbross, A couple of ideas for you - My room is not yet complete, but I bought a Krown Phone Flasher, which is a bright strobe light designed to flash when the phone rings. I wanted it so I could tell when a phone call was coming in, but to be silent in case I was in the middle of recording a take. For my Recording sign, I bought one of those wireless lamp switches, so I wouldn't have to worry about hard wiring the sign. If you can simply find a beacon that plugs into the wall, one of these switches would complete the package. The switches cost about $12 on ebay shipped - search for HandySwitch. Also I searched for an inexpensive Recording sign, and finally bought a fluorescent sign from the ebay user "mfshop2008" for something like $30 shipped. It shipped from overseas, so it took a while to get, but I'm very happy with it, and with the wireless switch I can put it where ever I want. Hope that helps!
