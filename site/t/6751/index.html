Hi, I had started a topic here however I am not going to invest a lot of money at this time. I do however have a lot of old barn boards. They range in width from 6 to 12" and are of varying thickness too. I'm wondering if placing these on the walls of my 11x12' room will help the sound and if so, is there a best way to place them. -- I also cannot find 703 or rockwool anywhere in my area- are there any other options? -- One more. I've read alot about the m-a-m design for walls. I have normal walls in that room with chip board plywood on the inside. Should I remove that inner plywood (exposing the framing) and then build an inner walls (2nd leaf) and without 703 can I hope to get any isolation with regular pink insulation? Thanks Scott.
johnz2 August 2006#2
you don't need special insulation to dampen resonance in between the leaves. just filling up the cavity with slightly compressed pink stuff will work as well. the 703's and rockwool's better sound attenuation really only comes into play to treat the room after isolation has been taken care of

I recorded drums in there and the ringing is crazy. I have a lot to do with the room but I will start with the floor. I'm sure this is a common problem. Thanks Scott.
