bukarin2 December 2015#19
Yes its a beam and block roof with a screed top so very massive but not as dense as the walls. The walls are built from 215mm wide dense concrete blocks (2000KG/m3) which each weigh around 26KG. The roof is made from Milbank beams infilled with 100mm medium density blocks (1500KG/m3 approx) topped with a concrete screed laid to falls with a minimum thickness of 50-60mm and max of around 130mm. I planned for the roof to be more substantial but when the builders got to the roof there were problems with getting the correct design in time (an oversight/miscommunication from the structural engineer). Ideally Milbank design the roof and specify the correct layout but this process takes about a month, I wasn't aware of this and neither was the builder so the structural engineer would only sign off on a less heavy roof as Milbank don't actually publish full details of their products. The design of the roof uses the close spacing of the beams and lighter blocks but my feeling is that the roof will be fine as its still very substantial! I may infill between the beams with plasterboard as there are small gaps between the blocks. My current idea is to finish my exterior super door and then test the TL rating of the shell with no additional penetrations. Anyone care to suggest a TL figure I should expect/hope for with this configuration for the shell only? The glass is 25mm laminated, I'm building the door using a 54mm FD60 blank with a hardwood frame, Norseal drop seal and perimiter seals in a bank vault arrangement. At present I haven't decided on what I'm going to use for the additional mass on the door yet. My budget is already over so I'm trying to shave off wherever I can and lead isn't cheap!!!

Soundman20205 December 2015#22
I would suggest that you paint the entire interior surface of the walls and ceiling with a good quality masonry sealant. All of that looks to be very porous, which is not good for acoustic isolation. Another option (even better, if you can afford it) would be to stucco all of those surfaces (plaster, mortar). That would both seal and and also add mass. It's looking good, though! Major advance to have the shell completed!!! :yahoo: :thu: - Stuart -
