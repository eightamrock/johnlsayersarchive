The IRC seems to love changing their website regularly, and hides the research documents deeper and deeper, in ever-more obscure ways. So if this link stops working yet again the next time they "improve" their website, (fifth time this decade) you will need to search using the title "Gypsum Board Walls: Transmission Loss Data".
