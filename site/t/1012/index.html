Just a quick question: Is there a way to build a sliding doors? Has anyone of you tried that?
Michael Jones24 November 2003#2
As simple as a door looks, it is actually a fairly complex little mechanism. If its not PERFECTLY square, and plumb, it won't function properly. Unless you have access to a joinery shop and considerable joinery skills, I'd hesitate trying to build even a solid wooden door. Add to that glass, runners on top and bottom, and a track for it to all run on, and I think you're out of the realm of ANY DIY'er. Now, what you CAN do is buy a solid core "blank", a pre-made pocket door frame, tracks and runners, and put it all together, but that's a solid core pocket door; not sure if thats what you're after.
