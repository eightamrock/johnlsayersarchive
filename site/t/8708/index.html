http://www.etfacoustic.com/demoroom.all.html Read carefully the paragraphs just below Fig. A - this will make it much easier to place your sub correctly. As to using the same sub with 2 different sets of speakers, this will usually work - but NOT without recalibrating the system, since different mains will have differing response near the crossover frequency between mains and sub - also, differing sensitivity (that part of speaker specs that reads something like, "97 dB, 1 watt, 1 meter") will mean that the LEVEL of the sub would most likely need to change as well as crossover frequency. HTH... Steve
