gullfo15 February 2008#5
for the parallel walls, you build out slats (or your preferred treatments) on angles to break down the corner angles and remove the parallel surfaces. you can use a similar tactic on the ceilings. otherwise, if you build angled walls, and find you need bass trapping etc. you'll have to add them anyways, so in larger rooms, building boundary walls as parallel is usually simpler and leaves room for treatments.
