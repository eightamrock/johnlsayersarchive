Hi All, I have a large office building which I am contemplating converting a portion of to a recording studio. rod gervais26 May 2008#3
Sorry, I can't agree with Ro,,,,,,,, It's a bad plan........ even forgetting the fact that it will add 33+ pounds per square foot of load to te deck (odd are the deck will never carry that much weight). Aside from that - sand itself is not a stable material - just laying some plywood on top of it and the placing finished flooring on that will not stop it from shifting over time. So you'll wind up with some load on it - and then any small vibration will cause the sand to shift ever so slightly (at first) which will lower the floor there - and raise it elsewhere - and eventually you'll have to rip the entire thing out just to level the sand all over again. You said you "have" the building - and are obviously a business person - not some fluffy headed kid with visions of grandure........ so hire a professional acoustician in your area and pay him to design a solution that will fit your needs. Sincerely, Rod
