I wouldn't count on it being "just fine". If the manufacturer does not provide the same kind of test results from independent acoustic laboratories that Green Glue Company provides, then the product is extremely suspect. Why would they NOT get their product tested, or NOT provide the results if they did get it tested???? So unless there is real data on this "local substitute", then it is a safe bet that it does NOT do what GG does. It isn't just about "elasticity" in any event... :) So if I were you, I'd save my money and not use anything rather than run the risk of wasting money on a product that does not work, or that hardens after a while, or that contains toxic chemicals, or that is a fire hazard, or that suffers from any number of other possible defects that you might never know about until it is too late.... But that's just my opinion. - Stuart -
