Soundman20206 May 2010#13
I was trying to copy the design of the CR room under studio plans...are my angles to sharp?
Your angles are a function of the entire room: Start by correctly positioning your mix position (your head) in the room (roughly 38%), then set your soffits so that the front panels as are large as you can make them (within reason!), preferably at least 48 inches, with the speakers centered in the soffits side to side, and the acoustic axes at 1.2m above floor height. Now adjust the speaker and soffit angles until the acoustic axes of the speakers intersect at a point about 20 cm or so behind your head, such that the axes just graze your ears. The intersection angle should ideally be 60 degrees, but it can go as high as 90 degrees, depending on room geometry. That's the basic idea to get you started. That's what Javier was talking about. - Stuart -
