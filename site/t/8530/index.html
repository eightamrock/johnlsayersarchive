Again - using the 10" instead of the 8" will gain you a couple of decibels - and leaving the free air instead of fillling it dead full with insulation will not penalize you anything that matters......... SO use the 10 unless you pay some huge penalty - and let it be flushwith the bottom (top or bottom is exactly the same - the bottom is just easaier) and enjoy......... You real big isolation is in the mass f the assembly - adding something with almost no mass value adds little - if (for example) yu were given R19 (6") and had a double 2x4 wall with a 1" air space - I'd say use it and don't worry - BUT - it would make little sense to throw away good money buying it instead of the other. I have had people go with r13 and r19 to fill the bay complete just because they could not work out suspending the material in place such that they felt comfortable that it wouldn't sag in time - and that makes sense - but not doing it because you gain so much in iso value.
