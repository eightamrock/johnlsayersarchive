I am maxed out on the housing budget so I doubt it will be possible. I am planning on doing a lot of the studio construction myself to help with the budget. Vocal booth / foley area (dead room) I will be uploading some of my thought soon for comment, Thanks again for any suggestions Greg Kraus

Do you forsee the studio and theatre being used simultaneuosly? If so, the sound isolation will be expensive. Have you considered combining the theatre and live room into one space? This would also give you a live room with significantly better acoustics. Andre
