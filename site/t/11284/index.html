Ro10 October 2008#2
Hai esoul, There's some excellent docs about just that on Ethan's site! (forgot the URL, but it's posted numerous times on this very forum) Basically you need absorption. Slot resonators (tuned) are used to address specific frequencies. Diffusers are more used in larger rooms. "standards": Corner(bass)traps, cloud and wall panel for RFZ, aborption panels on walls, bigger aborption at the opposite wall where the speakers stand. etc. You can't really speak of "standards" since no room is the same, but there are some common treatments to start with indeed !
