gullfo11 November 2007#28
the polycone looks interesting... not sure what it will do... the polyQRD was something i was goofing around with and i have no idea if it would be effective or not. it you feel like building one and testing it... :) the bass trap should have a layer of 4" 3pcf rigid insulation behind the cloth, the closet should be lined with 4" 3pcf rigid insulation about 4"-8" off the walls, ceiling, and floor, and if you have space, a line of hangers consisting of 1/2" or 3/4" Homasote or other soft fiberboard wrapped with soft insulation. you can use chain or low stretch rope. if the hangers are about 6' long you should be able to get pretty decent level of trapping.

gullfo12 November 2007#30
thanks... i've been working CAD since around 1980... as far as the room, its like football - stick with the fundementals - get good bass trapping and make sure the reflections off the back walls are reduce enough (but not dead) so you get proper imaging and so on. start with the super chunks in the corners and use some of that space for storage, put up a cloth cover with some widely spaced slats and diffuser of your choice - polycone or small skylines. see how it works for you. if you find you need more mass trapping, maybe you'll need some panel traps as well once you measure the room and see where the problem frequencies are...
