John Sayers2 October 2003#3
Bryan is right Tenkas - your way will create what we call a triple leaf (i.e. layer, airgap, layer, airgap, layer) whereas what you want is a double leaf - layer airgap layer. - so you can go layer - airgap - layer layer. In your case the first layer is the upper floor - then RC - layer, layer. BYW - the wood is called "floor joists". ;) cheers john
