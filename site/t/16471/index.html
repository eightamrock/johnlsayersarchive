Soundman202022 July 2011#5
To be honest: None of the above! Some of them are not even air conditioners: they are evaporative coolers :!: :shock: Stay away from those. The rest actually are air conditioners, but none of them is much use in a studio since the are all self-contained units that vent to the outside. That implies two things: 1) Noise. Lots of it. Those things have motors, fans, and compressors. You don't want all of that inside the studio, of you plan to do any serious work in there. 2) A big hole in the wall for the hot exhaust air. I would seriously consider getting a small split system. Where I live they are cheap: you can get them for a few hundred dollars, and installation costs another couple of hundred. I put two of those in my house a couple of years back (one in the master bedroom, one in the home office), for well under a thousand dollars total, all included. Mine are 9,000 btu, but you can't guess: do the calculations to figure out how much cooling you actually need for YOUR room. - Stuart -

It works but takes 3 hours to show results. I unscrewed the mini stove's metal vent tubing and then put the air conditioner's hose inside of the tubing.
