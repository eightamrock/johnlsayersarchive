Soundman202011 September 2009#2
Mass is mass. Sound waves don't really care how you put it there, or how much you paid for it. All the "see" is the mass. But what is important, is consistency. If you have a wall that is all 1,500 kg/m3 everywhere (for example), except in one place where it is only 300 kg/m3, that your isolation for the entire will be much closer to what the 300 kg part gives you, than to what the rest gives you. So if you already have mass in most of your wall, except for one part, then you need to get roughty the same mass in that part too, or else it will be the weak link. And please read the stickies and use the "search" feature. You'll find tons of information on mass and MSM there.
