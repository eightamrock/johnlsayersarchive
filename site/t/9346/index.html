My only other points are that: I do speaker reviews occasionally and would prefer to not use sofit mounted speakers as I need to switch out and line up monitors for test purposes, plus I am looking at barefoot micro mains which are side ported for bass. I have a large keyboard rig and percussion collection which needs to be housed in the control room (theoretically) as well as clients and my recording gear (which right now is just an 8 channel control surface). Any help and layout would be appreciated. I cannot figure out sketch up yet sorry. I may be able to knock down a wall and add 3 to 4 more feet to the 20" length, but that would be way more costly. gullfo2 September 2007#5
you might build an inner ceiling to get the isolation you need, and then a false ceiling over the rooms to put in hanging traps. this way the rooms feel proportional and have all the right angles, plus you get to use the overhead space for trapping and that may save some space on the floor. search around this site for numerous pros and cons of floating floors - you might only need it in an acoustic drum booth. it really depends on how much isolation you really need though... here's what i came up with - Kendale's design is more pragmatic and may be more effective.
