DanDan31 December 2020#2
Master Handbook of Acoustic by Alton Everest Build it like the Pros by Rod Gervais https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/topics/noise/app.html The Ratio recommendations are almost a myth. Sound travels slower in fibre, so the dimensions of the space will acoustically change with treatment. Plus, many of those ratios were derived from large spaces, concert halls, and they do not scale.
