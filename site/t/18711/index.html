Welcome to HVAC design! :) That's the way it goes, always...
I don't have the room to put silencers for these 3 Air supply and conditioning holes inside so they will have to go on the outer structure I guess.
For a small room, you might be able to fit them in between the inner and outer leaf of your wall... That only works if the total volume of the room is low enough to allow the silencer to be thin while still having a large enough cross section. But it is worth considering.
In your or anyone else on the forums opinion does the positioning of the in and out air vents in a very small one room studio also matter? For example can both in and our air vents be situated just below the ceiling or would it be better if the air in was near the ceiling and air out nearer the floor?
I prefer the other way: outlet near or in the ceiling. Hot air rises, so that's naturally where the stale, used, warm air will be: up near the ceiling. The inlet can also be up near the ceiling if that is all that will work, and I often do it that way, but HVAC contractors sometimes recommend that the inlet should be closer to the floor. Either way is fine. But do try to keep the inlet and outlet on opposite sides of the room, not on the same wall or adjacent walls if possible, to ensure that you get a good flow of air across the entire room. - Stuart -
