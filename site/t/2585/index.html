beautyfish2 November 2004#1
This may be a little different for you guys, but here it is: I have a detached, cinder block garage which is 24'6" square, with the perimeter walls being 7'6" high, and the roof peaking at 14'. I do almost all my work in the control room, so I'd like some help with a layout that accomplishes the following: 1. Incorporates a fairly small iso (maybe 6' x 8') area for recording. Big enough for vocals, a guitar amp, an acoustic guitarist. Anything that needs more space, I will cut in the control room with headphones or do somewhere else. 2. A small machine room adjacent to the control room, where I can put my CPU, drives, etc. where they will be quiet yet accessable. 3. The LARGEST control room possible taking 1 and 2 into account. I'd really like a comfortable, living room type of vibe where I can do composing and mixing (probably 80% of my work). My thought was to somehow make the iso booth and the machine room work as mirror image spaces on either side of the control room, allowing for non splayed walls, etc. I've searched this site and the SAE site, and haven't seen a design for what is basically a one room facility of this size. Any help would be appreciated. Thanks, Jim
