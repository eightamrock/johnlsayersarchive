Here is the situation, with a little background. This year, my mother developed cancer and passed away. I still have not gotten over this loss, and it will be a very long time until i do. One amazing thing about my mother was she always supported my desire to make a living with music, and for the most part it was that support that allowed me to do so for several years as a musician. After years of experience in other facilities and my own (small!) home studio, i'm ready to build my own recording facility. My mother wanted to make sure my sister and i were "taken care of", and consequently i am buying a house half a mile from my current home on the east side of Indy. This house has a 2 car garage at the rear of the property, and i would like to convert this into a recording studio. At this moment i am still in the planning process. here are 2 pics of the garage in its current condition:

sharward13 December 2005#2
Welcome to the forum, AdamJay! First of all, I'd like to offer my condolences for your mom's passing. Very nice initial post... And you seem to have a nice clean (albeit small) canvas from which to work. I'm sure you'll get some good design ideas here -- perhaps you can locate some layouts around here with similar dimensions that you can modify to meet your needs... Often folks post a layout and then get some advice on how to improve it. I'm not a design expert around here... But I am really good at worrying. ;-) So I'd be remiss in my worrywort duties if I didn't point you to the "Permits, Codes, Licenses -- and WHY YOU SHOULD CARE!" thread. You'll want to check in with your local building/planning department to make sure there's no problem with your intent to build a studio in your neighborhood. Check out sandman's thread and how he has a few hoops to jump through, but nothing too major. Still, it's best that you feel out the legalities first before you get too far along in your design process, and certainly before you spend your money. 8) In closing, I'll just mention that I spent a few days in downtown Indianapolis back in 2000 on business -- cool town! :D Best of luck to you in your project... I'm looking forward to seeing it come along. :) --Keith :mrgreen:
