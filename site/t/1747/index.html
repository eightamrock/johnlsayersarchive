knightfly4 June 2004#2
To paraphrase your slogan, "The walls are, like, living in flutter echo" - is all that double line stuff really glass? And, is the ceiling also parallel to the floor? Can you run away and join the circus? (Sorry about that last one, JK) Seriously, corners between ceiling and walls also work for trapping; it's a good thing, because it looks like NONE of your wall corners is usable for corner traps. One possible way to help some might be to build/buy some gobo panels and place them in a kind of sawtooth pattern around the room to break up flutter - without them, that room must sound like a "rain barrel from hell" - Also, what's on your floor? If it's carpet, that should go. If your ceiling height will allow it, most of the ceiling should be absorbed by hanging clouds, again preferably in a "sawtooth" pattern (not parallel to the ceiling) Hope that helped some... Steve
