dymaxian29 April 2004#2
It could be a lot of things. Do you have an EQ in the signal chain to the Tannoy's somewhere? If you can, get an out-board unit to put there. Monitor speakers almost always need a little EQ adjustment to get them comfortable in a room, no matter how good or bad the room is. Since I know how challenging your room is, I'd recommend you wait until you get it treated before just buying gear, but that'd be my next step. Kase www.minemusic.net
Stratcat29 April 2004#3
There is currently no EQ anywhere in the chain. Are you suggesting I put a graphic EQ in there and adjust it so that a commercial cd sounds "correct" to my ears?
dymaxian30 April 2004#4
Yup, that's pretty much it. You don't need to get a 31-band EQ or anything like that; just something that'll allow you to cut the mids down, and adjust the highs and lows to match your room. You'd have to do this with almost any monitor speakers, in almost any control room. Look for a good-sounding EQ- go for higher quality over multiple bands. A 4-band with sweepable mids is plenty. Kase www.minemusic.net
