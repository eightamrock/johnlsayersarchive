Everything I have read here seems to indicate that, is this the best approach? As a side note, I had not tried SketchUp until I started reading things here. The sound lock and the entrance are doing the same thing...and the isolation booths are going to be hindered by the small size. I would think that, given the room, delete the soundlock/entrance...find a way to have that one entrance without the loss of footprint, and delete the booths all together with. Put the vocals into the bigger live room, but if a isolation booth just has to be part of this project, put it in the live/tracking room.

gullfo19 April 2011#20
i think the challenge is finding the right amount of CR versus LR space - so if you're doing a lot of band recordings - a larger LR is generally a plus although most times you can have the keys, bass, guitar, and reference vox in the CR, the drummer in the live room, and acoustic instruments in the booths and with enough glass it's reasonably visible and more than enough isolation (if that is desired). if you do a lot of recording with everyone in the same room, the larger LR would be better, but consideration for critical listening in the CR will guide you as to how small you might make that. it could just be a matter of a couple of feet to change it to where you need it - ignore the posts for now...
