Hi I'm Kim, 28 years old from Norway. I just found this site and is overwhelmed by all the information :) But after a couple of days of intense reading and learning, I finally feel like my plan is starting to get a little clearer. I found out after meeting you guys that my original plan was flawed :) Maybe not logically, but acoustically haha. If that makes any sense. So my starting point was a green lawn, I will post some pictures later. I have been doing some work and I will soon be ready to order a truckload of concrete. The dimensions of the concrete floor will be: 10.20m x 5.20m x 0.15m The height of the studio will be 2.70m. So it will be a 53m2 building with a studio downstairs and a storage upstairs. The studio will contain a Control room(app. 3mX5m) and a Live/Rehearsal room(app. 7mX5m) The dimensions will change as the walls creep in on me during construction. I have not yet decided how many layers i will use :) It will be a 2 leaf, room-in-room design. with a inner frame (2"x4") and outer frame (2"x4") So it's going to be a rehearsel room for the 5-piece metal band i'm in, so I want it to be as "soundproof" as I can get it with my budget, knowledge and construction skills. we want to have the opportunity to play whenever we like, day or night. I need all the tips and tricks I can get from you guys The budget: 30.000USD maybe a little more Wish me luck :) I'm really excited, looking forward to start building and using all the tips I have picked up here. Uploaded a simple sketch up drawing, for me electricity, getting xlr-cables from CR to LR and ventilation will be the biggest challenges. On the drawing you can see 2 seperate tubes going into CR and LR, this will be the electricity cables going into the house. Is this a good solution?

"I need all the tips and tricks I can get from you guys" Kim, the problem with your approach is this. Your approach is "it is better to ask for forgiveness than permission." You are doing things or will be doing things and then asking why either it isn't working or why people are getting up your skirt about why you did such a thing, since it (whatever it might be) is clearly a dis advantage. So going slow and getting a design is far better than giving pictures of something that is flawed....we have a lot of those around here, from well intention persons who thought that "got it" after a couple days. I still walk slowly even after how ever many years I have been here, with my background in building. Just a simple caution, Good luck,
