another option is to mix it up. you can divide the wells behind the resonators and use the upper and lower sections for low frequency control and the middle section for mids. this way you're taking advanage of the corners of the room (wall-wall, and wall-ceiling) for bass trapping, and the middle section at roughly ear height for mids and the diffusion the more open slats will provide. consider also, instead of have the corner trapped as you have it, instead, making the corner as shown below - this will give you move depth than simply staddling the corner without significant reduction in useable space.
