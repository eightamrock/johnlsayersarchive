Soundman202021 September 2011#6
- What type of wood do I have to use for the slat resonator in the front?
What slat resonator? I don't see anything on your diagram that looks like a slot resonator.
- Do you think I should use for corners SuperChunk or a bass trap panel?
In the corners, I would use only Superchunks. If you also need panel resonators, they should go against the walls, never in the corners. Panel traps are pressure-based devices, which means they must go where the pressure nodes are in the room. While it is true that pressure nodes are present in the corners, it is physically impossible to get a large enough panel trap into a corner, so the next best place is flat against the wall, at the pressure node of the specific mode that is causing problems. So superchunks are better in corners.
- I have a sliding door that in theory should go on the back of the corner trap, do you think that it would work?
It should be fine. Just mount the superchunk module on wheels, so you can roll it out of the way when you need to. But I don't see that sliding door marked on your Sketchup: Where is it? One other comment: I would not use QRD diffusers on the first reflection points in a small room like that! They are way too close to your ears and to the speakers. Either angle the walls for an RFZ design (or maybe used angled slot walls), or put thick absorption spaced away from the walls at the first reflection points. Your front-most cloud also seems to be angled too much, and is too close to the front wall, to be properly treating your first reflection area above the console. I'd suggest that you bring that more directly over the console and angle it better. - Stuart -
