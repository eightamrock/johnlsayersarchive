i have some mackie 824's going into a small 8'x11'x8" space, i had planned just to sit them on the desk with me but is there a benefit to flush mounting them up in front of me? i see a lot of posts about how to flush mount them but not really why it's a benefit any help would be appreciated thank you
John Sayers21 March 2010#2
In the early days before anechoic chambers to test a speaker they would go to an open field and bury the speaker flush with the surface, then they'd hang a mike over it and make measurements. A speaker box radiates 360 degrees in the low frequencies but when it's mounted flush to the surface only the front projections get to you. Otherwise you would get all the rear sound reflecting off the walls and coming to you later and interfering with the original sound. (that's eliminated in an anechoic chamber where speakers are tested today). Soffit mounting - or more correctly Flush Mounting, does the same thing. You achieve a truer and cleaner low end response.
