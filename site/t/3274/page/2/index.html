John Sayers18 March 2005#30
That's great sergio :) I can see it all now - the stairs are different but it all fits - I'd shorten the front of the of the control room so you miss that post intersection at the right speaker when you put in your monitors. - the office will work fine - you are just having the one recording space?? A word about sketchup - when you make a piece of wood or any wall etc group it immediately (encircle it with the mouse to highlight it and right click 'make group') and work totally in groups. Then you can select say all the rafters just by clicking on each one and group them all together OR put them on the one layer which you can then switch on and off called rafters (STUB). To edit a group just double click it and it gets highlighted and the rest of the model fades. You'll find it a lot easier because you can switch off the rafters whilst you work inside. cheers john
