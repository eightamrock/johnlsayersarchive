Hi there, A few quick thoughts to get you started: first, avoid Foam By Mail. The effectiveness of their projects has been pretty much debunked; in addition this company publishes false data about their stuff. Or so I hear anyway. ;-) Regardless, there are more cost-effective solutions than foam anyway. Secondly, A really good place to start is Ethan Winer's Acoustics FAQ. That will answer most/all of your questions at this stage on how to plan your new room.
