Skai_Penalva28 January 2009#3
You could just use GIK Acoustics traps. They have room kits that would do a great job in your room and perfect if you want mobile treatment in case you want to move or record or mix in another room. http://www.gikacoustics.com There's also Real Traps but they're way more expensive. If you're good for the money then Real Traps is an awesome choice. Although if you're good for that much money you could get MORE GIK Acoustics traps :) Oh, I would also suggest you place your set-up on the side of the wall where the bed is on your drawing and move the bed to the back or sides. That way you can have the long part of the room behind you and place your acoustic panels in the ideal positions to absorb early reflections.
Skai_Penalva28 January 2009#4
You want your listening position to be 38% of the room's Length from front to back in your case. Your speakers should be placed in the corners of an equilateral triangle made from your head and both speakers. In other words, the distance between each speaker must be the same distance between each speaker and you forming a triangle of three equal sides. Check out this link for the layout of the room kit's placement for the treatment. http://www.gikacoustics.com/images/room_kit_01.jpg This other thread also holds a design for a room just like yours with other treatment options. viewtopic.php?f=1&t=4206 Hope I've helped!
