Gregwor30 September 2019#3
Can someone chime in on that "rule" or where I can find more info about it on the site?
ITU-R BS.1116-3 states the following for a control room: For monophonic or two-channel stereophonic reproduction: 20-60 m2. For multichannel stereophonic or advanced sound system reproduction: 30-70 m2. For a live room, typically, the bigger the better but as John said, make it a size that works for you (budget, time frame to build, etc, etc) Greg
