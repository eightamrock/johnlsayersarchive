Soundman202030 March 2010#2
Hi Giancarlo, There is an announcement at the top of the forum about what to do to assure getting as many responses as possible. The announcement leads to this post (click here). Actually, several people, who are experts on this forum, will most likely not reply if you don't do what is written in that post. Many others who are very helpful, will most likely not reply out of respect for the moderators' wishes. In any event, none of your proposed layouts makes sense acoustically, mainly because of the shape / size / layout of the control room. There are some basic guidelines for control rooms, and all four of your layouts do not follow them. To start with, you need symmetry. The control room should be symmetric. None of yours are symmetric. You also want the control room to widen out from the front. None of yours do. You should also have the room deeper than it is wide, but all of your proposals are wider than deep. Also, both your control room and your live room ("Studio") need to be properly isolated. In your layouts, the control room is not isolated at all, and it also looks like your live room inner-leaf is coupled to the building outer leaf, in several places. Finally, there are certain places inside the control room where certain acoustic treatments work best, but in all of your diagrams you have those places occupied with other things. I'd suggest that you look around the forum for designs that are close to what you need and might fit your space, then modify them a bit until they fit. - Stuart -
