knightfly30 October 2003#3
Ron's right - if you're doing this for the look, then get what you like the look of. If you're asking about acoustic performance, BOTH are marginal so it doesn't matter. If you want this SPECIFIC look but still want it to work, mount your choice of "look" over 2" rigid fiberglas, and you have EVERYTHING. If you can't do that, and still want both looks AND function, buy the 4" thick tiles and, if possible, mount them to furring strips in order to space them off the wall a couple inches. remember, these people are SELLING foam - the number they give you for thickness is the PEAK measurement, not the valley. This makes the foam an even poorer value, and whatever surface it has cut into the foam makes no difference at all other than to make the overall absorption even LESS by reducing the usable thickness... Steve

knightfly30 October 2003#5
STC is the ability to BLOCK sound - foam is next to useless for that. For absorption, if it's open cell foam it would probably be at least as good as the 4" sonex, maybe better. If it's closed cell, you already found the only two good uses for it - padding, and sleeping babies... Steve

knightfly31 October 2003#9
Here's the trick - do NOT put foam of any kind where you don't need treatment, it will only suck out the highs and high mids. It's only a little better than thick carpet this way. First, get your space sounding good with the RIGHT treatments - rigid fiberglas, etc, as we explain in this and other forums - then, to "shut up the dummies", pick a couple of opposite fiberglas absorbers and glue whatever kind of foam on the fronts of them that you think looks "high tech" enough to satisfy the "foam-a-holics" you have to deal with - Just don't get carried away with the glue, as Tod mentioned - you don't want to impact the REAL acoustic treatment under the foam. I personally always thought either the pyramid or the wedge foam LOOKED more business like - the egg crate stuff looks like mattress pads. If you look around, you can find several colors of foam so you might be able to "make a fashion statement" while yer at it - just don't expect to hear much difference, if any - that's why we use the other stuff... Steve
