jhbrandt14 October 2010#7
We have similar construction here in Indonesia. As Glenn said, you will have to deal with the flanking noise through the structure. What kind of ceiling do you have? Concrete or wood. Many times they will build the ceiling out of wood framework and attach 3mm plywood panels for finish. Above that, many times, is the roof rafters and roofing tiles are laid directly on wood strips. - No acoustic isolation at all. Check to see if the separation walls are structural. Cheers, John
