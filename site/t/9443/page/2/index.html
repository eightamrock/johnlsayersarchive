bpape wrote:
If we do our design correctly, each wall will have a different resonant frequency so there is theoretically half of the energy at each WALL resonance to be transmitted through the space to the next wall. If then we also take it a step further and have different spacing between walls, those cavities will also have different resonances. Even if we don't do different cavities and assume that it will all average out to a single resonance for the cavity itself, the mass on one side of the equation will have things spread more evenly. No one frequency will dominate.
Bryan, I have (for years now) tried again and again to point out that this is just not worth the effort......... Coincidence is a high enough frequency that the TL value of the wall ( at that frequency) even though it's taking a dip from the frequencies above and below it - is still so high a TL value that the dip is just abouit meaningless............ But the added mass of maintaining the multiple thicker layers (versus thining drywall out to compensate for coincidence) is VERY meaningful for low frequency TL values - which is where we have the problems. Take some time and study any of the double wall assembllies and you'll see exactly what I mean. Read through IR 761 as thoroughly as I have and you will have it inscribed in your brain in permanate marker (color of your choice). It just isn't worth the effort........... Put as much mass on the wall as you can afford- and forget the rest - becuase it isn't of much more value than any salesman's snake oil............ and a LOT cheaper in the end if you have to deal with subs labor. Sincerely, Rod
