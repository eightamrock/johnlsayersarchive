Hello all, I've been working on a design of my own, after reading this forum at length. Now i've been given (wife) approval to go ahead and build it, as there will be an addition to our family in six months :lol: , and i have to get my gear "outside the house" - a common occurance for many on this forum i believe. Anyway, here are the details... It will be a newly built structure, a bit smaller than a double garage. Due to council regulations, it must match the house and therefore be of brick veneer, with a colorbond (tin) roof. An engineer designed slab is needed. Luckily I work next to a bunch of registered civil engineers so I can get that part done for nil. The plan is to get the basic shell framing and roof trusses prefabricated, then i'll add in all of the tricky internal walls. General construction will be of double layer 10mm sounchek plasterboard, and soundstop insulation, with an isolated ceiling (using those rubber rondo mount things). Attached is my first floor plan. It consists of a control room with large window (1.2 x 1.8m) into the live room, and also a small amp iso room. There is an external window in each room which i am thinking of turning into false windows, blocked off on the inside, but looks like a window to the outside. I saw someone do that in a thread somewhere. It would be nice to get natural light, but the cost could be prohibitive. I've got six months to get this up and going.. Any comments from those in the know, to tell me if i'm doing something drastically wrong, would be appreciated. Cheers, anthony.
