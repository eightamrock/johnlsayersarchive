knightfly11 January 2005#42
If your inner wall frames will bear under the ceiling joists, then you DEFINITELY want the RC under that wall's gypsum layers; otherwise, you have a serious flanking path up into (and down from) the living space above. Are the concrete blocks in the one drawing hollow, or?? Basically, anything that comes into hard contact with main structure should be resiliently mounted - floors are not QUITE as critical since they are (slabs, at least) in contact with earth which will damp them a little. A better option, where possible, is to use free standing wall frames and resilient sway braces, with NO RC - this stiffens the wall panel by making the stud frame and the gypsum into almost a "monolith", so will improve LF isolation some... Steve
