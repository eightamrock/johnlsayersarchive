Jester11 December 2009#151
Just finished my bass hangers and thought I would post some pictures. I constructed the hangers using celotex fiberboard and then gluing and taping on insulation. I used fiber glass insulation that I had removed from a bunch of left over flex ducting. Because the fiberboard is fairly flimsy I cut up a plastic tube and glued it into the hole I drilled in it. I then ran some wire through the tubes and hung the hangers from some small eyelet screws. It was neat to see the effect others have talked about where you move one hanger and all the other hangers start to move.

Jester8 February 2010#171
I finished the trim around the windows. With the slat resonators the walls look incredible thick. For the front speakers I made a removable frame around the speakers but for the rear speakers the whole bezel is removable. It is made out of ¾” OSB with a layer of finished ½ plywood glued to it.
Jester8 February 2010#172
I made all of the cloth frames out of ¾ plywood cut in to 1 ½” strips. The cloth was stretched over the frames and stapled. I used Lou's method for attaching the frames using finish nails and cutting off the heads before hammering them all the way in. I then used a needle to pull the cloth up. It looks really good and if I ever need to remove the frames it shouldn’t be too hard. I put all of the speakers in and wired them up. I set the bass control to -3 on all of the speakers. I have to say my speaker sound really really good! It sounds like the center speaker is on even when it isn’t. I can’t wait to start mixing in my room! I still have to make clouds for the control room. I am thinking of making 3 4’x4’ clouds and 6 2’x4’ clouds. I also need to do a lot of trim. Then I am done with construction for the control room and it only took me 4 years. Of course I still need to wire everything up but it feels really good to be so close to being done with at least one room. I mostly do postproduction so I can start doing some work in the studio even though the other rooms aren’t done. I’ll be focusing on the vocal booth next.
