AndreasB7 August 2008#3
You'd have to seal every single connection between all the pine panels in order to get it airtight, so I'd not go for that as the leaf closest to the rockwool. Just put a layer of gypsum and then add the pine panel. I'm not really sure but the pine panel in itself does not have any acoustic benefits in comparison to any other flat surface, except that it might look good. best a :arrow:
