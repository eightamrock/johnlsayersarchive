gullfo26 February 2008#9
flanking refers to sound paths through openings around the isolation assemblies (such as openings in the ceiling between rooms or duct work) or through the structure itself (such as shared walls, floors, etc. which conduct sound). room mode treatment as well as first reflection points is best started by using broadband treatments such as porous absorbers made from rigid fiberglass across corners to low frequency control, and a variety of absorbers on walls and ceilings to break up reflections to get the control room nice and tight, or without killing the ambiance of the live rooms. you can build treatment walls on angles to get absorption, diffusion, and reduce echoes.
