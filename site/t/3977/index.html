You really might want to consider bringing in another specialized acoustician/architect with experience in this sort of thing for a project of this size/technicality. I know some good ones in NYC if you decide to go this way. Edison had a rotating film studio, but not exactly the same thing. I think this will be very hard to pull off and will not be anywhere's worth the effort. It'd probably be cheaper to build 5 control rooms and 5 studios than to ever have this thing work and meet code.
