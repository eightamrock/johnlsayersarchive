Soundman202014 March 2012#3
I would not mount speakers to the wall like that, no. Number one, you do stand a very good chance that there will be transmission of sound through that bracket and into the wall. Number 2, it places the speaker in fixed location, so there's no chance of fine tuning the room by moving the speakers around a bit and testing for improvement. And number 3, it most likely places the speaker at the wrong distance from the wall. I would either soffit-mount the speakers, or put them on heavy, massive, decoupled stands. - Stuart - (PS. Yes, you should keep all of your questions related to your build in your own thread: It makes it easier to find them months or years down the line, when you just know that you asked something but can't find the answer in your own thread, and don't recall which section you asked it in...
