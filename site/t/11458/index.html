AVare2 December 2008#2
Any diffusion material adds surface area and absorption. Bad for a reverb chamber. Finish to as hard a smooth surface you can. Plaster is good. Newell has a section about reverb chambers in Recording Studio Design. IF you want low absorption, the best way is to Helmholtz type absorbers with low frequency design cut off. Schultz in the IPRF acoustic uses for perforated metals: Principles and Applications is considered the standard for such design work. Reverb chambers can be made with results these days, but you will have to get all the information required. Good luck with your design and build! Andre
