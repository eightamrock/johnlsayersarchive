Without testing results, it's hard to say what an automotive product would do in a studio environment, although they share some common goals (quietness when inside) - One possible way to mount glass using some of your idea would be to set the glass using spaced high-duro EPDM blocks, then caulk behind, between and around them with Butyl caulk (butyl has high damping characteristics and might improve the glass performance a small amount) Just a thought... Steve
