gullfo31 October 2010#6
equipment depends on your budget. basically you'll need a small mixer, a PC/Mac an A/D converter for the mixer into the PC/Mac or get a mixer with firewire (IEEE1394) i/o, a good pair of monitors and a good pair of headphones. you'll want a decent preamp, decent mic, sturdy boom stand, and you want to buy or build a decent desk to hold the equipment. a desk which supports a 61-76 key keyword and a PC/Mac keyboard+mouse, video monitor, mixer, and the rack equipment. a patch panel or two to make switching around the rack gear and mixer connections is a good idea. power filtering is important plus a UPS unit for the PC/Mac is a good idea. moving up a bit having the mixer with motorized controls is a nice way to control your DAW software. for wiring, consider running enough cable for 4 mics XLR, 4 headphones TRS, 2 speaker, 2 network, 2 USB, 2 coax, 4 TRS for instrument or line levels (like guitar re-amping).
