wall around control room will be as shown on the cad draw (double wall, with 2 layers of gypsum boards - figured it out with knightfly already).. Question: Should I make it go all the way from the floor to the ceiling of the existing structure or just to the splayed joists.
It's hard following your construction technique - what country are you in?? As I understand it you have a double wall for the control room. Normally the outer wall goes fully up to the existing ceiling whilst the inner wall follows the line of the control room with it's angled ceiling. With your studio it appears you are building a room with in your existing room - to get windows like in the picture you will need to add an internal window the same size as your existing windows but THEY DO NOT JOIN. Each window frame is in it's own wall structure. As for the isolation through the ceilings they are the same as your walls - separate frames with a drywall layer separating each room. hope this helps - as i said - your design is a little confusing in it's construction method - you don't appear to have any baseplates in your wall structure? cheers john
