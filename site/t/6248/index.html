Hello, I've got what I think is a pretty unusual, stupid problem. I've got a few spiders living between my laminated glass and exterior fixed tempered glass. There's acoustic treatment that lines the window well, which I thought I installed pretty snuggly. I guess it doesn't take much room for a spider to crawl up in there. So they're making webs and a mess of things inside my beautiful windows. Anyone else have this problem? Removing the glass on a regular basis to clean the space would be a real pain in the butt. Spraying for bugs is not a guarantee. Does anyone have any suggestions? thanks! andrew

knightfly21 May 2006#5
'bout all I can suggest is to take one of the glass out ONCE - re-do whatever you're using for the perimeter cover to include (maybe UNDER black cloth) some bug screen - this is available in nylon as well as galvanized steel, and can be stapled in place without causing hard connection between frames. Then clean thoroughly and replace the glass - unless the spidies can lay their tiny eggs THRU the mesh, should keep 'em in the walls (where they may improve insulation absorption a bit :wink: ) Steve
