Earl, You have been here a long time as gauged by your location so you should understand how things work ;) The metal building has no exterior mass to speak of but it is a leaf in your proposed wall assembly. Then it has the OSB on the interior and when you add your interior walls you develop, stop me if you have heard this before, a three leaf wall assembly. Multiple air cavities produce multiple resonance which reduce your low frequency isolation abilities. Since you are in a hot/humid climate the engineering of this wall assembly suggests that the VDR (Vapor Diffusion Retarder) is on the exterior of the building as represented by the 3" insulation with plastic facing. But the interior OSB would need to come off to remove the additional air space, and that could only be achieved after consultation with a qualified person in your specific area to determine if the structures ability to withstand the elements or resist racking would be altered adversely by the removal of the OSB. Even if the OSB can be removed you will risk code violation due to the plastic facing [flammable material] and simply building an interior wall may not satisfy the requirement. I suspect your overhead ceiling looks somewhat similar to this process, or maybe just has faced insulation with no OSB, is that about right?
