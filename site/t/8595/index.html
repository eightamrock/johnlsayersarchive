drfrankencopter13 April 2007#2
Perhaps you have a bit of a mode problem, and could benefit from some bass trapping in your booth. How thick is your fiberglass room treatment? Thin treatments generally result in too much top end removed, leaving the low end boomy and uncontroleld. Cheers Kris
