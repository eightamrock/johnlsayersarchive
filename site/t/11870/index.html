xSpace wrote:
MadMax has a story to tell about his experience with this product: http://recording.org/ftopic-21631-days0 ... c-630.html
And THEN some! It's a bit of a toss-up as to whether SilenSeal or OSI is the "better" product... The OSI is much stiffer and goes a LOT quicker. The SilenSeal is much thinner and goes a LOT slower and often needs to be applied a 2nd and 3rd time to fill a gap. The OSI, being much thicker, probably doesn't push into a gap as thoroughly as the SilenSeal, but I prefer working the OSI a LOT more... Thin viscosity fills 95-100% of any given gap, but the shrinkage factor is quite unexpected... where the OSI shrinkage is almost nil.

Man... tough question... I'd probably still use both, to be honest with ya'. I would probably use the SilenSeal at the exterior perimeters, where you're into rough framming through the first layers. You really need to be 100% confident that you have any gaps filled as best you possibly can. Then I'd move on to using the OSI for the inner layers... it goes so much faster! Not only that... but it would be a LOT cheaper, too! As far as finding the OSI... Toolup has it for $3.99... which doesn't appear to be that bad of a price. About the best price I'm finding for SilenSeal is $7.00/tube at case price. As far as other caulking... You GOTTA check out Speciality Products and Insulation.
