hi all. i am new around here and have been reading and researching the site for my next studio. good stuff! i wanted to thank john, and everyone for their expertiece and help. anyway, i have drawn up a plan based on the corner control room setup and was hoping for some feedback to make sure i am on the right track. i am on a mac so i drew the diagram freehand (lord help us all) so it might be a bit hard to read. we are building a new garage (outside dimensions are 36ft. X 24ft.). the studio is going to be completely free floating in within the 24 x 24 area. the plan at the moment is to float the floor and build the frame of a new floor on it, so all cables can be run under the flooring and to appropriate areas of the studio. the ceilings are going to be 9 ft. from ceiling to the concrete (so with the new floor it will be closer to 8 1/2ft.). let me know if you need anymore info. any feedback would be appreciated. thanks for this great forum! -john
