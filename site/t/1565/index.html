knightfly26 April 2004#5
Dobro, been busy with "gators", the nice weather brings 'em out... Your idea of the 2" fiberglass 6" off the wall is probably the best you'll do - just forget the egg cartons :wink: - your "cloud" shouldn't need to be larger than 8' side to side, and 4' front to back - that leaves a couple pieces, which would do ONE front absorber behind one speaker - if you can get a couple more pieces, you're set. If you still have ringing, just standing a couple of pieces of plywood up beside the mix desk will take care of most of that - closer together at the front of the room (toward the speaker end) and further apart at the rear. 6" to a foot of splay per side will do... John Sayers26 April 2004#6
Dobro - hi mate - I'd put all the insulation you have on the rear wall behind you and see what happens then. ;) Putting the isulation of the front wall will only affect the low end because your speakers are directional (except the low end) and the high end is directed to your rear wall. It's the high end giving the ringing. cheers John
