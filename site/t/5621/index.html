knightfly10 February 2006#2
That corner could work, but you'll need a LOT of absorption; the corner behind the mixer should be completely absorbed, also at least half of all four walls and about half the ceiling. mid-high frequency sounds fired into a square corner will reflect back parallel to the source, so early reflections are the main problem... Steve

knightfly11 February 2006#4
You could work at the top center of your drawing, if you can build a portable absorber to slide behind the speakers (and one for each side wall) - these could be stashed out of the way when not in use. 50mm rockwool batts in light wooden frames (no back, just corner reinforcement - otherwise, absorption is limited to depth of the material instead of total depth to the wall) - something like this http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/download.php?id=2884 That's about all I can think of... Steve

knightfly12 February 2006#8
Somewhat - to work WELL, you'd need REALLY thick foam or at least 75mm rockwool on all three sides, preferably spaced a few inches from the walls; you'd also need both sides to extend far enough so side reflections are absorbed; and you'd need a ceiling "cloud" of the same stuff. But the basic concept is good... Steve
