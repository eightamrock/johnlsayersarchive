dymaxian7 February 2004#2
A square room that's 12'x20'? Or did you mean the walls are all paralell? The parallel wall thing is easy to fix, especially if you're planning on building a room-within-a-room. I'd recommend it. Are the ceiling joists/trusses exposed? As far as the layout, you could split it down into 2 rooms and use one for tracking, but if you want to get a whole band playing all at once, it may not be a bad idea to make the whole space into one room and use gobos for separation. I personally would prefer a larger, all-in-one space over a pair of rooms that were both too small to do what I need. There's plenty of designs around the board that'll give you ideas for room shapes. If you need more help, give us a sketch of what the existing space floor plan looks like, and we'll come up with something. With the info you've given us, there's not much we can do... Good luck! John Sayers11 February 2004#8
sickyboy - unfortunately that foam is NOT the same as Auralex. Don't go buying standard foam for this application. as well as not being acoustically similar it is dangerous to use as it is a fire hazard and burns creating a lethal smoke. The alternative to auralex acoustic foam is to use semi rigid insulation like owens corning's 703 or John Manvilles spinglas. Do a search for these two products on this site and you will see what I mean.;) cheers john
