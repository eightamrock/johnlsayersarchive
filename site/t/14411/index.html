Soundman202026 May 2010#2
wanted to know the "best" placement for my monitors to minimize "leak" or "bleed" out of the door area
Unfortunately, "placement" isn't the problem: Sound level is the problem! There isn't really anything you can do to re-position your speakers that will prevent sound from getting out the door. The only way to stop sound is with mass. Lots of it. If the sound you are making is annoying other people in the house, then the only option you have is to seal the room air-tight, then place a massive barrier all around it, such as another wall a few inches away from the existing wall. But you don't have enough space to do that, so your only real option is a good set of headphones!
I was thinking about putting some 703 insulation on the door,
That is good for TREATMENT, not ISOLATION. Two entirely different things. 703 on the door will help your room sound better, but it won't stop sound getting out to the rest of the house. The only thing you could maybe try, is sealing every last tiny air gap that leads out of the room, but even then it will only help you by a couple of dB, since your real problem is flanking through the house structure, and low transmission loss through walls, floor, ceiling, windows, and doors. - Stuart -
