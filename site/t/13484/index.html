stuntbutt22 August 2009#11
If you want to use the whole room, it looks like your height is limited by the steel beam. Your ceiling framing would have to go under that. A 2x6 ceiling joist with 2 layers of 5/8" drywall would be 6 3/4". Leave a 1/4" clearance under the beam and your new ceiling would be 7" lower than the beam. If moving the air supply/returns is doable, then move them to the "right" side of the beam. Then you would have a lot more headroom on the "left" side. Unfortunately you would only be able to build your room on the "left" side of the beam, which looks like only 9' or 10'. If you are really tenacious, you could take advantage of the fact that your sewer isn't under the slab and dig down a foot and pour a new slab.
