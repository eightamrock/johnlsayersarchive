Henrik wrote:
OK, cool. I'll try it myself some day. It DOES seem a bit tedious to make them out of 1156 17 mm wide pieces of wood...
That's why you see more of the 2D diffusors built for wider-band absorption- you know, the ones with vertical wells of different depths instead of the 3D ones with squares. If you've got a fairly absorptive ceiling, it doesn't probably matter if what hits it is diffused or not. One thing that's interesting to me is the idea that when you make a *2D* diffusor without walls between the wells, it drastically reduces it's effectiveness: I wonder if it's true with the 3D versions?
