knightfly24 December 2005#2
Most, if not all, the pix you chose look to me like separate frames for inner leaf; these wouldn't need RC. All that would be required is a good seal, and probably putty packs around/behind the fixtures for more mass. You could also build a double drywall box around fixtures, provided they're rated for complete seal and still pass fire code. If you MUST mount light fixtures on an RC ceilng, you can always run an extra row of RC where the light bars (surface mount) will go, or TWO extra runs of RC (one on either side of the row of recessed lights) Make sure you research your light fixtures to make sure they are rated for completely sealed operation, and I'd also use X-core drywall for your ceilings just to be safe...
