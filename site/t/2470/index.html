Help with low ceilings

Started by acorredor on 12 October 2004. 2 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 2470.

acorredor12 October 2004#1
Hello: I have some problems with my control room; it is small (3.30 x 5.10 mts), and the ceiling is angled in the front, but low (2.20 mts). What kind of material should i use in the ceiling, absortive ( i have tons of high density foam) or diffusors ( i have two Obeta Diffusors) and where should i place this materials, or should i use both? I appreciate any help. Alejandro
dymaxian12 October 2004#2
With a ceiling that low, absorb as much as you can. If you can get 2 inches of rigid fiberglass on the ceiling, that'll help out a lot. The foam will work too, if you've got it lying around (open cell foam, right?) but the diffusors will most likely cause more problems than they solve. Hope this helps!
acorredor13 October 2004#3
Thanks a lot Dymaxian! I will start making my ceiling as absortive as possible. Do you think it will do any good to place the diffusors in the back wall? Thanks a lot.
