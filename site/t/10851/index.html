AVare7 July 2008#2
cchord59 wrote:
I'm wondering if using a drop ceiling with ceiling panels is an effective way of bass traping on a ceiling. I was going to install it 6" below the drywall ceiling, and the fill the void with dense insulation.
Yes and close. If you are going to fill the void, use light insulation. If it is 6" then R19 is the one to use. double check on the R value, but there is a standard home insulation 6" thick. Andre
cchord598 July 2008#3
Great, thanks, Will this give me effectiveness down to 50 hz? How low?
AVare8 July 2008#4
cchord59 wrote:
Great, thanks, Will this give me effectiveness down to 50 hz? How low?
? There is no standardized testing data for that low. 6" will give you flat free field absorption down to 125 Hz, the lowest standard band for testing at a minimum. Extrapolating from test data, I guess you will get usable absorption down to the 80 Hz band. Andre
