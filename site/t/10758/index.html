Hello All, I'm planning to build a small detached personal studio in my backyard. It will double as a home office, sans any office equipment. It will normally have one occupant but I would like to have occasional company. I have around a $5000 budget and will do most of the construction myself. This will be new construction, nominally 12' x 16' with, preferably, a 12' side facing my house. I am planning on a standard 8' ceiling. I'm considering an 18" easement from the two fence runs the building will face. I would like to have a window on each of the non-fence sides. I am planning on constructing with a concrete slab and independent double walls. I plan on using double 5/8 DW on interior, but I'm not certain about exterior. I'm leaning toward a simple rectangular interior due to size. I believe I want a reflective floor, but could be convinced otherwise. Currently there exists a large brick BBQ/Fireplace and a 3" slab at the site which I will demolish soon. My next door neighbor has a large maple by the fence whose roots have raised the existing 3" slab a little. I can excavate these somewhat, but I'm not sure about risks. My closest neighbor is about 25 yards away. I would like to sing 24 hours a day and play my child's tiny drum set well past 10 pm. I mostly play guitar and will play a bass infrequently. I play at moderate volumes, shouting, not screaming. Sorry if this is not accurate enough. Pictures to follow. Questions ========= How thick does the slab need to be? Are the double walls necessary, could I get away with RC on interior with single frame? Are the roots a deal killer for this location? Do those double hose portable A/Cs really provide fresh air? Do I vent the space between the ceiling and roof at all? Are there any safety issues to be aware of? What am I missing? Thanks in advance for any input, Brian
