Hi Clifford, They are all "good" cables... some thoughts below...
natas wrote:
I am making my own Mic/inst cables. Here are my choices; Canare Quad Mic cable.
Excellent cable, but fairly high capacitance that will affect high frequencies over distance. For this reason, many studios that do classical music shy away from the quad stuff. Consider L2T2S instead for a Canare product.
natas wrote:
Gotham Quad Mic cable.
Again, excellent cable, but the same issues as above.
natas wrote:
Mogami Mic cable.
Can't go wrong here... great cable.
natas wrote:
Which one and why? Thanks, Clifford.
Personally, IMHO, I would say stay away from the quad cable... other considerations are size and flexibility. The quad is also more time consuming to solder. The Mogami or Canare L2 would give you the best bang for the buck. Dave.
