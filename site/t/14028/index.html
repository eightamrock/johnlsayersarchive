Soundman20205 February 2010#5
why do the resonances look bad? is it because of close low frequencies resonances might cause beating?
The problems I see with modal issues for that ratio and those dimensions is the uneven spread at the very low end. You have several major modes (forst order axial modes) that are located all by themselves, totally separate from their nearest neighbors. Such as 21.5 Hz, then nothing until 34.8, then nothing until 40.9 but with 43.0 right next to it, then nothing until 55.3 but with 56.5 right next door, etc. It's not a smooth distribution, which is what you would get from using a Sepmeyer or Louden ratio. That's also a pretty long room: nearly 8m. What do you intend to use the room for? CR? LR? Something else? That all makes a big difference. - Stuart -
