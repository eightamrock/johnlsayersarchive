dymaxian14 October 2004#8
We need a little more info about what you're looking to accomplish up there... what are your sound isolation requirements? Do you need to be able to fire up a jet engine without being able to hear it outside? How big of a tracking room will you need- full band? just drums? Where are the stairs? I don't think it's impossible to get a control room and tracking room in there, but it might be tricky. My recommendation would be to build a 12' wide control room into the little "nook" between the low ceiling areas, maybe 14' or 15' long, sticking out into the remainder of the space by however far. It'd be awfully convenient if the stairs were located under one of the low roof areas... it'd sure be easier to accomodate. But yeah. Let us know more about what you need the space to do for you, and we'll be more help. We can just rattle off designs all day, but they'll work for you better if we know more. Help us help you. =)
