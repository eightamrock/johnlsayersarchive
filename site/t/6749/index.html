Hi John, I was wanting to know the materials and dimensions of laptopop's windows covers. I also wanted to know if this cause problems with excess humidity or any type of moisture between to box and window. Thanks for your time.
John Sayers2 August 2006#2
Humidity problems only occur when you have a double pane window in a single wall because the air is trapped between the two panes. In a double wall, where one pane is attached to one wall and the other attached to the second wall there is air movement between the panes in the wall cavity so humidity isn't a problem. When you have a single wall with two panes you must add silica crystals that absorbs any moisture in the air stopping humidity misting up the windows. The silica crystals are the same thing you get in a small sachet when you buy a camera or sensitive electronic gear. In aussieland you can buy cans of it from chemist shops, in the US I assume it's a drug store. cheers john
