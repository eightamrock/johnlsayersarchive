welcome! would it be possible to post a simple drawing which includes measurements of where things are actually located? the gas meter - depth, height and position from the bottom wall. the post position on the floor, same for the water heater. any option to move the water heater to somewhere next to the water meter? are there any windows etc? ducts? pipes? beams? one challenge will be the low ceiling height and trying to get decent isolation. you might consider going with isolation clips and hat channel for 3x 16mm drywall layers, plus 3x 16mm drywall between the floor joists to add mass there. you would want to check if the structure can support that weight.
