Ethan Winer17 October 2008#6
adaebb wrote:
Would placing broadband panels in various "mirror" positions be of any real value, or am I going to need much more extensive treatment?
Reflection points are more applicable to smaller listening rooms where the side walls are close to the speakers and listeners, and the reflections are strong and thus damaging. A large space like this just needs large amounts of absorption spread more or less evenly around the room. --Ethan
