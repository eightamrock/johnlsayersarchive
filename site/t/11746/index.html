John Sayers2 March 2009#6
biggator wrote:
Thanks for the help, John. So - with completely decoupled rooms (version 2 in your doc above), does it still make sense to use RC on the ceiling and/or walls (let's assume GG is a given) ?
GG is not a given as it's two totally isolated structures. Similarly with RC - RC is only beneficial when you only have one structure as you do when you are dealing with floor joists in a basement ceiling or when you only have one single stud wall. When you have two totally isolated structures as in double wall with it's own independedent ceiling (as per version 2) neither RC or GG are required.

John Sayers3 March 2009#8
I'm not saying GG isn't going to help - but on a double wall system the RC adds no benefit. GG for your ceiling with RC is a good idea as I said in my first post. If you go to a double structure then weigh the extra db gained with the extra cost. An additional layer of drywall is probably cheaper than GG.
