Soundman20201 March 2011#4
Hi Len, and Welcome! What concerns me about your build is the available height: 85" before you even start isn't much, and form the photos it looks like you have some serious intrusions into that height. Especially considering that you want to isolate acoustic drums down to practically nothing 20 feet away: that's a tall order. You need something like 65 dB of isolation to do that. I can't see your finished ceiling being any higher than about 81", without even considering the treatment! That means that the overhead mics on your drums will be very close to the ceiling, and thus will suffer from all sorts of acoustical nasties, such as comb filtering, early reflections, and other stuff. I'm not saying that you can't do it: if that's the only space you have, then that's what you have! I'm just saying that you need to be realistic with your expectations: Yes, you can isolate your rooms well, and yes you probably can do a reasonable control room (CR) in that height, but it will be very hard to get good drum recordings in there, or most other instruments for that matter, due to the limited height. So is it a usable space? That depends on you! If you reckon you can live with the limitations, then go for it! Folks here will help you out as best they can, and you will get the best possible studio for that space, if you build it right. As long as you realize the limitations, then all is well. So if you are OK with all of that, then please post your SketchUp model of the space that you have, and others here will probably be able to take that and make some suggestions on how to optimize the use of your space. - Stuart -
