Ethan Winer22 August 2007#2
duda wrote:
I would like your commentaries and suggestions
Small rooms like those will do better with broadband absorption rather than tuned Helmholtz traps. Small rooms have problems at all low frequencies, not just those frequencies related to the room dimensions. --Ethan
duda22 August 2007#3
Is it better to forget the Helmholtz and to only place bass traps?
gullfo24 August 2007#4
its important to balance the room - typically you start with broadband treatments - corner traps, broadband slats, etc. then you measure to determine what problems might still existing and you can use more fine tuned treatments like a tuned resonator or panel trap to correct those.
