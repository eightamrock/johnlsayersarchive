knightfly3 November 2004#26
Not unless the specific brand claims it's fire rated - these are two entirely different materials. Acoustic caulk's main claim to fame is permanent flexibility and sealing of air gaps for sound control; fire putty is intumescent and pretty high mass, seals SORTA OK until it gets HOT, at which time it swells up as much as 10X and forms a hard char which is supposed to withstand even a fire hose. People have used the acoustic caulk and "buttered" outlet boxes with it, but as far as I know none of the common acoustic caulks claims to be intumescent; maybe non-flammable, but it's not the same... Steve
