Soundman202013 April 2009#2
Well, the way I see it, that sure looks like a decent plan to me, especially considering your limitations on attaching things to walls and ceiling! My only comment would be that your wooden framework will probably need some kind of triangular bracing where the verticals meet the horizontals, for sturdiness, but apart form that it looks good. I like the idea of the hinged bass trap on that rear plywood "door". That makes sense. Have you thought of a method to damp any resonance in that plywood? It might not be a problem, but it might be worth thinking about in advance, so you have a plan if it does turn out to be an issue. - Stuart -
Ro13 April 2009#3
Overall, it seems like a plan. Since your room is small I'd suggest covering, *at least*, the corner traps with plastic to keep it from sucking to much mid/high frq. You can't have too much bass absorption (in a small room) but you sure can have too much broad/mid-band trapping!
