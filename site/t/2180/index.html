May seem like a silly question, but you only got one quote? My rule of thumb when dealing with things I am not familair with is get at least three quotes and then go from there. Andre (edit as in added after some thought) It seems reasonable depending on what are asking for. If it is to a finish ready for high quality painting, the quote is quite reasonable. At least three trips involved (tear down, install new wall, cut new opening install new door trip one. Sand, second mud second trip. Sand third trip). Regardless of your location and size of job, some things are constant. Time to your place, getting equipment out, cleaning and packing equipment. With a smaller job these constant prices are spread over less "final product" cost, so the cost is higher.
