BradJacob12 December 2007#30
Cram wrote:
So, anyone know the best outlet box to deal with 2 layers of 5/8 on the wall?
Lowes and Home Depot makes adjustable "new construction" boxes that have a tiny silver screw to adjust the depth. (the blue plastic boex) - No nails, just a mteal clip on the face that has two holes for either screws or nails. I bought 17 of them in my studio and they're just great. Check out the bottom right corner of the picture "connecting_to_muffler.JPG" in my build thread to see them: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewt ... c&start=15 If you plan on using putty-pads to seal up your boxes: MAKE SURE - after your rough wiring is done, that you adjust the boxes to their finished depth (1 1/4" inches) - and THEN seal them up. If you don't, when you adjust the depth, the wire moves (it slides outward) and breaks the seal. This happened to me and I caught it before I sheetrocked :wink:

I'm not sure about the good/bad of too much compression, but if your framing is wood, I would attach a piece of lattice or wire to the back of your studs. I spent spare time in the room today taking care of - Mud the tapered joints on first layer - Knightfly has a contribution to one of the sticky threads in the construction forum (http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=20003). This section -
Place the first layer, [...] Wallboard is tapered along the long edge for mud, so be sure to treat this seam as normal drywall finish coat, bringing this "taper" up level with the rest of the wall - you do NOT want any VOIDS between layers. Along this line, also remember this TAPER is only on ONE side of the boards, so it must be placed to the side AWAY from the frame for each layer.
I brought the tapered seams up to the level surface with tape and mud. This was just really quick skim work so it didn't take much. It's actually good because I have some compound which I wouldn't use for finish work because it's old and has some dry clumps in it. I can waste that stuff on the 1st layer and pick out anything too anoying. Putty Pads - I've been doubling up on putty pad usage for all my outlets. I bought way too much putty pad and I'm also using deep boxes (provided for free by one of my brothers).
