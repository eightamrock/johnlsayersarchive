Hi, Quick question about screwing schedule, I'm using a a more dense 15mm drywall board than the standard board, its made by gyproc and its called Duraline. Its density is 927 kg/m3.... Do i use the same screwing schedule, Edges: 8 inches apart, Field: 16 inches apart. my studs are @ 24" centers. Also is it safe to do a half schedule on the 1st layer and then a full schedule on the 2nd final layer? thanks for any advise hally
Soundman202016 July 2018#2
Do i use the same screwing schedule, Edges: 8 inches apart, Field: 16 inches apart. my studs are @ 24" centers.
I would expect that the same schedule is applicable, but don't take my word for it! Ask the manufacturer.
Also is it safe to do a half schedule on the 1st layer and then a full schedule on the 2nd final layer?
That should be fine, as it implies that the first layer will have 50% more than is needed, and the second layer will have the right amount, so I don't see a problem there. Just remember that the screws for the second layer need to be 15mm LONGER than the ones you use on the first layer, to ensure the correct penetration depth into the studs. But check with your local building code and/or inspector, to make sure that is approved practice where you live. - Stuart -

Soundman202019 July 2018#10
Any suggestions on what my minimum air gap filled with insulation could be?
As Greg mentioned, that depends on what your isolation needs are. How much isolation do you need (in decibels), and what's the lowest frequency that you need it for? Those are two key questions. Personally, I would not go much below 4" (10cm) for the air gap, regardless of wall construction or material densities, as the other resonances inside the wall can start t be significant with small gaps. - Stuart -
