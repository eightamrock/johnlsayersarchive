I've just bought a house in Los Angeles with a 3 car garage, and I'm going to turn the 2 car (20'x20') into my writing studio. All I need is the writing/control room, and a small machine room/closet. I won't be doing much live recording, if at all. I've called some of the major dudes - Boto Design, Studio 440, etc. and they all rough-quoted around $75,000 to design and build this single room!! There is no info I'm leaving out - I didn't request something special - some crazy design, or a specific African imported wood or something....just a room that will sound good that I can trust.
