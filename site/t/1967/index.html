John Sayers26 July 2004#10
fitZ - I've always considered a membrane/panel absorber to be just that - a method of absorbing low frequencies. Usually you see them used on walls where you want to absorb bass but not highs. They can be angular or curved to add diffusion as well as breaking up parallel walls. Whereas a bass trap is an area where you attempt to trap the bass, like in the cavities/corners using the depth off a wall like I've suggested be created at the rear of the room in this thread. Simply by lifting the 703 off the wall you lower the absorption frequency - it's recently been discussed in the acoustics forum and Eric kindly answered all that area. Hangers could also be used in the corner cavities. The depth off the wall could also be used for slot resonator in the rear corner if you want to keep the high end up and the reflections won't get back to the sweet spot, but a slot resonator is not a bass trap as they don't go that low efficiently. cheers john
