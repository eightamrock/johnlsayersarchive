Aloha and welcome to the forum Chris :D Congrats on your studio build! Any chance you could please:
Edit your profile to include your location. This is very important, because this is a worldwide resource, and as such, material costs and availability vary widely. For example, masonry is cheaper than gypsum in some parts of the globe, whereas it's the exact opposite in other regions.
This will really come in handy two or three posts down the road. It's also part of the forum guidelines: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=3231 Thanks! Hey, nice drawing Chris!
Insulation and sound proofing will come from a Gyproc Soundblock layer with mineral wool inbetween layers.
There's a couple of threads here somewhere pointing out cost/effectiveness of regular gyproc/drywall vs the Soundblock line. You might be able to save some $$$ by comparing the two. A search on Soundblock might prove fruitful.
As you can see from the pic the control room will look out into the live room.
Without having any measurements of the control room, I would hazard a guess that you might want to reorient the room 90 degrees so that your monitors fire down the length of the room. Looking forward to your build! Aloha 8)
