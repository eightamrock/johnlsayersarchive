Well it depends on how big you NEED the drum room, your space is similar to mine (26x12) more exactly 26x11.5 (was mine). My Live room is 12x10, Fits a nice full drum kit and a Bass player or 8 people comfortably. I get a great Drum sound out of her. Of Course it gets loud in there when the Drummer cranks it up. LOL My Control room is 13x11. Fits about 6 people (max) 4 comfortably. So I;d probablt got 12x14 on the CR and 11x12 on the live room based on your dims. (25x12) I saw a nother dim that said 29x12) if so then I;g go more like 12x18 for the CR and 11x12 for the live room. Realize these are ruff dimensions for the room without treatments. Just my suggestions. My Clients spend more time in the CR than in the booth. and wehn we cut sessions, I usually have bass and keys in the CR/ Drums in LR and add Gtr later or run him through a pod or something while he is in the CR.
