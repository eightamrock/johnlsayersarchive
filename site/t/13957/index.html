jbassino17 January 2010#2
First you should read the forum rules before posting. But due that i'm in a good mood right now, i'll share my thoughts. Big rooms behave different that small rooms, perhaps, a big live room kept untreated might sound great for drums or a real piano, You can even attach false wood slats just for looks and still have a nice big room sound. For small rooms, you might keep an eye on low bass build ups and strange modes that might play with your recordings, and then you should look for devices like resonators, absorption or whatever you need to contain those bad things. Some big live rooms found in famous studios still have some acoustic treatment, some are pure brickwalls, others are fancy modern buildings designed by modern studio designers to get a perfect or desired response and reverb. It all depends on what sound you are looking for

Soundman202028 January 2010#25
That looks a bit better, except for a couple of points about the CR: The room is now wider than it is long. The ideal is to have your speakers firing down the long axis of the room, rather than across the short axis. You seem to have a sink in the rear corner of the CR! Is that a sink? If so, then I reckon you would not want to do that! Several major issues come to mind: noise from running water, humidity from water, splashed water, acoustic isolation issues (water pipes are greater conductors of sound), and probably a few other too. There is also the issue that everyone has to go through the CR to get to the bathroom! Probably not a good idea to have the bathroom directly attached to the CR anyway, for the same reasons mentioned above. I think I'd try to separate all the utility areas from the acoustic areas. You have no place to put your corner bass trapping! All four vertical corners are occupied, three of them with doors and the fourth with the sink. You also have no place to put your speaker soffits: once again, the front doors are in the way. Finally, you do not seem to have any isolation at all between your rooms! I see what might be two-leaf walls around the entire perimeter, but all the other walls seem to be just a single leaf. I'd also suggest that you do your design model in SketchUp, where ii is much easier to make changes. - Stuart -
