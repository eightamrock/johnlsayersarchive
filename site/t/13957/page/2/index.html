We would like to use John's example here to build something MUSICAL rather than continue struggling with my various concepts when there's a perfectly amazing design right there to go with.
Maybe you need to take a look at room ratios, rather than "how deep / wide". You might know this already, but I have some free time, so I don't mind putting it in words: The ratio is all about the proportion of the width and length to the height, and is expressed as 1:W:H (usually). There are a number of know "good" ratios that have been worked out over the decades (Sepmeyer, Louden, etc. Go with one of those for your CR, and you'll be fine "musically". There are also some ratios that you absolutely should stay away from, such as whole number multiples, like 1:1:2, or 1:2:3. Bad idea! I didn't check John's design, but I'll bet you find that it is fairly close to one of the good ratios, and a long way away from all of the bad ones! The easiest way to do this is to use one of the room ratio calculators here on the forum, or elsewhere on the internet. Start with your ceiling height, plug in whichever other dimension is a limiting factor for you, and out will pop the remaining dimension.
"One thing I would like answered is how they control the sound with those sliding glass doors? Where do they get doors like that? Are they some kind of special acoustic treated doors? That is the only thing that would scare me about that design...those things might be 2 grand a pop."
Yup, that sounds about right! Not quite sure how expensive they might be in your neck of the woods, but when I looked into them here, they were way out of my price range, so I erased them from my design, and replaced them with more traditional very massive solid-core doors, with double seals. Those sliding glass things are great, if you have the budget, but for the rest of us beefed-up thick heavy wood is the only option, with smaller fixed glass windows in the walls. - Stuart -
