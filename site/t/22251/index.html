Gregwor28 January 2020#2
He cut a 3" hole through my double 5/8" drywall/Green Glue decoupled interior wall and straight through to the exterior wall.
So he didn't follow your instructions and put as "S" shape with the bundle of goodies from your inner to outer leaf?
How should I fill these large gaps?
Colored caulk has about twice as much surface density as drywall. So, if you can fill caulk to at least half the depth of your drywall you will be good to go. Chances are his caulk job is thin considering he clearly doesn't realize the importance of keeping the holes small and/or studio building whatsoever. You might be smart to try and carefully dig out his caulk job and redo it yourself. Greg
