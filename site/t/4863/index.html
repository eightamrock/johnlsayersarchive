Glass thickness - don't want to over or under spec!!

Started by JohnGardner on 19 December 2005. 2 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 4863.

JohnGardner19 December 2005#1
My walls are: 2 x 16mm plasterboard on the control room side. 1 x 16mm plasterboard on the live room side. Seperate wooden frames with insulation in stud cavities. The air-gap between layers of plasterboard is 125mm in total. I don't want to over (or under) spec the glass for the windows. The airgap between the actual glass will be 125mm. What thickness glass do I need to order to keep up with the walls JohnG
Image not preserved: Window.jpg
bert Stoltenborg19 December 2005#2
Glas is about 3 x the weight of plasterboard, so you need 1/3 of the thickness of the wall construction.
JohnGardner19 December 2005#3
thanks 10mm and 8mm it is. JohnG
