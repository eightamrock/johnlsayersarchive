I don't think I would want my chillout room on the opposite side of the control room from the studio entrance. Any time someone comes or goes, they have to go through the control room, which can be really distracting. But maybe that's just me. :D
Soundman202024 July 2012#3
I was thinking the same thing about the chill-out room! The sound lock also seems to be wasting some space, and I think if you turn the control room around and slide it over a bit, you could put in some glass and get a nice view of the live room. Just a suggestion... Also, the doors in the rear of the control room are right where the bass traps should go, for maximum effect. I would move the doors further forward, to allow space for those. - Stuart -

Soundman202025 July 2012#6
Here's another curve ball for ya: If you slide the control room all the way over to the left (all the way up to the "misses storage"), then you can move the sound lock to the other side, with its door into the live room, so you no longer have to go through the control room to get to the chill-out room: now you can also go through the live room. And since the live room already has another door, you now have two paths to everywhere. Yes, you could use the sound lock as an iso booth. You could even design the chill-out room to do double duty as an emergency second "min-live room"... for drums, for example. Just some more ideas... :) - Stuart -
