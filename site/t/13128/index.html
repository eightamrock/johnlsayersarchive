So as I get closer to breaking ground.. I've realized something. While my joists can handle the additional weight, I realized that I will not be able to add mass to the subfloor over the entire room, but the area where I cannot add it will be over the largest air mass. Detailed explanation and diagram: The room will be approximately 10', with the front of the room sloping down to approximately 7' (see diagram) both for acoustical purposes as well as to avoid existing ductwork, pipes, etc. I realized that I would have to completely remove all the aforementioned ductwork, pipes, etc to get up between the joists to add mass to the subfloor above them - which may be more trouble than it's worth. The area in the diagram that is RED is the area that would be very difficult to get to. So - if I can't add mass to the subfloor all the way to the vertical wall, is it a waste? Or should I add it where I can?
