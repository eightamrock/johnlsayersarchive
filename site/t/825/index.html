knightfly16 October 2003#12
The main thing to get right while you have extra help is the construction - adding wall treatments, depending on how elaborate they are, can be done without much (or any) help. Your booth needs to lose the parallel walls if possible. The minimum TOTAL splay between two opposing walls to kill flutter echo is at least 10 degrees - John has had consistent luck with 12 degrees. If you are splaying a single wall enough to get a 12 degree angle, it takes an offset of 25.5" per 10 feet of wall to get 12 degrees. If you're doing a symmetrical room with 6 degrees each side, then half that, or 12.75" per 10 feet is what you need. If you can't splay the walls, you may be able to build some of John's angled slat resonator traps, shown here under "Side Wall Absorber" = http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm Those could be added after the fact as well - don't rush building this sort of thing, they need to be done right and tight... Steve
