DanDan23 December 2021#2
Nice looking workroom, but it's fun to work there. Looks like your speakers are quite far from any solid boundary. I don't know the Atomics but have seen other Amphion rigs lacking LF. I believe that room acoustics, including virtually anechoic, utterly dominate what we hear. So I see no point to secondary reference speakers. Unless they are in another room. Dirac, two sets of speakers at unlikely locations...... Simplify I reckon. Speaker and corresponding Listener location are the most powerful treatment.
