sharward19 July 2006#7
I know you're not going to like this... :roll: ...But I saw an illustration a book that I bought that shows a "service corridor" concept along the edge of a wall -- basically, a two foot gap between the outer wall and the framing of the inner wall. The corridor gives access to such things as sump pumps, plumbing valves, electrical service panels, etc. In your case you could really benefit from one of those along your "bottom" (looking at the image) wall because it would increase the distance between your outer leaves where those two windows are, in addition to providing a pathway to the electrical service panel -- assuming such a plan would meet code (it might -- you'd have to check with your local building department on that issue to be sure). You would also have access to the windows, which is really good. Yet you might not be able to get away with it, codewise, on the basis of emergency egress... :roll: At any rate, the benefits would cost you pretty dearly -- two linear feet all along that wall. :roll: On the other hand, I didn't realize until now that this is a basement, so if that electrical service panel is below ground (and it appears that is the case), then maybe I'm overreacting to the whole thing. (Entirely possible). Yes, I missed your initial "New house, help with basement studio" thread... :oops: --Keith :mrgreen:
