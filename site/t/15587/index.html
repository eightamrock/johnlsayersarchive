gullfo11 February 2011#6
looks like a decent plan. i would suggest some clouds spanning the peak to reduce the focusing effect. in the CR this may be a hard surface (maybe a membrane absorber) to also the symmetry of the ceiling so the peak is behind the desk and then use absorption to control the reflections. in the live room, just hanging down some absorbers (spaced apart maybe 300-500mm) to use the overhead peak for trapping. some scattered absorbers on the angled walls to get the room response to where you want it.
