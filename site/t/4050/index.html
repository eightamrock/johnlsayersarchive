John Sayers18 July 2005#9
transformer wrote:
i have 2" tape machine and also a patch bay that conects to the console
The control room is a similar size to your original. The 2" machine can go under one of the rear speakers and the rack gear goes in the rack directly behind the console area.
and i also need space for guitarists and keyboard players to have enough room to move when there are other people in the control room.
the keys sit on the rack bench and the guitarist has plenty of room.
I also woulds not like to have a drum kit in the small area of the studio, as thats how my current set up is and i would like to have more air around drum kits ( i record lots of live drums) this studio will mainly record live rock bands.
I put the drums in the main room mainly to show the kit in scale - set the drums up in the middle of that room and that's about as live as you will get in the space you have. this studio will mainly record live rock bands.
what do you think about using concrete blocks for the outside walls.
Besser Blocks are easy to work with and have a good STC - if you paint them both sides, or better still render them, the STC is increased. What are your sound isolation requirements? we need to know where you are going to build this relative to outside sound getting in and out. Cheers john
