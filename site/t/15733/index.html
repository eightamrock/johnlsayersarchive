welcome! in the control room, you want to treat any reflection points which interfere with your mix position. this includes the floor, ceiling, side walls, and back walls. typically in the back corner of the room you depicted, you would span that corner with a large trap. its probably too small to benefit from a numeric diffuser but could possibly use a poly on the face of the trap to help. in the isolation room - treat surfaces which could result in comb filtering into the mic. ceilings are pesky that way as well as the floor. in the live room, using a scattering of absorption on the walls and ceiling will help you to incrementally add absorption to the space without making it dead. depending on the ceiling height, this should be the first placement for absorbers over the drum kit.
