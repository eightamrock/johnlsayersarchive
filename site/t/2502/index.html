I'm just wondering if anybody has thought about using LED lighting schemes in a studio. You could have the room in just about any color you want to set the mood.
BriHar17 April 2012#2
I have them installed in my studio, and I have to say, the quality and amount of light is not good enough for working in. As a mood enhancement, particularly when being musically creative, and even tracking, they are ideal. Even during mixing - when listening back they can be conducive to the session. My main lighting are 12 halogen spots - switched on 3 separate circuits (no dimmers). The LEDs are on a fourth circuit. You may notice when using programmable colouring, there is a tendency to flimmering with a lot of the commercial products.
