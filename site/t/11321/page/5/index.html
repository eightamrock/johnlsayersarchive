BriHar31 August 2011#110
If you used proper Ductboard you should be alright. All Specs I checked out were explicitly treated against mold, mildew, fungus, and other microbial growth. Apart from that, installing a particle/dust filter in the input to the unit is always a good idea. Also adequate A/C and dehumidification in general to keep moisture levels down is recommended. Remember too that access to these units should be provided for cleaning the units internally occassionally.
