Adding to what Glenn said: This is exactly what Javier did in his live room, and it worked out great. Inside-out walls with slats on them. He built out a bit from the inside-out frames and put the slats across that, taking care to seal the top bottom and sides, of course. And that room turned out very well. In my opinion, it's one of the best-sounding live rooms in Chile. In fact, the way I understand John's concept of inside out walls, this is one of the major benefits: you get to use the space between the studs for treatment. In other words: you don't need to build a separate sealed module to place over your inside-out wall, and in fact if you did that, you'd end up with a three-leaf wall, since hte back of your slat module would act as a third leaf...
