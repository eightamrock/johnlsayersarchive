I need to make a decision regarding my flooring. I have a 9x18 isolated concrete slab on grade (installed with no vapor barrier). I'm trying to decide which flooring to get and how to install it. Should I get hardwood or engineered hardwood? Glued down or 'floated' over 1/8" underlayment? vapor barrier or seal over concrete? If I got a nice thick hardwood, wouldn't that resonate a lot less than a thin laminate? Thoughts, suggestions?
