camistan27 June 2006#3
Your wall design looks ok except for the "sheetblock type material". I would think it would be better to skip that and just do your frame with mineral wool, resilient channel, then drywall or "wallboard" as you call it. It seems like by adding the "sheetblock material you're creating a 3 leaf system. (assuming that the other side of the frame with mineral wool just has drywall (wallboard) on it). If so, then you would have: Mass-Air-Mass-Air Mass. By deleting the "sheetblock material" you end up with: The other side of your frame having drywall (wallboard) which is your MASS, your frame with mineral wool which is considered your AIR, your resilient channel with drywall (wallboard) attatched which is considered your MASS. You might want to think about having your electrical panel star ground since it's a new installation. Here's some links for you on that... http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=3950 http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=2206 http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=1330 http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=4625 Also, when you install your receptical box it's recommended using putty packs for sound isolation. (As long as it's acceptable by code in your area) Here's some additional info on installing outlet boxes... http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=1352 http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=163 http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewt ... &start=180 As far as your question about sealing your walls...YES, I would say you definately want to do that. There are others on here that are more qualified than me to talk about this stuff, but hopefully this info will lead you in the right direction. Hope that helps...
