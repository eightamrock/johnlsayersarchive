BriHar14 November 2011#3
In general the design looks pretty good. You will probably want to add some absorption on the rear wall, and possible the front between the speakers -although this might also be good for slats (front only). Not quite sure if that's a window you're covering on the left side, but you might want to address that a bit differently. Add some superchunk absorbers in the rear corners, and fill the front corners or use the entire free space for hangers. The 12° angle of the splayed slat walls left and right can be reduced given the room size. This angle only need be minimum 6° each for opposing walls. (12° for single surfaces) Also consider an angled hardback cloud above you and your desk 12° with the lower part at the front. The sofa looks a bit large for the room!
