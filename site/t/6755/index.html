gullfo13 August 2006#5
the drawings are done with Sketchup. I found some images of the mixers you described and their associated size specs. I then simply drew some boxes to approximate their shape and imported an the image onto it... the components are modeled in Sketchup, as components, that I use in designing the desk and layouts. same principal - approximate box with image on it and some cases "extended" the controls so in b+w mode they still look like something... when I post the photos and drawings of the desk, one of the sketchup files will have the components available for other folks to use... (mostly Behringer equipment, but its what I have/use... :-)

gullfo10 December 2006#7
John - I've watched the master in action... 8) in general I go to the web site (or google the images) to find a nice square-on shot and size specs and then clean up the image. I create a shape sized per specs and import the image on it. then explode it so I can make cuts etc... if i want it to survive losing the color or image, I'll take some time to draw out shapes over the buttons and knobs and extrude them to make it 3-d. last I'll shape the case to size to it accurately reflects the specs. maybe we can have a sticky to post sketchup components to? i'm sure people would like to have the ability to effectively model their layouts... I'll be glad to post my components up. I have a bunch including your wall units modeled...
