When installing RC in runs longer than 3M, should you make the RC joint at a vertical stud or is it okay to have them not touch but just a few mm gap in the middle of a stud wall "space?"
rod gervais12 February 2012#2
Paul881 wrote:
When installing RC in runs longer than 3M, should you make the RC joint at a vertical stud or is it okay to have them not touch but just a few mm gap in the middle of a stud wall "space?"
Actually - you should notch them so they interlock in the middle of a stud bay and fasten that joint (before installing the drywall) with a Tek screw.. http://www.bestmaterials.com/detail.aspx?ID=17160 It would be unwise to break the joints at a stud face seeing as you can't screw them at that point - and you do not want this stuff floating at which point you can develop a rather annoying vibration after your wall is a finished. Rod
