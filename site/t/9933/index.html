rod gervais27 December 2007#9
doublehelix wrote:
1) From the drawings in your book (pages 94 & 96), it looks like the stops are cut or notched to hold the rubber, is this true?
DH, Yes - but the drawing deliberately exaggerates the angle and depth so no one misses it - the rubber compresses a lot and easily - so make the kerf as shallow as possible - your best bet would be to make a few samples 6" in length until you found a perfect fit that allowed you to lock in the rubber. 2) To replace the rubber, you need to remove the stops?
Yup - sure nuff would - but - seeing as this faces no weather - no sun - and has nothing rubbing it in its length - - I don't forsee you having to ever replace the rubber in your lifetime........ Rod
