My one room studio needs minimal power consumption for my needs. I am getting some monitor hum when my laptop is plugged in and I get AC noise, clicks etc from my amp. When I place a ground adapter on my laptop the hum goes away. Bad idea I know. I only need one outlet for my needs. I read that one way to solve this problem was to run a seperate ground wire to a cold water pipe (Copper) and connect this to a ground adapter at the outlet. Use this plug/outlet for all equipment. Is this correct and safe? If not, what should I do? FYI. I'm using: Digi -002R 2 external hard drives daisy chained from laptop to -002R via firewire MIDI Controller Keyboard Laptop Powered Monitors 1 guitar amp That's it. Thanks.
