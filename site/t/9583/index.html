Clicking the link above ( I believe John Sayers developed that reference material) then construction, then the walls and ceilings tab and scrolling all the way to the bottom. Have you considered doing or have you applied dense material in this manner? That alone will make a large difference in who is telling who to turn down what in your home:) Something I noticed, the very picture I referenced shows three layers of material on the living side of the ceiling joist. The STC chart reads that only one point is gained by using three layers of material vs. two. And that only if you have a tile floor already installed on the top side of your ceiling. Just a thought,
