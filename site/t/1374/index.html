dymaxian26 February 2004#5
Try to make your room symmetrical around your mixing position. Otherwise your stereo image will be skewed. If you build your walls using the 'inside-out' theory you can get decend absorption without taking up any more floor space. Then you can dedicate the back wall to absorption. Kase www.minemusic.net

dymaxian27 February 2004#7
It started out as a layout thread, so I think you're ok. ;) Angling the ceiling in the mix position will help the sound a little, if you can do that. If you do have a flat ceiling, make it absorbant. You could hang an insulation cloud over the mix position, too, if angling the ceiling doesn't work out for you. If you can do it, try to angle the ceiling in the tracking room, too- that'll help out a bunch. As far as the floor goes, anything reflective is good. That one is pretty much dealer's choice. Good luck! Kase www.minemusic.net
