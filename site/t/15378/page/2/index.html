Is it possible by using a room with a good ratio (1:1.6:2.33) that you can arrange your control room across the width instead of the length? Or is this a very bad thing? I have seen some control rooms that look like the are orientated this way.
gullfo10 February 2011#27
the ratios can be applied in any of the 3 directions but typically is H:W:L. the reason for going along the length for the largest dimension is simply return time for the sound from the back wall. the shorter this is, the more you need to dampen that response to get it right. i saw the GS conversation and understand JHB's point on straight walls versus angled walls but i think some of the confusion on this topic is due to designs where the hard boundaries don't accommodate the necessary treatments. see the attached image as a reference in this regard. note that the side wall boundaries can be inside-out as desired.

gullfo23 February 2011#46
i prefer duct liner instead of 703 as its designed for the this purpose. if i use insulation, i wrap it in plastic. as a general approach, making the box 2x the area of the inlet (or the return) will reduce the air flow velocity which can reduce air movement noise - usually more critical in places where you use microphones. you also want the flow to be balanced in the CR so any noise is evenly dispersed.
