giles1176 May 2004#5
I am not the metric mac, but looking at your floor plan you have 4.4x7.4 meters, so dividing the space in half, you have 4.4 x 3.7 for each room or 14 x 12'. My entire studio was a 13x 12 room with a 13x 11 control room. I can cut drums in the live room. I guess if I threw up a baffle, I could cut gtr as well. (lots of bleed though) There actually IS enuff room for all 3 main players, but you will deal with bleed. Here is one possible scenerio however, Bass player and drummer in booth, gtr player in control room. of course with that scenario you could mic the gtr player behind your head and cut him whilst he is in the control room. :) Just be quiet when cutting Your space is not humongus so recording a rock band would require some placement creativity. Your Studio/Live room would then become a multipurpose room. Another scenario...... You have drums in booth, bass and gtr player in studio going direct. Afterwards (drums done) you bring the gtr player back in the studio so you can MIC his gtr and relay his parts (I am not a rocker but I know you guys love a mic'd cabinet) Just some ideas. jdean15 May 2004#15
did you also planned in your drawing the mixing desk facing east?
i planed on it being in about the same place you put it in your drawing. if that is east then yes.
the wall on the bottom of the drawing, what type of construction did you think of for the slat resonators. The ones in the trackingroom i understand, those are on John's example pages.
the wall construction depends on how much isolation you need from the outside and how much you have to spend. if the existing walls cut down enough of the outside noise, build the bottom (south?) wall out of wooden studs 24" (about 60cm) on center. place rigid fibreglass between the studs, cover the wall with fabric, then put up the slats. the space behind the wall could be used for hangers (bass trap), or you could put in a small door and use the space for computers and other noisey equipment.
Same also for the rear wall with the door in it.
the rear wall would be just like the "rear wall absorber" on john's page. the only "real" walls in the back of the room would be for the tunnel between the two doors. ethan's traps seem to work well (i only have two built). you could use them along with or instead of the rear wall absorbers. 2mx2m sounds about right for the drum riser, maybe 1.5mx2.5m. vocal booth could be about the same size, but if you've got the space and money, go bigger.
I i have the possibility to extend the building, what would then be a good size in square meters for control, studio, drumbooth and vocalbooth? 2x the size right now ?
1.5 to 2 times the size, i guess. if you're going to increase the size of the facility, my first suggestion would be to put in a bathroom.
