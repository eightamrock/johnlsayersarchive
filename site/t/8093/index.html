The isolation overall isn't THAT critical. The building had an existing plywood wall with hard foam insulation in it. gullfo8 February 2007#18
Ro wrote:
just looked at your floorplan, the CR shows double wall, but if I'm guessing the "inner" wall will not really be a wall (no mass).
thats correct, the CR "walls" sit inside an isolated room and merely guide the sound around the mix position, along with broadband absoption and some materials to keep some life in the room. behind the angled walls are some hangers (as well as overhead) to add additional trapping. Ro, you raise a good point about the backwall. it has a lot of absorption plus some reflective materials (about 25%) to keep it from being totally dead, plus some diffusion. the outer wall is somewhat "light" so the lowest bass will be passing out and some will be converted by the resonance of the outer wall as well. the back corners have some hanging traps as well. a main concern was the outer plywood wall becoming a third leaf but due to its construction and limitations on replacement so we opted for the other interior wall even if it meant less than optimal isolation.
