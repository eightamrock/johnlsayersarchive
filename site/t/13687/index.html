Soundman202016 October 2009#6
Starting from the outside... - Dry Wall - AudiMute Peacemaker - Rockwool - Dry Wall - Audimute Sound Absorption blankets on inside of booth
The basic idea is OK, but forget that "peacemaker" stuff. It appears to be something similar to MLV, which is basically just very expensive mass. Another sheet of plain old drywall would work just as well, and be quite a bit cheaper. The sound waves don't care how much you paid for your mass. All they care about is how much mass there is, and how well it is sealed. A very curious statement from the website for that stuff: "A 20 sheet set of Peacemaker sound insulation covers 125 sq. ft. and weighs approximately 2 lbs. per sheet, much lighter than other sound deadening materials." Ummmm... so if it is "much lighter" than other isolation materials, that also implies that it is "much less effective"! 20 sheets is 125 sq.ft, so each sheet is 6 sq.ft and weights just TWO POUNDS???? 0.3 pounds per square foot? Why would they advertise that??? You can't beat the laws of physics, not even with marketing hype... How can light weight be good for sound isolation? ( And then there is THIS statement form the same web site: "Sound travels in waves. Longer waves are fast and produce lower tones while short waves travel more slowly and produce a higher tone. " :shock: :!: :!: HUH?? :) Personally, I don't think I'd buy anything from folks with such a strange understanding of sound!!!! ) Anyway, you also need to address the issue of decoupling: you didn't mention where your stud frames go, but I imagine what you meant was something like this: - Drywall (1 or 2 layers) - Studs - Air gap with rockwool - Studs - Drywall (1 or 2 layers) - Acoustic treatment That's the normal way of building a proper decoupled MSM wall: Two leaves of mass, decoupled and separated by an air gap that is damped with insulation, with no mechanical connections between the leaves. And that also applies to the ceiling. Two leaves, separate leaves, fully decoupled from each other. - Stuart - (Edited to fix spelling errors)
