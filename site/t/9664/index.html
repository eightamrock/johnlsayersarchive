dynamike27 October 2007#4
the inside out wall approach (from my understanding) is more for situations where you know you want absorption as the finished surface. ie: vocal booths, iso booths, etc. if you know you're putting soffits in the front corners, and slot resonators on parts of the sidewalls, then those sections need to be finished off. you could build an inside out wall, then build the sealed soffits/ resonators and push them up against the walls, but that seams like more work to me. also, if you do that. . . you need to make sure you're not creating a 3rd leaf with your surfaces. what's the main reason for the inside out wall choice? do you have a build thread i can read somewhere? if not, maybe more details on what you're trying to accomplish. . . thanks for the word about my build. . .i'm SOOO excited to finish.
