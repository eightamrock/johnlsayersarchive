groundhog14 February 2010#1
I am considering building a portable sound isolation booth and came up with this. The sketch has insulation on the outside and a door that swings into the room in case I ever decide to upgrade it into a booth within a booth, although I will most likely just add a second door with airspace in front of the first door, as the door seals are the weakest link. It's basically 2' panels of double 5/8'' sheetrock with GG joined by pine board connectors that are lined with 1/4'' neoprene. all panels are 2' except for the ones on the door side. I made them only 2' -3' wide so that they'd be easier to move around. For the floor and ceiling, I'm thinking of 4 2' panels with male connectors that will fit into female connectors formed by extending some of the pine board connectors upwards and downwards (I hope that made sense). I'm having a really hard time visualizing a workable design for the floor/ceiling panels though. Any thoughts on how to make it work?
