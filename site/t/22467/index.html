You either pour them at different times so that you can access it, or if pouring at the same time use something easy to remove such as polystyrene, EPS or whatever. You could use a layer of polystyrene on each side of the form if you were worried that the polystyrene by itself would not be substantial enough, then once the concrete has hardened you can break away the polystyrene and remove it.
