Soundman202030 December 2008#2
A couple of comments on your control room: First, it is not symmetrical. That probably needs fixing. You want it as symmetrical as possible. Second, you have no space for bass trapping in your rear corners: One corner is a door, the other is a couch. If you could move the door over to the middle of that rear wall, you would go a long way to solving both problems! - Stuart -

Soundman20204 January 2009#4
So I just make bass traps next to the slot resonators on the back wall symmetrically
Yup! You want to keep the room as symmetrical as you possibly can.
Will the two parallel walls in the control room (left and right) cause me any problems? Should I have a diffuser on one of them to break the reflections?
That's not a very big room, so diffusors would probably not be a good idea. My guess is that absorption would be a better idea: You could make your entire back wall absorptive, several inches deep (except where the door is, of course!), plus the bass traps in the corners. - Stuart -
