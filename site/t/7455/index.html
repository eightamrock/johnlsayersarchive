DemoKing wrote:
The rise is about 8 inches for every 1 foot of stringer run. Is that too steep? I'm not looking to meet any kind of building codes, I'm just looking for "its decent" or "its steep but doable" or "its a death trap/ladder"!
According to this page...
    Per most Building Codes, the rise should be between 5" and 7¾" with the ideal rise for a residence being 7", [and] the tread should be between 9¼" and 14". An intermediate tread is 12".
So, you don't think you care about code compliance, eh? :roll: Check out the "Permits, Codes, Licenses -- and WHY YOU SHOULD CARE!" sticky thread over in Studio Construction forum. If there is an alternate method of entry and exit to the room, and the stairs you will be building are only for convenience and aren't likely to be used often, then there's a chance that you would be allowed to build an unconventional set of stairs that would actually be classified as a ladder of sorts. You should seek consultation from your local building department to come up with a safe and legal plan. --Keith :mrgreen:
