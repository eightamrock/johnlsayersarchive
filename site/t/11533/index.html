Forget about the Auralex on top of you panels. It's not a great solutions, and it's gonna cost ye.. as you already figured. For basstrapping you need VOLUME (amount of wool). Further more, we, indeed, need more info on your room. But in general; more wool = more trapping. Thicker=lower absorbing
