xSpace24 July 2011#91
simo wrote:
Unfortunately I have to admit I'm having trouble understanding your statement - sorry :oops: Could you please elaborate on that if you get the chance. Grazie
You have a roof with rafters with insulation and then you have sheetrock on these rafters (a coupled wall assembly in effect)' Then you are framing an interior ceiling which you will hang more sheetrock on. It would have, if it were possible, better to add the mass directly to the rafters (top side of rafters and possibly mass it up like you would do if this were an overhead floor) and then frame the interior ceiling with sheetrock on the interior side of the newly framed ceiling, in the attempt to make a fully decoupled mass/spring/mass system. What you have will be effective enough. But if I had it to do again...:)
