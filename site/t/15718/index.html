Mystic7 March 2011#1
So I'm looking for some help/advice on a studio I'm hoping to build this summer. I've been basing the design off the SSL Shed of your Dreams but I've run into a couple issues through my audio engineering courses (for those who don't know or haven't seen the design, I've included pictures). To start, I'm trying to do this on a budget of around $5,000ish USD. I need a structure outside of my house to work in and this seemed like the best way to go about doing it, but the more I've read into my course covering subjects like soundproofing in a "proper" studio atmosphere, the more I've come to realize that, for one, this design simply doesn't work and for two, it's going to get very expensive, very quickly. The original SSL design goes for $250,000 with the SSL gear. The cabin by itself goes for $25,000 to have Summerwood build it and ship it. The plan I had was to buy the plans from Summerwood since they sell those and build it myself. The problem came in with soundproofing the building. I don't have a lot of neighbours where I live, but sound seems to carry here since I live in a valley and if I'm tracking drums or vocals, I don't want a whole lot of noise coming from the inside. The more I looked at the design and the more I studied my course, 2 and 2 didn't really add up and I'm not sure what my options are at this point other than the following: I go with the Summerwood design and put in a proper booth to do live recording in. or I scrap the whole Summerwood design and go with something else that will be more soundproof. The problem is, when I started looking at what was involved with soundproofing, I came into the reality that the cost I was looking to spend was beyond what I'm willing to put into this because I'll be putting a much larger studio in once we build our new house in a few years. Right now I just need a place to work where I can think and is larger than the space I'm working in right now. The 16 x 24 shed looked like it would be enough room for just myself and my gear for the moment. So basically, based on my budget, what would you guys suggest or do if you were in my position? One last thing... concerning HVAC, I'm trying to decide what to do. I have a lot of gear and would hate to see it all destroyed by humidity, but I'm starting to think an HVAC system is going to be far too much to install on this particular project. Is there a simpler way to deal with the Pennsylvania humidity than to have a large HVAC system installed? Thanks guys.

Soundman20207 March 2011#2
Hi Mystic, and welcome! You should try to put some numbers to this, to determine what can and cannot be done with your budget. A couple of questions: that looks like it is simple one-leaf wood construction, with no special isolation at all, so I'd hazard a guess and say that you are getting maybe STC-20-something on that? Does that sound about right? How much isolation do you need? If you have a sound level meter, then you should do some measurements to figure out how loud you are inside there, how loud you are outside, and how quiet you need to be. Based on that (real numbers) we can figure out what type of additional structure will get you there, and roughly how much it will cost, then you can take the decision: "Is it worth it?". You are the only person who can answer that, but you do need to have real numbers in hand in order to make that call.
... it's going to get very expensive, very quickly.

is the building isn't built yet
Ahhh! OK, gotya! I was thinking that you had already built it and were not happy with the results! So this is good news: You basically have a blank slate, and you didn't blow any money on it yet! I agree with your initial assessment: there's no way that the shed you showed is going to provide anywhere near what you need in terms of isolation. Alright, so to put some goals on this: You want to build a studio in a space of roughly 16' x 24'. You need a control room big enough to put your SSL console in, and a small vocal booth. You need isolation of around STC 45 for the CR, and STC-60 for the booth. Your budget is around US$ 25k. The budget might be a bit on the low side, but the basic plan sounds entirely doable, and the isolation goals are modest. I would suggest that you put together a basic SketchUp model of what you have in mind, then post it here so we can take a look at it and offer suggestions. If you look around the forum you'll find many designs, in-progress builds and completed studios, and you might see something that you think would work for you, so you can use that as the basis. This sounds like a fun project! - Stuart -
