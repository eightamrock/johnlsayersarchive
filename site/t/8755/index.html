Fill cracks & such with matching cement; Paint with Oil Based Concrete Paint with grit in the paint for non skid surface. Anyone want to chime in on this or some other solution? TomVan10 May 2007#2
Jon, Almost there, Yes clean the floor first with a degreaser if needed Typically you would acid etch first and then nuetralize with TSP. But you cant find real tsp in Oregon anymore. Dont know about the rest of the country. So any High Ph soap will work to nuetralize the acid The acid actually leaves a residue that you need to clean off with TSP or soap. Make sure its cleaned well and then very dry before applicating your coating. Just an observation. Enamels in high traffic areas generally dont last very long and need to be touched up periodically. Also adding grit makes it harder to clean. Unless you are putting this in your mail room and need it for traction from rain tracked in on the feet I would not recomend grit. Unless you feel you need it for safety reasons. Enamels are basically very economical but a budget 2 part epoxy would last much longer as long as your prep is done correctly Tom

frostgfx wrote:
I was told to use TSP, then a steam cleaner to mop up the TSP. Then come back with Muriatic Acid to neutralize the alkalinity of the concrete.
Jon, Flip those - it's backwards............. first acid - then neutralize the acid with TSP.
Fill cracks & such with matching cement;
Not cement - you are better off using a product like levelastic for very small patches - it's self leveling - easy finish and adheres great.
Paint with Oil Based Concrete Paint with grit in the paint for non skid surface.
I wouldn't use a concrete paint on the slab of my dogs doghouse........... Your mailroom is a fairly small area - go the couple of extra duckies and do an epoxy finish........ Here's a comany with some nice products: http://www.dexotex.com/index.php?href=p ... query=0-26 Have fun, Rod
