sharward6 July 2006#3
Welcome to the forum! I'm not the expert around here, and I'm far from a design guru. Hopefully John can chime in with some thoughts when he can. Off the top, I'd say your 2x6 walls are not sufficient. You should do double framed walls -- using separate 2x4 frames for each side. Also, the farther apart the frames are from each other, the better isolation you'll get. If they're five inches apart, your leaves will be 12" apart (3 1/2" stud, plus 5" of space, plus 3 1/2" stud), filled with insulation of course. Have you seen some of the designs that John has come up with over the years and some of the "tricks" he uses to get things symmetrical in control rooms without wasting a lot of square footage? He's a genius when it comes to that stuff. Often you can come up with an idea, and he can make it better with a twist here and a tuck there. Buy Rod's book. You'll be so glad you did. I wish it had been available when I started my planning -- it would have saved me a lot of painful hours on the laptop! Consider isolated slabs in your case, with deep (12"+) footings along the edges of each room. This is covered in Rod's book. Also, up the depth of the slab to 6" and use rebar, not a wire mesh. And just for fun, throw in some fibermesh! That's what I did in my project. Hopefully that's enough to at least help you feel acknowledged ;-) and to get you book buying. And hopefully others will contribute as well. (Bear in mind we're coming off of a holiday weekend here in the states, so that may explain the lack of action on your thread thus far.) --Keith :mrgreen:
