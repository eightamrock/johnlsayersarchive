Stick21 September 2005#1
Greetings. Ok, so, I'm getting close. I'm talking with some architects here in town to draw my plans based on this rough plan in order to get the needed permits and to help me through some of the construction details I don't have time to figure out myself. But before I get into it with them, I'd like to run my rough plan through the ringer here. My questions are mainly acoustic treatment issues at the moment, hence posting here in the design forum. When I get closer to the construction I'll head back over there. I tossed around soffit mounting the speakers, and went back a forth a few times, and have decided to go floor standing because I don't want to commit to my speakers I have now, I'm not terribly crunched for space, and I need to cut construction costs where I can. I figure I can always go back for them if I decided that my new monitors will work well that way. I'm not opposed to rearranging, but I like this orientation for the control room (the window looks out over the front yard and beyond into the trees). Oh, and the ugly wood box to the left of my chair is a MIDI keyboard... too lazy to draw it, and I couldn't find a pre-drawn component. Everything's moveable except the fireplace, including the bathroom, though it does have to go on the wall at the bottom of the drawing for plumbing purposes. To the drawing: 1. Speaker placement/listening position Am I going to have problems with reflections off the window on the left and the (maybe glass) door to the machine room on the right? The walls are angled, but I'm unclear as the whether that's enough to eliminate the right reflections. Also, what about the window to the front? What if I made that front window even bigger? I really want to be able to see outside while working... 2. Low end trapping Should I be planning some sort of treatment to the front? Bass traps in the corners behind the speakers? I don't have a good feel for how much trapping I'm going to need. I've drawn in a slot trap on the right, but is my room going to be too bright as it is? Should I just make it a straight bass trap with a soft front? I was assuming I wanted hi frequency reflection there symmetry with the other side of the room. Maybe it doesn't matter so much because it's past my listening position? On the bottom of the picture, those are floor to ceiling bass traps with a queen sized Murphy bed (you know, the beds that flip up into the wall) in between. I figured I could finish the underside of the bed with a cloth frame of some sort so that the matress would be additional absorption. How are those big traps constructed? Solid rigid fiberglass (or probably Roxul in my case), or just the part near the cloth with fiberglass, and air space behind it? 3. Ceiling Unfortunately, it's probably going to have to be parallel to the floor, probably around 8 feet. I was guessing a series of clouds would be the way to break it up. I'm guessing the clouds should be angled toward the back of the room, but should they be hard on the underside to reflect hi freqs, or would I be better served by more absorbtion to "raise the ceiling" acoustically? 4. Tracking room (mainly for vocals and guitars) Ok, I've got the non-parallel walls going, except for the ceiling, but again, probably an absorbtive cloud will do the trick for the flutters. What about low end in there? My guess is that I should put corner traps across some of the corners (or all of them?) The size of them is limited by the windows to a degree, but there's probably enough to make is sound ok... 5. Other details I haven't worked out how to deal with cable conduits. I'll be building on a cement slab, so underground isn't going to work. (Though, I will be up on a couple of layers of stuff under the floor... maybe an inch and a half or so... hmmm) And of course, punching holes in the sound walls isn't good for isolation... What other solutions have you guys come up with? The bulk of my gear will go in the machine room to the right, but obviously there are video monitor connections, MIDI, speakers, and other stuff, not to mention the mic and heaphone lines from the tracking room and back of the control room... perhaps I should be thinking about a small patchbay and rack for mic pres in the control room (or built into a custom desk in the future) for connections. I also haven't really thought about lighting in the room either. I like the look of halogen cable lighting, and am fine without dimmers as long as I've got a few options for zones... so maybe IKEA is the way to go there, with them strung in between my clouds on the ceiling, and up the walls in the back of the room or something. What am I forgetting? I know this is a ton of questions and a lot of issues. Feel free to pick one out to discuss, and hopefully others can chime in on what's left. Much appreciated, brian
