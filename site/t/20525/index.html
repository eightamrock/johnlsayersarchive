Paul8816 June 2016#1
For various work related reasons, I never got to fix the Gyproc layer to my studio but I'm now planning to restart the project in September this year. :D So I now have further questions. Would the additional cost of laying 5mm foam tape to the resilient bar be worth the effort and outlay; would I get measurable additional isolation through to the timber stud work?
Soundman20206 June 2016#2
Not really. Once the drywall is decoupled (via the RC) then decoupling it again won't gain you anything. There might be a very slight damping effect from the foam, but probably not noticeable, and not worthwhile. Save your money (and time!) to spend in places where they can have more benefit. - Stuart -
Paul8817 June 2016#3
Brilliant, many thanks Stuart!
