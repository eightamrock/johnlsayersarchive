Hillside21 January 2006#3
Hi Deluk, fibre cement is sheeting just like plasterboard exept much heavier because its made with cement and is also quite reflective. The sheets are 7mm thick and the same width and length as plasterboard or drywall, the ceiling is completey covered with 2 layers of fibre cement board then above that are the rafters with insulation with a 300mm air gap which continues down through all four walls, above the rafters there is another 3 layers of the fibre sheeting then the roof. Ive built the structure from scratch and it is seperate from my house, the outside walls are 140mm thick concrete blocks all core filled with concrete inside the inner cavity to help with sound proofing. You might say its overkill but its what i needed to do so my local council whould approve the studio along with an acoustic report that set me back $1000. The room specs are 8.1m x 4.7m x 2.8m the ceiling is pitched at 20 degrees so the height at the centre of the pitch is 3.5m the walls are plasterboard and the floor is concrete and no control room im a drummer so i need all the room space i could get. Sorry that i dont have pictures a digital camera wasnt in the budget gonna try borrow one today. Mick.
Deluks23 January 2006#4
Sound like a great project, we'd all love to see some photos (plus if you are asking a question, a picture tells a thousand words :idea: ) Well I'm no expert, but it looks to me like your ceiling will be a big weak spot in your soundproofing. The walls seem to be perfect, 140mm concrete kicks butt, and then I presume theres an insulation filled airspace? ...and how many layers of plasterboard? I'm not sure that 14mm of any material is thick enough for a leaf, especially where drums are concerned. You probably shoulda gone with an inch of plasterboard instead. Incidentally, what is the outer roof made of? The whole kit will sound terrible if you haven't treated your room acoustically, the hits will bounce of the hard surfaces and cause all sorts of muddiness, you should definitely be thinking about some clouds on that ceiling. Cheers Deluks
