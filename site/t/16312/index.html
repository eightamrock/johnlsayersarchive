gullfo24 June 2011#2
welcome! depending on how you like to work, over the console view or side view there are pros and cons. having the side view - you could make a larger window/door configuration so it's easy for everyone in the CR and live room to see each other. over the console view may make it easier for some folks to manage riding the controls but often there is a lot less visibility. on the room - if you looked out over the console the room is much less deep and much wider - this can work but may be more effective to shoot down the long path.

To be more specific, I am seeking advice on creating non parallel walls in the control room. As it turns out I am quite close to the Golden Ratio on the dimensions so that's a plus. gullfo27 June 2011#4
on the walls- you can make the isolation walls parallel and use angled treatments, or use angled isolation walls (minimum 6 degrees each, more is better) then add treatments as needed for LF trapping and HF reflections. on the ratio, a golden ratio isn't necessarily the best one and there are lots of ratios to choose from as well as ranges. Chris Whealy's control room spreadsheet is very useful to help fine tune those dimensions.
