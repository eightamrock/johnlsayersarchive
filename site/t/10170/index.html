Hi guys, I've been looking around on these forums for quite some time now and found some very valuable information up until now. I've looked trough quite some drawings and plans, but I didn't find one that matches my situation though :) So here I go: my "studio" is located on the attic and has the following measurements: Width: 3.5 Meters Length: 8.5 Meters Height: 3.7 Meters There is one thing though: the roof is angled from the floor up to the top (highest point 3.7 meters) making some sort of triangle. On one side of the roof there is a window that is approx 0.90X1.25 Meters that has the same angle as the roof has. The roofs are made of particle board, covered with 'standard' rockwool roof insulation (sorry to be kind of vague on this but I don't check under my roof every day). The floor is concrete, covered with (thin) carpet. Sorry I couldn't deliver any pictures/drawings, I'm at work at the moment ;) My aim is to provide at least a decent mix spot. I do record vocals/guitars in the same room, so its desirable to treat the room in such a way that also recording can be done with a reasonable result. Loudness is not the first priority since the only loud thing I record is an electric guitar which i could set at a 'decent' level. Furthermore, at first I would like to spend about € 500, so $700. I understand this is a very tight budget and I might do more later on, but at this point this is what I'd like to start with. The main thing that I don't know what to deal with is the angled roof and the effect it has on the acoustics, just some info on this would really help me out.
