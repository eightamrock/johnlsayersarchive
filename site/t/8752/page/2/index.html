camistan21 June 2007#27
Hey Redeye, I was looking at your pics particuarly the one with your door frame. I'm not sure if having steel studs makes the difference but you need to add 2 king studs on each side of your jack stud (the jack stud is the wood 2x4 you've installed that are the "sides" of your door frame. You "may" not need the king studs if you will only have a regular door there, but if you're going to "beef up" your door, it will become a lot heavier, thus you will need more support from your door frame. In the event you need to add the king studs, do a search on here and you'll find some posts that show you what the frame should look like.

Hey Redeye, I'm not so sure it's the 6x2 frame as much as it is the king studs being attatched to the jack stud, being attatched to your door jamb. I would think it's the strength of all 3 studs that helps support the extra weight. The weight of the door would be pulling more from the hinge side of your frame I would think. Also, as I said, you have STEEL framing so it COULD be that the steel would be strong enough to support a heavier door. If you have Rod's book, check pg. 93-98 for some good info.
