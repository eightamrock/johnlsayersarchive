xSpace28 March 2008#2
Locate the diminsions of the panel on the above ceiling with a light pencil mark. Come inside those marks 2-3" both ways. I'm thinking that if it looked like it was simply "there" after the install this would be OK? Anyway, install a 3"(*) X 1/4" eyebolt at the located spots on the ceiling, into the ceiling joists/wood preferably. A smaller size eyebolt should be installed after pre-drilling into the four outside corners of the panel. Some small stranded wire and clamps can be used to attach it to the eyebolts. (*) 3" may be 1/2" too short or long so you get what you need ;) Thats my hack for the day.
