AVare18 November 2007#2
stevesdata wrote:
1. where to find the most professional guide possible to build a helmholtz resonator, also any companies that sell them?
Helmholtz resonator design is straightforward physics. The best document with design AND test verification is BBC RD 1992-10. Of particular importance is section 7.2. As you design for the problem frequencies, you can use a single unpartitioned rear chamber. Depending on your prof's personality, he will readily accept it, or jump down your throat.
2. how to tune bass traps to specific frequencies.
You build them, install them, and then measure the effect of them in the room, and modify. Repeat.
3. why do some experts in this forum favour broadband solutions over tuned? - my other research suggests this too : /
Somewhat vague. Most advice follows: Treat mf and hf issues with hf absorption Use bass traps for general LF control Use tuned absorbers for modal peaks/nulls. Where it normally falls apart is when writing to people who are good carpenters, but have no desire to do calculations. Cut and build makes them happy.
4. I would love opinions on how to attack these frequencies, the rest of the room is actually not too bad due to book cases. Its literally only these 3 modes we would like to address.
My suggestion is a common cavity Helmholtz resonator with two frequencies absorbed by cavities and the third by membrane absorption in the absorber. RD 1992-10 gives the core idea from studying section 7. Enjoy and let us know how it turns out! Andre
