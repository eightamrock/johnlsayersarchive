gullfo19 April 2011#2
presumably you don't need a high degree of isolation so from a treatments perspective it should be a fairly straight forward exercise - one - use rigid insulation based absorbers - most times it more effective in lower frequencies and if you build them, a lot less money. two - 4" (100mm) thick rigid insulation absorbers on your first reflection points - side walls, ceiling cloud, between the speakers (mounted on stands behind your desk close to the front wall), and rear wall. on the corners stand up rigid insulation vertically with pink (lightweight) insulation and cover it with plastic and cloth. acoustically measure the room to identify any additional problem frequencies - floor-ceiling modes likely to be a problem, leverage the closet to be a bass trap if possible.
