I'm considering using 4" thick Thermafiber insulation. If I pack it too tight between inner and outer wall, will I loose my isolation? i.e. how much can I compress insulation before it starts to conduct structureborn sound? Creature82
sharward8 November 2006#2
I'll take a stab at this before more knowledgeable folks can be more specific. The catchphrase around here (from Steve most of the time) is that slight compression is what you're aiming for -- basically, enough to apply a little pressure on the drywall or sheathing for the purpose of dampening, but not so much that it becomes a structure-borne flanking path. How wide is your air gap? --Keith :mrgreen:

rod gervais9 November 2006#7
BTW - to answer the original question - unless you fill the entire cavity (from the front wall to the real wall) with material - this isn't going to be an issue. If the 2 walls are not connected - compressing insulation within the individual bays will never created a source for flanking tansmissions. It could very well give you a slight decrease in isolation - but unless the 2 individual frames connect - and are thus "bridged" - flanking will not occur. Sincerely, Rod
