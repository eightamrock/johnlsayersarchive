John Sayers27 July 2008#5
The idea of the bottles has legs as I first suggested it a while back. Each bottle would act like a helmholtz resonator and the different bottle sizes would broaden the frequency range. I figured that if you had a range of different sized bottles and you stacked them against a wall they would act as low mid absorbers plus add diffusion to the room.

John Sayers28 July 2008#8
I can't find the link scair - basically if you blow across the top of a bottle it will make a note - if you record it you should be able to find it's frequency on a realtime analyser. Stuffing it with insulation will absorb the note. If you had a range from 4 liter (1gal) up to small coke bottles you should get a fairly broadrange of absorption and if you stacked them against the wall they should offer some complex high frequency diffusion.
