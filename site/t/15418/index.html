I would keep it and use it for the monitor mixer. Just tap the inputs needed off your patch panel. Then get the onyx for your recording needs and feed the sends/returns via the patch panel so you can tap them. Your remaining audio is then fed via the FireWire. Another option if you need automation on the mixer - consider something like an m-audio device which has I/o and automated sliders and recall etc.
