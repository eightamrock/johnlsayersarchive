Tom, not only will it get those results - it DID get those results. You're looking at test data of an 8' high x 10' long wall assembly. HOWEVER - you may not acheive those total results depending on a lot of other things - construction of ALL assemblies would need to rerach those limits in order for you to realize the benifit. One of the reasons these walls work well is the " floppy" nature of the light guage metal studs. Rod
