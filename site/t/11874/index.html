OK - getting close to finalizing details so I can get started.. HVAC is the last stumbling block (for now). Here is my plan - I fully expect you fine folks to find major faults with it.. but that's why I'm here. So.. important facts first: Adjacent to my planned studio build (2 rooms, control room & drum/amp room) is a 'mechanical' area. My whole-house breaker boxes are there, network patch panels, video patch panels, etc.. and also 2 of the 3 air handlers for my house. The two air handlers are for the floor where the studio will be built, and a 2-zone for the main floor of the house. The system uses forced-air gas heat.. so the same air handlers/ducts are used for both. The area where the studio will be built was designed by the builder as a home theater, and is currently fed by the downstairs air handler with HVAC - this room was designed to be serviced by the whole-house HVAC including a lot of equipment and a half-dozen people watching movies. I opted to go for studio instead of theater. The plan.. I would like to move the existing duct/register that feeds the studio area over into the mechanical area, and possibly run second duct/register from the 2nd floor air handler into this area as well.. This would keep the mechanical area well cooled/warmed as needed - and would function as my exchange chamber. I should be able to run a fairly large duct from there into each room - probably 10 or 12". I can then run the return back into the mechanical area, or vent it out elsewhere. The plan is to have the fan on the supply side, creating positive pressure in the rooms. So, as I see it - normally, using part of the whole-house HVAC is a no-no.. but in this case, it was designed to cool this area including a lot of bodies and equipment.. I also have the benefit of two air handlers worth of supply air, cooling, heating. So - there's my plan.. I think it's fairly well thought-out (probably wrong).. poke holes in it, please!! PS: any issues with not having a grill on the supply registers? was thinking of just leaving the gaping hole, and hiding it with the ceiling 'cloud' above the listening position. Thanks in advance!
