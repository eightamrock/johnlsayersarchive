Max, If it's annealed, any commercial glass shop should be able to cut it - it's a simple matter of scoring both sides and then using a pair of grips with a thin rubber protector on both edges to perform the break. If it were laminated you would be able to see it easily from the edge - so it isn't laminate - Tempered glass IS safety glass - and cannot be cut by any means - it's physically not possible. Rod
