I thought the whole reason of calling it a sunroom was due to the windows involved. Your saying this is an all tin room? The room in a room you want will require you to add additional mass to the exterior part of the structure since the tin has no ability to stop much sound at all. "its 14.5' x 19.5'." Sound is 3D in nature...we have to take into account the height, if you would be so kind.
