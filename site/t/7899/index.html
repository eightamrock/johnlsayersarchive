Aloha Bob, Congrats on your new space/place! One issue that keeps popping up for above ground locations is "can your floor support the weight of the studio - extra walls, drywall, equipment, instruments, and people?" Safety During Demolition, Construction - http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=3393
Be sure not to overload your joists. Adding just one layer of gypsum to your ceiling could transform an otherwise safe ceiling to one that can kill. This span calculator (courtesy the American Wood Council) is helpful in determining the maximum span of a joist under a variety of weight parameters. For more information about loads and span tables, see Understanding Loads and Using Span Tables by Paul Fisette, as it appears on the American Wood Council Web site. When in doubt, consult a professional structural engineer.
Span Calculator – The American Wood Council http://www.awc.org/calculators/span/cal ... cstyle.asp Be safe. Looking forward to your build! Aloha 8)

gullfo9 February 2007#15
put in a wall in front and on the side of the console and some trapping along the wall by the stairwell. not much else you can do, and everything less than 3-4' high isn't something you can stand under although you can tuck equipment in. it not optimal but if you cannot use the main room for a CR you're going to be constrained for size and sloped walls. putting a wall in front of the stairs will just make the CR even smaller... another option is to swap the living space with the CR so you have some more symmetry and room. you'll need to add in a big window/door to see through there... if it was me - I 'd set up my living space in the far corner of the big room :)
