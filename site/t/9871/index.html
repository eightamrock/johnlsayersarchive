dynamike11 December 2007#2
i used self leveling mortar in my bathroom before i tiled it. not sure the square footage you need to cover, but that stuff works great. it was all uneven, i poured just enough to cover the whole floor with about 1/4" at the thinnest point. dried overnight and it was perfect.
Cram11 December 2007#3
i'll second the self levelling mortar. I did this for my parents basement. 250 year old house and the floor was a bit dodgy to walk around for the laundry room. 7 years and still looks good. have someone out to give you an estimate on work to level it? I bet there are several ways to look at this.
