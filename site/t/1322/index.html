barefoot15 February 2004#2
Congratulations on your new speakers Sen! The woofer crossover is 420Hz. This has a wavelength of 82cm, which is much larger than the distance separating the woofer from the midrange and tweeter. So the orientation of the woofer makes no difference. However, the position of the woofer and ports could have some influence on the excitation of room modes. But this still doesn't mean the bottom is the best orientation. The left, right, or even top position could turn out to be best in this regard. Thomas
