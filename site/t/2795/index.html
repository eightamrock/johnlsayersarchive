barefoot12 December 2004#9
Drake, Keep in mind that the wall will only act as an effective diffuser down to frequencies whose wavelengths are about 1/2 the average feature depth (D) of the stones. Lower Frequency Limit ≈ 6900/D Where D is in inches. I would guess that the average depth D is around half the depth of the deepest 'caverns' in your rock formation. ;) Thomas
