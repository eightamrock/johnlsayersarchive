knightfly8 April 2006#2
Nope; windows are pretty much just walls you can see through; a single leaf of glass gets beat by a spaced double leaf, even if it's only a couple of feet wide. Naturally, it's better to have two fully independent leaves with insulation between for ANY sound barrier, but even a single frame wall with panels on both sides is better than having panels on only ONE side. Windows, partly due to their lack of insulation for damping, can benefit by having vents between the panes leading into the surrounding, insulated, wall cavities - I don't have specs on how much, but this will let the window "breathe" which will lessen the amount of sound that passes through the window slightly...
