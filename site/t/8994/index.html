camistan22 June 2007#4
For my speakers I'm planning on supporting them on what I call their own "island". Basically, I'll build my speaker stands with concrete/cinder blocks and fill the insides with sand. I'll then mount the speakers on the blocks and build my soffit frame around the stand-speakers. By doing this I'll be decoupling the speakers from the frame which should decrease flanking sound transmission. There have been others to do this method, but the only one I can think of is Aaron. Be SURE to read through all of his soffit construction as I believe he wished he had done some part of his speaker stand construction differently.
