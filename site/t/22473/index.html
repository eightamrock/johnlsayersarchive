Hi guys, I posted this on another forum which already gave me great info but then came across this one which looks perfect. I'm having a garden house built in a few months and want to move my studio into it. I don't work crazy loud and don't record live instruments like drums, guitar amps. Only work on studio monitors and record singers. I don't work crazy loud. I did a measurement with iPhone apps but they seem all over the place. It gave me readings between 80dB and 100dB (which is a huge difference) of the loudest levels I'm working at. I have a drawing of the garden house. I went to the showroom and tested my studio speakers inside the house at the loudest level I'd ever work at. Surprisingly it already soundproofed quite a bit but I do need a bit extra to be able to work at night. After doing some research and with help from others I think I need a double leaf wall system. Unfortunately there's not enough space to make the ceiling double leaf too so I was planning to suspend the ceiling double layer plaster boards with whisper clips. I have a few diagrams and drawings of I think it should work. Any advice / tips would be much appreciated. I know that the windows / doors will be a whole other issue and I'm already looking into it.
