orangenumerik19 November 2008#4
Hi Fulltato Stickies are at the top of the list of the forums topics. Concerning your 41hz problem, That frequency is very low, to have a sensible reduction at that frequency you"ll either need to be able to built something that is at least 1/4 of the wavelenght of 41hz in thickness (6.75ft) filled with wool or bulit a tuned panel bass trap or membrane resonator, call it what you want, wich would aslo be huge. All of wich are not aplicable in you situation imho. Six inches deep Broadband traps are definitely not enough to control reflexion at those frequency. More and bigger trapping and in ALL corners (3 inches is far from enough 24 inches would be a minimum), trapping all your ceiling would also help. The smaller the room the more problematic they become especially at low freq. You problem may also reside outside your room , that frequency maybe a reflexion from other boundaries outside you room. In any case, this a simple problem with a complicated solution that woul need a lot more info and lots of money for an acoustic solution. keep looking, good luck.

I wonder if a Helmholtz resonator would work for you?
Altough they work at low frequency,they usually are more suited for broadband low-mid control. That being said building a slat resonator tuned at 41hz would look like this : slats 1 inch thick by 8 inches wide spaced 1/8 inches appart with a 3 feet deep cavity behind the slats. there are myriad of variation but they all take lots of space. If you are willing to loose 3 feet of floor space that could be a solution.
