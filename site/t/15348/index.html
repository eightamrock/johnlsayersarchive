gullfo7 December 2010#2
welcome! please update your profile to include your location. it sounds like you want to remove the amp from the active monitors and then soffit mount them with the amp externally accessible and also with a means of redirecting the heat? or just take the amp out and put it on the side? couple of thoughts: 1) the monitor might be affected by the removal of the amp in terms of the speaker cabinet and possible any wiring dependencies, or exposure of the electronics; 2) the amp should be out of the way (which it sounds like you want to put the amp on the side) of the monitors to ensure the heat does not cause thermal issues or some electrical/magnetic interference with the response. in theory it should be possible. you might need to put some metal shield between the amp and the speaker if you experience interference. ensure safety so no high voltage is exposed - maybe the metal shield could be a generic ventilated box large enough to mount the amp into. http://media.digikey.com/photos/LMB%20H ... 5%20PL.jpg
