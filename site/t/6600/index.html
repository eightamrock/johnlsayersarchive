In your plan the right rear speaker was where the door is which would inhibit you using a true 5:1 setup and as you mentioned that you did 5:1 it would be prudent to have a proper 5:1 setup. the spaces at the rear allow for rear trapping which is where it should be and you plan didn't allow for that. Many have soffit mounted those Mackies with good results. What you lose in the rear port you gain back in the soffit mount, and better IMO.

nathanvacha12 July 2006#9
Actually antho, they do. They have bass traps designed into the rooms but they are a part of the construction so you don't notice them if you don't know what to look for at least. How big are those rooms you're talking about though, too? For a small-ish room though, like you're going to get in a residential space, you'll need much more bass trapping, and broadband treatment because you have much more potential for acoustical problems.
