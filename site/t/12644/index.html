Soundman202011 May 2009#5
A couple of quick comments on your plan: 1) You seem to have your control room (CR) walls splayed the wrong way. They should be closest together at the front of the room (where the speakers are) and widening out towards the rear. 2) Your CR is also not symmetrical. There is some type of furniture on the right side that looks like it might be a bookcase or something like that, and it destroys the symmetry of the room. In order to mix accurately your room must be as symmetrical as you can possibly make it, so that bookshelf should go in the storage area, not the CR. 3) It's hard to say from that rough outline model, but it does look like you have a four-leaf wall between your CR and LR. If that's the case, then you definitely should fix that. Regarding door placement, the best place for the access door would probably be in the center rear of the control room, and perhaps with a pair of sliding glass doors between the CR and LR, in the location where you have the window at present. Or you could keep the window and use the vocal booth for access to the LR. You could put access doors pretty much anywhere you feel like in the LR, I think, as dictated by the surrounding structure. - Stuart -
