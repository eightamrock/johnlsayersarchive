Overview: Finished converted 2 car garage for Drums/ band Rehearsals/Tracking Isolation is good enough. Goals 1) Reverberation time at about 0.5 sec. 2) Diminishing the intensity of early reflections uniformly all around the room. 3) About 85% to 100% coverage for acoustic treatment. (sorry I don't like any flat hard drywall around me) it's kind like small and loud in here.... Questions: 1) I would like to achieve the goals above but I need to keep it very simple.. it's just a garage, how? 2) (see images), the last layer of drywall toward the studio, (walls only), does it help for Isolation ? absorb some extra bass frequencies? or it worsen mode and resonances and should be removed to match the ceiling? Options: 1) I'm open to modifying the interior shape/dimension of the room if suggested as an acoustical treatment. Construction details: See images, "The density of the high density rigid mineral wool is 0.61 x sqf. (the formula is 0.61 x 12 divided 5/8" thickness.)" rigid acoustic tile material.
