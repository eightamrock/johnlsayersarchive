Hi yann, and welcome to the forum! Sorry about the LOOONG delay in responding to your thread: it's been a crazy month for me, and there don't seem to be any other people responding right now...
I respected the angles of 30 ° angles or will the monitor speakers and 12 ° to the side walls
30° for the monitors is fine, but you might not need to angle your walls by 12°. The purpose of the 12° angle is to get rid of "flutter echo", but there are other ways of doing that, that don't wast so much space. And even so, you only need to do the front half of the room like that: not the rear half. So you can make your side walls at the rear parallel, without any problem, which gives you much more space at the back.
The final volume is correct?
The volume should be at least 1500 cubic feet (43 cubic meters) for a really good control room, but it can also be less than that and still be very good. It looks like you have concrete floor in your room, so you could just leave it like that if you want (concrete is a really good acoustic floor), or you could put laminate flooring on top, to make it look nicer. - Stuart -
