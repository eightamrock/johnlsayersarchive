Soundman202012 March 2012#5
if I did this, how much gap would be required between the old joists and the inside out ceiling's plasterboard to avoid too much resonance?
The gap there doesn't really matter: as long as there's enough clearance to prevent mechanical contact, that's fine. The resonance occurs mostly between the leaves, very little happens between a leaf and the joist in front of it. As long as you can get some insulation in there (but without jamming it in!) then that's all you need. Maybe an inch or so is fine. I suggested the inside out more for the sake of simplifying construction than for acoustics: either of your first two methods will work, but having the drywall all on top or all below the joists makes it easier to build, and easier to seal. If you don't mind the extra work, then probably your first idea is the best overall. On those joists: do they have to be 2x6? Any chance you can use a tougher type of wood and maybe bring them down to 2x4? Just a suggestion. By the way, please check the forum rules for posting: there's something that you seem to be missing.... :) - Stuart -
