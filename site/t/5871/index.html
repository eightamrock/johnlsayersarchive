Opinions on my monitor short list:

Started by lomky on 22 March 2006. 2 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 5871.

lomky22 March 2006#1
In order of present preference: Dynaudio BM5a Tannoy Reveal 5a KRK V6 KRK V8 My current setup is TannoyProto J's w/ Hafler TA1600. The TA1600 has developed the common problem of a bad side. I have read and searched that others have the same problem w/ this amp. I've searched/read through peoples thoughts on the preceding monitors. So now I am looking for anyones specific experiences. This list is also based on availability in Edmonton, Alberta. They are all in about the same price bracket (except the V6's are a little cheaper than the rest) My room is as yet untreated, so I am going to concentrate on that before I bring anything home. Currently we are renting and I am using a 17'x10' with 8 foot ceilings. We plan on buying a house in the next year with an unfinished basement, and with my construction/contracting experience I'm going to design and build a new space. But as my amp is crapped out I need something in the mean time. I want something that will grow with me, as I am tired of the buy cheap buy twice. But $1200 CDN is my budget. The studio that I track in uses KRK V8's and Tannoy 800's. I like to listen to the Tannoys, but I trust the V8's more. I find they are a nice compliment to each other though. I plan on doing some critical listening in the stores this weekend, but unfortunately I will be bouncing between a few different dealers.
