gullfo5 July 2006#5
if you get 14 U rack units on casters, they should put the desk at the right height and allow it to move around. the desk itself sits squarely on the 2 rack cases so stability shouldn't be an issue. the back table would only hold the smaller speakers and monitors. if you use tube type monitors as opposed to LCD panel, then a beefier table would be needed.
John Sayers5 July 2006#6
the common desk height is 72cm or 28" It's a good idea to lean the units back so they slope back at around 6 -7 degrees, it makes it easier to see what the settings are at a glance from above. cheers john
valgaer5 July 2006#7
About the common height yes, that´s the average and has some ergonomic reasons to be like that, but what I´ve found is that with that heigh if you have a chair with armrests it could hurt your elbows after long periods of time, I´m thinking, at least for my size 64.5 cm from floor to keyboard height would be fine (I`m 180cm); plus if you have a lower height you will have a clearer version of your controller. My suggestion is to sit on your chair and put your arms in a position you feel confortable about and measure it :D
