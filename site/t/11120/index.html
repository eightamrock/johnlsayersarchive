A lot of information that I have read suggests that this should be taken as a one room project. I realize it looks "cool" to have a live room and a control room, but not only will people be cramped, hard to get air in and/or out of and also difficult to tune PLUS the money goes up...but you will not get either of the two small rooms to sound near as good as one larger room. If I were you, I would read much more about this subject, read until you decide that you do not "need" two small rooms. Welcome
