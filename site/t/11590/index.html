Great! OK, so to summarize:You do not need any isolation at all, since you are quiet and don't bother anyone. All that you need is acoustic treatment to make your room into a good practice / recording / mixing space for acoustic and electric guitar only, no other instruments. The room is for this purpose only, nothing else. The room has no windows and only one door. Is that about right? Assuming the above is correct, it is going to be hard to use that space well, due to the slope of the ceiling. You have to get more than a meter away form the front wall before you have enough height to be useful. Even at 1.37 m your ceiling height only just reaches 2 m. So basically, there isn't much that you can do in the first meter or so of the room (starting from the front wall, moving towards the back wall). You can't even fit in small speakers at normal listening height until you are about 1 m into the room (allowing a few inches for minimal treatment on the ceiling/wall behind the speakers. The walls are not plumb or parallel, or even perpendicular to each other or to the floor, in places, so it's really hard to even find the center lines of the room, in order to place anything! Are you sure all those measurements and angles are correct? If so, then you sure do have some strange shape walls, that lean over at one end and are vertical at the other! One good thing about: You won't have any problems with flutter echo! :) I think the room it might be workable. I'd suggest the standard approach for acoustic treatment: bass trapping in the rear corners, live floor, absorbent ceiling (or at least a cloud over your head), and deep absorption at first reflection points. You have lots of space for bass trapping in the unusable area in the first meter or so of the room, but I'm not really sure how you should treat that steeply angled ceiling. My guess would be absorption, but it's just a wild guess! Then once you have the basics in place, I'd measure the room, and see what additional treatment you might need. Anyway, if you can check all those dimensions and shapes and angles accurately, and post a new SketchUp, then maybe someone can try to figure out a workable layout for you. - Stuart -
