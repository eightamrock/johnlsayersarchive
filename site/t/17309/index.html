Drums122510 January 2012#1
Hey all, First time poster here, but I have been reading the forum for a few months, now. I was about to make the mistake of buying Auralex studio foam, but when sticker shock sent me to Google in search of a cheaper and more effective solution, thanks to this and other forums, I learned of the benefits of (DIY) acoustic panels made with OC 703. Anyway, I'll have plenty of questions as we go forward, but for my first post I wanted to let my (Northeastern) American brethren know that I picked up 12 sheets of 2" OC 703 from KAMCO SUPPLY of New England for $92. Since even the cheapest places online charge over $70 for 6 pcs, I figured I'd share this significantly lower price. If you're within driving distance of a Kamco Supply, you can save yourself quite a bit. They had to order it, but they just include it in their next OC shipment, whenever that happens to be. For me, it was about a week. Best, Chris
