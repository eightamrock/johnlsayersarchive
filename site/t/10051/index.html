I'm building superchunk bass traps. The face will be 24" across. I want to do a really good job and have the fabic nice and tight. I don't want any sagging. What are some of the ways others on this forum have attached their fabric across the face? Thanks!
jwl16 January 2008#2
Hi Brad, A common approach is to build a separate frame for the cloth.... think giant speaker grille.... and attach it to vertical framing on the wall, probably with diagonal cuts so the frame sits flat on the support boards. This way the frame can be as tight as you make it and the absorption will be totally hidden behind it. Since the frame is separate, you can pull the cloth nice and tight and attach it from the rear. If the fabric stretches or ever loosens for any reason, it's a reasonably quick fix (pull the panel down, tighten the fabric, and reinstall).

BradJacob wrote:
I'm building superchunk bass traps. The face will be 24" across. I want to do a really good job and have the fabic nice and tight. I don't want any sagging.
I found that doing material attachment is a lot like installing porch screen Brad. I staple one of the short sides first. Then pull the opposing short side tightly to place and staple that. The longer sides can then be stapled, one then the other. Watch your fabric when placing the first longer side so that any visible design lines in your fabric are not pulled so tight that the design warps;)
