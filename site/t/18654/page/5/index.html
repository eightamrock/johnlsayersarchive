There's the usual SBIR from the rear wall, and there's the usual bass rise (how do you have the controls set on your speakers?), and what might be a floor bounce, but nothing out of the ordinary. I'd say your room might be a candidate for digital tuning. However, there are some issues: the room is a bit too dry overall: You should have a decay time of around 200 ms for that room, but yours is more like 140 ms. The good thing is that it is fairly even across the spectrum, with only a slight dip in the low mids, but it could do with livening up a little. You might want to consider plastic over your bass traps. You might also want to consider some form of diffusion: your room is borderline-big enough to do that, I think. By the way, you are probably looking too closely at your REW data (zoomed in a but too much: you are seeing trees, but not forest). THe setting I normally use for looking at the low end is to set the "limits" button to 18 Hz and 500 Hz for frequency, 35 to 105 db for intensity, and -1440 to +1440 for phase. That gives you a good general view of where you are. You can also smooth the graph a bit: 1/24 octave is reasonable, especially for the high end. One other thing I noticed is an unusually large difference between your left and right speakers, particularly around 135 Hz. Any idea what that might be? What other treatment do you have planned for the room? - Stuart -
