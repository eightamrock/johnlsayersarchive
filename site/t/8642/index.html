This is a great site. I've turned many of my students on to it. And now I find myself in dire need of some idea's/inspiration for expanding my current home studio. The need for the expansion stems from turning my hobby studio into a home business studio. Making it a comfortable space and an attractive space that clients will expect when paying an hourly fee to record at my "garage". If I provide too much info, please forgive me in advance. My studio is basically half of a 2 car attached garage. The other half is a game room. Though the front of the garage has 2 separate garage doors, only one moves (game room) the other is fixed in place (studio). Total garage size is 22w x 21L. Garage inside has vaulted ceiling which is unfinished. I plan to do R19 and cover with 1/2 drywall the entire ceiling. Current studio is a room inside the garage 10w x 12L x 8 tall. It's just 2x4's with ply on outside, and finished with drywall 1/2 inside, it is insulated. I am on concrete slab. One of the walls shares the wall with the outside of the garage, the other 3 walls are all separate and free standing. I have one door into the studio which you enter only from inside the garage. I also have 1 door which enters to the garage from the back yard. From this door you enter into a space 10w x 7.5 L which is the pics I have shown with the pinball machine. It is this space I would like to convert into a control room, cut a window into the studio wall adjoining, building a wall to basically cut the entire Garage in perfects halves. That was my initial idea. And my other issue is air conditioning . Maybe using the gameroom, though large, as a cold room, then venting in that air to the studio and control room? But thats a question for another forum heading. So for certain I will need a separate vocal booth inside the studio. Other than that I am in need of ideas. Is the 2 room the best way? Or do the 1 long room? And other possibilities. ? I am hoping to use as much of the existing studio as possible, so knocking it all down and starting from scratch is not really an option. Also if I do the control room idea, that size of 10w x 7.5 L is going to force me to place my console perpendicular to the studio window. I'll stop now. Please any suggestions will not be taken lightly. Budget: $2500 materials (not incl HVAC), and this is all DIY. As for the pictures, the Pinball machine is the area I am talking about for the expansion/control room. You can just see the top of the door going into the studio. And the other door you see is the one leading to the backyard.
