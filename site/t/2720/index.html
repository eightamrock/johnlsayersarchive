jonbarrett25 November 2004#1
I have seen several references to Helmholtz resonatrs, etc. for the small studio. In particular, I was wondering if the ceiling could be finished off with pegboard, rockwool, and covered with cloth so that the ceiling and the unused spoace above it become a broand-band absorber?
AVare25 November 2004#2
Helmholtz absorbers are tuned absorbers, not wideband. If you use 6" of insulation you will get flat absortion down to around 90 Hz. if you 10.5" (eg. 3 layers of Roxul Safe n Sound) toy will get flat absorption down to 50 Hz! How wideband are you looking for? See fig 1, in [url=http://www.bbc.co.uk/rd/pubs/reports/1992-11.pdf]BBCD 1992/11] Widely absorbent; Andre[/url]
