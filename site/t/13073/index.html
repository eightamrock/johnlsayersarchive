You can if you want. More is always better. But with the SKP that you posted, it's pretty clear. General comment: Your control room seems to be backwards, in that it gets narrower at the back, on each side of the couch, with those 45° angle walls. Those are taking up the place where your bass trapping needs to go, and they don't seem to serve any purpose. I'd get rid of those. At the front, your door to the LR is in the position where your right speaker soffit or bass trap will be. Same thing: it has to go elsewhere. Also, your control room is not symmetrical, and your console and mix position are not centered in the room. Unless you fix those two, you won't ever be able to get a good stereo image in there, and your mixes won't translate well. Very important. Nest, that air conditioning duct down the middle of the control room is a HUGE pain. Move the entire CR over, so that it is outside of the room, and the problem goes away. You can just duct it in through the side wall. Speaking of side walls, your are not splayed! The are parallel, which implies flutter echo and also complicated first reflections. Splay them by 6°, and both problems go away. I roughed out a new design for you that gets rid of all these issues: And the SKP to go with it:
Recovered file: studio 5--G.skp
The main door into the CR is now on the left side, behind the first reflection point on that wall. I made the door from the CR to the LR into sliding glass doors, but you could also do swinging door there if you wanted. You could also splay the walls only half way, which would make the room a bit bigger. There are other minor things, but at least that should give you some ideas to work on... - Stuart -
