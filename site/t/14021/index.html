All right. project finished! Here are some pics.. I couldnt take pictures of the sanding etc stage but Thoma's pictures are pretty much the same thing. I did everything by the book except on the wool thing. In my case I found some little foam pieces used to fill some pillows and i though they would work as well. At least the resonance in the cavity was gone after I filled it with the foam pieces. I tried the speaker before the fill and I liked it a lot. It almost had the same mid range as the NS-10's but will less low end and smooth highs.
