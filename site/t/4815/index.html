knightfly9 December 2005#2
Depends on whether the builders care what you want and will listen; I'd opt for bottom floor first of all, so you have a snowball's chance in hell of doing a decent floor; don't let them build a 2-leaf partition between you and the rest of the space, you should add the inner leaf yourself - as much headroom as you can get so you can do a sturdy inner ceiling - no interconnected HVAC ductwork (you'll need to do your own for sound control) - get enough space to include a foyer for an airlock door - and read/re-read the REFERENCE section to pick up any points I'm forgetting. Just a start... Steve
