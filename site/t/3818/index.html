knightfly31 May 2005#2
I've not tried anything close to that, and I doubt John has either; he claims slot absorbers (Helmholtz) don't work much below 150 hZ, and I've not tried it myself so can't argue one way or the other. Maintaining a 1 mm gap between boards should be nearly impossible, especially with that thick a board; I think you'd be better off with panel traps at that low frequency. Placement is important too - for panel traps they need to go at highest sound PRESSURE, not velocity... Steve
