xSpace wrote:
What does the EPS do on the lower portion of the walls? Is it for a thermal barrier?
The EPS is a Vapor Barrier. The Rockwool gives all the Thermal you need. LOL. I plan on putting in a Mini-Split. My 1st studio, in the thick of winter with 10 below zero temps would Idle at 68 degrees with the computer running 24/7. Any warmer and the A/C had to run 24/7. Even with just 3 small lights (40W) burning, those rooms will get to 100+ degrees in no time.
giles1178 August 2009#16
Just some addendums... I am in debate about what speakers I will soffit Mount. Some oldies I have or some others. I am thinking of a Double speaker soffit design. 10" 3 ways up top at an angle and a Mackie HR Surround sound rig at 45" height (Ear Level) I have decided on the following Mix Desk build. I am moving totally over to the Euphonx Control surface Model and love this little desk they have for it. It is 65" wide allowing for plenty of room behind and on the side of the mix desk to the walls. I'll mount some small (5" or so) nearfields on here. I am leaning towards the Yammie HS-50M's. For straight cutting I am using Eurekazone Tools, Inexpensive rails allow for perfectly sized AND straight cuts. No, I am not a salesperson, I just LOVE these tools and for the guy like me who is NOT a carpenter, they work fantastic!!! I have my Router on rails (sorta like the Festool) and a Hitachi Circular with Saw Brake.
