I noticed you never did complete your vapor barrier. As a rule, an unbroken barrier is >required< in order for it to display the attributes of a barrier. Not only is yours "broken" it is missing entirely in some of the areas. This is only mentioned in that when you have moisture issues somewhere down the short road, you may have an avenue to trace the path to what is or is not creating this problem. A solid unbroken moisture barrier is a requirement, anything less is unacceptable. Vapor will creep around the broken areas much in the same way anything else water related will take the path of least resistance. It should be noted that this product is closed cell, more a thermal break than specifically used for a barrier. Unless in the case of SIP(Structurally Insulated Panel) or ICF to be more exact, of which this application does not meet that definition. I appreciate having lowered costs, we all benefit from this. But follow through and strict adherence to contemporary building technique is as important as anything in a basement application.
