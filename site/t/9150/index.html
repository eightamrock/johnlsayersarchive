Eric_Desart3 August 2007#3
I've seen something similar used. But the ones I saw were (as often) lightweight and become a panel absorber in itself with a clear resonance range. Such kind of diffusers should be stable. For labs as rule of thumb a minimum of 5 kg/m2 is described (better more). I know a lot of diffusers will not comply with this rule of thumb, but I also believe a lot of commercial related stuff will show side-effects not described by the seller. And it also depends on shape and so on.
