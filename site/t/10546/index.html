I'm planning on building a home studio in a house I am moving into soon. The building itself is about 200 years old but I will build the studio in a 1 story cinder block addition. I had a design idea to use recovered brick for one interior wall of the recording area a sort of a nod to the history of the house. So it would be stucco - cinderblock - insulation - brick. Is this wise from an isolation or reflection standpoint? Obviously the wall would get som treatments but I'd like to be able to see some of the brick when its all done. I like the idea but i don't want to deviate too far from standard practice and hurt the acoustics of the room.
