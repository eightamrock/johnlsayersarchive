knightfly8 April 2006#3
Stan, your CR isn't plane symmetrical; this is important for stereo imaging, whether you go for the "Dave Moulton, live sidewall" approach or the more usual "absorbed first reflection point" approach. Either way, you need to draw a line down the exact center of the CR and, for at least the front half, EVERYTHING on ONE side of that line should have an acoustic counterpart on the OTHER side of the line. Your door setup violates that, as does the slat res on the right-hand wall. Also, I'm not clear on why you've set the front of the CR back from the outer wall so far; is this something to do with room ratios? And did I miss a ceiling height measurement? I think the front of your CR and the entry between it and the vox booth can be much simpler, but need your answers to the above first... Steve

Aloha Stan, Thanks for the PM. :D A couple of thoughts to chew on :mrgreen: - Regarding the "Knightfly tip 2.JPG" - Would incorporating the helmholtz slat design into the door be viable? There's a couple of guys that have done this, one being Blue Bear Sound (click on update #6 and scroll about halfway down the page) http://www.johnlsayers.com/Studio/Mainp ... uebear.htm It might get tricky figuring out the clearance and which way it allows you to open, but it makes for a real clean install. - I think insulation in the area behind the diffusor on the back wall would be better than nothing at all. With all of the bass freq's that build up at the rear wall, it's better to have all the bass trapping you can get. (By the way, in reference to a post by Steve regarding diffusors, if you are sitting closer than 11' or so from them, you may hear some comb filtering going on. With a 23' control room, your mix position up front should be fine, but if you position a sofa near the back wall, you may hear some of the effects of the diffusor a bit there.) - Regarding the "Knightfly tip 4.JPG" - Good move on the side wall angles. :wink:
