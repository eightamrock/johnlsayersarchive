no, you don't need 8 yards. If it's 56" wide, you can cut it in (just over) 2' sections, and use the 56" wide part over the 48" part of your absorber. You have 6 absorber, 2.5' wide cloth (enough to wrap around the edges) each = 21 feet = 7 yards.
