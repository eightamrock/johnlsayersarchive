Thought I would be able to get rid of this corner closet all together and finish the wall to the edge - making a nice 90 degree corner for some 6" thick floor to ceiling broadband absorbers, or possibly "superchunks". However once I was actually able to see inside the closet, I found that this darn gas meter and pipe stick out a lot farther than expected. see pics. The closet projects about 12" forward from the rest of that wall, and the gas meter nearly as much. I'm not having much luck talking with the gas company about moving the meter - So finishing the wall even with the rest is no longer looking like an option. Any recommendations? Let's start simple - Should I Keep the closet though it would be relatively useless once I mount absorbers across the protruding corner (wouldn't be able to open closet door this way). Or remove the closet as planned - finish it flush at the bottom where I can and box it out w/ a trap panel (meter may need to be accessed at some point by gas co.)? Someone even suggested keeping the closet as is, but stuffing it full of insulation - It is a hollow door so I guess the low freq's would pass right through the door. But I'm not so sure how this would work in practice. I'm wide open for suggestions - but need to make a quick decision to keep up with the builder. This is for a mixing room that does not require heavy isolation - a good sounding room is the only goal here. The corner in question would be the front right when seated at mix position. See Gullfo's fantastic illustration below for a look at my room layout. Thanks in advance guys!
