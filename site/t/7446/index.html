John Sayers9 November 2006#2
That's a tough question. I can see why you like it - two good sized rooms for a cool little studio with kitchen and bathroom. You could soundproof it enough but it'd mean a floated floor and room within room construction - i.e. probably beyond your budget. How much of the construction can you do yourself? labour is a big cost factor. BTW - which room did you have in mind for the live room?? If it was the smaller room you could probably float that within your budget and keep your control room levels cool without bothering to float it. cheers john
