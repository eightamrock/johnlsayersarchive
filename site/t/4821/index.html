J I have been in a few smaller rooms that sound gorgeous! the BEST sounding one was about 8 foot across the front with about 22 degreed splayed walls on the sides going back for about 13 feet. The room was heavily treated with panels and tube traps. The front wall did not have a window in it but was about 2 foot deep rockwool covered by material. If I can find the pictures of it I'll post them. I personally am of the opinion that if you are not listening at high volumes you can have NICE room around the 11 by 13 foot size. Tom
