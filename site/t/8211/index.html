How important is slat thickness?

Started by etflutes on 17 February 2007. 2 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 8211.

etflutes17 February 2007#1
I'm making slats for my slat resonators and the wood that I have available is finished at less than the recommended 1" thickness. They are made from cedar and they are about 13/16". Is that acceptable? I didn't know how much latitude one had in regard to measurements. Thanks, Geoffrey
gullfo17 February 2007#2
check this spreadsheet: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=1363 depending on how you construct your resonator will determine the center frequency of it. a 3/16" difference means the center frequency will be different. if you are creating an angled "broadband" slat wall, its probably less critical than a high-Q resonator where you are targeting a small band of frequencies.
