What is a green roof?
guinsu25 April 2008#3
Basically a roof garden, you cover the top of the flat roof with anywhere from 4"-12" of soil and plant flowers, grass, vegetables, etc.. It has a lot of environmental perks, helps with storm drainage and has insulating properties. I am assuming that much soil would also help with soundproofing. It's big in Europe and is catching on in the US, here's some examples: http://www.roofmeadow.com/assemblies/savannah.shtml
gullfo25 April 2008#4
i think this is definitely one of those cases where you'll need some structural engineering as well as having someone with experience in designing green roofs - basically you're adding 20+psf static load to your roof which needs to be factored into the ability to handle the new static plus any live load. such as storage of rain water, etc that would be quite heavy... from a size perspective, it seems decent enough - you can probably get a good drum booth and control room in that space.
