AVare19 August 2008#3
Yes it is MAMAM. Have a look at fig 5 in IR 586 and see how the TL at 80-100 Hz drops with a triple leaf. If you want the best isolation with the materials you have shown, use 4 layers of drywall on one side. The limiting factor will become the window (assumed). The idea of having the bare brick wall is good. It will also give some high frequency diffusion. Andre

cyeazel26 August 2008#9
The best way to maintain a MAM envelope between your two rooms will have to involve you using your existing brick wall as one of the leafs, not a sandwiched layer b/w two separate partition walls as shown in your first drawing. This will include a bridge for sound to travel but it can be reduced by adding mass. If you build two new partition walls on both sides of the brick wall then you will have 3 leafs. It will be better to have two leafs even though one of the leafs (brick wall) will be coupled with existing construction.
