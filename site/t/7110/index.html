Greetings and Welcome to the site. First off, if you could update your profile to include city or country you're in, it would be much appreciated. Second, congrats! You're using a mac. It's all plug and play. Plug in the hardware, per the manual...such as your core card and/or other cards you have, connect the cables to the interface, plug in the power and turn it on. Install the software, and off you go. When I bought my first system with a mac, it literally took me a maximum of 45 minutes to unbox all the gear, install the hardware, load the software, and I was tracking within 45 minutes. If this is a new system, and you have problems, digidesign will walk you through any issues, or the company you bought it from (sometimes). :D
