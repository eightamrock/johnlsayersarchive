BriHar27 April 2011#34
For the bird's eye view you want: In the view menu activate the views toolbar, then click on the icon that looks like the roof. Next, in the camera menu, select "Parallel Projection" Zoom in or out as necessary. That should do it. Generally, for angles, use the protractor tool. Establish your reference axis by clicking on your point then moving the mouse to the end of an existing line or 'til it shows "on xxxx axis" (red, blue green) and click again. Next, move the mouse to determine the direction of the angle (rotation around the first point you clicked), then you can type an exact angle in the text box. Rotating existing entities/objects works the same. The best advice however is: for every object you create, either group it or make it into a component. This will save you hours of grief when you need to manipulate things.

gullfo13 May 2011#44
30 amps for the CR and LR should be enough (unless you have an older large console...) - probably split it on 3 15 amp breakers (i know it = 45) where one is the light circuit (not likely to take more than a few amps but 15 amp breakers are typically as low as you go...) and 2 more circuits for the outlet power - one per room. use a single leg off the main power for the outlet side to avoid any potential for 220v due to wiring or equipment defects. use the other leg for your lighting and HVAC (or other induction motor equipment). star ground for the outlets etc etc.
