gullfo4 February 2007#9
if you have the budget, you can have people design and build one for you, even equip it and do the engineering. Someone I used to work with a number of years ago recently finished "his" studio but he had it designed, built, and then hired a manager and an engineer to manage it and work the technical aspects. this enabled him to do what he loves (playing music - plus renting it out to others to pay for it...) and he didn't get burdened with the details - as John pointed out - he let the pros handle the parts he knew he couldn't. but it does cost more than doing it yourself...
