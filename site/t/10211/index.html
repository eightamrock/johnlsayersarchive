Splitting up a long space?

Started by Bonker on 17 February 2008. 1 reply.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 10211.

Bonker17 February 2008#1
My studio shall be a room within a room design and once these internal walls are built, my available floor-space is approx 3m x 6m x 2.4m high. I intend for the studio to be for my own use only (no clients) playing country and pop music. I originally wanted a large rehearsal space where I could play without disturbing anyone, but seeings as I am spending all this money and time building it, I would hate to not have the chance to add some kind of control room for recording. So my use for the studio will be: 60% time will be for me to practice my Keys/Bass/Drums without disturbing anyone. 20% will be used for recording music. 20% will be used for my various band rehearsals/jam sessions. However, I'm having a hard time splitting up this long/thin space into a control room + live room. I've had a good think and I cant seem to fit in both a control room AND a live room large enough for 4-5peice bands to rehearse. Can anyone else manage this ? So, my current design is aimed at using the control room as a rehersal room, and then building seperate iso booths for drums and vocals for when I would want to record. Would anyone care to point out mistakes in my design or suggest possible alternatives for me to play around with? Also, Im not sure if I have made my vocal and drumm booth too small but I didn't want to eat up anymore of the control room space as it also needs to be big enough to double as a practise room for a full band. Please let me know your thoughts. Thanks all.
