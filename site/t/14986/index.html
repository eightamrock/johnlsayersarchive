I am a student at a Recoding engineering school, and was assigned aa project to design a studio from scratch, inside an existing undetermined building, I looking for ideas on a design that fit the requirements, as well as what materials to use during construction, any help would be appreciated. With a budget of 30,000 USD build a recording studio space of 15'X20' (10' ceiling), and 20'X40 (12' ceiling), with both rooms sharing a common wall, design the most diverse studio possible. Being an acoustic assignment, your first priority is to create an acoustically sound space using room analysis. Following this, proceed to gear, aesthetic, and other concerns you may find in a studio design. Also required will be a floor plan detailing the layout and dimensions of your space. This essay should be approximately 1,500 words detailing your reasons behind the design, layout, and gear purchases of your studio. Electrical and building codes are not important, the equipment inside the room is the most important.
