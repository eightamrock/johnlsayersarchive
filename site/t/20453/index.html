FOUND: Insulation provider for NSW

Started by Samm on 5 April 2016. 4 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 20453.

Samm5 April 2016#1
After a solid few months of trying to nail down a provider who had 48kg/m3 insulation and would sell individual packs, I found one. Figured I'd share it here so other people can get in on it. Just-Rite have an online website where you can order boards of Bradford's Ultratel 2400x1200x75, containing 2 boards with no facing p/pack. Not insanely cheap, but the only option I could find. Ignore the 250 minimum order in their FAQ, when I went through the order process you could order a single pack. A word of warning though, their customer service was crap. The item was not delivered to my address. What's worse, the delivery driver knew it and no-one had bothered to contact me. I ended up contacting Just-Rite myself, who informed me that the driver had dropped it off at the neighbours house 5 days ago. Good luck, get in while you can! Cheers, Sam

Just-Rite's prices are nuts. Ambisol is cheaper and Network Building Supplies are even cheaper than Ambisol. I paid about $300 for 6 Boards at 2.4mx1.2mx50mm, but the delivery fee from NSW to Melbourne was about $300 too. Customer service was quite good. These items take up a lot of space so interstate shipping is inevitably costly.
