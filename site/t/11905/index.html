xaMdaM12 April 2009#2
Brinks is ok... I'm using them currently. In your area, I think they might be used to the video being transmitted across FIOS. If not, then I would think that they would be able to guide you to gear that they know is compatible with their alarm systems. The reason I'd go ahead and suggest Brinks is because their monitoring is indeed very quick and dependable. If they won't handle the video side, then I'd look at http://www.ezwatch-security-cameras.com/ to roll your own. One thing I keep hearing over and over... for exterior CCTV security to work (and be worthwhile)... you need to be able to CLEARLY read a license plate. If not Brinks, then ADT... The one thing I don't like about ADT in our area is that their monitoring fees are 15% more than Brinks, for the same thing.
