jaye21 October 2007#1
i ve been doing some research and found out drywall comes in two sizes over here, 60 cm wide and 120 cm wide.. is there any advantage in use in one over the other? also i plan to add 2 layers of drywall. would adding a third layer increase the performance of the wall by much or is it just really worth it, considering the extra cost/ time/ labour. Thanks!
PhilR22 October 2007#2
In a standard room, 2400x1200 sheets are good because they're long enough to reach to the ceiling and you don't have to worry about butt joints (which are tricker to finish if you're inexperienced with drywall finishing). Which makes the overall job quicker. In my case though, it's simply a case that 2400x1200 sheets are too big to be transported to the floor my studio is on without insane amounts of manual labour (carrying 3 pallet loads of plasterboard up 3 flights of steel fire escape is NEVER a fun plan!). Also, there's no way in hell 2 people lacking a drywall lifter can install the big sheets on a ceiling by hand. But the smaller ones can be lifted manually with a decent work platform.
