AdamZuf6 November 2007#6
You must understand that going from 5x5cm pieces to 2x2cm pieces is not to be underestimated, in regard to time spent. Time is money, sometimes you don't save a dime from DIY, just gain knowledge- I can't see the profit in this case- and take it from someone who tried it. Ahhh no problem I can make a DXF myself if anyone needs help - if this is saved from the CNC operator it will cost a lot less. Plus you can get creative, as it's your own design 8) CNC is worth checking, if you care about looks, and the price is reasonable in the states. Routing yourself will take a lot of time - really, I've tried it. A router table would make things easier, but yet time consuming- as you can't cut all the depth at one shot, at least 2 or 3 times in order not to stress the router and bit. Using a jigsaw will not yield good looking results- but that depends on what's your threshold for "good" :D Another option is to make it not so fine looknig and covering with a nice fabric of yor choice. RPG also mentions that this raises the corresponding NRC values of the diffuser and makes it more linear. But for myself, I prefer clients to say "what the hell is it? I never seen something quite like it!" I never seen a QRD or Skyline in Israel :)
