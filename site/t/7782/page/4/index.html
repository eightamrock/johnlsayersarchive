Dingo11 July 2007#82
Hi Stan - thx for the kind words! The indirect blue lighting effect (or any color for that matter) has been very popular with the people who've seen the studio "in the flesh" as it were - which is nice. It's quite simple to do actually...you just buy a light snake - the one's that are like a garden hose with LEDs inside them - and lay them haphazardly on top of the clouds. No-one will be able to see the light snake itself, yet it emits a light soft indirect beam around the top of the studio - very cool. Adds a lot of groovy atmosphere and aura to the place. The cabling is *almost done* - at least done enough to record some jamming ;) It took about 6 hours, patch bay taking the most time - and no, I didn't solder the cables then. Most of the cables were already made and transported from the old studio setup to this new one ;) Pics on the way... Cheers, D.
