Hi Dave, maybe contact the city's building office (usually town hall) to ask them about the permits needed and so on. the building inspector will often be able to point you to the forms and provide guidance on the type of people you need to do the work (although if this is your home, you may be allowed to do a bunch of the work yourself). http://www.toronto.ca/building/index.htm http://www.toronto.ca/building/building_permits.htm typically you'll need building, electrical, and permits for HVAC and possibly plumbing. http://www.toronto.ca/building/fastrack.htm (home building)
