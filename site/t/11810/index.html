Big bay doors!

Started by fenikner on 17 March 2009. 2 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 11810.

fenikner17 March 2009#1
Hey everybody. Long time lurker, first time poster. My partner and I have recently bought a building here in Tucson AZ that currently houses a lamination shop, and we're planning on building a project studio into about half of the building. It's a large industrial space- super high ceilings, concrete floors, cinderblock walls. We're going to be doing a lot of treatment for sure, but first we have to deal with a big problem- a large bay door. As in, a garage door made of that roll up aluminum type business, about 20' x 20'. The problem is, there's still a lot of large equipment in the building, and it may have to exit sometime, so the door needs to remain functional. We're thinking of building a wall out from the inside wall enclosing the bay door, and fitting that wall with a large set of double doors. We don't need to completely soundproof the place as it's not really a residential neighborhood, but currently sound pretty much travels in and out of that door as it would through open air. So, anybody ever had to deal with a similar situation? What did you do?
