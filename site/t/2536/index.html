Remo - sorry I missed your post - look your room is tiny really - you can't approach it like a large room with diffusors and bass traps etc. If you build those bass traps you'll end up with a live small room. Similarly with the diffusors.... not a good idea in such a small room. I'd look at putting in corner traps, either slots or just straight 703. Your one advantage is the ceiling where you could easily drop a false ceiling with hanger traps throughout. like this

ok ppl.. here are my semi custom non permanent bass traps! I finally got the time to make them! Let me know what you think of my handywork.. btw the sides are all 25mm MDF and the backs on the big ones are 18mm MDF.. they are also caulked air tight... I will be slotting 100mm of semi rigid fibreglass into the front of the deep bass traps (the fibreglass will be gently held in place with the lengths of dowel that run through the trap). the skinny one is more just a frame to hold a single sheet of fibreglass... I will be stacking the two big ones at the rear of my room and putting the skinny one at the front at head height.
