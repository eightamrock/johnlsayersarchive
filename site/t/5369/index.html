knightfly8 January 2006#4
Clearsonic are OK to reduce sound between drummer and other band members enough to make it less painful, but will NOT change the amount of sound that escapes the room. Where most of your construction is concrete already, that is good; Making plugs for windows would be the next step, also improving the seals on doors. Beyond that, there isn't anything that will make much of a difference except building a room inside a room. Your room's floor might take the extra weight, but it sounds like you don't have enough space as it is... Steve
