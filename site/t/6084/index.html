knightfly30 April 2006#2
You mention "golden section" but don't give ceiling heights; if it's your live room that's supposed to be 1:1.6:2.6, that would put your ceiling at 6.25 feet. Also, if this is to be a dedicated space, you will (I believe) cause more problems with parallel walls than you would by deviating from golden section ratios - right now, if your dim's are 8x10x16 (assuming "normal" ceiling heights) you already have a "hole" in your modal distribution (Bonello method) at around 80 hZ, and again in the 40-50 hZ range. The parallel walls will require more absorption placed on walls to combat flutter echo (only part of which is audible from a "clap" test, the rest will color your recordings without being obvious WHY) - IF you can add ceiling height to your dimensions, we can help you find good ratios and (if you're up for it) maybe a better layout...
