knightfly31 August 2004#2
John, that should be OK with the relatively light wallboard layers you're using - if they were heavier, that wood joint would limit wall performance. As is, you've got about the equivalent mass of two layers of 15mm gypsum where the 4x2's join, since wood is lighter than gypsum by a bit. The long screws and caulking thoroughly shouldn't hurt your performance in this case... Steve
