bpape23 January 2008#5
It's real diffusion - in the physical dimensions. It's even actually smoother than a QRD and has no effective top limit like most QRDs. However, a poly does not effectively shift things in the time domain the way a QRD does - nor does it provide any inherent absorbtion from in-well cancellations. Bryan
