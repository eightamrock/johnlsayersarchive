Hi all, seems like a great resource. Thanks for the help in advance. Well, this one seems tough. I just bought a condo and have converted one of the back rooms into an office / studio. The room is 12x14x9. Almost a cube. The room is actually fairly soundproof. The walls between condo units in my building are conrete and plaster block (yes they used to build them solid in the 1920's!) and the floor below me is thick concrete slab and damping material as well. Thing is, it's primary use is an office (clients do occasionally visit) which is why the desk is positioned as it is. There's really no other way for me to put it. The doors and large window make any symmetrical treatments impossible. Most of the issues I'm having seem to be bass-centric, although truthfully the room is not nearly as bad as I thought it would be. i think my Dynaudio monitors help out, though :) My personal thoughts were to put down a thick rug (I've already ordered a rug that will cover most of the floor except for 1' hardwood border), and take care of HF absorption with fiberboard panels on the left wall or ceiling. I was thinking of putting fiberboard/ insulation bass traps in the three corners. But... i am not an expert, and that's why I'm here!
