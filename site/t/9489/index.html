Okay, here goes, first the obligatory thanks: thanks to John for hosting the forum and all the invaluable advice he hands out, along with all the other experienced builders and designers. Now on to the craziness. I have been recording out of rental houses specific to each project I do for the past four or so years, I'm happy with this but it's time to upgrade. So naturally I bought the biggest thing I could find, an old aircraft hangar on a decent sized lot. Now before anyone begrudges me for my luck, the building is a metal structure of decent heft but a good source for an outside leaf it ain't. The hangar measure roughly 100' x 60' with a 15' ceiling on 20' of the length and 30' for the rest. I really don't have a place to start other than the designs at SAE and don't get me wrong they are good but I can't figure out all the details. So the questions are: 1) Do I need to build a frame and put up drywall and insulation on the inside of the metal exterior or does this constitute a third leaf (I know, bad)? 2) The "golden ratio" measurements are for a rectangular room with parallel walls, does that mean inserting walls at the corners to break up the lines? 3) I don't have an unlimited budget, but I guess 30,000 is a good guess, given that, I will take any suggestions. I didn't think it prudent to post a sketchup file of a big box, so my best guess is a roughly 16'x23' control room (I'll make it fit the ratios), a tracking room of about 1000 sq.ft., another room around 600 sq.ft., and two smaller booths, I'm thinking 16'x16' (obviously not exactly square, but those are good lenghts). Well I have sufficiently disregarded the don't give us a room size and ask what to do rule, but I'm just stuck at this point and don't know where to go. Thanks for any advice, I'm working on a sketchup of the setup described but I'm slow so it'll be a minute before I'm done. Thanks again.
