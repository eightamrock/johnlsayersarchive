So I thought a change of subject line might be worthwhile... please slap me if I'm wrong :oops: Anyway, can some enlightened soul please give me some advice as to where the best position for thermal insulation in an isolated floor is? I have two options: 1. Under the foundation slab 2. On top of the foundation slab but with a flooring screed on top of this and on top of that the wooden studion floor. My architect for simplicity sake has proposed the insulation under the slab but with a strnge 'supporting' arrangement for the inner floor (essentially, to me, a floated floor) The insulation material is either extruded polyurethane or expanded polystyrene. FWIW I have read Rod's book (essential in discussions with the architect) and the proposed studio structure is as follows: Inner room: 100mm HD concrete block with concrete beam and block roof Outer room: 140mm HD block with wooden frame pitched roof, spaces between beams infilled with plasterboard. There will also be a ventilation system. A structural engineer has also been consulted and will ultimately 'sign off' the structure so everything is above board and legal. Hope someone can help me soon as I'm putting in building warrant applications as we speak! :shock: The studio will be detached from the house although is relatively close (~4m) to neighbours.
