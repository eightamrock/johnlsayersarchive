Soundman202027 February 2011#5
Hi John. Welcome! For your control room, the basic idea looks right, but I don't see any isolation in there. Your inner leaf seems to be directly attached to your outer leaf in most places, and in other places it IS your outer leaf, by the looks of things. You say that everything except the bathroom is fair game: Does that include the small walls between the CR and LR? Can those be taken down, or are they structural? If you can take those out, then I'd rotate your CR 90° clockwise, enlarge it a bit, adjust the shape a bit, and isolate it properly. Regarding your questions:
1. Any obvious flaws im unaware of?
The isolation. Are you aware of how two-leaf decoupled MSM walls work? If not, then you might want to look into that. It is the best way of isolating at reasonable cost.
2. New east wall connected to existing east wall, is this a problem?
Very likely, yes. In order to isolate, you need to decouple, which basically means that the inner-leaf of each room should have no hard mechanical connections to any other room, nor to the outer leaf of the building.
3. Storage room at rear of control room houses elec.panel so i need acess but bass hangers maybe? will symetry be an issue here?
Bass hangers would probably work fine there, and symmetry should not be too much of a problem, since this is in the rear of the room.
4. Raising the ceiling would be a real PITA, cloth ceiling fill bays with insulation? slot ceiling? Remove drywall and sub floor add bass hangers above from metal joists?
Maybe do an inside-out ceiling for your inner-leaf? I don't see the purpose of putting bass hangers between leaves, though.
5. Raising the floor on furring strips and adding sub floor. only reason being cable runs. Good, bad?
Probably not a good idea. You run the risk of creating a resonant cavity under the floor, which is a really bad idea.
6. Any recomendations as far as treatment and placement go?
Large bass traps in as many corners as possible, slot walls for the sides, absorption on all first reflection points, ceiling cloud, hard floor, soft ceiling. You already have the hangers slated for the back, so that should be fine.
7. If i decide against soffit mounting should i keep the same shape or extend east and west walls to north wall?
I'll answer that question with another question: Why would you NOT want to soffit mount, when you have the space to do it right? :)
Sorry im running on here i have a million more questions but im getting a bit lost. Any advice or critisisms you may have would be greatly appreciated.
Do you have Rod's book? If not, I'd suggest that you get it (Home Recording Studio: Build it Like the Pros). What kind of budget do you have for this job? - Stuart -
