sundayhouse wrote:
i need some advice on where to treat and put my nearfields.
You should put your speakers in front of the northwest wall, facing toward the southeast wall. Then set the mix position 38 percent of the way back in the room. That's a little more than 6 feet back from the front wall you face while listening. Then add as many corner bass traps as you can manage, plus first reflection treatment on the side walls and ceiling. I imagine the room may still be a bit to live, so plan for a few more mid/high frequency absorbers elsewhere around the room. --Ethan
