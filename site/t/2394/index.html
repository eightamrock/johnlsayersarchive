Okay, I have finally got a floor plan of the studio I am going to build (due to various stupid reasons it's turned out to be tougher than pouring and raking out 15 tonnes of concrete!). I would like the following... To record to a high-quality demo standard (I dont think I will ever want to get other bands in - it's just not big enough). To rehearse with a 5 piece band (2 guitars, 1 vocals)... And most importantly... NOT TO PISS OFF THE NEIGHBOURS! I've been wondering whether to build a drum booth and have the control room as a rehearsel room. I dont think the space will be big enough to record a band "live" (although I may be wrong). I am putting it to the expertise on this board as to whether or not the space I have will support 1/2 or 2+drum booth (ideal)...
