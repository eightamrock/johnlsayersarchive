This page: http://www.ta-inc.com/newshtml/rc.htm has a decent explantion and pics for resilient channel. Look at the pic on the page, the thing to be careful about: What we call "hat channel" is no good, it has no holes and provides no sound isolation benifet. Resilient channel has holes in it, and does not attach the same way to the frame material, giving it sound isolation benifit of decoupling. Hope this helps. Someone else may be able to give a better discription than I. Marc
