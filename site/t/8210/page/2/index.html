camistan23 June 2007#43
Medboyle, By using that design looks like you're back to using CCTV. Is that really what you want to do? You've got a serious question to ask yourself about stopping where you're at and trying to salvage used building materials to re-build it the way you had it (The way John designed it with windows). If nothing else FIND OUT where there is a studio with CCTV and regular studio windows. See if you can sit in on a recording session at both places to get a "feel" for EXACTLY what CCTV will be like compared to regular studio windows. In my initial plans I was going to do the CCTV thing, but as was stated, it does seem somewhat "impersonal". My view from my control room to my vocal booth will be through two (single) 36" French Doors. I will be using cameras in my studio, but it's mainly for internet purposes. In John's diagram you have views into your other rooms. This is a big decision to make. My advice would be to go for the windows. (Another thing...what about storage space for cables, headphones etc etc. can you incorporate that into your design somewhere?...Just a thought)
