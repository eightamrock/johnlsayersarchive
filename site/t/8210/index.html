kendale18 February 2007#10
Aloha, In Rod Gervais’ book “Build it Like the Pros,” http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=5670 he points out both L.W.Sepmeyer’s & M.M. Louden’s formulas most widely used in professional studios worldwide. He also notes that the orders listed are (top to bottom) the first to third best ratios in both cases. M.M. Louden Height Width Length 1.00 1.40 1.90 As such, with a 9' ceiling, M.M. Loudens ratios would give you a 9’ x 12’6” x 17’1” (H x W x L). This would take up a good portion of your overall room available, but will give a good place to start for your control room. - How many people do normally record at one time? - How many people do you find in the control room at any given time? - What is your preference in regard to how much room you need in the control room? Here's a quick skp of the floor layout for a control room using Louden's rations for starters. If you find this acceptable, we can then flesh out the rest of the room. Aloha 8)

Aloha, Hey! That's pretty good for your first one. Again, I had to resize your pic to eliminate scrolling (forum guidelines). If you could keep your pics down to about 700 pixels wide, that would be great. Thanks! Okay, I think you've got the idea now. What you want to remember is to try and keep your room ratios as close to optimum as you can to avoid modal problems and since most of the critical listening and adjustment decisions are done in the control room, you may want to keep the control room ratios at optimum and reduce the live room measurements to fit it all in. L.W. Sepmeyer Height Width Length 1.00 1.14 1.39 9’0” x 10’3” x 12’5” (9’ = 108 Inch(s)] = 274.32 Centimetre(s) x 10’3” = 123 Inch(s)] = 312.42 Centimetre(s) x 12’5” = 149 Inch(s)] = 378.46 Centimetre(s) Aloha 8)
