gullfo29 April 2021#6
i'm in agreement on adding the walls. if there will be noise which interferes with your work, then you'll want to increase the isolation by doubling up (a pair of 2x4 walls) or isolation clips+hat channel on the interior (less desirable but much more effective than RC-2). use 1/2" ply with 2x 5/8" drywall on the frame or hat channel.

There are many useful Rules of Thumb. You will never satisfy all of them. Symmetry is fairly easy to do at HF, and it doesn't really exist at LF. So let's take that out of the decision process and see what happens. That looks like a big space. You will be listening in the near field. So I would advise identify your options ergonomically, pragmatically, and aesthetically..... Windows to outside are nice. How about placing your speakers at the West Wall. Or the South?
