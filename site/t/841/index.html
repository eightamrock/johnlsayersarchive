knightfly17 October 2003#4
Carl, first of all dumb questions are the ones you don't ask til you've already done it wrong... Air space between wall leaves is a "dimishing return" thing - going from 4" to 8" gets you about 5 dB, no other changes - going from 8" to 16" gets you another 5 dB, but the next 5 dB will cost you another 16" - each doubling of air space nets you about 5 dB improvement. 8" with 3 layers of sheet rock would probably get you about the same as 12" with two layers, so if you need the space add layers. Check back on your "complete section" thread, most of the answers are there - Air space between walls is best filled to mild compression with insulation. This helps a little because of direct attenuation, but mostly because of panel damping. The two layer combos that most people report as working best are sheet rock/soundboard/sheet rock, and 5/8" - 3/8" - 1/2" all sheet rock. One person I really respect on another forum did his last walls with all sheet rock as above, and hears NOTHING with drums playing on the other side. Oughta be good enough... Steve

knightfly18 October 2003#6
"Would you suggest 3 drywall layer for the live room since I will mostly be tracking drums and bass in there? And the sandwich w/ soundboard for the control room?" - sure, that should work really well. Be aware, though, that this does nothing for acoustics, only sound proofing. The stiffer you make your walls, the more bass trapping you'll need. The thing is, there isn't really a lot of choice if you want good isolation. If you make the wall leaf in a room where you want bass trapping with lighter materials so they flex more and act as panel traps, you lose isolation. So if you want BOTH isolation AND bass trapping, you just about have to first make it soundproof and THEN deal with the bass buildup. John's designs are his own, you'll need to talk to him about wall construction. If you want to use my ideas, you're welcome. John may have some improvements on my suggestions. If so, I'd recommend listening to him, I'm just the new guy... Steve
