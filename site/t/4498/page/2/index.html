knightfly28 October 2005#27
(a) STC 78/50 dB @ 50 hZ (b) STC 83/42 dB @ 50 hZ The thinner block with wider air gap is better at low end, which is harder to stop. Therefore (A) is your best choice, provided there are no windows to muck it up... Steve
