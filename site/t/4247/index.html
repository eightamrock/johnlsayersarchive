knightfly24 August 2005#4
For a slanted wall or a corner trap, the average depth is found by adding the minimum depth to the maximum depth, and dividing that sum by two. So your example of 100mm to 300mm depth would be 100 + 300 = 400mm, divided by two = 200mm. This would be the value you enter into the cell for depth from wall to see the center frequency; however, you can also enter the minimum and maximum depths to see the RANGE of frequencies that will be affected. Works this this formula correct with Metric sizes? No, you would need to convert to inches/pounds first or calculate a new formula; I probably won't get enough time to do an Excel file that does both metric and english units until the wet weather gets here.
