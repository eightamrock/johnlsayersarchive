P750souza15 December 2005#19
Shame they didin´t work.
Dependind on what it is made of, it actualy wont work :!: What did you made them of :?: To be effective you must understand what is happenig on the waves propagation inside your room. As I saw in the pics you do have a lot of corners. Please, treat the corners. Two meeting walls = a boost problem on mid low/low freq. Three walls meeting yields a doble boost from the former, and so on. If you have treated one corner on ceiling using, say panel: Good, surely there's an improvement, though it may not be effective as you desired. So, work on vertical walls as I send you a pic, those places are really important for the monitors placement. Apply panels to each corner on ceilings. If 440Hz still bores you, try a slot resonator, you can build them to work around that specific mid-low range. By the way, you should use rockwool on the glass around the A/C Unit, especially beause it is a place where parallel wall will cause to you early reflections. I'm preparing a drawing that I think will help you. GO ON! See u ... :!:
