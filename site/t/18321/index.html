For sure. Absolutely. But there probably are good layouts that would allow you to have a corridor along one wall, for access between the two ends of the building without going through the actual studio space. It might also be a legal requirement, to have emergency exists accessible through both ends, from all rooms. If this is going to be a commercial studio, then you are subject to a bunch of different laws that don't affect the average home studio, such as ADA for example: wheel-chair accessibility, wide doors and grab bars and rails in the bathroom, ramps and no steps, doors that can be opened from a wheelchair, etc. You might want to look into that.

You need heavy doors anyway, regardless. Assuming that you need good isolation, of course. If isolation is not important, then you can get away with not-so-heavy doors (but still not light weight).
