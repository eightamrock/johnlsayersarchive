A1: Most likely not necassary, although it will help with isolation. Answer really depends on wether you want to extra isolation gain and whether or not you want to bother with the extra work. The big thing about the decision of course is it'll be a lot hard to add an expansion joint after wards. Also if you're going for full isolated slab to truly get the most of the expansion joint then your exterior/outer walls would go on there own footer (small slab) then each of your rooms and inner walls would go on there own slab... each of these slabs would have to have a footer as well as you can not (well should not :D ) build on the edge of a slab with no footer. Also see Sharwards mighty thread: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=2921 A2: Is a Floating Floor Right For You? Answer: Probably NOT.
