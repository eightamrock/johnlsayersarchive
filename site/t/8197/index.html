gullfo17 February 2007#12
steel framing for the walls works. if you're going to support your isolated ceilings with these walls, you will need to go to a heavy gauge steel. You'll need to consult with a structural engineer to assess this correctly. in addition, the ceiling joints will need to support the load of the drywall and you'll want the proper structure defined there as well. Most people do not like ceilings caving in on them... :shock: Since you appear to already have a number of concrete walls, you can use the inside-out wall technique spaced from the concrete wall and then appy the interior walls - slats for treatment (which is what Sideshow had mentioned). In that case, instead of the treatment sections (shown in yellow), you'd build out your walls to add the angles.
