Hello, Thanks for this great forum. Much valuable information in here. I read many of the posts to learn how to construct a studio. Jhon makes some incredible designs, i was amazed! I am now getting into the phase of actual construction in a few days. I saw the sketchup designs and with all the info came up with 3 possible designs for my studio. I would appreciate some feedback on if i did it ok, or not. And how much sound isolation will i get aproximately obtain with each design. I need the best sound isolation i can get because im also a percussionist and drummer. I will be rehearsing and eventually recording some personal projects and maybe for others. The studio will be constructed in a semi-basement (i attached some pics and plans that i draw). The basement has 2 rooms, one will be for live tracking (room2 in the back) and the other as control room (room1). room 2 has no windows. room 1 has 1 door and 1 window. All doors will be double doors and windows are isolated. I'm the new guy on the block! The naughty neigbours are aprox. 20 to 30 feet away. My designs for the live tracking studio: Fig.1) Room design 1: room within a room construction based on wooden construction only, with 2 to 3 layers of plywood, OSB or gypsum board. Rockwool isolation in between studs. Use of 1 layer of 2mm sheetblock sound barrier on all surfaces. Fig.2) Room design 2: A room within a room construction based on metal framing that is not touching the concrete walls or concrete ceiling structure. it will stand on its own. The metal framing is stuffed with rockwool and closed on both sides by plywood and gybsum board. Also has sheetblock layer in between. (i'm afraid of metal resonance with this design, i imagine that the entire metal framing will resonate on low frecuencies..is this possible?????). Fig.3) Room desing 3: Going for the heavy duty sound isolation here!lol. I construct an entire brick room inside the actual room and reinforce it with 2 layers of osb plywood or gybsum board. Also use sheetblock just in case. Rock wool between studs. The ceiling a 3 layer wooden ceiling will hang from the existing concrete ceiling by metal u-hangers! I go for a similar approach of room design 1 but i reduce to only 1 layer of plywood plus 1 layer of gypsum to reduce the loss of room space! (im afraid i will get a boomy sound because of the brick wall????is this possible). If you were to choose a design, what would it be?? for best sound quality and best isolation. This is just the isolation construction. I will do the interior design later on. (i attached a drawing of a possible room design with angles and absorbers). I will get more into this later. I will also construct a drum booth inside the constructionm just in case. The room is not so big (see dimensions in drawing). Bye, Dan
