x-t12 March 2006#1
First, I just want to thank everyone who has contributed to this site. It is an amazing resource for so many and i have not found any other that compares, so THANK YOU! now, down to business. myself and three of my friends are planning on building a studio here in cincinnati, oh. Our location is one half of the third floor of a 1959 warehouse building with thick (at least 6") concrete floors, and 8" concrete block walls(not sure if they are filled or not). the building is supported by 2 ft diameter concrete beams that are indicated in the diagram as well as a freight elevator to transport gear. There are 5'x5' windows on the East & South walls which we plan to fill with glass block. the building is located in an industrial area, so we do not have any neighboring buildings closer than 500 feet. there are train tracks to the West about 1,000 ft away. closest main road is at least 700 ft away as well. the biggest noise problem will be us, we must not disturb the businesses in the other half of the building as well as the floor below us. our ceiling height is 19 ft and the floor below us has a ceiling height of 16 ft. as far as budget goes, we have decided to focus the majority of our finances on construction and build up our gear as we get more gigs. we also have a hook-up with a building supply company where we can get supplies at cost (which helps us greatly) we have approximately $20,000-$25,000 for our budget. We plan on running new electric & HVAC for our use. Again, we can get a discount on this as well. the work we plan on recording is from singer songwriter to full on metal bands. with this in mind, i tried to design a nice large live end dead end (LEDE) tracking room to accomodate a full band as well as multiple iso rooms to obtain isolation when needed. I have also tried to design these rooms to maintain visual contact between all rooms. there is no need for us to encorporate a lounge in this design, as we have a lounge exclusively for our use and shared restrooms on the North side of the elevator. all pictures are orientated so that North is top. i have submitted multiple pics to make it easier to see exactly what we invision. I don't necessarioly have a specific question at this point, i am mainly looking for any feedback (good or bad). though i have been reading and researching as much as i can, i am still new to this aspect (designing) and am open to any suggestions. Thanks in advance. Studio Ceiling Height =117.5 ft (avg) Control Room Ceiling Height = 14 ft (avg)
