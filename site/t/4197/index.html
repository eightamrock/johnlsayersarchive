Hello there, I have been reading this forum a lot and really appreciate all ideas that are made in these threads. Now it is my turn to ask for good ideas. I put all the files there at http://xthost.info ... You have to log in in order to the files user = stud pass = studio so here is the description of the files studnow = studio with the things (drums and mix area) that can not be split (yes drums and mix area should not be in the same room, but mix area shouldnt not be broken as I need to have all my midi and synth stuff near the mackie 24/8) studdim = studio with the dimensions in meters studempty = for your creations ;-) studprop(x) = my propositions but I am really not satisfied with them The things you need to know : - all the soundproofing is already done - the studio can be seen with real pictures at www.pulseconcept.com then go to studio74 - the white rectangle are pylons that can not be destroyed - the grey things are pipe that have been covered during the ceiling proofing (like a big "U" around the pipes) What I need : - place to mix - place to record drums - place to record the band - place to just play with the band (I dont want each person to be in a bocal during not-recording-sessions) - place to do internet (small office is needed) - place to record vocals (I plan to buy a static mic but I am afraid about noise of PC and so on ... noise from outside is ok according to the soundproofing) What I was going to do is to take the third part (on the right) and to place windows-doors between the two 2m40 "holes" and then I have a cabin. This third part now is used to stock the flight case and books and so on ... So feel free to share your plans if you think it is possible in this place (I know it is not really a big room about 55m2 in total but maybe it is possible to do it in a better way than the way I plan to do (the cabin in the third area) Thanks to all to share ideas (and when possible please use the xthost account so the files will always be there -- no problems of hosting) bye PHil
