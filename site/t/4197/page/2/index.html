pulse18 November 2010#35
So here are the news: Finally I decided to keep it that way meaning I definitely love to have one big room ... Everything is perfect apart the fact that the sound inside the studio is not top about the bass frequencies. So, while keeping the main idea, I moved everything so that it is now in a symetric way, and guess what ... yes the sound is much better. Currently I am doing superchunk bass-traps in the corners of the 1m.97 part ... Each bass-trap is 1,08 m x 1,08 m. I dont plan to add other superchunk bass-traps in the back of the room cause I dont have the place to do it (one side is the door and in the other angle I have my Marshall cabinet 4 x 12) My question of the day: are my 1m 08 x 1m 08 bass traps too big ??? I already construct the wooden structure for both front angles (in the 1m97 part) and filled one partially (60% is done) and already the sound is really better for the left speaker (dynaudio BM15a), more definition ... I am afraid to construct too big bass traps, what do you think? The diagonal is 1m50 cm approx and is filled with rockwook 60 kg/m3. I have been told today that normally superchunk bass traps diagonal are 85 cm (and not 1m50). Should I rebuild everything to 85 cm?
