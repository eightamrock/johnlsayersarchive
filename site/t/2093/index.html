Jai10 August 2004#5
hey all, I agree with john fully, the rock wool will put less hazarous material in the air as is absorbs the low frq energy. However, you will have to wrap your traps with a loose woven fabric to keep the rockwool in place for years to come. The 703 can just be glued to your Mass (Plywood, drywall, laminate, etc,) and forgotten about. another words..... the rock wool is to limp to stand up on its own. just my 2 cents jai
