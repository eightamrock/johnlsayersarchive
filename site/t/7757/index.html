knightfly21 December 2006#2
Assuming no resilient mounts, approx. STC 43, with 20 dB TL @ 50 hZ; changing stud spacing to 24" improves to STC 45, and adding resilient channel or RSIC clips raises to STC 51 - no change at low end, because this is primarily a function of gap and mass. These are all approximations based on a now unavailable demo of a VERY expensive program... Steve (the demo only allows standard weight household insulation, so the fire batts would improve things in midrange by another dB or two over what I gave)
