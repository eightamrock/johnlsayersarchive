Ethan Winer17 August 2008#3
That looks pretty good, but more bass trapping can only help. The front and rear walls especially will benefit from thick absorbers placed flat on those surfaces. If you space the absorbers off the wall, they'll work even better.
chrissss wrote:
there is a slight flutter echo. Should I put up some of those plastic auralex diffusers?
You'll probably find my recent video about diffusors useful. See this: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=11089 --Ethan
