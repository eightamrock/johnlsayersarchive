AndreasB13 March 2007#27
Thanks! No, the monitors are almost in position, they'll be lying down and be somewhat tilted forward to get in right position ('le sweetie spot, as the french call it :D). The back wall is now about 500mm away from the back of the speakers, the wall is angled 8-10° and there's a 95mm layer of Roxull behind those slots.. hopefully this will be enough. Anyways the Genelecs I'm using has a rolloff switch so if necessary I'll reduce some more. Haven't actually got around testing it yet as there's way too many other things to get done before that. In a worst case scenario I've to add bigger absorbers behind the speakers. Tomorrow I'll be building all the panels, about 20 smaller ones (1200x580mm) and 1 big cloud for the CR ceiling (2400x1200mm). I'll bring my camera to the woodshop tomorrow and post them here later on. a :arrow:
