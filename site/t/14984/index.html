gullfo24 October 2010#6
the reason for stripping down to the frame is that it allows you to beef up the exterior walls with drywall laid against them and then you build a room within a room from there. this gives you a nice air gap and then your only main isolation issues will be the flanking via the structure and floors, and any remaining windows and the doors. the garage door will be removed or doctored depending on your requirements. having the upstairs room for your live room could work out well but you'll still likely have a booth downstairs because your control room would not take up all the space given the 9' ceiling limit.
