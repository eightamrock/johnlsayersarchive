buchton28 July 2008#1
Hi, I’m in Montreal, Canada. English is not my first language (I speak french), so I’ll try to do my best. I have a new house (1 year old) with half of the basement usable for my studio. This is the planning phase (Design). I wish to build a control room and a live room. The space that I can use is in the basement and is doing a “L” shape and the electric panel is in the corner :cry: . I can’t move it so I have to leave an easy access to it (I've read that somewhere on the forum…). I have a Sketchup file for more details (real scale in inches). I want to be able to record loud drums (heavy rock) without bothering my neighbors (20-25 feet away). I want to do the inside-out wall system because I don’t have a lot of space and it will drop the cost a little bit to. I’d like the live room to be big enough to have 2 or 3 people in it. I have read the Rod Gervais’s book at least 3 times and I still discover (or understand) new stuff each time… :D . I’m reading this forum for months now. It’s amazing! I’ve ordered a spl meter and will take measurement of how loud I am as soon as it arrives. Edit: loud drums inside, 100 db C, outside, 80 db C. My budget is around 5000-10000$ edit: I'll do the job myself except for ventilation. Existing basement: ¾ of the basement is not used and I want to use ½ of the whole basement for my studio leaving ¼ for lounge tv etc… room. It is a concrete slab so I’ll build my boxes right on it. No floating. The height is 7’8” from slab to joist. The joists are open web joists 13” deep 19.2” OC sitting on the 8” poured concrete foundation. There are already plasters (1/2”) on walls all around the basement on a 2x3 frame. The ceiling has no plaster yet. The air gap between the foundation and the plaster is filled with sprayed foam (polyurethane, I think) 3-4” thick! It’s even thicker between the joists just over the foundation walls, up to 6” and maybe more. Effective for heat in winter but very bad for sound insulation! Look at the photos to see what I mean. So first of all, I want to do a big box that will contain 2 separate smaller boxes: control room and live room. 1- I want to beef up my external leaf which is the ceiling and that little portion between the foundation and the upper floor. For the ceiling, can I add mass between joists the same way as a normal joist because open web joist only give 1 ½” of surface to put support for the mass. There is a lot of nails to…. 2- little portion between the foundation and the upper floor : since there is sprayed foam, that is almost impossible to remove, is there an easy way for me to add mass and leaving all that insulation there? Or do I have to remove it all to finally put some mass on the very exterior leaf? It is a really narrow place to work… I understand that if I leave the foam there, I’ll create a third leaf and it is to be avoided. 3- The existing walls are literally melted in that insulation making them almost impossible to remove. Can I leave them there and build separates boxes. It’s going to again create a third leaf (and eat space) but since the third leaf is 12” poured concrete with earth on the other side, maybe I can just ignore these walls? Or at least I can remove the plasters… 4- Electric panel: It is in the corner where I want to build and I have to leave an access to it. Can I just put a door to access it from the studio or do I need an open corridor without any door to access it? 5- Before the slab was poured, they’ve put some blue foam panels under to isolate from cold and then pour the concrete slab over it. Will the slab crack under the load of the studio? I’ve included a sketchup file that shows a plan with a corridor to access the panel, but I lose a lot of space this way. I can live with it if it’s the only way. I used the Eric Desart 1, 1:35, 1:85 ratio for CR. It’s a good size due to my low ceiling… and is maybe better than louden’s first ratio… I’ve put my others attempts to show others possibilities. I’m very open to any design if someone have an idea. Thanks Yohann
