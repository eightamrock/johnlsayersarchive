rod gervais7 October 2009#2
giz...... I actually use deadbolts.......... the type with a thumb latch inside - with all of the trim going on the door /frame this will require a deeper than normal deadbolt. Typical backset for deadbolts are either 2 3/8" or 2 3/4" (inside/outside respectively) this just does not work - Schlage makes a 5" backset deadbolt (Schlage 5" Backset B/BA300-Series Deadbolt) that works perfectly. Do not worry bout the thru penetration of the door - you have to do this somehow - just keep the bores as tight as you can......... Rod
