Vocal Vendetta: Step 1 - Booth Location

Started by mpedrummer on 12 February 2009. 0 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 11691.

mpedrummer12 February 2009#1
OK, imaginary internet friends! Let's do this thing! Here's the room in question: http://hotelmorrell.com/studio/Basement02.jpg I'm still working on learning Sketchup. I think I fucked up with the initial basement - I think that I started with the EXTERIOR measurements of the house, not the INTERIOR measurements, so I'll need to redo this before I pass the skp file on to you geniuses. Anyway, pretty picture, gives you a concept. The first issue is location, location, location. I have two potential sites. Site 1: the current mix position. Frame in the support poles, live happily ever after. Probably a double inside out wall, since the dang poles mean that there would have to be a minimum 10" airgap in the interior. Also saves me from mudding. Site 2: the corner in the top left of the image. There are pros and cons to both locations. Site 1 Cons:
  • Floor is not level, there are humps around the poles and there's a french drain tilt going on.
  • Two windows
  • Bigger (therefore a more expensive build)
  • I'd need to mothball the studio during the build
  • I'd need to find a new mix position. Probably just outside of the new booth, though.
  • Walls show some moisture
  • http://www.hotelmorrell.com/studio/above_mix.jpg
Site 1 Pros:
  • Bigger (therefore probably easier to control sound)
  • Potentially even large enough to double as a practice room for my kit, though too small to record in
  • Less pipes in the way (but still, pipes in the way)
  • Closer to mix position, so probably better interaction with the talent
Site 2 Cons:
  • Pipes, pipes, pipes. The copper meets the black iron right here, dropping the available height to 6'7". That's not a good starting point.
  • Smaller
  • Further away
  • http://www.hotelmorrell.com/studio/corner.jpg
Site 2 Pros:
  • Floor is level
  • Walls are bone dry
  • Smaller, so probably a cheaper build
  • Closer to other musicians, so probably better interaction for the band
  • No need to putz with the mix position, which I like
So that's the outline. Site 1, in my mind, has both more pros and more cons - not just in number, but in significance of each. More Details
  • All numbers are accurate - where I measured them.
  • The house is 103 years old - nothing is square, ever.
  • The main goal: a vocal booth so my wife doesn't need to feel constrained in the house by my recording
  • Secondary goal: booth large enough to get a reasonable acoustic guitar sound
  • Probably can't afford it goal: meet goal one and two, but also be able to record amps without irritating wife/neighbors
  • The floor is poured concrete - no idea how thick
  • Floor to underside of floor: 8'2"
  • Floor to bottom of joists: 7'4"
  • Floor to bottom of pipes: 6'7"
  • Money is very much an object
So, there's the first post of this project! I'd love input on where to put the damn thing...then I can start figuring out the site-specific issues I'll need help with. Thanks Tim
