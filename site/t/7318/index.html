Enlightened ones know that STC is "a very old method for ranking walls over the frequency range of 125 - 4000 Hz" and that "STC is not without significant limitations, and for a great many applications it is not a good measure of sound isolation at all" (quoted from Green Glue Company's awesome STC debunker article, "Understanding STC, what it means and doesn’t mean" -- a must read!). Officially, we know that STC stands for "Sound Transmission Class." Let's think of some new spelled-out names for STC. Me first:

rod gervais19 October 2006#2
LOL.............. that is cute........ But, sorry Keith, The STC rating is an excellent tool when used for it's designed purposes. No one constructing walls for hotels, meeting rooms, etc. are ever dissapointed when the work is completed according to design and tested results. The fact that it isn't a standard to be effectively used for Studio construction (or any other construction where LF transmissions are really an issue) does not change the fact that it serves a very usefull purpose. A ruler is not a stupid tool just because it can't be used as a pry-bar.......... same goes with STC ratings. Sincerely, Rod
