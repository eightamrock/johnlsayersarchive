knightfly13 September 2004#5
If soundproofing isn't important, you can build with a single layer of 5/8" plywood or OSB, using 2x4's only for corners and door frame - you'll get a better sounding booth though, if you use odd size dimensions - for example, a booth that's 7' tall inside, with the other two dimensions of 3' and 5', even though it's harder to build, will not have modal problems (it will sound better, even BEFORE you add absorbent materials in about half the wall area) - I'm off to work right now (12-hour graveyard shifts) but if you need an actual plan, I can probably draw up something that would work for you - let me know... Steve

hey bad news. i did a project like this last year. dimensions were roughly 7 tall by 5 by 6 see: http://www.johnlsayers.com/Studio/Pages/Eric_henry.htm simple construction, 2X4's and drywall. materials were really cheap to make. it was great for singing and acoustic guitar. things i wish i'd done differently: 1. made it bigger. it gets really small in there and it's just really hard to control the low mids in a small room. i sing pretty quietly, but once you start belting it out it really makes a difference to have a little more space. 2. made it with a window. when it was just me, it was fine, if i was recording someone else, it was a little weird. 3. made the door open out! doh!
