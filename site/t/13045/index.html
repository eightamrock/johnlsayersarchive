Decision time - Which way to go?

Started by JoeNovice on 15 May 2009. 2 replies.

JoeNovice15 May 2009#1
After months of planning and budgeting and changing and..... (you get the idea).... it's time to pull the trigger and finish this room! Here are my wall options based on budget. 1) Use double 5/8" 'rock with Green Glue (3 tubes per 128sqft), attached directly to the framing. or 2) Use min-clips and hat channel with double 1/2" 'rock and Green Glue (3 tubes per 128 sqft). or 3) Use triple layers of 1/2" 'rock with double Green Glue (1.5 tubes per 128 sqft per layer) Walls are part of an attic truss system. Weight is engineer approved. Main concern is some reduction of in-home transmission at mix levels but primarily isolation from exterior noises; dogs barking, cars driving by, etc. My STC weakness is a door and window. I am replacing the hollow-core door with a solid-core door with a seal kit. I plan to make a tight fit 1/2" foam insert to cover the window. The floor will be 1/2" particle, Green Glue, 1/4" MDF, recycled rubber underlayment, hardwood. (I'm not looking for the scientific absolute answer. Just an educated opinion based on my options.)
