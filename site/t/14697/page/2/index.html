gullfo31 October 2010#29
if you install the window in the treatment wall, you need to extend the isolation out to that window. otherwise you simply have a 3'x5' hole in the isolation wall! otherwise, put the massive windows in the isolation walls and use a lighter weight clear panel to preserve the angles and view. the RPG products are just an example where the clear panel also has absorption properties.

gullfo8 November 2010#38
you have a direct connection from the floor to the ceiling. you'll need to (at least) decouple the lower drywall and also better to add mass on the upper floor (underneath adding 2x-3x 5/8" drywall, sealed and held in place with cleats) and dampen the cavity below (R32 batts). then suspend the lower ceiling or if the structure is not suitable a new ceiling supported off the isolation walls below. check with a structural engineer to assess what additional load can be added. 22" trusses (depending on the span) seem like a decent candidate for suspending the ceiling.
