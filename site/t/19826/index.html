thismanysounds12 February 2015#2
Hi Les, That is a good height, and it seems like a decent size. I would recommend re-drawing it using Sketchup, it's free, and allows us to share designs easily. There seems to be some errors I can see: - You are missing some details which are required by the forum: viewtopic.php?t=3231 - You have msm structures and the existing walls, creating triple leafs. Unintuitively, these will actually decrease your isolation. - The control room faces away from the live room, wasting the opportunity for sight lines. - What else is in the building?
