gullfo26 February 2011#4
you could reduce it to 2" but then i'd use very light insulation in that cavity to damp it - probably 10kg/m3 max to allow air flow but still a lot of friction loss for LF. what will help you is the looseness of the air spring in that cavity so as much flow around the wall space will help. on the glass block, the mass still predominates the isolation of it so you need to adjust the outer windows as well (if you haven't already). in most cases where i use glass block, it try to minimize the size of the window on the exterior and the interior. say 300mm w x 700mm h max or even 150mm x 400mm - enough to let light in but also minimize the size of the penetration. use the heaviest glass block you can find.
