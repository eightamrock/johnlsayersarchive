I'm planning to use resilient clips like these: http://www.professionalacousticsco.com/rsic_dc04.html But is there a good way to secure the angled frame to the non-angled concrete wall? At the furthest point, the concrete wall will be about 2 1/2 feet from the frame.
knightfly21 July 2006#2
Didn't we cover this here? http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewt ... 8372#38372 If not, what am I missing? (for varying distances, just change the length of the horizontal 2x4 between the iso bracket and the wall frame) - If you're wondering about getting the angled wall to "line up" with the non-angled concrete, you can't. Just rotate the resilient bracket til the flat part is horizontal, then rotate your extended 2x4 between inner and outer wall til it's horizontal, fasten it to the underside of the top plate instead of the stud, and screw it to the bracket. This will allow several degrees of rotation without problems... Steve
