i was at lowes and home depot and they have some acoustic ceiling tiles available that are semi-rigid and backed with something that at least looks similar to OC 703. It's about 3/4" in thickness. These are the same tiles that are used in a drop ceiling. My question is ... would these work in trapping frequencies - especially bass frequencies using the frame method as described in the sticky **here**
SonicClang24 March 2006#2
No. "Acoustic" ceiling tiles are made more for office buildings. They diffuse sound more than a flat panel, and they stop some sound, but not a whole lot. But I wouldn't depend on them and them alone in a studio for your bass trapping or sound proofing.
bigerock24 March 2006#3

Ethan Winer24 March 2006#4
Rock, > would these work in trapping frequencies - especially bass frequencies using the frame method < Yes, those will work fine. But you need enough of them to get a sandwich at least three inches thick, and you should also peel off the plastic facings on all the panels except the one facing the room. --Ethan
