John Sayers29 March 2010#24
Duki - I said further back in the thread that maybe you need more treatment - if you add up the surface area of the room it's around 110sqm - your treatment in the corners would account for around 6sqm or approx 5.2% of the surface area. You have some diffusion at each end of your room because of these units but in real terms it's really not enough IMO. play with the reverb calculator in the stickeys and you'll see what I mean.
