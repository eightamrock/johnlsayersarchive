Sunnburst8 November 2004#7
Hi Julian, i was told one time that a hole that is 1% of the total surface area of the structure (be that structure a wall, door, window, panel resonator, bass trap) will reduce the soundblocking capability of the structure to 10%! Maybe the percentage numbers are not exactly on the mark, but the point is that even tiniest of holes will completely wreck the isolation. Airtight is the only way to go. David
