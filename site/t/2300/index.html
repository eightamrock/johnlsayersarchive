G'day again Chris I was specifically looking for an open weave fabric to cover wall treatments. I'm about to start building a new music/HT/demo room. Isolation is no problem (at least 100mm concrete on all surfaces) but the room needs a lot of absorbtion on the walls/ceiling and a pair of large bass traps in the rear corners. I'll need to cover around 75m^2 in fabric. The speakers will be hidden behind this fabric so it has to be completely transparant, acoustically. My initial searches have found Autex polyester to be about the most cost effective absorbtion in this town. It's nice to work with, too, and is available in black, so it won't show though the walls. I might use rockwool for the bass traps (like these: http://www.bobgolds.com/TrapHarder/home.htm ) but much larger. The polyester would be a bit harder to cut accurately and stack. Cheers William Cowan

Hi Just for the record, we finally settled on "Duet" fabric sold by Macquarie Fabrics. They have a wide range of colours and patterns and the "Duet" variety is quite cheap for a 60% wool content. Check out their website.
