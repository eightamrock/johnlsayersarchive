Soundman20204 February 2009#3
In my opinion, I'd really suggest that you use proper shielded audio snake cable, and separate runs for MIDI, Ethernet, telephone, video, or whatever else you might need. You MIGHT be able to get away with other cable, but the risk just isn't worth it to save a few dollars. I've seen all kinds of really-hard-to-trace problems in post production studios, where customers have tried to save a couple of bucks with "unusual" cables and connectors, and usually the best way I find to fix that is to rip out their cheap stuff, and then install the correct stuff. So they end up wasting a lot of money from trying to save a some pennies. It really isn't worth it. - Stuart -
