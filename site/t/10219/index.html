GreenGlue $avings / suggestion

Started by Jeff Chitouras on 18 February 2008. 3 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 10219.

Jeff Chitouras18 February 2008#1
So, I'm installing a "floating floor" of 1 inch 703 with 2 layers of OSB on top of the 703 (thank you Rod Gervais) using 3 tubes of GreenGlue on the back of the 2nd layer of OSB when the end cap of a tube of GreenGlue falls out and GreenGlue goes all over my pants and on the floor. What a mess! Not to waste the expensive GreenGlue I took a piece of cardboard and 'squeegeed' it on the floor in the 4 x 8 space where a layer of OSB was to go. Doing it this way I found I could use only 2 tubes of GreenGlue and get FAR more even coverage than using 3 tubes and randomly applying it the the back of the 4 x 8 sheet as is recommended by GreenGlue website. (There is something satisfying of seeing GreenGlue ooze out all around the edges of the 4 x 8 sheet when applying the screws.) I have had the occasion to break apart 2 layers if OSB after applying GreenGlue as is suggested by their website and discovered that there were numerous "voids" where the GreenGlue did <not> spread. Of course, this is much easier for doing floors as opposed to walls but it might be useful/economic for some of you using/considering GreenGlue. By the way, GreenGlue works very well in my experience.
