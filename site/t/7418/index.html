I have 21x22. The floor is over the garage, brand new, suspended on I-joists with a 9 foot flat ceiling. I'd like to leave about a 3 foot walkway where the garage meets house, so if I make two rooms they will be approx 10-11 feet wide and 17-18 feet deep. Am I better off trying to get a control room and live room out of it rectangularly, or going with something like the SAE design and splaying walls but having little tiny rooms? Also, if I use 2 rectangles... the width and length will depend on where I put the rear wall, and how much space I leave between the double walls seperating the rooms. Is there a "best" size that I can make the rooms with the amount of space listed? I mean like going with 10 feet wide but 17 deep so that its not 2 even dividable numbers? Bass traps will extend the entire length of the room if you think I should, and maybe even where floor meets wall about 6 feet out from the mix posi wall. Does that help any?
