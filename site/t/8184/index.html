jwl13 February 2007#2
shakestheclown wrote:
1. Will I need to layer drywall on the inside and outside of my frame, between the existing wall and my new studs/insulation?
No. In fact you are better off not doing that. Put all the drywall on the same face. The more mass the better, and all the mass should be on the same side. Depending on how many layers you install, you may need to build reinforced frames.
shakestheclown wrote:
2. Is there any difference in spacing studs 16 or 24 inches apart?
Yes. 24" performs better acoustically, believe it or not. But check the code requirements in your area. They may not be legal, especially if they are load bearing, bringing us to.... You ask about the ceiling, I'd build out the rooms and then give each room its own ceiling, using rafters attached to your walls. Put as many layers of sheetrock on the ceiling as you do on the walls, and make the whole assembly airtight. There is a diagram of how to do this in the stickys of this forum.
