John Sayers19 January 2006#3
That's good Marc - you've resolved it very well - No it's not too much treatment for a room that size - the smaller the room the more treatment required because all the reflections are short - I wouldn't put in that diffusor for the same reason - the reflections are too short. If you are going to float the floor and drop a ceiling I'd go for two 2 x 4 outer walls instead of the one 6" with the inner wall sitting on the new floor totally isolated from the outer wall. Don't forget about Aircon - if you are sound proof you are airtight so some fresh air will be required. cheers john
