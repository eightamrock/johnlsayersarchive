First, welcome to the board; before anything else, I need you to update your PROFILE to include a LOCATION - it’s not possible to suggest anything without this info because there are so many differences worldwide in what materials are available that will work, and what they cost. My first question would have to be, is this “wall” you want to isolate, 1 gypsum panel on EACH side of a frame, or are both panels on the SAME side of the wall frame? This is VERY important. From your drawing, am I correct in assuming that your “neighbors” place is part of the same building? Does your floor extend past the wall and into their space? What about the walls and ceiling? These things will make it even MORE expensive to isolate your space from theirs. The plain truth is, you may be in for a disappointment - isolating sound is NEVER cheap, and can be really difficult when the structure wasn’t designed for sound control in the first place - Let me know the answers to the above, so we can find out whether you’re in luck or in trouble… Steve
