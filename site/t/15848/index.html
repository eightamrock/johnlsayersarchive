gullfo16 April 2011#2
welcome! please update your profile to include your location information. is this diagram showing the plan view or section view? if this is the plan view (meaning your wall is angled and not your ceiling) then you could build out the front wall (left) on an corresponding angle to force your listening position and boundaries to fit symmetrically. on the soffit, some absorption around it in proportion to the other absorption in the room should do it.
