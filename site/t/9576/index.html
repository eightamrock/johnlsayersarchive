rod gervais20 October 2007#3
I agree with Dave - But - beyond that - what does one have to do with the other???????? DO you think (for a moment) that by soffit mounting your speakers you suddenly don't have modal issues? Not only is the answer "yes you do" - in your case - with a (basically) square room - you will have double the problems of most - meaning you will have the same modal issues in 2 axial directions - so you might well need more traps than you have now. Try getting a little more exact with your plans (you are very VERY vague about what you want to do) ands we might be able to help you further. Sincerely, Rod
