I've been on Google, and this forum for the past months searching for duct line for my baffle boxes. On GS I've seen Rod Gervais advicing Linacoustic R-300 as lining for inside the baffle box. Unfortunatelly that's not available here. So I had to go through a ton of searching to find something suitable available here in Europe and even preferably in the Netherlands. I've found the products of Merford, and apparently their Akotherm Basic is suitable for lining baffle boxes, according to them. The question now is, is the acoustical performance good enough for my baffle boxes? Here's a PDF link (https://www.akoestiekwinkel.nl/pub/medi ... _d40_1.pdf), it's in Dutch so the specs are listed in the screenshot underneath. D40 = Density 40kg/m3 the 25 after it is thickness in MM From the info I gathered, and compared to other lining, this seems to be ok, but I just want to make sure I'm not making any mistakes here.
