Soundman202014 March 2013#6
Actually, I'm planning to build two more semi isolated rooms within that large space.
Then you only need to span those rooms! You need one inner-leaf ceiling for each room, and since the garage already has its own roof, you don't need to span anything there. So your maximum span is the width of each room, not the width of the entire garage. In other words if your control room is 10 feet wide, then you need to span ten feet for that, and of the drum room nest to it is 8 feet wide, then the ceiling joists of that only need to span 8 feet. Etc. Each room has its own individual ceiling, and they do not touch each other. So no, you do not need engineered beams. Just the right size joist for each room, most likely 2x8, or some such, depending on the span, the load, the deflection, and the type of wood. It would help if you could post your SketchUp model of the entire studio, so we can check it over and see if there might be ways to improve it, or at least so we understand it better! :) - Stuart -
