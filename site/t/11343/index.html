How to mount heavy acoustic panels?

Started by daiwa on 25 October 2008. 4 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 11343.

daiwa25 October 2008#1
I am planning to buy Primacoustic broadband acoustic panels and their Fulltrap bass traps. But they are heavy mothers. Not the sorts i have seen mentioned in the threads on this forum which are 10-12lbs. Recomendations from their site is to use wall anchors capable of holding at least 55lbs. I will need to mount these panels on both my double wall made of gypsboard (the usual-2 gypsboards,metal stud filled with rockwool construction) and on beefed up brick wall which will be beefed up with some rockwool and 2 gypsboards on metal stud. Extreme care will be taken to properly finish these walls and now it looks like i will have to drill into them and possibly create problems. 1. Is it possible to mount such heavy panels on gypsboard wall at all? 2. How do i mount heavy acoustic panels on walls withot compromising isolation? :cry:

frostgfx28 October 2008#5
I would contact your builder or a contractor, or your local hardware store about heavy duty anchors... 55# sounds like a lot of weight even if it is shear loading and only a couple inches thick! You will need to be dead on with your screws/anchors that you anchor into the studs... Drywall isn't going to support a 55# load as far as I know. You might be able to place heavy duty screw eyes into the ceiling joists and hang the panels with picture wire which is available to hold a 55# load. Probably the best solution overall. Best of luck on your build.
