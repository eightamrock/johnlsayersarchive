I am in the research/planning stages of building a 4' W x 5' L x 7' H (interior dim.) iso booth that will be used mostly to practice and record ac & el guitar, percussion (congas, bongos, cowbell, etc), and vocals/voice-overs. I'm hoping to avoid bothering the neighbors (and my wife!) and to be able to get dry, relatively noise-free recordings. I'll be putting the booth in my apartment for now, but my plan is to build it in such a way that I can disassemble it and take it with me when I move into a bigger place. The apartment is on the 2nd floor of a 3 story building with neighbors above, below, and on 3 sides. My budget is around $500. I know I could/should do the research myself and I'm in the process of doing so but if it is a good deal I need to buy it before it's gone. Apparently this place has "a few pallets of it" and I don't know fast they will move it. I'm planning on using an inside out wall design and I'd use this stuff between the studs. QUESTIONS 1) Is Roxul RHT 80 ok to use in the cavity between the studs in an inside out wall design? 2) Is $6 per 2" x 4' x 6' sheet a good price for Roxul RHT 80 Mineral Wool Insulation? 3) Would I be better off using some other type of insulation? Cost is definitely a big factor here. I'm trying to save wherever I can. P.S. I just found this forum last week and my head hasn't stopped spinning!
