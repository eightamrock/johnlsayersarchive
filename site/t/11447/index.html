Soundman202030 November 2008#2
Hi Phybeau! Looks like you and I have the same problem! That's my thread you are referencing, and I'm still trying to to get everything to line up properly... Did you actually measure the height of your own head while seated normally in the exact chair you are planning to use? 110 cm seems a bit low. The usual reference height is 120 cm. Maybe you could raise the chair a bit? Perhaps think about a footrest if that would put your feet off the floor? Just some ideas... You could try dropping the height of your desk a bit, too, or using shorter monitors, or taking the stands off the monitors and mounting them a bit lower down, directly on the wall. These are all things I'm considering in optimizing my design. The only reason I've seen mentioned regarding the 10 degree angle is that the human ear has some kind of issue with accurately identifying the direction and source of a sound (ie, "stereo imaging") if the sound comes from more than 10 degrees above horizontal, but that was just a comment on another thread here, with no references. However, it does sound plausible. I'm still looking for verification.
