barefoot21 October 2003#6
A 1.5A fuse in line with the tweeter sounds right. This would allow up to about 20W of power dissipation in the tweeter before the fuse blows. The series resistance of a fuse increases with increasing current, creating dynamic compression. This certainly isn't ideal, but I guess it's a reasonable compromise if you're continuously burning out tweeters. Another alternative is to modify the crossover as shown here: http://www.dillonacoustics.com/Loudspea ... _XOYA1.htm However, this will significantly change the sound character of your NS10s. In fact, it will make them more accurate - which you may or may not want. And since you get rid of the response peak in the lower frequency range of the tweeter, you will improve its power handling. You might consider adding both the fuse and the crossover modification. Combined with the crossover modification you might be able to get away with using larger fuse like 2A. Thomas
