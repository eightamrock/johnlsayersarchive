Hey Guys and Gals? Been sucking poly fumes for a few days and think I've burnt some brain cells :shock: Thank God I'm finished with all the staining and touch up paint! Medboy Wrote:l
Cant wait to see all the gear go in
Me Too!! :D I'm actually going to pick up Pro Tools HD/3 Acell with the Control 24 tommorw :D :D :D Before I configure everything, I still have about 250 TRS cables jacks to wire up and 3 Patch Bay's to hook up. I'm going to have all my cables custom sized and neatly organized so there isn't a huge pile of extra cabling everywhere. With the 3 bay's, I'll be able to re-route my little butt off! XSpace, Ro, Dynamike, and Joey, thanks so much for your compliments. Yeah, I watch alot of the design programs on HGTV :D I figured because it was such a small space, I would go with nicer, stain grade trim, seeing the doors were birch laminate, it was hard for me to paint them. Also, the desk was birch plywood and so pretty. I just couldn't bring myself to painting it all black and it really did turn out beautiful after 5 coats. XSpace, I think the camera angle is creating an optical illusion. I pride myself on everything being square, plumb and level. I ran across a bit of a promblem with the automatic door bottoms on 2 of the doors because the concrete floor in my garage was un-level and had some little humps in it :evil: half of the door would seal to the floor, but where the hump was, it had air space underneath :twisted: All the other doors sealed good and with the double doors, it's great isolation :) What sucks is the problem doors are between the CR and DR. I'll probably have to jamb something between the double doors if neccessary to fill the gap during drum recording??? Hey Lou, I love the way you guys in the U.K. talk :D and thanks for the great compliment kind sir. January, I'll be learning Pro Tools HD and all my new toys :? I'm not going to miss suckin' dust, itching from insulation, cutting wood, getting splinters, painting, staining, crawiling around on the floor, falling off ladders, cleaning the floor for the millionth time, etc if you know what I mean. Why do you ask? Just curious. You don't want me to fly over and help get you finished do you? :shock: Well, Here's a few pics of the lounge so far. I hung thick tapestry type curtains acorss the whole back wall which leads to the house. I serves 2 purposes by convering the door into my house and providing some acousitcal dampening. There's 8 panels on an 8' wall so there pretty bunched up and thick. I was also actually able to get some furniture in there along with a little fridge and water cooler (still in box). I still need to get a flat screen to put on the wall. Also, heres the desk stained before it gets covered up with gear. I'm going to paint the little keyboard stand black. Later, Dan

Hi Everyone, Hope you all had a great Holiday season. I sure did. I'm all geared up but still need to hook everything up. Before I post my final pic's, I need to finish all of my acoustical treatments. Here are some absorber panels I built from 1x4's, Mineral wool and fabric. Very simple design but very effective. I hung six in the control room and can't believe the difference already. Once the extra LENRD bass traps and wedge foam go in, as well as my rugs and fabric chairs, I expect a pretty nice sounding room. Here are a few photos of how I did the boxes and hung them. I actually used construction adhesive to attach the slats to the wall which the panels hung on. Check it out. Dan

I'm using the KRK's and sub for an aux reference mix and the bass is boomin' as well as real tight and clean. The sophit traps and LENRD foam are really taking care of the bass signals and the two chairs are also helping absorb I'm sure. I cranked it as loud as it would go and could barely hear the bass outside. You can't hear nuthin in the house at all. I am noticing better isolation over time as the green glue cures. I will tune the rooms soon. Dan
