Dan Scott21 November 2007#27
Oh, when the rock install begins, I'm gonna be sick that week:)
Darn, I thought you were going to come over and help :shock: Yes, I did the framing with a little help from my friend. It helps to have someone hold the other end of a board if you know what I mean. I've decided to hire a crew to hang and finish the rock . I built 5 houses last year and the same crew of my Mexican friends did excellent work for the down low and are FAST. They should be because this is all they do day in and day out. Of course, my project is new to them and I will be there every minute following behind with backer rod and caulk. I'll have to make sure things are getting done correctly if you know what I mean. One of the reasons for the fast progress is because I'm actually not working right now. The realestate market has crashed in Atlanta and things are bad for a lot of builders and folks in realestate. I had gotten into buying foreclosures about 5 years ago then moved into flipping/building houses. Prior to that, I owned a home based production studio. It wasn't as pro as my new one will be but I stayed pretty busy. Now I'm much better financially able to do it right this time so I'm going balls out with the construction and new gear. I am blessed and really exited about getting it started again. I never dreamed I would be going back into the recording business with a totally pro studio but when things took a turn for the worst with the housing market, I can say I'm glad I have other "skillz" like music production and engineering. The realestate business was good but alsovery stressful and risky. I'm kind of relieved it's over. One cool thing is I won't have to complain about the high gas prices cuz I won't have to leave the house to go to work :D Heck, I won't even have to get dressed if I don't want to. Now, I know I'm not the only one who has mixed audio in my underwear :shock: So basically, a BIG motivator for me to move along as quick as possible and work my tail off to get cranked up is because I don't have a job! No cashflow :o I'm basically creating myself a job so my goal is to get things complete by the 1st of the year. I'll most likely get the insulation in over the weekend and call of Monday morning for insulation inspection. If the inspector comes out Tuesday, I should be able to start sheetrock and get the first layer on by the end of next week. Check back for more pic's once insulation is in. Thanks for the interest in my project and I appreciate you guys and this forum. Everybody enjoy your Turkey Day and behave with the Pecan and Pumkin Pies :D Dan
