Edo Peters21 January 2007#4
There is a way to 'pimp' weed fabric or felt... just dye wash it in a nice colour and add a little starch when you rinse the stuff clean with water afterwards. Then when it's still a bit wet put it on your absorbers or wherever you want to use it on, and let it dry completely. You will get a nice tight fit and it looks way cool.
