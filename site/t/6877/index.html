sharward22 August 2006#2
Temporary and sound resistant are practically mutually exclusive when it comes to construction. There are ways to treat walls and ceilings with temporary lightweight materials to make the space sound better... But when it comes to achieving any decent levels of isolation, I'm afraid there is no substitute for mass-air-mass. FYI, "sheetblock," also known as "mass loaded vinyl," is a rip-off... So don't even think about it. See this thread (and many others) for testimony on that. --Keith :mrgreen:
