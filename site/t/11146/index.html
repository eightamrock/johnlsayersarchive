John Hile4 September 2008#7
Jett98, You may not want to compress the insulation. It would be better to have it in the cavity loosely as insulation's purpose is to absorb mid and higher range frequency. The NRC has stated that insulation in a single stud wall (which is what it looks like you are doing since I see no decoupling mention) really doesn't do much but I always recommend it. You may also want to consider using a damping compound like Green Glue between your 2 panels. You mentioned that you will be using 3/8" DW and 1/2" soundboard as your panels. I would recommend using 1/2" DW as opposed to soundboard due to its mass which will yield better performance. Soundboard is best used in floors as an absorber, and (to a certain extent) floor system decoupler. I don't think anyone can disagree with Andre's 90/10 Law. John
