Hey everyone, i'm a student that has just recently started a music technology course. Now with all of this being completely new to me, I'm a little stuck. I've been set an assignment where I've been asked to design a studio to record commercial music. This is how it goes;
a) Design a studio to record commercial music. This should include both popular music genres (drums, bass guitars and vocals) as well as small classical acoustic sections. You may also specialise in other areas such as TV/Film audio post-production and full orchestra recordings, though this is not obligatory. Your design should include a floor plan detailing equipment layout and an equipment list with individual item costs. You are not required to provide costing for cabling, acoustic treatment and materials. Expenditure on equipment should not exceed £750,000 and should be calculated at current retail or realistic second hand prices. You need not include capital (building) or design costs. b) Support your choice of equipment and provide a critical analysis of the strengths and weaknesses of your design for the above recording requirements. c) Describe in clear detail an appropriate signal path for recording and monitoring a vocal in your studio. This should include an appropriate choice of microphone.
I was just wondering if some people could point me in the right direction of what main equipment I will need, how many mic's etc? I'm finding it really difficult as I dont know where to turn! Any advice on how to approach my essay would be greatly appreciated. Alex
