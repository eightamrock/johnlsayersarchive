Sounds like you have your work cut out for you. However, if you want the free-of-charge wisdom of the experts who hang out here, I suggest you do your homework and post according to the instruction given at the top of the forum(s). My first thought though; soundproof the controlroom while you're at it. If you plan on using it alongside other people, consider the fact that they might want to listen at some extreme levels, at some awkward times of day. Just a thought.
