Caulk question

Started by Finally on 22 January 2005. 1 reply.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 3058.

Finally22 January 2005#1
Where the ceiling meets the walls...what brand of "soundproof" caulk can be painted. How difficult is it to finish the caulk to look like a normal mudded joint.
knightfly22 January 2005#2
You don't - you caulk the joints in the 1/4" gap you leave around the perimeter, layer by layer - then you can use flexible plastic inside corner mold and LIGHTLY mud it in place for a finished look without completely rigid coupling between wall and ceiling. As far as I know, there aren't any good acoustic-rated caulks that should be painted; their properties should be that they never skin over, this keeps them pliant and maintains airtight seals so important to maximum isolation...
