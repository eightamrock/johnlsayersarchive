Ted White15 December 2009#9
We tested soundboard many times. At least one manufacturer recommends installing the drywall with screws into the soundboard and not the stud... Like installing to cardboard. When screws are set through the soundboard into studs, the performance is very mediocre. Too dense to decouple (screws notwithstanding) and too dense for any beneficial absorption. Not bad as an underlayment. I'd suggest as other have to use a base layer of plywood or OSB, then layers of drywall.

Ted White17 December 2009#11
It's really about the mass, not so much the densities. Particleboard is dense, and so tends to build nice massive partitions. MDF does as well, as does drywall. Personally, if I was looking to have a nail base in a wall or ceiling I'd be looking at plywood or OSB. They hold screws much better than particleboard or MDF
