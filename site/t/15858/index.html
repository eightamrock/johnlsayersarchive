overall the design looks good. are you considering soffit mounting the monitors? on the isolation - assuming the walls and ceiling are decoupled - you're better off with the more mass inside if you have to make that trade off. if you go to studiotips forum and find the MSM spreadsheets from Eric Desart, you will be able to make reasonable calculations on the best allocation and spacing for the walls and ceiling. the steel framing for the ceiling though is going to be a challenge unless you can suspend that weight off the primary structure using isolation mounts.

gullfo20 April 2011#5
the density seems off since each layer doesn't increase the density, just the overall mass. so if all walls are composed of GWB, just use the 650kg/m3 density. the 600mm "On Center" (oc) means the wall studs (or in the ceiling joists) are spaced so the center line of each member is separated by 600mm. correspondingly, the spacing of the drywall screws would be 600mm across the spans and 150-200mm along the spans depending on the fastener schedule.
