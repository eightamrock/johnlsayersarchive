Ethan Winer25 July 2009#7
ajoantmug wrote:
I am hoping someone can give me some advice on placement of this in my room and how that may or may not effect the number of absorbers I build.
Bass traps go in as many corners as you can manage, and mid/high frequency absorbs go at the reflection points. In a small room more absorbers or diffusors on the rear wall can help further. That's 90 percent of it. For the rest, here are some good resources: Acoustics FAQ RealTraps Articles RealTraps Videos --Ethan
