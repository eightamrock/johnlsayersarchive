Ah sunday. I did my 1st tests, with a small system . A Factotum Performance from Voice system, it s a compact amplification system with 2 sattelites & a subwwofer/amplifier/mixer ( I use it for rehearsing & small events). So this is not a hifi sound system. I put the satellites in the bass traps & subwoofer in front of the room. But it s good after all this work to seat & relax & listen to some music. I v listen to Urban Dance squad / Peter Gabriel / my last record Earth Link / Daft Punk & a flamenco guitar solo cd. Because of the system I couldn t judge the equalisation but : stereophony is incredible with a stable image even when you walk in the control room details and depth are very very good Lede control room works very well wood in the back give a good mood and ambience This is not a dead laboratory with a small sound completly absorbed walls angles don t send backsoundwaves there s life and warm mood balance between treble medium and low frequencies stay constant at low level and at 96dBa it was very cool ... and very few sound loss at the exterior of the building, I will makesound proofing tests when I can close all the doors ( need to finish painting doors, clean the floor, take all the tools out ... ) ...... Now I need to pack everythink at the existing studio before .... the Big removal day, so I have to wait a bit for real test with Ns10 and Alesis Monitor 2 & JM Reynaud systems.
