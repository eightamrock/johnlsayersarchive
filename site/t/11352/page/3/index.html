garlo wrote:
I think that his 13mm gb is not really Mass. Real Mass is the big 40cm wall with cement blocks & bricks.
Wow, hold your horses there partner! 13mm gb IS mass! Not denser or heavier than 150mm of concrete blocks of course, but it alwaysl behaves as a leaf. I don't know what I'd do in your situation, it's tricky, the experts should answer this, but IF I were you, I'd probably just raise a frame with 2-4 layers of gb on the inside, touching the cement blocks and insulation covering the rest of the frame. If you do it like this, you're gonna add mass to the cement blocks wall and use the insulation for acoustic treatment. I'm not sure how will it affect the transmission loss of that wall assembly. It will increase obviously, but how much? You'll still have good isolation with that 3 leaf wall assembly, it will be less than a 2 leaf wall's on the low end of frequency range though... I don't know if you could live with that, specially with your neighbour on the other side of that wall... Anyways, good luck with your build, friend! :thu: John
