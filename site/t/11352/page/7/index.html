That being the case, I would say your wall thickness would indicate that 1.5cm of glass is going to be greater than the mass of your walls. Lighting from overhead and sunlight coming in from an exterior window is far more likely to cause glare on the windows as you have them.

Ro4 September 2009#165
garlo wrote:
Hi again xaMdaM I ve found a translation of your measurments, so 1" is 2,54cm. Do you think I must find glass thicker than 1 & 1,5cm ? my walls are 3 X 13 gypsum board /90 glasswool /10 or 20 air / 90 gw / 3X13gb. Do you think its ok ?
(you prolly have 3x 12,5mm boards..) The glass is just a part of the wall. So, it has to be atleast as heavy as your gypsum boards (GB) layer. Knowing that glass is about 3 times as heavy as GB (2500 vs 700 kg/m3), you should have atleast 12,5mm glass in one layer.
