Ro13 August 2009#128
Laminated glass is therefor also known as Safetyglass. the way to install glass; - Add a rubber or foam strip all round the frame - add 2 windows setting blocks at 1/4 from the side on the bottom part of the frame to put the glass on. - add glassstops, thick/storng enough to keep the glass in place. add rubber/foam on the inside (where the glass will be) also. - Leave a gap (about 6mm in depth and 4mm in width) above this rubber/foam strip to caulk the window (use sealant for glass) - paint if you like Laminated glass is the way to go.

garlo24 August 2009#145
Bass traps need air to work just in the bass frequencies. A whole corner of rockwool & fabric will work in a larger array of frequencies. So bass trap is made with 9 layers of acoustished = rockwool 8cm ( 90Kg/m3) with a space of 15cm of air between each. Small pieces of acoustished 15cm high are there just to hold the layers. Walls The front of the cr & roughly 55% of the walls are made with Rockwool 8cm & fabric. The back wall & the rest of the walls are made with wood. There s just small bass traps at the back in the corner. Ceiling The whole ceiling is made with rockwool 8cm (90Kg/m3 ) + air 7cm + rocksol 4cm ( rockwwool 120Kg/m3) except in the middle of the cr in the false ceiling there s more bass traps + rockwool 8cm + air + rockwool 8cm.

That is a good idea. It is no secret that LF waves are the one thing folks with musically tuned rooms have the biggest issue with. The longer and stronger low frequency waves need dense/heavy things to slow them down so you can understand my surprise when I see what is basically a bass aborber cut in half. The air doesn't help, you can ask any body who is anybody, this is not a good idea when it comes to slowing down the effects of LF. It is akin to creating multiple skinned wall layers in an attempt to arrest the overall sound frequency. Not that it doesn't work, but it takes so much room, so much material, and so much labor it is just not worth the effort. I can see some aspect of what is a true bass trap in this design. Problem, to me, is that it does not have the depth or breadth that a true bass trap has in order to make this type of panel design function. But hey...I'm just a carpenter :)
