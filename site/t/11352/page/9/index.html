garlo16 January 2010#203
This is one of my last post, I will send some good pictures & datas when I have time to make measurments & find a good photographer. Removal party was fun, big work (50m3) but many friends. We finished during the night listening to favourite cd's of friends. Everybody was impressed with the sound imaging & depth & many understand now what I was doing intensively for 14 months. I worked the whole week on my new office & studio unpacking stuff & trying to find a new place for everything ... I want to thanks everybody on this site both on design & studio construction. Some gave me some advices, others encouragements, some were against my ideas, others were shouting, some helped me when I was mystaking : that’s a real living forum. Sometimes it was difficult because of the english language and sometimes I was not on the same wave lenght with contributors but this forum is a real big thing. In this mp3 world, with the decline of cd’s & labels, with the future uncertainty for recording studios, where nobody can say how people will enjoy music tomorrow, it’s good to know that everywhere in the world there’s people interessed by sound & recording studios & rooms sounds & building small or big control rooms. Thanks to all contributors who gave time to write & share their knowledge Thanks to Guildo Bassrocket Ro Lilith Soundman Xspace Jominer Bassman Jbassimo Russrussels for sharing with me ( & of course if you come in Bordeaux it will be a pleasure to share some music & wine with you ) and of course ... a big big Thanks to John Sayers Chapeau bas Monsieur.
