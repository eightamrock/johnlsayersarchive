Soundman202018 November 2008#19
Cut the top of my walls is it a joke ??
Like Ro said: no, it's not a joke. If you have your inner leaf directly bolted to your outer leaf, then there really isn't much point in having two leaves at all, since you have a direct path for sound to pass through the entire structure. Unless, of course, you really don't need much isolation at all? But if that's the case, then you probably don't need an inner leaf, either. Two questions: 1) How much isolation do you have right now? I guess you already measured that with a sound level meter? 2) How much isolation do you need? If you don't know those numbers yet, then why are you building it like this? If you need isolation at the level of practically all of the studios on this forum, then I don't see how you can get it if your inner leaf is directly bolted to your outer leaf. That was the reason for my suggestion: Cut the tops of the inner walls, and join the walls together with joists to form a proper ceiling structure. That way, the walls won't fall down (since they'll be tied together with the joists), and you'll also have the high level of isolation that most studios need.
