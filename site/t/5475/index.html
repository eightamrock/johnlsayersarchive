Hi everybody, I'm a noob here and I'm gonna move our band's studio to a new place. This place has 2 rooms with dimensions shown in the attached picture http://jan02.imgshare.us/rptqvd.jpg Is my designed layout gonna works? We mostly recorded our band music (Metal style) and some other kinds of music (Hiphop, Country, maybe...) The control room is poorly a cube (we dont have any choice) so I try to make it non-paralelled by using differents absorbers' textures on the walls with lots of bass traps in corners and in the cloud upon the mix area. The vox booth's double doors are glass-typed so no windows is needed. The exact positions of absorbers and diffusors will be determined when I observe the room with a RTA meter. Currently we have 2 pairs of NS-10Ms made in Japan so we're gonna add a sub. Please give your opinions on this layout !!! Thanks for reading !! Sorry for my bad English !!!

kendale20 January 2006#4
Aloha, Here's a couple of thoughts at first glance: - Parallel walls - try to avoid by using side wall absorbers perhaps? http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm - Control room door to vox booth – Looks a little tight...is there enough access for door/corner trap in the way? Perhaps a sliding glass door instead or entry through kitchen? - Is it possible to raise the cloud over mix position closer to ceiling (say about 7.6cm - 10cm from ceiling and angled front low/rear higher?) - the room may be too short for diffusors - larger corner traps/side absorbers? - Is there enough room for the drums and mix position? (Besides the fact that they're loud...it may make it difficult to make critical mic/sound adjustments) - Is it possible to move drums into vox/kitchen space and record acoustic guitars out in the control room or overdub them from the vox booth after the drums are done? Hope this gives you a few ideas to start with. Aloha 8)
