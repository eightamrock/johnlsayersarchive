eddiemunster66622 November 2009#8
Here's what Rod had to say: The rubber seal I use is Water and Ice dam = it is used on the bottom of shingle roofs before the application of shingels -and is meant to protect the structure in the event that ice daming at the eaves occurs. what this does in the application of the window is to create a seal of the window cavity from the interior of the wall cavity - which will help to stop the formation and migration of dust within the window cavity. Any rubber strips will do - however I like this product because one side of it has an afhesive applied to it - so it comes prepared to make a perfect seal without the use of any fasteners. I hope this helped/ Rod
