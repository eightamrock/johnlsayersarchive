Hi all, Ok, here is the overview... I am opening a music lessons studio in a Minneapolis suburb. I currently own one place and am opening a second. This new lesson studio will be located within a music instrument store, also being built from scratch but I am not the owner of the store, just the music school. A unique feature of my current school is that I built a recording studio in the space for students to record, as well as people in the community. The room is nice, but way too small. The new store/school will be large enough for me to build a bigger and better recording studio. Now, the space is not way huge, but it is all I am able to carve out for myself. The space I have to work with is 20' x 20', with an added chuck sticking out 6.5' and 11' long. The ceiling is 12' in both the CR and the live room. See picture 1... http://img385.imageshack.us/img385/3123/studiodesignemail20ar.jpg Now, the live room will be used for recording purposes, as well as a small classroom for pre-school music classes, group guitar, etc... For the construction, I am open to do just about anything...money permitting. The space is currently empty, no walls or anything. So, I am able to construct my studio/classroom any way I want, within this given space. I don't think I will be floating the floor, but I will build a drum riser that will be floating, since drums usually make the most noice through the floor, or so I have noticed. I also want to build a small amp closet...nothing fancy but just a small closet to hold an amp. I do not need a room for the drums...I like the live sound and I've never had a major problem with leakage from the bass or guitars. Plus, I don't have much room to build another room. The placement of the drum riser and amp closet can be changed...if you have a better place, please let me know. One of the big concerns is making sure there is enough room for children, small ensembles, etc to use the space without feeling too cramped. therefore, I put the drums up in front of the control room and the amp closet near the CR. As for budget, I may be totally off here but I would like to keep the construction cost under $8000. That is not a hard number, but a good area that I can be at with a little room to move. Ok, so here come the questions: (I apologize if these questions are posted in the wrong section of the forum, but since I am asking about design and construction regarding acoustics, I wasn't sure which category would be better.) 1. In the live room, is there anything I can do design/construction-wise that will make the room have a good live feel, without being a gymnasium. If need be, I can purchase Auralex type stuff and treat the room, but I wanted to know if there were any construction designs that might be better and possibly cost less than simple room treatment. Again, I need this room to sound as good as possible for recording, but as large as possible to accomidate small classes and Kindermusic type classes. I know its a tall order, but its definately original... 2. In the control room, would it be better to design and construct the room as shown, or would building a rectangular room and adding wall treatment be a better and/or cheaper solution? 3. Also in the CR, would designing and building a slanted ceiling be better and more cost efficent than just treating a typical ceiling. Ok, I think those are the main questions I had at this point. Please, feel free to question, criticize, and redesign at leisure. I am looking for all the input I can get and from what I have read on the forum, some of you really know your stuff. If I need to post this in the acoustics or construction forum, please let me know and I will repost ASAP. Thanks in advance... Jordy A
