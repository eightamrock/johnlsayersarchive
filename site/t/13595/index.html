A question regarding requested (or not) parallelism of walls/walls/ or floor/ceiling. I though that parallelism was pretty much compulsory because all the commercial studios I work in have slanted walls and ceilings - but that could be simply fabric stretched over various treatments covering parallel walls. I was then told that provided that you treat the walls with absorption and the ceiling too, then it as OK to have walls parallel to each other, and ceiling parallel to floor. Like many people and hobbyists I can have an opinion, but as I know nothing compared to expert professionals who build commercial studios for a living, having an opinion is just like tossing a coin especially since acoustics are so counter-intuitive... So: parallel with absorption treatment, or not parallel (well, with treatment as well anyway)???
