Hi, I have a question regarding my studio. A friend of mine which is a sound engineer from Germany, suggested me to place hanging panels in my studio ceiling. He said the there are some low mid frequencies that needs absorption. How should I do it? He said that I should put a 1m x 2m or 1m x 1m thin plywood, and put insulation glued behind the panel, with the wood side facing the floor. Any comments? Ari
Ethan Winer6 December 2007#2
Ari,
bolehnggak wrote:
He said that I should put a 1m x 2m or 1m x 1m thin plywood, and put insulation glued behind the panel, with the wood side facing the floor.
I disagree with that advice, because insulation hidden behind plywood will do little. What you need is bass traps in those corners. --Ethan
