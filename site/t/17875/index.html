deedz19 October 2012#2
ok well ur best option is to do a room within a room construction. basically its 2 walls with an 1'' air between then do double 5/8s drywall with greenglue sandwiched between sheets. make sure to acousitic seal every nook n cranny ex. drywall joints, top and bottom plate. your going to have to get rid of the suspened ceiling cuz that does nothing for soundproofing, you need mass. then electrical and hvac are whole other ball game. u need an air in and air out. with a room air tight room u need fresh air in and out else simply u could die lol research dead vent thats the best option for this. doors are another thing to, ull need to a double door system and add mass to each door and seal properly. or if your budget is big get a studio door which runs thousands lol this is just general info but if u need help msg me. hope this gets u in the right direction.
