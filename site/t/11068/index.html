The idea is that when fan A is on (the inner of the two ducts) you get cold air coming into booth and the booth is slightly pressurized which i read is important. If you turn on fan B as well (outer duct on sketch) you get more circulation and fresh air is introduced. All you have to do is install cold air intake close to floor level and hot air exhaust close to ceiling level. Any feedback is welcome! Thanks.

i just don't get how you want to do such design using only one duct. Air does not enter and exit the duct at the same time. It enters of exists according to the air pressure changes or the help of fans.
The air flow depends on which fan is on. A or B or A+B. Fans are indicated, air flow is indicated. The only thing i didn't indicate is which is fan A and which is B. That is indicated in the follow up post. Fan A is the one sucking cold air from the CR. Fan B is Sucking hot air from booth and fresh air from out side. When fan A is on there is positive pressure in the booth because of the damper. If damper opens air will flow out side or back into the control but that will not be a substantial flow. If you turn fan B on as well it sucks air from outside into the CR. In a booth this small it is enough to pump air in with just one fan and air will exit through an exhaust with out the need for another fan sucking air out. I put another fan for the fresh air. I'm not sure how to better explain it.
