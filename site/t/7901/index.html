Denis, unless the "soundboard" you're referring to is one of the newer high density gypsum boards that are actually HEAVIER than wallboard, you do NOT want to use it in a wall construction - the name of the game is MASS-air-MASS, and the more of each you can incorporate into a wall the better it will perform - especially at lower frequencies. Haven't seen a sketch or anything - what type of walls are you building, single or double framed, or??!? STeve
