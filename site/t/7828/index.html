Hi Kendale, yeah I enclosed the speakers in 20mm boxes but the back is open to cool it down. Lately I started mixing at lower level so hopefully there will be no problem with the speakers getting too warm. Right now I'm planning on testign everything out for a couple weeks and then I'll made some fine tweaking. BTW the boxes are supertight, and so far (after testing it at full blast volume) nothing rattles. The bass are really tight but I don't hear any major bump down in the bottom. I remember Barefoot saying that when you mount speakers in soffit you should put a -6dB correction on the low range but what I hear is just more extenson on bass frequencies (like an octave more or so). Right now I don't have any hangers in the bottom but I plan to put some in there in the next few days. I'm working on the cloud now (is basically three of my gobos stuck together) and next I'll have to plan out the side absorber. Thanks to all who helped. Cheers.
