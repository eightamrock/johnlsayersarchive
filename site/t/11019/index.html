Hello Seanpace, As you mentioned - ISOVER are the people in South Africa. This was the same company as Owens Corning, just bought and sold a few times in SA. CAVITYLITE - Energylite 47.5 x 50mm in rolls in 1200mm x 5.0m ROLL This is exactly the same thing as 703 Rigid Fibreglass panels. It is 47.5 Kg / M3 or 3 lb/cubic foot. They sell it in different thicknesses, but this is perfect for your application. The rolls are approximately R400 per roll EXCL VAT They are out in Kuilsriver and I found them to be very helpful. I believe they will sell you less than tonnes of the Cavitylite. They also stock SONIC LINER for Lining Air Conditioning ducts, in various sizes - 25mm and 50mm thicknesses in 5 to 10m rolls. How much to use? - The thicker the panels the more effective the absorbtion, but typically people use from 50mm to 100mm of the 47.5 kg/M3 Cavity lite. This is pretty standard. Sometimes people use 50mm with a 50mm air gap behind to save on fibreglass costs. There are many posts on this Forum about making your own panels, often with great photo step x step guides. See the Stickies as well. PreludetoJune is right that they dont expect to have Rockwool and that the fibreglass is a pain to work with, but good protection, masks, gloves ETC and work outside and it is not so bad. Good luck Marz
