I have about 7 slat modules (2'x2') thay have slats but no backs. Anyway, what is the best way to make these "airtight" now? I am thinking of using weatherstrip foam between the frames and the plywood backing. Any ideas? I could put caulk down, but I am not sure if it would do much.
kendale14 December 2006#2
Aloha, I put a cleat running along the inside of the frames; set to the thickness of plywood backing, then screwedl the plywood into place. If you desire a more airtight seal, you could run a bead of caulking along the joining surfaces of the cleat and plywood, and then screw/nail into place. Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
