Just use the existing concrete floor! Great acoustically. No other flooring required. But if the concrete is ugly, or not in good condition, or you don't like the idea of concrete, then lay laminate flooring on it. Takes up about half an inch, total. Or even less.
Here's a link to the current SKP file 17MB
You forgot to purge it! After purging, it is only about 4 MB... :)
Would I benefit from using more of the space between the left side control room wall and the outer wall making the broadband slat resonator deeper to address lower frequencies?
I think you are wasting space by splaying the entire wall. Just splay the first section, the front part, up to roughly where your head is. That's where you need it most, and will allow you to get a much better angle on that part, improving your chances of getting a true RFZ design, which you do not have at present. So you cannot use slot walls for the front section of the walls, as they are at your first reflection points. That area needs to be absorptive, not reflective, unless you go for true RFZ. So I would flatten out your side walls from the mix position backwards, and increase the angle of the side walls from the mix position forwards. You should be able to get RFZ, I think: I didn't test it, but just eye-balling it, I think it will work. Also, angle your slot walls a bit extra, in addition to the angle of the actual inner-leaf isolation wall. In other words, make the space behind the slats themselves a bit deeper at the front of the room, and a bit shallower at the rear: that makes the slots more broadband, lass tuned to specific frequencies, which is good.
