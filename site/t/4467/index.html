HOLA QUERIDOS AMIGOS!!! My name is Daniel and I recently moved from Montreal, Canada to Buenos Aires ARGENTINA with all my studio equipment and hopefully we can all add up to my new life project. Things down here are done very different than in the US or CA, there are no building permits or laws or inspectors or even insurance in residential properties (they exist but as we all know it´s a 3rd world country and things work very different than in teory). We will be starting with a layout base 3.8 mts wide by 12.50 long made out of concrete wich is allready built! THAT IS ALL WE HAVE NOW... NO WALLS, CEILINGS, ONLY THE BASE, SO THIS IS THE STARTING POINT Things here are built with different materials and construction methods, for example, walls are made with solid bricks, hollow bricks, cement coverings and some other things that I am not familiar with. House ceiling are usually 2.4 mts high and for what people have told me, gypsum walls are only used in appartment buidings or offices. STUD FRAMING IS UNKNOWN BY EVERYONE I ASKED, istead they make CONCRETE columns filled with structural wire. Walls in a house are usualy 15 cm wide, but other widhts used are 20, 30 and 45 cm. SO IF ANYONE IS EXPERIENCED WITH THESE MATERIALS.... PLEASE HELP!! Studio will record live bands of latin music as also midi sequencing, maybe 2 or 3 people at a time in the live room, and control should fit me and maybe 3 or 4 more people sitting around comfty on a couch. There should be a bathroom and a little kitchen with a small gas stove to heat up water for the famous MATE (argentinian tea). So I guess the layout will have 3 divisions, live room, controll room, and the last one with the bathroom and kitchen. I HAVE NO CLUE IN WHAT DESIGNING REQUIERES, SO, if anyone is interested I would ask you to start drawing something and discussing different aspects of the studio plan... WALL WIGHTS, ANGLES, CEILING HEIGHT, CONSTRUCTION MATERIALS COMPARISON. MONEY IS NOT A PROBLEM FOR NOW SO HIT ME WITH SOMETHING NOT OVER 40000 DOLLARS, WICH IS A LOT DOWN HERE NOW BECAUSE OF THE EXCHANGE. WHEN WE FINISH THIS, EVERYONE WHO ADDED AND IDEA OR COMMENT IS INVITED TO MY HOUSE FOR A HUGE BARBQ!!!! THANX EVERYONE, THIS FORUM IS FULL OF AWESOME PEOPLE SO I KNOW I´M IN THE RIGHT PLACE ONE MORE THING... MR SAYERS YOU ROCK!!! GOD BLESS YOU! HASTA PRONTO
