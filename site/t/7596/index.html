Hi greeny, i'll give it a shot, some other member will confirm or correct me if i am wrong. #3 I think this is not a good idea. The plaform you are going to build will never have enough mass, you'll just end up creating a huge boomy box. Unless it's filled with sand (and make sure the floor can support it) i would not do it.
the walls will be 2 seperate stud walls with an air cap of 3" between them
Good
i will be using 2 layer of plaster board on the outside 1 on each faceing board and 2 on the inside wall a total 6 layers all filled with rockwool and sealed with mastic sealant .
I am not sure i am following you but from what i understand there should be no plaster boards in between your walls all plaster boards only goes on the outside of each walls. Please re-read article in the references section before doing anything. #5 Personaly i wouldn't spend money on fiberboard (not enough mass if it's what i think it is) i'd rather go with OSB or MDF if you want walls different thickness/ densities. Or better yet use some green glue between layer of plasterboard. #6 I'd put the control room the other way around and make it bigger, you have the space to do it. Also strive for symetry in the CR. I'd splay side walls if you can (6degres min. each). According to your plan, people will have to go from the studio through the CR to get to the bathroom ! The way you've design the whole thing, the CR become a huge "virtual corridor" ! A door from the studio to the chillout room is needed. A Bathroom next to the chill out room seems like a better design. This is just a personal observation but i wouldn't put a door that goes direcly from the CR to the studio, doors are weak points in those construction. Having a TL of 120 (just exagerating for the sake of argument) for your walls, won't do anything good if the doors only have a TL of 30. But that is just me. I'd use the chillout room as an "air lock" between the two. I think you should re-work your plan.
