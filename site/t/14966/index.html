So someone suggested to dig down below the existing footer? If, and this is a big if because I first do not know your actually environment but second have a better than average understanding of the principals involved this was completed by someone local and authorized, then they have it incorrect. First thing is you do not disturb the interior of a concrete foundation in a manner that allows the load to place undue stress, E.G the earth is removed from around the footer allowing pressure from the load and the outside to be exerted in unacceptable ways. But if this was not the case, good. But the placement of a class one vapor retarder which qualifies as a vapor barrier, poly, is placed in direct contact with the ground and directly underneath the concrete. But since no concrete is used, the poly should still be placed in direct contact with the earth since this is where you are actually trying to protect the upper area from capillary action of the water at and below grade. To protect the poly, sand of about 4 inches would be poured on top of the poly and then the gravel. It sounds suspect to me, you have any actual pictures of this process and the stage where you are at currently?
