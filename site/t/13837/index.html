xSpace11 December 2009#5
Thomas, You aren't doing anything, it is just a busy time of the year and folks like to stay focused on a build from beginning to end, if at all possible. I agree, like jbassino mentioned, your plan looks like the walls are basic construction and do not include any decoupling. This is an important aspect to isolation. But I think, based on my limited experience, your design is a good start. As a matter of fact, the control room looks fine especially with the room directly at the rear which could prove to be a fine bass trapping area. There are many acoustical techniques that can correct the flutter echo and other issues that parrellel walls present. So in that respect, your design isn't completely bad. But I would agree that more time spent in research will reward you in this effort. Looking around you will find many at this forum that start off running only to slow down and build more correctly as the learning they receive shows that something needs to be corrected in the pursuit of a better isolated/acoustical environment.
