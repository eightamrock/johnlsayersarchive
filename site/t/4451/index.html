lowdbrent1 October 2005#2
You are always going to have problems with low end, unless you get into a larger space. If it were me, I would go for one large room, with the gear in iso racks or a properly designed equipment closet. That way you could go bo off a smaller space to record, and then have the distance that you need front to back and left to right for proper mixing dimension ratios. That's what I did. It is great, especially if you are the musician that you are engineering.
JCBigler1 October 2005#3
If you are building a whole new house, you might as well build a real recording studio into it. I mean, why build a house with a garage, and then turn the garage ito a studio, when you can build it with a studio, meant to be used as a studio. The house layout looks nice though.
