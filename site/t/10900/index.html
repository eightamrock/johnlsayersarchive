delusion15 July 2008#3
Roberto, The answers to your questions are in the "stickies" at the top of the forums. You're probably not getting help because those questions have been asked and answered many times here. Foam is next to worthless for "soundproofing" isolation. You are going to need a " room within a room" if you are serious about not disturbing the neighbors unless they are really far away. We're talking 2 completely decoupled walls and ceiling all the way around. A pot of coffee and a few days reading here would be the best favor you could do for yourself. It can be overwhelming at first, but it will be worth it if you do your homework.
