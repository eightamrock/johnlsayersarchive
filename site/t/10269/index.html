AVare29 February 2008#2
Please read and follow the second sticky at the top of this forum. I think you know a complete answer could fill books. Trying to keep it simple for a post, the amount of absorption needed is defined by several equations. Several have been formalized and put into recommendations. The Grammy 5.1 document in the studio design section sticky references several of them. Without knowing your knowledge of math, the general equation is: RT=0.25((V/Vref)^0.333) where: RT=Reverb Time V=Room volume Vref=reference room Volume of 3500 ft^3 A more general guideline is an average absorption co-efficient of 0.4. What helps make it more confusing is that good low end treatment requires a lot of material, more than for highs. There are relatively simple ways to control higher frequency absorption, like using FRK faced insulation for full range absorbers, or covering with plastic or aluminum foil. Keep asking questions! The more you know, the better your space will be. Andre edit: corrected order of operations in equation
