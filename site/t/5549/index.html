Edit your profile to include your location. This is very important, because this is a worldwide resource, and as such, material costs and availability vary widely. For example, masonry is cheaper than gypsum in some parts of the globe, whereas it's the exact opposite in other regions.
This will really come in handy two or three posts down the road. It's also part of the forum guidelines: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=3231 Thanks! :wink: Tough situation...a dilemma indeed! To achieve isolation, both from sound getting in or sound getting out, requires what has been described on this forum as the M-A-M or mass-air-mass principle. Isolation also requires making the environment airtight and vibration free. As you say being that you are in an apartment and cannot build such a structure, it doesn't look like you'll be able to accomplish the isolation you're after. Another approach is an iso-booth type of structure (as in Whisper Room: http://www.whisperroom.com/) but they are very expensive/less effective at isolation than the M-A-M approach (but another approach nonetheless.) That being said, here's a link to some acoustical treatments to at least improve the sound of the work area you're in. (However they do not accomplish the isolation you are after): http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm and here for more good info: http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html (be sure to click on the tabs at left and then at the top) By the way, I am in a somewhat similar situation as you, only in a house. If you care to, here's a couple of links to check out the learning curve I went through, the construction phase, and the listening test results: http://johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic ... ght=#15598 and here: http://johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=4091 Hope these help, Aloha 8)
