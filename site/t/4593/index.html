Not only are you running into practical limitations on that very low headroom issue, you're almost certainly going to run into legal limitations as well. :( Have you been through my "Permits, Codes, Licenses -- and WHY YOU SHOULD CARE!" thread yet? :roll: Codes typically call for minimum finished overhead clearances to be 7' 6" in habitable spaces. Space becomes "habitable" as soon as you add air conditioning or heating. At least, that's how I understand it. I hate to be such a downer, but I don't see how you can get this project airborne within the law. I hope that I'm wrong and that you can prove me so.

What's the date on the certificate of occupancy? Might it have been issued prior to the basement getting finished? :roll: Don't forget to look into business licensing / home occupation permits while you're at it. You'd hate to get legal permits to build your studio, then find out later that you're prohibited from running such a business out of your home and be effectively shut down if/when a neighbor complains. (It happens all the time.) If it's a hobby/pastime that you're not charging money for, then that's one thing (not a business)...
