Louden’s formulas most widely used in professional studios worldwide. He also notes that the orders listed are (top to bottom) the first to third best ratios in both cases. L.W. Louden Height Width Length 1.00 1.40 1.90 1.00 1.30 1.90 1.00 1.50 2.10 Given the dimensions of your room, it is possible to achieve a starting point as far as ratios go, however, how big/what function do you require the iso booth to be?
