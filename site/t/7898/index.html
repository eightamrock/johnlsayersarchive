knightfly11 January 2007#8
The entire reverb time looks really short to me; can you post more info on the room such as size, shape, what materials used where, etc? I would think everything played in that room would sound very dry, probably too much so. Generally a control room should be somewhere around .2 seconds and a live room anywhere from .3 to .5 seconds, depending on room volume and the type of music - classical tends to longer reverb times than pop, for example. It usually sounds fine if the low end has a slightly longer RT... Steve
