It'll not dampen anything so perhaps it would be a better idea to build an isolated wall (if you need isolation) and then build slot resonators or something that's got well documented befits and is easier to build. a :arrow:
sandledfoot19 February 2007#3
Theres two distinctions you need to clarify... was it an actuall wall, ie, gypboard, or was it acoustical treatment. My guese it was the latter... in which case, i've seen the "louvre" effect done in many places to treat room reflections. I didn't search on this forum for more technical reasons, (which i'm sure if you did search, you'd find them) however, i found some pictures of something that may be similar. This is pictures from my church's construction, and you can see the builders installing those panels, each is essentially a fabric wrapped 2'x8' panel of OC 703 (i'm not positive on thickness, I think variably 2" or 4") hung on the aluminum rails. each of the empty pockets in the wall recieved those, to note however, the lower pockets recieved flush mounted panels, not louvred.
