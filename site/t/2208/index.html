giles11728 August 2004#2
Nice design idea. I'd do the side walls john portable slat resonator style instead of the realtraps (absorption front) style. The corners, and the ceiling ... I like, I wouldnt change from yur design. The space behind the mix desk, yes keep absorbed as well, especially behind the monitors. The rear of your room is cool. The diffusor on your ceiling, I'd get rid off unless you have a very TALL 10' or more ceiling. If not, keep it absorbed like the front of your room.. JMO
