fido2 March 2005#1
Hi everyone! This is a room I was offered. It´s 14,6' x 11,8' with 10,8' ceiling. The problems are obviously the windows and the door. The wall which is without any window or door verges (right word?) on another room with noisy neighbours. Verging to the wall with the door is another small room with ca. 14,6' x 4' which could be used as vocal booth. The main restriction (right word?) is that I didn´t want to make splayed walls but to use acoustic elements or maybe movable gobos ... although the ceiling may be too high to build gobos that cover all the wall (height). Now my first thought was to use the room "lengthways", but on one side there is this very unsymmetrical window and on the other side there are neighbours. Then there is that one window on the right side & I think it will reflect sound to the listening position. If I "spin" the room 90 degrees, I would have a more symmetrical front and the reflections from the window on the left would be out of the listening position. But the rear wall would may be be too near to the listening position. What do you think? Another thing is: I can´t spend more than 1000 EUR, which is ca. 1300 USD. I wanted to get me some primacoustic elements "pyramid-foam" and maybe mount it on thin wood so I can hang it instead of glueing it on the walls/ceiling. If you think this room really can´t be handled without splaying, I will have a second thought on it - but I tried to figure out WHERE it could be splayed and none of the possibilities looked very good to me. On one design I lost all of the windows, on the other the door was gone, in the third the window is in the back of the room ... Maybe you have an idea I didn´t think of or couldn´t imagine? Thanks very much!
