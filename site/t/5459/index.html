- I plan to use wood studs, and bulid a room with in a room. This includes using a double leaf wall with an 8" span. 2 sheets of 5/8 dry wall on each leaf. - The only exception to this is the exterior concrete block walls. In this case the block wall is one leaf then a gap of 2"s then the studs and the two sheets of 5/8 drywall as the other leaf. Hope this stuff is making sense. - I want to "put a lid" on each room bringing the height down to like 8' or a bit higher. I figure this will help keep the sound isolated from upstairs. Challenges: - The ducting runs up the middle of the room with this triple joist thingy (see pic below) I can not run ducting through it. This limits how far the wall can go to the right of the plan.

How Loud?: - About 85 - 95db mixing and like 110 -115 max for drums and or guitar amps. Budget: - Trying to keep the cost of studs, flooring, insulation, drywall and caulk (building supplies) to like 5,000.00 for now if possible. Questions: - Any ideas for floor plans? - Suggestons for materials and or construction techs for keeping it within my budget? - Any tips on "putting a lid" on each room? - Advice on splitting the outake duct into both rooms? - On my budget do I even attempt to float the floors? I am on 3/4 of an acer and have like 100 plus feet on each side of the house. The studio is below the center of the house.
