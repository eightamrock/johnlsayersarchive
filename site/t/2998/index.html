John Sayers14 January 2005#2
jazzsax - there are two issues here. 1) sound isolation - i.e the sound that gets out of the room and annoys the neighbours. The only way to stop this is with mass by adding extra drywall layers - or double leaf isolation (room within a room construction) 2) the sound remaining in the room bouncing around and ringing in your ears. This requires acoustic treatment to lower the reverb time of the room across the frequency spectrum. i.e. absorbers and traps. cheers john
