AndrewMc15 June 2003#2
The fiberglass board is dense fiberglass - same as the pink stuff in walls but much more dense. It's not the same type of fiberglass you make a fiberglass cast out of. You buy it in sheets ready to cut to size and use. If you check the studio construction pages in Johns site there are a lot of photo's where you can see the dense fiberglass installed in walls. Owens Corning 703 is a typical make but there are other manufacturers also.
