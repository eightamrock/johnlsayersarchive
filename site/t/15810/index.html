gullfo9 April 2011#5
maybe this approach? add a bit of wall to the side by the corner into the live room so you can add a set of saw tooth slats on both walls. put as much absorption on the front walls as you can. an angled cloud over the desk (your mostly sitting there) and use 2" scatter absorbers on the ceiling through out. in the live room, same idea - angled small floor to ceiling slats to rbreak up the reflections and add some broadband absorption. one the back of the "CR" maybe build out a wall to level with the live room wall - it may extend your bathroom a bit - then use the remain space as a deep bass trap or a closet.
