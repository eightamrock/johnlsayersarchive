I currently operate a mobile recording rig, but I am wanting to create a usable space for mixing and recording select clients in my basement and I am mostly at a loss and need some input from some knowledgable people. The basement is entirely underground (no walkouts) and is partially finished. The walls are cinderblock and the ceiling is finished w/gypsum board. The measurements are 18' x 18' (give or take an inch) with a 7'3" ceiling. The AC/water heater partition will be finished with doubled gypsum and insulated stud walls. The stairs are wood up to the room boundary and then are poured concrete and have a gypsum/stud wall partition. The ducts are exposed and wrapped in insulation. My plans are to treat the walls w/ homemade absorbers (entire block surface most likely)along w/some diffusers, and the ceiling w/absorber clouds. The ducts are probably going to be covered and treated hopefully in a non-boxy manner. I am also building several 7' entirely treated gobos for separation/isolation. Isolation from the rest of the house is not a high priority as my wife is also the studio manager. My budget for this makeover is very small (less than $1000). Thanx in advance for any suggestions.
