Neutrino23 November 2005#1
Hi everybody, a friend of mine is planing to build a studio and he asked me to help him a little bit with the planning. So I sat down and made a few sketches of what might be a possible solution. As indicated in the topic the room is rectangular 7,28m x 6,20m and has a lot of doors and windows as you can see in the picture below. The height is about 3m (although I'm not sure about the exact value right now). The goal is to get a control room and 2 or 3 Studios out of that room, where you have good sound isolation, which makes it possible to record e.g. drums and not hear much in the other room. The acoustics should also be really good, to make it possible not only to record in the studio but also use the control room as a mixing environment. Right now there is a thin carpet on the foor and wooden panels at the ceiling, but we think about floating the floor and even build a false ceiling (angled?), to get a good sound isolation. So my questions are: 1. What do you think about the two plans below? Which one is preferable (Plan01 is our favorite)? I tried to keep symmetry in the control room, but the one in Plan01 maybe not has the right proportions (length/width). The difficulty is, that you can't do it another way because there are doors and windows. 2. Maybe you have some good ideas for improvements or even completely other suggestions. My friend also suggested to remove the wall between Studio A and Studio B in Plan01, to have a even bigger recording room. But the Vocal Booth still is very small. Do you think it is big enough to get a natural voice recording? 3. The windows and doors cause a problem when trying to divide the room. The windows also destroy the symmetry in the control room. Do you think this is a big problem? Are there any possible solutions beside removing the windows? 4. Is it worth the cost to float the floor? All measures in the plans are in cm. Thanks! Waldemar
