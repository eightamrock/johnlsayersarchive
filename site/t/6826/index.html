sharward16 August 2006#3
Lasso wrote:
tracking room has to have a room within a room design (noisy neighbor) and floating floor (noisy workshop below).
How much dead weight will the existing floor safely handle? Rooms within rooms, especially with floating floors, need to be extraordinarily heavy, and often their weight makes them impossible to do without risking a catastrophic collapse. Making the floating floor or floating room lightweight could actually make the room perform worse in terms of isolation. Please provide extensive details of the existing construction. You may need a structural engineer to investigate and report back with the weight limits under which you will need to build. Is this space in your possession already, or are you still just researching before signing a lease? --Keith :mrgreen:
