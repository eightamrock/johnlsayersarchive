knightfly17 June 2005#4
Hey John, welcome to the board - First, "The first consultant is suggesting the 3 layers of 3 different materials on each side of the walls with air in the walls instead of insulation." - unless you have moisture problems with the walls, this isn't a good plan; but without seeing what's there it's hard to be sure. Second, "The current plan is to add 3 layers to the existing drywall that is on the ceilings and leave it at that." - have you checked with anyone to see whether your existing joists can support that much more weight? Adding three MORE layers of 5/8" drywall would raise the dead load requirement by 7 PSF, more than enough to cause failure of the structure in a lot of cases. Do you know what the actual size of your basement ceiling joists is, along with distance from center to center? This will have a large effect on how much mass it will take to isolate. Third, until some tests are done your upstairs noise (especially 2 floors away) could just as easily be WALL flanking as anything. Finally, if you're interested in pursuing this further I'm quite a bit closer to you than "down under" - like about 70 miles south of Portland. If you like, PM me for more details. Once again, welcome... Steve
