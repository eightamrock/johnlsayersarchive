Hi guys, Quick question about fastening insulation. It's been relatively simple for me to affix "rigid" type (702, IB board, etc) to the ceiling and inside walls using Insulation hangers: here's a link below to demonstrate what I've been using: http://www.apidistribution.com/products ... angers.php My concern is with attaching roxul rockwool as it is much more dense, limp and tends to break apart under its own weight...
