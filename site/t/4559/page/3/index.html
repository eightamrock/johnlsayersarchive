lowlife wrote:
Bump + new questions: 1) What to do with cavities in soffits? Fill with loose insulation or just have rigid insulation at the wall part (back) and have empty space for the rest?
yup great opportunity to get rid of all your insulation off cuts, clothes you don't wear anymore and anything similar. :)
2) Since I do not have backs to my soffit speaker boxes (the ones holding the actual monitor and being jammed between center studs) I was wondering about the design John's made with a plywood back a bit away (allowing circulation of air)? How would I do that correctly? Do I need a plywood back (remember it is all tied to the inside out wall)
just make sure you have air circulation - the back is neither here nor there.
3) Speaking about speaker boxes, how should they be connected to the front framing and the monitor shelf? Hard connection, screwed and sealed tightly or should it just be placed between the front studs and upon the shelf?
You are after rigidity - ideally the whole unit would be the speaker box - so make sure everything is built solid, as you would a speaker box.
