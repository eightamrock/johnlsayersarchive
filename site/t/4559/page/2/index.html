Ro17 July 2009#34
John has, somewhere, excellent drawings of a "general" flush-mounted monitor construction. Even for active monitors. (as the Behringer B3031A are). It needs ventilation on the back. (no, you don't heat up the baffle itself :) ) The amp in the monitor radiates heat, if you don't take care of it you'll destroy the amp. You need to get rid of the heat. It's all in John's drawings. Add it to the corner together with whatever low-end absorption you will use (hangers, chunks, whatever you want) By the way, I'd advise to either not flush mount them and use stands OR buy a descent pair of monitors. I'm not saying those Behringer B3031A are bad but I can imagen, bare with me, you will want to "upgrade" in the near future to new monitors. Once you got them "old" once buildin the wall, you're gonna have to reconstruct the whole darn flush-mounting corner device just to fit them "new" monitors. UNLESS you're sure ye gonna sick with them Behringer B3031A for a long period of time (which I doubt) Putting mons on a (heavy) stand ain't bad at all. And save you a lot of trouble... pick your style. :) It's what I do most of the time... not into flush-mounting that much (see for above reasons)
