you can buy backer rod at any home center. it's in the weatherstripping aisle, called caulk saver. no one in the store will have heard of it or be able to direct you to it. it's much cheaper to get bulk at http://www.loghelp.com/backerod.html
johnz1 October 2006#3
i forgot to answer your question, backer rod is a closed cell foam rope that comes in various sizes and lengths. if you want to seal a quarter inch gap between drywall and other surface use 3/8 inch backer rod so it compresses and makes tight seal. then seal with sound caulk. it not only saves caulk, it's most useful at it alows the caulk to cure with only two hard points of contact. this gives it the flexability not to crack over time
