James Johnson9 December 2007#15
Code minimum is 36" from the bottom tread/riser intersection to any wall / door, Finish to Finish. If no permit is pulled then you can do whatever you want. The best way to tip a stair into it's opening is to make the stair just shy of the total rise Finish to Finish and tip it "up" so the tread ends point down. If you tip the stair the other way, you'll always catch yourself scraping the tips of your shoes going up the stair, or even tripping up the stair. It won't feel right when you walk it. a general tip of roughly 1/32" heavy per tread is a good rule of thumb. resin will definitely help "fill in" any voids and slumps and make the hdwd install look and go in much better.
