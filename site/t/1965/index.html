wmb16 July 2004#2
I started another thread about the woes of installing Owens Corning 703 in my space. Lot's of work because I have a big room, etc. I was feeling pretty down about the money I had spent and not really getting much change from my monitor system during tests. Tonight I rehearsed with my band and it was mind-boggling how much better it sounded. All over the room I could hear everything clearly and the drums have never been so clear. Wow! So far I have used 3 of the 4 boxes I bought for the job. It was about $500 US for all of it. In your room half that amount would kill it dead like a hammer. Put it in the corners and around the walls. Good luck.
