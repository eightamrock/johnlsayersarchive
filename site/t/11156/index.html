andrebrito28 August 2008#2
No problem you leave the floor as concrete but then the ceiling must have absorption and diffusion (this is a rehearsal room, not a Control Room) placed homogeneously. The walls you can go just with absorption (and may use bass traps) or just do like the ceiling, depending if you want your RT60 around 0.4 s or higher, for a liver room, such has 0.7-0.8 s. Then you use diffusion to spread the sound in the room and obtain a live sound without the typical acoustical problems.

There is an announcement at the top of the forum about what to do to assure getting as many responses as possible. The announcement leads to this post. Actually, several people, who are experts on this forum, will not reply if you do not do what is written in the post. Many others who are very helpful, will not reply out of respect for the moderators' wishes. Having written that, a quick reply with what you have given, is use 30 m^2 of 100 mm minimum thickness mineral wool evenly distributed with 40% on the ceiling and the rest on the walls and fill the corners with superchunk type bass absorbers. Then listen to the room and if the highs are too dead cover the superchunks with plastic, slated wood etc to bring back liveliness. Andre
