Hi blackmatchstick. Please check the forum rules for posting: You seem to have missed something! :)
i would like to know is it ok to put a glass window between the control room and recording room, while the recording room is just in front of control room,
Glass is an excellent option, provided that it is done correctly. Most professional studios have glass between the control room and live room: It is very common, and very effective.
i heard few people advising me that i should not put a glass window as it causes problems in stereo imaging while mixing and recording
No it does not cause problems with stereo imaging if the room is designed and built correctly. Whoever told you that doesn't know what they are talking about.
they say i should put a camera and a lcd tv to see my talent in the recording room
You CAN use a CCTV system like that, if you want, and some places do, but normally that is only done where the control room and live room are not physically connected, or where there are structural issues with the building itself that would prevent you installing glass (for example, a concrete pillar right where the window should be). Take a look around the forum at some of the rooms that have been designed and built successfully, and you'll see that glass windows at the front of the room are VERY common. It is possible to do that, provided that it is done correctly. - Stuart -
