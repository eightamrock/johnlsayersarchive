Soundman202025 October 2008#30
If I were to hang small drywall plates with 20x57cm (skrew distance in RC x channel distance) I wouldn't feel any risk of having them fall down to the floor some day. So why should I fear this with the boards at big size?
How many layers of drywall are you planning to hang from that RC? And what thickness? How much does each layer weigh per square meter? How many screws are supporting it? Do the math, and see how many Kg you have hanging from each screw. Then find out how much force is needed to pull out one screw from your MDF. Then add in a huge safety margin. I share Lou's concern: I'm not sure if I would want all those Kg of drywall hanging over my head, supported only by a few screws embedded in MDF, which might weaken and crumble over time... (temperature changes, humidity changes, vibration from high SPL levels for prolonged periods, etc...) Maybe you need to find a structural engineer who can tell you if you have enough structural integrity there for safety. [/quote]
