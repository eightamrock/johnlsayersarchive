rod gervais4 September 2008#4
The conduit is not always required - BUT - what you have done (from what I see) is to pass the wire straight through holes in the metal studs - and that is bad - they make special gromets you are supposed to use to protect the wires from damage over time. You really should fix that - low voltage wiring will eventually be damaged - line voltage wiring could actually cause death if it shorts out against metal framing that doesn't run to ground (could even if it DOES run to ground). As far as the use of the rubber beneath the frame - it isn't worth all that much - it doesn't really isolate well and the screws into the floor will still create a short even with the rubber between them and the metal sill. Rod

Ro11 September 2008#20
loose the "federschiene" and add gypsumboards directly to your roof, else you'll end up with a multi-leaf ceiling. The "federschiene" are NOT resilient (if that's your goal) if you mount'm like the pic says. There's also a little "space" between the soft fibre wood plate.. loose that too by lifting the wood plate. Else you'll have a resonation panel in your construction.. Add more MASS if you will by adding gypsum in between the joists.

More than 2 leafs :) If you've read this forum, you'd know. It's all over this place. Having more than 2 leafs in a construction is bad. MAM (Mass Air Mass), remember? If not, read, study, buy Rod's book etc. That's seems to be the mantra for this site anyway.

What you are looking for is Resilient Channel. Auralex sells it in Europe. That stuff you showed (federschiene) isn't, and I repeat myself here, resilient. It's known as "hat" channel on this forum.
