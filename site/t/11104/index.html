rsmooth20 September 2008#8
3 quick questions: will adding green glue add to the dead load of the ceiling? any tips on using corner bead for the double drywall installation? will adding room treatments add to isolation to the rest of the house or is it only for reflections, in room acoustics, etc? thanks in advance
John Hile24 September 2008#9
3 quick answers: 1 - Green Glue is heaviest when first applied, yet 2 quarts per 32 sq.ft. is not a lot of weight to start. Green Glue is water based and therefor much of it's weight will dissipate as the Green Glue cures (up to 30 days) 2 - If doing a double drywall assembly, stagger your seams and use a 3/8" - 1/4" bead (depending on the gap) of acoustical caulk, also known as 50 year flexible caulk, around the parameter. Then tape/mud. 3 - Room treatments do not add to room isolation. You are correct in stating that they are for in room reflections, and acoustics (room sound quality.) I hope this helped. John

Just a quick update about the isolation of my build.. unfortunately i dont have any readings from a decibal meter or what not, but before the room was built, conversation coming from down there was heard as if someone was talking right next to you (from the room above and even on the 2nd floor in the bedrooms!). I did a sound check with a 15" powered mackie at full volume at 1am, and the results were astounding! In the room directly above, you can hear that someone is playing music but sounds extremely muffled. From the bedrooms on the 2nd floor, you cannot hear a peep! Also, the police did not show up which means that my neighbors were not able to hear my wonderful music selection. I still have some work to do in the iso booth so i cannot determine the isolation in that regard, but as far as isolation from CR to the rest of the house i must say it is a success and am very pleased with the results. All my friends thought i was crazy with how meticulous i was was with the isolation as far as caulking and puddy pads, but they sure are impressed with the amount of isolation at the end result! Just goes to show that taking extra time to make sure everything is done right and paying close attention to detail gets the desired results. Couldnt have done it without this forum and rod's book! Thanks again
