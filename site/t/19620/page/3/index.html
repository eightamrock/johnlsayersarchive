Soundman20204 May 2015#53
HI "Tombot". Please read the forum rules for posting (click here). You seem to be missing a couple of things! :)
Do you know if there is much difference in the absorption using duct liner compared to to material covered rockwool?
The difference in absorption isn't the issue: it's the difference in ablation, as the air flow removes fibers from the mineral wool, then sprays them all round the room... Doesn't happen with proper duct liner, which is specifically designed for the job. - Stuart -
