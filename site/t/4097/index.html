John Sayers1 August 2005#3
Roberto - please look at these figures of the sq footage area you are adding to the room A = 2' x 4' x 5 + 3 x 2' x 2' = 52 sq ft. B = 2' x 4' x 1 = 8 sq ft C = 2' x 4' x 2 = 16 sq ft D = 2' x 4' x 1 = 8 sq ft total = 84 sqft. Room is 15' x 12' x 7'7" = 765 sq ft. Your panels account for 84 sqft which is around 11 - 12% of your total room wall area. you need much more than that to tame a small room like that. ;) cheers john
