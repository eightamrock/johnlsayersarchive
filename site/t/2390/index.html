Hey all, Here is an alternate studio design that I made up. It's a little bit wider than the last one (24' by 36' versus 20' by 36'), but it let me put in a restroom and a lounge. The main room is a little smaller than the last one. Giles, my buddy and I really liked the design that you suggested. However, eventhough we liked the idea of having three separate rooms, we're really more into the old school thing of having the band in the same room. To us, the vibe and interaction of the band is more important than having an absolute sonically perfect recording. Anyway, what do you guys think about the design overall.
