xSpace9 June 2011#8
Room ratios assume a ten(10) foot height. Use that as your (1) and you will most likely get past this hurdle. That said, a 6 degree slant in the walls from the front to the back, can help create a diffuse environment. Simple thing is to NOT build a square room, a room with measurements divisible one by the other. The treatments on the interior side may be more complex as to what you have to do and where you have to do it...but if it eases your mind, it can still be done. It's like borrowing money with a 12 month interest free rate. Your going to pay it...it's just a matter of how interested are you in staying out of debt and for how long?
