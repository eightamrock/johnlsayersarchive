Hi! I'm starting home-basement-studio -project. I've been drawing some plans by hand, but now I found SketchUp and here are the results included. As I said I'm planning to build little studio to my houses basement, where I have some unused space. There is tree little rooms, concrete walls, concrete floor and ceiling. Room sizes (mm): HxWxD "controlroom" 2300 x 2700 x 3500 "liveroom" 2300 x 2900 x 5050 "vocal booth" 2300 x 2050 x3500 (in plan this room is divided in two sections) Pink walls are concrete, thicker part is 300mm (12") and thinner 180mm (>7"). These walls cannot be modified. These rooms are under ground. Money & time I can use all money what i can get (10 000 -20 000€) for this project and same goes for the time. I try to build controlroom (ready at end of may '08) first then liveroom and vocalbooth is last one. Purpose: I will use this studio for remixing and recording and liveroom is supposed to be my bands rehearsal room too. All kinds of opinions and advice are highly appreciated.
