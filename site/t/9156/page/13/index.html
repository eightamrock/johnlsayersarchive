Lou30 April 2008#317
Hey Snowdog99 - That's not 'buzzkill' it's a damm good point! To answer your question, the position of everything has basically all been designed to accommodate me. I agree that the photos do make the desk look far more in the way than it actually is, however the Air 15's tweeter is well above the top rack sections, and I assure you that the woofer's direct focus will not 'bounce' off the side. :wink: I'm sure you know as well as me that the bass end of these babies goes just about everywhere within reason, and so consequently it's not as crucial to have a totally clear line of sight so to speak. Please also bear in mind that once I've got the desks and monitoring in, plus at least a CD player, it's a massive tune up of the room to iron out any potential niggles. Well spotted though my friend. The front monitors are upwards of the surround, and angled down to get their focus above the meter bridges on the desks (for the same reason), yet still 'hit' the sweetspot. :wink: :wink: Kind regards, Lou. 8)
