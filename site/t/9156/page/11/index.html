Now, one thing that I haven’t mentioned yet is that the Building Inspector asked where the W.C. was. I simply blagged it by saying that “we’ve had a change of heart and decided not to bother now.” “Fine, but we will need a declaration to that effect and, of course, confirmation that a W.C will never be installed.” :roll: :twisted: :roll: At this point Gentlemen I was, in all honesty, about to explode.
