dynamike21 October 2007#153
Dan, check out this thread, its at the top of construction forum titled "useful links" or something like that. http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=20003 second post describes how to do it. third post shows a sketch. you're wasting your time if you backer rod and caulk between each layer. it's just where the walls meet other walls, or the floor/ceiling. it strictly for decoupling. you don't need to decouple EACH sheet from one another. just the entire wall from any other wall.
