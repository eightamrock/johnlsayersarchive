Hi everyone, Im really happy about finding this forum. Ive found a lot of usefull informations, but still i need some help. Id really appreciate if someone knowledgeable could take a look on design of my home studio. I work as a post production audio engineer, but id like to use this space mostly for music purposes. I play guitar, bass, and I like to play with softsynths. If it would be possible to track drums in second, smaller room - i would be really happy. Its not a necessity though, i use software drummachines most of the time anyway. Im only in planing phase now, trying to figure out how can i make best out of what i got. The place where I want studio to be is basement of my house, closest neighbors 100 m away, so the loudness is nor really an issue, and because of the fact that it is in basement not much of a noise gets inthere (the house is in the forest). I just want to make this room sound as good as it is possible. I like to mix around 85 db, sometimes a little louder but that depends on the thing im working on. Ive got two rooms. Ive decided that larger should be a control room, because small one is just to small. For now one I want to concentrate on control room. Measures are on the pictures.
Image not preserved: Studio FINAL1.jpg
It is not completely regular rectangular space 561x374x234 cm which is 18.4x12.27x7.67 in imperials : ) Walls surrounding the room are made of bricks varying from 30 cms (12'')on the side up to 70 cms (27.5'') ("south" and "east" walls). Northern wall is about 50cm. (20'') It is basement, so basically walls are surrounded by earth. :) Obviously main inconvenience is that there are doors in corner (leading to garage). Doors on the west wall are leading to pool room. Ive put together best thing i could basing on my knowledge and ive made sketchup project out of it. And about ceiling - I was thinking about suspending it by 15cms. id put 5 cms of insulation on it and leave 10 cms air gap. It is made of airblocks, so afterall there should be plenty of air to absorb low freq. or im wrong ? Now my main concerns - should i put 10 cms of insulation on every single wall ?
