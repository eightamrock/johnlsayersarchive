DanDan7 August 2021#8
Just a caution...... OC is Glass Wool, and afaik relatively rigid. Some mineral wools are quite floppy, they would sag, particularly with the weight of a lot of fluffy over them. So I recommend try to find something glass, longer fibres, and a higher density if necessary. Polyester is an option these days. Depends on where you are, but some examples from this part of the world- Knauf Earthwool Ecose Universal Slab 100KG Caruso Isobond WLG035
