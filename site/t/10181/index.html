Welcome to the forum. 1. Buy Rods book: http://www.amazon.com/o/ASIN/1598630342/ref=pe_snp_342 2. This forum is a hefty read. It can take weeks to go through every thing and understand concepts. While waiting for the book please look through the reference area: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=2125 3. Design questions and conversation happens on the design section of this forum. Take a trip over there and wonder around the threads. Grab Google's free Sketchup and download some of John's studio designs in 3D. 4. There is http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html but be aware some of the info in there is out of date. 5. Click on John Sayers Productions at the top right of this page. Click studios designed and browse the "center fold" of this forum. 6. Finally with that type of budget you may want to contact and hire John to design studio for you. He's one a few with a great amount of credibility in studio design. Good luck. Marc
