Soundman202027 August 2016#2
My vote goes with "something weird". And to that I'd add "unpredictable". So if you want an unpredictable weird wall, then go for it! :) Seriously, if you can't find any research on it where that has been tested in a proper acoustic lab, and the results show a substantial improvement, then forget it. Acoustics is often non-intuitive, and things often don't actually work the way you though the would at first glance... until you analyze them in detail and figure out you forgot to account for some effect or other. Carpet is porous, has air trapped in it, is flexible, but I doubt that it would act as a CLD, and certainly not as limp mass in that configuration, so I really wouldn't bother, unless you are up for a few weeks of experimentation with different arrangements, and careful testing... :shock: - Stuart -
