WHat sound are you going for?? Perhaps some swatch of carpet you can lay down when you need a darker sounding room. Either wood or laminate for your primary. Both are nice and reflective and give good tone. I have a ton of drum tracks cut on a laminate floor. All sound great.

Real wood flooring offers a better foot feel, but its price is very high. Laminate flooring is strong abrasion resistant, but thie type of flooring can't make your room more valuable. Carpet is water resitant, but it is more difficult to maintain.
