Soundman202013 March 2010#9
i was thinking the that the control room could be a little room maybe 12' x 12'??
That is square. Bad idea. You certainly do NOT want to have a square room!
My idea of small is about 14' by 10'.
That's sounds better! but you are missing one dimension! Rooms are 3D, not 2D. How high is the room? 14 x 10 might be great, or it might be terrible, depending on height. It is all about room ratios... Look for one of the good ones, and try to get your dimensions as close as possible to that, within reason. Once you have chosen your CR ratio and rough dimensions, you need to use the usual layout guidelines to get your speaker and mix position geometry right, then your basic treatment will fall into place, and you'll be well on your way to a good design. - Stuart -
