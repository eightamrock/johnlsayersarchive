For a tracking room, what I would do is to create a few "sweet spots" for recording in the room that have plenty of absorption on the ceiling and on nearby walls, the idea being that you don't want the microphones to hear any first reflections. Good areas for this in a tracking room are above where the drum kit will be, or above where acoustic instruments are recorded. Once you have these sweet spots taken care of, you will want to continue to add absorption, spaced evenly throughout the room, until you achieve the RT60 you are looking for.
