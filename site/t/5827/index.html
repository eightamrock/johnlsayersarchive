knightfly16 March 2006#9
OK, I still need speaker and head positions shown with dimensions included in your drawing - the first thing I noticed is that your woofers are within an inch of dead center vertically, that needs to be changed. If you can find a way to raise the speakers by 8 or 9 inches, that's a start. Your head should ALSO NOT be centered vertically, right now your speakers (and possibly your ears) are in two axial nulls, one at 78 hZ and another at 234 hZ; if your head is centered front-to-back, there are two MORE nulls; one at k49 hZ, and another at 147 hZ. I'll have to wait for your dimensions before knowing if your speakers are in more than just the vertical nulls. If you can, those front two corner absorbers need at least TWICE as wide rockwool in front of them in order to do much, and since the rear corners are doors I would also make corner absorbers the same way, only at least 2 feet wide (3 is better) and put them across your wall-ceiling corners. So, to sum - I need to know where exactly your speakers and head are. You need to score more rockwool and build more absorbers as mentioned. You need to raise your speakers by about 9 inches, and make sure your head is also NOT centered vertically - same height as speakers if possible. Carpwftx - leaning up in corners is fine; although it's better if they're wider, thicker, and reach all the way to the ceiling. Both - check out this http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=2441 For the freak :wink: use your AVERAGE dimensions - since part is 16', and part tapers to 8', I arbitrarily used 13' for width instead of halfway between 8 and 16 feet. See if raising your speakers helps any, it should - but I have a feeling it's gonna take more than that... Steve
