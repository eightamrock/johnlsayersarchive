TomVan6 November 2007#4
Brad. Just remember to caulk the crap out of them. Its something you will probably never look at again so just make sure its tight. Once you have completed them have someone yell in one end and put your ear to the other end. You will be pleasantly surprised. As far as fibers being set free you can line the 703 with any kind of breathable fabric/material. As far as my build I haven't noticed any ill effects of floating fibers in the air. And I am a clean freak. More dirt and dust come into my space than anything else. All those long haired musicians :lol:
