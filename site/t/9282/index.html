xSpace25 August 2007#4
..you will be surprised at how quickly you can pick up tricks. ..
You will be surprised at how quickly you can waste money by poor methods in the hands of the inexperienced. Construction is not easy and it requires many talents not the least being knowing/having good math skills. Vibration being the biggest kill in the quest for room silence, any material that is placed directly against another is subject to rub/squeak or allow vibration to live on if not decoupled in some fashion. That means the wood you use for walls should not come into contact with the concrete you have for a floor. High quality construction adhesives / caulk are to be the norm not the exception when developing any project wherever you are trying to get better isolation. Three things you have to work with when building ANY structure. Plumb, level and square. You get those three right your work can flow properly. You get any one or more than one wrong, your work becomes tedious. Brien
