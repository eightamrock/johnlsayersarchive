Diffusors reflect a big percentage of the source signal. So, i don't see why they would install absorbers behind. That room was designed by George Massenberg. Here is some more infos i got from their Myspace page : STUDIO C – Welcome to the 21st Century! Primarily designed by George Massenburg, this is a tracking, overdubbing, and mix room. Designed for stereo and multi-channel mixing. Arguably the finest 5.1 mix room on the planet. It is the perfect blend of art and technology. Totally digital for fast recalls. You have the choice of a DIGI Icon or Sony Oxford console. The use of analog outboard gear is up to you. Very hip private lounge with dark blue velvet walls. Walnut floors and trim in this totally diffuse listening environment. Video synch a cinch! Not finished & that's incerdible! Behind us you can see Studio C, which is the only studio of its kind in the world. The walls and ceilings were designed by George and contain thousands of wooden rods. No two robs are the same length, so you get the cleanest sound possible. Here are some more pics of this fascinating studio All the pics i found are attached & they are amazing
