sharward26 May 2005#2
Welcome to the forum, and congratulations on your new home! :mrgreen: Before going much further, I would suggest that you look into your options from a building/zoning point of view first. Find out what kinds of limitations may apply to your situation. There is probably a minimum distance between whatever structure you build and your property line (setbacks). There may be limits to how large the structure can be, in height and in square footage. You may be prohibited from including certain features, such as plumbing. There may be easements that could prevent you from putting a structure in a particular part of your parcel. :? This is generally the best place to start. It's better to know what (if any) those limitations are before getting your mind and heart set on a particular plan that, for one reason or another, is impossible from a municipal point of view. Don't assume that just because a neighbor has a shed or building in a similar position as what you want to build means that you'll be able to do so. Sometimes others have the benefit of a grandfather clause that permitted them to build, whereas you might not have that benefit. Or, perhaps it was built without a permit at all! :shock: (And, no, building such an elaborate thing without a permit isn't a viable option... Not unless you are willing to risk being fined many times the amount of the permit fees and be ordered to tear it all down too!) There are some basic "per square foot" cost estimates on the board... Personally, I'm betting that your total cost would be more towards the upper end of your range, and that's without luxury frills. But, I'm not the authority on that. :roll: FYI, you may want to check out my thread on the construction forum. Although your project differs from mine in a lot of ways, there are some similarities, and you may benefit from some of the lessons I've been learning over the past few months. Best of luck to you on your project, and congratulations again on your new home. 8) --Keith
