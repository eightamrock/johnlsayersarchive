Soundman202025 April 2010#6
split the difference: How about 9 degrees, then? :) Or make the slot wall a bit shorter, so it doesn't eat so far into the room. Are you talking about your first CR? The one you competed last year? If so, then its a reasonable size room. I think you would have space to go for 12 degrees plus hangers or superchunks, or at least 10 degrees. The trouble with 6 degrees is that you might start getting first reflections off the slats. Even 12 might not be enough. Have you figured out the RFZ geometry yet? - Stuart -
