lazlominimart20 June 2005#4
beautyfish -- I have a just-shy-of-7' ceiling, and I'm doing a cloth ceiling, as well. We nailed up two layers of plywood/OSB into the subfloor, then suspended "the pink stuff" on wires stapled just inside the bottom of the joists (to keep the insulation from pressing on the cloth... ). I then stapled black polyester batting (a sewing material) pulled extremely taut to the bottom the joists (king size sheets of the Poly Request stuff found here: http://www.hickoryhillquilts.com/blackpoly.htm). The poly bat is only there to keep fiberglass shed out of the studio. I then finished with black vinyl insect screen. There's no end of great things about this stuff for this purpose -- it's cheap, readily available, comes on giant rolls, is extremely uniform in appearance, and stretches nicely to pull taut to the joists. We were actually able to pull it tight to the exterior joists of the room and only felt it necessary to staple where the seems met -- so, in essence, I have a gigantic black "cloth" ceiling that has almost no staple "dimples," and should be acoustically transparent to all but the highest frequencies. I'm sure the pink stuff won't trap bass frequnecies as well as 703, but I'm hoping the sheer quantity of it that I put up there will do the trick. I'm still fully expecting to need bass traps in the corners and probably a diffusor or two on the back wall, as well. This ceiling was extremely cheap to produce (esp. since I had the insulation lying around), if labor-intensive. The result looks fantastic and will hopefully sound great (hard to tell, as my walls aren't up just yet... those are happening soon, and I'll report back!). Lazlo
