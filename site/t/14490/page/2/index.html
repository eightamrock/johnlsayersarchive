xSpace2 September 2010#33
Well, depending on how you guys build in your country, the insulation shouldn't be an issue since it is installed after the completion of the framed walls. I understand your concerns with the corners, based on that, try to make the least amount of voids (holes) in your corners when you build the individual walls. Use solid studs instead of blocks when you make up your corners, if that helps. Do not put something together that creates a hole that you will not be able to get to once the wall is standing up right. Good luck and keep us n the loop,
