Soundman202015 May 2013#27
First of all, cr is going to have angled ceiling, that isn't symmetrical to listening position / room shape. Is that a problem?
Yes, it is a problem. Room symmetry is critical, and that includes the ceiling too: sound waves travel in 3D, not just 2D, and the ceiling is a major part of that.
If it is, can it be fixed with treatment / clouds in later stage?
Not really. I would suggest framing up a "fake" ceiling on the higher side of the room, to mirror the angle on the lower side. Make the left side of the room a mirror-image of the right. Or maybe choose another layout for your rooms, where you get symmetry automatically in the CR, and don't need to do the extra ceiling?
Also I did some raytracing, and didn't notice any problems with reflections to listening point..
Looks good to me!
I couldn't find dispersion charts for Adam A8X's, that i'm going to use as my monitors, so i had just to guess how it'll spread the sound..
That's one of my pet peeves about Adam: They make fantastic speakers, among the best in the world, but their documentation is terrible, among the WORST in the world, frankly. The only graphs I have every found for their speakers, have been done by reviewers in magazines. You almost wonder why they are so afraid to publish their own graphs, plots and diagrams: are they hiding something? Hmmmm..... - Stuart -
