digipenguin2 February 2006#5
I have a two story converted garage studio. The first floor is the live room and vocal booth. It is fully soundproofed and lightly treated. It has a 9' ceiling. The second floor is the control room/lounge and is lightly soundproofed and heavily treated. It has an 8' 4" ceiling. The outer perimeter of the building is 26' X 18'. I planned on spending 4K in construction costs and got away with about 5K. That includes treatments. My walls have a noise reduction of about 60dB and my control room has a very nice frequency response. I was able to keep the costs low by performing almost all of the work myself.
