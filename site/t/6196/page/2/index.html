Bearing in mind the above rave now consider the original plan I proposed. 1. it gives an entrance area where people can wait, chill out, whatever before they are involved with each studio. The entrance has direct access to each work space and the whole unit is selfcontained within the initial available space. 2. The smaller studio is isolated from the main studio, by the the main control room. It doesn't matter so much if a control room leaks to another control room as it would another control room leaking to the studio. 3. the high ceiling area above the entrance allows for aircon units above a standard ceiling to feed ducts to both control rooms and down the hall to the main studio. you could achieve that - that's real ;) Cheers john
