gullfo12 October 2007#12
i like the smaller and more numerous side treatments. you could build inside-out and regular walls also as a means of preserving the space and adding the absorption and diffusion. here's what i was thinking about for the ceiling - adding 3 soffits overhead to house the AC and some bass trapping. in-between are rows of poly diffusers and lighting. the proscenium of the stage should allow for lighting, TV screens, and PA. over the staging you would have a solid ceiling to project acoustically and leave some audio on the stage for performers. alternately, you could have a moveable ceiling so you have overhead fly space and or some trapping. i'd leave some windows - with drapes - for natural daylight and avoid the large tomb effect... the rear wall would be about 30-40cm deep slats/absorbers to reduce reflection off the back without killing it... above the soffits and polys would be large hanging panels to provide absorption for bass and reduce the overhead reverberation to the desired level.

gullfo13 October 2007#14
the program is free - sketchup.google.com the bass trapping etc would need to be predecated on the room construction, volume etc... to see what would be the right balance. in this drawing i'm assuming some coupling into the space above the soffits and poly diffusers. and in that space would be trapping to balance the amount of bass reverberation you have. you might not need much. or you might need a bunch. the angles are arbitrary at this point and meant to implie some level of ceiling compression towards the rear of the theater to preserve sound intensity for the back audience, and allow expansion and direction in the front - attempting to equalize the delay of reflected sound across the audience is one goal. you would make your best calculations and then construct the isolation portions of the room, take measurements, add the broadband treatments such as the soffits and polys, take more measurements, and do your final tuning based on that. keeping in mind that an audience has absorption power which is not trivial...
