I would suggest you float the whole structure on a floating floor if you want play drums.
2. I see the air-gap in your design is only 25mm, is this for space-reasons (coz the room is so narrow) or acoustic reasons? I thought the normal recommended minumum gap between leaves was 10cm, right?
yes - you are very space limited. If you angled the ceiling from low in the front up to the middle then angle back down at the rear you will increase the total air cavity.
3. Should I try slanting the ceiling at all?
see above
4. Can I use my existing GiK traps as the filling for my inside-out walls/ceilings?
yes.
