if you stand on the ceiling with the mirror you can use the same trick as the walls :twisted: otherwise it's usually just a bit forward of the side ones depending on how high your speakers are mounted. if you have your speaker acoustic center at (say) 42" in an 8' high room, and the front of the speaker is 12" off the front wall, assuming a 30 degree angle for the HF, then your reflection point on the ceiling is roughly 3'7" and the floor reflection is about 3'.
