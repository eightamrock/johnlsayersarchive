lowdbrent20 February 2005#5
The 4412's are an updated version of the 4311's. They went with updated drivers in the same configuration. The 4410's IMO were the best sounding, but could not be used on their side. The 4312 had too high of a bass roll off, and was quite flabby sounding. Many video post houses used them and the large 4425's and 4435's. They were not well accepted in the US market for music mixing.
