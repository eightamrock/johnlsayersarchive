knightfly8 September 2006#6
John, I have one of those that's commercially made by Luxo - looks just like the lamps but has a 5/8 mic thread instead of the lamp. I stopped using it onstage (very handy when you're playing keys part of the time and guitar/bass other times) - the reason I stopped using it is because the combination of the hollow square tubes and those springs caused excess feedback at around 6.3 k - when I changed to a more normal mic stand, I stopped having to pull the 6.3k slider all the way down on the graphic for our monitor system :roll: I may break it out again when I'm setting up my new video editing room tho, no monitors on to feed back and it sure is handy 8) Steve Maybe I should fill the frames with sand next time :wink:
