Ro25 July 2008#5
remember that the thicker the panel, the lower the frq it will absorp. Having a 30' wide panel, only 1" thick will absorp the hight frq in ye room. Leaving you with a very bass-sounding room. (the balance between low and high frequencies is disrupted) Making a 3'x3', 4" thick will be more broadband and there for result in a better frq-absorbing balance. As per Ethan: you can't have enough basstrapping. But be carefull not to absorp too much highs when adding "never enough" basstraps. Putting a screen in front (cloth/wood/plastic/whatever) could work to only trapp bass and leave highs in the room. It's really a matter of measuring your room at that point... (do I make sense?)
