g-slam16 September 2008#1
We have a big recording room for grand piano, 85m2. Because there are some fluttering echos between parallel walls, I decides to build some Helmholtz-resonators. I found very cheap mdf-panels with holes in it. The holes are 8mm in diameter and 14mm distance, 5102 hole per m2. The panels have 4mm thickness. I calculated, that starting at the small sid with 30cm thickness, the resonator has a frequency of 535HZ and coming down to the 5cm side ist 1312 HZ. If I need more absorption in the highs, I would stick kind of Illbruck or other foam absorbers. MY PROBLEM: Do I need kind of absorption material inside? How does it change the absorption and frequency?
