jwl25 February 2008#6
crooker, I would give another thought to a one-room design. I think it would be a more efficient use of space, and once you get into the workflow it can be quite nice. Additionally, the smaller the room, the harder it is to get it sounding good (all else being equal). The only real reason, imho, to build booths and separate rooms is if you need isolation while recording more than one performer at a time. And even then, good gobos/mic placement can go a long way.
