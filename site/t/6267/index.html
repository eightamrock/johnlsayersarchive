new software release to convert Sketchup to CATT files. Rahe-Kraft releases Demo of SU2CATT - a SketchUp export plugin for acoustic models Press release from: Rahe-Kraft GbR, Berlin Published date: 12-20-2006 10:34 AM - CET - Industry, Real Estate & Construction (openPR) - SU2CATT is a SketchUp extension to export SketchUp models to CATT-Acoustic files. It exports models generated within the 3D Software Sketchup to the native .geo-format of CATT-Acoustic, including export of layers into several .geo-files and separate file with absorption material definition (ABS-directive in CATT). There is a Demo available for download, only limitation is in the number of surfaces that can be exported (100). www.rahe-kraft.de/cms/su2catt Rahe-Kraft is an acoustic consultants office, specialized in electroacoustic systems, located in Berlin - Germany. Rahe-Kraft is founded in 2001 and managed by Jakob Kraft and Peter Rahe. The office is focused on design, measurement, optimising and servicing public adress systems in any kind of acoustic environment. Rahe-Kraft is operating worldwide.
