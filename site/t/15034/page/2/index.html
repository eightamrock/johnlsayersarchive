xSpace2 November 2010#44
Eikon wrote:
thanks Brien, but could you clarify that a bit? i was planning on caulking all seams around the new room, but what do you mean by an unbroken piece of whatever i use?
Anything that you use to support the wall in an effort to "decouple", unless it is an unbroken, contiguous piece will have no benefit since it will have air gaps between the piece, or pucks. There is no data to support if a supposed decoupled framed wall will work in a better way than if it rests on a heavy concrete slab. I recognize that it is said around here, do this and do not do that, but in the end you will find that what was said to not do 12 years ago, no one is having an issue with these days. Still, no data...only applied use and acceptable expectations with little to no testing :)
