gullfo27 March 2008#7
have you done any testing? using hammers (metal and rubber) to check the structure borne transmission between rooms and upstairs/downstairs? also check to see how much sound is actually getting out of the existing structure? it looks like from the drawing you have a 12" slab (nice) on a compacted base and its connected to the walls... the walls look to be 2 layers with insulation in the cavity so it may already be performing decently. one risk is adding another isolation wall inside it and creating a 3rd leaf which would likely reduce your isolation... so maybe bring in a drum kit or PA and blast some bass and drums and see how the building is behaving. meanwhile, i think without the added room, you go with your design - if possible use RSIC isolation mounts to hang a thick drywall ceiling in the rooms to isolate to outside and the attic. in the attic, consider a similar construction to reduce sound escaping from your control room.
