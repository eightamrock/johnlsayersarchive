Aloha, To quote the direction I was given;
I've yet to use Real Traps, but have seen quite a few positive comments on the Mini's - If you're at all into DIY, though, you can beat price AND performance of just about ANY commercial unit...
That said, what I've found (and read here) is that in most cases, in order for your corner traps to make any kind of significant difference, they need to span floor to ceiling and in at least the four corners of your room. I think this is where the DIY approach wins out in being more cost effective. I used a modified approach to John's wall units and placements, and the difference it makes is quite remarkable. If you care to check out a couple of recent threads using these absorbers, try here: http://johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic ... ght=#15598 and here: http://johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=4091 More later, Aloha 8)
