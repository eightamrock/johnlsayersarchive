Soundman202025 April 2010#2
Have you checked your grounding system in your studio? AC cable next to XLR should not produce that kind of buzzing. I do it all the time at events, even with stage lighting cables running right next to my mic cables for tens of feet, and I don't get that kind of problem. I'd expect it from LINE level cables, for sure, but not from XLR. That's the entire point of using balanced cables: they reject noise. So my guess would be that you have a grounding problem somewhere, or that your connectors are not wired correctly (confusing pin 1 with pin 2, maybe?). Or that your snake cable isn't shielded properly. I'd check things like that before you start ripping out wall. - Stuart -
