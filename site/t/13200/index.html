I did a whole studio in pure wool - it was so good to work with. :) It was in the mid 90s and the aussie wool industry was facing a downturn in wool sales and they were trying every use of wool, so a Toowoomba company started making wool insulation. I had a call from the owner of Music Farm - he had stripped all the rockwool/fiberglass acoustic treatment out of the studio, he'd even gone in with high pressure water guns and cleaned out every particle of synthetic fiber all because Olivier Newton John was about to record and she'd just recovered from breast cancer and he wanted a pure environment for her to be creative in. So as well as having the opportunity to re-do Music Farm I also ended up as an engineer that worked on her Gaia album.
