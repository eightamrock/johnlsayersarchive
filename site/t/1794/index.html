Don't record till the transmission shop is closed for business. ( I hate this idea) 2. Make a ceiling that sound will not penetrate through. Now if we were to build the ceiling off of the steel beams that support the ceiling wouldn't any sounds created from the ceiling still travel through the new ceiling. Or would we have to build a completely new ceiling that would hang below the steel beams. Or more likely than hang be supported by the walls of the studio.
