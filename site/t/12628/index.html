Ro7 May 2009#5
Adding (corner) absorbers is a very good plan. Especially in a small room you need basstraps. I'd suggest you put wool into the shelf/cabin. Just stuff it (don't over compress) and put a sheet of cloth in front. That way you'll end up with a big basstrap behind your mixing position, which is good. The monitors will beam right into it. Speaking of which, you should place your monitors at the right angels, height and distance. There's plenty of info on that on this forum. Place atleast two broadband on the side walls at first reflection points of your monitors. It doesn't have to be an expensive solution that way. Draw your plans into sketch-up if you will.
