Ro23 April 2008#2
Razor, There's been many discussions about that already. Laminated glass are 2 panes stacked together without air but with a dampening layer in between. Double glass (I presume you mean with the airgap) will not be as efficient as laminated. Don't use 2x double-glass cuz then you'll end up with a 4 leave construction. Granted, laminated glass is expensive but it's worth the buck!

Ro25 April 2008#9
Sorry for the misunderstanding. Laminated glass are 2 panes stacked together and therefor we speak of 1 pane. With double glass I mean thermo-glass, you know 2 panes with AIR in between. So you need 2x laminated glass for optimum iso. Separate the panes to have air in between. .. confusing, yeah I know... it's actually simple. Use the Mass-Air-Mass (mam) principle. The glass panes are the MASS. so you'll end up with such construction: laminatedglass - air - laminatedglass. Check this forum for examples, even drawings.
