Sigert2 January 2008#6
Throwing in some thoughts: _you could decide not to construct inner walls for your entrance room as there wont be a lot of noise coming from it. The booth and control room wont suffer loss of effectiveness, cause sound coming from those rooms will still encounter a double leaf construction in every direction. It might save some space and money not having to construct double doors. _Maybe you could use the cavity between inner and outer wall construction as chill-area. I really do not know if it's effective in any way, but if it were it could save you another square meter of comfort and some money in construction materials. :)
