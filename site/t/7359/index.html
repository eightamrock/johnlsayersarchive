Alex-Zcrew25 October 2006#1
Hello all! I'm an italian musician who just loves the world of recording studios and everything that comes with it. This forum is really a god-send for me. A few years ago I started a small project studio of my own in my basement. The whole thing began to grow more than i expected, and now i find myself in need of a properly built studio. And here comes the tricky part. In my country recording business is not very popular, so the help I can get is very little, and designing it by myself could really screw things up. Right now i am (sonically speaking) limited because of the room itself. My goal is to obtain a complete small studio, with a control room, an iso booth (for drums/guitar/bass/etc) and a vocal booth. I really hope for some usefull input! dimensions of the room are: length 7,6 meters width 5,1 meters height 2,2 meters (the SketchUp file is built with most precise measurements) The whole room is made of concrete and is almost totally under the ground, a very convenient placement that rids me of the problem of external sound-proofing. I had bands play in this room till 3am, neighbors never came to complain. The floor is a simple floating parquet, while the ceiling has been covered with 4cm of high density rockwool and plasterwork. At this time, the walls are all unpainted concrete (exept for a small brick area), covered with those egg-packaging-things (sorry, i really don't know the term in english), but i plan on tearing them down asap. The problem in this room, is a round corner that makes the sound act really strange and creates unwanted echoes. I have no idea how to solve this. Overall, this room sounds really muddy, so i need to 'flat it'. Also, concrete loves to vibrate, so playing a loud bass guitar gives you this 'earthquake' feeling. I have included in the SketchUp file a rough model of my mixing desk, placed where it actually is right now. Looking to other projects on this forum, I tried to make one myself, but it does not satify me at all. Any suggestion for an optimal layout is more than welcome. I have no problem building new walls and my budget is more than enough to do pretty much anything. Thank you very much for your attention and please let me know if I forgot any important detail. Alex
