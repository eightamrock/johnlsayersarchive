knightfly27 November 2006#10
Been too swamped with life to get here as often as I'd like - one thing to keep in mind: the formula used in the slat resonator calculator is essentially the same as the one in Everest's Master Handbook of acoustics - when that was written, about the only type of material available was OC 703 fiberglass. If you change to a different material, you will change the airflow resistance of the absorber, and this will affect the center frequency. Don't ask me how, I'm not into heavy math and haven't had time to get that deep into absorber theory (even tho I bought several book$ on the subject :oops: ) Since you're using variable depth absorbers, I don't think that fact is as critical as if you were targeting a specific frequency - see Len Morgan's thread for his experience on this very subject... Steve
