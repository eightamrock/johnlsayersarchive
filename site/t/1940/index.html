So really, you don't have much you have to wire. About the only things you have to wire would be: Outboard Tielines (ext. outputs & inputs, extra patch points) Multi-track inputs & outputs Plate 1, 2 Audio File O/P Echo returns Rep coil I/P & O/P?? FB Amp Mon Send Delay device spkr I/P Buss A & B output STD Play I/P As far as your multi-track 2 inputs & outputs. If you know or have an idea of what you will connect to these, you can build the connectors such as DB25. Otherwise you can just build snakes w/ TT's on one end and various other connections on the other. XLR, DB25, EDAC (Elco), 1/4", etc. This will allow for any combination of an additional machine or hard disk recorder. Maybe you'll have Pro Tools in for a session one day, and need to bring up the outputs on the console. For DB25 cables, it's easiest to do 8 pair snakes (cables). EDAC, depending on what you're wiring, usually 8 pair as well. If you'll have a 2 inch machine, usually a 24 pair cable for in's and 24 for out's. (if it's a 24 track) If you have some outboard mic pre's, you can just hardwire from the live room with another snake directly to the pre's, then have the outputs of the pre's come up on the extra patchbay. This way you don't have to worry about phantom power running through the patch bay. Also allows the most direct signal from the mic to the pre. BTW...just noticed an 80C module being sold on ebay. knightfly14 July 2004#16
Cool thread guys, please don't go private with this. I'm sure a lot of our members would agree - Just a small point for clarity - the term "phono" is never (in my experience) used to refer to a 1/4" connector, it's synonymous with the term "RCA connector", as in consumer stereo gear. The term "phone" refers to 1/4" connectors, "TS" is a 2-conductor 1/4" phone connector. "TRS" is a 3-conductor 1/4" phone connector, and can be used either for a balanced single-channel connection or for two channel un-balanced connection (headphones, for example) Those are the terms as I've seen them for the last 45 years, anyway - not trying to nitpick, just noticed that Aaron seemed confused by this as did I...
