Ethan Winer14 May 2008#15
matta wrote:
but how does one do that in a live room?
Sorry, I was speaking too generally. In a live room it's not a stereo imaging issue as much as basic comb filtering off the walls and ceiling etc. This is especially a problem in small rooms where no distance is greater than 10 feet. That's the distance at which reflections become "early" and comb filtering dominates. Not that you want obvious slap echo from a more distant boundary either! So generally speaking, the smaller the room, the more surface needs treating proportionally.
Should I apply some absorbers to the ceiling as well?
Yes, especially anywhere you'll use overhead microphones. --Ethan
