Hi gullfo I thought about diffusion on the back wall. I was hesitant to add it at all (philosophically, absorption on the back wall makes more sense to me). I like the idea of lots of thick absorption in the rear of the room. The soffits and the angled slot resonators will be sending lots of sound that way. Absorption just makes sense to me. gullfo11 July 2007#49
i create "absorbers" off to one side of the drawing - maybe 2" x 2' x 4', or 4" thick etc... to make it a "standard size", group it and, then use ctrl, left-click+drag to put it in about the right position, then use the move tool to final position it. i do have some components made but its very fast to just draw and drag it and paste in the material i mostly just draw them. as far as grouping, typically I build the room - group it - sometimes lock it, build my inner walls, group it, build treatments - group and clone as needed. then i can go back and edit each group with doors and windows etc... this way lines and surfaces don't interact in unexpected ways...
