jhbrandt27 July 2010#2
John, Your questions are a little too general for an accurate response. Have you read 'Recording Studio Design' by Philip Newell & 'Master Handbook of Acoustics' by F. Alton Everest? -- Recommended reading to get the answers your are looking for, unless you want 'opinions'. And, as you know, we all have opinions. haha. I prefer to give facts and let you come to the proper conclusions. Usually when I design a 'variable acoustics' tracking room, I will usually go for a 50% swing on either side of 'balanced'. The totally dead and totally brilliant are not all that usable in a tracking room, IMO. The exception being small vocal booths, which should be totally dead with plenty of trapping to remove standing wave effects. You could probably get what you want by hiring a designer (PM John Sayers here) but neither the design nor the construction will be cheap. 8) Cheers, John
