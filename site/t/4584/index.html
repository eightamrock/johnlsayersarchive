Ethan Winer28 October 2005#5
Arpit, > What kind of frequencies will these traps be useful for? < As with all porous absorbers, they do less and less as you go lower. You don't show how thick the rigid fiberglass is, and that's the key. If you use six inches thick they will still be useful probably down to 40 Hz. More to the point, most rooms don't need as much absorption at 40 Hz as at higher bass frequencies. --Ethan
