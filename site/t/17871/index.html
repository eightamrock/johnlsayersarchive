Soundman202013 October 2012#2
Hi "tux", and welcome to the forum! :) Sao Paolo, Rio, or another place? I've only been to those two, and liked them both. But Rio is prettier! :) Anyway, you didn't give us much information to go on, but it seems to me that the best layout for that room, assuming you just want a control room, would be to set it up facing "down" the page, with the speakers on the wall labeled "5.00 m". That would be the front of the room, and the back would be the angled wall at the top of the page. That layout will give you the best geometry and symmetry for setting up your speakers and mix position, and provides good locations for installing your acoustic treatment. However, there is a problem: You have two identical dimensions. Both the width and height of the room are 5 m, and the length is related, at 7.5m. You will probably have to build another room inside that one, or at least some false walls, to fix that problem. If the dimensions are the same or related, then you will have major modal problems: you need a ratio between the dimensions that is favorable to the room acoustics. 5m x 5m x 7.5m is not favorable. In fact, it is really bad. - Stuart -
