giles1178 May 2004#4
a CRT is basically aLARGE tube. the front of the screen attracts the electrons from the rear of the tube. (all magnetic) When you place a magnet near the CRT it deflects the stream of electrons (hence the discoloration) if the magnet stays near the CRT long enuff it will remagnetize the grid at the front of the screen (polarization grid) that attracts the electrons. Why is an LCD safe. No magnetically sensitive parts. Liquid Crystal Display has tons of FIXED pixels. They are either on or off. The magnets cannot affect this operation. Liquid is not sensitive to magnetism like the electrons in a CRT. I hope I made that simple enuff. I skipped a lot of deeper tech talk :) Bryan giles
