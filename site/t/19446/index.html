Soundman202022 May 2014#3
my tentative plan to beef up the door I've got (standard solid core exterior) is to put sheets of MDF (5/8"?) on either side.
I would only add the MDF to the inside of the door, not the outside. Make each layer smaller that than the previous layer, so you can stagger the the seals easily too.
Is green glue a good option to attach them?
:shock: :ahh: NO! Green Glue is NOT adhesive! Despite the name, it is not actually glue at all, and cannot be used to stick things together. Just use normal construction adhesive if you want to glue the layers together, and also use plenty of long screws.
Should they be screwed in also?
Yes! - Stuart -

Soundman202022 May 2014#5
I ask some dumb questions sometimes, eh?
Not at all! As one of my teachers used to say: "The only dumb question, is the one you DIDN'T ask."! :)
Any comment about the door closer as replacement for handle and latch?
A good door closer on a well-hung door will be able to keep plenty of pressure on the seals, but if you want to be even more certain about perfect, seals, then replace one level of seals with magnetic seals, like you see on your refrigerator door. They magnets keep them perfectly sealed, and also keeps the door "latched".
The walls around it are two layers of 5/8" wallboard, so should I make go a little thicker and use two layers 1" MDF?
You'd probably be OK with two layers of 3/4" MDF. Plus the door core itself, that's more than enough. And it is also very HEAVY!!! :) Remember to beef up your door framing, jambs, and hinges..... - Stuart -
