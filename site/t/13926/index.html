papaver10 May 2010#6
Hi, 1. Yes, I have a cloud made of 3 panels of rigid rockwool. At the beggining I was going to build an entire ceiling but I really don't need it. The cloud works well. Is in the mixing point. 2. Monitors are active: Mackie HR624. I have build the heat vents, but it don't appear in the photo. It are too high. I worked 6 hours continuosly and it work fine (the HR625 have a circuit that stops the amplification in case of overheat). I'll see it in august, eith the highest temperatures. 3. The hangers works very well. The solcionate the little number of it I covered all the wall with insulation, so there is no direct vent to the hangers. So it's like a double trap (mid and bass). It works very well. John sayed here to a guy that maybe this will create overabsortion, but I can't compare with other studios. 4. The sound is amazing! Probably it don't sound like the pro's but I'm really happy. The stereo image is very clear, if you close your eyes you can see every instrument in his place. All the frecuencies are ok. Maybe it's too loud on the highest frecs and with some productions it's a little irritating, specially with the "s" of the singers but that's not really important. The mids are very defined and reveals a lot of information. The lows are in his place. there isnt any annoying stacionary wave that disturbs increasing or decreasing the frecuencies. This is very satisfying. I have aplied the -4db filter on the mackies to avvoid the increase of low frecuencies produced by the soffit mounting, but maybe the cut is too high and the bass frecuencies seems to be too low, but it are very clear. In the other room it sounds more powerfull but maybe is fore the room modes. Anyway it sounds very good, and this are minor problems. Now I can work very fast, in a second I can see if a sounds work or not and the place that should stay. It was worth the work. 5.The room is 2,7 - 4,3 - 2,8 meters
