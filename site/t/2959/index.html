tenkas wrote:
and for my first question about effeciency, do you believe a gobo without any backing absorbs as much bass (if I put it in the corner of the room) as a gobo with backing?
I'm not sure a 2'x15"x3" panel will do enough for bass absorption. Most of the plans I've seen for bass absorption panels have been 2 sheets of OwensCorning 703 2"x4" panels wrapped in a open weave cloth, and suspended from picture hangers. I don't know if it's necessary to wrap a frame around them for added physical support or not, but I don't think so. http://johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=2930
