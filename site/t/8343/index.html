tmix4 March 2007#3
You may want to consider the same square footage ( if limited by zoning) but different dimensions. PLUS... I would probably make a nice vocal booth , a small ventilated equipment / computer room (to silence every thing) and keep the rest in one larger control / tracking room. Use free standing gobos it you want to break up the room for multiple members. I have worked this way for years and LOVE it. Unless you are going to track multiple members of a band (including drums) at the same time, you wont miss the extra iso booth. I found it useful to lay out the rooms to cover what I need for 90% and "wing it" for those few times something unusual comes along. My opinion obviously. Tom
