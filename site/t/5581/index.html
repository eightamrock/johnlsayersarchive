Dreamin, thanks for getting back to me so quickly and thanks for the great design. I feel that the design that you came up with is more ideally suited for music production. I hope to create a facility designed for post-production media. I would only really require two sound proof, tuned rooms. One room for mixing and one room for recording foley and sound effects (having a third vocal booth would be great, but I think I can get by with recording VO in the foley/effects room.) The main mixing room would be the bigger of the two rooms. Here, you would sit at a console, watch your picture projected on a screen and mix the sound. The room should represent a 5.1 surround theater the best it can so that your mixes can be ideally calibrated for film and media exhibition. The foley room should be smaller, but it should be big enough to have foley pits (different surfaces accessible under the floor. You can pull up the floor boards and walk on these various surfaces (e.g. concrete, dirt, gravel) to record various footsteps). It should also have enough space to move around in because foley/effects recording sometimes requires some larger movements and props. In the instance of recording foley/effects, the main mixing room would serve like a control room in music recording, and you would record the effects through the console in there while communicating to the foley/effects artist through the talkback and a window connecting the two rooms. Other than these two rooms, I don't need anything else. I just need a space separate from these two rooms where the office stuff takes place and where meetings with clients can occur and where general business can be conducted without interfering with a mix or a recording that might be going on. This third space doesn't have to be sound proofed or anything. Ideally, I would like this space to be by the windows. So, do you think my space might work for such a set-up??? Currently, I work in a post-production sound studio. Our main mixing room is roughly 19' Long by 17' Wide. Our foley/effects room is something like 19' Long by 9' wide. I figure I won't be able to fit that kind of set-up in my smaller space (especially since I want a third space for office work), but that gives you some idea of how the mixing room and the foley room are related. I would prefer to have a foley room that is at least 7-8 foot wide, but it needn't be nearly 19' long, that's overkill. Thanks again!
