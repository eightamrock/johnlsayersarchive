gullfo31 August 2007#4
this might be workable. angled inside treatment walls. outside shell has drywall layer inside and multiple layers outside as needed. bases are separate and walls are separate as well instead of stagger studs. ceiling uses 2x12 with wide slats. cloth, and insulation to diffract and absorb. angled walls are slat resonators, straight inside walls are slats over absorbers. add some hanging fabric covered 703 absorbers on the walls to treat if too live. note: insulation not shown but assume to line the backs of the slat walls and between drywall in outside shell.

the only additional cost might be the extra set of 2x6 & 2x4 around the top and bottom plates. much more isolation though... ultimately you're building it so if you find the extra 2x6 and 2x4 are out of budget then go with the staggered studs. its all about tradeoffs... if MDF is less costly and therefore meets your budget needs, then you should be able to use it realizing its a different density than drywall.
