Eric_Desart11 August 2007#2
That depends. But reading your question literally then NO ... GG exists for a couple of years now. All over the world, people have build gypsum board walls totaling square miles, without GG, before and during the existence of GG. And most likely GG covers only fraction of a percent of the walls build nowadays. Most of these walls fulfill their purpose (otherwise most of these architects, engineers and DIYers should be idiots). A BMW exists. Does one need it do drive? A 2CV did a nice job as well. Should I personally use it in a studio (assuming drywall constructions)? If the budget was available (hence conditional), probably yes. It's not only TL, but GG makes mounting errors or weaknesses, difficult edge and frame adjustments, flanking, drum-skin effect very forgivable. I find it very useful to have around in such circumstances. :) But I admit that others pay the bill.
