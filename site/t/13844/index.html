xSpace11 December 2009#7
Whatever John says in the linked post is what I would do, which is kinda why I presented it. As to diffusion in a room with a ten foot wall length, it will not help. It is just too small a room to do anything that will yield any valid acoustic response, especially with diffusion. You need absorption mostly and especially to counteract the modal buildup.
