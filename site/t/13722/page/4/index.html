some good news! did some sound level tests today and although I need to snag some bits in the amp room, mainly caulking, the results were pretty good. I cranked up some monitors to over 100dbc in the amp room (aprox 50db ambient) and upstairs (where my main concern is) it read at around 60db - pretty good I thought. You can here something, but as the control room isn't built yet, and there is leakage from the amp room downstairs - doors not quite fitting properly and a boarded up window not sealed - I think i can get a bit more, especially when the room is skimmed with plaster (?). One thing I was a little disappointed with was room to room levels (only 25-28db reduction), but as i said there's only one wall built as yet, and read somewhere that all walls act as one unit, so am expecting a bigger reduction than initial tests. I will, however, be beefing up the one of the doors , although it's solid core - 1 3/4 inch, it leaks sound. So I'll be attaching another door to it My wife will be happy!! chris
