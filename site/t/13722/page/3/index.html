AVare16 November 2009#66
xSpace wrote:
Measurements of the sound insulation of building facade elements. http://www.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/obj/irc/doc/p ... /ir818.pdf
Thanks xSpace. Your posting the link to IR818 made me think. IR586 has test results relavent to this discussion. Pdf page 19 has test results for a double 90 mm block wall with insulation on isolated mounts and without. This broadly equivalent to brick. Isolated mounts yields an STC of 77 and on a common mount 62. Flanking is the limiting factor. Chris: Read the entire IR586 to get a good feel for the physics and real world results of them. Andre
