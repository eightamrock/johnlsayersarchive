Pennywizz614 November 2005#3
Looks pretty good, Will that size be sufficient? It looks like you could hardly fit anything in the control room besides yourself, let alone a console and some racks. Because of the small size you will want to work on good acoustical absorbtion. Is the room to the right an iso booth or an all around live room? If you are looking for a live room that size I wouldnt even bother and just extend the control room with a small single or dual performer iso booth. Also, I cant really tell if its parrallel or not, but the left and right wall on the right room should be slightly offset so you wont get wicked reverb. Fill
