gullfo30 March 2007#9
its a fairly tight space (9' on one dimension) so I'd think about turning the CR to get a better room ratio, or if you need more live space, I'd put the CR off to one side to allow easier load in/out to the live room so bands don't need to go through the CR... if storage is also needed, you could put a small closet in on the live room side rather than trying to squeeze it between walls. on the back of the CR you could use an inside-out wall with absorption and let bass flow into the furnace room.
