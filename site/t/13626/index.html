xSpace29 September 2009#5
"Yet i have not seen anywhere where it says minimum dimensions or anything of that sort." It may be difficult to find on line but in the acoustic handbook "Master Handbook of Acoustics", Alton Everest refers to 1,500 cubic feet as his reference in what determines what is a small room (less than 1500) and a large room (larger than 1500). This number is used, as I understand it, on a per room basis and is not considered if you "total" the volume(s) of multiple rooms. It also uses nine feet (2.74 meters) as the ceiling height. Your current room has 13,555.5 cubic feet! With an area this large and a plan that will no doubt be as big as the space, it is in your best interest to get your hands on F. Alton Everest "Master Handbook of Acoustics". It is very important to what you are involved in. Good luck
