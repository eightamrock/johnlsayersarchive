Spyrow29 July 2007#1
Hi everybody! We are planing to upgrade to a bigger and better studio, and build it from scratch in a village near the city. I would like to post here from the first steps (now) till it's totally built. Any help is welcome! This is our first design:
External image, not preserved — original: http://farm2.static.flickr.com/1404/943118070_7b7314a5f5_o.gif
The studio is supposed to support big groups like corals or bands, so the big Room 1. It's supposed to be able to record a rock band playing live, watching themselves. Room 2 is for the battery, Room 3 the vocalist and Room 1 the rest. Hall is supposed to be used as sound lock but big enough to carry a grand piano inside Room 1. The control Room is so large because we want to be able to give a class to 10 - 20 students. Room 2 is going to be a "rest room" for the people we don't want to disturb us when working so they can go there, seat, listen to the music, watch the performance... etc. Acoustically Room 1 is going to be a more or less neutral but with a more reflective side and a more dead side (from left to right). I want Room 3 to enhance a vocal performance so I have to study it, I don't know right now, maybe doing it with not so much absorption and a lot of diffusion to get good early reflections. Any idea is welcome. Room 2 is going to be between neutral and live. We prefer to keep that room useable for any purpose. If somebody wants to get a "very live" sound of the battery then we will probably use the live corner of the Room 1 with more reflective panels (tiles or rock) hanging from the ceiling. Almacen is the store and oficina is the office (obviously). I'm afraid that Room 3 is so small I can't deal standing waves with proper bass traps or the Control Room so much big (too long RT). As I said, any comment is welcome!
Spyrow30 July 2007#2
I forgot to talk about insulation. The external walls are going to be: concrete + wood studs with fiberglass + air + wood studs with fiberglass + gypsum board + green glue + gypsum board. The internal walls are going to be: gypsum board + green glue + gypsum board + wood studs with fiberglass + air + wood studs with fiberglass + gypsum board + green glue + gypsum board
gullfo2 August 2007#3
nice design! you could consider reversible treatments on the walls of the live rooms and booths. take a look at some of John's designs where he has incorporated reversible treatments. the small booth should be ok for vox and my choice is to be slightly more live than dead so using slat resonators will help balance the room and you could consider a more dead ceiling treatment and reversible hanging absorbers/reflectors. i would make room 2 a nice quality booth and make the hall the lounge for people to hang out. if need be, you could drop the room 3 in favor of the larger hall/lounge. in the CR, if you're going to make it a teaching facility, you should think about the seating for viewing vs active participants - raised level seating in back, and/or lower level in front of the CR window for viewing. depends on your console and monitor arrangements.
