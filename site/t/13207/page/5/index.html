donal_finn2 April 2010#106
Yah ,its unfortunate but there is no face to face contact with the musicians, The Control is upstairs directly over the live room, so the setup prohibits it, video feed is ok though, theres no latency as the cameras just direct feed the tvs, so its ok for the time being. Luckily enough the tvs have a facility to record devices direct onto a hard drive so thats pretty cool! The cameras also have mics in em so i get talk back into the bargain ( i didnt know that when i bought em ! so bonus there.) The glass doors dont seem to be very soundproof in the human voice range or in the low frequencies but in the very high end (where i need serious isolation from the plastic roof and rain noise outside) they work a treat (they were designed with this in mind so i guess the guy did a good job. We had a band in over the last few days recording some covers and the place sounds good to me. :oops: (but being a beginner really, what would i know!) Its definitly a different world of sound than it was before i started. One thing i did notice was when the bands bass player was playing, a bit of sound was leaking (from his huge ampeg rig) from below up into the cr. (guess i should have went with the hat tracks and new ceiling after all) but ya make your calls and then live with em so onward and upward. I will post an mp3 of one of the recordings so ye folks can mock me! (tried but the exstension mp3 is not allowed any thoughts?) I still havent had the chance to do any serious sound checks but a pal of mine who has the nessecary gear and experience is going to help me out next week for a day or two just to make sure there are no major accoustic problems.

donal_finn15 June 2010#111
Hi Just to let folks know, The percussion tracks were recorded live in a very small room!! There wasnt a huge amount of care given to the setup of mics etc.. They are just scratch tracks for a customer which we recorded 17 tracks in one day and done some overdubs the next day with Bodhran and Cajun drums. In the room at the time there was live, 4 people. One guy playing a set of three senagalese Dun Dun drums. (one rode M3 centre overhead mono ) very loud and bassy. Two Guys with Very very sharp, highly tuned Ghanian Djembe drums on either side (overhead stereo set again rode M3s) And one guy playing 5 bells just using ambient from all three mics to be captured. It was massively loud in there!!!!!! I gotta say i am delighted with the tracks I was expecting them to be really muddy with the setup i used and they actually bettered my expectations ten fold. Also on a very sweet point, in full swing, they were barely audible in my garden which means also my isolation / soundproofing is much much better than i had anticipated!!!!! I have some folk musicians in today to start an album and a few more projects lined up in the next few months, Life is good. I owe a lot to this forum and the help and advice i recieved on it. (hate to even imagine where i would be if i went my own way) So Big up to everyone on the site but most especially Xspace for his constant patience and clear concise help!!!! and John, expect a donation soon!!
