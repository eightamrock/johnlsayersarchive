gullfo21 March 2008#40
the door on the entry would likely have an absorber on it as part of the treatment process so i don't think it matters much except that it does free up other wall space for more long and angled treatments. i think a "typical" brick wall tends to be about STC 45 and then there's a whole bunch of other bits to consider like flanking. leakage, reverberation, and coupling it with a plaster/drywall wall for absorption and finish. here's a reference to an AU article: http://www.australbrick.com.au/national ... erties.php

gullfo15 April 2008#48
isolation is generally not portable. acoustic treatments can be. will 25K be enough for the size of your project? not likely. it would probably be enough for the CR and a small isobooth, and "maybe" some acoustic treatments in the larger space (consider plywood or plexiglass polys hung from the ceiling). it really depends on how you source your materials. check into recycled materials - block, drywall, framing, insulation, doors etc.
