1, is 30' really long for a CR?
It might be, or might not. More than the length, I would look at the room ratio, the floor area, and the total room volume. If they all pan out, then 30' is fine. It means you will have modal support down to very low frequencies, which is good, provided that the spread is smooth and even. It also means you probably can use diffusers in the room, which is also good.
2, notice the bottom right corner of the CR, the wall splays INWARD 2'5", starting 9'9" from the rear wall, which all lines up almost exactly where my left monitor will pointed, will the slanted wall really smear my mixing? if so what can i do to correct it?
I wouldn't worry about that too much. It is all the way back at the rear of the studio, where symmetry is not so important, and the angle is slight. Just put a large bass trap in the corner, and then use the rest of the angle for some type of broadband treatment: A slot wall comes to mind...

You have 40 bass traps in a room? :shock: How did you manage that, when there are only 12 corners in a room? :)
i put the room dimension into some room calculator and the CR said "good for CR" but i didn't understand all the numbers really, (but the bonello curve thing looked good!
Yes, but the bonell graph is only part of the picture! You seem to have missed this part
- 1.1w / h < l / h < ((4.5w / h) - 4): Fail - l < 3h & w < 3h: Fail - no integer multiple within 5%: Fail (ratio3 = ratio1 * 3)
In other words, your room fails all three of the BBC tests for rejecting a room as a critical listening room. You'd need to cut the length down to 18'8" to get a good ratio and pass all the tests.
