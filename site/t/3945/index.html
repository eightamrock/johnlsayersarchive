AVare25 June 2005#2
I just read somewhere on this site that diffusers are a bad idea if they are less than 10-12ft from the mix position? Is that correct?
Misleading. Teh number is corect for the commercially made difuser that the author was referring to. It has ben quoted too much out of context. Difusers start to show lobing at frequenicies whose wavelength is greater than 3 times the distance from the diffuser.
Would diffusers on the side walls be a be idea since they are close?
Vageue question as you have defined "good". Acoordign to current recommendations, there should no early reflections int he first 15 mc less than 10 dB down. Diffusers will not do that full range in your setup.
How much angle is required on splayed walls to gain any significant benefit?
Use the mirror image technique in looking at teh layout. 12 degrees works out to tbe the usual figure, but dot he analysis. If you down't to loose that much space ont eh sides, you can dogtooth, or setp, teh splayed walls. Good luck! Andre
