gullfo15 April 2008#4
i'd skip the acoustic foam and build up porous absorbers to place on first reflection points and as clouds. i'd turn the mix desk to face the live room but i'd still be concerned about the lack of symmetry in the mix position.

gullfo16 April 2008#6
from an isolation perspective - the nice dense walls should work pretty well. keep in mind you need to consider structure borne and the ceiling as other aspects of isolation - plus ducting etc. from a quality perspective - some of that depends on the room size as well as the types of treatment you use to shape it.
