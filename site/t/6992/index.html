knightfly7 September 2006#3
With that ceiling height, a CR of either 12 x 10 or 12 x 13 is modally usable; this would also leave enough for a booth big enough for at least a basic drum kit, and the booth would have better sound than a smaller one. This is assuming your REAL, FINISHED ceiling height is really 7.5 feet; have you taken into account a true mass-air-mass construct between studio and upper floors? If not, ceiling height may get even lower. Main thing at this time is to NOT let anyone finish your space inside - you do NOT want anything but STUDS showing from inside the space, you will need to take it from there. This will likely include adding a couple layers of drywall "beef" inside the studs, then insulation, and (depending on just how MUCH isolation you need) possibly separate inner frames; at the least, RSIC isolator clips and hat channel to mount at least two layers of 5/8 drywall on. Any chance of pix or sketches so we can see what's what? Steve
