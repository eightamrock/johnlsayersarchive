Your question is way too vague. To give a couple of ideas, the NARAS 5.1 paper suggests a 3500 ft^3 room with 11 ft ceilings, so about 300 sq. ft. Old BBC docs recommned 1500 ft^3 MINIMUM for vocal booths. You can can get by with less, depending on your wants, what you are williong to compromise on. Andre
