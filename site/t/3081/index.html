John Sayers26 January 2005#2
Dave - you've done well. I only have one suggestion and it concerns your right wall. I wouldn't use a staggered stud, I'd build an inside/out wall - i.e. you build it first and add the drywall to the outer side - the studio side. Then build another wall for the studio isolation but drywall it normally. You can then use the cavity of that inner wall in the control room as the resonator cavity for the slots. The cavity behind your left slot is the whole area to the side wall? Hope this helps - let me know if I need to draw it for you :) cheers john
