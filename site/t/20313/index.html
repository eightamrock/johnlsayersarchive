Soundman202023 December 2015#2
A bit on the heavy side for optimal bass trapping, That's about twice the density that fiberglass needs to be for that. I wouldn't use it inside an MSM wall cavity or a bass trap, but it should be good for mids and highs. However, it would be best to get the manufacturer's actual lab tests, to see just how good or bad they might be. You never know for sure unless you have the real data... - Stuart -
