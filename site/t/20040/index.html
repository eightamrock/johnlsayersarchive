ooo2729 June 2015#1
I'm planning on building a recording studio on my property. I don't know exactly what size yet as I've yet to determine if the area I want to build in is even viable. My goal is 400-500 sqft ... I think. Honestly I'm not sure if that's enough space for what I want to accomplish, or if its even overkill. Goals: * Free standing structure on concrete slab (unless someone suggests an alternative) ~450 sqft(?) * Allow my band to play and record out loud. Drums, bass, guitars, vocals. * A separate control room would be nice but is not a hard requirement * Temperature control * Sound lock Usage: My band practices weekly. We usually do so in my garage in a headphone mix with electric drums. Some times we'll rent a practice space to play out loud. We gig every now and then for fun, once a month or so. We want to get a little more serious about recording and we want to be able to do it out loud. On practice nights I hit record in Pro Tools, only occasionally stopping it if we take a break or something. Then, on a weekend night, I'll go in the garage and chop up the session, sometimes touch up the mix a little, and bounce each song to mp3, including jams and whatever other funny stuff I stumble upon. The bounces go in to a shared dropbox folder and we all get to listen during our work week :) A control room would be nice so that I could bring in an engineer to record us properly. But again, it's not absolutely necessary. I also wouldn't mind other bands to occasionally use the space to record, but it's primarily for my band. I live in a neighborhood that has a pretty strict HOA. Fortunately the neighbors aren't terribly close. I'm on a little over an acre, and most lots are at least this size. My nearest neighbor is 150 to 200 feet away. Still, I want as little sound to escape as is feasible on my budget. Budget: $50K I should mention that our drummer owns a construction company and is planning to help in some ways. We'll be buying materials at his rates and he can act as the general contractor pro bono. He'll help me hire the sub contractors we need for various tasks (like foundation, framing, etc) and help me when there's stuff we can do on our own. Questions: 1) Is my budget realistic? 2) Is there a rule of thumb in terms of $/sqft? 3) Am I allocating enough space? 400-500sqft? Is there an optimal size? 4) Is there an existing plan I could use versus having to design it from scratch? 5) I've been reading a lot of this forum and others, as well as Rod Gervais' book. But I still have no idea where to begin. Help? 6) What other information can I provide here to enable others to advise? I'm sorry if any of these questions are addressed in the FAQ. I went through it and didn't see anything, but maybe I missed it. Thanks in advance for any advice.
stevev29 June 2015#2
Hi ooo27 and welcome. You have left out one bit of info which is how much isolation do you require in terms of Db? If you're an amplified band with a drummer then you're probably pushing 120db. If you need to be 50db outside the studio then you need 70db isolation. That's the first thing you need to work out as it's what defines how you construct your building to achieve the required level of isolation. as for the questions....

To be honest, I don't think so. My studio which is around the same size that you are talking about cost me 50k.....but that's with me doing 85-90% of the work. Did the slab myself, was on the trowel with the brickies, was on the roof with the plumbers, was running cable for the electrician and did the whole of the internal construction with timber and plaster myself as well as internal acoustic treatment and finishes. If I had to pay contractors for the whole build and did none of it myself then I think i would've been up for around 150K :shock: You guys might be able to get it done under 100k if you have your friend on board and can get good deals on materials, but I think 50k won't quite get you there if you're hiring a lot of tradesmen. Maybe 50k in materials plus whatever labour you need.
ooo27 wrote:
2) Is there a rule of thumb in terms of $/sqft?
No, but there is a rule of thumb regarding room ratios. ie: width vs length vs height. Look arounf the forum for info on room ratios and you'll get the idea. As you're starting from scratch you can pick and choose what will fir your site best.
ooo27 wrote:
3) Am I allocating enough space? 400-500sqft? Is there an optimal size?
see above.
ooo27 wrote:
4) Is there an existing plan I could use versus having to design it from scratch?
John has quite a few designs posted up on the forum. I actually forget where they are at the moment, but look around and you'll find some of his layouts which would be a great place to start.
ooo27 wrote:
5) I've been reading a lot of this forum and others, as well as Rod Gervais' book. But I still have no idea where to begin. Help?
Figure out how much isolation you need in terms of db. Figure out the dimensions of the space you want to build by looking at room ratios and the site that you have to build on. Look at some of John's design and see what suits your needs best. That'll get you started. Then download sketchup and make some plans of what you're looking to build, post them up here and you'll get some advice on whether you're ticking all the right boxes or not.
ooo27 wrote:

see above regarding isolation requirements and sketchup plans.
