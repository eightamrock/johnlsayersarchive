len-morgan27 April 2006#17
Were your measurements of street noise actually ON the street? Have you made any measurements inside the area where you're going to build the studio? You mentioned that you thought you'd need 50 dB isolation and that is easily attainable but if you have 100+ on the street, that you leave you with 50+ dB AFTER you've finished in the studio. That's probably not going to work for any serious music. What is the surface of the brick wall like now? Is it just bare brick? If so, you can gain a few dB of isolation by "rendering" (applying motar or cement) those walls before you begin. Is the door you have going into your studio open to the street or is there another door to the outside that you haven't drawn? If you're going to have any problems, it's likely to come in from door(s). len
