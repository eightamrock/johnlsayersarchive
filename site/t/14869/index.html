Inner Shape of Small Production/Tracking Studio

Started by RTS406 on 29 September 2010. 6 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 14869.

RTS40629 September 2010#1
We are on the fourth floor of a 12 floor, steel-structured building with thick concrete floors and brick walls. The building originally housed heavy machinery on each floor, but now contains music rehearsal spaces (most of which are not well isolated). We plan to isolate outside airborne and flanking noise via room-within-room construction. Double doors lead into the space from the vestibule, and are part of our M-S-M strategy denoted by the outer and inner room lines. The inner room is roughly 18.5' x 15' x 9' ceiling. For the existing outer leaf, we completely covered the original plaster-on-brick walls and plaster-on-concrete ceilings with 5/8" drywall backed with a healthy dose of GreenGlue. We then affixed 4" Roxul to the aforementioned surfaces. For the proposed inner leaf: - The floating floor will be double 3/4" plywood/GG mounted on 2x6 joists insulated with 4" Roxul AFB. The joists will rest on a properly calculated distribution of neoprene (yes, we read that thread :D ). - The inner walls will be double 5/8" drywall/GG on 24" OC 2x4 studs and insulated with 4" Roxul AFB. - The inner ceiling will be double 5/8" drywall/GG mounted on 2x4 wood frames and insulated with 4" Roxul AFB. These frames will be hung from the outer ceiling using neoprene isolation hangers. Studio goals: *** Accurate mixing environment (near-field monitors w/ sub) ** Vocal tracking (no booth) * Live drum tracking (occasionally) With this thread we're hoping to gain insight on the most strategic use of our inner space given our spatial constraints and studio goals. The red lines represent a proposed inner-room which would make maximal use of the inner space. Any suggestions pertaining to room shape, layout, treatment, and mixing position would be greatly appreciated. Please let us know if we can provide any additional information. Thank you.
