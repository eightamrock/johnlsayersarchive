Hi All, Thanks to John, Steve, Ethan and anyone else who contributes to this awesome site! heres the background info: (hope i don't miss anything!) I've been perusing the site for a couple months now. I'm in the design phase of building a new project studio and trackingroom/home theater in WV USA. Mainly want a decent drum room! I know the end goal of a live room is well balanced, and the HT is dead, but i'll compromise on that (the home-owners mandate includes usefullness and aesthetics!) I will probably have everything from rock to bluegrass musicians, so there will be a different array of noise from the trackign room. Isolation is only important between upstairs and downstairs. There is a hardwood floor upstairs, so I plan on insulating the joists and adding a layer of 5/8th drywall. from there, I was thinking of building aesthetic and functional sophets to hide ducting and lighting and add seperation. where there are no sophets I was goign to add bass traps to the ceiling and just acoustic tile elsewhere (for a variety of material and another leaf?) I will build a seperate iso/vox booth as part of the control room. (in back wall or somethign). I have an empty basement as my beginning, in a new house(2003). Block walls, Cement Floor, 9'1" floor to bottom of open wooden joists. There are two "major" problems, and numerous smaller ones. The first is the back wall being a curved block wall. I have No Idea how to treat this. I know drywalling a curve into the room is a bad idea, so??? we were thinking of applying a false concrete to texture it like large rocks(the upstairs has this same facade on the fireplace, looks awesome. not sure if it would help the acoustics! diffuser?) The second is the HVAC running through the room. I'll talk about that, since I have a gameplan for it. I plan on building a sophet around the entire system (as in the sketchup), insulating the outside of the ducting and then adding two 5/8 sheetrock to the sophet. mainly to keep noise from the rest of the house and visaversa. I have attached a sketchup file with a rough layout, but dimensions are fairly accurate (within an inch or so!). (My initial studio idea is on the "buildout" layer. My budget is about $5k for materials. I will be doing all the installation. (as keeping in spirit of the forum!). also, i'm wondering how people are cleaning the inside face of a dual glass wall that has been biult seperately, but do not open for cleaning, or is it just a too bad... theres dust on your glass window? If there is anything else for me to know please let me know, i will try and follow the rules as much as possible! Thanks for your time! (i'll try and get some pix up soon too!)
