Hi all, Just had my first visit to 'code enforcement' in our little municipality. It went well and I heard most of the things I wanted to hear. 1) Q - How big can I go? A - 75% of your residence size. 2) Q - Can I put a bathroom in there? A- Yes. 3) Q - How close to the neighbor can I go? A- 4 feet. 4) Q - What about noise, are there any limitations that I need to be aware of? (this one was a bit more ethereal of an answer) A - Well, if you're running a compressor 8 hours a day it could make people wonder what's going on in there. Alot of this depends on how well you get along with your neighbors. So anyway, we're moving forward and I can now meet with the contractor and give him some real numbers and draw up some real plans. Guess I'd better get learning Sketchup, eh? Again - should I start a new thread or just re-name this one somehow? -Doug
