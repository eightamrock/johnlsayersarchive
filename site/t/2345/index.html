linus22 September 2004#8
Here's my advice, having just built a small iso booth in the basement of tenament apt building in Manhattan: Don't build. Buy a pre-made iso-booth. 4'x4' is a really common size and if you have $2000 to work with you will definitely be able to get one. The best part is when you are done with it you will be able to sell it and make back part of your cost. If you build it you will likely have to demolish it when you are done, and be able to recoup none of your initial costs. These things pop up on Craigs List and Ebay pretty frequently. Just two months ago there was one available on Craigs List in NYC for $1600. I built a 5'x6'x7' iso booth. It cost >$2000 and took me almost 6 month of my spare time. If I had bought I could have been recording 5 months ago instead of doing a construction project.
