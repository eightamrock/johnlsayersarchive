Speakers and Treatment of Live Recording Room

Started by abnc on 28 February 2010. 3 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 14137.

abnc28 February 2010#1
I am nearing completion of a live recording room (22' x 15' x 10') that has gypsum on walls and ceiling. I am considering placing an acoustic drop ceiling just below the hard ceiling. I also am considering placing diffusors in the drop ceiling so as to not lose all the acoustic energy of the room. As this room is going to be used to record music in the classical/sacred genre, I want to achieve a decent amount of natural reverberation. I would appreciate any suggestions on wall treatment for a live room. More diffusion than absorption? Or, perhaps there is a good ratio between the two as a starting point. Also, what are some suggestions on placing speakers in this room (location, distance from walls, etc.). Thanks, AB
jhbrandt1 March 2010#2
AB With the dimensions you gave, you have some serious mode problems with numerous double incidences below 250Hz. I would suggest moving a wall or lowering the 'hard' ceiling. Adding 2 additional layer of 1/2" gypsum board to the ceiling will cure this. Are those dimension exact to the fraction of an inch? Why do you want to drop the ceiling? You must trap the room and corners are the most effective place for LF trapping. Cheers, John
