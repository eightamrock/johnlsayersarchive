jwl19 December 2006#9
You could make the vocal booth a bit bigger (you could almost double its volume) by sliding the rear door that you have directly behind the listening position down (in the drawing) to the angled wall. That would open up room for a bigger booth. Also, what are you going to do for acoustically treating that room? A room that small needs lots of absorption to get the low end under control. Especially, I would think, along the rear wall, since most of the sound will be reflected back there. Other than that, I like the drawing. Nice design for a very small space.
