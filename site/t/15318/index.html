gullfo28 November 2010#2
nice location to build! i think there are several aspects to this design that i would consider: the snack/food bar right next to the control room entry encourages people to loiter there - maybe thats the purpose. the rehearsal room right next the bathrooms could be noisy for either party plus it would be nice for an extra isolation room. the vox booth seems small and is the only entry into the live room - so basically people need to load their instruments in through the control room, the tight small airlock, and finally the vox booth - moving drums and pianos will be tricky. the storage room has access only from the live room. the CR has no available space for wall treatments and seems a bit small for the overall size of things.
