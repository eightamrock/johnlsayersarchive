Soundman202019 April 2016#5
So I plan to leave current room layout same, but just to make some acoustic treatment.
I would suggest that you replace your speaker stands with something a lot more massive (heavy). The ones in the photo are very light and flimsy. Speaker stands must be rigid, heavy, and decoupled. You also need to push those speakers right up against the front wall. Apart from that, you seem to have your room geometry set up correctly, but it would be good if you could get a smaller desk, and raise your speakers just a bit (maybe 4 cm or so), to help reduce desk reflections and comb filtering. Your acoustic treatment looks about right, but you might need more bass trapping, and you definitely need absorption panels between the speakers and the front wall. When you have all of that completed, I would suggest doing a test with REW and posting the results here, so we can see how well that is working. - Stuart -
