jwl15 May 2007#5
I don't like those off-centered, nonsymmetrical room designs. It's mostly an aesthetic thing for me. I'd keep the room symmetrical. Face the window, and have large curtains you can draw if need be. Put superchunks on the wall/ceiling corners. Use John's angled slot resonators to break up the parallel walls. Float a ceiling cloud, and put as much absorption on the back wall as you can.
