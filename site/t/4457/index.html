Ethan Winer2 October 2005#2
Kir, I didn't see any specific questions, so here's my general advice for room treatment: * Bass traps in as many corners as you can manage, including the wall-ceiling corners. * Mid/high frequency absorption at the first reflection points on the side walls and ceiling. * Some additional amount of mid/high absorption on any large areas of bare parallel surfaces, such as opposing walls or the ceiling if the floor is reflective. --Ethan
