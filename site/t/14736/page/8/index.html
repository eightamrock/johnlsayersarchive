gullfo12 January 2011#197
once you get the room up and running and the desk built. i think the desk you've picture (or something like it) should be OK. take some measurements and see where you're getting any residual reflections. you might have to put some bits of absorption around to finalize things. in general, after going though all the work, life and something like it take over and "stuff" moves in. as long as when you hang guitars, put in bookshelves, cabinets, etc etc it's not interfering with your work, then its all good. so the amp and guitars might be necessary right away, maybe put up some hooks for the guitars on the walls. cover the amp with a packing blanket when not in use (dust and reflections...) maybe keep the bookshelf in another room unless you need it handy. in which case, go with it.
