Hi! I'm not an expert, but here's what I've planned out for comparison. I've ended up with 60 mic lines coming in to a patchbay in the control room. I'm putting multiple wall plates in to reduce cable clutter as much as possible. Live Room A - 16 Live Room B - 4 Live Room C - 4 Vocal booth - 8 Guitar Iso - 4 Control Room A - 4 Control Room B - 4 Control Room Desk - 4 Hallway - 4 Lounge A - 4 Lounge B - 4 I'll also have a Speakon patchbay in the control room with pairs of speaker cable running to all other rooms. These are run separately, away from mic runs, to minimise interference. Also an RJ45 patchbay in the machine closet where the 30 Cat5 cables will terminate, with 2 coming in from each wall plate and a few extras around the place. They're for HearBack mixers, LAN and phone. The one thing I've left out is Midi, but I intend to make up Midi-XLR cables which should be fine for my short runs. To summarise, I recommend going for overkill! Best to put conduit in if you can, so you can pull more in future. An extra couple of hundred dollars now might save you thousands later on.
