Greetings and welcome! Deciding whether to go with a all-in-one-room studio or to cut it into smaller rooms is going to be mostly a matter of what you're most comfortable with. Monitoring isn't too big of a deal if you have good headphones. I can see 2 sides to the argument... If you're doing vocal and guitar stuff, you won't need to have a huge tracking room for drums. With 2 separate spaces, you'll still have room to do what you need. But a single, larger room means fewer walls (less money!) and a better sounding room to start with, so probably less wall and ceiling treatment. But whichever you go with, try to not build square rooms. Hope this helps. Kase www.minemusic.net
