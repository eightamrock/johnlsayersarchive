knightfly2 March 2005#9
"I've forgotten where I came across it, but one school of thought has the CR wider over depth. I guess so there is less (or easier to control) side wall reflection." - WSDG (Walters-Storyk Design Group) tends to this approach; but they are a high end pro designer and the rooms I've seen are deeper the SHORT dimension than most here are in the LONG dimension - unless you have enough space so that your side walls are at least 15 feet from your head, you would STILL need first reflection points absorbed. Also, for a smaller room this would put the rear wall even CLOSER than before, making even MORE deadening necessary to control modes, etc... So until your CR is large enough to have a short dimension that's wide enough for about 10-12 feet behind your head, firing length-ways is more sensible... Steve
