A good studio chair is one of the best investments you can make. I find it funny that people will think nothing of spending mondo dollars on a new microphone, computer plug-ins, etc.. but yet they will sit all day on a $100.00 chair from Staples. The Herman Miller Aeron chairs are outstanding. They can be had for between $550 and $700 on Ebay. After 12 hours of sitting in one of those puppies your back and legs will thank you. Worth every seingle penny.
