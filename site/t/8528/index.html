depends on your needs. if you check the stickies it has a detailed list of things that help when discussing your design. its a very narrow space but very tall so you could put a lot of trapping overhead. since it is narrow getting a decent RFZ would be tough. check some of John's wall unit designs for ideas on outfitting a small room. another option is building in some low and angled slat resonators to help guide the sound and help treat the space.
