igloo8 April 2005#1
Hi, I'm a new member to this forum, but I've been lurking around and I'm finally posting. I'm on a bit of a dilemma. As you can see from my archaic pictures (would've at least used photoshop; sorry for that :oops: )... The natural room orientation suggested by my CR's original construction is challenging to say the least. On one side, I cannot build ordinary soffit/bass trap structures from floor to ceiling due to a couple of door openings on the front wall: the restroom (doubles as a "machine room" with a booth for my computer, modem, line regulators, etc.) and a closet that I'll be using for mics and stuff. On the other hand the rear wall has a fixed glass and that'll be a problem reflection-wise. My possible solutions are: building soffit mounts over the height of the front wall's doors. And angling down the monitors to compensate for it. Using the corners to place large hangers as bass traps leaving free the doors. I added the option of a 15" false wooden wall to even the simmetry and building corner panels from there at each side. Second solution: placing a very thick curtain on the rear wall that I'll close when I'm mixing. What do you think? Any help is very much appreciated! PS. the drawings are far from being scaled, but hopefully you'll get the idea. Rodrigo Montfort
