abstractrhythm21 November 2008#29
ok I just spoke with a (assistant) chief over at the Brooklyn Department of buildings He said that covering up a sprinkler head would not be a problem as long as the remaining area is covered by the spray radius of the adjacent sprinklers. He also said that if I were to build a wall NEXT to a sprinkler head the required space for clearance was 4" there are 8 sprinkler heads in our room each set in a 18" from the outside walls. each head has a spray radius of 7ft.

xSpace22 November 2008#32
No sir, actually I wasn't suggesting. I had to look back over the thread and I see you have picked out a set of ratios that you are already working with. :) As has already been commented on, a control room needs balance/ symmetry for no other real reason then the fact that you have two ears. If this is modified, in relation to your two ears, early reflections and even direct reflections could cause mixing errors based on what you think you are hearing. I wouldn't say "don't do that" but I would say, educate yourself on the whys and wherefores in respect to having a trapezoidal control room. Now an isosceles trapezoid...that's worth looking into.
