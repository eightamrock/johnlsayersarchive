gullfo1 March 2007#3
possibly you already answered this: does this happen with all your audio monitors or just a particular pair? is it possible your monitors are the cause? I ran a room simulation in CARA based on your room and as much of the construction and treatments as I could add, and I don't see the 159hz as a problem even when I shift speakers and listening position around (which goes to show that simulations only get you so far as opposed to measurements...) this doesn't mean that something in the room isn't causing the 159hz to be elevated but usually you'd expect to see it in only a few places in the room - especially a mostly square room - due to modes - so either something is causing a resonance at that frequency (maybe some hollow cavity is reinforcing it? maybe the desk is creating a resonant chamber? the panel behind the drums?) or I'm thinking the speakers (or something in the amplification chain) is causing it.
