gullfo8 May 2011#2
welcome! so you're going to turn to point down the long way of the room? ok. skip the carpet. laminate is fine. on the soffits - if you're not angling the side treatments - you should (based on the drawing) widen the space between the speakers a bit more so the focus is about 18-24" behind you. this will widen the sweet spot - but make sure the distance is something your speakers can accommodate - in general - 5"-6" LF driver - around 5'-6', 8"-10" - about 6'-7', 12"-15" about 8'-10' are good rough estimates to start with. you could build angled slat treatments on both sides of the desk - obviously for the window you would make it top and bottom and for mix down, a very heavy drape to cover the window. the curved treatment would be OK on the back wall but not necessarily good on the side wall.

gullfo18 May 2011#6
On the ceiling, use hard backed clouds to create the symmetry over the mix position and absorption on the ceiling itself. The slats should be broadband and you can just make them 8' high or all the way up. If you make them 8' then plan on adding super chunk absorption above them on the wall ceiling corner.
