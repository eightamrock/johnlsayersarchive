Hi everyone, my first post here. As long as you use high-density styropor and perhaps coat it with something to give it a more reflective surface it might work although I would recommend you to use wood if its not to inconvenient for you. When it comes to placement the general rule of thumb is to diffuse if the time difference between source-listener and source-reflection-listener, is longer than the “Haas-zone”, witch is between 10-20 milliseconds depending on program material. If the distance were shorter than roughly 3 meters then you would normally absorb instead.
