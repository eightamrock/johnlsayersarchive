FPR11 March 2008#1
Hello everyone, I found these great forums few days ago & it's a gold mine for someone like me who start a project. So, i decided to ask your advises regarding the following: I decided to start building a studio (control room, rec room, machine room & the HVAC room) for the start. I am looking for the best solution to keep the outside light. I'm aware that windows & studios are not going much together but this case is special. As it's a big Barge (canal boat) & i have no problem if the sound leaks out. Also, it would be a shame to loose that great view i have from inside and it will add some originality to the place. So please let me know how you guys would place the walls in there. The sketchup file can be grabbed here: http://download.yousendit.com/76664C0939869B26 All the area that can be used for the project is shown in red. As you see, i have some restrictions: - it's the lower floor of the barge. That means the stairs you see is the main entrance. - There is a lot of metallic part a bit everywhere. Like the metallic structures & the main metallic wall in the middle. This metallic wall can be a great separationbetween the control room & the rec room. But the best place for the rec room is where the ceiling is the highest(339cm) but i will have an emptu place in between. - One last detail, as it's the lowest floor of a barge, i have an empty space of 70cm under the wooden ATM floor. That might complicate things with resonances. I hope i'm wrong... So every good idea is welcomed & much appreciated. Sorry for the windows i couldn't intersect properly with the walls. But it give you a clear idea of where they are placed. Attachements : 2D & 3D projects.

gullfo12 March 2008#5
i think you'll want to treat the walls and windows for isolation purposes - if nothing else - sound intruding into your space. so it should be possible to keep a bunch of the windows but expect to add a layer to ensure isolation needs are met. otherwise it sounds like a very cool project. are the stairs the main entryway into the space? is there another? this way if you decide the live rooms should be near the stairs, the folks in the CR (near the bow? bow lounge?) you'd have access without having to go through the live space (if needed).
