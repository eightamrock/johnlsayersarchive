Soundman202029 January 2009#2
Look on the back / bottom / in the manual for each piece of equipment, and note down the power consumption it says it needs (in "amps") Now add up all those numbers. Presto! You have the total consumption for all of your equipment. That's what you need to wire for. Of course, your ACTUAL consumption will be much less than that, but you still need to wire it for the rated peak. And don't forget to leave a large margin for expansion! If you get to, for example, 12 amps, then wire for 20 anyway (or two circuits of 10 each). If you get to 19 amps, then wire two circuits of 15 each. Etc. - Stuart -
