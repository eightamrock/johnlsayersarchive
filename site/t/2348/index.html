ejbragg17 September 2004#2
Best thing to do? Can you get your money back? Pick up the one you suspect is NOT the best door. Install it (careful not to scratch it). Then test it. Then take it back and trade it in. Test the other one. If you were right, you won't have to do another trade-in. If you were wrong, you make a third trip. You can hypothesize all day long, but this will answer that particular question the most thoroughly. (Generally speaking, metal conducts heat and sound more than wood, but with no information, how can you really know?)
