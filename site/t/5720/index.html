knightfly26 February 2006#2
OK, that makes more sense; although you could have just continued your other thread, answering the questions there - no matter, if you like I can delete the other one and we'll just use this one. First, don't build a booth with 2 sides the same; you won't like the sound, and it's in a frequency range where you'll notice it. Better if you have 5x6, especially if those will be the INSIDE dimensions, to build it 5x6x7 - modally that combination works really well. You need to read my first long post in this thread http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=4278 so you understand how walls work for sound isolation; then, you need to find out if you can use the existing walls in that space and beef them up, only building 2 new walls with a door. Pay particular attention in the above thread to comments about only having two leaves of mass in a sound wall. Your existing construction is very likely to already have BOTH of these leaves of mass in place (see definitions in the above link) but those leaves were NOT built for decent sound isolation, so may need to be improved upon - adding still MORE leaves of mass in the construction will NOT help, and will even make things WORSE. Another thing - that small a booth will need air recirculation; this is another tricky part, but it can be done. Not sure if it's doable within your budget, but probably close... Steve
