Before going further, I would suggest considering that a roomful of cool equipment does not necessarily make it a "studio." The design and acoustics of the room are just as (if not more) important as the equipment that goes into it. By the way, was the 10k/20k you mentioned the budget for the entire project or just the studio build? :D :shock: A very broad overview of possible equipment needed: - recorder - (format? DAW, hard disk, tape?) - monitors - (near/mid field? - passive/active? sub/s? headphones?) - control surface - (mixer/mouse?) - mics - (large & small diaphram condensors, dynamic) - mic pre's - - cables - very important, but usually "not figured in budget" :roll: (recorder, monitors, mic) - processing - (plug ins, outboard?) - mixdown - (format? CD-R, DVD-R, DAT, tape?) Try doing some research on what other studios are doing/using. Here's a good place to start: http://www.recordproduction.com/welcome ... tudio_.htm Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
