Aloha,
Wow! That`s a lot better than what I had...
Glad to hear it's on the right track. :wink:
I was thinking of making one of the walls of the live room of exposed brick or mampostery to get a little brighter sound.
There's a thread around here somewhere about the pros/cons in using brick/stone/etc in room acoustics. A combination of hardwood floors and John's side wall slat absorbers help to even out the room response while keeping the the room "live." You can check them out here: http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm
Are those speakers the Dynaudio Air 15? I was thinking of getting them for that room!
The pics are the Air 6's. (Wish I had a pair!) For the size of room you have, the Air 15's may be too much speaker for the room, maybe/maybe not.
What do you think of the ceilling`s height?
Height is fine, if that's what you've got to work with. You may want to avoid sitting with your ears at the mid point floor to ceiling. Also, an angled cloud or two above the mix position will also help to break up reflections between the floor/ceiling surfaces. I'll try to have a couple of ideas posted soon, Aloha 8)
