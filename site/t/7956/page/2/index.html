March 8 --- This week has seen real tangible progress! Started the week with moving the power panel; Finishing the South wall egress door RO and the 29' West Wall with solid blocks; The detail photo shows what the North wall will include after removing the existing windows & frames and replacing with solid concrete block walls. The block will be laid flush with the existing brick wall (white). There is a 2' gap then the 7" steel beam. The North wall will be built to soffit around the 4" drain from the Apollo Grill next door and above me. This air space should help with isolation quite a bit. The photo with the rock hammer shows the futility of trying to break what you think is concrete and turns out to be granite... 4 hours to do the little damage I could to one bollard. I called Witch Equipment and they are going to come out next month (when the dumpsters get moved for access) to cut the two bollards of granite and saw cut 2' off the right side of the ramp to make more room for the Drum booth and Iso booth. Closet/storage along the other side of the skinnier ramp for mic stands, cables, coats, etc. The ceiling photo shows the detail of the ceiling above and the layout for determining ceiling height somewhat. The floor boards are 2.5" thick; the steel beam is also supporting a timber that runs from 6" thick show here to 4" at the North wall for drainage of the roof structure above. It looks as though the finished ceiling height will be somewhere between 9' 10" and 10' 4". I have to allow access and a plywood deck for sprinkler servicing down the road... sof figured to put in 3/4" plywood decking on top of the TrusJoists spanning the main room. With 3 layers of 5/8" fire rock. I am Ok with the lowered deiling height. What does everyone else think??? More photos of door install and sprinkler changeover coming soon. Namaste, Jon
