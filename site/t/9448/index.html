gullfo22 September 2007#2
you could just wrap up some mineral wool or 703 insulation in cloth and tuck it in that space. it will help a little bit but it would be helpful if you had a floor plan or photo of your room.... you probably want to put some absorbers on the corners, walls, and ceiling to create a balanced and diffusive environment. not too much though or it becomes a dead space.
