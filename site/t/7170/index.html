"Good lighting is essential in a studio and ideally a separate circuit should be allowed for it." The air conditioner is now being installed. packrat3 November 2006#3
You will have so many leaks from this room I wouldn't worry about the walls. In my opnion it is only drums that you really need to worry about. Bass would be the most difficult to isolate but most DI bass anyway. That shower looks pretty large. If it is not built i'd consider enlarging the bathroom and/or shower stall. In any case do the triple sheet rock wall treatment around the bathroom and add a solid insulated door leading into the bathroom. Absorbers could be added to tame the echo or not if it sounds good. These absorbers could be brought back into the main room. I can't say if it will make for a good vocal booth but you could certainly mic up an amp in the bathroom and get a good sound.
