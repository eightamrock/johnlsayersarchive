barefoot15 January 2004#3
Yeah, with all that sheetrock you probably have a very tight room. There's nowhere for the sound to dissipate. The Auralex will absorb high frequencies, but will do nothing for the bass. A balanced room will almost invariably be the opposite situation. You'll wind up installing quite a bit of low frequency absorption and rather little high frequency absorption. Thomas
