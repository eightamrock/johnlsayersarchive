redstudio wrote:
What is the best cloth/fabric to use to cover acoustic treatments...
For panels meant to absorb mid and high frequencies, an open weave fabric is best. For bass traps almost anything will do, and there can even be a benefit from using a closed weave fabric. --Ethan

gullfo18 August 2007#9
the answer was provided, now you want to know where to buy it. John suggested google to find sources close to you or via Internet you could purchase. felt is one option, other folks have used muslin, burlap, speaker cloth, etc depends on its use. as a general rule of thumb, you want to balance the effects of treatments to avoid having one trap from impacting another range of frequencies. this is a common issue where a bunch of soft things are in the space absorbing highs but leaving low and mids untreated, or vice versa where its too live (a better problem imho). for instance, a deep pile of insulation in a corner will do wonders for bass trapping but also suck in a bunch of high end - so covering it with a denser cloth like felt or high thread count muslin (or even a layer of plastic under the cloth, or widely space wood slats over top) of it can all keep the high frequencies alive. on reflection point abosrbers, you want mid-to high frequencies so a more open weave cloth like burlap on a 703 type insulation will impede mid-high frequency. for a slat resonator, you'd want cloth (if using one) which has little impact on air flow like speaker cloth. all of which you need to consider treating with fire retardents (which you can buy online). hth
