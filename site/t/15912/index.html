BriHar3 May 2011#5
Something to consider- You might want to consider putting a layer of thin plastic foil over the insulation and under the fabric on your cloud. This is not so much for the sound, but rather to prevent the fine glass particle dust from falling down onto your console and invariably shortening the life of your faders.
