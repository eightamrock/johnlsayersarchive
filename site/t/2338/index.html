cadesignr19 September 2004#4
In the land of a million studios, there has to be a good reason why this has not been done rampantly throughout Hollyhood's stripmalls...
There are a few reasons. First, sound isolation, as stripmall partition walls/ceilings are a joke. Therefore, the only way to do it is to build a massive outer shell/enclosure, as the existing shell does nothing. Not a good way to spend money in a rented environment, even if the owners approved it. Massive walls, require massive footings. That means tearing up the existing floor, as a 4" slab does NOT constitute a structural foundation. Then you have the inner shell, which is light weight, yet with an airgap, still uses precious square footage AND it's own footings, as you are supporting a COMPLETE FLOATED ROOM. That is only the BEGINNING. Consider the time and econimic frames for.....design, permit submittal, approval/reject/ redesign/construction contract procurement/and actual construction. In LA, could last as long as a year. That whole time is part of your lease period. :shock: Should you hire an architect/licensed designer, it could be as short as three months. Still, your paying while waiting. Should I go on? fitZ :)
