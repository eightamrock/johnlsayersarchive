What kind of bass traps are you looking to build? There has been a ton of discussion on this board and others about a) what exactly a bass trap is (ie, which methods of acoustic treatment count as being a "bass trap"), and b) how to build them. The membrane absorbers linked in the previous post are useful across a narrow band, but for most small-room studios there are probably better, more effective options. Can you maybe give some details about your room, and about what you've done to it so far?

Well, I am not into recording business, and I don't know ANYTHING about recording (so I don't know what the heck I am talking about, :wink: ), but I've read about ESS (Early Sound Scatering) studios.
