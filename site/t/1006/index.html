barefoot25 November 2003#2
Bricks are great, but fairly permanent. I would strongly suggest leaving a big enough opening for future monitor upgrades. Also, you should finish the brick with a smooth surface. An exposed brick surface will cause unwanted high frequency reflections from the front wall. Thomas
