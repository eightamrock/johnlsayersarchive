gullfo23 February 2011#6
if you are trying to isolate a drum kit from your neighbors - concentrate on getting the isolation correct first and then work on the treatments. yes you would want to treat the corners of the room plus likely need a cloud overhead plus the first reflection points treated. one advantage of polishing and staining the concrete floor is if you decide you want to add a layer of wood, it's easy to do, but you might find the concrete works. you could build a drum platform to help isolate some of the kit from transferring through the floor but drums tend to be very loud and isolation is a lot of work.
