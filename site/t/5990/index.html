John Sayers18 April 2006#15
Cristiano - you want the ceiling to slope upwards to just past the listening position. You should be able to get a 12 degree angle in your 2.5m ceiling height. The front one can have either a hard surface in front with insulation behind, reflective, or the reverse with the insulation exposed, non reflective. - either way you need a hard surface to beakup the floor/ceiling parallel. For the rear it's best to make a straight cloud with no hard surface, just quality insulation to make it a bass trap. cheers john
