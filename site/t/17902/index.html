Room volume and 100% absorption ceiling

Started by G-Sun on 18 October 2012. 3 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 17902.

G-Sun18 October 2012#1
Hi! I'm planning to make a approx. 70m3 one-room studio (that's suggested min. size isn't it?), with 100% absorption in the ceiling. But, then: Is height above the false ceiling counting in room volume? In what way? What should be my guidelines for planning my dimensions for such a design? BTW: My roof will possibly be triangle-shaped with a ridge-beam or similar in centre.
bert Stoltenborg19 October 2012#2
If the ceiling is acoustically transparant, like Rockfon, it counts. And if you place mineral wol in the void between false and real ceiling, (often 30 - 40 cm) you are creating a nice bass trap. The sound can enter the wool and will be absorped. If the ceiling is hard the sound will be reflected and the ceiling is the boundary for sound.
