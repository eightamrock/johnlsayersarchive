knightfly8 March 2005#4
So, you have a double layer of rock with screwed up screws; is this a single frame, single frame with RC, staggered stud frame, steel stud frame, double wood stud frame, double steel stud frame, ?? In most cases, removing the missed screws and filling the holes, not with caulk (not as heavy) but with drywall joint compound ("mud") will be best. This will keep the mass of the wall higher, and not leave slightly lighter weight "plugs" here and there in the wall. I've talked to 4 different contractors in my area about using Resilient Channel; all four claimed the stuff "didn't work" - three, when I asked how they installed it, said they put the RC on the existing wallboard on one side and added another layer of wallboard. Duh... The fourth one did this also, but just nailed through the outer layer of sheet rock and through the RC and into the studs. D-D-D-D-D-DUHHHH... Consider yourself lucky; this was a relatively inexpensive "lesson" about contractors - compare to this if you want to feel better - http://www.recording.org/ftopict-22469-kitchen.html Now, don't you feel lucky??!? :roll:
