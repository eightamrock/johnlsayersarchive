gullfo16 November 2007#2
the tall ceiling height is nice. if you can adapt one of John's control room designs that would be a good first start. I would take advantage of the tall ceiling to add in overhead trapping so you can get as much usable space on the floor - although mix and mastering rooms usually have different configurations. as far as floating, i think the walls sitting on the floating floor will give you more effective isolation as the whole assembly is ensured to be separated and sealed and less likely to have gaps or structure shifts in the case where the floor and walls were floated separately.
