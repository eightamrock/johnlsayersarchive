jwl14 November 2007#2
There is some debate about whether or not the board width and the gaps have to be varied or not. Most times you see that they are, but in Rod Gervais' book he indicates that it isn't necessary. I don't know who is right here, just saying there are different opinions out there. There is a spreadsheet calculator to determine board width and spacing, it's in the stickies in the acoustics forum. Make sure you use the corrected one as there was a typo published in an acoustics book years ago that everyone copied, giving the wrong formula and also the wrong results. As far as board thickness, most people seem to just use standard 1x lumber (ie, 1x4s, 1x3s, 1x6s etc). At least that's how we refer to them in the states....
