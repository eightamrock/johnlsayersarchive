Scott, you need to reverse the side angles - the plan is to have early reflections from walls be re-directed behind you, where they can be absorbed. Check a few of the other drawings here, you'll see what I mean - just think like a pool player - you're shooting from the speaker location, and no matter where you hit the cushion (wall) you DON'T want to hit the guy in the chair (except for a straight shot) Hope that helped...
