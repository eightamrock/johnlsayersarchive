Hello people. i plan to build a studio at the end of my garden. i want to achive good isiolation to keep out train noise which is approx 30 feet away. and not to annoy the neighbours approx 80 feet away. as i will be making rap hip hop rnb music. i love my sub. i have a garage at the end of my garden at present. well a poor excuse for a garage, so down it comes. i hope to achive a big control room, booth and a machine room however i am working slightly back to front. by this i mean i will construct the footings for the walls, the walls and roof of the outer shell as big as possible which is 30 square meters here in the uk to avoid planning permission. then i will plan the studio or control room to fit. i dug the footings by hand on my own a total of 75 feet by 1.5 feet by 3 feet. 3 8 yard skips including the old garage. £ 450. took 2 weeks. made the form work out of ply. cut it to 6 inch strips total £50 i then filled the bottom of the footing 1 foot with crushed hard core and compacted it, as it was clay. which was 5 bulk bags at a total of £200. dug the soak away 1.2 meter cube 6 meters away from the footings took a day. and run the pipe in. then made a footing round the edge. blocked it u with gaps to let storm water out. wraped it with a permable membrain and back filled the out side edge with pea shingle. put a piece of ply on the top and poured a few inch thick with slap with rebar and covered with soil. done. cost £150 concrete time i phoned round a few redy mix companies. however i wanted a pump because i was concerned about the drains in the alley beside the house getting damaged. found a guy who would do 6.1 meters of concrete £530 it was a 25 mix. the pump lorry was an extra £250. cool. so he told me to get a few bags of cement to prime the pump. i told him i would need at least 150 feet of pipe. he said 'thats no problem mate' , i said do i need any thing else. he replies 'no' . so he turns up with the concrete truck. proper nice fella says to me wheres the pump mate. i look at him and say have no idea , he says well he should be here set up already. so i say i was told that it would be between 10am and 2 pm and its 1. so he phoned the pump driver. its alright mate he s only round the corner. so the pump driver turns up. here we go moan moan moan moan moan. why did youbother getting a pump the concrete truck will fit down ther so i asked if he would like to take responceability for the drains 'no mate' well then 'well i cant do it' why 'my boom wont fit over the phone lines' i diddnt ask for you to use the boom i wanted a pipe. 'well i aint got enough pipe' why not at this point he turns to the concrete driver and says is that one of those trucks that mix it on site and the other driver says yes. and he says thats no good for a bloody pump. at which point i lost my temper and told him to go if he could not do it. i called the concrete company who i ordered the concretwith and he said he would talk with the pump driver. then the pump driver started saying well where can i wash out my pump. to which i replyed i diddnt know you had to. i know it might make senseto you guys who have had concrete delivered before but for me this was the first time i had had it delivered and no one told me. then he started moaning about putting the legs down on the truck as it would take up the hole road. at this point im pissed off. guess what he said next well if you want to use the pipe your going to have to help me unload them. what. ive been waiting since 1 pm its now 3pm and all because you couldnt be bothered to get the pipe off the lorry. so me him the other driver and my 2 brothers layed out the pices of pipe and he clamped them together. finally ready to go its nearly 4 now. all goes well in the end takes 35 mins. then we empted out the pipes hosed em through and empted the hopper on the back of the pump about 5 wheel barrow loads . all that moaning for that . any way all done looks good. had to do a bit of cleaning the next day with the jet wash because he left it in a mess , but to tell you the truth i was glad he was gone. next will be the walls which will be 8 by 8 by 16 hollow dense blocks with rebar and some kind of filling. i will post up some pics soon. as for the budget well. i plan to pursue producing as my career im only 23 so all i can earn is invested. after bills that is ha ha. any one who wants to jump in feel free all words appreciated. oh and i have rods book as well. i highly recomend this to anyone considering building or just macking there room sound better. thanks for reading joey

rod gervais17 January 2008#25
[quote="joey"] question 1 will the exchange chamber be big enougth? It will assuming that the unit you install is sized properly. question 2 what size fan should i use to exhaust air from the rooms to the exchange chamber? I would use a multi speed fan and dump about 600 cfm min 900 cfm max into the control room - and would dump 1oo cfm into the iso booth. question 3 would i have to have 2 duct runs to and from each room, giving a total of 4 ducts ? It would be the right way to do it. I would distribute the supply air with 2 grilles over the seating area at the desk and 2 more about 1/2way between there and the back wall - and then do a left and right high return air at the very back opf the room. BTW - one thing missing from your calcs - and yes I did speak of this in the book - and that is you have to get the info for the humidity that the fresh air will being into you room as a part of that cooling load calculation. That is missing right now and can be a big number....... Rod
