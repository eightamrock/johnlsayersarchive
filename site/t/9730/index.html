andrebrito30 November 2007#7
You can make a module from the MSc Audio Acoustics at Salford Univ. in distance learning. They have a module on Room Acoustics (part is small room acoustics, another part is large room acoustics and also absorbers/diffusors). Not sure if the module is different when I did the MSc in Acoustics there. I suppose is the same. You will need some basis on math but actually this is one of the easiest modules in the program. It is a bit theorical as well, but most degrees are. They also have another module Numerical Tehcniques which is related to software prediction (using Odeon and FEM/BEM) but the only part of interest in Odeon and limitations in software prediction in large room acoustics, the assignments regarding FEM/BEM were about loudspakers. If you understand spanish Le Salle Univ. also offers distance learning in acoustics
