Soundman202015 February 2013#4
Is it possible that 2 layers of 5/8" drywall would make the structure rigid enough to not need sway braces?
I wouldn't count on drywall for structural integrity. It's fragile and brittle, and not much good in sheer. Also, if your municipality is saying that you need sway braces, then you need them! Regardless of what someone might tell you on the internet, those are the guys who make the rules, and they do it for a reason: your own safety. So I would go with their recommendations.
Or does it have to be a layer of OSB / plywood to not need sway braces?
OSB or ply will only help with sheer, which are forces along the surface of the wall itself, trying to collapse it sideways. OSB and play don't to much for the other forces acting on the wall, that are trying to topple it over. That's what you need sway braces for. Unfortunately, you don't have a lot of choice here. Talk to some of those manufacturers, take their suggestions to the inspector, and see which one(s) he will approve. - Stuart -
