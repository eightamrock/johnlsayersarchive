Ethan Winer17 September 2008#2
unsound wrote:
1. How much bass trapping do I actually need in a room this size? All 4 corners top to bottom?
As much as possible. More than just the four wall-wall corners. I'd have traps in wall-floor corners too where possible. There are also corners where your walls meet the ceiling.
3. Should I make them all using 4" 705 or should I use a combination of 2" and 4"? 703 and 705?
Four inches thick is best. 703 works well, but 705 is nicer to cut and handle though it's twice as expensive.
4. Should I cover the doors?
Only if they're in corners or at reflection points.
5. How far away from the wall should the desk be?
The only way to know for sure is to measure the room's LF response at high resolution while you move things around. See this: How to set up a room --Ethan

What's above the ceiling tiles? Your best bet is to fill the entire cavity above with fluffy insulation. That will give a lot of bass trapping that costs very little and takes no effort to make look good because it's hidden. --Ethan
