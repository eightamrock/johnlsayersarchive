Very small control room for tracking only?

Started by Wurlitzer on 25 July 2009. 3 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 13311.

Wurlitzer25 July 2009#1
I'm moving house in a month and will be looking to build a new studio room at the end of the garden. I'll probably have up to about 30 square metres internally to play with. I'll want this for a variety of purposes - practising (mostly piano and keys, some drums), teaching, possibly rehearsing, simple small-scale recording. I'm torn between two approaches: 1) Building one single large room in which I can do anything I want in terms of playing, rehearsing, teaching etc - while limited recording possibilities to MIDI and working through headphones to track vocals etc; and 2) Dividing the space into two smaller rooms, standard live room / control room combo, for more usefuless in recording, being able to track drums, vocals etc without having to work through headphones. But I'm considering a third option: What if I had one pretty big room, where I could rehearse pretty much anything I want, and do limited recording using MIDI, electronic drums through phones etc, AND this was my general mixing and editing room, with decent space and monitors. And then I have a very small room just to use as a control room when I want to track live instruments. With literally just a computer to record on, a simple small mixer and monitors. I would then do all the mixing and editing work afterwards in the larger room. I'd be looking at maybe 8-9 m2 for the tracking room, and 20-22 m2 for the live room / mixing room. I suppose I'm modelling this on situations where I've seen live recording engineers and people recording in churches etc, set up in all kinds of not-ideal spaces, and still get decent enough results in tracking. Even though you wouldn't want to use those spaces for making detailed informed decisions in mixing. It would be a compromise, but then so will anything be. I don't do enough recording (outside of MIDI etc) to justify devoting a larger space to control room than the room for playing and teaching in. OTOH I don't want to cut myself off from being able to do it when I need to. Any thoughts?

Bearing in mind I'll have roughly twice as much space to play with as in your design (up to about 30m2), how could it be adapted to open up a wider space? Say the width of the room was about 4.5 metres for example, having a couple of sliding doors in the middle of the partition would still leave a lot of wall either side...
