Very Odd Shaped Room

Started by Fligel on 9 May 2008. 11 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 10594.

Fligel9 May 2008#1
I am looking for some assistance on a layout for an extremely odd shaped room. This room will be used as a mixing/post production room for a large church in Las Vegas. There will be 96 channels coming to this room from the FOH mixer. We have Adam A7 and Adam Sub10 for monitors. Because of the obscurity of the room, I am not sure how best to lay things out. I am hoping to not only have the audio mixing/editing station but also a video editing station as well in this room. We have an acoustics company that is going to build custom panels for us as a donation to the church, so we can get done whatever we need to from an acoustics perspective. This room is directly below the light/video booth in the back of the main sanctuary. Not much sound bleeds out of the main room as it is entirely insulated and treated with acoustic paneling. I am not worried about bleed as we will be recording live what is happening in the main sanctuary and therefore will be identical to what people are hearing live. Of course it is not a perfect situation, but the goal is to be able to mix for recording and for remote broadcasts separate from the FOH mix. Budget is also not much of a factor as we are spending $150,000 on this project in total and I have access to a contractor to do whatever is necessary. This room is also so small that I can't imagine that there are many options. Any ideas would be greatly appreciated. Thanks!!! Eric

Welcome to JLS' RSD! Sorry, but to fit everything you said into such a small an restricted space seems just about impossible. Assuming that you will have a machine room someplace else, the smallest audio board I can think of for 96 lines is a Yamaha DM1000 with additional outboard pres and ADCs. If you place the outboard gear underneath the console, you have still have a 3x3' footprint. Then the video equipment. Is the vocal booth necessary? If not, what about the video in that corner and audio in the east curved area? Andre

i'd probably remove that short wall which defines the vox booth and then use the pointed end to create the booth and the deep part of that point (right hand side) to make a trap for the booth. that should give you enough room to position the desk and treatments and hopefully reduce some of the convex wall effect. make the "back wall" very absorptive.
