Marc, explain to us exactly how and what they installed - some sketches of the air handler unit and ducting would be a big help - in fact - if they have it on cad - email it to me. Pictures would help as well. If it sounds like it's raining - my first guess would be buffetting taking place at the corners of the turns - suggesting square edge corners instead of rounded - and (or) no turning vanes in the corners. This would also suggest that the ducting is undersized and your velocity is greater than it should be for a studio. The other info I want is - what exactly is the velocity of the air at the outlets - what are the supply registers themselves (manufacturer and model numbers) and what is the static pressure on the system. Has the system been balanced (if yes - get a copy of the balancing report for us to review.) Get us all of that info and we should be able to come up with some answers. Rod
