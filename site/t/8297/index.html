The idea of an open floor plan is to give the appearance of spaciousness. In order to keep that open look the colors need to be light. Any light color will do but it depends on the atmosphere you're trying to create. To give warmth to the space use warm colors like yellows, reds, oranges, to make it cool and airy use greens or blues. To bring the space closer together (give the appearance of less the space) use dark colors like eggplant or a deep rich blue. Neutral colors will give it a more outdoor feel.
