Just had some time to kill.. is this design any better then my previous? I did away witht eh double doors entering the vocal booth and replaced it with a single sliding glass. Main reason is I'm not so worried about sound leaking between the two, but moreso from outside my area. Using the center sliding glass allows room for soffit mounted monitors. Again half the time this room will be used for live band practicing, so I didn't want to eat up too much square footage. But if I'm going to put my time, money and effort into building something, I want it to work and be as functional as possible! Thanks -Scott ps. note that the rear wall would have sufficient sound treatment. I didn't want to angle the wall because 1) it would eat more floor space and 2) the existing wall is new, fireproof sheetrocked, and has electric and a switch on it. I'd be adding outlets and lights to their own (separate) electric add on box for the rest of the room, but was hoping I could use what's there for that wall. Thoughts?

giles11716 July 2004#27
Loose the sheetrock wall that angles back inot your mix field. Just keep a straight resonator style wall all the way from your soffit to just past mix position. You dont want to have sound reflecting all around in your mix position. Bryan Giles Just putting this design up as a reference for your style of room. This is actually the design I used to build my room.
