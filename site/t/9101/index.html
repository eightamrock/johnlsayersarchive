gullfo2 August 2007#11
a quick way to verify - use the smallest, largest first, and average depth second. also, to be more effective, you can separate the well sections to isolate each of the octaves covered by the slats. so if your upper and lower range looks to cover 2 octaves, you should consider splitting out the wells on each octave. it will still work without this division but efficiency can be improved. in this case, you could put the close slots (3mm) at the top and bottom and the wide slots (12mm) in the middle and put in a divider where they slots change over. this puts the "air spring" for the lower frequency in 2 upper and lower wells, and the higher freqencies into the middle well. you could also take this further by some further sub-divisions to mix up the slats even more but this is probably close enough.
