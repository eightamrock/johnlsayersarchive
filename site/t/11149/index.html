CptVagabond29 August 2008#3
If you're determined to achieve the control room in that design, within the room you have, I would flip the design vertically and rotate it 90 degrees. This will place the lower door at the same position as the door in the design. Then you would need to basically gut the wall with the window and door. I'm assuming that's a window between your monitors in the design, so you could recycle the one you already have. Then it's just a matter of building the room!
