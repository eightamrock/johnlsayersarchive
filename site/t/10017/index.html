Well after a bunch of revisions I think this is the direction I would like to head. The budget is about $40K and construction won't begin for at least two years but I thought I would get some opinions. The design criteria were that if we sell the house I some of the rooms have to be considered habitable, hence the windows. I also wanted room for a pool table and to park our 4 runner if I felt like it hence the carriage type doors in the live room. Any thoughts, criticisms, etc. would be greatly appreciated.
