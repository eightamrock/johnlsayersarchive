Hi folks - Is denim a good choice of fabric for the inside out wall construction? - jatougas
knightfly29 December 2004#2
Should work OK - you should, however, seriously consider applying a fire retardant chemical to any cloth that isn't already spec'd that way - one place to get this online is at http://www.natfire.com/ Denim, with its relatively tight weave, would probably keep the room just a bit brighter than a more open weave; this is usually a GOOD thing, so no biggie... Steve
AndrewMc30 December 2004#3
Burlap looks really nice. You have to double layer it otherwise you can see thru it - but it's dirt cheap.
Innovations31 December 2004#4
You generally should look for a fabric which is accoustically transparent. Denim in general would impress me as too heavy and tight a weave. Although there are no doubt denim-looking fabrics that are lighter and looser than the stuff used for pants.
