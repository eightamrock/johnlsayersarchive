len-morgan5 October 2006#2
The first bit of advice would be to read and follow the very first post of every forum here. Fill out as many items as you can and make sure to update your profile with your location. I'm afraid you won't get much help here until you do. The second bit of advice would be to get Rod Gervais' book (also in the stickies). Once you've read that a few times, you'll be able to answer a lot of your own questions and be able to see how the information in the book pertains to your particular situation and you'll be able to ask much more specific questions. The last bit, once you figure out what your budget is going to be for the design you want, double it!!! I'm not kidding. If you end up with money left over, it's not really a "loss" is it? :-) Also, make sure you've got your budget money all set before you start. Unfortunately the kind of construction you have to do for a studio don't allow much room for doing half of it now, and the rest later. If your current funds are limited, you would be much better off completing one entire room that you could use, rather than a full-size half completed studio that won't have ANY useable space. Just a thought. len
