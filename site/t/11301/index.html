Ethan Winer14 October 2008#2
Forget room mode prediction tables because they're rarely accurate once the room is built. The proper way to assess modes in a room is to measure with software: ETF, Windows, $150 FuzzMeasure, Mac, $150 Room EQ Wizard, Windows and Mac and Linux, Freeware This article explains how I use ETF, but the principles apply to all such programs. Also, a room that size will probably do better with simple bass traps made from rigid fiberglass rather than tuned Helmholtz traps. That way you absorb all frequencies in all corners. Versus trying to build 75 different trap types. :) --Ethan
