most time, the isocabinets are usually a speaker in a cabinet with a microphone. the cabinets often are heavy to avoid vibration and to reduce the amount of sound escaping. sometimes the walls of the cabinet are lined with sand to increase the mass even more. here's a product to house an existing amp: http://www.vocalbooth.com/products/ampboxes.html check this thread: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=6080
