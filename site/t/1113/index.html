A Lot of us have done the wood parquet flooring. Cheaper then the other wood manufacturers and you get the wood sound. I am sure there is a difference in tone between laminate (PLASTIC) and wood. :) Heck, walk on them and you hear a tonal difference. Now whether it will affect what you hear, only the golden ears can tell you this one...... And personally, I like the sound of wood in a room versus laminates. Bryan Giles
