xSpace24 February 2009#5
What matters, with ceiling framing members, is that the wood should only be spanned according to span charts. A 16 inch layout will allow a longer span of the framing member. Naturally, it is important to the integrity of the building to have maximum support under each member that carries a load. So while a 24 inch layout is recommended for a more flexible wall, everything matters. The size(2X?) of the ceiling joist framing, how far it spans and the load that it will carry.
