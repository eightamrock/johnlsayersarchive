knightfly16 June 2004#4
Celotex won't work as well as rigid fiberglas - when mounting the fiberglass, the ONLY time it would be OK to put a panel on the back is if the fiberglass will be against a wall anyway - otherwise, the further you get the material away from a wall or ceiling, the lower frequency will be absorbed. This means open backs - but perimeter frames are OK - you can cover the fiberglass with cloth and leave the frame showing, or cover the entire thing depending on the look you're after. Just placing 3 or 4" rigid fiberglass by itself across a corner will help any room a lot. For free-standing units, have you looked here? http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm Hope that helped... Steve
