That's also good. Concrete makes a great floor, acoustically.
The control room can be on the smaller side, but it will have to accommodate a console and a two 2" multitrack machines.
You might want to consider putting the multitrack machines in a machine room: they can be a bit noisy and distracting in the control room. Especially if you ever decide to record someone right there in the control room.
And lastly, a great sound! hopefully it will be warm and natural in tone.
If the tone is "warm", then it isn't natural! If it is "natural", then it isn't warm! :) The usual goal is to aim fairly flat response in your room, with fairly constant RT-60 across the entire spectrum, no first reflections at the mix position, excellent symmetry, and a clear, tight, precise sound stage. - Stuart -
