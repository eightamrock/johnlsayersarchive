knightfly6 August 2003#3
Right, mineral wool is the generic term. Rockwool is a trademark, don't remember whose. Similar products are slag wool, made from steel mill byproducts. Here is a PDF file on one type of SAFB's made from mineral wool, but probably not available in NZ. I'm posting it only so you can print it out and show people what you're talking about. http://www.thermafiber.com/PDFs/TF885.pdf In Australia, there's a company called Insulco that has similar products made from compressed fiberglas - the fluffy kind is NOT what you want for best performance though. If you go this route, the Semi-Rigid is the stuff - http://www.insulco.com.au/professional/ ... rial.shtml Maybe John will kick in here with a more local source of actual mineral wool, being he's kind of a "next-door-neighbor"...
