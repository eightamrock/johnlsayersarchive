Hi, I built several broadband absorbers. The holes have 8mm diameter, 14mm from center to center, 25,6% of the panel are holes. The panel has 4mm thickness. I calculated a range from ca. 312 HZ to 1.5kHz cause of the increasing modul depth. My question: Should I place the basotect (or glaswool) right behind the frontpanel or should I mount it on the backpanel, so theres some space between the holes and the absorbing material? John has in his reference material some air gap in the slat-absorbers.

Soundman202012 February 2010#2
I may be wrong here, but as far as I know you should put the wool at the back of the box, or at least away from the front panel. IIRC, if the wool is up against the front panel, then it interferes with the motion of the air plugs in the holes, basically destroying the resonance characteristics. But like I say, I may be wrong about that. Hopefully one of the experts can comment. - Stuart -
