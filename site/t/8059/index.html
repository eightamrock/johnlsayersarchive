orangenumerik26 January 2007#2
Pic 2 shows one idea, but I am concerned that there isnt enough contact between joist and frame top plate
i'd go with #2. Ceiling joist do not need to rest on the full width of the top plate, there is a calculation that exist (wich i don't know :? ) that will tell you according to the load and span of the ceiling joists what would be the minimal support lenght needed. But resting your ceiling joist on, say 2 1/2 " of the top plate then use the 1" left to "rest" the firestop is a safe bet IMHO, but nevertheless verifying with a structural engineer would be your best option. #3 is a no go as far as i'm concern.
