knightfly21 August 2006#2
First concern would be symmetry; if your speakers will be soffited in those front angles, tape it out on the floor (the whole floor plan) then tape a line perpendicular to each speaker center and extending til they meet, then add lines + and - 15 degrees from each of those; where they form a diamond shape, that's your approximate "sweet spot" - continue the line from your right speaker over to where the sliding glass door goes, measure that angle and "reflect" it off the glass. If it misses the "sweet spot", then you could use slat absorbers on the opposing side. If not, you may wanna build some portable absorbers to park in front of the glass (and the opposite side) while mixing, so your stereo isn't phased wierd due to early reflections... Steve

Antho29 August 2006#7
I found out yesterday that the fact that I have pot lights in my space will negate 80% of the benifit of RC or any similar decoupling system. I knew from the start that potlights where a less then desirable solution in a true soundproofing application but assumed that since my space was only for mix and post work, that the transfer of sound would be lower to start with and I could live with a less then "sound proof" space. Also, I tend to think that most of the lighting that I see guys putting in studios (ugly 80's track lighting, etc.) looks really bad. Basically, I liked the look of reccesed lights enough to sacrifice a bit of sound isolation. In the end I will be using a simple neoprene tape ("green tape" from super sound proofing) on all of the points where drywall makes contact with the structure. It is a minimal improvement, but will be about the same as spending a lot on a more expensive system since I have big holes in my ceiling! If I was building a recording space, I wouldn't accept this, but I feel confident that this set up will serve well for my application. I have finished the ceiling insulation and 80% of the walls and will be posting pics later today. Also, I plan on building the hanging 703 units today so I'll get some pics up of those as well.
