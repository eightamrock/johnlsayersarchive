First, you should establish your research topic. Then you search for source and information. Write down main points on a paper and then assemble the related research under each. Prepare an outline of the main points. Then arrange them in a specific order. Then prepare the first draft. After that use footnotes or endnotes to document source then prepare a bibliography. Completing bibliography revise the first draft and proofread the final draft. And you can place an order for SPAM at I-AM-A-SPAMMER.COM .
