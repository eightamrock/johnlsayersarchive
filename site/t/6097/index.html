SonicClang26 April 2006#2
If you seal it, make sure you seal the bottom, where a fire would normally go. Don't just go up on the roof and seal the top... that'd be very bad. Someone buys the house after you and starts up a nice cozy fire on a cold night, next thing you know the whole house has burned down. :) So don't do that. You can't keep the fireplace open as it is. You might as well just cut a big hole in your studio, because that's what it is.
