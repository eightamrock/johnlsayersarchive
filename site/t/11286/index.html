Howdy Calimero welcome to the JLS forum!
My questions are the following : - Should I reverse the room ?
Yes, or you'll have sound from monitors reflecting back earlier 'cuz of the splayed walls TOWARDS your RFZ.
- Should I treat my control room as LEDE ? (I don't know if it's possible because of the length of the room 14.07ft) - if yes, can you help me to place my absorbers/basstraps (Dead end) and diffusors/bass traps (Live end)
No, that's something to consider in a tracking/recording room.
- Should I put a wooden floor instead of complete carpet ?
You've got my vote :) If so, make a "soft" ceiling. Wood floor is okay. Carpet can suck out all the "highs" in your room. With wood you have more control (and adjust reflections at your ceiling using absorbers)
- and should I use a cloud ceiling on the all ceiling surface ?
not on all but atleast above your mixing place. Use cloud and wall absorbers on first reflection positions (use the "mirror" trick) to create an RFZ.

Yes, that's considered a triple-leaf and there for "wrong". Looks like you want your monitors placed WITHING the inner layer of the room.. something that seems like a trend these days. I've seen more topics about that recently. I advise not to go that route. First add a wall in your existing room covering all walls (and ceiling). Than ADD a soffit "wall" which isn't a wall with mass. (huh?). Consider M-A-M. Mass-Air-Mass. That's the best way to build. hmm, did I make myself clear? darn.... :oops:
