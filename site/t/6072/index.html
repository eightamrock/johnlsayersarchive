Hi: My brother is giving me rolls of lead sheeting (I think it's wide, like 3 or 4 feet, and is 1/32 inch thick; like 25 or 50 ft rolls). I was wondering if applying the lead sheeting around the (galvanized steel) heating ducts in my basement would be a viable option? I realize that I would likely have to provide more support to keep the ducts suspended safely. 2 ducts run between the joists, and a main duct runs perpendicular along the center beam. Also, would using lead sheeting in-between layers of sheetrock on the ceiling be a good option.... or? (Again, I realize the lead is like 2 lbs pre square foot, and needs to be figured in for its support). Which layers or leaf of a wall would be best to have lead sheeting? Just looking for a good use for this free lead. thanx, K
