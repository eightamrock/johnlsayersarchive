teekman77725 August 2009#2
Hi, I newly joined but have been hanging around for quite some time doing a lot of studying. I am actually a general contractor so I can give you some insite regarding your question...is $8k enough? In my opinion it isn't. If you plan on buying a shed for $5k you are left with around $3k. Then you have the issue of sound proofing and treatment. Depending on what kind of STL (sound transmission loss) you are looking for you may be able to cut corners but not much. You will have to reinforce the floor of the shed, most of those sheds are built using 2x4's for the floor. You will have to elevate it from the ground due to water and moisture, and to properly do that without any danger you will need footings dug and poured and then properly fasten the shed to the footings. I guess you could do a turned down slab but that is more concrete and labor. By the time you do all of that, you will have no money left. You are in SC I think you said. Don't you have a basement or garage you could use? You should really read some of the other threads to get an idea of what it takes to isolate a room from the outside. If you are far enough from neighbors then you may get away with heavy insulation and double layers of sheetrock thoroughly calked. You may have to go with an elevated slab to better isolate as well. It is do-able but I think you may need more money. All the best, Teek

Soundman202026 August 2009#6
How is the garage built? (what materials). Concrete or brick walls would be great! Typical garage construction, not so much. And it's not just the "other 3" walls that you need to worry about: it is the other FOUR walls, #4 being the ceiling. Plus any doors, windows, vents, or other orifices. Those are all weak points. In order to isolate effectively, you need to have a total airtight seal around you. Even a small hole, just a half inch in diameter, will destroy your isolation. So if you can seal up the garage well, and put plenty of mass-air-mass around you, then you'll be fine. Having said that, a garage sounds like a MUCH better idea then an HD shed... A lot of what you need is already there. You just need to add some more. - Stuart -
