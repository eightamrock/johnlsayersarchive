Overview Hey all. Sean (Gizmo) here from Tampa, FL in the USofA. Recently purchased a house, and the wifey has given me the go ahead to build out a studio how I see fit in the front area. I’m basically a tech head turned gearslut and have gone back to school for audio (actually in studio design 1 at the moment). Basically rebooting my life slowly. Been a minor DJ a while, as well had the opportunities to record in some cool acts in local studios helping them get some songs tracked out. My MAIN goal is to have a mixing space with the option to record Vocals. My budget for this project is capped at about $5,000 (a lil flexible here). This will have to include all the framing, drywall, insulation, and mounting materials. Gear will be a entirely different budget (later down the road) I’ve got my father in law (who is surprisingly very supported of my endeavors) who will be the main contractor lined up (he's been a builder for the past 30+ years). What I don’t have is a high level of confidence in a design that can be used to get rolling on construction. My Goals A nice home studio at my residence that is great for recording and mixing for music as well for film (post). I MAY get into surround mixing at a later date. Loudness – Probably not very loud. This is a single family home which is detached for other houses. My goal is to keep the sound/music in the room where it belongs if at all possible. When I measured dB in my current room, I averaged about 75 to 85dB in the room. Speaking of neighbors – I live in Tampa, FL where all the neighborhoods are crammed for the most part. My nearest neighbor to the closest to my house is about 15 feet away from my outside wall, however I believe it’s the side where their garage is. My Limitations I tried to use Sketchup, (attached my attempts) but I it's a high learning curve to bust out a lot of things. Very slow process for me. I’m attaching the drawings of what I have to start with. Current Dimensions Height - 9 feet 2 inches from carpet to ceiling. Length - 21 feet 5 inches from end to end. Width - Max Allowable (due to walkway areas) is 10 feet 6 inches. I may want to use wood floor laminate for cosmetic reasons, otherwise I can stick with carpet if needed for acoustical purposes. My father-in-law will also be my electrician for the most part as well. He'll be running all the sockets in the room/area into a separate breaker in the fuse box, that way if I suck too much juice (heaven forbid) I won't have the wifey coming in cussing a storm because the bedroom lights went out. I'd like to PREVENT the standard ground loop hum is possible as well (so any suggestions on that would be greatly appreciated) I believe the area already has A/C piped so I don't THINK I'll need/want much modification in this area. The A/C compressor is on the other side of the house and the Air mover is in the garage (away from the room) Finally, I hope I’ve followed most of the guidelines for posting. I read them several times. There is some amazing resources here that have helped me immensely thus far. Thanks for doing what you do. Thanks again, Gizmo Tampa, FL USA
