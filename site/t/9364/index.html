Eric_Desart13 September 2007#13
Spyrow, First I apologize that I haven't finished the new version yet. This angle of incidence is a very controversial topic on the net, since I studied the influence of this angle of incidence AND did measure this effect in real live circumstances, while you will find NO (AFAIK and understood from others) American books describing this phenomenon. Basically the traditional formulas start from the assumption of a PLANE wave at straight incidence (in fact a very simplified lump mass-spring system excited in the axis of the system). If you should see some Univ courses you will notice that they DO distinguish here. This 20 to 25° that I entered there (but which is adjustable) is for me (and for now) an empirical representation of random incidence. If you should see that angle in some courses you will notice that this angle is larger than what I use. I was busy also checking a relative recent Dutch paper from the Belgian scientific institute for the building industry (WTCB) which uses yet another constant for random incidence (and I know they do a lot of study and have their own accredited measurement rooms). But I still haven't checked how that this relates in the context of my other data. Hope to do this shortly. Also the amount of filling and it's gas-flow resistance will influence this MSM, which as (if recalling correct now - no time to investigate) should mean a difference of a +/- factor 1.4 between filled and empty (but without exact data in function of gas-flow resistance and very clearly not a linear phenomenon, and as suggested related to speed of sound in its turn related to this 1.4 factor = ratio specific heat of gas at constant pressure to the specific heat at constant volume). If I should find enough statistical data it should be easier, even without agreeing on the exact physics involved, to incorporate regression algorithms which I did for personal use already, but not supported by enough data to distribute such stuff. My belly/instinct is still a very important source for me. Basically I find differences between sources, which doesn't make it easy for me. Hence I made it adjustable hoping once to find more exact data. For instance: Basically my new version in Metric does work mathematically. I entered an additional relationship, for which I'm rather sure now the theoretical math is correct. It's fun to play with (at least for me that is). Still I get results where my belly wonders: "Is this possible, did I overlooked something, if so where?".
