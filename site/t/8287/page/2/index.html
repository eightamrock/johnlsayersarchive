Jamie M10 December 2007#33
Cheers guys, I really appreciate your comments. The final budget? Well, I spent about £25,000 in all, but that included everything - including gear. I had two mics and a Hammond organ when I started the build in Jan of this year, so I had to fully equip the studio, pretty much from scratch. Roughly speaking it was £10,000 on gear (not including one on-going lease for the Pro Tools) and £15,000 on the building and fixtures and fittings. I think the end result has certainly been worth the money spent, and I've had a comment from a very experienced studio chap who said he thought I'd spent twice as much, so I'm very pleased. But the whole thing couldn't have been done without massive help from my Dad (who moved back to England for four months to help me build it), my wife (who, despite being pregnant at the time, was prepared to almost single handedly paint the place) and loads of friends who helped out with favours. I feel very lucky right now, and I'm now working like a dog to prove to everyone that all their efforts were worth it! All the best, Jamie.
