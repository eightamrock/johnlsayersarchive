Once again it's time to ask for the advice of fellow forum users that have more experience than we do in studio design and construction. John Sayers and many others out there have been extremely generous in providing us with guidance, and as complete novices we are truely indebted to all of you for your patience and superb advice. We have finally found and acquired a reasonably acceptable commercially zoned space in which to locate our facility. This space was a former demo studio in the heart of the city, which was already outfitted with a floating wooden floor in the live room. Not all of the work done by the previous studio is up to our desired STC/TL standards and building codes, so it will require some HVAC and electrical reworking by qualified contractors, but I guess that is to be expected in any rebuid!. Attached are floorplans of the exisiting space, and our rough draft of what we are considering doing to the space. The walls we hope to remove are seemingly not load bearing. The 11' high non-spalyed wood and gyprock ceiling was already dropped 18" using T bars with acoustical tiles and the space directly above the tiles has been layered with 4" of thermal OC fiberglass (should this be replaced with 2" OC 703 or 3" Roxul AFB??) Since the original CR was poorly designed and was way too small, we suspect that the best way to proceed is to leave the live room basically alone (other than adding some acoustic treatments and beefing up the CR to Live Room single stud wall by replacing the R13 insulation with 3" Roxul AFB and adding additional mass via another gyprock layer to each side). We also hope to add a soundlock area that can double as an ISO booth if needed. At this point, we hope to demolish the vast majority of the existing partition walls (other than the CR to Live Room wall) and replace them with double framed steel studded walls with serious TL and STC levels as per NRC specifications. We intend to splay the inner walls by 6% each and use these inner walls to enclose slot resonators and absorbers as per designs by Mr. Sayers and Mr. Wiener. I was hoping to have the opinions of some of you much more experienced studio builders and designers as to how best to utilize the space at hand and if our CR and soundlock designs can be improved upon. As usual, all assistance and suggestons would be greatly appreciated... PS- Would there be any benefit at all to rehanging the suspended acoustic tile ceiling so it then has a 12% splay upwards towards the rear of the CR??? I know if it were a gyprock ceiling on RC this would be suggested, but as just T bars with acoustic tiles and insulation is this suspended splay going to be considered as a non paralell ceiling???? Ron MONTREAL[/code]
