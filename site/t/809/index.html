Hey Aaron, If you press "Alt Ctrl" and hold down while clicking the "Try it Free" dialogue -- you get a free 30 day extension -- Smart draw folks told me. Might get you through.
John, on this drawing, any suggestion on the equipment room (power amps, power supplies, computers, etc.) The only other thing on this design, it blocks the small windows on the side wall
I wont speak for John, but I assume you will have some sort of computer/tape machines. IMO get them out of the control room. In particular, I hate computer noise. Looks like you might have room on the South west side of the vocal booth? As far as blocking the small windows, well they are at least small. :lol: Also, I suggest Philip Newells book "project studios - a more professional approach" for lots of common pitfalls before you build. LIKE don't forget to properly deal with your mains and grounding etc. (This even effects computers) You may know this stuff already, but I didn't and I'm throwing it your way. Also Jeff Cooper's book "building a recording studio" has some useful stuff. Best, Dave
