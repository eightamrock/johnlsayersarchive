Jai19 February 2004#79
hey all, Just my 2 cents. But not having a couch in the back of the room is a big big big problem. In fifteen years of engineering in small, medium, and large rooms, with varying degree of poor, good, and excellent acoustics, with poor to excellent gear, etc. etc. basically working in every environment out there, The NUMBER ONE problem and distraction is having to pull chairs in from the lounge to seat people. Because when you do that there is only one place to put them..... Next to you at the console. around you, behind you, in front of you. Basically they are in your space. If you plan on doing any commercial sessions, and not just recording yourself on keyboard or guitar you will have this problem. Then, if you do not have enough space you get people leaning on your walls. if they lean on a 703/fabric wall, now you have an indent in that wall that you have to restretch. i might be off here, I just looked over the 3d drawing and the 2d from before and still do not see where you would seat people without a couch??? :shock: Just me weebit lil' 2 cents :D jai www.themixstudio.com
