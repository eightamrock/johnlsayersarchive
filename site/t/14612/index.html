Hello crew! After FINALLY getting a place for our studio we have alredy completed the other side of our studio and now it's time to finish the build. I've been drawing for a long time and have one design that really feels good. I just need your expert oppinions on the final design. Heres a little background... We found a place for our studio last winter at the basement of a commercial building located at an idustrial area of Helsinki. It's a war time shelter complitely under ground with thick concrete walls(30-50cm thick) and three rooms. We built the first room last winter while the other side was used as a storage space so we basically had one room to track and mix in. I designed the room as control room, and it sounds very nice. Now we have emptied the other side and are ready to start building. But along the way I decided that the room we were now using would be the tracking room and the CR would be built on the empty space. So now I've been drawing a new control room and a small booth. The reason I wanted to build a new CR is because there's more room for a (full) band in the room that's already built and the other side is big enough for a small CR. About the drawings: There's a "chicken shack" closet far left next to the CR and booth occupying about 5-6m2 of the space pretty much rendering it useless. It houses a war time AC unit and some other stuff. We'll have wool and sheet rock covering it. An AC pipe runs through the whole space(you can see it at the bottom of the drawing) from the "chicken shack" to the studio. I will cut this and run all the cables inside of it. There's also a concrete pole you can see on the bottom picture that restricts the design a little. I'm sure I left some important info out but I'm in a hurry just now and I appologize for that. So please ask, comment, suggest or whatever as long as you do. I've never seen a CR shaped like this one so just wanted some reassurance... Thanks -Tomi-

jhbrandt2 August 2010#9
Tomi, For a substantial improvement in isolation you will need to decouple the walls and ceiling in the new TR. Some isolation products similar to these http://www.mason-industries.com/masonind/private/hanger/hanger_main.cfm are needed. You will need to decouple the radiator pipes where they enter and exit the structure. Testing should be done to identify specific problems areas, ie; flanking paths and ceiling transmission - even if it is as unscientific as your control room tests. ;) - use a contact mic on the pipes upstairs while playing drums, contact mic on floor & then walls... simultaneous recording with test mic in TR and test mic upstairs in target room... will all help to identify exactly what needs to be addressed. Cheers, John
