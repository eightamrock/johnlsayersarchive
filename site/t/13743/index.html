Hello Myraid. Back to square one huh? Man, do I understand. And people wonder why it takes so long to build personal studios in personal spaces. Its not like planning a commercial space with a big budget is it? I'd like to offer my help, but there is ONE isuue from the getgo that I would address first and formost. And thats the garage door. Not that there aren't others, but that one alone is a biggie. Addressing it after the fact is always a nightmare for most folks. Heres why. First, I fully understand the "resale" thing, which is always the rule that governs everything. So right off the bat, you have to address the design to reflect the ability to "demo" the studio in order to bring the space back to its intended space...ie...a garage. In that respect, I see a few options to look at prior to building the actual house.
I just need to know what to tell my builder so I can get a quote and get the building of my house started!
From this I gather you are STILL in the planning stage which is really good. Many things can be addressed from the start, that most people have to deal with after the fact. Like HVAC, the slab/footings, the house/garage partition wall/ roof-ceiling framing and sheithing choices, and doors/windows--the big garage door especially. From the outset, it would appear, that the mere fact that you are a "rocker", says a ton. Personally, I would address the isolation issue as if your WIFES future happiness depended on it. :mrgreen: Not to mention your own. You have the opportunity to design this from the get-go to pre consider all the elements it takes to properly isolate this space. However, there is a ton of info I would need in order to offer my help correctly. But just for the sake of illustrating my point, here is the "usual" solution for AFTER THE FACT isolation most people would have to do...in order to keep their home appearance intact, while still giving a modicum of isolation. Personally, given the fact that you do have the opportunity to consider alternatives, I'd approach this differently. But we'll talk about that later. Here is what I mean. If it did turn out this is what you have to do, at least now you can plan on it from the get go...meaning you can have the builders frame this during construction as a REMOVABLE element. :mrgreen: Not only that, but they can plan the framing at the ceiling to interface with it. Anyway, heres what I mean. BTW, this is TWO DOUBLE GARAGE DOORS. Remember, this is only an example.
