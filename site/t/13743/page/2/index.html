Cold19 November 2009#49
Hi again, I understand your frustration with all of this. Stop, take a deep breath, and figure out what you truly need. I live about 100 ft off of a major hwy. It would drive me nuts having traffic noise in all of my recordings. I also don't like having to watch the clock when playing at night so I don't disturb the neighbours. So I'm building as isolated a building as I can afford to build. If you don't have any inclination of ever recording a whole band at once, or even playing music with a whole band late into the night without worrying about the neighbours, then don't bother going whole hog on the isolation front. This will limit the use of your building though, and adding more isolation after the fact is painful and expensive. Build it the best you can afford....... Regards, Steve
