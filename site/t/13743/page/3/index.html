xSpace23 January 2010#56
A couple of things. I would take away from the entry area and add it to the vocal booth. The booth as it is diagrammed is square and will produce a bad sound for acoustic instruments. I would also strongly consider fully decoupled walls rather then staggered stud. You are going to have a lot going on in this recording room so isolation is better to have then to cut corners on. If I were constructing this it would be easier to build the individual walls, for me, then it is to make a staggered stud type assembly. Not only does a staggered stud couple the walls but your ceiling can flank as well from either side of the room(s). Pitch that to the guys and see how they think about it in terms of cost to build.
