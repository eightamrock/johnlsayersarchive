Soundman202019 October 2011#3
Hi there "vegas4ever". Welcome! I guess your post fell through the cracks, so to speak. Thanks for bumping it! You seem to have a reasonable sized space, but your goals and specs are rather vague, to say the least! Maybe that's why nobody even tried to come up with something. The other issue is that this forum is more of a "self-help" place, where folks learn the basics of studio design themselves, post their attempts at designing something, and ask for suggestions on how to improve it. This isn't really the place for saying: "I have this empty room: design my studio for me, for free!" Some of the members here make a living out of designing studios, and while they are totally willing to help someone who is trying to learn, and making a valiant attempt to do it themselves, they aren't too keen on handing out freebies for folks who can't (or don't want to) do at least some of the work themselves. There's nothing wrong with not wanting to get directly involved in the design! Some folks need to build fast, and don't have enough time to learn all that needs to be learned (and there is a LOT that needs to be learned!). Others don't have the skills or background knowledge needed. Others just don't want to, for whatever reason. And all of that is fine! If that's the case, for people who just can't do any of it themselves, it is best to just contact John directly, and ask him for a quote for designing your studio. If John is too busy, or can't take on your job for whatever reason, then contact one of the others by PM, and ask for a quote. But it's unlikely that someone is going to do the whole design for free! On the other hand, if you do decide to do most of it yourself, then you'll find that the folks here are more than willing to help out with suggestions, comments and information. There are some very helpful, very knowledgeable people around here! So if you want to take that path, then add some basic layout ideas to your SketchUp model, and see what kinds of comments you get. But you'll also need to provide a LOT more information on your goals, plans, needs, budget, restrictions, and the available space! There really isn't enough to go on, with what you posted about it so far. - Stuart -

gullfo1 November 2011#6
the sketch i provided assumed 2 rooms pretty well isolated from each other and the other spaces. if you wanted to make one a live room for drum recording it would be feasible and certainly more isolation than gobos. from a cost perspective - 2x4 and drywall, R19 insulation, a small pair of windows between rooms if desired, and 2 exterior grade doors w/ seals. room treatments would be 2'x4' absorbers - span corners, first reflection points, scattered on ceiling and walls. additional absorption later.
