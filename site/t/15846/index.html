Soundman202015 April 2011#2
Hi "psiligoose" (love the handle! :)) and welcome!!! A couple of comments about that layout: Firstly, your inner-leaf walls are splayed the wrong way: the room should be narrower at the front, and wider at the back. You should probably fix that. If you are going for an RFZ design then you'll need to splay each wall at least 6 degrees, and most likely more than that, but if you are only after dealing with flutter echo, then 6° is enough. Or you could also go with straight walls (not splayed). Either way will work. Next, that "corridor through several rooms from CR to LR" does not look like a practical solution to me. That will get tired real quick, as you have to open and close multiple doors to get from one room to the other. I'd re-think that approach. Next: I don't see much room for bass trapping in the vertical corners on that design...
I have no large monitors to soffit mount but would not like to rule out a future that includes them.
What monitors are you planning to use in there? If you are thinking of soffit mounting in the future, then you might as well do it now with whatever you have, and use Barefoot's design to make it easy to swap them out later. Trying to retrofit soffits a year or two down the line is going to be a major PITA, but if you do it now it's just a bit of extra work. Plus the benefits ate well worth it, many times over. IMHO.
There will be a window into the live room at the front of the room which is 6’L x 3’H. should I angle that window?
Yes no maybe. ! :) It all depends: If you angle the glass then that means you reduce the isolation between rooms, since you'll have a narrower gap in one place. On the other hand, if you don't angle the glass then you might end up with annoying glare from your lights, reflected in the glass. Careful planning of your lighting might help with that. Or you could angle it anyway and just use thicker glass to compensate. Yes no maybe.... :)
My thoughts are to build a floating room within the CR
What do you mean by "floating room"? Are you talking about floating your floor and the entire room on top of it? If so, that's a major expense: do you really need that? Or are you just talking about normal 2-leaf decoupled MSM construction?
I am thinking some kind of false ceiling with fabric covered 703 rather than making the ceiling the full 11’. How high should I make it based on the dimensions I have?
I would make it as high as you possibly can, and build inside out if you want to go with the 703. More height is practically always good, acoustically. If you have 11 feet up there, then make the most of it!
Should it be flat or have a slope?
Yes no maybe.... :) Once again, it depends on what you are trying to achieve, and also on personal preference. For RFZ and/or flutter echo control, then yes, tilt it. But if you are not going the RFZ route, your flutter is already taken care of with the 703 up there, so in that case you could leave it flat.... - Stuart -
