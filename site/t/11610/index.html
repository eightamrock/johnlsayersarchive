eun31921 January 2009#2
We've also been considering buying an old stone outbuilding for a studio in Spain, so have been doing some reading about the pros and cons of having a stone wall. I wouldn't necessarily say that it is "very undesirable", as this depends. For the control room part of it, you generally would want the room to sound as neutral as possible, and there is a lot of help in these forums for that - if you search for Wes Lachot's control room design and read up about reflection free zones/the problems inherent in any room, that might be a useful start. As for the live room side of things, having a stone wall would be not a problem at all, as far as I'm aware; rather you might find yourself with a room that gives you special character you might otherwise not achieve. On the whole, you would still want to eliminate general room problems such as flutter echo. Unless you're aiming for a completely dead or really neutral space to record in, I would keep the beautiful wall, at least in the live room!
