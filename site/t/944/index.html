knightfly7 November 2003#2
Ryan, I've not done any serious construction since hooking up with John, and he's the "hanger man" - however, I thought I saw comments from him to the effect that softer, "homasote"/Celotex type material was used for hangers, wrapped in soft fiberglas batts. That would make sense, because the function is for the hanger to add inertia to the trap as well as absorb what it can. The softer board would absorb lows, where the MDF wouldn't as much. As far as size vs. quantity, whichever way gives the most surface area... Steve

knightfly10 November 2003#4
You definitely want it absorptive to treat reflections off the rear of the console - if there's not room for hangers, then probably a couple layers of 703 separated by a few inches, one even with the front and the second separated by maybe 6", mounted parallel to the front. The cloth is a given, due to the nature of the fiberglas... Steve
