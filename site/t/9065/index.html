The owner of the place plans to start working on fixing it up starting tomorrow, respectively, before we can officially move in. I wasn't sure if it would be a good idea to wait or show him what we're planning to do thus a design of the structure or idea etc. The place is completely isolated from other homes (many homes in the area aren't) and its only one floor so we don't have to worry about people upstairs complaining about the bass. I was thinking of making the room in the middle (largest) the vocal/drum booth. We have a lot of both large and thin board fiberglass Insulation (in the vicinity of 4'x1.5'x1.5' (LXWXH) and 3'x2'x2" inches) which I may recommend we put the large ones on top of the ceiling. Thanks again, Edit: 1. there is also a radiator by the other window, like that of the window near the door, in the booth room 2. We also have 2 3'x3' (maybe slighter bigger on one side) sound proof windows for the booth.

gullfo4 July 2007#2
you cannot really make assumptions about the noise levels, so you should probably invest in a sound meter and find out how much is getting in, and using a sound system or live drummer/bassist to find out how much is getting out. then you can determine what you're isolation needs are. given the house is not attached is a good sign. I'd let the owner clean the place up but you should also be upfront about the purpose of the house and what you're thinking of doing if you're not the owner - if you're renting most owners won't want permanent changes or other features which could impact the safety of the occupants, risk their insurance coverage, or reduce the value of the property. but maybe something like this works?
