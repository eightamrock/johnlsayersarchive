Seriously -- you want to plan at least couple of weeks of just absorbing as much information as possible on this (and other) forums before you put your first two pieces of lumber together. Really. Back in January, I thought I was only a couple of weeks away when I started my thread here, but I didn't pull my permits until August. It takes time to plan everything. gullfo18 November 2005#17
the other option for basement studios (like I'm planning on building after I spend a few more months reading up here and everywhere else... :-) is to have proper "egress" windows. most homes have "wood" staircases so having at least one or more windows sized to allow people to escape (generally 24" high x 32" wide and < 60" off the floor) will be a good idea... in my case, this means digging down and replacing 2 windows and adding 2 more on the front of the house. another benefit - light and air... just watch the drainage...
