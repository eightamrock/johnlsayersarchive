Finish Doorway Between Isolated Room and Outer Room

Started by abnc on 16 November 2006. 5 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 7515.

abnc16 November 2006#1
I have constructed an isolated room within a room using double-wall constuction. I am coming to the point to finish the doorway area. I would appreciate some help on how to finish out the doorway so it will look right cosmetically but not provide a mechanical link to the outer room. The doorway is a 2.8 standard opening, and the air space between the inner room and the outer room is 12". How should I proceed to give the doorway a finished looked without mechanically connected my inner room doorway to the outer room doorway? Thanks for any help and suggestions. A. Brock

I'm pretty sure Rod has this detailed in his book. If you don't already have it, it's probably the best $25 investment you could make for your studio. I beleive (off the top of my head) that you fill the gap with rigid insulation and cover with fabric; but I'm nowhere near that stage and definitely no expert, so you might want to wait for either a) the book, or b) other's opinions. Good Luck! Andy.
cyeazel16 November 2006#3
In Rod's book, he reccomends the "super door" which is actually firmly connected to both frames. He goes on to explain that this is the one area where he feels that "bridging" the frames isn't a big issue because of how heavy the door is. Check out his book. There are also drawings on this forum that may help. Just do a search on doors or whatever.
