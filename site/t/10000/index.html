SZ, I think you're getting hospital grade receptacles confused with wire - there is no such thing as hospital grade wire. Romex wire is a perfectly good cable for use in any construction conditions that permit it;s use. So there isn't a whole lot of reasons to consider other tan that unless you have an abundance of conduit available for your use for free or little cost - at which time one could argue that running individual srtands (THXX) might make sense if installing a star ground system.
