The only way to be certain of any of that, is to do the HVAC calculations for your room. First, you need to figure out how much air flow you need (how many cubic feet per minute) to meet the "room changes per hour" criteria, then you need to figure out if the existing system has enough capacity to supply that. After that, you need to figure out your sensible heat load and your latent heat load, to figure out if your existing HVAC system can handle that, or if you will need to install extra heating/cooling capacity. Then you need to figure out the duct sizes that will be needed, taking into account the maximum air flow velocity that is recommended for studios, and finally you need to calculate the static pressure of that entire system to see if the existing fans are able to handle that load, and if not then you'll need to install booster fans that can handle that. It's a bit more complex than it looks at first glance... - Stuart -
