xSpace14 May 2011#2
Like you already stated, more mass is important. I do not have the time to look at the data sheet but what could the non fire rated possibly have that would make it more suited to better isolation? So use the fire rated type. "Is there no reason at all to go for the soundscreen plus product," What is the soundscreen plus product? Typical pink fluffy insulation is recommended so use it :)
gullfo14 May 2011#3
I agree with Brien, use the 16mm type x fire code drywall. Looking over their site there isn't much info on any special formulations and some generally bad ideas like directly using furring channel to attach the drywall... I do like their perforated drywall though, that looks like a neat product in certain cases.
