John Sayers27 June 2006#9
Yes, it's imporant that you group everything you make. then you can move it around, rotate it etc. Plus you can put each group on a layer. Layers are all accessed through Window/Entity Info - just assign a group to a layer. You can also open the Window/Layers and select a layer as being current and all objects you add will be on that layer. Sketchup has a whole series of tutorial videos on their site - they are worth checking out.

Heading way OT... What I still find odd is not having to manage vertices and faces. That seems to be continuously re-calculated by the engine. I used to map stuff out on paper first (like a human face) before I'd even bother trying to build in 3D, as each triangular face is 'built' through one process or another. Of course that kind of detail isn't needed for this type of modelling, so it's strongest point is the rapid development style, and simple to use tools to make it happen. It's virtually the same thing as an 'object' after you've grouped it, but it's a really substantial change to the workflow I got used to for several years.
