lucaslz3 February 2011#2
hello Rom and welcome, I am no expert either and have recently joined this forum concerning a practice room that i am building in a basement, however if you spend some more time reading other threads and the basics of design and construction that you can find here, then you will learn a great deal. The dimensions of your garage shed are not so good for acoustics (no one length should be a multiple of any other) so 9 x 3 are not the best dimensions. You could of course divide the room up, but 3 meters is not a lot of space to move around in, especially after building a room in room, or beefing the walls up. Also the height of the room is extremely limiting! So if I were you I would consider raising the roof and then adding on another room preferably on one of the long sides. Keep digging around here in the forum and give it some careful consideration . just my 2 cents... Lucas
