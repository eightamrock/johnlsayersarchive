Ro24 April 2008#28
The thing is, you need mass-spring-mass. (spring can be air, wool etc) bigger (1 leaf!) mass is better isolation (less transmition of sound) Connecting the 2 brickwalls to make 1 big mass wall is good. You can either build the wall 2 bricks wide or poor sand/cement/concrete in the airspace to make en even bigger, heavier wall. Why would you make 2 brick walls? Is the interior wall gonna be part of a room-in-room construction? If so, don't fill the airspace and just render the wall with cement. easy. You don't really need GB, you've already got 2 walls this way. And those are two heavy ones too!
