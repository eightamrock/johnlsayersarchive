That's a 3-leaf wall ye building. Either lose the airgap and build a more solid (claybrick) wall or lose the wool. With a double builded brickwall there's gonna be good isolation if it's build correct and without leaks. The extra drywall might not be needed, but for esthetics it could. Tho it'll also, again if build correct, stop any leaks from your brickwall. But I wouldn't add'm. Only if necessary (leaks in wall) You could also consider a room-in-room construction (keep the inner (brick) wall for a false ceiling to rest on while it don't touches the exterious wall and concrete roof.

billshoup23 April 2008#4
Ro's right - it's a 3 leaf wall. Insulation (pink stuff or rock wool) is considered the same as an air space (per Rod's book) so you have a mass-air-mass-air-mass (brick-air-brick-insulation-drywall) wall.
