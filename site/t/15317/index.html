gullfo18 December 2010#4
looks like a nice space! what are the stud walls going to do? are those the treatment frames or were you considering another isolation layer? if the latter, this will end up being a triple leaf between the CR and live room and perhaps elsewhere. you don't actually need to angle the windows between the rooms other than to change how they reflect light. the 2m x 1m with that separation should be OK but the dual glazed with 10mm gap will result in a quad leaf effect so you're better off getting some heavy glass - 10mm on one side and 12mm on the other. what are you thinking about for doors?
