kendale12 February 2006#2
Aloha and welcome to the forum! :D Congrats on your studio build!
So first off which end should i mix from....the narrow end or the wide end
I would suggest mixing from the narrow end, maybe start with your mix position about 38% from the front wall. This would put you just past a null around the 6' mark, and then fine tuning the position with your monitor placement and acoustic treatments.
how should i treat it with acoustic foam, diffusers, and or bass traps
If you're handy at DIY projects, heres' a good link with plans for corner traps, side and rear absorbers, as well as where to put them. While they look cool, I think the room is too short for using diffusors. http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm Also:good link for info on acoustics and treatments (click on left hand column and tabs at top of pages) http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html
should the front of the room be fairly dead...especially behind my monitors? or live?
Are you soffit mounting or using stands?
my vocal booth is 4' x 6'.....should this room be fairly dead?
I think most here would agree with a "dead approach" for that size booth.
I also plan on using smarrt to try and get the flattest response i can from each room...especially from the mix position. If anyone has some decent professional experience I would greatly appreciate the advice.
Not having used this program, I'll leave this to the "professionals." :wink: Hope this helps. BTW, if I may ask, it that Kelowna, BC? Aloha 8)
