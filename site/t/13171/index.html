xSpace14 June 2009#2
"Is it the best option for an independent ceiling or are there other ones out there?" It is the more difficult one to build. If you were to simply frame up walls and overhead ceiling then hang sheetrock on these framing members, it is a cut and dried solution. With an inside out type overhead your framed walls would have to be made temporarily rigid in order to lift the pre-built panels into the overhead placement area. Not only is it tricky, the panels can be cumbersome to get into place. When I see John's inside out construction it is mostly used were space is at a minimum so acoustic treatments and mass placement become issues.
