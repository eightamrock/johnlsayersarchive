When you say "Staggered studs", are you referring to this? I couldn't quite see what was being driven at by the picture... I see the air gap, but not what the drywall was attached to.
Image not preserved: Staggered Stud.png
If so, option B with 2 walls, completely separated by an air spring will give you more isolation, EXCEPT be careful re: the specific locations of the drywall. You wouldn't want that interior sheet on the inside towards the air gap, because that will actually lower your sound isolation. This is one of those places where simple is actually better. Here's a link to some info on the differences for different construction... check out down at number 6 to see how the placement of the mass (i.e. drywall) can impact the ratings. http://www.stcratings.com/assemblies.html In regards to attaching to the floor, I asked about that myself in my design thread. It's not absolutely necessary to put a layer of material between the concrete and the wood when attaching depending on how much isolation you need. Do you have a specific isolation target planned? The dimensions generally look ok for a small control room... I ran them through this link: http://www.bobgolds.com/Mode/RoomModes.htm and they came out with a pass... though what was the height? I assumed 8 feet, but that's an important bit. Also, heads up about the door placement. Leave yourself enough room for bass trapping in the corners.
