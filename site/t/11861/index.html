Part of it depends on your needs in terms of isolation for the singer and good sound. I personally would never build a 4'x4'x7' vocal booth, generally such rooms are far too small to add anything like useful room sound. Additionally, you don't want 2 dimensions to be the same. For more of my thoughts on this subject, see this article.
