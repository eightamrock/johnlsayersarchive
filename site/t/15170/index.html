bnafoster wrote:
Is there a general rule of thumb that dictates how much of an air gap must exist between a stud wall (or a double stud wall) and an outer wall before the outer wall is not considered a leaf of the design?
The short answer is no. The goos answer is that as the gap becomes greater, the gap is no longer "wasted space" but becomes usable floor area. I am certain you have slept in rooms with quadruple leaf partitions and noticed no sound from the room on the other side.
