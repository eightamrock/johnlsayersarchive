Soundman20206 October 2009#6
Hi Simbo. Welcome. When you say "soundproof", do you mean that you need to isolate the control room from the outside world? In the sense that you have to stop loud sounds from getting into your room, and also stop stop your own sounds from getting out of the room, annoying the neighbors, family, etc.? If so, then you need to build a "room within a room" to accomplish that kind of isolation. The good news is that you already have concrete walls around you on all sides, so you should have reasonably good isolation already. That existing concrete shell will be your "outer leaf". The bad news is the size of your room: construction of the "inner leaf" will eat up about 6 inches on each side of the room, and also on the ceiling, so your final ceiling will be rather low, and your small room will get even smaller. Then, once you have isolated the room as above (to stop sound getting in and out), you will need to treat it, so that it sounds good, and can be used as a control room. Isolation and treatment are two very different things. If you can be more specific about what it is that you want to do, then you will probably get more response from folks here. - Stuart -
