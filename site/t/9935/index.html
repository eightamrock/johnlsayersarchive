Hey boss, good to see ya here. I think that you may find that building anything in your attic area is going to prove to be a poor idea. Not much of anything existing can be moved without special instruction, special permission and trained yahoos to move it, IF it can be moved:) It will be most difficult to get the counter-levered beam resting on the girder to be moved simply because it has a function. And in order to remove it, there will need to be something of sufficient and most likely engineered strength to replace it. Your £500 ($1000) plus, just got used up. And there are so many other concerns. The extra weight alone in building material is an issue in trying to develop this area into anything other than what it is. Building a wall, hanging sheetrock and running a caulkgun is one thing. Retro fitting a room into an attic/loft area without proper experience, supervision and knowledge is a potential structure failure waiting to happen. What happened to the garage/workshop downstairs area? This is usually where many others begin and have a successful build. I wish ya luck,
