BF16 December 2007#4
rod gervais wrote:
I would rip the corner backer to maintain the angle and a full face fit to the slats.
Exactly what I did. Set the angle on your table saw, set up the gate (check it a few times) and rip. I also cut the angles on the ends of my slats with a miter box so the ends of the slats are parallel with the sides of the trap. I drilled pilot holes and attached with screws, although if I were to do it again finishing nails would probably be a lot less work.
