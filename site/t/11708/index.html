The air lock is like any air lock in commercail buildings. It allows a person to enter the 1st door without opening the 2nd door and letting air(sound) out or in, in the case of a studio. The canvas roof on the tennis centre in Perth is held up only with air pressure. so all entries have an air lock. Once the 1st door is sealed you can then enter the building. lil
