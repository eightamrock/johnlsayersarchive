The real difficulty is the "grounding" has two different meanings and sometimes both apply at the same time. Grounding: a) Connecting to a common point or to a common reference. b) Connecting to good old mother earth. Item (b) has more to do with safety from thunder & lightning and electrical power problems. Star Ground = Running a short wire from each item (that needs to be grounded) to a common point then running a single wire from that point to Ground. When star grounding only connect your A/V equipment to the A/V star ground.
