Soundman202028 October 2010#29
1. Given the use of inside out construction, do I need the angled walls along side the console and to just behind the listening position, or can I just put slats on the straight walls?
I'd certainly keep those angled frames, and I'd go with slats on them. Make them into broadband slot walls. (If you were to put drywall on them, you'd have a third leaf, which you DON'T want!) Slats is what you need there, except at the first reflection points, where absorption would be better.
If the angled walls are better, do I need insulation both on the outer wall and the false angled wall?
Not really. You need one or the other, but you don't really need it on both places, as its a slot wall, not an isolation wall. Where you put it changes how it works! :)
2. Does it appear that I have enough bass treatment in the back of the room (given that the room is so small)?
It's looking good to me, for a start, but that slight "kink" in the rear bass trap frame isn't really necessary, and might make your construction harder. It won't do any harm to your bass trapping, so you can leave it there if you like it, but it does add complexity to the build. You might also need more bass trapping elsewhere in the room: Don't forget that the room has 12 corners, not just 4!
3. Any suggestions for non-permanent sealing techniques for the window in the brick wall
Non-permanent bricks? :) Seriously, I'd just build a plug with plenty of mass on it and a couple of separate seals, then screw it in place tightly. - Stuart -
