guitardad729 May 2006#6
Advice, At least make a simple drawing of the existing space. Yes you've given the dimensions and partial info on one window. What about existing doors and other obstacles in the room, this is a garage right? There could be a bunch of things in there to work around. The free SketchUp (top thread in this forum) is something that will help you: Draw a simple sketch of existing space. Figure out angles from other pics you’ve downloaded (import them into SketchUp) AND/OR at least provide some real pics of existing space. Slow down a bit, with out a plan for both design & construction you may be headed for trouble. It seems that when posters put in a reasonable amount of effort with questions, suggestions are usually made IMO. And that’s a good thing. Marc
