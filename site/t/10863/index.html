... S i g h So... After I started realizing that something was wrong with my re-write I went back to SAE, re-read that whole chapter and still could not figure out what was wrong. So I opened up Rod's book and re-read chapter nine. Once I got to page 179, at the bottom, I read... - "Be careful if you go this route. Sometime, back a ways, there was an error in this calculation that made its way through the internet." (d*D) + (r+w) should be (d*D) * (r+w) When solving for D, that gives: D= ( r / (f/c)^2 * d(r+w) ) Well... now, at least, the expression is returning some reasonable results and also shows that these so called low-frequency pegboard panel absorbers, as written about in the SAE-pages, do not work within reasonable dimensions. Just like the people replying to this thread have stated before. This is some kind of high-mid range absorber, at best. Usually...
