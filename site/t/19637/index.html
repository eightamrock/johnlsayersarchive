Not much you can do to really improve this cramped environment. But there are some things to consider. The mixing position always wants symmetry...always. The slope, I am thinking that is the underside of a staircase, needs to be removed from your mix position since it completely unbalances the listening position. When I say removed, I recognize this is most likely not something that can be altered structurally so you should want to move your gear to eliminate this feature. This room being almost square and with a 7 foot ceiling is not helping much either. Low frequency trapping in all corners and broadband panels mounted at the sidewalls where your mix position is will help reduce destructive early reflections. The best you can hope for is at least a room that doesn't overwhelm your ears with low frequency and the loud noise that is a by product of small square rooms with low ceilings.
