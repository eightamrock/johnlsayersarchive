xSpace30 March 2009#8
I am thinking that replacing the diagonal bracing with solid blocking would be of more benefit to you jlehan. Diagonal is tedious, old hat and can be updated by installing 2 X lumber with something that measures the height of your joist area. Then you can nail or screw into the joist > to the blocks, offsetting the blocks from your existing bracing center line, as you replace them to allow unrestricted nailing.
