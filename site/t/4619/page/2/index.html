I think you mean vapor retarder... Yes, there's a difference. See:
    [url=http://www.buildingscience.com/resources/mold/Read_This_Before_You_Design_Build_or_Renovate.pdf]Healthy and Affordable Housing: Practical Recommendations for Building, Renovating, and Maintaining Housing[/url] Excerpt:
      In new construction and significant renovations where there is a choice of vapor retarders, paper faced cavity insulation can and should be used in place of plastic interior vapor barriers. . . Building codes do not call for the installation of vapor barriers. They call for the installation of vapor retarders -- and only in cool and cold climates or colder . . .
Based on that excerpt, I'm thinking that neither a vapor barrier nor a vapor retarder is needed, since he's in Southern California -- an area which is not in a cool or cold climate according to Figure 21 on page 17 of the document (which is page 23 of the PDF). Does anyone disagree with this assessment? :roll:
