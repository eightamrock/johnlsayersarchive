sharward6 November 2005#8
Right. "Staggering" means making a brick wall type pattern with the drywall sheets. "Rotating" means alternating the direction of each layer of sheets. Both done together increase strength and flatness.

knightfly9 November 2005#19
Shift one layer (the second one, now) over by TWO feet and same lengthwise; cut half-sheets to fill the 2 foot blank areas, then continue with full sheets to the end, then fill with whatever size works for the last sheet in each dimension - that way, you have at least a 2 foot offset for each joint in each direction (assuming your joists are on 2 foot centers) HTH... Steve
