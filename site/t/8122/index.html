Hi folks... I'm expanding my studio and building a 10'x15' foley, ADR, Walla room and need to get a good sized video picture. Up to now, I've simply been using a 19" monitor outside the glass of my iso/vocal booth, but I'll need something bigger for the new room. What would you suggest? Either a 37"-42" plasma/LCD screen wall-mounted? Or a ceiling-mounted lcd projector (but hopefully without a loud fan?)? Thanks for your help, I'm trying to get this finished by next week!! cheers rich
lilith_envy4 February 2007#2
Wow sounds great! Most plasmas still have a large latency, 12-20ms. LCD's are better but you still need to be aware of the spec when buying. Creating a sound proof, roof mounted housing for the projector would be good. I think there's a web site somewhere on soundproofing and acoustics :shock: Do you have natural light in the studio?

lilith_envy6 February 2007#4
the sony CX and Es ranged are not that loud, nor hot. the fan will need an escape port out in to the roof cavity. you could build a minature version of the old 35mm film projector set-ups. With in and out vents inc. filters. Or if you had room you could rear project with a short throw lens from a joining area in the studio. Ratios of 1:1.2 size to distance
