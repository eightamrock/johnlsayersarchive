Connectors are not the place to go on the cheap in your setup. Switchcraft or Neutrik are the way to go. Be weary of "off-brands".
Aaronw10 August 2005#3
Yeah. As Sam said, buy Neutrik or Switchcraft. You'll be much better off. I've tried and used off brand connectors before, and bottom line...they've always sucked. I've had pins pull out, they don't fit w/ other connectors well (tight fit or loose fit), etc.
LiveWire24 August 2005#4
There is nothing wrong with connectors from ebay, just make sure they are Neutrik or Switchcraft and not knockoffs. Those connectors show are Chinese knockoffs and when they make them, they always don't fit right. I had a friend that bought a few hundred maybe even thousand i don't remember and none of them would work with a standard Neutrik/SC connector. Those no-names are bad news. And if the do work they are built like junk so they will break over time which you rarely ever see with good connectors.
