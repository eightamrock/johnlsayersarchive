Aloha and welcome to the forum, Could you provide some info regarding:
space on a second floor in a truck station
There have been several concerns regarding psf loads for studios built above ground floors. Is your building able to support the additional weight incurred with a studio build + equipment + members? One way to find out is to retain the services of a structural engineer (if you have one available in your town) and have him assess the proposed situation for you.

Aloha, Just happened to notice that your ceiling height along the west wall is only 1.560m (perhaps a bit on the short side, eh?) and would slant sideways across the control room (better to have it slant upward from the front of the control room to the rear.) John Sayers on ceiling height SAE site - http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html
I suggest that a control room ideally should be at least 6m x 5m with a minimum ceiling height of 2.4m. (19’7” x 16’4” x 7’9”)
I understand the desire for the lock down, but is that set in stone or negotiable? Aloha 8)
