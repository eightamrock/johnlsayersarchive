I plan on building a sophet around the entire system (as in the sketchup, however, the sketchup doesn't show it completely encased, as it will actually be), insulating the outside of the ducting and then adding two 5/8 sheetrock to the sophet. John Sayers30 October 2006#12
Yes - you can save on cost by using normal insulation in the walls. I'm not sure which doors you are refering to - the ones between the CR and TR can be glass but you need to use standard glass but different thicknesses in each door. i.e. 1/4" and 3/8" or better still, 3/8" and 1/2". You can easily remove the laminated glass and replace it in the outer door. I've attached a skp of a sealed glass door. cheers john
