TomM9 November 2007#5
The framing from the floor to ceiling (to the right of the duct in your drawing) isn't decoupled? Same goes for the other room too I guess.. is that fastened to the ceiling? For the framing to the right of the duct, if it's not connected to the ceiling, then you might have problems with the weight of the drywall around the duct pulling the frame towards the floor. I used a RSIC clip to attach it to the concrete wall. Knightfly helped me out on this design and it seems to be working really well although it was a pain to do.. It might be hard to see in the picture, but I ran 2X6's in between the joists and then used these clips to attach the perpindicular framing in between the joists.... http://www.acousticalsurfaces.com/rsic_ ... b.htm?d=46 I think this way might save space too... give you a little extra room under the duct compared to what you have in the drawing.
