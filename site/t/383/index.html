barefoot3 July 2003#7
Just a point.... BIG doesn't necessarily have to mean LOUD. One very good approach to low distortion loudspeaker design is to use a large array of drivers with a lot of surface area. This way you can move the same volume of air (sound volume) with much less effort on the part of each individual component.... smaller amplitudes and greater heat dissipation resulting in reduced distortion and dynamic compression. My problem with this system is its predilection towards compression drivers, horns, and tube amplifiers - components with a lot of snake oil hype, but rather abysmal performance specs. Thomas
