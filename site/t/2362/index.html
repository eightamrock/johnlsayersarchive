John Sayers30 September 2004#15
1) Does your new design assume that the hayloft is left intact and that I'll have 8ft. cielings in the CR? This would save me nice storage space up there and save me some demolition/structural expenses.
Yes - there's no need to use the full ceiling height.
2) If it does, how do I manage the change in cieling from the shed structure to the barn structure? Remember, the shed only has 7ft cielings on the side you have the drums on.
I'd continue the ceiling height in the shed through to the control room.
3) I'll still need access to the hayloft via a ladder...where shoudl this go?
I'd make the room directly in front of the control room with a high ceiling to increase the room volume - it could even be used as a live drum room. You can have a ladder in either of the "green rooms."
4) Can I swap out your "windows" for "sliding glass doors" going into the live room?
sure - not a problem - you'd need double doors to get a decent isolation.
5) Finally, what do you see these three "green" rooms functioning as?
Deader booths for vocals etc. as I mentioned before the larger room could be turned into a live drum room using the full ceiling height.
p.s. any way to get natural light in here?
Possibly on the other side where the drum rrom would be - it's really a matter of how much you wish to alter the exterior of the building. cheers john
