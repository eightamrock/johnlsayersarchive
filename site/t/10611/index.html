I need to build a mobile GOBO for my studio with a small window. Does anybody have a plan?
frostgfx15 May 2008#2
Hey, Do you want to close in the window frame, or build a gobo with wheels? If you are going to fill in the window frame; Measure the depth and height/width. Build a 2x4 frame to fit the window space. Cover the back of the frame with 1/4" CDX plywood and paint black for your neighbors. Fill the frame with either rockwool or OC 703 fiberglas. Staple fireproof fabric (www.interfacefabric.com) to the front of the filled gobo frame. You can then put a trim piece over the front of the frame to hide the staples. Whalla... Have a nice day. Jon
