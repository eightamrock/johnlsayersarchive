xSpace2 May 2009#15
Bryan, If you look at the third column in the PDF file you have, it gives you a date and the house that ran the testing on these assemblies. Most of these ratings are approaching 40 years old. For a more up-to-date document repository, go to : http://irc.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/index_e.html As to a primer of sorts, to get a handle on what an OITC is and how it stacks up to the much celebrated STC, goto : http://irc.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/pubs/ir/ir818/ For a better indication of an assemblies performance in a low end frequency environment, the OITC rating is a better indicator. An stc is based on what the human ear can detect so while it is one indicator it is not, or shouldn't be, considered the final indicator of sound related panels and assemblies. Good luck, Brien
