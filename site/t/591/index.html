knightfly19 August 2003#5
On your triangle question - the correct spelling is isosceles, but that's not the type triangle you want (usually) - an isosceles triangle has TWO equal sides and angles, where an Equilateral triangle has ALL THREE sides/angles equal. Head position, as long as it's centered, doesn't seem to be really critical from my experience - if you put the point of the equilateral triangle that's NOT one of the speakers, maybe 3-4 inches behind your head, it should be fine. If you have clients/etc on a couch behind (probably bigger room than yours) you could aim the speakers a little "wider" (not too much) so they get a better image. Too much though, and your own sweet spot gets pretty small... Steve
