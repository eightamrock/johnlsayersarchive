I'll give you a quick answer, but you must remember what you paid for it. If this were one of my projects, I would ask myself if re-nailing the sheathing is possible. If it is, then I would do it, the re-nailing. On most occasions I would take the sheathing like what you have, off. But since I cannot see what I am doing, so to speak, and do not know with any certainty how much this sheathing is involved in the structures ability to stay upright, I would only suggest making it tighter with more "clavos". As it stands at this viewing, the wall in question is not a two leaf wall, just in case that was another question as well. "I'm scared they would just contribute to sound transmission" Well, that fear may or may not be unfounded. But you are in need of a quick answer, and most likely one that agrees with your thinking, so move forward as discussed, and you will not be able to tell, one way or the other, if it contributes or detracts. That I will bet on :)
