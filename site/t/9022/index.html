there's a couple of factors to consider - what is the transmission loss of the walls? versus the windows? if you have the windows set in solid brick walls, then you want to try to match the TL and resonant frequencies of that brick wall. which probably means you either need a frame to place a sealed brick "panel" orver the window, or something with enough mass to match it. maybe looking at how studio doors are constructed with dense wood, lead, drywall, MLV, etc... to achieve 40db TL at lower frequencies would be what you need to construct. smaller but similar.
