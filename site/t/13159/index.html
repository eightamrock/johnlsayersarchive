bluesbaz12 June 2009#3
Just build a frame large enough to fill the opening caulk and seal it maybe throw some insulation to kill resonance two layers of 5/8th greenglue if your using it elsewhere and there you go. Treat it like any other wall section. When its all said an done you are harming nothing really and when you leave break the hold caused by the caulking around the plug clean off anything sticking to the opening and move out.

xSpace16 June 2009#5
Is that a gas or wood burning appliance? I don't see this chimney as being a stumbling block but I do see that whole wall being one. Where geographically do you live? Thing is that the fireplace is most likely an insert and inserts use metal ducting. So even if you plug the hole, sound that gets into the wall gets into the metal duct and escapes into your attic, or upper floor and finally out into the air, far above the neighbors house. The duct is attached hard to the existing structure. It will probably be more of an issue to you and yours then to those at some distance away. This is a guess, only a guess ;)
