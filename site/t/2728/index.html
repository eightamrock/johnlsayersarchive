One little suggestion: you might address the concrete wall area at the front left of your control room. If you build a stud/drywall/whatever wall on each side of that concrete wall, you could end up with less isolation. Perhaps build just one of those walls, and use treatments to break the parallels. If the concrete wall isn't thick enough (but it probably is) you could add mass there.
