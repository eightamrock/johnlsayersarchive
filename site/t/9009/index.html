This is a wonderful forum. :lol: Ok, I have a 11.5' x 20.5' x 7.5' ceiling with 1 AC port coming into it and 1 entrance door. I figured I best ask and search the forums for the best options. I desire to have this room split into 2 rooms. I want the live room to be able to do drums or vocals. I was thinking of making the live section to be 9' feet from the back wall and the width (11.5' feet) of the whole room. I felt this would be a good size to hold a small 3 piece band or a small singing ensemble with some breathing room. This would leave 11.5 feet for the control room (pre-wall constructions). This room was a patio converted into a room connected to a garage that was converted into rooms. Behind the paneling is sheet rock. I'm not sure where to start but I was thinking about covering the existing wall with new drywall before proceeding but I think it needs to be a new wall over the existing one with insulation. I have read in these forums how some like the one room concept. I prefer a 2 room setup. I also realize I don't have much room to play with dropping the ceiling or even raising the floor. My setup is the typical Protools PC setup, with dual LCD monitors and Tapco studio monitors (14"Deep x 11"Wide x 16.5"Tall)
