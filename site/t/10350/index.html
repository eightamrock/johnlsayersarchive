Think of the King stud as the one that goes all the way from the header to the baseplate. it provides the strongest point of support against vibration back and forth. The king provides the most support... The jack is what "jack's" up the header of the door or window. From what I know which is a modest amount with sparring moments of clarity in design... I would use both for stability. It's just good design. I've even doubled up on the king studs because I wasn't sure what type of door I'd be using and whether I'd have a door closer on it. A closer, slows the door prior to it shutting and avoids those cursed slams that can take off limbs...
