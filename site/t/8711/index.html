Ro3 May 2007#4
Okay, it's a start. I assume, since you talk about 2 panes of glass, you're planning a double wall. Don't need to stagger that. How much sheets of gypsum/mdf yer planning? As I told you, density of glass is roughly 3 time higher than GB/MDF. Going with 1 sheet of 3/4" GB and 1/4" glass is, mass wise, just fine. But 2 sheets of 3/4"GB need atleast 1/2" of (laminated) glass (equivalent mass). (per wall side!)
