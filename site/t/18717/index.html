How do I go forward with this? Should I ask John for wall ceiling angles (pay him of course if needed)?
You could hire John to design the studio for you, or you could learn how to design it yourself, which will be cheaper but will take longer. Realistically, it takes about 6 months to learn the basics of acoustics and studio design well enough to be able to design a studio yourself, then another 2 to 3 months to actually design it. If you have the time, then that's a very good way of doing it, as you will end up thoroughly understanding every aspect of what you are doing. But not everyone has that much time available. The wall and ceiling angles need to be calculated properly to ensure that you do create a proper Reflection Free Zone (RFZ), which is the design concept that John used for that studio. Those angles are different for every studio, and are based om many factors. So it's not just a matter of using any angle that looks good. But you sure do have a nice space to build your studio in!
