gullfo13 October 2007#2
for the walls, the first options sounds about right but without measurements of the transmission loss of the existing walls its hard to say. if the basement is mostly underground then you're biggest concerns are openings like doors and windows and your ceiling. for the ceiling, if you have the option to add drywall under the existing floor, insulation, then RC and more drywall (see Rod's book on home studio construction) should give you pretty good isolation given the limited head space.

Folks, an empty concrete block wall is a one leaf - but not as isolating as when sand filled......... So add air space - a wood frame wall with insulation in it and then a face of drywall and you have a 2 leaf system. which would be much better than the other option I see questioned by the poster.
