welcome! you might want to read up a bit more - the recording manual is an excellent start to help understanding the important concepts such as isolation and room treatment. in general, good isolation requires having your walls decoupled from the outside - usually a room within a room - and simply spraying insulation will not provide a complete solution however, that depends on the details of your requirements. the read me before posting sticky has a lot of questions you can answer which will help us to help you.
