kendale15 April 2006#8
Aloha,
So I assume we can just build a ceiling below the existing drop ceiling?
I believe the diagram refers to the original ceiling (the 30'-40' ones in your case) and the new ceiling is to be built in place of the drop ceiling.
The main issue, I guess, would be how to isolate sound from outside the studio and prevent it from coming into the studio via the space above, which is the same space as the other room. For example, a group may be meeting in the area outside the studio. Their noise would float into the space and back down into the studio and the studio noise would do the same.
If isolation is an issue, then the drop ceiling will not help your situation. In order to realize isolation you need what has been described as the mass-air-mass principle. Perhaps a search on this site will prove useful for more info. In any case, you may want to consider some bass hangers in the area above the new ceiling. Here's a good link to review: http://johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic ... sc&start=0 Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
