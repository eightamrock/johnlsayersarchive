jwl1 May 2008#2
There are lots of ways to mount panels. You can build cleats, use eyehooks, d-rings, molly bolts, and hanging wire, you can space them from the walls (a good thing), you can build the panels right into the walls. I like to build panels on stands so they can be moved around as needed.
