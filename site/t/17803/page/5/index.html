The walls were covered in 4" thick insulation with a 1" gap behind. I then put insulation angled at 45 degrees covering the wall to ceiling corners. The reason the original and finished curves do not match are because they are using to different interfaces.
Tat should not make any difference at all: you did the calibration for both of them, and both the calibration files are in use, so any difference between the interfaces are irrelevant, since REW is correcting for them. And in any case, the difference are much, much bigger than can be explained by switching interfaces.
The speakers and mic were setup exactly the same way throughout and REW was calibrated correctly as far as i'm aware
Did you measure the exact positions of both speakers and the mic after the first test, accurate to 1/4", and make sure that it was exactly the same for the second test? It is critical to have the mic in the precise same location for every test: If not, then you can't compare the graphs. Each graph is still valid, for that position of the mic, but they are not comparable, since they are showing different locations. I think that's what happened here.
