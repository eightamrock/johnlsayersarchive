Soundman20202 June 2013#91
Those are the points where the sound emitted by the speakers bounces off a surface and gets directly to your ears. Usually on the side walls and on the ceiling above the desk, and also possibly the front wall. There's an easy way to find them, known as the "mirror trick". You need an assistant and a mirror: Set up your speakers and chair in the exact positions they will be when the room is finished, and get your assistant to hold up the mirror against the walls and move it around all over. Any place you can see either of the speakers reflected in the mirror, is a first reflection point. It needs treatment. - Stuart -
