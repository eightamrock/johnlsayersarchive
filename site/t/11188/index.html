Ok, here goes... Where am I? Bolton in the United Kingdom, North West England to be more exact, if you want to find us on a map just look for a rain cloud and we are right under that. I am lead to believe that Noah set up his first boat yard around here What am I attempting to do? At the moment i use the place as a rehearsal room with added recording, i want to go mainly with the recording side with a couple of rehearsals a week. there are very definite limits to how far i can take this room, just want to make sure that i get as near to those limits as i can Where am I right now in the project? the rooms already being used for rehearsals with minimal treatment, and a couple of 'albums' have been recorded here, i had given consideration to moving, but i cant find anywhere the right size, so am gearing up to shut down rip out, rebuild and then grand open type thang. How loud am I? Not had a complaint yet, and in two years i only ever balled out one guitar player, but as i needed ear muffs outside the room it was more a health and safety issue, my neighbors have never complained, but i do go out of my way to recognize and respect their times and days (mainly Muslim residents) What do I record? both sorts, punk and rock, although ska, electronica and one set of guys who just record and sample everything are also fairly frequent visitors, oh and a very healthy dose of metal. What is my budget? £18.50? to be honest it will be minimal at about £2000, but that will be parts only, I can sort the labour myself. as i said the rooms 'usable' so just looking to try and improve whats there and add some aesthetic treatment along the way ... or make the Abbey Road of the north (think i will go for the first option) I will be going for rooms within the room, but only doing what will make a difference, so no floating floors for a start. I want a CR, vocal booth and live area, first idea here :- What do I have to work with? its on the first floor. 9760mm(L)x4330mm(W)x2360mm(H). south wall has three large windows with double glazing that are covered with rockwool and gyprock at the moment, but after they will be full of this stuff (first weak link identified). the only door is in the North West corner, as in right in the corner. now the biggest problem!! the floor!! joists at 400mm, 25mm floor boards, joists are 225mmx50mm aprox, and there is a 250mm void under that to a 2" thick concrete roof to the room below there are also 3 air vents 150mmx150mm. the roof is a flat roof. What’s it made of? brick all around, north wall is a mill wall and about 625mm thick, south and east walls are standard cavity wall, west wall attaches to the mill toilets (the studio is an old storage space). What questions do I have? i will edit this as i think of any more to reflect asking them in the thread. 1. What do i do with the floor? first thoughts are to use the air vents as inputs/expel for air (I wont go to full HVAC as i dont need it) attach a couple of the baffle things that LOU used in his build to silence them down, but just going for air movement. then fill the void with acoustic hangers, very happy to rip all the floor boards up if this is a workable item. replace the boards with Birch ply boards 2400mmx1200mm as the floor boards are not air tight, and replacing them would be cheaper than trying to make them air tight. Iain
