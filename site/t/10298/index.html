I’ve attached a drawing below in reference of my first question: With the useable space shown in the drawing, would it be better to leave it as one large room and uses mobile partitions and gobos if recording drums and such, or create two separate rooms (control and isolation area) to provide more isolation from the instruments/vocals while recording?
