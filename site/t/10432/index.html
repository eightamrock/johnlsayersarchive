Ro7 April 2008#2
Hai Eeza and welcome to this forum. You came to the right place with your plans/questions. This sure is the most reliable resource on building audio-rooms. But I have to ask you to click: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=3231 Order a copy of Rod's fantastic book while browsing the web. Looks like you've done some good reading already. That's a good startingpoint. Dunno if you've read this forum, but there sure is some good info here. you talk about a garage and concrete floor. Could you tell (or take pix) more about the current situation; how's it build, what materials, dimensions etc. A room-in-room constructions could work, but depending on your current walls and ceiling (the multiple-leaf theory..) An 8" airgap is a lot, but if you can spare the space it's recommendable to 'ave. The mentioned materials are correct. A frame of wood or metal(studs) filled with wool and GB on the inside (or if ye building inside-out walls, look at johns site for that, it's, ofcourse, on the 'outside') Dunno about your floor, but you prolly don't need it floating, check various threads about that (there's even a sticky on that topic) Think about acoustics later, there's a forum-part for that which you should study first. My advise is to first come up with a design, post it in the "design" forum part. After all parties agree, hai gulfo ;), come back to this construction part and see HOW it's best to build your design. After that, acoustics.... cheers.
