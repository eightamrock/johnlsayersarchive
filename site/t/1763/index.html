Well, first off, try to make the control room symmetrical on each side of the listening position. That angled wall throws off the symmetry, and it'll make your stereo image sound unbalanced. There's piles and piles of posts around here on how and where to place your sound treatment and bass traps, so I won't type it all out again. They're under so many different post subjects that you'll probably just have to read as many of these posts as you can. Hope this helps!
