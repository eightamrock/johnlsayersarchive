SonicClang29 March 2006#4
Matt, welcome to the forums. Some advice I can give is, forget everything you think you know about sound proofing and acoustic sound control. Unless you heard it here from us, or in a book about this stuff, or other forums about this, anything you've heard about the subject is 100% wrong. Don't listen to "construction guys" about building a studio. There are 100 times more myths about studio construction than there are actual ways to build one, and every guy I know who does construction seems to know them all. No, foam is not for sound proofing, it is for acoustic control. No, the best way to make a wall soundproof is not to simply add 3 inch insulation. And be prepared to feel like you're in school for the next couple months. And don't feel like a dork if you take notes. :)
