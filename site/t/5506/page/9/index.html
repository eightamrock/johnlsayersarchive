Little update, just got back from holiday and made more panels. There's a total of six panels on the walls, and another dozen will be mounted on the ceiling. There are 3 panels on the east wall and 3 on the west wall. Chess-board placement; no panel faces another one. The north and south walls are filled with load-end panels already which will act as mid-range too. No need for extra panels. Perhaps an acoustic reading will clear up details. Dunno if I'm gonna, after this room isn't high-profile. Here's a couple'o pix from the wall-panels. And, I have scheduled an appointment with an acoustic engineer to test the isolation of the room.
