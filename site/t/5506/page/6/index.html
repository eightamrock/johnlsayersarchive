Ro9 March 2012#135
Bawoo wrote:
Is that carpet padding in the silencers?
No, it's agglomerated foam (common name is "Isofix"). This stuff is typically installed in your car-seats. It comes in various densities. Mine is about 60kg/m3. I used this 'cuz it is easy to install (compare that to wool). And, to be frank, I just have a bunch of this stuff as leftover. Might as well use i"t, right!

Ro17 March 2012#137
Letting the air flow Aight, started installing ductwork for the fresh air supply. Three floors witch three live rooms will be attached to the air supply system. The top floor holds the fans and controllers. Duct work goes through the roof where air will be sucked in and blown out. It's an active-active system. That means both inbound and outbound duct work will have a fan to control the overall airflow. There's silencers on both side of the walls. On the inside of the live rooms air can be controlled individually and can be heated till max 16 degr cel (in case of cold winters, remember we do not have heat-radiators in the building) As always, pics to back my story up :) you'll see the installation of the duct work on the top floor. cheers mates.

Ro13 July 2012#143
Riders on the storm... Hey, hey peeps. It's DOOR time. Create a double wall with matching mass doors. two doors, twice the fun :) I constructed custom doors as I designed in this thread: viewtopic.php?f=2&t=12664 I used a piece of pine (2 by 4) as the hinge side. Added a sheet of 15mm and a sheet of 22mm MDF for the core. Sealed the whole thing with a sheet of 6mm MDF on both sides. Total mass around 90KG. Using four Paumelle hinges. As always, I got proof it happened. Next up: glass placement between live and control room. Already purchased the glass, two sheets of 6:8 laminated glass. 80x120cm. But more on that after my summer break. cheers, enjoy the show.
