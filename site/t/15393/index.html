gullfo19 December 2010#13
basically it's room within a room (if you need isolation) - 2x4 frames and drywall and pink insulation. the corners are super chunks and there are some angled slats on the walls to break things up. clouds on the ceiling. the live room some long slat walls to provide angles and the booth which is another isolation section. costs? assuming just materials, if you don't need to build out the room within a room and just the isolation booth, dividing wall, and treatments, then probably not too bad for a hobby studio. if you need isolation and want better finishes then it will cost you. if you simply divide off the rooms and don't need isolation, then start simple and treat the rooms, skip the isolation booth and start using the space. later if you find you really need high isolation etc, take down the dividing walls and redo the space with the room within a room etc. if you're just a beginner and not a large budget, work smart and build up over time. if you can spare the extra money and effort for soffit mounting your monitors, then do it will be a more rewarding workspace.
