Really not that deep. Do a quick cal to see what is the narrowest spacing you'd do (most people it's 1/4" or 1/8") and the widest slot spacing. For me that was 3/4" That will tell you the bottom and top frequencie of absorption by the resonator. Nothing to fret about. :) The main reason I recommended it was to keep the room from being to dead. :)
