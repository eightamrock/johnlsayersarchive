jlemon5 November 2008#13
The precise height of the monitors actually depends on the listener's body measurements and the preferred seat height. On average, it's 120cm... From the video you posted on your old topic, I could tell by the way you sit on the drums (geia sou sunaderfe!) that you are a tall fellow. Sit comfortably on your chair and have someone measure the distance from the floor to your ears. That's where your speakers height should be. In the meantime, use the 120cm height as Andre suggested. I worked on your wall design for a few minutes and I have a sketchup file for you to check out. If I was going to build my wall with concrete blocks, I would build it like this: One layer of 1,5cm drywall, one layer of 10cm concrete blocks and one layer of 5cm insulation on each side with a 5cm air gap in between. Total thickness: 38cm. Pretty close to your estimation. I've also included the listening position and ray of sound from your speakers on 60 degree and 90 degree angles. Conclusion: You can forget all about soffit mounting, if you insist on having those windows. You can use the double sliding doors that John's suggesting on his designs instead. Lower STC but more viewing area than a normal studio door and more space available for soffits. Keep a cool head, John
