Started by G-Sun on 30 May 2013. 6 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 18376.

G-Sun30 May 2013#1
For a small room, what is best: Suffitmounted speakers in the corners or Freestanding speakers closer to center? Thanks!
Soundman202030 May 2013#2
Soffit mounted is much, much better, for many reasons, but even so, they should not be in the corners. They should be about 30% of the room width away from the room corners. - Stuart -

I'm not sure I understand the problem: there is no issue here with space. If you soffit mount with the speakers in the corners or with them closer together, does not affect the usage of space in the room. The soffits take up roughly the same amount of space into the room in both cases. - Stuart -
