knightfly5 November 2005#4
You have the common goal of isolation for both uses; a HT wants a fairly dry acoustic since surround is generally encoded for that type space. If you have a good quality electronic reverb unit you can use that for needed ambience in recordings. It's not optimum, but it's doable. Instead of RC 8, I'd consider using staggered stud wall framing; almost exactly the same result isolation-wise, cheaper than RC, no chance of free help mucking up the RC install. (It can be tricky) Another layer of drywall is the cheapest and best way to increase isolation; other "magic bullets" are 'way more expensive, and usually not as effective even if they WERE as reasonable in cost. If you want more balanced sound, I'd look for mineral wool and build your own absorbers instead of using a bunch of foam; more even absorption, plus you can even paint pictures on the cloth covers if your decor works with it.
