xSpace3 September 2009#2
Where is your build thread? Shouldn't we be in the build thread? It is difficult for me to get a handle on what it is you are saying...it reads like you have insulated every other open cavity in a framed wall...surely this is not what you are saying is it? Kraft faced insulation is kind of frowned on for these reasons you are discovering. It hinders the insulations ability to work the magic in mids and high frequency areas. It is a barrier that sound can bounce off of and help flutter to live on. In a thermal environment you face the paper to the living side of the building which I can only assume this is what you are doing. But it should have been unfaced from the start. Like the song goes, "there's your trouble."
