rod gervais13 June 2004#6
0hz wrote:
The R rating for the soundbloc boards are 0.06 m2K/W However the R rating for the 30mm Thermal board is 0.55 m2K/W so is the larger number better or worse ? I am guessing that the 30mm thermal board is better than a 12.5 mm soundbloc board which is not suprising as its 17.5mm thicker.
0hz, well you're guessing wrong. The thermal board is thicker - and has a better R value - but it has less mass. It's a gypsum product bonded to a foam backing - so although it has good thermal values - it has poor sound isolation values. What matters with sound isolation is mass - not thickness per-se. You would be better off with the soundbloc....... although you can acheive the same isolation in the end by using standard sheetrock....... Rod
