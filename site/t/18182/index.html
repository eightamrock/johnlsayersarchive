FriFlo6 March 2013#5
I am in the middle of building my own first serious studio, so I am by no means as good to help you, but I think I see the following problems: In the control room (20x21 feet) you will have a bad ratio, because it is almost a square. Look into good room ratios for all room, especially the control room, where exact listening is critical. Further, I am wondering where you plan to place your speakers into that control room. If you want to face the window, you would rather adjust the room to be symmetrical to that axis, which it is not. So I guess, you plan to be facing north. The arrow shape is not good for creating a ER-free sweet spot. If you plan to soffit mount your monitors, that could be ok. The room is a little wide for that IMO. Well, Stuart! I wanted to at least try helping someone else out a little. Now tell me I am all wrong! :horse: :cop:
