Soundman202016 September 2011#10
My goal here was to see if I could save 4" of ceiling height ...
Have you considered building your ceiling "inside out"? That places the drywall on TOP of the joists, instead of underneath them, which means that the joists are exposed to the room, and therefore the space between the joists can be used for room treatment. With normal construction you have to hang your treatment below the finish ceiling, taking up even more headroom, but with "inside out" you can use the space between the joists for that treatment. Worth looking at, I reckon... On the Kinetics isolation hangers: that's what I was referring to in my previous post with "isolation mounts". Those will work, if you do the math and load them correctly. Post some photos of what you have up there, so we can get a better idea of wat you are dealing with. There are other techniques that might help... - Stuart -
