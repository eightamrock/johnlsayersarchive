John Sayers13 June 2003#2
Hi Dave - welcome to the site. I've attached a pic of you plan so everyone can see it. The first thing I notice is that you are not soffit mounting your speakers, any reason why not?? You could also have glass doors between you and the studio for better visual/communication. Those booths are very small - how do you access them and are you going to put windows in for communication? An alternate layout would be the garage studio design like this where you run the control room across the building. With all that gear at the rear of your room you still only get a 4.6m deep control room which is the width of the building. cheers john
