overall looks like a decent layout. i'd probably flip the CR and live room so you don't have to carry stuff through the CR... plus you could also consider opening up the door between the lounge and live room and treat the lounge so you could use that as spill over space as needed. the vox booth i'd use all of the ceiling height and use the top 2-3' for trapping. with reversible treatments you could use that for vox and an amp room.
