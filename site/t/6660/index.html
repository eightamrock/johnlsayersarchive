Dingo19 July 2006#5
The old wood cladding has been ripped down. The small window will be bricked up shortly. Regarding design constraints, there are 2 main things to consider: * the room has a steel support beam running the entire length of the ceiling * the ceiling height is only around 2.20m (7 1/4 ft) Soundproofing for the control room is not necesarry, as high volumes are seldom used there - however, the recording area will occasionally be used to record live drums, so a decent measure of soundproofing is desired here (I sometimes record quite late at night ;) ). Fortunately, I have been able to source some extremely cheap wood for frames, plasterboard and insulation. It's all waiting there for me to order in the quantities I need. Now we "simply" ;) need to decide on a plan/layout. I personally prefer designs that have the console facing the studio glass, as opposed to having the glass sideways to the console. The walls are 1ft (30cm) thick stone. There is a barn/loft above it. I'll try to sketch a floor plan of the building too. Dingo :D
