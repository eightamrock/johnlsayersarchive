TomVan17 September 2009#3
Osse, Its possible there is a slight interpretation problem, In the US we call it Joint compound or sheet rock mud. It is made of gypsum and used for filling nail/screw holes and taping and filling of the joints. You can also purchase a small can of spackle in the US. If it is used for sheetrock it is gypsum based. Sweden might have a different translation, so just ask your drywall supplier what is used for taping and filling of joints and filling nail/screw holes. More than likely you have the correct stuff since I have never seen a 2 gallon bucket of spackle and cant read Swedish :lol: PLus everything on that page is used for joint compound installation Peace Tom
