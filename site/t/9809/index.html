sandledfoot28 November 2007#3
lordwud... greetings, and welcome to the forum. your space looks great, full of potential! however, having a budget of less than 1k will severly limit what you are trying to accomplish. for starters, no insulation will provide you with isolation. there is a large difference between isolation (goals 1,2,3) and just treating your room to make it sound good. (goal #4). to start, there are some excellent buildouts here of garages, look at T-Mixes and sharwards builds... the isolation techniques they used are basically what you want to do too. your first 1k$ will probably go to sheetrock between the existing joists, all the way around the walls and ceiling, then using acoustical caulk, seal the entire thing... that will start your isolation, then when you ahve more money, you can fill in with insulation, then build your second wall (room within a room)... good luck... read on... many good things on this forum, but you have to actually read it to get the info. (especially since many of the moderators have been detained by other things lately! (tennis???) lol! ) kevin

Lordwud, Lovin the room. Surprised Glen hasn't jumped in yet. You have somehwere around 900-1000 square feet of wall and ceiling by my calcs. Hard to determine with that wonderful ceiling. So what that tells me is for one layer of rock you need approx. 30 sheets. which will cost around $6 per sheet or 200 bones. But be careful with your ceiling/roof. If you can find the specs you need to know the "Design Loads" The dead and live loads which a truss is engineered to support Your caulk is going to cost you anywhere from $4 a tube to $7. And by the time you are finished you will need "A LOT" I would guess between 50 and 75 if you caulk everything you are supposed to. Look in your area for a contractors supply house that deals with windows and or waterproofing. They should at least be able to guide you somewhere if they don't have it. And then insulation....if memory serves me right I think it is about $.70 per square foot. So if I did my math correctly and my prices are about right ...your first mass with one layer of sheet rock will cost about $1k. And thats all the stuff you will never get to see once your interior walls are up. :shock: I think Brad said it in DH's thread. Its a shame to cover up all that hard work Peace Tom

gullfo30 November 2007#7
i concur - add the drywall to the existing walls and roof (repeat - if you're not sure, get a structural engineer to verify you can add the load). if you can only get one layer on the ceiling, then do it. each bit will help... frame out the existing door and put in a single door to significantly reduce sound escaping through that garage door... the open insulation inside may be too much absorption so consider insulating every other space instead - alternate like a checker board. cover with a flame retardent treated cloth. stack plastic covered rolls of insulation in the corners. if you find some money let over, consider cutting plywood strips and making slats to get a bit more life in the room on the walls. once you get some more budget you can consider building our a nice control room and live room...
