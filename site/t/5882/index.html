knightfly26 March 2006#2
If you can't float a concrete floor/room in your space, you're not gonna get close to enough isolation to track drums - period. Best you could do is make it less obnoxious to the neighbors with a heavy wood platform floating on 3" rockwool, but that'll only improve things by maybe 10-15 dB, and you'll likely need more like 40 or 50 dB minimum. No magic bullets for this one, sorry... Steve
