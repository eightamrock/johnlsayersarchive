Soundman202027 July 2011#4
Once the new wall is upright im left with a 4" gap between the existing wall and the new one. Obviously the large air gap is desirable, but how would I fill the space? I would need truckloads of insulation.
You don't need to fill it completely! Yes, doing so will increase the level of isolation, and not putting in any at all will decrease the isolation drastically, but as long as you have a few inches of isolation in there, you'll get good isolation (provided that you do the entire design and build right, of course! :) ) - Stuart -
