Hello everyone. I originally started a thread over in the Construction Forum but I have since spent some more time browsing and decided that the Design Forum would be an appropriate place to begin. I have purchased Rod's book but I have not gotten through much of it yet. Let me introduce myself. My name is Kevin. I live in South Jersey right outside of Philadelphia. I have been a musician for many years. Mostly piano/keyboard and a lot of midi work. I been in a few bands and the last year or two I began teaching myself the ins and outs of digital recording. gullfo21 September 2007#13
on the side window where i have the inside-out wall - you ould put in a window AC unit and then add some holes in the inside-wall to supply-return air. use that space as an "AC room". as far as good dimensions - you have what you have - if adding the wall helps in what you're trying to do, then its good. not having the room square or 2 similar dimensions is good. if isolation is critical - using the additional walls may be necessary. perhaps add another layer of drywall on them? or add some rc and 1" insulation and 5/8" x2 layers drywall on that - cheap second interior wall - it may help without adding another framed wall. the wall between the two rooms can be either doubled or use a single frame with rc on it. i'd just use a door with a window in it or slider door between the rooms - no sense have both a door and window in that small a space.
