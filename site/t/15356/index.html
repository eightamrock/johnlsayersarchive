gullfo13 December 2010#10
an amp room is an isolation booth where you can place your guitar or bass amps (or keyboards) so the player sits in the CR and performs and the signal is run via a DI box to the mixer plus the amp in the isolation room. also useful for "re-amping" when you need to change the sounds - run the raw guitar signal through another (or changed) amp. a small room could be used for voice etc as well. definitely spend some time reading up.

gullfo18 December 2010#16
there are options for the cloth - plastic behind it helps preserve HF, and if you were concerned about fibers there is micromesh type cloth which will help retain fibers better than an open cloth itself. between the inside out drywall and your existing wall - a light insulation which will dampen the cavity but not couple it. cloth goes under all the slats. for the slat cavities built on the inside out walls - you can use vertical plywood panels to build up the cavities so they are sealed (very important) and you would fill the cavity with light insulation behind the rigid insulation. the rigid insulation sits about 25mm inset from the cloth. are you floating the floor? if your considering it, instead consider an interior floor using 50mm rigid insulation on the floor and then 2x 19mm MDF with seams staggered and sealed and then engineered wood finish. the walls would sit separately on 5mm neoprene strip of 40 hardness. this separates the floor and walls and gives you options in terms of floating or not (it could be done later if you need it - for now just polish the concrete).

gullfo19 December 2010#18
with the slat resonator, you need to have sealed cavities. the simplest approach is to extend out from the stud with a plywood panel to the slat frame and then seal it all. inside the slat cavity, the rigid insulation helps esp on the lower frequency units but no need to fill the entire unit with rigid so fill out the rest with light pink insulation. yes, you want air flow through the slats so leaving some air space there before the rigid insulation helps slightly with efficiency.
