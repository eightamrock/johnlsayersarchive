I am trying to get an idea of my ceiling loading and span requirements for figuring out what kind of joists to use. I have used a calculator here: http://www.awc.org/calculators/span/cal ... cstyle.asp If I am going to have 3 layers of 5/8 gyp, that gives me a base dead load of 3.9 PSF. I am amazed how little is needed to hold this up. The calculator is telling me I can use a structural grade Douglas Fir 2x4 placed 12" OC and get a max span of 11' 6". Does this sound right?? Am I missing something basic? If this is right, then that is great! I want to loose as little ceiling height as possible and 2x4's would be better than 2x6's. Anyhow, just trying to check out my facts here..... Thanks, ashley

bassman19 November 2007#4
I knew I had to have missed something. I misread the gyp info from 5/16" instead of 5/8", DOH!!! :shock: :shock: :shock: Anyhoo, using a conservative 2.5 PSF for 5/8" Type X gyp, that gives me 7.5 PSF for three layers. Now to figure the rest of the stuff...... Ductwork: will be held by the upper floor joists. But the vents will be held up by the drywall where they go in. Vents will have fire dampers per code plus a bit of flex duct that isn't being held by the upper joists. 5lbs per damper, 2 lbs for extra metal stuff, times 4 ducts at most. Call it 30 lbs. Electrical Wiring and Lights: to minimize the penetrations, I want to use track lighting and only a few (3-4) boxes in the ceiling. I'd say 40 lbs outta cover it. Acoustic Treatment: OK, here's where there will definitely be some more weight up there.... At least one main cloud, call it 100 lbs. Toss in another 40 lbs for possible future treatments (diffusion perhaps). Round up to 150 lbs. Joists: I need to calculate the weight of the joists. I found this: http://www.csgnetwork.com/lumberweight.html Using 2x6 Pine, boardfeet=linear feet so roughly 160 boardfeet of white pine (??? they didn't have a douglas fir option) = 480 lbs green weight (round to 500) HVAC = 30 Electrical = 40 Acoustics = 150 Joists = 500 ________________ 720 Round up to 750 lbs extra weight over 203 SF. That's another 3.7 PSF dead load. That brings us up to 11.2 PSF. Still not bad a'tall. Using the Spruce/Pine/Fir structural grade 2x6 at 16"OC with a dead load of 15 PSF and a live load at the minimum 10 PSF since there is no live load on this ceiling, that gives me a span of 15' 2". That is very nice. Have I missed anything or vastly underestimated something? Thanks, ashley
