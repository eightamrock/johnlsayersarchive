Hey Juston. Welcome! Liek Ro said: read the stickies, and search the forum. Ro isn't being harsh or unfriendly: He's giving you the best advise you can possibly get. This forum is just jam-packed full of solid, sound, tried and tested information on acoustics and studio construction. There is an announcement at the top of the forum about what to do to assure getting as many responses as possible. The announcement leads to this post (click here). Actually, several people, who are experts on this forum, will not reply if you don't do what is written in that post. Many others who are very helpful, will not reply out of respect for the moderators' wishes. Use the "search" button at the top of the page to look for keywords related to your room and what you want to do in it. I have found, from personal experience, that the very best way to learn about acoustics is to just read through the forum, follow the links off-site, come back and read some more, then ask specific questions and post your own diagrams and plans of what you are about to do, for comment by others. You'll gain a much deeper understanding of what you are doing that way, and WHY you are doing it. It really is the best way to learn! - Stuart -
