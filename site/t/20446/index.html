Hi again! Since my original post was flooded with many ideas on my end (most of them crappy), i decided to create a new topic in order to better document the whole process of what is going to be my future mixing room. I went for a "reflection free" (RFZ) type of build with soffits. The room measurements are about 4.9mx3.7m and ceiling height is 2.8m. Since i have a column in the ceiling that makes it uneven i'm going to create a gypsum ceiling with heigh of 2.5m. This makes my room stand right in the middle of the bolt area (good thing i think :P) Room modes calculations: http://amroc.andymel.eu/?l=490&w=370&h=250&re=EBU%20listening%20room Design:
