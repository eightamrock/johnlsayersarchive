Here are a couple of links to some helpful websites with a lot of info for most of your questions. http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm Okay, on to your questions with the "in a nutshell" answers. :wink:
Me and my friends produce techno-electro & electro-poprock (something like that:)). So there's a lot of low frequencies and some of the vocals that we combine.
You will need some bass traps and bradband absorber units. (see "wall unit" link above)
1. Lenght of the control room in your file is same like mine (4.0 m) but width of my room is 2.40 m. Does that make any trouble?
That depends. How tall are your ceilings? Can you post a floor layout with measurements, and location of doors, windows, obstructions, etc. please?
2. Do I need to change the angles of walls or bass traps or can I leave them like they are?
Angling parallel surfaces helps to reduce/redirect reflections. If you have any parallel surfaces in your room (most likely, yes?) you will need to either angle or absorb, depending on what & where they are.
2. I'm going to use mineral wool for insulation 5cm thick. Is that OK?
Most applications have been calling for at least 5.08cm - 7.62cm thick (2"-3") and a density of 2.5 pcf (pounds per square foot). If the thicker mineral wool is not available, you may be able to double your 5cm in layers.
3. On the wall behind console you have cloth right next to insulation but on the other walls there's a little empty air space (about 2cm at the sides and about 4cm in front of the console). Is that right?
eh?
4. Which material do I need for clothes? How about cotton? Which thicknes?
Depends on what's available in your area. Out here, alot of people use Guilford of Maine materials. Burlap & weed cloth are also alternatives. What's important is that the material is acoustically transparent. If it has a loose weave, and you can blow air through it, it's probably okay. Be aware of the fire rating of the material you do choose.
5. What is a difference among cloth and black cloth in your project?
More often than not, color.
6. Which material do I need for slots? And which thicknes?
see 2nd link above
And I have just one question about studio room. We are going to record only vocals in there. Do we need to derange a geometry of the studio room like in control room or we can leave it square, like it is now?
If you have parallel surfaces in the room, you will probably need to treat them in some manner. Obviously, the subject has much more depth than what can be answered in a few short questions, and there are usually differing approaches to any given situation. If, by the way, you are still in the design phase of your project, perhaps you may want to post your questions there, and then when you're ready to build, return to this forum. Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
