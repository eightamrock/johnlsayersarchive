skylabfilmpop24 June 2009#2
I think you are way over the top, its a small space to begin with. Frame up just behind the garage door to seal it , and be sure you have a good seal on the entry door on the other side. Put down a rug, perhaps throw some FSK faced insulation in the rafters, and make some Corner traps, broadband diffusors and a cloud. The exposed brick will give some natural diffusion and great aesthetic vibe. Just add treatment until the room is comfortable, as no matter how well you design you will gave room modes to deal with.

As I'm not a big fan of long text; here's some short words: Leaves Build the frames as you posted in your picture, seems fine. you might wanna add a double stud frame for the wall seperation (where the door's in). The others should be single frame, single boards. The exterior wall is your first leave. Add as much mass to the inner walls as possible. Floor Depending on your current floor. As this is no live room I'd just add a finishing floor, that's all. your current floor is prolly concrete? That would be very good. You might even wanna paint the concrete. Read stickies to find out about floors and when to float it. Are you gonna add a second ceiling? your roof seems the weak spot in your design. Acoustics Worry about that later, focus on your floorplan first. It already looks great, you obviously did your homework :) keep on designing and asking (but keep it short, haha.)
