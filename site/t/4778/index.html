If you have a tough time getting your hands on Roxul Acoustical Fire Batt (AFB) - ≥2" (2.5 pcf), a similar/competing product is Thermafiber Sound Attenuation Fire Blankets (SAFB) - Nominal Density (2.5 pcf).
