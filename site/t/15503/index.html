gullfo27 January 2011#6
for the floor - metal and rubber mallets - tapping, measuring the amount of sound making its way out - above, below, sides. using speakers - bass/drum music + sine sweeps - measure sound below and above - how much isolation do you really have? for the construction - you want 24"oc not 16" and somehow if you want the higher STC, you'll need to do more to decouple the walls and ceiling - separate frames or isolation clips + green glue, etc.

gullfo29 January 2011#9
if you're going ahead with the sleeper on uboats, make sure you fill the cavity space sufficiently to dampen everything. otherwise you'll end up with a drum head instead of isolation. another option is simply to lay the flooring right on the insulation to dampen the entire panel. on the walls, you could float those separately or ensure the supports under them is sufficient to support then and remain decoupled. your uboats (or other isolators) need to account for that weight of the walls and ceiling.
