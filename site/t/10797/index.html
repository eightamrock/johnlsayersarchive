What is it for? Does it provide a better sound?
AVare30 June 2008#2
The purpose of a floating floor is to increase the sound isolation provided by the floor. The problem with floors is usually the low frequencies. Think of a building near a train track or truck highway. The building itself vibrates. Walls can not stop the floor from shaking, so they are floated. Floating floors are not used to get a better sound. They can make it worse if not properly designed as part of the room system. Andre
