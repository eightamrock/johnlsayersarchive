Camistan Yes the ceiling is slanted both in the "stage" area of the studio as well as the control room. I really only have had 1 year of solid work on the studio even though I started in 10-2004. That fall and winter I only fixed the bad wall, and then it sat until last spring. We really began major construction then. As for planning, you only need read Knighfly's posts to recognize that planning is "ALL". I do 90% of my work on AutoCad and Excel spreadsheets. I have been generally a month or two ahead on planning versus whatever labor I am on. As much as I have thought ahead, I missed things, fortunately nothing major, but a bit annoying.
