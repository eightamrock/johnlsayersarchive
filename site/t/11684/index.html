When building the door frames (one inner leaf, one outer leaf) to my control room with a double stud wall, what method is used for filling in the cavity between the doors on the floor and ceiling area while allowing it to remain decoupled? I'm not an expert, but I've seen it done two ways. One way is to extend the jams so that they almost touch leaving maybe a 1/8" gap. The other way is to fill the cavity with 703 wrapped in fabric.
