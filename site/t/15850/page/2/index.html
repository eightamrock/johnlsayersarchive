Soundman202018 October 2018#47
Framing usually adds about 30% to 50% of the weight of the sheathing, for a ceiling or wall, assuming typical framing. Then there's also nails, caulk, insulation, etc. That's not a huge amount of extra weight from those, but it all adds up. There's a lot more weight in a ceiling module than just the drywall. Be careful! When you are working with things that weigh a few hundred kg hanging over your head, be VERY careful. Never stand directly under it, always wear safety boots and a hard hat, always have a secondary backup that will support the object in the event that the primary fails (eg, wood bracing, ropes, winches, etc.). And make sure that whatever you use for raising the object is way over-rated for the load it will be carrying. Riggers and gaffers usually use the "ten to one" rule, meaning that the rigging hardware should be rated to support at least ten times the weight of the load itself. And check your local building codes, too: sometimes they have safety regulations in them, that you need to follow. Work safe. Not fast. - Stuart -
