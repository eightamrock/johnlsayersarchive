Hi. Please read the forum rules for posting (click here). You seem to be missing a couple of things! :)
With 10.6 ft width I don't have room to build angled walls.
Why not? Seems big enough to me. John managed to build a studio inside a shipping container with angled soffits, so I don't see why 10 feet is not enough space for that.
I have been torn since I started this project 9 months ago on if I will flush mount my monitors or not
For the best possible sound from your speakers, with the fewest possible artifacts coming form SBIR and other issues, then soffit mounting is the way to go, hands-down, no questions asked.
I have seen of people flush mounting I can't remember seeing anyone using non angled walls.
:) Yup. There's a reason for that! Everyone angles there soffit faces, because there's no other way to do it. If you don't angle your soffit faces then you cannot angle your speakers either, so they won't be aimed at your ears: you cannot get the correct speaker geometry ("triangle") if you don't angle your speakers, and if you do angle your speakers then you have to angle the soffit faces as well.
Lastly, does the top part of the soffit structure need to be sealed?
If ti cannot go up to the ceiling then it does need a lid, yes. If not, then you are sort of defeating the purpose of the soffit.
I have joists of 6 inch depth I'm leaving open to fill and cover for bass trapping / reflection control.
It sounds like you are planning to build your inner-leaf ceiling "inside-out"? That's one way of doing it. Another is to build it "conventionally" and just hang a cloud for reflection / flutter echo / modal issues. - Stuart -
