i'm not really sure what type of ceiling the guy is talking about, but my impression is this is some "office-type" acoustic ceiling which will not do a lot when it comes to soundproofing drums etc. what you need is some resilient mounted (resilient channel) mass leaf (gypsum board) below the ceiling with one airspace inbetween. (filled with insulation) i mentioned above where you get information about it. carpet upstairs would help to lower footsteps-noise from above but will not prevent drums and bass crawl upwards. and you will have to do the walls as well as it seems to prevent flanking noise. depends on how "soundsensitive" your family is. :?

the dreamer18 January 2005#6
knightfly can give you professional advice here. the guys suggesting drywall/gypsumboard are right. you will need 2 to 3 layers, preferably resilient mounted. this means on resilient channel or rsic clips with hatchannel. to make it short: you need it to be decoupled in order to prevent direct noise or flanking noise to go upwards. isolation (mineral/glasswool) is a must between drywall / ceiling. again: if you read the sticky section-stuff and the sae-stuff you will know a lot about it.
