Ethan Winer2 April 2004#3
Rio, Yes, cover the corners - all of the corners, not just the front or just the rear - with rigid fiberglass. Two inches thick is okay, but doubling them up to make each panel four inches thick is even better. And don't forget the ceiling corners. And yes, you can use the same type of panels flat on the walls to catch early reflections. --Ethan
