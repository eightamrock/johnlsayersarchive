Keeping the above in mind, manufacturers of aluminium composite panels also introduced the composite wood aluminium panels. These panels are fireproof, antistatic and anti-bacteria, just like the other composite panels. These too are made using high grade aluminium alloy and plastic. The back of the panel is non-corrosive aluminium. These wooden finish composite panels have a PE core bonded between two aluminium sheets and the front is in patterns that imitate wood. These composite panels give a different touch to the elegance of the place. They are finding use in kitchens, interior decoration and even in commercial establishments. Some manufacturers even offer you the facility of custom made panels in colors of your choice.
