You can use lightweight Muslin or open weave burlap - either will work fine and are much less expensive than Guilford. Neither is recommended for in front of speakers but in front of treatments is fine. If you get a cheaper cloth like this, plan on getting a wash in or spray on fire retardant. Anything attached to the walls should be fire retardant. GOM is Class A fire rated already.
