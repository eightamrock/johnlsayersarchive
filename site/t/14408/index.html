Questions: Is RW60 appropriate for this? I have read it´s too dense, actual is 4.4 lbs/ft3 ASTM C 553. I have tried sourcing 703 with no success. If it is not appropriate, should I trash it? Or can I use it to treat FRPs? Roxul RW60 is absolutely fine. Note that Corner Absorbers are better value for money than SuperChunks, nearly as good, much more moveable when you move on. See http://www.studiotips.com Note in the comparison between SSC and Corner Absorbers, the SSC is 34 inches wide, while the Corner AB is 24 inch. 2) I will treat all FRPs. including front, back, and cloud over mix position (CR) and cloud over drums (LR) and according suggestions I have read by Ethan Winer on other posts and his website, I am thinking of covering most of the walls in a checkerboard pattern (LR). I don't know what an FRP is! I presume Flutter and Reflection killers. Cloud over Drums, Vocal spot, and Mix position. Use a mirror and a friend to be sure where to put these. Checkerboard is good. See Ready Bags for a nice looking finish. If you are in a Metric country your insulation will be a different size. Questions ("CR"): What do you suggest I do about the weird south wall? Should I cover the entrance to the kitchen with some kind of gobo? or should I treat the kitchen´s walls with absorption? I think I will put some throw rugs on the floor…is this appropriate? Can I keep the furniture in there? Furniture is good. I will look at the other questons later. Questions("LR"): Since I can´t modify the walls in order to get rid of room modes, what do you suggest I do, given this is a rental? Gobos? What do you think about the closet? Should I stuff it with insulation? Or treat it the same as the walls (checkerboard pattern) ? Gobos would be useful for many purposes but hard enough to construct. You could use them to shut off an area when mixing. SuperChunk and Corner Absorbers are good with modes. It is worth putting FRK, Paper or Plastic film/membrane on traps to get extra LF action. I would include this on the back of RFZ and Cloud panels. An airgap behind Cloud and RFZ panels pretty much doubles the action, for free. Closet? Can you lose the doors and fill it with light cheap attic insulation? You have hard floors can ceilings. There will be flutter and modes between them. Lots of thick Clouds with FRK on the back and airgap, would be great. You will hardly be able to cover the whole ceiling so certainly some rugs will help. Audimute have large areas of cheap treatment that you can just hang on the wall. Great for temporary work. Leave an airgap behind them. Best, DD
