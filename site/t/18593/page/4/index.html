studioNorWay wrote:
I will probably be way of the original Sorbothane calculation. I am bit unsure how this is calculated and how to tighten the turnbuckles for correct tension.
As you can apply tension via the turnbuckles, you don't need to calculate. You just tighten until you get the correct deflection. Measure the height of the sorbathane prior to applying any tension/weight. Then tighten the turnbuckles until the height is 15-20% reduced. The weight of the speaker would only matter if you were resting the speakers on the sorbathane without any turnbuckles etc. If the sorbathane underneath the enclosure can't be compressed enough with the lighter speakers (unlikely to be the case), just remove an inner sorbathane pad to distribute more weight on the others. Dan
