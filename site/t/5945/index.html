Aloha Jason, Okay, here's some preliminary ramblings on my part :wink: Overall available space: L x W x H 8500mm x 3180mm x 1969mm 8500.000 millimetre [mm] equivalent to: 27.88708 foot [ft] 3180.000 millimetre [mm] equivalent to: 10.43305 foot [ft] 1969.000 millimetre [mm] equivalent to: 6.45996 foot [ft] Room Ratios - In Rod Gervais’ book “Build it Like the Pros,” he points out both L.W.Sepmeyer’s & M.M. Louden’s formulas most widely used in professional studios worldwide. He also notes that the orders listed are (top to bottom) the first to third best ratios in both cases. L.W. Sepmeyer Height Width Length 1.00 1.14 1.39 1.00 1.28 1.54 1.00 1.60 2.33 M.M. Louden Height Width Length 1.00 1.40 1.90 1.00 1.30 1.90 1.00 1.50 2.10 If one was to use the "best case" ratios for this formula, beginning with a predetermined height of 6’ 4”, your room proportions would look something like this: H x W x L (Sepmeyer) 1.00 1.14 1.39 6’4” x 7’2” x 8’8” or H x W x L (Louden) 1.00 1.40 1.90 6’4” x 8’8” x 12’ This would/could be a good starting place for your control room. Which of the two is your choice, although Mr. Louden's ratios gives you a bigger room to start with. Here's a few more bits of information that I've collected that also pertain to the control room: Setting up the Mix Position - roughly 38% (of total length of the room) from the front wall - avoid peaks/nulls (refer to Axial Waves calculations) - equilateral triangle formed by left, right/just behind mix position - monitor angles @ 60 degrees (30 degrees each) - Monitor height - center of woofer at ear level (avoid mid point of floor to ceiling measurement – either sit higher or lower if necessary) - absorption panels – left/right/over mix position - If monitors are more than 3’ in height off the floor, they must be angled down. Side walls 6-12 degrees (12 is better) Ceiling 12 degree slope to avoid "pinging" (not possible in your case) That's alot to chew on. As always, read, verify info, keep what's useful, file the rest. Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
