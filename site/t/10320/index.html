Cram13 March 2008#5
Purusha - In the building materials section of the site, there may have been a person from your area in europe that has wondered the same thing. Execute a search with keywords relating to bordering countries for the insulation product terms you're interested. Rokwool/Fiberglass - These products do not pass easily through or get expelled from our systems well or at all, so one answer to your question is - yes, these products if inhaled in large quantity can hurt your resp. system. In the sticky section for reference links, there is a study mentioned to support all of this. The detail is not my expertise. I only know my experience and the general concept of how it can hurt. That being said - A good resporator/mask that forms a nice seal around your face to cover the mouth and nose will stop anything from getting into your lungs. Long sleeves, pants & a simple hat or hood will stop irritation from affecting you too much. Eyes may be a concern so you could get tight conforming safety glasses. The breathing is the most important part. Which is why we wouldn't want to line a conduit (muffler, insulation box for HVAC for example) with uncovered rock wool or fiber board. The fibers would become airborn. My experience with this stuff is about 3 house construction projects from start to finish. and many other rooms & additions for spotty work I used to do in order to help me through college. (And help my family out). I'm not a pro of 30 years or anything, but I don't worry too much about it because I feel confident in my interaction with the stuff. Take that for what it's worth. Check out the building materials section. Call a manufacturer like roxul or any other's you are interested in if you like, to see if they have distribution in your area. Be sure to post information if you find it. ;)
