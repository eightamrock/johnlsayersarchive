Hello Everyone, I have been allotted this space to build two audio suites for mixing TV commercials and shows plus a voiceover booth and machine room. Currently, I'm in the planning stage. I'm just not sure what is the best layout. The space is on the 2nd floor of a concrete and brick building. One wall has windows (facing a small quiet lane), another is double brick. I have included a floorplan of the whole floor. My area is marked in red. Plus a zoom in and a rough layout that I came up with. The blue square is a column and its position is approximate. All partitions shown in black on the floor plan are sheetrock walls and will be torn down. Floor and ceilings are concrete. Also, construction will probably be done in two phases. Initially, the first control room, booth, machine room and perhaps the shell of the second control room will be built. Don't know if this info is superflous or not. Just figured I'd throw it out there. Eventually, the rooms will be set up for 5.1. The control rooms don't need to be completely quiet (after all, nobody watches TV in complete silence!). I'm planning on keeping the windows. The booth should be reasonably quiet. By the way, I'm not married to the shared booth idea. It just seemed like a good way to save money, space and time. So my questions are: What is the most efficient layout for this space? How can I apply one of John's designs to the space? Is it feasable to build the first control room and booth for $20,000 USD. The measurements are in feet and I quote in US dollars but I am located in Sydney, Australia. I'm a recently transplanted Yank.
