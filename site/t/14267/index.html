adam28 April 2010#5
Looks good Bij! It's a funny thing about this forum - if you post something wrong you'll get immediate responses, but if it looks right then everyone sits back & watches. Congrats on taking the time to study up, learn Sketchup, and get it right. About the ceiling beam, you need to find span tables for the timber available in your area, to see what you'll need to support the load. Your timber supplier should be able to help. Here in Australia I would use LVL beams (laminated veneer lumber) for those. The manufacturer publishes span tables as well as software which tells you what size it needs to be, based on your span & load. Of course, it may also be more economical to use something else like steel, especially if you have limited headroom.
