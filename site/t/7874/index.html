sharward7 January 2007#2
There are too many variables to answer your question. By writing "how much sound this will be keeping out of the building," I assume you're mean you are focused on preventing outside noise from leaking into the studio. Does that mean you do not care at all about keeping the noise from the studio from leaking to the outside of the building? What are the conditions outside of the studio? How loud is the environment? Are you next to a busy street? A freeway? Train tracks? An airport? A high school with a sports field that is totally exempt from local noise ordinances and a PA system they use at sporting events with reckless impunity? Oh, sorry... That was our last house... :roll: Are you on the ground floor? What's overhead? Remember that sound doesn't just travel sideways. Your walls are only part of the equation. --Keith :mrgreen:
