welcome Matt! if you could trim your signature down a bit it will help improve overall site performance - text is usually best. on the design, it looks like a really nice space where building out a proper set of isolation structures should be pretty straight forward. on the budget side, in mosts cases a good rule of thumb is to take the average ft2 cost for a high quality home to determine what your construction costs may be. so if a high end home in your area is $250/ft2 then you're probably looking at $600K+ for a studio of around 2500ft2 and probably $25K-45K for a proper design. since you're looking to do most of design and the labor and manage the construction, you're probably looking mainly at materials costs which could be $350K+. then if you consider maybe going down the scale - lower isolation or smaller rooms, site built windows and doors, lots of sweat equity etc. you might be able to move this down to say $250K or so - again highly dependent on the finish expected. not trying to scare you off but it's better to consider the probable costs now rather than try to go back and ask for more money later. since this building is a thin metal structure, you should take some noise measurements to gauge how much isolation you really need and determine if you have any LF noise or vibrations coming in.
