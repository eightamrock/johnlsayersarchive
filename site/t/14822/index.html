Soundman202019 September 2010#3
Agreeing totally with what Steve said (slow down! design it well, and only then build it). And adding that I'd take out the frame that you have in the bottom of the closet itself: you certainly don't want to build another resonant cavities inside what is already a resonant cavity! If that was going to be a floating floor, then forget it: that isn't the way to do it, you most likely do not need to float your floor anyway, and if you actually do need to float it, then you are going to nee a huge budget: you cannot float a floor successfully without spending lots if money... - Stuart -
