Hi Wayne, We moved here in February of this year, and haven't seen any signs of seepage yet. So I don't think it's going to be a problem. We did seal the floor anyway to help thwart off any moisture. People also have asked if I was worried about hurricanes down here on the coast. The answer is yes, somewhat. We are about 7 miles off the coast, and about 70 feet above sea level. So it's something to think about, but not a show-stopper. I rode out Katrina in our old house, and they are definitely not a force to ignore. But I think we'll be OK; this building has been there for 10 plus years with no problems (yet). I'm just plotting and planning here around the holidays, while the friends and family visit. Between that and my daytime job, I won't have much time to do any actual construction. :cry: I hope to get started the week between Christmas and New Years, but we'll see.
