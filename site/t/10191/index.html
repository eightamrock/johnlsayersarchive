orangenumerik14 February 2008#2
Hi GMo First, congrat on your project. Can you please resize your pics to the forum rules. Your thread would be more suited in the design or acoustic forums, but i'll give it a shot anyway. Leave the front wall of your room flat in the corners so you'll have more space for the bass traps that are going there. Due to your room size, your rear wall will have to be made almost dead. And i would inverse the angled in your rear walls, putting the peak toward the inside of the room. 1) Well i'm axious to hear someone who can predict the way mode would behave in irregular rooms. I don't think you'll get a definitive answer on this one. 2)There are many school of thoughts on that subject. From my point of view there are serious benefit from using slanted ceiling, providing you have high ceiling. But you don't have the height to built one without compromising your room volume, wich is already small IMHO. I'd go with a flat with acoustic treatment. 3) Roughly, Corners for bass traps, side wall for slat resonators, other members are more knowledgable than me on this topic, ask Gulfo, John, Ethan, or Eric Desart in the acoustic forum. Regards
