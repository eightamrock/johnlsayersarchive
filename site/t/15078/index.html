jhbrandt9 November 2010#2
Fx, As a rule, I recommend a minimum of 42 cubic meters for a critical listening/mix space. 1. The calculations are valid for any dimension but they assume a perfectly reflective enclosure. I do not hold 'stock' in any so-called ideal ratio. These ratios are starting points. Many times, given real world situations/dimensions, the ratios can produce a bad acoustical environment. Never go by ratio alone. Use a good mode calculator and be sure that you understand it. :wink: 2. These must be the internal dimensions to the hard shell internal wall surface. Cheers, John
