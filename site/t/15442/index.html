Ted White31 December 2010#2
Hi. I don't see a lighter wall giving you 70dB at 80Hz. Your wall would need to be massive and deep and have a fundamental low frequency resonance at 50Hz or so. A double stud wall with double 5/8" board on either side and insulation would get you to a theoretic 35Hz resonance (Fmam) or so, however the Transmission Loss at 80Hz would not nearly be 70dB. You might get 40dB. Adding a third sheet to each side might get you 45dB. These are very rough approximations.

700kg/m2? AAC is about 20% of the weight of regular block not including any mortar, fill, grouting, rendering, etc needed. i think that helps but you'd want to verify that #. also, you want to investigate the structural sound transfer to determine if you need to float or floor or not. it might be more effective to isolate the basement ceiling instead. you need to consider the weight of the structure and ducting, plumbing, wiring etc etc in the basement if you will be suspending any of that off the floor above.
