Aloha and welcome to the forum,
'll be glad if someone could give me some pointers if I really need bass-traps in every four corners of the control room (hollow spaces shown in the picture).
In a room that size you will need as much bass trapping as you can get. You may also want to consider side wall slat resonators and perhaps a couple of clouds over the mix position as well.
Please give me some input. Is there something I have forgotten besides putting the doors and speakers in place.
You may want to consider a similar kind of treatment for your iso booth as well - slat resonators/bass traps/cloud. Here are a couple of good sites for more info - http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm - [slat resonators, bass traps] http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html - [Be sure to click on the "acoustics", "absorbers" & "construction" tabs in the left margins, and the tabs that appear at the top] Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
