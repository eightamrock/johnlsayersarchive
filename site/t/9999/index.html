orangenumerik14 January 2008#2
Rune W Yes it is a weak spot in terms of mass. In all case make sure it is sealed But your question all depends on the goal your trying to achieve. Is it important that the sound is not disturbing the sourroundings of the house ? Or are you just trying to keep the sound from escaping your home theatre thru the other rooms of your house. What are the "normal condition" of listening in that room, is it very loud ? if it,s not maybe you can just leave it like that. If you go for it, just make sure you put the GB direcly on the wood joist, do not create a situation where you would make another air space between your newly instaled GB and the wood joist. With the info i have this is my answer, hope it helps. regards
