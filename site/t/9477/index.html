John Sayers30 September 2007#5
nyves - from your pdf file it looks like the glass ceiling is well behind you and therefore shouldn't be a problem provided the rear of the room is absorption thus stopping any returns from the rear. Placing a diffusor above your listening position WOULD be a problem IMO as the scattering of reflected frequencies would cloud your imaging.
