jbassino30 June 2009#20
Often, John splays the side walls with an angle to reduce early reflections on mixspot and flutter echo, but that's being done since the splayed walls are hard surfaces like slot resonators. If you were to put insulation covered with cloth walls (angled) they won't reduce early reflections and flutter echo because they are angled.. its because the are absorptive. catch me? So, if you want to finish with slot resonators on your side walls, the best way to do it is like John told you: Build it a rectangle, and splay the inner treatment since it will be a slot resonator (hard surface) I hope its more clear now
