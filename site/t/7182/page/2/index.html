Ro23 December 2006#34
Hanging boards with an angle will DEFLECT sound (not diffuse) so it'll do something but I'd bet my money on absorption first. Are these only wood or is there going to be some 703 (or alike) hanging there? Absorption will take care of flutter better than deflection ps. That window looks like one giant sound leak (no mass)
