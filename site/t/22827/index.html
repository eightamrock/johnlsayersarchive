Could you recreate the offset on the opposite side, nudge the traps and diffuser along to accommodate, and then extend both corner traps out so that both "offsets" are behind the corner traps? As for 5 or 7 fractals... well... it's up to you. What do you want to do? Here's my oversimplified opinion on it: Any amount of diffusion is going to colour the direct sound (which I am not a fan of) so how much colour do you want?

Well all you need to do is stop HF reflecting off... I don't know the size of that bit which is offset but I'm assuming it's no wider than about 100mm, which means most of the frequency spectrum will diffract around it into your deep rear trapping anyway, and any frequencies that don't will be absorbed by the 100mm or so of absorbent. Just put some fluffy and make it look like it's part of the bigger traps. Making it symmetrical is definitely the way I would go.
