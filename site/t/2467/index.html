Hello! This site is great, I've been lurking for a short while and I think I'm ready to convert my garage to a studio with multiple rooms. What I have is a 29.5' x 29.5' interior, excellent condition concrete floor, 9' ceilings, and a stairwell heading up to a finished room over. The a/c unit is located in an attic above the garage, but there are two vents currently poking down into the garage area. The intake however is upstairs in the loft room. I figure the upstairs vents will be detached to dedicate the HVAC system solely to the studio, and I will use window / area units upstairs for the rare occassion it's occupied. There is a 5" beam running across the ceiling, a garage door that I plan on removing and closing in, and several windows that I plan on removing as well. There is also a decorative brick structure 12" tall that runs around the interior of the room walls with a 2x4 painted sitting atop that. What I'm looking for is a control room, large studio space, and a smaller room that could double as a drum booth or hold loud amps. The stairwell area is pretty much a must as it allows people to enter the back door and not have access to the studio but allow them upstairs to the loft. I am attaching eyeball drawn pics that aren't exact, but represent rough dimensions that I would like to see acheived. I really have no clue where to start with this, I'm having a friend come down soon who owns a construction company and want to be prepared with things to ask for. My budget is $15,000 to $25,000 maximum. Obviously, I'd like to do it for $5k if possible! I live in the country and my neighbors don't complain in my untreated loft space when I play drums and record at night. I don't need isolation like a gun practice range, but I want good separation, quiet air system, etc. Any advice appreciated. I searched and searched but couldn't find anyone with near my dimensions etc. War PS: The space dedicated for the stairs doesn't actually exist, it's in both pictures though as I want to keep this space only for loft entrance.
