Aloha J,
I thought about treating all the walls of the vocal booth with rockwool slabs, basically make it really dead sounding. Is that any good?
From what I've read, and have been advised, yes, dead is better. However, what you do want to do is avoid having a "square" booth.
Be careful, a dead square booth will have low end issues (you'll hear the issues on your mic.) Bryan Giles FOH Live, Live Remote & Studio Engineer Producer
Yeah, 4x5x7 feet inside dim's is about the best modal compromise for a small booth - Steve (Knightfly)
As for your control room area, perhaps you could try a modular approach using the wall units at http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm This is the approach I based my acoustic treatments from. If you care to check it out you can find it here: http://johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic ... ght=#15598 and here: http://johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=4091 Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
