Here is the link: viewtopic.php?f=2&t=8173 There is a lot of good information in the linked-post for someone who may have real concerns. A plane, large or small, is over your head radiating noise downward so the roof/ceiling is an important place to keep in mind while gathering information on how to best approach your build. There are ways around the floating floor with concrete dilemma but you will have to educate yourself on those, once you can determine what it is you are up against and what may be the best use of materials and practice to help in your isolation.
