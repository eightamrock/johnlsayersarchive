gullfo21 October 2006#2
definitely get Rod's book... one option is to polish the concrete - you can stain it and polish to get a hard durable finish and then add rugs as needed. depends on your temperatures - concrete might be too cold. as far as float - it depends and it could be expensive and less effective than not floating. it may just make sense to add a finished wood floor over a subfloor - probably about a 1 1/2" loss of height.
