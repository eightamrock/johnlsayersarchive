Soundman202026 July 2011#4
is there anything you'd change about my design?
Yup! I would definitely NOT have the rear end of the room narrowing down like that! That is going to cause you big issues in several ways. Rather, take your isolation wall to the corners of the existing room, and use those corners for superchunk bass traps. I'd also soffit mount the speakers, and try to get better symmetry around the desk and rack up front. Symmetry is very important in the front half of the room, and it looks like that design of desk and rack might upset things, since one speaker is firing over the desk surface while the other is firing over empty space. Maybe you could build the rack into the desk top, with some equipment on each side, or put it under the desk extend the desktop to cover it). Your listening position is also in a really bad place: it seems to be in the middle of the room! You should fix that too, and get your speaker / listening position geometry right. For example, your speakers are too high above the floor. - Stuart -
