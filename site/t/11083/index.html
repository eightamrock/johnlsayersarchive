Buying house. Is this a good control room?

Started by grant on 12 August 2008. 3 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 11083.

grant12 August 2008#1
I'm house shopping and am looking for a house that will allow me to mix in a good control room. Recording/studio spaces may follow later but are not yet a concern. I'm looking at a house that has an already finished room with dimensions: W 11.5 ft L 19.29 ft H 9 ft I want to know if this will acoustically be a good choice. I mix largely symphonic music as well as rock. My primary concern is having a good mix room. I'm not as concerned about disrupting neighbors, family, etc. I don't anticipate outside noise leaking in to be a large concern either. It's mostly about how the room affects my mix decisions. I know I will need to treat the room acoustically for early reflections, and with bass traps and the like. I believe walls and ceiling are 5/8 or 1/2" gypsum board. It's reasonable to assume that it's 2X4 wood stud construction so let's assume that. Floor is carpet but is something I may later upgrade to hardwood. One long wall--the right wall--has about three windows that are approximately 3ft X 4 ft. (I should have measured them!) And the back wall (the short one) has one window centered that is also about 3ft by 4ft. I realize that with three windows in the right wall and none in the left that I'll need to treat the windows and the opposite wall symmetrically. I assume this isn't a huge problem as at least one of them is where side wall acoustic treatment would need to be placed anyway. Budget. Originally I was going to strive for an unfinished basement and build it there. (In fact, I built my previous home and had ten foot ceilings in the basement but was unable to finish due to long hours working and commuting. And now I must move.) I planned to allocate at least $6,000 to it and would gladly add to that budget in years to come. The same budget applies here. But now I'm looking at modifying a finished room. And if I don't get this house, I may be asking here about another house soon. Oh yeah, I'm mixing stereo. No surround yet. Only the slight possibility of surround in the future; but it's not a design concern yet. So the big quesiston is this: do you think this could be a good mix room? How do I qualify "good?" Well, I'm a full time professional sound engineer and this will be used for paying clients. So it needs to be good but I realize it won't be world-class. Thanks, everyone. Grant Colorado, USA.
