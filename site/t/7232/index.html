knightfly9 October 2006#4
Welcome; trust me, Rod's book will MORE than repay its cost. Meantime, with that much NATURAL isolation (inverse square law applies here) forget a floated floor unless you're planning a separate control room and want max isolation between it and the drums, for micing purposes (it's much easier to mic a set of drums if you can NOT hear them coming thru your structure, but ONLY thru your speakers). Only other reason for a floated floor would be if you have heavy traffic rumbling by. I'd spend more on ceiling and walls, the "stickies" and Rod's book will be mandatory reading on this. Plan on a double layer of sheathing outside your frame for starters, including strong enough roof framing for double sheathing there too. This can be your OUTER leaf, so should be more massive than normal. Then inner SEPARATE frames for walls with a SEPARATE inner ceiling frame strong enough for 2 layers of wallboard, lotsa insulation, and you're on your way... Steve
