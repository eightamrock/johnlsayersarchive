Ro3 August 2011#4
If you have them filled with sand, concrete or anything else heavy it will do. For speakers you want heavy, rigid stands. You don't want the vibrations of the speaker to go into the stands and make them vibrate as well. If yer gonna mount speakers on'm, place a spring between speaker and stand if possible to break connections.
