lilith_envy22 June 2009#2
Ha funny thing that. I was sitting in my half built control room last night (as you do) staring up at my ROOF, soon to be ceiling thinking......That it would be so much easier to do a standard ceiling with clouds rather than attempting an inside-out one. ( I have inside-out walls ) And probably much cheaper! But you will either need to pre-plan that your drywall will over hang the height of the walls by 140mm (height of joist) and somehow make a very good seal once you lay the ceiling drywall on top. Assuming your have plenty of crawl space above your ceiling height for a person and drill. What other guys have done is, simply put blocking in-between each joist (joist off cuts) sealing around them. This also helps with keeping the joist plum and if your handy with the nail gun you might not need brackets. Which will save a few $$$ John’s suggestion is to attach ceiling joist as normal, then build light weight frames that insert in between each rafter. Insulation in the frame cloth stapled over top. My concern wit this is the extra timber for the frames and the not so neat finish that a drooping cloth ceiling has. As I’m a solo builder, a dry wall lifter and standard ceiling will be heaps easier for me.
