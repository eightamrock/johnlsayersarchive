Hello everybody - what a great useful forum - thank you! :) I would like to share my project (before construction time). We have big cool place in industrial building (no problems with neighbors) - it is 270m2 4,1m high - 4th floor (but great strong industrial ceiling 800kg/m2) Plan is to have 3 rooms plus control room plus some relax cafe/bar place , stage, office. It will be place where musicians can practice in clean good rooms with record possibility and than relax with beer, meet other people and even play on some meetings concerts, workshops. Thats why we need good isolation between rooms (not because of neighbors). Rooms in project are 3,5m high (all dimension based on rooms modes) - rooms in rooms MAM- double plasterboard (2x1,25cm), wood construction firs wall 5x10cm slabs with 80cm ISOVER PT80 (80kg/m3) than 30cm(sometimes even more) air space and again second wall with isover and plasterboard. Outer rooms wall is higher (to the ceiling) 4,1m. Doors will be strong custom steel with sand inside (more than 100kg). Than we will do some basstraps and diffusion to have good sound inside. My project is in autocad - it can be little confusing - but it is really easy :) - look: green is wood, black is outer construction concrete wall, circles are columns building construction, yellow -isover, red plasterboard. Whole construction will sit on 1,5cm rubber on construction flor(not on floating floor). Floor - floating 10cm concrete slab on 10cm isover pt80 isolation - it is max weight here i think. All dimensions are in meters. I can do some zooms - and dimensions in feet/inches if You want. Will try also skethup basic project. How it is look for You? What You think about that project and isolation? What is weak point here? All opinions are welcome! I will do blog with details etc. - ive done my best and read a lot but now need opinion from outside:) best! nextaz

Seems you have it figured out, good luck. On the fourth floor of a concrete structure, you are asking a lot from pretty much everyone that gets involved. I have worked in these kinds of places, the wait for an elevator can take hours some times. And the material load is HUGE.
