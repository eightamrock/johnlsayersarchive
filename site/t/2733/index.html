Tom Zartler30 November 2004#3
I just started working with a structural engineer to remove a post in my basement. That's who you'll need to talk to for your info. I drew up plans of the shell of my basement and where the beams and posts sat which saved him a trip to my house and me a couple of $. One thing to find out is will the town where you live require you to present a set of prints with an architect's stamp on it. Another option is to talk to a builder in your area to get an idea of cost. Good Luck, Tom
