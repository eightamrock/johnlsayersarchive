Ethan Winer6 June 2007#2
Bob, No need to worry about treating specific frequencies in a room that size. The best solution is broadband absorption anyway. Why? Because modes account for only some of the peaks and nulls. But you can have peaks and nulls at any frequency. You name a frequency and I promise I'll be able to find a place in your room where there's a peak and another place where there's a null. Likewise for each location. You choose a spot and I'll find a frequency that peaks there and another that nulls. --Ethan
