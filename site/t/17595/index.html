Hi Matt, and welcome to the forum! :) And also sorry for the lack of response! Lots of things going on here lately, and it looks like I'm the only active moderator right now...
I've put in a small mezzanine floor from the control room out into the live room
I would really try to change that: move the CR down to ground level and pout something else up there (office, storage, etc). The issue with building on the second floor is firstly isolation (hard to do), and secondly weight (structural issues). It would make your life a whole lot easier if you keep everything on the ground. Your split level also introduces sight-line issues. - Stuart -
