John Sayers12 July 2005#10
Lex - if you look closely at Blue Bear you will see there are two sized slats - 6" and 4". The gaps don't vary because the depth off the wall varies as those slots are angled to the wall. It's not about aiming for 200hz - it's about having a broadband across the 200hz range so you have one set of slots working in the 150 - 300hz area and another in the 300 - 500hz area and the 703 does the rest in the upper range, 500hz up. If you take a look at the stickey with the 3D basement studio you'll see how to build your slots. hope this helps. cheers john
