barefoot21 January 2004#4
Mastering typically requires very accurate full range speakers capable of solid reproduction below 40 Hz. So $400 each is a bit tight for mastering speakers, but we'll see what we can do. To get enough performance in this price range I recommend a 2-way, dual midbass kit with high excursion 6.5" or 7" drivers. Here are a few sites with some models to consider: http://www.zalytron.com/ Usher U-2.5 Hiquphon - Mentor6 http://www.northcreekmusic.com/ Borealis - North D28 Project http://www.speakercity.com/catalog.shtml Scan Speak MTM-18 Thomas
