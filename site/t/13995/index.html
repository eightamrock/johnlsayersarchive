rod gervais26 January 2010#3
John, even if their claims are real - the numbers blow it out of the water Their cheapest product (the 510 - 1/2" thick) costs 49.95 per sheet per 4 x 8 sheet (for bulk buys). The good stuff I have seen priced at 135 usd per 4 x 8 sheet. Green Glue did lab test comparisons of their product vrs this product (see the results here: http://www.greengluecompany.com/greenGl ... Panels.php ) and GG beat them hands down in the lower frequencies - fairly equal in the upper - but then again - we all know the upper frequencies are the easy to deal with areas. Price wise - for the 5/8" product tested by GG - the cost of the standard GG wall (db 5/8" each side with GG on both sides versus the Quietrock 5/8" both sides was a no brainer...... GG cost per square foot of wall - 2.24 per s.f. Quietrock - cost per square foot of wall - 5.86 per s.f., lets see - i can spend 3.62 per square foot more for a product that under-performs in the LF range - hummm - that's a hard call. And (as i said) that is not the expensive stuff......... Isaid it in my book and will say it again here - this stuf will perform - but there is no way at the cost per product that i would EVER consider using this product. Rod
