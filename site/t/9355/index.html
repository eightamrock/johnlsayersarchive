I agree with John, and will add one more reason I prefer wood - In order for steel framing to be costs effetive it has to be lightweight - at least in this part of the world anyway......... Lightweight steel (25ga) iso walls do not have the same TL values for LF transmissions as wood frame - and this is our problem area when it comes to isolation. Rod
