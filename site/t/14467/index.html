Hi Neal, I'm going to speak from what I know as a Builder in Australia. Not sure what kind of warranties your house does have but unless you are altering structural components or wiring work you do should not void a warranty. Of course, do not take my word for this, just my opinion on your concern. Creating a dangerous situation - If you are not confident enough in your skills get proper contractors. If you know proper tradespeople, maybe they can come in and advise you, have a look at what you have done and advise accordingly. Resale - I presume any house with a studio is not going to appeal to everyone. So long as the people who buy it wont have to tear half the house down or spend a lot of money to change the studio into a more suitable room, It should not effect it that much. Again, only from my experience. of course, never try electricals if you have no idea especially in a highly massed and insulated wall. Hope this is some food for thought. Paul
