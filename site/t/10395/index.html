gullfo30 March 2008#15
if you build the corner traps - superchunk style or spanning the corner with 4" 3pcf rigid insulation, you'll have a bunch of absorption for quite a range (called broadband trapping). then on the walls and ceilings, place some absorbers (say 2'x4'x4") about 2' apart so you have absorption and wall. make the opposite walls staggered so wall reflects to absorber. on the ceiling, just space the absorbers about 1' apart over the kit. see what happens, you can add more absorption if you need it. you really don't want a dead room for drums... you could also build some slats across the corners to change the wall angles plus add absorption without making the room dead. then you could still add porous absorption to deaden it when needed. http://img444.imageshack.us/img444/6908/petercrosbierender1gw3.th.jpg http://img440.imageshack.us/img440/691/pearlsnaprendertz6.th.jpg http://img442.imageshack.us/img442/1661/alejorendersp6.th.jpg http://img144.imageshack.us/img144/4297/alejotg7.th.jpg http://img184.imageshack.us/img184/6566/petercrosbierender2fe9.th.jpg
