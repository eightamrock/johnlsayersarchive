xSpace14 October 2009#7
Jason, I haven't followed or considered your design, I am reading your words. It is a habit of mine :) "For the ceiling I was reading on Ethan Winer's acoustics page that for a low ceiling your best bet is to cover it with 2" fiberglass with a gap between the ceiling." Essentially you want to remove the hard boundary(the ceiling), this is achieved with the 2" rigid fiberglass. The gap allows the absorption to go to a lower frequency...and pushing frequencies down is your goal. But, it is not the complete truth in what you are trying to establish in such a low profile room. There are things happening above the ceiling that can help to A: make it taller and B: make it just as or more absorptive. It is a process and it depends on how much you want to get out of it. I came here a few years ago and haven't finished my personal space yet!!! But what I have is solid, correct and above reproach :)
