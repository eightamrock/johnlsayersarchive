knightfly13 November 2006#2
Not familiar with the term, "rigips" - is this a typo of "rigid" or something else? Concrete maybe? Treatment of any room first requires that you know what you already have - for this, you need some sort of test gear/software/mic. You would then place a speaker (driven by test gear) in one low corner, and your test mic (omni pattern) in the opposite HIGH corner, and test for RT60 - The goal should be an even reverb time across the audio spectrum, for live rooms somewhere in the range of 0.3 sec (dry) to 0.5 sec. (wet) - for control rooms, a shorter time is best (so you don't think that the reverb your CR is adding is actually RECORDED ) Basically, we'd need a lot more info about your rooms AND your equipment before we can make useful suggestions... Steve
