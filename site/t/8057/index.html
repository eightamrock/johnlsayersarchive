Greetings to all I'm going to make some sealed box panel absorbers but have a question regarding the insulation. As I understand it the insulation makes the Q wider, therefore absorbing over a wider range. If my panel is 90mm deep, insulation is 50mm - Is it OK to mount the insulation material against the wall - leaving a 40mm gap between the membrane of the panel and the insulation? Would there be any benefit is having the insulation mounted away from the wall? I'm guessing not but any advice is appreciated. Mudrock

jwl25 January 2007#3
my understanding is that you want the insulation to be as close as possible to the membrane, without actually touching it. I don't think distance away from the membrane will affect Q or resonance frequency, as much as the amount of absorption given. The point of the insulation in a sealed-panel design is to dampen the resonance of the membrane, and if the insulation is too far away it will be less effective at this.
