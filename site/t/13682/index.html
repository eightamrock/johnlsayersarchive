"Now, is the Damp-proof membrane (and the fact that its all rumped as I couldn't get it to lay completly flat, gonna cause the acoustic caulk seals to break in time? Will the second Backer-Rod and Caulk around the perimeter of the 18mm OSB be okay?" Depends. Is the damp proof membrane run up the wall structure or does the wall sit on top of the barrier? As long as the top side of the barrier stays dry the caulk should remain effective. But if this membrane is terminated in some way, or cut short, in such a way that if there is moisture buildup underneath the barrier (on top of the concrete) then it will most likely seep out to the edges of the barrier...that is where the problems with this type of procedure will begin. If the leakage moves to the edge of the barrier and at the edges is where your caulk and backer rod is then it [moisture] could very well reduce the effectiveness of the caulking. There may be an issue with mold/smells and rot depending on the installation methods you used and how much attention was given to what is happening under this barrier.
