Should the ceiling above the reflective and open areas be at an angle to the floor?
I don't understand the question. The only part that has to angled is the reflective surface. You can make the absorbent area parallel or angled. The upper ceiling is the current one. Make a copy of the drawing and mark it up to show what you mean.
How hard of a reflective surface are we taling about? Sheetrock or wood?
Either will work. It is not critical. The more massive it is to reflect lows in a similar fashion, the better.
If my structural ceiling is at 10', where should the "dropped" ceiling start and end at? (height).
The question is vague. The drawing is done to scale. The back absorbent area is 9' high if that helps answer your question.
I'm not using soffit mounted monitors, going with nearfields for this room.
Not critical. The speaker location is for determining the locations of the speakers, reflective surface angles and listening position. Andre
