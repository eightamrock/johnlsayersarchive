len-morgan22 January 2006#7
If you think about it, WHY would you want two panes of glass in the first place? The answer is to decouple the sound from one side of one wall to the other. If you only have one wall, I don't think you're going to have anything to decouple. If you create a sound that vibrates the first pane, that vibration is going to "shake" the frame more than the air between the two panes, and the frame is the same one in your case. From your pictures, it looks like you could build a wall between the two rooms without too much trouble. If the ceiling joists run left and right in the pictures, you could even cut the current ceiling in between the two walls so you'd almost have a double wall construct and the ceiling and walls wouldn't couple to each other. It wouldn't cost that much in floor space. len

knightfly24 January 2006#12
On the single frame, double glass - the Auralex link is the better way. If you stop and think about it, a single layer of 5/8 gypsum has approx. STC 28 - adding a second layer, on single frame without RC, changes it to STC 42, assuming 4" air gap. The TL at 50 hZ goes up from 15 to 22 dB with the second leaf. This is on the SAME frame. The laminated glass is better damped than plate, so will behave better at resonance. The 1/4" thickness of glass will give about the same performance as 3/4" gypsum, probably slightly better - so two leaves of this glass on the same frame, 4" apart, would come in at around STC40 to 42, with low end at around 24 dB or so. A single pane of 1/4 glass would be about STC 27 considering less damping than gypsum. HTH... Steve

len-morgan25 January 2006#14
I don't think I would use PVC pipe at all for such a short distance. It conducts sound pretty well and you'd have a "hard" connection between the two rooms (the PVC itself). I think you'd be better off just pushing the snake through, leave a pull string if you want to add something later. Better yet, buy some snake cable and install it now - you don't have to wire it, just have it in place. For what you'd spend in time and money putting in the PVC, you'd pay for a pretty good length of bulk snake cable. len
