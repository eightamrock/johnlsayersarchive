knightfly31 July 2004#2
If it's bearings that are going out, try reversing the flow - most fans expect to see more static pressure at the outlet than at the inlet; by reversing the flow (turning the fan around) you will preload the motor bearings to the opposite side, and the fan may like that better.) If it's failing electrically, there is just too much restriction for that specific fan, and you would need to find a different one. Possibly something in the centrifugal (squirrel cage) type, if you're not already there. A few more details of opening size, box size, fan type, etc, would help... Steve
