gullfo10 December 2010#52
use the pink stuff. 3/4-1.2lb/ft3 (12-18kg/m3) density. the air is a critical component. using the denser stuff can end up coupling the walls and the lighter stuff has better TL for LF in many cases. if you have a lot of MF and HF coming through it might make sense to go with the denser stuff but then you may have other issues (like lack of mass or some coincident resonances due to construction or materials). another reason for choosing the denser stuff - availability sometimes the heavier mineral / rock wool is available for less or the same than the lighter fiberglass and you have to fit it into your budget and/or the designer has deemed it necessary - trade offs.

gullfo20 December 2010#72
yes, a bunch of 2x4x4" panels and spaced off the surface 4" where possible. leaving space between them and also make sure the facing surfaces have staggered arrangements so no two surfaces are directly facing each other (avoid slap echoes). there are numerous examples of absorber construction on this site - do search on the acoustics forum or simply scan the stickies there. if you can leave the sides of the absorbers open, then that will help to expose more of the absorbing surfaces.
