Ro10 August 2011#152
consider: you have a beefed-up double stud wall with both 2 layers of 5/8 GB. Then you make a gap in it. To make up for that leak you need to beef up again. Hanging a 3/4ply box on it will be step 1. Surrounding only the outer shell with another layer of 5/8GB equals (give or take) one wall. The 3/4ply weights about the same as the 5/8GB. To compensate for the OTHER wall, that also has a gap, you need to beef up the box with another set of 2x5/8GB. Since your box isn't a separated construction (as the walls are) you still are under-isolated. . . . you have the same amount of mass on the box but it lacks the space to separate layers. Oh well, sometimes you gotta live with what you got. It's "just" a little gap, right.. hmm. (adding gb as your last 2 options will not do the trick, the gap stays the same). bottom line: try, atleast, to match the mass of the whole wall(s) construction. and not only on the box.... gotta box in your ducts too. woooot? uhm. yeah.... or else your ducts will be the weak point. Put wool in there too to dampen the resonating-ducts..) aah, don't you hate it.

The box is small enough not to make it a disturbing 3-leaf installation. Like I said, as the box becomes part of the wall, surround it with GB that equals your wall. Skip the top/bottom pieces and fold around the box with more gb (add it to the top/bottom). Sound will go through your wall 'cuz of that new hole you created. Since there's another big hole in there, it'll pass through and enters your silencer box. Now if you don't isolate that box well enough it'll creep through the box and becomes a soundpath after-all. THEN it'll pass through the hole in the silencer and enters your ducts... now, you told me your ductwork is INSIDE the wall, but your pics tell another story.
