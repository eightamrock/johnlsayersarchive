Aloha, Hey, thanks for editing your profile. :wink: In Rod Gervais’ book “Build it Like the Pros,” http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=5670 he points out both L.W.Sepmeyer’s & M.M. Louden’s formulas most widely used in professional studios worldwide. He also notes that the orders listed are (top to bottom) the first to third best ratios in both cases. L.W. Sepmeyer Height Width Length 1.00 1.14 1.39 - 7’7” x 8’6” x 10’5” 1.00 1.28 1.54 - 7’7” x 9’7” x 11’7” 1.00 1.60 2.33 M.M. Louden Height Width Length 1.00 1.40 1.90 1.00 1.30 1.90 - 7’7” x 9’8” x 14’4” 1.00 1.50 2.10 In your case, L.W. Sepmeyer's 2nd ratio is almost a perfect fit, so perhaps something like this for starters? Aloha 8)
