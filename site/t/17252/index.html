:shock: Totally agree with Brien: egg crate and carpeting is not the solution, and will do absolutely zero to isolate your room. Contrary to popular belief, these things do not and cannot stop sound getting in or out. They are not intended for "soundproofing". You can no more stop sound getting out of a room with carpet and egg-crate foam that you can stop water coming out of your kitchen tap bu putting a sponge in front of it: it simply does not work. That isn't the purpose of either foam or carpet. In general, common house / office carpet has practically no acoustical uses anyway. To isolate your room, you need mass, and lots of it. "Mass" means things like multiple layers of 5/8" drywall, all around your room, on separate stud frames separated by an air gap. That's the kind of mass you need. The laws of physics can work in your favor if you use mass wisely. But the same laws of physics rate carpet and egg-crate foam as zero usefulness for isolation ("sound proofing"). - Stuart -
