Single stud walls. 3 layers of 5/8" gypsum each side hung on RC, final layer glued not nailed, R13 in between.

Okay.. I can safely say, the best advice about building studios I can give you is this... If you get frustrated...take a night off...relax...come back and take a second look at the problem. You will have a whole new perspective and chances are you will see the solution is obvious. Of course we would all like to plan our moves so that we don't have problems but they can and will happen. For instance, I was having all sorts of troubles with the door installation. Nothing would seal, the closer sucked, and so on. I came back to it tonight...put the closer on the CORRECT WAY, adjusted the hinges, did a light leak test, which exposed leaks around the seal. Took care of the leaks by adjusting the seal and voila!! I have a perfectly sealed control room door. Here are some pix. Questions and comments are of course welcomed.

knightfly24 January 2006#39
Len, standard doors come in either interior or exterior - while there may be solid core interior ones available (not sure) most codes require exterior doors to be solid core for security - hardware for interior doors is designed for 1-3/8" total thickness, while hardware for EXterior doors is set up for 1-3/4" total thickness. The hinges are correspondingly larger so the hinge pins end up about the same distance away from the edge of the door. It's probably a good thing Andy isn't planning double doors, since using the hollow ones with MDF makes it already a 2-leaf barrier... Steve
