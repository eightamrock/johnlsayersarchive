I took your drawign and pointed out the spots were id imagine most of the bass is coming from. The red rings is prolly where most of the bass is happening. Id reccommend bass traps in all corners, and some sort of barrier between the amps that are next to eachother. Also, id reccommend you dampen your drums. If done right it will take away the ringing for a cleaner sound. Aslo is re-arrange your monitors, the out of sync monitors might cause an irregular reverb, and if its in stereo you wont have a very good stereo field how you have it. Fill *edit* i forgot between the drum and amp on the top and drum and bass amp on the left, another likely spot.
