Hi: I would carefully consider what type of material you are going to put in close proximity to recessed lighting fixtures if I were you. I am finishing a commercial studio build (MEOW MIX) and I was careful to use flame retardant cloth for my wall and ceiling treatments. Even in a home situation, wouldn't you want the peace of mind.... knowing that there was less likelihood that one of your recessed lights isn't going to burn down your studio??? Interfacefabrics.com offers a line of Class 1 Fire Retardant fabrics in a number of colors. You can buy by the yard, or in bolts of about 60 yds. x 66" wide. Not cheap, but better than having a deja vu from the nightclub fire in Rhode Island. Good luck. Jon
