xSpace28 June 2008#2
One idea would be angle brackets. Using the bottom picture for reference, you could install angle brackets underneath the bridge that would be screwed into the in-side of the bridge and to the top of the existing desk. Or, you could drill and place dowels into the desk and bottom of the bridge. This would be a cleaner look but may be more difficult for you, depending on your skill level.
