knightfly25 December 2005#2
A square room is far from ideal, but you could help it quite a bit with a LOT of absorption if there's room for that PLUS your gear - however, if the ceiling is the standard 8 feet then you've got modal problems in all three axes. IF you're doing pro work and expect your mixes to translate, you'd probably be better off building from scratch so you can at least get decent room ratios, maybe even do an RFZ room with geometry instead of absorption. Are you planning on any surround work, or just stereo? Steve
