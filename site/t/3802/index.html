aaronious27 May 2005#2
Figure out how much room you need for your drums by measuring how much space they take up by themselves. Do you have a 3 pc. kit with 2 cymbals? or maybe a 10 pc. w/ double kicks and 8 cymbals? Then factor in how much room you'd need on top of that for moving in and out from behind the kit. Use your noodle a little, it's not rocket science. :)
