John Sayers5 June 2009#5
Lime wash Stu is basically a varnish with white lime added - the lime gets trapped in the wood grain and emphasises it. Usually pine goes a yellowy colour when varnished, with lime wash it stays the original light colour. An experienced painter will make his own but you can buy it already made up.
