xSpace8 June 2009#5
Your first post: viewtopic.php?f=3&t=13064&p=89681#p89681 Says nothing about what you just mentioned. I'm going to move past the idiosyncratic and make a simple suggestion. If you are making a build thread, then set it up like the stickies suggest. what are the room sizes, what monies will you spend, what are the measurements of the room, etc. John suggests enough information from the start to avoid having clarification latter that a pole was in the middle of the room. Pole, aquarium...same difference. You have to treat every viewer like they have never seen your place and know nothing about it. Because they have not and they do not ;) I suspect this looks or reads antagonistic, but no one can help in a situation where guess work is the lead player. Most of what goes on in an acoustic environment is a very exacting science. And it can not be left up to a few players to know everything as much as it can not be left up to you to expect others to already know what it is you are trying to mend. So I ask with much humility, help those willing to help you, to help you.
