You must look at it acoustically - if you were to build a vox booth say - 6' x 6' you would end up with a 6 x 6 vox room and 6' x 6' less in your exisiting room. I suggest you treat your existing room correctly and that parameter will also apply to your vox room, provided that for the period of the performance you wear headphones......so what?? cheers john
