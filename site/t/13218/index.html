John Sayers29 June 2009#9
I can relate to that Ian, I live in a small farming town and no one really cares what you build :) Just don't run your power parallel to your audio. I usually run my power and lighting through the ceilings and drop down for wall power outlets. I then run all the audio via the floor. You will need an audio duct and a power duct to your console so run them from opposite directions.
