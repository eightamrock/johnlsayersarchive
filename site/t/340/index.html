John Sayers14 June 2003#2
Welcome to the site Rivers. Your layout looks to be correct for that shaped room, you have a nioce angled ceiling above the mix area. The flat ceiling at the 10' mark should be treated to break up the floor/ceiling standing waves. I'd heavily treat the rearwall of the room possibly with a treatment which will also break up the parallels like angled slots. This will also tame the drum recording area. Where you have the 703 panels it would be beneficial if you could also break up the parallels with some of the wall units I've detailed here http://johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm cheers john
