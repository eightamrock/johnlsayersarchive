jbassino4 May 2009#2
How loud are you now? Without creating a room in a room, solutions are pretty limited regarding isolation. Perhaps you could put a better acoustic door to improve isolation to the rest of your house (wife) and reinforce windows (to prevent street noise coming into your room) Also, how thick are your concrete walls? all of them are concrete? You should test first your noise level before, maybe you dont even bother your neighbour if the concrete wall is thick enough About room treatement, I suggest spending a few DAYS or WEEKS on this forum to learn more about it. myself I spent months and I'm still learning a lot. So, please do your homework and propose us a design we could comment, because, one of the rules are NOT to post something like "hey, my room is 10 x 20 x 15, what should I do ? " The more effort you put on this, the more we will put at assisting you Saludos
