:mrgreen: :mrgreen: To be honest: No I have a series of bags in lots of colors, and I checked them, including how the seems were and the zippers (works with zippers). It's so nicely made that you almost must search for the zippers. Hence you can wash them. :twisted: Since I'm a difficult guy, just checked one and can't find a label how to wash them ......
