sharward11 March 2005#6
knightfly wrote:
. . . no one's Mini Split provides fresh air either; so that problem would still have to be addressed . . .
I've heard this mentioned before, but I'm not sure what to make of it. Technically, my 4 ton central heating and air conditioning system inside my house does not provide any "fresh air." The air gets sucked into the return, through the system, and out through the ducts into the rooms. There's no actual "freshness" (i.e., air coming in from outside) going on there. My take on the ductless/mini-splits is that they do essentially the same thing as a central system, except there is no ducting and the equipment is smaller. I realize there are some central HVAC systems that do factor in fresh air -- my in-laws have one of these modern systems. I also used to have a "whole house fan" which required my windows to be open in order to work properly -- that was "maximum fresh air." I'm certainly not arguing the need for fresh air; I'm just questioning the logic of a ducted system supposedly providing it vs. a ductless system that does not. Maybe I've missed something, and if so, I look forward to the edification process that will hopefully follow this. ;)
