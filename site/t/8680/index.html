Good evening Acousticians! I just picked up 'Build It Like The Pros' and though I just started reading it, am eager to start posting already. I guess that's the 'hey I'm a novice' warning. I live in a suburb about 10 minutes out of L.A. and just reached a professional goal with which I’m rewarding myself with a new studio. Just moved my studio into my spare bedroom (and it’s definitely not cutting it) after having a huge building in Hollywood for the past 7 years. I plan on hiring a builder to do the brunt of the work. I have no construction experience but am eager to get down and dirty and do all I can to make this happen. And to make up for the loss of the garage, I need to designate some space for laundry and storage. Currently considering a tuff shed for this. But if someone can convince me that an ADDITION is fea$ible for me I will do it. ALL OPINIONS WELCOME!!! Even to say f off get lost.....

Sideshow4 May 2007#3
Hey there, I think with the addition of the building on the left you will go over budget. You will need to do a lot yourself - I reckon $50K if you you want someone else to do it + your extra room. Your drum area does not seem big enough. Maybe the kit is not to scale? Measure up a kit and place mics with some space for "air" - envisage the kit facing at least 2 ways , allow squeeze room to adjust /access mics (even ambient). You are on a roll - keep rolling and I look forward to updated designs. Don't hesitate to call John once you are 2-3 months into planning (he will probably save you about 25% of your budget) and have read at least 150 posts and 3 books on the subject and your brain is tired and you have that "relentless" "obsessive" glint in your eyes... :lol: Then you know you are on track and it is going to happen. I look forward to your progress, all the best and Take Care Eliot
