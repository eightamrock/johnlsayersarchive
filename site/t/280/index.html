knightfly24 May 2003#5
Luis, so far I've only found NRC info on painted concrete and (real) wood; wood seems to average about .14 at 125 hZ, where concrete is close to zip - both are nearly identical at higher freqs, and I've not yet found #'s for vinyl. Wood is going to be even higher NRC if it's over a wood floor and therefore has some panel absorber tendencies. Of course, all these #'s are for 90 degree incidence so mic placement is still critical... Steve
