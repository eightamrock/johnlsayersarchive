dymaxian19 August 2004#2
If you're stuck with those room sizes, here's what I'd do with the space (in general) The 24x20 space is a nice big room. It's not big enough to divide up into a big control room and decent tracking room, tho. If you made that space into a combination of control and main tracking room, you could get a pretty good sound out of it. Then you could use the remaining space for storage and isolation. Of course, the 24x10 space wouldn't be too bad of a drum booth, either. A little bit like a tunnel, but it'd work. Can you move the door between the 24x10 and 24x20 spaces? You could split the 24x10 space into a drum booth and storage or iso area, and just leave the rest or the space for a kick-ass tracking room. I don't know that I'd want to put the tracking room in the 24x10 side, but it's certainly possible. Even with the 24' length, a 10' wide and 8.5' tall room is a little tight. It'd require a bunch of treatment to sound good, but it'd leave you with a 24x20 tracking room, which would rule. You could put an iso booth at one end of the 24x10 space (maybe 7x10 iso booth and 16x10 control?). Just thinking out loud. The other folks on the board will come up with good ideas for specific designs, but the overall layout is something you'll need to decide on first. Hope this sparks some thought.
