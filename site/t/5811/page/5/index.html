Daily Planet3 October 2006#102
I have been working (making music) in my Control Room B, my ISO room B, and my Studio A via Tie Lines. Mixed a record in my Control Room B and our Mastering Engineer said is was pretty good...http://www.kitchenmastering.com I had NO treatments up...just raw room. So now that that deadline is off my back, I am trying to move ahead getting this B Control Room closer to finished. I have decided to move ahead with creating a shell around the mix position to suspend 2" Thermafiber from it and track fabric as well. The shell is not physically attached to the room. There is a 4.5" gap on the big wall to the sheetrock and a 2" gap on the side from the sheetrock walls, as to give the fiber a chance of absorbing some low end.
