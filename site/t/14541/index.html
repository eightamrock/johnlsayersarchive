If I were to instead of using "rockwool" use Tontine Acoustisorb ( in my case Acoustisorb2 ) in my wall leafs would that not be quite ok?
Well, comparing the specs for that stuff with "Rock Wool" brand mineral wool products, the 4" thickness of "Tontine" is roughly the same as the 3" thickness of RHT40 3pcf, and 4" of RHT40 is way superior. 4" "Tontine" is also similar to 4" 703. So I think I'd go with ordinary 3pcf mineral wool. It's probably much cheaper, too. - Stuart -
