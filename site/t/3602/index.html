John Sayers21 April 2005#4
Your biggest problem is the large size and small amount of treatment you can do - I'd suggest what I've drawn because it cuts off the left end of the room, which will ring and distort your sounds if it's not treated, and puts you in a tight nearfield environment suitable to your speakers. You only need build 4 walls and you've got some low mid absorbers in slots - add the auralex and tame the rest of the room. Run a cable to the Gtr amp when you build the walls and you can play your amp yet listen to it on your speakers. ;) cheers john
