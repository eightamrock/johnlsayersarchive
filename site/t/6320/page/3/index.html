A bit simpler way: scribe a line along the outer edge of the "picture frame", remove the frame, glue/clamp wider boards to the INSIDE of the hole in the soffit, then glue or screw the picture frame to these "backer" boards. End result looks almost identical, no routing required - only an ACCURATE cut along the scribed lines... Steve
