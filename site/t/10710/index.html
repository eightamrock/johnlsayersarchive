rod gervais9 June 2008#2
the first question is - why are they vibrating? If the vibration is mechanical in nature (i.e.: the fan motor is vibrating the duct) then they need to be decoupled from the fan through the use of a canvas connector. If they are vibrating due to velocity - then they really should be larger duct - You will not stop duct noise by applying insulation on the outside of the duct. Rod
