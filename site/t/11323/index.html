Soundman202019 October 2008#4
I haven't actually done it myself, but I did quote optical snakes not too long ago for a church. Just looking at list prices my guess would be that a 32 channel optical cable setup would cost you around US$10,000, and the same thing in analog cable would cost maybe US$1,000. The cost is not in the distance that that optical cable runs, but in the A/D converters and pre-amps on the live-room end, plus the D/A converters or direct console interface on the control-room end. Optical only gets to be cost effective with really long cable runs, hundreds of feet, where the price of the multi-core analog cable rises drastically.
