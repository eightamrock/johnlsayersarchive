Soundman202013 January 2010#3
Q:I have about 4 square feet of bed foam in pieces of 41" long, 4" wide, 6" deep with peaks along two opposing sides. Do you think I could cut these in half cover with fabric for aesthetics and use for diffusion?
First, I'd suggest you read the stickies on the front page of this forum if you'd like to get responses to your posts. Second, I don't know what you mean by "bed foam", but most likely it wont work as acoustic treatment (unless it is open-cell foam). Most common foam you find around the home is closed-cell foam (a the microscopic level). Closed-cell foam is no use as an acoustic absorber: only open cell works. Unless you can find out what kind it is for certain, don't bother. It also wont work as a diffuser, since diffusers are not useful in such a small room anyway. Some experts here say that diffusers only work effectively in large rooms. FWIW. - Stuart -
