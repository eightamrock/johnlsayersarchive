knightfly17 July 2005#6
The type of blown cellulose you seem to be referring to gets a bit stiff after installation, so will tend to "bridge" between frames, which will lessen the isolation somewhat; the heavier the bridge, the more the frames will act like single frames - so best case, no difference between blown cellulose and rigid wools - worst case, 8-10 dB difference (assuming a REALLY bad buildup stuck to both frames) Depending on your situation, you might be able to place some of that "ground cover mesh" used in gardening across your studs, staple it in place, then blow the cellulose in thru small holes you make in the mesh every few feet - you don't want to use NON-perforated material like Visquene (poly) film, because it will act as a membrane and create a third leaf effect (worse bass isolation) I've not done this, so can't be much more specific... Steve

Why not fill the cavity with Thermafiber Sound Atteuation Fire Blankets (SAFB) - Nominal Density (2.5 pcf) or Roxul Acoustical Fire Batt (AFB) - ≥2" (2.5 pcf)? That's what I plan to do on my project. 8)

knightfly19 July 2005#15
What do people normally do when space is constrained like this? Gently fill it with loose fiberglass, unfaced - whatever you use, it should NOT be even close to rigid. If you're concerned with the stuff falling down after the walls are closed up, you can use either the netting you're talking about or you can put light lath across the outer studs, place the unfaced thin batts, and maybe do a loose stitch through the batts and around the lath every so often with something like a sail needle, or even 9/16" T50 staples (kind of like button-tufting on car upholstery) - By "constrained", am I correct in assuming your steel inner studs are already in place? If not, I'd have the OUTER frames blown with cellulose, trim back any that sticks out much, and THEN put up your steel frames and use standard fiberglass batts there. IF you use paper faced batts, put the paper next to the wallboard side so it doesn't create a third leaf... Steve BTW, only one layer of wallboard on the ceiling??!? For DRUMS??
