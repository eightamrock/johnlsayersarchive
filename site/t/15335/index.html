gullfo3 December 2010#2
welcome. please fill out your profile with your location information. please explain a bit more about how you intend to use this space - besides tracking - do you intend to do the mixing there? commercial or self? mastering? if you're just tracking or used to mixing in an open space then i'd leave it open. if you intend to spend a significant amount of time mixing and want more accuracy, then an actual control room would probably be a better bet. if you need a lot of isolation, maker some decent size isolation rooms may also be less costly than trying to isolate the entire space to a higher level.
