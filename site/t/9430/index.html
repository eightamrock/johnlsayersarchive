Hallo there Robert... I think, first of all, you should consider your sound isollation needs and find out if floating the floor is right for you(right Keith? :D ) this is a good place to start: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=8173 However, i can tell you that if you decide that floating the floor is the thing to do then you should be very carefull on what you'll do....floating floors are really complicated constructions and doing more damage than good with a wrong aproach is very possible. Floating floors are supposed to be a MASS - SPRING -MASS system, so proper calculations should be made. There is tons of information on this subject here and at the studiotips forum. Plus on the studiotips forum you should take a look at Paul Woodblock's studio diary, probably the best "floating floor DIY guide" on the net. Finally here's a thread i started regarding floating floors and MSM calculation (i'm lost in the floating floor :shock: :shock: :shock: .....i'm a floating floor freak :D :D ) http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=9311 There you can find very helpfull posts by the true experts and masters of the subject mr. Eric Desart and mr. Rod Gervais....... Actually, the first thing you should do is buy Rod Gervais book "Build it like the pros" which is probably one of the best recources on home(and not only) studio construction.
