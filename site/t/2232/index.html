dymaxian31 August 2004#2
IMHO some experimentation would be good, but for the basics of the studio design, go with what is proven to work. There's some room to play with the treatment of the walls, and what you do with the ceiling, but moving walls around gets costly, so do that stuff right the first time. That's a pretty impressive budget. You shouldn't have any trouble building two tracking/control clusters into a 20m square building, either. As far as layout advice, I'd put almost everything on the ground floor except for the lounge/kitchen area. Putting control on a different floor than it's tracking room is going to make it tough for your clients to keep eye contact. And it'd make their lives a little easier if there's no need to haul a guitar cabinet up or down stairs. This is all general stuff. If you want more specifics, you'll need to give us more info on precisely what you want. It sounds like you're looking more for general guidance, not so much for us to design it for you. So the best thing to do is just read as much of this forum as you can. =) Almost every conceivable question on studio design has been asked by someone out there. Good luck!
