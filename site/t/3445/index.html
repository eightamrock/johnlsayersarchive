Fabio - I'd do something like this - its a soffit mounted speaker syatem that improves your bass response as well as breaking up the parallel walled box you have now - it also adds a reflection free zone across the front with the added slot resonators. Behind you I've placed insulation with heavy deeper treatement across the rear wall. This stops early reflections coming back at you from behind which is also a no no. I'd also place an angled cloud over the mix area. cheers john
