Sending mic signals to two locations

Started by MightyM on 21 July 2008. 3 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 10986.

MightyM21 July 2008#1
My studio will consist of two rooms, a main control room, and a smaller isolation booth. For the bulk of my work, I plan on having my desk in the control room, but from time to time I expect to roll my desk into the iso booth to free up the larger room for tracking drums and such. I plan on using panel mounted db25 connectors in the control room to feed the various mic signals to my daw system. I would like to have a duplicate set of db25 connectors in my iso booth as well for when I have my desk in that room. Is it acceptable to simply solder an additional 8channel cable to the back of the control room db25 to send the signals to the iso booth? The only potential problem I can forsee is possibly some noise issues related to the break in shielding at the mid run db25, or at the end of run db25. Any thoughts?

EK Sound17 August 2008#4
There are several sudios in the area that do a similar thing will no ill effects. One studio I know has mic input panels in four places around the room. There are 16 channels that are the same on all the panels. The 16 lines are split out from punch down blocks behind one of the panels. They have not had a problem with noise. If you take care to keep the exposed wires as short as possible, and twist the paris that are unshieled (at the back of the connectors) you should be fine. Dave.
