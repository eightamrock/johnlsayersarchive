knightfly15 January 2005#2
Yeah, pretty much - but only where the riser is; it won't help sounds made in other parts of the room from escaping. But for practice rooms, it should be good - main thing besides good isolation, if you want a waiting list for your rooms, is make them SOUND good; no cheesy carpet, no foam, just rockwool across the corners and hung from the ceiling, and all three dimensions of each room NOT the same as any other dimension - simplest way to get close is to make all 3 dimensions of a room odd-numbers of feet, like 9-11-13, 7-9-11, etc - not sure if this also works for meters, so you'd need to convert feet to meters... Steve
