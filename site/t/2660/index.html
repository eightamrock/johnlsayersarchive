The noise is being caused by expansion/contraction of the pipes. My 1st guess would be that you do not have proper expansion loops installed in the system if you picture a straight run of pipe - an expansion loop would create something like this - (gonna paint a picture) pipe runs straight - pipe suddenly turns right and runs for 2' - then back to the original direction for 2' - then suddenly left for 2' - and then again back in the same original direction - Shucks, that cost em 6' of pipe and 4 ells when they could have done the whole thing with just 2' of pipe......... why'd they go and do that? 2nd best guess - pipe does not pitch to drain in it's entirety - This will cause uneven heating /cooling of the pipe - Rod
