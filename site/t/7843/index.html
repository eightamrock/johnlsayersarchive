jwl2 January 2007#3
Hi Seth, you don't want square walls for your studio. My own space is 15x17, just a bit smaller than yours. If it were me, I'd probably put in a vocal booth, but leave the rest of the space a one-room studio. You definitely want to design everything around the control room, then use the space left over intelligently for your booth and/or live room.
gullfo7 January 2007#4
if outside isolation isn't a problem you could try something like this. one or more clouds in each room to break up the floor-ceiling reflections, a couple of carpets to soften the floor a bit. I'd just do a polished concrete floor. easy, cheap, durable. if you need more interior isolation, double up the interior walls in an STC 63 configuration. all rooms connected by glass windowed doors. flip the outside door as needed for workflow...
