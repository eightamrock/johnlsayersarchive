andy svejda19 November 2009#2
build a nice sized control room. I would float it up 10 feet high and make a lounge underneath. Then get a "shit ton" of warehouse acoustics.and about a dozen 4x4 foot portable gobo isolation walls to reduce mic. bleed. plus you can rent the space out for photo shoots and music videos when your not recording. this is the way zepplin recorded, big open warehouse and a little mic isolation for that big sound.dig it. use the space if you can get away with not building a live room. saves you a ton of money and well that about it for now. good day and good luck.
