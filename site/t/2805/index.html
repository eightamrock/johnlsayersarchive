The STC is around 33. The LF TL is around 15 dB. It is useles for drum isloation. Fill the cavity (3-6 dB increase) with RW3 etc. Live with the results with that is your budget. At least you know the results.

John, Andre is right on this - there is NO SHORTCUT for soundproofing that I'm aware of. you need either great distances, or large masses, in order to stop low frequency sound - usually BOTH.
