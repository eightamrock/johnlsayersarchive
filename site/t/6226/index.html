Hi all, this is my first post. I lurked around a bit during the past weeks and found a lot of great infos. I downloaded Sketchup and started playin' around with it and finally understood a bit of it. I made a sketch of my space, and would like to get some input from you. This space is in my house (the wall opposite to the bathroom is my kitchen wall). Windows and doors cannot be moved. The guy is standing at the entry door (the other door gets you to a terrace). Unfortunately the room is on upper level so no chance to cut the floor in order to reduce noise between rooms. Right now the space is full with stuff, but the images show the bare room as it will be before starting the project. As far as budget I really don't knwo what to say. I'd like to keep it as cheap as possible, around 5000USD (aprox 4000€) in construction only. I already have all the gear (an 24ch analog console, DAW, 2", some outboard). I plan to record an mix small rock bands, electric and acoustic (the usual stuff, drums, bass guitars, vocals). It will be a private studio, it's not goin' to be a commercial facility (been there, done that). Hope I gave you all the infos needed. Thanks in advance for all the inputs you might give me. Great site, congrats. EDIT: the overall height is 2.7m, all walls are concrete.
