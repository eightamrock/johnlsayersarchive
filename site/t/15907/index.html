gullfo2 May 2011#2
welcome! nicely detailed post! you might consider putting the desk under the skylights - this makes the left-right symmetry a bit better for the desk and it opens up the space with the drums to be something where musicians can all be in one spot. then run your guitars amps, Leslies, etc downstairs via your stage wiring. useful approach for re-amping as well. then in the corners - slats or superchunks. in each window, suspend a 100mm thick absorber in the opening which leaves around 100-150mm gap all around it so you get light but also absorption there. on the peak - consider some spaced absorbers (100mm thick) hung down as 50mm. on the sloped wall opposite your desk, scatter 100mm absorbers (like a spaced out checkerboard) to break that up. in the corners by the drums - slats or superchunks. on the side walls by the desk - 100mm absorbers at first reflection points and perhaps on the door and opposite wall.
