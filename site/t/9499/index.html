Aloha, While we're working on the design:
My goal is to get it quiet enough to be able to record live drums, guitar, bass the works as well as rehearse late into the night, say midnight without waking my 2 year old son.
Wise decision...noble task. :wink: In addition to treating your room acoustically, one of the main concepts to get a handle on is going to be the Mass-Air-Mass principle to address your isolation requirements. The second issue to consider may be any flanking paths - (The path that sound takes around, instead of through, a partition. Flanking paths include floors underneath walls, ceilings, pipes, air ducts, doors and electrical conduits.) Here are a few recommended readings and references: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=2921 http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=2125 http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=1168 Happy reading! :D Aloha 8)
