Hi Gianluca, and welcome: We'd need a lot more information than that to be able to do much! Please read the forum rules for posting (click here). I'd suggest two books for you, to help you get started: "Master Handbook of Acoustics" by F. Alton Everest (that's sort of the Bible for acoustics), and "Home Recording Studio: Build it Like the Pros", by Rod Gervais. Those are both vary good books that teach you how to design your studio, and how to understand sound and acoustics. Are you looking for information on actually designing and building a "room within a room"? Or are you just looking for information on how to treat your room, acoustically? - Stuart -
