Hi, I've spent some time reading lots of helpful information at this forum (from lots of helpful folks) and just wanted to see if I'm on the right track with my layout. Some background: My company is moving offices and we will be building a recording studio in addition to the typical office layout for a company. While the studio space will be used to record local artists/bands (primarily), it will also serve as a weekly rehearsal space for a five piece rock band. Considerations: - to make sure that the live room was large enough to accommodate a five piece rock band at least once a week with thoughts to constantly loading and unloading for shows. - the control room was large enough to spend considerable time in without feeling claustrophobic. - some separation between the two functionalities (offices & studio). Thanks, and any suggestions would be greatly appreciated. -Billl

The dead end corridor is a code issue. Two distinct exits is also. If one path is blocked by fire, you need an egress alternative. If the exits aren't seperate, a single fire can block both. I believe code also says the exits should be seperated by about half the distance of the diagonal, but i'm not looking at the code right now. it think it also depends on the occupancy type. Does the office and studio need be seperated? Who all will use the lounge?
