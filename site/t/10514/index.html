Welcome Yardley and thank you for filling in your location. That is a nice size space you have. What you need is absorption. Suggestions are: 1. absorbers (insulation in frames with pretty fabric covering) equally distributed on the walls with twice as many per square foot on the ceiling. 2. if on a very low budget, carpets will help on the highs and some mid reflections and reverb. Andre

Smaller rooms have an emphasis on bass traps because the the room modes start to become separate (bad) at frequencies where significant music energy is still present, causing unwanted coloration of the sound. In your room the separation of starts (going down in frequency) with one hole around 67 Hz. In other words you do not have to worry about room modes and do not have to put absorbers in the corners. You have to treat the ceiling also, not just the walls. The ceiling is only place that absorbs in the vertical plane. It is difficult to walk on absorbers placed on the floor. :D (So that other people know how the ratios were developed) The walls have a surface of area 1176 ft^2, the floor/ceiling 880 ft^2 for a total surface area 2056^2. The ratio of walls to floor/ceiling is about 4:3. Since you are not going to walk on absorbers double the second factor in the ratio, giving 4:6. Mount 4 absorbers on the walls for every 6 on the ceiling. How many? If I were designing the acoustics professionally, I would use at least 400 ft^2 (20%) of the surface. Assuming 2'x4' absorbers, that would be 50 absorbers. If I was in your situation, I would start with intentions of building 20. 2 for each wall and 12 for the ceiling. Then listen and decide how close it is to what I want. When mounting them, alternate the locations on the walls so that each absorber is an untreated surface on the opposite wall. If you are using 2" insulation, leave a gap of 2-4" between the absorbers and the wall/ceiling. If you do some price research you can probably some material so cheap that you will just make thicker absorber and not have to worry about gaps. Read this for more on prices. Andre
