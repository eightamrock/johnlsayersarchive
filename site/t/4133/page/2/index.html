DDev5 August 2005#31
Good to see you back here again, Bryan. Doni - Your last control room, in my opinion, is pretty close but I would, as Bryan suggested, splay the side walls from front to back. If you go in 1-2 ft from each corner in the front and draw the wall on an angle such that it meets the side wall at the point where you have the rear corner panels meeting the side walls you will add just enough to keep the sidewall reflections from bouncing back to the mixing position. I'm not sure what that little bump in the wall next to the console on the right side is for but I think it might create a problem. Just my opinion, though. Darryl.....
