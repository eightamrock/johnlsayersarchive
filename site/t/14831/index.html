Where are the bedroom and bathroom located on the SketchUp mode you did? Obviously,m your comment means that NOT all of the space shown is available for the studio. Please identify what is what on the model.
It will be as loud as live drums and guitar amps for rock music. I think not exceeding 100dB???
A drum kit will normally put out around 115 dB, or even more when played hard. A lot of that energy is in the low end of the spectrum, which is the hardest part to isolate effectively. Isolating high frequencies is easy: isolating low frequencies is not. How quite do you need to be in the "bedroom and bathroom"? In terms of decibels, what level are you aiming to have in those rooms that you mentioned as being critical, while you are tracking live drums in your studio? - Stuart -
