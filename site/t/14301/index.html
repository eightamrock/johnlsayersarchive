dB reduction at different volumes.

Started by Steeeve on 22 April 2010. 1 reply.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 14301.

Steeeve22 April 2010#1
Hi folks. If I played music @ 90dB, and transmission loss of wall was 10dB at a given frequency, would Music played @ 105dB also be reduced by the same 10dB, or would it be less ? Just curious as I know TL figures get thrown around alot. In fact, the reason this question popped into my head is that I brought some ear-plugs that specify reductions of various frequencies, but I thought, surely it depends on how loud the source is to begin with ? Regards
rod gervais22 April 2010#2
Steeeve Nope - the TL value of a wall is constant - if it will stop 10 dB at a particular frequency - then at 40 dB - 30 will travel through the wall - and at 110 dB 70 will travel through the wall. Nothing changes due to volume - the only changes are due to frequency - in other words - you may have a TL value of 30 db @ 1 frequency and 60 Db @ another - but once the assembly is constructed - those values do not change unless there is a physical change to the assembly itself. I hope this helped. Rod
