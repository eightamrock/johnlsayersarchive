Started by migraine99 on 19 October 2007. 1 reply.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 9620.

migraine9919 October 2007#1
Overview: I plan to use part of this space for a rehearsal space/live room. The other part I would like use as a control room. Dimensions: 37' x 29' x 8.5' Building is a converted textiles mill in New Bedford, MA USA. The room used to be a private men's club complete with bar, stage, and bench seats. All of which can be removed. There is a bathroom and storage closet that I did not include in the drawing as they can't be altered and are not part of the floor plan. I have attached a crude MS Paint drawing until I can get SketchUp to work in Vista. Materials/surfaces: Floor – Painted concrete Ceiling – Drop ceiling with 2x4 melt-away styrofoam panels (can't be altered) Walls – Left wall is painted drywall (if looking through door into room), Far wall is painted brick, Right wall is ¾ painted brick and ¼ painted drywall, Back wall is painted drywall. Volume: The room will be primarily used for Hard Rock music. Neighbors are all bands as well so isolation may be a problem. I'm willing to build new walls if necessary for room-within-a-room. My questions are: What are some design ideas for a tracking room and control room? What can I do to treat the rooms to make it acceptable for tracking and as a control room? Your advice and insight is much appreciated. Thanks, -Chris
