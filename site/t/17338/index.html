Monitor stand build

amarshism22 January 2012#1
I couldn't find any that weren't overpriced or shit. The argosy ones in the shop near me were 320 with the krk10s and they barely fit. To make things worse they didn't feel overly sturdy. I nearly tipped them when I gave em a love tap after the guy tried to assure me they were solid. He certainly ate his words with his heart in his mouth. Instead I made these sand filled ones. Not too purty but they do the job. I filled them with sand and put in four support struts that I attempted to decoupled from the bottom and top with these rubber cutouts I made to go between the supports and bracket and the brackets and the top and bottom platforms.
