Hello to all you fine people on this forum. I’ve been poking around here for a while and I love all the info you guys give. I have an idea I wanna run by you. Would it be possible/practical to have guitar amp iso rectangle area beneath my live tracking room? Possibly two? So I could practice with my drummer and track guitar amps separately. xSpace11 July 2009#2
Tall order. Have you guys ever cut concrete before? No small task. Thing is with a concrete slab is that it is a designed system so to modify it takes more then a demolition saw and a hose with running water. Once you penetrate the slab two things happen. You just changed the load distribution on the concrete and you invite whatever moisture, bugs, etc that may be taking up residence below the concrete into the open area. In order to reestablish a sealed concrete layer you would have to make the opening at least 16 inches wider all around in order to form up and re-pour new concrete. And that is the short version. It's more trouble then it is worth. Maybe building an iso booth would suit your needs?
