I have placed hangers on the ceiling of my Studio room, as recomended in this thread: http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=3585 I'm currently building a wooden structure below the hangers, to have an absorptive sloped ceiling. One of the joists is about 30 cms apart from the wall. The object of this is to attach a slat resonator structure allong this entire wall. As proposed in the SAE site, but the larger portion of the wall will be on the ceiling, and the 10 cms part will be at floor level. This because I don't want to loose too much space at floor level. My question is: once I have the slat resonator wall, should I put bass traps/broadband absorption on each corner? Even though the entire ceiling is filled with hangers (about 34 of them) Or am I better off leaving just the sloped wall and a couple of corner traps on the opposite wall? The idea of the slat resonator wall is to minimize standing waves/flutter produced between parallel walls only 1.62 meters apart! I'll attach my designs and some pictures as well. Thanks for any input! Rodrigo
