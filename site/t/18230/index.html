Perhaps, and perhaps not. You seem to be tuning to just three specific frequencies, but you didn't say what those 2 are, or why you chose them. The frequency also depends on slot depth (the width of the slats) and cavity depth, which you did not show above. Also, slot walls act more as a set of individual resonators tuned to specific frequencies if the total percentage "perforation" is less than about 5% to 10%, and they act more as a general broadband absorption device tuned to teh average frequency if the percentage perforation is more than about 15% to 20%. I'm not sure if that is of any help to you, but it is important to take into account. - Stuart -

There's a calculator for slot resonators somewhere on the stickies, here on the forum. It uses the correct version of the equation, so it is safe to use. Beware that there are several calculators (and even text books) that have the wrong version, where there's a "+" sign instead of a "x" sign... watch out for those! Obviously, they give you the wrong answer... - Stuart -
