knightfly13 July 2003#7
Lookin' pretty good Paul - the only thing is, have you priced good quality sliding doors, with tight seals? In order to get one that seals well enough to have good isolation, it would cost anywhere from $400 to $800 just for the door. Do you need that much glass? That will lower the isolation by quite a bit compared to double drywall construction either on staggered studs or with resilient channel on one side of the frame. You might be better off getting a pre-hung, solid core, basic exterior door and installing it carefully, caulking to avoid acoustic leaks around the frame. It's your room, I just wouldn't want you to be dissatisfied with the isolation you get... Steve
