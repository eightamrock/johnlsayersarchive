gullfo27 February 2007#8
if you have a short space or large console, the 90 degree soffit (45 each) will keep perspective. if you wanted to do 90 and since your room is fairly square, why not put it along one wall and leave that nice window and heater open? you could put up a drape on the window and short wall by the door to knock of some highs. put in a bunch of 703 absorbers along the top wall-ceiling "corner" over the couch (or keyboard rack) and a 703 cloud overhead of the console.

Yes, a cloud over the mix position will help, provided your ceiling can support it. :wink: Depending on how much bass build up you have, you could do a corner bass trap at the wall/ceiling junction (full length), and a few strategically placed absorbers on the door and rear wall will also help.
