THAT OPTION WILL BE RESERVED FOR WHEN/IF THE BUILDING IS TRANSFORMED INTO FAMILY/GREAT ROOM. 10-FOR MASS AND TO HELP WITH ISOLATION OF SOUND, THE STUDIO WILL HAVE AT LEAST 2 LAYERS OF 5/8” DRYWALL ON ALL THE INTERIOR WALLS AND CEILING AREAS OF THE TRACKING ROOM. OVER THE CONTROL ROOM WILL BE A MIXDOWN LOFT WITH BASICALLY THE SAME DIMENSIONS AS THE CONTROL ROOM. NO FLOATING FLOORS AT THIS POINT, (BUT POSSIBLY AN ISOLATED SLAB BETWEEN THE TRACKING ROOM AND CONTROL ROOM AREA) THIS FLOOR WILL BE REINFORCED USING #4 REBAR WHICH WILL BE 12” O.C., ALONG WITH FIBERMESH IN THE CONCRETE TO FURTHER REDUCE THE CHANCES OF CRACKING. THE DISECTED WALL DIAGRAM FOR THIS STUDIO’S WALLS AND CEILINGS WILL BE: (EXTERIOR TO INTERIOR): ** STUCCO ON OUTSIDE FACE **10FT “HOUSE WRAP” BARRIER AROUND STRUCTURE **DOUBLE LAYER OF OSB 7/16 EXTERIOR SHEETING **2X6X8 EXTERIOR WOOD STUD WALL FRAMING **2X4X8 INTERIOR WOOD STUD WALL FRAMING **UNFACED INSULATION BETWEEN STUDS ON EXTERIOR WALLS AND INTERIOR TRACKING ROOM WALLS **A 3” AIR GAP ALL THE WAY AROUND (ROOF AND SIDES) OF TRACKING ROOM **2 LAYERS OF 5/8 SHEETROCK ** ALL TRACKING ROOM WALLS, THE DRYWALL SHEETS WILL BE “GREEN GLUED” TOGETHER **ALL SEAMS WILL BE SEALED WITH ACOUSTICAL CAULKING INTERIOR WALLS WILL THEN BE PAINTED (ALSO NOTE: THERE WILL BE BROADBAND ABSORPTION PANELS (4X8 703 FRAMED PANELS) THROUGHOUT THE STUDIO AND CORNER BASS TRAPS. the dimensions seem off. if the actual roof height is 18 feet, and you have a 9/12 roof pitch, assuming the 8' floor-ceiling height in the bottom is correct, you're only looking at 5-6 feet in of ceiling in the loft area. 8 feet bottom floor-ceiling + 1 foot (approx for joists, subfloor, floor etc) + 3 feet from top for roof truss = you have 6 feet left. if you're not right on the concrete slab (maybe you're building a wood floor off the slab for under-floor hvac and plumbing) then that's likely another 1 foot out of the 18 - leaving 5 feet. this is likely why you're struggling with Sketchup... maybe you can leave out the loft and just have a high ceiling all around?
