gullfo23 November 2006#27
AJ - thanks for the compliments, I'm glad to assist. you might check with your building department before putting the bath in the garage - in my town they wouldn't allow that without re-sizing the garage. i think the plans over doable but IMHO I would still pass on the loft only because I think it doesn't really add much space-value for the complexity of it and having the full floor plan open for a proper CR and live room (w/ iso booths) will be much more effective and possibly easier to construct. I think you need isolation all around to be effective. one thought - instead of 2x6 walls use 2 2x4 walls built as an STC63 configuration. this way the walls are already built to isolate. then you need to consider the ceilings - this may need a similar construct to support enough sheetrock to actually isolate. Rod's book may help with this although I don't recall a vaulted ceiling example (except that major pro studio...). enjoy your holiday!
