Hi Stuart, Thank you. We get loads of work done then we just go for a wander or a look with the view to clear our heads. There's always something - the light on the mountains when you're hanging washing out (undercover on another roof here), the sound of the grillos or the beeaters / abejeros (now gone), the swallows that wake you at some unearthly hour, the buho chico every night, spring flowers like we've never seen, the mule watching you while it grazes... Y el espanol (sin accentos aqui) - lo hice yo pero un amigo de Asturias lo corregio todo con su padre.
