Soundman20205 November 2010#2
Hi, and welcome. First big problem: the control room is not symmetrical. At the very least, the front half should be absolutely symmetrical. Without symmetry you cannot get a clear, accurate, balanced stereo image. You also seem to be wasting space with your diamond-shaped sound-lock, and it creates a rather strange shape in that are of the live room. Overall, I think you could make better use of space by re-arranging the rooms. - Stuart -
