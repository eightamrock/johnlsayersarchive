gullfo19 April 2012#2
if isolation isn't a concern why make a dead closet? are you close mic'ing the cabinet anyways? if you need isolation from your recording/mixing room, then that may be a reason to do it. first thing would be to replace the door and or seal it really well. otherwise you might find just hanging heavy blankets in the closet on the existing poles to dampen it and just close mic.
