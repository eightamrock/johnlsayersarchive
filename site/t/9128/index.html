camistan25 July 2007#4
Greetings Eoin! Welcome to the forum. Unfortunately, I can't really give you any advice for what you're asking presently, but I can tell you to hang tight and don't give up because of no responses yet. The moderators and some of the more senior members have been busy lately with their own projects and personal endeavors. Someone will get to you soon. In the meantime be SURE to do a thorough search specifically for studio "threads". I've seen a LOT of people on here building Studios/practice rooms in garages. I would think there would be some info in those threads that may help you with your build. I CAN tell you that you need MASS in order to reduce the sound travel. (You seem to have a grasp of this already). Drums seem to pack a high db level and you'll probably need a substantial amount of mass to reduce the sound travel. Just remember that even though you can build to try & soundproof your garage most likely there will be sound leakage. The level of that leakage may be acceptable to you and your neighbors if you build correctly. Your ideas about a "perfect world" build would work pretty good, but as you indicated, space is an issue. Looks like green glue may be of interest to you somewhere along your build http://www.greengluecompany.com/ especially when space is an issue. You will also need to be thinking about some type of ventilation. With you being a drummer, your movements will produce more heat than someone playing in a relaxed state. Come up with a plan to bring air in and exhaust air out. Even if you use a window-type Air Conditioner, you will also need to incorporate fresh air into your room. With your room being small, you'll need that airflow man...hot-n'-stuffy ain't the way to go!! A lot of the stuff you need is on this site, you'll just need to take the time to do some thorough research. Some of the post I've seen on here for garage Studios have good build info that you can at least "start" with. Check the stickies and reference areas out. Researching isn't easy. Some info is "buried" deep in posts that start out one way and veer another. If you REALLY want this, go get it...it's on this site, and you CAN find it...You can do it! Many Blessings on your Studio!
