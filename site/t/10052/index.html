rod gervais21 January 2008#10
I never had the need for fancy bull***t........ a finish nailer would be useless - 14 1/2" wide strips don't weight squat......... I've always used a standard caulk gun = loading a powered gun is a pain larger than the gain in MHO. I always ripped strips by just using a standard 1" wide tape measure as a guide - set it for width (backwards so that the outside tip @ 0" is your cutting guide)- hold the tape and lock it in at that dimension at the sheet edge - and then just run down the edge creating a score with the knife to create a prefect edge. Like I said - nothing could be easier....... Rod
