Actually the reference to wood framed construction has nothing to do with the panels you are interested in. The panels will most likely not give you any more isolation than a typical plywood sheathed/shingled roof...especially since there is a thermal break of Styrofoam which has no isolation properties what so ever. It makes no difference if it is framed with sheathing or framed with these panels, the decoupled interior environment is what will get you the isolation....not the panels specifically. But the exterior and the interior parts of the build have to be considered individually...as a collective...not individually as the one will help the other, since this is often just not true.
