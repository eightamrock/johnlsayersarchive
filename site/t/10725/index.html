jwl12 June 2008#3
703 is likely to be more expensive than rockwool, but this varies throughout the world. 703 will hold its shape better over time than rockwool will. Acoustic performance should be more or less interchangeable. Also, I wouldn't bother making both 2" and 1" panels. Pick one or the other for your first reflection points, and use 4" panels as bass traps. I'd put the bass traps in the corners (as many of the 12 corners as you can), on the rear wall, and possibly on the front wall behind your speakers.
