dymaxian9 August 2004#2
You may have more luck with the sound of the control room if you turn it 90 degrees clockwise, and design it so that the window to the live room is on the side-wall. Having the speakers fire down the longer axis of the room means that the reflections off the back wall will be weaker and delayed from the direct sound more. Otherwise it looks ok. Without any dimensions or any thoughts on what things you'll be recording, this is about all I can say. Hope it helps.
