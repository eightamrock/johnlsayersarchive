John Sayers1 May 2007#11
tb - slots work in the low mids area (150hz - 500hz) which is the area where the absorption of 703 type insulation starts to roll off. They also reflect high frequencies which 703 absorbs. In other words you can balance the absorption by using a combination of both. yes you use the average to find the frequency but in a wall as long as yours it would be wise to divide it up by adding divisions behind the slots so you could in fact target tow or three different frequency ranges. see attached pic cheers john
