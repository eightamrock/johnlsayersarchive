niceson20 December 2007#1
hello everybody, my name is Kurt, I live in Belgium and plan to build a small project studio. at this moment I have a mobile setup: I record band demos in rehearsal rooms, livingrooms, bedrooms, garages, ...I mix at home mainly on headphones since I haven't got a decent room with proper acoustics. in January our constructor will start the renovation of an old house we've bought and there will be a new garage to be built which is not connected to the house. Part of the garage will be my "playing ground". I've included a picture of our architect's plan, haven't got a scanner at this moment so sorry for the lousy quality. the design of the studio is not fixed yet meaning that the double door can move and that the separation between tracking and mix area can change. there's also a window but that can also move or eventually be left out. the dimensions of the room are approximately w=3.30m, l=5.80m, and for the height there is a special thing to be taken into account: the roof is slightly sloping from 2.60m at the outside to 3.40m at its maximum on the dotted line which runs across diagonally over the studio (hope this is clear). the complete garage/studio is built on a concrete slab and will be made of concrete cell blocks. so a few questions regarding design, there will be a lot more questions once I start construction. my goal is to: - have a decent mixing room with good acoustics - have the possibility to record vocals, acoustic and (loud) electric guitars. if possible it would be nice to be able to record drums (I know it's a very small space and I won't get great drum sounds but just to have the opportunity would be nice). - make it as soundproof as possible, closest neighbour houses are approximately 15m away, my own house is approximately 5m away. - it would be nice to keep the window to have daylight in the studio. - have good ventilation, there is no heating but I plan to have electric radiators or something. my questions are: - should I go for a 1 room studio or build a separate booth for vox, drums, guitars? - could any of the sketchup wizards over here share some ideas on a good design to get the best results as to isolation and acoustics. - since I haven't got a room for the band to chill out (only have a toilet) do you think it would be better to concentrate on the mix thing and continue to do mobile recordings with the occasional overdubs (like vocals and guitars) at my place. I can't accomodate a full band but 1 or 2 persons in such a small room should be ok). - I have seen the speakers being soffit mounted in small rooms, but is this really necessary? at the moment I own a pair of Yamaha HS-50 monitors, but I will probably upgrade in the future so soffit mounting is not such a good idea I guess since my next pair of monitors will probably be bigger. I hope I have everything covered, it's a lot of info so thanks a lot already if you've made it till here ;-) Finally: my budget for the build (isolation and acoustics) is about 6000 dollars. I will try to do the entire construction by myself with the help of my brother who is a carpenter. I bought Rod Gervais book but since English is not my mother tongue I find it very difficult to understand all technical terms and the building material is also very different in Europe. so thanks a lot for reading and hope to get some useful tips. greetings from Belgium! Kurt
