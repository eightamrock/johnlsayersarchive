Soundman20203 July 2018#2
Hi there " Midnight378", and welcome! :) I'm not aware of any books specific to designing a vocal booth, but there are several that deal with acoustics and studio design in general, and they would provide enough information for you to be able to design a vocal booth. I'd suggest two books: "Master Handbook of Acoustics" by F. Alton Everest (that's sort of the Bible for acoustics), and "Home Recording Studio: Build it Like the Pros", by Rod Gervais. The first one will give you the background in acoustics that you need to be able to design a studio, and the second one will give you the basics for actually designing it and building it. Also, look around the forum! There are many threads by forum members who have successfully designed and built their studios that do include vocal booths. You could probably pick up several tips from them. - Stuart -
