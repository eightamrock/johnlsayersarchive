petrovinksy22 April 2009#55
If i were you I would eliminate the hallways and just have one set of doors from the control room to the live room, the rooms would gain considerable volume and I'm sure you could find a ratio that would work. heres a post i have in my fav. folder about ratios. http://forum.studiotips.com/viewtopic.php?p=5570 also even if you couldnt follow a perfect ratio, without the hallways the added volume of the room will make up the difference sonically (plus some probably). as for splayed walls, they aren't that much harder to install especially for a skilled contractor they also dont take significantly more materials. so i suggest: - no hallways - bigger rooms - less sets of doors - non parallel walls
