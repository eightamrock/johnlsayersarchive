rocktavian6 September 2005#2
you shouldnt hang the bass speakers. for one they are heavy and they lose their low end in my opinion. If you position them under the stage you may need build a box to absorb some of the sound coming from the back. I would just put them under the stage and try them first but my experience has always been that the stage turns into one giant bass cabinet and the sound is washed out low end mud. i say trial and error. let your ears be the judge. if you get the exact dimensions i am sure these gurus can be alot of assistance as well.
