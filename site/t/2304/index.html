I doubt that the reflections from the pillars would cause any problems big enough to worry about. But since they are there, why not build an insulated enclosure around them. Plain old fiberglass insulation and covered in a nice fabric. This will eliminate any problems altogether, because it will stifle any high frequency reflections, which are really your only concern.
