gullfo17 January 2007#19
just some thoughts. if you go with the single CR approach because you're primarily doing the work yourself, and have just a couple of smaller rooms for isolation that you might need, that can work. if you need enough space for 5-6 musicians and equipment, plus have enough isolation (from noise) to get clean acoustic recordings, then maybe a slight smaller CR would seem to be a better choice with a big enough live room and a couple of booths. maybe putting some keyboards into the CR is good but maybe a booth for drums makes sense for both your use and other people you record? you could always move the drums into the live room (for example) if you needed to... its always tricky to come up with a design that works... even just for yourself... 8)
