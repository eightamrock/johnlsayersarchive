I was hoping to add material to an outside wall of my tracking room. It's residential type construction. 2x4 stud wall with 1/2" drywall inside, fiberglass bats insulation and wood siding with 1x6"s 16" apart on the outside. Concrete shingle roof. Kind of a cabin look. I'll see if I can post a pic.. I was thinking of taking off the 1x6"s and adding some material, maybe soundboard to the existing siding. screw that in and then another layer of siding screwed into the soundboard, and re-add the strips to preserve the original look of the house. would this give me more isolation. it's a distance to the neighbor, maybe 100feet. I don't worry as much about the other side since there are 3 interior walls and rooms. another problem is a 4x6' window and a door. I'm probably only as isolated as the worst leakage among those items but I'm working on sealing the window and the door. thanks
