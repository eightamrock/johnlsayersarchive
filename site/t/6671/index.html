If you're looking for the greates Isolation the answer is neither of the above. Personally - I would remove the existing drywall carefully cutting exactly at the edges of the framing. I would then remove the left over strips of drywall from the wood. The I would install the drywall pieces between the studs - up tight against the exterior sheathing - and caulk the edges........ I would then install 4 layers of drywall on the inside face of the wall and use green glue between the layers. I would not bother with green glue against the exterior becasue your weak link is the studs themselves. Rod

Form GG's website 17. How much do I need? Recommended application is 2 tubes of Green Glue per 4' x 8' area - 2 tubes per standard sheet of drywall. If you are not on a budget, utilizing 3 tubes per sheet will improve performance. Each case of Green covers about 192 square feet, or 128 square feet if used at the 3 tubes coverage rate. Do not use more than 3 tubes per 4' x 8' sheet as performance will actually decline above 3 tubes per sheet. You are just wasting your money! 18. I need to soundproof on a budget, how can I reduce the cost of a Green Glue room? Wonderfully, Green Glue performs at an only slightly lower level if you use it at 1.5 or even 1 tube per sheet instead of the recommended 2. about 70% of the raw damping performance is retained when using 1 tube per sheet, 3rd party tests coming soon. In fact, Green Glue can improve performance if used at even less than 1 tube per sheet.
