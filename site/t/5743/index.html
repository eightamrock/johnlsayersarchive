clicktrack6 March 2006#8
I seriously looked into Quiet Solutions when building the Click Track Audio mobile truck. As much as these guys were willing to help us, the price was outlandishly high. As well, they didn't have any suppliers in Canada meaning we'd pay through the nose for border issues. As well the requirements they placed on the construction seemed overly restrictive in order to get a real solution. That would have caused us EXTRA fees in overtime if we wanted our builder to adhere to all of the requirements. Whereas they would be able to build a standard isolated vehicle wall in, say, a day, it would take roughly 1.5 times longer to build the same wall following these techniques....we're doing this on a very limited budget and quiet solutions priced themselves out of the market. Interesting product...but too bad.

knightfly17 March 2006#10
Not necessary; your best bet to get the isolation you want at a price that won't kill your beer budget for the next two decades, is to follow the common mass-air-mass construction techniques covered here in nearly every thread. Standard drywall on double frames takes more time because of having to do multiple layers, but 3 layers of 4x8 5/8" gypsum wallboard only costs about $25 (per side, 4x8 section) vs. $125 or so, and 3 layers of rock on the outer sides of a double frame wall will kick any of the specialty products' ass... Steve
