Ro28 October 2008#2
1st. please adhere the forum rules 2nd. read stickies Having said that (it seems like one of THE mantras around here)... you need, indeed, to have your room acoustically dressed up correctly. Adding absorbers (esp. basstrapping in them corners) will make instruments sound more "tight" and less reverb/echoing/whatever. You'll end up with amps on 9 instead of 11. The drummer can still hit his skins hard as concrete (do you have a gorilla playing drums?) and still keep the sound "nice" and "focused". Check the acoustics forum and visit Ethan's site, there's great room dressing tuts in there! you're already in the right direction by adding panels and clouds, now go fill them corners ;)
