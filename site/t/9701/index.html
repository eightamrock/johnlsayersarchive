Chaotic5 November 2007#1
I framed my booth with 2x4s. Put 2 layers of drywall on the outside, 1 layer on inside. There is nothing between layers of drywall, just air. Then on the interior of the booth, Im putting 4" OC 703 wrapped in fabric. Should I have put insulation between the layers of sheetrock, or is it better to have an air pocket. How will the sound of the booth be effected and how will the isolation be effected. Im thinking it wont affect the sound, but not sure about the isolation.
bassman5 November 2007#2
You should put some sort of fluffy stuff ie R19 in the walls to dampen the resonance of the drywall. It will affect the TL of the wall. -ashley

Eric_Desart5 November 2007#4
Isn't stupid at all. I often wonder what people are talking about. TL = Transmission Loss, hence the isolation you search for. TL increases by adding absorption in cavity. :twisted: In Europe TL = R, but that shouldn't have helped you, just writing this.
