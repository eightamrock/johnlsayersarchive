gullfo21 September 2007#8
maybe do some measurements of the outside/inside sound/noise levels during various parts of the day. also, set up something inside your proposed studio space (drum/bass, or recording with PA) and take measurements of the levels inside and out. make measurements inside on low frequencies as well so you can determine what frequencies from the airport and trucks are penetrating your building the most. based on this information, you can calculate how much isolation you'll need - both from a general level and frequency specific levels. low frequencies are generally harder to stop and if there is too much of it, it can get expensive to solve effectively (which is where the multiple time of day measurements come in handy since it may affect your hours of operation if the budget isn't available).
