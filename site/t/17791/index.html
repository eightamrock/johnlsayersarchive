Hi Snyderman! I am not a moderator here but I'm going to try my best to give some advice based on the advice I have been given by other more experienced members here. One thing that definitely needs some clarification is your isolation needs. Are you building the inner leaf walls because you need more isolation from some other space (the rest of the house I'm assuming)? What are the current walls made of? I'm assuming concrete since you said it's in a basement. Just want to be sure. I can tell you for sure that you need to buy a SPL meter and do some tests so you know and we know how much isolation you actually need (in terms of decibels). Post those numbers ASAP! Honestly, until you know how much isolation you need, it will be hard to give much more advise. If you need a lot of isolation, the inner leaf will eat up more space and a decent room ratio will have to be determined based on those parameters. If all you need is a little bit of isolation, the inner leaf can be less substantial (maybe even just 2x4s with a single layer drywall...though that's not going to do much for low frequencies) and a decent room ratio will have to be determined based on those parameters. And by decent room ratios, I'm referring to tested ratios that provide a more even modal response in comparison to say a cube (which is the worst possible ratio). You might play around with this calculator a bit to decide where you're at (>>>>>>the ratio of your current room without an inner leaf is actually pretty decent as far as I can tell...definitely would need some input/confirmation on this from more experienced members though<<<<<<). Also, what is your budget??? This is very important information for the forum members here to be able to help you out. Without it, it's kind of impossible to know what is possible ( :lol: ) in terms of your build. Hope this helps some... cheers Trevor
