jhbrandt30 July 2010#5
Fox, Flush mounting is required if you need accuracy. 8) I would do things much differently with your layout. Are the dividing walls already there?? Basically your CR is a square. bad. then you slant your walls. You will have modal issues. I recommend that you orient the CR facing south in the drawing and use the entire 5m space for your length. Keep it rectangular except for acoustic treatment. TR to your left when mixing/monitoring. Entry to TR on left as well as viewing window. Enlarge the TR. Tell the band members waiting, to go outside to smoke.. ;) What are your TL requirements? what type of construction are you considering? Cheers, John
