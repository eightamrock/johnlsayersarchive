dymaxian27 March 2004#2
It's not that having 3 leaf systems are really bad; it's just not nearly as cost-effective... you don't get your money's worth out of adding the extra leaf. There are a lot of John's projects that are remodeling jobs, built into existing structures. The outer walls on some of them are pretty flimsy looking, and won't stop much sound from getting thru. In these cases, he wall-boards the inside of these existing outer walls, then builds the inner walls as the second leaf. Are these the projects you're looking at? Kase www.minemusic.net
