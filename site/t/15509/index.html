Hi all I'm in the final stages of putting my new room together.... At the front of the room I've got superchunk bass traps running from floor to ceiling in both corners. I've got loads of vinyl which needs to be stored in the room, and I was planning on building shelves for the vinyl which would sit in front of the bass traps. The shelves would cover around half of the bass traps width-wise, and around 40% vertically. In other words - the far side/half of them would still be exposed and so would the top half/60%.
