Peter, please keep all your posts about your build in one single thread: It helps us moderators (who sometimes have to follow dozens of threads from dozens of different people) to keep things straight about who is doing what, but more important it will also help you many months down the line, when you actually start building and need to find the answer to a question that you know you asked some place, but don't recall where.... Anyway, to answer this one: go with whatever gives you the highest mass at the best prices. If you find stuff that has very high density, then that's OK, but at the same time don't use stuff that is too thin, too flexible, or that has unusual resonant frequency characteristics. Some people even use sheet lead in their builds, which is several TIMES more dense than drywall. But for most people, the issue of cost also comes into play... - Stuart -
