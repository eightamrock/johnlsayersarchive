John Sayers6 January 2005#2
i suppose you really have to consider "what is a band rehearsal room?" In my experience people who go to the trouble of building a studio with a control room and live room end up rehearsing in the control room with the drummer in the studio and the doors open ;) everyone plugs into the console and listen through the monitors. Something you may wish to consider. ;) cheers john
