Your drawing shows 4 inches thick, which is what you really need. So hopefully 2" is just a typo. I'd also add more bass traps in ceiling corners.
I am curious what effect having the entryway "cubby" and bathroom will have on the acoustics.
Lack of symmetry behind you is not necessarily a problem.
is it ok to use Auralex acoustic foam behind the monitors
The front wall is not a particularly important place to treat IMO since your speakers don't face that way.
