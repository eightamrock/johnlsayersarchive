Hey Carl, in the room everyone worked on that John put the finishing touches on for me that I built, the sweet spot is about 5' from the front wall. I have a narrower room too. so the angle of the speakers play into that as well. Also I sit about 4-5" Inside the sweet spot when mixing. (or my head is place about 4-6" from the point at which the sound from both speakers converge.) My room is 13' deep from stem to stern. :) So to shorten the depth of the sweet spot, you need to play with the speaker soffit angles. Bryan Giles
