Alright, this is my first post. I've been recording for many years now, and have been visiting this site numerous times everyday, using the search, reading posts, etc. I've been recording bands for a few years here at my house, and while I'm getting pretty decent results, I think it's time to actually build a dedicated space. I own the home, and have never had any problems with neighbors, law enforcement, etc. I mainly record loud punk stuff, and I'm also a drummer. Currently I have my control room upstairs, and record drums downstairs where there are also a few closet iso booths for cabs. The new construction will use part of my basement, and include refinishing the one car garage so all the rooms will be on the same level. My goal is to have a control room, drum/live room, and maybe one iso booth if space permits. If I have room for a vocal booth, that would be rad too, but I could always record vox in the live room if need be. The Live Room will just be used for recording drums, so I won't need the room to double for band practice or anything like that. I don't want the live room to be too small though. I only play a 4 piece kit, but I'd like to have a little extra room if someone brings in something larger. I would like the control room to be a decent size. My desk is about 70" wide x 40" deep, and I'll need room for 2 19" racks of gear. I'm having problems coming up with a good layout for the rooms, where to build walls, etc. One reason for that is the two support beams always seem to mess up the configuration. I may just use that area for an iso booth. The other issue I have here, is fairly low ceilings. The ceilings are 7' 6" and there are a few small places that overhead pipes will only give about 6' 8" of headroom. I've put all this in my drawings below. I'm thinking I'll have to go with a drop ceiling because of access to the pipes in case of leaks? The outside walls are cinder block, and I plan to frame the walls and add insulation/drywall also. The garage door will be blocked in too, with probably a single door added there. Had a contractor over here the other day too, to ask about codes and all that good stuff. He said it would be no problem doing the build, so full steam ahead once I'm done the planning stage. I just started using sketchup, so don't laugh too hard :D Sketchup file included now too in zip below. And the wall in front of the washer/dryer and water heater, isn't there yet, so it can be whatever angle needed. Any guidance would be great. Thanks, Matt

Sigert4 January 2008#12
I've tried various combinations, and the one you suggested above as pic titled "basement 2d1layout2.jpg" seems like the most sensible solution to me. Only, I'd leave the path of separate vocal booth/drum room and go for the single live room approach, because you'll always maintain poor visual communication due to the supports standing in your way. As compensation, you could make flexible acoustic treatment. The kind where you can choose how live you want the room to be. There's some nice schematics on this forum. I'll post em when I find em. Cheers, Sigert
