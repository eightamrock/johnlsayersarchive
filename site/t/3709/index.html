knightfly14 May 2005#3
Florian's exactly right; you need to put ALL your mass in only TWO places; the OUTER wall and the INNER wall. With a 300mm air gap (insulated) and concrete, you should get really good isolation with a lot less cost. What you're proposing will actually cost you MORE and give you LESS isolation. Check out the REFERENCE section near the top of this forum for more explanation... STeve
