Ted White16 February 2011#10
Both open and closed cell foams are less desirable to open, fluffy batt or blown-in insulation. Using a foam to seal small cracks is fine, larger cracks need mass to seal as mentioned. Foam can conduct a vibration and offers much less accessible surface area for a sound wave to bump up against and lose energy. The insulation must provide resistance without the opportunity for conduction.
