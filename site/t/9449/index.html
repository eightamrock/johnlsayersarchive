Aloha and welcome to the forum,
I am starting to layout my studio in my basement and wanted to see what you guys think of the layout before I start putting walls in.
It looks like you've got a nice room to start with. :wink: You may want to reconsider the control room design, though. A few things to consider and establish are: - optimum room ratios/minimum modal issues - eliminating any parallel walls - left/right room symmetry (at least within a foot or two of ear height) for good stereo imaging. How tall are your ceilings? Perhaps this might be helpful - http://www.saecollege.de/reference_material/index.html (Be sure to click on the "acoustics" and "absorbers" tabs on the left and those that show up on the top) DIY Wall Units - http://www.johnlsayers.com/HR/index1.htm
