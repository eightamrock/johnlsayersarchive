gullfo29 January 2011#2
you can definitely get reflections off hard surface, but are they "mix killing"? probably not. that said, hanging the cloud high enough and angling the cloud sufficiently can help move the reflections. a 1.5" and 5.5" surface will reflect ~9Khz+ and ~2.5Khz+ respectively. if you're really concerned, considered adding a 1" layer of absorption over the face with openings for the lamps. you're more likely to have problems off the console and floor and adjunct surfaces closer to your head than the reflections off the cloud due to the angle where the sound hits your ears. ETC measurements will show the reflections, but your ears will behave differently.
