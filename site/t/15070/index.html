dbrane3 November 2010#1
Hey Guys, I am embarking on a project of creating a control room out of a 30'L x 30'W x 8'H room above my live room in my house. My Live room is 26'L x 25'W x 18'H, which actually sounds pretty great after applying treatment (clouds, traps, diffusion, etc.) but I'm a little perplexed when trying to apply Louden ratios to the potential control room, primarily bc the ceiling height is only 8'. It was an 11' peak ceiling but the city made me drywall in an attic space for fire or some other ungodly code requirement. So, alas I think I may be stuck with 8' for height. The Live room is on the other side of the South wall of where I'll be putting the control room. The control room will look down onto the Live room. My friend pointed out that is how Abbey Road is. I laughed... really hard. Anyway, the Live room is made of 8" concrete and a decoupled interior wall that has 3 layers of sheetrock. Needless to say, no sound escapes it. The proposed control room is not so lucky. It has typical wood construction with drywall already in place and sits over my garage. Its a decent size space at 30' x 30' but I will be adding a bathroom and closet space leaving me with 24' for width (3.5 of which is currently an open staircase from the floor below which I'm not sure how to deal with acoustically) [i]see attached drawing[/i]. I will also be using an oddly shaped room off of this room as the iso booth. Since I will not be cranking it in the control (no louder than normal loud mixing levels) I'm not concerned with sound escaping or coming in necessarily since I will be tracking in the Live room or Iso booth. Not to mention the neighbors are across the street to the East and that is where the bathroom and closet will go. I have no neighbors on the 3 remaining sides. The floor in the control room is Bamboo over Insulayment over 2" particle board. Walls, as I mentioned, are typical drywall hung on 16" wood studs. My budget for acoustic treatment and room modifications (excluding bathroom, closet, sliders with lam glass, fixtures, etc.) is around $3000 unless there is some magic pixie dust I can buy. Question 1) Am I totally screwed due to the 8' ceiling height? What are my options?? Should I just rip out the drywall ceiling to get an 11' peak and risk going to city planner jail??? :cop: Question 2) Is there a room dimension I should go with if I am stuck with the 8' height? I shouldn't have a problem getting it over 2500 cu ft of volume but I'd like to have a great sounding room not just a passable one. I considered a 20'L x 12'W x 8'H (1.00, 1.50, 2.50) but that seems a little small and definitely under the 2500 cu ft. Question 3) What and where do you suggest I place my mixing space, couch and treatment in this control room based upon an optimal dimension? Do I need to create angled walls, etc, etc.? (keep in mind this is my house so misuse of space is frowned upon by my wife and domestic use is rewarded :-) Also, I won't be mounting speakers as I'm more of a nearfield, NS10's or MicroMain 35 kind of guy. Question 3.5) I used a ton of Ready Traps bass traps in my Live room along with building some broadband absorbers and a bunch of diffusion tiles. That room is quite nice right now. Big but tight. However, I spent a ton of cash on them. Any cheaper but effective alternatives??? Thanks hugely in advance for your thoughts and advice. I did search the forum before posting. Amazing how many millions of results you get when you put in 8' ceiling. Please hit me with any questions whatsoever. Thanks again!!! Cheers, Dan
