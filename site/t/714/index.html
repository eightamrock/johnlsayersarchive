Forgot - the narrower the air gap, the more difference a slight change will make (inverse square law) Also, going from 2 layers to three isn't as effective as from one to two - mass law, double the mass = 5-6 dB, so to get the same improvement in dB from 1 to 2 layers, you'd need to go from 2 to 4. At this point, you'd probably be better off calling in the Redi-Mix truck... Steve
