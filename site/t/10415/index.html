Is it recommended to fill the entire "joist-space" with fiberglass, or is it recommended to leave an airgap. gullfo8 April 2008#2
depends on the space available. if you can put a layer on the upper floor and on the lower ceiling and leave an air gap, that's probably ideal. if not, then filling the space is the next best option to ensure both the upper floor and lower ceiling are dampened. just don't pack it tight.

Rune, you can always count on Glenn for good advice........ you really want to damp both surfaces if at all possible - and as long as you don't over pack (thus creating a bridge between the leaves) then you'll be just fine filling the entire cavity....... Rod
