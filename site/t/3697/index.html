The first two would be a total JOKE to try and run a full band through - when you include bass, you want 15" drivers and at least 200 watts power handling. The last one might do it for a while, but if you crank cheap speakers, you get the chance to buy ANOTHER set. What's your MAXIMUM budget for this? (Keep in mind that buying cheap almost ALWAYS means buying OVER) Steve
