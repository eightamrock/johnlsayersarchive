gullfo19 October 2007#2
my recommendation - work with some of the local construction supply companies including home depot, lowes, SPI, etc to donate the materials. find volunteer carpenters and electricians to help build it. move some of the "can't move" desks to extend the wall and make a vocal booth. inside the live room and the control room - add slat resonators. put in a window between the live room and control room. use glass windowed exterior grade doors between the rooms to allow visibility. visit guitar center and other large music supplies to get a snake or two, and some mics etc.

gullfo1 November 2007#10
use laminated glass - plexiglass is not dense enough and scratches easily. or you could just get solid exterior grade windows and mount a pair of them back to back with about 2-3" of air space. it won't win prizes for isolation but you're limited by the amount of isolation you can get on the walls anyways and construction will be easier than trying to build a dedicated control room window.
