Hi Folks, Here's the design for my new studio in dublin Ireland. Control room is situated on the far left, with a booth. Main entrance is through the triangular structure, with doors leading into the control room and live room a. Double doors in live room a. Adjoing wall between live room a and live room b (angled room at right of drawing) connect through a 2m62 gap. The Walls separating Live room a and b are supporting walls and are made of old black stone..Height is 3700mm (Apologies about the drawing..still trying to get used to sketchup...) Materials arrived today... Here we go... :shock:
