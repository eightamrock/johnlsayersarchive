Well, if it's just at the house, if you're only doing a few connections, and if you want to do it on the good, but on the cheap, here's what I'd do. Go to Radio Shack, get one of their bigger project boxes, like this. RS#270-1809 Drill holes in it for your jacks and your wire. Use Switchcraft or Neutrik Female chassis mount connectors, like the Switchcraft Y3FPC for XLR or you could use the Neutrik combo jacks for either XLR or 1/4". If you really are running only a few lines, it might not even be worth your money to buy multipair cable, it'd just make a tighter sealing hole when you run it through the wall. Solder 'em up and you're set to go. If you're really only doing one or two runs, you might just consider something like this. They work with a standard box and you wouldn't have to drill anything.
