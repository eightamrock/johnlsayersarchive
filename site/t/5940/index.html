Aloha Kallio, I seem to remember a post around here somewhere asking a similar question. If I remember correctly, if one were to build just the bezels for the monitors (using the correct formula for the width/height measurements) and support it on some kind of frame, fit the monitor through the bezel opening, it should work. I can't seem to recall where it is exactly, but I think Steve (Knightfly) might have been the one who came up with the solution. Also, you might want to check out Sjoko's semi-soffit bezels here: http://www.johnlsayers.com/Studio/Mainpage/MP-Sjoko.htm Be sure to click on all of the updates as well. Hope this helps, Aloha 8)
