John Sayers15 November 2004#5
The timber finish is just for looks. YOu could add a nother layer of plywood or MDF but a timber or timber veneer finish will look nicer. The plywood also separates the two boxes - the upper that holds the speaker and the lower the trap area. The hangers are made from celotex (soft fiberboard) wrapped in insulation and hung by wire or string. cheers john
