fallenmunk24 February 2011#10
John Dewers wrote:
But digging out eight feet of dirt around the perimeter of the house to most would seem somewhat extreme, don't you think?
Depends on how much moisture you are dealing with. If you have 50K you might consider hiring a backhoe operator for a quote. One thing that is going to limit how much deeper you dig the floor down is how high the water table is. If you build the floor in the dry season, and find out it is below the water table in the wet season, there is no sump pump or water sealer that will be able to keep it dry. Also, it might sound easy to dig down a few feet, but when you have to haul the dirt out by hand in buckets it becomes a major undertaking. I started out with a half basement and figured that it would take me a few weekends to dig the other half. It took one day every weekend for a year. I felt like I was in "Shawshank Redemption". Then I had the house lifted a few feet and poured a new basement floor on top of the old slab. That was the only way to get head room without getting into the water table. All new footings and foundation walls, all sealed inside and out. New perimeter drain system going into sump. Haven't seen a single drop of water in there since. Now, I'm just about ready to start buying some studio construction supplies as soon as the permits clear. So anyway, "somewhat extreme" is all relative. :) Munk
