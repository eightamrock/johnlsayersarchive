dymaxian27 February 2004#3
Greetings! The design does look pretty complicated, but it looks good. I wasn't going to comment on it because all I have to say is that it looks good. *shrug* As far as floating floors go, if you're not going to have live drums in here too often, you probably don't need to worry about it so much. If you have to turn your guitar amps up to get the right sound once in a while, you might annoy your neighbors, but if you warn them ahead of time it shouldn't be too bad. Depending on where you live, leaving off a heating/AC system may be a mistake. You're not going to want to drop what you're doing every 30 minutes to open the windows and get some air, are you? At very least, you should put a vent system in to change the air, whether it be heated or not. If you have in-floor heating, it may be enough for you to just exchange air with another room that gets fresh air current from outside. Rod Gervais may have more thoughts for you on this one... Good luck! Kase www.minemusic.net

Jai28 February 2004#5
Hey dreamer, Just my 2cents.... I think the room is cool, but my concerns would be this. 1. I would hate to have my session going on behind my back. You have the couch by the window to the tracking room, so what you will get is interaction between friends and band mates, but you will be stuck in front of the room hunched over a console/DAW not in the loop. you wont be able to see body language or facial expressions. And sooo much of capturing what is in an artists heart is in their body language. :D Can you flip the room around and put the couch behind you and you next to the tracking room? Plus by turning around you can make that space that was going to be a booth a big bass trap.... bringing me to my next concern..... 2. I really only see one very small bass trap. Are you traping in the ceiling as well? If not, I would make that booth in the front of the room a closet and bass trap. (Hanging) How tall is the ceiling? You could make the trap the width of the room but in the center only hang your traps down about 3 feet, on the sides go for full length. This will give you a broader absorber plus give you room to make the closet as well. But, If you do all this, it leaves you with only one booth. What are you mainly recording? Can you live with one booth? if not maybe we can come up with some other ideas. John is great at coming up with perfect fit stuff in bizzar spaces. Hope this helps a bit, jai www.themixstudio.com
