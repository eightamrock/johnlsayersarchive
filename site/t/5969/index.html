Greetings: This is my first post and thanks for this forum, John! A PDF of my studio is attached. As you can see, the desk and monitors face the recording area with nothing in between. I have built a gobo that is 4' x 6', with one side reflective and one side absorbing. Should I use this gobo to "divide" the room by placing it directly behind the monitors, with the reflective side facing me? The monitors are old Event 20/20 bas, front ported designs. So, since I can't position the monitors to take advantage of wall reflection, should I create reflection, or be happy that I don't have to deal with it in the first place? David French9 April 2006#2
Hi Blake, this is my first post here as well! (Hi to the few peeps here that already know me!) I would be glad that I ddn't have to deal with the reflection. There's no need to separate the rooms, and purposely introducing a reflector behind the speakers will only create boundary interference problems, and you don't want that. :D I would treat the space as a whole; what's best for tracking can be what's best for mixing as well.
