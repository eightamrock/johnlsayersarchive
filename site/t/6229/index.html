Hello all, Well construction on the new home and studio has been complete for about six months now and prior to moving in all the gizmos gadgets and hundreds of feet of cable, I mounted my foam to the walls with a spray adhesive (generic spray adhesive that is). The kind that comes with a bundle of foam off ebay. Thinking all adhesives were alike I mounted probably around 320 square feet of foam with this stuff and now.... you guessed it... it's all coming loose and falling off. Now just to test my application, I remounted one entire wall about 6 weeks ago using the same spray (tons of it) and it is coming off again. So I'm convinced the spray is crap seeing as how it was basically free with my order.. Now comes the hard part. I need to remount all the foam in the room with a spray that actually holds and will last for years upon years and here's the kicker... I don't want to diconnect every piece of equipment and move it all out just to put the foam back up. So I need a spray that doesn't have a huge "mist" to it. The thicker and webbier, the better. So I'm just wondering what everyone's experience was regarding foam mounting, and what you'd recomend in this situation. Thanks! -ToneMonkey

len-morgan18 May 2006#5
Did you follow the directions on the spray adhesive EXACTLY? Most of the spray on adhesives I've seen (Auralex, Scotch 80, etc), require you to apply the adhesive to BOTH items to be glued together, wait at least 60 seconds, and THEN put together (or press on the wall in your case. If you don't want to just spray a bunch of glue on the wall and hope it's in the right place/shape for your foam, spray an excess amount on the foam the very quickly press the shape against the wall so that some of the glue comes off and sticks to the wall. Pull the pieces apart and wait one minute, then reapply. As to the spray pattern, there should be an adjustment on the top of the can (moving the nozzle left and right) that adjusts the spray width. len
