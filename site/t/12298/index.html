jbassino16 April 2009#2
Why are those areas a problem? You can straddle the panels with no problems to those corners anyway. Like Ethan says, you can never have too many bass traps, so start placing them first on your front wall behind the speakers, then on your side, then at your back wall. Also put a couple or one on each corner of the room IF you cant put a trap on the back-right corner, then just leave it like that. Put all the panels and then listen how your room sounds, also , if possible, measure it after the panels are installed to see whats left to do. You can also put a couple of panels above your listening position. Cheers

Soundman202023 October 2010#5
Personally, if I only had ten of those, I think I'd do two diagonally across the front corners (vertically), two diagonally across the back corners (vertically), one on each side at first reflection points (vertically), one hanging either vertically or horizontally on the front wall between / behind the speakers, two overhead for a cloud, and one horizontally on the back wall somewhere. But just ten of those isn't much. It's a start, but you'll need much more. - Stuart -
