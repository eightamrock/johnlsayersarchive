I fnally got around to selecting pictures to show the construction progress of my small garden studio: http://www.flickr.com/photos/99314602@N ... 681739957/ The specification was that it had to look good and fit into our garden and that it could be reverted easily to a garden room or hobby room if we needed to sell the house. Acoustically, it needed to provide a decent space to play mainly acoustic instruments and occasional electric guitars not played at full crank and it would never have to track drums in it. I am currently fitting Rockwool rwa45 to the walls; then it's RC1 and then two layers of Gyproc - 19mm planking and 15mm acoustic board with a layer of GG in between. Any questions and suggestions would be gratefully received. 8) My budget was £15k - about US$23k and I'll just about do it for that. This includes the groundwork and base brickwork that accounted for about a third of that cost.
