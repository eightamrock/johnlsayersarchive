It's a tunnel-ish room, but that might work to your advantage. Build some wall units per John's design (via the link provided by Mr Giles) but try to make the back end of the room as live as possible. Put the drums at one end, and the mixing desk at the other. It's not a horrible situation, and you'll be able to do good work there if you spend some time on the room. The stuff you put in there doesn't even need to be permanent.
