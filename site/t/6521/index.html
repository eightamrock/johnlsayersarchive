John Sayers28 June 2006#2
Simone - it's natural that the linen closet is the quietest area because it's tucked away from the traffic noise. what I mean is : forget trying to soundproof the studio area as you don't have the money - even with $500. It doesn't matter so much if the work area has traffic noise, it's where you record that should take priority. What I'd suggest is you knock out the walls that make up the two closets - the two red lines in your drawing and seal that off as a soundproof booth like in the drawing. Then spend some $$$ on good insulation and plonk it on the wall as I've shown. cheers john
