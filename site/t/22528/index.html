gullfo29 December 2020#5
depending on the volume levels and frequencies, the structural transfer is going to become the loudest sound levels passed. so using the "ideal assembly" should be used and consider either a third drywall layer or a viscoelastic layer between the two. on the "interruption" side - consider changing how the walls butt, (presuming the perpendicular wall is not a support wall for the isolation wall), to each other to carry the drywall (isolation drywall) across to complete as much sealed mass space as possible. the fasteners should not have air penetration since you'll be alternating the layers and staggering the screws as well as sealing each layer, those should not be a problem. :-)
