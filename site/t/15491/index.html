Soundman20202 March 2011#7
The floor is concrete (dry), and I really want to build this as little as possible to keep as much headroom as possible.
You don't need to "build" the floor at all! What you have is fine. Concrete is good flooring, acoustically. If you don't like it aesthetically, then you could put down laminate flooring, which takes up very little space (vertically).
How much clearing do I need between the two structures - minimum?
Enough so that it cannot ever touch, under any circumstances. Maybe a couple of cm. The important part for acoustic isolation is that you have at least 4 inches (10cm) between the two LEAVES, but the distance between the FRAMES only needs to be enough that the are not in contact, and never can be even considering vibration, swaying, sagging, tremors, etc. - Stuart -
