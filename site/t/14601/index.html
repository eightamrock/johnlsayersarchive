xSpace22 July 2010#2
If your exterior walls are concrete, explain this: "Live Room is a Room in Room design of Staggered wood 2X6 construction." Your exterior is the first barrier for a room-in-a-room, the interior wall being the second. If you have a mechanical room, code is going to want a fire rated partition and quite possibly proper venting if you have gas/ open flame. I think that local code is going to dictate how you do this, which may well wind up being a multi leaf.

Thanks Xspace I must tell you, your comment of: "Your exterior is the first barrier for a room-in-a-room, the interior wall being the second" is such a straight forward and simple answer to this question (and I've seen this question posted on many discussion forums). Thanks so much for the answer. My design is now three out of four walls with two layers of 5/8 + GG and ample air space + insulation, then the exterior walls are concrete block (of my building) and brick on the older buildings attached. The 4th wall is a staggered design with 2 layers of 5/8 and GG on one side and one layer of 5/8 on the other. There's a large airspace (up to 30" for code reasons) then the exterior block wall has been sprayed with foam for a vapor barrier (I understand that this has virtually no STC properties) Thanks again. The project is coming together... Brian
