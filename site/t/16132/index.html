Hello everyone! Let's kick it off with a little introduction. I'm a 26 year old guy from Finland, living in Malmö, Sweden at the moment. I moved here because of a special someone, and because trying to make a living in the industry in Finland is.. well, let's just leave it at that! :D Anyway, the time has come now that my journey towards my own studio has begun for real, and due to some of the ways things work around here, I have to go with things in this order: Find place Get a rough sketch of what the layout could look Get accepted by the landlord Confirm financing Build I can't unfortunately affect that order. :/ So, now I am in the phase where I am looking at different spaces, and I'd like to run them by you good people here, as you are way more knowledgeable in this matter than I am. Ok, let's move on to what I want in general: - Control room - Live room OR 2 smaller booths [I mainly do popular music / urban styles / pop-rock, so getting huge airy drums is often not needed at all] - Some little space to have a simple desk in, to do office stuff on - A little place for talent to sit outside of control room I realize I can't have all that, depending on space. But those are the "in optimal situation" things I would like to have in the place. Generally I will need good isolation, especially for the live room(s). I hardly ever play music so loud in the control room that massive isolation would be needed. Again, place dependant of course, but like I stated first, it's impossible for me to change the order in which I have to do these things. Budget is about 32k€ US dollars, converted from the Swedish Krona. It's a bit on the low side, yes... but that's what I have to do this with. I can extend it a tiny bit, but I'm keeping that aside for costs that will arise. What am I forgetting? Am I forgetting something? RIGHT! I'm attaching floorplans of the place, in the state that it is now, and the state that it could be in after I take down existing structures. The landlord asked me to come up with a sketch on what the new plan would look like, so that he can think about it. (I have several other places in the pipe too, I just don't have pics of them yet, and this place is at an amazing location etc.) The place is 74m2 or 796sq. feet if I understand correctly? I don't unfortunately have measurings of the place yet, but I will get them ASAP if the landlord accepts the sketch, and you guys think this place is good. There are neighbors on the "top" side of the drawing and on the left side of the drawing. The weird looking thing on the right side of the toilet is a small kitchenette, and I would love to keep it there :P I'm not allowed to demolish any load bearing structures, move water lines etc. Anyway! Do tell me if I am forgetting something, and I'll do my best to fill in what I'm forgetting! And do tell me straight up if you think this place is not good!
