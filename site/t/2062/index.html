Why the plastic, that won't really stop any serious sound. It all depends on how much isolation you need, how much space you have and how much money you want to spend. The USG Handbook and Complete Section stickies in the Studio Construction section will be your primary resouce. I suggest after getting a design set up you post all wall building questions to the Studio Construction so they will get answered faster. If you have the money you could contract with John Sayers like I have to custom design your area. He has a couple of different wall layouts depending on your situation.
