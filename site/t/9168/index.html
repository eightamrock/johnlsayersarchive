rod gervais6 August 2007#4
Reality is that the connection is such a small amount of the whole that the TL loss from the coupling doesn't amount to a hill of beans. It's one of the areas that I continnually tell you in the book - if you have to live with it - live with it. NOW - I will also point out that my concern for this really comes down to when double 2x4 walls are used - as I do not trust a single 2x4 structure to satisfy the load requirements for one of these doors. In that case I insist in using the frame as a double brace - and count on the 2 wals to keep the frame intact. However, if you are using double 2x6 or 2x8 construction then I have no problem with a split frame being used. I have a details for a double door assembly using split door frames with 2x6 walls if anyone could use them. Sincerely, Rod
