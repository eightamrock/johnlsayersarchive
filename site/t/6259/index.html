knightfly20 May 2006#5
In fact, these days a "2x4 doesn't even START at that size; newer "head rigs" (sawmill term for the saw that actually cuts the logs into boards) is all computer controlled, and is no longer a circular saw but a large band saw. The thinner blade allows the wood to be cut to less than 1/8" over the finished size of 1.5" x 3.5", the kerf (thickness of cut, a function of blade thickness) is typically only a little under 1/8". Only in cabinet making are "stated" dimensions the same as "actual" dimensions these days... Steve
