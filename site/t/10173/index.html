jwl25 February 2008#4
That's a small space, shocker. But if that's what you have, then you can make it work. Arrangement wise, a good starting point is to be facing the 7' wall, 38% of the way back from the front wall. This should give you space for a desk in front of you, as well as a table etc. behind you for keyboards and gear. Depending on how much stuff you have you will be cramped. Don't forget to think about acoustic treatment.... a room that small will need a lot (proportionately) of bass trapping, and I'd recommend a fair amount of absorption to keep things under control.
