Wow homestudiogallery, you sure have a lot of stuff in a small area! I would have to say, IMHO, that for your control room you would want to go with colors that weren't bright or loud as it may be. That is the room you are going to spend a great deal of time working in and I think that brighter colors, such as bright oranges, yellows, greens would make for an atmosphere that was tiring to work in. The "coffee" or earth shades are very neutral and soothing and therefore a good choice. Also those colors make it relatively easy to match furniture. I also think that some of the darker colors like burgundys, dark blues, olives, and such in medium to larger rooms can work very well. The shade of the wood and/or stain should also be complemented by the color you choose for the room. I also think that the lighting in the room plays a big part in the actual color of the room. Therefore I think having lighting with different "scenes" available is necessary. I prefer to work with the lights a little dimmer so that they are not "screaming at me" while I am working. However, care doe need to be taken with selecting dimmers so as not to cause hum or interference with your equiptment.
