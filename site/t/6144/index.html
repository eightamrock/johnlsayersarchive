len-morgan2 May 2006#2
How are you calculating your 2" air gap? It is supposed to be measured from the back of the outside wall (the one closest to the inside of the room) to the back of the first layer of drywall (or whatever) on the inside wall. In other words, it's not the gap between the two frames but the gap between what you hang ON the frames. It sounds like you're on the right track with the possible exception of the "sound board." I believe it's been determined here that you get more for your money with plain drywall. You also want to make sure that the two walls never touch in any way (with the possible exception of the insulation between the studs). Since you already have a slab, the walls will share a floor but that should be the only place. The inside structure should be strong enough to stand on it's own. len

More like 7"... 2x3 lumber is 1 1/2" x 2 1/2" nominal. 2 1/2" + 2" + 2 1/2" = 7". Personally, I wouldn't mess with 2x3s -- I'd use 2x4s.
