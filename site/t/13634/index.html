slowjay wrote:
Hello, I plan to compress the needed mass of (in this case 10lbs perhaps?) r30/20 whatever fiberglass into a chicken wire type form for my corner traps, two 4ft tall traps per corner.
I doubt you will gain a lot, except a lot of additional work. The main cost item of mineral wool is the amount of spinned fiber, hence the price per weight/mass unit (within the same brand and type) Less dense material is cheaper per volume unit, but you need more (and vice versa). It's not completely linear with amount of fiber, since there are some fixed or volume related costs (handling, transport).
