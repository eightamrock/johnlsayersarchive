SonicClang15 December 2006#9
Yeah man, I was NOT expecting color photos. I look through that paper all the time, and I hardly ever see color photos. And yeah, I really wish I had the time to put up the trim work around the window, but if you knew how insanly busy I was the two weeks leading up to that interview, you'd think it's amazing I had it looking as good as it ended up looking. I even took a week off work so I could work and finish it up. I was literally working 20 minutes before the guy showed up. It was the most stressful time of my adult life, hands down. So yeah, when I look at the photos now, I have to remember that. When the guy contacted me about the studio, it was still a total construction zone... very messy, no paint, no mud, ceiling wasn't done... and when the guy showed up it looked pretty good.
