left-right corner absorbers with different volume?

Started by Thrawn on 30 August 2006. 2 replies.

Originally posted at johnlsayers.com, topic 6944.

Thrawn30 August 2006#1
Hello, in our studio (see my first post at http://www.johnlsayers.com/phpBB2/viewt ... 610d854e31 ) - currently under construction - we want to place two splayed walls at the front of our control room (see picture below). In the corners we want to put corner absorbers. On the right side we have an entrance door made of glass that we want to keep there to let some daylight in. So we are thinking about a removable solution for the splayed wall in front of the door (thin black lines) that can be put in place in critical situations (e.g. mixing). Our plan is to build a wall element that can be shifted into the cavity of the right corner absorber. Without treating the left corner accordingly this would result in two cavities with different volumes. Now my question is: Does this have any negative effect, especially on the acoustical symmetry of the room? For example, could there be a bass frequency shift between the two corners? Or would this effect be not worth mentioning it? If this problem is already discussed in another thread, please forgive me for double-posting ... in this case I'd be glad if you could post me the link. Thanks a lot Ben
