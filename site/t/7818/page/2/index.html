gullfo21 January 2007#33
you had also asked about putting a door in the wall in sketchup... one way is to draw a rectangle on the wall surface and extrude (push) to the opposite face to cause it to be removed. then using the push/pull tool, adjust each wall face within the hole you just made to size it for the door you want (to me this is useful in existing walls). generally I like to draw the floor plan first then extrude it to 3D. so in the following series of images I'm first defining the location of my door within a wall, then extruding the wall, then marking the top level of the opening and extruding the top section over the door to complete the opening. then just pop in the door... remove lines to cleanup as you desire. note: if you draw the walls using rectangles or the offset tools correctly, even angled walls will extrude evenly across the top of the door opening. if you're drawing in the details of the wall - framing, insulation, rc, and sheetrock, its much more complex so make sure you've worked out the design using simple objects in sketchup first... as far as resizing once you have the door in place - remove the door (if its a component you can delete it, if its a group you probably want to move it), you can move the opening by using the push/pull tool to put the opening where you want then you re-add the door (its in the existing components in model assuming you used a components...)
