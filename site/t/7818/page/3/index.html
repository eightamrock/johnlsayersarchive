The final design will be a twin leaf construction exactly as per John’s recommendations on the SAE site and of course Rod’s book. Plus, as already mentioned, I’ll need separate drawings for all electrics, HVAC, audio tie lines, and plumbing etc. :roll: It is my sincere hope that by then my sketchup skills will have improved enough to just get on with it, and then post designs for approval, improvement, or moderate alterations. :twisted: Then, and only then, will I feel like I have enough detail behind me to take on Knightfly, and Sharward (to name just 2 of the ‘Gurus’) in the construction forum! 8) And now for a couple more screenshots as I’m out of the frame (level of attention-wise), from tomorrow!
