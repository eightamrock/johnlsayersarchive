lovecow6 September 2007#2
Ceiling tile material is typically similar to 703/705 - usually a mineral fiber of moderate density. However, I would think that the tiles would be more expensive than if you just bought the raw FG or mineral fiber boards? Also, depending on how they're faced (on both sides) might affect (for good or bad) their performance as "bass traps". That aside, there'd be nothing wrong with making "bass traps" out of them. Strapping three of them together would give you almost 2". IMO and depending on where/how you plan to mount them in the room, you might consider at least 6 of them to get close to 4" thick.

lovecow7 September 2007#7
Rolled insulation (like the "pink stuff" or equivalent) typically has a density on the order of < 1 lb/ft³. This is inferior, in many respects, to something like 703/705 in terms of effectiveness for treating low-end problems. However, in thicknesses greater than 12", uncompressed, can be useful. If you want to compress it and use several layers, it will provide better performance, but then it becomes difficult to make it look like anything presentable. :? And, again, by time you get enough to compress and layer, you're probably getting into the neighborhood of buying 703 or 705 outright for the same $$.
