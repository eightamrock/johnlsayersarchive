Thanks! The room is 2,5m high and the sides are 3m by 4m. Maybe my first decision would be speaker placement. For such a small space I would like to consider soffit mounting the 8030´s... I heard of soffit mounting the much bigger 8050A´s, but how it´ll work with 8030´s is unclear. At least there´s that 6dB dip switch :) Here´s what I see as the advantages of soffit mounting (in addition to the improvement in monitoring): 1. Saves space ! 2. Can be built into a basstrap ( I´ll have one in all corners ) 3. It will act as the reflecting end of the room with all the hard surface around the speakers ( the opposing wall will be absorbing) This should be in line with the "dead end / live end" concept recommended for recording acoustics. 4. The usual improvements in frequency balance and stereo imaging with soffitmounting. So what do you think? Am I on the right track? Is soffit mounting the 8030A´s even sensible? Just to provide more info: -No need for sound insulation, 24/7 permit to be as loud as I want. -Budget around 1000€ -One door opposite to the speakers, no windows.

Hai indoorfella! 3x4x2,5 is a very tiny room. adding a soffitmounted speaker system seems like wasting space imho. dunno..
