knightfly22 March 2006#3
I'd NOT use drywall anchors (at least not the plastic ones) for hanging ANYTHING overhead, period. One way is to map out beforehand where your cloud will go, and if necessary add an extra RC or two where you can get to them with screws; then screw whatever hooks you intend to use to the ceiling, going thru the layers of drywall and into the RC. Depending on the total weight of your cloud, it shouldn't take much extra to support it. It may help to NOT sink the screws you use for this right at first; instead, leave about 1/4" gap, put a daub of caulk around the screw where it enters the drywall, and THEN finish driving the screw. If you have good insulation in the ceiling and don't miss the RC with your screws, I don't think you'll notice a difference in isolation from this... Steve
