gullfo8 April 2008#2
good first effort! sounds like a cool project. one suggestion though - if you're going to build from scratch - foundation and all - you should really look at what space options you have to ensure you're not cramping up access points - such has the CR airlocks - which in an emergency (as well as general access) can be a problem - esp if you have an idea on commercial use - consider a bathroom, ADA compliance, etc. probably a good idea to sit down with the town building officials and talk things through to make sure you have any of their requirements in hand so you don't get surprised later. if you can build it all on one level, that probably makes things easier as well - a spiral staircase can get tricky... consider separated or floated foundation sections to maximize isolation. i'd add a "reception/waiting" area (next to a bathroom...) so people have a place to sit when not involved in a session, place to hang a coat, get a soft drink etc.

Yes, an "inside-out" wall IS the interior wall. It's just build inside out. So the drywall is in the air-cavity towards the exterior wall while the wool is on the inside of the room (for absorption IN the room instead of in the air cavity).
