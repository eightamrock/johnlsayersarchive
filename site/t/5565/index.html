knightfly10 February 2006#6
No real difference, other than gravity - you need different ways of keeping the insulation in contact with (and not sagging to the bottom of) the walls. Otherwise, the physics doesn't change just because it's vertical instead of horizontal. The term "Studio Grade" insulation.. means that person either doesn't know or is trying to sound like they do - for years, OC 703 was the "standard" for studio insulation, because it will work for ANYTHING. Maybe that's where the term came from... However, thanks largely in part to our Canadian friends and their tireless experimentation we now know it isn't the BEST for in-wall use, the lighter stuff improves overall isolation when full-range sound needs to be blocked. HTH... Steve
