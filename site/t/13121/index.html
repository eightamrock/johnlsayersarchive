The Room sits on floor joists (which have been reinforced with a 9x 3 beam underneath to cater for the added weight being placed on them, this work was overseen by the engineer). 1 layer of ¾” osb sits on these joists. The perimeter walls have 2 layers of ½” plasterboard added to their original composite, all sealed airtight. These walls will be forming the outer leaf of a room within a room structure. Room Dimensions: W 12’ X L 24’ X H 7’8” @ the apex of the vaulted roof. Floor Square footage: 288sq feet The Roof: The space between the rafters has been beefed up with 3 layers of ½” plasterboard (see photo), all sealed, etc. I will be stuffing fiberglass insulation in the remaining cavity and hanging 2 layers of ½” plasterboard on resilient channels attached to the rafters @ 16” centers (this will form the inner leaf of the room within a room). The added weight to the roof structure was not an issue for the engineer…

Soundman20204 June 2009#6
I'm with Ro on this: shoot your speakers down the long axis of the room, and try to get the symmetry as close as you can. RFZ="Reflection Free Zone". It means angling walls and ceiling, and strategically placing absorption, such that no first order reflections hit the mix position at all. Such that at the mix position all you get is direct sound straight from the speakers, with no reflections coming at you off the walls, ceiling, floor, console, desk, furniture, or anything else. - Stuart -
