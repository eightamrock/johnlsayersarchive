FYI (no question, discussion maybe) Something I learned today from one of the USG articles: If you have two rooms you should not build a partitian wall on a shared floating floor, because noise flanks through the floating floor. Better is to have the partitan wall to the real floor, and a floating floor within each room (walls are not on the floating floor). Better still is to build two isolated rooms, each with their own floating floor.
