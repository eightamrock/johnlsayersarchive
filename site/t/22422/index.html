DanDan9 June 2020#2
Is that a window behind the speakers? To minimise SBIR I suggest moving the speakers to almost touch the wall, or even the window. Bass is non directional. Higher frequencies are much easier to treat, so don't sweat symmetry. Studiotips SuperChunks are sometimes built 'lite' 24" wide. Very disappointing in my experience. Go 32" Or much better go for square or rectangular corner traps. You could even hide that HVAC in one to visually match the other side. Perhaps HVAC ducting, damped, is a great bass trap.....
