Electrical and wiring

This section gathers forum discussions on the electrical infrastructure of home and project recording studios, with an emphasis on grounding, wiring, and power distribution. The most active topic is star grounding, a scheme for connecting audio equipment safety and signal grounds back to a single point to reduce hum and noise; multiple threads work through its principles, practical wiring, and points of confusion for newcomers. Other threads cover related power questions, including whether a single-phase electrical service is adequate for a separate studio building, how to size circuits and calculate amperage for equipment loads, and how to wire isolated circuits with dedicated ground lugs. Additional posts address the physical routing of cables, such as passing lines between isolated rooms without compromising sound isolation, terminating snake cables for multi-room I/O, and running headphone feeds to multiple spaces. A general resources thread collects external links on studio wiring and installation. Together these threads reflect the concerns of builders wiring a studio from scratch: minimizing electrical noise, meeting power demands safely, and organizing cable runs between rooms.
