Materials reference

This section gathers technical reference material on the building materials commonly used in home-studio construction, drawn from the forum's ongoing discussions of acoustic treatment and wall assemblies. A curated "Reference Area" thread indexes links to wall-construction terms, nominal lumber sizes, and weights and densities of common materials, serving as a starting point for newcomers. Several threads compile hard data: measured absorption coefficients for Owens Corning 701, 703, and 705 fiberglass at varying thicknesses and densities, and tabulated weights of standard drywall panels converted between imperial and metric units. Other threads clarify terminology and equivalence, explaining that "703" is a trademarked name for a specific density of Owens-Corning fiberglass rather than a unique product, and that mineral wool can serve as an acoustically comparable substitute. A separate discussion addresses health concerns raised about synthetic mineral fiber products, weighing claims of increased cancer risk against available information. Together these threads function as a practical reference shelf for builders comparing insulation products, sourcing construction materials, and evaluating safety considerations before purchasing.
