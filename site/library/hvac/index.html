HVAC and ventilation

HVAC systems present a persistent challenge in studio construction: conditioning and ventilating isolated rooms without introducing airborne noise or compromising the isolation the walls were built to provide. Threads in this section cover the full range of approaches, from basic DIY questions about ducting, fans, and whether gas or water connections are needed, to detailed silencer box and baffle designs intended to quiet airflow through rectangular ductwork and joist cavities. Several builders discuss split-ductless and humidity-controlling air conditioners as alternatives to full ducted systems, along with heat- and energy-recovery ventilation for exchanging stale air with fresh air in sealed rooms. Smaller vocal booths and iso booths raise their own concerns, since limited space makes silencing airflow while preventing a stuffy "sweat box" feel especially difficult. A few threads also address ventilation code requirements, including how commercial building codes and UK regulations for air exchange apply to converted spaces. Together these discussions form a practical reference for anyone planning how to move air through a soundproofed studio without letting noise move with it.
