Build diaries

This topic collects first-person build diaries chronicling home and project studio construction from planning through completion. Contributors document real-time decisions on framing, isolation, flooring, and insulation as their builds progress, often over years and hundreds of pages, with community members offering technical feedback along the way. The projects span a range of spaces and structures, including garage conversions, basement builds, a barn conversion, and a shipping-container studio, reflecting the variety of spaces forum members have adapted into working studios. Several threads, such as Sharward's Partial Garage Conversion and the Artifact Recording basement floor thread, rank among the archive's most-viewed and most-replied topics, indicating their value as long-running references others returned to for guidance. Readers can follow a build diary as a continuous record of one project's construction challenges and solutions, or dip into individual pages for specific techniques, such as floating floor installation over concrete or acoustic hanger placement in soffits. Together, these diaries offer a practical, thread-by-thread history of how forum members actually built their rooms, complementing the more theoretical design discussions found elsewhere in the archive.
