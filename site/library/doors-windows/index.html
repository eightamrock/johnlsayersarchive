Doors and windows

Doors and windows are among the most vulnerable points in a studio's isolation shell, since any opening in a wall or door assembly threatens to undo the mass and decoupling built elsewhere. Threads in this section cover both problems together. On doors, members compare commercial "acoustic" doors against home-built alternatives using solid-core or beefed-up stock doors, discuss installing a door between a studio and adjoining living space, and work through how doors should be handled where two isolated walls meet, including whether a door is needed on each leaf of a double-wall gap. Sourcing airtight seals, such as automotive rubber gasket stock, and troubleshooting construction problems with leakage-prone door and window assemblies also come up. On windows, discussions address acoustically sealing existing windows without changing their exterior appearance, and step-by-step guidance for installing new studio windows using setting blocks, glazing tape, and caulking. Together these threads offer both conceptual guidance on isolating door and window openings and practical, hands-on troubleshooting for builders working through the same problems.
