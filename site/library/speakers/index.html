Speakers and soffits

This topic collects discussions on soffit mounting and flush mounting of studio monitors, a technique in which speakers are built into a wall or soffit rather than left freestanding. Contributors weigh the acoustic rationale — reducing nearby boundary reflections and comb filtering, particularly at low frequencies — against practical construction questions such as enclosure isolation, cabinet angles, and the maximum recommended downward tilt for a soffit. Several threads address flush-mounting freestanding monitors that were not designed for soffit installation, including methods for isolating cabinet vibration and filters that compensate for the loss of "baffle step" boost when a speaker is pulled into a wall. Others troubleshoot problems after construction, such as monitors losing midrange and high-frequency definition once soffited. Specific monitor models discussed include Adam S2A and A7, Mackie HR824, and Klein & Hummel O300, alongside a DIY reference-monitor build. Readers will also find soffit planning folded into broader room designs, from garages and basements to dedicated mix rooms.
