Acoustic treatment

This section gathers forum discussions on acoustic treatment: the panels, bass traps, and diffusers used to control reflections and low-frequency buildup in recording and control rooms. Contributors document do-it-yourself construction methods, most centered on rigid fiberglass or mineral wool (such as Owens Corning 703/705) framed in wood and wrapped in fabric, including budget panel builds, "superchunk" corner bass traps, and slat-type Helmholtz resonators and diffusors, with one thread correcting a published resonator formula. Other threads address specific applications, including vocal booth treatment, acoustic hangers for suspending panels, and rockwool density and fabric covering choices. Several long-running threads follow individual studio projects through extended treatment and tuning phases, while others debate technical questions such as the "edge effect" in absorption coefficient measurements. Together these threads range from quick reference how-tos and material lists to in-depth technical debate, making this topic a practical starting point for readers planning or troubleshooting the acoustic treatment of a home or professional studio space.
