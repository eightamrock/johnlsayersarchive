Walls, floors and ceilings

This section gathers forum discussion on framing and assembling the walls, floors, and ceilings of a decoupled recording studio. A recurring theme is leaf design: threads compare double-leaf and triple-leaf wall construction, including cost trade-offs and construction details for corners where multiple leaves meet. Members also discuss the air gap between leaves — whether insulation should fill it, how wide it needs to be, and how it affects STC ratings — along with insulation choices such as fiberglass density inside stud cavities. Floating floors receive extensive debate: several threads question whether a floating floor is actually necessary for isolation, particularly on concrete slabs, and weigh alternatives such as cutting or isolating the slab, or using a smaller floating drum riser instead of floating an entire room. Practical construction questions round out the topic, including the sequence for hanging and caulking drywall on a framed leaf. Full studio build logs, from rural New England to a garage bonus room, show these framing, insulation, and decoupling choices being worked out in real projects.
